# Curriculum Spot Review

Reviewed 2026-10-02 as a focused quality check of one math and one science/STEM assignment from each expanded Grade 4, 5, 7, and 8 folder. This is not a standards audit, classroom test, source-link audit, or exhaustive validation of these tracks.

## Files checked

- Grade 4 math: [`multi-digit-math-workshop.md`](../curriculum/grade-4/math/assignments/multi-digit-math-workshop.md). The ten listed operations are answerable and internally consistent. Checked results: 6,245; 3,328; 8,365; 4,537; 6,657; 4,173; 252; 348; 312; 882. Added concrete submission directions, prerequisites, the verified educator key, and three brief review criteria.
- Grade 4 science: [`water-cycle.md`](../curriculum/grade-4/stem/assignments/water-cycle.md). The four requested stages and Sun-energy explanation make a concise, suitable diagram task. It has no answer guidance or rubric; the interactive is described as educator-provided rather than linked to a shipped tool.
- Grade 5 math: [`fraction-operations-studio.md`](../curriculum/grade-5/math/assignments/fraction-operations-studio.md). The listed common-denominator, scaling, and division operations are mathematically sound (including $3/4+1/3=13/12$, $1\,2/3+3/8=2\,1/24$, and $1/3\div3=1/9$). The fifth prompt incorrectly switched from lemonade to a dessert pan and contained a placeholder-like “Learner” repetition. Corrected it to divide the remaining $1/3$ quart of lemonade among three people and updated the response template. No worked key is included.
- Grade 7 math: [`surface-area-design-studio.md`](../curriculum/grade-7/math/assignments/surface-area-design-studio.md). The open-ended equal-volume box design is mathematically coherent, but the brief is a short blank prompt: it does not provide an example, specify a common volume or dimensions, or include a worked check. Learners need prior volume and surface-area formulas; state these prerequisites and add an optional worked example or educator check.
- Grade 7 science: [`forces-and-motion-design.md`](../curriculum/grade-7/stem/assignments/forces-and-motion-design.md). The variable-control and three-trial structure is a reasonable starting investigation, but the prompt does not specify an observable outcome to measure, a data table, or an interpretation check. It claims a broad investigation of force, friction, and mass while asking learners to choose just one variable; narrow the stated goal or provide separate variable-specific options. The generic safety boilerplate mentions hot water and lasers despite no such materials in this activity.
- Grade 8 math: [`pythagorean-theorem-studio.md`](../curriculum/grade-8/math/assignments/pythagorean-theorem-studio.md). Checked results are consistent: the roof rafter is 15 ft; each collar tie is 7.5 ft; triangle sides are 6, 8, and 10 km with perimeter 24 km; $D$ is closer to A ($\sqrt{82}<\sqrt{90}$); and the 3-D diagonal is 13 m. Corrected a doubled backslash before `\Delta` so the triangle symbol renders properly. The challenge set includes no answer key or explicit prerequisite reminder for square roots and coordinate distance.
- Grade 8 science: [`newtons-laws-investigation.md`](../curriculum/grade-8/stem/assignments/newtons-laws-investigation.md). The interpretation note correctly warns that ideal fixed-angle ramp acceleration does not decrease merely because mass increases, and that target slide distance alone does not establish impact force or momentum conservation. Preserve that caution. The lab still asks for impact-velocity estimates without giving a measurement method and needs an educator-approved alternative dataset or simulation if timing, scales, ramps, or collision apparatus are unavailable. There is no answer key.

## Follow-up

Add concise educator keys or check criteria for calculation tasks, state prerequisites in each brief, and replace broad or ambiguous investigation goals with measurable questions, controlled variables, and interpretation guidance. Review source links, accessibility, local standards, and hands-on safety separately before assigning. These eight files were spot-checked only; other grades, subjects, quizzes, and linked resources were not reviewed.
