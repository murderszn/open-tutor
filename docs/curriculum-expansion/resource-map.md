## Alignment Sources

Use national frameworks as reference points and verify individual codes/descriptions during each audit. No state or district was specified, so these are proposed pathways; high-school science and social-studies order varies by system.

- [Common Core mathematics](https://www.thecorestandards.org/Math/) — numerical reasoning, operations, algebra, geometry, statistics, and modeling.
- [Common Core English language arts](https://www.thecorestandards.org/ELA-Literacy/) — reading, writing, speaking/listening, language, and disciplinary literacy.
- [Next Generation Science Standards](https://www.nextgenscience.org/search-standards) — grade/band expectations integrating scientific practices, disciplinary ideas, and crosscutting concepts.
- [NCSS C3 Framework](https://www.socialstudies.org/standards/c3) — inquiry across civics, economics, geography, and history; it does not prescribe one national grade-by-grade content sequence.

These framework landing pages were opened on 2026-10-01. Specific alignment remains to be researched and documented per unit.

Never copy an internal guide's “current” population, government officeholder, market price, or scientific constant without checking its date and source. Keep historical snapshots labeled with their original dates. Record dataset column definitions, units, source, and whether values are real, rounded, or fictional practice data.
