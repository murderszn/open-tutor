# Contributing

OpenTutor is parent-operated learning material. Contributions should make a teacher's next action and the learner's evidence clear. Check repository guidance in [AGENTS.md](./AGENTS.md), use first names only, and keep private student information out of public files and examples.

Do not add learner submissions, grades, real schedules, identifying details, or cross-grade assignment links. Grade-specific material belongs in exactly one canonical `curriculum/grade-<grade>/` folder. Shared assignment material must be grade-neutral.

## Lesson and resource review checklist

Before adding or substantially revising a lesson, assignment, or grade resource, confirm:

- [ ] The goal describes observable learning, and the intended grade or range is clear.
- [ ] Important prerequisite knowledge is named, with a brief readiness check or a way to review it.
- [ ] The task gives concrete learner steps and produces visible evidence (work sample, explanation, model, data, or performance).
- [ ] A teacher check, answer guidance, or rubric explains what successful evidence looks like and how to respond when it is missing.
- [ ] Any grade adaptations change the task or expected reasoning, not just the grade label.
- [ ] Required materials, adult supervision, offline options, and accessibility choices are stated when relevant.
- [ ] References are reputable, age-appropriate, and attributed. Links are descriptive and relative for repository files.
- [ ] Claims about grade-level or standards coverage are scoped to what the content actually teaches; update a coverage note when the collection's limits change.
- [ ] Indexes and cross-links are updated, and relative paths resolve.
- [ ] Markdown headings, lists, tables, CSV formatting, and HTML links render as intended.

Curriculum contributions belong in exactly one matching grade folder under `curriculum/grade-<grade>/<subject>/`. Do not link assignments across grades or add learner work. Follow the expansion layout and review requirements in [the curriculum plan](./docs/curriculum-expansion/README.md) and [unit requirements](./docs/curriculum-expansion/unit-requirements.md). K–8 profiles in [the planning hub](./resources/k-8/README.md) are supplementary; the matching grade track's audit/scope and sequence is authoritative.
