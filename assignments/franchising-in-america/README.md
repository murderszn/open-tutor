---

## Why This Matters

Franchising is one of the most common ways Americans own businesses. About 1 in 8 businesses in the U.S. operates as a franchise, and together they generate over $800 billion per year. Understanding how franchises work gives you a real-world foundation for thinking about entrepreneurship, economics, and community wealth building.
