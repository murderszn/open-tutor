# 🚀 Solar System Tour

**Subject**: STEM (Astronomy)
**Goal**: Design a travel brochure for three planets in our solar system, explaining their unique characteristics to future "tourists".

## 📋 The Mission

Congratulations! You've been hired as the lead Travel Agent for *Intergalactic Tours Inc*. Your job is to convince humans from Earth to take their next vacation on another planet.

### Your Tasks:

1. **Pick Three Planets**: Choose three planets from our solar system (excluding Earth).
2. **Design a Travel Brochure**: You can draw this on paper or use Google Slides/Docs.
3. **For Each Planet, Include**:
   *   The name of the planet.
   *   How long it takes to get there (rough estimate based on distance from the Sun).
   *   The weather they should pack for (temperature and atmosphere).
   *   One major "Tourist Attraction" (e.g., The Great Red Spot on Jupiter, the rings of Saturn).
   *   A fun slogan (e.g., "Mars: It's out of this world!").

| **Information** | Includes accurate facts (weather, distance, attraction) for all 3 planets. | Includes facts for 2 planets, or some facts are missing. | Missing many facts or inaccurate information. |
