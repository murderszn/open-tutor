# 💪 STEM Assignment: Strength Training Basics

**Subject:** STEM / Physical Health  
**Grade Level:** 5th–8th Grade  
**Equipment:** Flat surface, water bottle (optional weight)

---

## 🎯 What You Will Learn

- What a push-up, squat, and dead lift are
- Proper form to avoid injury
- How to count reps and sets
- Why strength training helps bones and muscles


**Core Repo Resource:**
- **[Student Guide: Physical Activity Fundamentals](../../resources/physical-activity-fundamentals.md)**
 — What is Strength Training?

Strength training means using your own body weight or light weights to make muscles stronger. You do a movement several times (reps), rest, then repeat (sets).

---

## 🔬 Part 2 — Warm-Up (Required!)

Do each for 30 seconds:
1. Arm circles — big forward and backward
2. High knees — march in place, lift knees high
3. Butt kicks — walk while kicking heels to glutes
4. Torso twists — sway side to side

---

## 🔬 Part 3 — Push-Ups

**Form:**
- Hands on floor under shoulders
- Body straight like a plank
- Lower chest to floor (or as low as you can)
- Push back up

| Set | How many? | Did I finish? |
|-----|-----------|-----yes----------|
| 1 | | |10
| 2 | | |

**Modification:** Drop to knees if needed. That's okay — it still counts.

---

## 🔬 Part 4 — Squats

**Form:**
- Feet shoulder-width apart
- Lower like sitting in a chair
- Keep knees behind toes
- Stand back up

| Set | How many? | Did I finish? |
|-----|-----------|------yes---------|
| 1 | | |11
| 2 | | |

---

## 🔬 Part 5 — Dead Lifts (Body Weight)

**Form:**
- Feet hip-width apart
- Hinge at hips — push butt back
- Keep back flat and flat, chest up
- Lower torso until you feel a stretch in hamstrings
- Squeeze glutes and stand back up

| Set | How many? | Did I finish? |
|-----|-----------|---------------|
| 1 | | |
| 2 | | |

**Modification:** Don't go as far down. Focus on form, not depth.

---

## 🔬 Part 6 — Cool-Down Stretch

Hold each for 15 seconds:
1. Arm stretch — pull one arm across chest
2. Hamstring stretch — touch toes (or shins)
3. Quad stretch — pull one foot behind you

---

## 💬 Part 7 — Reflection

1. Which exercise was the hardest?
2. Which was the easiest?
3. What is one way to make each exercise easier or harder (progression)?
4. Why does rest between sets matter?
