# 🌳 STEM Assignment: Daily Outdoor Activity

**Subject:** STEM / Physical Health  
**Grade Level:** 5th–8th Grade  
**Platform:** Outdoor / Home neighborhood

---

## 🎯 What You Will Learn

- Why vitamin D matters
- How to observe nature scientifically
- How to track heart rate before and after activity
- How 20 minutes outside helps focus and mood

---

## 🔬 Part 1 — Vitamin D Facts

Type this into a search to learn (write down 2 facts):

```
why do humans need vitamin D from sunlight
```

**Questions:**
1. What does vitamin D help your body do?
2. What time of day is best for vitamin D?

---

## 🔬 Part 2 — Heart Rate Check

1. Sit quietly for 1 minute.
2. Find your pulse on your wrist or neck.
3. Count beats for 30 seconds. Multiply by 2 = resting BPM.
4. Now do 20 jumping jacks.
5. Count again for 30 seconds after exercise.

| Time | Beats / 30 sec | x2 = BPM |
|------|----------------|----------|
| Resting | | |
| After jumping jacks | | |

---

## 🔬 Part 3 — Outdoor Observation

Go outside for at least 20 minutes. Write down:

1. 3 things you saw (trees, birds, bugs, etc.)
2. 2 sounds you heard
3. 1 thing you touched or felt (wind, sun, grass)
4. Was the temperature warm, cool, or hot?

---

## 🔬 Part 4 — Neighborhood Activity

Pick ONE:
- Walk around the block once
- Play catch, basketball, or soccer
- Ride a bike or scooter
- Help a neighbor with a small task (with permission)

Afterward, answer:
1. What did you do?
2. How did your body feel after?
3. Did your mood improve?

---

## 💬 Part 5 — Reflection

1. Why is outdoor time important, even on busy school days?
2. Would you rather do this in the morning or afternoon? Why?
3. What outdoor activity would you do every day if you could?
