# U02 Formative Quiz — The Constitution, Federalism, and Institutional Powers

**When:** end of Week 10 (W10S4), before the unit assessment. **Time:**
20 minutes. **Format:** closed-notes except the Lesson 04 text-only
alternative. 10 items, 20 points. Answers and scoring notes are in
[answer-key.md](../answer-key.md) — never beside these questions.

1. (2 pts) Classify each power as enumerated, reserved, concurrent, or
   denied: (a) coining money; (b) licensing drivers. Give the clause or
   amendment for each.
2. (2 pts) State Marshall's *McCulloch* test for an implied power as
   three gates, in your own words.
3. (2 pts) Under *Gibbons*, what did "commerce" include, and what
   happened to New York's steamboat monopoly?
4. (2 pts) In *Marbury*, why did the Court refuse Marbury's request —
   and what power did it claim by refusing?
5. (2 pts) State the Supremacy Clause's rule in one sentence, and the
   Tenth Amendment's rule in one sentence.
6. (2 pts) Distinguish a categorical grant from a block grant, giving
   one example of each.
7. (2 pts) A federal law orders state police officers to enforce a
   federal program, with no money attached. Name two doctrines from
   this unit that limit or analyze that order, and state what each one
   says about it.
8. (2 pts) Article V: name both proposal paths with their fractions,
   and both ratification paths with their fractions.
9. (2 pts) Show the arithmetic: how many House votes, Senate votes, and
   state ratifications are needed to amend the Constitution? (Explain
   each rounding.)
10. (2 pts) Name two forms of informal constitutional change and give
    one example of each.

**Readiness signal:** 16+/20 with items 2, 4, and 8 substantially
correct indicates readiness for the unit assessment. Below 12/20, or a
blank on item 8, triggers re-teach of Article V and *Marbury* before
the assessment (see [teacher-guide.md](../teacher-guide.md)).
