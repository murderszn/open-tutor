## federalism-town-scene.png

- **File:** `federalism-town-scene.png` (PNG, raster; the only image in
  this unit's `assets/`).
- **Prompt (verbatim):** "A flat educational illustration for an
  11th-grade civics lesson, clean simple style, plain light-cream
  background, high contrast, no text, no letters, no numbers, no words
  on any building or sign. A single American town scene with four
  clearly distinct public buildings side by side along one street: on
  the left a small local town hall with a flagpole and a fire truck
  parked beside it; next to it a public school with a playground and a
  school bus; next to it a larger state capitol building with a dome;
  on the right a tall federal courthouse with columns and stone steps.
  A paved road, a bridge, and a mailbox row run along the street in
  front of the buildings. A few simple adult and student figures
  walking on the sidewalk, faces simple or turned away, no distorted
  faces. Each building visually distinct in size and style. No labels
  or words anywhere in the image."
- **Tool:** Muse built-in image generation (`media.generate_image`).
- **Date generated:** 2026-10-21.
- **Learning purpose:** Used in
  [Lesson 04](../lessons/lesson-04-federalism-in-practice.md),
  Activity A ("Match the building to the level"). The learner studies
  the scene, matches each building to a level of government, names one
  service each building represents, and names one service that needs
  two levels working together. The same image returns in the
  [unit assessment](../assessments/unit-assessment.md) (Task 4) so the
  learner re-analyzes a familiar source under assessment conditions.
- **Factual review (2026-10-21, inspected before linking):** All four
  buildings match the prompt — a small town hall with flagpole and fire
  truck; a school with playground and bus; a domed capitol; a columned
  courthouse with steps — plus a road, bridge, and mailboxes. No
  readable text, letters, or numbers appear in the image; no distorted
  faces. The image is symbolic, not a depiction of any real town,
  government, or event — it is labeled an AI illustration in the
  caption and in the lesson. Nothing in it asserts a factual claim
  about any real place; it is a reasoning prompt, not evidence. The
  flag on the town hall is drawn without readable detail and carries
  no text.
- **Caption:** "An AI-generated illustration of one street, four levels
  of government at work: town hall and fire service (local), school
  and bus (local/state), domed capitol (state), columned courthouse
  (federal). Which level runs which service — and which services need
  more than one level?"
- **Alt text:** "Four public buildings along one street: a small town
  hall with a flagpole and a fire truck; a public school with a
  playground and a school bus; a larger domed state capitol; a tall
  columned federal courthouse. A road, a bridge, and mailboxes run
  along the street."
- **Text-only alternative (used in the lesson and assessment):** "From
  left to right along one street: (1) a small town hall with a
  flagpole, a fire truck parked beside it; (2) a public school with a
  playground and a school bus; (3) a larger state capitol building
  with a dome; (4) a tall federal courthouse with columns and stone
  steps. A paved road, a bridge, and a row of mailboxes run along the
  street in front of the buildings." The question can be answered from
  these descriptions alone.
- **Known limitations:** Stylized flat illustration; building sizes
  are symbolic (a real federal courthouse is not necessarily taller
  than a state capitol), and the scene compresses four levels that in
  reality are rarely on one street. The lesson says this explicitly so
  no learner mistakes relative size or co-location for a factual
  claim.
