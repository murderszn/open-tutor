# U01 Formative Quiz — Foundations of Constitutional Government

**When:** end of Week 6 (W6S4), before the unit assessment. **Time:** 20
minutes. **Format:** closed-notes except the triptych text alternative.
8 items, 20 points. Answers and scoring notes are in
[answer-key.md](../answer-key.md) — never beside these questions.

1. (2 pts) Name the three theories of political legitimacy from Lesson 01
   and write a one-phrase definition of each.
2. (2 pts) In Locke's social-contract theory, what are the government's
   *only* legitimate job and the *source* of its authority? (Two short
   answers.)
3. (3 pts) The Declaration's argument has three parts. Name them in order
   and state what each part does in one sentence per part.
4. (2 pts) List two specific powers the Articles of Confederation
   *withheld* from Congress, and for each name the historical problem that
   exposed the gap.
5. (3 pts) Madison (Federalist No. 10) and Brutus (No. 1) disagreed about
   whether a *large* republic could stay free. State each one's position
   in one sentence, then name the mechanism each one relied on
   (Madison's mechanism; Brutus's mechanism).
6. (2 pts) Name the five constitutional principles from Lesson 05. For
   *checks and balances*, give one concrete example (which branch checks
   which, and how).
7. (3 pts) Article V provides two ways to *propose* an amendment and two
   ways to *ratify* one. Name all four, with the required fractions.
8. (1 pt) In one sentence: what is the strongest *internal* tension in the
   Declaration's claim that "all men are created equal"?

**Readiness signal:** 16+/20 with items 3, 5, and 7 substantially correct
indicates readiness for the unit assessment. Below 12/20, or a blank on
item 5, triggers re-teach of the ratification debate before the
assessment (see [teacher-guide.md](../teacher-guide.md)).
