# Resource Pack — Unit 01: Foundations of Constitutional Government

**Goal:** Give a grade-11 learner (ages ~16–17) direct access to the
founding-era primary sources and reputable explainers behind this unit's
lessons, with free no-account routes for every core objective.
**Checked:** 2026-10-14. Links marked *(opened)* were opened and verified
on that date; links marked *(search link)* are clearly labeled
preferred-channel searches, not verified items.

## Focused queries

Use these verbatim in a search engine when you need more than the pack
lists:

1. `site:archives.gov founding fathers "declaration of independence" transcript`
2. `Federalist No. 10 Madison faction "extended republic" full text`
3. `Brutus No. 1 Anti-Federalist "large republic" full text`
4. `Shays's Rebellion 1786 causes articles of confederation`
5. `Article V amendment process propose ratify steps`
6. `three-fifths compromise 1787 constitutional convention explained`

## Videos (labeled)

No video below replaces the primary-source readings; all supplement them.
Where an exact episode could not be verified, a labeled channel search is
given instead, per the Resource Pack format.

- *(search link)* **Khan Academy — US government and civics: "Foundations
  of American democracy"** — search `site:khanacademy.org US government
  foundations of American democracy`. Free, no account; high-school level;
  covers natural rights, the social contract, and the founding documents.
- *(search link)* **CrashCourse — U.S. Government and Politics playlist
  (PBS Digital Studios / Complexly YouTube channel)** — search
  `CrashCourse U.S. Government and Politics playlist`. Free, no account;
  fast-paced overviews of the Constitution, federalism, and the Bill of
  Rights. Adult previews for tone.
- *(search link)* **National Constitution Center — "The Constitution"
  video resources** — search `site:constitutioncenter.org constitution
  videos founding`. Free, no account; nonpartisan civic-education
  organization.
- *(search link)* **iCivics — game and video library** — search
  `site:icivics.org games constitution`. Free; interactive games (e.g.,
  on the branches of government) that reinforce Lesson 05. No account
  needed for most games.

## Web references

1. *(opened)* **National Archives — Declaration of Independence
   transcription:** https://www.archives.gov/founding-docs/declaration-transcript
   — the authoritative public-domain text used in Lesson 02.
2. *(opened)* **National Archives — Constitution transcription (with
   amendments):** https://www.archives.gov/founding-docs/constitution-transcript
   — used in Lessons 05–06; shows original spelling and amendment links.
3. *(opened)* **National Archives — Articles of Confederation:**
   https://www.archives.gov/historical-docs/articles-of-confederation —
   adoption dates and context used in Lesson 03 (full Articles text via
   the linked Our Documents page).
4. *(search-result verified 2026-10-14)* **UCF Pressbooks, *American
   Government 2e* — Federalist Papers #10 and #51:**
   https://pressbooks.online.ucf.edu/pos2041bw/chapter/federalist-papers-10-and-51/
   — open-textbook full text of Federalist No. 10 (Madison, Nov 23, 1787),
   used in Lesson 04. Free, no account.
5. *(search-result verified 2026-10-14)* **The Constitution Society —
   Anti-Federalist Papers "Brutus":**
   https://constitution.org/1-Constitution/afp/brutus00.htm — full text of
   all sixteen Brutus essays (No. 1, Oct 18, 1787), used in Lesson 04.
   Free, no account.
6. **National Constitution Center — Interactive Constitution:**
   https://constitutioncenter.org/the-constitution — nonpartisan,
   scholar-written clause-by-clause explainers; useful for Lessons 05–06
   extension work. Free, no account. *(Listed as a recommended starting
   point; not opened this run — verify before assigning.)*
7. **Annenberg Classroom — "The Constitution":**
   https://www.annenbergclassroom.org — free lesson resources and the
   *Guide to the Constitution* from a reputable civic-education program.
   *(Recommended starting point; not opened this run.)*
