# Unit 05 — Resource Pack

**Goal:** give the learner and the adult verified, free, no-account
starting points for every task in this unit. **Age fit:** grade 11
(U.S. government and civics); all sources are official, academic, or
established civic-education publishers. **Checked:** 2026-11-11
unless noted otherwise. Re-check every link and every figure before
assigning — officeholders, court composition, and budget figures
change.

## Free, no-account alternatives for core learning

Every core objective is reachable without an account or a payment:
Congress.gov, house.gov, senate.gov, archives.gov, cbo.gov,
supremecourt.gov, the Federal Register, and the National Constitution
Center are all free and require no sign-in. The YouTube search links
above are free to view (advertising may appear; the adult previews
each video). If video access is unavailable, the lessons stand alone:
every video task has a text-based equivalent in the lesson itself
(the diagram, the EO 9981 excerpt, the FY2024 data table).
