# Week-12 Quiz — Unit 03 (Lessons 01–03)

**Time:** ~30 minutes. **Total:** 25 points. Take in W12S5, after
Lessons 01–03 and before Lesson 04. Answers are in
[answer-key.md](../answer-key.md) — kept separate from these questions.

1. **(2 pts)** Define civil liberties and civil rights in one sentence
   each, and give one example of each.
2. **(2 pts)** What did *Barron v. Baltimore* (1833) hold, and which
   amendment later provided the mechanism for applying the Bill of
   Rights to the states?
3. **(3 pts)** Name three incorporation cases with the right
   incorporated and the year, from three different decades.
4. **(3 pts)** State the *Brandenburg* test in full (both prongs).
   A speaker urges a crowd to "rise up someday and overthrow the
   system." Protected or unprotected? Apply both prongs.
5. **(3 pts)** State the *Tinker* test. A school bans a student's
   silent armband protest because "other students might be upset."
   Does the ban survive *Tinker*? Why or why not?
6. **(3 pts)** Name two categories of speech that receive no First
   Amendment protection, with the case or test for each. Name one
   category people often wrongly believe is unprotected.
7. **(3 pts)** A city bans all rallies in its central park after 9
   p.m., applying the ban to every group. Is the rule content-based
   or content-neutral? State the three-part test that applies and
   apply it.
8. **(3 pts)** Explain the tension between the Establishment Clause
   and the Free Exercise Clause in two sentences, and state what
   changed in establishment doctrine in *Kennedy v. Bremerton* (2022).
9. **(3 pts)** A governor orders a newspaper not to publish a story
   about a state contract. What doctrine governs, what is the
   government's burden, and which case is closest on point?
