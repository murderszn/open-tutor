# Resource Pack — Unit 03: Civil Liberties, Civil Rights, and Equal Protection

**Goal:** Give a grade-11 learner (ages ~16–17) direct access to the
constitutional text, the founding-era and court sources, and reputable
explainers behind this unit's lessons, with free no-account routes for
every core objective.
**Checked:** 2026-10-28. Links marked *(opened)* were opened and verified
on that date; links marked *(search link)* are clearly labeled
preferred-channel searches, not verified items; links marked
*(search-result verified 2026-10-28)* were confirmed in search results
on that date; links marked *(recommended starting point; not opened
this run)* are listed for the adult to verify before assigning.

## Focused queries

Use these verbatim in a search engine when you need more than the pack
lists:

1. `site:archives.gov founding-docs bill-of-rights-transcript First Amendment`
2. `site:constitution.congress.gov selective incorporation Fourteenth Amendment Gitlow`

4. `Tinker v Des Moines 1969 "schoolhouse gate" substantial disruption`
5. `Brown v Board of Education 1954 "separate educational facilities are inherently unequal"`
6. `Shelby County v Holder 2013 section 4 coverage formula section 5 preclearance`

## Web references

1. *(search-result verified 2026-10-28)* **National Archives — The
   Bill of Rights: A Transcription:**
   https://www.archives.gov/founding-docs/bill-of-rights-transcript
   — the authoritative public-domain text used in every lesson
   (First Amendment; ratified December 15, 1791). Free, no account.
2. *(search-result verified 2026-10-28)* **National Archives — The
   Constitution: A Transcription:**
   https://www.archives.gov/founding-docs/constitution-transcript
   — Fourteenth Amendment text (Citizenship, Due Process, Equal
   Protection Clauses) used in Lessons 01, 04, and 06. Free, no
   account.
3. *(search-result verified 2026-10-28)* **Congress.gov, Constitution
   Annotated — Application of the Bill of Rights to the States
   Through the Fourteenth Amendment and Selective Incorporation** —
   search `site:constitution.congress.gov selective incorporation
   table` — Library of Congress table of incorporated provisions with
   decisions (Gitlow 1925, Near 1931, De Jonge 1937, Cantwell 1940,
   Everson 1947, McDonald 2010) used in Lesson 01. Free, no account.
   (The essay URL carries a search-highlight suffix; use the search
   route above to reach the current essay page.)
4. *(search-result verified 2026-10-28)* **National Park Service —
   1954: Brown v. Board of Education:**
   https://www.nps.gov/articles/brown-v-board-of-education.htm
   — the unanimous decision and the "separate educational facilities
   are inherently unequal" holding, used in Lesson 04. Free, no
   account.
5. *(search-result verified 2026-10-28)* **U.S. Department of Justice,
   Civil Rights Division — The Shelby County Decision:**
   https://www.justice.gov/crt/shelby-county-decision
   — the official summary: §4(b) held unconstitutional June 25, 2013;
   §5 itself not ruled on; preclearance inoperative without a
   formula. Used in Lesson 06. Free, no account.
6. *(search link)* **Oyez — *Brandenburg v. Ohio* (1969), *Tinker v.
   Des Moines* (1969), *Brown v. Board of Education* (1954),
   *Shelby County v. Holder* (2013)** — search `site:oyez.org
   Brandenburg Ohio` (and likewise for Tinker, Brown, Shelby County).
   Free, no account; plain-language case facts, holdings, and opinion
   summaries for Lessons 02, 04, and 06. The adult confirms the case
   page before assigning.
7. **National Constitution Center — Interactive Constitution:**
   https://constitutioncenter.org/the-constitution — nonpartisan,
   scholar-written clause-by-clause explainers; useful for Lessons
   01–06 extension work. Free, no account. *(Recommended starting
   point; not opened this run — verify before assigning.)*
