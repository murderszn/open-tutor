# Week 18 Quiz — Unit 04: Elections, Representation, Participation, and Political Parties

**Time:** ~25 minutes. **Closed book** except the turnout table in
Question 9, which is provided. Answers are in
[answer-key.md](../answer-key.md) only.

1. A state has 11 House seats. How many electors does it have? Show
   the formula.
2. Name the two states that do **not** use winner-take-all allocation
   of electoral votes, and state how they allocate them instead.
3. What happens if no presidential candidate reaches 270 electoral
   votes? Name the body that chooses the president and the voting
   rule it uses.
4. A state has 3,000,000 people and 5 House seats. What is its ideal
   district population? A proposed district holds 660,000 people:
   how many percent over the ideal is it?
5. Define **packing** and **cracking** in one sentence each.
6. What did *Rucho v. Common Cause* (2019) decide, and what did it
   explicitly **not** decide?
7. A Super PAC airs an ad supporting a candidate, designed with no
   contact with the campaign. A second Super PAC airs an ad built
   from footage shared in a private strategy meeting with the
   campaign. Classify each ad's spending and state the rule that
   separates them.
8. Name two functions of political parties, and give one concrete
   example of each.
9. Use this table (Census Bureau, citizen voting-age population,
   reported voting): 2020: 66.8%; 2018: 53.4%; 2022: 52.2%; 2014:
   41.9%. (a) Compute the 2020→2022 drop in percentage points.
   (b) Is 2022 turnout closer to 2018 or to 2014? Justify with
   numbers.
10. State one difference between pluralist democracy and elite
    democracy, in one sentence each.
