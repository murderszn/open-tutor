# Resource Pack — Unit 03: Energy, Work, Power, and Conservation

**Summary (adult planning judgment):** Free, no-account
resources that support a grade-11 learner's work–energy
reasoning: work and its sign, kinetic and potential energy,
conservation and nonconservative losses, efficiency, power, and
spreadsheet energy budgets. **Checked 2026-10-27.** Videos
supplement the written lessons; they do not replace them.
Captions are required on all videos the adult assigns.

## Access notes

- Every core objective has a free, no-account route: the
  written lessons plus PhET (no account) and the internal
  references. Videos are supplementary.
- The adult previews every video for advertising, comments,
  age fit, and captions before the learner opens it.
- If a simulation is keyboard-inaccessible for the learner, use
  the worked data sets in Lessons 04 and 06 (bar chart and
  budget table) with the same analysis demand.
- No resource in this pack requires an account, a purchase, or
  a download beyond a browser.
