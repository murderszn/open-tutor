# Week-04 Formative Quiz — Unit 03 (Session S18)

**~25 minutes · 20 points · closed book.** Show working with
units. `g = 9.80 m/s²`. Answers are in `answer-key.md` (adults
only) — do not open it during the quiz.

1. (2 pts) A force of 50 N pushes an object 4.0 m in the
   direction of motion. How much work does it do?
2. (2 pts) A force acts at 90° to an object's displacement.
   How much work does it do? Why?
3. (2 pts) A 3.0 kg object moves at 4.0 m/s. Find its kinetic
   energy.
4. (2 pts) A net work of 90 J acts on a 5.0 kg object starting
   from rest. Find its final speed.
5. (2 pts) A 4.0 kg object sits 2.5 m above the datum. Find
   its gravitational potential energy.
6. (2 pts) A spring with `k = 80 N/m` is compressed by
   0.25 m. Find the stored elastic potential energy.
7. (2 pts) A 1.0 kg ball is dropped from rest from 5.0 m
   (air resistance ignored). Find its speed just before
   hitting the ground.
8. (2 pts) A system starts with 500 J of mechanical energy
   and ends with 350 J. How much energy became thermal
   energy?
9. (2 pts) A device takes in 800 J and delivers 240 J
   usefully. Find its efficiency.
10. (2 pts) A motor does 1200 J of work in 6.0 s. Find its
    average power.

**On-track score: 16+/20.** Missed clusters are re-taught in
the same session before the unit assessment (S19).
