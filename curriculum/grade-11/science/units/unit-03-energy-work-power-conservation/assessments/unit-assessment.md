# Unit Assessment — Unit 03: Energy, Work, Power, and Conservation (Session S19)

**28 points · one sitting · closed book.** Show working with
units and signs. `g = 9.80 m/s²`. Answers are in `answer-key.md`
(adults only).

## Task 1 — Work (3 pts)

A rope pulls a crate with a force of 150 N at 60° above the
horizontal while the crate moves 10 m horizontally. Kinetic
friction of 40 N opposes the motion. Find (a) the work done by
the rope, (b) the work done by friction, and (c) the net work.
(`cos 60° = 0.500`.)

## Task 2 — Work–energy theorem (3 pts)

A 2.0 kg object moves at 3.0 m/s and then experiences a net
work of +40 J. Find its final kinetic energy and its final
speed.

## Task 3 — Conservation (4 pts)

A 0.60 kg ball is dropped from rest from a height of 8.0 m
(air resistance ignored). (a) Find its gravitational potential
energy at the top (datum at the ground). (b) Find its speed
just before hitting the ground. (c) At a height of 4.0 m, find
its kinetic energy and its speed.

## Task 4 — Spring energy (3 pts)

A spring with `k = 250 N/m` is compressed by 0.12 m and then
released, launching a 0.050 kg ball horizontally (ignore
gravity during the short launch). Find (a) the stored elastic
potential energy and (b) the launch speed.

## Task 5 — Nonconservative forces (4 pts)

A 3.0 kg block slides down a rough ramp from a height of
1.2 m and reaches the bottom moving at 2.0 m/s. Find (a) its
initial potential energy, (b) its final kinetic energy,
(c) the energy converted to thermal energy, and (d) the
ramp's efficiency as a PE → KE converter.

## Task 6 — Power (2 pts)

A winch lifts a 25 kg mass 10 m vertically in 5.0 s. Find the
work done and the average power.

## Task 7 — Energy budget (4 pts, HS-PS3-1)

A closed cart system's total energy is 800 J. Complete the
table and explain how you found the missing values.

| Instant | KE (J) | GPE (J) | Thermal (J) | Total (J) |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Start (at rest, top) | 0 | 800 | 0 | 800 |
| Middle | 500 | 200 | ? | 800 |
| End (bottom) | 300 | 0 | ? | 800 |

Explain in two or three sentences what the thermal column
tells you about this system.

## Task 8 — Open response (5 pts)

Two identical carts start from rest at the top of two ramps
of the same height. Ramp A is smooth; ramp B is rough. A
learner claims: "The heavier cart would win on ramp B,
because weight is what makes things go." (a) Predict which
cart arrives at the bottom faster, and why, using energy
accounting. (b) Explain where the rough ramp's missing
mechanical energy went. (c) Correct the learner's claim about
weight, using the energy equations.
