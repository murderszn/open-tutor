# Resource Pack — Unit 01: Measurement, Vectors, Motion, Uncertainty

**Goal:** Give a grade-11 learner (and guiding adult) free, no-account
routes to every core objective: SI units and dimensional analysis,
significant figures, accuracy/precision/uncertainty, vector components
and resultants, motion graphs, constant-acceleration kinematics, and
projectile-motion prediction. **Age fit:** high-school physics
(grade 11); the adult previews every link for advertising, comments,
and suitability before the learner opens it.

## Focused queries

1. `SI base units prefixes dimensional analysis factor label method examples`
2. `significant figures multiplication division addition subtraction rules practice`
3. `accuracy vs precision uncertainty repeated measurements mean range physics lab`
4. `vector components trigonometry resultant magnitude direction worked examples`
5. `position time velocity time acceleration time graphs slope area meaning`
6. `projectile motion independence horizontal vertical range prediction worked example`

## Web references

Checked 2026-10-14 unless noted.

1. **PhET "Projectile Motion"** — free, no-account browser simulation
   (University of Colorado Boulder). Fire a cannon at set height,
   speed, and angle; measure range, time, and height; toggle air
   resistance. Used in the project.md simulation alternative and
   Lesson 05 extension. Adult previews before the learner opens it.
   <https://phet.colorado.edu/en/simulation/projectile-motion>
   (Page opened and confirmed live 2026-10-14.)
2. **PhET "Vector Addition"** — free, no-account browser simulation
   (University of Colorado Boulder). Drag vectors, change length and
   angle, display magnitude/angle/components, and verify tip-to-tail
   sums. Used in Session S7 to check hand-computed resultants. Adult
   previews before the learner opens it.
   <https://phet.colorado.edu/en/simulations/vector-addition>
   (Page opened and confirmed live 2026-10-14; corroborated by the
   ComPADRE record citing this canonical URL.)
3. **NIST — The International System of Units (SI), 2019 Edition
   (SP 330)** — the U.S. official interpretation of the SI: the seven
   base units, derived units, and prefix rules behind Lesson 01. Use
   the publication page as the authority for unit names and symbols;
   the unit's descriptions are paraphrases, not reproductions.
   <https://www.nist.gov/publications/international-system-units-si-2019-edition>
4. **The Physics Classroom — Vectors toolkit page** — teacher toolkit
   index for vector lessons, including the PhET Vector Addition
   teacher-contributed lab guides (30-minute and two-period versions)
   the adult may use to structure Session S7.
   <https://www.physicsclassroom.com/Teacher-Toolkits/Vectors/Vectors-Complete-ToolKit>
5. **The Physics Classroom — 1-D Kinematics tutorial index** — free
   tutorials on describing motion with words, diagrams, position–time
   and velocity–time graphs, free fall, and the kinematic equations,
   with check-your-understanding questions; a reading alternative for
   Sessions S12–S14. The adult previews and selects the matching
   lesson pages (Lessons 3–6 fit this unit).
   <http://www.physicsclassroom.com/Physics-Tutorial/1-D-Kinematics>
   (Tutorial index confirmed via search 2026-10-14.)
6. **HyperPhysics (Georgia State University) — Trajectories** —
   concise reference pages on projectile trajectories: the "Angle of
   Launch" page (range vs. angle, complementary angles) and the
   "Horizontal Trajectory" page (horizontal-launch calculators with
   the motion equations); best used as a lookup, not a first read.
   <http://hyperphysics.phy-astr.gsu.edu/hbase/trajs.html>
   and <http://hyperphysics.phy-astr.gsu.edu/hbase/hortraj.html>
   (Both pages confirmed via search 2026-10-14.)
