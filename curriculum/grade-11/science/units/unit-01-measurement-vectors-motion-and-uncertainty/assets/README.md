- **Filename:** `assets/projectile-launch-illustration.png`
  (PNG, raster, 1920×1280)
- **Used in:** `lessons/lesson-05-projectile-motion-predicting-range.md`
  (image activity; items 5.4 and 5.5 reference it).
- **Prompt (verbatim):** "A clean educational physics illustration of a
  projectile launched horizontally: a small steel ball rolling off a
  short wooden ramp at the edge of a sturdy wooden table, arcing
  through the air toward a marked landing zone on the floor below. A
  smooth dotted trajectory path traces the full arc from the ramp exit
  to the landing point. Along the dotted path show exactly five ghost
  (translucent) copies of the ball at equal time intervals: the first
  ghost just after leaving the ramp moving right, then evenly spaced
  horizontally with vertical spacing that grows as the ball falls, the
  fourth ghost just above the floor, the fifth ghost resting at the
  landing zone. Horizontal spacing between consecutive ghosts is
  constant; vertical spacing increases downward, showing gravity
  accelerating the ball downward while horizontal motion stays uniform.
  The trajectory should be a symmetric-looking smooth parabola. Simple
  flat illustration style with clean lines, soft neutral colors, plain
  light background, no people, no text, no numbers, no labels, no
  measurements. Clear and uncluttered for a high school physics
  lesson."

  (`media.generate_image`), via the structured media pipeline.
- **Date generated:** 2026-10-14
- **Learning purpose:** Supports Lesson 05 items 5.4–5.5. The learner
  reads the launch scene directly: equal horizontal gaps between the
  six ball positions show the horizontal velocity staying constant,
  while the growing vertical gaps show the downward velocity
  increasing — the visual anchor for the independence of horizontal
  and vertical motion. The slightly downward-tilted ramp feeds item
  5.4b (which motion sets the flight time).
