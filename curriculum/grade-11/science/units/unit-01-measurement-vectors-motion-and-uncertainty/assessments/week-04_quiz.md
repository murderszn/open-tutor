# Week-04 Formative Quiz — Unit 01

**Session S18 · ~25 minutes · formative (reteach follows same session)**
Calculator allowed. Show reasoning, not just answers. Carry units
through every step; report significant figures.

**Q1 (units).** a) Convert 3.60 km to meters. b) Convert 90.0 km/h to
m/s. Show the conversion factors with canceling units.

**Q2 (significant figures).** a) Compute 6.25 m × 2.0 m with correct
significant figures. b) Compute 100.0 g − 98.3 g with correct
significant figures. Name the rule used in each part.

**Q3 (accuracy vs. precision).** The accepted value of a lab constant
is 25.0 units. Group X reports 24.9, 25.1, 25.0, 24.9. Group Y
reports 27.3, 27.5, 27.4, 27.3. Classify each group as accurate,
precise, both, or neither, and name the likely error type for the
group that is not accurate.

**Q4 (uncertainty).** Five timing trials (s): 4.12, 4.18, 4.15, 4.14,
4.16. Compute the mean, the range, and the ± report.

**Q5 (vector components).** Resolve an 18.0 m displacement at 40.0°
above the *negative* x-axis into components. (cos 40.0° = 0.76604,
sin 40.0° = 0.64279.) State the sign of each component and why.

**Q6 (vector addition).** **A** = (4.0 km, 1.0 km), **B** = (−1.0 km,
3.0 km). Find the resultant by components: R_x, R_y, magnitude, and
direction with a direction reference.

**Q7 (motion graphs).** A velocity–time graph: 0 → 12.0 m/s linearly
over 3.00 s, then a constant 12.0 m/s for 5.00 s.
a) Describe the motion in words.
b) Compute the total displacement.
c) Find the acceleration during the first phase.

**Q8 (constant acceleration).** An object is dropped from rest from
12.0 m (up = +). a) How long until it hits the ground? b) What is its
velocity just before impact? Use g = 9.80 m/s².

**Q9 (projectile).** A ball is launched horizontally from 1.00 m
height at 4.00 m/s. Compute the flight time and the range. Explain in
one sentence which motion (horizontal or vertical) determines the
flight time.

**Q10 (error analysis).** A learner writes: "A 5.00 m vector at 30.0°
above the +x axis has x-component 5.00 × sin 30.0° = 2.50 m."
Identify the error, write the correct computation, and explain the
mistake in one or two sentences.

*Scoring (adult): 2 points per question (Q1–Q10 = 20 points).
