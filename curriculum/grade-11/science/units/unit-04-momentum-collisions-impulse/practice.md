# Practice Sets A–F — Unit 04

Work with the adult or a partner where a set says so; otherwise work
independently and check against the answer key only after finishing
a set. Right is positive throughout. Give every answer with sign
(where direction matters) and units. Two significant figures unless
a problem states otherwise.

## Set A — Momentum (Lesson 01; Session S2)

A1. A 0.30 kg ball rolls right at 9.0 m/s. Calculate its momentum.

A2. A 1,200 kg car travels right at 25 m/s. Calculate its momentum.

A3. A 2.0 kg object moves left at 3.0 m/s. Calculate its momentum.

A4. An object has momentum +48 kg·m/s and speed 12 m/s to the right.
Find its mass.

A5. An object of mass 5.0 kg has momentum −20 kg·m/s. Find its
velocity, with direction.

A6. A 60 kg runner moves at 5.0 m/s and a 0.20 kg ball moves at
100 m/s, both to the right. Calculate both momenta and say which
object is harder to stop.

## Set B — Impulse and force–time graphs (Lesson 02; Session S4)

B1. A constant force of 50 N acts for 0.40 s on a 4.0 kg object at
rest. Find the impulse and the object's final speed.

B2. A force–time graph is a triangle with base 0.50 s and height
80 N. Find the impulse, and the velocity change it gives a 2.5 kg
object at rest.

B3. A 0.15 kg ball moves right at 12 m/s, hits a wall, and rebounds
left at 10 m/s. Find its change in momentum.

B4. A 0.40 kg ball moving at 10 m/s is stopped in 0.080 s. Find the
magnitude of the average stopping force.

B5. Two pushes act on identical objects at rest. Push A: 100 N for
0.060 s. Push B: 30 N for 0.20 s. Compute both impulses and say
whether the final speeds are equal.

## Set C — Conservation of momentum (Lesson 03; Session S7)

C1. A 2.0 kg cart moves right at 3.0 m/s and sticks to a 3.0 kg cart
at rest. Find the common velocity.

C2. A 4.0 kg cart moves right at 2.0 m/s and a 1.0 kg cart moves left
at 3.0 m/s. They stick together. Find the common velocity.

C3. **Recoil.** A 60 kg skater at rest pushes off from an 80 kg
skater at rest. The 80 kg skater moves right at 1.5 m/s. Find the
velocity of the 60 kg skater.

C4. **Explosion.** A 6.0 kg cart at rest splits into a 2.0 kg part
moving right at 4.0 m/s and a 4.0 kg part. Find the velocity of the
4.0 kg part.

C5. In Session S7, run PhET "Collision Lab" (Explore 1-D, adult
previews) with a 1.0 kg ball at 2.0 m/s striking a 1.0 kg ball at
rest, elasticity 0%. First predict the common velocity from
conservation of momentum (total momentum 2.0 kg·m/s, total mass
2.0 kg), then record the simulated common velocity and state
whether it matched within rounding, and what you would check first
if it had not.

## Set D — Elastic vs. inelastic (Lesson 04; Session S9)

D1. Two 2.0 kg carts collide elastically: one moves right at
5.0 m/s, the other is at rest. State the velocities after the
collision and verify total kinetic energy before and after.

D2. A 2.0 kg cart moves right at 4.0 m/s and a 2.0 kg cart moves
left at 1.0 m/s. They stick together. Find the common velocity,
the kinetic energy before and after, and the energy lost.

D3. A 3.0 kg cart moves right at 2.0 m/s toward a 1.0 kg cart at
rest. After the collision the velocities are 1.0 m/s and 3.0 m/s.
Verify momentum and kinetic energy before and after, and classify
the collision.

D4. A 5.0 kg cart moves right at 3.0 m/s and sticks to a 5.0 kg cart
at rest. Find the common velocity, the kinetic energy before and
after, and the energy lost. What fraction of the initial kinetic
energy was lost?

## Set E — Collision safety (Lesson 05; Session S12)

E1. A 50 kg occupant travels at 8.0 m/s and stops. Find the momentum
change, then the average force for a 0.040 s stop and a 0.40 s stop.

E2. A 0.20 kg payload falls from 1.5 m, reaching 5.4 m/s at impact.
Find its momentum at impact, then the average stopping force for a
0.020 s hard stop and a 0.20 s cushioned stop.

E3. Two designs protect the same payload with momentum change
9.0 kg·m/s. Design A stops it in 0.030 s; design B in 0.15 s.
Compute both average forces and choose the better design on force
alone.

## Set F — Mixed retrieval, Units 01–04 (Lesson 06; Session S14)

F1. **Vectors (U01).** A velocity of 10 m/s points at 30° above the
horizontal. Find its horizontal and vertical components (two
significant figures).

F2. **Kinematics (U01).** An object accelerates at 2.0 m/s² from
3.0 m/s for 4.0 s. Find its final velocity and the distance
travelled.

F3. **Dynamics (U02).** A 5.0 kg object accelerates at 3.0 m/s².
Find the net force.

F4. **Energy (U03).** A 2.0 kg object is held 4.0 m above the
ground. Find its gravitational potential energy (`g = 9.80 m/s²`).

F5. **Momentum (this unit).** A 4.0 kg cart moves right at 3.0 m/s
and sticks to a 2.0 kg cart at rest. Find the common velocity.

F6. **Impulse (this unit).** A 25 N force acts for 0.80 s on a
5.0 kg object at rest. Find the impulse and the object's final
speed.
