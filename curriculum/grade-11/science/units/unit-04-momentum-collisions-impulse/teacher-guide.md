# Teacher Guide — Unit 04 (Adults Only)

**This page is for the guiding adult. It contains no student answers
beyond teaching guidance; solutions are in `answer-key.md`. Do not
hand this page to the learner as a worksheet.**

## How this unit fits

Unit 04 is the hinge between the forces/energy units and the rest of
the year: it introduces the second great conserved quantity
(momentum) and the engineering habit of judging a design by a
measured criterion rather than by a claim. The midyear checkpoint
(Session S19) is deliberately cumulative — a learner who can do
Unit 04 but has lost Unit 01's vectors or Unit 03's energy will show
it there, before Unit 05 builds on orbital models.

## Lesson-by-lesson notes

- **Lesson 01 (S1).** The only new idea is the sign. Spend the time
  on it: every later error in this unit traces back to a dropped or
  flipped sign. If the learner writes momenta without signs in
  Set A, stop and re-teach the convention before Lesson 02.
- **Lesson 02 (S3).** The graph-area idea is the hard part, not the
  formula. Have the learner shade each area and label its value
  before dividing by mass. The bounce-vs-stop doubling (2.5) is the
  lesson's most-tested idea; return to it in the S5 clinic.
- **Lesson 03 (S6).** Insist on the four-step routine (system,
  direction, before total, after expression) on every problem, even
  easy ones. The routine is the transferable skill; the arithmetic
  is not. The image task (3.3) must use the text alternative's
  numbers, not the illustration's relative cart sizes.
- **Lesson 04 (S8).** Learners often "verify" elastic collisions by
  checking momentum only. Require both rows of the two-quantity
  test, every time. Allow ±0.1 J rounding on the 0.67/2.67 check
  (task 4.3); do not let rounding disputes hide a real mismatch.
- **Lesson 05 (S11).** The sentence to correct is "devices reduce
  the momentum change." Drill the correction ("same change, longer
  time, smaller force") until it is automatic, because the project
  report will be judged on exactly that distinction.
- **Lesson 06 (S13).** This lesson is a study skill as much as
  physics. The "Given / Asked / Tool" header should become a habit
  for the checkpoint and for Unit 05; praise it explicitly when the
  learner uses it unprompted.

## Common misconceptions and reteach moves

| Misconception | Reteach move |
|---|---|
| Momentum is a scalar / sign does not matter | Two carts head-on at equal momentum: total zero, not double. Re-do Lesson 03 Example 2 with arrows. |
| Momentum and kinetic energy are the same thing | Same momentum, different KE: a 0.060 kg ball at 50 m/s vs. a 0.50 kg ball at 6.0 m/s (both 3.0 kg·m/s; KE 75 J vs. 9.0 J). Compute both. |
| Kinetic energy is conserved in sticky collisions | Lesson 04 Example 2: 9.0 J → 6.0 J, with the 3.0 J named (deformation, heat, sound). |
| Impulse uses peak force, not graph area | Lesson 02 Example 2: peak gives double the true impulse. Shade the area. |
| Safety devices reduce the momentum change | Lesson 05 Example 1: 720 kg·m/s in both stops; only the time, and therefore the force, changes. |
| Average force equals peak force | Lesson 05 extension: a triangular pulse peaks at twice its average for the same impulse. |
| "The second cart started moving, so momentum increased" | Recompute the total: the first cart lost exactly what the second gained. Check signs first, system second. |

## Readiness, reteach triggers, and next steps

- **Ready for the assessment** if the learner scores 16+/20 on the
  quiz and can state the system and condition for a conservation
  problem without prompting.
- **Reteach Lesson 03** if conservation problems are set up without
  a before total, or if signs are dropped more than once in Set C.
- **Reteach Lesson 05** if the project conclusion claims a reduced
  momentum change, or if the project score is below 13/20: repeat
  the refinement step before the assessment.
- **After this unit:** Unit 05 (circular motion, gravitation, orbital
  models) reuses vector components (U01), centripetal reasoning from
  Newton's laws (U02), and energy (U03). A learner weak on the
  checkpoint's D2 (incline components) needs that re-taught before
  Unit 05's orbital-component work.

## Safety and administration

- Approve the project procedure before any drop; enforce the clear
  drop zone, the 1.5 m height (no standing on furniture), and eye
  protection where fragments are possible. Use the simulation
  alternative without hesitation if supervision or materials are
  inadequate.
- Preview every external link (PhET, videos) for advertising,
  comments, and suitability before the learner opens it, per the
  resource pack's check dates.
- Keep all learner work, names, and grades in private storage; do
  not upload completed work to this public repository.
- This unit is a validated draft, not educator-reviewed curriculum.
  If you find an error, record it against issue #51 rather than
  silently correcting a student copy.
