# Week-04 Formative Quiz — Unit 04

**Unit 04 · Session S17 · ~25 minutes · 20 points (2 points each)**
**Covers unit objectives 1–5.** Right is positive. Show working;
a bare number earns at most 1 point. This quiz is formative: the
adult reteaches missed clusters in the same session (S17) before the
unit assessment (S18).

1. A 1.5 kg object moves right at 4.0 m/s. Calculate its momentum.
2. A 2.0 kg object moves left at 3.0 m/s. Calculate its momentum,
   with sign.
3. A constant force of 40 N acts for 0.25 s. Calculate the impulse.
4. A force–time graph is a triangle with base 0.30 s and height
   60 N. Calculate the impulse.
5. A 2.0 kg cart moves right at 3.0 m/s and sticks to a 1.0 kg cart
   at rest. Find the common velocity.
6. A 70 kg skater at rest pushes off from a 50 kg skater at rest.
   The 50 kg skater moves left at 2.8 m/s. Find the velocity of the
   70 kg skater.
7. A 2.0 kg cart moves right at 2.0 m/s and sticks to a 2.0 kg cart
   at rest. Find the kinetic energy before and after, and the
   energy lost.
8. A 60 kg occupant travels at 10 m/s and stops. (a) Find the
   momentum change. (b) Find the average force for a 0.030 s stop
   and for a 0.30 s stop. (c) Which stop is safer, and why in one
   sentence?
9. A problem gives a stopping time of 0.50 s and asks for the
   average force. A second problem gives a stopping distance of
   2.0 m and asks for the same cart's final speed from energy.
   Name the tool for each problem (impulse–momentum or
   work–energy) and give the equation you would start with.
10. A learner says, "In a sticky collision, momentum is lost,
    because the carts slow down." Explain, in two or three sentences,
    why this is wrong: what is conserved, what is not, and where the
    kinetic energy goes.

**Scoring guide (adult):** 16+/20 is on track for the unit
assessment. Cluster reteach: questions 1–2 → Lesson 01; 3–4 →
Lesson 02; 5–6 → Lesson 03; 7 → Lesson 04; 8 → Lesson 05; 9 → Lesson 06;
10 → Lessons 03–04. Full solutions: see `answer-key.md`, Quiz.
