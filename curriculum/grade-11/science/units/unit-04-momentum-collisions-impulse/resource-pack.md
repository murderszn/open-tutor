# Resource Pack — Unit 04: Momentum, Collisions, and Impulse

**Goal:** Give a grade-11 learner (and guiding adult) free, no-account
routes to every core objective: momentum, impulse and force–time
graphs, conservation of momentum, elastic vs. inelastic collisions,
collision-safety design, and choosing between impulse, work–energy,
and Newton's laws. **Age fit:** high-school physics (grade 11); the
adult previews every link for advertising, comments, and suitability
before the learner opens it.
