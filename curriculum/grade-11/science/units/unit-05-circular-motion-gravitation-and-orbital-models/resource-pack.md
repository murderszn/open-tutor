# Resource Pack — Unit 05: Circular Motion, Gravitation, and Orbital Models

**Goal:** Give a grade-11 learner (and guiding adult) free, no-account
routes to every core objective: uniform circular motion and
centripetal acceleration, centripetal force, Newton's law of
universal gravitation, Coulomb's law, orbital speed and Kepler's
laws from real planet data, satellites and escape velocity, and
the Orbit Investigation. **Age fit:** high-school physics
(grade 11); the adult previews every link for advertising,
comments, and suitability before the learner opens it.

## Safety & notes

- The adult previews every video and simulation for advertising,
  comments, and age suitability before the learner opens it.

  sim runs on the learner's device before Session S16.
- Captions: require captions on any assigned video; where a video
  lacks captions, the adult narrates or substitutes the
  Physics Classroom reading.
- No video or simulation replaces the written lessons; each is a
  supplement to a specific lesson or project step, as mapped above.
