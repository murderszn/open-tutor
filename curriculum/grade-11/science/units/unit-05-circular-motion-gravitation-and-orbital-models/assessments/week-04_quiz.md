# Week-04 Formative Quiz — Unit 05

**Unit 05 · Session S18 · ~25 minutes · 20 points**
**Covers:** unit objectives 1–5 (Lessons 01–06). Calculator
allowed. Show the equation you use before substituting numbers.
Answers are in `answer-key.md` (adults only).

## Questions

**Q1. (2 pts)** A merry-go-round of radius 3.0 m makes one full
turn in 6.0 s. Find the speed and the centripetal acceleration of
a rider at the edge. State the direction of the acceleration.

**Q2. (2 pts)** A 1,000 kg car rounds a flat curve of radius 40 m
at 10 m/s. What force must static friction provide, and in which
direction?

**Q3. (2 pts)** The gravitational force between two bodies is
900 N. What does it become if the distance between their centers
triples? State the scaling rule you used.

**Q4. (2 pts)** A 60.0 kg astronaut stands on the Moon
(`g_moon = 1.62 m/s²`). What is their weight there, and what is
their mass there?

**Q5. (2 pts)** Two charges of `+3.0 µC` each sit 0.20 m apart.
Compute the electrostatic force between them and state whether
it is attractive or repulsive.

**Q6. (2 pts)** A satellite orbits Earth at `r = 3 R_earth`
(`R_earth = 6.371 × 10⁶ m`). Compute its orbital speed in km/s.

**Q7. (2 pts)** A planet orbits its star (mass equal to the
Sun's) at 2 AU. Find its orbital period in years using
`T² = r³` (with `r` in AU and `T` in years).

**Q8. (2 pts)** Explain in two sentences why a satellite in orbit
does not fall to the ground, even though gravity is still pulling
on it.

**Q9. (2 pts)** State the escape velocity from Earth's surface
and explain in one sentence what it means.

**Q10. (2 pts)** A learner writes: "A satellite stays up because
the centrifugal force balances gravity." Identify the error and
rewrite the sentence correctly.

## Scoring

- 16–20: on track; move to the unit assessment (S19).
- 12–15: re-teach the missed cluster (circular motion, gravitation,
  or orbits) in the same session before S19.
- Below 12: re-teach Lessons 01–03 with the adult, redo the
  missed practice set, then retake the quiz before S19.
