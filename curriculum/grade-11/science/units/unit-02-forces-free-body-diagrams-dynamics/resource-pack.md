# Resource Pack — Unit 02: Forces, Free-Body Diagrams, and Dynamics

**Goal:** Give a grade-11 learner (and guiding adult) free, no-account
routes to every core objective: Newton's first law and inertia,
third-law pairs, complete free-body diagrams, `F_net = ma`,
kinetic and static friction, incline components, connected
systems, and the ramp investigation. **Age fit:** high-school
physics (grade 11); the adult previews every link for advertising,
comments, and suitability before the learner opens it.

## Focused queries

1. `Newton's first law inertia net force zero constant velocity examples`
2. `Newton's third law action reaction pairs two objects misconceptions`
3. `free-body diagram drawing forces normal tension friction worked examples`
4. `Newton's second law F net ma friction static kinetic coefficient problems`
5. `inclined plane normal force mg cos theta downhill component worked example`
6. `Atwood machine connected system tension acceleration two methods`

## Web references

Checked 2026-10-21 unless noted.

1. **PhET "Forces and Motion: Basics"** — free, no-account browser
   simulation (University of Colorado Boulder). Tug-of-war,
   motion, friction, and acceleration screens; set forces, masses,
   and friction and read the net force and acceleration directly.
   Used in Sessions S4 and S8 and in the project's simulation
   alternative. Adult previews before the learner opens it.
   <https://phet.colorado.edu/en/simulations/forces-and-motion-basics>
   (Simulation page confirmed via search 2026-10-21; the ComPADRE
   record cites this canonical URL and describes the sim as free
   access, HTML5, for balanced vs. unbalanced forces, friction,
   and Newton's second law.)
2. **LibreTexts — "Drawing Free-Body Diagrams" (OpenStax University
   Physics I, §5.4, via Georgia State University)** — free,
   no-account reference with worked free-body diagrams including
   a mass on an inclined plane, friction examples, and the
   two-body (tension) setup used in Lesson 06. Use as a reading
   alternative for Lessons 03, 05, and 06; the adult selects the
   matching figures.
   <https://phys.libretexts.org/Courses/Georgia_State_University/GSU-TM-Physics_I_(2211)/06%3A_Newton's_Laws_of_Motion/6.04%3A_Drawing_Free-Body_Diagrams>
   (Page confirmed via search 2026-10-21.)
3. **University of Tennessee — "Friction" (Physics 221 lab manual
   core module)** — free reference defining free-body diagrams,
   the normal force on flat and inclined surfaces, and kinetic
   friction, with the worked statement that a free-body diagram
   includes only forces on the object. Use as a second check on
   Lesson 03–04 definitions.
   <http://labman.phys.utk.edu/phys221core/modules/m3/friction.html>
   (Page confirmed via search 2026-10-21.)
4. **California Department of Education — CA NGSS content
   standards record for HS-PS2-1** — the official restatement of
   the performance expectation used for this unit's standards
   note, including its clarification statement (ramp and pulled
   objects as data examples) and its assessment boundary
   (one-dimensional motion, macroscopic objects, non-relativistic
   speeds). Adult-facing reference; the unit's descriptions are
   paraphrases, not reproductions.
   <https://www2.cde.ca.gov/cacs/id/web/29147>
   (Page confirmed via search 2026-10-21.)
