# Week-04 Formative Quiz — Unit 02

**Session S18 · ~25 minutes · formative (reteach follows same session)**
Calculator allowed. Show reasoning, not just answers. Draw a
free-body diagram (or list its forces) before any `F = ma`
division. Carry units through every step.

**Q1 (first law).** A hockey puck slides on smooth ice at
constant velocity after being struck. (a) What is the net force
on the puck? (b) A learner says a forward force must keep
acting on it. Explain the error in two sentences.

**Q2 (third law).** A person pushes a heavy box across a floor
with 120 N. (a) Name the third-law pair for the person's push
on the box (both forces, both objects). (b) The box accelerates
slowly. Does that mean the pair is unequal? Explain.

**Q3 (free-body diagram).** A 4.00 kg block rests on a flat
table and is pulled to the right by a rope with 15.0 N, but
does not move. List every force on the block with magnitude
and direction, name the friction type, and state the net force.

**Q4 (second law).** A 6.00 kg crate has a net force of 24.0 N
acting on it. (a) Find its acceleration. (b) What net force
would be needed to give the same crate an acceleration of
6.00 m/s²?

**Q5 (friction).** A 10.0 kg block slides on a flat floor with
`μ_k = 0.300`. (a) Find the normal force. (b) Find the kinetic
friction force. (c) If it was pushed with 50.0 N, find its
acceleration.

**Q6 (incline components).** A 5.00 kg block sits on a 30.0°
incline. (a) Compute its weight. (b) Compute the normal force
and the downhill component of weight. (sin 30.0° = 0.500,
cos 30.0° = 0.866.)

**Q7 (incline acceleration).** The block in Q6 slides down the
incline with `μ_k = 0.200`. Find its acceleration. Show the
net force before dividing.

**Q8 (connected system).** An Atwood machine has masses
4.00 kg and 2.00 kg. (a) Find the acceleration. (b) Find the
tension, checked two ways.

**Q9 (error analysis).** A learner writes: "The book's weight
(10 N down) and the table's normal force (10 N up) are a
third-law pair, so they cancel and the book stays still."
Identify the two errors (pair membership, and what
"cancels"), and rewrite the reasoning correctly.

**Q10 (data and HS-PS2-1).** A cart of mass 1.50 kg is pulled
on a nearly frictionless track. Measured data:

| Pulling (net) force (N) | Acceleration (m/s²) |
|---|---|
| 3.00 | 2.00 |
| 6.00 | 4.00 |
| 9.00 | 6.00 |

(a) Show that acceleration is proportional to net force.
(b) Compute `F / a` for each row. (c) State, in one sentence,
what claim these data support about the relationship among
net force, mass, and acceleration.
