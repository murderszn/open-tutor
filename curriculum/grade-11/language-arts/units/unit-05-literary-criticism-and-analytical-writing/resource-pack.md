# Resource Pack — Unit 05: Literary Criticism and Analytical Writing

**Goal:** Give a grade-11 learner (and guiding adult) free, no-account
routes to every core objective: naming and applying the formalist,
historical/biographical, reader-response, and feminist lenses; tracing
pattern, tension, and unity; using context as evidence without
anachronism; and writing a lens-based analytical essay with a
counter-reading. **Age fit:** high-school grade 11; the adult previews
every link for advertising, comments, and suitability before the learner
opens it. Checked 2026-11-13 unless noted.
"Verified" = the source page's text and publisher were confirmed on
that date; the adult opens each page before assigning and confirms
quotations against the edition used. "Search link" = a clearly labeled
preferred-channel search the adult runs and previews (no invented
titles, runtimes, or captions).

## Access notes

Every core objective has a free, no-account route: the Purdue OWL
overview, the Gutenberg texts, and the Poetry Foundation poems all open
without an account or payment. YouTube search links are supplements —
the adult runs the search, previews, and picks; no video is required,
and no video replaces the explicit teaching in the lessons. If internet
access is unavailable, the unit still works: the anchor texts can be
printed in advance, and the lens table, pattern–tension–unity frame,
and essay-plan table are all printable from the lessons themselves.
