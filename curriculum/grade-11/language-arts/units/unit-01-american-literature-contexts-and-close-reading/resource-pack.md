# Resource Pack — Unit 01: American Literature Contexts, Themes, and Close Reading

**Goal:** Give a grade-11 learner (and guiding adult) free, no-account
routes to every core objective: the American literature periods, the
close-reading routine, theme tracking in *Gatsby*, comparing Douglass /
Whitman / Dickinson, and figurative language in context. **Age fit:**
high-school grade 11; the adult previews every link for advertising,
comments, and suitability before the learner opens it. Checked 2026-10-14
unless noted. "Verified" = the page was opened and the content confirmed
on that date; "search link" = a clearly labeled preferred-channel search
the adult runs and previews (no invented titles, runtimes, or captions).

## Focused queries

1. `close reading strategies annotate question connect summarize high school`
2. `Great Gatsby themes American Dream green light analysis`
3. `Frederick Douglass "Fourth of July" 1852 speech rhetorical analysis`
4. `Whitman Song of Myself section 6 grass meaning analysis`
5. `Dickinson "Hope is the thing with feathers" extended metaphor analysis`
