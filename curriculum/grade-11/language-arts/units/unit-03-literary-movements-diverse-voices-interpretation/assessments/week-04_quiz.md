# Formative Quiz — Unit 03: Literary Movements, Diverse Voices, and Interpretation

**Week 13, session S18 · 20 points · about 30 minutes.**
Answer in your notebook. Do not use the lessons while you work;
the point is to find what needs re-teaching before the unit
assessment. The adult scores with `answer-key.md` and reviews
misses with you in S19 preparation.

**1. (2 pts)** Name the movement for each belief, in one phrase
each: (a) literature should trust feeling and the individual
imagination over cold reason; (b) literature should show human
beings shaped by forces larger than themselves.

**2. (2 pts)** Define *ambiguity* and *symbol* in one sentence
each, using "Young Goodman Brown" as your example for at least
one of them.

**3. (2 pts)** Read this original passage and classify it with
the four-part format (label, trait, quotation, what the trait
does): "The committee's decision arrived by post on a Tuesday,
and by Wednesday the family had learned to live with a smaller
table, a colder room, and a future that no longer asked their
opinion."

**4. (2 pts)** In "The Yellow Wallpaper," the narrator writes,
"John laughs at me, of course, but one expects that in
marriage." State the stated meaning and the meant meaning in
one sentence each.

**5. (2 pts)** Name one thing a first-person narrator can show
that a third-person narrator cannot, and one thing the reverse.
Give a text from this unit for each.

**6. (2 pts)** Hughes's "I, Too" ends with the speaker at the
table. What does the poem's future tense ("Tomorrow, I'll be at
the table") argue that a present-tense statement ("I am at the
table") could not argue? Answer in two sentences.

**7. (2 pts)** A classmate says the Lesson 05 illustration is
"wrong" because Gilman never describes that exact room. In two
sentences, explain why "wrong" is the wrong word, and name the
question that should replace it.

**8. (2 pts)** Compare, in three sentences: Gilman's confinement
(a room) and Chopin's confinement (roles). What does the
comparison show about the word "confinement" itself?

**9. (2 pts)** RL.11-12.7 asks you to evaluate how a version
interprets its source. Name two of the four evaluation questions
from Lesson 05 and apply one of them to any version you studied
this unit, in two sentences.

**10. (2 pts)** Write one sentence for each of two movements of
your choice, stating what each believed literature should do.
Then write one sentence on where the two movements disagree.
