# Grade 11 — Independent Curriculum Track

This folder is an anonymous, grade-specific track in the public OpenTutor curriculum library.

**Contents:** Draft audits and scope-and-sequences are available for mathematics, language arts, science, social studies. Math Units 01–02 (issues #50), science Unit 01 (issue #51), and language arts Unit 01 (issue #52) are delivered as validated drafts; all other units and review packages are planned, not yet written. No subject track is complete. All materials still require educator review.

**Do not refer a learner to another grade's track.** Each track is
self-contained for one learner at a time; a guiding adult adapts pacing,
materials, and supports. Keep completed learner work, names, photos, schedules,
and grades in private storage — never in this public repository.

See also: [curriculum index](../README.md).

## Subject tracks

- [Mathematics](math/README.md) — Algebra II pathway; audit and
  scope-and-sequence are a validated draft (issue #50); Units 01
  (function transformations, composition, inverses) and 02
  (polynomial functions, zeros, complex numbers) delivered as
  validated drafts (issue #50).
- [Science](science/README.md) — Physics pathway; audit and
  scope-and-sequence are a validated draft (issue #51); Units 01–02
  (measurement, vectors, motion, and uncertainty; forces, free-body
  diagrams, and dynamics) delivered as validated drafts in draft
  PRs; units 03–08 planned.
- [Language arts](language-arts/README.md) — American literature, rhetoric,
  and research pathway; audit and scope-and-sequence delivered as a validated
  draft (issue #52); Units 01 (American literature contexts, themes, and close
  reading) and 02 (founding rhetoric: speeches and primary texts) delivered
  as validated drafts; units 03–08 planned.
- [Social studies](social-studies/README.md) — U.S. government and civics
  pathway; audit and scope-and-sequence delivered as a validated draft
  (issue #53); Units 01–02 delivered as validated drafts in draft PRs
  (issue #53); units 03–08 planned.
