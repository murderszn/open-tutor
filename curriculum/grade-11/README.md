# Grade 11 — Independent Curriculum Track

This folder is an anonymous, grade-specific track in the public OpenTutor curriculum library.

**Contents:** Draft audits and scope-and-sequences are available for mathematics, language arts, science, social studies. Math Units 01–03 (issue #50; Unit 02 in draft PR #179, not yet merged), science Unit 01 (issue #51), and language arts Unit 01 (issue #52) are delivered as validated drafts; all other units and review packages are planned, not yet written. No subject track is complete. All materials still require educator review.

**Do not refer a learner to another grade's track.** Each track is
self-contained for one learner at a time; a guiding adult adapts pacing,
materials, and supports. Keep completed learner work, names, photos, schedules,
and grades in private storage — never in this public repository.

See also: [curriculum index](../README.md).

## Subject tracks

- [Mathematics](math/README.md) — Algebra II pathway; audit and
  scope-and-sequence are a validated draft (issue #50); Units 01
  (function transformations, composition, inverses), 02 (polynomial
  functions, zeros, complex numbers; draft PR #179, not yet merged),
  and 03 (rational expressions, functions, asymptotes) delivered as
  validated drafts (issue #50).
- [Science](science/README.md) — Physics pathway; audit and
  scope-and-sequence are a validated draft (issue #51); Unit 01
  (measurement, vectors, motion, and uncertainty) delivered as a
  validated draft in this run's draft PR; units 02–08 planned.
- [Language arts](language-arts/README.md) — American literature, rhetoric,
  and research pathway; audit and scope-and-sequence delivered as a validated
  draft (issue #52); Unit 01 (American literature contexts, themes, and close
  reading) delivered as a validated draft; units 02–08 planned.
- [Social studies](social-studies/README.md) — U.S. government and civics
  pathway; audit and scope-and-sequence delivered as a validated draft in
  this run's draft PR (issue #53); units planned.
