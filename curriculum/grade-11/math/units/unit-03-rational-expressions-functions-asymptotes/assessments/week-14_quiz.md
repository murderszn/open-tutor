# Week-14 Formative Quiz — Unit 03: Rational Expressions, Functions, and Asymptotes

**Session S18 · 30 points · ~30 minutes, then reteach.**
State restrictions before simplifying, and check every equation in
its original form. Show the working — a bare answer earns at most
half the item's marks. Answers are in
[answer-key.md](../answer-key.md), kept separate from this student
page.

1. **(5 points)** Simplify `(x^2 - 4)/(x^2 + 2x)` and state the
   domain.
2. **(5 points)** Add and simplify: `1/(x - 2) + 3/(x + 2)`. State
   the domain.
3. **(5 points)** For `f(x) = (x^2 - 9)/(x^2 - 3x)`, give: domain,
   simplified form, hole (if any) with coordinates, vertical
   asymptote, x-intercept, y-intercept (or "none", with the reason).
4. **(5 points)** Give the horizontal asymptote of
   `f(x) = (4x^2 + 1)/(2x^2 - 3)`, and say whether the graph crosses
   it. Show the solving step for the crossing question.
5. **(5 points)** Find the slant asymptote of
   `(x^2 + 5x + 2)/(x + 2)` by division, and state the vertical
   asymptote.
6. **(5 points)** Solve `2/(x + 1) = 3`, with the restriction stated
   first and the check shown in the original equation.

**Scoring guide for the adult:** 27–30 = secure, move to the unit
assessment; 21–26 = one reteach session on the missed objective
(usually restrictions or the degree rule); below 21 = re-teach
Lessons 01 and 04 before the assessment, using the practice sets'
starred items. A solution with no restriction stated loses 2
points on that item even if the algebra is right — restrictions
are an objective, not presentation.
