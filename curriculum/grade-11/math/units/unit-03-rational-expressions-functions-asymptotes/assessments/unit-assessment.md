# Unit Assessment — Unit 03: Rational Expressions, Functions, and Asymptotes

**Session S19 · 50 points · ~50 minutes.**
State restrictions before simplifying, check every equation in its
original form, and justify asymptote claims from degrees or
division — not from a remembered graph shape. Answers and scoring
guidance are in [answer-key.md](../answer-key.md), kept separate
from this student page.

## Part A — One function, fully analysed (20 points)

Analyse `f(x) = (2x^2 - 8)/(x^2 - x - 6)` completely:

1. Domain and simplified form (5 points).
2. Hole (if any) with coordinates, and vertical asymptote(s)
   (5 points).
3. x-intercept(s) and y-intercept (or "none", with the reason)
   (5 points).
4. Horizontal asymptote, end behaviour in words, and a sketch
   description: number of branches, where each branch sits relative
   to the asymptotes, and one test point per region (5 points).

## Part B — Asymptotes from degrees and division (15 points)

1. **(6 points)** Find the slant asymptote of
   `(x^2 - 2x - 8)/(x - 3)` by division (show the multiply-back
   check), and state the function's domain and vertical asymptote.
2. **(4 points)** Give the horizontal asymptote of
   `g(x) = 3x/(x^2 + 1)` and its end behaviour in words.
3. **(5 points)** Give the horizontal asymptote of
   `h(x) = 5x^2/(x^2 - 4)`, and say whether its graph crosses that
   asymptote. Show the solving step.

## Part C — Equations and variation (15 points)

1. **(5 points)** Solve `(x + 1)/(x - 2) = 2`, with the restriction
   stated first and the check shown in the original equation.
2. **(5 points)** Solve `x/(x - 4) = 4/(x - 4)`, and classify the
   result: genuine solution, extraneous solution, or no solution.
   Show the check that justifies the classification.
3. **(5 points)** A quantity `y` varies inversely with the square
   of `x`: `y = k/x^2`. When `x = 3`, `y = 18`. Find `k`, find `y`
   when `x = 9`, and describe what happens to `y` as `x` grows
   without bound, in asymptote language.

**Scoring guide for the adult:** 45–50 = secure on all five unit
objectives; 38–44 = secure with one reteach target (name it in the
teacher guide's reteach log); 30–37 = re-teach the weakest part
(usually Part A feature-finding or Part C extraneous solutions)
before Unit 04; below 30 = the unit is not yet secure — use a
flex week to re-teach Lessons 03 and 05 with the practice sets
before moving on. This assessment is a classroom check, not a
standardized or validated test.
