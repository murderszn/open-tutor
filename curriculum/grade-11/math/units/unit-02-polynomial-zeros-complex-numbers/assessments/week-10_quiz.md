# Week-10 Formative Quiz — Unit 02

**Session S18 · ~30 minutes · closed notebook except the vocabulary
card; calculator and Desmos allowed for the marked items.**
Scoring guide: `../answer-key.md` § Week-10 quiz. This is formative:
the adult uses it to plan the S18 reteach, not to grade the unit.

**1.** Write each in standard `a + bi` form. (Objective 1)
   a) `(5 − 2i) + (3 + 7i)`
   b) `(2 + i)(4 − 3i)`

**2.** Solve `x² − 8x + 25 = 0`. Write the solutions as `a ± bi` and
   verify one by substitution. (Objective 1)

**3.** Factor `p(x) = x³ + x² − 4x − 4` completely by grouping, then
   use the Factor Theorem to confirm one factor by evaluation.
   (Objective 2)

**4.** Find all zeros of `f(x) = x⁴ − 3x³ − 3x² + 11x − 6`. (Hint: `x =
   1` and `x = 3` are zeros.) List any complex zeros as conjugate
   pairs. (Objective 3)

**5.** Sketch a rough graph of `g(x) = (x + 2)(x − 1)(x − 4)` showing
   zeros, end behavior, and a valid turning-point count. (Objective 3)

**6.** Show that `x² + 4 = 3x + 1` has no real solution, and state
   what this means for the two graphs. Then (Desmos allowed)
   approximate the real solution(s) of `x³ = x + 3`, verifying by
   substitution. Report approximations with `≈`. (Objective 4)

**7.** **Explain (3–4 sentences):** A degree-4 polynomial has zeros `2
   − 5i` and `0`. What else must be true about its zeros? What can
   its graph look like at most — how many turning points, and how
   many times can it cross the x-axis?
