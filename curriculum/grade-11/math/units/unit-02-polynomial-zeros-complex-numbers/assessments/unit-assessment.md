# Unit Assessment — Unit 02: Polynomial Functions, Zeros, and Complex Numbers

**Session S19 · ~50 minutes · closed notebook; calculator and Desmos
allowed for the marked items.**
Scoring: `../answer-key.md` § Unit assessment. Each item names its
objective. Partial credit follows the teacher guide.

## Part 1 — Complex numbers (Objective 1)

**1.** Compute and write in standard `a + bi` form. Show the `i²`
replacement as its own line.
   a) `(6 − 4i) − (2 + 9i)`
   b) `(3 + 2i)(3 − 2i)`
   c) `(1 + 5i)²`

**2.** Solve `3x² + 12x + 19 = 0`. Write the solutions in lowest `a ±
bi` form and verify one by substitution.

**3.** (Enrichment) Write `(7 − i) / (2 + 3i)` in standard form.

## Part 2 — Polynomial structure (Objective 2)

**4.** Factor completely:
   a) `x³ − 6x² + 3x + 10` by testing integer candidates with the
      Factor Theorem, then dividing out.
   b) `x⁴ − 2x³ − 13x² + 14x + 24` given that `x = 2` and `x = −1` are
      zeros.

**5.** Use the Remainder Theorem — no long division — to answer: when
   `p(x) = 2x³ − 3x² + 4x − 9` is divided by `(x + 1)`, what is the
   remainder? Is `(x + 1)` a factor? Explain in one sentence.

## Part 3 — Zeros and graphs (Objective 3)

**6.** Find **all** zeros of `f(x) = x³ − 4x² + x + 6`, listing complex
   zeros as conjugate pairs where they occur.

**7.** For `g(x) = −2(x + 1)²(x − 3)`:
   a) List the zeros with multiplicity.
   b) State the end behavior and the maximum number of turning
      points.
   c) Sketch a rough graph.

**8.** A degree-5 polynomial with real coefficients has zeros `−2`,
   `1 + 4i`, and `3` (multiplicity 2). List all five zeros and state
   the maximum number of turning points.

## Part 4 — Intersections and modeling (Objective 4)

**9.** Solve `2x² − 5x + 2 = x² − x + 2` exactly and give the
   intersection points of the two graphs.

**10.** (Desmos allowed) A ball's height is `h(t) = −16t² + 40t + 6`
   feet, `t` seconds after it is thrown. A drone hovers at a constant
   `25` feet.
   a) Set up and solve `h(t) = 25` exactly. Interpret both solutions
      — are both physically meaningful? State the units.
   b) Approximate (Desmos) the time(s) when the ball is at `20`
      feet, verify by substitution, and report with `≈` and units.

**11.** **Written reasoning (5–6 sentences):** Explain the journey of
   this unit — how inventing `i` to solve `x² = −25` eventually lets
   you sketch the graph of a degree-4 polynomial from its zeros.
   Name at least three ideas from the unit and connect them in
   order.
