# Resource Pack — Unit 02: Polynomial Functions, Zeros, and Complex Numbers

**Goal:** Give a grade-11 learner (and guiding adult) free, no-account
routes to every core objective: complex arithmetic, quadratics with
complex solutions, polynomial factoring and the Remainder/Factor
Theorems, zeros-to-graph sketching, and solving `f(x) = g(x)`.
**Age fit:** high-school Algebra II (grade 11); the adult previews
every link for advertising, comments, and suitability before the
learner opens it.

## Focused queries

1. `complex numbers a+bi multiply i squared equals -1 practice algebra 2`
2. `quadratic formula negative discriminant complex solutions a+bi examples`
3. `remainder theorem factor theorem test factor polynomial examples`
4. `factor cubic polynomial grouping sum difference cubes`
5. `polynomial zeros graph end behavior turning points sketch`
6. `solve f(x)=g(x) intersection approximate graphing calculator`

## Web references

Checked 2026-10-15 unless noted.

1. **Paul's Online Notes (Lamar University) — Complex Numbers** —
   free, no account; a full college-algebra treatment of `i`,
   standard form, conjugates, arithmetic, division, and powers of
   `i`, with worked examples and the "convert square roots of
   negatives first" rule of thumb. `.edu` source; matches Lessons
   01–02 closely. (Verified 2026-10-15: page opened and content
   read.)
   <https://tutorial.math.lamar.edu/Classes/Alg/ComplexNumbers.aspx>
2. **Paul's Online Notes (Lamar University) — Factoring Polynomials**
   — free, no account; greatest common factor, grouping, quadratic
   factoring, and special forms with the "multiply back to check"

   and content read.)
