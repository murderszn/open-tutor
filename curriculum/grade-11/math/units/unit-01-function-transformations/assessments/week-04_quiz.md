# Week-04 Formative Quiz — Unit 01

**Session S18 · ~25 minutes · formative (reteach follows same session)**
Calculator allowed. Desmos allowed for verification *after* a prediction
is written. Show reasoning, not just answers.

**Q1.** Let `f(x) = x² − 3`.
a) Predict the graph of `g(x) = f(x + 2)` in words (direction of shift
   and why), then verify by computing `g(−2)` and `g(0)`.
b) Write the equation of the graph obtained by reflecting `f` across
   the x-axis and shifting it up 1.

**Q2.** Classify `f(x) = x⁶ − 4x²` as even, odd, or neither. Show the
`f(−x)` computation.

**Q3.** For `f(x) = 2x − 8` and `g(x) = x²`:
a) Compute `(f∘g)(x)` and `(g∘f)(x)`.
b) Evaluate both at `x = 3`. What does the difference tell you about
   composition?

**Q4.** Decompose `h(x) = (4x + 1)³` into an inner function and an
outer function, and show the chaining check.

**Q5.** Find the inverse of `p(x) = 6x + 2` by swap-and-solve, and
verify with both compositions.

**Q6.** A plumber charges `C(h) = 90 + 65h` dollars for `h` hours of
work (`0 ≤ h ≤ 8`).
a) Interpret `C⁻¹(285)` in a sentence (do not solve — just say what
   the expression *asks*).
b) Now compute it, with units, and check by evaluating `C` at your
   answer.

**Q7 (error analysis).** A learner writes: "`f(x) = x² + 1` shifted
left 3 is `g(x) = x² + 1 + 3`." Identify the error, write the correct
`g(x)`, and explain the mistake in one or two sentences.
