# Unit Assessment — Unit 01: Function Transformations, Composition, Inverses

**Session S19 · ~50 minutes · summative for the unit**
Calculator allowed. One page of notebook notes allowed. Answer in
complete mathematical sentences where asked; justify algebraic steps.

## Part I — Transformations (objectives 1, 5)

**1.** Let `f(x) = |x|`.
a) Describe, in order, the transformations that produce
   `g(x) = −2|x + 3| − 1` from `f`. (3 pts)
b) Track the vertex `(0, 0)` through your chain and verify the final
   point by direct substitution into `g`. (2 pts)
c) State the domain and range of `g`. (2 pts)

**2.** The graph of `f` passes through `(−1, 2)`, `(0, 5)`, `(2, 9)`.
The graph of `h(x) = f(2x)` passes through three corresponding
points.
a) Find those three points. (2 pts)
b) Is `h` a horizontal stretch or compression of `f`? Explain using
   one of your points. (2 pts)

**3.** Prove that `f(x) = 5x³ − 2x` is odd, starting from the
definition `f(−x) = −f(x)`. Then state one graphical consequence of
your result. (3 pts)

## Part II — Composition (objectives 2, 3, 5)

**4.** A theater sells tickets online: `t = 40s + 15` tickets for `s`
shows listed; revenue is `R(t) = 12t − 200` dollars.
a) Write revenue as a function of `s`, with units on each quantity,
   and show the units cancel. (3 pts)
b) Compute revenue for `s = 6` two ways (composite and
   stage-by-stage) and confirm agreement. (2 pts)

**5.** Let `f(x) = √(x)` (domain `x ≥ 0`) and `g(x) = x − 5`.
a) Write `(f∘g)(x)` and `(g∘f)(x)`, each with its domain. (3 pts)
b) Explain why the two domains differ, in terms of what each machine
   refuses. (2 pts)

**6.** Find the intersection points of `y = 2x − 1` and
`y = x² − 2x − 2` exactly. Then write the equation whose solutions
are the x-coordinates of those points. (4 pts)

## Part III — Inverses (objectives 2, 4, 5)

**7.** Let `f(x) = (3x − 7)/2`.
a) Find `f⁻¹(x)` by swap-and-solve, showing each step. (3 pts)
b) Verify with both compositions. (2 pts)
c) If `f` maps study hours to quiz points, interpret `f⁻¹(85)` in a
   sentence with units. (2 pts)

**8.** A delivery fee is `D(w) = 6 + 2.25w` dollars for a package
weighing `w` pounds (`0 ≤ w ≤ 70`).
a) Write `D⁻¹` and state its domain and range as intervals. (3 pts)
b) How many pounds can ship for $40? Solve with `D⁻¹`, verify by
   evaluating `D`, and confirm the units make sense. (3 pts)

**9.** Does `f(x) = (x − 2)² + 1` have an inverse on all real numbers?
Justify with the horizontal line test, then either write the inverse
or restrict the domain so one exists and write that inverse. (4 pts)

## Part IV — Synthesis (objectives 1–5)

**10.** A classmate claims: "If `f` is even and `g` is odd, then
`f∘g` is even." Prove the claim or refute it with a counterexample —
either way, show the `f(−x)`-style algebra. (4 pts)

**11.** You are given a composite `H(x) = 3(2x − 8)² + 5`.
a) Decompose `H` into two functions `f` and `g` with `H = f∘g`, and
   verify by recomposing. (2 pts)
b) Describe how the graph of `H` can be obtained from the graph of
   `y = x²` by transformations, in order. (3 pts)
c) Explain how parts (a) and (b) are two views of the same
   structure. (2 pts)
