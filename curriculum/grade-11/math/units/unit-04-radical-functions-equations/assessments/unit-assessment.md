# Unit Assessment — Radical Functions and Equations

**40 points · ~50 minutes · Calculator allowed for decimal checks
only; exact form required unless a question asks for a decimal.**
Show the check for every equation solution. Scoring guidance is
given per item; the adult records a reteach plan for any objective
scored below half marks.

## Part A — Radical skills (24 points)

**A1. (4 pts)** (a) Evaluate `32^(3/5)`. (b) Simplify `√288`.
(c) Rationalize the denominator of `7/√7`.

**A2. (4 pts)** For `f(x) = √(x + 3) − 2`, state the domain,
range, and starting point, and give one additional plotted point
with its computation.

**A3. (4 pts)** For `g(x) = ∛(x − 1) + 2`, state the domain,
range, and center, and say whether the function is increasing or
decreasing on its domain.

**A4. (4 pts)** Solve `√(3x + 1) = x − 1`. Show the squaring step,
both candidates, both checks, and the final solution set with any
extraneous solution named.

**A5. (4 pts)** Solve `√(x + 3) − √x = 1`. Show the isolation and
squaring steps, the candidate, and the check.

**A6. (4 pts)** (a) Solve `∛(2x − 5) = 3`. (b) A cube has volume
512 cm³. What is its edge length? If its volume grows to
1000 cm³, what is the new edge length?

## Part B — Root models (8 points)

Fictional practice data (a pendulum timed with a hand stopwatch,
rounded to hundredths of a second):

| Length `L` (m) | 0.16 | 0.36 | 0.64 | 1.00 |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Period `T` (s) | 0.80 | 1.20 | 1.61 | 2.01 |

**B1. (3 pts)** Compute the successive rates of change
(change in `T` ÷ change in `L`) between consecutive points, and
state what shape they suggest: linear, square-root, or
exponential. One sentence of reason.

**B2. (3 pts)** Fit the model `T = a√L` using the point
`L = 1.00, T = 2.01`, then predict `T` at `L = 0.49` m. Round the
prediction to hundredths of a second, as a hand stopwatch allows.

**B3. (2 pts)** Explain, using increments or residuals, why a
straight-line model through the first and last data points is the
wrong shape for this data.

## Part C — Cumulative retrieval, Units 01–04 (8 points)

**C1. (2 pts)** Let `f(x) = √x` and `g(x) = x² + 1`. Write
`f(g(x))` and state its domain, with a reason.

**C2. (2 pts)** For `p(x) = (x − 2)(x + 3)²`, list the zeros and
describe the end behavior as `x → ∞` and as `x → −∞`.

**C3. (2 pts)** For `f(x) = (x − 1)/(x + 2)`, state the domain,
the vertical asymptote, the horizontal asymptote, and the zero.

**C4. (2 pts)** In two or three sentences, explain why solutions
of a radical equation must be checked in the original equation.
Use the word "sign" in your explanation.

**Scoring summary:** Part A 24 + Part B 8 + Part C 8 = 40.
Readiness for Unit 05: Part A at 18+ and Part C at 6+; otherwise
use the reteach guidance in the [teacher guide](../teacher-guide.md)
before starting exponential and logarithmic functions.
