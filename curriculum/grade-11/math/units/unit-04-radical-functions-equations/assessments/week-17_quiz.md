# Week-17 Formative Quiz — Radical Functions and Equations

**20 points · ~30 minutes · Calculator allowed for decimal checks
only; exact form required unless a question asks for a decimal.**
Show the check for every equation solution. The quiz is named for
Week 17, the last content week; it is given in Week 18 (see the
[unit README](../README.md#session-pacing-20-sessions-50-minutes-each)).

1. (2 pts) Evaluate `81^(3/4)`.
2. (2 pts) Simplify `√98` to simplified radical form.
3. (2 pts) State the domain of `f(x) = √(x − 5)`.
4. (2 pts) State the range of `g(x) = √x + 3`.
5. (2 pts) Solve `√(x + 2) = 7`, showing the check.
6. (2 pts) Solve `√(2x − 1) = x − 2`. Give the full solution set
   with checks, and name any extraneous solution.
7. (2 pts) Solve `∛(x − 3) = 2`.
8. (2 pts) Give the starting point of `f(x) = √(x + 2) − 1`.
9. (2 pts) A pendulum of length 0.25 m has what period? Use
   `T = 2π√(L/9.8)` and round to hundredths of a second.
10. (2 pts) A dataset's increments are shrinking as the input
    grows by equal steps. Which model family — linear, square-root,
    or exponential — does that suggest? One sentence of reason.

**Scoring guidance (adult):** 2 points for a correct answer with
required working (check, domain statement, or reason as asked);
1 point for a correct value with missing or incomplete working;
0 for a wrong value. Question 6 earns full marks only if both
candidates are checked and the extraneous one is named.
