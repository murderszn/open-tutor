# Resource Pack — Unit 05: Exponential and Logarithmic Functions and Models

## Summary

**Goal:** give the learner and the guiding adult free, no-account
routes to support exponential graphs and percent rates, logarithms
as inverses, logarithm properties, solving exponential and
logarithmic equations, and fitting exponential models with residuals.
**Age fit:** grade 11 (about 16–17); all items are symbolic,
graphical, or adult-facing reference, with no reading required below
grade level.

   `https://oer.physics.manchester.ac.uk/Math1/Notes/Notes/Notesse3.xht`
