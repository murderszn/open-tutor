# Formative Quiz — Unit 05 (Week 22)

**For the learner.** 10 items, about 25 minutes, calculator allowed
for approximations only — exact logarithmic answers are required
where the item asks for them. The adult scores it the same day and
maps every miss to a lesson (see the scoring guide at the end). Full
solutions are in [../answer-key.md](../answer-key.md), for the
guiding adult only.

1. For `f(x) = 5 · (0.9)^x`, state the initial value, the percent
   rate, and whether the function grows or decays.
2. Evaluate `f(2)` for the function in item 1.
3. Classify the table as linear, quadratic, or exponential, and give
   a reason (not just a name): `x = 0, 1, 2, 3` → `y = 6, 18, 54, 162`.
4. Convert `log_4(64) = 3` to exponential form.
5. Evaluate `log_2(1/16)` without a calculator.
6. Expand `log_3(9x^2)` for `x > 0`.
7. Condense `2·log(x) − log(y)` to a single logarithm.
8. Solve `2 · 3^x = 162`.
9. Solve `log(x) + log(x − 3) = log(10)` (common logarithms). State
   the domain first and check every candidate.
10. A dataset has outputs 300, 330, 363, 399 at `t = 0, 1, 2, 3`.
    Write an approximate exponential model and predict at `t = 5`,
    with one limit sentence.

## Scoring guide (for the guiding adult)

- Items 1–2 → Lesson 01 (reteach: base vs. percent rate).
- Item 3 → Lesson 02 (reteach: the ratio test before naming).
- Items 4–5 → Lesson 03 (reteach: conversion as translation).
- Items 6–7 → Lesson 04 (reteach: expand/condense as inverse passes).
- Items 8–9 → Lesson 05 (reteach: isolate-then-log; domain-first
  checking). Item 9's extraneous candidate is the key diagnostic:
  a learner who keeps it has not yet made checking a habit.
- Item 10 → Lesson 06 (reteach: ratios, then parameters, then limits).
- 9–10 correct: secure; move to the unit assessment. 7–8: repair the
  missed lessons in Session 16, then assess. Below 7: re-teach the
  missed lessons fully before assessing; do not let a weak logarithm
  foundation carry into Unit 06.
