# Unit 03 — Culminating Assessment

**Session 16 · about 15–20 minutes, with a break halfway if needed.**

For the guiding adult. All tasks are oral, pointing, or drawing; the adult
scribes dictated words. Each task maps to one unit objective. Record
*secure / developing / emerging* per task plus one evidence line. This
assessment is observational, not standardized or validated; it checks
readiness for Unit 04, not a grade for the repository.

## Tasks

**Task 1 — Past, present, future [Obj. 1].** The adult reads five events:
"yesterday it rained," "we are learning now," "tomorrow is a new day," "we
woke up this morning," "we will eat lunch later." The learner sorts each
into past / present / future. *Secure: 4–5 correct. Developing: 3 correct
or 4 with one prompt. Emerging: 0–2.*

**Task 2 — Timeline order [Obj. 2].** The learner orders five Willow Street
morning cards earliest to latest: lamp-lighter puts out lamps at dawn,
baker opens the shop, cart driver loads crates, children walk to school,
school bell rings (the adult adds the bell card and reads all five aloud).
The learner then explains one placement with "before" or "after."
*Secure: correct order + explanation. Developing: correct order, explanation
needs a prompt, or one adjacent swap defended with reasoning. Emerging:
cannot order.*

**Task 3 — Long ago and today [Obj. 3].** Using the lamp pair and the travel
pair, the learner names, for each area, one change and one stay-the-same.
*Secure: both areas, both parts. Developing: one area complete, or both
areas with one part each. Emerging: cannot name a change or a stay-the-same.*

**Task 4 — Source kinds [Obj. 4].** The learner names the three source kinds
(picture cards allowed) and sorts six examples (2 photographs, 2 objects,
2 stories — as in Session 10) into the right kinds, including answering
"none — it is a drawing for practice" for the Willow Street illustration if
the adult shows it.
*Secure: three kinds named + 5–6 sorted. Developing: two kinds or 3–4
sorted. Emerging: fewer.*

**Task 5 — Evidence answer [Obj. 5].** The adult shows one real source
(a printed old-street photograph or a real old object, previewed) and asks
one question about the past it can answer. The learner answers with the
evidence frame: "The ___ shows ___, so ___."
*Secure: evidence sentence, evidence actually visible in the source.
Developing: correct answer but no evidence pointed to, or evidence with a
prompt. Emerging: guessing from prior knowledge, not the source.*

**Task 6 — Two perspectives [Obj. 6].** "A child long ago carries water to
a basin every morning. You press a button and a machine washes. How might
that child feel about the machine, and how do you feel about the basin?
Why?" *Secure: both feelings with a reason tied to what is normal for each
child. Developing: one feeling with a reason, or both feelings without
reasons. Emerging: cannot compare.*

## Reflection (not scored)

- "What was your favorite part of learning about the past? Why?"
- "What do you still wonder about long ago?" (The adult notes the question
  — it seeds Unit 07's figure questions.)
- "Show me your Willow Street strip. Which panel are you most proud of?"

## Readiness and next steps

- **Ready for Unit 04** (community leaders, rules, decision making): secure
  or developing on Tasks 1, 2, and 4, with no emerging on Task 5's evidence
  habit — Unit 04's decision work reuses discussion rules, not source work,
  so a developing Task 3 or 6 is fine to carry forward with review games.
- **Reteach before Unit 04** if Task 2 (ordering) is emerging: replay the
  Session 4 timeline game with 3 cards for two short sessions in the Week
  15–16 flex time, then re-check with Task 2's first three cards.
- **Reteach if Task 5 is emerging:** replay Lesson 5's photograph example
  with the evidence frame posted as pictures, then re-check with a different
  source. Do not move on while the learner answers from guessing rather
  than from sources — Unit 07 depends on this habit.
