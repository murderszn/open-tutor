# Unit 03 — Formative Quiz (Week 14)

**Session 15 · about 10 minutes, gently — "show me what you know."**

For the guiding adult. All items are oral, pointing, or drawing; the adult
scribes dictated words. Mark each item *got it / getting there / not yet*
plus one evidence line. Do not score the learner aloud. Items map to the
unit objectives in brackets.

1. [Obj. 1] The adult says: "We ate breakfast this morning." "We are doing
   this quiz now." "We will play later." For each, the learner points to the
   past / present / future basket (or holds up the arrow/star card).
2. [Obj. 2] The adult hands over three shuffled picture cards: waking up,
   eating breakfast, coming to class. The learner orders them earliest
   first and says one "before" or "after" sentence.
3. [Obj. 3] The adult shows the lamp pair (oil lamp, electric light).
   "What changed? What stayed the same?"
4. [Obj. 3] The adult shows the washing pair (basin, washing machine).
   "What changed? What stayed the same?"
5. [Obj. 4] The adult shows three kind cards (camera, hand holding a thing,
   talking mouth). "Point to the photograph card. Point to the object card.
   Point to the story card." Then: "Is the Willow Street drawing a
   photograph, an object, a story — or none of these? Why?"
6. [Obj. 5] The adult shows a printed photograph of an old street (or the
   Willow Street illustration, labeled as practice only) and asks: "How did
   people travel here long ago? Show me your evidence."
7. [Obj. 6] "Nia, a child long ago, washed clothes in a basin. You use a
   washing machine. How might Nia feel about a washing machine? Why?"
8. [Obj. 1–2, retrieval] The adult shows a 4-event strip with one card out
   of place. "What is wrong? Fix it." (Any 4-event day strip; the adult
   builds it from the learner's real day or the Willow Street morning.)

**After the quiz:** run reteach stations for the wobbliest objectives
(Session 15 remainder): timeline-strip station (Obj. 2), changed/stayed
sort station (Obj. 3), source-sort station (Obj. 4), evidence-frame
station (Obj. 5–6). Aim to move at least one objective up a level before
Session 16. Full solutions are in [answer-key.md](../answer-key.md) §Quiz.
