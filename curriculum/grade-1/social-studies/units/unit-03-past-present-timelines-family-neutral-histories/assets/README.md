# Unit 03 — Assets

## willow-street-past-and-present.png

- **File:** `willow-street-past-and-present.png`
- **Prompt (verbatim, as sent to the image tool):** "A cheerful flat
  children's-book illustration, two side-by-side scenes of the same small
  fictional street, with no words or letters anywhere in the image. Left
  scene, long ago: a horse-drawn cart on a dirt road, a tall oil street
  lamp, a person washing clothes by hand in a basin outside a small house,
  clothes drying on a line. Right scene, today: a small car on a paved road,
  a tall electric streetlight, a house with a washing machine visible
  through an open doorway. Same houses and big tree in both scenes so
  children can spot what changed and what stayed the same. Bright, simple
  shapes, warm colors, suitable for six-year-olds."
- **Generation tool/model:** Muse built-in image generation (media tool),
  2026-10-23. No model name was reported by the tool; recorded as
  "Muse built-in image generation" rather than inventing one.
- **Date generated:** 2026-10-23.
- **Learning purpose:** Session 6 (Practice B) spotting activity — the
  learner finds 3 differences and 1 stay-the-same between the long-ago and
  today panels, and names which side is long ago with evidence. Also
  referenced in Lesson 3 (preview) and the project (drawing reference).
- **Factual review (inspected 2026-10-23 before linking):** the left panel
  shows a horse-drawn cart, an oil street lamp with a flame, hand washing
  in a basin, and a clothesline on a dirt road; the right panel shows a
  small car, an electric streetlight, a washing machine in an open doorway,
  and a paved road. The same small house, big tree, hills, and chimney
  smoke appear on both sides. No words or letters appear in the image. The
  street is fictional ("Willow Street"); the picture is an illustration
  made to help learners practice, **not a photograph of a real street and
  not historical evidence** — Lesson 4 teaches this distinction explicitly.
- **Caption:** Willow Street, long ago (left) and today (right) — the same
  street, drawn to help you spot what changed and what stayed the same.
  This is a made-up illustration for practice, not a photograph of a real
  street.
- **Alt text:** Two side-by-side drawings of the same small street. On the
  left, long ago: a horse pulls a wooden cart on a dirt road, a tall black
  oil lamp glows, a person washes clothes by hand in a basin, and clothes
  hang on a line beside a small house with a big tree. On the right, today:
  a small red car drives on a paved road, a tall gray electric streetlight
  stands at the corner, a washing machine is visible in the house doorway,
  and the same house and big tree appear.
- **Text-only alternative (contains everything needed for Practice B):**
  Long ago side — vehicle: horse-drawn wooden cart; road: dirt; street
  light: tall black oil lamp with a flame; washing: person scrubbing
  clothes by hand in a basin, clothes on a line. Today side — vehicle:
  small red car; road: paved gray road; street light: tall gray electric
  streetlight; washing: washing machine inside the open doorway, laundry
  basket beside it. Stayed the same on both sides — the small house with
  blue shutters and brown roof, the big green tree, the green hills, and
  smoke coming from the chimney. Questions answerable from this text alone:
  name 3 differences (lamp, vehicle/road, washing method); name 1
  stay-the-same (house, tree, hills, or chimney smoke); which side is long
  ago (left — horse cart, oil lamp, dirt road).
- **Known limitations:** the "today" panel has no clothesline, so a learner
  who expects one must be told the basket replaces it; the oil lamp's
  flame is stylized, not a technical diagram of how oil lamps work; the
  illustration is undated by design — "long ago" here means "before the
  learner was born," not a specific year. Do not use this image to teach
  dates, lamp mechanics, or any real street's history.
