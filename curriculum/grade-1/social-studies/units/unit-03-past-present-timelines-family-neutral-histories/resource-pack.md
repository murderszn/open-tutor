# Unit 03 — Resource Pack

**Goal:** give a guiding adult free, no-account materials that show — rather
than just tell — what past, present, timelines, and historical sources look
like for a six-year-old. **Age fit:** read-aloud and watch-together resources
for ages 5–7; the adult previews everything and teaches from it — the learner
never browses alone. External links checked **2026-10-23**; the adult
re-checks availability, advertising, and age suitability before each use.
At least one free, no-account route supports each core objective: the
NCSS/Loc.gov/PBS hubs below are free and need no learner account.

## Web references (verified 2026-10-23)

1. **NCSS — National Curriculum Standards for Social Studies**
   `https://www.socialstudies.org/standards/` — the C3 Framework's home page;
   teacher-side background on the four dimensions and the inquiry arc. No
   learner assignment; supports the standards notes in the unit README.
2. **Library of Congress — Teachers / Classroom Materials**
   `https://www.loc.gov/teachers/` — free, no-registration Primary Source
   Sets, lesson plans, and analysis tools for photographs, prints, and
   objects; teacher-side selection of one age-appropriate photograph or
   object print for Lessons 4–5 and the Session 10 source sort. The LOC's
   primary-source approach for elementary classrooms (observe → reflect →
   question) is the model for Lesson 5's evidence frame.
3. **Library of Congress — Primary source analysis approach (teacher guide
   background)** `https://www.colorincolorado.org/article/primary-sources-library-congress-and-english-learners`
   — describes the LOC's free, no-registration classroom materials and its
   Primary Source Sets; teacher-side background only, not assigned to the
   learner.
4. **PBS LearningMedia** `https://www.pbslearningmedia.org` — free,
   standards-aligned videos, interactives, and lesson plans from PBS member
   stations; educator account is free. Adult searches PreK–2 "past and
   present" to supplement Lessons 1–3.
5. **Khan Academy Kids** `https://www.khankids.org` — free, no-ads app for ages
   2–8 with first-grade lessons, read-aloud books, and sequencing content;
   family/teacher use; supports read-aloud routines and the Lesson 2
   first/next/last language.
6. **Reading Rockets — Using primary sources in the classroom**
   `https://www.readingrockets.org/blogs/sound-it-out/using-primary-sources-classroom`
   — teacher-side article on why elementary learners love primary sources
   and how the LOC's Teacher's Guides and Analysis Tools frame photo
   analysis; supports Lessons 4–5 for the adult, not assigned to the learner.
