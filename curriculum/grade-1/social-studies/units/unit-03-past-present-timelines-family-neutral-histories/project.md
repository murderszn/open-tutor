# Project — Willow Street Through Time

**Sessions 13–14 · about 15–20 minutes each, plus a short gallery share.**

## The task

Build a three-panel timeline strip of the fictional town of Willow Street:
**long ago**, **today**, and **one idea for the future**. Use picture sources
as your evidence for the first two panels: the long-ago panel must match
something a source shows (for example, the horse cart, oil lamp, or wash
basin from the unit's picture work), and the today panel must match
something true about streets today (car, electric light, washing machine).
The future panel is an *idea*, labeled as an idea — not a fact about the
future. Finish with a source card: name the kind of source you used most
(photograph, object, or story people tell) and one thing it showed you.

This project deliberately uses a fictional street. No learner family history
is needed, asked for, or displayed. If the learner adds their own life to a
strip at home, that version stays in private storage.

## Materials

- One long paper strip divided into three panels, labeled (by the adult)
  "long ago," "today," "future idea"
- Crayons, pencils; picture cards from Lessons 3–5 and the Willow Street
  illustration (printed or on screen) for reference
- A small "source card" (adult prints or draws): "My source kind: ___.
  It showed me: ___."
- Optional: textured shapes or stickers for low-vision learners; a floor
  version with three hoops instead of a paper strip

## Steps

**Session 13 — panels 1 and 2.**

1. **Recall (3 minutes):** look again at the Willow Street illustration and
   name one change and one stay-the-same. The adult writes the learner's
   words on the board or a card.
2. **Long-ago panel (5 minutes):** the learner draws the long-ago street:
   at least two long-ago features (for example, horse cart, oil lamp, wash
   basin, dirt road, clothesline). The adult labels with the learner's
   words (2–3 word labels).
3. **Today panel (5 minutes):** the learner draws today's street: at least
   two today features (for example, car, electric light, washing machine,
   paved road). One feature must be something that stayed the same as the
   long-ago panel (house, tree, or washing as a job) — the adult checks
   this explicitly.
4. **Check-in:** the learner points to each panel and says "long ago" or
   "today" for it. Fix any swapped panels now.

**Session 14 — future panel, source card, gallery share.**

5. **Future-idea panel (5 minutes):** the learner draws one idea for the
   future street (for example, a light that changes color, a cart that
   drives itself — any idea counts) and the adult adds the label "IDEA —
   not yet real" in the learner's words.
6. **Source card (3 minutes):** the learner completes the source card by
   pointing to or naming the source kind used most, and dictating one thing
   it showed ("the picture showed a horse cart").
7. **Gallery share (5 minutes):** the learner presents the strip in order —
   long ago, today, future idea — saying one sentence per panel, then
   answers one question from the adult or a classmate ("What stayed the
   same?"). Applause and one kind comment, per Unit 01 discussion rules.
8. **Keep or store:** the fictional strip may be displayed in class. Any
   personal version the learner made at home stays private.

## Deliverables

1. A three-panel strip: long-ago panel (≥2 long-ago features), today panel
   (≥2 today features + ≥1 stay-the-same feature), future-idea panel
   (labeled as an idea).
2. A completed source card (source kind + one thing it showed).
3. A short oral presentation (one sentence per panel) plus one answered
   question.

## Rubric

| Criterion | Secure (2) | Developing (1) | Emerging (0) |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Order** — panels in time order | Long ago → today → future idea, in order, and the learner can say which is which | Order mostly right, or needs one prompt to fix a swap | Panels out of order and the learner cannot say which is which |
| **Past–present comparison** — changes and stay-the-same | ≥2 long-ago features, ≥2 today features, and ≥1 stay-the-same feature shown | Two of the three present, or features present but unlabeled | Fewer than two features, or long-ago and today features mixed up |
| **Source use** — source card | Names a source kind and one thing it showed, matched to a panel | Names a kind or a thing shown, but not both, or mismatched | Cannot name a kind or anything shown |
| **Presentation** — explain in order | One sentence per panel + answers one question | Sentences for two panels, or answers with heavy prompting | Cannot present the strip in order |

**Scoring:** 7–8 = secure; 4–6 = developing (reteach the weakest criterion
in Session 15 stations); 0–3 = emerging (rebuild one panel with the adult
in Session 15, then re-present). The rubric is adult-observed; no grade is
recorded in the repository.
