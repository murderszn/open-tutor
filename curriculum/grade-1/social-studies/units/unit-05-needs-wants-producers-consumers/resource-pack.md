# Unit 05 — Resource Pack

**Goal:** give a guiding adult free, no-account materials that show — rather
than just tell — what needs, wants, scarcity, producers, consumers, and
saving look like for a six-year-old. **Age fit:** read-aloud and
watch-together resources for ages 5–7; the adult previews everything and
teaches from it — the learner never browses alone. External links checked
**2026-11-05** unless a different date is noted; the adult re-checks
availability, advertising, and age suitability before each use. At least one
free, no-account route supports each core objective: the NCSS, Ben's Guide,
PBS LearningMedia, and St. Louis Fed hubs below are free and need no
learner account. No real money, prices, or shopping are assigned anywhere
in this unit.

## Web references (verified 2026-11-05 unless noted)

1. **NCSS — National Curriculum Standards for Social Studies**
   `https://www.socialstudies.org/standards/` — the C3 Framework's home page;
   teacher-side background on the four dimensions and the inquiry arc. No
   learner assignment; supports the standards notes in the unit README.
   Home page confirmed loading 2026-11-05.
2. **Ben's Guide to the U.S. Government (U.S. Government Publishing Office)**
   `https://bensguide.gpo.gov/` — free, no-account civics site with an
   Apprentice Level for ages 4–8 (verified on the home page 2026-11-05);
   teacher-side selection of one age-appropriate page about community jobs
   for Lessons 4–5. The adult previews and reads aloud; nothing is
   assigned to the learner to browse alone.
3. **PBS LearningMedia** `https://www.pbslearningmedia.org` — free,
   standards-aligned videos, interactives, and lesson plans from PBS member
   stations; educator account is free. Adult searches PreK–2 "needs and
   wants," "community helpers," and "goods and services" to supplement
   Lessons 1 and 4. Home page confirmed loading 2026-11-05.
4. **Federal Reserve Bank of St. Louis — Economic Education, K–2 lessons**
   `https://www.stlouisfed.org/open-vault/2019/august/back-to-school-free-lessons-personal-finance-economics`
   — free lesson roundup whose Grades K–2 section covers opportunity cost,
   saving, and spending through companion books (including *Alexander, Who
   Used to Be Rich Last Sunday* for opportunity cost). Teacher-side only:
   the adult reads the roundup and adapts one idea orally; no book text is
   reproduced in this unit and no purchase is required. Page content
   verified via search result 2026-11-05; the adult re-checks the page
   before use.
5. **Library of Congress — Teachers / Classroom Materials**
   `https://www.loc.gov/teachers/` — free, no-registration Primary Source
   Sets and lesson plans; teacher-side selection of one age-appropriate
   photograph of a market or producer at work for Lesson 4 or Practice D
   contrast ("photograph of a real market" vs. "our invented drawing").
   The LOC's observe → reflect → question approach is the model for the
   picture questions in Practice D. Verified as the LOC Teachers program
   page; a 2024 crawl is the latest text fetch available, so the adult
   re-checks before use.
6. **Khan Academy Kids** `https://www.khankids.org` — free, no-ads app for
   ages 2–8 with first-grade lessons, read-aloud books, and routines;
   family/teacher use; supports read-aloud routines and the Lesson 3
   benefit/cost chant. Listed in prior unit packs (verified 2026-10-29);
   a text fetch on 2026-11-05 was blocked by the site's client challenge,
   so the adult re-checks availability before use.
