# Unit 05 — Culminating Assessment

**Session 16 · about 20 minutes · oral / pointing / drawing; adult-scribed.**

This assessment is a performance check against the unit's six objectives —
it is not a grade and is not standardized or validated. The adult reads
every task aloud. The learner may answer by speaking, pointing, drawing,
or acting out. Scoring is in [answer-key.md](../answer-key.md)
§Assessment — **for the guiding adult only.** Total: 20 points.

## Task 1 — Needs and wants sort (4 points)

Sort these 6 cards onto the *need* and *want* mats: water, apple, warm
coat, toy robot, ice-cream treat, board game. Then point to one need card
and say why it is a need.

- 4 points: 5–6 cards correct, plus a "must have it" reason.
- 2 points: 4 cards correct, or a correct sort with no reason.
- 0 points: 3 or fewer cards correct.

## Task 2 — Scarcity choice (4 points)

"Two friends, one swing. And you have tokens for one thing: a sticker
sheet or a balloon." (a) Name one fair way to share the swing. (b) Choose
the sticker sheet or the balloon, and say what you give up.

- 4 points: a fair plan (share, take turns, vote, wait) plus a choice
  plus the give-up named.
- 2 points: two of the three parts.
- 0 points: one or none.

## Task 3 — Benefit and cost (4 points)

"Maya has 6 tokens. She can spend 3 on stickers now, or save all 6 for a
puzzle later." (a) If she spends on stickers, name one benefit and one
cost. (b) If she saves for the puzzle, name one benefit and one cost.

- 4 points: a correct benefit and cost for both sides.
- 2 points: a correct benefit and cost for one side only.
- 0 points: benefit and cost swapped, or neither correct.

## Task 4 — Producers: goods and services (4 points)

Name 3 producers and, for each, say whether they make a good or do a
service, and name the good or service. (The learner may use the producer
cards, or name producers from the market picture, or both.)

- 4 points: 3 producers, each with a correct good/service label and item.
- 2 points: 2 producers correct, or 3 producers with one label wrong.
- 0 points: 1 or fewer producers correct.

## Task 5 — Product journey (2 points)

Put the 4 apple journey cards in order (orchard, truck, market stall,
child eating) and name the consumer at the end.

- 2 points: journey in order, consumer named (Maya, the child who eats
  the apple).
- 1 point: journey in order, consumer missing or wrong.
- 0 points: journey out of order.

## Task 6 — Earn, spend, save (2 points)

The learner is given 6 tokens "earned" for a market job. "Sort your tokens:
some to spend, some to save, and 1 to share if you wish. Tell me why you
saved what you saved."

- 2 points: tokens sorted into at least two jars/cups with a "for later"
  reason for saving.
- 1 point: tokens sorted, but no reason for saving.
- 0 points: tokens not sorted, or saving described as losing tokens.

## Reflection (not scored)

"Draw or tell me: one thing you can do now that you could not do before
this unit, and one thing you still want to practice." The adult scribes
the answer and uses it to plan Unit 06's opening retrieval.

## Readiness

- **16–20 points:** ready for Unit 06. Note any single-task weakness for
  Unit 06's warm-ups.
- **10–15 points:** ready with support. Reteach the lowest task in
  Unit 06's first week using the teacher guide's reteach stations.
- **Below 10 points:** reteach the two lowest objectives in full sessions
  before Unit 06's new content, using Lessons 1–5 in shortened form.
