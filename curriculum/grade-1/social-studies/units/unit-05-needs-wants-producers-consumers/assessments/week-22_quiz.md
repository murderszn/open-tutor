# Week 22 Formative Quiz — Needs, Wants, Producers, and Consumers

**Session 15 · about 15 minutes · oral / pointing / drawing; adult-scribed.**

This quiz is formative: it tells the adult what to reteach in Session 14's
follow-up and in Unit 06's opening retrieval — it is not a grade. The adult
reads every item aloud. The learner may answer by speaking, pointing, or
drawing. The adult records *got it / getting there / not yet* plus one
evidence line per item. Solutions are in [answer-key.md](../answer-key.md)
§Quiz — **for the guiding adult only.**

Items 1–8 check Unit 05 objectives; items 9–10 retrieve Units 04 and 02.

1. **Needs and wants.** Sort these 4 cards: water, warm coat, toy robot,
   ice-cream treat. Then: "Point to one need and tell me why it is a
   need."
2. **Scarcity.** "Three apples, four friends. What is the problem called,
   and name one fair way to handle it?"
3. **Benefit and cost.** "Maya spends 3 tokens on stickers and saves 3
   tokens for a puzzle. What is one good thing about spending on stickers
   now? What does she give up?"
4. **Producers — good or service.** "The baker makes bread, and the
   barber gives a haircut. Which one makes a good, and which one does a
   service? How do you know?"
5. **Consumer.** "Maya eats the apple she chose at the market. Is Maya a
   producer or a consumer right now? Why?"
6. **Journey order.** Put these 4 cards in order: market stall, orchard
   tree, child eating, truck on a road. Then: "Who is the producer in
   this journey?"
7. **Earn and save.** "The baker earns pay for baking bread. She keeps
   some pay for a new oven later. What is the pay called, and what is
   keeping some for later called?"
8. **Jar choice.** The learner is given 6 tokens. "Put 2 tokens where you
   would spend them, 3 where you would save them, and 1 in the share cup.
   Tell me one reason for your save jar."
9. **Retrieval — Unit 04.** "Put these 4 step cards in order: vote, do,
   listen, propose. What did our class vote decide in Unit 04?" (Any true
   classroom decision from Unit 04 counts; the order is what is scored.)
10. **Retrieval — Unit 02.** The adult shows a simple map of Willow Street
    with the market, the library, and the school. "Point to the market.
    Is the market next to the library or far from it?"

**After the quiz:** the adult picks the lowest-scoring objective, runs one
5-minute reteach station for it (see [teacher-guide.md](../teacher-guide.md)
§Reteach stations), and notes the plan for Unit 06's opening week.
