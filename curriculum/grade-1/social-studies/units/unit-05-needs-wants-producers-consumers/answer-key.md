# Unit 05 — Answer Key

**For the guiding adult only. Never place beside a learner question.**
Every item below was worked independently against the lesson text; open
responses list the acceptable range, not a single "correct" sentence. When
the learner's answer is defensible but different, accept it and note the
reasoning.

## §L1 — Lesson 1: Needs and wants

- **Guided 1** ("water on a hot day"): **need** — the body must have water.
- **Guided 2** ("a second ball, exactly the same"): **want** — an extra of
  something already owned; the learner can live without it.
- **Guided 3** ("a warm coat that fits, on a cold day"): **need** — clothes
  that fit and keep us warm are needs. Sorting question: "Must we have it?"
- **Guided 4** ("an ice-cream treat after lunch"): **want** — extra food
  for fun; Maya can live without it.
- **Independent** (6 cards): needs — water, apple, warm coat; wants — toy
  robot, ice-cream treat, board game. One reason each mat, e.g. "Water is
  a need because our bodies need it" / "A robot is a want because it is
  fun but we can live without it."
- **Exit check**: 5–6 cards + a reason = secure; 4 cards, reason shaky =
  developing.

## §L2 — Lesson 2: When we cannot have everything

- **Guided 1** ("three apples, four friends"): **scarcity** — fewer apples
  than friends; not enough for everyone to have a whole apple.
- **Guided 2** ("Maya picks the balloon"): she gives up the **sticker
  sheet** — for now.
- **Guided 3** ("one swing, four friends"): any fair plan — **share, take
  turns, vote, or wait**. The person who waits gives up **swinging now**.
- **Guided 4** ("both want the red crayon"): any two of share, take turns,
  vote; the learner picks one.
- **Independent** (4 two-want cards): sticker or balloon; storybook or
  puzzle; apple slices or crackers; swing first or slide first. For each,
  the choice plus the give-up, e.g. "I choose the storybook; I give up
  the puzzle."
- **Exit check**: scarcity in own words + choice + give-up = secure.

## §L3 — Lesson 3: Benefits and costs

- **Guided 1** ("spends 3 tokens on stickers"): benefit — **fun today**
  (stickers now); cost — **the puzzle waits** (3 tokens left, puzzle
  needs 6).
- **Guided 2** ("saves all 6 tokens"): benefit — **the puzzle sooner**;
  cost — **no stickers today**.
- **Guided 3** ("shares her crayons"): benefit — **a friend can draw too**
  (and may share back); cost — **fewer crayons in her own hand right
  now** (waiting for a color).
- **Guided 4** ("keeps her crayons"): benefit — **all her colors when she
  wants them**; cost — **her friend cannot draw**. Keeping is not always
  wrong: every choice has a benefit and a cost, and the pair is what is
  judged, not the side chosen.
- **Independent** (two boards): (a) eat the apple now — benefit: tasty
  now; cost: no apple later. Save the apple for after play — benefit: an
  apple to look forward to; cost: waiting while hungry. (b) play first —
  benefit: fun right away; cost: sorting still waits. Help sort first —
  benefit: job done, then free play; cost: play starts later.
- **Exit check**: benefit + cost for spend-or-save = secure.

## §L4 — Lesson 4: Producers make goods and services

- **Guided 1** ("carpenter makes a chair"): **good** — you can hold a
  chair; the hold-it test answers it.
- **Guided 2** ("teacher teaches a lesson"): **service** — teaching is
  help someone does; you cannot hold a lesson.
- **Guided 3** ("farmer vs. barber"): farmer — **good** (apples); barber —
  **service** (haircut).
- **Guided 4** ("a producer you have seen"): accept any true producer
  with a correct good/service label, e.g. cook/meal (good), cleaner/
  cleaning (service), singer/song (service).
- **Independent** (6 cards): goods — baker/bread, farmer/apples,
  carpenter/chair; services — barber/haircut, teacher/lesson, bus driver/
  ride. Hold-it sentences, e.g. "I can hold bread, so it is a good."
- **Exit check**: 2 producers + good/service + item each = secure.

## §L5 — Lesson 5: Consumers and where things come from

- **Guided 1** ("4 apple journey cards"): order — **orchard tree → truck
  → market stall → child eating**. Producer: the **farmer**. Consumer:
  **Maya** (the child who eats the apple).
- **Guided 2** ("bread journey"): wheat field (grown) → baker baking
  (made) → shop stall (sold) → family eating (used). "Made" is the baker
  baking step; "used" is the final step: the family eating.
- **Guided 3** ("bus driver earns pay"): driving for pay is **earning**;
  keeping some pay for later is **saving**.
- **Guided 4** ("Maya saves 3 of 6 tokens"): **No, they are not lost** —
  they are in the save jar, waiting for a bigger project later.
- **Independent**: apple journey in order + producer (farmer) + consumer
  (Maya); bread journey in order + producer (baker and/or wheat farmer) +
  consumer (the family); jar sort — 2 spend (paper apple), 3 save (bigger
  project later), 1 share (a friend with none), each with a reason.
- **Exit check**: 3-step journey in order + consumer explained = secure.

## §Practice — Practice sessions A–E

- **Practice A** (12 cards): needs — water, apple, warm coat, bed, milk,
  warm hat; wants — toy robot, ice-cream treat, board game, kite, candy,
  toy drum. Trap question: a second, identical kite is a **want**.
  Draw-and-dictate sentences must match the learner's own chosen need
  and want.
- **Practice B**: scarcity named ("not enough for everyone"); fair plans
  — share (cut an apple), take turns (three eat today, one first
  tomorrow), or wait; swing order first/second/third/fourth, with the
  fourth friend giving up swinging now; two-want frame completed ("I
  choose ___; I give up ___").
- **Practice C**: read-aloud A answers — earned for sorting apples;
  saving toward the big puzzle; gave up getting the puzzle today; saved
  tokens are not lost. Choice board 1 — see §L3 Guided 1–2. Choice board
  2 — see §L3 Guided 3–4. Jar sort — 2 spend, 3 save, 1 share, each with a
  reason; saved tokens described as "kept for later."
- **Practice D** (picture questions): 1 — baker, bread, **good** (can
  hold it). 2 — farmer, apples, **good**. 3 — barber, haircut,
  **service** (help someone does). 4 — child with the bag, **consumer**
  (choosing apples to use). 5 — shopkeeper, **producer**; goods on the
  stall: carrots, cucumbers, greens. 6 — any other producer in the
  picture with a correct good/service. 6-card sort: see §L4 Independent.
  Text-only alternative answers are identical.
- **Practice E**: read-aloud B answers — orchard (tree); a truck; the
  shopkeeper; Maya, the consumer, because she used the apple by eating
  it. Apple journey in order; bread journey in order; producers and
  consumers as in §L5 Independent. Swapped order (stall before truck)
  does **not** work — the apple must arrive in town before it can be
  sold.

## §Quiz — Week 22 formative quiz

1. Sort: needs — water, warm coat; wants — toy robot, ice-cream treat.
   One need with a "must have it" reason.
2. **Scarcity**; any fair plan (share, take turns, vote, wait).
3. Benefit of stickers now: **fun today**. Give-up: **the puzzle waits**
   (3 tokens left, puzzle needs 6).
4. Baker — **good** (bread, can hold it). Barber — **service** (haircut,
   help someone does).
5. **Consumer** — Maya uses the apple by eating it.
6. Order: **orchard tree → truck → market stall → child eating**.
   Producer: the **farmer**.
7. Pay is **income**; keeping some for later is **saving**.
8. Jar sort: 2 spend, 3 save, 1 share, with a "for later" reason for the
   save jar.
9. Order: **listen → propose → vote → do**. The decision named must be a
   true Unit 04 classroom decision (any such decision counts).
10. Market pointed to on the map; "next to the library" or "far from it"
    must match the map the adult shows (the adult checks the answer
    against that map, not against this key).

## §Assessment — Unit assessment (20 points)

- **Task 1** (4): needs — water, apple, warm coat; wants — toy robot,
  ice-cream treat, board game; plus a "must have it" reason. 5–6 correct
  + reason = 4; 4 correct or no reason = 2; 3 or fewer = 0.
- **Task 2** (4): a fair swing plan + a sticker/balloon choice + the
  give-up. All three = 4; two = 2; one or none = 0.
- **Task 3** (4): spend side — benefit: fun today; cost: puzzle waits.
  Save side — benefit: puzzle sooner; cost: no stickers today. Both
  sides = 4; one side = 2; swapped or neither = 0.
- **Task 4** (4): any 3 true producers with correct labels and items,
  e.g. baker/bread (good), farmer/apples (good), barber/haircut
  (service). 3 correct = 4; 2 correct or one label wrong = 2; 1 or
  fewer = 0.
- **Task 5** (2): orchard → truck → market stall → child eating, plus
  consumer Maya = 2; order only = 1; out of order = 0.
- **Task 6** (2): tokens in at least two jars/cups with a "for later"
  saving reason = 2; sorted without a reason = 1; unsorted, or saving
  called losing = 0.
