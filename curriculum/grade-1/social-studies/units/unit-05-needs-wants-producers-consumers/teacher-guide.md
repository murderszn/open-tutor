# Unit 05 — Teacher Guide

**For the guiding adult only.** This guide gives session-by-session
directions, the misconception map, reteach stations, and readiness
guidance. Solutions to every item are in [answer-key.md](answer-key.md).

## Before Week 19

- **Prepare once:** 12 needs/wants picture cards (Practice A); 3 apple
  cards and 4 place mats (Practice B); two choice boards, 6 play tokens,
  and 3 picture-labeled jars — *earn*, *spend*, *save* — plus a share cup
  (Practices C and E, project); 6 producer cards and *good*/*service*
  mats (Lesson 4, Practice D); apple and bread journey cards and a
  timeline strip (Lesson 5, Practice E); drawing paper, crayons, service
  offer cards, and the market poster (project). No real money is used at
  any point; tokens are counters.
- **Preview the read-alouds** in [practice.md](practice.md) and the
  market illustration in [assets/willow-street-market.png](assets/willow-street-market.png)
  before the sessions that use them. Say out loud in Practice D that the
  picture is an invented drawing, not a photograph.
- **Privacy rule:** never ask what the learner's home buys, what anyone
  in their home earns, or what the learner does or does not have at home.
  All sorts use pictured objects and Willow Street characters. Learner
  work stays in private storage.

## Session-by-session directions

| Session | Adult moves |
|---|---|
| 1 — Lesson 1 | Run the readiness check (Unit 03 timeline retrieval), then teach needs vs. wants with the must-have/would-like question. Work all 4 worked examples aloud before guided practice. Do not introduce scarcity yet. |
| 2 — Practice A | Shuffle the 12 cards; let the learner sort at their own pace. Ask the second-kite trap question last, after the sort is secure. Scribe the two dictated sentences exactly as spoken. |
| 3 — Lesson 2 | Teach scarcity with counting, not with fairness lectures: count apples, count friends, name the gap. Work the swing example fully — it is the one learners remember. |
| 4 — Practice B | Act out the apple plan with the cards before asking for words. If the learner insists one friend should simply go without, accept "wait" as fair only if a turn tomorrow is named — otherwise redirect to share or take turns. |
| 5 — Lesson 3 | Teach benefit and cost as a pair, always both sides, with the sentence frames "The good thing is ___" and "What I give up is ___." Keep the tokens visible on the table while you talk. |
| 6 — Practice C | Read "Maya and the Three Jars" slowly, pausing after Maya's choice. Then run both choice boards, then the jar sort. If the learner calls saved tokens "lost," stop and re-count the save jar together. |
| 7 — Lesson 4 | Teach the hold-it test before naming any producer. Work the baker/barber pair first — it is the clearest good/service contrast — then add farmer, carpenter, teacher, bus driver. |
| 8 — Practice D | Say the "invented drawing" line first. Ask the 6 picture questions in order, letting the learner point before speaking. Then run the 6-card good/service sort. Offer the text-only paragraph instead of the picture whenever the learner prefers words. |
| 9 — Lesson 5 | Lay the timeline strip out before teaching. Order the apple journey together, then let the learner order the bread journey alone. Teach earning and saving only after the journeys are secure. |
| 10 — Practice E | Read "The Apple That Traveled," then order both journeys, then plant the swapped-order error and let the learner catch it. Do not correct the swap yourself — wait for the learner's reason. |
| 11 — Project 1 | Frame the market question, help each learner make or rehearse one good/service, scribe stall cards verbatim, assign market jobs, and pay 6 tokens per job with the earning sentence spoken aloud. |
| 12 — Project 2 | Open the market, count token totals aloud twice, scribe choice sheets verbatim, and close with the 5-minute reflection. Post class totals only — never one child's token count. |
| 13 — Review | Run three stations in any order: needs/wants relay (sort 6 cards against a timer the learner controls), producer match (card to good/service mat), journey race (order 4 cards). Record which objective is weakest. |
| 14 — Reteach + share | Reteach the weakest objective with its station below, then run the market gallery share (learner presents the poster, no names or individual counts). |
| 15 — Quiz | Read every item aloud; accept speaking, pointing, drawing, or acting out. Record *got it / getting there / not yet* plus one evidence line per item. Do not teach during the quiz. |
| 16 — Assessment | Read every task aloud; score with the assessment's point bands. Close with the unscored reflection and note the Unit 06 warm-up plan. |

## Misconception map

| Misconception | What to say / do |
|---|---|
| "I want it a lot, so I need it." | Return to the sorting question: "Must we have it, or would we like it?" Use the water/robot pair. |
| "Every food is a need." | Use the apple vs. ice-cream-treat pair: food the body counts on is a need; a treat is extra. |
| "Scarcity means someone is being mean." | Count, don't blame: count the things, count the people, name the gap. |
| "A fair choice means nobody gives anything up." | Name the give-up in every fair plan: sharing means smaller pieces; turns mean waiting. |
| "Benefit and cost are the same word." / swapped | Anchor with frames: benefit = "the good thing is," cost = "what I give up is." |
| "Saving means losing my tokens." | Re-count the save jar together: the tokens are there, waiting for their job. |
| "Only shopkeepers are producers." | Name teacher, bus driver, and barber as producers of services. |
| "Producer and consumer are kinds of people." | Use Lesson 5, Example 4: the same person is both at different times. |
| "The market picture is a photograph." | Say the Practice D line out loud every time the picture is used. |

## Reteach stations (5 minutes each)

- **Needs/wants:** re-sort with only 4 cards (water, coat, robot, treat),
  then add one card at a time.
- **Scarcity:** replay Practice B with 2 apples and 3 friends, then 3
  apples and 4 friends, naming the give-up each time.
- **Benefit/cost:** re-run one choice board only (stickers/puzzle) with
  the two sentence frames on cards.
- **Goods/services:** re-sort with only the baker/barber pair, then add
  the farmer, then the rest.
- **Journeys:** re-order the apple cards only, with the four motion cues
  (crouch, drive, hold out hands, mime eating).
- **Jars:** re-sort 6 tokens with the adult narrating: "earned for work,
  spent now, saved for later, shared with a friend."

## Readiness and reteach guidance

- **Ready to move on (Unit 06):** the learner sorts needs/wants, names a
  benefit and a cost, names 2–3 producers with good/service labels, and
  orders a 3-step journey. Assessment 16–20 points.
- **Ready with support:** one objective shaky. Use its reteach station in
  Unit 06's first week; do not hold the whole unit back for one objective.
- **Reteach before Unit 06:** assessment below 10 points, or two
  objectives at *not yet*. Re-run the two weakest lessons in shortened
  form (worked examples + guided practice only) before Unit 06's new
  content.
- **What "secure" looks like, per objective:** see each lesson's exit
  check and the unit README's measurable objectives. When in doubt,
  re-check with a different response mode (pointing instead of speaking)
  before judging an objective unmet.
