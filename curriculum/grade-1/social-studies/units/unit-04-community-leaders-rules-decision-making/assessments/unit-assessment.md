# Unit 04 Assessment — Community Leaders, Rules, and Decision Making

**Session 16 · about 20 minutes maximum, with a break halfway · oral /
pointing / drawing; adult-scribed.**

Matched to the unit's six objectives. The adult reads every item aloud and
records *got it / getting there / not yet* plus one evidence line per item.
Solutions and scoring guidance are in [answer-key.md](../answer-key.md)
§Assessment — **for the guiding adult only**. This is a classroom check,
not a standardized or validated test.

## Part 1 — People and jobs (Objectives 1–2)

1. Name the 5 people in authority from this unit (teacher, principal,
   mayor, firefighter, police officer). For each one, say one job they do.
   *(Cards face-up as support; 1 point per role + job pair, 5 points.)*
2. "A fire starts in a shop on Willow Street. Who is counted on to put it
   out? Why that person and not the mayor?" *(1 point: firefighter +
   reason.)*
3. Name 2 community helpers who are not leaders, and say why each job
   matters. *(1 point per helper + reason, 2 points.)*

## Part 2 — Government and rules (Objectives 3–4)

4. "In your own words, what is a government?" *(1 point for any version of
   "a group of people whose job is to make rules for the town and run the
   things we share.")*
5. "Name one thing a government does." *(1 point.)*
6. Sort these 4 rule cards onto the classroom / school / town mats:
   "raise your hand to speak" (classroom), "stay in your line" (school),
   "do not litter in the park" (town), "return library books on time"
   (town or school — accept either with a reason). *(1 point per card,
   4 points.)*
7. "Pick one card from item 6 and tell me why that rule exists — safety,
   fairness, or both — and how you know." *(1 point.)*

## Part 3 — Deciding together (Objectives 5–6)

8. Put the 4 decision-step cards (listen, propose, vote, do) in order.
   *(1 point for the full correct order.)*
9. "Our class votes: bear story 5, space story 2. Which story do we read?
   You voted for the space story. What do you do on reading day?" *(1 point
   for winner + graceful acceptance.)*
10. "Is this a fair vote: one child votes twice because her friend is
    absent? Why or why not?" *(1 point: no + one person, one vote.)*
11. "Our classroom decision this week — what did we decide, and what did
    you do to help carry it out?" *(1 point.)*
12. "Name one thing about our decision that went well, and one thing to
    change next time." *(1 point: both parts named.)*

**Total: 20 points.** Readiness: 16–20 = secure, move on with Unit 05's
opening retrieval as planned; 11–15 = developing, reteach the weakest
objective in Unit 05 Week 19's first session; 0–10 = emerging, reteach the
two weakest objectives before starting Unit 05, using the reteach stations
in the [teacher guide](../teacher-guide.md).
