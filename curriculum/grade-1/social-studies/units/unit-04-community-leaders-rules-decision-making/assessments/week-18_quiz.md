# Week 18 Formative Quiz — Midyear Check (Units 01–04)

**Session 15 · about 15 minutes · oral / pointing / drawing; adult-scribed.**

This quiz is formative: it tells the adult what to reteach in Session 14's
follow-up and in Unit 05's opening retrieval — it is not a grade. The adult
reads every item aloud. The learner may answer by speaking, pointing, or
drawing. The adult records *got it / getting there / not yet* plus one
evidence line per item. Solutions are in [answer-key.md](../answer-key.md)
§Quiz — **for the guiding adult only**.

Items 1–6 check Unit 04 objectives; items 7–10 retrieve Units 01–03.

1. **Roles.** Point to the picture cards (teacher, principal, mayor,
   firefighter, police officer, shuffled) as the adult names each role.
   Then: "The school needs a new rule for the whole school. Who is in
   charge of the whole school?" (Principal.)
2. **Helpers matter.** "Name two helpers who are not leaders, and tell me
   one thing the town could not do without each of them."
3. **Government.** "In your own words, what is a government? Name one
   thing it does."
4. **Rules by setting.** Sort these 3 rule cards: "listen while others
   talk" (classroom), "walk in the hall" (school), "dogs on leashes"
   (town). Then: "Pick one card and tell me why that rule exists."
5. **Decision steps.** Put the 4 step cards (listen, propose, vote, do)
   in order. Then: "The vote chose the space story, but you wanted the
   bear story. What do you do on Friday?"
6. **Reflection.** "Our class decision this week: name one thing that went
   well and one thing to change next time."
7. **Retrieval — Unit 01.** "Name one right and one responsibility you
   have in our classroom."
8. **Retrieval — Unit 02.** "Point to the library on this simple map of
   Willow Street. Is the park near the school or far from it?"
9. **Retrieval — Unit 03.** "Put these 3 picture cards in order: waking
   up, eating breakfast, coming to class. Which happened first?"
10. **Retrieval — Unit 03.** "Is this picture a photograph of a real moment
    or a drawing someone made up? How do you know?" (Show one real
    photograph and the unit illustration side by side.)

**After the quiz:** the adult picks the lowest-scoring objective, runs one
5-minute reteach station for it (see [teacher-guide.md](../teacher-guide.md)
§Reteach stations), and notes the plan for Unit 05's opening week.
