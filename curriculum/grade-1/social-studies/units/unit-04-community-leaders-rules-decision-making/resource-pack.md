# Unit 04 — Resource Pack

**Goal:** give a guiding adult free, no-account materials that show — rather
than just tell — what community leaders, government jobs, rules, and voting
look like for a six-year-old. **Age fit:** read-aloud and watch-together
resources for ages 5–7; the adult previews everything and teaches from it —
the learner never browses alone. External links checked **2026-10-29**; the
adult re-checks availability, advertising, and age suitability before each
use. At least one free, no-account route supports each core objective: the
NCSS/Ben's Guide/PBS hubs below are free and need no learner account.

## Web references (verified 2026-10-29)

1. **NCSS — National Curriculum Standards for Social Studies**
   `https://www.socialstudies.org/standards/` — the C3 Framework's home page;
   teacher-side background on the four dimensions and the inquiry arc. No
   learner assignment; supports the standards notes in the unit README.
2. **Ben's Guide to the U.S. Government (U.S. Government Publishing Office)**
   `https://bensguide.gpo.gov/` — free, no-account civics site with an
   Apprentice Level for ages 4–8 (verified on the home page 2026-10-29);
   teacher-side selection of one age-appropriate page about government jobs
   or community rules for Lessons 3–4. The adult previews and reads aloud;
   nothing is assigned to the learner to browse alone.
3. **PBS LearningMedia** `https://www.pbslearningmedia.org` — free,
   standards-aligned videos, interactives, and lesson plans from PBS member
   stations; educator account is free. Adult searches PreK–2 "community
   helpers" and "rules" to supplement Lessons 1–4.
4. **Khan Academy Kids** `https://www.khankids.org` — free, no-ads app for ages
   2–8 with first-grade lessons, read-aloud books, and routines; family/
   teacher use; supports read-aloud routines and the Lesson 5
   listen–propose–vote–do chant.
5. **Library of Congress — Teachers / Classroom Materials**
   `https://www.loc.gov/teachers/` — free, no-registration Primary Source
   Sets and lesson plans; teacher-side selection of one age-appropriate
   photograph of a community helper at work for Lesson 1 or Practice B
   contrast ("photograph of a real helper" vs. "our invented drawing").
   The LOC's observe → reflect → question approach is the model for the
   picture questions in Practice B.
