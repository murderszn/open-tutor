# Unit 02 — Assets

## neighborhood-from-above.png

- **File:** `neighborhood-from-above.png` (PNG, 1920×1280)
- **Prompt (verbatim):** "A warm children's picture-book illustration of a
  small fictional neighborhood seen straight from above, as if a bird is
  looking down. Simple flat shapes, top-down view only: a winding blue river
  flowing through green land, a small round pond, several small houses with
  red and brown roofs, a school building with a playground, a park with round
  green trees, and curving gray roads connecting the houses, school, and park.
  Bright friendly colors, simple and uncluttered. No text, no words, no
  letters anywhere in the image. No people, no photographs."
- **Tool:** built-in image generation (`media.generate_image`, Muse
  pipeline), 2026-10-16. No model name reported by the tool.
- **Learning purpose:** used in Lesson 2 ([Session 3](../lessons/lesson-02-looking-down-like-a-bird.md),
  guided practice — spot the river, roads, and houses from above and explain
  how you know the view is from above), Lesson 3 (choose a symbol for the
  river), and Lesson 5 ([Session 9](../lessons/lesson-05-land-and-water.md),
  guided practice — point to two different waters). It is the mentor example
  for the Session 13–14 "My Neighborhood Map" project.
- **Factual review (inspected 2026-10-16):** top-down picture-book
  neighborhood: winding blue river across the top and right, round blue pond
  at lower right, green land, houses with red/brown roofs (tops only, no
  doors/windows), a larger school-like building with a playground, a park
  with round green trees, curving gray roads. No legible text anywhere. No
  real people, no real learner photos, no real place depicted — the
  neighborhood is fictional. **This is an AI-generated illustration, not a
  photograph, a real map, or a primary source** — labeled as such in the
  caption.
- **Caption:** "A neighborhood seen from above (AI illustration). What can
  you spot that a bird would see?"
- **Alt text:** "A colorful top-down picture-book illustration of a fictional
  neighborhood: a winding blue river, a round blue pond, green land, several
  houses with red and brown roofs, a school building with a playground, a park
  with round green trees, and curving gray roads."
- **Text-only alternative** (usable for every activity above): "The picture
  shows a neighborhood from above. A winding blue river flows across the top
  and down the right side. A round blue pond sits at the lower right. The land
  is green. There are several houses with red and brown roofs, a large school
  building with a playground, a park with round green trees, and curving gray
  roads connecting them. Everything is seen from the top — you cannot see any
  doors or windows." All spotting questions are answerable from this
  description.
- **Known limitations:** stylized illustration — roof colors, tree shapes,
  and the river's course are artistic, not documentary. The image carries no
  measurements, labels, borders, or geographic claims, so no precision risks
  apply. It is a fictional place and must never be presented as a real
  neighborhood.
