# Unit 02 — Answer Key

**For the guiding adult only. The learner never sees this file.** Every item
below was solved independently against the lesson as written and reconciled
with the lesson's success criteria. Accept any wording that carries the same
meaning; grade-1 answers may be oral, pointed, drawn, or dictated.

## §L1 — Lesson 1: Where is it?

- **Worked examples:** Ex. 1 — the ball is *next to* the adult; *above* the
  floor and *below* the light (one thing can be above one thing and below
  another). Ex. 2 — book on the learner's *left* (L-hand side), cup on the
  *right*; when the adult moves, the book is no longer on *their* left —
  left/right move with the person.
- **Guided practice:** 1. ball higher than the book. 2. ball lower than the
  book. 3. shoe touching or almost touching the cup. 4. any far object pointed
  to with the L-hand. 5. above. 6. one step toward the non-L-hand side.
- **Acceptable variations:** "on" counts as above when the thing rests on top
  (ball *on* the book = above it); "under" counts as below; "beside" counts as
  next to. Do not accept "over there" without a location word.
- **Exit check:** secure = 4+ of the 6 words used correctly across the lesson;
  developing = 2–3; emerging = 0–1.

## §L2 — Lesson 2: Looking down like a bird

- **Worked examples:** Ex. 1 — side view: tall cup, side visible; above view:
  circle, only the top. Ex. 2 — illustration: no doors/windows visible; roofs
  only; blue winding shape = river from above; gray curves = roads from above.
- **Guided practice:** 1. river or pond (water). 2. roads. 3. roofs seen from
  above (accept "the red/brown squares"). 4. from above — we see tops, not
  sides. 5. accept "a picture of a place seen from above" or equivalent ("a
  drawing of what a bird sees").
- **Independent practice:** any 3 of: river, pond, houses/roofs, school,
  playground, park/trees, roads.
- **Exit check:** secure = defines a map with the above view + names the view
  difference; developing = "a picture of a place" without above; emerging =
  cannot distinguish views.

## §L3 — Lesson 3: Map symbols and keys

- **The five card meanings:** blue wavy line = river (moving water); green
  triangle = tree; red square = house; gray curving line = road; blue circle =
  pond or lake (still water).
- **Worked examples:** Ex. 1 — blue wavy line *could* mean river, but only the
  key makes it certain. Ex. 2 — reading the demo key: 3 houses; yes, a tree
  beside the road; none of it required visiting.
- **Guided practice:** 1. pond/lake (blue = water; circle = still). 2. in the
  key. 3. no — without the key the symbols are guesses. 4. blue wavy line.
  5. any simple drawing + a stated meaning (the stating is the scored part).
- **Exit check:** 5 cards in mixed order — secure = 4–5 with "stands for"
  language; developing = 3; emerging = 0–2.

## §L4 — Lesson 4: My route map

- **Worked examples:** Ex. 1 — route home→park; landmarks in order: big tree,
  corner store, park; only on-the-way features drawn. Ex. 2 — right landmarks
  in wrong order = a lost traveler; order is everything.
- **Guided practice:** 1–3. depend on the chosen real route (adult pre-verified
  the answers before the session). 4. "first ___, then ___, last ___" in the
  verified order. 5. the big tree (on the way); never the far house.
- **Route-map check:** secure = ≥3 landmarks in order + line of travel;
  developing = 2 landmarks or shaky order; emerging = cannot sequence.
- **Privacy:** maps say "home"/"the park" — never a street address or house
  number.

## §L5 — Lesson 5: Land and water

- **Worked examples:** Ex. 1 — illustration river = water (blue agreement);
  globe blue = water (oceans); same agreement at both sizes. Ex. 2 — accept
  any real water (puddle, creek, lake, ocean; bathtub counts if the learner
  then sorts it as indoor water); accept any land shape (hill, flat field,
  mountain).
- **Guided practice:** 1. blue areas = water; green/brown = land. 2. river and
  pond (two different waters). 3. any real water seen. 4. any land shape seen.
  5. green means land — a green river breaks the agreement and confuses
  readers.
- **Color-a-map:** water (river, pond) blue; land green; one dictated label
  each. Acceptable: labels in home language.
- **Exit check:** secure = both globe points correct + one landform and one
  water body named from experience; developing = points only; emerging =
  confuses blue/green.

## §L6 — Lesson 6: Places have features

- **Worked examples:** Ex. 1 — school = cultural (people made it); river =
  environmental (people did not). Ex. 2 — park trees = environmental (trees
  grow); the park's paths/benches/planted rows = cultural; one thing can carry
  both kinds.
- **Guided practice:** 1. houses — cultural. 2. pond — environmental. 3. roads
  — cultural. 4. park trees — environmental. 5. any street feature people made
  (sidewalk, mailbox, school, store). 6. any not-made-by-people feature (tree,
  hill, creek, sun/sky).
- **Edge-case rulings:** planted garden/flower bed = cultural (people arranged
  it; note the plants themselves are environmental — accept either with
  reasoning); mowed lawn = cultural; wildflower patch = environmental; bench =
  cultural (built) even though wood comes from trees.
- **Two-column sort:** ≥2 items per column; secure = one of each kind named +
  states the difference ("people made it" vs. "they did not").

## §P — Practice sessions and read-alouds

- **Practice A:** 5 clues → accept any object that truly matches each clue
  (adult verifies placements before the session); picture-map secure = 3
  features in sensible positions.
- **Read-aloud A (*Where Is Bear?*):** honey pot location = below the window,
  next to the flower pot, near the window, far from the door, to Bear's left.
  Accept any 3 location words in the retelling.
- **Practice B:** the two drawings must differ (sides vs. tops); the above-view
  drawing is map-like.
- **Practice C:** Round 1 = the five §L3 meanings; Round 2 = same, by pointing;
  own key = 3 symbols each with a stated meaning; the adult must be able to
  read a mini-map from the key alone.
- **Walk practice:** landmarks numbered in the walked order; finished route map
  = ≥3 landmarks + line of travel + ≥2-symbol key. Indoor-route alternative
  scores identically.
- **Read-aloud B (*Mia's Walk to the Park*):** Mia's order = home (square)
  → big oak tree (triangle) → corner store (rectangle) → park (circle).
  Learner compares with their own first/last landmarks.
- **Globe practice:** 3 stops × land/water = all correct for secure; "more
  blue" for the stretch question; coloring follows the agreement.

## §Q — Formative quiz (Session 15)

- **Q1:** points above (ceiling, light) and below (floor, rug) — any correct
  pair. Obj. 1.
- **Q2:** "from above, like a bird" + evidence (roofs/tops, no sides/doors).
  Obj. 2.
- **Q3:** blue wavy line = river; red square = house; green triangle = tree.
  Obj. 3.
- **Q4:** traces the travel line; names landmarks first→then→last in the
  walked order. Obj. 4.
- **Q5:** points to blue (water) and green/brown (land) on the globe. Obj. 5.
- **Q6:** one people-made + one not-made-by-people neighborhood feature.
  Obj. 6.
- Each item: secure/developing/emerging per the quiz file; the lowest item
  chooses the Session 15 reteach.

## §UA — Unit assessment (Session 16)

- **Task 1:** placements — ball next to book (touching/almost); cup below book
  (lower); point left (L-hand), point right. Secure = 4/4.
- **Task 2:** accept "a picture of a place seen from above" or equivalent;
  follow-up: the top. Secure = definition + view.
- **Task 3:** reads the printed key first (secure behavior), then: 3 houses
  (three red squares), yes a river (two blue wavy lines), yes — the triangle
  touches one square (the adult draws it that way per the assessment).
- **Task 4:** ≥3 room features placed sensibly from above + key explaining
  every symbol = secure.
- **Task 5:** globe points + one landform and one water body from the
  learner's experience = secure.
- **Task 6:** one of each kind + the difference stated = secure.
- **Totals:** 10–12 secure; 7–9 developing (reteach lowest tasks, re-check
  those only); below 7 emerging (revisit practices before Unit 03).
