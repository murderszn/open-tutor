# Unit 02 — Formative Quiz (Session 15)

Adult-led, oral/point/draw. The adult reads each item, the learner responds,
and the adult records against the objective in parentheses. Scribe any words
the learner dictates. **Learners never see the answer key**
([answer-key.md](../answer-key.md) §Q).

**Q1.** "Point to something *above* you. Now point to something *below* you."
(Obj. 1 — location words)

**Q2.** The adult holds up the bird's-eye illustration. "Is this picture made
from the sidewalk or from above, like a bird? How do you know?" (Obj. 2 —
bird's-eye view)

**Q3.** The adult shows three symbol cards (blue wavy line, red square, green
triangle). "What does each one stand for?" (Obj. 3 — symbols)

**Q4.** "Show me your route map. Trace the line of travel with your finger and
name the landmarks in order: first, then, last." (Obj. 4 — route map)

**Q5.** The adult shows the globe. "Point to water. Point to land." (Obj. 5 —
land and water)

**Q6.** "Tell me one thing in our neighborhood that people made, and one thing
people did not make." (Obj. 6 — cultural/environmental features)

## Scoring

Each item: *secure* (correct, unprompted), *developing* (correct with one
hint), *emerging* (incorrect or no response after a hint). The adult uses the
tallies to choose the Session 15 reteach: re-teach the lowest objective with
its lesson's worked examples before the Session 16 assessment. Full solutions
and acceptable variations: [answer-key.md](../answer-key.md) §Q.
