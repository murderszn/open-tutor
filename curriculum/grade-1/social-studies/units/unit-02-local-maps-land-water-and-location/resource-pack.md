# Unit 02 — Resource Pack

**Goal:** give a guiding adult free, no-account materials that show — rather
than just tell — what bird's-eye views, map symbols, land and water, and
neighborhood features look like for a six-year-old. **Age fit:** read-aloud
and watch-together resources for ages 5–7; the adult previews everything and
teaches from it — the learner never browses alone. External links checked
**2026-10-16** unless noted; the adult re-checks availability, advertising,
and age suitability before each use.

## Free, no-account routes per core objective

- Objectives 1–2 (location words, what a map is): the unit's own lessons,
  scavenger hunt, and illustration need no account and no internet.
- Objective 3 (symbols and keys): printable symbol cards drawn by the adult;
  no account needed.
- Objective 4 (draw a map): paper and crayons — no resource needed at all.
- Objective 5 (land and water): a globe or printed world map plus the unit's
  color-a-map; USGS pages are teacher-side background only.
- Objective 6 (cultural/environmental features): the neighborhood walk and
  the unit's own illustration — no account needed.

Every core objective can be fully taught offline with the materials in this
unit; videos and links supplement teaching rather than replacing it.
