# Unit 01 — Resource Pack

**Goal:** give a guiding adult free, no-account materials that show — rather
than just tell — what communities, rights, responsibilities, and rules look
like for a six-year-old. **Age fit:** read-aloud and watch-together resources
for ages 5–7; the adult previews everything and teaches from it — the learner
never browses alone. External links checked **2026-10-09**; the adult
re-checks availability, advertising, and age suitability before each use.

## Free, no-account routes per core objective

- Objectives 1–2 (communities, rules): the unit's own lessons, illustration,
  and original read-alouds need no account and no internet.
- Objective 3 (sort rights/responsibilities): printable picture cards drawn by
  the adult; Khan Academy Kids app is free with no ads (optional).
- Objective 4 (discussion rules, fairness): the poster and sentence frames are
  made on paper; PBS LearningMedia videos are a free-account supplement only.
- Objective 5 (carry out a responsibility): real classroom jobs — no resource
  needed at all.

Every core objective can be fully taught offline with the materials in this
unit; videos and links supplement teaching rather than replacing it.
