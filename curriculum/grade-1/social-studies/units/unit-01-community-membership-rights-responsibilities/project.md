# Project — Our Classroom Charter

**Sessions 12–13 · two ~20-minute sessions, adult-facilitated.**

The learner helps write, illustrate, and "sign" a classroom charter: a poster
that states the community's rights, responsibilities, and agreed rules. This is
the unit's investigation: *What does OUR community promise each member, and
what does it ask of each member?*

## Materials

- One large poster board or taped-together paper, divided by the adult into
  three columns labeled **OUR RIGHTS** / **OUR RESPONSIBILITIES** / **OUR RULES**
- Crayons, markers; the learner's drawings from Sessions 2, 6, and 8
- Sticker stars or a stamp pad for "signing"
- The discussion-rules poster from Lesson 5

## Steps

### Session 12 — Draft our charter page

1. **Gather evidence (5 min).** The adult spreads out the learner's earlier
   work: the communities drawing, the rights drawing, the rule drawings, the
   three "fix" cards from Practice B, and the invented rule card from Practice C.
   "Our charter will be built from YOUR work — nothing new to invent."
2. **Choose (5 min).** The learner picks: one right to promise every member,
   two responsibilities members will do, and one rule the class will follow.
   The adult asks the Lesson 3 detective question for each responsibility:
   "Whose right does it protect?"
3. **Make the page (10 min).** The learner draws each choice in its column
   (simple pictures: a raised hand, ears listening, toys in a bin, a walking
   figure). The adult scribes the learner's dictated labels: "Everyone gets
   to be heard." / "We listen." / "We walk inside." Keep labels to the
   learner's own words — this is their charter, not the adult's.
4. **Fairness check.** The adult asks: "Does our charter treat every member
   the same? Is there anyone it leaves out?" Revise one item if needed.

### Session 13 — Gallery and signing

1. **Present (5 min).** The learner walks the adult (or household members)
   through the poster, column by column, using the sentence frame: "This is
   our right to ___. This is our responsibility to ___. This is our rule to
   ___."
2. **Sign (5 min).** The learner "signs" the charter with a sticker star, a
   thumbprint, or a dictated name the adult writes. The adult signs too —
   members *and* authorities keep the charter.
3. **Put it to work (10 min).** The poster goes on the wall at the learner's
   eye level. The learner picks one responsibility from the charter to do
   *today*, does it, and reports: "I kept our charter when I ___."
4. **Reflect.** "Which part of the charter are you proudest of? Which part
   will be hardest to keep?" (The adult notes the answers — they are the
   Session 16 reflection seed.)

## Deliverables

1. The illustrated, labeled charter poster (kept in the learning space; a
   photo may go in the learner's *private* portfolio — never in this public
   repository).
2. The learner's oral walk-through of all three columns.
3. One same-day kept responsibility, reported aloud.

## Rubric — observable criteria

| Criterion | Emerging | Developing | Secure |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Rights** | Cannot name a right for the charter, even with choices offered | Chooses a right from offered choices and repeats its name | Independently names a right and explains it in own words ("everyone gets to be heard") |
| **Responsibilities** | Cannot connect a job to the community | Names a responsibility with the adult's detective prompt ("whose right does it protect?") | Names two responsibilities and states whose right each protects, unprompted |
| **Rules** | Cannot state a rule for the charter | Repeats a rule from earlier sessions | Proposes or adapts a rule and names which of the two jobs it does |
| **Participation** | Watches; does not contribute choices | Contributes with heavy prompting | Chooses, draws, presents, and signs with light prompting only |
| **Keeping the charter** | Does not attempt the same-day responsibility | Attempts it with reminders | Carries it out and reports who it helped |

**Scoring:** the adult circles one level per row and writes one evidence line
("said 'we listen so everyone is heard'"). *Secure* in 3+ rows = ready for
Unit 02's community-map work; *emerging* in 2+ rows = re-teach Sessions 5–8
before moving on.

## Accessibility and safety

- All drawing may be replaced by pointing to picture cards the adult arranges;
  dictation may be replaced by the adult offering two word choices ("heard or
  safe?").
- The charter stays inside the learning space. No names, photos, or schedules
  go into this repository — the poster itself is never committed.
- No contact with outside authorities and no public posting of any kind.
