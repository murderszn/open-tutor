# Unit 01 — Formative Quiz (Session 14)

**Format:** observed, oral, drawn, or pointing. The adult reads each prompt,
the learner responds in any mode, and the adult scribes dictated words the
same day. This quiz is **formative** — it tells the adult what to re-teach in
Session 15, not what grade the learner earned. Keep all responses and notes
in private storage; nothing learner-specific enters this repository.

**Scoring:** for each item mark **got it** (secure), **getting there**
(developing), or **not yet** (emerging). Sample acceptable responses are in
[answer-key.md](../answer-key.md) §Q.

1. **Communities.** "Tell me two communities you belong to, and name one
   person in each." *(Objective 1)*
2. **Right.** Point to the raised-hand picture card. "What right is this
   child using?" Then: "Tell me one right you have." *(Objective 1)*
3. **Responsibility.** Point to the toy-bin picture card. "Is this a right
   or a responsibility? Whose right does it protect?" *(Objective 3)*
4. **Rules.** "Why do we have rules? Tell me one rule from home and one rule
   from our classroom." *(Objective 2)*
5. **Fairness.** Show two plates — one with two crackers, one with none.
   "Is this fair? How would you make it fair?" *(Objective 4)*
6. **Discussion rules.** "We are going to talk about our charter. What are
   our three discussion rules?" *(Objective 4; accept gestures for listen /
   hand-raise / respond.)*

**What to do with the results:** any item marked *not yet* becomes a
Session 15 review-game station. If items 2 and 3 are both *not yet* (rights
vs. responsibilities still blurred), re-teach the Practice B sort before the
unit assessment. Record the pattern, not just the score: e.g. "names rights
easily, hesitates on who they protect."
