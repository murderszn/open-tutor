# Unit 01 — Culminating Assessment (Session 16)

**Format:** performance tasks observed by the adult — oral, drawn, pointing,
and doing. No paper test; a six-year-old shows civics by living it. The adult
records evidence lines ("said…", "drew…", "did…") in private storage. Allow
about 20 minutes; offer a movement break halfway. Acceptable responses and
scoring guidance are in [answer-key.md](../answer-key.md) §UA. **This is a
draft, teacher-made check — not a standardized or validated assessment.**

## Task 1 — My communities (Objectives 1, 3)

"Draw yourself in the middle. Now draw two communities you belong to around
you, with one person in each." Then: "Point to one person and tell me one
right they have and one responsibility they have in that community."
*(Looks for: two communities named; a right and a responsibility per
community — sentence frames allowed.)*

## Task 2 — Right or responsibility? (Objective 3)

Lay out five picture cards: *child raising hand; child listening; toys being
put away; child sharing crayons; child being read to.* "Sort these into rights
and responsibilities. For one card, tell me why you put it there."
*(Secure = 4–5 correct with a reason for the explained card.)*

## Task 3 — Why rules? (Objective 2)

"Our classroom has a rule: we walk inside. Why does that rule exist?" Then:
"Tell me one rule from home and one rule from a public place like the park.
Why does each one exist?" *(Secure = gives the two-jobs idea — safe / helps
everyone learn and play — in own words for at least two of the three rules.)*

## Task 4 — Fairness and discussion in action (Objective 4)

Run a real 3-minute group talk: "Should we keep our charter poster by the
door or by the rug? Discuss, then vote." The adult observes: does the learner
listen, wait for one speaker at a time, and respond to someone else's idea?
After the vote: "Was that fair? Why?" *(Secure = follows all three discussion
rules with at most one reminder; explains fairness as same-rules-for-everyone
or everyone-gets-what-they-need.)*

## Task 5 — Keep the charter (Objective 5)

"Choose one responsibility from our charter and do it right now." The learner
does it, then answers: "Who did your work help?" *(Secure = completes the job
and names who it helped; developing = completes with reminders.)*

## Reflection (not scored)

"Which part of this unit are you proudest of? What do you still wonder
about communities?" The adult notes the answer — it opens Unit 02's retrieval
warm-up.

## Overall judgment

- **Ready for Unit 02:** Tasks 1–4 at least *developing*, Task 2 *secure*,
  Task 5 attempted. Unit 02's map work revisits community vocabulary, so
  developing-level ideas keep growing there.
- **Re-teach first:** Task 2 *emerging* (rights/responsibilities blurred) →
  replay Practice B and Lesson 3 before Unit 02. Task 4 *emerging* →
  keep the discussion-rules chant daily through Unit 02's first week.
