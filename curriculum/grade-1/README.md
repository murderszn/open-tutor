# Grade 1 — Independent Curriculum Track

This folder is an anonymous, grade-specific track in the public OpenTutor curriculum library.

**Contents:** Four subject starter lessons are available. Draft audits and scope-and-sequences are available for [Mathematics](math/scope-and-sequence.md), [Language arts](language-arts/scope-and-sequence.md), [Science](science/scope-and-sequence.md), [Social studies](social-studies/scope-and-sequence.md). Mathematics Units 01 (Counting, place value, and tens) and 02 (Addition strategies within 20), Language arts Unit 01 (Phonemic review, short vowels, and decoding), Science Unit 01 (Questions, observations, and safe investigations), and Social studies Unit 01 (Community membership, rights, and responsibilities) are delivered as validated drafts. Remaining units are planned; no subject track is complete. Materials still require educator review.
