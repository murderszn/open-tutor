# Resource Pack — Unit 04: Fluency, Retelling, Characters, and Key Details

## Summary

**Goal:** give the guiding adult free, no-account routes to
support grade-1 fluent oral reading, retelling with key details,
story parts and lessons, feeling/sense words, narrator
awareness, and comparing two stories. **Age fit:** grade 1
(about 6–7); all items are visual, musical, or read-aloud, with
minimal reading required of the learner.
