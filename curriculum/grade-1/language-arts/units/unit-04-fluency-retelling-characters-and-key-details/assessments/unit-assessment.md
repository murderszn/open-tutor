# Unit Assessment — Unit 04 (culminating)

Adult-administered; the learner answers orally, by pointing, or
by drawing, except where a short written or dictated sentence is
asked for. Do not show the learner the answer key. This
assessment is a teacher-made check of unit objectives, not a
standardized or validated test.

## Part A — Fluent reading (objectives 1)

Read "Ana and the Lost Mitten" (unit README) aloud twice. The
adult listens to both reads and records: accuracy (words read
correctly), rate (not racing, not stalling), expression (sad
voice when Ana is sad, happy voice at the end), and one
self-correction the learner makes or is prompted to make.

**Ready:** second read smoother than the first, mostly accurate,
expression matches at least one feeling, and the learner fixes at
least one word that does not make sense (or explains a fix).

## Part B — Key details and retelling (objectives 2, 3)

1. Who are the characters in the Ana story?
2. Where does the story happen?
3. What is the biggest problem in the story?
4. Retell the Ana story in order: beginning, middle, end.
5. What lesson does the Ana story teach?

## Part C — Story parts and evidence (objectives 4, 5)

6. Name one feeling word and one sense word from the Ana story.
7. Who tells the Ana story — Ana or a narrator? How do you know?
8. Look at the unit illustration
   ([milo-red-boat-pond.png](../assets/milo-red-boat-pond.png)):
   name one character, the setting, and one event it shows, and
   say which part of the Milo story it shows (beginning, middle,
   or end). Text-only alternative: "A boy in a yellow raincoat
   kneels at a pond and launches a small red sailboat; a brown
   dog sits beside him watching; a tree bends in the wind."

## Part D — Compare and classify (objective 6)

9. Name one thing that is the same and one thing that is
   different about Milo's and Ana's adventures.
10. Is "All About Ponds" (a book of pond facts with headings) a
    storybook or an information book? How do you know?

## Scoring

- Parts A–D together: 10 checkpoints (A counts as 1 readiness
  judgment; B items 1–5; C items 6–8; D items 9–10, with item 9
  needing both a sameness and a difference).
- **Ready for Unit 05:** 8/10 or better, including the retelling
  in order (B4) and the fluent second read (A).
- **Re-teach before Unit 05:** retelling out of order → Lesson
  03 and Session C; flat or racing read → Lesson 01 and Session
  A; narrator confusion → Lesson 05 and Session F; comparison
  with only one side → Lesson 06 and Session G.
