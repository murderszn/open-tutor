# Week-04 Quiz — Unit 04 (formative)

Adult reads the questions aloud; the learner answers orally, by
pointing, or by drawing. Do not show the learner the answer key.
10 items; a score of 8/10 with the retelling in order means the
learner is on track. Re-teach any missed objective in Session H
before the unit assessment.

1. Read this sentence twice: "Milo puts the boat in the water."
   Which read was smoother — the first or the second? Why?
2. Who is Milo's dog?
3. Where does the Milo story happen?
4. What happens to the boat in the middle of the story?
5. Retell "The Slow Race" in three parts: beginning, middle, end.
6. Name one feeling word from any unit story.
7. Name one sense word from any unit story.
8. Who says "Slow and steady wins the race" — the narrator or
   Turtle?
9. What is the same about Milo's and Ana's adventures? Name one
   thing.
10. Is "Ana and the Lost Mitten" a storybook or an information
    book? How do you know?

**Scoring:** 8/10 or better, with the retelling in order (item
5), means the learner is on track. Re-teach any missed objective
in Session H before the unit assessment.
