# Teacher Guide — Unit 04: Fluency, Retelling, Characters, and Key Details

## How to use this unit

Each lesson is 20–25 minutes, four sessions a week. Lessons 01–06
introduce the skill; practice sessions A–H rehearse it; the
project (Sessions 12, 14) is the performance piece. The adult
reads all story text aloud first — the learner's job in Week 15
is rereading, not cold decoding. By Week 18 the learner reads the
Ana story aloud twice for the assessment.

## Lesson-by-lesson notes

### Lesson 01 — Reread like a storyteller

Fluency is taught, not timed. Never reward speed alone; if a
learner races, cover all but one sentence and reread for sense.
Self-correction is the highest-value habit in this lesson: model
"Did that make sense?" daily, before correcting any word. A
learner who reads accurately but flat needs more echo reading,
not more passages.

### Lesson 02 — Ask and answer key-detail questions

Keep answers anchored in the text: "Show me the sentence." The
trick question ("What does Milo eat for lunch?") matters — it
teaches that not every question has a story answer, which stops
guessing. Accept pointing answers first; full sentences grow
across the unit.

### Lesson 03 — Retell beginning, middle, end

Three sentences beat a word-for-word recitation. If the learner
recites, praise the memory, then ask for the three-sentence
version. The rope is the unit's central tool — keep it visible
all four weeks; the project reuses it directly.

### Lesson 04 — Characters, settings, events, and the lesson

Distinguish event from lesson relentlessly ("Turtle wins" is an
event; "slow and steady wins" is the lesson). For unstated lessons
(Ana), accept any story-supported wording; correct only lessons
the story contradicts. Setting includes when ("one cold day") as
well as where.

### Lesson 05 — Feeling words, sense words, and who tells the story

The narrator/character distinction is the unit's hardest concept.
Re-read a narrator sentence with no quotation marks whenever the
learner says "Milo tells the story." The illustration is a
beginning-scene picture: do not let learners claim it shows the
boat tipping over — it shows the launch.

### Lesson 06 — Compare adventures; storybook or information book

A comparison needs both a sameness and a difference; prompt the
missing side. The storybook test is characters + problem vs.
facts + headings — not real vs. made up (ponds are real, and
"Milo and the Red Boat" is still a storybook).

## Misconceptions across the unit

- Racing = reading well (Lesson 01: praise smoothness and sense).
- Every detail is a key detail (Lesson 02: does the story need
  it to make sense?).
- Retelling = reciting (Lesson 03: three sentences, key details).
- Event = lesson (Lesson 04: "What does that teach us?").
- The speaking character tells the whole story (Lesson 05:
  narrator sentences have no quotation marks).
- A comparison with only one side (Lesson 06: prompt same AND
  different).

## Reteaching paths

- **Fluency flat or racing:** Lesson 01 + Session A echo/partner
  rereads; shorten passages to 4–6 sentences.
- **Retelling out of order:** Lesson 03 + Session C rope work;
  use picture-ordering before sentence retelling.
- **Key-detail guessing:** Lesson 02 + Session B; require
  pointing to the answering sentence.
- **Narrator confusion:** Lesson 05 + Session F; sort narrator
  vs. spoken sentences daily for a week.
- **Comparison one-sided:** Lesson 06 + Session G; fill the
  chart's empty column together first.
- **Weakest objective overall:** Session H re-teach before the
  unit assessment; re-quiz only the re-taught parts.

## Readiness and next steps

Ready for Unit 05 (informational reading) means: second read
smoother than the first, retelling in order with key details, a
story-supported lesson, and a same-and-difference comparison.
Learners who miss only the comparison or only the narrator item
may start Unit 05 while those skills keep being rehearsed in
read-alouds — both return in Unit 05's text-feature and
question work. Learners who miss retelling order or fluency need
another week of Lessons 01/03 routines before Unit 05.
