# Project — My Retelling Storybook

**Deliverable:** a three-page storybook (one page each for
Beginning, Middle, End) for one unit story, plus a read-aloud
performance for the adult.

## Materials

Drawing paper (3 sheets) and crayons, pencil, the learner's
retelling rope from Lesson 03 / Session C, the chosen unit story
(Milo, Ana, or Slow Race).

## Steps

### Part 1 (Session 12) — Draw the three parts

1. Learner picks one unit story.
2. Page 1 (Beginning): draw who the story is about and where it
   starts. Dictate or write one sentence about the picture.
3. Page 2 (Middle): draw the big problem (boat tips over; mitten
   lost; Hare naps). Dictate or write one sentence.
4. Page 3 (End): draw how it is fixed and how everyone feels.
   Dictate or write one sentence.
5. Adult checks: are the three pages in order? Does each page
   show a key detail, not just a decoration?

### Part 2 (Session 14) — Sentences and read-aloud share

1. Learner reads their three sentences back, fixing any that do
   not match their pictures.
2. Learner practices reading the storybook aloud twice — second
   read smoother, with expression (a sad voice for the middle,
   a happy voice for the end).
3. Share: learner performs the storybook for the adult (or a
   family listener), reading the sentences and showing the
   pictures, then states the story's lesson in their own words.

## Rubric

| Criterion | 3 — Strong | 2 — Growing | 1 — Starting |
|---|---|---|---|
| Order | Beginning, middle, end in order, each with a key detail | Order mostly right; one part thin or out of place | Parts missing or out of order |
| Key details | Each page shows a detail the story needs (characters, setting, problem, fix) | Some key details; some decoration only | Pictures do not match the story |
| Sentences | Each sentence matches its picture and is readable or clearly dictated | Sentences mostly match; one unclear | Sentences missing or unrelated |
| Fluent read-aloud | Second read smoother, with expression matching the story's feelings | Reads accurately but flat, or smooth only in parts | Word-by-word, or gives up mid-read |
| Lesson | States a story-supported lesson in own words | Lesson stated with adult prompting | No lesson, or one the story contradicts |

**Readiness signal:** 3s and 2s across the row, with order and
read-aloud at 2 or better, means the learner is ready for Unit
05's informational reading. A 1 in order means re-teach Lesson 03
before moving on; a 1 in read-aloud means more echo/partner
rereads of short passages first.
