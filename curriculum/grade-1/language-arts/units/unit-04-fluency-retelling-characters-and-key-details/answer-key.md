# Answer Key — Unit 04: Fluency, Retelling, Characters, and Key Details

Adult only. Do not show this file to the learner. Open responses
accept any story-supported wording; the samples below show the
expected content, not the only acceptable words.

## Lesson 01 — Reread like a storyteller

- **Guided:** 1 — echo read, smooth like talking. 2 — reread
  slower, grouped ("Milo has a red boat."). 3 — "Go, boat, go!"
  in a cheering voice. 4 — "Bat" does not make sense; the word
  is *boat* (Pip gets the boat). 5 — learner reads four
  sentences; adult praises one smooth part by name.
- **Independent:** 1 — second read smoother than the first.
  2 — learner names a self-corrected word and what did not make
  sense (e.g., *sand* for *sad* in the modeled example).
  3 — star on the smoothest sentence. 4 — "Splash!" read
  surprised (the story's fit); happy or quiet accepted if the
  learner justifies it, but surprised is the story match.
- **Application:** three reads, each smoother; learner names
  what improved (speed, smoothness, or voice).
- **Exit check:** second read of the three sentences smoother
  than the first, accurate, plus one named improvement.

## Lesson 02 — Ask and answer key-detail questions

- **Guided:** 1 — Pip. 2 — In the water, at the pond.
  3 — It blows hard; the boat tips over. 4 — Sad. 5 — learner's
  own question using a Who/What/Where/When/How card; adult
  answers from the story.
- **Independent:** 1 — Pip jumps in, gets the boat, brings it
  back. 2 — They go home happy. 3 — drawing of Pip bringing the
  boat back + one matching sentence (dictated accepted).
  4 — two learner questions, answers checked in the story text.
- **Application:** three rounds each of question-card play.
- **Exit check:** "The wind" tips the boat over; the story
  happens "at the pond" (in the water at the pond accepted);
  learner points to or quotes a story sentence for at least one
  answer.

## Lesson 03 — Retell beginning, middle, end

- **Guided:** 1 — Beginning: Milo and Pip go to the pond; Milo
  puts the red boat in the water. 2 — Middle: wind blows hard;
  boat tips over; Milo is sad. 3 — End: Pip brings the boat
  back; Milo hugs Pip; they go home happy. 4 — Order: Milo goes
  to pond → boat tips over → Pip brings boat back. 5 — full
  three-sentence retelling, one sentence per rope box.
- **Independent:** 1 — retelling from rope boxes without the
  text. 2 — three drawings (beginning, middle, end) correctly
  matched. 3 — leaving out the middle means the listener does
  not know the problem. 4 — the missing detail is Pip (or
  whichever detail the adult omits — adult notes it before
  starting).
- **Exit check:** three parts in order, each with at least one
  key detail (pond/boat; wind/tip over; Pip brings it back/happy).

## Lesson 04 — Characters, settings, events, and the lesson

- **Guided:** 1 — Hare, Turtle. 2 — In the snow; by the big
  tree; on the path (any one accepted, two preferred).
  3 — Hare naps while Turtle walks on; Turtle wins. 4 — "Slow
  and steady wins the race." 5 — learner's chosen story:
  characters, setting, one major event.
- **Independent:** 1 — drawing of snow, big tree, path +
  dictated sentence. 2 — order: drops mitten → looks → finds
  cat → finds mitten → goes home happy. 3 — acceptable lessons:
  keep looking; helping others feels good; look carefully (any
  story-supported version). 4 — piles: Characters: Milo, Pip;
  Setting: pond; Event: boat tips over.
- **Exit check:** Hare and Turtle; a setting detail (race path /
  under a tree); Hare naps, Turtle wins; lesson in own words
  ("slow and steady wins" counts).

## Lesson 05 — Feeling words, sense words, and who tells the story

- **Guided:** 1 — sad, happy. 2 — soft, cold (snow accepted as
  a sense detail). 3 — Turtle. 4 — setting: a pond, a tree
  bending in the wind. 5 — words say "go home happy"; picture
  shows Milo smiling as he launches the boat — either with
  evidence accepted.
- **Independent:** 1 — two feeling words (sad, happy) + one
  sense word (splash, soft, cold, fast, hard). 2 — sad face
  matched to the boat tipping over; happy face to going home.
  3 — a narrator tells the Milo story; Milo only speaks inside
  quotation marks. 4 — one sentence using a picture detail and
  a words detail (e.g., "The picture shows the red boat on the
  pond, and the words say Milo puts the boat in the water.").
- **Exit check:** one feeling word + one sense word with story
  evidence, and "a narrator" for who tells the Milo story.

## Lesson 06 — Compare adventures; storybook or information book

- **Guided:** 1 — both feel sad, then happy. 2 — pond vs. snow.
  3 — Pip the dog helps Milo; the little cat helps Ana.
  4 — storybook: it has characters, a problem, and an ending.
  5 — any plausible information-book title about ponds + one
  true simple fact (e.g., "All About Ponds — ponds have ducks").
- **Independent:** 1 — one more same (both lose something; both
  get help) and one more difference (wind vs. snow; boat vs.
  mitten). 2 — "Ana and the Lost Mitten": storybook; "All About
  Snow": information; "The Slow Race": storybook. 3 — both go
  home happy. 4 — two drawings (storybook pond story vs. pond
  facts) with a told difference.
- **Exit check:** one sameness + one difference + "Milo and the
  Red Boat" classified as a storybook with a reason (characters
  and a problem).

## Practice sessions

- **A:** echo read smooth; partner read then solo read; star on
  smoothest sentence; one named fluency win.
- **B:** three rounds each of question-card play; trick question
  answered "The story does not say!"; two learner questions
  recorded for reread.
- **C:** Milo retelling from rope boxes; gap caught (missing
  middle of Ana story) and supplied; Ana rope built; Ana retold
  from rope without text.
- **D:** picture shows boy (Milo), dog (Pip), pond, windy tree,
  red boat launch; part = beginning (boat has not tipped over
  yet); next events: wind blows, boat tips, Pip rescues it.
- **E:** clap on sad/happy; tap on splash/soft/cold/fast;
  Feeling pile: sad, happy, warm; Sense pile: splash, soft, cold,
  fast, hard; both piles: cold, warm.
- **F:** "Ana has a soft red mitten" = narrator; "Slow and
  steady wins the race," says Turtle = character speaking; Milo
  narrator sentence + spoken sentence ("Go, boat, go!" or "Good
  dog, Pip!"); picture matches: red boat, pond, wind-bent tree;
  picture shows the beginning (boat still upright).
- **G:** Same/Different chart as Lesson 06; Hare vs. Turtle:
  fast vs. slow, naps vs. keeps walking, loses vs. wins; sort:
  Milo storybook, All About Ponds information, Slow Race
  storybook, All About Snow information; one sort choice with a
  reason.
- **H:** favorite story reread twice (second smoother);
  retelling chain completed in order; Unit 03 sorts correct
  (cake/kite a_e, boat/oa, bee/ee, snail/ai, snow/ow) and heart
  words read by sight; re-teach targets the weakest quiz
  objective.

## Week-04 formative quiz

1 — second read smoother, with a reason (speed, smoothness, or
voice). 2 — Pip. 3 — at the pond (in the water at the pond).
4 — the wind blows hard and the boat tips over (splash).
5 — beginning: Hare brags / race starts; middle: Hare naps,
Turtle walks on; end: Turtle wins + lesson stated.
6 — sad, happy (cold/warm accepted as feelings). 7 — splash,
soft, cold, fast, hard. 8 — Turtle (a character speaking).
9 — both lose something / both feel sad / both end happy.
10 — storybook, because it has characters and a problem (it
tells a story; it does not give facts).

## Unit assessment

- **Part A:** second read smoother than the first, mostly
  accurate, expression matches at least one feeling, at least
  one self-correction made or explained.
- **Part B:** 1 — Ana and the little cat.
  2 — in the snow; by the big tree; on the path. 3 — Ana drops
  and cannot find her mitten. 4 — beginning: Ana has a soft red
  mitten, drops it in the snow; middle: she looks (snow, tree,
  path), finds a cold cat; end: cat sits on the mitten, Ana
  finds it, they go home happy. 5 — any story-supported lesson
  (keep looking; helping feels good; look carefully).
- **Part C:** 6 — feeling: sad, happy; sense: soft, cold.
  7 — a narrator (Ana speaks nowhere in the story; the story is
  told about her). 8 — character: boy/dog; setting: pond with
  wind-bent tree; event: launching the red boat; part:
  beginning.
- **Part D:** 9 — same: both lose something / both feel sad /
  both end happy; different: pond vs. snow, dog vs. cat helper,
  boat tips over vs. mitten hides. 10 — information book: it
  gives facts with headings and has no characters with a problem.
