# Lesson 02 — Blends at the End

**Goal (learner hears):** "I can read words where the blend team stands
at the END — like *hand* and *jump*. Point, say every sound, push the
team, finish."
**Time:** 20–25 minutes · **Materials:** letter tiles, 8 word cards:
*hand, jump, fast, bank, tent, mask, gift, pond*.
**Standards:** RF.1.3.b; RF.1.2.b.

## Readiness check (1–2 min)

Tiles **h-a-n-d**. "Where is the team — front or back?" (Back: nd.)
"Say every sound slowly: /h/ /ă/ /n/ /d/. Push the back team fast:
'n-d' — **hand!**" This is yesterday's rule, moved to the end.

## Explicit teaching

Blends can stand at the **front** of a word (frog — fr at the front)
or at the **end** of a word (hand — nd at the end). The rule never
changes: **hear both sounds, say them fast together.**

The End Team card set: **nd, nt, st, sk, mp, nk, lt, ft, sp, ct**.
*Adult reads each aloud, stretching the two sounds, then pushing them
fast.*

Spot the team first: "In *jump*, the team is at the end — mp. Point
under it: m-p. Say 'mp' fast. Now the whole word: /j/ /ŭ/ /mp/ —
jump!"

### Modeled example 1 — *nest*

*Adult points at n-e-s-t:* "The team is at the END: s-t. Point:
/n/ /ĕ/ /s/ /t/. Push the end team: 'st'. Blend all: **nest!**
Check: a bird's nest is a real word. Real word — check!"

### Modeled example 2 — *bank*

*Adult points at b-a-n-k:* "Team at the end: n-k. Say it fast:
'nk'. Now stretch the whole word: /b/ /ă/ /nk/ — **bank!** Real word?
Yes — where money lives. Check!"

## Guided practice (adult does it with the learner; 5 tasks)

1. Tiles **t-e-n-t**. "Team: front or back?" (Back — nt.) Read it.
2. Card **jump**: "Point to the end team. Say it fast. Read the word."
3. Card **fast**: "Where is st — front or back?" (Back.) Read.
4. Tiles **m-a-s-k**: "Team at the end. Say all four sounds, push the
   team: mask."
5. Adult segments "/s/ /ă/ /n/ /d/"; learner says the word, names the
   team (nd, back).

## Independent practice (4–6 short tasks; adult checks same day)

1. Read the cards *pond, belt, gift, best* aloud with pointing.
2. Sort 6 cards (hand, mask, tent, jump, bank, nest) into a FRONT-team
   tray and a BACK-team tray — all six go in BACK (the adult adds
   *frog, stop* as front decoys for the sort).
3. Build *jump* and *nest* with tiles from memory; check against the
   cards.
4. Draw one end-blend word (a tent, a mask) and copy the word under it.

## Application

**Back-team hunt:** the adult reads — "The ant sat in the sand near
the lamp." The learner raises a hand for each word with an END blend
(*ant, sand, lamp*) and names the team. All four words are in the unit
set; the adult points at each card as proof.

## Exit check

Three cards: *hand, jump, fast*. The learner reads all three,
circling (with a finger) the end team in each. Success: 3/3 with both
sounds of each team heard.

## Supports

- **Seated-table variant:** learner lays tiles, then "walks" two
  fingers (pointer + middle) together to show the end team pushing
  fast.
- **Floor-play variant:** letter cards in a line on the floor; learner
  tiptoes the sounds, then stomps the end team once, saying it fast.
- **Language access:** act out each word (jump, hand wave, sleep in a
  tent made of a blanket) before reading it.
- **Alternate response mode:** learner sorts cards into trays and the
  adult reads each; the learner thumbs-up if the team was said fast.
- **Extension:** hunt END blends in the Unit 01 word set — *six, fix,
  box* (x = /k/ + /s/, a team of two sounds!) — and preview Lesson 05
  spelling.

## Teacher note — misconceptions

- **The front-back mix-up:** learners new to end blends often read the
  END blend's sounds in the wrong order (*hand* → "had-n"). Stretch
  left to right, slowly, then push only the team.
- **Silent partner:** dropping one end-blend sound (*jump* → "jum").
  Tap the table once per letter: 4 letters, 4 sounds.
- **Adding a vowel at the end** ("hand-uh"): keep the two sounds
  tight, like the front team.
