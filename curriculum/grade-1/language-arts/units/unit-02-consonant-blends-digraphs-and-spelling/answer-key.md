# Answer Key — Unit 02: Consonant Blends, Digraphs, and Spelling

**Adult eyes only.** Every practice, quiz, assessment, and project item
below is solved independently here; reconcile the learner's work against
these solutions. Phonetic (invented) spellings earn full credit when
every sound is mapped in order and teams are written as pairs —
celebrate them as wins (L.1.2.e).

## Lesson 01 — Blends are sound teams

**Guided practice:**
1. flag — team **fl**, sounds /f/ /l/ /ă/ /g/
2. snap — team **sn**, /s/ /n/ /ă/ /p/
3. strap — team **str**, /s/ /t/ /r/ /ă/ /p/
4. clap — team **cl**, /k/ /l/ /ă/ /p/
5. drink — front team **dr**, /d/ /r/ /ĭ/ /ng/ (ng end team, Lesson 04
   preview — praise noticing it)

**Independent practice:** 1. sled /s/ /l/ /ĕ/ /d/; stop /s/ /t/ /ŏ/
   /p/; plan /p/ /l/ /ă/ /n/; trip /t/ /r/ /ĭ/ /p/. 2. "grab."
   3. /f/ /l/ /ă/ /g/ → "flag." 4. Drawing of a frog labeled frog
   (accept adult-scribed).

**Exit check:** crab (**cr**), flag (**fl**), sled (**sl**).

## Lesson 02 — Blends at the end

**Guided practice:**
1. tent — back team **nt**, /t/ /ĕ/ /n/ /t/
2. jump — end team **mp**, /j/ /ŭ/ /m/ /p/
3. fast — **st** at the back, /f/ /ă/ /s/ /t/
4. mask — **sk** at the back, /m/ /ă/ /s/ /k/
5. "sand" — back team **nd**

**Independent practice:** 1. pond /p/ /ŏ/ /n/ /d/; belt /b/ /ĕ/ /l/
/t/; gift /g/ /ĭ/ /f/ /t/; best /b/ /ĕ/ /s/ /t/. 2. All eight cards
   (hand, mask, tent, jump, bank, nest, frog, stop) sort correctly:
   frog and stop go to FRONT; the other six to BACK. 3. jump, nest
   built with tiles. 4. Drawing labeled (adult-scribed OK).

**Exit check:** hand (**nd**), jump (**mp**), fast (**st**).

## Lesson 03 — sh and ch: two letters, one sound

**Guided practice:**
1. ship — sh is ONE sound; /sh/ /ĭ/ /p/
2. wish — digraph at the back; /w/ /ĭ/ /sh/
3. chat — digraph **ch**, one sound /ch/; /ch/ /ă/ /t/
4. lunch — 4 sounds: /l/ /ŭ/ /n/ /ch/
5. Shelf: /ch/ at the end → **peach** card; /sh/ at the start → **ship**
   card.

**Independent practice:** 1. shop /sh/ /ŏ/ /p/; dish /d/ /ĭ/ /sh/;
   shut /sh/ /ŭ/ /t/; chip /ch/ /ĭ/ /p/. 2. sh-team tray: ship, fish,
   shop, wish; ch-team tray: chick, lunch, much. 3. ship, chat built
   with s+h and c+h traveling together. 4. Drawing labeled fish.

**Exit check:** ship (**sh**, start), chick (**ch**, start; ck end),
   wish (**sh**, end), lunch (**ch**, end).

## Lesson 04 — th, wh, ck, ng: more letter teams

**Guided practice:**
1. thumb — team **th** at the start; tongue tip between teeth; quiet
   th. /th/ /ŭ/ /m/
2. this — **buzzy** th (throat vibrates). /th/ /ĭ/ /s/
3. back — team **ck**; short vowel **a** before it. /b/ /ă/ /k/
4. whale — team **wh**; read as taught whole word (vowel team comes in
   Unit 03).
5. song — 3 sounds: /s/ /ŏ/ /ng/; end team **ng**.

**Independent practice:** 1. that /th/ /ă/ /t/; thick /th/ /ĭ/ /k/;
   when /wh/ /ĕ/ /n/ (taught whole); which /wh/ /ĭ/ /ch/ (taught
   whole); sock /s/ /ŏ/ /k/; rock /r/ /ŏ/ /k/; king /k/ /ĭ/ /ng/;
   wing /w/ /ĭ/ /ng/. 2. th tray: thumb, bath; wh tray: when, whale;
   ck tray: back, duck; ng tray: sing, thing. 3. sock, sing built with
   pairs together. 4. Thumbs-UP (has a target team): thumb, when,
   song, brick (br + ck); thumbs-DOWN: fish (sh — not one of the four
   new teams). Note: fish IS a digraph word; accept "up, but it's an
   sh word" as strong.

**Exit check:** this (**th**, start, buzzy), back (**ck**, end),
   sing (**ng**, end), whale (**wh**, start).

## Lesson 05 — Spell it out: writing what you hear

**Guided practice:**
1. ship → S-H-I-P (map: /f/? no — /sh/ /ĭ/ /p/, 3 fingers)
2. sock → S-O-CK (ck rule: short vowel o + /k/ at the end)
3. swim → S-W-I-M (4 sounds, honest mapping)
4. well → W-E-LL (double rule: short vowel e + /l/ at the end)
5. box → B-O-X (/b/ /ŏ/ /ks/, x team)

**Independent practice:** 1. Dictation: back → B-A-CK; fish → F-I-SH;
   lamp → L-A-MP; chick → CH-I-CK; well → W-E-LL. Accept phonetic
   attempts with every sound in order (e.g., BAK for back — note ck
   rule for re-teach, but score the mapping as a win). 2. clock,
   brick, shock, chick, sock — ck underlined. 3. truck → accept TRUK
   or TRUCK; dress → accept DRES or DRESS; adult writes the
   conventional spelling beside. 4. "The frog sat in the pond." —
   capital T, spaces, period.

**Exit check:** chick (ch + ck teams, in order); jump (j-u-m-p, every
   sound). Phonetic attempts with full mapping earn full credit.

## Lesson 06 — Reading our new words in lines

**Guided practice:**
1. "A frog can jump in the pond." — choral read with expression.
2. "This ship can go fast." — solo read; taught whole words: This, go.
3. Teams in "A chick sat with the hen.": chick → **ch** (start),
   **ck** (end); with → **th** (end); the → **th** (start).
4. "That fish is quick!" — telling voice: period; asking voice:
   question mark; excited voice: exclamation point — the mark here is
   **!** → excited voice.
5. Adult misread "pong" → learner fixes to **pond** (self-correction
   praised).

**Independent practice:** 1. All three strips read with tracking.
   2. Example circles: strip 1 (blend: **cr** in crab, **fr** in frog;
   digraph: none — accept "no digraph in this line"); strip 2
   (digraphs: **sh** in fish, ship; **qu**, **ck** in quick); strip 3
   (digraphs: **ch**, **ck** in chick; **th** in with, the).
   3. Learner's own line — any two unit words used correctly. 4. Drawing
   + read-aloud to a stuffed animal.

**Exit check:** "A chick can swim in the pond." — every word decoded,
   period read with telling voice; retell: a chick swims in a pond
   (acceptable variation: "a baby chicken is swimming in the pond").

## Practice sessions

**Session A (blend sort):** blend tray: frog, stop, crab, sled, flag,
   swim, plan, trip; no-blend tray: cat, dog. Speed round teams: fr,
   st, cr, sl, fl, sw, pl, tr, bl, sn (order varies).
**Session B (final-blend tiles):** 1. hand, jump, nest, bank, mask,
   tent built correctly. 2. Teams: nd, mp, st, nk, sk, nt. 3. Example
   chain: hand → sand → band → bend (real word — check!).
**Session C (shelf sort):** /sh/ pair: ship, fish; /ch/ pair: chair,
   peach; /wh/: whale; /th/: thumb. Position game: /ch/ at end →
   peach; /sh/ at start → ship. Word piles: sh pile: shop, dish,
   wish; ch pile: chick, lunch, much.
**Session D (digraph hunt):** BLEND: frog, swim, hand, nest;
   DIGRAPH: ship, think, duck, fish; SINGLE: cat. Two-team words:
   drink (dr+nk), shock (sh+ck), string (str+ng), smash (sm+sh).
   Throat check — BUZZ: this, that, with; QUIET: thin, thick, bath.
**Session E (dictation):** ship, duck (ck rule), swim, well (double
   rule), back (ck rule); truck → TRUK/TRUCK accepted; dress →
   DRES/DRESS accepted; sentence "The frog sat in the pond." —
   capital, spaces, period.
**Session F (review games):** 1. BLEND pile: frog, stop, swim, hand,
   nest, crab; DIGRAPH pile: ship, fish, chick, lunch, back, sing
   (12 cards). 2. "shik" → **chick** (wrong team: sh for ch);
   "jum" → **jump** (missing p — end team mp incomplete). 3–4. Adult
   notes fluency and real-word judgments.

## Formative quiz (week 10)

**Part A:** 1. frog (fr); 2. hand (nd); 3. ship (sh); 4. chick (ch,
   ck); 5. sing (ng).
**Part B:** 6. swim → BLEND ("fr"? no — **sw**: two sounds); fish →
   DIGRAPH (sh: one sound); cat → SINGLE.
**Part C:** 7. duck → D-U-CK (accept DUK as phonetic, note ck rule);
   8. lamp → L-A-MP; 9. well → W-E-LL (accept WEL as phonetic, note
   double rule).
**Part D:** 10. /wh/ at the start → **whale** card; /sh/ at the end
   → **fish** card.
**Re-teach routing:** miss on A1–A2 → replay Session A/B; miss on
   A3–A5 or B → replay mouth cues + Session C/D; miss on C7–C9 →
   replay Lesson 05 stretch–map–write–check; miss on D10 → replay the
   shelf (Session C).

## Unit assessment

**Task 1 (4 pts):** crab (**cr**), stand (**st**), nest (**st** end),
   jump (**mp** end) — 1 pt per word read + team named.
**Task 2 (4 pts):** shop (**sh** — one sound), this (**th** — one
   sound), whale (**wh** — one sound; taught whole), song (**ng** —
   one sound).
**Task 3 (3 pts):** "/s/ /t/ /r/ /ă/ /p/" → **strap**; "/b/ /r/ /ŭ/
/sh/" → **brush**; "think" → /th/ /ĭ/ /ng/ (accept "th-i-ng" as the
   learner names the teams).
**Task 4 (2 pts):** /ch/ at the start → **chair**; /ch/ at the end →
   **peach**.
**Task 5 (4 pts):** chick → CH-I-CK; pond → P-O-ND; miss → M-I-SS
   (accept MIS as phonetic, note double rule); quick → QU-I-CK.
**Task 6 (3 pts):** "The frog sat in the pond." — capital T (1),
   all words readable/mapped (1), period (1).
**Total: 20.** 16+ secure; 12–15 secure with re-teach of the missed
   team type; below 12 → re-teach per the teacher guide before Unit
   03.

## Project — The Sound Team Book

**Acceptable variation:** any 3 blend teams and 3 digraph teams from
the unit sets; words may include taught whole words (*when, whale*)
on wh pages. Illustrations may be simple; labels may be adult-scribed.
**Teach-back:** accept any correct statement of the 2-sound/1-sound
test with one blend and one digraph example. **Spelling check:** all
six words mapped in order with teams as pairs = full credit; phonetic
attempts accepted (e.g., THUM for thumb).
**Rubric guidance:** "Strong" = badges correct, real words, teams
pushed in reading, test explained, spelling mapped. "Developing" =
minor team mislabels or prompt-needed reading. "Starting" = teams
confused or heavy help needed — re-teach blends vs. digraphs with
Session D before Unit 03.
