# Project — The Sound Team Book

**Type:** investigation / creation project across Sessions 12 and 14,
with the share in Session 14.
**Driving question:** "Can I build a book that teaches someone else
the difference between blends and digraphs — and proves I can read and
spell both?"

The learner authors a 6-page book: each page features one sound team
(3 blend pages, 3 digraph pages), 3–4 real words, one illustration, and
one spelled word — then reads the whole book aloud and teaches the
blend-vs-digraph test to someone else.

## Materials

6 half-sheets of paper or index cards, crayons/markers, letter tiles
for drafting, word cards from the unit word sets (see [unit
README](README.md#unit-word-sets-authored-for-this-unit-reused-across-lessons-and-practice)),
the picture shelf illustration for inspiration, a stapler or yarn
(adult use).

## Part 1 — Plan and draft (Session 12)

1. Choose 3 blend teams (e.g., **fr, st, nd**) and 3 digraph teams
   (e.g., **sh, ch, ck**). Write each team at the top of a page —
   blend pages get a **two-sound badge** ("2 sounds!"), digraph pages
   a **one-sound badge** ("1 sound!").
2. For each page, pick 3–4 real words from the unit word sets: e.g.,
   **fr** → frog, from, front; **sh** → ship, fish, wish.
3. Draft-read each word with tiles: point–say–blend–check. Any
   non-word is rejected — the book only prints real words.
4. Draw one picture per page showing one of its words (a frog for
   **fr**, a fish for **sh**). Label the picture with the word —
   adult scribes if needed.

## Part 2 — Build, read aloud, share (Session 14)

1. Staple or tie the pages; make a cover: title ("My Sound Team
   Book"), author first name only (privacy), one drawing of the
   learner's favorite team.
2. **Proof reading:** the learner reads every page to a stuffed
   animal — pointing under each word, pushing teams fast, announcing
   "blend!" or "digraph!" for each team.
3. **Teach-back:** the learner teaches the blend-vs-digraph test to
   the adult or a family member using two pages: "Frog has fr — I hear
   TWO sounds, so it's a blend. Ship has sh — I hear ONE sound, so it's
   a digraph." The audience asks one question ("How do you know ck is
   a digraph?"), which the learner answers.
4. **Spelling check:** the adult dictates one word from each page;
   the learner spells it on the back of the page (phonetic attempts
   accepted; teams must be written as pairs).

## Deliverables

1. The book: cover + 6 team pages (3 blends, 3 digraphs), 3–4 real
   unit words each, correct badge on every page, one illustration per
   page.
2. A proof reading (observed by the adult; every word decoded with
   teams handled).
3. A teach-back of the blend-vs-digraph test (observed).
4. Six spelled words (one per page), teams written as pairs.

## Rubric

| Criterion | Strong (3) | Developing (2) | Starting (1) |
|---|---|---|---|
| Team pages | 6 pages, teams correctly labeled blend/digraph, badges right | 4–5 pages, or 1–2 mislabeled teams | Fewer than 4 pages or teams confused |
| Word choice | 3–4 real decodable words per page, teams used correctly | 2–3 words per page, mostly correct | 1–2 words or frequent team errors |
| Proof reading | Every word decoded, teams pushed, self-corrects | Most words decoded, needs prompts on teams | Cannot decode without heavy help |
| Teach-back | Explains the 2-sound/1-sound test with examples | Explains with prompting | Cannot explain the difference |
| Spelling | All 6 words mapped in order, teams as pairs | 4–5 correct, teams mostly paired | Teams split apart or sounds missing |

**Readiness signal:** "Strong" or "Developing" on Team pages, Word
choice, and Proof reading shows Objectives 1–4 are secure. Spelling at
"Developing" is fine — spelling keeps growing all year.
