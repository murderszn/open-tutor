# Formative Quiz — Unit 02, Week 10 (Session 15)

**Purpose:** quick check of Objectives 1–5 before the review week ends;
drives same-session re-teaching. Oral/observed plus short written tasks;
the adult reviews everything the same day.

**Scoring:** 10 tasks, 1 point each (partial credit for phonetic
spelling where noted). 8+ = secure; 6–7 = re-teach the missed team
type, then move on; below 6 = replay the paired practice session and
re-quiz one team type at a time.

## Part A — Read the teams (oral; Objectives 1–3)

The adult shows one card at a time. The learner reads the word and
names the team.

1. *frog* (team: fr, blend)
2. *hand* (team: nd, blend)
3. *ship* (team: sh, digraph)
4. *chick* (teams: ch, ck — digraphs)
5. *sing* (team: ng, digraph)

## Part B — Sort the teams (observed; Objective 3)

6. Three trays: BLEND / DIGRAPH / SINGLE. Cards: *swim* (blend),
   *fish* (digraph), *cat* (single). The learner sorts all three and
   names each team's sound count ("fr: two sounds"; "sh: one sound").

## Part C — Spell what you hear (written; Objective 5)

The adult dictates each word twice. Phonetic attempts earn full credit
if every sound is mapped in order and teams are written as pairs;
conventional spellings earn full credit regardless.

7. *duck*
8. *lamp*
9. *well* (accept WEL for the phonetic attempt if the double-l rule
   was missed, but note it for re-teach)

## Part D — Picture shelf check (oral; Objective 3)

Using [`assets/digraph-shelf.png`](../assets/digraph-shelf.png) (text-only
alternative: ship, fish, chair, peach, whale, thumbs-up hand):

10. "Point to the picture that starts with /wh/. Point to the picture
    that ends with /sh/." (whale; fish.)
