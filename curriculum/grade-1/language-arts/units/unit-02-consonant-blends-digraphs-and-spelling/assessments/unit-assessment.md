# Unit Assessment — Unit 02: Consonant Blends, Digraphs, and Spelling

**Purpose:** culminating performance check of all five objectives
(Session 16). Oral/observed plus short written tasks; the adult scores
the same day and uses the teacher guide's re-teach planning if any
objective scores below "secure."

**Scoring:** 20 points total. 16+ = secure, move to Unit 03; 12–15 =
secure with re-teach of the missed team type during flexible time;
below 12 = re-teach the lowest objectives (see
[teacher-guide.md](../teacher-guide.md#readiness-and-re-teaching)) before
Unit 03.

## Task 1 — Read blend words (oral; Objectives 1–2; 4 points)

The adult shows one card at a time; the learner reads each, naming the
team: *crab, stand, nest, jump*.

## Task 2 — Read digraph words (oral; Objective 3; 4 points)

*shop, this, whale, song* — the learner reads each and says whether
the team is "one sound" or "two sounds."

## Task 3 — Blend and segment (oral; Objective 4; 3 points)

- Blend: "/s/ /t/ /r/ /ă/ /p/" → the learner says "strap."
- Blend: "/b/ /r/ /ŭ/ /sh/" → "brush."
- Segment: "think" → the learner stretches it: /th/ /ĭ/ /ng/.

## Task 4 — Shelf sounds (observed; Objective 3; 2 points)

Using [`assets/digraph-shelf.png`](../assets/digraph-shelf.png)
(text-only alternative: ship, fish, chair, peach, whale, thumbs-up
hand): "Which picture starts with the /ch/ sound? Which picture ends
with the /ch/ sound?" (chair; peach.)

## Task 5 — Dictation (written; Objective 5; 4 points)

The adult dictates each word twice: *chick, pond, miss, quick*.
Phonetic attempts earn full credit if every sound is mapped in order
and teams are written as pairs.

## Task 6 — Sentence (written; Objectives 1–5 + RF.1.1.a review; 3 points)

The adult dictates: "The frog sat in the pond." Score: capital letter
(1), every word readable/mapped (1), period at the end (1).
