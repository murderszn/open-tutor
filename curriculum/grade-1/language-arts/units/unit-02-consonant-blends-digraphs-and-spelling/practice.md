# Practice Sessions — Unit 02: Consonant Blends, Digraphs, and Spelling

Named sessions referenced by the [16-session pacing](README.md#16-session-pacing-4-weeks--4-sessions-20-25-minutes-each).
Each runs 20–25 minutes. Adult observes and notes which objective each
session served.

## Session A — Blend sort game

*Serves Objective 1 (initial blends). Pairs with Lesson 01.*

Word cards: *frog, stop, crab, sled, flag, swim, plan, trip* (initial
blends), plus decoys *cat, dog* (no blend).

1. Sort into BLEND TEAM / NO BLEND trays. For each blend card, the
   learner names the team ("fr!") and reads the word.
2. **Speed round:** the adult holds up cards one at a time; the learner
   calls only the team ("sw!", "st!", "cl!"). 10 cards.
3. **Build-it-back:** the learner rebuilds 2 words from tiles from
   memory; adult checks the team is intact.
4. Finish: read all 8 blend cards aloud, pointing under each team.

## Session B — Final-blend tile building

*Serves Objective 2 (final blends). Pairs with Lesson 02.*

Tiles plus cards: *hand, jump, nest, bank, mask, tent*.

1. The adult says a word; the learner builds it with tiles, stretching
   left to right, pushing the end team fast.
2. **Team hunt:** learner circles (finger) the end team on each card,
   names it, reads the word. 6 cards.
3. **Change one:** *hand → sand → band → bend?* ("bend" is a real word
   — check!) Change only one letter each round; the adult judges
   real/non-real.
4. Sort the six cards by end team (nd, mp, st, nk, sk, nt) — say each
   team as you sort.

## Session C — Digraph shelf sort

*Serves Objective 3 (digraphs). Pairs with Lesson 03. Uses the
generated illustration.*

Open [`assets/digraph-shelf.png`](assets/digraph-shelf.png). Text-only
alternative: the six cards show a ship, a fish, a chair, a peach, a
whale, and a thumbs-up hand.

1. Name all six cards together. "Which two share the /sh/ team?"
   (ship, fish.) "Which two share /ch/?" (chair, peach.) "Which have
   /wh/?" (whale.) "Which have /th/?" (thumb.)
2. **Position game:** "Point to the card where /ch/ is at the END."
   (peach.) "Where /sh/ is at the START." (ship.) Six rounds.
3. Deal word cards *shop, dish, chick, lunch, much, wish*: the learner
   places each under the matching PICTURE card's team (ship/fish pile
   for sh; chair/peach pile for ch).
4. **Say-it-like-me:** the adult says "shhh" or "chugga" — the learner
   points to a card with that team and reads its name with the same
   mouth shape.

## Session D — Digraph hunt

*Serves Objective 3 (digraphs). Pairs with Lesson 04. Blends vs.
digraphs review.*

Three trays labeled **BLEND** (two sounds), **DIGRAPH** (one sound),
**SINGLE** (one letter).

1. Deal 9 cards: *frog* (blend), *ship* (digraph), *cat* (single),
   *think* (digraph ×2), *swim* (blend), *duck* (digraph), *hand*
   (blend), *fish* (digraph), *nest* (blend). Learner sorts, naming
   the team and counting its sounds.
2. **Two-team words:** the adult shows *drink, shock, string, smash* —
   the learner finds BOTH teams in each ("dr + nk: blend and blend!;
   sh + ck: digraph and digraph!").
3. **Throat check:** *this, that, with* (buzzy th) vs. *thin, thick,
   bath* (quiet th) — hand on throat, sort into BUZZ / QUIET.
4. Speed round: adult flashes *whale, sing, back, thumb, when* — the
   learner names the team in each ("wh!", "ng!", "ck!", "th!", "wh!").

## Session E — Dictation and spelling practice

*Serves Objective 5 (spelling). Pairs with Lesson 05.*

1. **Stretch–map–write–check** dictation (adult reads each twice):
   *ship, duck, swim, well, back.* Learner writes; adult notes the
   step each word missed, if any.
2. **ck hunt:** cards *clock, brick, shock, chick, sock* — the learner
   underlines the ck pair on each, says "short vowel, ck at the end!"
3. **Double-letter hunt:** cards *off, well, miss, class, kiss* —
   the learner circles the doubled letter and names the short vowel
   before it.
4. **Phonetic bravery round:** the adult dictates *truck* and *dress*
   (untaught patterns). Any honest mapping wins; the adult writes the
   conventional spelling beside it for later.
5. One dictated sentence, checked for capital, spacing, and period:
   "The frog sat in the pond."

## Session F — Review games

*Serves all objectives. Session 13, before the project share.*

1. **Team relay:** two piles — BLEND cards and DIGRAPH cards. The
   learner reads a card, runs (or marches) it to the right pile,
   reads the team aloud. 12 cards.
2. **Dictation duel:** the adult writes a word wrong on purpose
   ("shik" for *chick*; "jum" for *jump*); the learner plays teacher —
   finds the error, fixes it, names the rule.
3. **Shelf quiz show:** the learner quizzes the adult on the picture
   shelf (role reversal of Lesson 04's application).
4. **Word ladder:** start at *fish* — change one team to make *dish*,
   *dish → wish →* ... the adult keeps score of real words made.

*Re-teach pointers:* if final blends are shaky, replay Session B;
if digraphs blur into blends, replay the mouth cues from Lessons 03–04
and Session D's sound-counting.
