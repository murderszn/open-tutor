# Resource Pack — Unit 02: Consonant Blends, Digraphs, and Spelling

## Summary

**Goal:** give the guiding adult free, no-account routes to support
grade-1 blend and digraph decoding (initial/final consonant blends;
sh, ch, th, wh, ck, ng, qu) and dictation spelling of common patterns
(ck, ff/ll/ss, qu, x) plus phonetic attempts — with songs and games a
6-year-old will enjoy. **Age fit:** grade 1 (about 6–7); all items are
visual, musical, or hands-on, with minimal reading required of the
learner.
