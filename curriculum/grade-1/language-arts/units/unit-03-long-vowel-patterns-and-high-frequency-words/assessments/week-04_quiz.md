# Week-04 Formative Quiz — Unit 03

**Adult-administered. 20 points.** Oral, pointing, and short written
responses all count. The adult reads every item aloud and reviews
written work the same day. Do not show the learner
[answer-key.md](../answer-key.md).

## Part A — Magic-e pairs (4 points)

Read each pair aloud. For each pair, say which word has the magic
e (long vowel).

1. *cap / cape*
2. *kit / kite*
3. *hop / hope*
4. *cub / cube*

## Part B — Vowel-team sort (6 points)

Read each word, then say its team: ai, ay, ee, ea, oa, or ow.

1. *rain*
2. *play*
3. *feet*
4. *beach*
5. *boat*
6. *snow*

## Part C — Endings (4 points)

Read each word, then name its ending and its base word.

1. *dishes*
2. *making*
3. *played*
4. *boxes*

## Part D — Long-Vowel Train (2 points)

Look at the Long-Vowel Train picture
([assets/long-vowel-train.png](../assets/long-vowel-train.png)).

1. Name the picture in the third car and say its pattern.
2. Name the picture in the sixth car and say its pattern.

## Part E — Heart words (4 points)

Read each word fast, by sight (no sounding out).

1. *said*
2. *was*
3. *of*
4. *have*

## Scoring

- 18–20: Strong — move to the unit assessment.
- 14–17: Developing — re-teach the weakest part with
  [Session H](../practice.md#session-h-review-games-and-re-teach)
  before the assessment.
- Below 14: re-teach the two weakest parts with their lessons'
  modeled examples, then re-quiz those parts only.
