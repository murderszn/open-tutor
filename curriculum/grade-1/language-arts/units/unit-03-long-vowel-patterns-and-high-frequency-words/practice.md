# Practice Sessions — Unit 03

Each session is 20–25 minutes. The adult reads everything aloud,
reviews short written work the same day, and accepts oral,
pointing, and drawing responses everywhere. All words come from the
unit word sets in [README.md](README.md#unit-word-sets-authored-for-this-unit-reused-across-lessons-and-practice).

## Session A — Magic-e flip book

*Pairs with Lesson 01. Session 2.*

1. Fold a strip of paper into a flip book: short word on top,
   magic-e word under the flap (*cap/cape, tap/tape, kit/kite,
   hop/hope, cub/cube*).
2. Flip each flap and read both words, saying the vowel's change
   ("short a ... long a!").
3. Adult shows *mad, made, pin, pine, not, note, bit, bite, cut,
   cute* in mixed order; the learner reads each and says "short"
   or "magic e."
4. Write (or dictate) two new pairs the learner invents from the
   unit sets (*mad/made* counts; invented spellings that follow
   the pattern count).
5. Exit: read *cape, pine, note* without help.

## Session B — ai/ay sort and decodable lines

*Pairs with Lesson 02. Session 4.*

1. Sort 10 cards into ai and ay piles: *rain, snail, train, mail,
   paint, play, day, say, stay, tray*. (Answer: ai — rain, snail,
   train, mail, paint; ay — play, day, say, stay, tray.)
2. Read each pile aloud, naming the team.
3. Read these decodable lines (all words use Units 01–03 patterns
   or taught whole words):
   - "The snail plays in the rain."
   - "Say the word and stay all day."
   - "The train is on the tray." (Silly sentence — the learner
     laughs and fixes it: "A train is big; a tray is small!")
4. Write (or dictate) one ai sentence and one ay sentence.
5. Exit: read *snail, stay, train* and name each team.

## Session C — ee/ea word hunt

*Pairs with Lesson 03. Session 6.*

1. Hunt: the adult hides 10 cards around the room (*see, bee,
   tree, feet, green, sea, tea, leaf, beach, dream*). The learner
   finds each, reads it, and sorts it into ee or ea piles.
   (Answer: ee — see, bee, tree, feet, green; ea — sea, tea, leaf,
   beach, dream.)
2. Read these decodable lines:
   - "I see a bee on a leaf by the sea."
   - "The sheep sleeps by the green tree."
   - "We drink tea at the beach." (Check: *drink* is a Unit 02
     blend word; *we* and *at* are taught whole words.)
3. Error hunt: the adult reads "I see a bee on a leef by the see"
   (wrong teams on purpose). The learner catches both errors and
   fixes them (*leaf, sea*).
4. Write (or dictate) one ee sentence and one ea sentence.
5. Exit: read *sleep, sea, sheep* and name each team.

## Session D — Long-Vowel Train sort

*Pairs with Lesson 04; uses the generated illustration. Session 8.*

1. Show the Long-Vowel Train picture. Name all six pictures in
   order: cake, kite, boat, bee, snail, snowman (*snow*).
2. Sort the six words by pattern: a_e (*cake*), i_e (*kite*), oa
   (*boat*), ee (*bee*), ai (*snail*), ow (*snow*).
3. Add six more cards to the sort: *game, pine, coat, feet, rain,
   blow*. (Answer: a_e — game; i_e — pine; oa — coat; ee — feet;
   ai — rain; ow — blow.)
4. Pattern stations: six station cards labeled a_e, i_e, oa, ee,
   ai, ow. The adult reads 6 words aloud; the learner places each
   at its station: *made* → a_e, *snail* → ai, *soap* → oa,
   *green* → ee, *pine* → i_e, *low* → ow. (Each word matches
   exactly one station.)
5. Exit: name the train pictures and give one word for each of the
   four long-vowel sounds (long a, long e, long i, long o).

## Session E — Ending sort and dictation

*Pairs with Lesson 05. Session 10.*

1. Sort 12 cards by ending: *cats, dogs, dishes, boxes, liked,
   played, wanted, jumping, singing, making, watches, cleaned*.
   (Answer: -s — cats, dogs; -es — dishes, boxes, watches; -ed —
   liked, played, wanted, cleaned; -ing — jumping, singing,
   making.)
2. -ed sound piles: sort *liked, jumped, helped, watched* (/t/),
   *played, rained, showed, cleaned* (/d/), *wanted, painted,
   landed, counted* (/ĭd/).
3. Dictation (adult dictates slowly, repeats each word): *dogs,
   boxes, played, making, wanted, jumping*. The learner writes
   each; phonetic attempts are praised, conventional spellings
   are shown after.
4. Drop-e drill: *make → making, hope → hoping, ride → riding,
   bake → baking, smile → smiling, shake → shaking*.
5. Exit: read *dishes, making, played* and name each ending.

## Session F — Heart-word game

*Pairs with Lesson 06. Session 13 (first half).*

1. Memory match: 12 heart-word cards face down in pairs (*said,
   was, of, have, some, come, do, you, are, they, what, want* —
   two copies each). Turn over two; read both fast; keep the pair
   only if they match AND both were read by sight.
2. Tricky-part hunt: for *said* (ai), *was* (a), *of* (f), *have*
   (final e), *some* (o), *come* (o), *do* (o), *you* (ou), *are*
   (are), *they* (ey), *what* (a), *want* (a), the learner points
   to the tricky part and says what it SHOULD say versus what it
   DOES say (e.g., "ai should say long a, but it says /ĕ/").
3. Fast read: the adult flashes all 12; the learner reads each in
   under 3 seconds. Missed words go back into the next round.
4. Write (or dictate) two sentences using heart words: "I said
   what I want." and one of the learner's own.
5. Exit: read *said, was, of, have* fast, by sight.

## Session G — "Jake and the Kite"

*Decodable story read. Session 13 (second half).*

Read the story three times: first the adult reads with expression
while the learner follows with a finger; second choral read
together; third the learner reads solo, self-correcting with
context. Every word uses Units 01–03 patterns or taught whole
words (listed in [README.md](README.md#unit-word-sets-authored-for-this-unit-reused-across-lessons-and-practice)).

> **Jake and the Kite**
>
> Jake has a red kite. He takes the kite to the lake. The wind
> blows the kite up. It sails past a tree. A bee sees the kite.
> Jake runs and jumps. The kite stays up. Jake smiles. What a day
> to play!

After reading: (a) retell the story in order (first/next/last);
(b) find three magic-e words (*kite, lake, smiles*); (c) find two
vowel-team words (*sails, stays, day, play* — any two); (d) find
the heart word (*What*).

## Session H — Review games and re-teach

*Review week. Session 15 (after the quiz).*

1. Pattern bingo: the adult calls patterns (a_e, i_e, ai, ay, ee,
   ea, oa, ow, -ed, -ing); the learner covers a matching word on
   a 3×3 card grid.
2. Re-teach the quiz's weakest pattern with its lesson's modeled
   examples (see [teacher-guide.md](teacher-guide.md)).
3. Two-syllable clap race: *sunset, lemon, tiger, robot, basket,
   pencil* — clap, break, read, faster each round.
4. Heart-word lightning round: all 12 heart words, fast.
5. Exit: the learner picks their hardest pattern and reads three
   words in it without help.
