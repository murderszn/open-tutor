# Resource Pack — Unit 03: Long-Vowel Patterns and High-Frequency Word Practice

## Summary

**Goal:** give the guiding adult free, no-account routes to support
grade-1 long-vowel reading (final-e and the vowel teams ai, ay, ee,
ea, oa, ow), inflectional endings (-s, -es, -ed, -ing), two-syllable
words, and high-frequency irregular words — with songs and games a
6-year-old will enjoy. **Age fit:** grade 1 (about 6–7); all items
are visual, musical, or hands-on, with minimal reading required of
the learner.
