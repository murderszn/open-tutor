# Answer Key — Unit 03: Long-Vowel Patterns and High-Frequency Word Practice

**Adult eyes only.** Every practice, quiz, assessment, and project item
below is solved independently here; reconcile the learner's work against
these solutions. Phonetic (invented) spellings earn full reading credit
when every sound is mapped in order and teams are written as pairs —
celebrate them as wins (L.1.2.e); exact team spelling is the goal for
the project, not a gate for the lessons.

## Lesson 01 — The magic e

**Guided practice:**
1. cape — magic e added to cap; a says /ā/
2. hope — magic e added to hop; o says /ō/
3. kite — i says its name, /ī/; the final e is silent
4. cube — the magic-e word (cub is short u)
5. made — the magic-e word (mad is short a)

**Independent practice:** 1. tape, pine, note, tune (all long
vowels, silent final e). 2. "bite" (/b/ /ī/ /t/). 3. /h/ /ō/ /p/ →
"hope." 4. Drawing of a kite labeled kite (adult-scribed OK).

**Exit check:** cape (a, /ā/), kite (i, /ī/), hope (o, /ō/).

## Lesson 02 — ai and ay say long a

**Guided practice:**
1. rain — team ai, a talks, /ā/
2. play — team ay, at the end, a talks, /ā/
3. play (rain has ai)
4. tray — team ay at the end
5. snail → ai pile; day → ay pile

**Independent practice:** 1. train, say, mail, stay. 2. "train"
(/t/ /r/ /ā/ /n/). 3. /p/ /l/ /ā/ → "play." 4. Drawing of rain
labeled rain (adult-scribed OK).

**Exit check:** snail (ai), play (ay), train (ai).

## Lesson 03 — ee and ea say long e

**Guided practice:**
1. see — team ee, first e talks, /ē/
2. beach — team ea, e talks, /ē/
3. seat (feet has ee)
4. tree — team ee
5. green → ee pile; leaf → ea pile

**Independent practice:** 1. sleep, sea, sheep, dream. 2. "seal"
(/s/ /ē/ /l/). 3. /f/ /ē/ /t/ → "feet." 4. Drawing of a tree
labeled tree (adult-scribed OK).

**Exit check:** bee (ee), beach (ea), feet (ee).

## Lesson 04 — oa and ow say long o

**Guided practice:**
1. boat — team oa, o talks, /ō/
2. snow — team ow, at the end, o talks, /ō/
3. The sailboat car — the word is boat, team oa
4. coat → oa pile; blow → ow pile
5. grow (road has oa)

**Independent practice:** 1. goat, slow, toast, crow. 2. "snow"
(/s/ /n/ /ō/). 3. /b/ /ō/ /t/ → "boat." 4. Drawing of a boat
labeled boat (adult-scribed OK).

**Application (Train station sort):** cake (a_e), kite (i_e), boat
(oa), bee (ee), snail (ai), snow (ow). The snowman stands for
snow.

**Exit check:** boat (oa), snow (ow), coat (oa).

## Lesson 05 — Endings: -s, -es, -ed, -ing

**Guided practice:**
1. cats — base cat, ending -s, more than one
2. dishes — base dish; sh endings need -es (the extra beat)
3. liked — base like, -ed sound /t/
4. wanted — base want, -ed sound /ĭd/
5. making — make, drop the e, add -ing

**Independent practice:** 1. dogs, boxes, played, jumping.
2. helped — h-e-l-p-e-d (phonetic attempts praised; conventional
spelling shown after). 3. /s/ /ĭ/ /ng/ /ĭ/ /ng/ → "singing."
4. Drawing of someone jumping labeled jumping (adult-scribed OK).

**Application:** dogs (-s), played (-ed), jumped (-ed).

**Exit check:** dishes (-es), making (-ing with drop-e), played
(-ed, /d/).

## Lesson 06 — Two syllables and heart words

**Guided practice:**
1. sunset — sun-set, 2 claps, sunset
2. lemon — 2 vowel sounds, 2 claps, lem-on
3. tiger — ti-ger, tiger
4. said — tricky part ai (should say long a, says /ĕ/)
5. was — tricky part a (says /ŭ/)

**Independent practice:** 1. robot (ro-bot), basket (bas-ket).
2. napkin — 2 claps, nap-kin. 3. have, some, come, do — read fast,
by sight. 4. Drawing of a tiger labeled tiger (adult-scribed OK).

**Application:** said, was, of, you — all read fast, no sounding
out.

**Exit check:** sunset (sun-set), lemon (lem-on), they, what —
heart words read by sight.

## Practice sessions

**Session A:** flip pairs cap/cape, tap/tape, kit/kite, hop/hope,
cub/cube. Mixed reads: mad short, made magic e; pin short, pine
magic e; not short, note magic e; bit short, bite magic e; cut
short, cute magic e. Exit: cape, pine, note.

**Session B:** ai pile — rain, snail, train, mail, paint; ay pile
— play, day, say, stay, tray. Lines: "The snail plays in the
rain" (snail, plays, rain); "Say the word and stay all day"
(say, stay, day); "The train is on the tray" (train, tray — silly
sentence, learner fixes the meaning). Exit: snail (ai), stay (ay),
train (ai).

**Session C:** ee pile — see, bee, tree, feet, green; ea pile —
sea, tea, leaf, beach, dream. Lines: "I see a bee on a leaf by
the sea" (see, bee, leaf, sea); "The sheep sleeps by the green
tree" (sheep, sleeps, green, tree); "We drink tea at the beach"
(tea, beach; drink is a Unit 02 blend word). Error hunt: "leef"
→ leaf, the second "see" → sea. Exit: sleep (ee), sea (ea),
sheep (ee).

**Session D:** train order — cake, kite, boat, bee, snail, snow.
Pattern sort: a_e cake, i_e kite, oa boat, ee bee, ai snail, ow
snow. Added cards: game (a_e), pine (i_e), coat (oa), feet (ee),
rain (ai), blow (ow). Stations: made → a_e, snail → ai, soap →
oa, green → ee, pine → i_e, low → ow. Exit: one word per sound —
long a (cake or snail), long e (bee), long i (kite), long o (boat
or snow).

**Session E:** ending sort — -s: cats, dogs; -es: dishes, boxes,
watches; -ed: liked, played, wanted, cleaned; -ing: jumping,
singing, making. -ed piles — /t/: liked, jumped, helped,
watched; /d/: played, rained, showed, cleaned; /ĭd/: wanted,
painted, landed, counted. Dictation: dogs, boxes, played,
making, wanted, jumping. Drop-e: making, hoping, riding, baking,
smiling, shaking. Exit: dishes (-es), making (-ing), played (-ed).

**Session F:** tricky parts — said: ai (should say long a, says
/ĕ/); was: a (says /ŭ/); of: f (says /v/); have: final e (should
make long a, says short a); some: o (says /ŭ/); come: o (says
/ŭ/); do: o (says /oo/); you: ou (says /oo/); are: are (says /ar/);
they: ey (team not taught yet — learn by sight); what: a (says
/ŏ/); want: a (says /ŏ/). Sentences: "I said what I want." plus
one of the learner's own. Exit: said, was, of, have — fast, by
sight.

**Session G:** retell order — Jake has a red kite; takes it to
the lake; wind blows it up; sails past a tree; bee sees it; Jake
runs and jumps; kite stays up; Jake smiles; "What a day to play!"
Magic-e words: kite, lake, smiles (takes also counts). Vowel-team
words (any two): sails, stays, day, play. Heart word: What.

**Session H:** bingo by pattern called; clap counts — sunset 2,
lemon 2, tiger 2, robot 2, basket 2, pencil 2. Lightning round:
all 12 heart words fast. Exit: learner's chosen pattern, 3 words
read without help.

## Unit assessment

**Task 1:** cake (a_e), pine (i_e), rain (ai), stay (ay), tree
(ee), leaf (ea), coat (oa), grow (ow) — 1 point per word read
correctly.
**Task 2:** cap short, cape long, pin short, pine long.
**Task 3:** dogs (d-o-g-s), watches (w-a-t-c-h-e-s), jumped
(j-u-m-p-e-d), hoping (h-o-p-i-n-g, drop-e). Conventional spelling
= 1 point; a phonetic attempt mapping every sound in order = half
credit.
**Task 4:** sunset (sun-set), lemon (lem-on), tiger (ti-ger),
robot (ro-bot) — 1 point per word broken into correct parts and
read whole.
