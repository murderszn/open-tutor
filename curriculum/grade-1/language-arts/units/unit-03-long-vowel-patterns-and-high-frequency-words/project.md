# Project — My Long-Vowel Book

**Type:** investigation + book-making project (Sessions 12 and 14,
with the share in Session 14)
**Question:** Can I make a book that teaches someone else the
long-vowel patterns I learned?

## Materials

- 6 sheets of paper folded into a book (or stapled by the adult),
  crayons or markers, word cards from the unit sets, the Long-Vowel
  Train picture for reference.

## Steps

**Part 1 (Session 12) — magic-e and ai/ay pages.**

1. Cover: title "My Long-Vowel Book" (adult scribes the title if
   needed) and a drawing of the learner's favorite long-vowel
   word.
2. Page 1 — Magic e: write 3 magic-e words (e.g., *cake, kite,
   hope*), draw one of them, and write or dictate one sentence
   using one of the words ("I like cake.").
3. Page 2 — ai/ay: write 3 ai/ay words (at least one ai and one
   ay), draw one of them, and write or dictate one sentence using
   one of the words ("The snail plays in the rain.").

**Part 2 (Session 14) — ee/ea, oa/ow, and heart-word pages.**

4. Page 3 — ee/ea: write 3 ee/ea words (at least one ee and one
   ea), draw one of them, and write or dictate one sentence using
   one of the words ("I see a bee.").
5. Page 4 — oa/ow: write 3 oa/ow words (at least one oa and one
   ow), draw one of them, and write or dictate one sentence using
   one of the words ("The boat is on the lake.").
6. Page 5 — Heart words: write 4 heart words from memory (say,
   spell, heart the tricky part for each), and write or dictate
   one sentence using two of them ("I said what I want.").
7. Share: read the whole book aloud to the adult (or a stuffed
   audience), pointing at each word. The adult notes which
   patterns are read without help.

## Deliverables

- A 6-page book (cover + 5 content pages) with 3 pattern words
  per pattern page, 1 drawing per pattern page, 1 sentence per
  page, and 4 heart words with hearts on their tricky parts.
- A read-aloud share (observed by the adult).

## Rubric

Score each row as Getting started (1), Developing (2), or Strong
(3). "Ready to move on" is 10+/12 with no row at 1.

| Criterion | Getting started (1) | Developing (2) | Strong (3) |
|---|---|---|---|
| Pattern pages complete | 1–2 pattern pages done | 3 pattern pages done, some words or drawings missing | All 4 pattern pages done, 3 words + drawing + sentence each |
| Words spelled with the pattern | Most words miss the pattern (e.g., *bot* for *boat*) | Most words show the pattern; 1–2 phonetic attempts | All pattern words show the correct team or magic e |
| Sentences use a pattern word | Sentences missing, or none uses a pattern word | Most sentences use a pattern word | Every sentence uses a pattern word from its page |
| Heart page + share | Fewer than 4 heart words, or no share | 4 heart words with most tricky parts hearted; share read with help | 4 heart words with tricky parts hearted; share read aloud with pointing |

**Sample strong book (adult reference — the learner's own words
and drawings will differ):** cover "My Long-Vowel Book"; magic-e
page: *cake, kite, hope*, drawing of a kite, "I like cake.";
ai/ay page: *rain, snail, play*, drawing of rain, "The snail
plays in the rain."; ee/ea page: *bee, tree, beach*, drawing of
a bee, "I see a bee."; oa/ow page: *boat, coat, snow*, drawing of
a boat, "The boat is on the lake."; heart page: *said, was, of,
you* with hearts on ai, a, f, ou, sentence "I said what I want."
