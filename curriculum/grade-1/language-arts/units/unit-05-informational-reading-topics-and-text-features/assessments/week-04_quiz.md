# Week-04 Quiz — Unit 05: Informational Reading

**When:** Session 15 (Week 22) · **Time:** 20–25 minutes with
review games · **Format:** adult-read; oral, pointing, and
drawing responses accepted. 6 items; the adult reviews answers
the same day.

Answer key: [answer-key.md](../answer-key.md#week-04-quiz).

## Part A — Find it fast (RI.1.5)

1. The adult asks: "What do beavers eat?" The learner may use
   only the table of contents and headings to find the answer.
   (Open "Beaver Food," page 4.)
   Answer: bark, twigs, and leaves.
2. "What page is the glossary on?" (Page 5.) "Look up *dam*
   and tell me what it means." (A wall built across a stream
   to slow the water.)

## Part B — Pictures and words (RI.1.6, RI.1.7)

3. The adult shows
   [`assets/beaver-dam-scene.png`](../assets/beaver-dam-scene.png).
   "Tell me one thing you learn from the picture only." (Any
   true picture-only fact: a duck swims in the pond; cattails
   grow at the edge; the dam is a big pile of sticks; the
   beaver holds a stick.)
4. "Tell me one thing you learn from the words only." (The
   lodge door is under the water; beaver teeth keep growing;
   beavers store food for winter.)

## Part C — Retell and connect (RI.1.2, RI.1.3)

5. "Retell Text A in two sentences." (Any two key details in
   order, for example: "Beavers build dams of sticks, mud, and
   stones. They live in lodges in the pond.")
6. "Why does a beaver build a dam? Finish the chain: dam →
   ______ → ______." (Slow water → pond; then: the deep pond
   keeps the beaver safe. Accept "slow water, deep pond" in
   either order if the learner can explain the link.)

## Scoring

- 5–6 correct: ready for the unit assessment.
- 3–4 correct: replay the matching practice game
   ([Session H](../practice.md#session-h-review-games-and-re-teach))
   before the assessment.
- 0–2 correct: re-teach the missed lesson(s) with the adult
   modeling first, then re-quiz orally.
