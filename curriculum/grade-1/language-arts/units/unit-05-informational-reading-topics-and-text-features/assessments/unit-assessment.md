# Unit Assessment — Unit 05: Informational Reading

**When:** Session 16 (Week 22) · **Time:** 20–25 minutes ·
**Format:** adult-read; oral, pointing, and drawing responses
accepted. All answers come from Unit Texts A/B or the generated
illustration. 8 tasks; the adult reviews answers the same day.

Answer key: [answer-key.md](../answer-key.md#unit-assessment).

## Task 1 — Book type and topic (RL.1.5, RI.1.2)

The adult shows Text A. "Is this a storybook or an information
book? How do you know? What is its main topic?"
(Expected: information book — it teaches true things about real
beavers; topic: beavers.)

## Task 2 — Key-detail questions (RI.1.1)

The learner asks the adult one question about a key detail in
Text A, then answers one adult question: "Where does a beaver
family live?" (In a lodge in the pond.)
(Asking the question is scored; any genuine key-detail question
counts.)

## Task 3 — Find a fact with features (RI.1.5)

"Use the table of contents or a heading to find: what is a
beaver's home called, and what is it made of?" The learner opens
"A Lodge in the Pond" (page 3) and answers: a lodge, made of
sticks and mud.

## Task 4 — Picture vs. words (RI.1.6, RI.1.7)

The adult shows
[`assets/beaver-dam-scene.png`](../assets/beaver-dam-scene.png).
"Name one thing the picture shows that the words do not, and
use the picture to describe one key idea about beavers."
(Expected: picture-only fact such as the duck or cattails; key
idea such as "beavers build dams of sticks in ponds" described
from the picture.)

## Task 5 — Word meaning (RI.1.4, L.1.4)

"The book says: 'A beaver gnaws wood with its teeth.' What does
*gnaw* mean? What helped you figure it out?" (Bite and chew
again and again; the next sentence explains it — a sentence
clue. Accept glossary as the tool.)

## Task 6 — Connection and reason (RI.1.3, RI.1.8)

"Why does deep water keep a beaver safe? Name the point and
the reason." (Point: deep water keeps beavers safe. Reason:
animals that hunt beavers cannot reach them easily in deep
water.)

## Task 7 — Compare two texts (RI.1.9)

"Text A and Text B are both about beavers. Tell me one way they
are the same and one way they are different." (Same: lodge as
home, glossary, topic. Different: A teaches teeth/dams/food
storage; B teaches kits/tail-slap warning.)

## Task 8 — Retell with key details (RI.1.2)

"Retell Text B in your own words, with at least two key
details." (For example: "Baby beavers are called kits. Kits stay
with their parents for about two years. A tail slap warns the
family of danger." Any two true key details in order count.)

## Scoring

- 7–8 tasks correct: ready to move on to Unit 06.
- 5–6 tasks correct: review the missed objectives with the
  matching practice session, then re-check orally.
- 0–4 tasks correct: re-teach the missed lessons (adult models
  first) and re-assess the missed tasks only.

This assessment is a classroom check, not a standardized or
validated test.
