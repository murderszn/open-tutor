# Resource Pack — Unit 05: Informational Reading: Topics and Text Features

## Summary

**Goal:** give the guiding adult free, no-account routes to support
grade-1 informational reading — telling information books from
storybooks, using headings, contents, glossaries, labels, and
captions to find facts, and checking beaver facts against
reputable sources. **Age fit:** grade 1 (about 6–7); all items are
visual, musical, or hands-on, with minimal reading required of
the learner. The adult previews every video and page before use.

## Web references

Checked 2026-11-05 unless noted (all reachable in a text browser).

1. **Smithsonian's National Zoo — Beaver (target page)**
   `https://nationalzoo.si.edu/animals/beaver`
   *Why:* the authority behind the unit's beaver facts — orange
   incisors that grow continuously, webbed hind feet, dams of
   sticks caulked with mud, lodges with underwater entrances,
   kits staying about two years, plant diet and winter food
   storage. Verified live 2026-11-05. Free, no account. Adult
   reference; read selected facts aloud.
2. **Britannica — Beaver**
   `https://www.britannica.com/animal/beaver`
   *Why:* a second reputable source confirming dams of branches,
   stones, and mud, dome-shaped lodges with underwater
   entrances, and deep water as predator protection. Free,
   no account. Adult reference.
3. **BioKIDS (University of Michigan) — American Beaver**
   `https://www.biokids.umich.edu/critters/Castor_canadensis/`
   *Why:* a kids'-inquiry species page written for young readers
   — lodge rooms, two entrances, dam building, and pond habitats
   in plain language. Free, no account. Adult reads aloud.
4. **TIES Center — Use text features to find information
   (Inclusive Big Idea #5, RI.1.5)**
   `https://tiescenter.org/topics/inclusive-instruction/ibi/ri-1-5`
   *Why:* a grade-1 RI.1.5 teaching page naming exactly this
   unit's features (title, table of contents, photos, captions,
   diagrams, headings, glossary, index) and why they help
   readers. Free, no account. Adult reference.
5. **Reading Rockets — Glossary (informational text)**
   `http://www.readingrockets.org/teaching/glossary/`
   *Why:* defines informational text as nonfiction that informs
   and lists its text features (headings, subheadings, captions,
   diagrams, photos, tables) — the adult's definition check for
   Lesson 01. Free, no account. Adult reference.
6. **Florida Center for Reading Research — Teacher Resource Guide
   (Student Center Activities)**
   `https://fcrr.org/student-center-activities/teacher-resource-guide`
   *Why:* free, printable, research-based K–1 center activities —
   the Comprehension Center row (K–1: story-sequence organizers
   and related activities) supports retelling practice, and the
   Vocabulary Center row supports glossary work. Verified live
   2026-11-05. Free, no account. Adult prints and adapts.
7. **Reading Rockets — Word Decoding and Phonics (target page)**
   `https://readingrockets.org/helping/helping/target/phonics`
   *Why:* carried over from Unit 03 — decoding support for the
   unit's read-with-support passages; the adult reference for
   keeping decoding demands inside Units 01–03 patterns.
   Verified live 2026-10-23 (Unit 03 check). Free, no account.
   Adult reference.
