# Practice Sessions — Unit 01: Phonemic Review, Short Vowels, and Decoding

Named sessions referenced by the [16-session pacing](README.md#16-session-pacing-4-weeks--4-sessions-20-25-minutes-each).
Each runs 20–25 minutes. Adult observes and notes which objective each
session served.

## Session A — Blending-ladder relay

*Serves Objective 1 (blend/segment). Pairs with Lesson 02.*

1. The adult calls a segmented word; the learner blends it and takes one
   step forward: "/t/ /ŏ/ /p/" → "top!" (step). 8 words: top, bed,
   rug, fin, hop, cup, van, lid.
2. Reverse: the adult says a whole word; the learner segments it and
   steps backward: "grin" → /g/ /r/ /ĭ/ /n/ (step back). 6 words: grin,
   stop, clap, sled, drum, frog.
3. **Chain round:** learner segments a word, adult blends it back —
   then swap. 4 rounds each.
4. Finish line: blend "/s/ /t/ ... /ă/ /n/ /d/" → "stand"? Too hard —
   finish with "/f/ /r/ /ŏ/ /g/" → "frog". Cheer the 4-sound blends.

## Session B — Sentence sort game

*Serves Objective 5 (sentence features). Pairs with Lesson 01.*

Strips (adult-made, large print): "The cat sat." · "I see Sam." · "We
can run!" · "Can you hop?" · "cat sat" · "the pig" · "hop to the top" ·
"red bus".

1. Sort into SENTENCE / NOT A SENTENCE trays. For each sentence, the
   learner points to the capital and names the ending mark.
2. For each not-a-sentence, the learner says what's missing ("no
   capital", "no ending mark").
3. **Doctor round:** the learner fixes two not-sentences by adding the
   capital and ending mark (adult scribes or learner writes).
4. Read all four fixed/readable sentences aloud with expression matching
   the ending mark.

## Session C — Vowel sort game

*Serves Objective 2 (long/short by ear). Pairs with Lesson 03.*

No print — the adult says each word aloud (cover your mouth for the
hard ones). Cards: cap, cape, pin, pine, hop, hope, cub, cube, pet,
Pete, bit, bite, not, note, cut, cute.

1. The learner sorts by ear into LONG TEAM / SHORT TEAM trays, saying
   the middle sound for each ("cape — /ā/ — LONG").
2. **Same-or-different:** pairs — cap/pin (same: short), cape/pine
   (same: long), hop/cube (different), pet/Pete (different).
3. **Umpire round:** learner calls LONG!/SHORT! with the arm signals
   for 6 new words: mad, made, sit, site, rob, robe.
4. The learner picks the trickiest pair and teaches it back to a
   stuffed animal: "Listen — cap... cape. Hear it?"

## Session D — Sound dots

*Serves Objective 3 (isolate sounds). Pairs with Lesson 04.*

Three numbered dots (1-2-3) or Elkonin boxes; a chip or counter per dot.

1. The adult says "map"; the learner pushes one chip per sound into the
   boxes while saying /m/ /ă/ /p/ — then points to box 2: "middle
   sound /ă/".
2. 8 words: cat, sun, bed, pig, hot, cup, ten, fox. For each: fill the
   boxes, then answer one detective question ("what's the last
   sound?", "what's the middle sound?").
3. **Mystery box:** the adult fills boxes for an unspoken word by
   tapping: tap-tap on box 1 while saying /d/, box 2 /ŏ/, box 3 /g/ —
   the learner blends: "dog!"
4. The learner chooses 3 words and runs the detective questions solo,
   stamping each "SOLVED" in the [detective notebook](lessons/lesson-04-sound-detective.md#application).

## Session E — Tile building and decodable lines

*Serves Objective 4 (decode). Pairs with Lesson 05.*

1. **Build-and-swap:** build "pan"; swap p→m ("man"); swap a→e? No —
   keep short-a: swap m→v ("van"). Read each with the 4 steps. Repeat
   with "big → dig → fig"? ("fig" not in sets — use big → dig →
   pig.)
2. **Decodable lines** (read each line twice — first for accuracy,
   second for smoothness):
   - "The cat sat."
   - "The pig is big."
   - "Sam has a red pen."
   - "The sun is hot."
   - "We can hop to the top."
   - "A dog ran to the log."
3. **Find-and-read:** the adult hides 6 word cards around the room
   (from the unit word sets); the learner finds each and reads it with
   the 4 steps before pocketing it.
4. **Check-step celebration:** for the last word found, the learner
   announces the check like a referee: "Real word? YES — I read it!"

## Session F — Review games

*Serves all objectives. Used in Sessions 13 and 15.*

Pick two per session:

- **Sound bingo:** 3×3 grid of unit words (adult-made). The adult
  segments a word ("/h/ /ŏ/ /t/"); the learner blends and covers "hot".
- **Vowel team snap:** long/short word cards; flip two — if same team,
  "SNAP!" and both players say the middle sounds.
- **Sentence fix race:** 3 sick strips; fix capital + ending mark; read
  with matching expression.
- **Ladder lightning:** adult gives a start word and a target ("cat →
  hot"); the learner climbs changing one letter per rung (cat → hat →
  hot). Any valid ladder wins.
- **Detective duel:** adult and learner alternate asking first/middle/
  last questions about unit words; a wrong answer costs a turn.

## Session G — Segmenting dictation

*Serves Objectives 1 and 4. Used in Session 13.*

1. The adult dictates 6 words, one at a time: **mat, fin, log, cup,
   bed, van**. For each: the learner (a) segments it orally with
   finger counting, (b) writes it (invented spelling welcome — phonetic
   attempts are the point this unit), (c) reads back what they wrote
   with the 4 decoding steps.
2. The adult checks each against the target: circle the sounds the
   learner heard correctly; name any missed sound ("you heard /m/ /ă/
   — listen for the last sound: mat... /t/").
3. **Dictation ladder:** dictate "cap" → "map" → "mop" → "top". The
   learner writes each; the adult highlights that only one letter
   changed each time — spelling is a ladder too.
4. The learner picks 2 words to re-dictate to the adult (role swap);
   the adult writes them with one deliberate error for the learner to
   catch.
