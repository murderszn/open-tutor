# Unit Assessment — Unit 01: Phonemic Review, Short Vowels, and Decoding

**Format:** performance tasks, oral and observed; the adult scribes.
Tasks 5–6 include short written work (4–6 words) reviewed the same day.
25–30 minutes, with a movement break between Tasks 3 and 4.
**Standards:** RF.1.1.a, RF.1.2.a–d, RF.1.3.b.

## Task 1 — Sentence check (Objective 5)

Show three strips: "We can hop!" · "hop to the top" · "Can you see the
fox?"

1. "Which strips are sentences?" (1st and 3rd.)
2. For each sentence: "Point to the first word, the capital letter, and
   the ending mark. What is the mark for?"
3. Fix "hop to the top": add the capital and ending mark, then read it
   with matching expression.

## Task 2 — Blend and segment (Objective 1)

1. Blend: "/f/ /r/ ... /ŏ/ /g/." → ?
2. Blend: "/m/ /ĭ/ /ks/." → ? (accept "mix")
3. Segment "plant": say every sound and count them. (5 sounds:
   /p/ /l/ /ă/ /n/ /t/)
4. Segment "bed": say every sound and count them. (3 sounds)

## Task 3 — Long or short (Objective 2)

The adult says each word aloud (mouth covered for the challenge pair).

1. "hope" — long or short o?
2. "cub" — long or short u?
3. "pin... pine" — which one says I's name?
4. **Explain:** "The vowel in 'note' — long or short? How do you know?"

## Task 4 — Sound detective (Objective 3)

Show the [Sound Detective illustration](../assets/sound-detective.png)
(or name the three cards: cat, sun, map).

1. "Pick a card. Tell me its first, middle, and last sounds."
2. "Which card's middle sound is /ă/?" (cat and map — accept either,
   bonus for naming both)
3. New word, no card: "fox" — first, middle, last sounds?

## Task 5 — Decode new words (Objective 4)

Five word cards the learner has not drilled this week (all from the unit
word sets): **jam, lid, pop, rug, wet**.

1–5. Read each with the 4 steps aloud (point, say each sound left to
right, blend, check it's a real word).
6. **Written:** the learner writes any two of the five from dictation
   (segment orally first), then reads back what they wrote.

## Task 6 — Word-family climb (Objectives 1, 4)

The adult builds -et with tiles.

1. "Read the chunk." ("et" — /ĕ/ /t/)
2. Add onsets p, g, w, m one at a time; the learner reads: pet, get,
   wet, met.
3. "What stayed the same in every word?" (the -et chunk)
4. Climb one rung solo: change "met" to a new real -et word by swapping
   one letter. (Acceptable: net, jet, vet, set, let, bet.)

## Scoring

| Task | Secure (3) | Developing (2) | Getting started (1) |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 Sentences | 2 sentences found; marks named with purpose; fix correct | 2 found with prompting; fix needs help | 1 or fewer found |
| 2 Blend/segment | 4/4 correct with counts | 3/4 correct | 2 or fewer |
| 3 Long/short | 4/4 incl. explanation | 3/4; explanation partial | 2 or fewer |
| 4 Detective | Card + fox fully solved | One sound missed | Two+ missed |
| 5 Decode | 5/5 with 4 steps; 2 written correct | 4/5; 1 written correct | 3 or fewer |
| 6 Families | Reads 4 + explains the chunk + climbs | Reads 3–4 with prompting | 2 or fewer |

**Totals:** 15–18 secure → Unit 02. 11–14 developing → re-teach the
lowest task(s) in a flexible slot before Unit 02. Below 11 → re-teach the
matching lessons during a flexible week; do not skip the re-teach —
Unit 02's blends and digraphs build directly on these sounds.
