# Formative Quiz — Unit 01 (Week 4 / Session 15)

**Format:** oral and observed; the adult scribes. Two short written tasks
(items 7–8, 4–6 words total) are reviewed the same day. 20–25 minutes.
**Standards:** RF.1.1.a, RF.1.2.a–d, RF.1.3.b.

## Questions

1. **Sentences.** Show "The dog ran." and "dog ran". "Which one is a
   sentence? Point to its capital letter and its ending mark."
2. **Blending.** "Robot talk: /s/ /ĭ/ /t/. Say it fast." Then with a
   blend: "/s/ /t/ ... /ŏ/ /p/. Say it fast."
3. **Segmenting.** "Stretch 'cup'. Tell me every sound and count them."
4. **Long or short.** "I will say two words: 'cap'... 'cape'. Which one
   says A's name?" Then: "'fin' — long or short i?"
5. **Sound detective.** "'Bed.' What is the first sound? The middle
   sound? The last sound?"
6. **Decoding.** Show the word card "mop" (not drilled this week).
   "Read it with the 4 steps out loud."
7. **Written (short).** Dictate: "sat". The learner segments orally,
   then writes the word.
8. **Written (short).** Show the sick strip "the sun is hot". The
   learner fixes it ("The sun is hot.") — capital T and period — by
   writing or dictating.

## Scoring guidance

- **7–8 secure:** ready for the unit assessment (Session 16) and Unit 02.
- **5–6 developing:** re-teach the missed items in Session 15's second
  half (see [teacher guide](../teacher-guide.md#re-teach-triggers));
  then proceed.
- **Below 5:** use a flexible week to re-teach the matching lessons
  before Session 16 — the track provides diagnostic/catch-up weeks for
  exactly this.
