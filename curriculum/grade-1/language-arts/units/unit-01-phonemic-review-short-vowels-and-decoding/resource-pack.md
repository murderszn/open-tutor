# Resource Pack — Unit 01: Phonemic Review, Short Vowels, and Decoding

## Summary

**Goal:** give the guiding adult free, no-account routes to support
grade-1 phonemic awareness (blending, segmenting, sound isolation),
long/short vowel discrimination by ear, CVC decoding, word-family work,
and sentence features — plus songs and games a 6-year-old will enjoy.
**Age fit:** grade 1 (about 6–7); all items are visual, musical, or
hands-on, with minimal reading required of the learner.

## Safety and access notes

- Every core objective has a **free, no-account route**: Reading Rockets,
  FCRR, thecorestandards.org, and NAEYC need no account; Starfall's free
  tier and the YouTube search links need no sign-in.
- Preview every video for advertising, comments, and age suitability
  before the learner watches ([content-review](../../../../../docs/content-review.md)).
- YouTube search links are conveniences, not endorsements of specific
  uploads; channel owners can rename or remove videos.
- FCRR activities are classroom-designed printables — the adult adapts
  them to one-on-one use (fewer players, larger print).
- Internal references: [teachers/ai-assistants/resource_finder.md](../../../../../teachers/ai-assistants/resource_finder.md)
  (format source).
