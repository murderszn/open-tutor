# Answer Key — Unit 01: Phonemic Review, Short Vowels, and Decoding

Adult-facing. Keep separate from all learner materials. Every item below
was solved independently against its lesson/practice/quiz/assessment;
where a response is open-ended, the key gives the acceptable range and
sample reasoning.

## Lesson 01

**Guided practice:** 1. "The" (first word, leftmost). 2. Big — capital T.
3. Period — a telling sentence. 4. Question mark — an asking sentence.
5. Exclamation point — strong feeling / excitement.
**Independent practice:** 1. SENTENCE: "I see Sam.", "We can run!" /
NOT: "the pig", "bus red". One reason each ("has a capital and a
period", "no capital", "no ending mark"). 2. "I". 3. W. 4. Exclamation
point. 5. "The pig." (capital T + period; adult scribes if dictated).
**Application:** "sam ran" → "Sam ran." (capital S, period); "the bus is
red" → "The bus is red." (capital T, period). Reads each with matching
expression.
**Exit check:** 1. "The". 2. T. 3. Period — for telling. 4. No — no
capital letter, no ending mark, and the thought is unfinished.

## Lesson 02

**Guided practice:** 1. "run". 2. "clap" (/k/ /l/ hold hands, then /ă/
/p/). 3. /g/ /r/ /ĭ/ /n/ — 4 sounds. 4. /b/ /ĕ/ /d/ — 3 sounds. 5. The
learner catches the schwa puffs: "Say it smooth — /h/ /ŏ/ /t/ — 'hot'!"
**Independent practice:** 1. "dig". 2. "sled". 3. /f/ /ŏ/ /ks/ (accept
/f/ /ŏ/ /k/ /s/ or /ks/ together — 3–4 sounds depending on how the
learner hears the x). 4. /d/ /r/ /ŭ/ /m/ — 4 sounds. 5. Error fixed:
"/h/ /ŭ/ /g/ — three sounds. You left off /g/!"
**Application:** speaker segments (e.g., /t/ /ŏ/ /p/), listener blends
("top!"), speaker confirms. Two rounds each role; both segmenting and
blending observed.
**Exit check:** 1. "win". 2. "grin". 3. /m/ /ă/ /p/ — 3 sounds. 4. /s/
/t/ /ŏ/ /p/ — 4 sounds.

## Lesson 03

**Guided practice:** 1. cub = short /ŭ/; cube = long /ū/ (says U's name).
2. pet = short /ĕ/; Pete = long /ē/ (says E's name). 3. Same team — both
short (/ĭ/ and /ŭ/ are both short vowels). 4. /ā/ opens wider and holds
longer. 5. Correction: "No! 'man' is /m/ /ă/ /n/ — /ă/ is short, it
doesn't say A's name."
**Independent practice:** 1. Short /ĭ/. 2. Long /ō/ (says O's name).
3. Same — both short. 4. Long /ū/. 5. Short /ŏ/ — "because it does NOT
say the letter O's name; it's the quick sound."
**Application (umpire):** made — LONG /ā/; bit — SHORT /ĭ/; note — LONG
/ō/; cut — SHORT /ŭ/; these — LONG /ē/.
**Exit check:** 1. Short a. 2. Long i. 3. Pete. 4. "The long vowel says
the letter's name; the short vowel is the quick sound — you tell with
your ears, not your eyes."

## Lesson 04

**Guided practice:** 1. /k/. 2. /ă/ ("the vowel!"). 3. /t/. 4. /d/ /ŏ/
/g/. 5. /f/ /ĭ/ /sh/ — accept /sh/ as one last sound (the /sh/ team gets
its full lesson in Unit 02).
**Independent practice:** 1. /p/ /ĭ/ /g/. 2. /k/ /ŭ/ /p/. 3. /t/ /ĕ/
/n/. 4. The sun card — "sun's middle sound is /ŭ/." 5. Ruled: "It's /d/
— 'bed' ends with /d/, not /t/." (If the learner still hears /t/, contrast
"bed/bet" aloud until the buzz of /d/ is heard.)
**Application:** 3 clue cards, each with first/middle/last sounds
recorded (drawn, dictated, or written). One card must use a vowel the
learner found tricky. Chief-inspector check: every sound correct.
**Exit check:** 1. /h/. 2. /ŏ/. 3. /t/. 4. "It's the vowel — it tells
which vowel team the word belongs to."

## Lesson 05

**Guided practice:** 1. /s/ /ă/ /t/ → "sat" (real word — a person sits).
2. /p/ /ĕ/ /n/ → "pen" ("a pen writes — real word"). 3. /m/ /ŏ/ /p/ →
"mop"; middle sound /ŏ/. 4. Fix: "You stopped early! /l/ /ĭ/ /d/ —
'lid' — a lid covers a pot." 5. b-i-g → d-i-g: /d/ /ĭ/ /g/ → "dig".
**Independent practice:** 1. /v/ /ă/ /n/ → "van". 2. /w/ /ĭ/ /n/ →
"win". 3. /r/ /ŏ/ /b/ → "rob". 4. /n/ /ŭ/ /t/ → "nut". 5. /g/ /ĕ/ /t/
→ "get". **Bonus:** any defended answer naming a step and a reason
("saying each sound, because a new word has no memory to help").
**Application (word hospital):** cag → **cat** (g→t); pog → **pig**
(o→i); sut → **sun** (t→n); beg → **bed** (g→d); hop → **hot** (p→t).
Each rebuilt with tiles and read with the 4 steps.
**Exit check:** 1. /j/ /ă/ /m/ → "jam" (check: "jam goes on toast —
real word"). 2. /m/ /ĭ/ /ks/ → "mix". 3. Point, say each sound left to
right, blend smooth, check it's a real word. 4. "The vowel is the engine
— without the middle sound you can't blend the word."

## Lesson 06

**Guided practice:** 1. can, pan, ran, van, man. 2. top, mop, pop, hop.
3. sun → run (s→r) → rug (n→g). 4. pet → get (p→g) → wet (g→w). 5. The
wobble is **rod** — it breaks the -ed family (real word, wrong chunk).
Fix: replace with a real -ed word (fed, led) or restart the ladder.
**Independent practice:** 1. sit, bit, kit, hit. 2. run, sun, fun. 3.
map → cap (m→c) → cup (a→u) → pup (c→p). 4. hot → hop (t→p) → pop (h→p).
5. "mat, sat, hat share the -at chunk — only the first letter changes.
mat, pig, sun share nothing, so every word is brand new."
**Application:** sample ladder — pin → win (p→w) → fin (w→f) → fun (i→u)
→ run (f→r). Any 5-rung ladder with one change per rung and all real
words counts; the learner reads it aloud smooth and fast.
**Exit check:** 1. ten, pen, men, hen. 2. log → dog (l→d) → dig (o→i).
3. The ending chunk — the vowel plus the last letter(s). 4. One.

## Practice sessions

- **A:** blends — top, bed, rug, fin, hop, cup, van, lid. Segments —
  grin /g/ /r/ /ĭ/ /n/ (4), stop /s/ /t/ /ŏ/ /p/ (4), clap (4), sled (4),
  drum (4), frog (4). Finish line: frog.
- **B:** SENTENCE — "The cat sat.", "I see Sam.", "We can run!", "Can
  you hop?". NOT — "cat sat", "the pig", "hop to the top", "red bus".
  Fixes: "The pig.", "Hop to the top!" (or "."), "The red bus."
- **C:** LONG TEAM — cape, pine, hope, cube, Pete, bite, note, cute.
  SHORT TEAM — cap, pin, hop, cub, pet, bit, not, cut. Same/different:
  cap/pin same (short); cape/pine same (long); hop/cube different;
  pet/Pete different. Umpire: mad short, made long, sit short, site
  long, rob short, robe long.
- **D:** map → /m/ /ă/ /p/, middle /ă/. Eight words: cat /k/ /ă/ /t/,
  sun /s/ /ŭ/ /n/, bed /b/ /ĕ/ /d/, pig /p/ /ĭ/ /g/, hot /h/ /ŏ/ /t/, cup
  /k/ /ŭ/ /p/, ten /t/ /ĕ/ /n/, fox /f/ /ŏ/ /ks/. Mystery box: /d/ /ŏ/
  /g/ → "dog!".
- **E:** pan → man → van; big → dig → pig. Decodable lines read twice
  (accuracy, then smoothness). Find-and-read: 6 cards read with the 4
  steps.
- **F:** bingo blends called and covered; snap pairs named by middle
  sound; sentence fixes with capitals and marks; ladder lightning
  sample — cat → hat → hot; detective duel questions answered.
- **G:** mat /m/ /ă/ /t/, fin /f/ /ĭ/ /n/, log /l/ /ŏ/ /g/, cup /k/ /ŭ/
  /p/, bed /b/ /ĕ/ /d/, van /v/ /ă/ /n/ — segmented, written, read back.
  Dictation ladder: cap → map (c→m) → mop (a→o) → top (m→t). Role-swap:
  learner catches the adult's one planted error.

## Formative quiz (week 4)

1. "The dog ran." is the sentence — first word "The", capital T, period
   (telling). 2. "sit"; "stop". 3. /k/ /ŭ/ /p/ — 3 sounds. 4. "cape"
   says A's name; "fin" is short i. 5. /b/ /ĕ/ /d/. 6. /m/ /ŏ/ /p/ →
   "mop" (check: "you clean with a mop"). 7. "sat" written (accept
   phonetic attempts; circle correct sounds). 8. "The sun is hot."
   Scoring: 7–8 secure; 5–6 re-teach missed items; below 5 use a
   flexible week.

## Unit assessment

- **Task 1:** sentences are "We can hop!" and "Can you see the fox?".
  Marks: ! = strong feeling/excitement, ? = asking. Fix: "Hop to the
  top!" (or ".") with capital H, read with matching expression.
- **Task 2:** 1. "frog". 2. "mix". 3. /p/ /l/ /ă/ /n/ /t/ — 5 sounds.
  4. /b/ /ĕ/ /d/ — 3 sounds.
- **Task 3:** 1. Long /ō/. 2. Short /ŭ/. 3. "pine". 4. Long — "because
  /ō/ says the letter O's name."
- **Task 4:** any card fully solved (cat: /k/ /ă/ /t/; sun: /s/ /ŭ/ /n/;
  map: /m/ /ă/ /p/). /ă/ middles: **cat and map** (either accepted,
  both = bonus). fox: /f/ /ŏ/ /ks/.
- **Task 5:** jam /j/ /ă/ /m/, lid /l/ /ĭ/ /d/, pop /p/ /ŏ/ /p/, rug /r/
  /ŭ/ /g/, wet /w/ /ĕ/ /t/ — each with the 4 steps. Written: any two
  dictated, segmented first, read back.
- **Task 6:** chunk "et" (/ĕ/ /t/); pet, get, wet, met; "the -et chunk
  stayed the same"; solo climb — net, jet, vet, set, let, or bet (any
  real -et word by one-letter swap).
- **Totals:** 15–18 secure → Unit 02; 11–14 developing → re-teach lowest
  task(s); below 11 → flexible-week re-teach before Unit 02.
