# Unit Assessment — Unit 03

**Session 16.** Four performance tasks, one per unit objective group.
Adult-administered, oral/observed/drawn responses. Solutions and scoring
guidance are in [answer-key.md](../answer-key.md#unit-assessment) only.
This assessment is a classroom check, not a standardized or validated test.

**How to score:** each task is Secure (2), Developing (1), or Not yet (0).
Total out of 8. 6–8 with no zero = ready for Unit 04. 4–5 = ready with a
noted re-teach (see [teacher-guide](../teacher-guide.md#re-teaching)).
0–3 = use a flexible week to re-teach the weakest objectives before
Unit 04.

## Task 1 — Darkness and light (Objective 1; 1-PS4-2)

The adult places an unfamiliar object (not used in lessons — e.g., a
rubber duck) in the dark box, lid closed.

- "Can you see it? Why not?"
- The adult shines the flashlight in. "Can you see it now? Why?"
- "Name one light source in this room, and one thing that is not a light
  source."

**Secure:** both "why" answers use the light rule, and both sort items
are correct. **Developing:** one "why" correct, or one sort item wrong.
**Not yet:** neither "why" uses the light rule.

## Task 2 — Beam test (Objectives 2–3; 1-PS4-3)

The adult presents the four framed panels in a shuffled order plus one
mystery material (a sheet of white paper).

- For each panel: "What word? What will the wall show?" (The learner
  may ask for a live test before answering.)
- Mystery material: "Watch the wall. What word for this one?"
- Shadow question: the adult holds the tree puppet mid-way. "Move it so
  its shadow gets bigger. Now smaller. What made it change?"

**Secure:** at least 4 of 5 words correct with matching wall results,
and both shadow moves correct with the distance reason. **Developing:**
3 words correct, or one shadow move wrong. **Not yet:** fewer than 3
words correct.

## Task 3 — Send a message (Objective 4; 1-PS4-4, K-2-ETS1-2)

The learner sends two messages of the adult's choosing from the agreed
code list, across the room, by flashlight — and reads one message the
adult sends.

- "Send 'come here.'" / "Send 'lunchtime.'" (Receiver reads from the wall.)
- The adult sends "yes" (1 flash). "What did I send?"
- "Show me your plan sketch. What part of the sketch did you actually
  build?"

**Secure:** both sent messages read correctly by the receiver, the
received message read correctly, and the sketch matches the build.
**Developing:** one message misread, or the sketch only partly matches.
**Not yet:** messages do not get through.

## Task 4 — Compare and judge (Objective 5; 1-PS4-4, K-2-ETS1-3)

Using the completed project comparison table:

- "In a noisy room, which messenger wins? Why?"
- "When the receiver is facing away from the wall, which messenger wins?
  Why?"
- "Is one messenger best everywhere? Why not?"

**Secure:** both condition winners correct with test-based reasons, and
the "not everywhere" answer names the condition dependence.
**Developing:** one winner correct, or reasons missing. **Not yet:**
claims one device wins everywhere, or cannot name a winner per condition.

## Safety check (Objective 6; not scored, must pass)

During all four tasks the adult watches for: beams pointed at walls
(never eyes), no sun-looking, mirror handled safely, string (if the sound
device is used) never near the neck. Any unsafe action is stopped,
corrected, and re-tested before the session ends; the unit is not marked
complete until the safety check passes.
