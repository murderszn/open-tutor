# Formative Quiz — Unit 03, Week 14

**Session 15.** Adult reads every item aloud; oral, pointing, drawing,
or gesture answers count. Part A is observed; Part B is a 4-item
recording task (draw + label, adult scribes words). Solutions are in
[answer-key.md](../answer-key.md#formative-quiz) only.

**How to score:** 6–8 correct = secure; 4–5 = developing (re-teach the
missed objective in Session 16's re-teach time); 0–3 = re-teach Lessons
01–02 before the unit assessment.

## Part A — Say, point, or show (4 items)

1. The adult puts a toy in the dark box, lid closed. "Can you see it?
   Why not?" Then shines the flashlight in. "Can you see it now? Why?"
2. The adult holds up the four framed panels one at a time (clear
   plastic, wax paper, cardboard, mirror). For each: "What word? What
   will the wall show?"
3. The adult holds the star puppet between the flashlight and the wall.
   "What shape is the shadow? What two things made it?"
4. The adult flashes 2 flashes on the wall. "What message did I send?
   How do you know?"

## Part B — Draw and label (4 items)

The learner draws one small picture per item; the adult labels from
dictation. Each item is one mark: correct = 1, otherwise 0.

5. Draw the dark box with the light OFF (nothing visible) and with the
   light ON (object visible). Both halves required for the mark.
6. Draw the four wall results: bright spot, dim glow, dark shadow,
   bounced light — each under its material word (adult writes the words;
   the learner draws the result).
7. Draw the star puppet close to the light (big shadow) and close to the
   wall (small shadow). The close-to-light shadow must be bigger for the
   mark.
8. Draw the flashlight messenger: sender, beam, wall with flashes,
   receiver. All four parts required for the mark.
