# Practice Sessions — Unit 03

Adult-led sessions referenced in the [unit pacing](README.md#16-session-pacing).
The adult reads everything aloud and scribes. Solutions are in
[answer-key.md](answer-key.md) only — never beside the tasks below.

## Session A — Dark-box detective (Session 2)

Follows Lesson 01. Materials: dark box, flashlight, 4 objects (toy animal,
crayon, ball, spoon), journal.

1. **Predict first.** The adult names an object ("the spoon") and puts it
   in the box, lid closed. The learner predicts: "Will you see it?"
   Then peeps and checks. Repeat for all 4 objects, one at a time.
2. **Two at once.** Two objects in the box, lid closed. "How many do you
   see?" (The learner peeps.) Then the adult shines the light in. "How
   many now?"
3. **Whose turn to hide?** The learner chooses an object, puts it in the
   box, and closes the lid. The adult peeps and says what they see
   (nothing). The learner shines the light in and the adult names the
   object. Swap roles once.
4. **Draw the rule.** In the journal, the learner draws the box twice:
   lid closed (dark, nothing visible) and light on (object visible).
   The adult labels from dictation: "no light — cannot see" /
   "light — can see."
5. **Error hunt.** The adult says: "The ball disappeared when I closed
   the lid, and came back when I opened it." The learner says whether
   that is right and why, then proves it by opening the lid (the ball
   was there all along).
6. **Tell it back.** "In your own words, why can we see the toy only
   when the flashlight is on?" The adult scribes the answer.

## Light-source hunt (Session 3)

Observation session. Materials: journal, crayons, room and home items.

1. **Walk and find.** Walk the room (or home) together. Find 3 things
   that make their own light and 3 things that do not. The learner points;
   the adult names and lists them.
2. **Sort the cards.** Picture cards (drawn by the adult): sun, lamp,
   flashlight, table, book, mirror, window, toy. Sort into "makes its
   own light" and "needs light shone on it."
3. **Tricky one.** "Is a window a light source?" Discuss: sunlight comes
   THROUGH the window; the window itself does not make light. Sort it
   accordingly.
4. **Moon question.** "Does the moon make its own light, like the sun?"
   (Adult-only note: moonlight is reflected sunlight. At grade 1, accept
   "the moon shines at night" and record the question for Unit 06; do
   not test it.)
5. **Journal record.** The learner draws one light source and one
   non-source, labeled by the adult from dictation.

## Light or no light game (Session 4)

Review game + Unit 01 retrieval. Materials: 8 picture cards (dark box,
lamp-on room, cave, flashlight, sunny window, closed closet, starry sky,
book in a dark drawer).

1. **Sort.** "Can we see in this picture?" Sort the cards into "we can
   see" and "we cannot see," saying why for each (light present or not).
2. **Fix the dark ones.** For each "cannot see" card, the learner says
   what light would fix it (open the closet door, turn on the lamp, use
   a flashlight).
3. **Unit 01 retrieval.** "Ask one 'I notice / I wonder' question about
   light." (Any testable question counts: "I wonder if light can go
   around a corner?" is excellent.)
4. **Speed round.** The adult flashes cards quickly; the learner calls
   "light!" or "no light!" for each. Three rounds, faster each time.

## Session B — Beam tests, one material at a time (Session 6)

Follows Lesson 02. Materials: flashlight, white wall/screen, the four
framed panels, journal.

1. **Set the fair test.** Same flashlight, same distance (adult marks
   the floor with tape), same wall. "What stays the same every time?"
2. **Test all four.** One panel at a time, in this order: clear plastic,
   wax paper, cardboard, mirror. For each, the learner says the wall
   result (bright spot / dim glow / dark shadow / bounced light) and
   the word (transparent / translucent / opaque / reflective).
3. **Order them.** "Put the panels in order: most light through to least
   light through." (Clear plastic, wax paper, cardboard; mirror sits
   apart — it bounces light instead of letting it through.)
4. **Mystery material.** The adult tests a new material (a sheet of
   paper, a plastic bag, a towel). The learner watches the wall and
   names the word. Test at least 2 mystery materials.
5. **Journal record.** The learner draws the four wall results in four
   squares, labeled by the adult from dictation.

## Session C — Picture investigation (Session 7)

Uses the generated illustration
[`assets/light-beam-materials.png`](assets/light-beam-materials.png) or
its text alternative in [`assets/README.md`](assets/README.md#text-only-alternative).

1. **Find the flashlight.** "Where is the light coming from? Which way
   does the beam go?"
2. **Read the three panels.** "What is on the wall behind the clear
   plastic? Behind the wax paper? Behind the cardboard?"
3. **Match the words.** "Which panel is transparent? Which is
   translucent? Which is opaque? How does the wall tell you?"
4. **Spot the mirror.** "Find the small mirror on the table. What is it
   doing to the light?" (Bouncing a small spot onto the table.)
5. **One-thing-wrong hunt.** "The picture shows all three panels in one
   beam. In our real tests, did we test them all at once, or one at a
   time?" (One at a time. The picture lines them up so we can compare
   results — it is a comparison picture, not a setup to copy.)
6. **Draw it back.** The learner draws the three panels with their three
   wall results from memory, then checks against the picture.

## Material sort game (Session 8)

Review + journal check. Materials: 8 everyday items (plastic wrap, wax
paper, cardboard, mirror, towel, window glass sample or photo, paper,
spoon), journal.

1. **Sort by word.** Sort the 8 items into four groups: transparent,
   translucent, opaque, reflective. (An item may sit in two groups —
   the spoon is both opaque and reflective.)
2. **Defend one sort.** The learner picks one item and explains its
   group with a wall result ("the towel is opaque because it would make
   a dark shadow").
3. **Journal check.** The adult reviews Sessions 6–7 journal pages with
   the learner the same day: are wall results matched to the right
   materials? Fix one error together if there is one.
4. **Fast sort.** Timed by the adult counting slowly to ten: how many
   items can the learner sort correctly? Repeat once, aiming to beat the
   first score honestly.

## Session D — Shadow hunt and shadow-size test (Session 10)

Follows Lesson 03. Materials: flashlight, white wall/screen, star and
tree puppets, paper strip, journal.

1. **Find 4 shadows.** Around the room (or outside with the adult, never
   looking at the sun): find 4 shadows. For each: what light made it,
   what object blocked the light?
2. **Measure near vs. far.** Star puppet close to the flashlight: measure
   the shadow in hand-widths. Star puppet close to the wall: measure
   again. Record both numbers in the journal (drawn hands count).
3. **Predict and check.** Tree puppet halfway: "Bigger than the near-wall
   shadow? Smaller than the near-light shadow?" Check by measuring.
4. **Shape check.** "Does the shadow show the puppet's shape?" Hold up
   each puppet and its shadow side by side.
5. **Lights-off proof.** Turn the flashlight off. "Where did the shadow
   go?" (Gone — a shadow is blocked light, not paint.)

## Shadow-puppet storytelling (Session 15)

Review session before the formative quiz. Materials: flashlight, white
wall/screen, all puppets, journal.

1. **Build a 3-part story.** The learner picks 3 puppets and orders them:
   beginning, middle, end. (Example: tree → animal → star: "The animal
   walks past the tree and wishes on the star.")
2. **Perform it.** The learner moves the puppets in the beam while the
   adult narrates (or the learner narrates). Shadows are the actors.
3. **Size for drama.** "Make the scary part BIG." The learner moves the
   puppet close to the light for the big shadow, then close to the wall
   for the small shadow.
4. **Retell with the rule.** "Tell the story again in one sentence, and
   tell me what made the shadows." (Light + puppets blocking it.)
5. **Quiz next.** After the story, the learner completes the
   [formative quiz](assessments/week-04_quiz.md).
