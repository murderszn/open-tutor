# Resource Pack — Unit 03: Light, Visibility, Shadows, and Materials

**Summary (adult planning judgment):** This pack supports a grade-1 unit
on seeing only when illuminated, testing transparent / translucent /
opaque / reflective materials in a light beam, describing shadow shape
and size, and designing a flashlight-code messenger. All items are
adult-previewed routes; videos supplement teaching and never replace the
hands-on lessons. Checked 2026-10-22.

## Web references (reputable; check dates before reuse)

1. **Smithsonian Science in Pre-K — "The Science of Light and Shadows"**
   — https://scienceinprek.si.edu/science-light-and-shadows (opened and
   read 2026-10-22; Smithsonian National Air and Space Museum early
   childhood page). States the unit's shadow rules in child-level form:
   shadows need a light source and an object; shadows show the object's
   shape; shadows change size with distance from the light; opaque
   objects block all light; transparent objects block none; translucent
   objects block part of the light. **Core reference for Lessons 02–03.**
   Free, no account.
2. **NSTA — NGSS standard display, 1-PS4** —
   https://my.nsta.org/ngss/DisplayStandard?view=dci&id=18 (opened and read
   2026-10-22; matches the track audit crosswalk checked 2026-10-02). **Adult background only**
   — framework wording, never assigned to the learner.
3. **NGSS Release, Topic Arrangements (April 2013, PDF)** —
   https://core-docs.s3.amazonaws.com/documents/asset/uploaded_file/409455/NGSS_Booklet.pdf
   (1-PS4-2/3/4 text corroborated 2026-10-22 via the document's published
   text). **Adult background only.**
4. **NASA eClips — Spotlite "Interactions of Light" teacher packet (PDF)**
   —
   https://assets.science.nasa.gov/content/dam/science/cds/eclips/assets/documents/nasa-spotlite-interactive-lesson-interactions-of-light-teacher.pdf
   (found 2026-10-22 via web search; vocabulary: transmit, reflect,
   transparent). **Adult planning background only** — the packet targets
   older students; the grade-1 lessons stay at the observe-and-compare
   level.
5. **NASA Space Place** — https://spaceplace.nasa.gov/ (repo-vetted shelf
   item; NASA's kids' site). Search within for light explainers. Free, no
   account. Intended audience: elementary — adult confirms grade-1 fit
   per item.
6. **Smithsonian Learning Lab** — https://learninglab.si.edu/ (repo-vetted
   shelf item). Search for light/shadow collections. Free, no account for
   viewing — adult confirms per item.
