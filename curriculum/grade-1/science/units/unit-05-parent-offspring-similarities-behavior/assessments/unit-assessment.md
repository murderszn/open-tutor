# Unit Assessment — Unit 05

**Session 16.** Adult reads every task aloud; oral, pointing, drawing,
or gesture responses count. The adult records a score per task and the
readiness decision in [teacher-guide.md](../teacher-guide.md#readiness-criteria).
Scoring guidance is in [answer-key.md](../answer-key.md#unit-assessment)
only — this page contains no answers.

**How to score:** each task is 0, 1, or 2. Total out of 8. 6–8 with no
zero = ready for Unit 06; 4–5 = ready with a note (record the weak
objective for Unit 06 warm-ups); 0–3 = not yet — use a flexible week to
re-teach before Unit 06. The safety check below is pass/fail and is
separate from the 8-point total: a failed safety check means re-teaching
the safety rules before any further hands-on work, whatever the total.

## Task 1 — Match three animal babies (Objectives 1, 5)

The adult lays out three parent cards (mother dog, mother duck, hen)
and three baby cards (puppy, duckling, chick), shuffled. The learner
matches each baby to its parent. For each pair, the learner names one
trait that is alike and one way they are different. Score: 2 = all 3
pairs matched with one alike trait and one difference each; 1 = 2 pairs
correct, or matches without differences; 0 = 1 or fewer correct.

## Task 2 — Compare a young plant with its adult (Objective 2)

The adult shows the sunflower seedling card and the grown sunflower
card (or the bean pair, if the learner worked mainly with beans). The
learner names one part that is alike and one way they are different,
and answers: "Is the seedling the same kind of plant as the grown
plant?" Score: 2 = one true alike part and one true difference, and
"same kind" answered correctly; 1 = one of the two correct; 0 = neither,
or a kind error.

## Task 3 — Name two behavior patterns (Objectives 3, 4)

The adult shows two behavior cards (for example, mother bird feeding
chicks; elephants surrounding a calf) and, for one of them, reads one
sentence aloud from the Lesson 04 text ("Parents take turns caring for
their youngsters after they hatch"). For each card, the learner names
the behavior and says what it helps the baby do; for the read-aloud
card, the learner also says it as evidence ("The book shows… so…").
Score: 2 = both cards complete (behavior + help, and the evidence form
for the read-aloud card); 1 = one card complete, or behaviors without
helps; 0 = neither.

## Task 4 — Mystery baby and the unit rule (Objectives 1, 5)

The adult shows one mystery baby card not used in Task 1 (a kitten, or
a calf) with the parent cards face down. The learner predicts the
parent from body parts, the adult turns over the predicted parent, and
the learner explains the match ("I matched them because both have ___,
but the baby is ___") and states the unit rule. Score: 2 = correct
parent, one alike trait as evidence, one difference, and the rule
stated; 1 = correct parent with one of evidence / difference / rule;
0 = wrong parent, or no evidence.

## Safety check (pass/fail, all lessons; track objective 10)

The adult asks three questions; the learner must answer all three
correctly (words, pointing, or gesture) to pass:

1. "What do we do with seeds and soil?" *(Never taste them; wash hands
   after touching them.)*
2. "What do we do if we see a wild baby animal?" *(Watch from far
   away, or in pictures — never touch, chase, or feed it.)*
3. "What do we do if an investigation would be unsafe to touch?"
   *(Ask the safe-alternative question: "What can I observe instead?")*
