# Formative Quiz — Unit 05, Week 22

**Session 15.** Adult reads every item aloud; oral, pointing, drawing,
or gesture answers count. Part A is observed; Part B is a 4-item
recording task (draw + label, adult scribes words). Solutions are in
[answer-key.md](../answer-key.md#formative-quiz) only.

**How to score:** 6–8 correct = secure; 4–5 = developing (re-teach the
missed objective in Session 16's re-teach time); 0–3 = re-teach Lessons
01–02 before the unit assessment.

## Part A — Say, point, or show (4 items)

1. The adult shows the puppy card and the mother dog card. "Do these
   belong together? What is alike? What is different?"
2. The adult shows the sunflower seedling card and the grown sunflower
   card. "Do these belong together? What part is alike? What is
   different?"
3. The adult shows the mother bird feeding chicks card. "What is the
   parent doing? What does it help the babies do?"
4. The adult shows the penguin parent keeping an egg warm card. "What
   is the parent doing? What does it help the baby do?"

## Part B — Draw and label (4 items)

The learner draws one small picture per item; the adult labels from
dictation. Each item is one mark: correct = 1, otherwise 0.

5. Draw a parent animal and its baby (any pair from the unit). The
   parent must be drawn bigger than the baby, and the drawing must
   carry one alike label and one different label (adult writes the
   words; the learner dictates or points).
6. Draw a parent helping its baby (feeding, warming, sheltering,
   protecting, or guiding). The drawing must show the parent doing
   something for the baby, and carry a "helps the baby…" label.
7. Write (adult scribes) one evidence sentence in the form "The
   book/picture shows ___, so ___ helps babies survive," using a true
   behavior from Lessons 03–04.
8. Mystery match: the adult shows one baby card the learner has not
   matched in this quiz (a calf, or a second duckling card). The
   learner points to its parent card and names one alike trait. Both
   the correct parent and one alike trait are required for the mark.
