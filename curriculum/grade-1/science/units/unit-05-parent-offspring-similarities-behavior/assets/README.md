small ducklings. Each duckling is yellow with a small flat orange beak

stem, three large green leaves, and one large yellow flower head with

small round green leaves. It has no flower.

stem and green leaves; they differ in height, leaf number and size, and
the flower (present on the adult, absent on the seedling).
