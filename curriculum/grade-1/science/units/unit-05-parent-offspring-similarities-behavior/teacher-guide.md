# Teacher Guide — Unit 05

Session-by-session guidance for the guiding adult. Student materials never
contain answers; this guide and [answer-key.md](answer-key.md) are the
only places solutions live.

## Session map and emphasis

| Sessions | Focus | Watch for |
|---|---|---|
| 1–4 | Animal alike/different + bean setup | "Exactly alike" — re-run the dog pair with size and spots. |
| 5–8 | Plant alike/different + seedling measures | "No flower means a different plant" — re-run the sunflower pair; flowers come with growing up. |
| 9–10 | Five parent behaviors + sort | "Nice, not helpful" answers — force action + help into every answer. |
| 11–12 | Evidence sentences + album start | "I think" instead of "the book shows" — re-chant the evidence sentence. |
| 13–14 | Mystery matching + album finish | Matching by color alone — re-run the yellow chick vs. duckling round. |
| 15–16 | Matching fair, quiz, assessment | Rushing the quiz — it is oral/observed; let the learner point and draw. |

## Misconceptions (all lessons)

1. **"Babies are exactly like their parents."** Size, color, and
   "not grown yet" differences are the correction — the dog spots and
   the sunflower flower rounds.
2. **"Babies are totally different from their parents."** Shared body
   parts (legs, beak, fur, feathers; stem, leaves) are the correction.
3. **"Color is the match."** The yellow chick vs. yellow duckling
   round is the correction: beak shape and webbed feet decide, not color.
4. **"A seedling is a different kind of plant."** Shared parts (stem,
   leaves) are the correction; growing up adds size, leaves, and flowers.
5. **"Parents help because they are nice."** Redirect to action +
   help: what the parent does, and what it helps the baby do.
6. **"Only mothers help."** Penguin parents take turns; meerkat groups
   and elephant herds help too.
7. **"Babies do nothing."** Babies signal — cheeping, crying, calling —
   and the signal starts the parent's response.
8. **"Any fact from a book is a behavior pattern."** Habitat facts are
   not behaviors; the Lesson 04 error-analysis round is the correction.

## Re-teaching

- **Objective 1 weak (animal alike/different):** repeat the Session 2
  matching game with only two pairs at a time (dog first, then duck),
  and keep the alike/different chart visible for every match.
- **Objective 2 weak (plant alike/different):** re-run the Lesson 02
  bean and sunflower pairs side by side, and re-measure the bean bag
  together; add a second seedling picture set if the bag has not
  sprouted.
- **Objective 3 weak (behaviors):** re-run the Session 10 sort with
  only two bins at a time (FEED vs. PROTECT first), then add the rest;
  keep the "helps the baby…" sentence attached to every card.
- **Objective 4 weak (evidence sentences):** re-chant "The book
  shows… so…" with the penguin page only, one sentence at a time,
  before adding the meerkat page.
- **Objective 5 weak (explaining matches):** re-run Lesson 05 with the
  parent cards face up (no mystery) and the explanation sentence
  chanted for each pair, then hide the parents again for one pair only.
- **Objective 6 weak (safety):** re-state the four rules before the
  next hands-on session and ask the learner to name the safe
  alternative for one unsafe idea ("What do we do instead of touching
  a wild baby animal?").

## Readiness criteria

- **Ready for Unit 06:** unit assessment 6–8 with no zero, safety check
  passed, and the learner can state the unit rule ("like, but not
  exactly like") and name two parent behaviors with their help without
  prompting.
- **Ready with a note:** assessment 4–5 — record which objective to
  revisit in Unit 06's retrieval warm-ups (Unit 06 opens with
  observation routines; fold the weak rule into its first two
  sessions).
- **Not yet:** assessment 0–3 — use a flexible week (diagnostic/catch-up
  or the final review weeks) to re-teach the weakest objectives; do not
  start Unit 06 cold.

## Safety notes (every session)

- Seeds are adult-handled: dry beans are a choking hazard and are never
  near the learner's mouth, on the table as loose play items, or tasted.
- Never taste seeds, soil, or plant parts; wash hands after soil, seed,
  or damp-bag work.
- Living things are handled gently and briefly; wild animals are
  observed in pictures only, never touched, chased, or fed by the learner.
- If the learner has allergies to seeds or soil materials, use the
  picture seedling alternative for every bean-bag session — the
  observation, measurement, and recording goals are identical.
- Small parts (picture cards, paper strips) stay away from the mouth;
  the adult pre-cuts all card sets.
- If the learner is sensitive to mess or damp materials, the adult
  handles the bag and the learner observes and draws only.

## Differentiation

- **Language access:** every new word is taught with an object or
  picture first, word second; home-language words are welcomed
  alongside English terms; the evidence and explanation sentences are
  taught as chants before they are expected independently.
- **Alternate response modes:** pointing, sorting cards, drawing,
  mime, and adult-scribed dictation are full evidence at every check.
- **Seated-table and floor-play variants** are given in every lesson;
  the floor variant is the default when the learner needs movement.
- **Extension learners:** Lesson 05's extension (an unseen animal with
  mixed traits) and the album's Page 4 free choice both add reasoning
  without adding reading load.
- **Learners who need more time:** the bean bag's slow growth is a
  feature, not a delay — "not yet" is a recorded observation, and the
  picture seedling keeps every session on schedule regardless of
  sprouting.
