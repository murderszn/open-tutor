# Answer Key — Unit 05

Teacher-only. Every practice, quiz, assessment, and project item is solved
here. Accept any response that carries the same meaning in the learner's
own words, drawings, or gestures; the adult scribes.

## Lesson 01

- **Guided practice:** (1) Cat: alike — fur, four legs, tail, ears,
  whiskers; different — kitten smaller, stripes lighter or placed
  differently. (2) Hen: alike — feathers, two legs, beak, wings;
  different — chicks small and fluffy, hen bigger with smooth feathers.
  (3) Error-analysis: no — the puppy is not exactly like its mother;
  it is smaller and its spots sit in different places. Fur alone is one
  alike trait, not the whole story. (4) Mix-up: no — puppy (four legs,
  fur) does not belong with the hen (two legs, feathers); body parts
  do not match. (5) Chart: alike — "both have four legs"; different —
  "puppy is smaller." (6) "A baby animal is like its parent, but not
  exactly like it."
- **Exit check:** cow + calf (or second cat set): alike — fur, four
  legs, tail, ears; different — calf smaller, markings placed
  differently. Second probe: no — a baby is like, but not exactly like,
  its parent.

## Lesson 02

- **Guided practice:** (1) Bean: both have a stem; grown plant taller.
  (2) Leaf count: seedling — two; grown plant — more. (3)
  Error-analysis: no — the seedling is a young bean plant; beans come
  later, when it grows up. (4) Bag: record whatever is true that day
  (nothing yet / root / shoot / leaves); next prediction is the next
  step in the pattern (root, then shoot, then leaves). (5) Chart:
  alike — "both have leaves"; different — "seedling is shorter."
  (6) "A young plant is like its parent plant, but not exactly like it."
- **Exit check:** bean bag vs. adult bean plant card: alike — stem and
  leaves (or, if not sprouted yet, "the seed will grow a stem and
  leaves"); different — seedling much shorter, fewer leaves, no beans
  yet. Second probe: a sunflower seedling will have a flower when it
  grows up; a bean plant will grow beans later — any "when it grows
  up" answer counts.

## Lesson 03

- **Guided practice:** (1) Duck: guiding — helps ducklings find water
  and food and learn to swim. (2) Cat: feed, warm, shelter all fit with
  a reason (milk = feed; cuddling = warm; den = shelter). (3) Elephant:
  calf inside the herd — protection; helps the calf stay safe.
  (4) Error-analysis: no — the mother feeds her chicks so they grow
  and survive; the cheeping is the baby's signal, not a song for its
  own sake. (5) Signal: a cheeping chick is telling its parent it
  needs food, warmth, or help; the parent comes, feeds, comforts, or
  protects it. (6) "Parents help their babies survive by feeding,
  warming, sheltering, protecting, or guiding them."
- **Exit check:** mother bird on a nest: warming/sheltering/protecting
  (accept any one with a reason) — helps the eggs or chicks stay warm
  and safe. Second probe: any of feed, warm, shelter, protect, guide.

## Lesson 04

- **Guided practice:** (1) Penguin re-read: feeding and protecting
  (accept either) — "The book shows parents taking turns caring for
  the youngsters, so feeding/protecting helps babies survive."
  (2) Meerkat: protect — "The book shows babysitters staying with the
  pups, so protecting helps babies survive." (3) Error-analysis: no —
  where penguins live is a fact, not a behavior pattern; evidence
  needs what the parents DO (keeping warm, feeding). (4) Picture:
  guide — "The picture shows a mother duck leading her ducklings, so
  guiding helps babies survive." (5) Two patterns in the penguin
  reading: warming and feeding (or warming and protecting).
  (6) "The book shows ___, so ___ helps babies survive" — any true
  behavior pair counts.
- **Exit check:** new meerkat sentence (gathering food, keeping watch):
  feed and protect — "The book shows meerkats gathering food and
  keeping watch, so feeding and protecting help babies survive."
  Second probe: "the book shows" names the source of the evidence;
  "I think" is a guess, not evidence.

## Lesson 05

- **Guided practice:** (1) Mystery kitten: four legs, fur, tail, ears
  → mother cat; alike — fur and four legs; different — kitten smaller,
  markings placed differently. (2) Mystery chick: two legs, feathers,
  small pointed beak, wings → hen; alike — feathers and beak;
  different — chick smaller and fluffier. (3) Error-analysis: no —
  check the beak and feet: the chick has a pointed beak and no webbed
  feet, so it is not a duckling; color alone is not evidence.
  (4) Ruling out: calf cannot belong to the hen — four legs and fur do
  not match two legs and feathers. (5) Plant mystery: baby plant —
  tiny, two small leaves, no flower yet → sunflower (adult card
  confirms). (6) "I matched this baby to this parent because both
  have ___, but the baby is ___" — any true trait pair counts.
- **Exit check:** calf (or second duckling set): parent — cow (or
  mother duck); alike trait — fur and four legs (or flat beak and
  webbed feet); difference — smaller, markings/color placed
  differently. Second probe: no — like, but not exactly like.

## Practice sessions

- **Session 2 (matching game):** pairs — dog/puppy, cat/kitten,
  duck/ducklings, hen/chicks, cow/calf, elephant/calf. Reasons: see
  Lesson 01 key. Swapped chick under mother duck is wrong: chick has a
  pointed beak and no webbed feet; ducklings have flat beaks and webbed
  feet.
- **Session 3 (bean bag):** prediction recorded as drawn (expected
  pattern: root first, then shoot); any prediction accepted — the
  Session 6 check is the graded science, not the guess.
- **Session 4 (chart + retrieval):** trait sort — Alike: "both have
  fur," "both have a flat beak," "both have four legs," "both have
  feathers"; Different: "puppy is smaller," "duckling is yellower,"
  "chick is fluffier," "hen is bigger." Unit 04 retrieval: plant part
  — roots drink water and hold the plant steady (or stem/leaves/flower
  with a correct job); animal part — beak helps a bird eat, webbed feet
  help a duck swim (or any correct part/job pair).
- **Session 6 (seedling check + picture investigation):** Part A —
  record the true state (nothing yet / root / shoot / leaves) and the
  finger-width measurement if a shoot is up; "not yet" is a correct
  observation. Part B picture answers: (1) Three ducklings.
  (2) Alike (duck) — same body shape, flat beak, feathers (any one).
  (3) Different (duck) — mother bigger and brown; ducklings smaller and
  yellow (any one). (4) Alike (sunflower) — green stem and green leaves
  (either). (5) Different (sunflower) — grown sunflower much taller
  with a big yellow flower; seedling tiny with two small round leaves
  and no flower (any one).
- **Session 7 (mixed sort):** pairs — puppy→dog, kitten→cat,
  duckling→duck, chick→hen, calf→cow, bean seedling→bean plant,
  sunflower seedling→sunflower, repeat baby→its own adult. Sunflower
  seedling under the bean plant is wrong: leaf shape and kind do not
  match; the adult confirms with the picture cards.
- **Session 8 (measure 2):** taller / same / not up yet — decided from
  the two recorded numbers or drawings, not from memory. Example:
  "2 finger-widths, now 4, so it grew taller."
- **Session 10 (behavior sort):** (a) bird feeding chicks → FEED,
  helps the chicks grow; (b) penguin keeping egg warm → SHELTER-or-WARM,
  keeps the egg alive until hatching; (c) duck leading ducklings →
  GUIDE, helps them find water and food; (d) cat in den with kittens →
  SHELTER-or-WARM, keeps kittens safe and warm; (e) elephants around a
  calf → PROTECT, keeps the calf safe; (f) chick cheeping → the baby's
  signal for food/help — accept FEED or its own "signal" answer with
  the reason "cheeping tells the parent the baby needs help."
- **Session 15 (matching fair):** all Session 2/7 pairs re-matched;
  behavior flashes — at least 4 of 6 named with their help (see Session
  10 key).

## Project rubric

- **Matching:** 2 = all 4 pages matched to the right parent with a
  shared body or plant part as evidence; 1 = 3 correct, or color-only
  evidence; 0 = 2 or fewer correct, or no evidence.
- **Alike / different:** 2 = every page has one true alike trait and
  one true difference; 1 = most pages have both, or one page has a
  wrong trait; 0 = labels missing or mostly wrong.
- **Parent helps:** 2 = every animal page names a behavior and what it
  helps the baby do; the plant page has a true growth note ("will grow
  taller and grow more leaves"); 1 = behaviors named without the help,
  or only some pages complete; 0 = no notes, or notes that do not
  match the picture.
- **Sharing:** 2 = shares one page with the alike trait, the
  difference, and the help, in any response mode; 1 = two of the three
  parts; 0 = does not share, or shares none of the three parts.
- Totals: 7–8 secure; 4–6 developing; 0–3 re-teach Lessons 01 and 03
  before the unit assessment.

## Formative quiz

- **Part A:** (1) Puppy + mother dog: alike — fur, four legs (any
  one); different — puppy smaller, spots placed differently (any one).
  (2) Sunflower seedling + grown sunflower: alike — stem and leaves
  (either); different — seedling much shorter, fewer leaves, no flower
  yet (any one). (3) Mother bird feeding chicks: feeding — helps the
  chicks grow. (4) Penguin parent keeping an egg warm: warming —
  keeps the egg alive until it hatches.
- **Part B:** (5) Drawings must show the size difference (big parent,
  small baby) with one alike and one different label each — both
  halves required for the mark. (6) Behavior drawing must show a
  parent doing something for a baby (feeding, warming, sheltering,
  protecting, guiding) with a "helps the baby…" label — both parts
  required. (7) Evidence sentence must follow the "The book/picture
  shows ___, so ___ helps babies survive" form with a true behavior —
  both halves required. (8) Mystery match must name the correct
  parent and one alike trait — both required.
- Scoring: 6–8 secure; 4–5 developing; 0–3 re-teach Lessons 01–02
  before the unit assessment.

## Unit assessment

- **Task 1 (animal matching, 0–2):** 2 = all 3 pairs matched with one
  alike trait and one difference each; 1 = 2 pairs correct, or
  matches without differences; 0 = 1 or fewer correct.
- **Task 2 (plant comparison, 0–2):** 2 = seedling vs. adult sunflower
  (or bean) with one alike part and one difference, both true;
  1 = one of the two correct; 0 = neither, or a kind error (calling
  the seedling a different kind of plant).
- **Task 3 (behavior pattern, 0–2):** 2 = names the behavior and what
  it helps the baby do, for both cards shown; 1 = one card complete,
  or behaviors without helps; 0 = neither.
- **Task 4 (mystery baby + rule, 0–2):** 2 = correct parent, one alike
  trait as evidence, one difference, and the rule stated ("like, but
  not exactly like"); 1 = correct parent with one of evidence /
  difference / rule; 0 = wrong parent, or no evidence.
- Totals: 6–8 ready for Unit 06; 4–5 ready with a note (record the weak
  objective for Unit 06 warm-ups); 0–3 not yet — use a flexible week
  to re-teach before Unit 06.
