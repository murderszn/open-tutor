# Project — My Family Album of Living Things

**Sessions 12 and 14 (plus sharing in Session 15's review).**
Adult-supervised; oral, drawing, pointing, or adult-scribed responses
count. The album is the learner's own original drawings and dictated
words — no copied pictures, no learner photos, no real family details.

## The task

Make a family album with **4 pages**. Each page shows one parent and
its offspring:

- **Page 1:** an animal pair of the learner's choice (for example, dog
  + puppy, or duck + ducklings).
- **Page 2:** a second animal pair, different from Page 1.
- **Page 3:** a plant pair (bean plant + seedling, or sunflower +
  seedling).
- **Page 4:** the learner's choice — any animal or plant pair from the
  unit, including the bean seedling from the Session 3 bag.

Each page must show:

1. A drawing of the parent and its offspring, with the parent drawn
   bigger (animals) or taller (plants).
2. One **alike** label: a trait both share (for example, "both have a
   flat beak" or "both have leaves").
3. One **different** label: a way they differ (for example, "the baby
   is smaller" or "the seedling has no flower yet").
4. One **parent helps** note: a behavior and what it helps the baby do
   (for example, "the mother bird feeds her chicks — feeding helps the
   chicks grow"). For the plant page, the "parent helps" note may be a
   seed note instead: "the parent plant's seeds grow into new plants"
   is not a behavior, so the plant page's fourth item is a growth note:
   "the seedling will grow taller and grow more leaves" — the adult
   labels it honestly as growth, not as parent behavior.

## Steps

**Session 12 — Part 1 (choose and start).**

1. The learner chooses the 4 pairs and tells the adult why each baby
   belongs to its parent (one alike trait each — this is the matching
   check before drawing begins).
2. The learner draws Pages 1 and 2 (parent + offspring, parent bigger).
3. The adult labels each drawing from dictation and writes the alike
   and different labels the learner dictates.
4. The adult checks: does each drawing show a real shared part, not
   just a color? If a page matches by color alone, the learner adds a
   body-part clue before moving on.

**Session 14 — Part 2 (finish and share).**

1. The learner draws Pages 3 and 4 with the same four items per page.
2. The learner adds the "parent helps" note (or growth note, for the
   plant page) to every page.
3. The learner shares one page aloud (or by pointing through it while
   the adult reads the labels): "This is ___ and its baby. They are
   alike because ___. They are different because ___. The parent
   helps by ___."
4. The learner does a self-check with the rubric below: point to each
   rubric row and say "yes," "almost," or "not yet" for their album.
   The adult records the self-check honestly — it is not graded down
   for honesty.

## Materials

Album paper or a folded-paper booklet (4 pages), pencils, crayons, the
picture cards from Lessons 01–02 as drawing references, the bean bag
from Session 3 (for the plant page, if sprouted), the science journal
for the alike/different chart.

## Deliverables

- The 4-page album (drawings + adult-scribed labels).
- One shared page (oral or pointing presentation in Session 14).
- The learner's rubric self-check, recorded by the adult in the journal.

## Rubric

Scored by the adult from the album and the sharing. Each row is 0, 1,
or 2. Total out of 8. Scoring guidance is in
[answer-key.md](answer-key.md#project-rubric).

| Row | 2 — strong | 1 — developing | 0 — not yet |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Matching (4 pages)** | All 4 babies matched to the right parent, with a shared body part or plant part as evidence | 3 babies matched correctly, or matches with color-only evidence | 2 or fewer correct, or no evidence given |
| **Alike / different (per page)** | Every page has one true alike trait AND one true difference | Most pages have both, or one page has a wrong trait | Alike/different labels missing or mostly wrong |
| **Parent helps (or plant growth note)** | Every animal page names a behavior AND what it helps the baby do; the plant page has a true growth note | Behaviors named without the help, or only some pages complete | No behavior/help notes, or notes that do not match the picture |
| **Sharing** | Shares one page with the alike trait, the difference, and the help, in any response mode | Shares one page with two of the three parts | Does not share, or shares without any of the three parts |

**What the score means:** 7–8 = the album shows the unit's ideas
securely. 4–6 = developing — re-teach the weakest row in Session 16's
re-teach time (for example, re-run the Session 2 matching game for a
weak Matching row). 0–3 = re-teach Lessons 01 and 03 before the unit
assessment, using the album pages themselves as the re-teaching
material.
