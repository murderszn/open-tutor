# Resource Pack — Unit 05: Parent–Offspring Similarities and Behavior

**Summary (adult planning judgment):** This pack supports a grade-1 unit
on young plants and animals being like — but not exactly like — their
parents, and on parent behavior patterns that help offspring survive.
All items are adult-previewed routes; videos supplement teaching and
never replace the hands-on lessons. Checked 2026-11-04.

## Access notes

- **Free, no-account route for every core objective:** the matching
  games, the bean bag, the picture investigation, the behavior sort,
  and the album are offline, hands-on work using household materials —
  no video or account is required to learn any objective. The
  National Geographic Kids pages and the ScienceWorld activity are
  free with no account.
- **Videos supplement, never replace:** if YouTube is unavailable, skip
  the video items; the lessons, picture investigation, and project are
  complete without them.
- **Adult preview required:** all video items (ads, comments, length,
  captions) and any NatGeo Kids page before showing.
- **Assessment boundary note for the adult:** 1-LS3-1 does not assess
  metamorphosis or hybrids. Do not add butterfly, frog, or hybrid
  examples from outside sources to this unit's lessons or assessments.
