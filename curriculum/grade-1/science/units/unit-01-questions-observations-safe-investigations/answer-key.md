# Answer Key — Unit 01

Worked solutions for every lesson item, practice task, quiz question,
and assessment task. The adult checks learner work against the reasoning
here, accepting any response that is scientifically equivalent.
Misconception notes live in [teacher-guide.md](teacher-guide.md).

## Lesson 01

**Guided practice (sorts):**
1. "Which pebble is the heaviest?" → **Testable.** Explore by holding
   one pebble in each hand and comparing, or using a simple balance.
2. "Do the pebbles like being in a pile?" → **Not testable (wonder
   list).** Feelings cannot be observed with the senses.
3. "What sound does a pebble make when I drop it in the cup of water?"
   → **Testable.** Drop and listen.
4. "How many pebbles fit in my hand?" → **Testable.** Try it and count.

**Independent practice:** any two learner questions, sorted by the
learner; the explored answer must match the question asked (e.g.,
"Which pebble is biggest?" → "This one — I lined them up and compared").

**Application:** accept any explorable sky question with an observation
method (look at three times of day; draw what is seen).

**Exit check:** "What color is the apple's leaf?" → testable (look);
"Does the apple dream at night?" → not testable (wonder list).

## Lesson 02

**Guided practice (mystery bag):** accept any honest sense descriptions;
the check is that the learner describes *before* guessing, then compares
guess to sight.

**Independent practice:** 4 observations across ≥2 senses, e.g., apple:
"red" (sight), "round" (sight), "smooth" (touch), "cool" (touch),
"sweet smell" (smell).

**Application:** touch — feel the soil. Accept "look and touch" as a
strong answer; the key point is choosing a sense deliberately.

**Exit check:** any 2 senses with 1 observation each.

## Lesson 03

**Guided practice:** 1 detail per station (e.g., lens: "tiny dots on the
apple skin"; ruler: "pebble A reaches 4, pebble B reaches 6 — B is
longer"; flashlight: "I can see the pebbles' colors again").

**Independent practice:** 3 measurements with numbers the learner
counted; 1 lens drawing with a detail the naked eye missed.

**Application:** the **ruler** — growth means getting taller/longer, and
the ruler measures length. Accept hand lens ("see new leaves") as
reasonable; discuss why the ruler answers the growth question best.

**Exit check:** hand lens → makes small things look bigger; ruler →
tells how long something is; flashlight → helps see in the dark.

## Lesson 04

**Guided practice (observation image):** any 5 true observations from
the picture using ≥2 recording ways. Verified facts about the image
(checked 2026-10-09 against the file): blue hand lens (1), open notebook
with pencil (2), potted seedling with three green leaves (3), yellow
flashlight (4), wooden ruler (5), **five** smooth gray pebbles (6),
red apple with one green leaf (7), paper cup of blue water (8).

**Independent practice:** 4–6 recorded items on a dated page.

**Application:** the tally — it is exact and shareable; memory is not.

**Exit check:** learner points to a record and explains it.

## Lesson 05

**Guided practice (sorts):**
1. Maya smells from a distance → **safe** (gentle, wafting).
2. Leo tastes a pebble → **unsafe**, Rule 2 (mouth). Alternative: rub
   with a finger to test smoothness.
3. Ana stares at the sun → **unsafe**, Rule 4. Alternative: observe her
   shadow.
4. Sam squeezes the seedling hard → **unsafe**, Rule 3 (gentle).
   Alternative: look closely, touch a leaf softly.
5. Jon uses the flashlight with a grown-up present → **safe** (Rule 1).
6. Priya experiments alone → **unsafe**, Rule 1. Alternative: wait for
   the grown-up.

**Independent practice:** drawing shows one rule; telling matches it.

**Application:** nose-in-soil plan → breaks **Rule 3** (handle living
things gently; also violates careful smelling). Safe alternative: waft
air above the soil toward the nose from a short distance, without
touching the plant.

**Exit check:** all four rules with adult prompts: grown-up present,
nothing in mouth, gentle with living things, never look at sun.

## Practice

- **P1:** explorable — check soil with a finger, observe over days.
- **P2:** wonder list — thoughts are not observable.
- **P3:** reshaped question + three time-of-day observations recorded.
- **P4:** two questions sorted; explorable one has a method.
- **P5:** prediction stated, test performed, comparison stated.
- **P6:** three sounds identified and described.
- **P7:** three smells described (any honest words).
- **P8:** smooth/rough distinguished by touch, confirmed by sight.
- **P9:** three rounds, each a new observation with a new sense.
- **P10:** at least one detail from 30 seconds of watching.
- **P11:** one lens detail per object.
- **P12:** three measurements; correct shortest→longest order.
- **P13:** the lined-up measurement; reason: same start every time.
- **P14:** shadow moves/changes shape; drawing shows the difference.
- **P15:** lens / ruler / flashlight / tally-recording / ears.
- **P16:** tally total matches drops (up to 8).
- **P17:** drawing shows 3 leaves and brown pot — checkable facts.
- **P18:** the sharp record ("gray and smooth"); reason: it tells what
  was actually observed.
- **P19:** 4 records in 5 minutes; one shared.
- **P20:** error found: 6 tallies but 5 pebbles → correct to 5.
- **P21:** rule numbers matched to actions.
- **P22:** three plans fixed — taste soil (Rule 2 → compare by
  rubbing/looking); poke ants (Rule 3 → watch without touching);
  closet alone (Rule 1 → wait for grown-up, do it together).
- **P23:** each rule placed where it matters in the room.
- **P24:** all four rules taught to the stuffed animal; note skips.
- **P25:** plan declared safe (with grown-up); Rule 1 or 2 named as the
  check that mattered.
- **P26:** 6 items found and recorded (living thing observed gently,
  not picked).
- **P27:** 8 questions sorted; explore-methods named.
- **P28:** correct tool/sense per job.

## Week-04 quiz

1. a ("What does the seedling's leaf feel like?") — explorable by touch.
2. Any explorable pebble question (e.g., "How many bumps does it have?").
3. Two sight + one touch observation of the apple.
4. The splash/plip sound (and ripples, if eyes open after).
5. Three tally marks (⁄ ⁄ ⁄) for three pebbles.
6. Drawing + telling that matches.
7. Unsafe — Rule 2 (mouth). Alternative: rub/smell from a distance to learn about soil.
8. Any two rules with prompting.

## Unit assessment

- **Task 1:** two questions, correctly sorted; explore-method named for
  the testable one.
- **Task 2:** 4+ observations across 2 senses + 1 tool finding.
- **Task 3:** 4+ items in 2+ recording ways.
- **Task 4:** two broken rules — **Rule 1** (alone in another room) and
  **Rule 2** (tasting). Safe alternatives: do it with the grown-up
  present; learn by looking/touching/smelling-from-distance instead of
  tasting.
- **Task 5:** question + method + finding all told; journal page
  supports the telling.
