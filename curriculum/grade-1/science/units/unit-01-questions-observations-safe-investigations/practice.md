# Practice Sets — Unit 01

Extra guided and independent practice for each lesson. The adult reads all
prompts aloud; oral, pointing, drawing, or adult-scribed responses count.
Solutions are in [answer-key.md](answer-key.md#practice).

## Lesson 01 — Testable questions (Sessions 2, 14)

**P1.** Sort: "Does the seedling need water?" → explorable (check the
soil, observe over days) or wonder list? Say how you would explore it.

**P2.** Sort: "What is the seedling thinking?" → which column, and why?

**P3.** Make it testable: the learner says, "I wonder why the sky is
blue." The adult helps reshape it into a first-grade-explorable question:
"What do I observe about the sky at breakfast, lunch, and dinner?"
(Draw or scribe the three observations.)

**P4.** Question chain: start from the observation "The apple has a small
brown spot." The learner asks two questions about it; the adult helps
sort each into explorable or wonder list.

**P5.** Predict then test: "Which is heavier — the apple or five
pebbles?" Learner predicts, then tests by holding, then says whether the
prediction was right.

## Lesson 02 — Senses (Sessions 4, 13)

**P6.** Mystery sound: the adult makes three sounds (clap, snap, tap the
table). Learner names what made each sound and describes it (loud/soft,
sharp/dull).

**P7.** Smell sort: with eyes closed, learner smells three safe kitchen
items the adult chooses (e.g., an orange peel, a cinnamon stick, a bar of
soap) and describes each ("sweet," "spicy," "clean").

**P8.** Touch pairs: two pebbles — one smooth, one rough — in a bag.
Learner feels each and describes the difference without looking, then
looks to confirm.

**P9.** Observation relay: the adult observes the seedling and says one
observation; the learner must add a *different* observation using a
*different* sense. Three rounds.

**P10.** Silent observation: learner watches the cup of water for 30
seconds, then reports every change or detail noticed (ripples settling,
light reflections).

## Lesson 03 — Tools (Session 6)

**P11.** Lens hunt: with the hand lens, find one detail on each of three
objects (fingertip lines, fabric weave, leaf edge). Describe each.

**P12.** Measure and order: measure the pencil, the crayon, and a pebble
with the ruler. Put them in order from shortest to longest.

**P13.** Fair check: the adult measures the same crayon twice — once
lined up at the end, once starting at the "3" mark. Learner says which
measurement to trust and why (the lined-up one; same start every time).

**P14.** Flashlight shadows: shine the flashlight on a pebble from the
side, then from above. Describe how the shadow changes. (Record with a
quick drawing.)

**P15.** Tool match: the adult names five jobs — "see tiny lines on a
leaf," "find out which stick is longer," "look inside a dark box,"
"count how many pebbles," "hear a quiet sound." Learner picks the right
tool for each (hand lens, ruler, flashlight, tally/recording, ears —
no tool needed).

## Lesson 04 — Recording (Session 8)

**P16.** Tally race: the adult drops pebbles into a cup one at a time
(up to 8); the learner tallies each drop, then reads the total from the
tally.

**P17.** Draw to show: learner draws the seedling so that someone else
could tell it has three leaves and a brown pot — then the adult checks
whether the drawing "answers" those two facts.

**P18.** Words that work: the adult shows a vague record ("it was nice")
and a sharp record ("the pebble was gray and smooth"). Learner says
which one a scientist would keep and why.

**P19.** Record the room: learner records 4 observations of the room
(any mix of words/drawings/tallies) in 5 minutes, then shares one.

**P20.** Fix the record: the adult shows a journal page with 6 tally
marks for "pebbles on the table" but there are 5 pebbles. Learner finds
the error and corrects the tally. (Error-analysis task.)

## Lesson 05 — Safety (Sessions 10, 14)

**P21.** Rule match: the adult describes four actions; the learner holds
up the rule number (1–4) each action follows or breaks.

**P22.** Fix-it cards: three unsafe plans — "taste the soil," "observe
ants by poking them," "do the flashlight test alone in the closet."
Learner names the broken rule and a safe alternative for each.

**P23.** Safety walk: walk through the science area together; the learner
points out where each rule will matter ("the plant — gentle hands,"
"the window — never look at the sun").

**P24.** Teach-back: the learner teaches the four rules to a stuffed
animal, using the gestures from Lesson 05. The adult notes any rule the
learner skips for reteaching.

**P25.** New-plan check: "I will observe which paper towel soaks up the
most water." Learner checks the plan against all four rules and declares
it safe (with a grown-up present), naming the rule that matters most.

## Mixed review (Sessions 13–14)

**P26.** Scavenger hunt: find and record (drawing + tally) — something
smooth, something rough, something you can measure, something living
(observe gently, do not pick), something that makes a sound.

**P27.** Question tournament: the adult reads 8 questions; the learner
sorts each into explorable / wonder list and names the explore-method
for each explorable one.

**P28.** Tool relay: five mystery jobs (see P15 for the format); the
learner grabs the right tool or names the right sense for each.
