# Unit Assessment — Unit 01: Questions, Observations, and Safe Investigations

**Session 16.** Culminating assessment matched to the five unit
objectives. Adult reads all prompts aloud and scribes on request; oral,
pointing, drawing, or dictated responses all count. Solutions and scoring
guidance: [answer-key.md](../answer-key.md#unit-assessment). This is a
curriculum-embedded check, not a standardized or validated test.

**Materials:** a familiar object the learner has not used in the project
(e.g., a different fruit, a shell, a pinecone), hand lens, ruler,
journal.

**Directions to the learner (read aloud):** "You are the scientist today.
Use everything you learned in this unit."

## Tasks

**Task 1 — Ask (Objectives 1, 5).** Look at the object. Ask **two
questions** about it: one you could explore by looking, touching, or
testing, and one that belongs on the wonder list. Tell me which is which
and how you would explore the first one.

**Task 2 — Observe (Objective 2).** Observe the object with **two
different senses** and tell me at least **four** observations. Then use
one tool (hand lens or ruler) and tell me one thing the tool showed you.

**Task 3 — Record (Objective 3).** Record your observations on a journal
page using **at least two** of the three ways (words, drawings, tallies).
Your page must show at least four recorded items.

**Task 4 — Safety check (Objective 4).** Read this plan (adult reads):
*"I will observe the object by myself in another room, and I will taste
a tiny piece to learn more about it."* Is the plan safe? Name **every
rule it breaks** and tell me a **safe alternative** for each broken rule.

**Task 5 — Communicate (Objectives 3, 5).** Show your journal page to the
adult and tell: your explorable question from Task 1, what you did, and
what you found. (This is the sharing-circle telling; the adult notes
whether all three parts are present.)

## Scoring

| Task | 3 — Strong | 2 — Developing | 1 — Starting |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 Ask | Both questions asked; correctly sorted; explore-method named | Sorted correctly with prompting; method vague | Cannot sort or no explorable question |
| 2 Observe | 4+ observations across 2 senses + 1 tool finding | 3 observations or 1 sense only | Fewer than 2 observations |
| 3 Record | 4+ items, 2+ recording ways, page readable back | 3 items or 1 way | Fewer than 2 items |
| 4 Safety | Both broken rules named (1: alone; 2: mouth) + safe alternative each | One rule named; alternative vague | Cannot identify unsafe steps |
| 5 Communicate | Question + method + finding all told clearly | 2 of 3 parts | 1 part or cannot retell |

**Total /15.** 12–15 = meets/exceeds (ready for Unit 02); 9–11 =
approaching — reteach the weak task's lesson, then re-attempt that task
only; below 9 = revisit the unit's lessons before Unit 02. Record results
privately; no learner scores are published in this repository.
