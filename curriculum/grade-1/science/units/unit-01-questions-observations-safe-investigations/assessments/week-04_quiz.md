# Week-04 Formative Quiz — Unit 01

**Session 15.** Adult reads every item aloud; learner responds orally, by
pointing, or by drawing. This quiz is **formative**: its job is to show
which objectives need reteaching before the unit assessment, not to grade.
Solutions: [answer-key.md](../answer-key.md#week-04-quiz).

**Directions to the learner (read aloud):** "Show me what a scientist
does. There are no trick questions — just show what you know."

1. **(Obj. 1)** I will read two questions. Point to the one a scientist
   your age could explore by looking, touching, or testing:
   - a. "What does the seedling's leaf feel like?"
   - b. "What is the seedling dreaming about?"
2. **(Obj. 1)** Ask me one question about this pebble that you could
   explore by looking or touching. (Adult holds up a pebble.)
3. **(Obj. 2)** Use your eyes: tell me two things you observe about this
   apple. Now use your hands: tell me one more thing.
4. **(Obj. 2)** I will drop a pebble in the cup of water. Close your
   eyes and listen. What did your ears observe?
5. **(Obj. 3)** Look at the observation picture from Lesson 04
   ([assets/scientist-observation-table.png](../assets/scientist-observation-table.png)).
   Make **three tally marks** in your journal for three gray pebbles you
   can see. (Adult may use the text-only alternative: read the object
   list aloud.)
6. **(Obj. 3)** Draw one observation of the apple in your journal, then
   tell me what your drawing shows.
7. **(Obj. 4)** Is this safe or unsafe? "Tia tastes the soil to see what
   it is like." If unsafe, what rule does it break, and what could she do
   instead?
8. **(Obj. 4)** Tell me two of the four safety rules. (Adult prompts with
   gestures if needed.)

**Scoring (adult use):** 2 points per item (1 = partial/needed prompting,
0 = not yet) → 16 max. 12+ = ready for the unit assessment; 8–11 =
reteach the missed objectives with [practice.md](../practice.md) sets,
then reassess those items orally; below 8 = revisit the lessons before
the culminating assessment.
