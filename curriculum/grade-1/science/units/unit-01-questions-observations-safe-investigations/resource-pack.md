# Resource Pack — Unit 01: Questions, Observations, and Safe Investigations

**Summary (adult planning judgment):** This pack supports a grade-1 unit
on asking testable questions, observing with senses and simple tools,
recording findings, and following safety rules. All items are adult-
previewed routes; videos supplement teaching and never replace the
hands-on lessons. Checked 2026-10-09.

   https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=5c9ngkyxIdQ (found 2026-10-09 via
   web search; independent kids' channel, not a major educational
   publisher). Senses song with actions — usable as a warm-up **only
   after the adult previews** for ads/comments and confirms the full
   video matches the description. Runtime/captions unverified.
2. **SciShow Kids — official channel search: "senses"** (labeled search
   route, unverified item): the channel publishes NGSS-structured
   episodes for grades 1–3 (corroborated 2026-10-09 via third-party
   listings naming episodes "Your Body's Senses" and "Senses You Didn't
   Know You Have"). Adult searches the official SciShow Kids YouTube
   channel and previews the senses episode before assigning.
3. **PBS LearningMedia — search "five senses"** (labeled search route,
   unverified item): https://www.pbslearningmedia.org/ hosts captioned,
   classroom-vetted clips; adult previews for grade-1 fit.
4. **Crash Course Kids — channel search: "senses" or "observation"**
   (labeled search route, unverified item): short episodes aimed at
   elementary learners; adult previews.

All video use is adult-supervised; offer the offline alternative below
for each core objective if video is unavailable.
