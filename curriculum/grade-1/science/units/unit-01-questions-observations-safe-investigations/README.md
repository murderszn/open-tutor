# Unit 01 — Questions, Observations, and Safe Investigations

**Grade 1 · Science · Weeks 3–6 (4 weeks, 16 sessions, 20–25 minutes each)**

Part of [Grade 1 Science](../../README.md) · [scope-and-sequence](../../scope-and-sequence.md) ·
Issue [#11](https://github.com/murderszn/open-tutor/issues/11). Draft curriculum —
not educator-approved, not aligned/certified by any state.

## Big idea

Scientists find answers by asking questions they can actually explore —
by looking, touching, and testing — and they do it carefully and safely.
This unit builds the habits every later unit depends on: asking a good
question, observing closely, recording what was found, and staying safe.

## Objectives (measurable, adult-assessed)

By the end of this unit the learner will be able to:

1. Ask questions that can be explored by looking, touching, or testing,
   and sort a question into "I can explore this" or "I cannot explore
   this (yet)" (Lesson 01).
2. Make careful observations with the senses and simple tools (hand lens,
   flashlight, ruler), describing at least four observable properties of
   an object (Lessons 02–03).
3. Record observations with words, drawings, or tallies (4–6 recorded
   items per task) and communicate findings orally, by pointing, or by
   drawing (Lesson 04).
4. Follow the four unit safety rules in every investigation, and when
   shown an unsafe plan, name the rule it breaks and a safe alternative
   (Lesson 05).
5. Ask questions and gather information to define a simple problem that
   can be explored (K-2-ETS1-1, at a practice level — the learner's
   investigation plan, Lesson 01 + [project](project.md)).

Oral, pointing, drawing, or adult-scribed responses count as evidence
throughout. The adult reads all prompts aloud; learner independence
grows only as far as writing stamina allows.

## Prerequisites

- From the diagnostic weeks: the learner has handled the science journal,
  practiced "I notice / I wonder" talk, and met the guiding adult's
  turn-taking routine (see scope-and-sequence Weeks 1–2).
- No K content is assumed secure; Unit 01 re-teaches observing and
  questioning from the ground up.

## Vocabulary

Adult introduces each word in context during the lesson; learners are not
expected to spell them.

- **observe** — to notice carefully with your senses
- **observation** — something you noticed
- **question** — something you wonder about
- **explore** — to look, touch, or test to find out
- **testable** — a question you can explore by looking, touching, or testing
- **tool** — something that helps your senses work better
- **hand lens** — a tool that makes small things look bigger
- **record** — to save what you observed (words, drawings, tallies)
- **journal** — the learner's science notebook
- **tally** — a mark you make to count (one mark per thing)
- **compare** — to tell how two things are the same and different
- **safe** — not going to hurt you
- **rule** — something you always do to stay safe
- **supervision** — a grown-up staying with you while you do science
- **living thing** — a plant or animal (handle gently)
- **communicate** — to tell or show what you found

## Materials (whole unit)

A science journal (any notebook), pencils and crayons, a hand lens, a
ruler, a flashlight, an apple or similar fruit, 5–8 smooth pebbles or
buttons, a small potted seedling or leafy houseplant, a paper cup of
water, a mystery object in an opaque bag (Session 4), printed [observation
image](assets/scientist-observation-table.png) or a screen to view it.
All investigations are adult-supervised; see [teacher-guide](teacher-guide.md)
for safety notes.

## 16-session pacing

Sessions are 20–25 minutes. Each names its activity and success criterion.

| # | Session | Success criterion |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | **Lesson 01** — Questions scientists ask | Learner can say what makes a question "explorable." |
| 2 | Practice — question sort game | Sorts 6 read-aloud questions into explorable / not-yet with adult support. |
| 3 | **Lesson 02** — Observing with the senses | Names 2 senses and 1 observation from each. |
| 4 | Practice — mystery-object bag | Gives 3+ sense-based observations before guessing. |
| 5 | **Lesson 03** — Tools help us see more | Matches hand lens → bigger, ruler → length, flashlight → dark. |
| 6 | Practice — tool stations | Measures 3 objects with the ruler; describes 1 lens discovery. |
| 7 | **Lesson 04** — Recording what we observe | Records 5 observations from the observation image (any mix of words/drawings/tallies). |
| 8 | Practice — journal page | Completes one dated journal page with 4–6 recorded items. |
| 9 | **Lesson 05** — Safe investigations | Recites the 4 safety rules with adult prompts. |
| 10 | Practice — safe/unsafe sorts | Sorts 6 scenarios and names a safe alternative for each unsafe one. |
| 11 | **Project** — choose object, ask a testable question, plan | Plan names one explorable question and one safe observation method. |
| 12 | **Project** — carry out observations, record, share | Journal shows 4+ recorded observations; learner communicates findings. |
| 13 | Review — observation scavenger hunt | Finds and records 6 observations around the room/home. |
| 14 | Review — question sorting game + safety review | Sorts 8 questions; restates 4 safety rules. |
| 15 | Formative check — [week-04 quiz](assessments/week-04_quiz.md) | Adult notes which objectives need reteaching. |
| 16 | Culminating — [unit assessment](assessments/unit-assessment.md) + sharing circle | Assessment evidence recorded; learner shares one finding. |

Sessions 11–16 revisit earlier learning (retrieval practice): Session 13
reuses questioning from Session 2, Session 14 reuses tool language from
Session 6, and the assessment asks the learner to apply all five objectives
to one new object.

## Safety and boundaries

Every investigation is adult-supervised. The unit teaches: a grown-up is
always present; science materials stay out of the mouth; living things are
handled gently; never look at the sun (observe shadows or pictures
instead). Observation-only alternatives are given for every hazard.
No learner data, names, or photos are recorded in this repository.
