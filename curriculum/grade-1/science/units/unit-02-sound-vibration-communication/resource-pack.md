# Resource Pack — Unit 02: Sound, Vibration, and Communication

**Summary (adult planning judgment):** This pack supports a grade-1 unit
on noticing and describing sounds, investigating vibration as the cause of
sound, observing that sound moves materials, and designing simple
sound-communication devices. All items are adult-previewed routes; videos
supplement teaching and never replace the hands-on lessons. Checked
2026-10-16.

## Web references (reputable; check dates before reuse)

1. **NIDCD — "How Do We Hear?"** — https://www.nidcd.nih.gov/health/how-do-we-hear
   (opened and read 2026-10-16; NIH institute page; describes the
   ear-canal → eardrum → three middle-ear bones → cochlea → auditory
   nerve path). **Adult background only** — ear anatomy is beyond the
   grade-1 band; never assigned to the learner. Supports the unit's ear-
   safety objective with authoritative wording.
2. **NASA — "Good Vibrations" classroom activity (PDF)** —
   https://www.nasa.gov/wp-content/uploads/2020/04/good_vibrations.pdf?emrc=4091eb
   (found 2026-10-16 via web search; NASA's background text states "all
   sounds are produced by vibrating objects" and the activity uses a rice-
   on-surface movement test — the same phenomenon as Lesson 03). Labeled
   grades 3–8: **adult planning background only**; the grade-1 lessons
   stay at the observe-and-compare level.
3. **NASA Space Place** — https://spaceplace.nasa.gov/ (repo-vetted shelf
   item; NASA's kids' site). Search within for sound/vibration explainers.
   Free, no account. Intended audience: elementary — adult confirms grade-1
   fit per item.
4. **Smithsonian Learning Lab** — https://learninglab.si.edu/ (repo-vetted
   shelf item). Search for sound/instrument collections. Free, no account
   for viewing — adult confirms per item.
5. **Internal:** [resources/physics_fundamentals.md](../../../../../resources/physics_fundamentals.md)
   — **adult background only** (grades 4–8 wave vocabulary); never assigned
   to the learner. The learner's language stays at "shake fast," "wiggle
   lines," "loud/soft."
