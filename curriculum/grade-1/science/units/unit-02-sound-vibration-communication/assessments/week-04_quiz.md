# Week-04 Quiz — Unit 02 (formative)

**Session 15 · Adult reads every item aloud · Oral, pointing, or drawing
answers · ~15 minutes**

This quiz is formative: it tells the adult what to reteach in Session 16,
not a grade. Record which objectives (O1–O5 from the unit README) each
item checks.

1. **(O1)** I will make two sounds: a *loud clap* and a *soft whisper*.
   "Which was loud? Which was soft?" Then: "Make one high sound and one
   low sound yourself."
2. **(O1)** Point to the picture cards (adult draws: bell, drum, bird,
   whisper): "Which two make HIGH sounds? Which make LOW sounds?"
3. **(O2)** The adult twangs the rubber band. "What did you SEE the band
   do? What did you HEAR at the same time?" (Looking for: it shook/blurred
   AND it made a sound.)
4. **(O2)** "Finish my sentence: vibrating things make ___." (sound)
5. **(O3)** The adult strikes the drum softly, then loudly, with rice on
   the head. "What did the rice do on the LOUD strike?" (hopped/jumped)
   "What does that tell us about loud sounds?" (they move things more)
6. **(O4)** Show the telephone image (assets/cup-string-telephone.png):
   "Point to the part that carries the message from one cup to the
   other." (the string)
7. **(O4)** "Your friend is across the room and cannot hear your whisper.
   Name one device you could build to get the message to them."
   (telephone, bell code, shaker signal — any built sound-carrier)
8. **(O5 — safety)** "I am about to bang this pot right next to your ear.
   What should I do instead?" (Bang it softly / move it away / cover
   ears.) "Where does the string go — around a neck or in our hands?"
   (hands)

**Reteach map:** items 1–2 → Lesson 01; 3–4 → Lesson 02; 5 → Lesson 03;
6–7 → Lessons 04–05; 8 → any lesson's safety moment. If O4 items are weak,
re-run the Session 8 telephone relay before the unit assessment.
