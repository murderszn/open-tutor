# Unit Assessment — Unit 02: Sound, Vibration, and Communication

**Session 16 · Adult reads every item aloud · Oral, pointing, drawing, or
adult-scribed answers · ~20 minutes**

Matched to the unit's five objectives. Every item has a worked solution in
[answer-key.md](../answer-key.md#unit-assessment). Do not present this
assessment as standardized or validated.

## Part A — What I know (objectives 1–3)

1. **Listen and describe.** The adult plays 3 sounds (bell, book tap,
   shaker). For each, the learner says **loud or soft** and **high or
   low**. (O1 — 6 labels, 1 point each)
2. **Show the rule.** The learner twangs the rubber band over the box and
   explains to a stuffed animal "teacher": what the band *did* and what
   the learner *heard*, and the rule that connects them. (O2 — must link
   shake → sound)
3. **Predict and test.** "I will strike the drum LOUDLY. Before I strike,
   predict: will the rice tremble or hop?" The learner predicts, the adult
   strikes, and the learner says whether the prediction was right and what
   it shows. (O3 — prediction + evidence reading)

## Part B — Build and communicate (objective 4)

4. **The challenge.** "Your grown-up is at the far end of the room. Send
   them the message 'story time' using the cup-and-string telephone you
   built." The learner assembles (with adult help on knots), checks the
   string is tight, sends the message, and the receiver repeats it back.
   (O4 — message arrives = full marks; message fails but the learner
   diagnoses "the string was saggy" and fixes it = full marks)
5. **Compare.** "Which worked better for sending a message far — the
   telephone or the bell? Tell me one reason, using what happened in your
   tests." (O4 / K-2-ETS1-3 — reason must cite an observed test result,
   not "it looks cooler")

## Part C — Stay safe (objective 5)

6. **Spot the unsafe plan.** The adult reads two plans; the learner picks
   the unsafe one and fixes it:
   - (a) "We test the bell with one gentle ring, standing an arm's length
     away."
   - (b) "We bang the pot as loud as we can right next to our ears to
     see if the rice jumps higher."
   (O5 — picks (b), names ear protection, proposes the safe version)
7. **String rule.** "Show me with the string: where may it go, and where
   may it never go?" (O5 — in hands / never around a neck)

## Scoring

| Points | Evidence |
|---|---|
| 13–15 | Strong: links shake→sound unprompted; predictions match evidence; message sent and compared with test data; safety rules stated and applied |
| 9–12 | Growing: needs one prompt per part; reteach the flagged objective in the Session 16 sharing circle with a 5-minute redo |
| Below 9 | Rebuild: re-run Lessons 02–04 core demos as a reteach block before moving to Unit 03; keep the journal work as the record |

**Readiness signal:** the learner may move to Unit 03 (light) when they
can state the vibration rule, run the rice test from memory, and send a
message through a sound device while following the safety rules.
