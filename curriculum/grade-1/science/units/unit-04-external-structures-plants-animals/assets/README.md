## Text-only alternative

For learners who cannot use the picture. It carries every part and job
the activities ask about.

> A sunflower plant grows on the left. Below the ground line, in brown
> soil, are its ROOTS: white, branching, spreading strings. Roots DRINK
> water and HOLD the plant steady. Above the soil is a tall green STEM.
> The stem HOLDS the plant up and CARRIES water upward. Two big green
> LEAVES grow off the stem. Leaves CATCH sunlight and help the plant
> MAKE food. At the top is a large yellow FLOWER. The flower MAKES
> seeds.
>
> On the right stands a brown duck on green grass. Its BEAK is flat and
> yellow-orange: it SCOOPS food from water. Its EYES help it SEE food
> and danger. Its WING folds on its side: wings help birds FLY and also
> cover and warm the body. Its TAIL is the short pointy feathers at the
> back. Its FEET are orange and WEBBED, with skin between the toes like
> paddles: webbed feet help the duck SWIM.
>
> In a small blue pond at the lower right swims an orange fish. Its FINS
> are flat parts on its side and back: fins help it SWIM and STEER. Its
> TAIL fin at the back helps it push and steer. Its EYE helps it see.
>
> Remember: in real life you cannot see roots without digging, because
> they live underground. This picture cuts the soil open so you can see
> them. And the duck's ears are hidden under its feathers — hidden does
> not mean missing; the duck can still hear.
