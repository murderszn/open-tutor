# Unit Assessment — Unit 04

**Unit:** [U04](../README.md) · **Session 16** · Adult reads every task
aloud. The learner responds orally, by pointing, by drawing, or by
demonstrating. No answers appear in this file; see the
[answer key](../answer-key.md) and the [teacher guide](../teacher-guide.md)
for scoring guidance.

## Task 1 — Plant parts and jobs (Objectives 1, 6)

The adult shows the real potted plant (or the generated illustration)
and asks:

1. Name the four outside parts of this plant.
2. For each part, say what it does for the plant.
3. Safety question: what must you never do with a plant part, and what
   do you do after touching soil? (Do not taste it; wash hands.)

## Task 2 — Animal parts and jobs (Objectives 2, 6)

The adult shows two toy animals or picture cards (for example, a duck
and a fish) and asks:

1. For each animal, name two outside parts.
2. For each part named, say what it does for the animal.
3. Safety question: what do you do when you see a wild animal — touch
   it, or watch it from a distance? (Watch from a distance.)

## Task 3 — Part-to-need matching (Objective 3)

The adult lays out six part cards (roots, leaves, webbed feet, long
beak, rabbit ears, turtle shell) and four need cards (FOOD, WATER,
MOVING, STAYING SAFE). The learner matches each part card to a need
card and gives a reason for each match.

## Task 4 — Show your nature-tool (Objectives 4, 5)

The learner shows their project: the sketch, version 1, version 2, and
the comparison table, and answers:

1. What human problem does your tool solve?
2. What plant or animal part does it copy, and what does that part do?
3. Which version worked better, and how do you know from your tests?
4. What would you change next, and why?

## Scoring guidance (adult use)

Record one level per objective. "Secure" = correct with at most one
adult hint; "developing" = correct with repeated hints; "not yet" =
incorrect or no answer even with hints.

| Objective | Secure | Developing | Not yet |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 — Plant parts and jobs | Names all 4 parts with correct jobs. | Names 3 parts, or jobs partly correct. | Names 0–2 parts, or jobs mostly wrong. |
| 2 — Animal parts and jobs | Names 2 parts per animal (4 total) with correct jobs. | Names 3 parts total, or jobs partly correct. | Names 0–2 parts, or jobs mostly wrong. |
| 3 — Part-to-need matching | Matches at least 5 of 6 with reasons. | Matches 3–4, or matches without reasons. | Matches 0–2. |
| 4 — Design from a sketch | Problem, copied part, and sketch all present and connected. | One element missing or disconnected. | No sketch, or design unrelated to the problem. |
| 5 — Test and compare | Winner chosen by counting three-try results, with a reason. | Comparison attempted but unfair or decided by liking. | No comparison, or results not from testing. |
| 6 — Living-thing safety | Both safety questions correct without prompting. | One correct, or correct with a hint. | Neither correct even with hints. |

**Readiness:** 5–6 objectives at Secure = ready for Unit 05. 3–4 at
Secure = ready with targeted re-teaching of the Developing objectives
during the flexible weeks (see [teacher guide](../teacher-guide.md)).
Fewer than 3 at Secure = re-teach Lessons 01–03 and the design cycle
before starting Unit 05; use the diagnostic/catch-up weeks, not speed.

This assessment is a classroom check, not a standardized or validated
test. Results stay in private storage — never in this repository.
