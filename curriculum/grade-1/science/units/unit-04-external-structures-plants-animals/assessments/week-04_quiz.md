# Formative Quiz — Unit 04 (Week 4)

**Unit:** [U04](../README.md) · **Session 15** · Adult reads every
question aloud. The learner answers orally, by pointing, or by drawing.
No answers appear in this file; see the [answer key](../answer-key.md).

## Part 1 — Say, point, or draw (6 items)

1. Point to the roots on the picture (or the real plant). What job do
   roots do?
2. Point to the stem. What job does the stem do?
3. Point to a leaf. What job do leaves do?
4. Point to the flower. What job does the flower do?
5. Look at the duck in the picture. Name one part that helps it swim,
   and say how.
6. Look at the fish in the picture. Name one part that helps it swim,
   and say how.

## Part 2 — Recording task (4 items)

Draw in the journal (adult labels from dictation):

1. Draw a plant with roots, stem, leaves, and a flower — each part in
   the right place.
2. Draw a duck's webbed foot, and write (adult-scribed) one sentence
   about what it does.
3. Draw a tool you would design to pick up tiny seeds. Circle the part
   that copies a bird's beak.
4. Draw one thing a plant needs and one thing an animal needs
   (for example, water for the plant, food for the animal).

## Scoring (adult use)

- Part 1: 1 point per item for a correct part **and** a correct job
  (5–6 = secure; 3–4 = nearly secure, re-teach the missed parts in
  Session 16's warm-up; 0–2 = re-teach Lessons 01–02 before the unit
  assessment).
- Part 2: 1 point per drawing for the right parts in the right places
  with a correct job or copy (3–4 = secure; 2 = nearly secure;
  0–1 = re-teach before assessment).
- A learner who answers orally or by pointing, with the adult scribing,
  earns full credit — writing independently is never required.
