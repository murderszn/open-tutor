# Resource Pack — Unit 04: External Structures of Plants and Animals

**Summary (adult planning judgment):** This pack supports a grade-1
unit on naming plant parts (roots, stem, leaves, flower) and animal
parts (legs/feet, ears, eyes, beak, fins, tail, wings), matching parts
to needs (food, water, moving, staying safe), and designing a tool that
mimics a plant or animal part. All items are adult-previewed routes;
videos supplement teaching and never replace the hands-on lessons.
Checked 2026-10-29.

## Access notes

- **Free, no-account route for every core objective:** plant and
  animal observation, the celery test, the beak-tools test, the
  matching games, and the nature-tool build are offline, hands-on
  investigations using household materials — no video or account is
  required to learn any objective. The NSTA, NPS, and Wikibooks pages
  are free with no account.
- **Videos supplement, never replace:** if YouTube is unavailable, skip
  the video items; the lessons, picture investigation, and project are
  complete without them.
- **Adult preview required:** all video items (ads, comments, length,
  captions) and any Smithsonian Learning Lab or NASA Space Place item
  before showing.
