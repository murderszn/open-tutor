# Resource Pack — Unit 02: Addition Strategies Within 20

**Summary (adult planning judgment):** First-grade addition fluency grows
from strategies, not memorization drills: count on from the bigger number,
turn addends around, make a ten, and anchor on doubles. The resources below
support the unit's four objectives — counting on, structure strategies,
missing addends, and the equal sign. All external links were checked
2026-10-16 unless labeled otherwise; unverified items are labeled as open
search links or carried references. Adult previews everything before showing.
