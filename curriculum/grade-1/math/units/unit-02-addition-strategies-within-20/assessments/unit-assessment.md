# Unit Assessment — Unit 02: Addition Strategies Within 20

**Culminating assessment** for the four unit objectives. Oral, pointing,
drawing, or manipulative responses are all valid; the adult scribes written
work when the learner isn't ready. Scored with the teacher guide's rubric.
Not a standardized or validated assessment.

## Task 1 — Count on (Objective 1)

Solve by counting on from the bigger number; say each hop aloud.

a. 8 + 4 = ___ (Expected: 12 — "9, 10, 11, 12")
b. 3 + 9 = ___ (Expected: 12 — start at 9: "10, 11, 12")

## Task 2 — Turn-around pair (Objective 2)

a. Build or draw 5 + 7 with two colors, then show its turn-around. Write or
   dictate both equations. (Expected: 5 + 7 = 12 and 7 + 5 = 12.)
b. Explain why both have the same total. (Expected: nothing was added or
   taken away — only the order changed.)

## Task 3 — Make ten (Objective 2)

a. 9 + 5: show the make-ten move on ten frames and write the rewrite.
   (Expected: borrow 1 → 10 + 4 = 14.)
b. Look at the unit illustration
   [`assets/make-ten-ten-frames.png`](../assets/make-ten-ten-frames.png):
   "What addition does it show, and what does the arrow do?" (Expected:
   9 + 4; the arrow moves one dot to complete the ten, making 10 + 3 = 13.)

## Task 4 — Doubles and near-doubles (Objective 2)

a. 7 + 7 = ___ (Expected: 14)
b. 7 + 8 = ___ — name the double you used. (Expected: 15, from 7 + 7 = 14,
   one more)

## Task 5 — Missing addend (Objective 3)

a. 6 + ? = 13 (Expected: 7 — count up: 7, 8, 9, 10, 11, 12, 13)
b. ? + 5 = 11 (Expected: 6)

## Task 6 — True or false (Objective 4)

For each, compute or reason about both sides, then judge:

a. 5 + 2 = 2 + 5 (Expected: true — turn-around pair, 7 = 7)
b. 9 = 4 + 5 (Expected: true — 9 = 9)
c. 6 + 4 = 8 + 3 (Expected: false — 10 ≠ 11)
d. What does the equal sign mean? (Expected: "is the same amount as" —
   both sides balance)

## Scoring (adult)

| Objective | Secure (2) | Developing (1) | Not yet (0) |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 — Count on | Both 1a, 1b correct with hop counts | One correct, or hop-count slip | Cannot start at the bigger number |
| 2 — Strategies | Tasks 2–4 all correct with explanations | 2 of 3 tasks correct | 1 or fewer |
| 3 — Missing addend | Both 5a, 5b correct | One correct | Neither |
| 4 — Equal sign | 6a–6c correct and `=` explained | 2 of 3 judged right | `=` read as "write the answer" |

**7–8 points:** ready for Unit 03. **4–6:** targeted re-teach on missed
objectives, then re-check those tasks only. **0–3:** revisit the unit's
practice sessions before Unit 03; Unit 03's unknown-addend work depends on
Objectives 1 and 3.
