# Formative Quiz — Unit 02, Week 10 (Session 16)

**Purpose:** check each of the four unit objectives after the review week;
oral/pointing/drawing responses welcome; the adult records secure /
developing / not yet per objective. Not a standardized or validated
assessment.

## Strategy stations (20 minutes, learner rotates)

**Station 1 — Count on (Objective 1).** The adult shows `7 + 3` and `4 + 9`.
The learner starts at the bigger number and counts on aloud. (Expected: 10;
13.)

**Station 2 — Make ten (Objective 2).** Ten frame with 8 dots plus 5 loose
counters. "Make a ten and tell me the new addition." (Expected: borrow 2 →
10 + 3 = 13.)

**Station 3 — Doubles (Objective 2).** `6 + 6 = ?` then `6 + 7 = ?`.
(Expected: 12, then 13 via the double.)

**Station 4 — Equal sign (Objectives 3–4).** `5 + ? = 12` and the true/false
card `8 + 2 = 9 + 1`. (Expected: ? = 7; true — both sides are 10.)

## Written check (5 short tasks; adult scribes if needed)

1. 9 + 4 = ___ (any strategy; expected 13)
2. Write the turn-around of 3 + 8 and its total. (Expected: 8 + 3 = 11)
3. 8 + ? = 11 (Expected: 3)
4. True or false: 7 + 7 = 15 (Expected: false — 7 + 7 = 14)
5. True or false: 4 + 3 = 3 + 4 (Expected: true)

**Ready to move on:** secure on at least 3 of 4 objectives, with Objective 1
(count on) secure. **Re-teach triggers:** hop-count errors → Session A;
make-ten borrow errors → Session F; `=` as "write the answer" → balance demo
in Lesson 05; missing-addend counting includes the start → Lesson 01 fix.
