# Answer Key — Unit 02: Addition Strategies Within 20

Adult-only. Every solution below was worked independently of the lesson text
and reconciled against it. Keep separate from learner materials.

## Lesson 01

- Guided 1: 6 + 2 → start at 6, count "7, 8" → **8**.
- Guided 2: 8 + 3 → start at 8, count "9, 10, 11" → **11**.
- Guided 3: 4 + 9 → start at **9** (bigger), count "10, 11, 12, 13" → **13**.
- Guided 4: 5 + 5 → start at 5, count "6, 7, 8, 9, 10" → **10**.
- Guided 5 (error analysis): the adult counted "9, 10" for 9 + 2 and
  announced 10 — wrong, because 9 was already counted. Only the 2 new hops
  count: "10, 11" → **11**.
- Exit check: 9 + 2 → start at 9, count on 2 → "10, 11" → **11**.

## Lesson 02

- Guided 1: 6 + 4 flipped → 4 + 6 = **10**.
- Guided 2: 3 + 7 → easier as 7 + 3 → "8, 9, 10" → **10**.
- Guided 3: 5 + 8 = 8 + 5 → **true** (both 13).
- Guided 4: 2 + 6 and 6 + 2 both make **8**.
- Guided 5 (error analysis): "7 + 4 is bigger than 4 + 7" is **wrong** —
  turn-arounds; both equal **11**. Order never changes the total.
- Exit check: 6 + 8 → turn around to 8 + 6 → count on from 8:
  "9, 10, 11, 12, 13, 14" → **14**.

## Lesson 03

- Guided 1: 9 + 3 → 9 needs 1 → move 1 → 10 + 2 = **12**.
- Guided 2: 8 + 4 → borrow 2 → 10 + 2 = **12**.
- Guided 3: 7 + 5 → either fill gives 10 + 2 = **12**; filling the 7 is the
  efficient choice.
- Guided 4 (picture): without moving, 9 + 4 = **13**; with the arrow's move,
  10 + 3 = **13**.
- Guided 5 (error analysis): moving 2 dots for 9 + 4 is wrong — 9 needed
  only 1. The miscount gives 10 + 2 = 12, but only 12 dots are accounted
  for; the true total is **13**. Move one dot back.
- Guided 6: 9 + 6 → borrow 1 → 10 + 5 = **15**.
- Exit check: 7 + 4 → 7 needs 3 → borrow 3 → 10 + 1 = **11**.

## Lesson 04

- Guided 1: 7 + 7 = **14**.
- Guided 2: 7 + 8 → anchor 7 + 7 = 14, one more → **15**.
- Guided 3: 9 + 9 = **18**; 9 + 10 = **19**.
- Guided 4: 4 + 5 → anchor 4 + 4 = 8, one more → **9**.
- Guided 5 (error analysis): "6 + 6 = 12, one more is 14" is wrong — one
  more than 12 is **13**.
- Exit check: 8 + 7 → anchor the smaller double 7 + 7 = 14, one more →
  **15**.

## Lesson 05

- Guided 1: 5 + ? = 13 → count up 6, 7, 8, 9, 10, 11, 12, 13 (8 hops) →
  **8**.
- Guided 2: 6 + 6 = _ → **12**.
- Guided 3: _ + 4 = 12 → count up from 4: 5…12 (8 hops) → **8**.
- Guided 4: 6 = 6 → **true**.
- Guided 5: 9 = 4 + 5 → **true** (4 + 5 = 9; the "answer" may sit on the
  left).
- Guided 6 (error analysis): reading 4 + 1 = 5 + 2 as true from the left
  side alone is wrong. Right side: 5 + 2 = 7; 5 ≠ 7 → **false**.
- Exit check: 9 + ? = 14 → count up 10, 11, 12, 13, 14 (5 hops) → **5**;
  8 + 2 = 9 + 1 → both sides 10 → **true**.

## Week-04 quiz

- Stations: 7 + 3 → **10**; 4 + 9 → **13**; make-ten 8 + 5 → 10 + 3 =
  **13**; 6 + 6 = **12**, 6 + 7 = **13**; 5 + ? = 12 → **7**;
  8 + 2 = 9 + 1 → **true**.
- Written: 1. **13** · 2. 8 + 3 = **11** · 3. **3** · 4. **false**
  (7 + 7 = 14) · 5. **true**.

## Unit assessment

- Task 1: a. **12** ("9, 10, 11, 12") · b. **12** (start at 9: "10, 11, 12").
- Task 2: a. 5 + 7 = **12** and 7 + 5 = **12** · b. nothing added or removed
  — only the order changed.
- Task 3: a. 9 + 5 → borrow 1 → 10 + 4 = **14** · b. the picture shows
  **9 + 4**; the arrow moves one dot to complete the ten → 10 + 3 = **13**.
- Task 4: a. **14** · b. **15** (from 7 + 7 = 14, one more).
- Task 5: a. **7** (count up: 7, 8, 9, 10, 11, 12, 13) · b. **6**.
- Task 6: a. **true** (7 = 7) · b. **true** (9 = 9) · c. **false**
  (10 ≠ 11) · d. the equal sign means **"is the same amount as."**

## Project rubric — scoring guidance

- **Strong:** all page examples compute correctly; explanations in the
  learner's own words name the strategy's mechanism ("I borrowed 1 to fill
  the ten"); equal-sign page labels true/false correctly.
- **Developing:** 1–2 pages need adult correction; explanations describe
  steps but not reasons — re-ask "why does it work?" for those pages.
- **Starting:** learner rebuilds the weak pages with counters before the
  showcase; this is normal and not a failure — the showcase then becomes
  the re-teach.

## Practice sessions — expected outcomes

- **A:** dice sums solved by bigger-start counting on; hop counts match the
  smaller addend.
- **B:** turn-around pairs verified with counters; learner picks the easier
  order and says why.
- **C:** floor-line landings match written totals for 8 + 3 (**11**),
  5 + 9 (**14**), 6 + 6 (**12**), 4 + 7 (**11**).
- **E:** station choices justified by hop count ("count-on corner was
  fastest because only 2 hops").
- **F:** make-ten borrows equal the empty-box count; totals unchanged after
  moving (picture study: 1 dot moves, 10 + 3 = **13**).
- **G:** doubles to 10 + 10 = **20** instant; near-doubles via smaller
  anchor (8 + 8 = **16** → 8 + 9 = **17**).
- **H:** sort results — TRUE: 6 = 6, 5 + 2 = 2 + 5, 9 = 4 + 5, 10 = 10,
  6 + 4 = 9 + 1; FALSE: 7 + 7 = 15, 8 + 3 = 12, 4 + 1 = 5 + 2. False cards
  fixed by changing one number (e.g., 7 + 7 = **14**).
