# Teacher Guide — Unit 04: Story Problems

**For the guiding adult only.** Keep this file and
[answer-key.md](answer-key.md) separate from the learner's pages.

## How this unit differs from Units 02–03

Units 02–03 taught computation. This unit teaches modeling, and the
adult's job changes with it: do not correct arithmetic first. When an
answer is wrong, ask "show me the story" before "check the math." Most
errors in this unit are modeling errors — right arithmetic, wrong
operation — and re-drilling facts will not fix them. Re-act the story.

## Session-by-session notes

- **Sessions 1–4 (Week 15, Lesson 01):** insist on counters before
  equations for the whole week. If the learner writes an equation
  first, cover it, act the story, and compare. The story-relay game
  (Session 3) is diagnostic: watch for learners who call the equation
  from the numbers without acting.
- **Sessions 5–8 (Week 16, Lesson 02):** the word "some" is the
  lesson's handle — teach the learner to point at it and say "that's
  my `?`." Start-unknown take-from stories (? − 3 = 12) are the
  hardest item type in the unit; expect them to take two sessions.
  The story-map diagram (start → change → result) stays on the table.
- **Sessions 9–12 (Week 17, Lessons 03–04):** compare stories need
  matching, not memorized rules. Never say "more means add" — Lessons
  03's rounds 2 and 3 exist to break that rule in the open. For
  three-addend stories, accept any correct grouping; praise the
  ten-pair grouping without requiring it.
- **Sessions 13–16 (Week 18, Lesson 05 + review):** equation-fit
  judgment is new, so model both questions aloud ("does it fit? is
  it true?") for the first three rounds of Session 14. Session 16's
  quiz is formative; the culminating assessment follows in the
  flexible review block. Use the remaining Week 18 time for the
  midyear Units 01–04 stations (counting, strategy flash, story sort)
  and for re-teaching the quiz's weakest story type.

## Misconceptions and reteaching

| What you see | What it usually means | Reteach with |
|---|---|---|
| Adds the two visible numbers in every story | Key-word or number-grabbing habit; story never acted out | Lesson 01 act-it-out routine; cover the equation until the acting is done |
| Correct arithmetic, wrong operation in compare stories | "More means add" rule | Lesson 03 matching rows; rounds 2 vs 3 side by side (same words, different unknowns) |
| Start-unknown take-from solved by subtraction (? − 3 = 12 → 9) | Treating the start as a result | Counters: build the result (12), put the 3 taken ones back — the start must be bigger than what is left |
| Three-addend answers missing the third group | Stopped after making ten | "How many groups have we counted?" after every first step; write both steps |
| Accepts true arithmetic as a fitting equation | Fit check skipped | Lesson 05 two-question routine, fit first; act the story, then compare actions |
| Calls a fitting equation true without computing | True/false check skipped | Balance routine: compute both sides, then compare |

## Readiness: what "ready for Unit 05" looks like

- Retells any add-to/take-from story with counters and writes the
  matching equation without prompting.
- Names the hidden part in start/change-unknown stories and checks
  the answer forward.
- Solves compare stories by matching (or by an explained subtraction)
  and can explain why "more" sometimes subtracts.
- Groups a ten pair in three-addend stories and shows both steps.
- Judges fit and truth separately and writes a story whose action,
  numbers, and answer all match its equation.

If two or more of these are not yet secure after Session 16, use the
Week 18 flexible block for re-teaching rather than starting Unit 05
on schedule — Unit 05's two-digit work assumes this modeling is
secure.

## Accessibility and language notes

- Every check in this unit accepts oral, pointing, drawing, or
  manipulative responses; the adult scribes written work when the
  learner isn't ready to write.
- Vocabulary is taught with objects first: "joins," "leaves,"
  "matches," and "the rest" are acted before they are named.
  Home-language labels for these actions are welcomed alongside the
  English terms.
- The generated illustration ships with alt text, a caption, and a
  text-only alternative (Lesson 03); color is never the only cue —
  the red/green groups are also separated by position (basket vs.
  pile) and counted aloud.
- Sessions stay 20–25 minutes with a movement break between sessions;
  the floor-play variants in each lesson are full alternatives, not
  extras.
