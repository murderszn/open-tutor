# Unit Assessment — Unit 04: Story Problems (culminating)

**Administered by the adult in the Week 18 flexible review block after
Session 16 · Oral/pointing/drawing responses welcome; adult scribes
when needed. Answers:** [answer-key.md](../answer-key.md#unit-assessment).
**Not a standardized or validated test; not timed.**

Answer by acting each story out with counters first, then writing (or
dictating) the equation. Name the story type or the hidden part before
solving.

1. **Add-to, result unknown.** "Sara has 7 hair clips. She buys 8
   more. How many hair clips does Sara have now?"
2. **Take-from, result unknown.** "There are 18 fish in a pond. 9 swim
   away. How many fish are left?"
3. **Start unknown, add-to.** "Ana had some crayons. She got 6 more.
   Now she has 16. How many crayons did Ana have at the start?"
4. **Change unknown, take-from.** "There were 14 cookies on a plate.
   Some were eaten. Now there are 5 left. How many cookies were
   eaten?"
5. **Put-together, total unknown.** "There are 8 red balls and 7 blue
   balls in a box. How many balls are in the box in all?"
6. **Compare, smaller unknown.** "Ben has 17 marbles. He has 6 more
   marbles than Ana. How many marbles does Ana have?"
7. **Compare, difference unknown.** "There are 19 red cups and 13 blue
   cups. How many more red cups are there than blue cups?"
8. **Three addends.** "A bead string has 3 red beads, 8 blue beads,
   and 7 yellow beads. How many beads are there in all? Show the pair
   you added first."
9. **Equation fit.** Story: "There are 9 pens. Mia gives away 6 pens."
   Which equation fits: 9 + 6 = 15 or 9 − 6 = 3? Explain in one
   sentence why the other one does not fit.
10. **Write and judge.** (a) Write a story for 14 − 5 = 9. (b) Is
    8 + 9 = 16 true or false? Explain in one sentence.

**Scoring guidance:** 9–10 secure = ready for Unit 05; 6–8 = re-teach
the missed story types with targeted practice sessions, then re-check
those items only; 0–5 = re-run the unit's lessons for the missed types
with counters before Unit 05, and use the midyear review week for that
re-teaching. The project rubric in [project.md](../project.md) is
scored separately and is not averaged into this assessment.
