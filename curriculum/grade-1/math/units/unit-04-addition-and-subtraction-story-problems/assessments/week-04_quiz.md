# Week-04 Quiz — Unit 04: Story Problems (formative)

**Session 16 of 16 · Week 18 (midyear review week) · Adult-administered,
oral/pointing/drawing responses welcome; adult scribes when needed.**
**Answers:** [answer-key.md](../answer-key.md#week-04-quiz). No timed
scoring; each item is secure / developing / not yet.

Answer by acting the story out with counters first, then writing (or
dictating) the equation. For each item, name the story type or the
hidden part before solving.

1. **Add-to, result unknown.** "Mia has 9 stickers. Her friend gives
   her 5 more. How many stickers does Mia have now?"
2. **Take-from, result unknown.** "There are 13 birds on a wire. 7 fly
   away. How many birds are left?"
3. **Start unknown.** "Sam had some marbles. He found 4 more. Now he
   has 12. How many marbles did Sam have at the start?"
4. **Change unknown.** "There were 15 apples in a basket. Some were
   eaten. Now there are 9 left. How many apples were eaten?"
5. **Take-apart, addend unknown.** "A box has 17 crayons. 9 are red.
   The rest are blue. How many blue crayons are in the box?"
6. **Compare, difference unknown.** "Ben has 16 toy cars. Ana has 9
   toy cars. How many more toy cars does Ben have than Ana?"
7. **Three addends.** "A fruit bowl has 5 apples, 6 oranges, and 4
   bananas. How many pieces of fruit are in the bowl in all? Show the
   pair you added first."
8. **Equation judgment.** (a) Is 12 − 5 = 7 true or false? (b) Story:
   "There are 11 apples. 4 are eaten." Does the equation 11 − 4 = 7
   fit the story? Explain both answers in one sentence each.

**Scoring guidance:** 7–8 secure = ready to move on (with the Week 18
midyear stations for Units 01–03 as review); 4–6 = re-teach the missed
story types with one practice session each before Unit 05; 0–3 = re-run
Lessons 01–03 with counters and re-quiz. A wrong operation with right
arithmetic is a modeling miss — re-teach the story type, not the fact.
