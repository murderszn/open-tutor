# Project — My Story Problem Book

**Sessions:** 9, 12, 14, 15 (about 20–25 minutes each, inside those
sessions) · **Unit:** [U04](README.md)

## What the learner makes

A four-page story problem book, written and illustrated by the learner,
with a cover and a teach-a-friend page. Each page holds one original
story: the story in words (written by the learner or dictated for the
adult to scribe), a drawing or counter layout, the equation, and the
answer with its check. The book is the unit's evidence that the learner
can *build* stories, not only solve them.

## Materials

- Paper (folded into a book) or a stapled packet, crayons, counters
- The lessons' story cards for models (not to copy — the learner's
  stories must be their own)
- The generated illustration
  [`assets/apple-basket-story.png`](assets/apple-basket-story.png)
  as a model of "one picture, several questions" (Session 9)

## Part 1 — Add-to and take-from pages (Session 9)

Write two stories:

- Page 1: an add-to story with a result unknown (numbers within 20).
- Page 2: a take-from story with a result unknown (numbers within 20).

Each page needs: the story (2–3 sentences), a drawing of the start and
the change, the equation, and the answer. The adult checks: does the
action match the operation, and do the numbers in the story match the
numbers in the equation?

## Part 2 — Put-together/compare page (Session 12)

Write one story that is either put-together/take-apart or compare.
The learner must name the type on the page ("This is a compare story")
and show the matching: two rows of counters or drawings, matched one
to one, with the leftover circled for a compare story; two parts
pushed into a whole for a put-together story.

## Part 3 — Three-addend page and cover (Session 14)

Write one three-addend story (sum ≤ 20) and show the grouping: circle
the pair added first, then add the third number. Then make the cover:
a title ("My Story Problem Book"), one picture, and the author's mark
(a drawing or symbol the learner chooses — never a real name, school,
or photograph in this public repository's copy; keep the learner's own
book at home).

## Part 4 — Teach-a-friend showcase (Session 15)

The learner reads one page aloud to the adult (or to a willing family
member), acts it out with counters, and explains how they know the
equation fits. Then they answer one question the listener asks about
it — "what if the start were hidden instead?" is a good one. The adult
records which page was showcased and whether the explanation named the
action, the hidden part (if any), and the check.

## Deliverables

1. The book: cover + 4 story pages (add-to, take-from,
   put-together/compare, three-addend), each with story, drawing,
   equation, answer, and check.
2. The showcase: one page read aloud and explained, with the
   listener's question answered.

## Rubric (adult-observed; each criterion 0–2)

| Criterion | 2 — Strong | 1 — Developing | 0 — Not yet |
|---|---|---|---|
| Story modeling (each page) | Action, numbers, and equation agree; the story type is named correctly | Action and equation mostly agree; one number or the type label is off | Equation contradicts the story's action, or numbers do not match |
| Computation and checking | Every answer is correct and checked a second way | Answers mostly correct; some checks missing | Answers incorrect or unchecked |
| Three-addend grouping | A ten pair (or doubles) is circled and both steps are shown | Grouping is shown but the steps are incomplete | Numbers added with no grouping shown |
| Showcase explanation | Names the action, the unknown (if any), and the check without prompting | Names two of the three with a prompt | Cannot explain why the equation fits |

**Scoring guidance:** 7–8 = ready to move on; 4–6 = re-teach the
weakest story type with one more practice session, then re-showcase;
0–3 = re-run Lessons 01–03 with counters before the Week 18 review.
The book itself is never graded on handwriting or drawing quality.
