# Resource Pack — Unit 04: Addition and Subtraction Story Problems

**Summary (adult planning judgment):** First-grade story problems are
won or lost on modeling, not computing: act the story out, name which
part is hidden, and only then choose an operation. The resources below
support the unit's five objectives — result-unknown stories,
start/change-unknown stories, put-together/take-apart and compare
stories, three-addend stories, and judging whether an equation fits.
All external links were checked 2026-10-29 unless labeled otherwise;
unverified items are labeled as open search links or carried
references. Adult previews everything before showing.

   — adult background; its situation table is the source of the
   add-to / take-from / put-together / take-apart / compare taxonomy
   and the "unknowns in all positions" progression used in Lessons
   01–03. Description checked via search 2026-10-29; adult opens and
   previews before use.

   situations"** —
   <https://mathematicalmusings.org/forums/topic/common-addition-and-subtraction-situations/>
   — educator-facing discussion of the same situation table,
   including why the grade-1 standard lists all five situation types
   with unknowns in all positions. Description checked via search
   2026-10-29; adult opens and previews before use.
4. **Education Week, "Common-Core Subtraction: Teaching Many Methods"** —
   <https://www.edweek.org/teaching-learning/common-core-subtraction-teaching-many-methods/2014/11>
   — adult background on why the standard (1.OA.C.6) teaches several
   strategies rather than one algorithm; carried from the Unit 03

5. **Math Coach's Corner, "1st Grade Essentials: Basic Fact
   Strategies"** —
   <https://www.mathcoachscorner.com/2020/04/1st-grade-essentials-basic-fact-strategies/>

   was verified 2026-10-16; not re-opened this run. Adult background;
