  ✓; total value 4 tens and 5 ones = 45 ✓. No text, numbers,
  letters, or people in the image ✓. Blue bars vs. red cubes are

- **Caption:** "An AI-generated picture of tens and ones blocks.
  Four blue bars stand for four tens; five red cubes stand for five
  ones. What number is shown, and what happens when 3 more ones are
  added?"
- **Alt text:** "Four long blue bars and five small red cubes on a
  white background."
- **Text-only alternative** (carries every count the activities need,
  so the activity works without the image): "The picture shows 4 long
  blue bars and 5 small red cubes. Each blue bar stands for one ten
  and each red cube stands for one one. That is 4 tens and 5 ones,
  which is 45. Adding 3 ones makes 8 ones, tens unchanged, so
  45 + 3 = 48. Adding 5 ones instead makes 10 ones, which trade for
  1 ten, so 45 + 5 = 50."
- **Known limitations:** AI illustration, not a photograph — stylized
  flat shapes. The bars are deliberately unsegmented, so the learner
  must be told (as the lesson does) that each bar *stands for* one
  ten; the picture alone does not show ten ones inside a bar. The
  learner verifies 45 + 3 = 48 with real rods and cubes in Lesson 01
  before trusting the picture. The picture does not prove anything
  about addition; it is a counting and reasoning prompt.
