# Resource Pack — Unit 05: Two-Digit Addition and Subtraction Foundations

**Summary (adult planning judgment):** First-grade two-digit
computation is place-value reasoning with piles, not counting from
one: tens with tens, ones with ones, trade at ten, and check every
subtraction by adding. The resources below support the unit's five
objectives — add ones (with and without a new ten), add multiples of
ten, 10 more/less mentally, subtract multiples of ten, and relate
models to written methods with checks. All external links were
checked 2026-11-04 unless labeled otherwise; unverified items are
labeled as open search links or carried references. Adult previews
everything before showing.
