# Teacher Guide — Unit 05: Two-Digit Addition and Subtraction Foundations

Adult-facing guidance for the unit. Learner materials never include
answer keys; keep this file separate from the learner.

## How the unit fits

Unit 05 (track weeks 19–22) is the first unit where place value does
real computation work. The moves arrive in a deliberate order:
**add ones without a new ten** (L1) proves the piles rule in its
easiest form; **compose a ten** (L2) handles the one case L1 excluded;
**add a multiple of ten** (L3) mirrors L1 on the tens side;
**ten more / ten less** (L4) takes L3's move into the head; and
**subtract multiples of ten** (L5) closes the unit with tens
subtraction plus the addition check that catches every earlier slip.
Unit 06 (length measurement) does not build on this computation, but
the tens/ones reasoning returns in every later number unit — so
Objective 1 must be secure before moving on, even though the track
order does not gate Unit 06 on it.

Two-digit + two-digit addition is explicitly out of scope: the
standard (1.NBT.C.4) covers two-digit + one-digit and two-digit +
multiple-of-ten only. If the learner asks for 34 + 27, model it with
piles as enrichment and say plainly that it is a later grade's
written method — do not teach a column algorithm here.

## Session-by-session notes

- **Sessions 1–4 (Week 19):** Keep ones totals at 9 or less all week.
  The wrong-pile slip (38 + 1 = 48) is the week's most common bug —
  catch it in Lesson 01's error analysis, not in the quiz. The
  generated illustration is a study object, not decoration — Lesson
  01's picture study is mandatory, and its honest counts (4 bars,
  5 cubes = 45) are recorded in the assets README.
- **Sessions 5–8 (Week 20a):** The trade must be physical at least
  once per problem — a learner who only hears "make a new ten" will
  forget it. The second step of the bridge (27 + 3 = 30, *then* + 2)
  is the one learners drop; always demand both steps. The shop
  project starts in Session 7; its add-ones page must show the
  "tens stay the same" sentence, not just an answer.
- **Sessions 9–10 (Week 20b):** Adding tens feels easy, which is
  exactly when the wrong-pile slip returns in mirror form (42 + 30
  = 45). Session F's wrong-pile hunt is mandatory, not optional.
- **Sessions 11–12 (Week 21):** Ten more/less must be answered
  without counting — the hundred-chart column is the bridge, and the
  chart is covered only when the learner asks for it to be. The
  90 → 100 edge case deserves its own minute: 10 tens = 100 is a
  genuine discovery, not a trick.
- **Sessions 13–16 (Week 21b–22):** Subtraction checks are
  non-negotiable: every L5 answer is written with its addition
  check. Zero answers (50 − 50 = 0) must be celebrated, not treated
  as "no answer." The Session 15 showcase is the project's real
  assessment — a learner who can explain all four pages has met
  Objective 5 even if one written answer was shaky.

## What "ready to move on" looks like

- Adds two-digit + one-digit by piles, naming whether a new ten is
  made and showing the trade when one is.
- Adds two-digit + multiple-of-ten by tens, stating "tens change,
  ones stay the same" unprompted.
- Finds 10 more and 10 less mentally, explaining that only the tens
  digit changes.
- Subtracts multiples of ten by tens and checks every answer by
  addition, including zero-difference cases.
- Explains a worked two-digit problem with piles and a check: what
  changed, what stayed the same, and why the method works.

## Re-teaching map

| Evidence | Re-teach with |
|---|---|
| Adds ones to the tens digit (38 + 1 = 48) | Lesson 01 cover-the-tens-pile routine; Session A zero-ones round |
| Forgets the composed ten (47 + 6 = 43) | Lesson 02 trade tray, physical trade every problem; Session B trade station |
| Bridge split at the wrong place | Lesson 02 "how many ones to the next decade?" ritual before splitting |
| Adds tens to the ones digit (42 + 30 = 45) | Lesson 03 wrong-pile hunt; Session F wrong-pile hunt |
| "10 more" answered as +1 (46 → 47) | Lesson 04 chart column: same column, same ones digit; Session G row races |
| Counting by ones for 10 more/less | Lesson 04 tens-digit sentence, chart covered only after the sentence is fluent |
| Within-10 fact slip in tens subtraction (9 − 6 = 2) | Lesson 05 check-catches-error routine; re-check the within-10 fact with counters |
| Refuses zero as an answer (50 − 50) | Lesson 05 zero case with rods removed and counted: "nothing left is an answer" |
| Counts everything from one | Unit 01 bundle games before new moves; keep piles on the table all unit |

## Scoring the unit assessment

See
[assessments/unit-assessment.md](assessments/unit-assessment.md#scoring-adult):
at least 9 of 12 points, with Objective 1 at Secure or Developing.
A learner below that bar re-takes the failed stations after the
mapped re-teach, not the whole assessment. The formative quiz
(Session 16) uses the same bar per objective: secure on at least 4
of 5, with Objective 1 secure.
