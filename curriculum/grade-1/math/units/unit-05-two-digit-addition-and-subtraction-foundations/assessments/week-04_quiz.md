# Formative Quiz — Unit 05, Week 22 (Session 16)

**Purpose:** check each of the five unit objectives after the review
week; oral/pointing/drawing responses welcome; the adult records
secure / developing / not yet per objective. Not a standardized or
validated assessment.

## Strategy stations (20 minutes, learner rotates)

**Station 1 — Add ones (Objective 1).** The adult shows `35 + 4`
and `48 + 5`. The learner builds each with piles and says whether a
new ten is made. (Expected: 35 + 4 = 39 — no new ten, 5 ones + 4 ones
= 9 ones; 48 + 5 = 53 — new ten, 8 ones + 5 ones = 13 ones = 1 ten and
3 ones, 4 tens + 1 ten = 5 tens.)

**Station 2 — Add tens (Objective 2).** The adult shows `29 + 30`
and `63 + 20`. The learner names what changes before solving.
(Expected: 29 + 30 = 59 — tens 2 + 3 = 5, ones 9 unchanged; 63 + 20 =
83 — tens 6 + 2 = 8, ones 3 unchanged.)

**Station 3 — Ten more / ten less (Objective 3).** The adult shows
`46` and asks for 10 more and then 10 less, chart covered if ready.
(Expected: 10 more than 46 = 56; 10 less than 46 = 36. Only the tens
digit moves.)

**Station 4 — Subtract tens (Objective 4).** Tens rods only: the
adult builds 80 and asks for 80 − 50, then demands the addition
check. (Expected: 80 − 50 = 30 — 8 tens − 5 tens = 3 tens; check
30 + 50 = 80.)

**Station 5 — Explain and check (Objective 5).** The adult shows a
worked problem, `27 + 6 = 33`, and asks: "Show me why with piles,
and tell me what changed and what stayed the same." (Expected: 7
ones + 6 ones = 13 ones = 1 ten and 3 ones; 2 tens + 1 ten = 3 tens;
so 27 + 6 = 33. What changed: the tens grew by one and the ones
became 3. The method: tens with tens, ones with ones, trade at ten.)

## Written check (5 short tasks; adult scribes if needed)

1. 54 + 3 = ___ (expected 57 — 4 ones + 3 ones = 7 ones, 5 tens
   unchanged)
2. 39 + 6 = ___ by trade or bridge (expected 45 — 9 ones + 6 ones =
   15 ones = 1 ten and 5 ones; 3 tens + 1 ten = 4 tens)
3. 10 less than 75 = ___ (expected 65 — 7 tens − 1 ten = 6 tens, 5
   ones unchanged)
4. 90 − 30 = ___ with its check (expected 60 — 9 tens − 3 tens = 6
   tens; check 60 + 30 = 90)
5. True or false: 41 + 20 = 61 (expected true — 4 tens + 2 tens = 6
   tens, 1 one unchanged)

**Ready to move on:** secure on at least 4 of 5 objectives, with
Objective 1 (add ones, including composing a ten) secure.
**Re-teach triggers:** forgotten composed ten → Lesson 02 trade
tray; tens added to ones pile → Lesson 03 wrong-pile hunt; "10 more"
answered as +1 → Lesson 04 chart column; missing or failed check →
Lesson 05 check-catches-error routine; counting from one → Unit 01
bundle games.
