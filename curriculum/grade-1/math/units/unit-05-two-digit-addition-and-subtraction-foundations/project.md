# Project — My Tens-and-Ones Shop

**Unit:** [U05](README.md) · **Runs across Sessions 7, 12, 14, 15**
**Type:** individual poster-book project (oral/drawing/manipulative;
adult scribes written words when the learner isn't ready to write)

## The job

The learner opens a pretend shop with four pages — one page per unit
move — that teaches a visitor how tens and ones work. Each page shows
an original problem the learner invented, a drawing or rod/cube
arrangement of that problem, the written method, and an explanation in
the learner's own words of what changed and what stayed the same. The
shop is the companion to Units 02–03's strategy books: together they
show that place value, not counting from one, is what makes big-number
arithmetic work.

## Materials

- 4 sheets of paper (or a folded booklet), crayons/markers
- Tens rods, ones cubes, and a hundred chart for planning each page
- Stapler or binder clip (adult operates)

## Parts

### Part 1 — Add-ones page (Session 7)

- **Shop sign:** shop name, "by [learner]" (adult may scribe the
  name), one drawing of tens rods and ones cubes on a shelf.
- **Page 1 — Add ones, no new ten:** the learner's own two-digit +
  one-digit problem whose ones stay below ten (for example, invent
  43 + 5 = 48), drawn as piles, with the sentence "Tens stay the
  same, ones change."

### Part 2 — Compose-ten and add-tens pages (Session 12)

- **Page 2 — Make a new ten:** the learner's own problem whose ones
  reach ten or more (for example, invent 36 + 7 = 43), drawn with the
  trade shown (ten ones circled, one tens rod drawn), and the
  explanation "Ten ones became one ten, so the tens grew by one."
- **Page 3 — Add tens:** the learner's own two-digit + multiple-of-ten
  problem (for example, invent 25 + 40 = 65), drawn as piles or as
  hundred-chart jumps, with the sentence "Tens change, ones stay the
  same."

### Part 3 — Ten-more/less and subtract-tens pages (Session 14)

- **Page 4 — Ten more, ten less, subtract tens:** the learner's own
  trio — one 10-more, one 10-less, and one subtract-tens problem (for
  example, invent 10 more than 52 = 62, 10 less than 52 = 42, and
  70 − 30 = 40 with its addition check 40 + 30 = 70) — drawn on a
  hundred-chart strip, with the explanation "Only the tens digit
  moves" and the subtraction's check.

### Part 4 — Shop showcase (Session 15)

The learner "opens" the shop for the adult (or a small audience),
pointing to each page and explaining the move. The adult asks one
question per page: "What changed here, and what stayed the same?"
and "How did you check it?" The learner's answers — not reading
fluency — are what count. One showcase question must be a problem
the visitor invents on the spot (any of the four moves) that the
learner solves with piles or the chart.

## Deliverables

1. A 4-page shop poster-book (cover + Pages 1–4 as above), each page
   with an original problem, a pile/chart drawing, a written method,
   and a "changed / stayed the same" explanation.
2. A spoken showcase (Session 15) answering one question per page
   plus one visitor-invented problem solved live.

## Rubric (adult)

| Criterion | Secure (2) | Developing (1) | Not yet (0) |
|---|---|---|---|
| Add ones, with and without a new ten (Objectives 1) | Both pages' problems correct, trade shown where needed | One page correct, or trade missing | Problems incorrect or no trade shown |
| Add tens and 10 more/less (Objectives 2–3) | Tens change and ones unchanged in every problem; tens sentence stated | Most problems correct, sentence sometimes missing | Tens and ones confused |
| Subtract tens with a check (Objectives 4–5) | Subtraction correct and addition check rebuilds the start | Subtraction correct, check missing or wrong | Subtraction incorrect |
| Explanation: what changed, what stayed (Objective 5) | Every page names what changed and what stayed the same | Some pages named, some missing | No changed/stayed explanation |

**Ready to move on:** at least 6 of 8 rubric points, with the
add-ones criterion at Secure or Developing. **Re-teach triggers:**
forgotten composed ten → Lesson 02 trade tray; tens added to ones →
Lesson 03 wrong-pile hunt; "10 more" as +1 → Lesson 04 chart column;
missing check → Lesson 05 check-catches-error routine.
