# Formative Quiz — Unit 03, Week 14 (Session 16)

**Purpose:** check each of the five unit objectives after the review week;
oral/pointing/drawing responses welcome; the adult records secure /
developing / not yet per objective. Not a standardized or validated
assessment.

## Strategy stations (20 minutes, learner rotates)

**Station 1 — Unknown addend (Objective 1).** The adult shows `10 − 6`
and `13 − 7`. The learner flips each to addition and counts up.
(Expected: 10 − 6 = 4 — 6 + 4 = 10; 13 − 7 = 6 — 7 + 6 = 13.)

**Station 2 — Count back / count up (Objective 2).** The adult shows
`18 − 3` and `16 − 14`. The learner names the move before solving.
(Expected: 18 − 3 = 15 by counting back — "17, 16, 15"; 16 − 14 = 2 by
counting up — "15, 16," 2 hops.)

**Station 3 — Back to ten (Objective 3).** Ten frames with 15 dots; the
adult asks for 15 − 7. (Expected: split 7 into 5 + 2; 15 − 5 = 10;
10 − 2 = 8.)

**Station 4 — Fact family (Objective 4).** The adult gives 7 + 8 = 15
and asks for both subtractions. (Expected: 15 − 7 = 8 and 15 − 8 = 7.)

## Written check (5 short tasks; adult scribes if needed)

1. 12 − 4 = ___ (any strategy; expected 8 — back to ten: 12 − 2 = 10,
   10 − 2 = 8)
2. 14 − 9 = ___ by unknown-addend (expected 5 — 9 + 5 = 14)
3. 16 − ? = 7 (expected ? = 9 — count up 8…16, nine hops; check 16 − 9 = 7)
4. True or false: 13 − 5 = 8 (expected true — 13 − 3 = 10, 10 − 2 = 8)
5. True or false: 11 − 6 = 4 (expected false — 11 − 6 = 5; fix by changing
   4 to 5)

**Ready to move on:** secure on at least 4 of 5 objectives, with
Objective 1 (unknown-addend) or Objective 3 (back-to-ten) secure.
**Re-teach triggers:** off-by-one hop errors → Session A/C hop-count
checks; back-to-ten split errors → Session F "how many loose ones?"
ritual; fact-family invented numbers → Session G triangle race;
missing-minuend subtraction instead of rebuilding → Lesson 05 rebuild
demo; one-sided true/false judging → Session H both-sides sort.
