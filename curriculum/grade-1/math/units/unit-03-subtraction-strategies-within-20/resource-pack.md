# Resource Pack — Unit 03: Subtraction Strategies Within 20

**Summary (adult planning judgment):** First-grade subtraction fluency
grows from addition, not from memorizing take-away facts: flip to the
unknown addend, count back or count up by choosing the shorter move, go
back to ten, and read subtractions off fact families. The resources below
support the unit's five objectives — unknown-addend, count back/count up,
back-to-ten, fact families, and missing numbers with true/false equations.
All external links were checked 2026-10-22 unless labeled otherwise;
unverified items are labeled as open search links or carried references.
Adult previews everything before showing.

1. **Verified (title and description checked 2026-10-22):** "Subtraction
   within 20 for Kids: Count Back, Back to Ten, Think Addition | Grade 1
   Math Ch 2" — <https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=JMw-pd0CM3Q> — covers
   crossing out (11 − 3 = 8), count back on a number line (12 − 3 = 9),
   back to ten (13 − 5 = 13 − 3 − 2 = 10 − 2 = 8), think addition
   (14 − 9: 9 + 5 = 14), fact partners (8 + 5 = 13, so 13 − 5 = 8 and
   13 − 8 = 5), and checking by adding; all numbers within 20. Same
