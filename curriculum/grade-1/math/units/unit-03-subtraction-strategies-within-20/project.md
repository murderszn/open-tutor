# Project — My Subtraction Strategy Book

**Unit:** [U03](README.md) · **Runs across Sessions 9, 12, 14, 15**
**Type:** individual book-making project (oral/drawing/manipulative; adult
scribes written words when the learner isn't ready to write)

## The job

The learner authors a 5-page **Subtraction Strategy Book** — a real book
with a cover and one page per strategy — that teaches a younger friend (or
a stuffed animal) how to subtract. Each strategy page shows an original
example the learner invented, a drawing or counter arrangement of that
example, and an explanation in the learner's own words of why the strategy
works. The book is the companion to Unit 02's Addition Strategy Book:
together they show that addition and subtraction are one family of ideas.

## Materials

- 5 sheets of paper (or folded booklet), crayons/markers
- Counters, ten frames, and a number line for planning each page
- Stapler or binder clip (adult operates)

## Parts

### Part 1 — Cover and unknown-addend page (Session 9)

- **Cover:** book title, "by [learner]" (adult may scribe the name), one
  drawing of taking away.
- **Page 1 — Unknown addend:** the learner picks their own subtraction
  (within 15), draws the subtrahend and the counted-up hops, and explains:
  "I flip it to addition because…"

### Part 2 — Count-back/count-up and back-to-ten pages (Session 12)

- **Page 2 — Count back or count up:** the learner's own pair of
  problems — one where counting back wins (small subtrahend) and one
  where counting up wins (close numbers) — drawn on a number line with
  the hops marked, and the explanation "I chose this move because…"
- **Page 3 — Back to ten:** the learner's own teen subtraction (one
  subtrahend 4–9), drawn on ten frames with the two-stage take-away, and
  the chain "___ − ___ = ___ − ___ − ___ = 10 − ___ = ___."

### Part 3 — Fact-family page (Session 14)

- **Page 4 — Fact family:** the learner's own triangle (two parts, one
  total), drawn with two colors, with all four facts written or dictated:
  two additions and two subtractions, and the explanation "one addition
  fact gives me two subtractions because…"

### Part 4 — Strategy showcase (Session 15)

The learner "reads" the book to the adult (or a small audience), pointing
to each page and explaining the strategy. The adult asks one question per
page: "Why does this strategy work?" and "When would you use it?" The
learner's answers — not reading fluency — are what count. One showcase
question must be a missing-number problem the learner invents on the
spot ("ask me a missing-number one!") and solves with a check.

## Deliverables

1. A 5-page book: cover + 4 strategy pages.
2. One original invented example per strategy page (not copied from lessons).
3. An oral explanation of each strategy at the showcase, plus one
   invented missing-number problem solved with a check.

## Rubric (adult-scored; share the language with the learner in advance)

| Criterion | Strong (3) | Developing (2) | Starting (1) |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Strategy accuracy** | Every page's example is computed correctly and the drawing matches the numbers | 1–2 pages have a number/drawing mismatch, corrected with help | Several mismatches; learner re-builds with counters before finishing |
| **Explanation** | Each page explains *why* the strategy works in the learner's own words | Some pages explain; others only show the answer | Pages show answers without explanations; adult re-asks "why?" |
| **Fact-family page** | All four facts correct; learner can cover one part and name it | Facts correct but cover-and-say needs prompting | Facts incomplete or invented numbers appear; re-teach with triangle cards |
| **Showcase** | Names the strategy and when to use it for all 4 pages; solves the invented missing-number problem with a check | Names strategies but can't say when to use 1–2 of them, or skips the check | Reads pages without naming strategies; adult prompts each one |

**Scoring:** 10–12 = ready to move on; 7–9 = one re-teach session on the
weakest page, then re-showcase; below 7 = revisit Lessons 01–05 practice
sessions before Unit 04.

## Differentiation

- Adult scribes all dictated words; the learner's job is the math thinking.
- Pages may be built with glued-down counters or stickers instead of drawn.
- A learner who finishes early adds a "Which strategy is fastest?" page
  comparing two strategies on the same subtraction (e.g. 15 − 7 by
  back-to-ten vs. by unknown-addend).
