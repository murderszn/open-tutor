# Resource Pack — Unit 01: Counting, Place Value, and Tens

**Summary (adult planning judgment):** First-grade place-value instruction
works best when every video or article supplements, never replaces, hands-on
bundling of tens. The resources below support the unit's four objectives:
bundling and counting by tens, reading/writing numerals to 120, teen and
decade number structure, and tens-first comparison. All external links were
checked 2026-10-09; unverified items are labeled as open search links.
