# Formative Quiz — Unit 01 (Week 4, Session 15)

**Format:** oral/observed; the adult scribes written numerals the learner
dictates. 10–15 minutes. Every item maps to a unit objective in brackets.
Full solutions in [answer-key.md](../answer-key.md#formative-quiz).

**Directions to the learner (read aloud):** "Show me what you know about tens
and ones. You can use the straws, point, or just tell me. There are no
trick questions."

1. [Obj. 1] The adult shows 4 bundles of ten straws. "Count the bundles by
   tens. How many straws?" *(Expected: 10, 20, 30, 40 — forty straws.)*
2. [Obj. 1] "Show me 70 with bundles." Then: "How many tens is 70?"
   *(Expected: 7 bundles; 7 tens.)*
3. [Obj. 2] "Start at 97. Count to 105 out loud."
   *(Expected: 97, 98, 99, 100, 101, 102, 103, 104, 105.)*
4. [Obj. 2] The adult dictates: "Write one hundred twelve."
   *(Expected: 112.)*
5. [Obj. 3] The adult shows 1 bundle + 8 loose straws. "What number? Tell
   me the tens and the ones." *(Expected: 18 — one ten and eight ones.)*
6. [Obj. 3] "I have 5 tens and 0 ones. What number?" *(Expected: 50.)*
7. [Obj. 4] "Which is greater, 63 or 36? Tell me the tens first."
   *(Expected: 63 — 6 tens beats 3 tens.)*
8. [Obj. 4] "Put the right symbol between 41 and 47: 41 __ 47."
   *(Expected: 41 < 47.)*

**Scoring (adult observation):** 7–8 correct with tens-first reasoning =
secure, continue to the unit assessment. 5–6 = developing: re-teach the
missed objective with the matching lesson's guided practice before Session
16. Below 5 = not yet: use Session 16 for re-teaching, and schedule the unit
assessment after a catch-up session (see the
[teacher guide](../teacher-guide.md#re-teaching-map)).
