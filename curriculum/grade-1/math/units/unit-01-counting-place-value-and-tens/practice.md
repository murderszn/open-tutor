# Practice, Game, and Review Sessions — Unit 01

Named sessions referenced by the [16-session pacing](README.md#16-session-pacing-4-weeks--4-sessions-20-25-minutes-each).
Each takes 20–25 minutes. The adult observes, gives hints on practice days,
and records exit checks. Fluency here is strategy-based — no timed speed
tests.

## Session A — Bundle-and-count trays

*Weeks 3 and 5 (after L1 and L2). Materials: 60+ loose straws, rubber bands,
trays.*

1. **Bundle race (guided):** The adult scatters 24 straws. The learner
   bundles tens until none are left (2 bundles), counts the loose ones (4),
   and announces "2 tens and 4 ones — 24." Repeat with 31 and 45.
2. **Draw and label (independent):** Draw each build as rectangles-with-bands
   plus loose lines; write the numeral beside it.
3. **Exit check:** Adult shows 3 bundles + 7 loose. Learner: "3 tens, 7
   ones — 37." Secure / developing / not yet.

*Answers: 24 → 2 bundles + 4 loose; 31 → 3 bundles + 1 loose; 45 → 4 bundles
+ 5 loose; exit check 37.*

## Session B — Build-a-number trays

*Week 5 (after L3). Materials: 1+ bundles, loose straws, number cards 11–99.*

1. **Build what I say (guided):** Adult calls 13, 18, 27, 34, 56. Learner
   builds each with bundles and loose straws, then writes the numeral.
2. **Say what I built (guided):** Adult builds 41, 29, 63, 90. Learner reads
   each as "tens and ones" and writes the numeral.
3. **Exit check:** Build 72 from the spoken words "seven tens and two ones."
   Secure / developing / not yet.

*Answers: builds must show correct bundle/loose counts; 90 = 9 bundles,
0 loose.*

## Session C — Tens-counting games

*Weeks 3–4 (after L1–L2). No writing required — oral and movement.*

1. **Tens march:** March in place, one big step per ten: 10, 20, 30 …
   to 120. Then march backward from 120 to 10.
2. **Hundred-chart races:** Token starts on a called decade number; race to
   find 70, then 110, then 40 on the chart. First finger on the right square
   names the tens count.
3. **Decade bingo (week 5 variant):** Bingo card of decade numbers 10–120.
   Adult calls "how many tens?" questions ("I have 7 bundles — what
   number?"); learner covers 70.

*Answers: sequence 10–120 by tens; 7 bundles = 70; 11 bundles = 110.*

## Session D — Build, draw, and circle review

*Review days (Sessions 4, 8, 12). Materials: paper, crayons, straws.*

1. The adult names a number (e.g., 46). The learner builds it, draws it,
   and circles the tens digit in the written numeral.
2. Repeat for 5 numbers across the unit's range so far (weeks 3–4: to 40;
   week 5: teens and beyond; week 6: any two-digit number to 99).
3. **Error hunt:** The adult writes three numerals with one deliberate
   place-value error (e.g., "3 bundles and 4 loose = 43" written as 34).
   The learner finds and fixes it with the straws.

*Sample fixes: 3 bundles + 4 loose = 34, not 43; 2 bundles + 9 loose = 29,
not 92; 1 bundle + 0 loose = 10, not 1.*

## Comparison card games

*Week 6 (after L5). Materials: number cards 10–99, symbol cards.*

1. **War:** Each player flips a card; both read the tens and ones, then
   place `>`, `<`, or `=` between them. Correct reasoning wins the round.
2. **Closest to 50:** Flip two cards each; the player whose number is
   closest to 50 (by tens first, then ones) explains why and scores.
3. **Exit check:** Compare 83 and 38; then 47 and 74. Learner states
   tens-then-ones reasoning and the symbol. Secure / developing / not yet.

*Answers: 83 > 38 (8 tens beats 3 tens); 47 < 74 (4 tens < 7 tens).*
