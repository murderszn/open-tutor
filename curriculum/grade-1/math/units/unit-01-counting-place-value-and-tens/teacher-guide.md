# Teacher Guide — Unit 01: Counting, Place Value, and Tens

Guidance for the adult leading this unit. Student materials never include
answer keys; this guide and [answer-key.md](answer-key.md) stay with the
adult.

## How the unit fits together

- **Sessions 1–4** build the core idea: ten ones = one ten (Lesson 01).
  Don't rush — every later lesson stands on bundling.
- **Sessions 5–8** extend to counting by tens and decade numerals (Lesson 02)
  with hundred-chart games.
- **Sessions 9–12** decode teen numbers as ten-plus-ones (Lesson 03) and
  launch the Ten-Bundle Shop project.
- **Sessions 13–16** teach counting from any number (Lesson 04) and
  tens-first comparison (Lesson 05), finish the project, check formatively,
  and assess.

## Common misconceptions and responses

| Misconception | What you'll see | Response |
|---|---|---|
| A bundle counts as "one straw" | Says 1 bundle + 6 loose = 7 | Always ask "one ten is how many straws?" — point to the bundle and say "ten" |
| Digit reversal (13 ↔ 31) | Writes 31 for "thirteen" | Build first, then write; read the build as "1 ten and 3 ones" |
| Comparing ones first | Says 29 > 31 | Chant "tens first"; rebuild both numbers so tens are visible as bundles |
| Freezing at decade crossings | Stops at 49, or says "100" after 99 then stalls | Pause, name the new ten aloud ("fifty — that's 5 tens"), then continue |
| 101 vs. 110 confusion | Writes 110 for "one hundred one" | Contrast 101 and 110 on the chart; point to the tens row |
| Loose piles of 10+ | Leaves 12 loose ones unbundled | "Whenever you see ten ones, what do we do?" — re-bundle together |

## Re-teaching map

| If the learner struggles with… | Revisit | With |
|---|---|---|
| Bundling / what a ten is | Lesson 01 guided practice | 10 straws, tie together, count "one ten" |
| Counting by tens / decade numerals | Lesson 02 guided practice | March-and-count; bundle rows on the chart |
| Teen numbers | Lesson 03 guided practice | Bundle + loose builds for 11–19 in order |
| Counting from a start number | Lesson 04 guided practice | Token on the chart; count on in short hops (start → +10) |
| Comparison symbols / tens-first | Lesson 05 guided practice | War card game; always narrate tens, then ones |

## Readiness criteria

- **Ready for Session 5:** can bundle ten ones and count 1–5 bundles by tens.
- **Ready for Session 9:** reads/writes decade numerals 10–100; counts by
  tens to 120 with at most one self-corrected slip.
- **Ready for Session 13:** builds any teen 11–19 as ten-plus-ones and names
  the tens and ones.
- **Ready for Unit 02:** unit assessment 9–10, or 7–8 with the missed
  objective re-taught and re-checked. Do not advance on a "not yet" in
  Objectives 1 or 3 — Unit 02's addition strategies assume secure
  tens-and-ones.

## Session tips

- Keep bundles and loose straws in separate tray zones every session —
  mixing them is the #1 source of counting errors.
- Say the math words every time: "3 tens and 4 ones," never just "34," until
  the learner says them unprompted.
- Short sessions beat long ones. If attention fades at minute 18, stop at
  the exit check and carry practice to the next day.
- The hundred chart stays the same orientation all unit (1 at top-left).
  Keep one chart for the whole track — Unit 05 reuses it.
