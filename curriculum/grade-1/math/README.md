# Grade 1 Mathematics

Independent, grade-specific materials in the public OpenTutor curriculum library.
This track is a **draft in progress**: the audit and scope-and-sequence are delivered,
and Unit 01 is delivered as a validated draft; the remaining seven units are planned,
not yet written. See [scope-and-sequence.md](scope-and-sequence.md).

**Do not refer a learner to another grade track.** This folder is self-contained for
one grade-1 learner at a time; a guiding adult adapts pacing, materials, and
supports. Keep completed learner work, names, photos, schedules, and grades in
private storage — never in this public repository.

See also: [Grade 1 track](../README.md), [curriculum index](../../README.md).

## What this track covers

A full-year (about 36 weeks) grade-1 math course organized around place value to
120, addition and subtraction strategies within 20, story problems of every
situation type, two-digit computation foundations, length measurement with unit
iteration, telling time to the hour and half-hour, data with up to three
categories, and shape attributes, composition, and equal shares.
Sessions are 20–25 minutes, about four per week, blending hands-on models with
short independent written practice (4–6 tasks) reviewed by the adult the same day.

## Track objectives (measurable, adult-assessed)

By the end of the year the learner will be able to:

1. Count to 120 from any starting number; read and write numerals to 120.
2. Explain two-digit numbers as tens and ones (teens as a ten plus ones; decade
   numbers as groups of ten).
3. Compare two two-digit numbers using `>`, `=`, `<`, reasoning about tens then ones.
4. Add and subtract within 20 — fluent within 10 — using counting on, making ten,
   decomposing to a ten, fact families, and easier known sums.
5. Solve word problems within 20 of every situation type (add to, take from,
   put together, take apart, compare), with unknowns in all positions, including
   three-addend problems (sum ≤ 20).
6. Interpret equations: the meaning of the equal sign, true/false equations, and
   the unknown in addition/subtraction equations.
7. Add within 100 (two-digit + one-digit; two-digit + multiple of 10); mentally
   find 10 more/10 less; subtract multiples of 10 (10–90).
8. Order objects by length, compare indirectly via a third object, and measure by
   iterating same-size units with no gaps or overlaps.
9. Tell and write time to the hour and half-hour on analog and digital clocks.
10. Organize, represent, and interpret data with up to three categories.
11. Distinguish defining from non-defining shape attributes, compose shapes, and
    partition circles/rectangles into halves, fourths, and quarters.

## Standards reference

Objectives reference the Common Core State Standards for Mathematics,
grade 1 domains (verified against the framework; not a claim of state
adoption or accreditation). Full crosswalk in

- Number and Operations in Base Ten: 1.NBT.A.1, 1.NBT.B.2–3, 1.NBT.C.4–6
- Measurement and Data: 1.MD.A.1–2, 1.MD.B.3, 1.MD.C.4
- Geometry: 1.G.A.1–3

## For the guiding adult

Grade-1 math bridges concrete and symbolic thinking: every new idea first lives in
bundles, ten frames, and hundred charts before it appears as written equations.
Read the scope-and-sequence before starting; it lists safe household materials,
accessibility supports, and the diagnostic, catch-up, and review weeks that keep
pacing humane. Fluency is strategy-based — no timed speed tests. Each future unit
will include its own teacher guide and answer key.
