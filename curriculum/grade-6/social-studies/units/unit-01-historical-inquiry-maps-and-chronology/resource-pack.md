# U01 Resource Pack

**Unit:** [U01 — Historical Inquiry: Archaeology, Maps, and Chronology](README.md)

**Goal:** Give the guiding adult verified, age-appropriate starting points
for every core objective of this unit — framing historical questions,
reading maps as evidence, classifying and evaluating sources, and building
BCE/CE chronology with archaeological context. **Age fit:** 11–12 (grade 6),
adult-guided; the adult previews every video for ads, age suitability, and
accuracy, and pre-selects all real-world examples.

All links below were checked 2026-10-12. Videos require captions or
transcripts; the adult confirms captions are on before showing. Every core
objective has a free, no-account route.

## Focused queries

Use these when you need more than the curated links. Prefer results from
museum (.edu / si.edu / .org museum), archive, and government (.gov) sites.
