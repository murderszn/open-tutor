# Week 04 Formative Quiz

**Unit:** [U01 — Historical Inquiry: Archaeology, Maps, and Chronology](../README.md) · **Grade 6**
**Time:** ~25 minutes · **Covers:** Lessons 01–03 (Weeks 3–4)

Write or dictate your answers. For every answer, give your reason — the
question type, the map feature, or the computation.

**Scoring (adult use):** 1 point per item; items 13–15 give 1 point for the
answer and 1 for the reasoning (18 points total). This is a check, not a
final grade: the adult reviews it the same day and re-teaches misses.

---

1. Label each question: **compelling (C)**, **supporting (S)**, or **not a
   history question (N)**.
   a. "Why did some ancient cities last for centuries while others faded?"
   b. "In what year was the first Olympic games traditionally held?"
   c. "Which ancient civilization was the coolest?"
2. Write **one supporting question** for: "How did rivers shape the first
   cities?" Then name the source kind that could answer it.
3. Explain in 1–2 sentences: how does a supporting question help answer a
   compelling question?
4. Study the fictional town sketch from Lesson 02. Label each as
   **observation (O)** or **inference (I)**:
   a. "The clinic is southwest of the library in the sketch."
   b. "The path near the clinic is dangerous."
   c. "Two requests were received."
5. Name **two things** the fictional town sketch cannot show.
6. Name **two things** the fictional source note cannot tell you.
7. On the [practice map](../assets/map-grid-scale.svg): what is the grid
   label of the well? How do you read that label aloud?
8. On the practice map: measure the straight-line distance from the town to
   the well using the scale bar. Show your segment count.
9. On the practice map: write one **evidence claim** about the market's
   location, citing the grid square and the key.
10. List the **six tools** on a good map and say what one of them (your
    choice) does.
11. A learner says, "The map proves the walk from the town to the market is
    short." What map information is missing that makes this claim fail?
12. True or false, with one reason: "A flat world map can show every
    country's true size and true shape at the same time."
13. Africa covers about 30 million square kilometers; Greenland covers about
    2 million. On many classroom world maps they look about the same size.
    a. About how many times larger is Africa than Greenland? Show the
       division.
    b. In 1–2 sentences, explain *why* the map shows them as similar in
       size (use the word **projection**).
14. Reword into a question history can answer: "What was the most awesome
    ancient invention?" Then write one supporting question for your reword.
15. Error analysis: "My compelling question is: What happened in ancient
    Rome? My supporting questions are: Why did Rome fall? Why do empires
    end?" Name **two problems** with this set and fix one of them.
