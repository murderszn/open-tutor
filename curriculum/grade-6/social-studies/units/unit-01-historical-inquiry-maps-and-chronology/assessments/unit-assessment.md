# Unit 01 Culminating Assessment

**Unit:** [U01 — Historical Inquiry: Archaeology, Maps, and Chronology](../README.md) · **Grade 6**
**Time:** ~40 minutes · **Session:** 16 of 16 (with the project due)

Answer every section. Give a reason for every answer — cite the map feature,
source clue, layer rule, or computation you used.

**Scoring (adult use):** Section A: 8 · Section B: 8 · Section C: 10 ·
Section D: 10 · **Total: 36.** 30–36 = strong; 24–29 = solid, review one
section; 18–23 = re-teach and revise; below 18 = redo with adult modeling.

---

## Section A — Map task (8 points)

Use the [practice map](../assets/map-grid-scale.svg).

1. (2 pts) The adult names two new grid squares (e.g., "B2" and "D4").
   For each, name the symbol in that square (or "empty") using the key.
2. (2 pts) Measure the straight-line distance between those two squares
   with the scale bar. Show your segment count and the distance.
3. (2 pts) Write one **evidence claim** about a place on the map, citing a
   grid square and the key.
4. (2 pts) Name **two things** this map cannot show, and for one of them,
   explain why a historian would care.

## Section B — Source comparison (8 points)

Read these two fictional sources about the same event — a new well dug in
the town of Riverbend:

> **Source 1 (document):** A town-council record: "The council voted 5–2 to
> dig the new well near the market. The two dissenting members said the old
> well was sufficient."
>
> **Source 2 (oral account):** An elder's remembered story: "My grandmother
> carried water from the old well every morning. When the new well opened,
> she said the water tasted of iron, and many families kept using the old
> well for years."

5. (2 pts) Classify each source by kind (artifact / document / image / oral
   account).
6. (2 pts) For the question "How did townspeople *feel* about the new
   well?", which source is more credible? Explain using **relevance**.
7. (2 pts) For the question "What did the council *decide*?", which source
   is more credible? Explain using **intended use**.
8. (2 pts) Name one **limit** of using only these two sources, and one
   source kind you would seek next.

## Section C — Timeline and chronology (10 points)

Use the [BCE/CE timeline](../assets/bce-ce-timeline.svg).

9. (2 pts) Order oldest to youngest: 2026 CE, 2500 BCE, 1 CE, 1200 BCE,
   500 CE.
10. (2 pts) Compute the years from 3000 BCE to 1500 BCE. Show your work.
11. (3 pts) Compute the years from 776 BCE to 2026 CE. Show both steps and
    explain why the naive addition is wrong.
12. (3 pts) A learner says, "500 BCE is a bigger number than 500 CE, so 500
    BCE is more recent." Explain the error and state which is older.

## Section D — Short responses (10 points)

13. (3 pts) Study the [fictional dig illustration](../assets/fictional-dig-trench.png)
    (or the text-only alternative in Lesson 05). Order the bone, the
    coin-like disk, and the pottery shard from oldest to youngest, and cite
    the layer rule.
14. (3 pts) A classmate says, "The pottery shard is in a higher layer, so
    the pottery people were more advanced than the bone people." What is
    right and what is wrong in this claim?
15. (4 pts) Write a **compelling question** about the ancient world, **two
    supporting questions** for it, and for one supporting question name the
    source kind that would answer it. Then write 2–3 sentences explaining
    how your supporting questions help answer your compelling question.
