# Unit Assessment — Unit 04: Ancient South and East Asian Societies

**Time:** about 40 minutes. **Total: 40 points.**
Answer in your inquiry notebook. No answer key is included in
this file; the adult holds the separate answer key. Show your

## Task 1 — Map and geography (10 points)

Using [asia-map.svg](../assets/asia-map.svg) (or a redrawn
copy), complete all of the following:

(a) Label the Himalayas, the Indus, the Ganges, the Yellow
River, and the Yangtze. (5 points, 1 per label)
(b) Draw and label the summer monsoon wind arrow and write one
sentence explaining why it brings rain. (2 points)
(c) Mark one sea route and one overland route, and write one
sentence for each explaining what traveled on it. (3 points)

## Task 2 — Evidence: what it proves and what it cannot prove (10 points)

Read the two evidence cards. For each, write what it proves
and what it cannot prove, with a reason.

**Card 1 — Indus:** "A Mohenjo-daro street crosses another at
a right angle. Houses along it use bricks of the same size. A
covered drain runs beneath the street. A seal from the same
area carries five script signs no one can read."

**Card 2 — Mauryan:** "A polished stone pillar carries an
edict, in Prakrit language and Brahmi script, urging respect
for parents, kindness to servants, and respect between
religious groups."

(a) Card 1: proves / cannot prove. (5 points)
(b) Card 2: proves / cannot prove. (5 points)

## Task 3 — Belief systems, described not ranked (10 points)

(a) Describe Buddhism's Four Noble Truths in your own words
(4 points; describe, do not evaluate).
(b) Describe one difference between Confucianism and Daoism
as a difference in emphasis, not a ranking (3 points).
(c) Trace Buddhism's spread in three steps — start, route,
arrival-with-change — naming at least one carrier and one
route (3 points).

## Task 4 — Comparison and change (10 points)

Choose two societies from this unit (one South Asian, one
East Asian).

(a) Using the same question for both, compare how each society
dealt with one shared problem (water, distance, or holding a
large area together). (4 points)
(b) Name one change in one of your societies, give two causes,
and rank them, defending your top choice in one sentence.
(4 points)
(c) State whose perspective the surviving evidence favors for
one of your societies, and why. (2 points)
