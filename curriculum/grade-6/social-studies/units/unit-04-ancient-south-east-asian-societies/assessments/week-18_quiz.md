# Formative Quiz — Week 18 (Unit 04)

**Time:** about 25 minutes. Answer in your inquiry notebook.
No answer key is included in this file; the adult holds the
separate answer key.

1. Which wind pattern brings heavy summer rain to South Asia,
   and which direction does it blow in summer?
2. Name the two great rivers of the Indian plain and the two
   great rivers of China.
3. What is a terrace, and what problem does it solve?
4. Name two features of Mohenjo-daro's city plan.
5. Why can't historians read Indus inscriptions today?
6. In one sentence, what did Ashoka's edicts proclaim? In a
   second sentence, state one thing the edicts do NOT prove.
7. Name one teaching of Confucius and one teaching associated
   with Daoism, each described without ranking them.
8. Put these in order: Han, Shang, Qin, Zhou.
9. Name two things the Qin standardized, and explain why one
   of them helped trade.
10. A farmer, an official, and a merchant are asked about a
    grain tax. Write one sentence for the farmer's view, in
    the farmer's own terms.
11. Compute, showing your work: about 1600 BCE → about
    221 BCE.
12. Compare Indus cities and Han China: give one similarity in
    how each society dealt with water, and one difference in
    what held each society together.
