# Resource Pack — Unit 04: Ancient South and East Asian Societies

**Goal (one sentence):** Give a grade-6 learner and their adult
trustworthy, free, no-account routes into South and East Asian
geography, Indus cities, Mauryan and Gupta India, belief
systems and their spread, and early Chinese dynasties, matched
to this unit's tasks.
**Age fit:** Grade 6 (about ages 11–12) with adult preview of
every video and page; captions or transcripts required where
available. All external links were checked 2026-11-01 unless
noted otherwise. The adult previews for ads, age suitability,
and accuracy before assigning anything.

## Safety and access notes

- Every video is adult-previewed; if a video has unskippable
  ads, graphic content, no captions, or ranks religions, do
  not assign it — use the lesson text and the references
  above instead.
- No core objective depends on a video: Lessons 01–05, the
  practice sets, and the project are fully teachable from this
  unit's files plus the free references above.
- Belief systems in References 1–5 are described, never
  evaluated; the adult frames temple, ritual, and monastic
  content descriptively, as in the lessons.
