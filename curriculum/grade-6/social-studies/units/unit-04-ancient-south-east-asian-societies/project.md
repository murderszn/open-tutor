# Project — Two Societies, One Change: A Comparison Exhibit

**Due:** Week 18, S16 (with the unit assessment).
**Work sessions:** launched Week 16, S7; built in Week 18, S15.
**Form:** an exhibit board or poster (paper, cardboard, or
slides) plus a one-page written explanation and a source list.
Work with the adult on source selection; all external sources
come from the [Resource Pack](resource-pack.md).

## The task

Choose **two societies** from this unit — one South Asian and
one East Asian, or two from different periods (for example,
Indus cities and Han China, or Mauryan India and Qin China).
Your exhibit compares them fairly, using the same five
questions for both, and explains **one change** that happened
in each society.

## Deliverables

1. **Map panel.** A labeled map (use
   [asia-map.svg](assets/asia-map.svg) as your base or redraw
   it): both societies' locations, one river each, one route
   each, and the physical feature that most shaped each
   society. The map is labeled "schematic — not to scale."
2. **Evidence panels (one per society).** For each society:
   three pieces of evidence (a city plan feature, a text or
   edict paraphrase, an artifact or innovation), each with
   one sentence on what it proves and one sentence on what it
   cannot prove.
3. **Belief panel.** Describe one belief system connected to
   each society (or one system that traveled between them) in
   its own terms, without ranking beliefs, and trace one
   idea's route on the map.
4. **Change explanation (one page).** For each society, name
   one change, give at least two causes, rank them, and defend
   the ranking. End with one sentence on whose perspective
   the surviving evidence favors for each society.
5. **Source list.** Every fact tied to a source: unit lessons,
   Resource Pack references, or adult-approved museum/library
   pages. Imagined details (such as a traveler's guide voice)
   are labeled "imagined reconstruction," never presented as
   ancient testimony.

## Materials

Exhibit board or large paper, markers, printed or hand-drawn
copies of the unit's map and timeline, notebook drafts, and
the adult's source list. No real artifacts, no water models
for this project — it is a paper/slides exhibit, so it is
fully accessible without special materials.

## Steps

1. **Choose (Week 16, S7).** Pick two societies and one change
   for each. Check with the adult that sources exist for both.
2. **Gather (Week 16, S8 – Week 17).** Collect three evidence
   items per society from lessons and the Resource Pack. For
   each item, write its "proves / cannot prove" pair.
3. **Draft the comparison (Week 18, S13).** Answer the five
   comparison questions for both societies, in the same order.
4. **Build (Week 18, S15).** Lay out the four panels. Keep
   text short on the board; put full reasoning in the
   one-page explanation.
5. **Review (Week 18, S16).** Trade exhibits with a partner or
   the adult: can they find the map labels, the belief
   descriptions, and the ranked causes without your help? Fix
   anything they cannot find, then submit.

## Rubric (20 points)

| Criterion | 4–5 points (strong) | 2–3 points (partial) | 0–1 point (missing/weak) |
|---|---|---|---|
| Map panel (4) | Both societies, rivers, routes, and shaping features labeled; "schematic — not to scale" noted | Some labels missing or one society unclear | Map missing or mostly unlabeled |
| Evidence panels (6) | Three items per society, each with proves/cannot-prove pair; at least one honest limit named (for example, undeciphered script) | Items present but pairs incomplete, or no limit named | Fewer than two items per society, or invented "facts" |
| Belief panel (4) | Two systems (or one traveling system) described in their own terms, no ranking; one route traced | Description present but ranked, or route missing | Beliefs caricatured, missing, or evaluated |
| Change explanation (4) | Two causes per society, ranked with reasons; perspective sentence per society | Causes listed but not ranked, or perspective missing | Single-cause only, or no explanation |
| Presentation + sources (2) | Readable layout; every fact sourced; imagined details labeled | Layout or sources partly complete | Unsourced claims, or imagined detail presented as fact |

**Scoring:** 18–20 exemplary; 14–17 proficient; 10–13
developing (revise one panel with the adult); below 10, redo
with adult support. The adult records the score privately; no
learner scores are published in this repository.
