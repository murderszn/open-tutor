# Formative Quiz — Unit 03, Week 14

**Session 14 · About 20 minutes · Adult-scored the same day.**
Answer in full sentences unless the question says otherwise. No
key appears in this file; solutions are in
[answer-key.md](../answer-key.md), §Quiz.

1. Match each civilization to its river(s): (a) Mesopotamia,
   (b) Egypt, (c) Indus Valley, (d) Yellow River valley.
2. Explain what silt is and why it mattered to river-valley farmers.
3. Name two irrigation methods or structures and state what each
   one controls.
4. Why was the Nile's flood easier to plan around than the
   Tigris–Euphrates floods? Give one reason.
5. Define *surplus* and explain how surplus made specialist jobs
   possible, using one example job.
6. Name two economic jobs of temples or palaces, with one example
   from Mesopotamia or Egypt.
7. Name the two writing systems studied in this unit, where each
   was used, and one material each was written on.
8. Compute, showing work: about 3300 BCE → about 1754 BCE is how
   many years?
9. State Hammurabi's Code in one sentence: who made it, about when,
   and how many laws it contains.
10. For Law 53 (canals), state what the law reveals about Babylonian
    society and one limit of laws as evidence.
11. Give one example of change and one example of continuity from
    a farming village to a river-valley city.
12. A palace inscription praises a king for building a canal. State
    one reason to trust the inscription and one reason not to take
    it as the whole story.
