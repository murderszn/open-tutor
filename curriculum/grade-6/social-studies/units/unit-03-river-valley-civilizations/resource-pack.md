# Resource Pack — Unit 03: River-Valley Civilizations

**Goal (one sentence):** Give a grade-6 learner and their adult
trustworthy, free, no-account routes into river-valley geography,
cities, writing, and Hammurabi's Code, matched to this unit's tasks.
**Age fit:** Grade 6 (about ages 11–12) with adult preview of every
video and page; captions or transcripts required where available.
All external links were checked 2026-10-25. The adult previews for
ads, age suitability, and accuracy before assigning anything.

## Safety and access notes

- Every video is adult-previewed; if a video has unskippable ads,
  graphic punishment dramatizations, or no captions, do not assign
  it — use the lesson text and the museum references instead.
- No core objective depends on a video: Lessons 01–05, the practice
  sets, and the project are fully teachable from this unit's files
  plus the free museum/library references above.
- Belief systems in References 1–8 are described, never evaluated;
  the adult frames temple and ritual content descriptively, as in
  the lessons.
