# Week-08 Formative Quiz — U02 (Weeks 7–8)

**Unit:** [U02 — Early Humans: Agriculture and Settlements](../README.md)
**Session:** 8 of 16 · **Time:** ~20 minutes · **Scoring:** adult-scored;
solutions are in the [answer key](../answer-key.md), never in this file.

Answer in complete sentences unless the item says otherwise. The adult may
read items aloud and scribe dictated answers.

## Part 1 — Environments and forager life (Lessons 01)

1. List the four daily needs of a forager group.
2. A forager band camps where a river meets the sea. Name one advantage and
   one constraint of this spot.
3. Explain in two sentences why most forager groups moved with the seasons.

## Part 2 — Causes and trade-offs (Lesson 02)

4. Name two causes of the agricultural revolution and explain each in one
   sentence.
5. Define **domesticate**. Give one plant example and one animal example.
6. Complete the benefit/cost chart for this situation: *A band considers
   planting barley near their camp.* Write at least two benefits and two
   costs.
7. Sort each as **change** or **continuity**. Write one sentence defending
   each sort.
   a. "People began storing food for months."
   b. "People needed drinking water every day."
8. A learner says, "Farming was obviously a great idea — that's why everyone
   did it." Write two sentences explaining what this argument misses.

## Part 3 — Reasoning (both weeks)

9. **Explain your reasoning:** Was the Natufian culture (sedentary foragers,
   about 12,500 BCE) already "farming"? Defend your answer in two or three
   sentences using the definition of farming.
10. A band must choose between two valleys. Valley X has a river, wild
    grains, and caves. Valley Y has richer soil for planting but no water
    source nearby. Which valley suits a **forager** band best, and which
    suits a band that wants to **farm**? Explain each choice in one sentence.

**Total: 10 items.** The adult records which objectives need re-teaching
before Week 9: items 1–3 → Objective 1 (environments); items 4–8 →
Objective 2 (causes and trade-offs); items 9–10 → reasoning across both.
