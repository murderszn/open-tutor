# U02 Unit Assessment — Early Humans: Agriculture and Settlements

**Unit:** [U02 — Early Humans: Agriculture and Settlements](../README.md)
**Session:** 16 of 16 · **Time:** ~40 minutes · **Scoring:** adult-scored;
solutions are in the [answer key](../answer-key.md), never in this file.

The adult may read items aloud and scribe dictated answers. Reference sheets
allowed: the agriculture timeline, the surplus-flow diagram, and the
learner's inquiry notebook.

## Task 1 — Environment reasoning (Objective 1)

A fictional band, the **Cedar Fork band**, must pick a winter camp. Two
sites are described:

- **Site A:** a south-facing valley with a spring, oak trees (acorns in
  autumn), a dry cave, and a ridge above the flood line.
- **Site B:** a flat plain with rich soil, no stream within a day's walk,
  no shelter from winter wind, but excellent visibility for miles.

a. Which site should a **forager** band choose? Defend your choice with two
   environmental reasons. (3 points)
b. Which site would better suit a band that wants to **start farming**?
   Explain in two sentences. (2 points)

## Task 2 — Benefit/cost analysis (Objective 2)

The Cedar Fork band considers planting wheat at Site B and carrying water
from a distant spring.

a. Complete a benefit/cost chart with at least three benefits and three
   costs. (6 points)
b. Write a 2–3 sentence verdict: should they do it? Your verdict must name
   one genuine cost you are accepting. (3 points)

## Task 3 — Timeline and multi-cause (Objectives 2, 4)

Use the [agriculture timeline](../assets/agriculture-timeline.svg).

a. About how many years passed between early cultivation (about
   9,600 BCE) and the founding of Çatalhöyük (about 7,100 BCE)? Show your
   subtraction. (2 points)
b. Write a 3–4 sentence multi-cause explanation for why farming began in
   the Fertile Crescent. Include at least three causes and mark any debated
   cause as debated. (4 points)

## Task 4 — Comparing regions (Objective 4)

a. Complete the comparison chart (4 points):

| | Fertile Crescent | Mesoamerica |
|---|---|---|
| One starter crop | | |
| Starter animals | | |
| Farming begins (about) | | |

b. What is the strongest evidence that farming was invented independently
   in the two regions rather than copied? Answer in two or three sentences.
   (3 points)

## Task 5 — Perspectives (Objective 5)

A farmer and a forager argue about whether settling down was a good idea.

a. Write the **farmer's** view in 2–3 sentences, using at least one piece
   of unit evidence. (3 points)
b. Write the **forager's** view in 2–3 sentences, using at least one piece
   of unit evidence. (3 points)
c. In one sentence, explain why both views can use real evidence and still
   disagree. (2 points)

## Task 6 — Surplus reasoning (Objective 3)

a. Explain the link between a farming surplus and a full-time potter in two
   or three sentences. (3 points)
b. Name one innovation from this unit and explain how it helped farmers
   grow more food per worker. (2 points)

**Total: 40 points.** Score bands (adult judgment, not a grade): 34–40 —
ready for U03; 26–33 — review flagged objectives, then proceed; below 26 —
re-teach flagged objectives with the teacher guide's reteach moves before
U03.
