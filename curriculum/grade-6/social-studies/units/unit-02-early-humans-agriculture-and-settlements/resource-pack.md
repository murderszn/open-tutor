# U02 Resource Pack

**Unit:** [U02 — Early Humans: Agriculture and Settlements](README.md)

**Goal:** Give the guiding adult verified, age-appropriate starting points
for every core objective of this unit — forager environments, the causes
and trade-offs of the agricultural revolution, surplus and specialization,
and the Fertile Crescent / Mesoamerica comparison. **Age fit:** 11–12
(grade 6), adult-guided; the adult previews every video for ads, age
suitability, and accuracy, and pre-selects all real-world examples.

All links below were checked 2026-10-19. Videos require captions or
transcripts; the adult confirms captions are on before showing. Every core
objective has a free, no-account route.

## Focused queries

Use these when you need more than the curated links. Prefer results from
museum (.edu / si.edu / .org museum), archive, and government (.gov) sites.

1. `site:si.edu agriculture origins lesson` — Smithsonian explainers on
   farming's beginnings.
2. `site:britishmuseum.org Neolithic farming tools` — museum objects with
   findspot and material records.
3. `Çatalhöyük kids virtual tour` — the Digital Kit and house reconstructions
   (preview for reading level before assigning).
4. `maize teosinte domestication for kids` — classroom-friendly tellings of
   the Balsas Valley research (check dates against the unit's "about 8,700
   years ago" before use).
5. `Neolithic Revolution causes trade-offs middle school` — classroom
   routines for benefit/cost framing (reconcile any single-cause telling
   with the unit's multi-cause approach).
