# Resource Pack — Unit 05: Ancient Greece

**Goal (one sentence):** Give a grade-6 learner and their adult
trustworthy, free, no-account routes into Greek city-states,
Athenian democracy, Mediterranean trade, and the spread of ideas,
matched to this unit's tasks.
**Age fit:** Grade 6 (about ages 11–12) with adult preview of every
video and page; captions or transcripts required where available.
All external links were checked 2026-11-07. The adult previews for
ads, age suitability, and accuracy before assigning anything.

## Safety and access notes

- Every video is adult-previewed; if a video has unskippable ads,
  graphic war dramatizations, or no captions, do not assign
  it — use the lesson text and the museum/library references instead.
- No core objective depends on a video: Lessons 01–05, the practice
  sets, and the project are fully teachable from this unit's files
  plus the free museum/library references above.
- References 4 and 6 are adult-side scholarly texts; learners use
  the lesson summaries, not the articles themselves.
- Slavery and exclusion are named plainly in the lessons; the adult
  frames them factually and briefly, as in the teacher guide.
