# Unit Assessment — Unit 05: Ancient Greece

**Session 16 · About 40 minutes · Adult-scored the same day.**
Answer in full sentences unless the question says otherwise. No
key appears in this file; solutions are in
[answer-key.md](../answer-key.md), §Assessment. Total: 40 points.

## Task 1 — Map and trade routes (8 points)

Use [aegean-trade-map.svg](../assets/aegean-trade-map.svg).

(a) Name the harbor of Athens and the sea between Greece and Asia
Minor. (2 points)
(b) Trace the grain route: name the two narrow straits grain ships
passed through on their way from the Black Sea to Athens.
(2 points)
(c) Name one import and one export shown by the map's routes, and
explain in one sentence why Athens needed to import grain.
(2 points)
(d) The map is labeled "SCHEMATIC — NOT TO SCALE." Explain in one
sentence what that label means for how you may use the map.
(2 points)

## Task 2 — Deliberation: who decides? (10 points)

The assembly must decide: spend League money on ten more triremes,
or on a new storehouse at Piraeus.

(a) Explain the two-step path of the decision: what the Council of
500 does first, and what the assembly does second. (4 points)
(b) A metic grain merchant asks to speak at the assembly about the
decision. Can he speak or vote? Explain why, using the
citizenship rule. (3 points)
(c) Name one way the council's procedure and the assembly's
procedure differ, and explain how that difference could change the
outcome. (3 points)

## Task 3 — Trade: supply, demand, and who gains (10 points)

The grain-price table below is **fictional classroom-model data** —
invented numbers for practice, not real ancient prices.

| Year | Grain ships arriving | Price (drachmas a measure) |
|---|---|---|
| Good harvest | 120 | 4 |
| Bad harvest | 50 | 9 |
| War closes the sea route | 30 | 14 |

(a) Explain why the price is highest in the war year, using supply
and demand. (3 points)
(b) The Delian League clears pirates from the Aegean, and 130 ships
arrive the next year. Predict whether the price would be above or
below 4 drachmas, and explain why. (3 points)
(c) Name one gainer and one loser from the high price in the war
year, and explain how the same price helps one and hurts the
other. (4 points)

## Task 4 — Significance and legacy (12 points)

(a) Apply the two-question significance test to the Delian League:
did it change how people lived, and did its effects last? Give one
reason for each answer. (4 points)
(b) Build a claim: "Athenian democracy's most valuable idea was
that ordinary people should vote on the laws that govern them."
Give two pieces of evidence for the claim and one limit of the
evidence. (6 points)
(c) Name one group the record does not let us hear from about
Athenian democracy, and write the honest sentence that belongs in
your answer. (2 points)
