# Formative Quiz — Unit 05, Week 23

**Session 14 · About 20 minutes · Adult-scored the same day.**
Answer in full sentences unless the question says otherwise. No
key appears in this file; solutions are in
[answer-key.md](../answer-key.md), §Quiz.

1. Define *polis* in one sentence.
2. Name the three groups who lived in Athens but could not vote.
3. What did the Council of 500 do, and what did the assembly do?
   Give one job for each body.
4. Explain what ostracism was and how long the exile lasted.
5. Name two parts of Sparta's government and state what each one
   did.
6. Compute, showing work: about 508 BCE → about 322 BCE is how
   many years?
7. Name two things Athens imported and two things it exported.
8. Explain what the Delian League was, and name one way it changed
   after 454 BCE.
9. A bad harvest in the Black Sea means fewer grain ships reach
   Piraeus. Predict what happens to the price of grain in Athens,
   and explain why with supply and demand.
10. Name one idea that spread through the Greek world by diffusion,
    and name one carrier (ships, colonies, or gathering places)
    that moved it.
11. Apply the two-question significance test to Cleisthenes: did he
    change how people lived, and did his effects last? Give one
    reason for each answer.
12. A temple inscription paid for with Delian League tribute calls
    Athens "the protector of Greece." State one reason to trust the
    inscription and one reason not to take it as the whole story.
