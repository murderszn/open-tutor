# Formative Quiz — Unit 02 (end of Week 8)

**Session S08 · ~25 minutes · Objectives UO5, UO6, UO7.**
Short, low-stakes: the adult reviews it the same day and reteaches before
S09. Solutions in the [answer key](../answer-key.md#week-08-quiz).

1. (3/4) ÷ (1/2) = ? Simplify. *(UO5)*
2. Rosa has 2 1/2 lb of masa. Each concha uses 1/4 lb. How many conchas?
   Write the division and solve. *(UO5)*
3. (5/6) ÷ (2/3) = ? Simplify, and give the multiplication check. *(UO5)*
4. Estimate, then compute: (a) 24.8 + 3.65; (b) 40 − 12.75. *(UO6)*
5. 2.6 × 3.4 = ? Show the whole-number product and the decimal placement.
   *(UO6)*
6. 9.6 ÷ 2.4 = ? What whole-number division did you actually compute?
   *(UO6)*
7. GCF(30, 45) = ? Write 30 + 45 as a multiple of a sum and check it.
   *(UO7)*
8. LCM(4, 10) = ? Show the multiple lists. *(UO7)*

**Scoring guide:** 7–8 correct = on track; 5–6 = review the missed
objective's lesson before the project; below 5 = reteach the missed
objectives in S09 before launching the project (shift the project one
session and compress S12).
