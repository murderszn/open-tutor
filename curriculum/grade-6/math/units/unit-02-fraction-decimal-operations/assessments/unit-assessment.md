# Unit Assessment — Unit 02: Fraction and Decimal Operations

**Session S14 · ~40 minutes · Objectives UO5, UO6, UO7.**
No calculator. Estimates required where marked. Full solutions and
scoring notes in the [answer key](../answer-key.md#unit-assessment).

## Part A — Fraction division (UO5)

1. Compute and simplify: (7/8) ÷ (1/4). Show the invert-and-multiply
   step.
2. A 5/6-yard ribbon is cut into 1/3-yard pieces for bows. How many
   bows? Write the division, solve, and verify with a multiplication
   check.
3. **Explain:** in two or three sentences, explain why
   (2/3) ÷ (3/4) = 8/9, using either common denominators or a
   multiplication check. (This is the standard's own example.)

## Part B — Decimal and whole-number fluency (UO6)

4. Compute: (a) 58.34 + 7.9; (b) 100 − 43.28. Show decimal alignment.
5. Estimate, then compute: 4.7 × 2.5. Write the estimate beside the
   exact answer.
6. Estimate, then compute: 86.4 ÷ 1.8. What whole-number division did
   you compute? Verify by multiplication.
7. 3,276 ÷ 12 with the standard algorithm. Check by multiplying back.

## Part C — GCF, LCM, and structure (UO7)

8. GCF(28, 42) = ? Write 28 + 42 as a multiple of a sum with no common
   factor inside, and check both sides.
9. Two bus routes leave every 9 minutes and every 12 minutes. They just
   left together. In how many minutes do they next leave together? Show
   the multiple lists and name whether you used GCF or LCM and why.

## Part D — Mixed problem solving (UO5, UO6)

10. **Error analysis:** "Maya says (1/2) ÷ (3/4) = 3/8 because
    (1/2) × (3/4) = 3/8." Explain her error precisely, give the correct
    quotient, and verify it.
11. A 2.5-lb bag of flour; each batch of pan dulce uses 3/4 lb. How
    many *full* batches can Rosa make? How much flour is left over?
12. Compute with a plan line written first: (2/3 + 1/6) × 4 − 1.5.

**Scoring:** 11–12 = exceeds; 9–10 = meets; 7–8 = approaching (reteach
the missed objective before U03); below 7 = revisit Lessons 01–04 and
the practice sets, then re-assess with the quiz items.
