# Practice — Unit 02: Fraction and Decimal Operations

Three leveled sets plus two games. Use Set A in S06, Set B in S07, Set C
in S12; games fit any practice session. All solutions are in the
[answer key](answer-key.md#practice).

## Set A — fraction division fluency (UO5)

1. (3/4) ÷ (1/2) = ?
2. (2/3) ÷ (5/6) = ? Simplify.
3. 5 ÷ (1/3) = ? Draw the model in words (how many thirds in 5 wholes?).
4. (1/2) ÷ 4 = ? What does the quotient mean in a sharing story?
5. Rosa has 9/10 liter of glaze. Each cake needs 3/10 liter. How many
   cakes? Show the common-denominator reasoning.
6. A 7/8-yard ribbon is cut into 1/4-yard pieces. How many pieces?
7. **Which is larger?** (4/5) ÷ (1/2) or (4/5) ÷ 2? Decide without full
   computation, then verify.
8. **Create and solve:** write a "how many groups?" story for
   (5/6) ÷ (1/3) and a "how much per group?" story for (5/6) ÷ 3.
   Solve both.

## Set B — decimal operations (UO6)

1. 45.6 + 7.89 = ? (Align; estimate first.)
2. 60 − 18.45 = ?
3. 3.05 × 4.2 = ? (Whole-number product first, then place the point.)
4. 0.45 × 0.8 = ?
5. 5.6 ÷ 0.7 = ? (What whole-number division is this?)
6. 73.5 ÷ 2.5 = ? Estimate, compute, verify by multiplication.
7. 2,156 ÷ 14 = ? Check by multiplying back.
8. **Find the planted error:** "12.5 × 4 = 5.00." The estimate was "about
   50." Explain the error and give the correct product.

## Set C — GCF/LCM and mixed review (UO5, UO6, UO7)

1. GCF(36, 54) = ? Then write 36 + 54 as a multiple of a sum.
2. LCM(6, 9) = ? Show both multiple lists.
3. 42 students and 56 parents need identical supply kits (everything
   used). How many kits? What is in each?
4. (11/12) ÷ (1/4) = ? Simplify.
5. 9.84 ÷ 1.2 = ? Estimate first.
6. Compute: (3/4 − 1/3) × 6 + 0.5. Plan line first.
7. Two juice offers: 2/3 L for $1.80 vs 3/4 L for $2.10. Better buy?
   (Price per liter, as in Lesson 05.)
8. **Explain your reasoning:** why does dividing by a fraction smaller
   than 1 give a quotient larger than the dividend? Use a story.

## Game 1 — Fraction Servings Sprint (S06, pairs)

**Materials:** a deck of 24 "supply cards" (adult prepares; each shows a
fraction amount: 1/2, 2/3, 3/4, 5/6, …) and 12 "serving cards" (1/2, 1/3,
1/4, 1/6, …). Fictional practice data.

**Play:** players draw one supply card and one serving card, then race to
write the division and quotient ("how many servings?"). Both players
check each other's work with a multiplication check; a correct quotient
with a correct check scores a point. First to 8 points wins.

**Rules that teach:** you must state the *question type* aloud before
computing ("how many 1/4s in 3/4?"); a quotient without the check scores
no point.

## Game 2 — Factor Pairs Race (S12, pairs or teams)

**Materials:** hundred chart, two colored pencils, a 1–12 die or spinner.

**Play:** roll twice to get two numbers (reroll pairs above the UO7
band: numbers ≤ 100 for GCF work, and keep LCM pairs ≤ 12 as the
standard requires). Teams race to (a) list factor pairs and name the
GCF, or (b) list multiples and name the LCM — the adult calls which
before each round. First correct team scores.

**Rules that teach:** the full factor/multiple list must be shown; a
bare answer scores nothing. Bonus point for writing the distributive
rewrite (e.g., 24 + 36 = 12(2 + 3)) in a GCF round.
