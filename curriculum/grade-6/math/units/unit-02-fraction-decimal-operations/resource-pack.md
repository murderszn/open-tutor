# Resource Pack — Unit 02: Fraction and Decimal Operations

**Goal:** Give the adult one shelf of free, vetted supports for teaching
grade-6 fraction division (meaning, models, invert-and-multiply),
multi-digit and decimal computation with standard algorithms, and
GCF/LCM with the distributive property. **Age fit:** grades 5–7 (about
ages 10–13); written for the adult to excerpt and explain at a 6th-grade
level, never assigned whole.

All external entries checked 2026-10-18. Videos are supplements to
teaching, not replacements. No account is required for any core route
below.
