# Week-04 Formative Quiz — Unit 01 (UO1–UO2)

**Session S08** · 12 items · adult reviews the same day ·
[answer key](../answer-key.md#week-04-quiz) is separate — do not show it to
the learner. Target: 9 of 12 correct with units on every rate.

## Part A — Ratio language and order (UO1)

1. A drawer holds 5 forks and 8 spoons. Write the ratio of forks to
   spoons. Then write the ratio of spoons to forks.
2. A paint mixture is 3 parts yellow to 2 parts blue. Write the ratio of
   blue to yellow. If someone writes 3:2 for "blue to yellow," what did
   they get wrong?
3. Write the ratio 4 to 9 in colon form and in fraction form. When is the
   fraction form misleading? (One sentence.)

## Part B — Equivalent ratios (UO1)

4. Complete the ratio table for 3:7 through scale factor 4 (rows ×1, ×2,
   ×3, ×4).
5. Find the missing value: 5:6 = 35:___. Name the scale factor.
6. A tape diagram shows 2 shaded parts of a 5-part bar (ingredient A) matched
   against a 5-part bar (ingredient B). Amara scales the recipe by 3.
   (a) What ratio does the diagram show? (b) What is the scaled recipe?
7. **Error analysis:** "2:3 → 6:9 by adding 4 to the first part and 6 to the
   second part." The final ratio is equivalent — but is the reasoning
   correct? Explain, and name the real scale factor.

## Part C — Unit rates (UO2)

8. 6 notebooks cost $10.50. Find the price per notebook, with units.
9. A bus travels 180 miles in 4 hours at constant speed. (a) Find miles per
   hour. (b) How far will it travel in 7 hours at the same speed? Check
   your answer.
10. Two offers for the same applesauce (fictional practice prices):
    24 oz for $4.80 and 16 oz for $3.52. Find each unit price and name the
    better buy with evidence.
11. A printer prints 90 pages in 6 minutes. Find pages per minute, then
    predict the pages printed in 15 minutes.
12. **Explain your reasoning:** A learner computes 3 ÷ 150 = 0.02 for "a
    car travels 150 miles in 3 hours" and reports the speed as "0.02."
    (a) What question does 0.02 actually answer? (b) Why is reporting it as
    the car's speed wrong? (c) Give the correct speed with units.
