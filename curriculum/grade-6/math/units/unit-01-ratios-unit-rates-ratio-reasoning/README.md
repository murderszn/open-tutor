# Unit 01 — Ratios, Unit Rates, and Ratio Reasoning

**Grade 6 Mathematics · Weeks 3–6 · 16 sessions (~45 minutes each, 4 per week)**

**Standards:** 6.RP.A.1, 6.RP.A.2, 6.RP.A.3.a, 6.RP.A.3.b, 6.RP.A.3.c
(first pass: percent as a rate per 100), 6.RP.A.3.d

**Issue:** [#30](https://github.com/murderszn/open-tutor/issues/30)
(section U01 — draft delivery; not educator-reviewed)

## Overview

This unit builds the ratio-reasoning engine the rest of the year runs on.
Learners first learn to **say what a ratio claims** — two quantities, in a
given order, compared as a relationship — then to **grow it without breaking
it**: equivalent ratios built by scaling both quantities by the same factor,
read from ratio tables, tape diagrams, and double number lines. Next they
**shrink the second quantity to one**: unit rates, with unit pricing and
constant speed as the two signature contexts. Week 5 adds the two
applications ratio reasoning unlocks: **percent as a rate per 100** (finding a
percent of a quantity; finding-the-whole waits for U05/U08) and **unit
conversion by ratio reasoning** with proper unit manipulation. The unit closes
with a week of review, the lemonade-stand investigation project, and the
culminating assessment.

The unit absorbs and supersedes the track's
[starter lesson](../../starter-lesson.md): its three sessions (ratio language,
equivalent ratios, unit rate) become the backbone of Lessons 01–03, with its
answer checks separated into the teacher guide as the audit required. The
starter lesson file remains available as an alternative entry point.

## Essential question

*When two quantities change together, how do we describe their relationship,
grow it fairly, and compare competing offers using the same mathematical
tools?*

## Prerequisites (grade-5 track end-of-year expectations)

- Fluent multi-digit multiplication (standard algorithm); division with
  up-to-four-digit dividends interpreting remainders from context
- Fraction equivalence and scaling reasoning; fraction-as-division;
  decimal place value to thousandths and decimal arithmetic to hundredths
- Measurement conversions within one system (length, weight, volume);
  reading a two-column table

The diagnostic weeks (Weeks 1–2) baseline these. Sessions 01–02 re-teach
equal-groups scaling before ratio language is assumed, and ratio order is
rebuilt from concrete comparisons even if the starter lesson was used.

## Unit objectives (measurable; adult-assessed)

| # | Objective | Track objective | Standard(s) |
|---|---|---|---|
| UO1 | Given a comparison of two quantities, state the ratio in order using ratio language, and generate equivalent ratios using tables, tape diagrams, and double number lines, including finding missing values. | 1 | 6.RP.A.1, 6.RP.A.3.a |
| UO2 | Compute unit rates from ratio data — including unit pricing and constant speed — with correct units, and interpret what the rate means in context. | 2 | 6.RP.A.2, 6.RP.A.3.b |
| UO3 | Express a percent as a rate per 100 and find a percent of a quantity; relate percent to equivalent-ratio reasoning. | 3 (partial) | 6.RP.A.3.c |
| UO4 | Convert measurement units by ratio reasoning, manipulating and transforming units appropriately when multiplying or dividing quantities. | 4 | 6.RP.A.3.d |

## Vocabulary

ratio · ratio language · order · equivalent ratio · scale factor · ratio
table · tape diagram · double number line · missing value · unit rate ·
per · unit pricing · constant speed · percent · rate per 100 · conversion
factor · equivalent measurement

(Vocabulary is taught with objects and drawings first, the word second;

introduced as the spoken form of a rate — dollars per notebook, miles per
hour.)

## Verified standards / source notes

Reference framework: **Common Core State Standards for Mathematics**,
Grade 6, Ratios and Proportional Relationships, as published at
[thecorestandards.org/Math](https://thecorestandards.org/Math) (Grade 6 RP
page at <http://thecorestandards.org/Math/Content/RP/>; codes and
descriptions re-verified 2026-10-11 for this unit). Exact official wording
used:

- **6.RP.A.1** — "Understand the concept of a ratio and use ratio language
  to describe a ratio relationship between two quantities."
- **6.RP.A.2** — "Understand the concept of a unit rate a/b associated with
  a ratio a:b with b ≠ 0, and use rate language in the context of a ratio
  relationship."
- **6.RP.A.3** — "Use ratio and rate reasoning to solve real-world and
  mathematical problems, e.g., by reasoning about tables of equivalent
  ratios, tape diagrams, double number line diagrams, or equations."
  - **6.RP.A.3.a** — "Make tables of equivalent ratios relating quantities
    with whole-number measurements, find missing values in the tables, and
    plot the pairs of values on the coordinate plane. Use tables to compare
    ratios."
  - **6.RP.A.3.b** — "Solve unit rate problems including those involving
    unit pricing and constant speed."
  - **6.RP.A.3.c** — "Find a percent of a quantity as a rate per 100 (e.g.,
    30% of a quantity means 30/100 times the quantity); solve problems
    involving finding the whole, given a part and the percent." (This unit
    covers the "rate per 100 / find a percent" half; finding-the-whole
    problems arrive with equation work in U05 and the modeling unit U08, per
    the track crosswalk.)
  - **6.RP.A.3.d** — "Use ratio reasoning to convert measurement units;
    manipulate and transform units appropriately when multiplying or dividing
    quantities."

**No state adoption, accreditation, or alignment certification is claimed.**
These codes identify the public framework the lessons were designed against.

## Pacing — 16 sessions

Session types rotate across concept lesson, guided practice, fluency/practice
game, review, and assessment. Every session names its specific activity and
its success criterion. Calculator may check work only after the written
method is shown — never as the first method. Fluency work is strategy-based,
never timed.

### Week 3 — Ratio language, order, and equivalent ratios (UO1)

| Session | Activity (named) | Success criterion |
|---|---|---|
| S01 | **Concept lesson** — [Lesson 01](lessons/lesson-01-ratio-language-and-order.md): a ratio compares two quantities in a given order; bead-tray and lemonade-stand models; the lemonade-stand illustration activity | Learner states ratios in order for pictured situations and explains why 3:5 and 5:3 are different claims |
| S02 | **Guided practice** — Lesson 01 guided + independent practice with the error-analysis item (additive vs multiplicative scaling) | 7 of 8 practice tasks correct, with the error-analysis explanation naming the equal-factor rule |
| S03 | **Concept lesson** — [Lesson 02](lessons/lesson-02-equivalent-ratios-tables-tape-diagrams-double-number-lines.md): equivalent ratios by scaling both quantities; ratio tables, tape diagrams, double number lines | Learner completes a ratio table to 4 rows and finds a missing value with a stated scale factor |
| S04 | **Fluency game** — Ratio Table Relay ([practice.md](practice.md#game-1-ratio-table-relay)): teams build equivalent-ratio rows against a target | Team reaches the target ratio in 4 of 5 rounds, naming each scale factor aloud |

### Week 4 — Double number lines and unit rates (UO1 → UO2)

| Session | Activity (named) | Success criterion |
|---|---|---|
| S05 | **Concept lesson** — Lesson 02 continued: double number lines as ratio tools; finding missing values between whole-number pairs | Learner reads a missing value from a double number line and checks it in the ratio table |
| S06 | **Concept lesson** — [Lesson 03](lessons/lesson-03-unit-rates-pricing-and-speed.md): shrink the second quantity to one; unit pricing and constant speed with correct units | Learner computes two unit rates, labels units, and interprets one in a sentence |
| S07 | **Practice + game** — Lesson 03 practice sets; Price-per-Unit Hunt ([practice.md](practice.md#game-2-price-per-unit-hunt)): compare grocery offers by unit price | Learner picks the better buy in 4 of 5 offers with unit-price evidence |
| S08 | **Review + formative check** — mixed UO1–UO2 practice; [Week-04 quiz](assessments/week-04_quiz.md) (12 items), same-day adult feedback, targeted reteach block | Adult records which of UO1–UO2 is insecure; reteach plan set before Week 5 |

### Week 5 — Percent and unit conversion (UO3, UO4)

| Session | Activity (named) | Success criterion |
|---|---|---|
| S09 | **Concept lesson** — [Lesson 04](lessons/lesson-04-percent-as-rate-per-100.md): percent means "out of 100"; ratio-table percent of a quantity | Learner finds a percent of a quantity two ways (fraction-of-100 and ratio table) and both agree |
| S10 | **Guided practice** — Lesson 04 practice with error-analysis; percent puzzles from practice.md | 7 of 8 tasks correct; error-analysis names the "per 100" meaning |
| S11 | **Concept lesson** — [Lesson 05](lessons/lesson-05-unit-conversion-by-ratio-reasoning.md): conversion factors as ratios; manipulating units when multiplying/dividing | Learner converts length, weight, and time measurements with units carried and canceled correctly |
| S12 | **Applied practice** — mixed percent + conversion problems; starter-lesson Session-3 problems revisited with keys separated | All revisit computations correct (50 mph; $3/notebook; 4/6 = 2/3) with unit labels |

### Week 6 — Review, project, and culminating assessment (all objectives)

| Session | Activity (named) | Success criterion |
|---|---|---|
| S13 | **Review** — plot equivalent-ratio pairs on the coordinate plane (6.RP.A.3.a); ratio-reasoning games from practice.md | Learner plots 4 equivalent-ratio pairs and explains what the line means |
| S14 | **Project workshop** — [Lemonade Stand investigation](project.md): recipe scaling, unit-cost comparison, conversion poster (draft) | Draft ratio table and at least one unit-price comparison complete |
| S15 | **Project finish** — revise poster, write the rate-interpretation paragraph, peer/aloud review | All deliverables present; rubric criteria at "solid" or above |
| S16 | **Culminating assessment** — [unit assessment](assessments/unit-assessment.md) (16 items), then project gallery | Assessment scored with [answer key](answer-key.md); reteach decisions recorded |

## Retrieval built in

- S01–S02 re-teach equal-groups scaling from grade 5 before ratio language.
- S05 double number lines reuse the S03 ratio-table numbers (same data, new
  tool).
- S06 unit rates reuse the S03–S04 equivalent ratios (shrink to one).
- S09 percent reuses the S03 tables with a 100-row.
- S11 conversion reuses UO2 rate division and UO1 scaling.
- S13 plotting on the coordinate plane previews U03's coordinate work.
- The Week-6 assessment spirals all four objectives; the teacher guide names
  what "ready for U02" looks like.

## Differentiation

- **Supports:** two-color counters and ratio-table trays with pre-labeled
  headings; oral/pointing response modes; adult scribes extended writing when
  stamina lags; pre-drawn tape-diagram frames for Lesson 02; fraction and
  decimal reference cards from grade 5.
- **Extensions:** non-whole scale factors in ratio tables (e.g., 2:3 →
  5:?); percent-finding with decimal benchmarks (17.5%); multi-step
  conversions (miles per hour → feet per second setup only, using unit
  factors); writing the general rule for equivalent ratios (multiply by n/n).
- **Language access:** vocabulary wall with objects; home-language labels;
  sentence frames ("The ratio of ___ to ___ is ___").
