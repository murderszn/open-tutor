# Resource Pack — Unit 01: Ratios, Unit Rates, and Ratio Reasoning

**Goal:** Give the adult one shelf of free, vetted supports for teaching
grade-6 ratio language, equivalent ratios, unit rates (pricing, speed),
percent as a rate per 100, and ratio-based unit conversion. **Age fit:**
grades 5–7 (about ages 10–13); written for the adult to excerpt and explain
at a 6th-grade level, never assigned whole.

All external entries checked 2026-10-11. Videos are supplements to teaching,
not replacements. No account is required for any core route below.
