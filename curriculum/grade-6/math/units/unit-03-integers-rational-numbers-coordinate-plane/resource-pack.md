# Resource Pack — Unit 03: Integers, Rational Numbers, and the Coordinate Plane

**Goal:** Give the adult one shelf of free, vetted supports for teaching
grade-6 opposite quantities, rational-number ordering, absolute value,
and the four-quadrant coordinate plane. **Age fit:** grades 5–7 (about
ages 10–13); written for the adult to excerpt and explain at a 6th-grade
level, never assigned whole.

All external entries checked 2026-10-25. Videos are supplements to
teaching, not replacements. No account is required for any core route
below.
