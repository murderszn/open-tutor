# Week-12 Formative Quiz — Unit 03 (UO8–UO11)

**Session S08** · 10 items · adult reviews the same day ·
[answer key](../answer-key.md#week-12-quiz) is separate — do not show it
to the learner. Target: 8 of 10 correct; any miss on items 4, 5, or 9
signals a reteach of Lessons 03–04 before the project.

## Part A — Opposite quantities and the number line (UO8, UO9)

1. A fictional cove report gives three heights relative to sea level:
   a gull at 6 meters above the water, a flag at the waterline, and a
   crab 4 meters below the surface. (a) Write each height as a signed
   number. (b) In one sentence, what does 0 mean here?
2. Write: (a) the opposite of −6; (b) −(−6); (c) the opposite of 0.
3. Order from least to greatest: −3.5, 0, −1, 2.5. Show your number-line
   reasoning in one sentence.

## Part B — Order and absolute value (UO10, UO11)

4. True or false, correcting any false statement: (a) −4 > −9;
   (b) −2 < −5.
5. Find |−8| and |3|. Then write two true sentences: one comparing −8
   and 3 by order, one comparing them by magnitude.
6. Two fictional balances: −$35 and −$20. (a) Which balance is
   greater? (b) Which debt is greater? (c) Write an inequality for
   each answer.

## Part C — Quadrants, reflections, distances (UO9, UO12)

7. Name the quadrant (or axis) for each point: (−3, 5), (4, −2),
   (0, 3).
8. Start at (5, −1). Write its reflection across the x-axis and its
   reflection across the y-axis, with the quadrant of each result.
9. Find the distance between (−2, 4) and (−2, −3). Show the
   absolute-value subtraction.

## Part D — Order in context (UO10)

10. Two fictional towns report −6 °C and −11 °C. (a) Which town is
    warmer? (b) Write the comparison with > or <. (c) In one
    sentence, explain why the warmer town's number has smaller-looking
    digits.
