# Unit Assessment — Unit 03: Integers, Rational Numbers, and the Coordinate Plane (UO8–UO12)

**Session S14** · 12 items · adult reviews the same day ·
[answer key](../answer-key.md#unit-assessment) is separate — do not show
it to the learner. Target: 10 of 12 correct for readiness for Unit 04;
below 8, reteach the missed objectives with Lessons 03–05 and Practice
Sets B–C before moving on. Scores guide teaching; this is not a
standardized or validated test.

## Part A — Opposite quantities, opposites, order (UO8, UO9, UO10)

1. A fictional trail guide lists a hilltop at 45 meters above sea level
   and a cave floor at 30 meters below sea level. (a) Write both as
   signed numbers. (b) What does 0 mean in this guide?
2. (a) Find −(−12). (b) Write the opposite of 7/2. (c) On a number
   line, between which two integers does −7/2 sit?
3. Order from least to greatest: −4.5, −1.25, 0, 2.75, −0.5.
4. Two fictional account balances are −$48 and −$12. (a) Write a true
   inequality comparing them. (b) Which debt is larger, and how does
   your inequality relate to that answer? (Answer in two sentences.)

## Part B — Absolute value (UO11)

5. Find |−15| and |9|. (a) Which number has the greater magnitude?
   (b) Which number is greater by order? (c) Write one sentence
   explaining how both answers can be true.
6. A fictional shop's balance is −$64. (a) What is the size of the
   debt? (b) A second balance is less than −$64. Is its debt greater
   or less than $64? Explain with absolute value.

## Part C — Quadrants, reflections, distances (UO9, UO12)

7. Start at (−6, −2). (a) Name its quadrant. (b) Write its reflection
   across the y-axis, with quadrant. (c) Write its reflection across
   the x-axis, with quadrant.
8. Find both distances, showing the absolute-value subtraction:
   (a) (5, 1) and (5, −6); (b) (−4, −3) and (3, −3).
9. **Error analysis:** Mia says the point (3, −4) is in Quadrant II
   "because 3 is positive." What did Mia ignore, and what is the
   correct quadrant? Explain in two sentences.
10. A fictional park map places a fountain at (−1, 3) and a bench at
    (−1, −4). (a) How far apart are they, in map units? (b) Reflect
    the bench across the x-axis. Where is it now, and in which
    quadrant?

## Part D — Mixed reasoning (UO8–UO12)

11. A fictional storm log lists −18 °C, −7 °C, −12 °C, and 0 °C.
    (a) Order them from coldest to warmest. (b) Which reading has the
    greatest magnitude? (c) Is the coldest reading also the one with
    the greatest magnitude? Explain in one sentence.
12. **Explain your reasoning:** −5 is less than 2, but |−5| is greater
    than |2|. In 2–3 sentences, explain how both statements are true,
    using the words *position* and *distance from 0*.
