# Resource Pack — Unit 05: Equations, Inequalities, and Quantitative Relationships

**Goal:** Give the adult one shelf of free, vetted supports for teaching
grade-6 substitution and solution testing, one-step equations
(x + p = q and px = q), inequalities as constraints with number-line
graphs, and dependent/independent variable relationships.
**Age fit:** grades 5–7 (about ages 10–13); written for the adult to
excerpt and explain at a 6th-grade level, never assigned whole.

All external entries checked 2026-11-06. Videos are supplements to
teaching, not replacements. No account is required for any core route
below. One candidate link was checked and is **not** listed: the NCTM
Illuminations "Pan Balance — Numbers" interactive
(nctm.org/Classroom-Resources/Illuminations/Interactives/Pan-Balance-Numbers/)
returned HTTP 404 on 2026-11-06, so it is not cited.
