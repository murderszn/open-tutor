# Formative Quiz — Week 20 (Unit 05)

**Session 08 · 10 items · adult checks the same day; corrections are
part of the lesson, not a penalty.**

**Covers:** Lessons 01–03 (UO18–UO20). Lessons 04–05 appear on the
culminating assessment.

1. Test x = 6 in x + 4 = 10. Is 6 a solution? Show the substitution.
2. Test n = 5 in 3n = 12. Is 5 a solution? Show the substitution.
3. Which of {7, 8, 9} make a − 3 = 5 true? Test each one.
4. Solve m + 8 = 21. Show the step on both sides and the check.
5. Solve y − 9 = 4. Show the step on both sides and the check.
6. Solve 6x = 42. Show the step on both sides and the check.
7. Solve 4.2n = 12.6. Show the division step and the check.
8. Solve (2/5)t = 6. Show the division step and the check.
9. A box of 12 pencils costs $18. What is the price of one pencil?
   Write the equation, solve it, and check it.
10. Error analysis: Sam solves x + 6 = 14 as x = 14 + 6 = 20.
    Explain Sam's error and give the correct solution with its check.
