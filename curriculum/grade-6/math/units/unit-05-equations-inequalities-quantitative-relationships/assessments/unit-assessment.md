# Culminating Assessment — Unit 05: Equations, Inequalities, and Quantitative Relationships

**Session 14 · 12 items · adult checks the same day; the teacher guide
records what each item reteaches.**

**Covers:** all unit objectives (UO18–UO22). This is a draft classroom
assessment, not a standardized or validated test.

1. Test each of {4, 5, 6} in 2x + 1 = 11. Which are solutions? Show
   each substitution.
2. Solve x + 13 = 29. Show the step on both sides and the check.
3. Solve 7y = 91. Show the step on both sides and the check.
4. Solve n ÷ 6 = 8. Show the step on both sides and the check.
5. Solve (3/4)m = 15. Show the division step and the check.
6. A gardener plants rows of 6 flowers. There are 48 flowers in all.
   How many rows are there? Write the equation, solve it, and check
   it.
7. Write the inequality for this constraint: "You must score more
   than 80 points to pass the game." Let s stand for the score.
8. List 3 solutions of w < 7. Then describe the graph of w < 7 in
   words.
9. Is 12 a solution of x > 12? Is 12.5 a solution? Explain both
   answers.
10. A van travels at a steady 50 miles per hour. Let t stand for hours
    and d stand for miles. (a) Write the equation. (b) Complete the
    table for t = 0, 1, 2, 3. (c) How far does the van travel in 4.5
    hours? (d) Which variable is independent, and which is dependent?
11. Error analysis: Lee graphs z < 5 with a closed dot at 5 and an
    arrow pointing right. What two things are wrong with Lee's graph?
12. A courier's load must be lighter than 20 kilograms (L < 20, where
    L is the load in kilograms). The courier's empty bag weighs 3 kg
    and each parcel weighs 2 kg. (a) Use substitution to test whether
    8 parcels is allowed. (b) What is the largest whole number of
    parcels the courier can carry? Show your testing.
