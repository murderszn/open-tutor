## Lesson 01 — Which Values Make It True?

**Readiness:** 1. 14 (3 × 4 + 2). 2. False (5 + 3 = 8, not 9).
3. 5.

**Guided:** 1. 3 + 8 = 11 — true. 2. 9 + 8 = 17 ≠ 11 — false.
3. 4 × 5 = 20 — true. 4. 4 × 6 = 24 ≠ 20 — false. 5. Only 3
(2 − 2 = 0; 3 − 2 = 1; 4 − 2 = 2). 6. Only 3 (5 > 12 false;
10 > 12 false; 15 > 12 true).

**Independent:** 1. Only 8 (6 + 7 = 13; 8 + 7 = 15; 10 + 7 = 17).
2. Only 7 (2 × 3 = 6; 2 × 5 = 10; 2 × 7 = 14). 3. Only 6
(4 ÷ 2 = 2; 5 ÷ 2 = 2.5; 6 ÷ 2 = 3). 4. 0, 1, and 2 (0 + 4 = 4 < 7;
1 + 4 = 5 < 7; 2 + 4 = 6 < 7; 3 + 4 = 7, and 7 < 7 is false).
5. Sam's error: 3 × 4 = 12, but the equation asks whether 12 = 15.
It is not, so 4 is **not** a solution. The correct solution is 5,
because 3 × 5 = 15. 6. Sample answers: (a) x + 2 = 8 (6 + 2 = 8,
true); (b) x < 7 (6 < 7 is true, and 5 is another solution: 5 < 7).
Accept any equation true at 6 and any inequality true at 6 with a
second solution shown.

**Application:** 2 × 4 + 1 = 9 (false); 2 × 5 + 1 = 11 (true);
2 × 6 + 1 = 13 (false). The mystery number is 5.

**Exit:** 2 × 8 + 1 = 17 — true. A solution is a value that makes the
statement true.

## Lesson 05 — Two Quantities That Change Together

**Readiness:** 1. 130 miles. 2. 195. 3. Time (2) on the horizontal
axis; distance (130) on the vertical axis.

**Guided:** 1. C = 2m. 2. C = 0, 2, 4, 6, 10. 3. m is independent —
the miles are chosen first and the cost follows from them.
4. P = 4s. 5. P = 4, 8, 12, 16. 6. d = 65 × 6 = 390 miles;
t = 130 ÷ 65 = 2 hours.

**Independent:** 1. (a) S = 5r. (b) S = 0, 5, 10, 15, 20.
(c) r is independent — the rows are chosen first and the seeds
follow. 2. (a) d = 40t. (b) 40 × 3.5 = 140 miles. (c) 200 ÷ 40 =
5 hours. 3. (a) A = 3, 6, 12, 15. (b) L is independent.
(c) A doubles: for example, L = 1 gives A = 3 and L = 2 gives A = 6.
4. Ana's error: the miles are chosen first and the cost follows from
them, so m is independent and C is dependent — not the other way
around. 5. The points (0, 0), (1, 65), (2, 130), (3, 195), (4, 260)
lie on a straight line rising from the origin, because each extra
hour adds the same 65 miles. 6. Sample: reading 10 pages a day,
P = 10d; table d = 0, 1, 2, 3 → P = 0, 10, 20, 30; d is
independent, P is dependent. Accept any consistent relationship with
equation, table, and variables named.

**Application:** (a) E = 8n. (b) E = 0, 8, 16, 24, 32, 40.
(c) 64 ÷ 8 = 8 deliveries. (d) n is independent; E is dependent.

**Exit:** t is independent; d is dependent. d = 65 × 5 = 325 miles.
