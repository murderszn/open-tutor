# Culminating Assessment — Unit 04

**Session 14 · 12 items · covers UO13–UO17. Show all work: expanded
forms for powers, substitutions for evaluations, transformations or
counterexamples for equivalence items. Partial credit for correct
setup with an arithmetic slip.**

1. Evaluate: (a) 4³ (b) 9² (c) 2⁹.
2. Write 5 × 5 × 5 in exponential form and evaluate it. Then write
   6⁴ in expanded form and evaluate it.
3. Write an expression for "the quotient of 48 and m, plus 7."
4. For 6(2 + x): (a) name the factors, describing (2 + x) as a single
   entity; (b) expand it.
5. Evaluate 3x² − 5 for x = 4.
6. Evaluate 2(5 + 3)² − 10.
7. A cube has side s = 3. Find V = s³ and A = 6s², with units.
8. Expand 5(2a + 3) and factor 24 + 36y.
9. Are 7t + 7 and 7(t + 1) equivalent? Justify with a transformation
   or a counterexample, and check with t = 2.
10. Are 4n + 10 and 4(n + 10) equivalent? Justify with a
    transformation or a counterexample.
11. Explain, in your own words, why testing one value is not enough
    to prove two expressions are equivalent. Use a pair from this
    unit as your example.
12. A stall sells 5 bags of k peaches plus 3 loose peaches. (a) Write
    the expression for the total. (b) Write an equivalent expression
    in a different form and name the property you used. (c) Evaluate
    both expressions for k = 8 and confirm they agree.
