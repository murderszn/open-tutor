# Formative Quiz — Week 16 (Unit 04)

**Session 08 · 10 items · adult checks the same day; corrections are
part of the lesson, not a penalty.**

**Covers:** Lessons 01–03 (UO13–UO15). Lessons 04–05 appear on the
culminating assessment.

1. Evaluate 6².
2. Write 2 × 2 × 2 × 2 × 2 in exponential form and evaluate it.
3. Write an expression for "11 less than twice x."
4. In 5y + 8, name the coefficient of y and the number of terms.
5. Evaluate 4a + 3² for a = 5.
6. Evaluate 20 − 3 × 2³.
7. Evaluate (6 + 2)² ÷ 8.
8. A cube has side s = 4. Find its volume V = s³.
9. A stall's total is 3n + 5. Find the total when n = 7.
10. Error analysis: Alex says 2⁴ = 8 because 2 × 4 = 8. Explain the
    error and give the correct value.
