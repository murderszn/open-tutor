# Practice — Unit 04: Expressions, Properties, and Variable Meaning

Work each set in order. Show the expanded form for every power and
the substituted form for every evaluation. The adult reviews the work
the same day; full worked solutions are in `answer-key.md` (adult
only — do not read ahead).

## Set A — Exponents (Lesson 01; UO13)

1. Evaluate: (a) 2⁷ (b) 3⁴ (c) 10⁵ (d) 1⁹ (e) 6³ (f) 5⁴.
2. Write each in expanded form, then evaluate: (a) 7³ (b) 2⁸.
3. Write each repeated multiplication in exponential form, then
   evaluate: (a) 9 × 9 (b) 4 × 4 × 4 × 4 (c) 10 × 10 × 10 × 10.
4. Compare by evaluating both: (a) 2⁵ vs 5² (b) 3³ vs 2⁶ (c) 10³ vs
   4⁴.
5. Error analysis: Kim says 4³ = 12 and 5² = 10. Explain both errors
   and give the correct values.
6. A square patio has side 12 feet. Write its area as a power and
   evaluate it, with units.

## Set B — Writing expressions and naming parts (Lesson 02; UO14, UO15)

1. Write an expression for each phrase:
   (a) 8 more than y (b) the product of 9 and k (c) 6 less than
   twice w (d) the quotient of 36 and t (e) subtract n from 20.
2. For 7x + 4: name the terms, the coefficient of x, and the
   expression's kind (sum, product, or quotient).
3. For 3(5 + 2): name the factors, and describe (5 + 2) as both a
   sum and a single entity. Then evaluate the expression.
4. For 2a + 5a + 9: how many terms are there? Combine the like terms
   and write the simpler expression.
5. A fruit stand sells 4 bags of m plums plus 7 loose plums. Write
   the expression for the total, then evaluate it for m = 6.
6. Write an expression for "the total cost of 3 notebooks at p
   dollars each plus a $2 pen." Then evaluate it for p = 4.

## Set C — Evaluating expressions and formulas (Lesson 03; UO15)

1. Evaluate for the given value: (a) 5x − 3, x = 6 (b) 2n², n = 5
   (c) 40 ÷ m + 6, m = 8 (d) (a + 2)², a = 7.
2. Evaluate using the order of operations: (a) 3 + 6 × 2² (b)
   18 − 2³ + 5 (c) (10 − 6)³ ÷ 8 (d) 4 × 5² − 20.
3. A cube has side s. (a) Find V = s³ and A = 6s² for s = 2.
   (b) Find V and A for s = 5. Give units (cubic units, square units).
4. A delivery charge is D = 3n + 8 dollars for n boxes. Find D for
   n = 12. Show the substitution.
5. Error analysis: Pat evaluates 3 × 2² + 1 as (3 × 2)² + 1 = 37.
   What did Pat do wrong, and what is the correct value?
6. Evaluate 100 − 5² × 3 + 2³. Show each step in order.

## Set D — Distributive property and equivalence (Lessons 04–05; UO16, UO17)

1. Expand: (a) 7(x + 2) (b) 5(3 + 4b) (c) 2(9 − c) (d) 6(2m + 3n).
2. Factor using the greatest common factor: (a) 14x + 21 (b)
   30y + 12 (c) 8 + 24z (d) 16a + 20b.
3. Combine like terms: (a) 9t + 4t (b) 2x + 7 + 5x (c) 3(y + 2) + y.
   (Hint for (c): expand first.)
4. Decide equivalence; justify with a transformation or a
   counterexample: (a) 3x + 3 and 3(x + 1) (b) 2n + 9 and 2(n + 9)
   (c) 10 + 4k and 2(5 + 2k) (d) a + a + a + a + a and 5a.
5. Error analysis: Sam factors 18x + 12 as 6(3x + 2) and then checks
   by expanding: 6 × 3x = 18x and 6 × 2 = 12. Is Sam's factorization
   correct? What check would have caught an error if there were one?
6. Two expressions for a garden's plants: 5(4 + t) and 20 + 5t.
   Show they are equivalent by expansion, then check both for t = 6.

## Set E — Mixed review and midyear retrieval (all lessons; UO13–UO17 + Units 01–03)

1. Evaluate 2³ + 4 × 3 − 10. Show each step.
2. Expand 4(2x + 5) and factor 27 + 18y.
3. Are 8m + 4 and 4(2m + 1) equivalent? Justify and check m = 3.
4. Write an expression for "5 bags of q marbles, plus 8 loose
   marbles, minus 2 marbles given away." Evaluate it for q = 7.
5. **Midyear retrieval — ratios (Unit 01):** A trail mix uses 3 cups
   of nuts for every 2 cups of raisins. How many cups of nuts go with
   10 cups of raisins? (Ratio table allowed.)
6. **Midyear retrieval — fraction and decimal operations (Unit 02):**
   Compute (a) 3/4 ÷ 1/2 (b) 2.5 × 4 (c) 9.6 ÷ 3.
7. **Midyear retrieval — integers and the plane (Unit 03):**
   (a) Which is greater, −3 or 2? (b) What is |−5|? (c) In which
   quadrant is the point (−2, 3)? (d) What is the distance between
   (2, 5) and (2, −1)?
8. **Midyear retrieval — mixed:** A rectangle's length is 3 times its
   width w. Write its perimeter as an expression in w, then evaluate
   it for w = 4 cm. (Perimeter = 2 × length + 2 × width.)
