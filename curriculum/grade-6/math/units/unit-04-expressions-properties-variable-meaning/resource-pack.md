# Resource Pack — Unit 04: Expressions, Properties, and Variable Meaning

**Goal:** Give the adult one shelf of free, vetted supports for teaching
grade-6 exponents, variables and expression parts, evaluation and
order of operations, the distributive property, and equivalent
expressions. **Age fit:** grades 5–7 (about ages 10–13); written for
the adult to excerpt and explain at a 6th-grade level, never assigned
whole.

All external entries checked 2026-10-31. Videos are supplements to
teaching, not replacements. No account is required for any core route
below.
