# Resource Pack — Unit 04: Earth Materials, Rocks, Minerals, and Cycles

**Goal:** support a grade-6 learner (with a guiding adult) in testing
mineral properties, classifying rocks by formation, modeling the rock
cycle, and tagging its energy drivers — with free, no-account routes
for every core objective. **Checked 2026-11-01.** Videos are labeled
search links (preferred channels, not verified titles) per the
repository Resource Pack format; web references below were checked
2026-11-01. All passages in the unit itself are original.

## Web references (checked 2026-11-01; free, no account)

- **NGSS MS-ESS2-1 (NSTA listing)** — performance expectation, clarification
  statement, and assessment boundary (no mineral identification/naming).
  [my.nsta.org/ngss/DisplayStandard.aspx?id=36&view=topic](https://my.nsta.org/ngss/DisplayStandard.aspx?id=36&view=topic)
- **NGSS MS-ESS2-1 (California Department of Education reproduction)** —
  same expectation and boundary, plus DCI ESS2.A wording.
  [www2.cde.ca.gov/cacs/id/web/29060](https://www2.cde.ca.gov/cacs/id/web/29060)
- **Utah Geological Survey — How Do Geologists Identify Minerals?** —
  color's unreliability, streak, hardness, cleavage, specific gravity.
  [geology.utah.gov/map-pub/survey-notes/glad-you-asked/how-do-geologists-identify-minerals/](https://geology.utah.gov/map-pub/survey-notes/glad-you-asked/how-do-geologists-identify-minerals/)
- **Utah Geological Survey — Igneous, Sedimentary, & Metamorphic Rocks** —
  the three families, extrusive vs. intrusive, compaction and cementation.
  [geology.utah.gov/map-pub/survey-notes/glad-you-asked/igneous-sedimentary-metamorphic-rocks/](https://geology.utah.gov/map-pub/survey-notes/glad-you-asked/igneous-sedimentary-metamorphic-rocks/)
- **LibreTexts — The Rock Cycle** — processes, uplift, burial,
  compaction and cementation, metamorphism without melting.
  [geo.libretexts.org/Bookshelves/Geology/Geology_of_California/02%3A_Minerals_and_Rocks/2.03%3A_The_Rock_Cycle](https://geo.libretexts.org/Bookshelves/Geology/Geology_of_California/02%3A_Minerals_and_Rocks/2.03%3A_The_Rock_Cycle)
