# Week-18 Formative Quiz — Unit 04 (10 points)

**Name (private — do not publish):** ______________ **Date:** ______________

Formative: this quiz shows what to reteach before the unit assessment.
It never asks you to name a mineral. Answers are in the
[answer key](../answer-key.md).

1. (1 pt) Which of these is **not** part of the definition of a mineral?
   - A. Naturally occurring
   - B. Inorganic (never alive)
   - C. Solid with an orderly crystal structure
   - D. Made by people in a factory

2. (1 pt) Samples C and D are both white with a white streak. Which
   pair of properties separates them most clearly?
   - A. Color and streak
   - B. Hardness and luster (C: hardness 7, glassy; D: hardness 1–2, dull)
   - C. Color and hardness
   - D. Streak and color

3. (1 pt) What is **streak**, and how is it tested? Answer in one
   sentence.

4. (1 pt) A sample scratches a fingernail (≈ 2.5) but is scratched by a
   copper penny (≈ 3). Between which two numbers does its hardness
   fall?

5. (1 pt) A rock has large, interlocking crystals and no layers. Which
   family is it, and what does the crystal size tell you about how it
   cooled?

6. (1 pt) A rock contains a shell fossil. Which family is it, and why
   can fossils survive in this family but not in the other two?

7. (1 pt) Name the two processes that turn buried sediment into
   sedimentary rock, in order.

8. (1 pt) Tag this arrow with its energy driver: weathering, erosion,
   and deposition. Choose S (Sun), E (Earth's interior), or "neither
   directly" — and give the one-sentence reason.

9. (1 pt) A cliff "disappears" as it weathers over many years. Was its
   matter destroyed? Answer yes or no, and explain in one sentence.

10. (1 pt) Which question can the rock-cycle diagram **not** answer?
    - A. Which process turns sediment into sedimentary rock?
    - B. How many years did one burial take?
    - C. Which energy source drives melting?
    - D. What can a metamorphic rock become next?
