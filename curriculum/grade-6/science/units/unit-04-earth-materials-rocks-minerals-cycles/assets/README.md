# Assets — Unit 04

## mineral-test-station.png (generated raster image)

- **Prompt:** "A clean educational illustration, viewed from slightly
  above, of a mineral testing station on a plain light-colored table:
  several rough rock and mineral samples in varied natural colors
  (gray, white, pinkish, dark), a white unglazed porcelain streak plate,
  a round hand lens, a copper penny, an iron nail, and a small blank
  notebook with a pencil. No text, no letters, no numbers, no people."
- **Tool:** Muse built-in image generation, 2026-11-01.
- **Learning purpose:** Lesson 01 activity — learners match each tool
  to the mineral test it serves (hand lens → luster/break inspection;
  streak plate → streak; penny and nail → hardness; notebook →
  recording results).
- **Factual review (inspected 2026-11-01):** the picture shows five
  rough samples (speckled gray; pink and layered; clear crystals; dark
  gray-black; mottled gray), one blank white rectangular streak plate,
  one round hand lens with a gold rim, one copper penny, one iron nail,
  one blank notebook and one pencil. No text, no people, no labels.
  It is an illustration of tools, not a photograph and not a record of
  any real test: no sample in it is named, and no answer in this unit
  comes from it alone.
- **Caption:** "An AI-generated illustration of a mineral test
  station. It is a drawing of tools and unidentified samples — not a
  photograph, not a record of any real test, and not an identification
  key."
- **Alt text:** "Watercolor-style illustration of a mineral testing
  station on a light table: five rough rock and mineral samples in
  gray, pink, white crystal, and dark colors; a blank white
  rectangular streak plate; a round hand lens with a gold rim; a
  copper penny; an iron nail; a blank notebook and a pencil. No labels
  or text appear in the picture."
- **Text-only alternative:** in Lesson 01, directly below the image.
- **Known limitations:** sample identities are deliberately absent;
  the nail is drawn pointed — in class the adult handles the nail
  (see teacher guide safety rules). The illustration must never be
  presented as an observation or as evidence for a sample's identity.

## rock-cycle-diagram.svg (deterministic diagram)

- **Source:** drawn by hand for this unit from the Lesson 03 process
  list (melting; cooling and crystallization; weathering, erosion,
  deposition; compaction and cementation; heat and pressure without
  melting; uplift). No generation involved.
- **Learning purpose:** Lesson 03's shared model — every path traced
  in the unit must be traceable on this diagram; energy tags S
  (Sun), E (Earth's interior), and "neither directly" are printed on
  the arrows.
- **Factual review:** five material boxes (magma, igneous rock,
  sediment, sedimentary rock, metamorphic rock); every arrow is a real
  process in the correct direction; the footer states that arrow
  lengths are not timescales.
- **Known limitations:** the diagram omits timescales, locations,
  rates, and living things by design (see Lesson 04's model audit);
  uplift is combined with weathering on the surface-bound arrows.
