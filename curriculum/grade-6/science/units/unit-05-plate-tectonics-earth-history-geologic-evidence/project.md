# Project — Plate Motion Evidence Board and Deep-Time Rope

**Deliverable:** (1) a 4.6-meter rope timeline with eight events
placed and labeled, and (2) an evidence board arguing that the
continents have moved, with a limits card. **Sessions:** launched in
S8 (plan + evidence choice), built in S12, showcased in S14.
**Adult role:** supervises measuring and floor space; checks the
board's claims against the evidence cards.

## What you are making

Two objects that argue two halves of the unit's case. The **rope**
shows deep time: Earth's 4.6-billion-year history at a scale you can
walk along. The **board** shows the evidence: the case that the
continents have moved, built from three independent families of
evidence, with its limits stated honestly.

## Materials

- A rope, clothesline, or taped-together paper strips at least 5 meters
  long; a tape measure; masking tape; eight label cards; a marker.
- A poster board (or a large sheet of paper); the evidence cards E1–E8
  from the [unit README](README.md); glue or tape; a marker.
- The boundary illustration (Lesson 04) and the geologic time scale
  bar (`assets/geologic-time-scale-bar.svg`) for reference — copy from
  them; do not trace the generated image as evidence.

## Part 1 — The deep-time rope (20 minutes, Sessions S11–S12)

1. Lay the rope on a clear floor. Mark **today** 20 cm from one end
   and **Earth's formation** 4.6 meters away. The scale is **1 meter =
   1 billion years**.
2. Place and label these eight events (values from Lesson 05):

   | Event | Years ago | Distance from "today" |
   |---|---|---|
   | Earth's formation | 4.6 billion | 4.6 m |
   | First cellular life | 3.3 billion | 3.3 m |
   | First fish | 530 million | 53 cm |
   | First insects | 480 million | 48 cm |
   | First dinosaurs | 240 million | 24 cm |
   | First mammals | 220 million | 22 cm |
   | First birds | 150 million | 15 cm |
   | First humans | 300,000 | 0.3 cm (3 mm) |

3. Mark the four time divisions with colored tape: Precambrian from
   Earth's formation to 53.9 cm from today; Paleozoic from 53.9 cm to
   25.2 cm; Mesozoic from 25.2 cm to 6.6 cm; Cenozoic from 6.6 cm to
   today. (Check: 539 million years = 53.9 cm; 252 million = 25.2 cm;
   66 million = 6.6 cm.)
4. Stand at the "today" end and look down the rope. Write two
   sentences about what you notice — especially where humans sit.

## Part 2 — The evidence board (30 minutes, Sessions S12–S14)

1. Title the board: "Did the Continents Move? The Evidence."
2. Build the case in three columns — **Fossils**, **Rocks and Fit**,
   **Seafloor** — with at least one card in each column:
   - **Fossils:** at least one of E1–E4, with the continents listed
     and the reason it could not have crossed an ocean.
   - **Rocks and Fit:** E5 (the matching five-layer stack) and/or E6
     (the continental fit).
   - **Seafloor:** E7 (the age table, labeled **practice data**) and/or
     E8 (young at ridges, old at trenches; oldest seafloor under 300
     million years).
3. Under the columns, write the verdict in two sentences: did the
   continents move, and what is the strongest reason?
4. Attach a **limits card** that names at least two limits honestly,
   for example: the seafloor table is practice data, not real
   measurements; the fit alone is one clue, not a case; magnetic
   stripes are not used in this unit (NGSS boundary); the board shows
   the southern continents best and says less about the northern ones.
5. In the S14 showcase, defend the board in two minutes: point to one
   card in each column and say what it shows and what it cannot prove
   alone. The adult asks one challenge question (for example: "What
   if *Glossopteris* seeds could float?") and you answer it.

## Safety and honesty rules

- Clear the floor before measuring the rope; never run with the tape
  measure extended.
- Label practice data as practice data. Never present the boundary
  illustration as a photograph or a measurement.
- Period and epoch names are out of scope (MS-ESS1-4 boundary): use
  the four big divisions only.

## Rubric (40 points)

| Criterion | 10 points | 7 points | 4 points | 0 points |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| **Rope timeline accuracy (10)** | All eight events placed within 5 cm of the correct distance; four divisions marked in the right places; scale stated (1 m = 1 billion years). | Six or seven events correct; divisions mostly right; scale stated. | Fewer than six events correct, or divisions missing, or scale unstated. | Rope missing or events unordered. |
| **Evidence coverage (10)** | All three columns filled with the right cards; every card lists its continents/values and its reason. | Three columns filled; one card incomplete. | One column empty or two cards incomplete. | Two or more columns empty. |
| **Argument quality (10)** | Verdict follows from the cards; the two-sentence verdict names the strongest reason; the showcase answers the challenge question with evidence. | Verdict follows from the cards; showcase answer is partial. | Verdict does not follow from the cards, or showcase answer ignores the evidence. | No verdict, or verdict contradicts the cards. |
| **Limits card and honesty (10)** | At least two real limits named (including the practice-data label on E7); no claim overstates the evidence; period/epoch names not used. | Two limits named; one small overstatement. | One limit named, or practice data presented as real. | No limits card, or a claim presented as proof from one card. |

**Passing bar:** 28 of 40 with no criterion at 0. Below that, revise
the weakest criterion with the adult and re-showcase in S16.
