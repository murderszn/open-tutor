# Resource Pack — Unit 05: Plate Tectonics, Earth History, and Geologic Evidence

**Goal:** support a grade-6 learner (with a guiding adult) in
explaining surface change at many scales, arguing past plate motion
from fossil, rock, and seafloor evidence, predicting features at the
three boundary types, and ordering Earth's history on the geologic
time scale — with free, no-account routes for every core objective.
**Checked 2026-11-08.** Videos are labeled search links (preferred
channels, not verified titles) per the repository Resource Pack
format; web references below were checked 2026-11-08. All passages in
the unit itself are original.

1. NGSS MS-ESS2-2 geoscience processes varying time spatial scales

  [purdue.edu/science/K12/LabPages/geochronology.html](https://www.purdue.edu/science/K12/LabPages/geochronology.html)

## Practice-data note

Evidence cards E1–E6 and E8 describe real distributions and patterns
from the sources above. Card E7 (the seafloor age table) is **fictional
practice data**, labeled as such wherever it appears: its numbers are
consistent with the real spreading rate (about 2 cm a year) but are
not measurements from a real ridge. The historic earthquake magnitudes
and the volcano list in Lesson 01 are real events of record. The
generated boundary illustration is a drawing, never an observation,
and no answer in this unit comes from it alone.
