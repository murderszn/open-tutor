# Week-22 Formative Quiz — Unit 05

**Formative, not graded.** 12 items, about 20 minutes. The adult
reviews it the same day and reteaches from the misses before the unit
assessment (Session S15). No item asks about paleomagnetic anomalies
or period/epoch names. Answers and scoring guidance are in the
[answer key](../answer-key.md).

**Name:** ______________________ **Date:** __________

## Part A — Choose the best answer (1 point each)

**Q1.** Which change is slow and large-scale?
(a) A landslide stripping a hillside in minutes
(b) A continent moving 2 centimeters a year for millions of years
(c) A volcano erupting in one day
(d) An earthquake shaking a town for 30 seconds

**Q2.** *Mesosaurus* fossils are found only in southern Africa and
eastern South America. What makes this strong evidence the continents
were joined?
(a) *Mesosaurus* was a large animal
(b) *Mesosaurus* lived in freshwater and could not cross an ocean
(c) *Mesosaurus* fossils are easy to find
(d) *Mesosaurus* lived a very long time

**Q3.** Where is the ocean floor youngest?
(a) At the trenches
(b) Near the continents
(c) At the mid-ocean ridges
(d) Under the oldest mountains

**Q4.** Two plates slide past each other sideways. What boundary is
this, and what does it mainly produce?
(a) Divergent; new crust
(b) Convergent; volcanoes
(c) Transform; earthquakes
(d) Convergent; mountains

**Q5.** In an undisturbed stack of rock layers, which layer is oldest?
(a) The top layer
(b) The middle layer
(c) The bottom layer
(d) The thickest layer

**Q6.** Which organism would make the best index fossil?
(a) One that lived for 300 million years on one continent
(b) One that lived for 10 million years and spread across four continents
(c) One that lived for 5 million years in a single valley
(d) One that still lives today all over the world

## Part B — Short answers (2 points each)

**Q7.** Name one fast, small-scale surface change and one slow,
large-scale surface change. Give the time scale for each.

**Q8.** The seafloor is 0 million years old at a ridge, 25 million
years old 500 km away, and 50 million years old 1,000 km away. What
is the spreading rate in kilometers per million years? Show the
division.

**Q9.** A coast has a deep ocean trench offshore and volcanoes inland.
Which boundary type is this, and what is happening to the ocean plate?

**Q10.** List the four big divisions of the geologic time scale in
order, oldest to youngest, and give the time span of one of them.

**Q11.** A fault cuts rock layers A, B, and C, but not layer D above
them. Is the fault older or younger than layer D? Give the reason.

**Q12.** A learner says, "The fit of South America and Africa proves
the continents moved." Rewrite the sentence so it is scientifically
honest, using the word "supports" and naming one more kind of
evidence the case needs.
