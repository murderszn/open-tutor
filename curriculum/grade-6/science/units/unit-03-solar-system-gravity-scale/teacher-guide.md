# Teacher Guide — Unit 03 (Solar System, Gravity, and Scale)

**Status:** teacher material — keep separate from student pages. Solutions
for every item are in the [answer key](answer-key.md); this guide covers
pacing, misconceptions, readiness, and reteaching.

## Pacing guidance

- **Week 11 (S1–S4):** Lesson 01, then slow table work. Learners who rush
  the table will memorize the order without the groups — insist on the
  rocky/giant sort with diameter evidence before moving on.
- **Week 12 (S5–S8):** Lesson 02 and the ball-and-string lab. Do the lab
  outdoors or in a cleared room; soft balls only. The lab's value is the
  fair-test structure (one changed variable, repeats), not the swinging
  itself — stop and record after each length.
- **Week 13 (S9–S12):** Lessons 03–04 back to back, then the S11 data task
  (Practice Sets C–D). The two scales must stay labeled at all times:
  "Earth = 1 cm" for sizes, "1 AU = 10 m" for distances. If a learner quotes
  a number without its scale, ask "at what scale?" until the habit sticks.
- **Week 14 (S13–S16):** Lesson 05, quiz, showcase, assessment, debrief.
  The quiz is formative: reteach before the assessment, do not grade the
  quiz as final.

## Misconception notes

- **Gravity is Earth-only.** Counter: gravity is a pull between any two
  masses; the Sun's mass dominates the system; Jupiter's surface gravity
  (24.79 m/s²) exceeds Earth's. Reteach: Lesson 02 worked example 2
  (weight on Mars/Jupiter).
- **Orbits are tracks or rails.** Counter: there is nothing to ride on;
  an orbit is a continuous fall combined with sideways motion. Reteach:
  Lesson 02's ball-and-string demo *plus* the explicit "no string in
  space" note — the demo without that note creates the misconception.
- **Mass = weight.** Counter: mass is matter (unchanging); weight is a
  pull (planet-dependent). Reteach: the 50 N worked example across Mars
  and Jupiter.
- **Textbook pictures are to scale.** Counter: the 3.5 km number (Earth =
  1 cm → Neptune ≈ 3.5 km) and the sub-millimetre Earth (1 AU = 10 m).
  Reteach: Lesson 04 worked example 2, done on paper together.
- **Ratios by subtraction.** Counter: "how many times" means divide.
  Reteach: Lesson 03 worked example 1, then Practice Set C Q6.
- **Pluto as a ninth planet.** Counter: dwarf-planet reclassification
  (2006); enrichment only in this unit. Reteach: Lesson 01's dwarf-planet
  note and Practice Set A Q5.
- **Moon counts as data.** Do not assess moon counts: the source file's
  counts are dated (Saturn 146 vs. current 274). If a learner asks, say
  counts change as new moons are confirmed and point to the NASA Planetary
  Fact Sheet for current tallies.

## Readiness: what "ready to move on" looks like

After the Week-14 quiz, the learner is ready for the unit assessment when
they can, without notes: name the planets in order; sort a sample of
planets rocky vs. giant with one diameter reason; explain orbit as
falling-around in two sentences; compute one ratio, one AU conversion,
and one weight conversion with the table; and name one distortion of each
model. Score the quiz per objective (Q1–2: Objective 1; Q3–5, 11:
Objective 2; Q6–7: Objective 3; Q8–9: Objective 4; Q10, 12: Objective 5);
reteach any objective below 2/3 correct before S15.

## Reteaching moves

- **Objective 1 weak:** rebuild the order strip from the table (no memory
  sentence) and re-sort with diameter evidence for each planet.
- **Objective 2 weak:** redo the ball-and-string demo with narration
  ("pull in, motion sideways, fall that misses") and re-solve the 50 N
  example on paper.
- **Objective 3 weak:** recompute two ratios and two AU values together,
  then state each pattern as a sentence with numbers before any new task.
- **Objective 4 weak:** rebuild one model only (the distance line is the
  more memorable one) and re-derive the 3.5 km number step by step.
- **Objective 5 weak:** audit one model aloud with the three questions
  (includes / leaves out / wrong-if-trusted) before attempting the audit
  in writing.

## Assessment scoring (unit assessment, 14 items)

- Items 1, 2, 6, 8, 13: one point per correct row/value/pattern element;
  accept ratios rounded to two decimals and AU values rounded to two
  decimals (30.05 for Neptune; 9.62 for the Saturn–Uranus gap).
- Items 3, 4, 11, 12: rubric-scored (2 = all required elements present
  with correct reasoning; 1 = partial; 0 = misconception repeated or
  missing). Sample reasoning is in the answer key; accept any wording
  that contains the required elements.
- Items 5, 7, 9, 10: one point for the correct value (nearest whole newton
  for weights; 18.3 cm; five hallway planets; 109 cm) with working shown;
  half credit for a correct setup with an arithmetic slip.
- Item 14: (a) one point for "longer" with the distance reason; (b) one
  point for "no — 2 AU sits between the groups" with the belt-region reason.
- **Passing signal:** 11/14 or better with no objective entirely missed;
  otherwise reteach the missed objective(s) with the moves above and
  re-assess those items only.

  4 hours to Neptune" are rounded accordingly and labeled as such.
