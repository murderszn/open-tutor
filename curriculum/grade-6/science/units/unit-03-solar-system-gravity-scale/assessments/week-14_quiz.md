# Week-14 Formative Quiz — Unit 03 (Solar System, Gravity, and Scale)

**Use the dataset table below for every calculation. Show your working.
This quiz checks readiness for the unit assessment; the adult reviews it
the same day and reteaches any objective scored below the "ready" line in
the [teacher guide](../teacher-guide.md).**

| Planet | Mean distance from Sun (million km) | Orbital period (days) | Equatorial diameter (km) | Surface gravity (m/s²) |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Mercury | 57.9 | 88 | 4,879 | 3.7 |
| Venus | 108.2 | 225 | 12,104 | 8.87 |
| Earth | 149.6 | 365 | 12,742 | 9.81 |
| Mars | 227.9 | 687 | 6,779 | 3.71 |
| Jupiter | 778.6 | 4,333 | 139,820 | 24.79 |
| Saturn | 1,433.5 | 10,759 | 116,460 | 10.44 |
| Uranus | 2,872.5 | 30,687 | 50,724 | 8.69 |
| Neptune | 4,495.1 | 60,190 | 49,244 | 11.15 |

1. Write the eight planets in order from the Sun.
2. Classify each as rocky or giant: Venus, Neptune, Mars, Saturn.
3. In one sentence, what is gravity?
4. The Sun pulls on Earth all the time, yet Earth never crashes into it.
   Explain why in two sentences, using the words "pull" and "sideways."
5. A rock weighs 90 N on Earth. Calculate its weight on Mars. Show the
   multiplication and division, and round to the nearest whole newton.
6. Compute Saturn's diameter ratio (Saturn's diameter ÷ Earth's diameter).
   Give the ratio to two decimals and say what it means in words.
7. Compute Uranus's distance in AU (Uranus's distance ÷ 149.6 million km).
   Round to two decimals.
8. On the 1 AU = 10 m distance line, where does Mars's marker go? Give the
   distance in metres from the Sun marker.
9. On the Earth = 1 cm size scale, about how far from the Sun marker is
   Jupiter? (Use 778.6 million km ÷ 12,742 km per cm, converted to metres.)
   Round to the nearest 10 m.
10. Name one thing the orbit illustration in Lesson 01 distorts, and one
    thing it shows correctly.
11. True or false, with one reason: "Gravity only works on Earth."
12. A planet is discovered orbiting at 25 AU. Would its year be longer or
    shorter than Uranus's year (19.20 AU)? Give one reason from the dataset
    pattern.
