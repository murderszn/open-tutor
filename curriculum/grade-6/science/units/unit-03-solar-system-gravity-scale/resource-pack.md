# Resource Pack — Unit 03 (Solar System, Gravity, and Scale)

**Goal:** support a grade-6 learner (with an adult) in ordering the
planets, modeling gravity's role in orbits, and building honest size and
distance scale models from real data.
**Age fit:** grades 6–8; reading level ~grade 6; every investigation is
adult-supervised. **Never look directly at the Sun.**

## Internal references

- [`resources/solar_system_planets.csv`](../../../../../resources/solar_system_planets.csv) —
  the unit dataset: mean distance from the Sun (million km), orbital
  period (days), equatorial diameter (km), surface gravity (m/s²) for the
  eight planets. Moon-count column deliberately NOT used in this unit: moon
  counts change as new moons are confirmed (the file's Saturn count, 146,
  is behind the current tally of 274).
- [`resources/astronomy_fundamentals.md`](../../../../../resources/astronomy_fundamentals.md) —
  reuse: the Sun / inner-planet / outer-planet summaries, excerpted and
  explained at grade level for Lesson 01 scene-setting; never assigned
  whole. (Audit note: the guide's "Mercury: no atmosphere" simplification
  is not used — Mercury keeps an extremely thin exosphere.)
- [`resources/physics_fundamentals.md`](../../../../../resources/physics_fundamentals.md) —
  reuse: the mass-vs.-weight passage and the "gravity near Earth's surface:
  g ≈ 9.8 m/s²" reference value, for Lesson 02.
- [`teachers/ai-assistants/resource_finder.md`](../../../../../teachers/ai-assistants/resource_finder.md) —
  drove this pack's queries and curation format.
- Track [`scope-and-sequence.md`](../../scope-and-sequence.md) — U03 weekly
  goals (Weeks 11–14) and the MS-ESS1-2 / MS-ESS1-3 crosswalk.

## Web references (checked 2026-10-25 unless noted)

1. **NASA Planetary Fact Sheet** (data)
   <https://nssdc.gsfc.nasa.gov/planetary/factsheet/> — metric table of
   diameters, masses, gravities, distances, orbital periods, and moon
   counts for the planets. Used 2026-10-25 to cross-check every value in
   the unit dataset table; agreement within rounding for all student-table
   values. Free, no account. Note: moon counts here are current tallies
   (Saturn 274) — the reason the unit does not use the dataset file's
   moon column.
2. **NSTA — NGSS MS-ESS1 performance expectations** (standards)
   <https://my.nsta.org/ngss/DisplayStandard?view=pe&id=230> (MS-ESS1-2) and
   <https://my.nsta.org/ngss/DisplayStandard?view=pe&id=231> (MS-ESS1-3) —
   verbatim performance-expectation wording, clarification statements, and
   assessment boundaries quoted in the unit README. Free, no account.
3. **California Department of Education — CA NGSS content standards**
   (standards) <https://www2.cde.ca.gov/cacs/science?c1=4&c0=2> — official
   state reproduction of the same MS-ESS1-2 / MS-ESS1-3 expectations used
   as the second verification source 2026-10-25. Free, no account.
4. **University of Colorado — Scale Model Solar System** (scale activity)
   <https://www.colorado.edu/fiske/scale-model-solar-system> — planet
   highlights in Earth-relative units (Sun 109× Earth diameter; Mercury
   0.38× Earth diameter; orbital periods) that corroborate the unit's
   scale numbers. Free, no account.
5. **NASA Solar System Exploration** (reference)
   <https://solarsystem.nasa.gov/> — the agency's solar-system hub, linked
   from the repository's astronomy guide; planet overviews suitable for
   adult-selected reading. Free, no account.
6. **Royal Museums Greenwich — Solar System data** (reference)
   <https://www.rmg.co.uk/stories/space-astronomy/solar-system-data> —
   diameter, mass, rotation, and tilt table in Earth-relative units;
   corroborates the unit's planet-size comparisons. Free, no account.

## Safety and access notes

- Every investigation is adult-supervised. The ball-and-string demo uses
  soft balls only, with clear space. **Never look directly at the Sun** —
  this unit uses no solar observation at all.
- All core learning is supported by free, no-account routes: the dataset
  table, the unit's own diagrams, and the NASA fact sheet. Videos
  supplement teaching; they never replace it.
- The generated orbit illustration is labeled AI-generated and not to
  scale in its caption and in the assets record; the text-only alternative
  in Lesson 01 carries the same information.
