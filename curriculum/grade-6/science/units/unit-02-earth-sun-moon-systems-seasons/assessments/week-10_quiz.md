# Week-10 Formative Quiz — Unit 02

**Use:** S13, before the unit assessment. Adult scores with
[answer-key.md](../answer-key.md); any item below ~70% correct triggers the
teacher guide's reteaching move for that lesson. 10 items, ~20 minutes.

**Q1.** Earth's rotation and revolution: which motion causes day and night,
and about how long does one rotation take?

**Q2.** A stick's shadow is long and points east. Where is the Sun (direction
and height), and is it morning or evening? One sentence of reasoning.

**Q3.** The Moon looks like a thin crescent tonight. Does that mean Earth's
shadow is covering most of the Moon? Answer yes/no and give the real
explanation in one sentence.

**Q4.** Use the orbit diagram (`assets/moon-phase-orbit-diagram.svg`):
the Moon is at position 4. Name the phase and say whether it is waxing or
waning.

**Q5.** Sketch the Sun–Earth–Moon lineup for a **lunar eclipse** (top-down,
three labeled circles). Mark whose shadow falls on whom.

**Q6.** It is a full moon tonight, but no lunar eclipse is happening. Why
not? Use the Moon's orbital tilt in your answer.

**Q7.** Name the two reasons a hemisphere leaning toward the Sun experiences
summer.

**Q8.** Earth is closest to the Sun in early January. Explain in two
sentences why this fact *rejects* (not supports) the idea that distance
causes the seasons.

**Q9.** In December it is summer in Australia. Explain why, using the words
**tilt** and **hemisphere**.

**Q10.** A classmate says, "My lamp-and-ball model proves the Moon is 30
Earth-diameters away." Is this claim inside or outside what the model can
show? Explain in one sentence (use the model-audit rule).
