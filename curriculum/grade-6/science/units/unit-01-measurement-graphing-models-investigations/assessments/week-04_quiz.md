# Unit 01 — Formative Quiz (Session 15)

Short written check across the unit's objectives. The adult reviews it the
same day; every miss becomes a corrected example before the unit assessment.
**No answers are printed here — see [answer-key.md](../answer-key.md#formative-quiz)
(for the adult only).** Oral and drawn responses accepted.

**Q1.** Convert: 5 cm = ? m

**Q2.** Convert: 2.5 L = ? mL

**Q3.** Convert: 340 g = ? kg

**Q4.** Convert: 1.2 km = ? m

**Q5.** Fictional practice data — three trials timing 10 swings of the same
pendulum: 14.3 s, 14.1 s, 14.4 s. What is the mean time for 10 swings, and
what is the per-swing time? Show your work.

**Q6.** A learner draws a graph of cooling-water temperature over time. The
vertical axis is numbered 50–85 but has no label. What is missing, and what
should the label say?

**Q7.** Dataset: "Number of rainy days in each month, January through June."
Should this be a line graph or a bar graph? Give the one-sentence reason.

**Q8.** A learner investigates "Does ramp height change roll distance?" and
tests heights of 10, 20, and 30 cm with the same car. What is the changed
variable, and what is the measured variable?

**Q9.** Observation or inference? "The thermometer reads 24 °C."

**Q10.** Observation or inference? "The plant grew taller because it got more
sunlight." Explain which part is the observation and which is the inference.

**Q11.** A learner's ramp model is a labeled drawing of the board, books, car,
and ruler. Name one thing this model includes and one thing it leaves out.

**Q12.** A learner rolls the car once and records the distance. Give one
reason why a single trial is not enough.

**Q13.** Which tool measures volume: a meter stick, a graduated cylinder, or
a stopwatch?

**Q14.** Testable or not? "Does warm water dissolve sugar faster than cold
water?" If testable, name the measurement that would answer it.
