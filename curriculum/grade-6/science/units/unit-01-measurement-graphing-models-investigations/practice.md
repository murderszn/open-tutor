# Unit 01 — Cross-Unit Practice Set

Work these across Sessions 6, 8, and 11, or as the adult assigns. All datasets
below are **fictional practice data** — they were made up for practice, not
measured by anyone. Answers are in [answer-key.md](answer-key.md#practice-set)
— **for the adult only.**

## Part A — Metric conversions (P1–P8)

Convert. Show the place-value move (which direction the decimal went, and
why).

- **P1.** 8 cm = ? mm
- **P2.** 350 cm = ? m
- **P3.** 0.9 m = ? cm
- **P4.** 2 km = ? m
- **P5.** 1,750 mL = ? L
- **P6.** 0.25 L = ? mL
- **P7.** 4.2 kg = ? g
- **P8.** 900 g = ? kg

## Part B — Table and graph interpretation (P9–P14)

Study this fictional practice dataset, then answer. A learner rolled the same
toy car down the same ramp set to three heights, three trials each, and
recorded how far the car rolled past the ramp.

| Trial | Ramp height (cm) | Roll distance (cm) |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | 10 | 62 |
| 2 | 10 | 65 |
| 3 | 10 | 63 |
| 4 | 20 | 91 |
| 5 | 20 | 88 |
| 6 | 20 | 90 |
| 7 | 30 | 118 |
| 8 | 30 | 121 |
| 9 | 30 | 119 |

- **P9.** Which variable was changed, and which was measured?
- **P10.** Compute the mean roll distance for the 20-cm ramp height. Show
  your work.
- **P11.** For the 10-cm trials, what is the range (max − min)? What does
  that range tell you about the steadiness of the measurement?
- **P12.** A line graph of this data would put which variable on the
  horizontal axis? Why?
- **P13.** Write one honest sentence describing the pattern in this sample.
  (Start with "In this sample…")
- **P14.** A classmate says, "This proves taller ramps always make cars roll
  farther, for every car in the world." Write the two-sentence correction.

## Part C — Investigation design critiques (P15–P20)

Read each plan. Name what is wrong (if anything), which variable rule it
breaks, and how to fix it.

- **P15.** "To test whether sunlight changes plant growth, I will put one
  plant in a sunny window and water it daily, and put another plant in a dark
  closet and water it weekly."
- **P16.** "To test whether ramp height changes roll distance, I will test
  heights of 10, 20, and 30 cm with the same car, released from the top each
  time, three trials each."
- **P17.** "To test whether the mass of a pendulum bob changes swing time, I
  will use a 50-g bob and a 200-g bob on strings of 30 cm and 60 cm."
- **P18.** "To test which paper towel absorbs the most water, I will pour
  50 mL onto each of three brands, wait one minute, and measure the water
  left in the tray. I will test each brand once."
- **P19.** A learner's data table has the column headers "Height," "Time,"
  and "Notes." Name two problems with these headers and write corrected
  headers for a pendulum investigation.
- **P20.** A learner draws a beautiful labeled diagram of the ramp
  investigation and writes, "This model shows exactly what happened." Write a
  two-sentence model-vs.-reality correction naming one thing the model
  includes and one thing it leaves out.
