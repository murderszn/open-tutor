# Unit 01 Practice Sessions

Mixed practice for the non-lesson sessions in the unit pacing
(sessions 2, 6, 8, 14). Each session is ~40 minutes. The adult
reviews every response the same day. Full solutions are in the
[answer key](answer-key.md#practice); nothing here is a test.

## Session 2 — Evidence practice A: "quote or paraphrase?" (Week 3)

Use "The Lantern in the Window" ([Lesson 01](lessons/lesson-01-explicit-versus-inference.md)).

**Part 1 — Cite it right (6 tasks).** For each claim, write the
shortest exact quote that proves it, plus the paragraph number.

1. The porch light was broken.
2. June was careful on the steps.
3. The grandmother watched June from the window.
4. The grandmother did not speak at first.
5. June stopped counting the steps.
6. The grandmother touched June at the door.

**Part 2 — Paraphrase check (4 tasks).** Each paraphrase below has
one flaw — it changes the meaning, adds a guess, or drops a key
detail. Name the flaw and fix it.

7. Original: "She held the railing with both hands."
   Paraphrase: "She was terrified of the stairs."
8. Original: "She did not wave or call out."
   Paraphrase: "She ignored June completely."
9. Original: "a square of yellow light fell across the yard."
   Paraphrase: "Light came from the window."
10. Original: "'Took you long enough.'"
    Paraphrase: "The grandmother was angry at June."

**Part 3 — Sort (6 items).** Label each EXPLICIT or INFERENCE and
give the evidence.

11. June's grandmother was waiting for her.
12. The porch light had been out for a week.
13. June felt safer after she laughed.
14. The grandmother pointed at the steps.
15. It was dark outside.
16. The grandmother loves June.

## Session 6 — Evidence practice B: fact-bank sorting (Week 4)

Use "The Dance That Points to Flowers" ([Lesson 03](lessons/lesson-03-inference-in-informational-text.md)).

**Part 1 — Fact or inference? (8 tasks).** Label each and quote
the supporting or contradicting words.

1. Bees dance on the honeycomb.
2. The dance happens in daylight.
3. A longer waggle run means food is farther away.
4. The follower bees smell the flowers on the dancer.
5. Karl von Frisch won a Nobel Prize.
6. The dance tells other bees the exact flower species.
7. A waggle run straight up means "fly toward the sun."
8. No other animal communicates directions this way.

**Part 2 — Number reasoning (4 tasks).**

9. Bee A's waggle run: 2 seconds. Bee B's: 5 seconds. Which food
   is farther, and what is the exact sentence that tells you?
10. A waggle run tilts 30° left of straight-up. Where is the
    food relative to the sun? Cite.
11. A reader claims the passage gives the *exact* distance in
    meters for each waggle duration. True or false? Quote the
    relevant wording and explain.
12. Why can't the follower bees just *watch* the dance? Two
    evidence quotes.

## Session 8 — Word-study practice + fluency check (Week 4)

**Part 1 — Root relay (10 tasks).** Split, name the root meaning,
assemble. Use the unit word sets.

1. inspector 2. portable 3. dictate 4. construct
5. reject 6. attraction 7. biography 8. transform
9. prediction 10. manuscript (*manu-* = "hand," bonus root)

**Part 2 — Academic verbs (6 tasks).** Choose *infer, imply,
suggest, indicate, conclude,* or *demonstrate*.

11. The muddy prints ___ a dog passed through.
12. The text never says she's lonely; it only ___ it.
13. After weighing both sides, I ___ the coach was kind.
14. The data ___ that mornings are busiest.
15. The reader ___ the grandmother was worried.
16. The shoulder squeeze ___ affection more strongly than
    words could.

**Part 3 — Fluency check (RF.6.4.a–b).** The learner reads "The
Dance That Points to Flowers" aloud. The adult records: words
correct per minute (optional), accuracy percentage, and every
self-correction. Target: ≥95% accuracy with at least one noted
self-correction. If accuracy is lower, the adult notes which
word parts tripped the learner and re-teaches them in Session 14.

## Session 14 — Reteach and review (Week 6)

Built from the quiz results — the adult picks the 8–10 weakest
items and re-presents them here in new clothing:

- **Inference relay:** the adult reads a new two-sentence
  scenario aloud; the learner states one inference and cites
  the exact words. Five rounds.
- **Root-card game:** using the Lesson 04 word cards, the adult
  shows a root; the learner says its meaning and invents a word
  containing it. Ten cards.
- **Fix-the-paraphrase:** the adult writes three flawed
  paraphrases of passage sentences; the learner names each flaw
  and repairs it.
- **Cite-routine chant:** state → quote → point, recited with
  one fresh example from any unit passage.
