# Week 6 Formative Quiz — Unit 01

**Adult-administered, ~25 minutes.** Covers Lessons 01–05. Score
the same day; every miss becomes a Session 14 reteach target.
Full solutions in the [answer key](../answer-key.md#week-04-quiz).
Total: 20 points.

**Directions to the learner:** Answer in complete sentences where
asked. For every inference, cite your evidence: state it, quote
the exact words, and give the paragraph number.

## Part A — Explicit vs. inference (6 points)

Read this original passage before answering.

> **Paragraph 1.** The recycling bin was already full by 9 a.m.,
> cardboard boxes stacked like a game of Jenga. Priya flattened
> each box before she added it, pressing the creases flat with
> her palms.
>
> **Paragraph 2.** "You're the only one who does that," said
> Devon, tossing his box — unflattened — on top. It slid off and
> landed by Priya's feet. She picked it up, flattened it without
> a word, and tucked it into the bin.

1. (2 pts) Is "The recycling bin was full by 9 a.m." explicit
   or an inference? Quote the exact words and give the
   paragraph number.
2. (2 pts) What can you infer about Priya's attitude toward the
   recycling job? Cite two pieces of evidence.
3. (2 pts) A reader says, "Devon is lazy." Is this a strong
   inference? Explain with evidence — for or against.

## Part B — Citing and paraphrasing (4 points)

4. (2 pts) Paraphrase "cardboard boxes stacked like a game of
   Jenga" without changing its meaning.
5. (2 pts) A classmate paraphrases paragraph 2 as "Devon helped
   Priya recycle." Name the flaw and fix it in one sentence.

## Part C — Word study (6 points)

6. (2 pts) Split *inspection* into affix + root (+ suffix).
   Name the root's meaning and assemble the whole word's
   meaning.
7. (2 pts) Use *imply* and *infer* correctly in two sentences
   about the recycling passage — one about the text, one about
   you the reader.
8. (2 pts) "The sign was *luminous* in the dark hallway; it
   seemed to glow from within." What does *luminous* mean?
   Name the clue type and explain.

## Part D — Informational inference (4 points)

9. (2 pts) From the bee passage in Lesson 03: a waggle run
   points 90° left of straight-up and lasts 4 seconds. What
   should the follower bees do? Cite the passage rule.
10. (2 pts) From the airplane passage in Lesson 05: why does
    the adult hand out candy during *descent* rather than at
    cruising altitude? Two evidence-based sentences.
