# Teacher Guide — Unit 01: Close Reading, Inference, and Textual Evidence

**Adult only.** This guide contains pacing notes, misconception
alerts, differentiation guidance, and readiness/reteach signals.
It does not contain student answers — those live in the separate
[answer key](answer-key.md).

## Unit at a glance

Five lessons across 16 sessions (~40 minutes, 4 per week).
Session rotation: modeled close-reading lesson → evidence
practice → word-study practice → review and discussion. The
through-line is the **cite routine**: state → quote → point
(paragraph number). Every lesson, practice, and assessment uses
it; by Week 6 the learner should run it unprompted.

## Pacing notes

- **Sessions 1–4 (Week 3):** Go slow on Lesson 01. The
  explicit/inference distinction is the unit's foundation; if
  the learner cannot sort statements by the end of Session 2,
  repeat Session 2's Part 3 with a new passage before moving
  on. Session 4's discussion is oral — let the learner argue;
  score participation on claims + citations, not on being right.
- **Sessions 5–8 (Week 4):** Lesson 03's number-reasoning
  (task 1, 9–10 in practice) is the hardest cognitive work in
  the unit; budget extra minutes. Session 8's fluency check is
  diagnostic, not graded — record accuracy and self-corrections
  for comparison in Session 16.
- **Sessions 9–12 (Week 5):** Lesson 05 is independent by
  design. Intervene only after two stuck minutes, and
  intervene with a question ("What are the exact words?"),
  never with the answer. The project draft (Session 11) should
  be messy; the rubric work happens in Session 12.
- **Sessions 13–16 (Week 6):** Score the quiz the same day it
  is taken. Session 14 must be built from actual misses —
  never re-teach what the quiz showed as secure. Session 16
  ends with the essential-question reflection; keep the
  learner's written answer as the unit's portfolio artifact.

## Misconceptions to watch (by lesson)

- **L01:** (1) Any reasonable guess counts as an inference —
  require cited evidence before accepting. (2) Quoting whole
  paragraphs — enforce the "shortest words that prove it"
  rule. (3) *Imply* vs. *infer* — chant: "the text implies,
  the reader infers."
- **L02:** (1) Setting detail = character trait ("sits alone,
  so he's mean") — demand a confirming action. (2)
  Single-detail inferences — require two citations. (3)
  Freezing the first impression — praise revisions out loud.
- **L03:** (1) Treating comparisons ("like a secret code") as
  literal facts. (2) Reversing longer-run = farther — have the
  learner recite the rule before each number task. (3)
  Inferring beyond the passage — enforce the "stay inside the
  paragraph" boundary.
- **L04:** (1) Splitting at the wrong boundary — always run
  the meaning test. (2) *Infer* for what the text does —
  re-drill the chant. (3) Reading past nonsense silently —
  reward every caught self-correction.
- **L05:** (1) Comparisons taken literally ("tiny drumhead").
  (2) Over-inferring from the candy detail. (3) Freezing on
  *Eustachian* — sound it out, move on.

## Differentiation

- **Needing more support:** Use the evidence banks (L02, L05),
  underline-instead-of-copy quoting (L01), oral/dictated
  responses, and the root bookmark (L04). Reduce independent
  task counts (choose 3 of 6) rather than simplifying the
  thinking.
- **Ready for more:** Every lesson has an extension task; the
  strongest stretch is writing trick questions (L05) and
  finding dual-inference sentences (L01, L02, L05).
- **Reading access:** All passages may be read aloud by the
  adult or via text-to-speech; comprehension is assessed, not
  decoding, except in the fluency checks where decoding *is*
  the point.

## Readiness signals (ready for the next step when…)

- L01 → L02: sorts 6 statements into explicit/inferred with
  correct citations (Session 1 exit).
- L02 → L03: produces 2 character inferences, each with 2
  quotes (Session 3 exit).
- L03 → L04: answers 3 number-reasoning items correctly
  (Session 6).
- L04 → L05: assembles 8 of 10 root words correctly
  (Session 8 practice).
- L05 → project: completes 4 of 6 tasks per part without
  hints (Session 9–10).
- Project → quiz: report scores ≥5/10 on the rubric.

## Reteach triggers

- Quiz Part A below 4/6 → re-teach L01 with a new passage;
  the distinction is not secure.
- Quiz Part C below 4/6 → re-teach the root method; check
  whether the learner is splitting at wrong boundaries.
- Quiz Part D below 3/4 → re-teach the "facts defeat guesses"
  rule with the bee passage's paragraph 3.
- Fluency below 95% in Session 8 → daily 5-minute rereads of
  unit passages in Week 5; re-check in Session 16.
- Any learner who treats one detail as proof → run the
  Session 14 inference relay until two-citation answers are
  automatic.

## Answer-key use

The [answer key](answer-key.md) gives worked solutions for
every practice item, quiz question, assessment task, and
project criterion, plus acceptable variation for open
responses. Score open responses against the reasoning, not the
wording — a differently worded inference with valid cited
evidence earns full credit. Never show the answer key to the
learner; never place key content beside a student question.
