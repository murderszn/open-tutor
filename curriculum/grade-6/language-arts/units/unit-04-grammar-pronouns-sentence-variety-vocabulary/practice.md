# Practice — Unit 04

Mixed practice for the Sentence Studio. Sessions A–E match the
unit pacing (Sessions 2, 4, 6, 8, 10). All items are original.
Solutions live in [answer-key.md](answer-key.md) (adult only) —
do not write answers here.

## Practice A — Pronoun case (Session 2)

1. "The trophy was awarded to Maya and ___ (I / me)."
2. "___ (She / Her) and Ana painted the banner."
3. "The judges thanked Ben and ___ (I / me)."
4. "___ (Us / We) set up the chairs before the show."
5. "Ben played better than ___ (I / me)."
6. "That trophy is ___ (he / his)."
7. "Give the microphone to Ben or ___ (I / me)."
8. Fix this sentence, and name the rule it breaks: "Him and Maya
   opened the show."

## Practice B — Intensive, vague, and shifted pronouns (Session 4)

1. "Ana herself wrote the invitations." Intensive or reflexive?
   Why?
2. "Ana taught herself the song." Intensive or reflexive? Why?
3. Fix the shift: "The singers warmed up their voices, and then
   he stepped forward."
4. Fix the shift: "When you enter the hall, a guest should sign
   in."
5. Fix the vague pronouns: "Maya told Ana she had won, and she
   cried." (Name both the winner and the crier.)
6. Fix the vague *this*: "Ana practiced for a month. This
   impressed everyone."
7. What is unclear here, and how would you rewrite it? "Ana put
   the guitar on the chair, and then she knocked it over."
8. What is unclear here, and how would you rewrite it? "The
   principal and Maya checked the lights. They worked."

## Practice C — Punctuation and spelling (Session 6)

1. "Ana our stage manager called places."
2. "The costumes that Ana sewed looked bright." (Punctuate, or
   leave as is and say why.)
3. "The show a spring tradition sold out."
4. "The banner printed by the art club hung over the door."
   (Punctuate, or leave as is and say why.)
5. "The judges one teacher and two parents took notes."
6. Choose: "The (principle / principal) visited the rehearsal."
7. Choose: "(Its / It's) time to begin."
8. Fill in the blanks: "The students left ___ (their / there /
   they're) coats ___ (their / there / they're)."

## Practice D — Vocabulary (Session 8)

1. "The attic was small." Would *cozy* praise it or criticize it?
   What about *cramped*? Explain each in one sentence.
2. "The snow whispered over the roof." Personification? Name the
   human quality and the effect.
3. "Her voice rang like a bell." Personification or simile?
   Explain.
4. Use the context clue: "The crowd was restless, shifting in
   their seats and checking the clock." What does *restless*
   mean?
5. Break apart *misread*: what does *mis-* mean, and what does
   the whole word mean?
6. Break apart *transport*: what do *trans-* and *port* mean, and
   what does the whole word mean? (Check a dictionary to verify.)
7. Compare: "The teacher walked into the room." vs. "The teacher
   stormed into the room." What does *stormed* add, and which
   sentence fits an angry entrance?
8. Connotation: "The soup was cheap." vs. "The soup was
   inexpensive." Which word would a menu use, and why?

## Practice E — Sentence variety and formal style (Session 10)

1. "When the lights dimmed, the show began." Simple, compound,
   or complex?
2. "Maya sang, and the crowd cheered." Simple, compound, or
   complex?
3. Combine: "Ana sewed the costumes. Ben built the sets."
4. Fix the run-on: "The curtain rose the actors froze."
5. Fix the fragment: "After the final song."
6. Combine all three into one sentence: "The show ended. The
   crowd stood. The cast bowed."
7. Rewrite formally: "We gotta have the gym Friday."
8. Spot the slip and fix it: "Dear Principal Alvarez, We thank
   you for your support. The show is gonna be great! We hope you
   will attend."
