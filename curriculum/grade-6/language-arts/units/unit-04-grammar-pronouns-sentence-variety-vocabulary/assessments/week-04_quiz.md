# Formative Quiz — Unit 04, Week 18 (Midyear Check)

**Time:** 40 minutes · **Adult scores the same day** and builds the
Session 14 reteach list from the results. This quiz is formative:
it finds what to reteach, not a final grade. Solutions live in
[answer-key.md](../answer-key.md) (adult only).

## Part A — Unit 04 skills (8 items)

1. Choose: "The award went to Ben and ___ (I / me)."
2. "Maya herself planted the beans." Intensive or reflexive? Why?
3. Fix the shift: "The students finished their work, and then
   she left."
4. Fix the vague pronouns: "Ana told Maya she had won, and she
   cheered." (Name both the winner and the cheerer.)
5. Punctuate: "Ben our drummer missed rehearsal."
6. "The songs that the band played were new." Punctuate, or leave
   as is and say why.
7. Rewrite this sentence to praise the cabin, using a word with
   the same denotation as *tiny*: "The cabin was tiny."
8. "After dinner ended, the students washed the dishes." Simple,
   compound, or complex?

## Part B — Cumulative Units 01–04 check (4 items)

Read this original passage (fictional Riverbend School; all
numbers invented for teaching):

> "The Riverbend School library held a book sale on Saturday.
> Hundreds of donated books covered six long tables. Maya, a sixth
> grader, organized the picture books by topic. The sale raised
> money for new shelves. Some visitors argued that the sale
> should happen every month. The librarian said the sale worked
> because families donated books and students volunteered. By
> noon, most tables were empty."

9. State the central idea of the passage in one sentence.
10. A student claims, "Every visitor wanted a monthly sale." Is
    that claim supported by the passage? Explain with evidence.
11. What does *donated* mean in this passage? Name the context
    clue you used.
12. Rewrite these two flat sentences as one complex sentence:
    "The sale ended. The tables were empty."

**Scoring guide (adult):** 12 items, 1 point each. 10–12: ready
for the unit assessment; review only missed items. 7–9: reteach
the missed standards in Session 14 before the assessment. Below
7: reteach the missed lessons (not just the items) and use the
Session 14 practice sets before assessing.
