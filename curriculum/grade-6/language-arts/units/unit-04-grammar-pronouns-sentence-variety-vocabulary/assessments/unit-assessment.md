# Unit Assessment — Unit 04

**Time:** 40 minutes · **Adult supervision.** This assessment is
not standardized or validated; it checks this unit's objectives
only. Solutions live in [answer-key.md](../answer-key.md) (adult
only). All passages are original and fictional.

## Part A — Edit the announcement (8 tasks)

Read "The Spring Fair Announcement" (fictional Riverbend School).
Do the eight tasks below it. Do not rewrite the whole passage —
answer each task.

> "The Riverbend School spring fair will be held on Saturday, May
> 16. Me and Maya are planning the games. The fair our biggest
> event of the year needs volunteers. The volunteers who sign up
> early will choose their jobs first. Ana told Maya she had won
> the poster contest, she cheered. The students will sell tickets.
> When you sell tickets, a seller should smile. The fair will have
> food. The fair will have music. The fair will have games. The
> games will be held their on the field. The fair is gonna be
> great."

A1. Fix the pronoun-case error in sentence 2.
A2. Punctuate sentence 3's appositive.
A3. Sentence 4: punctuate it, or leave it as is and say why.
A4. Fix the vague pronouns in sentence 5. Name both the winner
and the cheerer.
A5. Fix the pronoun shift in sentence 7.
A6. Combine sentences 8–10 into one sentence, losing no fact.
A7. Fix the spelling error in sentence 11.
A8. Rewrite sentence 12 in formal English.

## Part B — Sentence patterns (3 tasks)

B1. "When you sell tickets, a seller should smile." Simple,
compound, or complex?
B2. "The students will sell tickets, and the teachers will judge
the contests." Simple, compound, or complex?
B3. Combine into one sentence, losing no fact: "The fair opened.
The band played. The crowd danced."

## Part C — Vocabulary (4 tasks)

C1. Would *flashy* praise or criticize a fair poster? Give a
praising word with a similar denotation, and explain the
difference in one sentence.
C2. "The morning sun smiled on the fair." Name the figure of
speech, the human quality, and the effect.
C3. Break apart *reopen*: what does *re-* mean, and what does
the whole word mean?
C4. Use the context clue: "The volunteers were eager, lining up
before the gates opened." What does *eager* mean?

## Part D — Formal letter (1 task)

D1. Write a formal letter (4–6 sentences) to the principal
inviting her to the spring fair on May 16. Include one detail
about the fair (food, music, games, or the poster contest) and
keep one formal style and tone throughout, with a formal closing.

**Scoring (adult):** Part A: 8 points (1 per task). Part B: 3
points. Part C: 4 points. Part D: 5 points (2 for formal tone
throughout, 2 for content — invitation, date, one detail — 1 for
formal closing). Total: 20 points. 17–20: objective met; 13–16:
review missed standards with the learner; below 13: reteach the
missed lessons before moving to Unit 05.
