# Resource Pack — Unit 04: Grammar, Sentence Variety, and Vocabulary

**Goal (adult planning judgment):** Give the guiding adult free,
no-account videos and references that reinforce this unit's five
objectives — pronoun case, intensive/shifted/vague pronouns,
nonrestrictive punctuation and spelling, sentence-pattern variety
with consistent formal tone, and vocabulary through connotation,
personification, and word parts — for a grade-6 learner (ages
11–12). Videos supplement the lessons; they never replace
explicit teaching, practice, or the adult's check.

All external links below were found via web search on 2026-11-01
and are described only as far as the search results establish
them. Runtimes and captions were not checked — preview before
assigning. YouTube serves ads; use an ad-blocked or supervised
session with the learner. Search links are labeled as such: they
are not single verified videos.

## Web references (5, found 2026-11-01)

1. [Pronoun Case — Purdue
   OWL](https://OWL.Purdue.edu/owl/general_writing/grammar/pronouns/pronoun_case.html)
   — adult-facing grammar reference: subjective, objective, and
   possessive pronoun tables and the rule for pronouns after
   forms of *to be*. Supports Lessons 01–02.
2. [Introduction to Defining Clauses — Purdue
   OWL](https://Owl.Purdue.edu/owl/general_writing/grammar/relative_pronouns/index.html)
   — adult-facing reference on relative pronouns and on
   restrictive (defining) vs. non-restrictive (non-defining)
   clauses, including comma treatment. Supports Lesson 03.
3. [E/LA Common Core Standards for Language, Grade 6 (PDF) —
   The Curriculum
   Corner](http://www.thecurriculumcorner.com/wp-content/pdf/6th%20Grade%20Language.pdf)
   — plain listing of the grade-6 language standards used in
   this unit's crosswalk (L.6.1a–e, L.6.2a–b, L.6.3a–b).
   Adult reference for the unit's standards notes; the
   authoritative framework is the published Common Core ELA
   standards.
4. [Vague Pronoun References — Pennington
   Publishing](https://blog.penningtonpublishing.com/how-to-teach-vague-pronoun-references/)
   — teacher guidance on vague pronoun references: antecedents,
   demonstrative pronouns used alone, and revision by adding a
   noun or recasting. Adult-facing; supports Lesson 02.
5. [Common Core State Standards, Grade 6 English Language Arts
   listing — NewPath
   Online](https://www.newpathonline.com/standard/common-core-state-standards/7/english-language-arts_Grade_6/1/6)
   — plain listing of grade-6 ELA standards including L.6.4
   (clarifying word meaning), L.6.5 (figurative language,
   connotations), and L.6.6 (academic vocabulary). Adult
   reference for the unit's standards notes.
