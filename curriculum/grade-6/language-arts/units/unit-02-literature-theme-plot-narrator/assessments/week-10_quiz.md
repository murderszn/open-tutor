## Reading — "The Quiet Harbor" (original)

> **Paragraph 1.** The ferry to the island left at dawn, and
> Tomas was the only passenger. He sat on the cold metal bench
> with his backpack hugged to his chest, watching the harbor
> lights blur and shrink behind the boat.
>
> **Paragraph 2.** His grandmother's letter was folded in his
> jacket pocket. *Come for the summer*, it said. *The lighthouse
> stairs are still too many for my knees.* Tomas had read the
> letter eleven times. He knew every crease in the paper.
>
> **Paragraph 3.** Halfway across, the captain — a woman with
> rope-burned hands — pointed at the gray line on the horizon.
> "There's your island," she said. "She's been waiting." Tomas
> did not know if the captain meant the island or his
> grandmother. He decided it did not matter. Both were true.

## Questions

**1. (RL.6.2)** Which is a *theme* of "The Quiet Harbor," not
just a topic?
- A. "a ferry ride"
- B. "Going somewhere new can feel both lonely and hopeful"
- C. "grandmothers"
- D. "The ferry left at dawn"

**2. (RL.6.2)** Which detail best supports the theme that Tomas
feels both nervous and comforted?
- A. "The ferry to the island left at dawn"
- B. "his backpack hugged to his chest" and "He had read the
  letter eleven times"
- C. "a woman with rope-burned hands"
- D. "the gray line on the horizon"

**3. (RL.6.2)** Write a **two-sentence objective summary** of
the passage. (No opinions.)

**4. (RL.6.3)** Which episode is the **climax** of "The Quiet
Harbor"?
- A. Tomas boards the ferry at dawn
- B. Tomas reads his grandmother's letter for the eleventh time
- C. The captain points at the island and Tomas decides "Both
  were true"
- D. The harbor lights shrink behind the boat

**5. (RL.6.3)** How does Tomas **respond** to the captain's
words, and what does his response show has changed since
paragraph 1? Answer in 2 sentences with one quoted detail.

**6. (RL.6.4)** "watching the harbor lights **blur and
shrink** behind the boat" — the words *blur* and *shrink*
suggest:
- A. the lights are broken
- B. distance growing; leaving something behind
- C. the boat is sinking
- D. Tomas needs glasses

**7. (RL.6.4)** *Come for the summer*, the letter said. *The
lighthouse stairs are still too many for my knees.* What is the
**tone** of the grandmother's words — warm, angry, or bored?
Quote the phrase that sets it most.

**8. (RL.6.5)** Why does paragraph 3 come **last**? In 1–2
sentences, explain what work it does for the story's ending
(theme or resolution).

**9. (RL.6.6)** What is the passage's **point of view**? Give
the pronoun evidence and name one thing the narrator
**cannot** tell us.

**10. (RL.6.9)** "The Quiet Harbor" and the poem "Two Small
Lanterns" (Lesson 06) both involve light in darkness. In 2–3
sentences, name one way the two texts treat a similar idea
**differently** (form or emphasis).
