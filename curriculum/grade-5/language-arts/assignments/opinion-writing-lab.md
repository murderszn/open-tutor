# 📝 Opinion Writing Lab — Learner

Welcome to the **Opinion Writing Lab**! In this assignment, you will choose a topic you care about, build an argument using the **OREO** framework, back it up with clear facts and examples, and write a polished 4-paragraph persuasive opinion essay.

## 🎯 Assignment Objectives
- Formulate a clear, bold opinion statement (thesis).
- Provide at least two distinct reasons backed by concrete evidence and real-world examples.
- Use smooth transition words (*first, in addition, for example, on the other hand, therefore*).
- Address a possible counter-opinion and explain why your viewpoint is stronger.
- Restate your opinion with power in a memorable conclusion.

## 🛠️ Step 1: The OREO Organizer

Fill in each section below before drafting:

```markdown
### My Chosen Topic:
[Type your topic here]

### O — Opinion (Topic Sentence / Thesis):
[State your clear opinion boldly]

### R — Reason 1:
[First major reason why you believe this]
### E — Evidence / Example 1:
[Specific fact, story, or concrete detail that proves Reason 1]

### R — Reason 2:
[Second major reason why you believe this]
### E — Evidence / Example 2:
[Specific fact, story, or concrete detail that proves Reason 2]

### Counter-Opinion & Comeback:
[What might someone who disagrees with you say?]
[Why is your argument still more convincing?]

### O — Opinion Restated (Conclusion):
[Restate your opinion in fresh words with a strong closing thought]
```

## ✅ Learner’s Polish & Submission Checklist
