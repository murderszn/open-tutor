# Grade 5 — Figurative Language Hunt

**Blank reusable assignment.**

## Goal and Instructions

Find a simile, metaphor, personification, hyperbole, and idiom in educator-approved texts. Record the source and explain the meaning or effect of each. Write your own original example of each device without copying source language.

## Blank Response Organizer

- Question or plan: ____
- Source title and author (if used): ____
- Diagram, table, draft, or calculations: ____
- Evidence or check: ____
- Explanation: ____
- Revision or next question: ____

## Deliverables

Provide the work requested above and a brief explanation of your method. Keep the completed response private; this file is a reusable prompt.

## Educator Review

Check completeness, accurate reasoning, labeled units or citations where relevant, and a clear explanation. Give feedback on one strength and one next step.
