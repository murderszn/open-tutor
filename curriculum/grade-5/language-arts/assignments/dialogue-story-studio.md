# Grade 5 — Dialogue Story Studio

**Blank reusable assignment.**

## Goal and Instructions

Create two fictional characters facing a shared problem. Plan their setting and goal. Draft a one- to two-page scene with correctly punctuated dialogue and a new paragraph for each speaker. Revise one exchange to reveal character through actions and speech.
