# Week-22 Quiz — Narrative Craft: Pacing, Dialogue, and Description

**Grade 5 Language Arts, Unit 05 (track weeks 19–22).** Answer on
your own paper. Do not look at the answer key while you work.

**Total: 10 points (1 point per question, unless stated).**

**1.** Put these five story-mountain stages in story order:
climax, exposition, resolution, rising action, falling action.

**2.** Which opening orients the reader? Write **A** or **B**, and
name one orientation fact the better opening gives.
A) "It was dark. She ran. Something shouted."
B) "Maya had been waiting all year for the second weekend in
October, when she was allowed to stay with Gran Rosa at the Gull
Point lighthouse."

**3.** Which sentence is punctuated correctly? Write **A**, **B**,
or **C**.
A) "The fuse has blown." she said.
B) "The fuse has blown", she said.
C) "The fuse has blown," she said.

**4.** Fix the punctuation and capitalization in this sentence:
*"the lamp is out". whispered maya.*

**5.** Punctuate this sentence with a mid-sentence tag:
*we have three minutes whispered maya before the lamp goes out*

**6.** Stretch or skip? Maya's hardest choice is whether to keep
climbing the dark stairs. Should that moment be stretched into a
scene or summarized? Write **stretch** or **skip** and one reason.

**7.** Name the dominant sense in this passage, and quote the detail
that proves it: "Maya took a deep breath, smelled oil and salt and
rain, and climbed on."

**8.** Rewrite this telling sentence as showing, with one sense and
one action. Do not use the word *scared*: "Maya was scared on the
stairs."

**9.** Which ending follows from the lighthouse story? Write **A**,
**B**, or **C**, and name the test the others fail.
A) "Just then, a pirate ship appeared, and Maya had to fight the
pirates all by herself. The end."
B) "In the morning, the storm had blown itself out. Maya stood on
the rocks with her notebook and wrote: *The light went out, and we
climbed the dark stairs, and we fixed it.*"
C) "And the moral of the story is that you should always be brave
and never give up, no matter what. The end."

**10.** Punctuate these two titles correctly: (a) the story *the
night the light went out*; (b) the book *the lighthouse cat*.
