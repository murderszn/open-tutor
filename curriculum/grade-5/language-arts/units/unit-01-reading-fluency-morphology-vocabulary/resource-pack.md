# Resource Pack — Unit 01: Reading Fluency, Morphology, and Precise Vocabulary

**Goal (adult planning judgment):** Give the guiding adult free,
no-account videos and references that reinforce this unit's five
objectives — fluent oral reading, multisyllable decoding, affix/root
word solving, context-clue and dictionary routines, and precise word
choice — for a grade-5 learner (ages 10–11). Videos supplement the
lessons; they never replace explicit teaching, practice, or the adult's
check.

All external links below were opened and confirmed on-topic and loading
on **2026-10-11**. Runtimes and captions were not checked — preview
before assigning. YouTube serves ads; use an ad-blocked or supervised
session with the learner.

## Web references (5 verified)

1. [Flip-a-Chip: Examining Affixes and Roots to Build Vocabulary — ReadWriteThink (NCTE)](https://www.readwritethink.org/classroom-resources/lesson-plans/flip-chip-examining-affixes?tab=4)
   — lesson plan where learners combine affix/root "chips" into words
   and place them in a context-clue paragraph. Checked 2026-10-11:
   page loads, on-topic, full lesson with objectives and extensions.
   Intended audience: teachers (the plan targets grades 6–8; the adult
   adapts it with this unit's word parts). Free, no account.
2. [Repeated Reading — Five from Five](https://fivefromfive.com.au/fluency/evidence-based-fluency-instruction/repeated-reading/)
   — evidence-based repeated-reading procedure: model the passage,
   3–4 rereads, feedback on accuracy/rate/expression, 10–15 minutes.
   Checked 2026-10-11: page loads, procedure confirmed. Intended
   audience: teachers/parents. Free, no account. Mirrors Lesson 01's
   three-read routine — use it to calibrate the adult's coaching.
3. [Teaching Vocabulary — Reading Rockets](https://www.readingrockets.org/topics/vocabulary/articles/teaching-vocabulary)
   — research summary: word-learning strategies are dictionary use,
   morphemic analysis, and contextual analysis; teaches choosing the
   context-appropriate definition. Checked 2026-10-11: page loads,
   on-topic. Intended audience: teachers/parents. Free, no account.
   Background for Lessons 02–05.
4. [Context Clues Vocabulary Guide — English Made Simple](https://englishmadesimple.org/using-context-clues-to-improve-vocabulary-how-to-infer-the-meaning-of-unknown-words-from-context-in-reading-passages/)
   — six clue types with signal words and a worked *perils* example.
   Checked 2026-10-11: page loads, examples on-topic. Intended
   audience: general learners (adult previews; some example words are
   above grade 5 — use the type taxonomy, not the word list). Free, no
   account. Supports Lesson 05.
5. [English Language Arts Standards — Common Core State Standards Initiative](https://www.thecorestandards.org/ELA-Literacy/)
   — official framework landing page for the ELA standards this unit
   references. Checked 2026-10-11: page loads. Intended audience:
   adults. Free, no account. No state adoption or accreditation is
   claimed by this unit.
