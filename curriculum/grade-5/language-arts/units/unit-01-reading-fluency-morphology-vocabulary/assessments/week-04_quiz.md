# Unit 01 — Week-04 Formative Quiz: Morphology and Fluency Strategies

**Unit:** Reading Fluency, Morphology, and Precise Vocabulary (Grade 5
Language Arts) · **Session 16**
**Instructions for the learner:** Answer in your own words. For
"break" questions, show the pieces. This quiz checks the strategies —
it is practice with a score, not a final grade. (Scoring: 10 points,
1 per question; the key is in the [answer key](../answer-key.md#week-04-quiz),
kept separate from this page.)

*Rebuilt for this unit from the track's two legacy morphology quizzes
(prefixes; suffixes) — trimmed, re-sequenced to the lessons, and
extended with fluency-strategy items. The legacy files are superseded
and are not linked as learner-facing material.*

---

**1.** What are the three parts of the fluency trio? In one sentence,
explain what *expression* means when you read aloud.

**2.** You read "the whole river *shivered*" and the sentence stops
making sense. What should you do, step by step? (Name the strategy.)

**3.** Break **misunderstanding** into its prefix, base, and suffix.
Then write what the whole word means.

**4.** The prefix in- ("not") wears four different spellings. Name all
four, and write one example word for each.

**5.** Is *uncle* built from the prefix *un-*? Explain using the meaning
test.

**6.** Break **fearless** into base + suffix. What does the suffix
*-less* mean, and what part of speech is *fearless*?

**7.** *Celebrate* is a verb. What part of speech is **celebration**,
and which suffix caused the change? Explain what the suffix means.

**8.** Break **transportation** into all of its word parts. Name each
part's meaning and write the assembled meaning of the whole word.

**9.** The letters *port* appear in both *import* and *important*.
In which word is *port-* ("carry") a real root? Prove it with the
meaning test.

**10.** Write the three spelling moves used when adding suffixes, with
one example word for each move.
