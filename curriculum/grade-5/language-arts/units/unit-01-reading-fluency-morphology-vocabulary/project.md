# Project — Word Detective Field Journal

**Duration:** 2 weeks (sessions 13–14 for workshops; collecting runs
the whole unit). **Standards:** L.5.4.a–c, L.5.5.c, L.5.6; W.5.4,
W.5.10 (writing routinely); SL.5.1 (discussing finds with the adult).

## The mission

Real detectives work cases from the field — not from worksheets. For
two weeks, the learner collects **20+ unknown or interesting words**
from their own reading (books, articles, recipes, game instructions —
any real text), cracks each one with the unit's toolkit, and finishes
by upgrading 5 of their own vague sentences into precise ones.

## Materials

- A notebook or stapled pages (the Field Journal)
- The [shared word-part cards](assets/word-part-cards.md) (printed or
  copied by hand)
- A print or digital dictionary and thesaurus
- The learner's own daily reading (30+ minutes a day feeds the journal)

## Steps

1. **Set up the journal (session 13).** Create the four-box template
   from Lesson 05's application: **Clue → Parts → Verify → Use.**
   Collect the first 8 words from current reading. For each: copy the
   sentence the word appeared in, name the clue type, break the word
   into prefix/root/suffix, and write an initial meaning guess.
2. **Keep collecting (ongoing).** Add at least 2 words per day until
   the journal holds 20+ entries. Every entry needs the source
   sentence — no word without its context.
3. **Verify (session 14).** For each entry: look the word up, copy the
   part of speech and the *sentence-correct* numbered sense, and write
   "guessed vs. verified" — was the guess right, close, or wrong, and
   which tool (clue, parts, or dictionary) did the most work?
4. **Upgrade (session 14).** Write 5 sentences of your own that use
   vague words (*said, big, good, walked, looked, happy, tired*).
   Rewrite each with a precise word from the journal or thesaurus,
   then run the swap-and-check on each and record the result.
5. **Present (end of week 6).** Read 3 favorite entries aloud to the
   adult: the word, the sentence it came from, how you cracked it, and
   the upgrade sentence you are proudest of. (SL.5.1 — discussion.)

## Deliverables

1. The Field Journal: 20+ entries, each with source sentence, clue
   type named, word parts broken, guessed meaning, dictionary
   verification (part of speech + numbered sense), and guessed-vs.-
   verified note.
2. Five vague→precise sentence upgrades with swap-and-check notes.
3. A one-paragraph reflection: which tool (context clues, word parts,
   or dictionary) helped you most, and one word you will actually use
   from now on.

## Rubric

Scored by the adult using [answer-key.md](answer-key.md#project-rubric)
scoring guidance. Total 20 points.

| Criterion | 4 — Strong | 3 — Solid | 2 — Developing | 1 — Starting |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| **Word collection (4 pts)** | 20+ words, every entry has its source sentence from real reading | 16–19 words, nearly all with source sentences | 10–15 words, or several entries missing source sentences | Fewer than 10 words, or words without contexts |
| **Word-part analysis (4 pts)** | Every entry breaks prefix/root/suffix correctly; meaning test applied to fakes | Most entries broken correctly; minor piece errors | Parts attempted but frequent errors (wrong root, missed disguise) | Parts missing or guessed without breaking |
| **Clue use (4 pts)** | Clue type named correctly for every entry; signal words identified | Clue type correct for most entries | Clue types named but often wrong or missing | No clue types named |
| **Verification (4 pts)** | Dictionary entry recorded (part of speech + correct numbered sense); guessed-vs.-verified note for every entry | Verification present for most entries; sense choice mostly correct | Lookups attempted but sense numbers often wrong or missing | No dictionary verification |
| **Precise-word upgrades (4 pts)** | 5 upgrades; each uses a genuinely more precise word; swap-and-check recorded and sensible | 5 upgrades; mostly precise; swap-and-check present | Fewer than 5 upgrades, or swaps that break the sentence | No upgrades or vague-for-vague swaps |

**Scoring bands:** 18–20 exemplary · 14–17 proficient · 10–13
developing (reteach the weakest criterion, then revise) · below 10
revisit Lessons 02–05 with the teacher guide before revising.

## Safety and privacy

- Words come from the learner's own reading only; no real names,
  photos, schedules, or grades appear in the journal.
- The journal stays in private storage — never in this public
  repository.
