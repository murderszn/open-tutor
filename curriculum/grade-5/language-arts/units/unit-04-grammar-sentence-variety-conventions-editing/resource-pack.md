# Resource Pack — Unit 04: Grammar, Sentence Variety, Conventions, and Editing

**Goal (adult planning judgment):** Give the guiding adult free,
no-account videos and references that reinforce this unit's
objectives — conjunction, preposition, and interjection jobs;
correlative pairs; sentence combining and reducing; perfect verb
tenses; comma rules; title punctuation; spelling checks; and formal
vs. informal register — for a grade-5 learner (ages 10–11). Videos
supplement the lessons; they never replace explicit teaching,
practice, or the adult's check. The unit's own original sentences
and draft carry all core learning; no external source is required.

All external links below were search-verified on **2026-10-31** as
described per item. Runtimes and captions were not checked — preview
before assigning. YouTube serves ads; use an ad-blocked or supervised
session with the learner. Nothing here is claimed as state-adopted or
accredited.
