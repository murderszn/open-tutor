# Week-04 Formative Quiz — Unit 04: Grammar, Sentence Variety, Conventions, and Editing

**Name:** ______________________ **Date:** ______________

This quiz checks the unit so far. It does not count as the unit grade;
misses are re-taught in the corrections discussion. Answer in your own
words unless a question asks for a correction. Do not use the answer
key — it is a separate file for the adult.

**Total: 10 points (1 point per question, unless stated).**

Read this paragraph (original, written for this quiz) and answer
questions 1–4:

> In the morning, Maya, Jon, and Lee carried banners to the river.
> They had painted the banners before breakfast. Either Maya or Jon
> will lead the march. The crowd cheered, didn't they?

1. In sentence 1, what is the job of *and*? Does it join words or
   clauses? Explain in one sentence.
2. What tense is *had painted* in sentence 2? Why is that tense
   correct here (which two past events does it order)?
3. In sentence 3, name the correlative pair and say whether its two
   halves are parallel. Explain in one sentence.
4. In sentence 4, which comma rule puts a comma before *didn't
   they*? Name the rule.

5. Fix the series punctuation: "We packed apples cheese and juice."
6. Fix the correlative pair: "Neither Maya or Jon will stay."
7. Fix both tense errors: "She paints the sign yesterday and hung
   it tomorrow."
8. Format these titles correctly: a novel called *the lighthouse
   cat*; a poem called *rain on tin roofs*. (Use italics for the
   novel and quotation marks for the poem.)
9. Rewrite this message formally for a school noticeboard, keeping
   every fact: "Hey! Wanna come Saturday? Gonna be fun!"
10. Combine these three sentences into one sentence without losing
    an idea: "The band played. The crowd danced. The lanterns
    glowed."
