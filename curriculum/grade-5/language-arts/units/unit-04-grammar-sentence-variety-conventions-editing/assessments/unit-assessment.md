## Part A — Reading and editing the new passage (14 points)

Read "The Night Market" (original, written for this assessment).
Paragraph 2 contains errors for question 4; paragraphs 1 and 3 are
correct as printed.

> **1.** On the first Friday of every month, the town square
> becomes a night market. Stalls sell bread, cheese, and jam.
> Lanterns hang above the tables, and musicians play near the
> fountain.
>
> **2.** Last month, my brother and I went to the market after
> sunset. We buyed tickets for the raffle, and we tasted bread
> cheese and jam at three stalls. The band played our favorite
> song, morning train, and the crowd cheered didn't they.
>
> **3.** By ten o'clock, we will have visited every stall, and we
> will go home with full bags. The night market is busy, but it is
> never rushed. It is the best Friday of the month.

1. (2 points) In paragraph 1, what is the job of *and* in
   "Lanterns hang above the tables, and musicians play near the
   fountain"? Does it join words or clauses, and why is the comma
   there?
2. (2 points) Find one prepositional phrase in paragraph 1, write
   it out, and name its object (the noun or pronoun after the
   preposition).
3. (2 points) In paragraph 3, what tense is *will have visited*?
   What does it tell the reader about *when* the visiting is
   finished, compared with going home?
4. (8 points) Paragraph 2 contains exactly 4 errors. Find all 4,
   write each correction, and name the rule each correction
   follows (2 points per error: 1 for the fix, 1 for the rule).

## Part B — Sentence variety, tense, titles, register (10 points)
