# Resource Pack — Unit 03: Informational Text Synthesis, Structure, and Evidence

**Goal (adult planning judgment):** Give the guiding adult free,
no-account videos and references that reinforce this unit's
objectives — main ideas and summaries, text structures, multiple
accounts, reasons matched to evidence, and synthesis with source
lists — for a grade-5 learner (ages 10–11). Videos supplement the
lessons; they never replace explicit teaching, practice, or the
adult's check. The unit's own original passages carry all core
learning; no external source is required.

All external links below were opened or search-verified on
**2026-10-24** as described per item. Runtimes and captions were not
checked — preview before assigning. YouTube serves ads; use an
ad-blocked or supervised session with the learner. Nothing here is
claimed as state-adopted or accredited.

## Web references (6 verified by search on 2026-10-24)

1. [Beavers Work to Improve Habitat — U.S. Fish and Wildlife Service](https://www.fws.gov/story/beavers-work-improve-habitat?page=40)
   — beavers as "ecosystem engineers"; dams create deep ponds that
   shelter beavers and store cool water for fish; ponds store
   groundwater that feeds streamside plants. Checked 2026-10-24:
   search result confirmed on-topic with matching description.
   Intended audience: general readers (adult reads or excerpts;
   some vocabulary is above grade 5). Free, no account. Background
   for Lessons 01 and 03; the pond-leveler idea in Lesson 03 is
   consistent with the Service's beaver-conflict material.
2. [Beaver Conservation Strategy — U.S. Fish and Wildlife Service](https://www.fws.gov/project/beaver-conservation-strategy)
   — beaver dams create wetlands that shelter many fish and wildlife
   species and help conserve water in drought. Checked 2026-10-24:
   search result confirmed on-topic. Intended audience: adults.
   Free, no account. Background for Lessons 01 and 05.
3. [Beaver — Yellowstone National Park (U.S. National Park Service)](https://www.nps.gov/yell/learn/nature/beaver.htm)
   — beaver habitat, foods (willow, aspen, cottonwood), dams on
   small streams, lodges and bank dens. Checked 2026-10-24: search
   result confirmed on-topic. Intended audience: general readers.
   Free, no account. Background for Lessons 01 and 03.
4. [Acadia's North American Beaver: The Ultimate Keystone Species — National Park Service](https://www.nps.gov/articles/north-american-beaver-acadia.htm)
   — beavers as ecosystem engineers; dams turn small streams into
   ponds and lakes used by other animals; lodges with underwater
   entrances. Checked 2026-10-24: search result confirmed on-topic.
   Intended audience: general readers. Free, no account. Background
   for Lessons 01 and 03.
5. [Internal Text Structures — LibreTexts (Community College of Allegheny County, Reading and Writing for Learning)](https://human.libretexts.org/Courses/Community_College_of_Allegheny_County/Reading_and_Writing_for_Learning/04%3A_Academic_Literacy-_Psychology_as_a_Science_(Week_4)/4.02%3A_Internal_Text_Features/4.2.02%3A_Internal_Text_Structures)
