# Culminating Unit Assessment — Unit 03: Informational Text Synthesis

**Name:** ______________________ **Date:** ______________

This assessment uses a passage you have not seen before. Read it
carefully, then answer in your own words unless a question asks for a

adult.

**Total: 24 points.**

## Part A — Reading the new passage (14 points)

Read "The Garden That Drinks Rain" (original, written for this
assessment):

> **1.** On a dry hill behind the school, the garden beds cracked
> every August. The soil was thin, the sun was strong, and watering
> cans could not keep up. In September, the garden club decided to
> change how the garden held water.
>
> **2.** First, the club dug shallow ditches across the slope. Then
> they lined the ditches with stones. Next, they planted grasses
> along the edges to slow the rain. Finally, when autumn rain fell,
> the ditches caught it, and the beds below stayed damp for days.
>
> **3.** Because the ditches slowed the rain, less soil washed away.
> As a result, the beds kept their soil, and the plants grew taller
> than the year before. In contrast, the old garden on the flat
> field next door, which had no ditches, lost soil in every storm
> and grew shorter beans.
>
> **4.** Two club members disagreed about the work. Maya said, "The
> ditches saved the garden. We should dig more of them before
> spring." Jon said, "The ditches helped the beds, but they took
> every free afternoon in September. We should ask the town for help
> next time, so the work does not fall on a few people." Both agreed
> that the beds stayed damp longer than before.

1. (2 points) Write the topic of the passage in three words or
   fewer, and write two main ideas of the whole passage, each as one
   sentence in your own words.
2. (2 points) For each main idea in question 1, give one key detail
   that proves it. Quote at least one detail exactly.
3. (2 points) Paragraph 2 is mainly which structure — chronology,
   comparison, cause/effect, or problem/solution? Quote two signal
   words that prove it.
4. (2 points) Paragraph 3 is mainly which structure? Explain your
   answer in two sentences, naming the signal words and the main
   job of the paragraph. (Note: paragraph 3 contains more than one
   structure — name the one that does the main job, and say what
   the other structure is doing.)
5. (2 points) Draw or write a cause/effect chain for paragraph 3
   with at least three links, using details from the passage.
6. (2 points) Maya and Jon: write one thing they agree on and one
   thing they disagree on, each with a quotation. Is their
   disagreement mainly about facts or about point of view? Explain
   in one sentence.
7. (2 points) Write a 3-sentence summary of the passage. It must
   keep both main ideas from question 1 and leave out small
   details.

## Part B — Synthesizing (10 points)

8. (3 points) Paraphrase this sentence from paragraph 3 in your own
   words, changing the words and the sentence shape, and keeping
   the meaning: "Because the ditches slowed the rain, less soil
   washed away."
9. (3 points) A writer claims, "The garden club's ditches helped the
   garden in two ways." Match each piece of evidence to the way it
   supports — (a) "the beds stayed damp longer" or (b) "the beds
   kept their soil" — or mark it "neither": (i) "the ditches caught
   it, and the beds below stayed damp for days"; (ii) "less soil
   washed away"; (iii) "the garden beds cracked every August."
   Explain one of your matches in a sentence.
10. (4 points) Write a 4-sentence synthesis answering: "Was the
    garden club's project worth the work?" Use at least two parts of
    the passage (for example, the results and the disagreement),
    include one short quotation with its paragraph named, include
    the counter-point (Jon's cost) fairly, and end with your own
    judgment. Your judgment may agree with Maya, with Jon, or with
    neither — it must be supported by the passage.
