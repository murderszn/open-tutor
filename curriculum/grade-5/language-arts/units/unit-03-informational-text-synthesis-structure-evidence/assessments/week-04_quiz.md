# Week-04 Formative Quiz — Unit 03: Informational Text Synthesis

**Name:** ______________________ **Date:** ______________

This quiz checks the unit so far. It does not count as the unit grade;
misses are re-taught in the corrections discussion. Answer in your own
words unless a question asks for a quotation. Do not use the answer
key — it is a separate file for the adult.

**Total: 10 points (1 point per question, unless stated).**

Read this paragraph (original, written for this quiz) and answer
questions 1–4:

> In autumn, the Willow Creek beavers repaired their dam. First they
> packed fresh mud into the gaps. Then they piled new branches on top.
> Because the wall grew stronger, the pond stayed deep through the
> winter. As a result, the beavers could swim under the ice to their
> food store.

1. What is the topic of this paragraph, in three words or fewer?
2. Write one main idea of this paragraph in your own words.
3. This paragraph uses both structures. Name one chronology signal
   word and one cause/effect signal word from it, and say which
   structure the last two sentences mainly use.
4. Name one cause and its effect from this paragraph.

Read this comparison paragraph (original) and answer questions 5–6:

> Willow Creek's pond and Dry Fork's stream are alike in one way:
> both flow through farm fields. However, they differ in July.
> Willow Creek's pond still held water, while Dry Fork shrank to a
> trickle. Similarly, both had minnows, but only Willow Creek had a
> heron.

5. Name one similarity and one difference from this paragraph, each
   with a quoted detail.
6. Underline the comparison signal words in the paragraph (in your
   copy, write them here): ______________________

Read these two sentences about the same event and answer questions
7–8:

> Tomas: "The field stayed under water for three days."
> Ana: "The marsh beside the creek filled with water in spring."

7. Do Tomas and Ana agree or disagree about the event itself (that
   water spread in spring)? Explain in one sentence.
8. Label each statement fact or opinion: (a) "The field stayed under
   water for three days." (b) "The dam should be lowered."

Answer questions 9–10 without a passage:

9. Paraphrase this sentence in your own words, changing the words
   and the sentence shape: "Frogs laid eggs among the pond plants."
10. A writer claims, "Beaver ponds help towns in dry summers," and
    gives this evidence: "Beavers gnaw trees with strong teeth."
    Does that evidence support that point? Explain in one sentence
    why or why not.
