# Assets — Unit 03: Informational Text Synthesis

## beaver-pond-illustration.webp

- **File:** `beaver-pond-illustration.webp`
- **Prompt:** "A clear, friendly educational illustration of a
  beaver pond in daylight for grade 5 students: a beaver swimming
  near a dam made of sticks and mud, a small lodge of branches,
  calm pond water reflecting trees, a heron standing at the water's
  edge, and a grassy bank with a few trees. No words, no letters,
  no labels, no people."
- **Generation tool:** Muse built-in image-generation pipeline
  (media.generate_image), 2026-10-24.
- **Learning purpose:** Used in Lesson 01's "Observe and say"
  activity. The learner lists what the picture shows that a text
  about beavers might also mention, and notices what the picture
  cannot tell (why beavers build, in what order, what happened
  next) — practising drawing on more than one source (RI.5.7).
- **Factual review (2026-10-24, inspected before linking):** The
  image shows a beaver with a flat tail swimming in a calm pond, a
  dam of sticks and mud crossing the water, a lodge of piled
  branches in the pond, a grey heron at the grassy bank, trees,
  and cattails. No words, letters, or labels appear. No people
  appear. The scene is plausible as an illustration; it is not a
  photograph, not a field observation, and not evidence about any
  real pond, and the lesson says so.
- **Caption:** AI illustration of a beaver pond, generated for
  this unit. It is an illustration, not a photograph, and it is not
  evidence about a real pond.
- **Alt text:** "A beaver swims in a calm pond beside a dam of
  sticks and mud. A lodge of piled branches sits in the water
  behind the dam, a grey heron stands at the grassy bank, and
  trees and cattails grow around the pond."
- **Text-only alternative:** Printed in Lesson 01 directly under
  the image: a paragraph describing the beaver, dam, lodge, heron,
  pond, trees, and cattails, so a learner who cannot see the
  picture can answer the same "Observe and say" questions.
- **Known limitations:** Watercolour-style illustration; relative
  sizes are artistic, not to scale. The heron's species is not
  labeled in the image and is not identified in the lesson beyond
  "heron." Do not use the image to teach beaver anatomy, dam
  measurements, or any real location — Willow Creek is fictional.
