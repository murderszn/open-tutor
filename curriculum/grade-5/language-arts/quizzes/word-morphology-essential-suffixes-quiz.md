# Grade 5 Language Arts — Word Morphology: Essential Suffixes Quiz

**Topic:** Word Morphology: Essential Suffixes  

This quiz evaluates your understanding of suffixes—word endings that change a word's meaning or grammatical part of speech.

## 📋 Quiz Questions

**1.** What is a **suffix** and where is it located in a word?

**2.** What does the suffix **-ful** mean? Give two example words.

**3.** What does the suffix **-less** mean? Give two example words.

**4.** How do the words *"careful"* and *"careless"* illustrate the opposite meanings created by these two suffixes?

**5.** What does the suffix **-able** (or **-ible**) mean? Give two example words.

**6.** What part of speech does the suffix **-ly** typically create when added to an adjective like *quick* or *quiet*?

**7.** What does the suffix **-ment** turn a verb into? (Example: *enjoy* $\rightarrow$ *enjoyment*).

**8.** What does the suffix **-tion** or **-sion** mean? Give two example words.

**9.** If *"navigate"* is a verb meaning to steer a course, what is *"navigation"*?

**10.** Identify the base word and suffix in the word **"fearless"** and write a sentence using it.

### Model Answers & Rubric (10 Points Total: 1 point per question)

**Question 1:**  
A suffix is a letter or group of letters added to the end of a base word that modifies its meaning or changes its part of speech.

**Question 2:**

**Question 3:**

**Question 4:**  
'Careful' means full of care or caution; 'careless' means lacking care or reckless.

**Question 5:**

**Question 7:**
