## 📋 Quiz Questions

**1.** What is the primary purpose of a published book review?

**2.** What is the danger of including too much plot summary in a book review?

**3.** What is a "spoiler" and why should reviewers avoid them?

**4.** Name the three main sections of a standard 5th-grade book review.

**5.** What should a reviewer evaluate when critiquing a story's **characters**?

**6.** What should a reviewer evaluate when critiquing a story's **pacing**?

**7.** Why is giving specific reasons and textual examples important when you award a book a 3-star or 5-star rating?

**8.** What is a **target audience recommendation** in a review?

**9.** Which sentence is more helpful to a reader in a review?
   A) This book was boring and bad.
   B) While the opening chapters set up an intriguing mystery, the middle section dragged because the characters repeated the same search three times without discovering new clues.

**10.** Write a brief 2-sentence recommendation advising who would enjoy reading a book like *Percy Jackson*.

### Model Answers & Rubric (10 Points Total: 1 point per question)

**Question 1:**  
To give readers an honest evaluation of a book's strengths and weaknesses so they can decide whether they want to read it.

**Question 2:**  
It bores the reader and spoils the surprises, leaving no room for actual critique and thoughtful analysis.

**Question 3:**  
A spoiler reveals key plot twists, mystery solutions, or the ending, ruining the discovery experience for future readers.

**Question 4:**  
1) Hook & brief premise/summary, 2) Critique of strengths and weaknesses (characters, pacing, themes), 3) Star rating & recommendation.

**Question 5:**  
Whether the characters felt realistic, had distinct personalities, showed believable motives, and grew over the course of the story.

**Question 6:**  
Whether the plot moved smoothly—neither rushing through critical moments nor bogging down in boring filler.

**Question 7:**  
Ratings without explanation are meaningless; specific reasons prove to the reader why the book earned that evaluation.

**Question 8:**  
A statement identifying what specific group of readers (age, interests, fans of other specific authors) would enjoy the book most.

**Question 9:**  
B (It provides concrete critique of pacing and plot construction rather than a vague complaint).

**Question 10:**  
Acceptable model: *"I highly recommend Percy Jackson to readers who love fast-paced mythology, clever humor, and non-stop action. Fans of Harry Potter will especially appreciate the camaraderie and epic monster quests!"*

**Grading Scale:**
- 10/10: Exemplary Mastery (A+)
- 9/10: Proficient (A)
- 8/10: Solid Understanding (B)
- 7/10: Review Needed (C)
- Below 7: Needs 1-on-1 reteaching session with educator
