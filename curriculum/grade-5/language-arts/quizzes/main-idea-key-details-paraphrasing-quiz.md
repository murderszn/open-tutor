# Grade 5 Language Arts — Main Idea, Key Details & Paraphrasing Quiz

**Topic:** Main Idea, Key Details & Paraphrasing  

This quiz evaluates your informational reading skills: finding the central idea of a passage, identifying supporting details, and paraphrasing without copying.

## 📋 Quiz Questions

**1.** What is the definition of the **main idea** of an informational article?

**2.** How does the main idea differ from the overall **topic** of a passage?

**3.** What are **supporting details** and what role do they play?

**4.** Read this passage: *"Honeybees are essential to global agriculture. As they fly from flower to flower gathering nectar, they transfer pollen that enables fruits, vegetables, and nuts to grow. Without bee pollination, crops like apples, almonds, and berries would produce drastically lower yields."* What is the main idea?

**5.** Identify two supporting details from the honeybee passage above.

**6.** What does it mean to **paraphrase** information?

**7.** Why is copying a sentence word-for-word from a website or book without quotation marks called plagiarism?

**8.** Paraphrase this sentence in your own words: *"The cheetah's flexible spine and large nasal cavities allow it to reach speeds up to 60 miles per hour during short sprints."*

**9.** True or False: A summary should include every single fact mentioned in the original text.

**10.** What are the three most important things to include when summarizing an informational passage?

### Model Answers & Rubric (10 Points Total: 1 point per question)

**Question 1:**  
The main idea is the central point or primary message that the author wants the reader to understand about a subject.

**Question 2:**  
The topic is simply the general subject (e.g., 'Honeybees' or 'Volcanoes'), while the main idea is the full statement or argument about that topic (e.g., 'Honeybees play a vital role in pollinating food crops').

**Question 3:**  
Supporting details are specific facts, statistics, descriptions, and examples that explain, prove, or expand upon the main idea.

**Question 4:**  
Honeybees are essential pollinators that make the growth of many agricultural crops possible.

**Question 5:**  
Detail 1: Bees transfer pollen as they gather nectar; Detail 2: Without bees, crops like apples, almonds, and berries would have drastically lower yields.

**Question 6:**  
Paraphrasing means expressing someone else's ideas completely in your own words while retaining the original meaning.

**Question 7:**  
Because using someone else's exact words or unique ideas without credit is taking their intellectual work dishonestly.

**Question 8:**  
Acceptable model: *"Cheetahs can sprint up to 60 mph in bursts thanks to their bendable backbones and wide nasal passages that take in plenty of oxygen."*

**Question 9:**  
False (A summary only includes the main idea and major key points, omitting minor details).

**Question 10:**  
1) The central main idea, 2) The most significant supporting key points, 3) Objective wording written in your own voice without personal bias.
