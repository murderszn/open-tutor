## 📋 Quiz Questions

**1.** Explain the purpose of each letter in the **OREO** opinion writing framework.

**2.** Read this sentence and identify the figurative language device used: *"The microphone was a hungry beast, catching every single whisper in the studio."*

**3.** What is the difference between a **simile** and a **metaphor**?

**4.** Correct the dialogue punctuation in this sentence: *"We won first place shouted Marcus running across the gym."*

**5.** What does the prefix **pre-** mean, and what does **un-** mean?

**6.** What does the suffix **-ful** mean, and what does **-less** mean?

**7.** Identify the main text structure used in an article titled: *"How the Invention of the Printing Press Transformed European Literacy Between 1450 and 1500."*

**8.** Join these two sentences into a compound sentence with a comma and a coordinating conjunction: *"Learner edited the raw footage. Learner composed the background music track."*

**9.** Identify whether the following statement is Fact or Opinion: *"The Amazon Rainforest produces roughly 20 percent of the Earth's oxygen supply."*

**10.** What is the most important lesson or storytelling technique you learned in Language Arts this semester that you will use in your future writing or YouTube videos?

### Model Answers & Rubric (10 Points Total: 1 point per question)

**Question 1:**  
O = State your Opinion clearly; R = Give a strong Reason; E = Provide concrete Evidence/Examples; O = Restate your Opinion with closing power.

**Question 2:**  
Metaphor (comparing the microphone directly to a hungry beast without 'like' or 'as'). Also contains personification.

**Question 3:**  
A simile compares two things using 'like' or 'as', whereas a metaphor compares two things directly by stating one thing is the other.

**Question 4:**  
*"We won first place!" shouted Marcus, running across the gym.*

**Question 5:**  
**pre-** means before (e.g., preview); **un-** means not or opposite of (e.g., unlock).

**Question 6:**  
**-ful** means full of (e.g., hopeful); **-less** means without or lacking (e.g., hopeless).

**Question 7:**  
Chronological / Sequential (or Cause and Effect).

**Question 8:**  
*"Learner edited the raw footage, and Learner composed the background music track."*

**Question 9:**  
Fact (this is a scientifically researched measurement that can be verified).

**Question 10:**  
Open student response. Look for thoughtful reflection on dialogue formatting, show-don't-tell sensory language, structuring arguments logically, or using figurative comparisons to make video scripts pop!

**Grading Scale:**
- 10/10: Exemplary Mastery (A+)
- 9/10: Proficient (A)
- 8/10: Solid Understanding (B)
- 7/10: Review Needed (C)
- Below 7: Needs 1-on-1 reteaching session with educator
