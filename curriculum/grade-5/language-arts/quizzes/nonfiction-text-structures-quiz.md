**Topic:** Nonfiction Text Structures

## 📋 Quiz Questions

**1.** Name the five common informational text structures.

**2.** Which text structure organizes events in the order they occurred in time?

**3.** Give two signal words that indicate a **Chronological / Sequential** structure.

**4.** Which text structure explains why something occurred and what happened as a result?

**5.** Identify the text structure: *"Both African and Asian elephants are large herbivores with long trunks. However, African elephants have much larger ears shaped like the continent of Africa and possess two finger-like tips on their trunks, whereas Asian elephants have smaller rounded ears and a single trunk tip."*

**6.** Identify the text structure: *"Because heavy rains fell continuously for forty-eight hours, the river crested above its banks, which caused widespread flooding in the low-lying farmlands."*

**7.** What signal words tell you an author is using a **Compare and Contrast** structure? (List at least two).

**8.** Describe the **Problem and Solution** text structure.

**9.** Why does recognizing the author's text structure help you comprehend and summarize a complex article?

**10.** What graphic organizer would you choose to take notes on a **Compare and Contrast** text?

### Model Answers & Rubric (10 Points Total: 1 point per question)

**Question 1:**  
1) Chronological / Sequential, 2) Cause and Effect, 3) Problem and Solution, 4) Compare and Contrast, 5) Description.

**Question 2:**  
Chronological / Sequential.

**Question 3:**  
Words like: *first, next, then, finally, previously, in 1942, afterwards, subsequently*.

**Question 4:**  
Cause and Effect.

**Question 5:**  
Compare and Contrast (contrasting African and Asian elephants).

**Question 6:**  
Cause and Effect (rain caused the river to overflow and flood).

**Question 7:**  
Words like: *similarly, on the other hand, however, unlike, both, in contrast, whereas*.

**Question 8:**  
The author presents a conflict, dilemma, or challenge (the problem) and then explains one or more ways it can be or was resolved (the solution).

**Question 9:**  
It reveals the author's logical blueprint, allowing the reader to predict what information comes next and locate key relationships quickly.

**Question 10:**  
A Venn Diagram (or a two-column comparison matrix).
