# Grade 5 Language Arts — Figurative Language: Similes & Metaphors Quiz

**Topic:** Figurative Language: Similes & Metaphors  

This quiz evaluates your ability to identify, analyze, and craft two of the most popular comparative literary devices: similes and metaphors.

## 📋 Quiz Questions

**1.** What is the definition of a **simile**?

**2.** What two linking words are characteristic of similes?

**3.** What is the definition of a **metaphor**?

**4.** How can you immediately tell the difference between a simile and a metaphor?

**5.** Identify whether this is a Simile or a Metaphor: *"The knight was a fortress on the battlefield, immovable and resolute."*

**6.** Identify whether this is a Simile or a Metaphor: *"Her voice rang out clear as a bell across the quiet auditorium."*

**7.** In the metaphor *"The internet is an ocean of endless information,"* what two things are being compared?

**8.** Explain the deeper meaning of this simile: *"The detective was as sharp as a tack."*

**9.** Turn this plain sentence into an engaging **simile**: *"The racecar was very fast."*

**10.** Turn this plain sentence into a vivid **metaphor**: *"The summer sun was very hot."*

### Model Answers & Rubric (10 Points Total: 1 point per question)

**Question 1:**  
A simile is a figure of speech that compares two different things using the words 'like' or 'as'.

**Question 2:**  
'Like' and 'as'.

**Question 3:**  
A metaphor is a figure of speech that directly asserts one thing is another thing to highlight a shared quality, without using 'like' or 'as'.

**Question 4:**  
A simile explicitly uses 'like' or 'as' to make the comparison, whereas a metaphor makes a direct equation (saying X is Y).

**Question 5:**  
Metaphor (it says the knight *was* a fortress).

**Question 6:**  
Simile (it uses *as* a bell).

**Question 7:**  
The internet and a vast ocean.

**Question 8:**  
It means the detective is highly intelligent, perceptive, and quick to notice subtle clues.

**Question 9:**  
Acceptable model: *"The racecar shot past the grandstands like a guided missile."*

**Question 10:**  
Acceptable model: *"The midday sun was a blazing gold coin pressed against the sky."*
