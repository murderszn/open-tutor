# Grade 5 Language Arts — Word Morphology: Power Prefixes Quiz

**Topic:** Word Morphology: Power Prefixes  

This quiz explores word parts, focusing on common prefixes that change the meaning of root words.

## 📋 Quiz Questions

**1.** What is a **prefix**?

**2.** Where does a prefix attach to a base word (beginning, middle, or end)?

**3.** What does the prefix **un-** mean? Give two example words.

**4.** What does the prefix **re-** mean? Give two example words.

**5.** What does the prefix **pre-** mean? Give two example words.

**6.** What does the prefix **dis-** mean? Give two example words.

**7.** What does the prefix **mis-** mean? Give two example words.

**8.** If the root word *"view"* means to look at, what does *"preview"* mean?

**9.** How does adding the prefix *"dis-"* change the meaning of the word *"appear"*?

**10.** Break down the word **"misunderstanding"** into its prefix, base, and suffix.

### Model Answers & Rubric (10 Points Total: 1 point per question)

**Question 1:**  
A prefix is an affix (letter or group of letters) placed at the beginning of a root or base word to alter its meaning.

**Question 2:**  
At the beginning of the base word.

**Question 3:**  
Meaning: not or opposite of. Examples: *unhappy, unlock, uncertain, unfair*.

**Question 4:**  
Meaning: again or back. Examples: *replay, rebuild, reread, rewrite*.

**Question 5:**  
Meaning: before. Examples: *preview, prepay, preheat, prehistoric*.

**Question 6:**  
Meaning: opposite of, not, or away. Examples: *disagree, disappear, disconnect, distrust*.

**Question 7:**  
Meaning: wrongly, badly, or incorrectly. Examples: *misplace, misunderstand, misjudge, miscalculate*.

**Question 8:**  
To view or inspect something before it is publicly shown or released.

**Question 9:**  
It turns it into the opposite action: to vanish from sight or cease to be visible.

**Question 10:**  
Prefix: **mis-** (wrongly), Base word: **understand**, Suffix: **-ing**.
