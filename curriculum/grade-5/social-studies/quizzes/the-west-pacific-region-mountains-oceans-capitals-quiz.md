### Questions

**1.** What is the capital of **California**, the most populous state in the nation? *(Hint: It is not Los Angeles or San Francisco!)*

**2.** What is the capital of **Washington State**, located near the scenic Puget Sound? *(Hint: It is not Seattle!)*

**3.** What is the capital of **Oregon**, located in the lush Willamette Valley? *(Hint: It is not Portland!)*

**4.** Which jagged, snow-capped mountain range stretches over 3,000 miles from Canada down through Montana, Idaho, Wyoming, and Colorado?

**5.** What is the largest state in the United States by land area, separated from the lower 48 states by Canada?

**6.** What is the capital of **Alaska**, which can only be reached by boat or airplane because there are no roads leading into it?

**7.** Name the 50th U.S. state, made entirely of a chain of volcanic islands in the central Pacific Ocean, and name its capital city.

**8.** What is the capital of **Colorado**, nicknamed the "Mile High City" because its elevation is exactly 5,280 feet above sea level?

**9.** What is the capital of **Nevada**, famous for its silver mining history and Sierra Nevada mountains?

**10.** What massive body of water borders the entire western coastline of the continental United States?
