### Questions

**1.** In 1620, which sailing ship brought 102 passengers (including the Pilgrims, or Separatists) across the stormy Atlantic to Cape Cod?

**2.** Before stepping ashore at Plymouth, the Pilgrims wrote and signed the **Mayflower Compact**. Why is this document considered a vital early milestone in American self-government?

**3.** Who was the Wampanoag man (also known as Squanto) who spoke English and showed the starving Pilgrims how to plant corn using fish for fertilizer and catch eels?

**4.** In the autumn of 1621, the Pilgrims and the Wampanoag people gathered for a three-day harvest celebration. What modern holiday traces its origins to this gathering?

**5.** Who served as governor of Plymouth Colony for over 30 years and wrote the famous history *Of Plymouth Plantation*?

**6.** In 1630, another group called the **Puritans** sailed to Massachusetts Bay to build a strictly religious society. What famous phrase did their leader John Winthrop use to describe their goal of being a moral beacon to the world?

**7.** Why was farming in the New England colonies so difficult compared to the southern colonies?

**8.** Because farming was limited to small family plots, what three major sea-based industries did New England colonists develop to build a thriving economy?

**9.** What was a New England **"Town Meeting,"** and how did it give ordinary male churchgoers direct democratic voting power over local village affairs?

**10.** Why did Roger Williams leave Massachusetts Bay to establish the colony of **Rhode Island** in 1636?

<summary><strong>Parent Answer Key (Click to Expand)</strong></summary>

1. **The Mayflower.**
2. **It was the first written framework of self-government in English North America**, where the signers agreed to unite under "just and equal laws" chosen by the community for the general good.
3. **Tisquantum (Squanto).**
4. **Thanksgiving.**
5. **William Bradford.**
6. **"A City upon a Hill."**
7. **The soil was thin and filled with rocks, and the winters were long, cold, and harsh**, leaving a very short growing season.
8. **Shipbuilding, fishing (especially Atlantic cod), and whaling / ocean trade.**
9. **A community gathering in the town meetinghouse where citizens met annually to discuss town issues, vote on local laws, set taxes, and elect local selectmen.**
10. **He believed in religious freedom and the separation of church and state**, and insisted that English settlers should fairly purchase land from Native Americans rather than taking it.
