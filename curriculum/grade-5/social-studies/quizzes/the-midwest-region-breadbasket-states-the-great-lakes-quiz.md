### Questions

**1.** Name the five **Great Lakes** that border several Midwestern states and Canada (Hint: Remember the acronym **HOMES**!).

**2.** Why is the Midwest commonly nicknamed "America's Breadbasket"?

**3.** What is the capital of **Illinois**, the state where Abraham Lincoln practiced law before becoming president?

**4.** What is the capital of **Ohio**, which is also the state's most populous city?

**5.** Which massive Midwestern city in Illinois sits along the shore of Lake Michigan and is famous for its towering skyscrapers, deep-dish pizza, and historic rail hub?

**6.** What is the capital of **Michigan**, the state known as the Motor City capital of automobile manufacturing?

**7.** What is the capital of **Indiana**, famous for the Indy 500 motor race?

**8.** What type of flat or rolling grassland with deep, fertile soil covered much of the Midwest before it was turned into farmland?

**9.** Name the two major rivers that meet near St. Louis, Missouri, creating one of the busiest inland shipping routes in the world.

**10.** What is the capital of **Wisconsin**, a state world-famous for its dairy farms, cheese production, and rolling hills?
