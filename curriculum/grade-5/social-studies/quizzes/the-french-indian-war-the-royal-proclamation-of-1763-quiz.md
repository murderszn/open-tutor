### Questions

**1.** Which two major European imperial powers fought each other for control of the Ohio River Valley in the **French and Indian War (1754–1763)**?

**2.** Why was the war called the "French and Indian War" from the British perspective?

**3.** What young 21-year-old Virginia surveyor and militia officer was sent into the western wilderness to deliver a warning to the French and helped build Fort Necessity?

**4.** In 1754, Benjamin Franklin drew a famous political cartoon of a cut-up snake with the slogan **"JOIN, or DIE."** What was he trying to convince the thirteen colonies to do?

**5.** Which European power won the French and Indian War, and what did the Treaty of Paris (1763) do to French colonial land in North America?

**6.** What was **Pontiac's Rebellion (1763)**, and why did Native American tribes attack British frontier forts after the French surrendered?

**7.** What was the **Royal Proclamation of 1763** issued by King George III?

**8.** Where was the imaginary boundary line drawn by the Proclamation of 1763, and what did it forbid American colonists from doing?

**9.** Why were American colonists and frontier settlers so furious about the Proclamation of 1763?

**10.** How did the massive national debt Great Britain accumulated while fighting the French and Indian War lead King George III and Parliament to begin taxing the American colonies?

<details>
<summary><strong>Parent Answer Key (Click to Expand)</strong></summary>

1. **Great Britain and France.**
2. **Because Great Britain and its American colonial militias were fighting against the French and their Native American allies.**
3. **George Washington.**
4. **He was urging the colonies to unite together under one common government (the Albany Plan of Union) for mutual defense.**
5. **Great Britain won;** France surrendered all of its territorial claims in mainland North America east of the Mississippi River to Britain.
6. **An armed uprising of Native American nations led by Ottawa leader Pontiac** to resist British settlers encroaching onto Indigenous lands after the war.
7. **An official royal order issued by King George III** forbidding British colonists from settling west of the Appalachian Mountains.
8. **Along the crest of the Appalachian Mountains;** colonists were forbidden from buying land, exploring, or settling west of that line, which was reserved for Native American nations.
9. **Colonists felt they had shed blood and paid taxes to win that western fertile land during the war**, and resented the King telling them they could not move or farm there.
10. **Britain's national debt had doubled during the war.** British leaders felt the American colonists should help pay for the British army that had protected them, leading to new colonial taxes.
