### Questions

**1.** Name the **four Middle Colonies** that were situated between New England and the Southern Colonies.

**2.** Why did the Middle Colonies earn the famous nickname **"The Breadbasket Colonies"**?

**3.** What European nation originally founded the colony of New Netherland and the trading town of New Amsterdam before the British took control and renamed it **New York** in 1664?

**4.** Who founded the colony of **Pennsylvania** in 1681 as a haven of religious freedom and tolerance for Quakers and people of all faiths?

**5.** What did the Quakers (The Religious Society of Friends) believe about equality, violence, and war that made them unique among European colonists?

**6.** What does the name of Pennsylvania's largest colonial city, **Philadelphia**, mean in Greek?

**7.** What famous polymath, printer, inventor, and founding father ran a successful printing shop in Philadelphia, published *Poor Richard's Almanack*, and experimented with electricity?

**8.** Name two major port cities in the Middle Colonies that grew into the busiest international trade centers in North America.

**9.** How did the diversity of people living in the Middle Colonies (Dutch, German, English, Scots-Irish, Swedish, and Jewish immigrants) compare to the largely English population of New England?

**10.** What important iron products and crafts were produced by skilled blacksmiths and craftsmen throughout the Middle Colonies?

<summary><strong>Parent Answer Key (Click to Expand)</strong></summary>

1. **New York, Pennsylvania, New Jersey, and Delaware.**
2. **Because their fertile soil and favorable climate allowed farmers to grow abundant grain crops, particularly wheat, rye, and barley, which were ground into flour and exported.**
3. **The Netherlands (the Dutch).**
4. **William Penn.**
5. **They believed that every person possessed an "Inner Light" from God**, making everyone equal regardless of race, gender, or status; they were strict pacifists who opposed all war and violence and treated Native Americans with fairness.
6. **"The City of Brotherly Love."**
7. **Benjamin Franklin.**
8. **Philadelphia and New York City.**
9. **The Middle Colonies were far more ethnically, culturally, and religiously diverse**, welcoming people of many different nationalities and languages.
10. **Iron kettles, horseshoes, nails, plows, wagon axles, and gun parts** forged in colonial iron furnaces.
