### Questions

**1.** Who were the colonial **"Minutemen,"** and why were they given that special name?

**2.** On the night of April 18, 1775, what signal was displayed in the steeple of Boston's Old North Church, and what did it signal to riders across the river? *(Hint: "One if by land...")*

**3.** Name two heroic express riders who galloped through the night shouting warnings that British Regulars were marching toward Concord.

**4.** Where were the first military shots of the American Revolutionary War fired on the misty morning of **April 19, 1775**?

**5.** Why is that opening gunshot known poetically around the world as **"the shot heard 'round the world"**?

**6.** What were the British troops searching for in the town of Concord, and what happened as they retreated along the 16-mile Battle Road back to Boston?

**7.** Fought in June 1775 overlooking Boston, what was the first major pitched battle of the American Revolution, commonly named after a nearby hill?

**8.** What famous command did colonial officer William Prescott give to his low-on-ammunition soldiers at Bunker Hill: *"Don't fire until you see the..."*?

**9.** Who was **Peter Salem**, and what heroic deed did this African American soldier perform during the Battle of Bunker Hill?

**10.** Who was chosen by the Second Continental Congress in June 1775 to serve as Commander-in-Chief of the newly created Continental Army?

<details>
<summary><strong>Parent Answer Key (Click to Expand)</strong></summary>

1. **Elite colonial militia volunteers** who trained to be armed and ready to fight at a single minute's notice.
2. **Two lanterns hung in the steeple;** meaning the British troops were crossing the Charles River by sea/boat ("one if by land, two if by sea").
3. **Paul Revere and William Dawes** (joined later by Dr. Samuel Prescott).
4. **Lexington Green, Massachusetts.**
5. **Because the idea of ordinary colonial citizens standing up against the mightiest empire in the world to demand liberty inspired democratic revolutions across the globe.**
6. **They were searching for hidden colonial military gunpowder and cannon stores;** on their retreat, thousands of colonial militia fired upon them from behind stone walls, trees, and houses, inflicting heavy casualties.
7. **The Battle of Bunker Hill (fought primarily on Breed's Hill).**
8. **"whites of their eyes!"** (saving precious gunpowder until enemy soldiers were within close, effective range).
9. **He stood his ground bravely and fired the fatal shot that took down British Marine Major John Pitcairn**, slowing the British advance and allowing his fellow patriots to safely retreat.
10. **George Washington (of Virginia).**
