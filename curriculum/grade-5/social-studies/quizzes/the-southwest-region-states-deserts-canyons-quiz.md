### Questions

**1.** Name the **4 states** that make up the Southwest region.

**2.** What is the capital of **Texas**, the largest state in the continental United States?

**3.** What is the capital of **Arizona**, located in the Valley of the Sun?

**4.** Which mile-deep, breathtaking natural wonder carved by the Colorado River is located in northern Arizona?

**5.** What is the capital of **New Mexico**, which is the oldest state capital city in the United States (founded in 1610)?

**6.** What is the capital of **Oklahoma**, famous for its rich Native American heritage and oil production?

**7.** What major river forms the international southern border between Texas and Mexico?

**8.** What type of dry, arid climate covers much of the Southwest, where plants like saguaro cactus and yucca thrive?

**9.** What unique geographical point in the Southwest allows a person to stand in four states (Arizona, New Mexico, Utah, and Colorado) at the exact same time?

**10.** How did ancient Indigenous peoples like the Ancestral Puebloans adapt to living in the hot, dry Southwest desert?
