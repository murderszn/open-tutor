### Questions

**1.** Where was the final major land battle of the American Revolutionary War fought in October **1781** on the Virginia coast?

**2.** Which British General was trapped on the Yorktown peninsula and forced to surrender his entire army of 8,000 soldiers to General Washington?

**3.** How did the French fleet commanded by Admiral de Grasse play a decisive role in the victory at Yorktown by winning the Battle of the Chesapeake?

**4.** Who was **James Armistead Lafayette**, and what extraordinary, dangerous role did he play as an undercover double agent for General Washington and the Marquis de Lafayette?

**5.** Who was **Wentworth Cheswell**, a courageous African American patriot and town leader from New Hampshire who made an all-night ride similar to Paul Revere’s to warn that British warships were approaching Portsmouth?

**6.** Approximately how many African American soldiers and sailors fought for the cause of American independence in the Continental Army and state navies?

**7.** What tune did the British military band famously play during the formal surrender ceremony at Yorktown as British redcoats stacked their weapons?

**8.** What official international peace treaty, signed in Paris in **1783**, formally recognized the United States as an independent, sovereign nation?

**9.** According to the Treaty of Paris (1783), what were the new western and southern boundaries of the independent United States?

**10.** Looking back over this semester’s geography and history journey, what makes the 50 states of America a truly unique and diverse nation?

<summary><strong>Parent Answer Key (Click to Expand)</strong></summary>

1. **Yorktown, Virginia.**
2. **General Charles Cornwallis.**
3. **The French naval fleet blockaded Chesapeake Bay**, cutting off British escape by sea and preventing British rescue ships from delivering food or reinforcements.
4. **He posed as a runaway slave working as a laborer and spy for the British**, gaining the trust of Generals Benedict Arnold and Cornwallis, and smuggled critical British troop movements back to the Marquis de Lafayette.
5. **A respected Black teacher, historian, archaeologist, and elected town leader** in New Hampshire who rode hard through the night as a patriotic messenger to summon the militia.
6. **Approximately 5,000 to 8,000 Black men** served in the Continental forces, fighting with unmatched bravery in nearly every major battle.
7. **"The World Turned Upside Down."**
8. **The Treaty of Paris (1783).**
9. **The Mississippi River formed the western border, and Spanish Florida formed the southern border.**
10. **The blending of dramatic physical geography (from mountains and deserts to great plains and coasts), rich cultural heritages (Indigenous, African, European, and immigrant peoples), and the enduring ideals of liberty, courage, and self-government.**
