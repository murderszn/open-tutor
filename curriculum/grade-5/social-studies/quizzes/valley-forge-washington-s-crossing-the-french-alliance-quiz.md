### Questions

**1.** On Christmas night in 1776, what icy river did General George Washington and his soldiers secretly cross in rowboats to stage a surprise dawn attack on Hessian troops in Trenton, New Jersey?

**2.** Why was the victory at Trenton so vital for the morale of the Continental Army and the survival of the American Revolution?

**3.** What two-part battle fought in upstate New York in October 1777 is considered the decisive **military turning point** of the Revolutionary War?

**4.** Why was the American victory at Saratoga so important in convincing the European kingdom of **France** to sign an official military alliance with the United States?

**5.** Where did General Washington and his 12,000 soldiers spend the grueling, bitter winter of 1777–1778, suffering from frostbite, lack of boots, hunger, and smallpox?

**6.** What Prussian military officer arrived at Valley Forge and transformed Washington’s ragtag army into a disciplined, precision-drilling fighting force using his manual of arms?

**7.** Who was the wealthy, 19-year-old French nobleman who sailed to America, volunteered to serve without pay, became like a son to Washington, and helped secure French troops and warships?

**8.** What naval captain commanded the *Bonhomme Richard* and famously shouted to a British warship: *"I have not yet begun to fight!"*?

**9.** How did colonial women (like Deborah Sampson, who disguised herself as a man to fight, and "Molly Pitcher," who brought water and loaded cannons) support the revolutionary war effort?

**10.** What was the biggest hardship Continental soldiers overcame at Valley Forge that proved their unwavering loyalty and determination?

<details>
<summary><strong>Parent Answer Key (Click to Expand)</strong></summary>

1. **The Delaware River.**
2. **The army had suffered repeated defeats, enlistments were expiring, and morale was rock bottom;** the surprise victory restored hope, re-energized the nation, and inspired soldiers to re-enlist.
3. **The Battle of Saratoga.**
4. **It proved to French King Louis XVI and his ministers that the American colonists were capable of defeating a major British field army**, convincing France to openly enter the war as an ally.
5. **Valley Forge, Pennsylvania.**
6. **Baron Friedrich von Steuben.**
7. **The Marquis de Lafayette.**
8. **John Paul Jones.**
9. **They served as nurses, couriers, and cooks, managed family farms and businesses while men were away, gathered supplies, and in some brave cases fought directly on the frontlines.**
10. **Surviving freezing winter temperatures in crude log huts without adequate food, warm coats, blankets, or shoes, and enduring rampant disease (over 2,000 soldiers died) without giving up the cause.**
