### Questions

**1.** What happened to Sir Walter Raleigh's English colony on **Roanoke Island** in the 1580s, earning it the nickname "The Lost Colony"?

**2.** What single cryptic word was found carved into a wooden post when English supply ships finally returned to Roanoke in 1590?

**3.** In **1607**, what became the very first permanent English settlement in North America, named in honor of England’s King James I?

**4.** Why was the swampy peninsula chosen for Jamestown a dangerous location for the first English settlers?

**5.** Who took leadership of Jamestown in 1608 and instituted the strict, commonsense rule: *"He that will not work shall not eat"*?

**6.** Which powerful Indigenous leader ruled over dozens of Algonquian-speaking villages surrounding Jamestown, and what was his daughter’s name?

**7.** What was the terrible winter of 1609–1610 in Jamestown called, when food ran out and the majority of settlers died of starvation and disease?

**8.** Which English settler introduced sweet West Indian tobacco seeds to Virginia, creating the lucrative cash crop that saved Jamestown from economic ruin?

**9.** What historic representative assembly met for the first time in a Jamestown church in **July 1619**, marking the birth of representative government in America?

**10.** What significant and fateful event occurred in Virginia in **August 1619**, when an English privateer ship called the *White Lion* arrived at Point Comfort carrying "20 and odd" captive human beings?

2. **"CROATOAN"** (or "CRO").
3. **Jamestown (Virginia).**

5. **Captain John Smith.**
6. **Chief Powhatan (Wahunsenacawh); Pocahontas (Matoaka).**
7. **"The Starving Time."**
8. **John Rolfe.**
9. **The House of Burgesses.**
10. **The arrival of the first recorded enslaved Africans in English North America**, marking the tragic beginning of chattel slavery in the British American colonies.
