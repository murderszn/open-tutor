## 🎙️ The Assignment Scenario

The year is **1775–1776**. The thirteen colonies are on the brink of an all-out war with Great Britain! King George III has sent redcoat soldiers, levied heavy taxes without colonial consent, and closed Boston Harbor.

You are an intrepid colonial reporter writing for the *Liberty Gazette* (or reporting live on the scene as a Revolutionary news anchor!). Your assignment is to cover three major historic events that changed the course of world history.

---

## 📜 Your Three Investigative News Reports

Write a gripping 1-to-2 paragraph news report for each of these three historic moments:

---

### Dispatch 1: The Boston Tea Party (December 16, 1773)
- **Location:** Boston Harbor, Massachusetts
- **The Event:** Over 100 colonists (Sons of Liberty) dressed in Mohawk disguise sneak aboard three British cargo ships (the *Dartmouth*, *Eleanor*, and *Beaver*) and dump 342 chests of tea into the icy saltwater to protest the Tea Act.
- **Your Story Must Include:**
  - What the harbor smelled and looked like that night.
  - Why the colonists were shouting *"No taxation without representation!"*
  - The furious reaction of the British Royal Navy.

---

### Dispatch 2: The Midnight Ride & Battle of Lexington (April 18–19, 1775)
- **Location:** Lexington Green & Concord, Massachusetts
- **The Event:** Paul Revere and William Dawes ride through the dark countryside shouting warnings that the British Regulars are marching to seize colonial gunpowder. On Lexington Green, colonial Minutemen stand face-to-face with British soldiers, and "the shot heard 'round the world" rings out!
- **Your Story Must Include:**
  - The signal lanterns in Boston's Old North Church (*"One if by land, two if by sea"*).
  - Who the Minutemen were and why they were called that.
  - What happened when the first mysterious shot was fired on the green.

---

### Dispatch 3: Spotlight on Revolutionary Heroes (Black Patriots & Spies)
Choose **ONE** of these legendary figures of the Revolution to interview or profile:
- **Crispus Attucks**: A brave sailor of African and Wampanoag descent who stood up for liberty in Boston and became the very first casualty of the American Revolution at the Boston Massacre (March 5, 1770).
- **James Armistead Lafayette**: An enslaved African Virginian who served as an undercover double agent for General George Washington and the Marquis de Lafayette, providing the vital intelligence that trapped the British army at Yorktown!
- **Peter Salem**: A freed African American soldier who fought with unmatched courage at Bunker Hill and Saratoga.

**Your Profile Must Include:**
- Who this hero was.
- What dangerous or heroic act they performed for the cause of American freedom.
- Why we must remember their name today.
