## 🎯 The Mission

Welcome to the ultimate American Geography Quest! By the end of this semester, you will know all **50 United States**, their **capitals**, their **postal abbreviations**, and where they sit on the map.

Knowing the states makes you a master traveler, a better reader, and an expert when looking at news, history, and sports!

## 🌟 Step 2: "State Spotlight" Video Script (Learner’s YouTube Producer Mode)

Pick **ONE** state that you think is fascinating and write a punchy **60-second video script** (like a YouTube Short or mini-documentary) teaching your viewers why this state is incredible!

### Your Video Script Outline:
1. **The Hook (0–5 seconds):** Catch your audience’s attention with a mind-blowing fact or riddle!  
   *(Example: "Did you know there's a state where you can stand in four states at the exact same second?")*
2. **The Fast Facts (5–20 seconds):**
   - State Name:
   - State Capital:
   - State Nickname:
   - Two Major Cities:
3. **The Big Feature (20–45 seconds):** Describe one famous physical landmark, historical event, or awesome place (like a volcano, space center, giant canyon, or national park).
4. **Why You Should Visit (45–60 seconds):** Tell your viewers the #1 thing you would do if you landed there tomorrow!
