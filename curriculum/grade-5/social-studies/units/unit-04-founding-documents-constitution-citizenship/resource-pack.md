# U04 Resource Pack — Founding Documents, Constitution, and Citizenship

**Goal:** Give the guiding adult verified, age-fit starting points for
the Declaration's ideas, the Constitution's structure, the Bill of
Rights, and citizenship practice — every link below was checked
2026-10-31 and is free with no account required. **Preview everything
before assigning.**

**Age fit:** Grade 5 (ages ~10–11). Videos supplement the lessons; they
do not replace them. Sources about slavery and exclusion are for adult
preview and selection — the unit's plain-language framing leads; videos
and pages follow.

## Primary sources (public domain; adult must pre-select and frame)

- **Declaration of Independence — full text** via the National Archives
  transcript (Reference 1), checked 2026-10-31. Archaic language;
  use short excerpts only, with the adult reading aloud and explaining
  "unalienable rights" and "consent of the governed."
- **Constitution and Bill of Rights — full texts** via the National
  Archives transcripts (References 2–3), checked 2026-10-31. Use the
  Preamble and short amendment excerpts only; the full texts are adult
  reference.
- **Madison's convention notes** — not linked directly; reach them
  through the Library of Congress or National Archives (adult-selected)
  for Lesson 05's "what did Madison argue?" question. The generated
  illustration is never a substitute for these notes.
