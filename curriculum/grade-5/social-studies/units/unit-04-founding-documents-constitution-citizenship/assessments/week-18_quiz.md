# Week-18 Formative Quiz — Unit 04

**Name (private — do not publish):** ______________ **Date:** ______________

Answer each question. No answer key is included in this file; your
guiding adult has the solutions. (10 points.)

**Q1.** What does "unalienable right" mean?
(a) A right a king gives you as a favor.
(b) A right you have just because you are a person, which cannot
rightly be taken away.
(c) A rule a class votes on.
(d) A prize for good behavior.

**Q2.** Which phrase from the Declaration means that the people being
governed must agree to their government?
(a) "Life, Liberty and the pursuit of Happiness"
(b) "The consent of the governed"
(c) "We the People"
(d) "A more perfect Union"

**Q3.** Which branch of government makes the laws?
(a) Executive
(b) Judicial
(c) Legislative
(d) The Cabinet

**Q4.** The President vetoes a bill. What can Congress do to make it
law anyway?
(a) Nothing — a veto is final.
(b) Pass it again with a two-thirds vote in both houses.
(c) Ask the Supreme Court to sign it.
(d) Wait one year and try again.

**Q5.** Which amendment protects freedom of speech, religion, press,
assembly, and petition?
(a) 2nd
(b) 1st
(c) 4th
(d) 10th

**Q6.** Before police search a home, the Fourth Amendment normally
requires what?
(a) The mayor's permission.
(b) A warrant based on probable cause.
(c) A vote of Congress.
(d) Nothing, if the police are in a hurry.

**Q7.** Put these bill-to-law steps in order (write 1–4):
___ A member of Congress introduces the bill.
___ The President signs it or vetoes it.
___ A committee studies it and votes.
___ The House and Senate both pass the same version.

**Q8.** How is the Constitution amended? Name both steps, with the
fraction needed at each step.

**Q9.** Which amendment gave women the right to vote, and in what year
was it ratified?

**Q10.** A class votes 15–2 to ban a game that two students love. The
majority says, "We voted, so it's fair." Using majority rule with
minority rights, is the vote alone enough to make it fair? Explain in
one sentence.
