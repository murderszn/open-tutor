## Part 1 — Branches and checks (10 points, 2 each)

Match each job or check to the branch that does it: **L** = legislative,
**E** = executive, **J** = judicial.

2. ___ Carries out the laws.

   Constitution.

## Part 3 — Sequencing (8 points)

    ___ The House and Senate both pass the same version.
    ___ The President signs it or vetoes it.
    ___ A member of Congress introduces the bill.
    ___ A committee studies it and votes.

## Part 4 — Claim with evidence (12 points)

12. Use your evidence chart from Lesson 01. Write a claim answering:
    **Did the Revolution fulfill the Declaration's promises?** You may
    agree, disagree, or partly agree — any position earns full marks if
    it is supported. Support your claim with **two pieces of evidence

    with reason: 5 points.)
