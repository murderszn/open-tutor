- **Factual review (2026-10-31, inspected before linking):** The image

  that distinction.

  enslaved people, children, and Indigenous nations — whose rights
  were at stake — do not appear. Lesson 05 turns that absence into a
  teaching point (task E3) rather than hiding it. The summer heat and
  the Convention's secrecy (windows shut) are not visible; the lesson
  supplies that context in words. Do not use this image as evidence for
  any historical claim.
