# Week-23 Formative Quiz — Unit 05

**Grade 5 Social Studies · Week 23, S3 · 10 questions · ~15 minutes**

**Instructions for the learner:** Answer on your own paper. Write in full
sentences where the question asks for an explanation. No answer key is in
this file — the adult marks it from the [answer key](../answer-key.md).

1. The Louisiana Purchase happened in which year, for how much money, and
   for about how many square miles?
2. How many states were in the United States in 1800, and how many by
   1850?
3. What did the Indian Removal Act of 1830 allow the president to do?
4. What did the Supreme Court decide in Worcester v. Georgia (1832)?
5. In the Cherokee removal of 1838–1839, about how many Cherokee people
   were forced west, and about how many died on the journey?
6. The Erie Canal opened in which year, and which two cities did it
   connect?
7. What is a steamboat, and why did it change river trade? Give one
   reason.
8. Account A (Lesson 03) says the removal was a "fair bargain." Account B
   says soldiers ordered families out before sunrise. For the question
   "Did the Cherokee Nation agree to removal?", which account is more
   useful, and why? (Two sentences.)
9. Explain interdependence in your own words, using the cotton shirt as
   your example. (Two sentences.)
10. Build a three-box chain: cotton gin (1793) → ______ → cotton farming
    spreads west. Fill the middle box and give one reason it belongs
    there.
