# Unit 05 Assessment — Early Republic: Expansion, Displacement, and Resistance

**Grade 5 Social Studies · Week 23, S4 · 50 points · ~40 minutes**

**Instructions for the learner:** Answer on your own paper. Read each task
fully before starting. No answer key is in this file — the adult marks it
from the [answer key](../answer-key.md).

## Task A — The map that doubled (10 points)

On your outline map of the United States, label the Mississippi River and
the Rocky Mountains. Then write the following ten states in the order they
were admitted, with their statehood years: Ohio, Louisiana, Indiana,
Mississippi, Illinois, Alabama, Maine, Missouri, Arkansas, Michigan.
(1 point per correct state with the correct year, in the correct order.)

## Task B — Timeline (8 points)

Put these eight events in order, earliest to latest, and write the year
next to each one (1 point each):

- Erie Canal opens
- Treaty of New Echota signed
- Louisiana Purchase
- Cherokee removal begins
- Indian Removal Act passed
- Worcester v. Georgia decided
- Lewis and Clark return
- Missouri Compromise

## Task C — Two perspectives (10 points)

Read Accounts A and B from Lesson 03 again.

1. List two facts both accounts agree on. (2 points)
2. Quote one opinion from Account A and one from Account B. (2 points)
3. Who is Account A's audience, and what is Account B's purpose? Give one
   clue from the text for each answer. (4 points)
4. For the question "How did Cherokee families experience the removal?",
   which account is more useful, and why? (2 points)

## Task D — Trade and interdependence (10 points)

1. The Erie Canal connected Albany and Buffalo. Name two ways it changed
   life in New York State — one about travel or shipping, one about towns
   or cities. (4 points)
2. A cotton shirt in 1840 might contain cotton grown in Mississippi, spun
   in Massachusetts, and sold in Ohio. (a) Name one kind of transport
   that could have carried the cotton from Mississippi to Massachusetts.
   (b) Explain in two sentences what this shirt shows about
   interdependence. (3 points)
3. Name one group that benefited when trade expanded in the early republic
   and one group that was harmed, each with a reason. (3 points)

## Task E — A claim from evidence (12 points)

Write a claim about an expansion-era development — for example, about the
Louisiana Purchase, the Erie Canal, or the Cherokee removal — and support
it with **two pieces of evidence from different sources**, with a reason
linking each piece to the claim. Then write one sentence naming something
your claim does **not** prove, or one question you still have.

- Claim (3 points): arguable, clear, and about an expansion-era
  development.
- Evidence 1 (3 points): accurate fact from a named source, with a reason
  linking it to the claim.
- Evidence 2 (3 points): accurate fact from a *different* source than
  Evidence 1, with a reason linking it to the claim.
- Honest limit (3 points): a real limit or open question, stated plainly.

**Total: 50 points.** The adult records the score and the readiness
decision in the [teacher guide](../teacher-guide.md).
