# U05 Resource Pack — Early Republic: Expansion, Displacement, and Resistance

**Goal:** Give the guiding adult verified, age-fit starting points for
mapping the early republic, treaties and removal, source comparison, trade
routes, and cause-and-effect claims — every link below was checked
2026-11-07 and is free with no account required. **Preview everything
before assigning.**

**Age fit:** Grade 5 (ages ~10–11). Videos supplement the lessons; they do
not replace them. Sources about forced removal and enslavement are for
adult preview and selection — the unit's plain-language framing leads;
videos and pages follow.

## Primary sources (public domain; adult must pre-select and frame)

- **Louisiana Purchase Treaty (1803)** via the National Archives
  (Reference 2 above), checked 2026-11-07. Archaic legal language; use
  short excerpts only, with the adult reading aloud and explaining
  "cession" and the $15 million price.
- **Worcester v. Georgia opinion (1832)** — Chief Justice Marshall's
  "distinct community" passage, via Digital History (University of
  Houston): https://www.digitalhistory.uh.edu/disp_textbook.cfm?smtid=3&psid=691
  — checked 2026-11-07. Adult selects the short quoted passage only and
  explains "sovereignty" in plain language.

## Internal references

- `resources/us_states.csv` — **dataset for Lesson 01 and the project**:
  columns include `name_common`, `capital`, `statehood_year`,
  `area_sq_mi`; statehood years verified 2026-11-07 (fifteen states
