# U02 Resource Pack — Colonial Societies: Labor, Trade, and Diverse Perspectives

**Goal:** Give the guiding adult verified, age-fit starting points for
colonial regions, economic life, labor systems, and perspectives work —
every link below was checked 2026-10-18 and is free with no account
required. **Preview everything before assigning.**

**Age fit:** Grade 5 (ages ~10–11). Videos supplement the lessons; they do
not replace them. Sources about enslaved labor are for adult preview and
selection — the unit's plain-language framing in Lesson 03 leads; videos
and pages follow.

## Primary source (public domain; adult must pre-select and frame)

- **Mayflower Compact — full text** (from William Bradford's *Of Plimoth
  Plantation*), via Plimoth Patuxet Museums:
  https://plimoth.org/for-students/homework-help/mayflower-and-mayflower-compact
  — checked 2026-10-18. Archaic language; the page notes only adult male
  passengers signed. Use with Lesson 01/04 to discuss *whose voices are
  absent* (women, servants, the Wampanoag people on whose land Plymouth
  was built).
