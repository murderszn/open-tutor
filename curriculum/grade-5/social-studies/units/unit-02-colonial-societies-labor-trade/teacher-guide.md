## Sensitive-content guidance (Lessons 03, 05; project Part 2)

- **Plain language, no graphic detail.** The unit names forced labor,
  the Middle Passage, and hereditary enslavement factually. Do not add
  descriptions of violence, and do not ask the learner to imagine being
  enslaved ("how would you feel as…?").
- **Center resistance and knowledge.** Lessons name West African rice
  expertise, spirituals, Anansi tales, family bonds, work slowdowns, and
  running away. Return to these whenever the narrative tilts toward
  passivity.
- **Correct the "only in the South" error.** Enslaved labor existed in
  all thirteen colonies; it was largest in the plantation South.
- **Correct the "basically slaves" error.** Acknowledge indentured
  hardship, then insist on the legal difference: temporary contract vs.
  lifelong, hereditary force.
- **Pause rule.** If the learner is upset, pause, talk, and resume later.
  The content is important; the pace is flexible.
- **Two-voice task boundary.** The writer analyzes perspectives (the
  four-questions paragraph); they do not inhabit a suffering person's
  interior experience.

## Misconception notes and reteaching

| Misconception | Where it appears | Reteach move |
|---|---|---|
| "The colonies" = one place, one way of life | L01, assessment A2 | Make the learner name the region before answering any colonial-life question; re-do A3 chains |
| Colony = state | L01 | "Ruled by Britain" vs. "independent state" — correct gently, every time |
| Trade benefits everyone equally | L02, L05 | Return to the tobacco chain: ask who is missing from the profit and why |
| Money has value in itself | L02 | "Why does the coin work?" — because everyone agrees to accept it |
| Indentured ≈ enslaved | L03, assessment B2 | Re-do the Worked example 1 table; insist on *who chose? / could they leave?* |
| Enslaved people accepted the system | L03 | Re-read the resistance list; ask "what does this show?" |
| One account is true, the other lying | L04 | Both can be sincere and partial — the job is comparison, not choosing a winner |
| Fairness = equal weight for all views | L04, D9 | Fairness = hear every perspective, then weigh with evidence; an enslaver's view explains the system without justifying it |
| The triangle was a law or plan | L05 | A profitable pattern from many separate decisions — not a rule |
| An illustration is evidence | L05 | Reconstruction *suggests*; proof comes from primary sources |

## Reteaching triggers (from weekly checks)

- **Week 7 check < 3/4 secure:** re-teach with map in hand; learner
  points and names each region and one fact before any writing.
- **Week 8 check weak on B9(2):** re-model specialization with a
  two-person trade (shoes ↔ wheat) before returning to regions.
- **Week 9 check weak on C9(2):** rebuild the four-questions labor table
  on paper together; learner fills the second row alone.
- **Quiz < 14/24:** revisit the missed lessons one at a time (see quiz
  scoring guide) before the unit assessment.
- **Assessment < 24/47:** do not proceed to Unit 03's new content cold —
  reteach the weakest section, reassess those items, then begin U03 with
  its Week 11 S1 launch.

## Differentiation

- **Reading access:** pre-read lesson explanations aloud; learner follows
  on the map, diagram, or image.
- **Alternate response modes:** drawn/labeled panels with dictated
  captions (then/now, voyage map, trade chain); physical sorting cards
  for labor kinds and region sorts; dialogue format for the two-voice
  task.
- **Extensions:** A10, B10, D9 (challenge items); L01 port-city paragraph;
  L02 mercantilism-vs-tariff comparison; L03 1619 source summary (adult
  pre-selects source); L04 real primary source with four questions
  (adult pre-selects).

## Readiness for Unit 03

Secure before the Revolution unit: region sorting with environment
reasons; specialization → trade reasoning; the plain servitude/enslavement
distinction; the four historian's questions applied to at least one pair
of accounts. If perspectives are shaky, Unit 03 will be hard — its entire
design is competing viewpoints. Reteach L04 with a fresh event if needed.
