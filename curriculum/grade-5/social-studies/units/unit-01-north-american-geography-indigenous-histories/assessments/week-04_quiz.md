# U01 Formative Quiz

**Unit:** [U01 — North American Geography, Indigenous Histories, and Sources](../README.md)
**Administered:** Week 6 S3, as the formative checkpoint before the unit
assessment (file named per the track's `week-##_quiz.md` convention).
**No answer key is included in this file** — solutions are in the [answer
key](../answer-key.md).

Use your North America map and `resources/us_states.csv` where noted. For
every claim, name the evidence.

**Map reading**

1. Your map's key shows a star for "national capital." What city does the
   star mark in Canada, and how do you know the star means "capital" rather
   than "big city"?
2. Using the scale bar, estimate the distance between any two state
   capitals on your map. Show the multiplication (segments × miles per
   segment).
3. A friend says, "The Rocky Mountains are in the eastern United States."
   Agree or disagree with map evidence, citing a symbol and the compass
   rose.

**Regions**

4. Sort these states into our 4-region model using the CSV `subregion`
   column: Vermont, Kansas, Georgia, Nevada.
5. Explain why the Southwest is dry while the Southeast is rainy. Use
   landforms and distance from water in your answer.
6. The Midwest is nicknamed "America's Breadbasket." Name one landform,
   one climate fact, and one water feature that explain the nickname.

**Culture areas**

7. Name two culture areas and, for each, explain how the environment shaped
   one part of daily life (food, shelter, or travel).
8. What are the Three Sisters? Name the three crops and explain how they
   help each other grow.

**Source kinds**

9. Name the four source kinds studied in Lesson 05. For one of them, write
   one thing it CAN tell us and one thing it CANNOT.
10. Label each as fact or opinion and explain your label: (a) "Totem poles
    are carved from cedar." (b) "Totem poles are the most beautiful art in
    the world."

**Scoring (adult):** 2 points per item (1 for the answer, 1 for the cited
evidence or explanation) = 20 points. 16+ = on track for the unit
assessment; 12–15 = review the missed topic's lesson before Week 6 S4;
below 12 = re-teach with the [teacher guide](../teacher-guide.md) before
the assessment.
