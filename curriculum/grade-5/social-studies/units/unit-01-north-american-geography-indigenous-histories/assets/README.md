## Asset records

### `pacific-northwest-coast-environment.png`

- **Used in:** [Lesson 03 — Culture Areas](../lessons/lesson-03-culture-areas.md)
  (image observation activity, Week 5 S2).
- **Generation prompt (verbatim):** "AI illustration of a Pacific Northwest
  coastal environment: tall evergreen forest, rugged rocky coastline,
  forested islands, snow-capped mountains, clear stream — no people, no
  buildings, no text."
- **Tool:** media.generate_image (built-in image pipeline).
- **Date generated:** 2026-10-11.
- **Learning purpose:** Culture-area environment observation for the Lesson
  03 activity — learners list 3 observable environmental features and
  explain for each how Northwest Coast nations might have used it for food,
  shelter, or travel.
- **Factual review:** Environment-only scene; no borders, labels,
  structures, or historical claims. Inspected 2026-10-11 — accurate to
  Pacific Northwest coastal features (evergreen forest, rocky coastline,
  forested islands, distant snow-capped mountains, clear stream); no text,
  no people, no buildings present.
- **Caption (as used in the lesson):** "AI illustration of a Pacific
  Northwest coastal environment — evergreen forest, rocky coastline,
  forested islands, distant snow-capped mountains, and a clear stream.
  Generated 2026-10-11 for this lesson's observation activity."
- **Alt text:** "AI illustration of a Pacific Northwest coastal environment:
  tall evergreen trees crowd a forested slope above a rugged rocky
  coastline; forested islands dot a gray-blue sea; snow-capped mountains
  rise in the distance; a clear stream runs through the foreground. No
  people, no buildings, no text."
- **Text-only alternative:** provided in the lesson (enumerates the same
  five features); carries identical instructional content for learners who
  cannot use the image.
- **Limitations:** AI illustration, not a photograph; not a primary source;
  learners must not cite it as historical evidence. It depicts an
  environment only — it shows nothing about any specific nation's history,
  and must not be treated as evidence about people.
