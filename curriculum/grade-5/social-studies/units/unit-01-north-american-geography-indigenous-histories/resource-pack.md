# U01 Resource Pack

**Unit:** [U01 — North American Geography, Indigenous Histories, and Sources](README.md)

**Goal:** Give the guiding adult verified, age-appropriate starting points
for every core objective of this unit — map skills, regions, culture areas,
living nations, and sources. **Age fit:** 10–11 (grade 5), adult-guided;
the adult previews every video for ads, age suitability, and accuracy, and
pre-selects all nation-specific sources from Indigenous-authored, tribal,
or museum origins.

All links below were checked 2026-10-11. Videos require captions or
transcripts; the adult confirms captions are on before showing.

## Focused queries

Use these when you need more than the curated links (e.g., a nation the
project list doesn't cover). Prefer results from tribal (.org tribal),
museum (.edu / si.edu), and government (.gov) sites.

1. `site:si.edu <nation name> culture history` — Smithsonian backgrounders.
2. `<nation name> tribal nation official website government` — find the
   nation's own site first.
3. `NPS <culture area keyword> ancestral pueblo / cliff dwelling / earth lodge lesson plan` — National Park Service educator materials.
4. `onondaganation.org OR americanindian.si.edu <topic>` — tribal and NMAI
   sources on Haudenosaunee / longhouse / wampum topics.
5. `ketchikanmuseums.org totem heritage <topic>` — Northwest Coast art and
   clan-history context.
6. `C3 Framework D2.Geo indicators 3-5` — the standards reference behind
   the unit's crosswalk (adult background).

## Internal resources

- [`resources/us_states.csv`](../../../../../resources/us_states.csv) — the
  unit's dataset. Columns used: `name_common`, `usps`, `capital`,
  `subregion` (values: `United States Northeast` / `Midwest` / `South` /
  `West`), `population_approx`, `area_sq_mi`, `population_density_per_sq_mi`.
  Figures are rounded approximations; `state_fact` is treated as unverified
  until checked. The `region` column (`Americas`) is not used in tasks. Do
  NOT use `resources/un_countries.csv`'s `population` column (empty).
- [`resources/world_facts.md`](../../../../../resources/world_facts.md) —
  starting shelf for continents/oceans context (Lesson 01–02); every figure
  re-verified at authoring before learner use.
- `resources/government_basics.md` is **not** used in this unit (it supports
  U04).
