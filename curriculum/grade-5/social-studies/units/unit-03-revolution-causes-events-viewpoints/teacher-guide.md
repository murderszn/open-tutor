## Misconceptions and reteaching

- **"The Revolution started July 4, 1776."** Reteach: argument 1763–1774,
  fighting April 1775, Declaration July 1776. Use the timeline SVG.
- **"Taxes alone caused the war."** Reteach: taxes were the spark in an
  argument about representation and rule. Ask: "If Parliament had repealed
  every tax but kept the right to tax, would the argument have ended?"
- **"All colonists wanted independence."** Reteach with three viewpoints in
  one event (Patriot, Loyalist, enslaved person) from Lesson 02.
- **"Primary = true."** Reteach with Lesson 03's five questions; a
  persuasive primary source is evidence of its maker's purpose.
- **"Molly Pitcher was one verified woman."** Reteach: traditional story,
  probably a composite. Saying so is good history, not a demotion.
- **"The 5,000–8,000 Black soldiers figure is exact."** Reteach: it is an
  estimate range because records are incomplete; always present it as one.

## Sensitive-content guidance

- War: keep descriptions at the level "soldiers fired and five colonists
  were killed." No graphic detail, no re-enactment of killing.
- Enslavement: name it plainly ("people forced to work without freedom or
  pay"), focus on systems and resistance, and never present enslaved
  people's choices as a puzzle for the learner to solve for fun.
- Indigenous peoples: nations are living communities with homelands. The
  Mohawk-disguise point is taught explicitly, every time the Tea Party is
  discussed, not as an optional aside.
- Loyalists and British perspectives: teach them fairly, with reasons.
  Fairness to a side is not agreement with it, and the adult should say
  that distinction aloud.

## Readiness and next steps

- **Ready for Unit 04** if the learner can: sequence ten events, state
  both taxation claims fairly, compare two accounts with audience/purpose,
  and write a claim with two pieces of evidence.
- **Reteach first** if the learner cannot yet sequence the timeline or
  confuses fact with opinion: repeat Weeks 11 and 13 S1–S2 with the
  practice banks before starting Unit 04's Declaration work.
- **Extension ready** if the assessment claim anticipates a critic: move
  to Unit 04's "did the Revolution fulfill the Declaration's promises?"
  question, which rewards exactly that skill.
