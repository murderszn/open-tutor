## Task A — Timeline (10 points, 1 per event)

Put these ten events in chronological order (write 1–10):

___ Treaty of Paris (1783) ___ Stamp Act (1765) ___ Saratoga (1777) ___
Boston Tea Party (1773) ___ French and Indian War ends (1763) ___
Yorktown (1781) ___ Lexington and Concord (1775) ___ Boston Massacre
(1770) ___ Declaration of Independence (1776) ___ Proclamation of 1763.

## Task B — Causes and effects (10 points)

Write two probable-cause claims, each with its reason:

1. (5 points) Why did Parliament pass the Stamp Act in 1765? Give the
   probable cause and the reason linking it to the Act.
2. (5 points) Why did the First Continental Congress meet in 1774? Give
   the probable cause and the reason linking it to the Congress.

## Task C — Two claims (10 points)

1. (5 points) State the colonial claim about taxation, with its reason.
2. (5 points) State the British claim about taxation, with its reason.
   Each summary must be fair enough that a person from that side would
   recognize it.

## Task D — Two accounts, one event (10 points)

> **Label — read first:** The two accounts below are **original classroom
> models invented for this assessment. They are NOT real documents from
> 1775.**

**Account C (model — colonial militia account):** "At dawn the regulars
marched into Lexington and ordered us to disperse. We stood our ground for
our town and our rights, and when the firing began we were driven back, but
we did not run from the cause."

**Account D (model — British officer's report):** "At dawn my detachment
entered Lexington and ordered an armed body to disperse. The rebels refused
and fired first, whereupon my men returned fire and continued the march to
Concord to destroy the stores."

1. (3 points) What do the two accounts agree on?
2. (3 points) What do they disagree about? Name one judgment difference.
3. (4 points) For the question "Who fired first at Lexington?", how useful
   is each account, and what would you need to check before deciding?

## Task E — Claim with evidence (10 points)

Write a claim about the past ("The single most important reason the
Patriots won was ______"), supported by two pieces of evidence from
different lessons in this unit, with a reason linking each piece to the
claim. End with one sentence a critic might use against your claim.

**Scoring:** Task A: 1 point per correct position. Tasks B–E: full points
for a complete, accurate, reasoned answer; half points for a partly
correct or unreasoned answer; see the teacher guide for acceptable
variation.
