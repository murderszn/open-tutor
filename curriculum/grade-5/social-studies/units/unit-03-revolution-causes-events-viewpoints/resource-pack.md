# U03 Resource Pack — Revolution: Causes, Events, and Competing Viewpoints

**Goal:** Give the guiding adult verified, age-fit starting points for the
road to revolution, Boston protests, source comparison, and turning points
— every link below was checked 2026-10-25 and is free with no account
required. **Preview everything before assigning.**

not replace them. Sources about war and enslavement are for adult preview
and selection — the unit's plain-language framing leads; videos and pages
follow.

## Primary sources (public domain; adult must pre-select and frame)

- **Declaration of Independence — full text** via the National Archives
  transcript (Reference 2 above), checked 2026-10-25. Archaic language;
  use short excerpts only, with the adult reading aloud and explaining
  "unalienable rights" and "consent of the governed."
- **Revere's Boston Massacre engraving** — described, not reproduced, in
  Lesson 02. Reach it through the Library of Congress via Reference 5's
  shelf (adult-selected); teach it as Patriot propaganda, not as a
  photograph of the event.
