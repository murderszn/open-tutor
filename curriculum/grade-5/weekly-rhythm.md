# Grade 5 — Independent Weekly Learning Plan

A flexible five-session outline, not a prescribed timetable.

| Session | Focus | Individual evidence |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | Preview a goal and read a short reference | Write one question |
| 2 | Mathematics practice | Show one worked solution |
| 3 | Reading and writing | Draft a short response |
| 4 | Hands-on science or social studies | Record an observation or source note |
| 5 | Review and revise | Explain one improvement |

Adjust workload, accommodations, and movement breaks to the individual learner. Keep completed work private.
