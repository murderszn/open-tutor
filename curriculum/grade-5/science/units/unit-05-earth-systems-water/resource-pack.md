# Resource Pack — Unit 05: Earth Systems, Spheres, and Water Distribution

**Goal:** give the adult and learner reputable, free starting points for the unit's core ideas — the four Earth spheres and their two-at-a-time interactions (5-ESS2-1) and the amounts and percentages of water in Earth's reservoirs (5-ESS2-2). **Age fit:** grade 5 (about 10–11); videos are supplements, not replacements for the hands-on investigations and graphing. **Checked:** 2026-11-06. The adult previews every video before the learner watches.
