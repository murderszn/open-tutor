# Week 22 Quiz — Earth Systems: Spheres and Water Distribution

**Unit 05 · Session 15 (Week 22) · ~15 minutes · Formative · 10 points**

Answer each question in your notebook. Write the sphere names in full — "geosphere," not "geo." Solutions are in `../answer-key.md` (teacher file — do not read it before finishing).

## Questions

**Q1.** A glacier is a river of ice that moves very slowly downhill. Which sphere does it belong to — geosphere, hydrosphere, atmosphere, or biosphere? (1 point)

**Q2.** Wind blows across a field and bends the grass. Which sphere is the wind? (1 point)

**Q3.** Ocean waves wear away a rocky cliff a little every year. Name the two spheres in this interaction. (1 point)

**Q4.** A tree takes water from the soil through its roots. Name the two spheres in this interaction. (1 point)

**Q5.** Out of 100 parts of all Earth's water, how many parts are salty ocean water? (1 point)

**Q6.** Out of the same 100 parts of all Earth's water, how many parts are fresh water? (1 point)

**Q7.** Out of 100 parts of fresh water, how many parts are frozen in glaciers and ice caps? (1 point)

**Q8.** Out of 100 parts of fresh water, how many parts are underground as ground water? (1 point)

**Q9.** Out of 100 parts of fresh water, how many parts are in lakes, rivers, and other surface water? (1 point)

**Q10.** In one or two sentences, explain why most of Earth's fresh water is hard for people to use. Use at least one number from Q7–Q9 in your answer. (1 point)
