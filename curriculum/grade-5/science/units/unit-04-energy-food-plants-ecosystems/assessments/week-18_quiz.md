# Week 18 Quiz — Unit 04: Energy in Food, Plants, and Ecosystems

**Formative quiz · Session 15 (Week 18) · ~30 minutes · 10 questions**

Answer in the notebook. The adult scores with `answer-key.md` and uses the thresholds below. This quiz is formative: it finds what to reteach before the unit assessment, and it does not by itself decide promotion or grades.

## Questions

**Q1.** A willow shoot weighs 5 pounds and grows to 169 pounds in five years. Its dried soil loses about 2 ounces. In one sentence, state what this proves about where the tree's growth came from.

**Q2.** A bean seedling weighs 2 grams and grows to 62 grams in six weeks. Its pot of soil loses 2 grams. Did the plant's new growth come chiefly from the soil? Answer yes or no, and give both numbers as evidence.

**Q3.** Draw the food chain: sun → grass → rabbit → fox. Label the producer and each consumer. Which way must the arrows point, and why?

**Q4.** Name all four things animals use food energy for.

**Q5.** Use the meadow picture from Lesson 05 (or its text alternative): name one producer, one herbivore, one carnivore, and one decomposer shown in it.

**Q6.** A learner draws a matter model: grass → rabbit → fox → soil, and stops. What is missing? Add the missing step in words.

**Q7.** A leaf bag weighs 40 grams at Week 0 and 27 grams at Week 4. How many grams left the bag, and did that matter vanish? Name two places it could have gone.

**Q8.** In one sentence each, state the difference between the energy story and the matter story in an ecosystem.

**Q9.** A bear eats berries and salmon. Draw both of the bear's food chains starting from the sun. What do we call the combined diagram of many chains in one ecosystem?

**Q10 (midyear retrieval — Unit 03).** A sealed bag of ice weighs 120 grams. After the ice melts, the sealed bag of water weighs 120 grams. State the claim and the evidence.

## Readiness thresholds (adult use)

- **9–10 correct:** ready for the unit assessment.
- **7–8 correct:** review the missed lesson's worked examples once, then take the unit assessment.
- **Below 7:** reteach the missed objectives with the teacher guide's reteach steps before the unit assessment; do not treat the quiz score as a final grade.
