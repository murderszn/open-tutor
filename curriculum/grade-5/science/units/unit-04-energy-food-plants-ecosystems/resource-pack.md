# Resource Pack — Unit 04: Energy in Food, Plants, and Ecosystems

**Goal:** give the adult and learner reputable, free starting points for the unit's core ideas — plants growing chiefly from air and water (5-LS1-1), energy in food as former sunlight (5-PS3-1), and matter moving among plants, animals, decomposers, and the environment (5-LS2-1). **Age fit:** grade 5 (about 10–11); videos are supplements, not replacements for the investigations. **Checked:** 2026-10-31. The adult previews every video before the learner watches.
