# Resource Pack — Unit 02: Particle Models, Properties, and Mixtures

**Goal:** give the adult and learner reputable, free starting points for the
unit's core ideas — particles too small to see (5-PS1-1), identifying materials
by properties (5-PS1-3), and mixtures. **Age fit:** grade 5 (about 10–11);
videos are supplements, not replacements for the hands-on investigations.
