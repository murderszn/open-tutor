## Safety & notes

- Every external item is kid-appropriate in tone; the adult previews all
  videos for advertising, comments, and age fit before the learner watches.
- At least one free, no-account route supports each core objective:
  measuring (Web 1 estimation game + hands-on stations), fair tests (Web 2 +
  ramp demo), graphing and models (in-unit practice; no external account
  needed).
- Videos supplement the lessons; they never replace the hands-on
  investigations.
- No learner data, names, or work are shared with any external site.
