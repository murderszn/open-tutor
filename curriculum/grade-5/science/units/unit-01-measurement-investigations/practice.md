# Unit 01 — Extra Practice

**Grade 5 Science · Unit 01 — Models, Measurement, and Controlled Investigations**
Status: draft. Use these sets in Sessions 2, 5, 6, 8, 9, 11, 12, or 15, or as
same-day follow-ups. Answers are in [answer-key.md](answer-key.md#practice).

## Set A — Conversions (Sessions 5–6)

All conversions are within the metric system. Show the multiplication or
division each time.

1. 90 mm = ____ cm
2. 4 m = ____ cm
3. 2,500 g = ____ kg
4. 3 L = ____ mL
5. 68 cm = ____ m
6. 0.75 kg = ____ g
7. 1.2 km = ____ m
8. 450 mL = ____ L
9. A ribbon is 2 m 30 cm long. How many centimeters is that?
10. A jug holds 1.5 L. How many 250-mL cups can it fill? Explain.

## Set B — Tools and units (Session 2, 15)

1. Name the tool and the unit you would use for each:
   a. the mass of a textbook
   b. the volume of water for a recipe
   c. the length of the classroom
   d. the temperature outside at noon
2. A learner weighs flour on a scale that still shows 15 g with nothing on
   it, then reads 215 g with the flour. What is the flour's real mass?
   What rule did the learner break?
3. Why is a graduated cylinder better than a wide bowl for measuring 50 mL
   of water? (Two reasons.)

## Set C — Fair tests (Sessions 8–9, 15)

*Fictional practice data where numbers appear.*

1. Plan: "Do seeds sprout faster in light or dark?" The learner uses two
   cups, different seed types, different amounts of water, one in light and
   one in dark. List everything that must be made the same for a fair test.
2. For the seed question in #1, name the variable, three controls, what is
   measured (with units), and the number of trials.
3. A test has 1 trial. Give two reasons the result can't be trusted.
4. *Fictional data.* Sponge A absorbs: 48, 51, 50 mL. Sponge B absorbs: 62,
   60, 63 mL. Compute both averages. Write one honest claim and one claim
   the data do NOT support.
5. **Error analysis.** "I changed the ramp height AND used a heavier ball,
   and the ball went farther, so height is what matters." Explain the flaw
   and how to fix the test.

## Set D — Graphs and models (Sessions 11–12)

*Fictional practice data where numbers appear.*

1. *Data:* Books read per month — Sep: 3, Oct: 5, Nov: 4, Dec: 6. Bar graph
   or line graph? Explain your choice, then list the axis labels (with
   units) you would use.
2. *Data:* A plant's height measured weekly: week 1 → 4 cm, week 2 → 7 cm,
   week 3 → 11 cm, week 4 → 12 cm. Plot the four ordered pairs. Describe
   the trend in one sentence.
3. A graph's y-axis is labeled "Distance" with no units, and the scale jumps
   0, 10, 15, 100. Name two problems.
4. First model: "The ice melted because the room is warm." New evidence: an
   identical ice cube in the same room, wrapped in a towel, melted much more
   slowly. Revise the model in two sentences.
5. Why is "my model was wrong" good news in science? (Two or three
   sentences.)

## Set E — Mixed review (Session 15)

1. Sort: testable or not? a) "How many grams of salt dissolve in 100 mL of
   warm water?" b) "Is winter the coziest season?" For (a), name the
   measurement.
2. Observation or inference? a) "The metal feels cold." b) "The metal was
   in the freezer."
3. Convert 1,750 mL to liters. Check backward.
4. Define **criteria** and **constraints**; give one example of each for
   designing a lunchbox that keeps food cold.
5. List the six parts of the investigation report in order, in your own
   words.
