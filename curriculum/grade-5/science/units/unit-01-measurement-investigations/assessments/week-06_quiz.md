# Formative Quiz — Unit 01 (Session 15)

**Grade 5 Science · Unit 01 — Models, Measurement, and Controlled Investigations**
Status: draft. Short written check (8 tasks); the adult reviews it the same
day. Oral or drawn answers accepted. **Answers are in [answer-key.md](../answer-key.md#week-06-quiz) — for the adult only.**

1. Testable or not? "How many milliliters of juice are in the carton?" If
   testable, name the measurement.
2. Observation or inference? "The playground slide is too hot to touch, so
   it must be over 100 degrees outside."
3. Convert: 240 cm = ____ m. Show your thinking.
4. Convert: 1.5 L = ____ mL. Check backward.
5. A learner asks: "Do bigger ice cubes melt slower than smaller ones?" Name
   the **variable** and **two controls**.
6. Why run three trials instead of one? Give one reason.
7. *Fictional practice data.* A learner grows two bean plants for two weeks.
   Sunny windowsill plant heights: 9, 10, 9 cm. Dark closet plant heights:
   4, 5, 4 cm. Compute both averages and write one honest claim the data
   support.
8. Define **criteria** and **constraints** in your own words, with one
   example of each.
