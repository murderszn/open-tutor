# Unit Assessment — Unit 01 (Session 16)

**Grade 5 Science · Unit 01 — Models, Measurement, and Controlled Investigations**
Status: draft. Culminating assessment matched to the unit objectives. Short
written responses (10 tasks); the adult reviews the same day. Oral, drawn,
or dictated answers accepted. This is a learning check, not a standardized
or validated test. **Answers are in [answer-key.md](../answer-key.md#unit-assessment) — for the adult only.**

**Part 1 — Questions and observations (Objectives 1–2)**

1. Which of these is a testable question? Explain your choice in one
   sentence.
   a. "Which brand of crayon has the prettiest colors?"
   b. "Which brand of crayon lasts the longest when coloring?"
2. For the testable question in #1, name what you would measure and the
   unit.
3. Read: "There are dark clouds, the air smells fresh, and the playground is
   empty." Write one **observation** and one **inference** from this
   description.

**Part 2 — Measurement (Objective 2)**

4. Match each job to the best tool: (i) balance, (ii) graduated cylinder,
   (iii) thermometer, (iv) hand lens.
   a. Find the mass of a rock. b. Measure 250 mL of water. c. Check whether
   soup is cool enough to eat. d. Examine the veins of a leaf.
5. Convert and show your thinking: 3.2 m = ____ cm; 750 g = ____ kg.

**Part 3 — Fair tests and data (Objectives 3–4)**

6. *Fictional practice data.* A learner asks whether a smooth floor or a
   carpet lets a ball roll farther. Smooth floor trials: 210, 205, 208 cm.
   Carpet trials: 95, 102, 98 cm.
   a. Name the variable and two controls.
   b. Compute both averages.
   c. Write one honest claim the data support, starting "My evidence is…".
   d. Name one claim these data do NOT support.
7. A friend ran one trial and declared a winner. Give two reasons a
   scientist would not stop there.

**Part 4 — Models and design (Objective 5)**

8. A learner's first model says: "The ball rolled farther on the smooth
   floor because smooth floors are magic." New evidence: a second smooth
   floor (tile) gives the same result, and a rough board gives a short roll
   like the carpet. Revise the model in one or two sentences.
9. Problem: "Design a package that keeps a cookie from breaking in a
   backpack." Write one testable **criterion** and one **constraint**.
10. List the six parts of an investigation report in order.
