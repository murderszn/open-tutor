# Formative Quiz — Unit 03 (Week 14)

**Grade 5 Science · ~20 minutes · 10 items · Adult-assessed; this quiz is not a standardized or validated test.**

Answer in your notebook. All tables are labeled practice data. Solutions are in `../answer-key.md` (teacher file).

**Q1.** A sealed bag of ice weighs 130 g. After the ice melts, the bag weighs 130 g. What does this show about melting? Answer in one sentence.

**Q2.** Water weighs 160 g. Sugar weighs 15 g. The sugar is stirred into the water until it dissolves. What should the mixture weigh? Show the addition.

**Q3.** A learner stirs sugar into water, cannot see the sugar anymore, and says the sugar is gone. Give a two-sentence reply using a scale reading as evidence.

**Q4.** Baking soda and vinegar are mixed. Bubbles of gas form, and the leftover liquid is not the same as the starting vinegar. Did mixing make a new substance? Name the evidence.

**Q5.** Milk and vinegar are mixed. White curds form. Did mixing make a new substance? Name the evidence.

**Q6.** Sand and water are mixed, then poured through a filter. The sand stays on the filter and the water passes through. Did mixing make a new substance? Explain with the recovery test.

**Q7.** Use this practice-data table. A sealed bag of baking soda and vinegar weighs 250 g before mixing and 250 g after mixing. An open cup of the same mixture weighs 210 g before mixing and 198 g after mixing. How many grams of gas escaped from the open cup? Show the subtraction.

**Q8.** In Q7, does the open-cup result break the conservation claim? Answer yes or no, and explain in one sentence using the word "system."

**Q9.** A learner says, "Mixing always makes a new substance." Give the counterexample from this unit and explain why it disproves her claim.

**Q10.** A fizzy drink is opened and gas escapes. Later the drink tastes flat. Did matter leave the drink when it was opened? Explain in one or two sentences.

**Readiness thresholds (teacher use):** 9–10 correct = ready for the unit assessment; 7–8 = review the missed lesson's guided practice with the adult before the assessment; 6 or fewer = reteach the missed topics (see `../teacher-guide.md`) and re-quiz with new numbers before the assessment.
