# Resource Pack — Unit 03: Matter Conservation and Chemical-Change Evidence

**Goal:** give the adult and learner reputable, free starting points for the unit's core ideas — weight conservation through heating, cooling, and mixing (5-PS1-2) and evidence for new substances when substances are mixed (5-PS1-4). **Age fit:** grade 5 (about 10–11); videos are supplements, not replacements for the hands-on investigations. **Checked:** 2026-10-24. The adult previews every video before the learner watches.
