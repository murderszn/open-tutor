# Resource Pack — Unit 05: Fraction Multiplication, Division, and Models

**Goal:** Give the adult one shelf of free, vetted supports for teaching
grade-5 fraction multiplication (whole number × fraction, fraction ×
fraction, area models, scaling), mixed-number multiplication, and
unit-fraction division. **Age fit:** grades 4–6 (about ages 9–12);
written for the adult to excerpt and explain at a 5th-grade level,
never assigned whole.

All external entries checked 2026-11-06. Videos are supplements to
teaching, not replacements. A free, no-account route supports every
core objective below.
