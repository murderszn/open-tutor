# Week-04 Formative Quiz — Unit 05

**Grade 5 Mathematics · Unit 05, Week 20 (Session S08) · 12 items ·
Objectives UO1–UO4**

This quiz revises the legacy
[Multiplying Fractions & Visual Area Models quiz](../../../quizzes/multiplying-fractions-visual-area-models-quiz.md)
(items 1–10, re-solved and re-worded here) and adds two scaling and
mixed-number items (11–12). The legacy embedded answer key has been
removed: answers live only in [answer-key.md](../answer-key.md). Show
your work for every item. The adult reviews the quiz the same day.

## Questions

**1.** Multiply a whole number by a fraction: 6 × 2/3.

**2.** Multiply: 5 × 3/4. Express as a mixed number.

**3.** Multiply two fractions: 1/2 × 3/5.

**4.** Multiply: 2/3 × 3/4. Simplify completely.

**5.** Find the area of a rectangle with length 4/5 meter and width
2/3 meter.

**6.** Multiply: 5/6 × 3/10. Simplify before or after multiplying.

**7.** Does multiplying a number by a fraction less than 1 make the
number larger or smaller? Explain.

**8.** Calculate: 3/8 × 16.

**9.** A garden plot is 3 1/2 feet long by 2 feet wide. What is the
area of the garden?

**10.** Solve: 4/7 × 7/9.

**11.** A recipe needs 2 1/2 cups of flour per batch. How much flour
do 2 batches need? Show the improper-fraction conversion.

**12.** Is (5/4) × 8 greater than 8, less than 8, or equal to 8?
Explain without multiplying first, then compute to check.
