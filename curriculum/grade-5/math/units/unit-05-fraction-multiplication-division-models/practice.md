## Game 1 — Partition relay

**Used in:** S02 (Lesson 01 follow-up). **Players:** the learner and
the adult, or two learners taking turns. **Materials:** a set of 12
cards, each showing a product like (2/3) × 9 or (5/6) × 12.

Each turn, the player draws a card and says the partition out loud
("Split 9 into 3 parts — each part is 3 — take 2 parts: 6") before
writing the answer. A correct partition-and-answer scores a point; a
correct answer with no partition spoken scores nothing (the reasoning
is the game). First to 6 points wins. **Sample cards:** (2/3) × 9 = 6;
(3/4) × 8 = 6; (5/6) × 12 = 10; (1/4) × 20 = 5; (4/5) × 15 = 12;
(2/5) × 10 = 4.

## Game 2 — Area-model match

**Used in:** S04 (Lesson 02 follow-up). **Materials:** 10 product
cards (for example, (1/2) × (3/5), (2/3) × (3/4), (5/6) × (3/10))
and 10 area-model cards (grids with the overlap shaded).

Lay the area-model cards face up and the product cards face down.
Draw a product card, compute the product, then find the area-model
card whose overlap matches it. A match scores a point; a mismatch
means the adult re-draws the model with the learner. **Sample pairs:**
(1/2) × (3/5) = 3/10 (3 of 10 cells); (2/3) × (3/4) = 1/2 (6 of 12
cells); (5/6) × (3/10) = 1/4 (15 of 60 cells, simplified).

## Game 3 — Scaling sort

**Used in:** S06 (Lesson 03 follow-up). **Materials:** 15 product
cards (for example, (2/3) × 18, (5/4) × 12, (3/3) × 7, (7/8) × 24,
(11/10) × 5) and three labeled piles: **Grows**, **Shrinks**,
**Stays**.

Sort every card into a pile **without multiplying** — decide from the
size of the fraction next to 1. Then compute each product to check
the sort. A card in the wrong pile is re-sorted with the number-line
reason spoken aloud. All 15 correct wins the round.

## Game 4 — How many servings?

**Used in:** S10 (Lesson 05 follow-up). **Materials:** a set of 12
division cards, half "sharing" ((1/3) ÷ 4, (1/2) ÷ 5) and half
"servings" (4 ÷ (1/4), 5 ÷ (1/3)).

Each turn, draw a card and first name the question type — "sharing"
or "servings" — then compute and give the multiplication check. A
correct type + answer + check scores a point. A wrong type scores
nothing even if the number is right: naming the question is the
skill. **Sample cards:** (1/3) ÷ 4 = 1/12 (sharing); 4 ÷ (1/4) = 16
(servings); (1/2) ÷ 5 = 1/10 (sharing); 5 ÷ (1/3) = 15 (servings).
