## fraction-area-model-2-thirds-x-3-fourths.svg (deterministic diagram)

- **File:** `fraction-area-model-2-thirds-x-3-fourths.svg`
- **What it is:** an exact, reproducible SVG drawn from the numbers, not a
  generated picture. One unit square (600 × 600 units) split into 3 equal
  columns (200 units each) and 4 equal rows (150 units each): 12 small
  rectangles. Two columns shaded blue (2/3 of the width); three rows shaded
  amber (3/4 of the height). The green overlap is 2 columns × 3 rows = 6 of
  the 12 small rectangles, so (2/3) × (3/4) = 6/12 = 1/2.
- **Used in:** [Lesson 02](../lessons/lesson-02-fraction-times-fraction-area-models.md),
  Worked Example 2.
- **Text-only alternative:** "A square is divided into 3 equal columns and
  4 equal rows, making 12 small equal rectangles. Shading 2 of the 3 columns
  and 3 of the 4 rows overlaps on 6 of the 12 small rectangles, so the
  product is 6/12, which simplifies to 1/2."
