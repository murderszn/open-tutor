# Unit 03 — Decimal Operations and Applications

**Grade 5 Mathematics · Weeks 11–14 · 16 sessions (~40 minutes each, 4 per week)**

**Standards:** 5.NBT.B.7 (with 5.NBT.A.1–A.3 place-value support from Unit 01
and 5.OA.A.1 multistep-expression support from Unit 02)

## Overview

Unit 01 taught learners what decimals *are*; Unit 02 taught them to multiply
and divide whole numbers. This unit puts the two together: learners add,
subtract, multiply, and divide decimals to hundredths, always starting from
place value, concrete models, or drawings — never from a memorized
"move the decimal point" rule they cannot explain. Addition and subtraction
line up by place value, not by the right edge. Multiplication counts decimal
places because tenths times tenths make hundredths. Division by a whole
number shares tenths and hundredths fairly, and a whole number divided by a
whole number can land *between* whole numbers (3 ÷ 4 = 0.75). The unit ends
in real applications — money, measurement, and a market-stall project — where
learners must choose the operation, estimate first, and check whether an
answer is reasonable.

## Essential question

*How can place value, models, and estimation tell me which decimal operation
to use — and whether my answer makes sense?*

## Prerequisites (from Units 01–02 and Grade 4)

- Decimals to hundredths (Unit 01): reading, writing, comparing, and expanding
  decimals; the 10× / 1/10 relationship between adjacent places
- Whole-number multiplication and division with remainders (Unit 02),
  including partial quotients and the standard multiplication algorithm
- Money notation to cents ($2.50 = 2 dollars and 50 cents = 250 cents)
- Rounding to the nearest whole number (Unit 01) for estimation

The Week 11 opening session re-checks these; nothing new is assumed.

Grade 5, Number and Operations in Base Ten, as published at
[thecorestandards.org/Math](https://thecorestandards.org/Math) (Grade 5 NBT

- **5.NBT.B.7** — "Add, subtract, multiply, and divide decimals to
  hundredths, using concrete models or drawings and strategies based on
  place value, properties of operations, and/or the relationship between
  addition and subtraction; relate the strategy to a written method and
  explain the reasoning used."

**Scope note (honest boundary):** this unit divides decimals *by whole
numbers* and divides whole numbers to give decimal quotients, following the
track's scope-and-sequence for Weeks 11–14. Dividing *by* a decimal (e.g.
4.8 ÷ 0.6) is an extension task only (Lesson 03), not a required objective,
because the Grade 5 framework's companion expectations for whole-number
division (5.NBT.B.6) cap divisors at two digits and this unit's models are
built for whole-number divisors.

## Differentiation and accessibility

- **Reading/language access:** every lesson states its goal in one sentence
  before any numbers appear; key words (*per*, *shared equally*, *change*,
  *total*) are pre-taught with a picture or gesture.
- **Alternate response modes:** learners may answer orally, point to a grid,
  or dictate steps to the adult; written answers are never the only evidence.
- **Supports:** hundredths grids, place-value mats, and play money are
  available in every session; no timed speed tests anywhere in this unit.
- **Extensions:** dividing by a decimal (Lesson 03), thousandths products
  (Lesson 02), and a "beat the budget" project variant (project.md) are
  optional and never gate the required objectives.
