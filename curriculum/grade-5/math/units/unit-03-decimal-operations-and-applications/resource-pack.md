# Resource Pack — Unit 03: Decimal Operations and Applications

**Goal:** Give the adult one shelf of free, vetted supports for teaching
grade-5 decimal addition, subtraction, multiplication, and division to
hundredths, with money and measurement applications. **Age fit:** grades
4–6 (about ages 9–12); written for the adult to excerpt and explain at a
5th-grade level, never assigned whole.

All external entries checked 2026-10-24. Videos are supplements to teaching,
not replacements.
