# Practice — Unit 03: Decimal Operations and Applications

Games first, then the session sets. All answers are in
[answer-key.md](answer-key.md) — never beside the questions here.

## Game 1 — Place-value lineup (Session 02)

**Players:** pairs. **You need:** digit cards 0–9, a decimal-point card,
a place-value mat.

1. The adult calls a decimal addition or subtraction, e.g. "6.4 + 2.35."
2. Each pair lays out both numbers on the mat, decimal points aligned,
   filling empty places with zero cards.
3. The first pair to state the correct sum or difference — and point to
   the column where regrouping happened, if any — scores a point.
4. Swap roles: learners call the problems, the adult lays out the cards
   (and the learners check the adult's work — mistakes are welcome).

**Rule that matters:** a pair that lines up the right edge instead of the
decimal point loses the point even if the digits "look right." Say why.

## Game 2 — Hundredths-grid race (Session 04)

**Players:** pairs or small teams. **You need:** blank 10 × 10 grids,
two colored pencils.

1. The adult calls a multiplication, e.g. "0.6 × 0.5."
2. Each team shades the grid: columns for the first factor, rows for the
   second, and counts the overlap.
3. The team that states the product *and* the overlap count (e.g. "30
   squares, so 0.30") scores a point.
4. First team to 5 points wins. No timers: accuracy beats speed, and a
   challenged answer is re-shaded together, not argued.

## Game 3 — Market change challenge (Session 06 warm-up)

**Players:** pairs. **You need:** play money, the Lesson 04 stall prices
(apples $2.50, oranges $1.75, lemons $0.60).

1. One learner is the shopkeeper, the other the customer. The customer
   orders fruit and pays with a $10 note.
2. The shopkeeper computes the total and the change aloud, step by step.
3. The customer checks: total by adding, change by subtracting, and the
   shopkeeper's change by adding it back to the total (it must reach $10).
4. Swap roles. Any error found by the customer scores a point for the
   customer; a clean round scores a point for the shopkeeper.
