# Teacher Guide — Unit 01

Session-by-session teaching notes, misconception alerts, reteach guidance, and
readiness criteria. **This guide references** [answer-key.md](answer-key.md)
**by section — keys are never duplicated here.** All keys were solved
independently and reconciled against the student files during writing.

## How to use this unit

- 16 sessions, ~40 minutes each, 4 per week. Session types rotate: concept
  lesson → guided practice → fluency game → review.
- Review every exit check and practice set the **same day**; the S08 quiz and
  S16 assessment drive reteach decisions.
- Fluency is strategy-based: **no timed speed tests** anywhere in this unit.
- The adult may scribe, and learners may answer orally or by pointing —
  every lesson lists an alternate response mode.

## Session-by-session notes

### S01 — Lesson 01: Digit-value relationships (UO1)

- **Teach the rule, then test it both directions.** Learners often memorize
  "left is 10 times" but freeze on "1/10 of the left." Drill both directions
  with the same pair (50,000 vs 5,000).
- **Misconception:** applying 10× to non-adjacent places (606 → "10 times").
  Fix: count the place-steps; each step is a ×10.
- **Ready to move on:** exit check names the 10× relationship for 0.55 with
  the two digit values stated. Key: [answer-key.md](answer-key.md#lesson-01).

### S02 — Guided practice: "Ten-times detective" (UO1)

- Let learners struggle with G4/I5 before hinting — the error-analysis items
  are the point of the session.
- **Reteach trigger:** if 2+ of the 9 tasks miss the *direction* (10× vs
  1/10), replay the mat: physically move a digit card one place and ask
  "bigger or smaller, and by how much?"
- Key: [answer-key.md](answer-key.md#lesson-01).

### S03 — Lesson 02: Decimals to thousandths (UO2)

- **The image activity:** display
  `assets/place-value-chart-illustration.png` (alt text and caption are in the
  lesson). If the image cannot be viewed, the lesson's **text-only
  alternative** carries identical information — the sample number is 347.582
  in both. Ask the three questions (numerals, words, expanded form) from
  whichever version the learner uses.
- **Watch for:** "and" inside the whole-number part; reading 3.024 as
  hundredths; treating 2.40 as bigger than 2.4.
- Key: [answer-key.md](answer-key.md#lesson-02).

### S04 — Fluency game: Place-value dictation (UO2)

- Start with 2-place decimals, then add thousandths. The bonus round
  ("one thousand two hundred and four thousandths" → 1200.004) exposes
  whole-part grouping errors — celebrate catching them.
- **Alternate mode:** the learner points to digits on the mat while the adult
  writes.
- Key (starter lists): [answer-key.md](answer-key.md#practice-bank).

### S05 — Lesson 03: Powers of ten (UO3)

- **Insist on digit-shift language** ("the 4 moved from ones to hundreds"),
  not "add two zeros." The zero-adding shortcut collapses on decimals and
  division.
- Exponents: the small number counts *factors of ten*, not a multiplier —
  expand 10³ = 10 × 10 × 10 aloud the first times.
- 10⁰: only if asked; use the divide-by-10 pattern (10³ → 10² → 10¹ → 1).
  Never required in student work.
- Key: [answer-key.md](answer-key.md#lesson-03).

### S06 — Guided practice: Powers-of-ten slider game (UO3)

- The physical slide is the learning — do not let learners skip to writing
  the answer. Require the spoken shift sentence every round.
- **Reteach trigger:** reversed shifts on ÷ cards → return to "dividing makes
  digits smaller, so they slide right."
- Key: [answer-key.md](answer-key.md#practice-bank).

### S07 — Applied: legacy powers-of-ten task (UO3)

- Source: [powers-of-ten-practice.md](../../assignments/powers-of-ten-practice.md)
  (linked, not duplicated). Expected products: 36 × 100 = **3,600**;
  7 × 1,000 = **7,000**; 4.2 × 100 = **420** — all re-verified.
- The "explain how the value of each digit changes" paragraph is the graded
  heart; a correct product with no digit story is half credit in your
  feedback.
- Learner-made missing-power problems must be solvable — check the proposed
  power is actually a power of ten.
- Key: [answer-key.md](answer-key.md#lesson-03).

### S08 — Week-04 formative quiz + reteach (UO1–UO3)

- 11 items in [assessments/week-04_quiz.md](assessments/week-04_quiz.md).
  Key: [answer-key.md](answer-key.md#week-04-quiz).
- **Same-day scoring.** Sort misses by objective:
  - UO1 misses (Q1, Q11) → replay S01–S02 with the mat; focus on direction.
  - UO2 misses (Q2, Q3, Q4, Q10) → re-dictate with the chart; drill "and"
    placement and trailing-place naming.
  - UO3 misses (Q5–Q9) → slider game with ÷ cards; re-teach exponent meaning.
- **What ready looks like (entering Week 5):** learner explains the 10×/1/10
  rule in their own words; reads/writes a dictated decimal to thousandths;
  evaluates ×/÷ by powers of 10 with a digit-shift story. Two of three is
  "proceed with support"; fewer → reteach the weakest strand in Week 5
  warm-ups before new content.

### S09 — Lesson 04: Rounding decimals (UO4)

- **Routine to install:** underline the rounding place, circle the deciding
  digit (one place right), then decide. Every problem, no exceptions.
- Normalize carrying (9.95 → 10.0) and the kept trailing zero (14.30).
- Key: [answer-key.md](answer-key.md#lesson-04). All rounding items re-solved
  during writing.

### S10 — Fluency game: Rounding relay (UO4)

- Turn-based, **not timed**. The score requires three parts: correct benchmark
  placement, correct rounded value, correct deciding digit.
- Card set of 24 is in [practice.md](practice.md#game-3-rounding-relay); full
  solutions in the key.
- Key: [answer-key.md](answer-key.md#practice-bank).

### S11 — Lesson 05: Comparing and ordering decimals (UO5)

- **Signature trap:** "longer decimal is bigger" (0.605 vs 0.65). Keep the
  mat out: a hundredths digit always outranks any thousandths digit.
- Teach equalizing with trailing zeros as a *checking* tool, not the first
  method — place-by-place from the left comes first.
- Key: [answer-key.md](answer-key.md#lesson-05).

### S12 — Sprint times + legacy review (UO4–UO5)

- Learners rank the swim times 1st–4th with place-by-place reasoning (key has
  the full ordering).
- Legacy quiz items come from
  [decimal-place-value-comparing-to-thousandths-quiz.md](../../quizzes/decimal-place-value-comparing-to-thousandths-quiz.md)
  — rework selected items **with the key kept separate** (the legacy file
  embeds its key; never show that section to the learner).
- Key: [answer-key.md](answer-key.md#practice-bank).

### S13 — Project launch: Powers-of-Ten City (all objectives)

- Approve each price list before building: every price must be a valid decimal
  to thousandths with a correct number name. Catch "and"-placement errors now.
- Deliverables and 4-level rubric: [project.md](project.md).

### S14 — Project build + review games (all objectives)

- Steer each learner to the game matching their S08 feedback (slider for UO3,
  relay for UO4, dictation for UO2).
- Scaling work: require the digit-shift story for at least one of the four
  scalings; check units stay attached to measurements.

### S15 — Mixed review circuit (UO1–UO5)

- Four stations; learners complete 3 of 4 with at most one error per station.
  Station answers: [answer-key.md](answer-key.md#practice-bank).
- Use errors to assign S16-morning warm-ups, not to delay the assessment.

### S16 — Culminating assessment + gallery walk (all objectives)

- 18 items + extended reasoning in
  [assessments/unit-assessment.md](assessments/unit-assessment.md).
  Key: [answer-key.md](answer-key.md#unit-assessment).
- Score the project with the rubric in [project.md](project.md); scoring

  [answer-key.md](answer-key.md#project-scoring-guidance).

## Reteach guidance (by objective)

| If this is weak… | Reteach move | Follow-up check |
|---|---|---|
| UO1 — 10×/1/10 rule | Mat work: move one digit card one place; learner states new value and the ×10 or ÷10 | 3 fresh digit-pair comparisons, both directions |
| UO2 — reading/writing/expanding | Dictation loop: adult says words → learner writes numeral → learner reads back → expanded form | 5 dictated decimals incl. one with interior zeros |
| UO3 — powers of ten | Slider game with ÷ and exponent cards; narrate every shift | 4 mixed ×/÷ by powers of 10 with shift stories |
| UO4 — rounding | Number-line placement ritual: underline, circle deciding digit, place, decide | 6 roundings across ones/tenths/hundredths/thousandths |
| UO5 — comparing/ordering | Place-by-place race on the mat; equalize-with-zeros as check | Order 2 fresh sets of 4 decimals with justifications |

## Feedback and next steps

- **After S08:** one strength + one next step per learner, named by objective
  ("Your digit shifts are exact — next: circle the deciding digit before
  rounding").
- **After S16:** record per-objective readiness for Unit 02 (multi-digit
  multiplication/division). Carry forward: digit-value language (used in
  partial products), powers of ten (estimation), and decimal reading (Unit 03).
- **What ready for Unit 02 looks like:** 14 of 18 assessment items correct
  including at least one item per objective; extended task shows a valid
  counterexample with a digit-shift explanation. Below that: reteach the two
  weakest objectives using the table above before Week 7.
