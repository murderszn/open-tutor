# Resource Pack — Unit 01: Place Value, Decimal Patterns, and Powers of Ten

**Goal:** Give the adult one shelf of free, vetted supports for teaching
grade-5 place value, decimals to thousandths, powers of ten, rounding, and
comparing decimals. **Age fit:** grades 4-6 (about ages 9-12); written for the
adult to excerpt and explain at a 5th-grade level, never assigned whole.

All external entries checked 2026-10-11. Videos are supplements to teaching,
not replacements.
