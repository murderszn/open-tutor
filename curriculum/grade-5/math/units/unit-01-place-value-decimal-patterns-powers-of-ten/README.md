# Unit 01 — Place Value, Decimal Patterns, and Powers of Ten

**Grade 5 Mathematics · Weeks 3–6 · 16 sessions (~40 minutes each, 4 per week)**

**Standards:** 5.NBT.A.1, 5.NBT.A.2, 5.NBT.A.3.a–b, 5.NBT.A.4

## Overview

This unit builds the place-value system the rest of the year's math rests on.
Learners first secure the core relationship — a digit in one place is worth
**10 times** the same digit one place to its right and **1/10** of the same
digit one place to its left — for whole numbers up to 1,000,000, then extend it
across the decimal point to decimals through the thousandths. Next they use
that relationship to explain what really happens when numbers are multiplied or
divided by powers of 10 (zero patterns, decimal-point movement, and
whole-number exponents such as 10³). Finally they round decimals to any place
using number lines and benchmarks, and compare and order decimals by digit
meaning — never by counting digits.

## Essential question

*How does the value of a digit change as it moves across the place-value
system, and how can we use that pattern to read, shift, round, and compare
decimals with confidence?*

## Prerequisites (grade-4 track end-of-year expectations)

- Place value to 1,000,000 with ten-times relationships between adjacent places
- Reading, writing, and comparing whole numbers; rounding whole numbers to any place
- Decimals to hundredths: notation, number-line location, and comparison
- Fluent multi-digit addition and subtraction

The diagnostic weeks (Weeks 1–2) baseline these; Sessions 01–02 re-teach
digit-value relationships before anything new is assumed.

## Unit objectives (measurable; adult-assessed)

| # | Objective | Track objective | Standard(s) |
|---|---|---|---|
| UO1 | Given a multi-digit number (whole numbers to 1,000,000; decimals to thousandths), explain in words and symbols that each digit represents 10 times the value of the same digit one place to its right and 1/10 of the value of the same digit one place to its left. | 1 | 5.NBT.A.1 |
| UO2 | Read, write, and represent decimals to thousandths using base-ten numerals, number names, and expanded form (both fraction form, e.g. 3 × (1/10), and decimal form, e.g. 0.3). | 1 | 5.NBT.A.3.a |
| UO3 | Explain the pattern in the number of zeros when multiplying by powers of 10, explain decimal-point placement when multiplying or dividing decimals by powers of 10, and use whole-number exponents (10¹–10⁶) to denote powers of 10. | 2 | 5.NBT.A.2 |
| UO4 | Round decimals to any place (ones through thousandths) using number-line placement and benchmark reasoning, and explain each decision from the digit to the right of the rounding place. | 3 | 5.NBT.A.4 |
| UO5 | Compare and order two or more decimals to thousandths based on the meanings of the digits in each place, recording results with >, <, and =. | 1 | 5.NBT.A.3.b |

## Verified standards / source notes

Reference framework: **Common Core State Standards for Mathematics**, Grade 5,
Number and Operations in Base Ten, as published at
[thecorestandards.org/Math](https://thecorestandards.org/Math) (Grade 5 NBT page
at <http://thecorestandards.org/Math/Content/NBT/>; codes and descriptions
re-verified 2026-10-11). Exact official wording used:

- **5.NBT.A.1** — "Recognize that in a multi-digit number, a digit in one place
  represents 10 times as much as it represents in the place to its right and
  1/10 of what it represents in the place to its left."
- **5.NBT.A.2** — "Explain patterns in the number of zeros of the product when
  multiplying a number by powers of 10, and explain patterns in the placement
  of the decimal point when a decimal is multiplied or divided by a power of
  10. Use whole-number exponents to denote powers of 10."
- **5.NBT.A.3.a** — "Read and write decimals to thousandths using base-ten
  numerals, number names, and expanded form, e.g., 347.392 = 3 × 100 + 4 × 10
  + 7 × 1 + 3 × (1/10) + 9 × (1/100) + 2 × (1/1000)."
- **5.NBT.A.3.b** — "Compare two decimals to thousandths based on meanings of
  the digits in each place, using >, =, and < symbols to record the results of
  comparisons."
- **5.NBT.A.4** — "Use place value understanding to round decimals to any
  place." (Official-text wording; the site's Grade 5 NBT page rendering skips
  this item — the same rendering quirk documented in the track's
  [scope-and-sequence](../../scope-and-sequence.md).)

## Pacing — 16 sessions

Session types rotate across concept lesson, guided practice, fluency/practice
game, and review. Every session names its specific activity and its success
criterion.

### Week 3 — Digit-value relationships; decimals to thousandths (UO1, UO2)

| Session | Activity (named) | Success criterion |
|---|---|---|
| S01 | **Concept lesson** — [Lesson 01](lessons/lesson-01-digit-value-relationships.md): digit-value relationships in whole numbers to 1,000,000, bridged into decimals | Learner explains the 10× / 1/10 rule for adjacent places in their own words and applies it to two numbers on the exit check |
| S02 | **Guided practice** — "Ten-times detective": compare repeated digits across places (Lesson 01 guided + independent practice, with error-analysis item) | 8 of 9 practice tasks correct, with the error-analysis explanation naming the adjacent-place rule |
| S03 | **Concept lesson** — [Lesson 02](lessons/lesson-02-decimals-to-thousandths.md): reading, writing, and expanding decimals to thousandths; place-value chart illustration activity | Learner reads the chart's sample decimal in words and writes it in expanded form; exit check in expanded form is correct |
| S04 | **Fluency game** — Place-value dictation game ([practice.md](practice.md#game-1-place-value-dictation)): partner dictates decimals in words; learner writes numerals, then reverses roles | Learner writes 4 of 5 dictated decimals correctly and reads a partner's numeral back accurately |

### Week 4 — Powers of ten and exponents (UO3)

| Session | Activity (named) | Success criterion |
|---|---|---|
| S05 | **Concept lesson** — [Lesson 03](lessons/lesson-03-powers-of-ten.md): zero patterns, decimal-point movement, exponents 10¹–10⁶ | Learner evaluates ×/÷ by powers of 10 using digit-shift reasoning (not memorized rules) on the exit check |
| S06 | **Guided practice** — Powers-of-ten slider game ([practice.md](practice.md#game-2-powers-of-ten-slider)): digit strips shift on a place-value mat | Learner correctly shifts digits for 6 of 8 slider prompts and states the resulting value |
| S07 | **Applied session** — Legacy task [powers-of-ten-practice.md](../../assignments/powers-of-ten-practice.md): evaluate 36 × 100, 7 × 1,000, 4.2 × 100; explain how each digit's value changes; create and solve a missing-power problem | All three products correct (3,600; 7,000; 420) with a digit-value explanation; the learner-made missing-power problem is solvable and solved |
| S08 | **Review** — [Week-04 formative quiz](assessments/week-04_quiz.md) (11 items, UO1–UO3), same-day adult feedback, targeted reteach block | Adult records which of UO1–UO3 is insecure for each learner; reteach plan set before Week 5 |

### Week 5 — Rounding and comparing decimals (UO4, UO5)

| Session | Activity (named) | Success criterion |
|---|---|---|
| S09 | **Concept lesson** — [Lesson 04](lessons/lesson-04-rounding-decimals.md): rounding decimals to any place with number lines and benchmarks | Learner rounds correctly to the named place and justifies from the digit to the right on the exit check |
| S10 | **Fluency game** — Rounding relay ([practice.md](practice.md#game-3-rounding-relay)): turn-based team relay placing numbers on benchmark lines (strategy-based, not timed) | Team places 8 of 10 cards on the correct benchmark interval and states the rounded value |
| S11 | **Concept lesson** — [Lesson 05](lessons/lesson-05-comparing-and-ordering-decimals.md): digit-meaning comparison and ordering to thousandths | Learner compares and orders using place-by-place reasoning (no "longer decimal is bigger" errors) on the exit check |
| S12 | **Practice** — Sprint-time ordering task + selected legacy quiz items reworked with the key separated (source: [decimal-place-value-comparing-to-thousandths-quiz.md](../../quizzes/decimal-place-value-comparing-to-thousandths-quiz.md)) | Learner orders the race times correctly with place-value justification; legacy review items show separated-key discipline (no key beside questions) |

### Week 6 — Review, project, and culminating assessment (all objectives)

| Session | Activity (named) | Success criterion |
|---|---|---|
| S13 | **Project launch** — [Powers-of-Ten City](project.md): design a decimal-priced city market; price list in decimals to thousandths; scale one object by powers of 10 | Market price list drafted with at least 6 items, all prices in valid decimal form with correct number names |
