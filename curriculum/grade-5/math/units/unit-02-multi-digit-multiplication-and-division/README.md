# Unit 02 — Multi-Digit Multiplication and Division

**Grade 5 Mathematics · Weeks 7–10 · 16 sessions (~40 minutes each, 4 per week)**

**Standards:** 5.NBT.B.5, 5.NBT.B.6, 5.OA.A.1

## Overview

This unit turns grade-4 multi-digit computation into grade-5 fluency and
reasoning. Learners first make **estimation** a habit: every big computation
starts with a rounded estimate and a predicted answer range, so unreasonable
results get caught before they are trusted. Then they master the **standard
algorithm for multi-digit multiplication** (checking it against partial
products and area models), learn **division with up-to-four-digit dividends
and two-digit divisors** through partial quotients and area models, and
— critically — learn that a remainder is not an answer until the **story
tells you what it means**. The unit closes with **parentheses in numerical
expressions**, so multistep pricing and packing problems can be written and
evaluated exactly as intended.

## Essential question

*How can we compute with big numbers accurately, and how do we know when an
answer makes sense in the real story behind it?*

## Prerequisites (grade-4 track end-of-year expectations, plus Unit 01)

- Multi-digit multiplication (up to four-digit × one-digit; two two-digit
  numbers) and division with one-digit divisors, with remainders
- Unit 01: powers-of-ten patterns and digit-value relationships (used in
  estimation and the standard algorithm's shifted rows)
- Fluent addition and subtraction with regrouping; multiplication facts to 10 × 10

The diagnostic weeks baselined the grade-4 skills; Sessions 01–02 re-teach
estimation and partial products before the standard algorithm is assumed.

## Unit objectives (measurable; adult-assessed)

| # | Objective | Track objective | Standard(s) |
|---|---|---|---|
| UO1 | Multiply multi-digit whole numbers fluently with the standard algorithm, showing each partial product and checking the result against a rounded estimate. | 4 | 5.NBT.B.5 |
| UO2 | Find whole-number quotients of whole numbers with up to four-digit dividends and two-digit divisors using partial quotients, area models, or place-value strategies, and check each quotient by multiplication. | 4 | 5.NBT.B.6 |
| UO3 | Interpret a division remainder in context: state how many full groups form, what is left over, and — for packing/story problems — how many groups are needed to hold everything. | 4 | 5.NBT.B.6 |
| UO4 | Write multistep word problems as numerical expressions using parentheses, and evaluate expressions containing parentheses, brackets, or braces. | 5 (emerging) | 5.OA.A.1 |
| UO5 | Judge the reasonableness of a product or quotient using rounding/compatible-number estimates and the inverse relationship between multiplication and division; find and correct one-step computation errors. | 4 | 5.NBT.B.5, 5.NBT.B.6 |

## Pacing — 16 sessions

Session types rotate across concept lesson, guided practice, fluency/practice
game, review, and project. Every session names its specific activity and its
success criterion. Every concept lesson opens with a retrieval warm-up from
Unit 01 (powers of ten, digit shifts) or grade-4 place value.

### Week 7 — Estimation and multi-digit multiplication (UO1, UO5)

| Session | Activity (named) | Success criterion |
|---|---|---|
| S01 | **Concept lesson** — [Lesson 01](lessons/lesson-01-estimation-before-computing.md): rounding and compatible-number estimates for products and quotients | Learner produces a rounded estimate for each of 4 problems and states whether the actual answer (checked against it) falls in a sensible range |
| S02 | **Guided practice** — "Estimate first, then compute": Lesson 01 guided + independent practice with error-analysis item | 8 of 10 practice tasks correct; error-analysis explanation names the over-rounding mistake |
| S03 | **Concept lesson** — [Lesson 02](lessons/lesson-02-multi-digit-multiplication-standard-algorithm.md): standard algorithm for multi-digit multiplication; partial products and the area model as checks | Learner computes 23 × 45 and 126 × 14 correctly by the standard algorithm and verifies each with partial products |
| S04 | **Practice** — Legacy tasks [multi-digit-multiplication.md](../../assignments/multi-digit-multiplication.md) (production budget: 14 lamps × $126, 18 microphones × $57, 25 boxes × $38, + $70 shipping) | All three line totals and the $3,810 grand total correct, with estimate-then-calculate shown |

### Week 8 — Division with one- and two-digit divisors (UO2, UO5)

| Session | Activity (named) | Success criterion |
|---|---|---|
| S05 | **Concept lesson** — [Lesson 03](lessons/lesson-03-division-with-partial-quotients.md): partial quotients for up-to-four-digit dividends with one-digit divisors | Learner divides 7,248 ÷ 6 and 5,670 ÷ 5 by partial quotients and checks both by multiplication |
| S06 | **Guided practice** — Partial-quotients pairs: Lesson 03 guided + independent practice; "remainder smaller than divisor" check drilled | 8 of 10 correct; every quotient accompanied by a check-by-multiplication line |
| S07 | **Concept lesson** — [Lesson 04](lessons/lesson-04-two-digit-divisors-and-remainder-stories.md): two-digit divisors (936 ÷ 24, 1,344 ÷ 32), area-model division, remainder stories with the [packing-workshop illustration](assets/packing-workshop-illustration.png) | Learner divides 936 ÷ 24 correctly, states 43 full boxes and 1 leftover for 775 ÷ 18, and names what the partly-full crate in the illustration represents |
| S08 | **Review** — [Week-08 formative quiz](assessments/week-08_quiz.md) (10 items, UO1, UO2, UO5), same-day adult feedback, targeted reteach block | Adult records which of UO1/UO2/UO5 is insecure for each learner; reteach plan set before Week 9 |

### Week 9 — Remainders in context and parentheses (UO3, UO4)

| Session | Activity (named) | Success criterion |
