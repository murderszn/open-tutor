# Unit Assessment — Multi-Digit Multiplication and Division

**Objectives:** UO1–UO5 · **Time:** about 45 minutes · **Calculator:** no

*Student-facing. The answer key is in this unit's `answer-key.md` — it is not
printed with this assessment.*

**Part 1 — Estimation and reasonableness (UO5)**

1. Estimate 287 × 42. Is your estimate high, low, or close? One sentence.
2. A learner computes 4,806 ÷ 9 and gets 534. Use a compatible-number estimate
   to judge whether 534 is reasonable.

**Part 2 — Multiplication (UO1)**

3. Compute **126 × 14** by the standard algorithm. Show both rows.
4. Compute **38 × 25**. Show an estimate first, then the exact work.
5. **Error hunt.** "46 × 32 = 92." Find the error, explain it in one sentence,
   and give the correct product.

**Part 3 — Division (UO2)**

6. Divide **9,216 ÷ 8** by partial quotients. Show the chunks.
7. Divide **1,344 ÷ 32**. Check by multiplication.
8. Divide **4,200 ÷ 35**. Write the quotient and the check.

**Part 4 — Remainders in context (UO3)**

9. 775 buttons are packed into boxes of 18. (a) How many full boxes?
   (b) How many buttons are left over? (c) How many boxes are needed to pack
   every button?
10. A ferry carries 250 passengers per trip. How many trips are needed to carry
    2,916 passengers? Show the division and explain what the remainder means
    for the answer.

**Part 5 — Expressions with parentheses (UO4)**

11. Write the expression for this order and evaluate it: 14 lamps at $126 each,
    18 microphones at $57 each, 25 boxes at $38 each, plus $70 shipping.
12. Evaluate: (a) 4 × (36 + 24) (b) (4 × 36) + 24. Then write one sentence
    saying why the answers differ.
13. Write the remainder from question 9 as a single expression of the form
    `dividend − (quotient × divisor) = remainder`.
14. Evaluate: 100 − [ (12 + 8) × 2 ]. Show each step.

**Part 6 — Mixed check (UO1, UO2, UO5)**

15. A theater sells 1,150 tickets at $24 each. Estimate the revenue, compute it
    exactly, and write one sentence saying why you trust your answer.
16. Divide 7,248 ÷ 6 two ways: once with partial quotients and once with a
    different set of chunks. Show that both give the same quotient.

**Extended reasoning (all objectives)**

17. The Packing & Pricing Co. project priced an order at $3,810 and packed 775
    buttons into boxes of 18 (43 full boxes, 1 left over, 44 boxes to hold
    everything). A new customer orders **double** the lamps, microphones, and
    boxes, with the same $70 shipping. (a) Write the new order as one
    expression with parentheses. (b) Evaluate it. (c) Could you have found the
    answer by doubling $3,810? Explain why or why not. (d) The workshop now has
    1,550 buttons. How many full boxes of 18 does that fill, and how many
    leftover? Show the work.

---

*Scoring guidance for the adult (details in the answer key): 14 of 17 at full
credit with work shown = ready for Unit 03. Items 9–10 (UO3) and 11–14 (UO4)
are the unit's new-thinking signals — partial credit rewards correct method
with a computation slip. The extended item is graded holistically: expression
structure, evaluation, and the doubling argument each count.*
