# Week-08 Formative Quiz — Multi-Digit Multiplication and Division

**Objectives:** UO1, UO2, UO5 · **Time:** about 25 minutes · **Calculator:** no

*Student-facing. The answer key is in this unit's `answer-key.md` — it is not
printed with this quiz.*

1. Estimate the product 48 × 63. Write your estimate and say whether you
   rounded up, down, or mixed.
2. Estimate the quotient 2,905 ÷ 29 using compatible numbers.
3. Compute **347 × 9** by the standard algorithm. Show the carried digits.
4. Compute **56 × 24**. Show both rows (ones row and tens row).
5. Divide **5,460 ÷ 6** by partial quotients. Write the quotient.
6. Divide **2,772 ÷ 28** by partial quotients or an area model. Write the
   quotient.
7. Divide **936 ÷ 24**. Then check your quotient by multiplication.
8. An area model for 17 × 35 is split into 10 × 30, 10 × 5, 7 × 30, and 7 × 5.
   Find the four partial products and the total.
9. **Error hunt.** A learner writes "2,508 ÷ 3 = 86." Is this reasonable?
   Show the error and give the correct quotient.
10. A bakery packs 1,000 cookies into boxes of 12. How many **full** boxes?
    How many cookies are left over? How many boxes are needed to pack **all**
    the cookies?

---

*Scoring guidance for the adult (details in the answer key): 8 of 10 correct
with checks shown = on track; items 7 and 10 are the priority re-teach signals
— if either is missed, revisit Lessons 03–04 before Week 9.*
