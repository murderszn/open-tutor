# Resource Pack — Unit 02: Multi-Digit Multiplication and Division

**Goal:** Give the adult one shelf of free, vetted supports for teaching
grade-5 multi-digit multiplication (standard algorithm), division with
two-digit divisors (partial quotients, area models), remainder interpretation,
and parentheses in numerical expressions. **Age fit:** grades 4–6 (about ages
9–12); written for the adult to excerpt and explain at a 5th-grade level,
never assigned whole.

All external entries checked 2026-10-18. Videos are supplements to teaching,
not replacements.
