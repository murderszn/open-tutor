# Practice — Unit 04: Fraction Addition, Subtraction, and Division

**Grade 5 · Use across Sessions S02, S04, S06, S10. Answers are in
[answer-key.md](answer-key.md) — adults only; learners work from this
page alone.**

Every set: rename first, then add or subtract, then simplify. Show the
renamed fractions for every item.

## Game 1 — Common-denominator race (S02, Lesson 01)

In pairs, one learner names a pair of fractions (for example, 3/4 and
2/3) and the other names their least common denominator and the two
renamed fractions. Swap roles. First pair to finish five correct rounds
wins the round; a wrong renaming sends the pair back one round. Use
only denominators up to 12.

## Game 2 — Fraction-strip build (S04, Lesson 02)

Build each sum with strips before writing it: lay out the first
fraction's strip, lay out the second fraction's renamed strip beside
it, and read the total off the combined length. Teams check each
other's builds; a build that mixes piece sizes is rebuilt, not argued
about.

## Game 3 — Between-two-wholes bingo (S10, Lesson 05)

The adult calls divisions (for example, "11 ÷ 4"). Learners mark the
pair of whole numbers the answer lies between (2 and 3) on a 0–10
board, then write the fraction and mixed number beside it. A row is
complete only when every fraction is written correctly.

## Practice Set A — Equivalent fractions and common denominators (UO1)

**A1.** Write 1/2 with denominator 12.

**A2.** Rename 3/5 and 1/10 with a common denominator.

**A3.** Rename 5/6 and 3/4 with a common denominator.

**A4.** Rename 5/8 and 2/3 with denominator 24.

**A5.** True or false? 3/4 = 9/12. Explain with multiplication.

## Practice Set B — Adding with unlike denominators (UO2)

**B1.** 1/5 + 1/10.

**B2.** 5/6 + 1/3.

**B3.** 3/8 + 5/12.

**B4.** 2/3 + 3/5.

**B5. Story.** Maya reads for 1/4 hour in the morning and 1/3 hour in
the evening. How long does she read in all?

## Practice Set C — Subtracting with unlike denominators (UO3)

**C1.** 5/6 − 1/2.

**C2.** 7/8 − 2/3.

**C3.** 9/10 − 3/5.

**C4.** 5/12 − 1/4.

**C5. Story.** A board is 7/8 m long. A piece 1/2 m long is cut off.
How much board is left?

## Practice Set D — Mixed numbers, estimates, and word problems (UO3–UO4)

**D1.** 2 1/3 + 1 1/2.

**D2.** 5 1/2 − 3 3/4. (Regroup.)

**D3.** Estimate 4/5 + 1/3 with benchmarks, then find the exact sum
and say whether the estimate supports it.

**D4. Story.** A bag holds 3 1/4 kg of flour. 1 1/2 kg are used for
baking. How many kilograms remain in the bag?

**D5. Error analysis.** A learner writes 1 1/2 + 2 2/3 = 3 3/5. Find
the correct sum and explain the learner's mistake.

## Practice Set E — Fraction as division (UO5)

**E1.** 6 ÷ 8, simplified, with the two whole numbers the answer lies
between.

**E2.** 13 ÷ 5, as a mixed number, with the two whole numbers the
answer lies between.

**E3.** 7 ÷ 4, as a mixed number, with the two whole numbers the
answer lies between.

**E4. Story.** 4 pizzas are shared equally among 6 children. How much
pizza does each child get? Check by multiplying back.

**E5.** 9 ÷ 2, as a mixed number, with a multiplication check.
