# Resource Pack — Unit 04: Fraction Addition, Subtraction, and Division

**Goal:** Give the adult one shelf of free, vetted supports for teaching
grade-5 fraction addition and subtraction with unlike denominators,
including mixed numbers, benchmark estimation, and fraction-as-division.
**Age fit:** grades 4–6 (about ages 9–12); written for the adult to
excerpt and explain at a 5th-grade level, never assigned whole.

All external entries checked 2026-10-31. Videos are supplements to
teaching, not replacements. A free, no-account route supports every
core objective below.
