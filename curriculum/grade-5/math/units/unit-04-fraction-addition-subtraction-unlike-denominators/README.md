# Unit 04 — Fraction Addition and Subtraction with Unlike Denominators

**Grade 5 Mathematics · Weeks 15–18 · 16 sessions (~40 minutes each, 4 per week)**

**Standards:** 5.NF.A.1, 5.NF.A.2, 5.NF.B.3 (with 4.NF.A.1 equivalent-
fraction foundations from Grade 4)

## Overview

Unit 03 taught learners to trust place value and estimation with
decimals. This unit teaches the fraction version of the same discipline:
you cannot add or subtract pieces of different sizes. Thirds and
fourths must first become twelfths — the same size, the same whole —
and only then do the numerators combine. Mixed numbers add one more
skill, regrouping a whole into fraction pieces, and the unit closes
with the sharing meaning of a fraction: a/b = a ÷ b, so a division
problem like 7 ÷ 3 has an exact fraction answer, 2 1/3, that lies
between 2 and 3. The Fraction Bakery project makes learners do all of
it at once: rename, estimate, compute, share, and explain.

## Essential question

*How can same-size pieces, benchmarks, and sharing tell me what a
fraction sum, difference, or division answer should be — before I
compute it?*

## Prerequisites (from Units 01–03 and Grade 4)

- Equivalent fractions and fraction bars (Grade 4): multiplying
  numerator and denominator by the same number does not change a
  fraction's size
- Fraction meanings: parts of one whole, and parts of a set of same-size
  wholes
- Multiples and least common multiples up to 12 (Unit 02 fluency)
- Decimal estimation habit from Unit 03: estimate first, then compute,
  then judge reasonableness

The Week 15 opening session re-checks these; nothing new is assumed.

## Unit objectives (measurable; adult-assessed)

| # | Objective | Standard(s) |
|---|---|---|
| UO1 | Rename fractions with a common denominator (least common denominator where useful) by multiplying numerator and denominator by the same number, and explain why the size does not change. | 5.NF.A.1 |
| UO2 | Add fractions with unlike denominators, including answers over 1 written as mixed numbers, showing the renamed fractions and simplifying. | 5.NF.A.1 |
| UO3 | Subtract fractions and mixed numbers with unlike denominators, regrouping a whole when the top fraction part is smaller, and check by adding back. | 5.NF.A.1 |
| UO4 | Solve word problems involving addition and subtraction of fractions referring to the same whole, estimate with benchmark fractions first, and judge whether an answer is reasonable. | 5.NF.A.2 |
| UO5 | Interpret a fraction as division (a/b = a ÷ b); solve whole-number division problems leading to fraction or mixed-number answers, and state between which two whole numbers the answer lies. | 5.NF.B.3 |

## Verified standards / source notes

Reference framework: **Common Core State Standards for Mathematics**,
Grade 5, Number and Operations — Fractions, as published at
[thecorestandards.org/Math](https://thecorestandards.org/Math) (Grade 5
NF page; codes and descriptions re-verified 2026-10-31).

- **5.NF.A.1** — "Add and subtract fractions with unlike denominators
  (including mixed numbers) by replacing given fractions with
  equivalent fractions in such a way as to produce an equivalent sum
  or difference of fractions with like denominators. *For example,
  2/3 + 5/4 = 8/12 + 15/12 = 23/12. (In general, a/b + c/d =
  (ad + bc)/bd.)*"
- **5.NF.A.2** — "Solve word problems involving addition and
  subtraction of fractions referring to the same whole, including
  cases of unlike denominators, e.g., by using visual fraction models
  or equations to represent the problem. Use benchmark fractions and
  number sense of fractions to estimate mentally and assess the
  reasonableness of answers. *For example, recognize an incorrect
  result 2/5 + 1/2 = 3/7, by observing that 3/7 < 1/2.*"
- **5.NF.B.3** — "Interpret a fraction as division of the numerator
  by the denominator (*a*/*b* = *a* ÷ *b*). Solve word problems
  involving division of whole numbers leading to answers in the form
  of fractions or mixed numbers, e.g., by using visual fraction models
  or equations to represent the problem."

**Scope note (honest boundary):** this unit follows the track's
scope-and-sequence for Weeks 15–18, which places 5.NF.B.3 in Week 17
of this unit and reserves fraction *multiplication* for Unit 05.
Multiplying fractions (5.NF.B.4–B.7) is not taught here; Lesson 05's
multiplication checks (for example, 3/4 × 4 = 3) only verify a sharing
answer. Week 18 is the track's midyear-review week in the
scope-and-sequence; this unit uses it as its closing week (project
presentations, review, assessment, feedback) and hands its assessment
data to the track's cumulative midyear review package (R00), rather
than duplicating that package here.

## Differentiation and accessibility

- **Reading/language access:** every lesson states its goal in one sentence
  before any numbers appear; key words (*same whole*, *share equally*,
  *regroup*, *benchmark*) are pre-taught with a picture or gesture.
- **Alternate response modes:** learners may answer orally, point to a
  bar or strip, or dictate steps to the adult; written answers are never
  the only evidence.
- **Supports:** fraction strips, paper bars, and a regrouping card
  ("1 whole = 4/4 = 6/6 = 8/8 = 12/12") are available in every session;
  no timed speed tests anywhere in this unit.
- **Extensions:** three-fraction sums and differences (Lessons 02–03),
  a recipe extension (Lesson 04), a 23 ÷ 5/6 sharing extension
  (Lesson 05), and a decimal-priced stall variant (project.md) are
  optional and never gate the required objectives.
