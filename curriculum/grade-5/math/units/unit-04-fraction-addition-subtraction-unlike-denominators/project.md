# Project — The Fraction Bakery (Unit 04)

**Grade 5 · Sessions S11–S12 (with presentations in S13) · Objectives
UO1–UO5**

## The task

You run a small bakery stall for one day. Design a bake-sale menu card
that a customer could actually use, and show every fraction computation
behind it. Your menu card must use the unit's skills — renaming,
adding, subtracting, mixed numbers, benchmarks, and sharing as
division — not just list recipes.

## Deliverables

1. **Menu card (one page):** at least 3 items. For each item, list the
   amounts of two ingredients as fractions with unlike denominators
   (for example, 2/3 cup of oats and 1/4 cup of raisins).
2. **Computation sheet:** for each item, the renamed fractions, the
   total amount of the two ingredients, and a benchmark estimate made
   *before* the exact computation.
3. **Tray and sharing plan:** one item's batch is shared equally among
   a stated number of people. Show the division, the fraction each
   person gets, the mixed number if it is more than 1, and the two
   whole numbers the answer lies between. Include one subtraction:
   an amount left over after part of a batch is sold.
4. **Presentation (2 minutes):** explain one computation from your
   sheet out loud, including why the denominators had to match first.

## Worked starter (do not copy — make your own)

Item: oat bars. Oats 2/3 cup, raisins 1/4 cup. Common denominator 12:
2/3 = 8/12, 1/4 = 3/12. Total: 11/12 cup. Benchmark: 2/3 is more than 1/2 and 1/4 is
positive, so the total must be more than 1/2; and 2/3 is less than
3/4, so 2/3 + 1/4 must be less than 3/4 + 1/4 = 1. The total lies
between 1/2 and 1, and 11/12 fits — reasonable. Sharing: 5 bars among 4 people =
5 ÷ 4 = 5/4 = 1 1/4 bars each, between 1 and 2.

## Materials

Paper, fraction strips, coloured pencils, the baking-scene picture
from Lesson 04 as a story prompt. No real baking is required; if the
adult chooses to bake, an adult supervises the oven and all food
handling, and learners with food allergies use the paper version only.

## Rubric

| Criterion | 3 — Strong | 2 — Developing | 1 — Starting |
|---|---|---|---|
| Renaming (UO1) | Every pair renamed correctly with the common denominator shown | Most pairs renamed correctly; one denominator error | Fractions added or subtracted without renaming |
| Add / subtract accuracy (UO2–UO3) | All totals and leftovers correct and simplified, with mixed numbers regrouped correctly | Most correct; one regrouping or simplification slip | Two or more computation errors, or denominators added |
| Word sense and benchmarks (UO4) | Every estimate made before computing and used to judge the exact answer | Estimates present but not used to check answers | No estimates, or estimates copied from exact answers |
| Sharing as division (UO5) | Division, fraction, mixed number, between-whole-numbers, and multiplication check all correct | Division and fraction correct; check or between-numbers missing | Sharing treated as a whole-number division or swapped numerator/denominator |
| Presentation | One computation explained clearly, including why denominators must match | Explanation given but the denominator reason is missing | No explanation, or the computation is read out without reasoning |

**Ready to present:** all four deliverables complete, rubric rows at
level 2 or above, and at least one row at level 3.

## Extension (optional, never gates the required objectives)

Price the stall: give each item a price in dollars and cents, and use
Unit 03 decimal skills to total a sample order. Keep the fraction work
as the graded core.
