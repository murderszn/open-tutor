# Unit Assessment — Unit 04: Fraction Addition, Subtraction, and Division

**Grade 5 · Session S15 · 16 items · Objectives UO1–UO5**

**Instructions:** Show the renamed fractions for every computation.
Simplify your answers and write mixed numbers where an answer is more
than 1. Explain in words where an item asks for an explanation.

1. Write 3/4 with denominator 12.
2. Rename 1/6 and 2/3 with a common denominator.
3. 2/5 + 1/4.
4. 7/8 + 1/6.
5. 1/2 + 5/6.
6. 5/6 − 1/3.
7. 9/10 − 1/4.
8. 7/12 − 1/3.
9. 1 3/4 + 2 1/2.
10. 6 1/2 − 3 3/4. (Regroup if you need to.)
11. **Story.** A bottle holds 2/3 litre of juice. Maya pours in 1/6
    litre more. How many litres are in the bottle now?
12. **Story.** A rope is 3 1/2 m long. 1 3/4 m are cut off. How much
    rope is left?
13. 5 ÷ 6. Write the fraction and the two whole numbers the answer
    lies between.
14. 11 ÷ 3. Write the fraction, the mixed number, and the two whole
    numbers the answer lies between.
15. **Error analysis.** A learner writes 3/5 − 1/10 = 2/5. Explain the
    mistake and find the correct difference.
16. **Explain.** In your own words, why must two fractions have the
    same denominator before you can add or subtract them? Use the
    example 1/2 + 1/4 in your explanation.
