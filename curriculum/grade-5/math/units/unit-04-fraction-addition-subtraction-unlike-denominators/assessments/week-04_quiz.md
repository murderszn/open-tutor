# Week-04 Formative Quiz — Unit 04

**Grade 5 · Session S08 · 12 items · Objectives UO1–UO5**

**Instructions:** Show the renamed fractions for every item. Simplify
your answers. The adult reviews this quiz the same day; it is a check
for reteaching, not a final grade.

1. Write 1/2 with denominator 8.
2. Rename 2/3 and 1/4 with a common denominator.
3. 1/3 + 1/6.
4. 3/4 + 1/8.
5. 5/6 + 1/4.
6. 5/8 − 1/4.
7. 7/10 − 1/5.
8. 3/4 − 1/6.
9. 2 1/2 + 1 1/4.
10. 4 1/3 − 2 5/6. (Regroup if you need to.)
11. 7 ÷ 3. Write the fraction, the mixed number, and the two whole
    numbers the answer lies between.
12. **Reasonableness.** A learner says 1/2 + 2/5 = 3/7. Use benchmarks
    to explain why 3/7 cannot be right, then find the correct sum.
