## 🔑 Parent Answer Key (Educator)

> [!NOTE]
> *This section is for Educator (educator) to quickly check Learner’s work, celebrate victories, and review any tricky steps together.*

**Question 1:**  
- **Target Answer & Key Steps:** $56 \times 4 = 224$; $56 \times 30 = 1680$; $224 + 1680 = 1{,}904$.

**Question 2:**  
- **Target Answer & Key Steps:** $16 \times 70 = 1120$; remaining $128$; $16 \times 8 = 128$. Quotient $= 78$.

**Question 3:**  
- **Target Answer & Key Steps:** $18.45 + 9.78 = 28.23$.

**Question 4:**  
- **Target Answer & Key Steps:** $50.20 - 17.65 = 32.55$.

**Question 5:**  
- **Target Answer & Key Steps:** $36 \times 24 = 864 \implies 8.64$.

**Question 6:**  
- **Target Answer & Key Steps:** $14.4 \div 12 = 1.2$.

**Question 7:**  
- **Target Answer & Key Steps:** $5.089 < 5.090$, so $<$.

**Question 8:**  
- **Target Answer & Key Steps:** Nearest tenth: $8.5$. Nearest hundredth: $8.46$.

**Question 9:**  
- **Target Answer & Key Steps:** Total saved: $18.25 + 24.50 = 42.75$. Remaining needed: $65.00 - 42.75 = \$22.25$.

**Question 10:**  
- **Target Answer & Key Steps:** $10.00 \div 8 = \$1.25$ per pen.
