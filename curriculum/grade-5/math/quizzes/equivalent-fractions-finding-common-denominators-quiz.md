## 🔑 Parent Answer Key (Educator)

> [!NOTE]
> *This section is for Educator (educator) to quickly check Learner’s work, celebrate victories, and review any tricky steps together.*

**Question 1:**  
- **Target Answer & Key Steps:** Sample answers: $\frac{6}{10}, \frac{9}{15}, \frac{12}{20}$.

**Question 2:**  
- **Target Answer & Key Steps:** Multiples of 4: 4, 8, 12, 16... Multiples of 6: 6, 12... LCM $= 12$.

**Question 3:**  
- **Target Answer & Key Steps:** LCM of 3 and 4 is $12$.

**Question 4:**  
- **Target Answer & Key Steps:** $\frac{2}{3} = \frac{8}{12}$ and $\frac{1}{4} = \frac{3}{12}$.

**Question 5:**  
- **Target Answer & Key Steps:** Multiples of 6: 6, 12, 18, 24... Multiples of 8: 8, 16, 24... LCD $= 24$.

**Question 6:**  
- **Target Answer & Key Steps:** $\frac{5}{6} = \frac{20}{24}$ and $\frac{3}{8} = \frac{9}{24}$.

**Question 7:**  
- **Target Answer & Key Steps:** GCF of 18 and 24 is 6. $\frac{18 \div 6}{24 \div 6} = \frac{3}{4}$.

**Question 8:**  
- **Target Answer & Key Steps:** $17 \div 5 = 3$ with remainder $2 \implies 3\frac{2}{5}$.

**Question 9:**  
- **Target Answer & Key Steps:** $(4 \times 3) + 2 = 14 \implies \frac{14}{3}$.

**Question 10:**  
- **Target Answer & Key Steps:** Common denominator 40: $\frac{3}{5} = \frac{24}{40}$ and $\frac{5}{8} = \frac{25}{40}$. Since $25 > 24$, $\frac{5}{8}$ is larger.
