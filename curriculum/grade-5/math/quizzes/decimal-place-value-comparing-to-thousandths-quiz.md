## 🔑 Parent Answer Key (Educator)

> [!NOTE]
> *This section is for Educator (educator) to quickly check Learner’s work, celebrate victories, and review any tricky steps together.*

**Question 1:**  
- **Target Answer & Key Steps:** Four hundred fifty-eight thousandths.

**Question 2:**  
- **Target Answer & Key Steps:** $3.024$

**Question 3:**  
- **Target Answer & Key Steps:** The hundredths place (value is $0.09$ or $\frac{9}{100}$).

**Question 4:**  
- **Target Answer & Key Steps:** $(4 \times 1) + (3 \times 0.1) + (5 \times 0.001)$ or $4 + \frac{3}{10} + \frac{5}{1000}$.

**Question 5:**  
- **Target Answer & Key Steps:** $0.650 > 0.605$, so $>$.

**Question 6:**  
- **Target Answer & Key Steps:** $=$ (they are equivalent decimals).

**Question 7:**  
- **Target Answer & Key Steps:** $0.304 < 0.34 < 0.403 < 0.43$.

**Question 8:**  
- **Target Answer & Key Steps:** $6.8$ (the digit 8 in hundredths rounds 7 up to 8).

**Question 9:**  
- **Target Answer & Key Steps:** $14.30$ (the 6 in thousandths rounds 9 up, carrying to 30 hundredths).

**Question 10:**  
- **Target Answer & Key Steps:** Runner B won 1st place with $11.085$ seconds (smallest time).
