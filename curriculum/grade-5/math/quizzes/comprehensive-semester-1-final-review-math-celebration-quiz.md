## 🔑 Parent Answer Key (Educator)

> [!NOTE]
> *This section is for Educator (educator) to quickly check Learner’s work, celebrate victories, and review any tricky steps together.*

**Question 1:**  
- **Target Answer & Key Steps:** $348 \times 6 = 2088$; $348 \times 20 = 6960$; $2088 + 6960 = 9{,}048$.

**Question 2:**  
- **Target Answer & Key Steps:** $16 \times 100 = 1600$; remainder $32$; $16 \times 2 = 32$. Quotient $= 102$.

**Question 3:**  
- **Target Answer & Key Steps:** $24.75 + 18.60 = 43.35$.

**Question 4:**  
- **Target Answer & Key Steps:** $40.00 - 15.38 = 24.62$.

**Question 5:**  
- **Target Answer & Key Steps:** $45 \times 8 = 360 \implies 3.60 = 3.6$.

**Question 6:**  
- **Target Answer & Key Steps:** Common denominator 20: $\frac{12}{20} + \frac{5}{20} = \frac{17}{20}$.

**Question 7:**  
- **Target Answer & Key Steps:** Regroup $4\frac{1}{3} = 3\frac{4}{3}$. $3\frac{4}{3} - 1\frac{2}{3} = 2\frac{2}{3}$.

**Question 8:**  
- **Target Answer & Key Steps:** $\frac{3 \times 8}{4 \times 9} = \frac{24}{36} = \frac{2}{3}$.

**Question 9:**  
- **Target Answer & Key Steps:** $6 \div \frac{1}{2} = 6 \times 2 = 12\text{ servings}$.

**Question 10:**  
- **Target Answer & Key Steps:** $V = 7 \times 5 \times 4 = 140\text{ cm}^3$.
