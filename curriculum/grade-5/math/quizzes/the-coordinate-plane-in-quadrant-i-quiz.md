## 🔑 Parent Answer Key (Educator)

> [!NOTE]
> *This section is for Educator (educator) to quickly check Learner’s work, celebrate victories, and review any tricky steps together.*

**Question 1:**  
- **Target Answer & Key Steps:** $(0, 0)$

**Question 2:**  
- **Target Answer & Key Steps:** The number $4$ is the $x$-coordinate (horizontal distance right). The number $7$ is the $y$-coordinate (vertical distance up).

**Question 3:**  
- **Target Answer & Key Steps:** Move 6 units to the right along the x-axis, then move 3 units up parallel to the y-axis. Mark the point.

**Question 4:**  
- **Target Answer & Key Steps:** $(8, 0)$ (lies on the x-axis).

**Question 5:**  
- **Target Answer & Key Steps:** $(0, 5)$ (lies on the y-axis).

**Question 6:**  
- **Target Answer & Key Steps:** It forms a square with side length $4$ units ($6 - 2 = 4$). Area $= 4 \times 4 = 16\text{ square units}$.

**Question 7:**  
- **Target Answer & Key Steps:** $x = 3 + 4 = 7$; $y = 4 + 2 = 6$. New point is $(7, 6)$.

**Question 8:**  
- **Target Answer & Key Steps:** It means after 3 hours, the traveler has traveled 150 miles.

**Question 9:**  
- **Target Answer & Key Steps:** No. $(2, 5)$ is 2 units right and 5 units up, while $(5, 2)$ is 5 units right and 2 units up. Order matters in ordered pairs.

**Question 10:**  
- **Target Answer & Key Steps:** $(1, 3), (2, 5), (3, 7), (4, 9)$
