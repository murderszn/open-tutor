## 🔑 Parent Answer Key (Educator)

> [!NOTE]
> *This section is for Educator (educator) to quickly check Learner’s work, celebrate victories, and review any tricky steps together.*

**Question 1:**  
- **Target Answer & Key Steps:** Common denominator 4: $\frac{2}{4} + \frac{1}{4} = \frac{3}{4}$.

**Question 2:**  
- **Target Answer & Key Steps:** Common denominator 6: $\frac{4}{6} + \frac{1}{6} = \frac{5}{6}$.

**Question 3:**  
- **Target Answer & Key Steps:** Common denominator 12: $\frac{9}{12} - \frac{4}{12} = \frac{5}{12}$.

**Question 4:**  
- **Target Answer & Key Steps:** Common denominator 10: $\frac{4}{10} + \frac{5}{10} = \frac{9}{10}$.

**Question 5:**  
- **Target Answer & Key Steps:** Common denominator 8: $\frac{7}{8} - \frac{2}{8} = \frac{5}{8}$.

**Question 6:**  
- **Target Answer & Key Steps:** Common denominator 12: $\frac{9}{12} + \frac{8}{12} = \frac{17}{12} = 1\frac{5}{12}$.

**Question 7:**  
- **Target Answer & Key Steps:** Common denominator 30: $\frac{25}{30} - \frac{9}{30} = \frac{16}{30} = \frac{8}{15}$.

**Question 8:**  
- **Target Answer & Key Steps:** $\frac{2}{8} + \frac{3}{8} = \frac{5}{8}$ of the pizza.

**Question 9:**  
- **Target Answer & Key Steps:** $1 - \frac{5}{8} = \frac{8}{8} - \frac{5}{8} = \frac{3}{8}$ of the pizza.

**Question 10:**  
- **Target Answer & Key Steps:** $\frac{8}{9} \approx 1$ and $\frac{1}{10} \approx 0$. Estimated sum $\approx 1 + 0 = 1$. (Exact is $\frac{89}{90} \approx 0.99$).
