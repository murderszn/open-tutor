## 🔑 Parent Answer Key (Educator)

> [!NOTE]
> *This section is for Educator (educator) to quickly check Learner’s work, celebrate victories, and review any tricky steps together.*

**Question 1:**  
- **Target Answer & Key Steps:** $\frac{6 \times 2}{3} = \frac{12}{3} = 4$.

**Question 2:**  
- **Target Answer & Key Steps:** $\frac{15}{4} = 3\frac{3}{4}$.

**Question 3:**  
- **Target Answer & Key Steps:** $\frac{1 \times 3}{2 \times 5} = \frac{3}{10}$.

**Question 4:**  
- **Target Answer & Key Steps:** $\frac{6}{12} = \frac{1}{2}$.

**Question 5:**  
- **Target Answer & Key Steps:** $\text{Area} = l \times w = \frac{4}{5} \times \frac{2}{3} = \frac{8}{15}\text{ square meters}$.

**Question 6:**  
- **Target Answer & Key Steps:** Cross-cancel: $\frac{1}{2} \times \frac{1}{2} = \frac{1}{4}$ (or $\frac{15}{60} = \frac{1}{4}$).

**Question 7:**  
- **Target Answer & Key Steps:** It makes the number smaller (scaling/resizing) because you are taking only a part/fraction of the original amount.

**Question 8:**  
- **Target Answer & Key Steps:** $\frac{3 \times 16}{8} = 3 \times 2 = 6$.

**Question 9:**  
- **Target Answer & Key Steps:** $3\frac{1}{2} \times 2 = \frac{7}{2} \times 2 = 7\text{ square feet}$.

**Question 10:**  
- **Target Answer & Key Steps:** Cancel 7s: $\frac{4}{9}$.
