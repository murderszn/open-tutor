## 🔑 Parent Answer Key (Educator)

> [!NOTE]
> *This section is for Educator (educator) to quickly check Learner’s work, celebrate victories, and review any tricky steps together.*

**Question 1:**  
- **Target Answer & Key Steps:** $\frac{1}{2 \times 3} = \frac{1}{6}$.

**Question 2:**  
- **Target Answer & Key Steps:** $\frac{1}{4 \times 2} = \frac{1}{8}$.

**Question 3:**  
- **Target Answer & Key Steps:** $3 \times 2 = 6$.

**Question 4:**  
- **Target Answer & Key Steps:** $5 \times 3 = 15$.

**Question 5:**  
- **Target Answer & Key Steps:** $6 \div \frac{1}{2} = 6 \times 2 = 12\text{ pieces}$.

**Question 6:**  
- **Target Answer & Key Steps:** $\frac{1}{3} \div 4 = \frac{1}{12}\text{ gallon}$.

**Question 7:**  
- **Target Answer & Key Steps:** $4 \times 5 = 20$.

**Question 8:**  
- **Target Answer & Key Steps:** $\frac{1}{18}$.

**Question 9:**  
- **Target Answer & Key Steps:** $5 \div \frac{1}{4} = 5 \times 4 = 20\text{ patties}$.

**Question 10:**  
- **Target Answer & Key Steps:** $4 \div \frac{1}{2} = 8$ (asks how many halves fit in 4 wholes). $\frac{1}{2} \div 4 = \frac{1}{8}$ (splits a half into 4 equal smaller slices).
