## 🎯 Mission Brief
You are a Lead Minecraft Architect designing a fortress base with storage vaults, watchtowers, and a secret perimeter navigation grid. You will calculate the internal volume of 3D structures using cubic meters ($1\text{ block} = 1\text{ cubic meter}$) and chart your base coordinates on a Quadrant I coordinate plane.

---

## 🏰 Part 1: Volume of Fortress Chambers

Remember the volume formulas:
$$\text{Volume} = \text{length} \times \text{width} \times \text{height} \quad (V = l \times w \times h)$$
$$\text{Volume} = \text{Base Area} \times \text{height} \quad (V = B \times h)$$

### Chamber 1: Main Storage Vault
- Length = $8\text{ blocks}$
- Width = $5\text{ blocks}$
- Height = $4\text{ blocks}$
- Calculate the total storage volume in cubic meters.

### Chamber 2: Tall Watchtower
- The base of the watchtower has an area of $16\text{ square meters}$ ($B = 16\text{ m}^2$).
- The tower rises $15\text{ blocks}$ high ($h = 15\text{ m}$).
- Calculate the watchtower volume using $V = B \times h$.

### Chamber 3: Composite Fortress (L-Shaped Barracks)
The barracks is made of two connected rectangular rooms:
- **Room A:** Length = $10\text{ m}$, Width = $6\text{ m}$, Height = $3\text{ m}$
- **Room B:** Length = $5\text{ m}$, Width = $4\text{ m}$, Height = $3\text{ m}$
1. Calculate the volume of Room A.
2. Calculate the volume of Room B.
3. What is the total combined volume of the entire barracks?

## 🗺️ Part 2: Coordinate Waypoint Mapping (Quadrant I)

Your fortress map is laid out on a grid where the fortress gate is at the Origin $(0, 0)$.
Remember: Ordered pairs are written $(x, y)$, where $x$ tells you how far to go **Right** (horizontal axis), and $y$ tells you how far to go **Up** (vertical axis).

### Base Waypoints:
- **Main Fortress Gate:** Point $G(0, 0)$
- **Archery Tower:** Point $A(3, 7)$
- **Gold Mine Depot:** Point $M(8, 2)$
- **Beacon Sanctuary:** Point $B(5, 9)$
- **Nether Portal Gate:** Point $P(10, 8)$

### Your Coordinate Missions:
1. Which landmark is farthest to the **East** (greatest $x$-value)?
2. Which landmark is farthest to the **North** (greatest $y$-value)?
3. Starting at the Fortress Gate $(0, 0)$, describe the step-by-step directions to walk to the Archery Tower: how many units right, and how many units up?
4. If a secret supply chest is hidden at $(6, 5)$, is it closer to the Beacon Sanctuary $(5, 9)$ or the Gold Mine Depot $(8, 2)$? Explain by counting grid hops.
