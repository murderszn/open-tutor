## 🔑 Parent Answer Key (For Educator)

<details>
<summary>Click to expand Answer Key</summary>

1. **Energy Driver**:
   - The Sun (solar thermal radiation).

2. **Water Cycle Stages**:
   - (a) Evaporation: Liquid water heated by the sun turns into invisible water vapor gas and rises into the air.
   - (b) Condensation: Rising water vapor cools down and turns back into tiny liquid water droplets, forming clouds.
   - (c) Precipitation: Water droplets in clouds grow heavy and fall back to Earth as rain, snow, sleet, or hail.

3. **Transpiration**:
   - Transpiration is the release of water vapor into the air through microscopic pores (stomata) on plant leaves.
   - It connects the **Biosphere** to the **Atmosphere**.

4. **Phase Change**:
   - No, it is still $H_2O$. Freezing is a **physical change** (change of state), not a chemical change.

5. **Scratch Coordinate Directions**:
   - `change y by 10`: Moves UP.
   - `change x by -10`: Moves LEFT.

6. **Scratch Movement Action**:
   - It makes the sprite perform a simple **jump** (moves up 20 pixels, pauses briefly, and returns back down).

7. **Water Cycle Diagram**:
   - Drawing should depict: Evaporation arrow pointing up from ocean $\to$ Condensation forming clouds $\to$ Precipitation arrow pointing down $\to$ Runoff/collection flowing back to ocean.

8. **Geosphere Formation**:
   - Limestone dissolution creates underground **caves** (caverns) or sinkholes (karst topography).
</details>
