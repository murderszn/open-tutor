### Questions

**1.** Why does the Moon shine in the night sky? Does it produce its own light like the Sun?

**2.** Name four of the primary phases of the Moon:
- (a) When the Moon is between Earth and Sun and looks completely dark: _________________
- (b) When the entire illuminated face of the Moon is visible: _________________
- (c) When the visible sliver is growing bigger each night: (Waxing or Waning?)
- (d) When the visible illuminated portion is shrinking each night: (Waxing or Waning?)

**3.** How does the Moon's gravitational pull affect the oceans on Earth? What daily phenomenon does it cause?

**4.** Examine this Python decision-making script:
```python
moon_phase = "Full Moon"

if moon_phase == "Full Moon":
    print("The night sky is glowing bright!")
else:
    print("Grab a flashlight for night exploring!")
```
What message will be printed? What would print if `moon_phase = "New Moon"`?

**5.** In Python, what is the difference between a single equals sign `=` and a double equals sign `==`?

**6.** What causes a **solar eclipse**? What causes a **lunar eclipse**?

**7.** **Diagram Challenge:** Draw a straight line showing the Sun, Earth, and Moon during a **Solar Eclipse**. Draw who is casting a shadow on whom!

**8.** If you visited the Moon, would your mass change? Would your weight change? Explain why you can jump 6 times higher on the Moon than on Earth.

## 🔑 Parent Answer Key (For Educator)

<details>
<summary>Click to expand Answer Key</summary>

1. **Moonlight**:
   - The Moon does NOT generate light; it reflects sunlight like a giant rocky mirror.

2. **Moon Phases**:
   - (a) New Moon
   - (b) Full Moon
   - (c) Waxing
   - (d) Waning

3. **Tides**:
   - The Moon's gravitational pull pulls on Earth's oceans, creating ocean bulges called **high tides** and **low tides** twice a day.

4. **Python `if/else`**:
   - Prints: `"The night sky is glowing bright!"`
   - If changed to `"New Moon"`, it jumps to the `else:` branch and prints `"Grab a flashlight for night exploring!"`.

5. **`=` vs. `==`**:
   - `=` is the assignment operator (stores a value into a variable: `x = 5`).
   - `==` is the equality comparison operator (checks if two values are equal: `if x == 5:`).

6. **Eclipses**:
   - Solar Eclipse: The Moon passes directly between the Sun and Earth, blocking sunlight and casting a shadow on Earth.
   - Lunar Eclipse: Earth passes directly between the Sun and Moon, casting Earth's shadow across the Full Moon.

7. **Solar Eclipse Diagram**:
   - Order: `[ Sun ] ======> [ Moon ] =====> (small shadow on Earth)`

8. **Mass vs. Weight on the Moon**:
   - Mass (the amount of matter in your body) stays exactly the same.
   - Weight (the gravitational pull on your mass) drops to about $1/6	ext{th}$ because the Moon is much smaller than Earth, so gravity is weaker!
</details>
