### Questions

**1.** Contrast **Rotation** and **Revolution**:
- How long does it take Earth to complete one full rotation on its axis? What does this cause?
- How long does it take Earth to complete one full revolution around the Sun? What does this cause?

**2.** What is the true cause of Earth's four seasons (Spring, Summer, Fall, Winter)?
*(Circle the correct answer)*:
- (A) Earth moves closer to the Sun in the summer and farther away in the winter.
- (B) Earth's tilted axis ($23.5^\circ$) points toward the Sun in summer and away from the Sun in winter.

**3.** When it is summer in the Northern Hemisphere (where we live), what season is it in Australia (Southern Hemisphere)?

**4.** In Python, what does the `int()` function do when wrapped around an input?
```python
age = int(input("How old are you? "))
print("Next year you will be:", age + 1)
```
What would happen if you forgot to put `int()` and tried to add `1`?

**5.** Why does the Sun appear to rise in the East and set in the West every day? Is the Sun actually moving around Earth?

**6.** **Diagram Challenge:** Draw Earth tilted on its axis orbiting the Sun. Label the Northern Hemisphere and show where the Sun's direct rays hit during summer.

**7.** What is a **leap year**? Why do we have to add February 29 to the calendar every 4 years?

**8.** In video editing, what is a "jump cut" and why should you trim out pauses like "um" and "uh"?

## 🔑 Parent Answer Key (For Educator)

<details>
<summary>Click to expand Answer Key</summary>

1. **Rotation vs. Revolution**:
   - Rotation: 24 hours (1 day). Causes **day and night**.
   - Revolution: 365.25 days (1 year). Together with axial tilt, causes **seasons**.

2. **Seasons Cause**:
   - **(B)** Earth's tilted axis ($23.5^\circ$). (In fact, Earth is slightly closer to the Sun in January!).

3. **Opposite Hemispheres**:
   - It is **Winter** in Australia.

4. **Python `int()` Conversion**:
   - `int()` converts the text string from `input()` into a real integer number so mathematical addition is possible.
   - Without `int()`, Python throws a `TypeError: can only concatenate str (not "int") to str`.

5. **Apparent Solar Motion**:
   - Earth rotates from West to East. This creates the optical illusion that the Sun and stars are traveling across the sky from East to West.

6. **Tilt & Seasons Diagram**:
   - Sun in center; Earth on right with northern axis tilted toward Sun (Northern Summer / direct concentrated rays); Earth on left with northern axis tilted away (Northern Winter / slanted rays).

7. **Leap Year**:
   - Earth's orbit takes approximately $365.2422	ext{ days}$ (about $365$ days and $6$ hours).
   - Adding 1 day every 4 years ($4 	imes 6	ext{ hours} = 24	ext{ hours} = 1	ext{ day}$) prevents the calendar from drifting out of sync with the solar seasons.

8. **Video Jump Cuts**:
   - Jump cuts remove silence and filler words ("um", "like"), keeping pacing fast, punchy, and engaging for viewers.
