## 🔑 Parent Answer Key (For Educator)

<details>
<summary>Click to expand Answer Key</summary>

1. **States of Matter**:
   - Solid: Molecules are tightly packed in fixed positions, vibrating in place; fixed shape and volume.
   - Liquid: Molecules are close together but slide smoothly past one another; takes the shape of its container, fixed volume.
   - Gas: Molecules have high energy, bouncing freely and rapidly with wide space between them; expands to fill any volume and shape.

2. **Melting & Freezing Point**:
   - Gaining thermal energy.
   - Freezing/Melting point: $32^\circ	ext{F}$ / $0^\circ	ext{C}$.

3. **Sound Waves**:
   - Sound is a mechanical compression (longitudinal) wave.
   - It cannot travel through space because space is a vacuum with no air molecules to collide and transfer sound vibrations.

4. **Speed of Light vs. Sound**:
   - Light is almost a million times faster ($pprox 300,000	ext{ km/s}$) than sound ($pprox 0.34	ext{ km/s}$).
   - The light from lightning reaches your eyes almost instantaneously, while the sound wave of thunder takes about 5 seconds per mile to reach your ears.

5. **Python Function**:
   - Function name: `celebrate`
   - Argument passed: `"Learner"`

6. **Hot Air Balloon Buoyancy**:
   - Heating air causes molecules to move faster and spread farther apart, making warm air less dense than surrounding cool air, causing it to float upward.

7. **Wave Diagram**:
   - Sine wave showing Crest at peak, Trough at bottom, and Wavelength horizontal distance crest-to-crest.

8. **Creator / Coding Lesson**:
   - Open reflection (patience in debugging, planning before filming, creative storytelling).
