### Questions

**1.** What is a **pulley**? Name one everyday place you see a pulley working.

**2.** Contrast a **fixed pulley** and a **movable pulley**:
- Does a single fixed pulley multiply your force, or does it just change the direction of your pull?
- If you use a double-pulley system with two rope segments supporting the load, how much force do you need to lift a $20\text{-pound}$ bucket?

**3.** What is a **wheel and axle**? Why is turning a large steering wheel or doorknob easier than trying to turn a bare metal rod with your fingers?

**4.** In Scratch, what block do you use to detect when your player sprite touches a collectible star or an enemy alien? (Fill in the blank):
`<touching [ _________________ v] ?>`

**5.** Look at this Scratch script for a player cat:
```scratch
when green flag clicked
forever
    if <touching [Spike Hazard v]?> then
        play sound [Ouch v]
        change [Lives v] by (-1)
        go to x: (-200) y: (0)
    end
end
```
What three things happen immediately when the cat touches the spike hazard?

**6.** Is a screwdriver an example of an inclined plane, a lever, or a wheel and axle? Explain how the handle and shaft work together.

**7.** **Diagram Challenge:** Draw a flagpole with a rope and a pulley at the top. Draw an arrow showing a person pulling DOWN on the rope, and an arrow showing the flag going UP.

**8.** What is B-roll in YouTube filming? Why does adding close-up B-roll shots make video editing look professional?

## 🔑 Parent Answer Key (For Educator)

<details>
<summary>Click to expand Answer Key</summary>

1. **Pulley**:
   - A simple machine consisting of a grooved wheel with a rope, belt, or chain wrapped around it.
   - Examples: Flagpole, sailboat rigging, window blind cords, gym weight machines, construction cranes.

2. **Pulley Mechanics**:
   - A single fixed pulley does NOT multiply force ($MA = 1$); it only changes direction (pulling down raises load up).
   - With 2 supporting rope segments, mechanical advantage is 2: lifting a $20	ext{-lb}$ bucket requires only $10	ext{ lbs}$ of effort force!

3. **Wheel and Axle**:
   - Two round objects locked together that rotate around the same center.
   - The large wheel has a bigger circumference; turning the outer rim gives you mechanical advantage, multiplying rotational force on the smaller axle.

4. **Scratch Sensing Block**:
   - `<touching [Star v] ?>` (from the Sensing category).

5. **Collision Execution**:
   - 1. Plays the "Ouch" sound effect.
   - 2. Subtracts 1 from the `Lives` variable.
   - 3. Teleports the cat back to the start position `(x: -200, y: 0)`.

6. **Screwdriver Machine Type**:
   - **Wheel and Axle**.
   - The wide handle is the wheel; the narrow metal shaft is the axle. Twisting the wide handle turns the shaft with amplified torque to drive the screw into wood.

7. **Flagpole Pulley Diagram**:
   - Pole with grooved wheel at top; rope loop; downward arrow at ground effort; upward arrow at flag load.

8. **B-Roll in Video**:
   - Secondary cutaway footage that shows the details of what is being discussed (e.g., close-up of fingers typing code or hands assembling a ramp).
   - Makes videos dynamic, covers up speech edits/cuts, and visually proves the narrator's points.
</details>
