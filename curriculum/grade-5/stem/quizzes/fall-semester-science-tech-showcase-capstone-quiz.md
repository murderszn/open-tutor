### Questions

**1.** Look back across your entire 5th Grade Fall Semester! What were the four main science and technology topics you explored?

**2.** Review Earth's Systems: How do the **Geosphere**, **Hydrosphere**, **Atmosphere**, and **Biosphere** work together to keep a forest healthy?

**3.** Simple Machines Master: Match each tool to its simple machine family:
- (a) A spiral staircase: _________________
- (b) A see-saw: _________________
- (c) A wheelchair ramp: _________________
- (d) A pizza cutter wheel: _________________
- (e) A doorstop wedge: _________________
- (f) A flagpole rope: _________________

**4.** Space Master:
- How long does Earth take to orbit the Sun?
- Why do we have day and night?
- Which planet is closest to the Sun?

**5.** Coding Showcase: Trace this Python code:
```python
missions_completed = 0
for day in ["Mon", "Wed", "Fri"]:
    missions_completed += 1
print("Total STEM Missions:", missions_completed)
```
What is the printed output?

**6.** In your Scratch game, what was your favorite feature you programmed (sound effects, jumping, scoring, or animations)?

**7.** **Diagram Challenge:** Draw your favorite science or engineering build from the whole semester! (Your terrarium jar, your simple machine, or your solar system model).

**8.** What is one STEM goal you want to tackle next semester? (Building a robot, coding more Python, or new science experiments)?
