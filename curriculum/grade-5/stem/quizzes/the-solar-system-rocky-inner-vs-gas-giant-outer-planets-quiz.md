### Questions

**1.** Name the eight planets of our solar system in order starting closest to the Sun! (Hint: *My Very Educated Mother Just Served Us Noodles*).

**2.** The four inner planets are called **terrestrial (rocky) planets**. List them. What are they mostly made of?

**3.** The four outer planets are known as the **Gas Giants** and **Ice Giants**. List them. What are they mostly made of?

**4.** What lies in the wide space between Mars and Jupiter separating the inner and outer planets?

**5.** Which planet is the largest in the solar system and has a giant storm called the Great Red Spot? Which planet has magnificent, bright rings made of ice and dust?

**6.** In Python, how do you ask a user for their name and save it into a variable? Complete the line:
`user_name = _________________("What is your name? ")`

**7.** **Diagram Challenge:** Sketch the Sun and the 8 planets in order. Make sure Jupiter is your biggest planet and Saturn has rings!

**8.** Why is Pluto no longer classified as a full planet? What category does it belong to today?

## 🔑 Parent Answer Key (For Educator)

<summary>Click to expand Answer Key</summary>

1. **Order of the Planets**:
   - Mercury, Venus, Earth, Mars, Jupiter, Saturn, Uranus, Neptune.

2. **Inner Rocky Planets**:
   - Mercury, Venus, Earth, Mars.
   - Made mostly of rock, metal, and dense solid crusts.

3. **Outer Giant Planets**:
   - Jupiter, Saturn, Uranus, Neptune.
   - Made mostly of thick gases (hydrogen and helium) and frozen ices (methane, ammonia, water).

4. **Asteroid Belt**:
   - The **Asteroid Belt**.

5. **Giant Planets**:
   - Largest with Great Red Spot: **Jupiter**.
   - Bright ring system: **Saturn**.

6. **Python `input()`**:
   - `user_name = input("What is your name? ")`

7. **Solar System Diagram**:
   - Sun $	o$ 4 small rocky planets $	o$ Asteroid Belt $	o$ 2 huge gas giants (Jupiter/Saturn) $	o$ 2 ice giants (Uranus/Neptune).

8. **Pluto Status**:
   - In 2006, the International Astronomical Union (IAU) redefined "planet": a celestial body must orbit the Sun, be spherical, and have "cleared the neighborhood around its orbit." Pluto shares its orbital zone with many Kuiper Belt icy objects.
   - It is classified as a **Dwarf Planet**.
