### Questions

**1.** Compare **Asteroids**, **Comets**, and **Meteors**:
- (a) What is an asteroid mostly made of?
- (b) What is a comet often called, and what creates its glowing tail?
- (c) What is a "shooting star" really?

**2.** Differentiate between a **meteoroid**, a **meteor**, and a **meteorite**:
- When it's floating out in space: _________________
- When it streaks and burns up in Earth's atmosphere: _________________
- When a fragment survives and lands on Earth's surface: _________________

**3.** Examine this Python `for` loop:
```python
for count in range(1, 6):
    print("Blastoff in:", count)
print("ROCKET LAUNCHED!")
```
List the exact numbers that will be printed before `"ROCKET LAUNCHED!"`.

**4.** What does a `while` loop do in Python? What dangerous bug occurs if the loop condition never becomes `False`?

**5.** Why do comet tails always point directly AWAY from the Sun, even when the comet is traveling away from the Sun?

**6.** Look at this Python countdown script:
```python
fuel = 3
while fuel > 0:
    print("Thrusters firing! Fuel remaining:", fuel)
    fuel = fuel - 1
print("Out of fuel!")
```
How many times will `"Thrusters firing!"` be printed?

**7.** **Diagram Challenge:** Draw a comet orbiting near the Sun. Draw the solid nucleus and draw the long gas and dust tail pointing directly away from the Sun.

**8.** What is the name of the giant meteor crater in Arizona, and what does it teach scientists about Earth's history?

## 🔑 Parent Answer Key (For Educator)

<details>
<summary>Click to expand Answer Key</summary>

1. **Space Objects**:
   - (a) Asteroids: Giant chunks of rock and metal left over from the formation of the solar system.
   - (b) Comets: "Dirty snowballs" made of ice, dust, and rock. As they near the Sun, solar heat vaporizes ice into a glowing gas and dust tail.
   - (c) A streak of light caused by a small space rock burning up from friction as it enters Earth's atmosphere.

2. **Meteoroid Stages**:
   - Space: Meteoroid
   - Atmosphere: Meteor
   - Ground: Meteorite

3. **`range(1, 6)` Output**:
   - `1`, `2`, `3`, `4`, `5`. (Python's `range(start, stop)` stops 1 before the stop value).

4. **While Loops & Infinite Loops**:
   - A `while` loop repeats a code block as long as its condition remains `True`.
   - If the condition never turns `False`, it creates an **infinite loop**, freezing the program!

5. **Comet Tail Direction**:
   - The solar wind (stream of charged particles) and sunlight radiation pressure push gas and dust particles straight away from the Sun.

6. **Countdown Output**:
   - It will print 3 times (when `fuel` is 3, 2, and 1). When `fuel` becomes 0, the loop exits.

7. **Comet Diagram**:
   - Sun in center; comet with icy nucleus; dust/ion tail radiating straight away from Sun.

8. **Meteor Crater (Barringer Crater)**:
   - Formed $pprox 50,000$ years ago by an iron-metallic meteorite. Proves catastrophic space impacts shaped Earth's surface and planetary history.
