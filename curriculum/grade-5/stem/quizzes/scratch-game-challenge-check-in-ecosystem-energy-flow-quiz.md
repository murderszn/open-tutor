### Questions

**1.** Review your Scratch Arcade Game:
- What is the title of your game?
- How does the player score points?
- What hazard makes the player lose?

**2.** In Scratch, what block do you use to make a clone of a sprite (like multiple falling space rocks)?
- `create clone of [ _________________ v]`
- What block starts the code for the clone? `when I start as a _________________`

**3.** What is an **ecosystem**? Give an example of a small ecosystem you might find in your backyard.

**4.** Explain the roles in a biological food chain:
- **Producers**: Make their own food using sunlight. (Give an example).
- **Consumers**: Eat other organisms for energy. (Give an example).
- **Decomposers**: Break down dead matter and return nutrients to the soil. (Give an example).

**5.** Look at this food chain:
$$\text{Sun} \to \text{Grass} \to \text{Grasshopper} \to \text{Frog} \to \text{Hawk}$$
- Which organism is the producer?
- What would happen to the hawks if all the grasshoppers disappeared?

**6.** In Python, how do you generate a random number between 1 and 10 for a guessing game? Write the two lines of code (including the `import`).

**7.** **Diagram Challenge:** Sketch an **Energy Pyramid** with four levels (Producers at the bottom, Primary Consumers, Secondary Consumers, Apex Predator at the top).

**8.** What makes a YouTube video thumbnail clickable and exciting for viewers? (List two design tricks).

## 🔑 Parent Answer Key (For Educator)

1. **Scratch Game Reflection**:
   - Learner should accurately describe his custom arcade project mechanics.

2. **Scratch Cloning Blocks**:
   - `create clone of [myself v]`
   - `when I start as a clone`

3. **Ecosystem**:
   - A biological community of interacting organisms (living) and their physical environment (non-living).
   - Backyard example: Under an old fallen log (moss, pill bugs, earthworms, moist soil).

4. **Food Chain Roles**:
   - Producer: Green plants, trees, algae (use photosynthesis).
   - Consumer: Herbivores, carnivores, omnivores (e.g., rabbits, wolves, humans).
   - Decomposer: Fungi (mushrooms), bacteria, earthworms.

5. **Food Chain Analysis**:
   - Producer: Grass.
   - If grasshoppers disappear, frogs lose food and starve; hawks have fewer frogs to eat, disrupting the population.

6. **Python Random Number**:
   ```python
   import random
   secret_number = random.randint(1, 10)
   ```

7. **Energy Pyramid Diagram**:
   - Broad base (Producers, $100\%$ energy) $	o$ Primary Consumers ($10\%$) $	o$ Secondary Consumers ($1\%$) $	o$ Apex Predator ($0.1\%$).

8. **Thumbnail Design Tricks**:
   - High contrast bright colors, expressive facial reaction, bold large 2–3 word text, clean outline around subject.
