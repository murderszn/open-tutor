### Questions

**1.** What is the key difference between **weather** and **climate**?
- (a) "It is 62°F and raining in Dallas today." $\to$ (Weather or Climate?)
- (b) "Texas has hot, dry summers and mild winters." $\to$ (Weather or Climate?)

**2.** Identify the three main types of clouds from their descriptions:
- (a) Puffy, white, cotton-ball clouds seen on fair sunny days: _________________
- (b) Low, flat, gray sheet-like clouds that often bring steady light rain: _________________
- (c) High, thin, feathery wisps made entirely of ice crystals: _________________

**3.** What is an **inclined plane**? Give two examples of inclined planes in everyday life.

**4.** Does an inclined plane reduce the actual *work* you do, or does it reduce the *effort force*? Explain the trade-off.

**5.** If you have to push a heavy $100\text{-pound}$ wheelbarrow into the bed of a pickup truck:
- Would you rather use a short $3\text{-foot}$ ramp or a long $10\text{-foot}$ ramp?
- Why? Which ramp requires less pushing force?

**6.** Look at this Scratch movement snippet:
```scratch
when green flag clicked
point in direction (90)
forever
    move (5) steps
    if on edge, bounce
end
```
What does `if on edge, bounce` prevent your sprite from doing?

**7.** **Diagram Challenge:** Draw an inclined plane with a box resting on it. Draw an arrow showing the direction of the pushing effort, and an arrow pointing down showing gravity.

**8.** What weather instrument is used to measure air temperature? What instrument measures wind speed?

## 🔑 Parent Answer Key (For Educator)

<summary>Click to expand Answer Key</summary>

1. **Weather vs. Climate**:
   - Weather is the short-term state of the atmosphere right now (hours to days). Climate is the long-term average pattern of weather over 30+ years.
   - (a) Weather
   - (b) Climate

2. **Cloud Types**:
   - (a) Cumulus
   - (b) Stratus
   - (c) Cirrus

3. **Inclined Plane**:
   - A flat supporting surface tilted at an angle, with one end higher than the other, used to raise or lower loads.
   - Examples: Wheelchair ramp, playground slide, loading ramp on a moving van.

4. **Effort vs. Work Trade-off**:
   - It reduces the **effort force** needed.
   - The total work ($W = F 	imes d$) stays the same (or slightly more due to friction), but you exert less force over a longer distance.

5. **Ramp Choice**:
   - The long $10	ext{-foot}$ ramp!
   - A gentler slope has a higher mechanical advantage, spreading the lift over a greater distance so you only have to push with a fraction of the force.

6. **Scratch Edge Bounce**:
   - It keeps the sprite from walking off the visible screen and getting permanently stuck beyond the stage boundary.

7. **Ramp Diagram**:
   - Sketch of triangle ramp with box; effort arrow points up along slope; gravity arrow points straight down toward Earth's center.

8. **Weather Instruments**:
   - Temperature: Thermometer.
   - Wind speed: Anemometer.
