# Independent Grade Curriculum

Four grade tracks have expanded legacy draft assignment and quiz libraries: **Grade 4, Grade 5, Grade 7, and Grade 8**. The collections are not complete, validated lesson sequences; they still need instructional, answer-key, prerequisite, and grade-placement review. The developing **K, 1, 2, 3, and 6** folders contain limited audit or starter material, not full units or courses. Select one grade folder for one learner at a time.

**Do not refer a learner to another grade's folder. Do not combine assignments across grades, ask learners to work together, compare their results, or refer to siblings/peers.** These are reusable teaching materials, not learner profiles or records.

The [expansion plan and structured GitHub backlog](../docs/curriculum-expansion/README.md) cover Kindergarten through Grade 12 in math, science, language arts, and social studies. Planned coverage is not finished curriculum. The [worker prompt](../teachers/ai-assistants/curriculum-worker.md) builds one substantive section per invocation.

Each listed starter is one supplemental lesson only. It is not an eight-unit course, and its presence does not establish full subject coverage. Grades 9–12 are planned additions and do not yet have curriculum folders. New core science content will use `science/`; existing `stem/` material remains available and will be reviewed for reuse. Each developing track must publish its status truthfully until its lessons, resources, assessments, keys, and review are complete.

## Using a Track

1. Choose exactly one grade folder.
2. Select a subject, then a blank assignment or assessment inside that same folder.
3. Preview external resources and check prerequisite knowledge, accessibility, safety, and readiness.
4. Keep completed work, names, grades, schedules, and feedback in private storage—not this public repository.
5. Treat these materials as adaptable drafts, not a formal accreditation or standards guarantee. Optional Christian enrichment is explicitly separate from secular instruction.

Some retained unit titles may be ambitious or unevenly aligned with grade-level standards. Review individual materials before assigning.

See [Teaching Library Usage](../docs/teaching-library-usage.md), [Assignments](../assignments/README.md), and [Resources](../resources/README.md).

Never cross-link a learner's assignment to a different grade track.
