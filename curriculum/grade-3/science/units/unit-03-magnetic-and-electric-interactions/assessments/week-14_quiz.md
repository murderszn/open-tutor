# Formative Quiz — Unit 03 (Week 14)

**For the learner, adult-supervised.** 10 items. The adult reviews
the quiz the same day. Oral, pointing, and drawing answers are
accepted. Answers are in the separate [answer key](../answer-key.md).

1. Sort these four objects by testing them with a magnet: steel paper
   clip, plastic spoon, iron nail, wooden block. Which are magnetic
   and which are nonmagnetic?
2. A magnet is held near a paper clip with a thin piece of cardboard
   between them. The clip moves toward the magnet. Did the magnetic
   force need contact? What kind of force is it?
3. Two magnets face each other north pole to south pole. Do they
   attract or repel?
4. Two magnets face each other south pole to south pole. Do they
   attract or repel?
5. Two magnets are attracting. The adult flips one magnet around.
   What happens now, and why?
6. A paper clip starts moving when a magnet is 4 cm away. The magnet
   is moved to 12 cm away. Will the pull be stronger or weaker, and
   will the clip move? Explain with the distance rule.
7. **Practice data (fictional).** One magnet holds a chain of 6 paper
   clips. Two magnets stacked hold 11. How many more clips do two
   magnets hold? Show the subtraction.
8. A balloon is rubbed on a sweater and held near tiny paper bits.
   The bits jump up to the balloon. What force is this, and did it
   need contact?
9. A balloon rubbed 10 times lifts a few paper bits. The same balloon
   rubbed 20 times lifts more bits. What changed, and what happened?
10. For a magnet latch on a box lid, should the facing poles attract
    or repel? Why would the other choice fail as a latch?
