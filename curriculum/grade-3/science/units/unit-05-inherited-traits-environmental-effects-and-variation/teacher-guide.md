# Teacher Guide — Unit 05: Inherited Traits, Environmental Effects, and Variation

**For the guiding adult only.** This guide stays separate from
learner pages. Solutions for every item are in
[answer key](answer-key.md).

## What "ready to move on" looks like

The learner is ready for Unit 06 when they can, without prompting:
name a trait and say whether it is inherited, environment-influenced,
or both, with a reason; read a range, a most-common value, and a
fair-share height from a small table; trace one offspring's traits to
the right parents in a table; and make a claim about the demo tray
with the two ranges as evidence, naming one limitation. Quiz
readiness is 7 of 10; assessment readiness is 10 of 12 (item 9 needs
the full claim with evidence); project readiness is 10 of 12 points.

## Before the unit begins — demo tray setup (adult-only)

Plant the demo tray at the start of Week 19, before Session 7. Use
the same kind of bean seeds, the same potting soil, and the same pot
size as the main tray from Unit 04. Put it in the same light as the
main tray. Label it "DEMO — HALF WATER" with the planting date. From
then on, water the main tray with one full cup and the demo tray with
exactly half a cup, on the same days. Change nothing else. If the
demo tray was not planted, say so in the journal and run the project
as a data-analysis investigation using the fictional Day-28 table in
Lesson 04 — the rubric still applies, and the limitation line must
say the numbers are practice data.

## Session-by-session notes

- **Session 1 (Lesson 01):** Do the picture sort before naming the
  rule. Learners who say "exactly like the mother" need the middle
  and right puppies most — the picture proves no puppy matches on
  every trait. Keep the language at "traits passed from parents" — do
  not introduce genes or DNA.
- **Session 3 (Lesson 03):** Trace one kitten fully before asking the
  learner to trace the others. The "both from the mother" kitten (C)
  is the trap: looking like one parent does not mean the other parent
  gave nothing.
- **Session 5 (Lesson 02):** Read rulers from zero; a common error is
  reading from 1 cm or from the table. Measure the real plants first;
  the Practice Tray numbers are fictional — say so out loud, and
  treat the learner's own measurements as the real evidence. The
  fair-share division (70 ÷ 5 = 14) may need counters: 70 counters
  shared into 5 equal piles of 14.
- **Session 7 (project plant):** The adult plants the demo tray or
  checks the planting. Approve the Day 1 entries before moving on —
  date, 0 cm, 0 leaves, labeled drawing.
- **Session 8 (review game):** Trait bingo — the adult calls a trait,
  the learner says "inherited," "environment," or "both" with a
  reason. "Same or different?" rounds use the dog picture and the cat
  table.
- **Session 9 (Lesson 04):** Compare the tallest demo plant with the
  shortest main-tray plant first — the 3 cm gap is the cleanest
  evidence in the unit. If the learner's real demo tray shows a
  smaller difference than the fictional table, accept the observed
  ordering and do not force the textbook ordering.
- **Session 11 (Lesson 05):** The scar card and the learned-trick
  question are the two hardest sorts; do them as guided practice, not
  as a test. Accept "both, with a reason" for height as the strongest
  answer.
- **Session 12 (project work):** Check the bar graph before the
  poster — every bar labeled with its number, color never the only
  cue (use patterns or labels too).
- **Session 14 (project showcase):** The talk is two minutes: question,
  method, numbers, claim, limitation. Ask one follow-up ("How do you
  know?" or "What would change your mind?") and judge the answer on
  evidence, not confidence.
- **Session 15 (re-check stations):** Four stations — dog-picture
  sort, cat-table trace, Practice Tray range, two-tray claim. The
  extension asks the learner to plan (not run) a light test: one
  variable (light), measured in centimeters and leaf counts.

## Common misconceptions and reteaching

- **"Offspring are exact copies of their parents."** Reteach with the
  dog picture: no puppy matches the mother on every trait, and the
  middle puppy's fur color matches neither the mother nor (visibly)
  anyone in the picture.
- **"Siblings should look the same."** Reteach with the cat table:
  same parents, three different mixes. The card-shuffle image helps:
  same deck, different hands.
- **"The short plants are a different kind."** Reteach with the
  Two-Tray Test: same seeds, same kind, only the water differed.
- **"Whatever happens to a parent is passed to its babies."**
  Reteach with the overweight dog and the dark-corner plant: the
  parent's weight and shortness came from the environment and do not
  travel to the offspring; the offspring's traits depend on their own
  parents and their own environment.
- **"A trait belongs in exactly one pile."** Reteach with height:
  the kind is inherited, the amount of growth depends on water and
  light — "both, with a reason" is the strongest answer.
- **"The fair-share height is a real plant's height."** Reteach with
  counters: it is a computed summary (70 shared into 5 piles of 14),
  and it would still be 14 cm even if no plant measured 14 cm.
- **"A prediction is a measurement."** Reteach with Practice Bean 1:
  18 cm on Day 28 is a guess from the 5-cm-a-week pattern; only the
  ruler tells you the real height.

## Reteach paths

- **Trait/inherit language shaky:** replay Lesson 01 with the dog
  picture and the bean plants; use the sentence frames ("The puppy
  inherited ___ from its mother because ___").
- **Range/fair-share shaky:** rework Practice Set B with counters and
  the bar chart; measure three plants only and compute the range
  before attempting the fair-share division.
- **Claim-with-evidence shaky:** use the frame from Lesson 04
  ("Less water made the plants ___ because ___") with the two-tray
  table in front of the learner; require the numbers in the sentence.
- **Sorting shaky:** sort three cards only (striped fur, dry-summer
  height, scar), with the two sorting questions asked aloud for each
  card.

## Safety reminders

Adult supervises every investigation and does or guides all watering.
Hands washed after soil work. Plants observed gently, never pulled up
or broken. Spilled water wiped at once. Nothing tasted or put in the
mouth. The demo tray uses the same bean seeds and potting soil as
Unit 04 — no new chemicals, fertilizers, or unknown materials.
