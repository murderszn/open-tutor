# Formative Quiz — Unit 05 (Week 22)

**For the learner, adult-supervised.** 10 items. The adult reviews
the quiz the same day. Oral, pointing, and drawing answers are
accepted. Answers are in the separate [answer key](../answer-key.md).

1. What is a **trait**? Give one example from an animal.
2. What does **inherit** mean?
3. Look at the [generated image](../assets/dog-family-inherited-traits.png)
   of the dog family (or its text-only description in
   [Lesson 01](../lessons/lesson-01-offspring-look-like-their-parents.md)).
   Which puppy looks most like the mother? Name the traits it shares
   with her.
4. In the same picture, which puppy has pointy ears? Does the mother
   have pointy ears?
5. **Practice data (fictional).** Five bean plants measured 12, 15,
   14, 17, and 12 cm tall. (a) What is the tallest height? (b) What
   is the shortest height? (c) How many centimeters apart are they?
   Show the subtraction.
6. **Practice data (fictional).** In the same five plants, which
   height shows up most often?
7. **Practice data (fictional).** At Day 28 the main tray (full
   water) ranged from 12 to 17 cm tall, and the demo tray (half
   water) ranged from 6 to 9 cm tall. Which tray grew taller? What
   was different about how the two trays were cared for?
8. A pet dog is given too much food and very little exercise, and it
   becomes overweight. Is the dog's weight an inherited trait or an
   environment effect? Explain in one sentence.
9. A bean plant grew short and pale in a dark corner. Its seeds are
   planted in good light and water. Will the new plants be short and
   pale too? Why or why not?
10. **Error-analysis.** A friend says, "A puppy's scar will be passed
    on to its puppies." Is this true or false? Explain in one
    sentence.
