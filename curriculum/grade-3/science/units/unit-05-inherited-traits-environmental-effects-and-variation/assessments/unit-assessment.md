# Unit Assessment — Unit 05: Inherited Traits, Environmental Effects, and Variation

**For the learner, adult-supervised.** 12 items. The adult reviews
the assessment the same day. Oral, pointing, and drawing answers are
accepted. Answers are in the separate [answer key](../answer-key.md).
Items marked **fictional** use practice data, not your plants.

1. What is a **trait**? Give two examples from animals and two
   examples from plants.
2. Explain what **inherit** means, in your own words. Use the word in
   a sentence about a plant or an animal.
3. Look at the [generated image](../assets/dog-family-inherited-traits.png)
   of the dog family (or its text-only description in
   [Lesson 01](../lessons/lesson-01-offspring-look-like-their-parents.md)).
   The mother dog has brown fur, floppy ears, and a white chest
   patch. For each puppy, write which of the mother's traits it
   shares and which it does not share:
   - Puppy on the left
   - Puppy in the middle
   - Puppy on the right
4. **Cat family (fictional).** Mother cat: orange fur, green eyes.
   Father cat: gray fur, blue eyes. A kitten has gray fur and blue
   eyes. Which parent gave the kitten its fur color, and which parent
   gave it its eye color?
5. Two kittens from the same parents look different: one has orange
   fur, the other has gray fur. Explain how siblings can look
   different even though they share the same parents.
6. **Practice data (fictional).** Five bean plants measured 12, 15,
   14, 17, and 12 cm tall. (a) Add all five heights. (b) Share the
   total equally among the 5 plants: what is the fair-share height?
   Show both steps. (c) What is the range of the heights — shortest
   to tallest — and how many centimeters apart are they?
7. **Practice data (fictional).** The same five plants had 6, 8, 7,
   9, and 6 leaves. Which leaf count appears most often? How many
   plants have it?
8. **Practice data (fictional).** Practice Bean 1 measured: Day 7 —
   3 cm, Day 14 — 8 cm, Day 21 — 13 cm. (a) How many centimeters did
   it grow each week? (b) Predict its Day 28 height. (c) Explain why
   a prediction is not a measurement.
9. **Practice data (fictional).** At Day 28 the main tray (full
   water) ranged from 12 to 17 cm tall, and the demo tray (half
   water) ranged from 6 to 9 cm tall. (a) What is the tallest demo
   plant? (b) Is it taller or shorter than the shortest main-tray
   plant? By how many centimeters? (c) What claim can you make, and
   what is your evidence?
10. A farmer's bean plants grow tall in good soil and water. She
    plants their seeds in the same good soil and water. Will the new
    plants probably be tall? What if she plants the seeds in dry
    soil instead? Explain both answers.
11. **Sort.** Write "Inherited," "Environment," or "Both" for each
    trait, with one reason each: (a) a cat's striped fur; (b) a
    plant's height after a dry summer; (c) a dog's floppy ears; (d) a
    scar on a horse's leg; (e) a bean plant's flower color; (f) a pet
    dog's weight after too much food.
12. **Explain your reasoning.** A friend says, "Plants and animals
    get all their traits from their parents." Use one example from
    this unit to explain why that is not the whole story.
