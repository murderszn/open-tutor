# Resource Pack — Unit 05: Inherited Traits, Environmental Effects, and Variation

**Goal (one sentence):** Give the guiding adult short, grade-3-appropriate
videos and references that reinforce inherited traits, variation in a
group, sibling differences, and how water, light, food, and exercise
can change a trait.
**Age fit:** grade 3 (ages 8–9); the adult previews every video and
co-views with the learner.

## Free, no-account coverage

Every core objective is covered without an account and without
payment: the Oak lesson video and both labeled channel searches play
free with no account stated on their pages; the NSTA display, the CDE
listing, the AKC lesson, and the Generation Genius article are free
to read; the Generation Genius video is the only item needing a free
account, and its companion article covers the same content. If video
is unavailable, the lessons, trait cards, growth log, and generated
image carry the same objectives offline. Videos supplement teaching;
they never replace the lessons, the planting, or the adult check.
