# Project — Unit 05: The Two-Tray Test

**Question:** Does less water change how tall bean plants grow?

## What you will do

You will grow two trays of bean plants side by side. Both trays get
the same seeds, the same soil, the same light, and the same care —
except one thing: the demo tray gets **half the water**. You will
measure both trays, compare them, and make a claim with evidence.

This is a fair test (Unit 01 routine): one variable changed, everything
else the same, repeated measurements, and a claim with evidence and a
limitation.

## Materials

- The main tray of bean plants and growth log from Unit 04
- A second small tray, pots or cups with drainage holes, potting soil,
  bean seeds of the same kind, plant labels, watering cup with a
  marked half-measure
- Ruler with centimeters, science journal, pencil, crayons, bar-graph
  paper or a blank table
- The [generated image](assets/dog-family-inherited-traits.png) is
  not used in this project; the project evidence is your own
  measurements

## Steps

1. **Plant the demo tray (Week 19, Session 7; adult-led).** Fill the
   pots with the same soil, plant 2–3 seeds per pot at the same depth
   as the main tray, water once until damp, and label the tray "DEMO
   — HALF WATER" with the planting date. Put it in the same light as
   the main tray. Make a Day 1 entry for the demo tray: date, height
   0 cm, leaves 0, labeled drawing — the same entry the main tray got
   in Unit 04.
2. **Water by the rule (Weeks 19–21).** The main tray gets one full
   watering cup each time the adult waters. The demo tray gets exactly
   half a cup. Same light, same soil, same care otherwise. The adult
   does the watering, or guides the learner's hand. Record every
   watering day in the journal.
3. **Measure both trays (Week 20 and Week 21).** At Day 7 and Day 14,
   measure every plant in both trays with the Unit 04 ritual: ruler
   zero at the soil line, read to the nearest centimeter at the
   tallest tip, count every leaf out loud, draw and label, record
   date, height, and leaves at once. Compare each demo measurement
   with the main tray's log entries from when those plants were the
   same age (Day 7 and Day 14).
4. **Build the data table (Week 21, Session 12).** One table, both
   trays, Day 7 and Day 14: plant, tray, height, leaves. Circle the
   tallest and shortest plant in each tray at Day 14.
5. **Draw the bar graph (Week 21, Session 12).** One bar per plant at
   Day 14, main-tray bars in one color and demo-tray bars in another
   (or with different patterns — color is never the only cue). Title
   it, label every bar with its number, and write one sentence under
   it: "At Day 14, the main tray ranged from ___ to ___ cm and the
   demo tray ranged from ___ to ___ cm."
6. **Make the claim poster (Week 21, Session 12).** On one page: the
   question, the claim ("Less water made the plants shorter" — or
   whatever the real data shows), the evidence (the two ranges and the
   tallest-vs.-shortest comparison), and one limitation (for example,
   "one tray each" or "only two weeks"). Draw the two trays.
7. **Give the talk (Week 22, Session 14).** Present the poster to the
   adult in about two minutes: the question, what you did, what the
   numbers showed, your claim, and your limitation. Answer one
   follow-up question with evidence.

## If the demo tray was not planted

If the demo tray was not planted at the start of Week 19, the adult
says so in the journal and the project runs as a data-analysis
investigation instead: use the fictional Day-28 two-tray table from
[Lesson 04](lessons/lesson-04-the-environment-changes-traits.md)
(main tray 12–17 cm, demo tray 6–9 cm, labeled fictional practice
data) to build the table, bar graph, claim poster, and talk. The
rubric still applies; the limitation line must say the numbers are
practice data, not the learner's own measurements.

## Deliverables

1. Two-tray data table (Day 7 and Day 14, both trays, heights and
   leaf counts).
2. Day-14 bar graph with labeled bars and a one-sentence reading.
3. Claim poster: question, claim, evidence, limitation, drawings.
4. A two-minute talk with one answered follow-up question.

## Rubric

| Criterion | Strong (3) | Good (2) | Starting (1) |
|---|---|---|---|
| Measuring | Every plant measured in both trays at Day 7 and Day 14; ruler zero at the soil line; dates, heights, leaves, and drawings all recorded | Most plants measured at both checks; one or two entries missing a date or a leaf count | Only one tray or one check measured; heights read from the table instead of the soil line |
| Data table and bar graph | Table complete for both trays and both dates; tallest and shortest circled; bar graph titled, every bar labeled with its number, ranges written correctly | Table mostly complete; graph drawn but one or two bars unlabeled or one range wrong | Table or graph missing; bars unlabeled; ranges not stated |
| Claim with evidence | Claim names the water difference and cites the two ranges and the tallest-vs.-shortest comparison; numbers are correct | Claim names the water difference and cites one correct comparison | Claim given without evidence, or evidence does not match the numbers |
| Talk and limitation | Two-minute talk covers question, method, numbers, claim, and limitation; follow-up answered with evidence | Talk covers most parts; limitation named but vague; follow-up answered partly | Talk is a list of numbers with no claim; no limitation named |

**Scoring:** 12 points total (4 criteria × 3). **10–12:** ready to
move on. **7–9:** review the claim frame and re-check one tray's
measurements, then re-present. **Below 7:** re-teach the fair-test
routine and the measurement ritual, then rebuild the table and graph
with the adult before re-presenting.

## Safety

Adult supervises every step and does or guides all watering. Hands
washed after soil work. Plants are observed gently, never pulled up or
broken. Spilled water is wiped at once. Nothing is tasted or put in
the mouth.
