# Week-08 Quiz — Balanced and Unbalanced Forces and Motion

**Time:** 15–20 minutes · **Scoring:** 1 point per item (10 points). 7+
shows readiness to continue; review missed items the same day with the
[answer key](../answer-key.md#week-08-quiz). The adult may read items aloud
and accept drawn, pointed, or dictated answers.

1. Circle **push** or **pull** for each: (a) opening a door, (b) pulling a
   wagon, (c) kicking a ball.
2. You push a toy car toward the window. Draw an arrow showing the
   **direction** of your push.
3. Which way does gravity pull objects — up, down, left, or right?
4. Two teams pull a rope equally hard in opposite directions, and the rope
   does not move. Are the forces **balanced** or **unbalanced**?
5. Look at the unit's tug-of-war picture
   ([assets/tug-of-war-balanced-unbalanced.png](../assets/tug-of-war-balanced-unbalanced.png)).
   Which scene — left or right — shows **unbalanced** forces? **Text-only
   alternative:** the left scene is a 3-vs-3 tie with the flag on the
   center line and nothing moving; the right scene has four kids pulling
   left and two pulling right, and the rope slides toward the left.
6. A ball sits still. You push it from the left side only. What **motion
   change** happens — start, stop, speed up, slow down, or change
   direction?
7. A rolling ball hits a wall and rolls back the way it came. Name the
   motion change and tell whether the forces were balanced or unbalanced.
8. A swing moves forward, back, forward, back, forward. What comes next?
   Write your prediction.
9. A car rolls on a bare board and on a towel. On which surface does it
   roll **farther**, and which surface has **more friction**?
10. **Fair-test check:** A student wants to test whether push *strength*
    changes how far a car rolls. She uses a gentle push on the towel and
    a firm push on the bare board. Is this a fair test? Explain in one
    sentence.
