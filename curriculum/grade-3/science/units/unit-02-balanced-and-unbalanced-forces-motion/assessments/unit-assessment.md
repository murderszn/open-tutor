# Unit Assessment — Balanced and Unbalanced Forces and Motion

**Time:** 30–40 minutes, adult-read allowed · **Total:** 24 points ·
**Scoring guide:** 20–24 ready for Unit 03; 15–19 review one target area
(see [teacher guide](../teacher-guide.md)); below 15 reteach with the
guide's follow-ups before Unit 03. All solutions in
[answer-key.md](../answer-key.md#unit-assessment).

## Part A — Forces as pushes and pulls (6 points)

1. (2 pts) Label each force as a **push** or a **pull**: (a) dragging a
   chair across the room, (b) pressing an elevator button.
2. (2 pts) Draw two arrows on the ball below (describe in words if
   drawing is hard): one arrow for a push to the **right** and one arrow
   for gravity's pull. Label each arrow.
3. (2 pts) A gentle push sends a car 30 cm; a firm push sends the same car
   90 cm in the same direction. What stayed the same, and what changed?
   What does this tell you about force strength?

## Part B — Balanced vs. unbalanced (8 points)

4. (2 pts) Define **balanced forces** in your own words. Your definition
   must include the ideas of *equal* and *opposite*.
5. (2 pts) A book lies still on a desk. (a) Name the force pulling it
   down. (b) Are the forces on the book balanced or unbalanced? How do
   you know?
6. (2 pts) In a tug-of-war, the left team has 4 pullers and the right team
   has 2 pullers. The rope slides left. (a) Balanced or unbalanced?
   (b) Toward which side does the rope move, and why?
7. (2 pts) **Error analysis:** "The box did not move, so no forces were
   pushing on it." Explain the error.

## Part C — Motion changes and patterns (6 points)

8. (2 pts) A still ball is kicked and rolls away. A rolling ball is caught
   and held. Name the **motion change** in each case, choosing from:
   start, stop, speed up, slow down, change direction.
9. (2 pts) A marble rolls in a bowl: left, right, left, right. (a) What
   is the pattern? (b) Predict the next direction and give your evidence.
10. (2 pts) **Explain your reasoning:** Your friend says predicting is
    just guessing. Using the marble example, explain the difference.

## Part D — Friction and investigation (4 points)

11. (2 pts) A car rolls farther on a smooth board than on a rough towel.
    (a) Which surface has more friction? (b) Which way does friction
    point compared to the car's motion?
12. (2 pts) **Investigation plan:** You want to test whether the *number*
    of people pulling a wagon changes how fast it starts moving. Name the
    **one variable** you will change and **two things** you will keep the
    same.
