# Resource Pack — Unit 02: Balanced and Unbalanced Forces and Motion

**Goal (one sentence):** Give the guiding adult short, grade-3-appropriate
videos, a hands-on simulation, and references that reinforce pushes/pulls,
balanced vs. unbalanced forces, motion patterns, and friction.
**Age fit:** grade 3 (ages 8–9); the adult previews every video and
co-views with the learner.

## Videos and interactives (verified or labeled search links)

1. **PhET — "Forces and Motion: Basics" simulation** —
   https://phet.colorado.edu/en/simulations/forces-and-motion-basics
   *Why good:* the learner applies pushes of different sizes to objects and
   watches the motion change — a screen-based, hazard-free way to test the
   "stronger force, bigger change" idea from Lessons 01–03. Runs in the
   browser; free, no account. Best used with the adult narrating force
   direction and strength. *Listed in the repo resource map; re-verified
   2026-10-17.*
2. **Oak National Academy — "Pushes and pulls" lesson video** —
   https://www.thenational.academy/pupils/lessons/pushes-and-pulls/video
   *Why good:* a full classroom lesson on pushes and pulls with checks for
   understanding (matching force effects to pictures; the "Lucas vs. Sofia"
   twist discussion), matching Lesson 01's vocabulary. UK curriculum but
   the force language transfers directly. *Found via search 2026-10-17;
   adult previews before showing; free, no account.*
3. **SciShow Kids — preferred-channel search** (search link, not a single
   verified episode):
   https://www.youtube.com/results?search_query=SciShow+Kids+push+pull+force+motion
   *Why good:* the channel describes its episodes as structured around
   NGSS and ideal for grades 1–3 (verified from the channel description,
   2026-10-10, Unit 01). The adult picks one short episode on pushes,
   pulls, or motion. *Labeled search — adult previews.*
4. **Crash Course Kids — preferred-channel search** (search link, not a
   single verified episode):
   https://www.youtube.com/results?search_query=Crash+Course+Kids+force+motion+gravity
   *Why good:* hosted by Sabrina Cruz for upper-elementary; the channel's
   gravity/force episodes name gravity as a pull and mass/strength ideas
   in kid language (episode transcripts reviewed via Nerdfighteria wiki,
   2026-10-17). The adult selects the episode matching the week's lesson.
   *Labeled search — adult previews.*
5. **NASA Space Place** — https://spaceplace.nasa.gov/
   *Why good:* NASA's kids' site with short articles and games; useful for
   the gravity thread (gravity as the pull that holds us on Earth) and for
   extension reading. *Listed in the repo resource map; free, no account.*
