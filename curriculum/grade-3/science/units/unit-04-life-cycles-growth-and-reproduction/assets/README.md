# Assets — Unit 04

## bean-growth-stages.png

- **File:** `bean-growth-stages.png` (PNG, 2736 × 912).
- **Prompt:** "A simple educational illustration for grade 3 science
  showing one bean plant growing in four stages from left to right
  in a single row against a plain light background: first a bean
  seed in soil, second a small sprout breaking through the soil
  with a tiny root, third a young seedling with two leaves and a
  stem, fourth a taller adult bean plant with several leaves and a
  flower. Flat, clear, child-friendly illustration style, no text,
  no labels, no words anywhere in the image."
- **Generation tool/model:** Muse built-in image generation
  (media.generate_image), 2026-10-30. The image was inspected before
  linking: four stages read left to right, no text, no garbled
  labels.
- **Learning purpose:** used in Lesson 02's image activity — the
  learner orders the four stages, identifies the seedling and the
  flowering adult, and draws the missing fifth step (new seeds with
  an arrow back to the first panel).
- **Factual review:** stage order is botanically correct for a
  flowering bean plant (seed, sprout, seedling, flowering adult).
  One stylization to name to the learner: the roots are drawn below
  the pots so they stay visible; in real soil the roots are inside
  the soil under the plant, not hanging below the pot.
- **Caption:** "One bean plant, four stages: seed, sprout, seedling,
  flowering adult. What comes next?"
- **Alt text:** "Four potted bean plants in a row showing growth:
  a bean seed in soil; a tiny sprout with two small leaves; a young
  plant with a stem and two larger leaves; a tall plant with many
  leaves and one purple flower."
- **Text-only alternative:** Panel 1: a bean seed in a pot of soil.
  Panel 2: a tiny sprout with two small leaves above the soil.
  Panel 3: a young plant with a stem and two larger leaves. Panel 4:
  a tall plant with many leaves and one purple flower at the top.
  The Lesson 02 activity questions are answerable from these four
  descriptions alone.
- **Known limitations:** AI illustration, not a photograph of a real
  plant; it shows idealized stages, not the learner's own beans,
  and it must not be presented as observed data. The learner's own
  growth log is the unit's real data.
- **Use in the unit:** embedded in Lesson 02 and referenced in the
  unit README pacing (Session 3) and Practice set A (item 4, via the
  text-only alternative).
