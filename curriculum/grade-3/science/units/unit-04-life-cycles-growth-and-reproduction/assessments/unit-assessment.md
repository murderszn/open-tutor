# Unit Assessment — Unit 04: Life Cycles, Growth, and Reproduction

**For the learner, adult-supervised.** 12 items. The adult reviews
the assessment the same day and scores it with the separate
[answer key](../answer-key.md). Oral, pointing, drawing, and card
answers are accepted. Item 5 uses the fictional practice dataset
from Lesson 04 — it is practice data, not measurements from the
learner's own plants.

1. What is a life cycle? Name its four parts in order.
2. Put the flowering plant stages in order: flower, seedling, seed,
   adult plant, seed. Then name the four-part pattern word for the
   seed stage (birth), the seedling stage, the adult plant stage,
   and the flower stage. Where does death fit in the loop?
3. Put the butterfly stages in order: chrysalis, egg, adult
   butterfly, caterpillar. Explain metamorphosis in your own words,
   and explain why a chrysalis is not "just a sleeping caterpillar."
4. Fill in the missing frog stage: egg, \_\_\_\_\_, froglet, adult
   frog. In which stage does the young frog swim with a tail and no
   legs?
5. **Practice data (fictional).** Practice Bean 1: Day 1 — 0 cm, 0
   leaves. Day 7 — 3 cm, 2 leaves. Day 14 — 8 cm, 4 leaves. Day 21 —
   13 cm, 6 leaves. (a) How many centimeters did it grow from Day 7
   to Day 21? Show the subtraction. (b) How many leaves did it have
   on Day 14, and how many more leaves is that than on Day 1?
6. A bean stem measures 5 cm from the soil line. A learner measures
   from the rim of the pot and writes 8 cm. Which measurement is
   correct, and why?
7. Compare a bean plant's cycle with a frog's cycle. Name two things
   that are the same and two things that are different.
8. Sort these by starting point — seed-starters, egg-starters, or
   neither (not a starting stage): apple seed, duck egg, bean seed,
   frog egg, tadpole.
9. Bean seeds are planted in damp, warm soil. What kind of plant
   grows, and what rule from Lesson 05 does this show? What will
   Unit 05 study about this rule in more depth?
10. **Model task.** Build (draw or arrange cards for) both cycle
    models: the bean plant wheel (five stages) and the butterfly
    wheel (four stages). Each wheel must be in order, labeled, and
    show loop arrows back to the start.
11. Write or dictate a claim sentence of this form: "All life cycles
    share ___, and my evidence is ___." Use one piece of evidence
    from a cycle model and one number from a growth log (real or
    fictional — label which).
12. You find a bird's nest with eggs in it outdoors. What do you do,
    and what do you not do? Give one reason for the rule.
