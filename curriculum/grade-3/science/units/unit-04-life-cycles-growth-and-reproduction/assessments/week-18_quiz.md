# Formative Quiz — Unit 04 (Week 18)

**For the learner, adult-supervised.** 10 items. The adult reviews
the quiz the same day. Oral, pointing, and drawing answers are
accepted. Answers are in the separate [answer key](../answer-key.md).
Item 6 uses the fictional practice dataset from Lesson 04 — it is
practice data, not measurements from the learner's own plants.

1. Put the four parts of a life cycle in order: growth, death,
   birth, reproduction.
2. Put the flowering plant stages in order: adult plant, seed,
   flower, seedling, seed. What job does the flower do?
3. Put the butterfly stages in order: caterpillar, adult butterfly,
   egg, chrysalis. What is metamorphosis, in your own words?
4. A frog's cycle is: egg, tadpole, froglet, adult frog. Which stage
   is the birth stage? Which stage comes right after the tadpole?
5. Which of these have a life cycle: sunflower, bicycle, chicken?
   Give one reason for the one that does not.
6. **Practice data (fictional).** Practice Bean 1: Day 7 — 3 cm, 2
   leaves. Day 14 — 8 cm, 4 leaves. Day 21 — 13 cm, 6 leaves. How
   many centimeters did it grow from Day 14 to Day 21? How many more
   leaves did it have on Day 21 than on Day 14?
7. A plant's tallest tip is 7 cm above the soil line. A learner
   measures from the table and writes 11 cm. What is the plant's
   correct height, and why is the other number wrong?
8. Name one thing that is the same in a bean plant's cycle and a
   butterfly's cycle, and one thing that is different.
9. A bean seed is planted. What kind of plant grows? A butterfly egg
   hatches. What kind of animal grows? What rule does this show?
10. A learner draws a life cycle as a straight line that ends at
    death, with no arrows back to the start. What is missing, and
    why does it matter?
