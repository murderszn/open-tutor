# Resource Pack — Unit 04: Life Cycles, Growth, and Reproduction

**Goal (one sentence):** Give the guiding adult short, grade-3-appropriate
videos and references that reinforce the four-part life-cycle
pattern, flowering-plant stages, butterfly and frog cycles, and
bean growth measuring.
**Age fit:** grade 3 (ages 8–9); the adult previews every video and
co-views with the learner.

## Free, no-account coverage

Every core objective is covered without an account and without
payment: the two verified videos play free with no account stated on
their pages; the NSTA display and the track crosswalk are free to
read; and if video is unavailable, the lessons, stage cards, and
generated image carry the same objectives offline. Videos supplement
teaching; they never replace the lessons, the planting, or the adult
check.
