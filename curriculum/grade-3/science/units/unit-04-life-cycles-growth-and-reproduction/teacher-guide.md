# Teacher Guide — Unit 04: Life Cycles, Growth, and Reproduction

## What "ready to move on" looks like

The learner is ready for Unit 05 when they can: (a) name birth,
growth, reproduction, death in order; (b) build both cycle models in
order with loop arrows; (c) read a growth-log entry (date, height
from the soil line, leaf count, labeled drawing) and compute a simple
growth difference; and (d) state one sameness and one difference
between two cycles. The Week 18 quiz (7 of 10) plus a complete
project display is the usual evidence. A learner who can order
stages by cards but not yet write them is ready — writing is not
the objective here. A learner who cannot yet separate living cycles
from nonliving changes needs Lesson 01 re-taught before Unit 05.

## Session-by-session notes

- **Session 1 (Lesson 01):** Drill the chant with hand motions until
  it is automatic; it carries the whole unit. Check the rock/toy-car
  sort explicitly — learners who pass the sort usually pass the unit.
- **Session 2 (Practice A):** Watch item 5 (missing adult plant and
  loop) — it predicts project errors. If most learners miss it,
  re-teach Lesson 02's loop before Session 3.
- **Session 3 (Lesson 02):** Spend the time on the image activity;
  the Panel 2 vs. Panel 1 distinction (sprout vs. seed) is the most
  missed detail in this unit.
- **Session 4 (Reading-and-talk):** Co-view the SciShow Kids seed
  video; pause after germination and ask "root down or shoot up?"
  before continuing.
- **Session 5 (Lesson 03):** Clap metamorphosis in syllables. Do not
  let "chrysalis = sleeping" stand — correct it in the session, not
  later.
- **Session 6 (Practice B):** Check subtraction direction (later
  minus earlier). Learners who subtract backwards get a smaller or
  negative number — re-teach with the number line, not with a rule
  alone.
- **Session 7 (Lesson 04):** Planting day. The adult judges which
  steps the learner does alone. Photograph nothing of the learner
  for the repo — the log drawing is the record.
- **Session 8 (Measuring practice):** Every learner measures a
  practice stem twice. If two readings differ, measure a third time
  (Unit 01 rule) and record the careful reading.
- **Session 9 (Lesson 05):** Name the sorting rule before sorting.
  If learners mix "starting point" with "stage," stop and re-sort
  with the rule spoken aloud.
- **Session 10 (Practice C):** Item 3 (flowers error) and item 4
  ("better") are the unit's two error-analysis checks; both must be
  reviewed the same day.
- **Sessions 11–12 (Project):** Wheels first, log checkpoint second,
  claim sentence last. A claim without evidence is sent back for one
  more sentence, not marked wrong outright.
- **Session 13 (Quiz):** Review the same day; log the missed
  objectives for Session 15.
- **Session 14 (Showcase + cumulative retrieval):** Retrieval game
  prompts (Units 01–04): one fair-test question, one force question,
  one magnet question, one life-cycle question. Keep it playful;
  this is midyear review, not a second assessment.
- **Session 15 (Re-check stations):** Station 1 — cycle-wheel
  fix-ups (re-order a shuffled wheel). Station 2 — ruler re-measure
  (soil line ritual). Station 3 — Units 01–03 highest-need re-teach
  from quiz evidence (fair test, balanced/unbalanced forces, poles).
  Station 4 — growth-log check (measure, count, draw). Rotate every
  7 minutes.
- **Session 16 (Unit assessment):** Score with the answer key the
  same day. File the project rubric and the assessment score
  together in the journal as the Unit 04 record.

## Misconceptions and reteaching

| Misconception | Reteach move |
|---|---|
| Nonliving things have life cycles (rock breaking, car rusting) | Four-part test: was it born, does it grow by itself, does it reproduce? If any answer is no, it has no life cycle. |
| A life cycle is a straight line ending at death | Redraw as a loop; ask "what do the seeds/eggs do?" until the learner supplies the return arrow. |
| Seeds are dead because they do not move | Seed = baby plant + stored food in a coat; it starts growing with water and warmth. Plant one and watch. |
| Chrysalis = sleeping | Sleeping animals wake up the same; inside a chrysalis the body changes (legs, antennae, wings form). |
| Caterpillar and butterfly are different animals | One life, four stages — re-order the cards and name the single animal at each stage. |
| Measuring from the table or pot rim | Re-teach the ritual: zero at the soil line, every session, until automatic. |
| "Better" as a comparison | Redirect to same/difference evidence; ban "better/worse" from chart language for this unit. |
| Offspring look exactly like parents | Correct gently: same kind, not identical. Exact trait patterns are Unit 05's work — preview, do not pre-teach. |
| A prediction is a measurement | Label predictions as predictions; check them by measuring on the stated day. |

## Reteach paths

- **Cycle order weak:** replay Lessons 01–03 card sorts for 10
  minutes a day for three days; reduce to two cycles (bean,
  butterfly) until both are automatic, then re-add frog.
- **Measuring weak:** daily 5-minute ruler ritual on household
  objects (books, cups) with the soil-line rule generalized as
  "start at the bottom of what you measure."
- **Comparing weak:** use the Same / Different chart with only one
  row filled in by the adult; the learner fills the second row, then
  a full chart.
- **Growth-log gaps:** if the real beans failed, switch to the
  fictional dataset for all measuring work and note the substitution
  in the journal — the objectives are still met.

## Safety checklist (every session)

Adult supervises; planting seeds are never eaten; hands washed after
soil work; pots on a tray; spills wiped at once; no wild eggs,
nests, tadpoles, or caterpillars collected or disturbed (look, do
not touch, tell the adult); no live-animal kit is required, and any
kit used follows its own care instructions exactly.

## Differentiation reminders

Oral, pointing, drawing, and manipulative responses count equally.
The adult scribes dictated observations on request. Stage orders may
be shown by arranging cards. Sentence frames from the unit README
stay posted for the whole unit. Color is never the only cue in sorts
or graphs; the generated image has a text-only alternative.
