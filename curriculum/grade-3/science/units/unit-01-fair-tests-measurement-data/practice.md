# Practice Sets — Unit 01

Three sets matched to the pacing in the [unit README](README.md). The adult
reads items aloud as needed; the learner may answer orally, in writing, or
by drawing. Solutions are in the [answer key](answer-key.md). All datasets
are fictional practice data and are labeled as such.

## Week 3 — Questions and observing (Practice set A)

1. Sort: "Which shoe is bouncier — the sneaker or the boot?" (testable?)
2. Sort: "What is the funniest animal?" (testable?)
3. Sort: "Does a heavier ball sink faster in water than a light ball?"
   (testable?)
4. Finish: "Does warm water dissolve sugar faster?" — I could find out by
   ___.
5. Finish: "Which brand of crayon lasts longest?" — I could find out by ___.
6. You estimate a book is 20 cm long, then measure 24 cm. Which number do
   you trust, and why?
7. A ruler's 0 mark is worn off. Your friend measures from the ruler's end
   instead. Will their measurement be right? Explain.
8. Draw or describe one observation you could make with a hand lens that
   your eyes alone could not make as well.

## Week 4 — Fair tests (Practice set B)

1. "Does the push change how far the ball rolls? I push gently on carpet
   and hard on tile." Fair or unfair? Fix it.
2. "Which cup keeps water warm longest — paper or foam? I pour the same
   hot water into each and check every 10 minutes." Fair or unfair?
3. "Do seeds sprout faster in light or dark? I put 5 seeds on wet paper in
   a sunny window and 5 seeds on wet paper in a dark closet." Fair or
   unfair?
4. Plan: "Does ramp height change roll distance? Change: height. Same: car,
   ramp, surface, release, no push. 3 trials each." Name the variable and
   two controls.
5. Write a fair-test plan for: "Does the surface change how far the car
   rolls?" (one change, two controls, trials)
6. Write a fair-test plan for: "Which paper towel absorbs more water?"
   (one change, two controls, trials, what you measure)
7. Look again at the [fair-test stations picture](assets/fair-test-stations.png)
   from Lesson 03. The left station tests surfaces fairly. What question is
   it answering?
8. Name one failure point in a ramp roll test (something that could go
   wrong) and how you would prevent it.

## Week 5 — Data and patterns (Practice set C)

Fictional practice table — paper-towel absorbency (milliliters absorbed,
3 trials):

| Towel | Trial 1 | Trial 2 | Trial 3 |
|---|---|---|---|
| A | 5 mL | 6 mL | 5 mL |
| B | 9 mL | 8 mL | 9 mL |

1. Total for towel A? Show the addition.
2. Total for towel B? Show the addition.
3. How many more milliliters did towel B absorb in total?
4. Pictograph: key says each 🧽 = 4 mL. How many sponge pictures show
   towel B's total of 26 mL? (If it is not exact, what would you do?)
5. Line plot of towel A's trials (5, 6, 5): where do the X's cluster, and
   what does that tell you?
6. Write a three-part claim for this table: claim + two evidence numbers +
   one limitation.
7. A classmate draws a bar graph of this data with no title and no units.
   Name the two missing pieces and why each matters.
8. "Towel B will ALWAYS absorb more water than towel A, forever." What is
   wrong with this claim?
