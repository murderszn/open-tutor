# Unit Assessment — Unit 01: Fair Tests, Measurement, Data, and Explanations

**Given in:** session 16 (culminating). **Time:** 25–30 minutes. The learner
may answer in writing, orally (adult scribes), or with drawings where
noted. All datasets are fictional practice data. Solutions and scoring in
the [answer key](../answer-key.md).

## Part 1 — Questions and measurement

1. Write one **testable** question about magnets.
2. For your question in item 1, name the **one thing you would change**
   (the variable) and **two things you would keep the same** (controls).
3. a. How many millimeters are in 3 centimeters?
   b. A straw's one end is at the ruler's 0 mark and the other end lines
   up with the 9 cm mark. How long is the straw?
4. Choose the best unit for each: **centimeters, grams, or milliliters**.
   a. The length of your desk. b. The mass of an orange. c. The water in
   a cup.

## Part 2 — Fair tests

5. Maya wants to know if light affects plant growth. She puts one plant in
   a sunny window and waters it every day, and puts another plant in a
   dark closet and waters it once a week. Is her test fair or unfair?
   Explain, and describe how to fix it.

## Part 3 — Data and graphs

Fictional practice data — ball bounce heights (cm), 3 trials each:

| Drop height | Trial 1 | Trial 2 | Trial 3 |
|---|---|---|---|
| 50 cm | 32 cm | 33 cm | 33 cm |
| 100 cm | 58 cm | 60 cm | 59 cm |

6. Find each row's total. Show the additions.
7. Sketch a **bar graph** of the two totals (title, labels, numbered scale,
   units). Which bar is taller?
8. How many more centimeters did the 100-cm drops total than the 50-cm
   drops?
9. Line plot: the six bounce heights are plotted as X's above a number
   line. Where do the X's cluster? Which height happened most often?

## Part 4 — Claims

10. Write a **three-part claim** for the bounce data: what you claim, two
    pieces of evidence with numbers, and one limitation.

## Part 5 — Applied task

11. A classmate ran this test: "Does ramp height change roll distance?"
    But they raised the ramp AND used a different car for the second set
    of rolls.
    a. Explain why this test is unfair.
    b. Describe exactly how to fix it.
    c. Name one limitation of **your own** Great Ramp Race project test
    (from the [project](../project.md)).
