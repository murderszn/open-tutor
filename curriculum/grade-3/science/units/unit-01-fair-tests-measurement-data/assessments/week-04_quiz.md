# Formative Quiz — Unit 01 (review week)

**Given in:** session 13 (the unit's review week). **Time:** 15–20 minutes.
Short written responses (the adult reviews the same day); oral answers
accepted. Solutions in the [answer key](../answer-key.md).

1. "Which magnet picks up more paper clips?" Is this a testable question?
   What would you measure or count to answer it?
2. "What is the prettiest rock in the jar?" Is this a testable question?
   Explain why or why not.
3. A pencil's one end is at the ruler's 0 mark and the other end lines up
   with the 14 cm mark. How long is the pencil?
4. You want to weigh an apple. Which unit do you use: centimeters, grams,
   or milliliters? Why?
5. Look at the left station in the
   [fair-test stations picture](../assets/fair-test-stations.png) from
   Lesson 03. *(Text-only alternative: the left station shows two ramps at
   exactly the same height on identical book stacks; the left ramp is bare
   wood, the right ramp is covered with a green towel; a red toy car sits
   at the top of each ramp.)* What is the **one thing** that changes
   between the two ramps?
6. "I test two ramps: I roll a car down a tall ramp and a ball down a
   short ramp." Is this test fair or unfair? Name the extra change(s).
7. Fictional practice data — paper towels (mL absorbed):

   | Towel | Trial 1 | Trial 2 | Trial 3 |
   |---|---|---|---|
   | A | 5 mL | 6 mL | 5 mL |
   | B | 9 mL | 8 mL | 9 mL |

   a. What is towel A's total? Show the addition.
   b. What is towel B's total? Show the addition.
   c. Which towel absorbed more, and how many more milliliters in total?
8. Pictograph: each 🌊 picture stands for 10 mL of water. Plant A shows
   4 water pictures; Plant B shows 6. How many milliliters did each plant
   get? How many more did Plant B get?
9. Using the towel data in item 7, write a claim with **one** evidence
   number and **one** limitation.
10. Why do scientists run 3 trials instead of trusting a single trial?
