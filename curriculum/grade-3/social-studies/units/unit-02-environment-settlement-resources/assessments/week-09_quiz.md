# Formative Quiz — Week 9 (end of S4)

**Unit 02 · 12 points · about 15 minutes · adult may scribe**

Covers Lessons 01–03 (settlement reasons, adapt/modify, resource kinds).
Every question is solved in [answer-key.md](../answer-key.md). The
illustration question includes a text-only alternative.

**1. (2 pts)** Look at
[`river-valley-settlement.png`](../assets/river-valley-settlement.png).
Name **two** things in the picture that show why this is a good place for a
town, and tell the need each one meets.
*(Text-only alternative: a wide blue river with two wooden boats and a
footbridge; six red-roofed houses on flat grass; a fenced crop field; a
water wheel; a dark-green forest on the right hill; snowy gray mountains
behind. Any two with sensible needs: river→water; flat land→building;
field→food/soil; forest→timber; mountains→stone; boats→fish/travel.)*

**2. (1 pt)** A family moves to a very windy, cold plain. They build a low,
round house with thick walls and wear heavy coats. Is this **adapting** or
**modifying**? One sentence why.
*(Adapting — they changed how they live; the plain is unchanged.)*

**3. (1 pt)** The same family digs a deep well to reach underground water.
Adapting or modifying? One sentence why.
*(Modifying — they changed the land itself by digging.)*

**4. (2 pts)** "A town builds a dam across its river." Write one **wanted**
effect and one **unwanted** effect.
*(Wanted: steady water for fields / power. Unwanted: less water downstream /
fish blocked / a valley flooded. Accept sensible.)*

**5. (2 pts)** Sort into the three boxes — **natural / human / physical
capital**: *sunlight · nurse · hammer · river water*.
*(Natural: sunlight, river water. Human: nurse. Physical capital: hammer.)*

**6. (1 pt)** "A baker's oven" — which kind of resource, and why?
*(Physical capital — people built it to make other things (bread).)*

**7. (2 pts)** "A town has skilled builders and plenty of tools, but the
nearest trees are 200 miles away and there is no stone nearby." What is
missing, and what is one problem the town will face?
*(Natural resources are missing — building wood and stone; the town must
haul materials far (costly/slow) or build with what it has.)*

**8. (1 pt)** In one or two sentences: why do geographers ask "and then
what?" after every modification?
*(Because one change causes effects, and those effects cause more effects —
including unwanted ones.)*
