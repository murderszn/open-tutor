# Resource Pack — Unit 02: Environment, Settlement, and Resource Use

**Goal (one sentence):** Give the guiding adult short, grade-3-appropriate
videos, references, and search routes that reinforce why settlements grow
where they do, adapting vs. modifying, the three kinds of resources, and how
places change and populations distribute.
**Age fit:** grade 3 (ages 8–9); the adult previews every video and
co-views with the learner.

**Fact-check record (2026-10-17):** settlement-reason framing (water, flat
land, resources) follows the unit's original instruction and standard
elementary-geography treatment; U.S. state density figures used in Lesson 04
were read from `resources/us_states.csv` on `main` (column
`population_density_per_sq_mi`; NJ 1064.8, IL 221.2, MT 7.4, AK 1.1) and are
labeled approximate practice data in the lesson. Census "Statistics in
Schools" page opened 2026-10-17 (teacher-designed K–12 Census materials,
free; "State Facts for Students" section). Ducksters geography hub opened
2026-10-17 (free kids' geography section). National Geographic Kids homepage
opened 2026-10-17 (free kids' content). No runtime, caption, or
age-suitability claims are made below that were not checked; every video is
either opened or explicitly labeled an unverified search link.
