# Formative Quiz — Unit 05 (Week 22)

**Grade 3 Social Studies · Week 22, S4 · 10 points · adult-scored.**
The adult may read questions aloud and scribe. All Cedar Hollow
material is fictional practice material; all prices are fictional
practice data. Every question is solved in
[answer-key.md](../answer-key.md).

## 1. Scarcity and choice (2 pts)

June has $5.00. She can buy a storybook for $5.00 or a jump rope
for $3.00 and keep $2.00. She picks the jump rope.

(a) What is her opportunity cost? (1 pt)
(b) Why is this scarcity, in one sentence? (1 pt)

## 2. Incentives (2 pts)

The library gives a sticker to every learner who reads five books
this month.

(a) Is the sticker a positive incentive or a negative incentive?
(1 pt)
(b) What choice does it nudge the learner toward? (1 pt)

## 3. Money and change (2 pts)

Milo buys 3 apples at 25¢ each and pays with a $1.00 bill.

(a) How much do the apples cost? (1 pt)
(b) What is his change? Show the subtraction. (1 pt)

## 4. The market illustration (2 pts)

Look at the market illustration
([`../assets/cedar-hollow-market-day.png`](../assets/cedar-hollow-market-day.png))
or read its text-only alternative below.

(a) Name the good each of the three stalls sells. (1 pt)
(b) Pick one stall and name one natural resource, one human
resource, and one physical resource it uses. (1 pt)

**Text-only alternative:** *A wide dirt path runs in front of three
wooden market stalls with cloth awnings. The left stall has a yellow
awning: a baker stands behind a counter with baskets of round bread
loaves, and a clay oven with a small fire glows beside them. The
middle stall has a green awning: a farmer stands behind baskets of
red apples, green cabbages, orange carrots, and green peas. The right
stall has a pink awning: a weaver stands beside a small wooden loom,
with folded cloth in orange, blue, purple, and yellow, and baskets of
yarn. Two shoppers carry woven baskets. Leafy trees and small wooden
houses stand in the background. No sign has words on it, and no
prices are shown.*

## 5. Profit (2 pts)

Milo's stand: price $1.00 per cup; cost per cup 40¢.

(a) What is the profit on one cup? (1 pt)
(b) What is Milo's profit if he sells 10 cups? Show the step. (1 pt)
