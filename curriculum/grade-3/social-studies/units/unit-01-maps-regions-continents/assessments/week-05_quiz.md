# Formative Quiz — Week 5

**Name:** \_\_\_\_\_\_\_\_\_\_\_\_\_\_\_\_ **Date:** \_\_\_\_\_\_\_\_\_\_

*Answer in words, pictures, or dictation — the adult scribes on request.
10 points. Solutions in [answer-key.md](../answer-key.md) — learners do not
open it.*

1. Name the seven continents. (2 points — 1 for ≥5, 2 for all 7)
2. Name the five oceans. (1 point)
3. A map's scale bar says `1 cm = 10 km`. Two cities are 3 cm apart on the
   map. How far apart are they really? Show your thinking. (2 points)
4. Look at the desert/rainforest illustration
   (`../assets/desert-rainforest-comparison.png`).
   Name **two** clues in the picture that the left side gets very little
   rain, and **two** clues that the right side gets a lot of rain. (2 points)

   **Text-only alternative:** The picture's left half shows a desert: orange
   sand dunes, a few palm trees around one small pond, a camel, a bright
   high sun. The right half shows a rainforest: tall layered green trees, a
   winding river, a colorful macaw, big leaves and ferns.

5. "The Sahara and the Amazon both get strong sun, but one is dry and sandy
   and the other is wet and green." In one or two sentences, explain how
   that is possible. (2 points)
6. Which of these is a **compelling** question? Circle it.
   (a) "How many states are in the West region?"
   (b) "Why do the Western states have such different landscapes?"
   (1 point)
