## Part 1 — Continents, oceans, and maps (6 points)

1. Label the seven continents and five oceans on the blank outline map your
   adult gives you. (3 points — 3 for 11–12 correct, 2 for 8–10, 1 for 5–7)
2. "A globe and a flat map both show Earth. When is the globe the better
   tool, and when is the flat map better?" (2 points)
3. The **equator** is: (a) a painted line on the ground (b) an imaginary
   line around Earth's middle (c) the biggest ocean. (1 point)

## Part 2 — Map scale (5 points)

4. A trail map's scale bar says `1 cm = 2 km`. The trail from the parking
   lot to the waterfall measures 5 cm on the map. How long is the real
   trail? (2 points)
5. "A map of our whole country shows less detail than a map of our block."
   True or false? Explain why in one sentence. (2 points)
6. Which map would you use to plan a drive between two cities in your
   state — a neighborhood map or a state map? Why? (1 point)

## Part 3 — Location and environment (6 points)

*Items 7–8 use the desert/rainforest illustration
(`../assets/desert-rainforest-comparison.png`).*

7. Name one animal from the desert half and one from the rainforest half,
   and write one sentence for each explaining how it fits its environment.
   (2 points)

   **Text-only alternative:** Left half (desert): sand dunes, palm trees
   around one small pond, a camel, bright sun. Right half (rainforest):
   layered green trees, a winding river, a colorful macaw, big leaves.

8. "The Amazon sits near the equator; the Sahara sits north of the
   equator in northern Africa." Using that location fact, explain in 2–3
   sentences why the Amazon is wet and green while the Sahara is dry and
   sandy. (2 points)
9. A friend says, "Deserts have no water and nothing lives in them." Use
   evidence to correct them. (2 points)

## Part 4 — Regions and inquiry (3 points)

10. Name the four U.S. Census regions. Your dataset says Ohio's subregion
    is "United States Midwest" — what region does Ohio belong to, and what
    map evidence confirms it? (2 points)
11. Write one **supporting question** that would help answer this compelling
    question: "Why do the four U.S. regions have such different
    environments?" (1 point)
