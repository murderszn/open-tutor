# Formative Quiz — Week 17 (Unit 04)

**Grade 3 · Social Studies · 10 points · about 15 minutes.** The
adult may read questions aloud and scribe answers. All Cedar Hollow
details are **fictional practice material**. Answers are in
[answer-key.md](../answer-key.md) — do not hand the key to the learner
with this quiz.

**Q1 (3 points).** Sort each need to its level. Write L (local),
S (state), or N (national):

1. Fixing a pothole on Cedar Hollow's Main Street.
2. Making driver's license rules for a whole state.
3. Printing the money used in every state.

**Q2 (2 points).** Who does each job — the MAYOR or the COUNCIL?

1. Votes on local rules and the town's budget.
2. Leads town meetings and carries out the council's decisions.

**Q3 (2 points).** Is each statement a RULE or a LAW?

1. "Raise your hand before speaking in class."
2. "Stop at a red light."

**Q4 (2 points).** Use Cedar Hollow's pretend budget ($100 total:
Roads $30 · Fire $25 · Library $15 · Parks $15 · Town hall $15).

1. Roads plus fire department together cost $___.
2. If the council moves $5 from parks to the library, the library's
   new amount is $___.

**Q5 (1 point).** Look at the town-hall illustration
([../assets/cedar-hollow-town-hall.png](../assets/cedar-hollow-town-hall.png))
or use this text-only alternative: *Five people sit at a long table
at the front of a meeting room — the mayor in the middle and four
council members. Six residents sit facing them, and one resident, a
woman with red hair, stands with her hand raised to speak. Through
the windows you can see a fire truck, a book cart, and a park
tree.*

Who is speaking, and who is listening? (One sentence.)
