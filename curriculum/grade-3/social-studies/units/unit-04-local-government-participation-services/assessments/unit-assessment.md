# Unit Assessment — Unit 04: Local Government, Participation, and Public Services

**Grade 3 · Social Studies · 30 points · about 30 minutes.** The
adult may read questions aloud and scribe answers. All Cedar Hollow
details are **fictional practice material**. Answers and scoring
guidance are in [answer-key.md](../answer-key.md) — do not hand the
key to the learner with this assessment.

## Part A — Levels, officials, and services (10 points)

**A1 (5 points).** Sort each need to its level. Write L (local),
S (state), or N (national). One point each:

1. New books for the Cedar Hollow Library.
2. Repaving a highway that crosses a whole state.
3. Keeping the country safe.
4. Fixing the swings in Cedar Hollow Park.
5. Making an agreement with another country.

**A2 (5 points).** Match each job to the person or group that does
it. Write the letter. One point each:

Jobs: (a) Votes on local rules and the budget. (b) Fixes potholes
and plows snow. (c) Keeps town records and meeting notes.
(d) Puts out fires and teaches fire safety. (e) Leads town meetings
and carries out the council's decisions.

1. Town council — ___
2. Road crew — ___
3. Fire department — ___
4. Town clerk — ___
5. Mayor — ___

## Part B — Rules, laws, taxes, and budgets (10 points)

**B1 (4 points).** Is each statement a RULE or a LAW? One point each:

1. "Walk in the hallway so nobody gets hurt."
2. "Wear a seat belt in a car."
3. "Put your shoes by the door at home."
4. "Do not take something that belongs to someone else" (stealing).

**B2 (3 points).** Use Cedar Hollow's pretend budget ($100 total:
Roads $30 · Fire $25 · Library $15 · Parks $15 · Town hall $15).

1. Library plus parks together cost $___ (1 point).
2. If the council cuts the library from $15 to $5 and gives the $10
   saved to roads, the new roads amount is $___ (1 point).
3. Show that the five new amounts still total $100 (1 point).

**B3 (3 points).** Put the four links of the tax-to-service chain in
order (write 1–4): ___ Departments spend it on services. ___
People and businesses pay taxes. ___ The council votes a budget.
___ The government collects the money.

## Part C — Deciding together and making a policy (10 points)

**C1 (4 points).** Cedar Hollow's council has $10 of pretend park
money. Maya says, "Buy a bench, because grandparents need a place to
sit." Jon says, "Buy balls, because ours are flat and everyone plays
with them."

1. Name the procedure the council should use to decide, from:
   consensus, vote, leader decides (1 point).
2. Give one reason why that procedure fits this situation (1 point).
3. Explain, in one sentence, why losing this decision would not
   mean the process was unfair (2 points).

**C2 (6 points).** Write a short policy proposal for a real problem
in your classroom or school. Your answer must include:

1. The problem, in one sentence (2 points).
2. One fair rule that handles the problem, with a reason (2 points).
3. How you will check whether the rule worked — what you will count
   or observe, and when (2 points).

Your proposal must not name, blame, or embarrass any child or adult.
