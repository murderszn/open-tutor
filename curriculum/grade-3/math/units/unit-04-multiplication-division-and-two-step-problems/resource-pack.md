# Resource Pack — Unit 04: Multiplication, Division, and Two-Step Problems

**Goal:** support an adult teaching a grade-3 learner to solve
two-step word problems with equations using a letter for the unknown,
judge reasonableness by estimation, explain multiplication-table
patterns, and build strategy-based fluency within 100.
**Age fit:** grade 3 (ages 8–9); adult-led, 30-minute sessions.

Items 1–2 were opened and read on **2026-10-30**; items 3–4 carry
Unit 03's 2026-10-23 verification and are marked confirm-before-sharing
below; video links are clearly labeled preferred-channel
**search links** (the adult previews before showing). Every core
objective has a free, no-account route.

## Web references (verified 2026-10-30)

1. **Common Core State Standards — Grade 3, Operations & Algebraic Thinking**
   `https://www.thecorestandards.org/Math/Content/3/OA/`
   Official source for the 3.OA codes used in this unit (A.3, A.4, C.7,
   D.8, D.9 — including D.8's whole-number/order-of-operations note and
   D.9's 4-times-even example). Opened and read in full 2026-10-30.
   Adult reference; no account needed.
2. **The Math Learning Center — free math apps**
   `https://www.mathlearningcenter.org/apps`
   Free, no-account web apps; the **Number Line** app supports
   two-step modeling, **Partial Product Finder** represents
   multiplication as arrays with matching dimensions, and the **Money**
   pieces app supports the project's play-money computations. Opened
   and read 2026-10-30.
3. **NCTM Illuminations — standards-based lessons and interactives (PK–12)**
   `https://www.nctm.org/illuminations/`
   From the National Council of Teachers of Mathematics; lessons and
   interactive tools for demonstrating two-step models and patterns.
   Opened and read 2026-10-23 (Unit 03 verification); index still
   listed as the NCTM interactives hub — adult to confirm the chosen
   activity before sharing.
4. **NRICH — multiplication and division problems (University of Cambridge)**
   `https://nrich.maths.org/`
   Free, no-account enrichment problems and two-step investigations
   for the Lesson 03/05 extensions; reputable university mathematics
   outreach. Opened and read 2026-10-23 (Unit 03 verification).
5. **Khan Academy — third-grade two-step practice (labeled search link)**
   `https://www.khanacademy.org/search?search_again=1&page_search_query=third+grade+two+step+word+problems`
   Free practice and videos organized by grade-3 topic; use the
   two-step units alongside Lessons 01–04. Free account optional, not
   required for videos. The adult previews before sharing.
