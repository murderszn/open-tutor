# Formative Quiz — Unit 04 (Session 15)

**Time:** about 20 minutes, then project Part 2. **Adult:** read the
story problems aloud if the learner's reading stamina is the barrier —
this quiz measures mathematics, not reading speed. No calculator;
multiplication chart allowed for Q8 only (fluency strategies are
taught, not assumed, at this stage).

1. A class has 4 boxes with 8 crayons in each box, plus 9 loose
   crayons. How many crayons are there in all? Show both steps.
2. A baker makes 6 trays with 7 muffins on each tray, then sells 15
   muffins. How many muffins are left? Show both steps.
3. Find n: 7 × n = 63. Show the fact and the division check.
4. A shop makes p = 5 × 6 muffins, then shares them equally among
   5 boxes. Let n be the muffins per box. Find p, then n.
5. A learner claims 8 × 7 + 14 = 80. Give an estimate, a verdict
   (reasonable / not reasonable), and the correct answer.
6. Find 4 × 8. Is the answer even? Prove it with two equal addends.
7. Find 9 × 7 with the ten-minus-one method. Do the digits of your
   answer add to 9?
8. Solve with a named strategy each: 8 × 9 and 72 ÷ 8.
9. Fifty-six stickers are shared equally among 7 friends. Each friend
   then finds 6 more stickers. How many stickers does each friend have
   now? Show both steps.
10. Explain in your own words: why can an estimate be different from
    the exact answer and still be useful?

**Scoring:** 1 point per item (Q1, Q2, Q4, Q9 need both steps for the
point; Q5 needs estimate + verdict + correction). 9–10: ready for the
assessment. 7–8: re-do the missed objective's practice session, then
proceed. Below 7: re-teach from the teacher guide before Session 16.
