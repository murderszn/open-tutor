# Unit Assessment — Unit 04 (Session 16)

**Time:** about 30 minutes. **Adult:** read story problems aloud on
request; this assessment measures mathematics. Multiplication chart
allowed only for Q8's fluency check-in (the item says so); all other
items are solved from strategies. Show both steps wherever a problem
has two steps — a bare final number earns half marks on those items.

1. A store has 5 shelves with 9 notebooks on each shelf, then sells
   18 notebooks. How many notebooks are left? Show both steps.
2. Seventy-two pencils are shared equally among 9 tables. Each table
   then receives 3 more pencils. How many pencils does each table have
   now? Show both steps.
3. Find n: n ÷ 6 = 8. Show the inverse check.
4. Find n: 9 × n = 54. Show the fact and the division check.
5. A learner claims 5 × 8 − 17 = 33. Give an estimate, a verdict
   (reasonable / not reasonable), and the correct answer.
6. Find 4 × 6. Is the answer even? Prove it with two equal addends.
7. Find 9 × 8 with the ten-minus-one method. Do the digits of your
   answer add to 9?
8. Fluency check-in (chart allowed, name a strategy anyway):
   7 × 7, 63 ÷ 9, 8 × 6.
9. Extra number: A hall has 7 rows with 6 chairs in each row and
   3 windows. Nine chairs are removed. How many chairs are left?
   Cross out the extra number first, then solve.
10. Cumulative (Unit 01): Round 68 and 34 to the nearest 10, estimate
    68 + 34, then compute exactly. Was the estimate reasonable?
11. Story to equations: A teacher buys 6 packs with 5 pencils in each
    pack and shares all the pencils equally among 3 groups. Let p be
    the pencils bought and n the pencils per group. Write both
    equations and solve.
12. Two-step: A class has 81 stickers. The class shares them equally
    among 9 students, then each student gives 2 stickers away. How
    many stickers does each student have now? Show both steps.
13. Explanation task: A friend says, "Every two-step problem needs
    addition." Do you agree? Give a counterexample problem — one that
    needs two steps but no addition at all — solve it, and explain
    why it disproves the claim.

**Scoring:** 1 point per item for Q1–Q12 (two-step items need both
steps; Q5 needs estimate + verdict + correction; Q8 needs all three
facts with strategies), 2 points for Q13 (1 for a valid counterexample
with solution, 1 for the explanation). Total 14 points. 12–14: ready
for Unit 05. 10–11: targeted re-teach of missed objectives, then
proceed. Below 10: re-teach the unit's missed objectives from the
teacher guide and re-assess those objectives only. Solutions:
[../answer-key.md](../answer-key.md), Assessment.
