# Assets — Unit 04

## Generated raster image

- **File:** `two-step-school-store.png` (1920 × 1280 PNG)
- **Prompt (as given to the image tool):** "A bright, flat,
  child-friendly illustration for a grade 3 math lesson: a small school
  store table at a classroom fair. On the table are several baskets of
  pencils sold in packs, stacks of notebooks, and a few loose pencils
  in a cup. A simple price sign shows packs and single items. Clean
  shapes, clear groups, no text other than a short simple sign, no
  people."
- **Tool:** built-in image generation (Muse media pipeline),
  generated **2026-10-30**.
- **Intended use:** Lesson 01 picture question — learners count
  baskets and packs per basket, then solve the two-step problem
  (3 baskets × 3 packs = 9 packs; 9 × 5 pencils = 45 pencils). Also
  the model for the project store poster (learners draw their own
  stock; the illustration itself is not their poster).
- **Factual review (inspected 2026-10-30):** the picture shows
  exactly 3 baskets, each holding 3 banded packs, and the sign reads
  "Pack of 5 = 10¢ · Single = 2¢" — consistent with the lesson's
  counts (3 × 3 = 9 packs; 9 × 5 = 45 pencils; pack price 10¢ =
  5 × 2¢ per pencil). The cup of loose pencils and the notebook
  stacks are deliberately *not* counted in the Lesson 01 question,
  and the lesson says so. The image is labeled an AI-generated
  illustration, never a photograph or a real store record.
- **Alt text:** "A school store table with three baskets of pencil
  packs, a cup of loose pencils, and stacks of notebooks under a sign
  that reads: Pencils: Pack of 5 = 10 cents, Single = 2 cents.
  AI-generated illustration for this lesson."
- **Caption:** "AI-generated illustration — a school store table. The
  sign says a pack holds 5 pencils. Count the baskets and the packs
  in each basket; do not try to count every single pencil in the
  picture."
- **Text-only alternative:** see Lesson 01 — the picture question
  states the counts (3 baskets, 3 packs each, 5 pencils per pack) so
  the question is fully answerable without the image.
- **Known limitations:** individual pencils inside packs are drawn
  decoratively and are not reliably countable; the lesson therefore
  never asks learners to count single pencils from the picture. The
  notebook stacks and loose-pencil cup are background detail, not
  data.

## Reproducible diagram

- **Purpose:** exact, reproducible two-step map for Lesson 01's picture
