# Resource Pack — Unit 05: Fractions on Models and Number Lines

**Goal:** support an adult teaching a grade-3 learner to name unit
fractions and a/b as parts of size 1/b, place fractions on a number
line, recognize equivalent fractions and whole numbers as fractions,
and compare fractions with the same numerator or denominator — all for
the same whole.
**Age fit:** grade 3 (ages 8–9); adult-led, 30-minute sessions.

All external links were checked on **2026-11-05**. Direct pages were
opened and read where noted; video links are clearly labeled
preferred-channel **search links** (the adult previews before showing).
Every core objective has a free, no-account route.

## Web references (verified 2026-11-05)

1. **Common Core State Standards — Grade 3, Number & Operations–Fractions**
   `https://www.thecorestandards.org/Math/Content/3/NF/`
   Official source for the 3.NF codes used in this unit; opened and read
   2026-11-05 (A.1, A.2, A.2.a, A.2.b, A.3, A.3.a–d verified, including
   the denominators 2, 3, 4, 6, 8 footnote). Adult reference; no account
   needed.
2. **The Math Learning Center — free math apps**
   `https://www.mathlearningcenter.org/apps`
   Free, no-account web apps; the **Fractions** app (bar or circle
   models, denominators 1–100) supports Lessons 01–02 and 05–06, and
   the **Number Line** app (fraction-labeled lines) supports Lessons
   03–04. Opened and read 2026-10-23 (Unit 03); Fractions and Number
   Line app descriptions confirmed in search 2026-11-05.
3. **Khan Academy — third-grade fractions practice (labeled search link)**
   `https://www.khanacademy.org/search?search_again=1&page_search_query=third+grade+fractions`
   Free practice and videos organized by grade-3 topic, including
   whole numbers as fractions and equivalent fractions; use alongside
   Lessons 01–06. Free account optional, not required for videos. The
   adult previews before sharing.
4. **Cuemath — Plot Fractions on a Number Line (free game)**
   `https://www.cuemath.com/free-online-math-games/plotting-fractions-on-a-number-line/`
   Free, no-signup browser game for grade 3: place fractions on a
   number line. Found in search 2026-11-05 (free, no ads or pop-ups
   per the page); adult previews before sharing. Use with Lesson 04
   and the Session 8 game.
5. **Cuemath — Match the Fractions (free game)**
   `https://www.cuemath.com/free-online-math-games/match-the-fractions/`
   Free, no-signup browser game for grade 3: match fractions to visual
   models. Found in search 2026-11-05; adult previews before sharing.
   Use with Lessons 01–02 and Session B.
6. **NCTM Illuminations — standards-based lessons and interactives (PK–12)**
   `https://www.nctm.org/illuminations/`
   From the National Council of Teachers of Mathematics; lessons and
   interactive tools for demonstrating fraction models. Opened and read
   2026-10-23 (Unit 03); free, no account for the interactives index.
7. **NRICH — fractions problems (University of Cambridge)**
   `https://nrich.maths.org/`
   Free, no-account enrichment problems and fraction investigations
   for the Lesson 05/06 extensions; reputable university mathematics
   outreach. Opened and read 2026-10-23 (Unit 03).
