# Unit Assessment — Unit 05: Fractions on Models and Number Lines

**Session:** 16 · **Time:** 30 minutes · **Instructions:** Read each item
carefully. Every diagram is described in words — you do not need a
picture to answer. Show your reasoning where the item asks for it, and
answer word problems in full sentences. This assessment is a check of
learning, not a timed test — there is no speed score.

**Same-whole warning:** In Parts C and D, every comparison is about
parts of the **same whole** — the same size tray, rectangle, or number
line. Fractions can only be compared this way when the wholes are the
same size.

## Part A — Naming fractions on models and number lines (1 point each)

1. A rectangle is divided into **8 equal parts**, and **3** of the parts
   are shaded. What fraction of the rectangle is shaded?
2. A number line runs from 0 to 1 and is divided into **6 equal parts**.
   A point is marked on the **fifth** tick mark after 0. What fraction
   does the point show?
3. A circle is divided into **3 equal parts**, and **1** part is shaded.
   What fraction of the circle is shaded? (This is a unit fraction.)
4. Draw a number line from 0 to 1 divided into **3 equal parts**. Mark
   and label the point **2/3**. How many equal parts from 0 is your
   mark?

## Part B — Equivalent fractions and whole numbers (1 point each)

5. Fill in the missing number: 1/3 = ?/6.
6. Fill in the missing number: 3/4 = ?/8.
7. Write the whole number **4** as a fraction with denominator 2.
8. Write the fraction **6/6** as a whole number.

## Part C — Comparing fractions (1 point each)

Write >, <, or = for each pair. Remember: the wholes are the same size.

9. 3/4 ☐ 2/4
10. 1/6 ☐ 1/3
11. 2/8 ☐ 1/4

## Part D — Story problem (3 points: 1 for each part)

12. A baker cuts one tray of brownies into **8 equal slices**. In the
    morning she sells **3/8** of the tray. In the afternoon she sells
    **2/8** of the tray.
    - (a) What fraction of the tray did she sell in all?
    - (b) What fraction of the tray is left at the end of the day?
    - (c) Is the fraction left more than, less than, or equal to 1/2
      of the tray? Explain in one sentence.

## Part E — Reasoning (2 points)

13. Explain in two or three sentences why **3/6** and **1/2** name the
    same amount. You may describe a drawing in words (for example, a
    rectangle cut into 6 equal parts with 3 shaded, next to a rectangle
    of the same size cut into 2 equal parts with 1 shaded).

**Scoring:** 4 + 4 + 3 + 3 + 2 = 16 points. 14–16 = objectives met,
ready for Unit 06; 11–13 = met with one re-teach target (name it);
10 or below = re-teach the missed objectives before Unit 06, using the
practice sessions for those objectives.
