# Formative Quiz — Unit 05, Week 04

**Session:** 15 · **Time:** 15 minutes · **Instructions:** Read each item
carefully. Every diagram is described in words — you do not need a
picture to answer. Write the fraction for each item, and show your
reasoning where the item asks for it. A bare number earns half credit.

1. A rectangle is divided into **6 equal parts**, and **4** of the parts
   are shaded. What fraction of the rectangle is shaded?
2. A number line runs from 0 to 1 and is divided into **4 equal parts**.
   A point is marked on the **first** tick mark after 0. What fraction
   does the point show? (This is a unit fraction.)
3. Draw a number line from 0 to 1 divided into **4 equal parts**. Mark
   and label the point **3/4**. How many equal parts from 0 is your
   mark?
4. Fill in the missing number: 1/2 = ?/4. Explain in one sentence how
   you know.
5. Write the whole number **3** as a fraction with denominator 1.
6. Compare: 5/8 ☐ 3/8. Write >, <, or =. Explain in one sentence why.
   (Remember: both fractions are parts of the **same whole**.)
7. Compare: 2/3 ☐ 2/6. Write >, <, or =. Explain in one sentence why.
   (Remember: both fractions are parts of the **same whole**.)
8. Error analysis: A circle is divided into 4 parts, but the parts are
   **not equal** — one part is much bigger than the others. One part is
   shaded. Sam says the shaded part is 1/4 of the circle. Is Sam right?
   Explain why or why not in one or two sentences.
9. A rectangle is divided into **8 equal parts**, and **6** of the parts
   are shaded. Write the fraction shaded. Then write an equivalent
   fraction with denominator 4.
10. Explain in two sentences why **2/4** and **1/2** name the same size.
    You may describe a drawing in words (for example, a rectangle cut
    into 4 equal parts with 2 shaded, next to a rectangle cut into 2
    equal parts with 1 shaded).

**Scoring:** 10 items, 1 point each (item 10: 1 point only if the
explanation says the two fractions cover the same amount of the same
whole). 9–10 = ready for the unit assessment; 7–8 = revisit the missed
objective in Session 16 review time; 6 or below = re-teach that
objective before the unit assessment.
