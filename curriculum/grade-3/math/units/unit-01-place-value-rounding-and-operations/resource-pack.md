# Resource Pack — Unit 01: Place Value, Rounding, and Multi-Digit Operations

**Goal:** support an adult teaching a grade-3 learner to round to the nearest
10/100, add and subtract within 1000, multiply by multiples of 10, and estimate
to check reasonableness. **Age fit:** grade 3 (ages 8–9); adult-led, 30-minute
sessions.

All external links were checked on **2026-10-10**. Direct pages were opened
and read; video links are clearly labeled preferred-channel **search links**
(the adult previews before showing). Every core objective has a free,
no-account route.

## Web references (verified 2026-10-10)

1. **Common Core State Standards — Grade 3, Number & Operations in Base Ten**
   `https://www.thecorestandards.org/Math/Content/3/NBT/`
   Official source for the 3.NBT codes used in this unit; opened 2026-10-10
   (page renders A.2 and A.3; the A.1 rounding expectation was cross-checked
   during the track audit). Adult reference; no account needed.
2. **Wikipedia — Rounding**
   `https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Rounding`
   Background on rounding conventions, including the round-half-up rule this
   unit uses (halfway values round up). Opened and read 2026-10-10. Adult
   reference — the article's reading level is above grade 3.
3. **The Math Learning Center — free math apps**
   `https://www.mathlearningcenter.org/apps`
   Free web apps with no account required for basic use: **Number Line**
   (rounding benchmarks) and **Number Pieces** (virtual base-ten blocks).
   Opened 2026-10-10; confirmed free and browser-based.
4. **Repository: `resources/math_fundamentals.md`** — teacher-side reference
   only (its band is grades 4–7); the adult may consult its rounding notes
   when writing reteach plans. Never assigned to the learner.
5. **Repository: unit SVG `assets/rounding-number-line-10.svg`** — the
   reproducible 230–260 number-line zoom used in Lesson 03; print it for the
   learner's desk.
