# Unit Assessment — Unit 01 (Session 16)

**Time:** 35 minutes · **Points:** 50 · **Covers objectives 1–5**

The learner works independently; the adult may read items aloud but may not
hint at methods. Scoring guidance is in the
[answer key](../answer-key.md#unit-assessment).

## Section A — Place value and expanded form (8 pts)

**1.** Write **704** in expanded form and as a number name. (4 pts)

**2.** Which build shows **5 hundreds, 3 tens, 2 ones**? Circle one: (a) 532
(b) 523 (c) 352. (4 pts)

## Section B — Rounding (12 pts)

**3.** Round **68** to the nearest 10. (3 pts)

**4.** Round **495** to the nearest 100. (3 pts)

**5.** Round **872** to the nearest 100. (3 pts)

**6.** Circle **all** the numbers that round to **600** when rounded to the
nearest 100: 649, 551, 625, 575. (3 pts)

## Section C — Add and subtract within 1000 (12 pts)

**7.** **358 + 467 = ?** Show the column work. (4 pts)

**8.** **812 − 436 = ?** Show the column work and check with addition. (4 pts)

**9.** Ben solved **504 − 278** and got **334**. Is Ben right? If not, name his
mistake and give the correct answer. (4 pts)

## Section D — Multiply by tens and estimate (10 pts)

**10.** **7 × 50 = ?** Show the tens-as-units reasoning. (3 pts)

**11.** A tray holds **60** juice boxes. How many juice boxes are in **8**
trays? Write the multiplication and solve. (3 pts)

**12.** Lena says **593 − 218 = 385**. Use rounding to check whether her answer
is reasonable: write your estimate and your judgment. (4 pts)

## Section E — Applied task (8 pts)

**13.** The market fair sold **214** apple pies, **389** peach pies, and **156**
plum pies.
(a) Round each number to the nearest 10 and write your estimated total. (3 pts)
(b) Find the exact total. Show your work. (3 pts)
(c) Is your estimate reasonable? Explain in one or two sentences. (2 pts)
