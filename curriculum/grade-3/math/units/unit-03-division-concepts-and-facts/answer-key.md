# Answer Key — Unit 03: Division Concepts and Facts

**Audience:** the guiding adult only. Every answer below was solved
independently of the lesson text and reconciled with it; where a lesson
gives a strategy, the key shows the same strategy's working. A learner
answer that differs in wording but matches in value, meaning, and check
is correct.

## Lesson 01

**Guided:** 1. 18 ÷ 3 = 6 (6 rounds of dealing; 3 × 6 = 18 ✓).
2. 30 ÷ 5 = 6 (5 × 6 = 30 ✓). 3. 16 ÷ 4 = 4 — not the same as #1's 6,
because both the total and the share count changed (4 × 4 = 16 ✓).
4. 12 ÷ 2 = 6 (2 × 6 = 12 ✓). 5. Mia is wrong: 3 × 3 = 9, leaving 6
counters undealt. Correct: 15 ÷ 3 = 5 (3 × 5 = 15 ✓).

**Independent:** 6. 28 ÷ 4 = 7. 7. 36 ÷ 6 = 6. 8. 45 ÷ 5 = 9.
9. 21 ÷ 3 = 7 pencils per table (3 × 7 = 21 ✓). 10. True: 8 × 5 = 40,
so 40 ÷ 8 = 5. 11. Sam is wrong: 4 × 6 = 24 ≠ 32. Correct: 32 ÷ 4 = 8
(4 × 8 = 32 ✓).

**Application (sample):** 24 ÷ 4 = 6 — e.g., 24 crayons shared among
4 friends, 6 each; fair because every share is equal and 4 × 6 = 24.

**Exit:** 1. 32 ÷ 4 = 8. 2. 27 ÷ 3 = 9.

**Extension:** 48 ÷ 8 = 6; 48 ÷ 6 = 8 — same total, fewer shares, more
in each share.

## Lesson 02

**Guided:** 1. 36 ÷ 6 = 6 groups (6, 12, 18, 24, 30, 36 — six counts).
2. 48 ÷ 8 = 6 groups (6 × 8 = 48 ✓). 3. 27 ÷ 9 = 3 groups; the 3 counts
groups of 9. 4. 54 ÷ 9 = 6 groups. 5. Tom is wrong: 8 groups of 8 = 64,
not 40. Correct: 40 ÷ 8 = 5 groups (5 × 8 = 40 ✓).

**Independent:** 6. 63 ÷ 7 = 9 groups. 7. 72 ÷ 8 = 9 groups. 8. 20 ÷ 5 = 4

the 5 counts boxes. 12. False: e.g., 56 muffins, 8 per box → 56 ÷ 8 = 7
boxes — here 7 counts groups, not muffins per share.

8 packs; the 8 counts packs, not pencils per pack.

**Extension:** 36 ÷ 4 as fair shares: 36 among 4 friends → 9 each; as
group counts: 36 in bags of 4 → 9 bags. Both quotients are 9; the first
9 counts items per friend, the second counts bags.
