# Unit Assessment — Unit 03: Division Concepts and Facts

**Session:** 16 · **Time:** 30 minutes · **Instructions:** Show your working
for every item: the equation, the strategy, and the multiply-back check
where the item asks for it. Answer word problems in full sentences that say
what the quotient counts. This assessment is a check of learning, not a
timed test — there is no speed score.

## Part A — Quotients and unknowns (1 point each)

1. 24 ÷ 4 = ?
2. 56 ÷ 7 = ?
3. 81 ÷ 9 = ?
4. Solve 7 × ? = 63.
5. Solve 48 ÷ ? = 6.
6. Solve _ ÷ 6 = 7.
7. Solve 90 ÷ 10 = ? and explain the pattern in one sentence.
8. Solve 100 ÷ 10 = ? and prove it with a multiplication fact.

## Part B — Word problems (2 points each: 1 for the equation, 1 for the full-sentence answer)

9. A baker shares 72 muffins equally among 8 boxes. How many muffins per
   box?
10. A rope 64 m long is cut into pieces 8 m long. How many pieces?
11. A classroom has 54 chairs arranged in 9 equal rows. How many chairs
    per row?
12. A playlist lasts 56 minutes. Each song is 7 minutes long. How many
    songs are on the playlist?

## Part C — Reasoning (2 points each)

13. Error analysis: Leo solved 35 ÷ 5 and wrote 6, "because 6 is close to
    7." Is Leo right? Show the multiply-back check and give the correct
    quotient.
14. Two readings: write a fair-share story AND a group-count story for
    42 ÷ 6 = 7, and say what the 7 counts in each story.
15. Explain your strategy: solve 72 ÷ 9, name the strategy you used, show
    the working, and multiply back.

**Scoring:** 8 + 8 + 6 = 22 points. 20–22 = objective met, ready for
Unit 04; 16–19 = met with one re-teach target (name it); 15 or below =
re-teach the missed objectives before Unit 04, using the practice sessions
for those objectives. Answers: [answer-key.md](../answer-key.md#unit-assessment).
