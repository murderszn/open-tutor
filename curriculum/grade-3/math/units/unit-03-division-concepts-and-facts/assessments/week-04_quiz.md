# Formative Quiz — Unit 03, Week 04

**Session:** 15 · **Time:** 15 minutes · **Instructions:** Show your working
for every item: the equation, the strategy (dealing, group counting,
unknown-factor question, or breaking apart), and the multiply-back check
where the item asks for it. A bare number earns half credit.

1. Share 28 counters equally into 4 shares. Write the equation and the
   quotient. What does the quotient count?
2. Make groups of 5 from 45 counters. How many groups? Write the equation.
   What does the quotient count?
3. Find 36 ÷ 6 by asking the unknown-factor question. Write the question
   and the equation.
4. Solve 8 × ? = 72. Prove it by putting the answer back in.
5. Solve _ ÷ 7 = 8. Prove it by putting the answer back in.
6. A teacher shares 54 pencils equally among 6 students. How many pencils
   per student? Write the equation and answer in a full sentence.
7. A hall has 42 chairs in 7 equal rows. How many chairs per row? Write
   the equation and answer in a full sentence.
8. Solve 63 ÷ 9. Name your strategy.
9. Error analysis: Sam solved 40 ÷ 8 and wrote 6. Is Sam right? Show the
   multiply-back check and give the correct quotient.
10. Explain in two sentences why 30 ÷ 5 = 6 can mean "6 in each share"
    AND "6 groups." Give a story for each meaning.

**Scoring:** 10 items, 1 point each (item 10: 1 point only if both
meanings are stated with a story each). 9–10 = ready for the unit
assessment; 7–8 = revisit the missed objective in Session 16 review
time; 6 or below = re-teach that objective before the unit assessment.
