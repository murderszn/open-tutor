# Resource Pack — Unit 03: Division Concepts and Facts

**Goal:** support an adult teaching a grade-3 learner to interpret
quotients as fair shares and as group counts, use division as an
unknown-factor problem, find unknowns in equations, solve division word
problems, and build strategy-based fluency within 100.
**Age fit:** grade 3 (ages 8–9); adult-led, 30-minute sessions.

All external links were checked on **2026-10-23**. Direct pages were opened
and read; video links are clearly labeled preferred-channel **search links**
(the adult previews before showing). Every core objective has a free,
no-account route.

## Web references (verified 2026-10-23)

1. **Common Core State Standards — Grade 3, Operations & Algebraic Thinking**
   `https://www.thecorestandards.org/Math/Content/3/OA/`
   Official source for the 3.OA codes used in this unit; descriptions
   corroborated against official-text mirrors in search on 2026-10-23
   (A.2, A.3, A.4, B.6, C.7 verified; the track's 2026-10-17 full-domain
   read is recorded in the scope-and-sequence). Adult reference; no
   account needed.
2. **The Math Learning Center — free math apps**
   `https://www.mathlearningcenter.org/apps`
   Free, no-account web apps; the **Number Line** app supports skip-count
   group counting (Lessons 02, 06) and **Partial Product Finder**
   represents multiplication as arrays with matching dimensions — ideal
   for checking division by its multiplication partner. Opened and read
   2026-10-23.
3. **NCTM Illuminations — standards-based lessons and interactives (PK–12)**
   `https://www.nctm.org/illuminations/`
   From the National Council of Teachers of Mathematics; lessons and
   interactive tools for demonstrating division models. Opened and read
   2026-10-23; free, no account for the interactives index.
4. **NRICH — division and multiplication problems (University of Cambridge)**
   `https://nrich.maths.org/`
   Free, no-account enrichment problems and sharing/grouping
   investigations for the Lesson 02/05 extensions; reputable university
   mathematics outreach. Opened and read 2026-10-23.
5. **Khan Academy — third-grade division practice (labeled search link)**
   `https://www.khanacademy.org/search?search_again=1&page_search_query=third+grade+division`
   Free practice and videos organized by grade-3 topic; use the division
   units alongside Lessons 01–06. Free account optional, not required for
   videos. The adult previews before sharing.
6. **Open Curriculum — Division as an Unknown Factor (topic outline)**
   `https://opencurriculum.org/resources/download/7079/`
   Topic outline linking 3.OA.2, 3.OA.6, 3.OA.3, and 3.OA.4, describing
   division as an unknown-factor problem with group-size and
   number-of-groups interpretations. Found in search 2026-10-23; adult
   background reference, not a learner page.
