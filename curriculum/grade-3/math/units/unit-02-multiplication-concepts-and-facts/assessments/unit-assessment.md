# Unit Assessment — Unit 02: Multiplication Concepts and Facts

**Session:** 16 · **Time:** 35 minutes · **Points:** 49 · **Covers objectives
1–5**

The adult reads items aloud if needed. Drawings are required where asked —
the drawing is part of the answer. Score bands and reteach moves are in the
[teacher guide](../teacher-guide.md#readiness-bands-unit-assessment-49-pts).

## Section A — What multiplication means

**1.** Draw **5 groups of 3** dots. Write the multiplication equation and the
product. (4 pts: 2 drawing, 1 equation, 1 product)

**2.** An array has **6 rows and 4 columns**. Write the multiplication
equation. Find the product by skip counting and show the counts you said.
(4 pts: 1 equation, 2 product, 1 skip-count work)

**3.** Write a one- or two-sentence story for **4 × 7 = 28**. (3 pts)

## Section B — Word problems

**4.** *The library has 8 shelves with 6 books on each shelf. How many books
in all?* Write the equation with a symbol for the unknown, solve, and label
the answer. (5 pts: 2 equation, 2 answer, 1 label)

**5.** *A grasshopper hops 9 centimeters each hop. It hops 5 times. How far
does it go?* Draw the hops on a number line, write the equation, and solve.
(5 pts: 2 drawing, 1 equation, 2 answer)

**6.** *A garden has 4 rows of 8 flowers and 2 more rows of 8 flowers. How
many flowers are there in all?* Show two multiplications and add the parts.
(5 pts: 2 + 2 multiplications, 1 total)

## Section C — Turnarounds and break-aparts

**7.** You know **9 × 4 = 36**. Write the turnaround equation and explain in
one sentence why the product is the same. (4 pts: 1 equation, 3 explanation)

**8.** Break **7 × 9** into two friendly pieces. Draw the cut (or describe
it), write both mini-equations, and find the total. (5 pts: 1 cut, 2 pieces,
2 total)

## Section D — Fluency

**9.** Find each product. (10 pts: 1 each)

a. 2 × 8 = __
b. 6 × 5 = __
c. 9 × 3 = __
d. 4 × 4 = __
e. 7 × 6 = __
f. 8 × 8 = __
g. 3 × 7 = __
h. 9 × 9 = __
i. 5 × 9 = __
j. 6 × 9 = __

## Section E — Explain your thinking

**10.** Explain why **6 × 4 = 4 × 6** using an array. Draw the array, show
the turnaround, and write two or three sentences. (4 pts: 1 drawing, 3
explanation)
