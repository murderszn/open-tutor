# Formative Quiz — Unit 02 (Session 15)

**Time:** 20 minutes · **Points:** 29 · **Covers objectives 1–5**

The adult reads each item aloud if needed; the learner writes the answers.
This quiz checks readiness for the unit assessment — score below 22 and
re-teach per the [teacher guide](../teacher-guide.md) before Session 16.

**1.** Draw **3 groups of 4** dots. Write the multiplication equation and the
product. (3 pts: 1 drawing, 1 equation, 1 product)

**2.** An array has **5 rows and 3 columns**. Write the multiplication
equation and find the product. (3 pts)

**3.** *There are 7 bags with 5 apples in each bag. How many apples in all?*
Write the equation with a symbol for the unknown, solve, and label the
answer. (4 pts: 2 equation, 2 labeled answer)

**4.** You know **6 × 7 = 42**. What is **7 × 6**? Explain in one sentence
why you do not need to rebuild the array. (3 pts: 1 answer, 2 explanation)

**5.** Break **8 × 6** into **8 × 5** and **8 × 1**. Write both mini-equations
and the total. (4 pts: 2 pieces, 2 total)

**6.** Find each product. Name the strategy you used for at least two of
them. (8 pts: 2 each)

a. 3 × 9 = __
b. 7 × 5 = __
c. 9 × 6 = __
d. 4 × 8 = __

**7.** Zoe says: "4 × 3 = 7, because multiplication means add." Do you agree?
Explain in one or two sentences. (4 pts: 2 verdict, 2 explanation)
