## Misconception notes (by lesson)

- **L1:** × read as "and" → 4 × 5 = 9. Fix: no equation until groups are
  built and counted group-by-group.
- **L2:** rows/columns swapped in speech and equations. Fix: finger sweep
  (across = row) said aloud on every array for two weeks.
- **L3:** keyword hunting ("each means multiply" without a drawing). Fix:
  retell-and-draw is non-negotiable this week; unlabeled answers are
  unfinished.
- **L4:** turnarounds applied to subtraction (9 − 4 = 4 − 9). Fix: the 9 − 4
  counterexample, stated as a rule — turnarounds are a multiplication (and
  addition) privilege.
- **L5:** single-piece answers (35 for 7 × 8 after breaking into 35 + 14);
  jagged cuts that break rows. Fix: "count the pieces, add the pieces"; grid
  paper.
- **L6:** nines slip (9 × 7 = 70 − 1); claiming memory while guessing. Fix:
  "take away a 7, not a 1"; oral spot-checks of the "Know it" pile.

| Signal | Reteach move | Session |

## Readiness bands (unit assessment, 49 pts)

- **42–49 — Ready for Unit 03 (division).** Celebrate the strategy language;
  keep the "Know it / Strategy" card alive as division will reuse it.
- **34–41 — Ready with a note.** Name the one weakest section (A–E) and
  schedule two 10-minute warm-ups on it during Unit 03's first week.
- **Below 34 — Reteach before Unit 03.** Use the reteach table above for the
  two lowest sections; re-assess with parallel items (change the numbers,
  keep the structures).

## Accessibility and pacing notes

- Every lesson lists language-access and alternate-response modes; the default
  is build-first, symbols-second, oral answers accepted with adult scribing.
- Sessions are 30 minutes by design. A learner who needs more time takes two
  sittings per session rather than rushed single sittings — the pacing table
  counts sessions, not days.
- The generated illustration is scene-setting only; all counts needed for
  questions are also given in text (see the Lesson 02 text-only alternative).
- No timed speed tests anywhere in this unit, per the track
  scope-and-sequence. Fluency is strategy + honest memory, never a stopwatch.

## What comes next

built here: equal groups become fair shares, arrays get read backwards
(□ × 6 = 42), turnarounds become fact families, and the "Know it / Strategy"
