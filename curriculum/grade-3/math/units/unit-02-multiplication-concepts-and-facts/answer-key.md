## Lesson 05

**Independent practice**

6. 6 × 8 = (6 × 4) + (6 × 4) = 24 + 24 = **48**.
7. 9 × 7 = (9 × 5) + (9 × 2) = 45 + 18 = **63**.
8. 8 × 9 = (8 × 10) − (8 × 1) = 80 − 8 = **72**. Subtracting works because
   9 = 10 − 1, so 8 × 9 = 8 × (10 − 1) = 80 − 8.

10. 6 × 9 array with cut line, pieces labeled (e.g., 6 × 5 = 30 and
    6 × 4 = 24), total **54**.

    **56**.
