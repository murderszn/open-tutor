# Resource Pack — Unit 02: Multiplication Concepts and Facts

**Goal:** support an adult teaching a grade-3 learner to interpret products
as equal groups and arrays, solve multiplication word problems, use
commutative and distributive strategies, and build fluent recall within 100.
**Age fit:** grade 3 (ages 8–9); adult-led, 30-minute sessions.

All external links were checked on **2026-10-17**. Direct pages were opened
and read; video links are clearly labeled preferred-channel **search links**
(the adult previews before showing). Every core objective has a free,
no-account route.

## Web references (verified 2026-10-17)

1. **Common Core State Standards — Grade 3, Operations & Algebraic Thinking**
   `https://www.thecorestandards.org/Math/Content/3/OA/`
   Official source for the 3.OA codes used in this unit; full domain text read
   2026-10-17 (A.1, A.3, B.5, C.7 verified; footnote confirmed that students
   need not use formal property terms). Adult reference; no account needed.
2. **The Math Learning Center — free math apps**
   `https://www.mathlearningcenter.org/apps`
   Free, no-account web apps; the **Partial Product Finder** represents
   multiplication as arrays with matching dimensions — ideal for Lessons
   04–05. Opened and read 2026-10-17.
3. **NCTM Illuminations — standards-based lessons and interactives (PK–12)**
   `https://www.nctm.org/illuminations/`
   From the National Council of Teachers of Mathematics; lessons and
   interactive tools for demonstrating multiplication models. Opened
   2026-10-17; free, no account for the interactives index.
4. **NRICH — multiplication and division problems (University of Cambridge)**
   `https://nrich.maths.org/`
   Free, no-account enrichment problems and array investigations for the
   Lesson 02/05 extensions; reputable university mathematics outreach.
5. **Khan Academy — third-grade multiplication practice (labeled search link)**
   `https://www.khanacademy.org/search?search_again=1&page_search_query=third+grade+multiplication`
   Free practice and videos organized by grade-3 topic; use the
   multiplication units alongside Lessons 01–06. Free account optional, not
   required for videos. The adult previews before sharing.
6. **Wikipedia — Multiplication**
   `https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Multiplication`
   Adult background reference for terminology (factor, product, commutative,
   distributive); checked 2026-10-17. Not a learner page.
