# Answer Key — Unit 04 (Adult Only)

Do not give this key to the learner. Sample open responses are marked
"sample — accept any correct version." Every other answer below was
solved independently of the lesson text and checked against it.

## Lesson 01

**Guided:** (1) *happy* adjective, *dog* noun, *runs* verb, *quickly*
adverb. (2) *She* pronoun, *carries* verb, *red* adjective, *kite*
noun. (3) *Sam* noun, *walks* verb, *slowly* adverb, *school* noun.
(4) *They* pronoun, *sang* verb, *loud* adjective, *song* noun.
**Independent:** (1) *tall* adjective, *tree* noun, *sways* verb,
*gently* adverb. (2) *He* pronoun, *reads* verb, *funny* adjective,
*book* noun. (3) *Birds* noun, *sing* verb, *sweetly* adverb, *morning*
noun. (4) *We* pronoun, *found* verb, *smooth* adjective, *stone* noun.
**Applied (sample):** "The girl flies a red kite." (noun *girl*,
verb *flies*); "The brown dog runs fast." (noun *dog*, verb *runs*).
Any true picture sentence with a noun underlined and a verb circled
counts.
**Exit:** *brave* adjective, *girl* noun, *climbs* verb, *carefully*
adverb.

## Lesson 02

**Guided:** (1) peaches, stories, men, teeth. (2) Abstract nouns:
*kindness, bravery* (*rock* and *kite* are concrete). (3) looked,
will play, reads. (4) "The birds sing." (plural subject → *sing*);
"Maya and Sam walk home." (two subjects → *walk*); "She has a kite."
(one subject → *has*).
**Independent:** (1) dishes, puppies, feet, deer. (2) *childhood*;
*joy*. (3) went, caught, taught, jumped. (4) sleeps, sleep, are, does.
**Applied (sample):** Past: "Yesterday Maya flew her kite."
Present: "Today she flies her kite." Future: "Tomorrow she will fly
her kite." Verbs underlined; agreement must hold in each.
**Exit:** were; women.

## Lesson 03

**Guided:** (1) higher; highest. (2) more interesting; most.
(3) more beautifully; fastest. (4) "We stayed home because it
rained."; "Maya can fly a kite, and Sam can ride a bike."
**Independent:** (1) bigger; biggest. (2) harder; hardest.
(3) softer; softest. (4) "Maya wanted a kite, so she saved her
coins."; "The dog barked because it saw the cat."
**Applied (sample):** Comparative: "My kite is higher than yours."
Superlative: "My kite is the highest of the three." Because-sentence:
"I stayed inside because it rained."
**Exit:** faster; "It is sunny, so we will play outside."

## Lesson 04

**Guided:** (1) simple; compound; complex; not a sentence (a
dependent clause alone). (2) "The dog runs across the park." (any
subject counts); "It was windy, so we flew kites." (comma + *so*).
(3) Sample: "We had a sunny, breezy day." — accept any stronger,
truthful replacement. (4) "We are going to fly kites."
**Independent:** (1) simple; compound; complex; not a sentence
("After lunch." has no subject or verb). (2) Sample: "The kite rose
because the wind blew." (complex) or "The wind blew, so the kite
rose." (compound) — either counts if the joining is correct.
(3) Sample: "The dog is enormous." (4) "I do not know where my kite
is."
**Applied (sample):** Simple: "The sun shines." Compound: "The sun
shines, and the dog runs." Complex: "The dog runs when it sees a
squirrel." Labels must match.
**Exit:** complex; "Sam went to the park." (any subject counts).

## Lesson 05

**Guided:** (1) "A Day at the Park"; "The Red Kite". (2) "Maya lives
at 12 Oak Street, Springfield, Illinois." (3) 'Maya said, "Let's fly
kites."'; '"It is windy," said Sam.' (4) "Maya's kite"; "the dogs'
toys"; "the children's book".
**Independent:** (1) "Sam and the Big Dog"; "My Trip to the Lake".
(2) "Sam lives at 8 Hill Road, Riverton, Ohio." (3) '"We won,"
shouted Lin.'; 'Lin asked, "Can you help me?"' (4) its; dog's
(one dog); children's.
**Applied (sample):** 'Maya said, "Look at my kite!"' / '"It is
high," said Sam.' Possessive: *Maya's kite* or *Sam's string*.
**Exit:** 'Sam said, "I lost my kite."'; "Lin's hat".

## Lesson 06

**Guided:** (1) hopping; hoping; cried; happiness. (2) Any *-ake*
word: *bake, snake, lake, wake*; "back" (ck after a short vowel).
(3) *milk*: yes — *map* (ma…) < *milk* (mi…) < *moon* (mo…).
*mouse*: no — *mouse* (mo-u…) comes after *moon* (mo-o…).
(4) because; friend; through; said.
**Independent:** (1) swimming; smiled; tries; funniest.
(2) Any *-ight* light word: *light* (also accept *bright*); ck.
(3) *peach*: yes — *park* (pa…) < *peach* (pe…) < *play* (pl…).
*pond*: no — *pond* (po…) comes after *play* (pl…).
(4) they; where; again; school.
**Applied (corrected paragraph):** "Maya is running to the park.
She is hopping over a puddle. Her friend Sam carried the kite. They
had a great day." Four fixes: running, hopping, friend, carried.
**Exit:** happiness. *supper*: yes — *sun* (su-n…) < *supper*
(su-p…) < *swim* (sw…).

## Formative Quiz (13 points)

1. adjective. 2. verb. 3. babies; feet (1 point each).
4. walked. 5. bark. 6. fastest. 7. compound.
8. 'Lin said, "Go."' (accept "Go!" for the exclamation).
9. "Sam's ball". 10. swimming.
11. Sample main idea: "Beavers build dams that make ponds where they
live." — accept any wording naming beaver dams/ponds as homes.
12. *un-* means "not" (*unhappy* = not happy).

**Scoring guide:** 12–13 = ready for Unit 05; 9–11 = reteach the
missed lesson's skill in Session 15; below 9 = reteach two skills and
re-quiz with Practice Sets before the assessment.

## Culminating Unit Assessment (40 points)

**Part A (8):** gentle adjective; wind noun; moved verb; slowly
adverb; She pronoun; held verb; shiny adjective; kite noun.
**Part B (10):** foxes; ladies; teeth; children; ran; played; sings;
sing; are; has.
**Part C (10):** (1) longer (2) shortest (3) more colorful (4) most
(5) simple (6) compound (7) complex (8) not a sentence
(9) "It rained, but we flew kites anyway."
(10) "Maya smiled because she saw her kite."
**Part D — Edit (8):** "Maya wrote a story called The Kite Day. Sam
lives at 3 Birch Lane, Dayton, Ohio. Sam said, "I love kites." The
dog's toy is lost. Maya is hoping for wind."
Corrections: (1) Maya (2) The Kite Day (3) comma after Lane
(4) Sam (5) I (6) dialogue comma + quotation marks
(7) dog's (8) hoping.
**Part D — Write (4):** Sample compound: "The wind blew, and the
kite rose high." Sample complex: "The kite rose when the wind blew."
Score 1 point for the correct type and 1 point for correct joining
and punctuation in each.

**Scoring guide:** 36–40 = strong; 28–35 = ready with one reteach
note; below 28 = reteach the two weakest parts before Unit 05 and
keep the project as the portfolio evidence.
