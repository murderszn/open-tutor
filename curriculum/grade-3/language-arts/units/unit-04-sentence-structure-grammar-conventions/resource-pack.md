# Resource Pack — Unit 04: Sentence Structure, Grammar, and Conventions

## Summary

**Goal:** give the guiding adult free, no-account routes to support
grade-3 sentence structure, grammar, and conventions — parts of
speech, plurals and verb tenses, agreement, comparisons, conjunctions,
simple/compound/complex sentences, word choice, titles, addresses,
dialogue punctuation, possessives, spelling patterns, suffix spelling,
and beginning-dictionary use.
**Age fit:** grade 3 (about 8–9); adult previews every video before
use; videos supplement the lessons and never replace explicit teaching
or the adult check. Checked 2026-10-30 unless noted.

## Free, no-account routes for core learning

Every core objective can be met without any account and without any
video: the unit's own sentences, the adult's read-aloud, the FCRR
routines, the Reading Rockets articles, and the CCSS standards text
are all free and account-free. Videos are enrichment only.
