# Formative Quiz — Unit 04 (Week 18)

**Name:** ______________________ **Date:** ______________

Adult: read the directions aloud if needed. Score with the
[answer key](../answer-key.md#formative-quiz). 13 points total.
Write the reteach list the same day (see
[teacher guide](../teacher-guide.md)).

1. In "The quick fox jumps gracefully," what job does *quick* do?
   (noun / pronoun / verb / adjective / adverb)
2. In "The quick fox jumps gracefully," what job does *jumps* do?
   (noun / pronoun / verb / adjective / adverb)
3. Write the plural of *baby* and of *foot*.
4. Choose the past tense: "Yesterday, Maya (walk / walked) to the
   park."
5. Choose the verb that agrees: "The dogs (bark / barks) at the mail
   carrier."
6. Choose: "Of the three dogs, Rex is the (faster / fastest)."
7. What type is this sentence — simple, compound, or complex?
   "Maya runs, and Sam cheers."
8. Fix the dialogue punctuation: 'lin said "go"'.
9. Rewrite with a possessive: "the ball of Sam".
10. Add the suffix: *swim + ing*.
11. Read this short passage (adult reads aloud first):

    > Beavers build dams across streams. The dams make deep ponds.
    > Beavers live in lodges in the ponds.

    What is the main idea of the passage, in your own words?
12. In the word *unhappy*, what does the prefix *un-* mean?
