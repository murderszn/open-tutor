## Part C — Comparisons, sentence types, joining (10 points, 1 each)

Choose the correct comparison:

1. "Of two ropes, this one is (longer / longest)."
2. "It is the (shorter / shortest) of the four ropes."
3. "Her kite is (more colorful / most colorful) than mine."
4. "This is the (more / most) colorful kite here."

Label each sentence *simple*, *compound*, *complex*, or *not a
sentence*:

5. "The wind blows."
6. "The wind blows, and the kite rises."
7. "The kite rises when the wind blows."
8. "When the wind blows."

Join each pair into one sentence with the conjunction in parentheses:

9. "It rained. We flew kites anyway." (*but*)
10. "Maya smiled. She saw her kite." (*because*)
