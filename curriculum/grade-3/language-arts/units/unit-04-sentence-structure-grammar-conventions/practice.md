# Practice Sets A–F — Unit 04

Work with the adult. Say each answer aloud or write it — either counts.
Full solutions are in the [answer key](answer-key.md#practice-sets).

## Set A — Part-of-speech jobs (Lesson 01)

Name the job (noun, pronoun, verb, adjective, adverb) of each word.

1. In "The soft wind blows gently": *soft*.
2. In "The soft wind blows gently": *wind*.
3. In "The soft wind blows gently": *blows*.
4. In "The soft wind blows gently": *gently*.
5. In "They play happily": *They*.
6. In "They play happily": *play*.
7. In "They play happily": *happily*.
8. In "A red bird sings": *red*.
9. In "A red bird sings": *bird*.
10. In "A red bird sings": *sings*.

## Set C — Comparisons and joining (Lesson 03)

1. Choose: "Of two bikes, mine is (faster / fastest)."
2. Choose: "Of three bikes, mine is the (faster / fastest)."
3. Choose: "This story is (longer / longest) than that one."
4. Choose: "It is the (longer / longest) story in the book."
5. Fill in: *good → better → ___*.
6. Fill in: *bad → ___ → worst*.
7. Join with *and*: "Lin swims. Lin reads."
8. Join with *because*: "We went home. It was dark."
9. Join with *when*: "The bell rings. We line up."
10. Join with *but*: "The kite is big. The kite is light."

## Set D — Sentence types, word choice, spoken vs. written (Lesson 04)

1. Label *simple*, *compound*, *complex*, or *not a sentence*:
   "The rain falls."
2. Label: "The rain falls, and the wind blows."
3. Label: "We read when the rain falls."
4. Label: "When the rain falls."
5. Combine into one sentence: "Sam built a kite. Sam painted it red."
6. Replace the weak verb with a stronger one: "The kite went up high."
7. Rewrite in written style: "I wanna go to the park."
8. Fix the run-on: "It was sunny we played all day."

## Set E — Titles, addresses, dialogue, possessives (Lesson 05)

5. Fix the dialogue: 'Sam said "look at that kite"'.

8. Rewrite with a possessive: "the string of the kite".

10. Choose: "The dog wagged (its / it's) tail."
