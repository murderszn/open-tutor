# Formative Quiz — Unit 02, Week 10 (Session 15)

**Grade 3 Language Arts · Literary comprehension: character and theme**
Name: ______________________ Date: __________

Read the story, then answer. For questions marked **[E]**, include the words
*"I know this because the story says…"* with quoted text.

> **Story: "The Lighthouse Cat"** (original)
>
> "Every evening, Marisol the lighthouse keeper's cat climbed the winding
> stairs to the lamp room. The gulls called her the bravest cat on the
> cliff — but Marisol was not brave at all. Her paws trembled on the cold
> stone steps.
>
> One stormy night the lamp flickered and went dark. Ships were coming, and
> the rocks were waiting. Marisol's heart pounded like a drum, but she
> climbed anyway — step, step, step — until she reached the lamp. She batted
> the loose wire with her paw, and the light blazed back on.
>
> The keeper found her curled beside the warm lamp, purring. 'My brave
> girl,' he whispered. Marisol purred louder. She was still scared of the
> stairs. But she had climbed them anyway."

1. **[E]** Where does Marisol climb every evening? (RL.3.1)
2. **[E]** Why did the lamp going dark matter? (RL.3.1)
3. What kind of story is this — fable, folktale, or myth? How do you know?
   (RL.3.2)
4. Name one **trait** Marisol shows and one **action** that proves it.
   (RL.3.3)
5. How does Marisol **feel** on the stairs? Quote one detail. (RL.3.3)
6. What is the **central message** of this story? Write it as advice.
   (RL.3.2)
7. List **one** key detail that carries your message in question 6. (RL.3.2)
8. "Her heart pounded like a drum." Literal or nonliteral? What does it
   really mean? (RL.3.4, L.3.5.a)
9. The gulls call Marisol "the bravest cat on the cliff," but the narrator
   says "Marisol was not brave at all." Whose point of view should you
   trust more — the gulls' or the narrator's? Why? (RL.3.6)
10. **Explain your reasoning:** A classmate says the message of the story is
    "Cats like lighthouses." Do you agree? Use story details to explain.
    (RL.3.2)

**Scoring:** 10 items × 1 point = 10. 8+ meets; below 8 → same-day reteach
(see teacher guide). Solutions: [answer-key.md](../answer-key.md#quiz).
