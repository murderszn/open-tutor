# Formative Quiz — Unit 05, Week 22

**Points:** 12 (6 items × 2 points) · **Time:** 20–25 minutes
**How to use:** the adult reads directions aloud if needed. The learner
answers in writing or orally; oral answers are fine and scored the same
way. Score with the [answer key](../answer-key.md#formative-quiz--week-22)
the same day; write the reteach list before the next session. This quiz
is formative — it is not a grade, and it is not a standardized or
validated assessment.

## Items

**1. (2 points) The beginning's job.** Read the beginning. Which
questions does it fail to answer — Who, Where, When, or What?

> "It was morning. Something exciting was about to happen."

**2. (2 points) Time words.** Choose the time word that fits, and say
why the other two do not fit: "Maya pulled the string gently. ___,
the tail slipped off the branch." (a) First (b) Suddenly (c) At last

**3. (2 points) Dialogue punctuation.** Rewrite this exchange correctly,
with a new paragraph for each speaker: maya said look the kite is
moving leo said pull gently

**4. (2 points) Dialogue that does a job.** Which line of dialogue shows
a feeling? (a) "Hi," said Maya. "Hi," said Leo. (b) "Wait!" cried Leo.
"Don't pull hard — you'll snap the string!" Explain your choice in one
sentence.

**5. (2 points) Closure.** Which ending is a closure, and why?
(a) "Maya got the kite. The end." (b) "At last the kite floated down
into Maya's hands, and she hugged it tight, knowing she would check
the wind next time."

**6. (2 points) Revision.** A learner's draft says: "Maya was sad. The
kite was stuck." Name the ARMS move that would improve it most, and
write the improved version (one or two sentences).
