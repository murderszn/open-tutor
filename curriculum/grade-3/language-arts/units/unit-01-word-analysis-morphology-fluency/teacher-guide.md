# Teacher Guide — Unit 01: Word Analysis, Morphology, Vocabulary, Fluency

For the guiding adult. Read the [unit README](README.md) and the
[scope-and-sequence](../../scope-and-sequence.md) before starting. Sessions
are 25–30 minutes, four per week (track weeks 3–6). Review practice the
same day; keep all learner work, names, and scores in private storage.

## How to use this guide

Each lesson below lists: the misconception to watch for, what "good
enough" looks like, and the exact reteach move if the learner struggles.
The [answer key](answer-key.md) solves every item. Nothing here is shown
to the learner.

## Lesson 01 — multisyllable decoding {#lesson-01}

**Watch for:** whole-word guessing from first letters; freezing after a
wrong break; counting silent e as a vowel sound.
**Good enough:** 6 of 8 new words broken and read; the learner can state
the one-vowel-sound-per-syllable rule.
**Reteach:** drop to two-syllable closed-syllable words only (*sunset,
basket, rabbit*); do the chin-drop test on every word before breaking.
If *y* confuses (*happy*), park it: "today, y is a vowel here — we will
meet it properly later."

## Lesson 02 — prefixes {#lesson-02}

**Watch for:** letter-matching without the removal test (*under, uncle*);
mixing up *un-* and *re-* meanings.
**Good enough:** 6 of 8 predictions correct with a sentence check stated.
**Reteach:** use only *un-* and *re-* for a day with physical cards on the
puzzle image; add *dis-* and *pre-* only after those two are automatic.
For the removal test, make it a game: "Take off the blue piece — is a
real word left? No? Then it was never a prefix."

## Lesson 03 — suffixes {#lesson-03}

**Watch for:** sounding out *-tion* letter by letter; treating *terrible*
as *terr* + *ible*; swapping *-ful* and *-less*.
**Good enough:** 6 of 8 Latin-suffix words read with the chunk named; 4 of
5 derivational pairs explained.
**Reteach:** teach *-tion* as a single sight-chunk with 10 examples before
any breaking; anchor *-ful*/*-less* as opposites with *careful/careless*
flashcards. Keep derivational (use) vs. Latin (read) as two separate
conversations until both are solid.

## Lesson 04 — word-meaning strategies {#lesson-04}

**Watch for:** guessing from "feel" without quoted evidence; stopping
after one failed tool; applying Tool 2 to non-affixed words.
**Good enough:** 6 of 8 inferences correct, each with the tool named and
evidence quoted.
**Reteach:** model the full detective script aloud for 5 cases before the
learner tries one ("Tool 1… nothing. Tool 2… *fear* + *-less*… check…").
Require the evidence quote before accepting any answer — no quote, no
credit, kindly.

## Lesson 05 — dictionaries and rule-breakers {#lesson-05}

**Watch for:** dictionary as first resort; grabbing any numbered meaning;
sounding out rule-breakers.
**Good enough:** 4 of 5 dictionary finds correct with part of speech and
meaning 1; 10 of 12 rule-breakers read on sight.
**Reteach:** practice ABC-order navigation as its own 10-minute game
before any lookups; for rule-breakers, drill in small sets of 4 with
see-say-spell-use until each set is automatic before adding more.

## Lesson 06 — fluency {#lesson-06}

**Watch for:** racing; skipping self-correction; performing expression
over inaccurate reading.
**Good enough:** ≤4 uncorrected errors on Read 3; visible expression
choices; 2+ self-corrections logged.
**Reteach:** if accuracy is below ~90%, stay on Reads 1–2 with shorter
chunks (one paragraph); echo-reading (you read a line, they repeat it)
builds expression without the decoding load. **Print both passages before
session 8** — the unit assumes printed copies from Practice D onward.

## Session notes

- **Session 8 (fluency reading 1):** first repeated reading of "The Puzzle
  Box" (paragraphs 1–4). Goal is accuracy only — do not push pace.
- **Sessions 14–15 (project workshops):** protect the full 25–30 minutes
  for dictionary building; do not let the review game eat workshop 2.
- **Session 16:** quiz first (fresh mind), fluency check second. Record
  the final "Puzzle Box" timing as a baseline for Unit 02's fluency work.

## Reteach paths {#reteach-paths}

| If the learner struggles with… | Return to | Do this |
|---|---|---|
| Breaking any 2-syllable word | Lesson 01 | Chin-drop + vowel-find on 10 closed-syllable words; no new content for 2 sessions |
| Prefix meanings | Lesson 02 | *un-/re-* only with physical cards; removal-test game |
| Latin suffix chunks | Lesson 03 | Sight-chunk drills: *-tion, -sion, -able, -ible, -ment* × 10 examples each |
| Meaning inferences | Lesson 04 | Adult models 5 full detective scripts; evidence-quote required |
| Dictionary navigation | Lesson 05 | ABC-order game; then lookups with adult handling navigation |
| Reading rate/accuracy | Lesson 06 | Shorter chunks; echo reading; stay on Reads 1–2 |

**Readiness to move to Unit 02:** formative quiz ≥ 21/31 and the learner
self-corrects audibly at least sometimes. The project dictionary (8+
entries) should exist — polish can continue into Unit 02's review weeks.

## Accessibility notes

- All word cards can be large-print; the puzzle image has a full
  text-only alternative in [assets/README.md](assets/README.md).
- Every lesson includes an alternate response mode (pointing, sorting,
  dictating, recording).
- For learners sensitive to timed tasks: all "races" and "sprints" are
  self-competition or untimed at the adult's judgment — the guide's
  success criteria never require beating another person.
- If the learner cannot see the puzzle image, the adult describes it
  once ("blue left, yellow middle, red right") and thereafter refers to
  pieces by color name; the text-only alternative carries every activity.
