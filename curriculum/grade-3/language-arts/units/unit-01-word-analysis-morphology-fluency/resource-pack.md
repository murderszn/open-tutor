# Resource Pack — Unit 01: Word Analysis, Morphology, Vocabulary, Fluency

## Summary

**Goal:** give the guiding adult free, no-account routes to support
grade-3 multisyllable decoding, prefix/suffix morphology (un-, re-, dis-,
pre-; -tion/-sion/-able/-ible/-ment; -ful/-less/-ness), word-meaning
strategies (context, affix, root), beginning-dictionary use, irregularly
spelled words, and oral reading fluency — plus songs and games an
8-year-old will enjoy.
**Age fit:** grade 3 (about 8–9); all items are visual, musical, or
hands-on, with minimal reading required of the learner. Adult planning
judgment: videos supplement the lessons; they never replace explicit
teaching or the adult check.
