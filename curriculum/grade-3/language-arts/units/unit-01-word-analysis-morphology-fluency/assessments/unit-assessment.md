# Unit Assessment — Unit 01: Word Analysis, Morphology, Vocabulary, Fluency

**Purpose:** culminating check of all six unit objectives. Adult-administered
over 1–2 sittings; the learner may answer orally or in writing. Every item
is solved in [answer-key.md](../answer-key.md). This is a curriculum
draft assessment, not a standardized or validated measure.

**Name (private — do not write in the repo):** ______________ **Date:** ____

## Part A — Break and blend (RF.3.3.c) · 8 pts

1. Break each word into syllables and read it: *magnet, napkin, insect,
   silent*. (1 pt each: 4)
2. *Dentist* — how many vowel sounds? How many syllables? Write the
   breaks. (2)
3. The adult says *pumpkin* and *carpet*. The learner writes each with its
   break line(s). (1 pt each: 2)

## Part B — Prefixes and suffixes (RF.3.3.a–b; L.3.4.b) · 12 pts

4. Match each prefix to its meaning: un- / re- / dis- / pre- → "again" /
   "before" / "not, opposite of" / "not, opposite of". (2)
5. *Unlock, rewrite, disappear, preheat* — for each: name the prefix, its
   meaning, the root, and the whole word's meaning. (1 pt each: 4)
6. Read: *station, division, breakable, enjoyment*. For each, name the
   Latin suffix and read the word. (1 pt each: 4)
7. Explain the difference between *careful* and *careless* using the
   suffixes' jobs. (2)

## Part C — Word-meaning strategies (L.3.4.a–d) · 10 pts

8. "The **weary** climbers rested at once." What does *weary* mean? Quote
   your evidence. (2)
9. "Unlike the **timid** kitten, the **bold** puppy barked at the vacuum."
   What does *bold* mean? Name the clue type. (2)
10. "The movers will **transport** the boxes across town." You know *port*
    = carry. What does *transport* mean? (2)
11. Use a beginning dictionary: find *preview*. Write the part of speech
    and meaning 1. Does it match the prefix prediction (*pre-* = before +
    *view*)? (2)
12. Is *under* a prefix word? Explain with the removal test. (2)

## Part D — Rule-breakers (RF.3.3.d) · 6 pts

13. Read on sight: *said, have, of, was, were, some, come, what, where,
    again, would, could*. (6 pts: ½ pt each)

## Part E — Fluency performance (RF.3.4.a–c; L.3.6) · 8 pts

14. Read "Word Parts Parade" (in
    [Lesson 06](../lessons/lesson-06-fluency-with-purpose.md)) aloud:
    accuracy (≤2 uncorrected errors — 2 pts), expression honoring the
    exclamation points and bold words (2 pts).
15. During the read, the learner must audibly self-correct at least once
    *or* stop and verify one word with context before continuing (2 pts).
16. After reading, summarize "The Puzzle Box" in 2 sentences using one
    temporal word (*before, after, during, until, then*) and one spatial
    word (*across, between, beyond*) correctly. (2 pts.)

**Total: 44 pts.** 38–44 = secure; 30–37 = targeted review of missed parts
with the matching practice set, then re-check those items; below 30 =
reteach per the [teacher guide](../teacher-guide.md#reteach-paths).
Record only in private storage.
