# Formative Quiz — Unit 01 (session 16)

**Purpose:** check the unit's core strategies before the unit assessment.
**Format:** 12 items, 31 points, adult-administered; the learner may answer
orally or in writing. Answers in [answer-key.md](../answer-key.md) — never
beside the questions.

**Name (private — do not write in the repo):** ______________ **Date:** ____

1. Break *pocket* into syllables and read it. (2 pts: break + read)
2. How many syllables does *celebration* have? Write the breaks. (2)
3. In *replay*, what does *re-* mean? What does the whole word mean? (2)
4. *Disagree* — strip the prefix, name the root, and give the meaning. (2)
5. Look at the word-part puzzle image
   ([word-part-puzzle-pieces.png](../assets/word-part-puzzle-pieces.png),
   **an AI-generated illustration**). Which color piece would *-ness* go
   on in *kindness*? Which piece would *kind* go on? (2)
6. What does *-less* mean? Use *fearless* in a sentence that shows it. (2)
7. Read these Latin-suffix words: *action, readable, payment*. Underline
   the suffix chunk in each. (3)
8. "The **frigid** air made our noses tingle." What does *frigid* mean?
   Quote the words that told you. (2)
9. "She was **careless** with her paints." Break the word into parts and
   explain what it means. (2)
10. Read these rule-breakers: *said, through, enough, bought, would,
    laugh*. (6 pts: 1 each)
11. Find *uncomfortable* in a beginning dictionary. Write its part of
    speech and meaning 1. (2)
12. **Explain:** Why do good readers reread a sentence when something
    sounds wrong? (2 pts: self-correction protects meaning; catching your
    own errors is a skill.)

**Scoring:** 27–31 = secure, move on; 21–26 = review the missed strategy
with its lesson's guided practice; below 21 = reteach per the [teacher
guide](../teacher-guide.md#reteach-paths) before the unit assessment.
