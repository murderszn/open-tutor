# Formative Quiz — Unit 03, Week 04

**Name (private — do not put in the repository):** _______________
**Date (private):** _______________
**Directions:** The adult reads each item aloud. Answer in your own
words, by pointing, or in writing. There are no trick questions: every
answer comes from a passage or picture in this unit, or from a skill
practiced in it. Solutions are in the [answer key](../answer-key.md#quiz-week-04)
(adult only).

## Part 1 — Read and answer (use "Beavers Build a Home," Lesson 01)

1. What is the main idea of "Beavers Build a Home"? Write it in one
   sentence, in your own words.
2. Name one key detail about the dam and one key detail about the lodge.
3. The passage says the lodge door is under the water. Why does that
   help the beaver family? Answer with the passage's own reason.

## Part 2 — Sequence and connections

4. Put these pumpkin steps in order (write 1–5): (a) The pumpkin turns
   orange. (b) The farmer plants a seed. (c) A vine grows and flowers
   bloom. (d) A tiny green pumpkin grows. (e) The seed sprouts.
5. From "Why Leaves Change Color" (Lesson 03): what causes trees to stop
   making chlorophyll? Name the cause and quote the signal word.
6. Name one way a wagon and a car are alike, and one way they are
   different. Name the feature for each.

## Part 3 — Features, pictures, and point of view

7. Using Lesson 04's pretend book "All About Bees": on which page does
   "From Flower to Honey" begin, and what does the glossary say nectar
   is?
8. Look at the pond illustration in Lesson 05 (or read its text-only
   alternative). What is the difference between the dam and the lodge,
   and where is each one in the picture?
9. Is "Beavers are the most remarkable builders in the animal world" a
   fact or the author's opinion? Explain, quoting one opinion word.

## Part 4 — Comparing two texts

10. Read these two short texts (original, written for this unit) and
    answer (a)–(c):

    > **Text G — "Apples in Fall"**
    >
    > In fall, apple trees are full of red fruit. Farmers pick the
    > apples, sort them, and sell them at markets. People eat apples
    > fresh, bake them into pies, and press them into cider.
    >
    > **Text H — "Apple Trees Through the Year"**
    >
    > In spring, apple trees bloom with white flowers, and bees visit
    > the flowers. In summer, small green apples grow. In fall, the
    > apples turn red and are ready to pick. The tree rests in winter
    > until spring comes again.

    (a) What is the main idea of each text, in one sentence each?
    (b) Name one important point shared by both texts.
    (c) Name one important point found only in Text G, and one found
        only in Text H.

**Scoring guide (adult):** 10 items, each worth up to 2 points (1 for a
partly correct answer, 2 for a complete answer with evidence or a quoted
signal/feature word where asked). 17–20 = on track; 13–16 = reteach the
missed lesson focus before the unit assessment; below 13 = reteach
Lessons 01 and 03 first, then re-quiz with Practice A/C items.
