# Resource Pack — Unit 03: Informational Text: Main Idea, Structures, and Evidence

## Summary

**Goal:** give the guiding adult free, no-account routes to support
grade-3 informational reading — main idea and key details, sequence,
cause/effect, comparison, text features and search tools, illustrations,
author's point of view, and comparing two texts on one topic.
**Age fit:** grade 3 (about 8–9); adult previews every video before use;
videos supplement the lessons and never replace explicit teaching or the
adult check. Checked 2026-10-24 unless noted.

## Web references

Checked 2026-10-24 (opened in a text browser) unless noted.

1. **Reading Rockets — "How Do I Teach Main Idea?" (Timothy Shanahan)**
   `https://www.readingrockets.org/blogs/shanahan-literacy/how-do-i-teach-main-idea`
   *Why:* the adult-side grounding for Lesson 01 — teach main idea as
   part of summarizing, start with short paragraphs, delete trivial and
   repetitive information, and paraphrase the key point in one sentence
   with the text out of sight. Verified live 2026-10-24. Free, no
   account. Adult reference.
2. **Florida Center for Reading Research — Instructional Routines**
   `https://fcrr.org/reading-success/instructional-routines`
   *Why:* scripted routines matched to this unit's skills — Third Grade
   "Comprehension: Create a Summary from Expository Text" (Lessons 01,
   06), "Comprehension: Compare and Contrast the Topics in Two Texts"
   (Lesson 06), "Comprehension: Identify and Discuss the Author's
   Purpose" (Lesson 05), and "Vocabulary: Multiple Meaning Words in
   Context" (Lesson 04 word work). Verified live 2026-10-24. Free, no
   account. Adult reference.
3. **Common Core State Standards — English Language Arts, grade 3**
   `https://www.thecorestandards.org/ELA-Literacy/`
   *Why:* **adult reference only** — the authoritative text of RI.3.1–10
   behind this unit's objectives and standards table. Opened and verified
   during the track audit, 2026-10-03; no state adoption or accreditation
   claimed. Free, no account.
4. **Reading Rockets — teaching glossary (main idea, text features)**
   `https://www.readingrockets.org/teaching/glossary/`
   *Why:* plain-language adult definitions that ground the unit's
   teaching vocabulary (main idea, text features, summarization).
   Verified live pattern 2026-10-10 in Unit 01; re-checked entry point
   2026-10-24. Free, no account. Adult reference.
5. **Library of Congress — classroom materials (informational and
   primary texts)**
   `https://www.loc.gov/classroom-materials/`
   *Why:* the adult's route to real informational texts and images for
   extension work (for example, historic photographs to practice
   "picture plus words" reading from Lesson 05). Free, no account. Adult
   reference; preview for age fit before showing anything to the learner.
