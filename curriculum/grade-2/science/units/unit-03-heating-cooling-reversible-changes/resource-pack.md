# Resource Pack — Unit 03

**Goal:** Support a grade-2 learner (ages 7–8) in taking apart and
rebuilding objects, observing heating and cooling changes, sorting
changes into reversible and irreversible, and arguing with evidence.
**Age fit:** all items below were checked 2026-10-23 for grade-2
appropriateness; nothing here requires an account. Videos supplement
teaching; they never replace the lessons.

## Videos

Direct video links are not claimed here: the search results returned
for this topic were transcript mirrors and activity pages, not verified
official video pages, so this pack uses clearly labeled
preferred-channel search links, as the Resource Pack format allows.
Open the search together with the learner and pick a current episode;
preview for accuracy, advertising, and age suitability before showing.

1. **Preferred-channel search (SciShow Kids):** search YouTube for
   `SciShow Kids frost` — the channel's winter-science episodes model
   frost forming and melting for early elementary viewers, supporting
   Lessons 03–04 (freezing, thawing, and the frozen-leaf example).
2. **Preferred-channel search (SciShow Kids):** search YouTube for
   `SciShow Kids icicles melting` — snow and ice melting in sunlight,
   supporting Lesson 02 (warming melts ice).
3. **Preferred-channel search (Sid the Science Kid):** search YouTube
   for `Sid the Science Kid ice pops melt` — an ice pop melting and
   refreezing storyline, supporting Lessons 02–04 (melt → freeze round
   trip, reversible change).
4. **Preferred-channel search (Crash Course Kids):** search YouTube for
   `Crash Course Kids states of matter` — solid/liquid changes for
   early elementary viewers, supporting Lessons 02–03 vocabulary
   (melt, freeze, solid, liquid).

## Web references

1. **NGSS disciplinary core idea PS1.A page** (nextgenscience.org) —
   official performance-expectation text for 2-PS1-3 and 2-PS1-4,
   grade-2 band. Reused from the Unit 01 verification (2026-10-09);
   wording re-corroborated 2026-10-23 against the sources below.
   https://www.nextgenscience.org/disciplinary-core-idea/ps1a-structure-and-properties-matter?page=4
2. **NSTA reproduction of the official NGSS 2-PS1 document** (PDF) —
   the full performance expectations, clarification statements, and
   assessment boundaries the scope-and-sequence was verified against
   (2026-10-03). Re-corroborated 2026-10-23.
   https://static.nsta.org/ngss/20130509/dci-individual/2-PS1-MatterAndItsInteractions.pdf
3. **ACS Inquiry in Action, Grade 2, Lesson 5.1 NGSS alignment** (PDF) —
   American Chemical Society classroom lesson on changes caused by
   heating and cooling: room-temperature butter heated in warm water
   and cooled in ice water, with the 2-PS1-4 alignment and the
   cause-and-effect crosscutting concept. Opened and verified
   2026-10-23; direct model for Lessons 02–03 and the project.
   https://www.acs.org/content/dam/acsorg/education/k-8/inquiry-in-action/second-grade/g2-l5.1-ngss.pdf
4. **KidsDiscover, "Heat, Cold, and Energy — The Science of Ice"**
   (teacher resource page) — why a hand feels cold on ice (heat leaving
   the hand), ice floating, and a salt-and-ice classroom activity.
   Teacher background for Lessons 02–03; search-verified 2026-10-23.
   https://kidsdiscover.com/teacherresources/science-of-ice/
5. **Science Sparks, "What is melting?"** — a simple ice-cube melting
   activity (cubes in different places, timed observations, melt
   tables) matching the project design. Search-verified 2026-10-23.
   https://www.science-sparks.com/fun-sparks-looking-at-melting/
