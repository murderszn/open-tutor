# Unit Assessment — Unit 03

**How to use:** The adult reads each item aloud. The learner may answer
orally, by pointing, by drawing, by moving objects, or in writing; the
adult scribes dictated answers. About 25 minutes. Check the same day
with [answer-key.md](../answer-key.md). This is a check for teaching —
not a standardized or validated test.

1. **Take apart and rebuild.** The adult builds a model from 7 bricks.
   The learner takes it apart, counts the bricks, rebuilds the same
   bricks into a new object, and counts again. Then the learner tells:
   (a) what changed, (b) what stayed the same, and (c) how counting
   proves no piece was added or lost.
2. **The count check.** "A puzzle has 12 pieces. After rebuilding, Sam
   counts 11 pieces." What might have happened, and what should Sam do?
3. **Warming — ice.** "An ice cube sits in warm sunlight for 30
   minutes." Describe what happens, name the change (melt/freeze/
   soften/harden), and name the cause.
4. **Warming — butter.** "Firm butter sits in a warm room for an hour."
   Describe what happens, name the change, and name the cause.
5. **Cooling — water.** "Liquid water sits in the freezer overnight."
   Describe what happens, name the change, and name the cause.
6. **Cooling — butter.** "Soft butter sits in the refrigerator for an
   hour." Describe what happens, name the change, and name the cause.
7. **Sort.** Sort these four changes under "CAN GO BACK" or "CANNOT GO
   BACK," and give a reason for each: (a) ice melts into water,
   (b) an egg is cooked, (c) water freezes into ice, (d) a leaf freezes
   and turns brown.
8. **Image question.** Look at
   [reversible-irreversible-changes.png](../assets/reversible-irreversible-changes.png).
   (a) Which scene shows changes that can go back? (b) Name one change
   in the other scene and explain why it cannot go back.
   *(Text-only alternative: the picture has two scenes. Left: a glass
   bowl in sunlight with ice cubes melting into liquid water, plus a
   small dish of soft, spreadable butter. Right: a frying pan holding a
   cooked egg that is white and firm, plus a brown, curled leaf covered
   with frost crystals.)*
9. **Heated paper.** "The adult shows two paper samples: fresh paper
   (flat, white, smooth) and paper that was heated and cooled earlier
   (brown, curled, stiff)." Can the browned paper go back to fresh
   paper? Is heating paper reversible or irreversible? Explain.
10. **Argument.** Build a full argument with the frame: "My claim is
    that some changes caused by heating or cooling can be reversed and
    some cannot. My reversible evidence is ___. My irreversible
    evidence is ___. This shows ___." Both halves must be present, and
    the "this shows" link must connect the evidence to the claim.

**Scoring guide:** 2 = correct with explanation or correct procedure;
1 = correct answer without explanation; 0 = not yet. Items map to
objectives: Q1–Q2 → Objective 1 (2-PS1-3); Q3–Q4 → Objective 2;
Q5–Q6 → Objective 3; Q7–Q9 → Objective 4; Q10 → Objective 5 (2-PS1-4).
16–20 = ready to move on. 11–15 = move on with Session 15-style reteach
of missed objectives. 10 or below = repeat the missed lessons before
Unit 04; keep the project poster as the reteach anchor.
