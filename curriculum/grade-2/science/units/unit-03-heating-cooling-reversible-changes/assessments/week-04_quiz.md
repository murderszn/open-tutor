# Week-04 Quiz (formative — Unit 03)

**How to use:** The adult reads each item aloud. The learner may answer
orally, by pointing, by drawing, or in writing; the adult scribes
dictated answers. Check the same day with [answer-key.md](../answer-key.md).
8 items, about 15 minutes. This is a check for teaching — not a grade.

1. **Take apart.** "Mira builds a house from 8 bricks, takes it apart,
   and rebuilds the same 8 bricks into a bridge." What stayed the same?
   What changed?
2. **Warming or cooling?** "An ice cube sits in the sun and becomes a
   puddle." Was that warming or cooling? How do you know?
3. **Cooling.** "Liquid water goes into the freezer overnight." What
   will it be in the morning, and what is that change called?
4. **Sort.** "Soft butter goes into the refrigerator and becomes firm
   again." Is that reversible or irreversible? Give a reason.
5. **Image question.** Look at
   [reversible-irreversible-changes.png](../assets/reversible-irreversible-changes.png).
   Which scene shows changes that can go back — left or right? Name
   one change in the other scene that cannot go back, and say why.
   *(Text-only alternative: the picture has two scenes. Left: a glass
   bowl in sunlight with ice cubes melting into liquid water, plus a
   small dish of soft, spreadable butter. Right: a frying pan holding a
   cooked egg that is white and firm, plus a brown, curled leaf covered
   with frost crystals.)*
6. **Read the table.** (Adult shows this table — fictional practice
   data, not the learner's data:)

   | Place | Cubes melted after 30 minutes |
   |---|---|
   | Sunny windowsill | 4 of 4 |
   | Shady corner | 1 of 4 |

   Which place melted the ice faster? Finish the sentence: "My evidence
   is ___."
7. **Cooked egg.** "An egg is cooked, then it cools down." Does it
   become a raw egg again? Why or why not?
8. **Claim or opinion?** "Ice melting can be reversed." Is that a claim
   that still needs evidence, or is it already an argument? Give one
   piece of evidence that would complete the argument.

**Scoring guide:** 2 = correct with explanation or correct procedure;
1 = correct answer without explanation; 0 = not yet — reteach the
named lesson (Q1 → L01, Q2 → L02, Q3 → L03, Q4/Q5 → L04, Q6 → L02,
Q7 → L04, Q8 → L05). Learners scoring mostly 0–1 revisit the Week 14
review sessions before the unit assessment.
