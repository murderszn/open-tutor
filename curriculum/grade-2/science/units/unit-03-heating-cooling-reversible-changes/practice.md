# Practice — Unit 03

**How to use:** The adult reads each task aloud. The learner may answer
orally, by pointing, by drawing, by moving objects, or in writing; the
adult scribes dictated answers. Do all six tasks across Week 11–13
sessions (see [README.md](README.md) pacing). Check the same day with
[answer-key.md](answer-key.md). This is practice, not a grade.

## Task 1 — Take apart and rebuild (Lesson 01)

The adult builds a small model from 6 bricks. The learner takes it
apart, counts the bricks, rebuilds the same bricks into something new,
counts again, and draws both objects.

- Before drawing label: "Before: ___ pieces."
- After drawing label: "After: ___ pieces."
- Finish: "Changed: ___. Stayed the same: ___."

## Task 2 — The missing piece (Lesson 01)

"Mira takes apart a puzzle with 12 pieces. She rebuilds it, counts, and
finds only 11 pieces."

- What might have happened?
- What should Mira do to check?

## Task 3 — Before and after: warming and cooling (Lessons 02–03)

Draw or describe the before and after for each change. Name warming or
cooling as the cause.

1. Ice cube in the sun for 30 minutes.
2. Firm butter in a warm room for an hour.
3. Liquid water in the freezer overnight.
4. Soft butter in the refrigerator for an hour.

## Task 4 — Maya's melt table (Lessons 02–03, fictional practice data)

Maya tested same-size ice cubes for 30 minutes. Her table is a
fictional practice dataset — it is not this learner's data:

| Place | Cubes melted after 30 minutes |
|---|---|
| Sunny windowsill | 4 of 4 |
| Shady corner | 1 of 4 |
| Refrigerator | 0 of 4 |

1. Which place melted the ice fastest? How do you know?
2. Which place melted the ice slowest? How do you know?
3. Finish the sentence: "My claim is that sunlight made the ice melt
   fastest. My evidence is ___."

## Task 5 — Sort: can it go back? (Lesson 04)

Sort these six changes under "CAN GO BACK" or "CANNOT GO BACK." Give a
reason for two of your sorts.

1. Ice melts into water.
2. Water freezes into ice.
3. Butter softens in warmth.
4. Butter hardens in the refrigerator.
5. An egg is cooked.
6. A leaf freezes and turns brown.

## Task 6 — Build the argument (Lesson 05)

Use the frame. Use one reversible example and one irreversible example.

"My claim is that some changes caused by heating or cooling can be
reversed and some cannot. My reversible evidence is ___. My
irreversible evidence is ___. This shows ___."
