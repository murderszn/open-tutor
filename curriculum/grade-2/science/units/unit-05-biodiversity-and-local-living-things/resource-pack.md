# Resource Pack — Unit 05

**Goal:** Support a grade-2 learner (ages 7–8) in learning what a
habitat is, counting the different kinds of living things in two
local habitats, comparing them fairly, and describing the pattern
behind the counts.
**Age fit:** all items below were checked 2026-11-05 for grade-2
appropriateness; nothing here requires an account. Videos supplement
teaching; they never replace the lessons.

## Web references

1. **California Department of Education, NGSS content standards,
   2-LS4-1** — official performance-expectation text ("Make
   observations of plants and animals to compare the diversity of
   life in different habitats"), clarification statement, assessment
   boundary, and the LS4.D core idea. Opened and verified 2026-11-05;
   the standard wording this unit teaches.
   https://www2.cde.ca.gov/cacs/id/web/28993
2. **Oklahoma Science Framework, "Second Grade Diversity in
   Habitats" (2-LS4-1)** — the same official performance expectation
   with classroom guidance: observe firsthand or from media, compare
   the number and kind of plants and animals, and discuss why some
   living things live in some habitats and not others. Search-verified
   2026-11-05; teacher background for Lessons 02–05.
   http://okscienceframework.pbworks.com/w/page/121703820/Second%20Grade%20Diversity%20in%20Habitats?rev=1511972183
3. **National Geographic Education, "Habitat"** — a habitat is a place
   where an organism makes its home and meets all the conditions it
   needs to survive: food, water, shelter, and space for animals;
   light, air, water, and soil for plants. Search-verified 2026-11-05;
   adult/learner background for Lesson 01.
   http://education.nationalgeographic.org/resource/habitat/
4. **Britannica Kids, "habitat"** — student-level definition: a
   habitat is the place where an organism or a community of organisms
   lives, including all living and nonliving factors of the
   surrounding environment; almost every place on Earth is a habitat
   for some kinds of living things. Search-verified 2026-11-05;
   learner background for Lesson 01.
   http://kids.britannica.com/students/article/habitat/325503?cmpCountryCode=US&cmpIsGdpr=false&cmpIsCcpa=true&cmpGeoRegion=GA
5. **National Geographic Kids, "Ocean habitat"** — an ocean habitat
   article for young readers: coral reefs hold many kinds of animals
   (seahorses, clownfish, sea turtles), kelp forests give food and
   shelter, and temperature, depth, and distance from shore decide
   which plants and animals live there. Search-verified 2026-11-05;
   reading-and-talk model for Lesson 05 (different habitats, different
   kinds).
   https://kids.nationalgeographic.com/nature/habitats/article/ocean
6. **Oklahoma Science Framework, "2026 Grade 2: Diversity in
   Habitats Bundle Analysis"** — bundle guidance for 2-LS4-1: students
   observe that plants and animals vary in number and live in a
   variety of places on land and in water, and that a parking lot
   holds little biodiversity compared with a grassy park.
   Search-verified 2026-11-05; teacher background for the comparison
   in Lessons 03–04 and practice task 5.
   http://okscienceframework.pbworks.com/w/page/163731420/2026%20Grade%202%3A%20Diversity%20in%20Habitats%20Bundle%20Analysis
7. **KnowAtom, "Exploring Biodiversity in Different Habitats"** —
   teacher background on habitats and biodiversity for grade 2: a
   habitat provides water, air, food, and shelter; biodiversity is
   the variety of living things in a habitat; rainforests are rich in
   biodiversity. Search-verified 2026-11-05; teacher background for
   Lesson 05.
   https://www.knowatom.com/science-phenomena-videos/2nd-grade-science/habitats?hsLang=en-us
