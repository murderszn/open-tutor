# Week 04 Quiz — Unit 05

**For the learner.** The adult reads each question aloud; the learner
answers orally, by pointing, by drawing, or in short writing. The
adult scribes dictated words. Answers are in
[../answer-key.md](../answer-key.md) — keep it separate from this page.
**Pass mark:** 6 of 8, or a reteach plan for missed items (see
[../teacher-guide.md](../teacher-guide.md)).

1. What four things does a habitat give a living thing?
2. A robin lives in a yard with a tree. Name one thing the yard gives
   the robin for food, and one thing it gives for shelter.
3. A learner sees 3 daisies and 2 roses in a flower bed. How many
   **kinds** of flowers is that?
4. Look at the garden picture in Lesson 04 (or in
   [../assets/garden-and-lawn-habitats.png](../assets/garden-and-lawn-habitats.png),
   left side). How many kinds of **plants** are in the garden picture?
5. Look at the lawn picture (right side). How many kinds of living
   things are in the lawn picture **in all**?
6. The garden picture holds 8 kinds and the lawn picture holds 4
   kinds. Which pictured habitat holds more kinds? What is your
   evidence?
7. A habitat with many kinds of plants often holds many kinds of
   animals. Why? Name one thing plants give animals.
8. What is a **microhabitat**?
