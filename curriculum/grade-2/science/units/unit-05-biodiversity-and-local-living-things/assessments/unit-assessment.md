# Unit Assessment — Unit 05

**For the learner.** The adult reads each task aloud; the learner
answers orally, by pointing, by drawing, or in short writing. The
adult scribes dictated words. Answers are in
[../answer-key.md](../answer-key.md) — keep it separate from this page.
This assessment is a learning check, not a standardized or validated
test.

## Part A — What a habitat is (Objectives 1, 5)

1. In your own words, what is a **habitat**? Name the four things a
   habitat gives living things.
2. A cactus lives in a desert. The desert gives it very little water.
   Is the desert still the cactus's habitat? Why or why not?

## Part B — Counting and comparing (Objectives 2, 3, 4)

Look at the picture in Lesson 04 (or in
[../assets/garden-and-lawn-habitats.png](../assets/garden-and-lawn-habitats.png)).
If you cannot see the picture, use this text instead: *Garden (left):
plants — sunflower, tomato, carrot, bean; animals — bee, ladybug,
sparrow, earthworm. Lawn (right): plants — grass, dandelion; animals
— ant, robin.*

3. How many kinds of plants are in the garden picture? List them.
4. How many kinds of animals are in the lawn picture? List them.
5. How many kinds of living things are in the garden picture in all?
   How many in the lawn picture in all?
6. Which pictured habitat holds more kinds of living things? Write
   one claim-with-evidence sentence: "My claim is ___. My evidence
   is ___."

## Part C — Fair counting and patterns (Objectives 3, 5)

7. Maya counted the garden for 10 minutes and the lawn for 30
   minutes. She found 8 kinds in the garden and 11 kinds in the lawn
   and said, "The lawn holds more kinds." Is her comparison fair?
   Explain why or why not.
8. Finish the pattern sentence: "A habitat with many kinds of plants
   often holds many kinds of animals because the plants give animals
   ___ and ___."
9. Name one way to care for a habitat while you observe it.

## Performance task

10. **Tally a new habitat from words.** The adult reads this
    description aloud (it is fictional practice data, not a real
    place): *"At the pond edge I see: reeds, lily pads, a duck, a
    frog, a dragonfly, and another duck. The reeds grow in the mud.
    The lily pads float on the water."*
    a) Make a tally chart with one row per kind. How many kinds of
       plants? How many kinds of animals? How many kinds in all?
    b) The garden picture holds 8 kinds. Does the pond edge hold
       more kinds, fewer kinds, or the same number of kinds as the
       garden picture? Give the two counts as evidence.
