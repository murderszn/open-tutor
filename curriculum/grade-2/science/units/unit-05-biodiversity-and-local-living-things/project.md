# Project — Habitat Detectives

**Investigation (Sessions 6, 12, 14).** The learner surveys two real
local habitats, counts the kinds in each, compares them, and shares
the findings on a poster. Adult-supervised throughout; the Unit 04
bean plants are checked weekly as a background thread (Sessions 2, 10).

## Materials

- Science journal, pencil/crayons, hand lens, timer or clock
- Two tally-chart cards (one per habitat)
- Poster paper or a large journal page, glue/tape for the two charts
- The generated picture
  ([assets/garden-and-lawn-habitats.png](assets/garden-and-lawn-habitats.png))
  for indoor practice if the weather keeps the survey inside

## Steps

1. **Choose two habitats (Session 6).** Pick two places the learner
   can visit safely — for example, a garden bed and a lawn, a lawn and
   a pond edge, or a flower bed and a patch of bare dirt. Write the
   plan: 10 minutes of looking in each, same rules, same time of day.
2. **Survey Habitat A (Session 6).** Stand still, look slowly, use
   the hand lens. Write one row per kind on the Habitat A tally
   chart. Draw anything you cannot name and write "not sure." Count
   the rows: Habitat A holds ___ kinds.
3. **Survey Habitat B (Session 12).** Same 10 minutes, same rules,
   same time of day. Fill in the Habitat B tally chart. Count the
   rows: Habitat B holds ___ kinds.
4. **Build the comparison poster (Session 12).** Put both tally
   charts side by side on the poster. Under them, write the two
   totals, one claim-with-evidence sentence ("My claim is ___. My
   evidence is ___."), and one pattern sentence ("___ holds more
   kinds because ___."). Add one drawing of a plant giving an animal
   food or shelter.
5. **Share the findings (Session 14).** Present the poster to the
   adult (or household). Answer two follow-up questions with evidence
   from the poster — for example, "Which habitat held more kinds?"
   and "Why do you think that happened?"

## Safety and care

- Observe, never collect. Plants are not picked; animals are not
  touched or chased; logs and rocks are put back the way they were.
- Stay on paths; stay with the adult; wash hands after outdoor work.
- **Indoor alternative:** if the weather or the setting keeps the
  survey inside, use the generated picture for both "habitats"
  (garden side = Habitat A, lawn side = Habitat B) and say so on the
  poster. The counting and comparing work is the same.

## Deliverables

- Two completed tally charts (one per habitat) with a total kinds
  count under each.
- A comparison poster with both charts, both totals, a
  claim-with-evidence sentence, a pattern sentence, and one
  food-or-shelter drawing.
- A short share (Session 14) answering two follow-up questions.

## Rubric

| Criterion | 2 — Strong | 1 — Getting there | 0 — Not yet |
|---|---|---|---|
| Fair survey | Both habitats counted with the same time (10 min) and the same rules; charts show one row per kind | One rule differs (time or lookers) but the learner can name it | Rules differ and the learner cannot say how, or only one habitat was surveyed |
| Tally accuracy | Every row is a different kind; totals match the rows (re-counted correctly) | One kind split into two rows, or one kind missed, but the totals are close | Kinds and individuals mixed up (e.g., 5 ants counted as 5 kinds) |
| Comparison claim | Claim names which habitat holds more kinds (or that they are equal) **and** gives both counts as evidence | Claim names the habitat but gives only one count, or no counts | No claim, or the claim contradicts the counts |
| Pattern explanation | Pattern sentence names food, water, shelter, space, or plants giving food/shelter, tied to the two habitats | Pattern named but not tied to observations ("gardens are better") | No pattern, or an always-rule ("more kinds is always better") |
| Care and safety | Observed only; nothing picked or collected; hands washed; logs/rocks replaced | One care rule missed but corrected when reminded | Living things collected or harmed, or safety rules ignored |

**Scoring:** 8–10 points = ready to move on. 5–7 = review the missed
criterion with [practice.md](practice.md) tasks 2–5, then re-share.
Below 5 = re-teach Lessons 02–04 with the picture tally before
re-surveying.
