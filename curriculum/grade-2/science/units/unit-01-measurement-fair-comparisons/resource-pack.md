# Resource Pack — Unit 01

**Goal:** Support a grade-2 learner (ages 7–8) in asking testable questions,
measuring length to the nearest centimeter, running fair tests, and reading
data from graphs. **Age fit:** all items below were checked 2026-10-09 for
grade-2 appropriateness; nothing here requires an account.

## Videos

Direct resources (checked 2026-10-09; do not rely on claimed runtimes —
open the link and confirm before showing):

1. **"Estimate and Measure with Centimeters"** — PBS / Math Mights, Season 2
   Episode 204 (TV-Y, closed captions). Learners create a centimeter ruler,
   then estimate and measure lengths with it. Supports Lesson 03.
   https://video.detroitpbs.org/video/estimate-and-measure-with-centimeters-fpkc5l/
2. **"Fair Strength Test"** — short animated comparison of paper and card
   using equal-sized strips and identical supports; teaches that a fair test
   changes only one factor. Supports Lessons 01 and 04.
   https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=WQpmOXuCe-Q
3. **"What Is Science? | Science for Kids Ages 4–7"** — Pip's Big World;
   observing, asking a question, guessing, and testing (float-or-sink).
   Gentle on-ramp for Lesson 01's question sorting.
   https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=QIS_S-iUyRU
4. **Preferred-channel search (SciShow Kids):** search YouTube for
   `SciShow Kids measuring` — the channel's measurement episodes model
   ruler use and estimation for early elementary viewers. (Search link —
   pick a current episode together with the learner.)
5. **Preferred-channel search (Sid the Science Kid):** search YouTube for
   `Sid the Science Kid nonstandard measurement` — the "measuring in
   Geralds" episode playfully shows why agreed-upon units matter, a good
   discussion starter for Lesson 03's extension.

## Web references

1. **NGSS disciplinary core idea PS1.A page** (nextgenscience.org) — official
   performance-expectation text for 2-PS1-1 and 2-PS1-2, grade-2 band.
   https://www.nextgenscience.org/disciplinary-core-idea/ps1a-structure-and-properties-matter?page=4
2. **NSTA NGSS standard display: 2-PS1-1** — performance expectation with
   clarification statement and CCSS connections (W.2.7, W.2.8, 2.MD.D.10).
   https://my.nsta.org/ngss/DisplayStandard?view=pe&id=59
3. **ACS Inquiry in Action, Grade 2, Lesson 1.3 NGSS alignment** (PDF) —
   American Chemical Society classroom lesson on absorbency testing of
   paper, plastic, felt, and foil, including the fair-test setup ("all
   materials need to be tested in the same way") and the 2-PS1-2 assessment
   boundary. Direct model for the unit project.
   https://www.acs.org/content/dam/acsorg/education/k-8/inquiry-in-action/second-grade/g2-l1.3-ngss.pdf
4. **NSTA reproduction of the official NGSS 2-PS1 document** (PDF) — the full
   performance expectations, clarification statements, and assessment
   boundaries the scope-and-sequence was verified against.
   https://static.nsta.org/ngss/20130509/dci-individual/2-PS1-MatterAndItsInteractions.pdf
5. **NASA Commercial Crew "Measuring with NASA" activity** (PDF, grades K–5)
   — printable practice: round lengths to the nearest centimeter using a
   provided cm ruler. Free, no account; good extra practice for Lesson 03.
   https://nasa.gov/sites/default/files/atoms/files/ccp_measuring_activity_0.pdf
