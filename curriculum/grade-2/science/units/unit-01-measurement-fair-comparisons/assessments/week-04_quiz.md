# Week-04 Quiz (formative — Unit 01)

**How to use:** The adult reads each item aloud. The learner may answer
orally, by pointing, by drawing, or in writing; the adult scribes dictated
answers. Check the same day with [answer-key.md](../answer-key.md).
7 items, about 15 minutes. This is a check for teaching — not a grade.

1. **Fair or not?** "Maya wants to know which ball bounces higher. She drops
   a rubber ball and a tennis ball from the same height onto the same floor."
   Is this a fair test? Tell one thing she kept the same.
2. **Spot the unfair test.** "Leo wants to know if a taller ramp makes the
   car roll farther. He rolls a car down a tall ramp and a truck down a short
   ramp." What is unfair here? How would you fix it?
3. **Measure.** Your adult hands you a crayon and a ruler. Show where you
   start measuring, then tell the length to the nearest centimeter.
4. **Read the table.** (Adult shows this table:)

   | Material | Trial 1 | Trial 2 | Trial 3 |
   |---|---|---|---|
   | Sponge | 5 spoons | 6 spoons | 5 spoons |
   | Foil | 1 spoon | 1 spoon | 0 spoons |

   Which material held more water? Point to the evidence in the table.
5. **Image question.** Look at
   [fair-test-comparison.png](../assets/fair-test-comparison.png). Which
   station shows a fair test — left or right? Name one thing that is unfair
   about the other station.
   *(Text-only alternative: the picture shows two test stations. Left: two
   identical ramps at the same height, one bare wood, one covered with a
   blue towel, a red toy car on each. Right: a tall ramp with a red car and
   a short ramp with a yellow ball.)*
6. **Claim and evidence.** "The sponge held more water than the foil."
   Is that a claim with evidence or just an opinion? Use the table from
   question 4 to finish the sentence: "My evidence is ___."
7. **Why three trials?** "Sam tested his ramp once and said it was the
   fastest." Should we believe him yet? Why or why not?

**Scoring guide:** 2 = correct with explanation or correct procedure;
1 = correct answer without explanation (or close measurement within 1 cm);
0 = not yet — reteach the named lesson (Q1/Q2 → L01, Q3 → L03, Q4/Q6 → L05,
Q5/Q7 → L04). Learners scoring mostly 0–1 revisit the Week 6 review
sessions before the unit assessment.
