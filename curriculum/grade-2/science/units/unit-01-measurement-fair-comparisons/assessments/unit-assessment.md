# Unit Assessment — Unit 01 (culminating)

**How to use:** Adult reads each task aloud and scribes dictated answers;
drawing, pointing, and manipulatives are valid responses. About 25 minutes.
Solutions and scoring in [answer-key.md](../answer-key.md). This assessment
is a draft instrument — it is **not** a standardized or validated test.

## Part A — Measuring and recording (Objectives 2, 3)

**A1.** Your adult gives you a spoon and a centimeter ruler. Estimate its
length first (say your estimate aloud), then measure from the 0 to the
nearest centimeter. Report: "My estimate was ___ cm. My measurement is ___
cm."

**A2.** Draw the spoon and label **two** parts or observations (e.g.,
"round bowl," "long handle," "shiny").

## Part B — Fair tests (Objectives 1, 4)

**B1.** "Priya wonders: does the kind of paper change how far a paper
airplane flies?" Help Priya plan a fair test. Name:
(a) the one thing she will change,
(b) two things she must keep the same,
(c) how she will measure the result (what tool, what unit).

**B2.** Look at these two test plans. Which is fair? Explain what is wrong
with the other one.

- Plan X: "I will drop a marble and a feather from the same height and see
  which lands first."
- Plan Y: "I will drop a marble from a chair and a feather from a table and
  see which lands first."

## Part C — Data, graphs, and claims (Objective 5)

Your adult shows you this data from a fictional practice test (clearly
labeled — not real observations):

> *Two materials were tested for how many spoons of water they hold.*
>
> | Material | Trial 1 | Trial 2 | Trial 3 |
> |---|---|---|---|
> | Cloth | 4 spoons | 5 spoons | 4 spoons |
> | Plastic | 0 spoons | 1 spoon | 0 spoons |

**C1.** Draw a bar graph of the **middle** trial for each material. Label
the axis "spoons of water held."

**C2.** Write or dictate: "My claim is ___. My evidence is ___."

**C3.** Which material would you choose to mop up a spill? Why does the
data support that choice? (2-PS1-2: best suited for a purpose.)

## Part D — Explain your reasoning (all objectives)

**D1.** "A scientist changes the ramp height AND the car, then says the
taller ramp made the car go farther." Do you agree with the scientist's
conclusion? Use the words **fair test** and **evidence** in your answer.

**Scoring:** Each numbered task scores 0–2 (2 = complete and correct;
1 = partially correct or correct with heavy prompting; 0 = not yet).
Total: 18. **Ready to move on:** 13+ with no zeros in Part B. **Reteach
paths:** Part A low → Lessons 02–03 + practice.md tasks 1–4; Part B low →
Lessons 01, 04 + image activity; Part C low → Lesson 05 + practice.md
tasks 5–8; D1 low → evidence-talk in Session 12 replay.
