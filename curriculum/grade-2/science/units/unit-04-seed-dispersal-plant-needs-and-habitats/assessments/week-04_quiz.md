# Formative Quiz — Unit 04: Seed Dispersal, Plant Needs, and Habitats

**Unit:** [U04](../README.md) · **Session 13 of 16** · **No answers on this page** — see
[answer-key.md](../answer-key.md) (adult only).
Answer orally, by pointing, or by drawing; the adult scribes when
needed. Target: 6 of 8 correct; reteach any missed item before the
unit assessment (see the [teacher guide](../teacher-guide.md)).

1. Name two things plants need to grow.
2. A plan says: Plant A in the sun with water; Plant B in the
   shade with no water. Is this test fair? Why or why not?
3. Open (or picture) a soaked bean seed. Name its three parts.
4. Look at the picture
   [`seed-travelers-scene.png`](../assets/seed-travelers-scene.png)
   (AI-generated practice picture). Name how seeds travel in
   each part: the left panel, the middle panel, and the bottom of
   the right panel.
   - Text-only alternative: Left — fluffy white seeds blow away
     from a flower in the wind. Middle — round spiky seeds stick
     to a furry animal's fur, and a bee visits a yellow flower.
     Right, at the bottom — a smooth brown seed floats on blue
     water beside a green leaf. (The furry animal also appears at
     the top of the right panel; the water clue is the floating
     seed at the bottom.)
5. Which seed travels by wind: a fluffy, light seed, or a heavy,
   smooth seed? Tell the feature you used.
6. In the fur model, what does the fleece stand for? What does
   the Velcro stand for?
7. In the bee model, what does the yellow powder stand for? What
   is it called when pollen moves from one flower to another?
8. Which place gives a seed a better chance to grow: a sunny
   garden bed with soil and water, or a dark, dry closet floor?
   Give one reason tied to what plants need.
