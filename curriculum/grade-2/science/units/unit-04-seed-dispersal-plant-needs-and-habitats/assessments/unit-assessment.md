# Unit Assessment — Unit 04: Seed Dispersal, Plant Needs, and Habitats

**Unit:** [U04](../README.md) · **Session 16 of 16** · **No answers on this page** — see
[answer-key.md](../answer-key.md) (adult only).
Answer orally, by pointing, or by drawing; the adult scribes when
needed. Part A uses a labeled fictional practice table (not the
learner's own plants). Parts B and C use the learner's models and
earlier units.

## Part A — What plants need: read the investigation (Objectives 1, 6)

*Practice investigation — fictional practice data. A class tested
one variable at a time. All plants got the same seeds, soil, and
cups. Stem heights are in centimeters.*

| Plant | Week 1 | Week 2 | Week 3 |
|---|---|---|---|
| Sunny + water | 2 cm | 5 cm | 8 cm |
| Shady + water | 1 cm | 2 cm | 2 cm |
| Sunny + no water | 1 cm | 1 cm | 1 cm |

A1. Which plant was tallest at Week 3, and how tall was it?
A2. How much did each plant grow from Week 1 to Week 3? (Show the
subtraction for each plant.)
A3. Write a claim about what plants need to grow, using one
measurement from the table as evidence. Start: "My claim is ___.
My evidence is ___."
A4. A seed lands in a sunny garden bed with soil and water. A
second seed lands on a sunny bare rock with no soil. Which seed
has the better chance to grow? Give a reason that checks
sunlight, water, and soil.

## Part B — Models that mimic animals (Objectives 2, 3, 4, 5)

B1. Name the three parts of a bean seed and one job for each part.
B2. Match each seed feature to how it travels: (a) fluffy and
light, (b) able to float, (c) hooked. Use wind, water, and animal.
B3. In the fur model, what does the fleece stand for, and what
does the Velcro stand for? Name one way the model differs from a
real animal.
B4. In the bee model, what does the yellow powder stand for?
Describe the two steps that move it from Flower A to Flower B.
Name one way the model differs from a real bee.
B5. Look at the picture
[`seed-travelers-scene.png`](../assets/seed-travelers-scene.png)
(AI-generated practice picture). Which travel way is shown on the
left, which in the middle, and which at the bottom of the right
panel? (Text-only alternative is in the quiz and in
[Lesson 03](../lessons/lesson-03-how-seeds-travel.md).)

## Part C — Midyear review: Units 01–04

C1. (Unit 01) A learner tests whether plants need water with
these plants: Plant A in sun with water; Plant B in shade with no
water. Is the test fair? What should change to make it fair?
C2. (Unit 02) A rain cover must keep a toy dry in rain. Which
property matters most: soaking up water, or not soaking up water?
Explain in one sentence.
C3. (Unit 03) Sort these two changes into reversible or
irreversible, with a reason each: (a) ice melts into water and
then freezes back into ice; (b) an egg is cooked.
C4. (Unit 04) A seed has hooks and sticks to a dog's fur. How does
it travel, and why is traveling good for the plant? (One sentence
each.)

## Reflection (not scored)

Name one thing plants need, one way seeds travel, and one thing
you would test next about plants.
