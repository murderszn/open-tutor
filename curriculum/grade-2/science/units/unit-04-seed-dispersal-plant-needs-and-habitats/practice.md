# Practice — Unit 04: Seed Dispersal, Plant Needs, and Habitats

**Unit:** [U04](README.md) · **No answers on this page** — see
[answer-key.md](answer-key.md) (adult only). Tasks may be answered
orally, by pointing, or by drawing; the adult scribes when needed.
The learner's own growth-log numbers are never replaced by the
fictional practice data in Lesson 03 — practice tables here are
for sorting and judging only.

## Task A — Growth-log checks (Sessions 4, 6, 10, 14)

**A1 (Session 4, measuring practice):** Measure 3 practice stems
(or pencils standing in cups) with a centimeter ruler. Record each
height to the nearest centimeter. Then measure your bean plants'
starting height: 0 cm above soil on planting day. Success: all 3
practice measurements read to the nearest cm, and the log's Day 0
row is filled in.

**A2 (Sessions 6, 10, 14, growth checks):** On each check day,
measure both plants, count leaves if any have opened, and draw
each plant. Write the change since the last check (new height
minus old height) for each plant. Success: both plants measured,
both changes computed, and one observation sentence written per
plant ("Plant A grew ___ cm and looks ___").

## Task B — Fair-test fix-ups (Session 2)

Each plan below is unfair. Name every extra change, then rewrite
the plan so only one thing changes.

**B1:** Plant A: sun, water every day, big cup. Plant B: shade, no
water, small cup. Question: does sunlight matter?

**B2:** Plant A: sun, water. Plant B: shade, water, plus plant
food. Question: does sunlight matter?

**B3:** Plant A: sun, water. Plant B: sun, water, but planted
twice as deep. Question: does water matter?

Success: all 3 plans fixed, with the extra change(s) named in each.

## Task C — Seed sort (Session 6)

**C1:** Sort these 9 seed cards (or real safe examples) into
wind, water, and animal. Give the feature reason for each card.

1. Fluffy white seed that floats in the air
2. Smooth seed that floats on water
3. Seed with hooks that sticks to a sock
4. Winged seed that spins as it falls
5. Hard seed found floating at a pond edge
6. Sticky seed that clings to fur
7. Light seed with a feathery parachute
8. Seed inside a fruit an animal eats
9. Round seed that rolls in the wind across bare ground

Success: at least 7 of 9 sorted correctly with a feature reason.
(Card 9 is the tricky one — see the answer key.)

## Task D — Model trials (Session 8)

**D1:** Run the Lesson 04 fur model for 3 trials. Record "seeds
stuck" and "seeds dropped at the new place" for each trial, then
the total stuck. Finish: "My model shows that ___." Then answer:
what would happen with smooth balls and no Velcro? Success: 3
trials recorded, total computed, and one model difference named.

## Task E — Habitat-fit sort (Session 10)

**E1:** Sort these 4 places into good chance / poor chance for a
seed, giving a reason tied to sunlight, water, and soil for each.

1. Sunny garden bed with soil, watered when dry
2. Dark, dry closet floor
3. Sunny bare rock with no soil
4. Shady pond edge with soil and steady water

Success: all 4 judged with a reason that checks sunlight, water,
and soil (not just one need).

## Task F — Review games (Sessions 12, 15)

**F1 (Session 12, evidence-talk and claim builder):** Finish
these sentences with evidence from the unit (the learner's own
log, the models, or the picture):

1. "My evidence is ___, so my claim is that plants need ___."
2. "My evidence is ___, so my claim is that hooked seeds travel
   by ___."
3. Fix this weak claim: "Plants grow because they are magic."
   Rewrite it with evidence.

Success: both sentences finished with real evidence, and the weak
claim rewritten with at least one piece of evidence.

**F2 (Session 15, seed-travel relay and midyear review cards):**
Relay: the adult names a seed feature; the learner runs (or
points) to the wind, water, or animal station. Then answer one
card from each earlier unit: (a) Unit 01 — what makes a test fair?
(b) Unit 02 — which property matters for a rain cover? (c) Unit 03
— is ice melting and freezing again reversible or irreversible?
(d) Unit 04 — name one way seeds travel and its seed feature.
Success: relay 4 of 5 correct; all 4 review cards answered.
