# Project — The Seed Travelers Fair

**Unit:** [U04](README.md) · **Sessions:** 8 (step 1), 11 (steps 2–3), 14 (step 4, share)
**Adult directions are part of the project.** The learner presents
orally, by pointing, or with drawings; the adult scribes written
parts when needed.

## The challenge

Host a Seed Travelers Fair with three exhibits and one claim:

1. **The bean-plant investigation** — your fair test, from plan to
   final measurements.
2. **The fur model** — your animal-dispersal model, demonstrated live.
3. **The bee model** — your pollination model, demonstrated live.
4. **The claim poster** — what plants need to grow, with evidence,
   plus one good place for a seed and why.

## Materials

- The bean plants and growth log (Lessons 01–02, Sessions 3–14)
- The fur model (Lesson 04) and the bee model (Lesson 05)
- Poster paper, crayons, the generated picture
  [`seed-travelers-scene.png`](assets/seed-travelers-scene.png)
  (AI-generated practice picture, labeled as such on the poster)

## Steps

**Step 1 (Session 8) — Set up the fair.** Arrange the three
exhibit stations. At the investigation station, post the plan
(variable, prediction, things kept the same) and the log so far.
At each model station, post a card naming what each model part
stands for. Write one sentence per station: what this exhibit
shows.

**Step 2 (Session 11) — Demonstrate the models.** Run the fur
model for 1 trial in front of your audience (the adult, a family
member, or a recorded retelling). Run the bee model from Flower A
to Flower B. After each demo, say what the model part stands for
and one way the model differs from the real thing.

**Step 3 (Session 11) — Draft the claim.** Using the latest growth
measurements, draft the poster claim in this form: "My claim is
that plants need ___ and ___. My evidence is ___. " Then add the
habitat judgment: "A good place for a seed is ___, because it has
___, ___, and ___." (sunlight, water, soil — in any order.)

**Step 4 (Session 14) — Share the fair.** Present all three
exhibits and the claim poster. Answer two audience questions with
evidence (for example: "What would happen if you changed two
things at once?" or "Why doesn't the bee model eat anything?").
Finish by naming one thing you would test next (for example, "I
would test whether plants need soil").

## Deliverables

1. Growth log: plan, prediction, and measurements for Sessions 3,
   6, 10, and 14, with growth changes computed.
2. Two model demonstrations with part-mapping cards and one named
   difference each.
3. Claim poster: claim, at least one measurement as evidence, and
   one habitat judgment with all three needs named.
4. A fair share: the learner presents and answers 2 questions
   with evidence.

## Rubric

Score each criterion as **Beginning**, **Developing**, or
**Secure**. "Secure" on all four criteria means the unit's
objectives are met at the project level; anything lower names the
reteach (see the [teacher guide](teacher-guide.md)).

| Criterion | Beginning | Developing | Secure |
|---|---|---|---|
| Investigation is fair and recorded (Obj. 1) | Plan changes more than one thing, or log has gaps | Plan is fair but one measurement or change is missing | Plan changes one thing only; log has all 4 check days with heights and changes computed |
| Dispersal model works and is explained (Obj. 4) | Model built but not demonstrated, or parts not mapped | Model demonstrated; most parts mapped; no difference named | Model demonstrated with trials; every part mapped; one real difference named |
| Pollination model works and is explained (Obj. 5) | Model built but pollen never reaches Flower B | Pollen reaches Flower B; most parts mapped | Pollen visibly moves A → B; every part mapped; one real difference named |
| Claim uses evidence; habitat judged on all 3 needs (Obj. 6) | Claim stated with no evidence, or habitat judged on one need | Claim has evidence but it is not a measurement, or one need missing from the habitat reason | Claim cites at least one measurement; habitat reason names sunlight, water, and soil |

## Safety reminders

- Never eat seeds or beans used in class; wash hands after
  handling soil, seeds, and model powder.
- Model "pollen" is dabbed, never blown; check sensitivities first.
- Handle living plants gently; observe wild plants without picking
  them. Never look directly at the sun when checking the sunny plant.
