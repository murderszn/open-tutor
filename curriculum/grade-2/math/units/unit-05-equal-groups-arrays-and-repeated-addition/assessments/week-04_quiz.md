# Formative Quiz — Unit 05 (Week 22, Session 14)

**Unit:** [U05](../README.md) · **Format:** oral/observed, adult-scribed
**Adult note:** read each item aloud; the learner answers with counters,
pointing, drawing, or words. The adult scribes. Solutions are in
[answer-key.md](../answer-key.md) — kept separate from this student page.
This quiz is a teaching check, not a standardized or validated assessment.

1. Pair 13 counters. Is 13 odd or even — and how do you know?
2. Look at the picture in [Lesson 04](../lessons/lesson-04-rectangular-arrays-rows-and-columns.md)
   (or its text alternative): it shows 4 rows with 5 apples in each row.
   Write the repeated-addition equation by rows, and tell me the total.
3. Write 14 as a sum of two equal addends.
4. Build an array with 3 rows of 4 counters. Write the row equation and
   the total.
5. Partition a rectangle 2 rows tall and 5 squares wide into same-size
   squares. How many squares are there? Write the row equation.
6. Explain in your own words: why can you count an array by rows OR by
   columns and get the same total?

**Scoring (adult):** 1 point per item (item 2 needs both the equation
and the total; item 5 needs both the count and the equation). 5–6 =
secure; 3–4 = developing (re-teach the missed objective before the unit
assessment); 0–2 = not yet (replay the matching lesson, then re-quiz with
fresh numbers).
