# Practice, Game, and Review Sessions — Unit 05

**Unit:** [U05](README.md) · **Sessions:** 2, 4, 6, 8, 10, 13 of 16
**Adult note:** every session is 25–30 minutes. The learner builds first,
writes second, and checks a second way third. Solutions are in
[answer-key.md](answer-key.md) — kept separate from this student page.

## Session A — Pair-and-sort trays

Use counters and the EVEN / ODD plates. For each number, pair the
counters, say the pairs and the leftover aloud, then sort the number
card onto the right plate. For even numbers, also write the two equal
addends.

1. 6 counters
2. 9 counters
3. 11 counters
4. 14 counters
5. 17 counters
6. 20 counters

**Success criterion:** 5 of 6 sorted correctly, with the pairs and the
leftover named aloud for each.

## Session B — Equal-group trays

Use counters and 5 small plates. For each round, make the equal groups,
check every plate holds the same number, then write the repeated-addition
equation and the total.

1. 3 groups of 4
2. 5 groups of 2
3. 2 groups of 5
4. 4 groups of 3

**Success criterion:** 3 of 4 equations correct, with the number of
addends matching the number of groups in every equation.

## Session C — Even-addend and array games

**Even-addend race (Session 4):** the adult turns over a number card
(2–20). If the number is even, the learner says the two equal addends
("14 — 7 + 7"). If it is odd, the learner says "odd — one left over"
and names the nearest even number below it ("13 is odd; 12 is 6 + 6").
First to 5 correct calls wins the round; accuracy beats speed — a wrong
call loses the point.

Suggested cards: 6, 9, 12, 15, 18, 20, 7, 14.

**Tile-the-rectangle race (Session 10):** the adult calls out a
rectangle size ("3 rows, 4 columns"). The learner draws and partitions
it on graph paper, counts the squares by rows, and checks by columns.
First to 3 correct rectangles wins the round; a rectangle with unequal
squares scores 0 for that round, even if the count was right.

Suggested sizes: 2-by-5, 3-by-3, 4-by-2, 5-by-3, 3-by-4.

**Success criterion:** the addend call is correct for at least 4 of 5
cards in the even-addend race, and at least 2 of 3 rectangles are
correctly partitioned and counted in the tile race.

## Session D — Array build

Use counters. For each array, build it, write the row equation and the
total, then write the column equation and check that the totals match.

1. 2 rows of 5
2. 3 rows of 3
3. 4 rows of 2
4. 5 rows of 3

**Success criterion:** 3 of 4 arrays correct by rows, with the column
check matching on at least 3 of them.

## Session E — Mixed review and error hunt (Session 13)

Solve each problem, then hunt the planted error in the last two items.
All problems use counters or graph paper first.

1. Pair 17 counters. Odd or even — and how do you know?
2. Write 12 as a sum of two equal addends.
3. 4 groups of 5 — write the equation and the total.
4. Build an array with 3 rows of 2. Write the row equation and the total.
5. Planted error: "14 is odd, because 7 + 7 = 14 has two equal parts."
   Is it? Find the error and correct it.
6. Planted error: "Sam counted a 4-row, 5-column array as
   4 + 4 + 4 + 4 = 16." Is it? Find the error and correct it.

**Success criterion:** 4 of 6 correct including at least one planted
error found and explained; the adult re-teaches the highest-need objective
from the exit checks before the Session 14 quiz.
