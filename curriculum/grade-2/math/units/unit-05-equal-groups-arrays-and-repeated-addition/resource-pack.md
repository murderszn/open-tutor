# Resource Pack — Unit 05: Equal Groups, Arrays, and Repeated Addition

**Summary (adult planning judgment):** supplements for teaching odd
and even numbers to 20, equal groups, rectangular arrays to 5 × 5, and
partitioning rectangles into same-size squares to a 2nd grader; every
core objective also has a free, no-account, offline path (counters,
sorting trays, graph paper).

**Check date:** 2026-11-05 (all items below). Video runtimes and caption
availability are reported only where the source page showed them; anything
else is marked unverified — preview before assigning.

## Web references

1. **Common Core State Standards — Mathematics** (framework source; the
   grade-2 OA and G codes/descriptions used in this unit were
   cross-checked against the official domain pages during the track
   audit, 2026-10-02, and re-verified against independent reproductions
   of the official text on 2026-11-05; no state adoption claimed).
   <https://www.thecorestandards.org/Math/>
2. **Boddle Learning — "Even and Odd Numbers + Adding Equal Groups of
   Objects"**: teacher-facing breakdowns of 2.OA.3 and 2.OA.4 with
   step-by-step notes on four short videos (pairing shoes, dividing
   objects into even and odd groups, counting groups of goldfish,
   counting equal groups of objects), including the exact examples
   Lessons 01 and 03 use. Free page; the linked videos are YouTube.
   <https://www.boddlelearning.com/2nd-grade/even-and-odd-numbers-adding-equal-groups-of-objects>
3. **Generation Genius — "Intro to Counting in Groups (Arrays)"**:
   arrays as equal rows, skip-counting and repeated addition to find
   totals (4 rows of 2 eyes is 2 + 2 + 2 + 2 = 8; 5 rows of 5 is
   5 + 5 + 5 + 5 + 5 = 25), with vocabulary and discussion questions.
   Page text free; the full video may need an account — preview first.
   <https://www.generationgenius.com/intro-to-counting-in-groups-arrays/>
4. **OpenUpResources — "OUR K–5 Math, Grade 2, Unit 8, Section B
   Practice Problems: Rectangular Arrays"**: free, no-account student
   practice on arrays (expressions with equal addends, arrays of 15
   counters, partitioning rectangles and writing two equations). Adult
   preview recommended — it is a practice set, not a lesson.
   <https://access.openupresources.org/curricula/our-k5-math/en/grade-2/unit-8/section-b/student_practice_problems.html>
5. **Oak National Academy — "Explain what odd and even numbers are and
   the difference between them"**: free video lesson (pairs and groups
   of two; odd numbers end in 1, 3, 5, 7, 9; even numbers end in 0, 2,
   4, 6, 8), matching Lessons 01–02. Free; preview for accent and
   pacing fit.
   <https://www.thenational.academy/pupils/lessons/explain-what-odd-and-even-numbers-are-and-the-difference-between-them/video>
6. **National Center on Intensive Intervention — "Using Repeated Addition
   to Illustrate Multiplication"**: teacher-facing video showing how an
   array of equal sets supports repeated addition and skip counting for
   learners beginning multiplicative thinking. Adult background for
   Lessons 03–04, not a learner assignment.
   <https://intensiveintervention.org/resource/using-repeated-addition-illustrate-multiplication>
7. **Khan Academy — "Adding with arrays" (practice)**: free practice
   items on describing an array's circles by rows and columns, in the
   get-ready-for-3rd-grade multiplication readiness set. Free; no
   account stated on the page. Best as a guided station after Lesson 04.
   <https://en.khanacademy.org/math/get-ready-for-3rd-grade/x3c950fa744f5f34c:get-ready-for-multiplication-and-division/x3c950fa744f5f34c:adding-using-arrays/e/repeated-addition>

## Access notes

- Every core objective is teachable fully offline: counters (or buttons
  and beans), paper plates, and graph paper require no account, no
  device, and no purchase beyond dollar-store supplies.
- All seven web references above are free to view with no account
  required, as far as their pages showed on the check date, except the
  Generation Genius full video (§3), which may need an account — its
  page text is free and the video is not required for any objective.
- Videos supplement explicit teaching; none replaces the lessons. Preview
  for ads, comments, and age fit (especially open-search results).
- Captions/runtime were verified for none of the video items; all are
  marked unverified above.
