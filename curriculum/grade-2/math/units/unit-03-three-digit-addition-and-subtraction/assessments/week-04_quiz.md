# Formative Quiz — Unit 03 (Week 14, Session 14)

**Unit:** [U03](../README.md) · **Format:** oral/observed, adult-scribed
**Adult note:** read each item aloud; the learner answers with blocks,
pointing, drawing, or words. The adult scribes. Solutions are in
[answer-key.md](../answer-key.md) — kept separate from this student page.
This quiz is a teaching check, not a standardized or validated assessment.

1. Build and add 342 + 125. Tell me the sum, column by column.
2. Look at the picture in [Lesson 02](../lessons/lesson-02-add-compose-a-ten.md)
   (or its text alternative): the top row shows 253 and the bottom row
   shows 166. Add them. Did any column need a trade? Which one, and why?
3. Build and subtract 465 − 139. Tell me the difference, naming every trade.
4. Build and subtract 403 − 158. What happens in the tens column, and why?
5. Mentally: 546 + 100 and 937 − 100. Which digit changed in each answer?
6. Explain in your own words: why does composing 10 tens into 1 hundred
   keep a sum the same?

**Scoring (adult):** 1 point per item (item 2 needs both the sum and the
trade named; item 5 needs both answers and the digit named). 5–6 =
secure; 3–4 = developing (re-teach the missed objective before the unit
assessment); 0–2 = not yet (replay the matching lesson, then re-quiz with
fresh numbers).
