# Unit Assessment — Unit 03: Three-Digit Addition and Subtraction

**Unit:** [U03](../README.md) · **Session 16 of 16**
**Adult note:** the learner builds with blocks first for items 1–6, then
writes. Items 7–10 may be answered mentally or with the written method,
as labeled. Solutions and scoring guidance are in
[answer-key.md](../answer-key.md) — kept separate from this student page.
This assessment is a teaching check, not a standardized or validated
assessment.

## Part 1 — Build, then write

1. Build and add 536 + 241. Write the sum.
2. Build and add 174 + 268. Write the sum, and name every trade.
3. Build and subtract 659 − 234. Write the difference.
4. Build and subtract 741 − 385. Write the difference, and name every trade.
5. Build and subtract 800 − 472. Write the difference, and say what
   happened in the tens column.

## Part 2 — Picture problem

6. Look at the generated illustration in
   [Lesson 02](../lessons/lesson-02-add-compose-a-ten.md)
   ([`assets/add-253-plus-166.png`](../assets/add-253-plus-166.png)):
   count each row, name the two numbers, add them, and say which column
   traded and why.

   **Text-only alternative:** The picture shows 2 hundreds, 5 tens, and 3
   ones (253) in the top row, and 1 hundred, 6 tens, and 6 ones (166) in
   the bottom row.

## Part 3 — Mental computation and strategy

7. Mentally: 385 + 100 and 762 − 100. Which digit changed in each?
8. Choose a strategy for 583 + 217, solve it, and write one sentence
   explaining why your strategy works.

## Part 4 — Check by the inverse operation

9. Solve 724 − 318. Then check it by addition: does your check agree with
   724? Show both computations.
10. Explain in your own words: why does decomposing 1 hundred give 10
    tens? Use the words "same amount" in your answer.

**Scoring (adult):** 1 point per numbered item (items 2, 4, 5, 6, 8, 9
need the explanation, trade, or check named as well as the answer; item
10 needs the "same amount" idea). 9–10 = secure; 7–8 = developing
(re-teach the missed objective in the flexible weeks before Unit 04);
0–6 = not yet (replay the matching lessons, then re-assess with fresh
numbers from the same item types).
