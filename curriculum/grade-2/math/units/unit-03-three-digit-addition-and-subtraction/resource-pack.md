# Resource Pack — Unit 03: Three-Digit Addition and Subtraction

**Summary (adult planning judgment):** supplements for teaching
three-digit addition and subtraction with composing and decomposing,
mental ±100, and strategy choice to a 2nd grader; every core objective
also has a free, no-account, offline path (blocks, place-value mat,
thousand chart).

availability are reported only where the source page showed them; anything

## Web references

1. **Common Core State Standards — Mathematics** (framework source; the
   grade-2 NBT codes/descriptions used in this unit were cross-checked
   against the official domain pages during the track audit, 2026-10-02;
   no state adoption claimed). <https://www.thecorestandards.org/Math/>
2. **Vedantu — "Add 3-Digit Numbers in Columns With Regrouping" (Class 2
   worksheet + step-by-step column method)**: the column steps match
   Lessons 02–03 (ones, then tens, then hundreds, carrying when a column
   reaches ten). Free worksheet; adult preview recommended — the page
   carries worksheet marketing around the steps.
   <https://www.vedantu.com/worksheets/class-2-maths-add-3-digit-numbers-in-columns-with-regrouping>
3. **Vedantu — "Subtract 3 Digit Numbers with Regrouping" (Class 2
   worksheet + FAQs)**: states the borrowing steps column by column
   (borrow 1 ten as 10 ones; borrow 1 hundred as 10 tens), matching
   Lessons 04–05. Free worksheet; adult preview recommended.
   <https://www.vedantu.com/worksheets/class-2-maths-subtract-3-digit-numbers-with-regrouping>
4. **OnlineMathLearning — "3-Digit Subtraction with Regrouping Game"**:
   interactive column-subtraction practice with single-regrouping,
   double-regrouping, and subtract-across-zero modes, plus borrowing
   boxes that bridge blocks and the written method. Free; no account
   stated on the page. Best as a guided station, not unsupervised play.
   <https://www.onlinemathlearning.com/3-digit-subtraction-regrouping-game.html>
5. **Math-Only-Math — "Subtraction of 3-Digit Numbers with Regrouping"**:
   worked column examples and the place-value facts behind regrouping
   (10 ones = 1 ten, 10 tens = 1 hundred). Free reference page.
   <https://www.math-only-math.com/subtraction-of-3-digit-numbers-with-regrouping.html>
6. **Teaching with Kaylee B. — "Subtraction Strategies for 3 Digit
   Numbers"**: teacher-facing explanation of why drawing base-ten blocks
   and circling a regrouped ten/hundred (the Lesson 04–05 method) builds
   understanding before column routines. Adult reading, not a learner
   assignment.
   <https://www.teachingwithkayleeb.com/subtraction-strategies-for-3-digit-numbers/>
7. **StudyPug — "Subtraction with Regrouping to 1000: Borrowing Method"**:
   a step-by-step borrowing walkthrough with single and double
   regrouping and a checking step. Labeled grade 3 on the page, so use
   it as adult background for Lesson 05's double trades, not as a
   learner-facing grade-2 assignment.
   <https://www.studypug.com/us/us-mn-math-grade-3/subtraction-with-regrouping-up-to-1000/?view=read>

## Access notes

- Every core objective is teachable fully offline: base-ten blocks (or
  banded straws), a hand-drawn place-value mat, and a thousand chart
  require no account, no device, and no purchase beyond dollar-store
  supplies.
- All seven web references above are free with no account required to
  view, as far as their pages showed on the check date.
- Videos supplement explicit teaching; none replaces the lessons. Preview
  for ads, comments, and age fit (especially open-search results).
- Captions/runtime were verified for none of the video items; all are
  marked unverified above.
