# Teacher Guide — Unit 03: Three-Digit Addition and Subtraction

**Unit:** [U03](README.md) · **Answer key:** [answer-key.md](answer-key.md)
(adult only — never beside student materials)

## How this unit runs

Sixteen 25–30-minute sessions across track weeks 11–14. Every session
opens with a 2-minute number-talk warm-up (a mental ±10 or ±100 chain, a
block build to name, or a trade call) and closes with the lesson's exit
check, recorded as **secure / developing / not yet** per objective. Keep
sessions hands-on: at grade 2, blocks first, drawing second, symbols last.
The written method is always introduced as a record of a trade the
learner has already made — never as a rule to memorize first.

**Session map:** 1 L1 → 2 practice A → 3 L2 → 4 game → 5 L3 → 6 practice
A (regrouping) → 7 L4 → 8 practice B → 9 L5 → 10 game → 11 L6 → 12
project P1 → 13 review D + re-teach → 14 quiz + feedback → 15 project P2
+ share → 16 assessment.

## Objective-by-objective guidance

### Objective 1 — three-digit addition with composing (L1–L3)

The unit's load-bearing idea, in two steps: first the column rule (ones,
then tens, then hundreds), then the trade rule (ten of one kind becomes
one of the next kind up). Do not rush Lesson 01: a learner who adds left
to right will survive no-regrouping problems and collapse on the first
double trade. Lesson 02's picture (253 + 166) is the model to imitate —
count the rows, add the columns, trade the tens, re-count the hundreds.

### Objective 2 — three-digit subtraction with decomposing (L4–L5)

Teach subtraction as taking blocks away, never as "borrowing a number
from the neighbor" in the abstract. The crossed-out digit in the written
method must always match a physical swap on the mat. Across-zero (Lesson
05) needs the two-step chain said aloud every time: "hundred → tens →
ones, leaving nine tens." If a learner writes 10 tens after the hundred
trade, they skipped the second step.

### Objective 3 — mental ±100 (L6, warm-ups throughout)

Anchor every mental answer in the place-value fact: 100 = 1 hundred, 0
tens, 0 ones. The self-check is "did any digit besides hundreds move?"
Keep all mental ±100 work inside 100–900, per 2.NBT.B.8; 900 + 100 = 1000
is a teaser for later, not an assessed item.

### Objective 4 — strategy choice and explain why (L6, project)

Strategy choice is taught, not left to preference: mental for ±100/±10,
blocks for new or double trades, written method once the trade is
understood. An explanation counts when it names a place-value fact
("10 tens make 1 hundred, so the total stays the same"); "I carried the
1" is a description, and the adult should prompt once more: "What
place-value fact makes that step legal?"

### Objective 5 — checking by the inverse operation (L4–L6, project, assessment)

Checking is a habit, not an extra worksheet: every subtraction in the
project ledger is checked by addition, every addition by subtraction, and
a disagreement means rework, not a second guess. Teach the check as "the
answer plus what you took away must rebuild the starting number."

## Misconception watch

| Signal | Likely cause | Reteaching move |
|---|---|---|
| Two-digit total left in a column (e.g., "11 tens" written as 11) | "One digit per column" not internalized | Return to the mat: trade the pile before writing anything |
| Composed but the carried block never added (253 + 166 = 319) | Carry written, then ignored | Point at the carried digit and say it aloud before adding its column |
| Decomposed but the column not shrunk (746 − 128 = 628) | Trade marked, column not re-counted | Re-count the column after every trade; keep the traded rod visible in a tray |
| Smaller-from-larger in each column (453 − 128 = 435) | Subtraction as digit-wise absolute difference | Blocks forbid it: you cannot take 8 cubes from a pile of 3 — rebuild and trade |
| Across-zero tens left at 10, not 9 | Second step of the double trade skipped | Say the chain aloud: "hundred → tens → ones, leaving nine tens" |
| ±100 changes the tens digit | 100 confused with 10 | Hundred-flats only: adding 100 is placing one more flat; rods and cubes never move |
| "I carried the 1" as the whole explanation | Description mistaken for explanation | Prompt: "What place-value fact makes that step legal?" |

## Re-teaching (Session 13 and flexible weeks)

Pick the highest-need objective from exit checks; replay the matching
[practice session](practice.md) variant, then give 4 fresh prompts — 3 of
4 correct to move on. If two or more objectives are "not yet" after
Session 16, use the track's flexible weeks (scope-and-sequence §5) before
Unit 04; three-digit computation is prerequisite for the word problems
in Unit 04.

## Readiness criteria

**Ready for Unit 04** when the learner is secure or developing on all
five objectives: exit checks secure on at least 4 of 5 objectives,
formative quiz ≥ 5/6, unit assessment ≥ 8/10, project score ≥ 5/10. A
"developing" on strategy choice alone may ride along (Unit 04's word
problems revisit strategy choice daily); a "not yet" on composing or
decomposing must be re-taught first — Unit 04 assumes three-digit
computation is available as a tool.
