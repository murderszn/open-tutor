# Unit Assessment — Unit 02: Addition and Subtraction within 100

**How:** performance tasks; oral, drawn, or built responses accepted; adult
scribes. Blocks, ten-frame, and number line available. ~25 minutes.
**Scoring:** 20 points total (see scoring guide). Full worked solutions in
[answer-key.md](../answer-key.md#unit-assessment).

## Tasks

**Task 1 — Make-ten (2 pts).** "Solve 9 + 6. Say the split first."
Expected: 9 needs 1; 6 splits 1 + 5; 10 + 5 = **15**.

**Task 2 — Subtraction two ways (3 pts).** "Solve 15 − 7 two different ways."
Expected: take-from-ten: 15 = 10 + 5; 10 − 7 = 3; 3 + 5 = **8**;
think-addition: 7 + ☐ = 15 → **8**. 1 point per correct strategy, 1 for
agreement.

**Task 3 — Add within 100 (3 pts).** "47 + 35. Break apart, then recombine."
Expected: 40 + 30 = 70; 7 + 5 = 12 → 1 ten + 2 ones; 70 + 12 = **82**.
1 point for the tens, 1 for the ones (with trade), 1 for recombination.

**Task 4 — Subtract within 100 (3 pts).** "63 − 28. Show the trade or the
step-down, then check with addition."
Expected: 63 − 20 = 43; 43 − 8 = **35** (or trade: 60 → 50 + 13;
50 − 20 = 30; 13 − 8 = 5; 30 + 5 = 35). Check: 35 + 28 = 63 ✓.
1 point strategy, 1 point answer, 1 point check.

**Task 5 — Three addends (2 pts).** "27 + 34 + 19."
Expected: tens 20 + 30 + 10 = 60; ones 7 + 4 + 9 = 20; 60 + 20 = **80**.

**Task 6 — Mental ±10 (3 pts).** "380 + 10 = ? 525 − 10 = ? Then tell me: why
did only the tens digit change?"
Expected: **390**; **515**; explanation naming that 10 = 1 ten + 0 ones.

**Task 7 — Explain why (2 pts).** "Your friend says 46 + 32 = 78 'because
4 + 3 = 7 and 6 + 2 = 8.' Is the answer right? Is the reason right? Fix the
reason."
Expected: the answer 78 is right, but the reason is wrong: it's 40 + 30 =
70 (tens!), not 4 + 3 = 7; 6 + 2 = 8 ones; 70 + 8 = 78.

**Task 8 — Shop story (2 pts).** "A basket costs 56 tokens and a scoop costs
29 tokens. A customer buys both, then returns the scoop. What is the final
total? Show your work."
Expected: 56 + 29: 50 + 20 = 70, 6 + 9 = 15 → **85**; 85 − 29: 85 − 20 =
65, 65 − 9 = **56**. Final total **56** tokens.

## Scoring guide

- **17–20:** secure — proceed to Unit 03.
- **13–16:** mostly secure — re-teach the missed task type in a flexible
  week, then move on.
- **Below 13:** re-teach with blocks before Unit 03; use the teacher guide's
  catch-up plan.

This is a teacher-made check, not a standardized or validated test. It
measures this unit's objectives only.
