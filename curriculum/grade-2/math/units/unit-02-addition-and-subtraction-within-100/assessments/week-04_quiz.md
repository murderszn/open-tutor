# Formative Quiz — Unit 02 (Week 10)

**How:** oral or observed; adult reads each item, learner answers aloud,
points, or builds with blocks; adult scribes. 10 items, ~15 minutes.
**Purpose:** formative — finds what to re-teach before the unit assessment,
not a grade. Full solutions in [answer-key.md](../answer-key.md#formative-quiz).

Say each item exactly as written, then wait. Blocks and the number line stay
available the whole time.

1. "8 + 5. Tell me the split first, then the answer."
2. "7 + 7."
3. "9 + 6 with make-ten."
4. "13 − 5. You may take from ten or think addition."
5. "14 − 8."
6. "26 + 43. Break it apart."
7. "58 − 21. Break it apart."
8. "72 − 36."
9. "24 + 31 + 15."
10. "460 + 10."

**Scoring (for the adult's notes only):** count an item correct when the
answer is right *and* the strategy step was shown (split, double, trade, or
break-apart). 8+/10 with strategies shown → proceed to the unit assessment.
6–7/10 → re-teach the missed objective in Session 16's re-teach slot, then
assess. Below 6/10 → use a flexible track week for re-teaching before Unit
03.

**Misconception watch:** items 6–8 catch the digit-adding error (4 + 3 = 7
instead of 40 + 30 = 70); item 8 catches the no-trade subtraction error;
item 10 catches concatenation (writing 4610).
