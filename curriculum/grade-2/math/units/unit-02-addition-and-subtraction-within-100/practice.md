# Practice, Game, and Review Sessions

Named sessions referenced in the [unit pacing](README.md#16-session-pacing-4-weeks--4-sessions-25-30-minutes-each).
All checks accept oral, pointing, or drawing responses; the adult scribes when
needed. Every session opens with a 2-minute retrieval warm-up from an earlier
lesson.

## Session A — Make-ten strategy trays

*Pairs with Lessons 01; used in Session 2.*

**Warm-up:** Say the make-ten need for 9, 8, 7: "9 needs 1, 8 needs 2,
7 needs 3."

1. The adult deals 6 sum cards: 9 + 3, 8 + 7, 7 + 6, 9 + 8, 6 + 9, 8 + 4. The
   learner solves each with cubes, saying the split aloud.
   (Expected: 12, 15, 13, 17, 15, 12.)
2. **Speed sort:** the adult calls sums; the learner holds up the "need"
   fingers (for 8 + 5, hold up 2) before solving.
3. **Find the lucky error:** adult shows 7 + 9 split as 7 + 2 + 7 = 16 with a
   wrong split; learner fixes it. (Expected: 7 needs 3, not 2; 9 splits 3 +
   6; 10 + 6 = 16 — same total, wrong split.)
4. **Draw it:** learner draws two solved sums as ten-frame + loose circles on
   paper.

**Success criterion:** 5 of 6 sums solved with a correct split named aloud.

## Session B — Make-ten match

*Game; used in Session 3.*

**Warm-up:** Chant the doubles 5 + 5 through 9 + 9.

1. Lay number cards 1–9 face up in two rows. The adult flips a "target" card
   11–18. The learner finds two cards that make the target *via a ten* (for
   13: 9 + 4, "9 needs 1, 4 gives it").
2. Each found pair scores a point if the learner says the split. Play to 5
   points.
3. **Twist round:** the adult secretly removes one card from a pair and asks
   "what's missing?" — a preview of unknowns (Unit 04).

**Success criterion:** finds and explains 4 pairs.

## Session C — Fluency review and readiness check

*Review; used in Session 4.*

**Warm-up:** Count-on ladder 8 + 3 on the number line (9, 10, 11).

1. Mixed rapid-fire (oral): 9 + 4, 7 + 7, 8 + 6, 6 + 5, 9 + 9, 7 + 8.
   (Expected: 13, 14, 14, 11, 18, 15.)
2. The learner sorts 6 solved cards into "make-ten," "double," "near-double,"
   "count-on" piles and defends one placement.
3. **Readiness check for Lesson 02's subtraction preview:** "13 − 5 — try
   think-addition." (Expected attempt: 5 + ☐ = 13 → 8.) If stuck, that's
   fine — Lesson 03 teaches it; just note it.

**Success criterion:** names a fitting strategy for 5 of 6 sums.

## Session D — Doubles chains and count-on ladders

*Pairs with Lesson 02; used in Session 6.*

**Warm-up:** Doubles chant to 9 + 9.

1. **Doubles chain:** adult calls 6 + 6 (12), then 6 + 7 (?), then 7 + 7
   (?), then 7 + 8 (?) — the learner climbs: 12, 13, 14, 15.
2. **Count-on ladders:** on the number line, 9 + 4 (10, 11, 12, 13) and
   15 − 3 (14, 13, 12).
3. **Near-double hunt:** from sums 8 + 7, 9 + 6, 5 + 4, the learner names the
   hidden double first. (Expected: 7+7, 6+6? no — 9+6 hides 6+6? Expected:
   8+7 hides 7+7=14→15; 9+6 hides 6+6=12→15; 5+4 hides 4+4=8→9.)
4. **Write two:** learner writes 2 near-double equations of their own and
   solves them.

**Success criterion:** completes the chain without restarting; ladders land
on the correct number.

## Session E — Doubles duel

*Game; used in Session 7.*

**Warm-up:** Each player rolls one die and doubles it.

1. Both roll two dice; each player makes the best near-double they can from
   their dice (e.g., 5 and 6 → "5 + 5 = 10, one more is 11") and says the
   total. Higher explained total wins the round.
2. Play 5 rounds; the learner must use a *different* strategy explanation at
   least twice (make-ten, near-double, count-on).

**Success criterion:** explains 4 of 5 round totals with a named strategy.

## Session F — Take-from-ten practice

*Pairs with Lesson 03; used in Session 9.*

**Warm-up:** Split 12, 15, 17 into 10 + ones with the ten-frame.

1. Solve with take-from-ten, always saying the "add back" step: 14 − 7, 16 −
   8, 12 − 5, 18 − 9. (Expected: 7, 8, 7, 9.)
2. Solve the same four with think-addition; check that answers match.
   (7 + 7 = 14 ✓; 8 + 8 = 16 ✓; 5 + 7 = 12 ✓; 9 + 9 = 18 ✓.)
3. **Lena's error, again:** adult shows 15 − 6 = 4 (forgot the 5 ones back).
   Learner corrects: 10 − 6 = 4, 4 + 5 = **9**.
4. **Number-line step-down:** 17 − 9 as 17 − 7 − 2 = 8 on the strip — a
   preview of Lesson 05's step-down.

**Success criterion:** all 4 solved both ways with matching answers.
