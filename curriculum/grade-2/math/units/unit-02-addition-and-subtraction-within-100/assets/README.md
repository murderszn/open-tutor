# Assets — Unit 02: Addition and Subtraction within 100

## shop-counter-blank-tags.png

- **Prompt (verbatim):** "A warm, child-friendly flat illustration of a
  pretend play shop counter: a wooden counter with two open baskets, one
  basket holding red crayons and the other basket holding blue marbles;
  three blank white price tags hanging from the baskets, completely empty
  with no numbers or words on them; soft simple shapes, bright cheerful
  colors, clean uncluttered composition, absolutely no text anywhere in the
  image."
- **Tool:** built-in image generation (`media.generate_image`), 2026-10-16.
- **Learning purpose:** story prompt for the
  [Two-Digit Tool Shop](../project.md) project — the learner imagines their
  own shop, writes their own two-digit prices on paper tags, and fills
  customer orders with break-apart addition and subtraction.
- **Factual review (2026-10-16):** inspected at full size. Two baskets on a
  wooden counter; left basket holds red crayons, right basket holds blue
  marbles; three white price tags hang from the baskets and are completely
  blank. No numerals, words, or readable labels anywhere — the design
  deliberately avoids AI-generated text, so there is no garbled-text risk.
  The image carries no quantities to verify: learners supply their own
  prices and counts, so the illustration cannot introduce false math.
- **Alt text:** "A flat illustration of a wooden shop counter holding two
  woven baskets, one with red crayons and one with blue marbles; three
  blank white price tags hang from the baskets."
- **Caption:** "The Tool Shop counter. The price tags are blank — you
  decide what each basket costs."
- **Text alternative:** "Picture a shop counter with two baskets — crayons
  and marbles — and three empty price tags waiting for you to write
  two-digit prices on them." (Used in [project.md](../project.md); the
  activity works fully without seeing the picture.)
- **Known limitations:** AI illustration, not a photograph of a real shop;
  labeled as such. No learner-identifying content.
