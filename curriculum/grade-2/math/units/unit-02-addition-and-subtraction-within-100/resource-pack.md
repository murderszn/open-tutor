# Resource Pack — Unit 02: Addition and Subtraction within 100

**Summary (adult planning judgment):** supplements for teaching mental
fluency within 20 (make-ten, doubles, take-from-ten), place-value addition
and subtraction within 100, adding up to four two-digit numbers, and mental
±10 to a 2nd grader. Every core objective also has a free, no-account,
offline path (rods/cubes, ten-frames, number line, the shop project).

**Check date:** 2026-10-16 (all items below). The Common Core grade-2 NBT
page was re-opened live on 2026-10-16 and matches the codes/descriptions in
the unit README. Video runtimes and caption availability are reported only
where the source page showed them; anything else is marked unverified —
preview before assigning.
