## Lesson 01

**Guided practice, round 4 (error analysis):** 9 rods are 9 tens = 90 ones —
not a hundred. Add one more rod to make ten tens, then bundle: ten tens =
one hundred = 100.

**Exit check:** ten tens make one hundred (10 tens = 1 hundred = 100 ones).

*Also:* round 3 — 4 flats = 40 tens; round 5 — 700 = 7 flats = 70 rods;
extension — 9 hundreds = 900 ones.
