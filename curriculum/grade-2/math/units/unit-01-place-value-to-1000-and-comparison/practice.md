# Practice, Game, and Review Sessions — Unit 01

Named sessions referenced by the [16-session pacing](README.md#16-session-pacing-4-weeks--4-sessions-25-30-minutes-each).
Each runs 25–30 minutes. The adult observes and records; all responses may be
oral, drawn, or built with blocks.

## Session A — Build-and-count hundred trays

*(Pacing: Session 2)*

1. **Tray build (10 min):** the adult calls hundreds — "4 hundreds," "7
   hundreds," "2 hundreds" — the learner builds each with flats on the tray
   and says the total two ways ("4 hundreds — 400"). Six rounds.
2. **Tens-inside check (8 min):** for two builds, the learner counts the rods
   hidden inside the flats ("3 flats = 30 tens") by pointing at each flat
   and counting 10, 20, 30.
3. **Draw-and-label (7 min):** the learner draws two of the builds as quick
   squares and labels each with its hundreds count and total.

*Success criterion:* all six builds use flats (not rods) and every total is
named both ways.

## Session B — Build-a-number trays

*(Pacing: Sessions 6 and 9)*

**Session 6 version (builds):** the adult calls three-digit numbers (include
two with zero tens, e.g., 307, 460); the learner builds each on the
hundreds/tens/ones mat and names every place. Six rounds.

**Session 9 version (expanded-form puzzles):** the adult writes expanded
forms on cards (600 + 40 + 7; 800 + 30; 500 + 9; 300 + 50 + 0); the learner
builds the number, then writes the numeral. Six rounds — the zero-place
cards are the point.

*Success criterion:* zero placeholders appear in every written numeral; each
build's places are named aloud.

## Session C — Hundred-chain and skip-count games

*(Pacing: Sessions 3 and 7)*

**Session 3 — Hundred-chain counting:** stand in a circle (or across the
table). The adult starts: "100." Each turn adds one hundred — anyone who
breaks the chain (skips 600, says "ten hundred") sits a round out and
re-enters at 100. Play to 1000 twice.

**Session 7 — Number-form matching:** the learner's recipe cards from Lesson
02 (numeral front, build drawing back) plus new cards the adult makes for
number names and expanded forms. Lay all cards face-up; match each numeral
to its name and its expanded form. Time the round, then beat the time.

*Success criterion:* hundred chain runs 100→1000 without a break; all 4+
card triples matched correctly.

## Session D — Build, draw, and label review

*(Pacing: Session 4)*

Three stations, ~8 minutes each; the learner rotates:

1. **Build:** draw 3 number cards; build each with blocks; the adult checks
   place naming.
2. **Draw:** the adult names 3 numbers (one with a zero); the learner draws
   quick flats/rods/cubes and writes the numeral.
3. **Label:** the adult shows 3 pre-built block groups (one with no rods);
   the learner writes the numeral and circles the hundreds digit.

*Success criterion:* 8 of 9 station tasks correct; misses become Session
12's re-teach list.

## Session 12 — Re-teach and practice (review week)

The adult picks the highest-need objective from Sessions 1–11 exit checks:

- **Hundreds shaky →** replay Session A with 10-tens-inside counting.
- **Zero placeholders shaky →** replay Session B's build rounds with 306,
  640, 509.
- **Expanded form shaky →** replay Session 9's puzzle cards both directions.
- **Skip-counting shaky →** replay the Session 3 chain by 10s and 5s.

End with 4 fresh exit-check-style prompts on the re-taught objective; the
learner must get 3 of 4 to move on.
