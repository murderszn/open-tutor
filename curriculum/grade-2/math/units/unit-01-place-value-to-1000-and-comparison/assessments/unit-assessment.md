# Unit Assessment — Unit 01: Place Value to 1000 and Number Comparison

**Format:** performance tasks with blocks, drawing, and short writing; the
adult scribes when the learner isn't ready to write. ~30 minutes across
Session 16. **Answer key:**
[answer-key.md](../answer-key.md#unit-assessment) (kept separate from this
page).

**Unit:** [U01](../README.md)

## Tasks

1. **Build 506** with flats, rods, and cubes. Explain what each digit
   means. *(Objective 1)*
2. Write **700 + 20 + 5** as a numeral and as a number name. *(Objective 2)*
3. Write **"nine hundred twelve"** as a numeral and in expanded form.
   *(Objective 2)*
4. **Skip-count by 100s from 200 to 1000.** Say each landing. *(Objective 3)*
5. **Compare 634 and 643.** Record with `>`, `=`, or `<` and write one
   sentence of hundreds-first reasoning. *(Objective 4)*
6. **Picture task.** Look at
   [`assets/compare-234-vs-241.png`](../assets/compare-234-vs-241.png) (or
   its text alternative). What number does each row show? Record the
   comparison with `>`, `=`, or `<`. *(Objectives 1, 4)*
7. **Order 870, 807, and 878** from least to greatest, writing the full
   `<` chain. *(Objective 5)*
8. **Number line.** On a strip marked 400 to 500, mark where 450 goes and
   explain the placement. *(Objective 5)*

## Scoring guidance (adult use)

Each task scores 0–2:

- **2 (secure):** correct answer with place-based reasoning (blocks, words,
  or the chain shown).
- **1 (developing):** correct answer but no reasoning, or reasoning with a
  small slip the learner self-corrects.
- **0 (not yet):** wrong answer or no place-based reasoning.

**14–16:** secure — ready for Unit 02.
**10–13:** developing — re-teach the missed objectives using the
[teacher guide](../teacher-guide.md), then re-check with parallel tasks.
**9 or below:** not yet — use the track's flexible diagnostic/catch-up
weeks before Unit 02.

This assessment is teacher-made for this unit; it is not a standardized or
validated test.
