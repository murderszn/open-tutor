# Resource Pack — Unit 04: Word Problems and Unknowns

**Summary (adult planning judgment):** supplements for teaching
one- and two-step word problems within 100 — all five situation types,
unknowns in all positions, bar modeling, and reasonableness checks —
to a 2nd grader; every core objective also has a free, no-account,
offline path (counters, bars, number lines, story cards).

**Check date:** 2026-10-29 (all items below). Video runtimes and
caption availability are reported only where the source page showed
them; anything else is marked unverified — preview before assigning.
