# Formative Quiz — Unit 04 (Week 18, Session 14)

**Unit:** [U04](../README.md) · **Format:** oral/observed, adult-scribed
**Adult note:** read each item aloud; the learner answers with
counters, pointing, drawing, or words. The adult scribes. Solutions
are in [answer-key.md](../answer-key.md) — kept separate from this
student page. This quiz is a teaching check, not a standardized or
validated assessment.

1. Act it out: "Jae had 45 shells. He found 27 more shells. How many
   shells does Jae have now?" Say the equation.
2. Look at the picture in [Lesson 02](../lessons/lesson-02-put-together-take-apart.md)
   (or its text alternative). Write one add-to story and one
   take-from story about the stall, choosing your own numbers within
   100. Solve both. (The adult checks that each story matches its
   type and that the arithmetic is correct; any sensible numbers
   count.)
3. Draw the bar: "A bag holds 83 beads. 47 beads are red and the rest
   are blue. How many beads are blue?"
4. Compare: "A red kite string is 69 meters long. A blue kite string
   is 44 meters long. How many meters longer is the red string?"
5. Find the hidden start: "Some stickers were in a book. 35 more
   stickers were added. Now there are 88 stickers. How many stickers
   were in the book first?" Say the equation and the replay.
6. Two steps: "Ana had 58 ribbons. She bought 26 more ribbons, then
   gave 31 ribbons away. How many ribbons does Ana have now?" Show
   both steps and circle the middle amount.

**Scoring (adult):** 1 point per item (item 2 needs both stories to
match their types with correct arithmetic; item 5 needs the equation,
the answer, and the replay; item 6 needs both steps with the middle
amount circled). 5–6 = secure; 3–4 = developing (re-teach the missed
objective before the unit assessment); 0–2 = not yet (replay the
matching lesson, then re-quiz with fresh numbers).
