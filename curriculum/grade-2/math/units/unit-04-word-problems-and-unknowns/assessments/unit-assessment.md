# Unit Assessment — Unit 04: Word Problems and Unknowns (Week 18, Session 16)

**Unit:** [U04](../README.md) · **Format:** oral/observed with short
written work, adult-scribed where needed
**Adult note:** read each item aloud; the learner may use counters,
bars, drawings, or equations. Solutions and scoring guidance are in
[answer-key.md](../answer-key.md) — kept separate from this student
page. This assessment is a teaching check, not a standardized or
validated assessment.

## Part A — Story problems (Unit 04 objectives)

1. Add to, result unknown: "Sam collects 36 bottle caps on Monday and
   48 bottle caps on Tuesday. How many bottle caps does Sam collect
   in all?"
2. Take from, change unknown: "A crate holds 91 oranges. Some oranges
   are sold. Now there are 64 oranges in the crate. How many oranges
   were sold?"
3. Take apart, part unknown: "A box holds 77 marbles. 39 marbles are
   glass and the rest are wood. How many marbles are wood?"
4. Compare, smaller unknown: "Mia has 82 ribbons. Mia has 26 more
   ribbons than Noah. How many ribbons does Noah have?"
5. Two-step: "Leo had 47 cards. He got 33 more cards, then gave 29
   cards to a friend. How many cards does Leo have now?" Show both
   steps and circle the middle amount.
6. Reasonableness: "Estimate 58 + 37 by rounding to the nearest ten,
   then solve it exactly. A classmate answers 85. Is 85 sensible?
   Explain with your estimate and an inverse check."

## Part B — Midyear review (Units 01–03)

7. Compare three-digit numbers: which is bigger, 528 or 582? Explain
   with hundreds, tens, and ones.
8. Add three-digit numbers with blocks: 364 + 275. Name every trade.
9. Subtract three-digit numbers with blocks: 503 − 168. Name every
   trade, including what happens with the zero tens.
10. Mental computation: 745 + 100 and 745 − 10. Which digit changed in
    each answer, and why?

**Scoring (adult):** Part A: 1 point per item (item 5 needs both
steps with the middle amount circled; item 6 needs the estimate, the
exact answer, and a correct judgment about 85). Part B: 1 point per
item (item 8 needs both trades named; item 9 needs the across-zero
chain named; item 10 needs both answers with the digit named).
Part A 5–6 = secure on story problems; Part B 3–4 = midyear skills
holding. Any objective below secure gets a re-teach plan in the
[teacher guide](../teacher-guide.md) before Unit 05 begins.
