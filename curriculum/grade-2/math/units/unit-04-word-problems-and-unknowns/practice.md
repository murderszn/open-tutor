# Practice Sessions — Unit 04: Word Problems and Unknowns

**Unit:** [U04](README.md) · **Sessions:** 2, 4, 6, 8, 10, 13 of 16
**Adult note:** read each story aloud first; the learner acts it,
draws it, or builds it before writing. Solutions are in
[answer-key.md](answer-key.md) — kept separate from this student page.

## Session A — Story theater (Session 2, after L1)

Act out each story with counters, then draw it and write the equation.
Say "grew" or "shrank" before computing.

1. "Maya picks 25 apples. Then she picks 33 more apples. How many
   apples does Maya have now?"
2. "A tray holds 71 cookies. Children eat 26 cookies. How many cookies
   are left on the tray?"
3. "Omar reads 48 pages on Saturday and 29 pages on Sunday. How many
   pages did Omar read in all?" (Trick check: is this one story action
   or two groups put together? Either reading works — the equation is
   the same — but name which one you used.)
4. "A box holds 86 crayons. 39 crayons are given away. How many
   crayons are left in the box?"
5. "A farmer collects 37 eggs in the morning. By evening she has 80
   eggs. How many eggs did she collect after the morning?" (Change
   unknown — a preview of Lesson 04; let the learner struggle
   productively, then name the box.)
6. Error hunt: "Jon had 54 marbles and lost 27. Jon says he now has
   37 marbles." Act it and decide: right or wrong?

## Session B — Story sort (Session 4, after L2)

The adult reads each story; the learner sorts its card into
ADD TO, TAKE FROM, PUT TOGETHER / TAKE APART, or COMPARE — before
solving anything. Only after sorting does the learner solve.

1. "A shop sells 58 notebooks in the morning and 34 notebooks in the
   afternoon. How many notebooks did the shop sell in all?"
2. "Liam has 73 stickers. His sister has 45 stickers. How many more
   stickers does Liam have?"
3. "A jar holds 66 buttons: 38 red and the rest blue. How many buttons
   are blue?"
4. "A class had 49 chairs. 17 more chairs were brought in. How many
   chairs are there now?"
5. "A rope is 91 centimeters long. 38 centimeters are cut off. How
   long is the rope now?"
6. "Sara has 27 dolls. Ana has 19 more dolls than Sara. How many dolls
   does Ana have?" (Compare, bigger unknown — a preview of Lesson 03;
   sorting it correctly counts even before the lesson.)

## Session C — Compare bars (Session 6, after L3)

Draw two bars from the same left edge for each story. Circle the
sticking-out part, or the missing bar, before computing.

1. "A red ribbon is 66 centimeters long. A blue ribbon is 41
   centimeters long. How many centimeters longer is the red ribbon?"
2. "Tom has 39 marbles. Ella has 24 more marbles than Tom. How many
   marbles does Ella have?"
3. "A book has 73 pages. A second book has 29 fewer pages than the
   first book. How many pages does the second book have?"
4. "Two teams score 58 points and 36 points. What is the difference in
   their scores?"
5. Error hunt: "Ben has 84 points, which is 31 more points than Sam.
   Sam has 84 + 31 = 115 points." Is Sam's score possible within 100?
   What is it really?

## Session D — Unknown hunt (Session 8, after L4)

For each story, first name which part is hiding — START, CHANGE, or
RESULT — then write the equation with the box in the right place,
then solve by undoing or comparing.

1. "Some children were on a bus. 23 more got on. Now there are 78
   children on the bus. How many children were on the bus first?"
2. "A baker made 92 buns. Some buns were sold. Now there are 64 buns
   left. How many buns were sold?"
3. "A box held some pencils. 31 pencils were taken out. Now 49
   pencils are left. How many pencils were in the box first?"
4. "There were 45 fish in a tank. Some more fish were added. Now
   there are 83 fish. How many fish were added?"
5. "A library had 97 books on a shelf. 58 books were borrowed. How
   many books are left?" (Result unknown — the hunt includes easy
   ones, so the learner practices naming, not just hard cases.)
6. Error hunt: "□ + 19 = 74, so □ = 74 + 19 = 93." Replay it and fix
   it.

## Session E — Two-step builders (Session 10, after L5)

Write both equations and circle the middle amount for each story.

1. "Kai had 45 marbles. He won 27 more, then lost 18. How many
   marbles does Kai have now?"
2. "A garden grows 31 tomatoes, 28 cucumbers, and 19 peppers. How many
   vegetables did the garden grow in all?"
3. "A shop had 94 scarves. It sold 38 on Monday and 17 on Tuesday.
   How many scarves are left?"
4. "A tank held 72 liters of water. 29 liters were used, then 14
   liters were added. How many liters are in the tank now?"
5. Error hunt: "Mia had 63 stickers, gave 26 away, then got 15 more.
   Mia's work: 63 + 15 = 78, 78 − 26 = 52." The answer is 52 — but did
   Mia follow the story? Rework it in order.

## Session F — Mixed review and error hunt (Session 13, midyear review)

Six stations, mixing Unit 04 with Units 01–03 retrieval. The adult
re-teaches the highest-need objective after this session.

1. Compare: which is bigger, 456 or 465? Explain with hundreds, tens,
   and ones.
2. Compute with blocks: 347 + 258. Name every trade.
3. Compute mentally, then explain: 500 − 100. Which digit changed?
4. Story, unknown in the middle: "A class needs 95 chairs. It has 62
   chairs. How many more chairs does it need?"
5. Two-step story: "Sam had 55 stickers. He bought 36 more, then gave
   27 away. How many stickers does Sam have now?"
6. Error hunt (three mistakes to find): "83 − 47 = 46. Check: 46 + 47
   = 93, so the answer is right." Is the check right? Is the answer
   right? What should both be?
