# Answer Key — Unit 04: Word Problems and Unknowns

**Unit:** [U04](README.md) · **Adult only — never beside student materials.**
Every solution below was solved independently of the student pages
and reconciled with them. Where a student page gives an expected
response in parentheses, it matches this key.

## Lesson 01 — Add-to and take-from, result unknown

- **Guided:** (1) 41 + 25 = **66**. (2) 68 − 31 = **37**. (3) 29 + 43 =
  **72**. (4) Error round: 46 + 28 is **74**, not 64: 40 + 20 = 60,
  6 + 8 = 14, 60 + 14 = 74. (5) "Did the pile grow or shrink?"
- **Independent:** 52 + 38 = **90**; 83 − 45 = **38** (83 − 40 = 43,
  43 − 5 = 38); 27 + 54 = **81**.
- **Application:** 63 − 18 = **45 books**.
- **Exit check:** 47 + 26 = **73**.

## Lesson 02 — Put together and take apart

- **Guided:** (1) 52 − 27 = **25 hearts** (27 + □ = 52). (2) 43 + 28 =
  **71 books**. (3) 61 − 29 = **32 blue marbles**. (4) Error round:
  31 + 37 = 68 ≠ 58; the missing part is 58 − 31 = **27**. (5) "Nothing

  77 − 39 = **38 wood beads**.
- **Application:** 26 + 33 = **59 seeds**.
- **Exit check:** 87 − 45 = **42 wood beads**.

## Lesson 03 — Compare stories

- **Guided:** (1) 71 − 48 = **23 points**. (2) 56 − 18 = **38 shells**

## Lesson 04 — Unknowns in all positions

- **Guided:** (1) □ + 26 = 70 → 70 − 26 = **44**. (2) 85 − □ = 57 →
  85 − 57 = **28**. (3) □ − 19 = 46 → 46 + 19 = **65**. (4) Error
  round: □ + 24 = 68 → 68 − 24 = **44** (replay 44 + 24 = 68); 92 is
  wrong because 92 + 24 = 116 ≠ 68. (5) "Undo addition with
  subtraction; undo subtraction with addition; then replay."
- **Independent:** □ + 38 = 91 → 91 − 38 = **53**; 64 − □ = 39 →
  64 − 39 = **25**; □ − 27 = 58 → 58 + 27 = **85**.
- **Application:** □ − 24 = 37 → 37 + 24 = **61 pencils** (replay
  61 − 24 = 37).
- **Exit check:** □ + 29 = 76 → 76 − 29 = **47** (replay 47 + 29 = 76).

## Lesson 05 — Two-step stories

- **Guided:** (1) 57 + 23 = **80**; 80 − 14 = **66**. (2) 41 + 29 =
  **70**; 70 + 18 = **88**. (3) 90 − 27 = **63**; 63 − 19 = **44**.
  (4) Error round: Mia's method is wrong even though 52 matches — the
  story's order is give-away first (63 − 26 = 37), then get-more
  (37 + 15 = **52**). The middle amount is 37, not 78. (5) "The middle
  amount — step 2 needs it."
- **Independent:** 38 + 45 = **83**; 83 − 26 = **57**. 63 − 28 =
  **35**; 35 + 17 = **52**. 29 + 36 = **65**; 65 + 24 = **89**.
- **Application:** 87 − 25 = **62**; 62 − 18 = **44 stickers**
  (check: 44 + 25 + 18 = 87).
- **Exit check:** 54 + 27 = **81**; 81 − 33 = **48**.

## Lesson 06 — Check: estimate, inverse, story fit

- **Guided:** (1) Estimate 60 + 30 = 90; 62 + 29 = **91**; inverse
  91 − 29 = 62. (2) Estimate 90 − 40 = 50; 85 − 38 = **47**; inverse
  47 + 38 = 85. (3) Error round: 74 − 29 = **45**, not 55 — estimate
  70 − 30 = 40 flags 55; inverse 55 + 29 = 84 ≠ 74 confirms it;
  inverse of the correct answer: 45 + 29 = 74. (4) Story fit fails:
  finding more must end bigger than 41, but 25 is smaller. (5)
  "Estimate first — it is fastest."
- **Independent:** 53 + 28 = **81** (estimate 80; inverse 81 − 28 =
  53). 96 − 47 = **49** (estimate 50; inverse 49 + 47 = 96). 38 + 17 =
  **55**, not 64 (estimate 60; the claim's inverse 64 − 17 = 47 ≠ 38).
- **Application:** 43 + 38 = **81 pieces of fruit**; estimate 80;
  inverse 81 − 38 = 43 — the claim is correct.
- **Exit check:** Estimate 70 + 20 = 90; 67 + 24 = **91**; inverse
  91 − 24 = 67; 91 is near 90 — sensible.
- **Extension:** 86 − 39 = **47** (inverse 47 + 39 = 86); the wrong
  answer 57 passes a rough estimate but fails the inverse
  (57 + 39 = 96).

### Session D — Unknown hunt

1. START: □ + 23 = 78 → 78 − 23 = **55 children**. 2. CHANGE:

### Session E — Two-step builders

### Session F — Mixed review and error hunt

1. **465 is bigger**: hundreds equal (4 = 4), tens 6 > 5, so
   465 > 456. 2. 347 + 258 = **605**: ones 7 + 8 = 15 → 5, carry 1

   4. 95 − 62 = **33 chairs**. 5. 55 + 36 = **91**; 91 − 27 = **64
   stickers**. 6. Both wrong: 83 − 47 = **36**, not 46; the shown
   check fails too, since 46 + 47 = 93 ≠ 83. Correct check: 36 + 47
