# Starter Lesson — Bundle tens and add within 100

**Status:** draft supplement. **Time:** three 25-minute sessions. **Goal:** Represent two-digit numbers with tens and ones and use place-value drawings to add within 100.

## Adult preparation and readiness

Use 100 large counters or sticks bundled into tens, paper, and pencil. Readiness: learner represents 34 as 3 tens and 4 ones. If not, practice building 10 and 20 before adding. Let learner draw base-ten sketches rather than use counters.

## Teach and model

Explain: “A ten is a group of ten ones. We can add tens to tens and ones to ones, then regroup ten ones as one ten.”

**Worked example 1:** 34 = 3 tens + 4 ones; 26 = 2 tens + 6 ones. Together: 5 tens and 10 ones. Trade 10 ones for 1 ten, giving 6 tens = 60.

**Worked example 2:** 47 + 25. Add tens: 40 + 20 = 60. Add ones: 7 + 5 = 12. Combine 60 + 12 = 72. Explain that 12 ones is 1 ten and 2 ones.

## Pacing and tasks

Session 1: model examples and guide tasks 1–2. Session 2: guided practice tasks 3–4, then learner independently solves task 5. Session 3: application task 6 and exit check.

1. **Adult-guided:** Draw 3 tens and 5 ones; name the number.
2. **Guided:** Compare 46 and 64; explain which digit place decides first.
3. **Guided:** Solve 32 + 15 with tens/ones sketches.
4. **Guided:** Solve 28 + 17; circle the ten ones and regroup.
5. **Independent:** Solve 53 + 24 and label tens and ones.
6. **Application:** A box holds 38 red blocks and 27 blue blocks. How many blocks? Show a model and equation.

**Answer checks:** 1) 35; 2) 64 > 46 because 6 tens > 4 tens; 3) 47; 4) 45; 5) 77; 6) 65 blocks. For 28 + 17, 8+7=15 ones, regroup 10 to make 4 tens and 5 ones.

## Exit check and product

Solve 36 + 28 and explain regrouping. Expected: 36 has 3 tens and 6 ones; 28 has 2 tens and 8 ones. Combine to make 5 tens and 14 ones, regroup 10 ones as one ten, and get 6 tens and 4 ones: 64. Product: one worked addition story with drawing and answer sentence.

## Review rubric

| Criterion | 2 — shows skill | 1 — developing | 0 — not yet observed |
|---|---|---|---|
| Place value | Correctly represents tens and ones | Builds with prompting | Revisit bundling ten ones |
| Addition | Adds two-digit values accurately | Correct with model support | Reduce to no-regrouping examples |
| Explanation | Describes why ten ones are regrouped | Names regrouping after prompt | Model with physical bundles |

**Adult guidance:** A frequent error is adding digits without attending to place (e.g., 28+17 as 315). Keep tens visually grouped and label columns. Accept oral/pointing answers, large-print cards, and adult scribing. Use non-food, safe large counters; supervise sticks and bundles. Fully offline; this one lesson does not cover the full grade-level operations sequence.
