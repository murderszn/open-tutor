# Project — My Information Book

**Deliverable:** a 4-page information book about beavers, made by the
learner with adult support, using at least four text features correctly.

## Materials

Paper (4 sheets folded into a book, or stapled), crayons or colored
pencils, a pencil. Nothing to buy. The generated picture from Lesson 03
may be looked at for ideas, but the learner draws their own pictures.

## Steps

1. **Plan (Project work 1).** Pick the book's main topic: beavers.
   Choose one focus per page: (1) A Beaver's Body, (2) Building a Dam,
   (3) A Home Called a Lodge, (4) Beaver Families and Food. Write or
   dictate the plan, including which text feature each page will use
   and two glossary words for page 4.
2. **Draft and illustrate (Project work 2).** Write or dictate 2–4
   short sentences per page. Add one picture per page that teaches
   something the words do not say (Lesson 03 skill), with a caption
   under each picture.
3. **Add features (Project work 3).** Add: a heading on every page;
   at least two bold words with meanings in a glossary on page 4; a
   title on the cover; and an index on the last page listing two
   topics and their pages. Read one page aloud, naming its feature.
4. **Share (Session 16).** Read the book aloud to the adult (or record
   it, if the adult prefers). The adult asks one who/what/where
   question and one why/how question about the book, and the learner
   answers with proof from their own pages.

## Rubric

| Criterion | 3 — Strong | 2 — Growing | 1 — Starting |
|---|---|---|---|
| Main topic and paragraph focus | Every page matches the beaver topic; each page's focus is clear and matches its heading | Most pages match; one page's focus is unclear or off-heading | Pages wander off topic or have no clear focus |
| Text features | Heading on every page; 2+ bold words defined in a glossary; captions under pictures; a 2-topic index, all used correctly | Three of the four feature types present and mostly correct | Fewer than three feature types, or features named but not used |
| Pictures clarify | Each picture adds a fact the words do not state; captions explain the picture | Some pictures add facts; some only repeat the words | Pictures decorate only, or do not match the words |
| Accuracy and completeness | All facts match the unit texts or the Resource Pack sources; 2–4 sentences per page; book complete | One factual error, or one page short of sentences | Several factual errors, or pages missing |
| Sharing | Reads a page aloud clearly; answers both questions with proof from the book | Reads with support; answers one question with proof | Cannot read a page or answer with proof |

**Ready:** mostly 3s, no 1s. **Reteach:** any 1 — revisit the matching
lesson (focus: Lesson 01; features: Lesson 02; pictures: Lesson 03)
and revise that page before the unit is marked complete.
