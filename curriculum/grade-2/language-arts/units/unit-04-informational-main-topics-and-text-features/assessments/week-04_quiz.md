# Formative Quiz — Unit 04 (Week 18, Session 15)

**Adult note:** read questions aloud if needed; learner may answer
orally, by pointing, by drawing, or in writing. 8 of 10 correct is the
ready mark; re-teach any miss the same day. Answers are in
[answer-key.md](../answer-key.md) only — never on this page.

Use "Beavers: Nature's Builders" ([Lesson 01](../lessons/lesson-01-main-topic-and-paragraph-focus.md)),
its glossary and index, the generated picture
([Lesson 03](../lessons/lesson-03-what-pictures-add.md)), "Why Ponds
Help Other Animals" ([Lesson 04](../lessons/lesson-04-authors-purpose-and-reasons.md)),
"How a Beaver Builds a Dam"
([Lesson 05](../lessons/lesson-05-steps-in-order.md)), and "Beaver
Families" ([Lesson 06](../lessons/lesson-06-two-texts-same-topic.md)).

1. What is the main topic of "Beavers: Nature's Builders"?
2. What is the focus of the paragraph under "Building a Dam"?
3. Use the glossary: what does **gnaw** mean?
4. Use the index: under which heading do you find *lodge*?
5. Name one fact the generated picture shows that the Lesson 01 words
   do not state.
6. What is the author's point in "Why Ponds Help Other Animals"?
7. Name one reason the author gives for that point, and the animal it
   helps.
8. Put these dam steps in order: (a) lay branches across the stream,
   (b) find a narrow place, (c) pack mud between branches.
9. Name one point both "Beavers: Nature's Builders" and "Beaver
   Families" make.
10. Name one point only "Beaver Families" makes.
