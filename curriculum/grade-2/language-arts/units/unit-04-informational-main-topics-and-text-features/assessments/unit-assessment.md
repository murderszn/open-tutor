# Unit Assessment — Unit 04 (Week 18, Session 16)

**Adult note:** read tasks aloud if needed; learner may answer orally,
by pointing, by drawing, or in writing. Score with the rubric in
[answer-key.md](../answer-key.md). No answers appear on this page.

## Task A — Main topic and paragraph focus (RI.2.2, RI.2.1)

Read "Beavers: Nature's Builders"
([Lesson 01](../lessons/lesson-01-main-topic-and-paragraph-focus.md)).
(1) State the main topic in five words or fewer. (2) State the focus
of the "A Home Called a Lodge" paragraph. (3) Answer one why question
with proof: why must a beaver gnaw on wood?

## Task B — Text features (RI.2.5, RI.2.4)

Using the text, glossary, and index: (1) find what a **kit** is, naming
the feature you used; (2) find which heading covers beaver food,
naming the feature you used; (3) explain in one sentence when you
would use an index instead of a glossary.

## Task C — What pictures add (RI.2.7)

Look at the generated picture
([Lesson 03](../lessons/lesson-03-what-pictures-add.md)) or its
text-only alternative. (1) Name two facts the picture shows that the
Lesson 01 words do not state. (2) Name one fact the picture confirms
from the words. (3) Write or dictate a caption for the picture that
names one dam fact and one lodge fact.

## Task D — Purpose, reasons, steps (RI.2.6, RI.2.8, RI.2.3)

(1) State the purpose and the point of "Why Ponds Help Other Animals"
([Lesson 04](../lessons/lesson-04-authors-purpose-and-reasons.md)) and
give two of its reasons with their animals. (2) Retell the six steps
of "How a Beaver Builds a Dam"
([Lesson 05](../lessons/lesson-05-steps-in-order.md)) in order.

## Task E — Two texts, same topic (RI.2.9)

Compare "Beavers: Nature's Builders" with "Beaver Families"
([Lesson 06](../lessons/lesson-06-two-texts-same-topic.md)). Name two
points that are the same (with proof from both texts) and two points
that are different (naming which text says each).

## Task F — Midyear review (Units 01–03 revisit)

(1) Read one sentence from a Unit 01 vowel-team list aloud with
accuracy and expression. (2) Retell the Unit 03 story "Milo and the
Runaway Kite" in order (beginning, middle, end) and name its lesson.
This task is a checkpoint, not a new grade: note any skill that needs
re-teaching in the Week 18 review and record it in the teacher guide
notes.

## Scoring

- **Ready to move on:** Tasks A–E each at least 2 of 3 parts correct
  (Task D: purpose + point + 1 reason, and 5 of 6 steps, count as the
  pass line); Task F completed with notes.
- **Reteach:** any task below its pass line — re-teach the matching
  lesson the same week and re-check with the parallel practice set.
