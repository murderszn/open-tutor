# Unit Assessment — Unit 03: Story Structure, Characters, and Multiple Perspectives

**How to take it:** the adult reads the new passage aloud first; the
learner re-reads it (or follows along) before answering. Answers may be
oral, pointed, drawn, or written; the adult may scribe. **40 points.**
Answers and scoring notes are in the [answer key](../answer-key.md) —
adults only. **Ready:** 32–40 with no task at 0. Below 24, or any task
at 0, re-teach that objective from the lesson named in the key before
moving to Unit 04.

## New passage — "Lena and the Library Book" (original; unseen in the lessons)

> On Friday, Lena borrowed a book about the sea from the school
> library. The book was called *The Big Blue Sea*. Lena read it after
> lunch. She read about whales and waves. Then she put the book on her
> bed and went outside to play. On Monday morning, Lena could not find
> the book. She looked on her bed. It was not there. She looked under
> her bed. There it was! It had slipped off the bed in the night.
> "I will keep it in my backpack from now on!" Lena said. On Monday,
> Lena took the book back to the library. The librarian smiled. "Thank
> you for bringing it back," she said.

## Tasks

### A. Key details (8 points; RL.2.1)

1. Who borrows the book, and from where? (2 points)
2. What is the book called, and what is it about? (2 points)
3. When does Lena find the book, and where? (2 points)
4. Why will Lena keep the book in her backpack from now on? (2 points)

### B. Retell in order (6 points; RL.2.2)

Retell Lena's story in three parts — beginning, middle, end — one
sentence per part, in order. (2 points per part.)

### C. How Lena responds (6 points; RL.2.3)

Describe how Lena responds when she cannot find the book. Name what
she does (not just how she might feel — the passage does not say she
cries or quits, so do not invent that). Then name what she learns to
do differently. (3 points for the response, 3 for the changed habit.)

### D. Beginning and ending (6 points; RL.2.5)

1. Which sentence introduces the story? Copy it or point to it, and
   say what it introduces (who + where). (3 points)
2. Which sentence concludes the story? Copy it or point to it, and say
   how it settles the trouble. (3 points)

### E. Whose words? (6 points; RL.2.6)

1. Who says, "I will keep it in my backpack from now on!" — Lena or
   the narrator? How do you know? (3 points)
2. The story is told by a narrator, not by Lena. Give one clue that
   proves it. (3 points; accept: the narrator calls her "Lena" and
   says "she," instead of Lena saying "I" throughout.)

### F. Picture and point of view (4 points; RL.2.7, RL.2.6)

Look again at the Lesson 04 picture (or its text-only alternative).
Which child thinks the rain is the best — Ana or Ben? Name one picture
detail and one story reason that prove it. (2 points for the child +
detail, 2 for the reason.)

### G. Two tellings (4 points; RL.2.9)

Two friends retell Lena's story. Sam says: "Lena lost the book
forever." Priya says: "Lena found the book under her bed and returned
it to the library." Who is right? Give two reasons from the passage.
(2 points for the choice, 2 for the reasons.)
