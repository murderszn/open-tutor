# Formative Quiz — Week 14 (Unit 03)

**Purpose:** a short check before the unit assessment. The adult reads
questions aloud as needed; the learner may answer orally, by pointing
or drawing, or in writing. **20 points (2 per item).** Answers are in
the [answer key](../answer-key.md) — adults only. Any item missed is
re-taught the same day (see the teacher guide); a score below 16 means
the learner repeats Practice Sets B and D before the unit assessment.

Stories are the unit stories: "Milo and the Runaway Kite" (Lesson 01),
"The Two Builders" (Lesson 02), "A Rainy Day, Two Ways" and its picture
(Lesson 04), and the sunflower versions (Lesson 06).

1. Who takes the new red kite to the park, and who goes with him?
2. Where does Milo's kite get caught?
3. Why does Milo feel sad in the middle of the story?
4. In your own words, what is Milo's lesson?
5. In your own words, what is the lesson of "The Two Builders"?
6. In "A Rainy Day, Two Ways," who thinks the rain is the worst day —
   Ana or Ben? Why?
7. Look at the Lesson 04 picture (or its text-only alternative). Name
   one picture detail that proves Ben is happy.
8. Put these Milo events in order (write 1, 2, 3): ___ Milo tapes the
   tear and flies again. ___ The string slips and the kite catches in
   the oak tree. ___ Milo takes the kite to the park.
9. Which sentence introduces a story, and which concludes one?
   (a) "On Saturday morning, Milo took his new red kite to the park
   with his sister Ana." (b) "Then Milo flew the kite again, holding
   the string tight with both hands."
10. Name one way the two sunflower versions are the same, and one way
    they are different (name both sides for the different).
