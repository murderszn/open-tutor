# Resource Pack — Unit 03: Story Structure, Characters, and Multiple Perspectives

**Goal in one sentence:** help a grade-2 learner retell stories in
order, name their lessons, describe character responses, hear two
points of view, and compare two versions of one story — with free,
no-account routes for every core objective; videos supplement, never
replace, the lessons.

**Internal references (repository, adult-side unless noted):**

- [Unit README](README.md) — objectives, stories, pacing.
- [Track scope-and-sequence](../../scope-and-sequence.md) — Unit 03
  weekly goals (Weeks 11–14) and the RL.2 standards crosswalk.
- `resources/language_arts_literary_terms.md` (on the
  `curriculum/expansion-plan` branch; teacher-side only) — adult
  background on story terms; grade-2 learners are taught *structure*,
  *point of view*, and *version*, not the full literary-terms list.
- `resources/language_arts_great_books_and_stories.md` (same branch;
  adult-side) — why Aesop's fables are candidates for retell and
  lesson work; the unit uses the Library of Congress text directly,
  not this guide's summaries, as learner text.

## Web references (checked 2026-10-23 unless noted; free, no account)

1. **Common Core State Standards, ELA — Grade 2, Reading: Literature**
   ([thecorestandards.org/ELA-Literacy](https://www.thecorestandards.org/ELA-Literacy/)):
   official framework; codes/descriptions cross-checked during the
   track audit 2026-10-03 and re-checked against official wording in
   search results 2026-10-23. Adult reference for RL.2.1–2.7, RL.2.9–2.10.
   No state adoption or accreditation claimed.
2. **Library of Congress, Aesop Fables — "The Hare & the Tortoise"**
   ([read.gov/aesop/025.html](https://read.gov/aesop/025.html)): page
   opened and verified 2026-10-23; full public-domain fable text with
   the moral "The race is not always to the swift." Adult read-aloud
   option for Lesson 02; not reproduced in this unit. Free, no account.
3. **Education World — "Teaching Story Sequencing with Picture Books"**
   ([educationworld.com](https://www.educationworld.com/a_lesson/Teaching-Story-Sequencing-with-Picture-Books)):
   found in search results 2026-10-23; a grade-2 sequencing lesson
   (beginning introduces, middle holds the problem, end wraps up).
   Adult planning reference only; its suggested picture books are not
   required. Free to read.
4. **Story Arts — "Getting Started" retelling guide**
   ([storyarts.org](https://www.storyarts.org/classroom/retelling/getstarted.html)):
   found in search results 2026-10-23; defines retell and version and
   gives a beginning/middle/end retelling routine. Adult reference for
   Lessons 02 and 06. Free, no account.
5. **Internal guide — `resources/language_arts_great_books_and_stories.md`:**
   repository guide (expansion-plan branch) noting Aesop's fables as
   public-domain retell candidates; adult-side only, as in the track
   audit.
