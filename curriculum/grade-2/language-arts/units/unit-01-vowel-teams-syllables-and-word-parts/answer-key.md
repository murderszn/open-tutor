# Answer Key — Unit 01: Vowel Teams, Syllables, and Word Parts

Every practice item, quiz question, assessment task, and project rubric row
solved independently and reconciled with the lessons. The adult re-solves
any item in doubt before scoring.

## Lesson 01

**Guided:** 1. *kit* = /k/ /ĭ/ /t/ — short. 2. *kite* = /k/ /ī/ /t/ —
long; the quiet e. 3. *tub* short /ŭ/ (one tap); *tube* long /ū/ (two
taps). 4. *mate* is long; *met, pen* are short. 5. Wrong — the quiet e
makes it *hope* (/h/ /ō/ /p/); the ignored pattern is vowel-consonant-e.

**Independent:** 1. LONG tray: *cape, pine, hope*. SHORT tray: *cap, pin,
hop*. 2. Reads: cap /k/ /ă/ /p/, cape /k/ /ā/ /p/, etc. — each word's
vowel matches its tray. 3. *tube*. 4. *bed* and *bet* rhyme (both short
e); *beet* is the odd one (long /ē/). 5. Accept: "The quiet e at the end
tells the i to say its name — vowel-consonant-e."

**Exit:** 1. *cube* — long /ū/. 2. *cub* — short /ŭ/. 3. "It tells the
first vowel to say its name (and stays silent itself)." 4. *fine*.

## Lesson 02

**Guided:** 1. *rain* — team **ai**. 2. *wait*: spot **ai**, say /ā/,
blend → *wait*. 3. *day*: team **ay** at the end → *day*. "ay, not ai,
because it sits at the end." 4. *green*: spot **ee**, say /ē/ → *green*.
*sea*: **ea** → *sea*. 5. Wrong — the **ai** team says /ā/: *paint*
(/p/ /ā/ /n/ /t/). Skipped the team; read it as a short-a word.

**Independent:** 1. *rain* (ai), *day* (ay), *bee* (ee), *leaf* (ea) —
each tile-built and read. 2. ai: *train, snail*. ay: *say, clay*. ee:
*tree, see*. ea: *each, dream*. 3. *ee* and *ea*; e.g., *tree* and *sea*.
4. Sentences read with ≤2 errors: "The rain came down on the green leaf."
/ "The bee sat by the sea to read." 5. Accept: "*ai* sits in the middle
of words; at the end of a word the /ā/ team is spelled *ay*."

**Exit:** 1. *away* — **ay**, /ā/. 2. *clean* — **ea**, /ē/. 3. *ai*
says /ā/ and usually sits in the middle. 4. Leaf card — team **ea**,
sound /ē/.

## Lesson 03

**Guided:** 1. *light*: spot **igh** → /ī/ → *light*. Quiet letters: g, h.
2. *coat*: **oa** in the middle → *coat*. *show*: **ow** at the end, first
try /ō/ → *show*. 3. *brown*: first try /ō/ → "brone" (not a word); flip
→ /ou/ → *brown*. 4. *moon*: **oo** moon-sound /oo/. *book*: **oo**
book-sound /ŏŏ/. 5. Wrong — **ou** says /ou/: *house* (/h/ /ou/ /s/).
Skipped the ou team.

**Independent:** 1. igh: *night, bright*. oa/ow: *boat, snow*. oo: *moon,
foot*. ou: *cloud, house*. 2. *high* (igh, /ī/); *grow* (ow, /ō/);
*room* (oo, moon-sound); *out* (ou, /ou/). 3. "The bright light is on the
boat at night." / "The brown cow ran down to the house." 4. *down*:
try /ō/ → "done"? no — flip → *down* /ou/. *yellow*: /ō/. *soon*: /oo/.
*look*: /ŏŏ/. 5. Accept: "*igh* always says /ī/, but *ow* spells two
different sounds, so it needs try-and-flip."

**Exit:** 1. *sigh* — three-letter team **igh**. 2. *down* — flip to /ou/.
3. Moon-oo (/oo/) and book-oo (/ŏŏ/). 4. *yellow* — /ō/, the snow sound.

## Lesson 04

**Guided:** 1. *picnic*: pic-nic (2 beats) → *picnic*. 2. *basket*:
bas-ket → *basket*. 3. *tiger*: ti-ger; long vowel in the FIRST beat
(*ti*, /tī/). 4. *rab* + reveal *-bit* → *rabbit*. 5. Wrong — *lemon*
is *lem-on* with a short o. Fix: clap-and-break, then check — "lee-mon"
is not a word.

**Independent:** 1. *pock-et, nap-kin, win-dow, pen-cil, mu-sic, pa-per* —
each sorted beat by beat. 2. Beats read separately, then blended. First
beats: *pock* (ŏ), *nap* (ă), *win* (ĭ), *pen* (ĕ) — short; *mu* (ū,
long), *pa* (ā, long). 3. *robot* — /rō/ in the first beat. (*sunset*
and *rabbit* have short first beats.) 4. Passage read with clap-and-break
on stuck words: "Lemon is a small robot. The robot can run and hop. At
sunset, Lemon sits by the window and hums a happy song." 5. Accept: "Each
beat has only one vowel sound, so you only ever read a small word at a
time."

**Exit:** 1. *fol-low* — 2 beats. 2. *o-pen* — long o in the first beat.
3. Two. 4. Check — is it a real word?

## Lesson 05

**Guided:** 1. *unhappy*: strip *un*-, root *happy* → "not happy." 2.
*replay*: strip *re*-, root *play* → "play again." 3. *fearless*: strip
*-less*, root *fear* → "without fear." A fearless kitten is NOT scared.
4. *jumped* → past (-*ed*). *singing* → happening now (-*ing*). 5. Faster:
strip *pre*-, read *view* → *preview* = "view before."

**Independent:** 1. *untie* = un + tie = do the opposite of tie (undo it).
*rewrite* = re + write = write again. *thankful* = thank + ful = full of
thanks. *hopeless* = hope + less = without hope. *wanted* = want + ed =
past. *reading* = read + ing = happening now. 2. *unlock, unkind,
unhappy*; *helpful, careful* — all real, all read. 3. *re*- means "again":
*redo, replay* (any two real re- words accepted). 4. "The careful driver
went fast and crashed" does NOT fit — *careful* = full of care. Fix:
"The **careless** driver went fast and crashed." 5. Accept: "*pre*- means
'before'; *school* is school; *preschool* = before school — figured out
from the parts."

**Exit:** 1. "Not" / "the opposite." 2. *un* + *lock* = do the opposite
of lock — open it. 3. Suffix (back-rider). 4. Heat before (*pre*- =
before).

## Lesson 06

**Guided:** 1. *mailbox*: *mail* + *box* = a box for mail. 2. *sunshine*:
*sun* + *shine* = light/heat from the sun. 3. *head*: try /ē/ → "heed"
(not a word); flip → *head* /hĕd/. Team **ea** saying /ĕ/ — the
inconsistent one. 4. *said, have, of, was, were* — instant reads. 5.
Wrong — *cowboy* keeps the /oi/ sound in *boy*: /k/ /ou/ /b/ /oi/.
Ignored the compound's real pronunciation; the check ("is it a word I
know?") catches it.

**Independent:** 1. *lighthouse* = light + house → a tower with a light
that guides ships. *popcorn* = pop + corn → corn that pops. *bedroom* =
bed + room → a room with a bed. *football* = foot + ball → a ball game
played (mostly) with the feet. 2. FAIR TEAMS: *leaf* (ea /ē/), *moon*
(oo /oo/), *snow* (ow /ō/). TRICKY: *head* (ea /ĕ/), *book* (oo /ŏŏ/),
*cow* (ow /ou/), *said* (ai saying /ĕ/), *have* (quiet-e but short a).
3. *said, have, of, was, were, some, come, what* — starred when instant.
4. *badge* (borrows -age from *cage*), *boil* (borrows -oy from *boy*),
*train* (borrows -ain from *rain*). 5. "The cowboy found popcorn in the
mailbox." / "I said I would come, but the rain came down." 6. Accept:
"*ai* usually says /ā/, but *said* breaks the pattern — no sounding out
can fix it, so it must be memorized."

**Exit:** 1. *bird* + *house* = a house for birds. 2. *bread* — flip **ea**
to /ĕ/. 3. *of* — instant. 4. "A pattern you know — *cage* → *badge*."

## Practice sessions

**Session A:** LONG tray: *cape, pine, hope, tube, kite, cube*. SHORT
tray: *cap, pin, hop, tub, kit, cub*. Speed round: same 12, ≤1 error
target. Planted adult errors to catch: *pine* in SHORT (wrong — long /ī/),
*cub* in LONG (wrong — short /ŭ/).

**Session B:** Built and read: *rain, train, day, play, bee, tree, leaf,
sea* — team named before each blend. Team swaps: *rain → train*, *day →
say*, *bee → see*, *leaf → leap* (real word; **ea** still says /ē/ —
good catch if the learner notices). Sentences read with pointing.
Dictation: *paint* (**ai**), *away* (**ay**), *dream* (**ea**), *each*
(**ea**).

**Session C:** ai/ay: *snail, wait* (ai); *clay, say* (ay). ee/ea:
*clean* (ee); *each, dream, sea* (ea). igh: *bright, high*. oa/ow: *road*
(oa); *yellow* (ow, /ō/). oo: *food* (moon-sound); *look* (book-sound).
ou/ow: *house* (ou); *brown* (ow, /ou/). Two-sound check: *food* → /oo/,
*look* → /ŏŏ/; *yellow* → /ō/, *brown* → /ou/.

**Session D:** Beat race: *sun-set, pic-nic, bas-ket, rab-bit, lem-on,
pa-per, ro-bot, ti-ger, mu-sic, pen-cil*. Cover-and-reveal: *o-pen,
e-ven, ba-by, hap-py, win-dow, pock-et*. Passage read (see practice.md).
Retell: Lemon is a small robot; at sunset Lemon sits by the window and
hums a happy song; a rabbit invites Lemon to play; Lemon opens a basket
full of music.

**Session E:** 8 built words (any 8 real, e.g. *untie, retell, helpful,
hopeless, played, careful, unlock, singing*) — each read with "part +
root = meaning." Meaning match: "not kind" → *unkind*; "tell again" →
*retell*; "full of care" → *careful*; "without hope" → *hopeless*. Time
travel: *play* (now) → *played* (past) → *playing* (happening now);
*hop* → *hopped* → *hopping*. Sentence fit: "The fearless kitten hid
under the bed" contradicts — *fearless* = without fear, so it would not
hide. Rewrite: "The fearless kitten explored the dark hall." (Any
sensible rewrite accepted.)

## Formative quiz (Session 15)

1. *rain* (**ai**), *day* (**ay**), *bee* (**ee**), *leaf* (**ea**) — each
   read + team named.
2. *light* (**igh**), *boat* (**oa**), *moon* (**oo**), *cloud* (**ou**).
3. *sun-set* → *sunset*; *ro-bot* → *robot*.
4. *unhappy*: strip *un*-, root *happy* → "not happy." *replay*: strip
   *re*-, root *play* → "play again."
5. Point to the bee card and the leaf card.
6. *said, have, of* — instant.
7. *bird* + *house* = a house for birds.
8. *train* (**ai**), *see* (**ee**) — written/tiled + team named.
9. *brown*: try /ō/ → "brone" (not a word); flip → *brown* /ou/. Both
   tries said aloud.
10. Accept: "Spot the team, say its sound, blend the word — and flip if
    it is not a real word."

## Unit assessment (Session 16)

**Task 1 (5 pts):** *cape* long, *pin* short, *hope* long, *tub* short,
*kite* long — each read correctly with the right label.

**Task 2 (8 pts):** *paint* (ai), *away* (ay), *green* (ee), *each*
(ea), *night* (igh), *coat* (oa), *soon* (oo), *house* (ou) — each read
with its team named.

**Task 3 (4 pts):** ai → the rain-cloud card. oa → the boat card. Bee
card: any real **ee** word (*see, tree, green, clean*…). Leaf card: any
real **ea** word (*sea, read, dream, each*…).

**Task 4 (4 pts):** *pic-nic → picnic*; *pa-per → paper*; *win-dow →
window*; *nap-kin → napkin*.

**Task 5 (6 pts):** *unlock* = un + lock = open / do the opposite of
lock. *rewrite* = re + write = write again. *careful* = care + ful =
full of care. *hopeless* = hope + less = without hope. *jumped* = jump +
ed = past. *singing* = sing + ing = happening now.

**Task 6 (8 pts):** *said, have, of, was, were, some, come, what* —
instant reads (≤2 seconds each; sounding out = half credit).

**Task 7 (6 pts):** Column A (usual sound): *moon* (/oo/), *snow* (/ō/),
*leaf* (/ē/). Column B (the flip): *book* (/ŏŏ/), *cow* (/ou/), *head*
(/ĕ/).

**Task 8 (3 pts):** *lighthouse* = light + house → tower with a light
that guides ships. *popcorn* = pop + corn → corn that pops when heated.
*toothbrush* = tooth + brush → a brush for teeth.

**Task 9 (3 pts):** *badge*, *boil*, *train* — spelled correctly by
borrowing the named pattern.

**Task 10 (4 pts):** Two original sentences, each using one unit word
correctly in context. 2 pts per sentence: 1 for a real unit word, 1 for
correct use. (Nonsense use — "The rain happy" — loses the second point.)

## Project rubric (Session 14)

- Team accuracy: all 6 team headers spelled and sounded right = 3.
- Word choices: 4–6 real words per page, every word containing the page's
  team = 3 (one misfit = 2; several = 1).
- Case sentences: 6 original, each using 2 of the page's words correctly
  = 3.
- Two-sound cases: oo page shows moon/book flip AND ou/ow page shows
  snow/cow (or equivalent) flip = 3.
- Teach-back: names team + sound + all three steps (spot–say–blend) = 3.
