# Resource Pack — Unit 01: Vowel Teams, Syllables, and Word Parts

## Summary

**Goal:** give the guiding adult free, no-account routes to support
grade-2 vowel-team phonics (ai/ay, ee/ea, igh, oa/ow, oo, ou/ow),
two-syllable decoding, prefix/suffix word analysis, compound words, and
tricky-word practice — plus songs and games a 7-year-old will enjoy.
**Age fit:** grade 2 (about 7–8); all items are visual, musical, or
hands-on, with minimal reading required of the learner. Adult planning
judgment: videos supplement the lessons; they never replace explicit
teaching or the adult check.
