# Formative Quiz — Unit 01: Vowel Teams, Syllables, and Word Parts

**When:** Session 15 (review week). **How:** adult reads each item aloud;
the learner answers orally, by pointing, or in writing — any mode that
shows the skill. **Time:** 15–20 minutes.

Not a grade — a flashlight. Every miss becomes a same-session re-teach
(see [teacher-guide.md](../teacher-guide.md#session-15-quiz-and-reteach)).

1. Read these 4 words and name each vowel team: *rain, day, bee, leaf*.
2. Read these 4 words and name each vowel team: *light, boat, moon, cloud*.
3. Clap-and-break, then read: *sunset, robot*.
4. Strip the part, read the root, tell the meaning: *unhappy, replay*.
5. Look at the four friend cards in
   [`assets/vowel-team-friends.png`](../assets/vowel-team-friends.png).
   Point to the two friends whose names use the /ē/ teams. (bee — ee; leaf — ea.)
6. Read these tricky words instantly: *said, have, of*.
7. Break *birdhouse* into its two words and tell what it means.
8. **Dictation:** the adult says *train* and *see*; the learner writes or
   tiles them, then names the team in each.
9. **Try-and-flip:** read *brown*. Say both tries aloud before settling.
10. **Explain:** "How do you read a word you have never seen that has a
    vowel team?" (Spot the team, say its sound, blend the word — and flip
    if it is not a real word.)

**Scoring:** 8 of 10 items fully correct → on track; re-teach missed
objectives in the rest of Session 15. See
[answer-key.md](../answer-key.md#formative-quiz-session-15) for expected
responses.
