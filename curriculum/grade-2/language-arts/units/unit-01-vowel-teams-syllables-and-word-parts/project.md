# Project — Vowel Team Detective Casebook

**Type:** investigation / creation project across Sessions 12 and 14,
with the share in Session 14.
**Driving question:** "Can I build a casebook that proves I can crack any
vowel team I meet?"

The learner authors a 6-page detective casebook — one page per vowel team
(ai/ay, ee/ea, igh, oa/ow, oo, ou/ow) — with an anchor drawing, 4–6 real
team words, one "case sentence" using two team words, and a solved
two-sound case. The learner then teaches one page to the adult.

## Materials

6 sheets of paper or card (one per team), crayons/markers, the unit word
sets (see [unit README](README.md#unit-word-sets-authored-for-this-unit-reused-across-lessons-and-practice)),
the generated illustration
[`assets/vowel-team-friends.png`](assets/vowel-team-friends.png) as the
anchor-drawing model, a stapler or yarn (adult use).

## Part 1 — Plan and draft (Session 12)

1. Label one page per team: ai/ay, ee/ea, igh, oa/ow, oo, ou/ow. Copy the
   team letters big at the top (adult scribes if needed).
2. For each team, choose 4–6 real words from the unit word sets — at
   least one word the learner found tricky this unit. Draft-check each
   word: spot the team, say its sound, blend. Any non-word is rejected.
3. Draw one anchor picture per page showing one of its words (a rain
   cloud for ai, a bee for ee — the generated illustration is the model,
   not a tracing sheet).
4. Write one "case sentence" per page using two of the page's words:
   "The **rain** fell on the **train**." (Original sentences only.)
5. For the oo and ou/ow pages, add a solved two-sound case: "moon says
   /oo/, book says /ŏŏ/ — I tried and flipped!"

## Part 2 — Assemble, rehearse, share (Session 14)

1. Order the pages, staple or tie with yarn, and make a cover: title
   ("My Vowel Team Casebook"), author first name only (privacy), one
   drawing of a favorite team friend.
2. Rehearsal: the learner reads every page to a stuffed animal — pointing
   at each team, saying its sound, blending each word, reading the case
   sentence.
3. **Teach-back:** the learner teaches one team page to the adult:
   "The team is **oa**. It says /ō/. Watch — boat, road, coat. The *oa*
   sits in the middle!" The adult asks one question ("How did you read a
   word you'd never seen?"), which the learner answers with the
   spot-say-blend steps.

## Deliverables

1. The casebook: cover + 6 team pages, 4–6 real decodable words each, one
   anchor drawing per page, one original case sentence per page.
2. A solved two-sound case on the oo page and on the ou/ow page.
3. The teach-back: one page taught to the adult with the three steps named.

## Rubric

| Criterion | 3 — Case closed | 2 — Getting there | 1 — Needs another look |
|---|---|---|---|
| Team accuracy | All 6 teams spelled and sounded correctly | 4–5 teams correct | 3 or fewer teams correct |
| Word choices | 4–6 real words per page; every word actually contains the page's team | Most words fit; 1–2 misfits | Several words do not contain the team |
| Case sentences | 6 original sentences, each using 2 of the page's words correctly | 4–5 sentences, or 1 word misused | Fewer than 4 sentences, or words misused |
| Two-sound cases | Both oo and ou/ow pages show a correct tried-and-flipped pair | One page shows it | Neither page shows it |
| Teach-back | Names the team, its sound, and all three steps (spot–say–blend) | Names team and sound; steps partial | Cannot name the steps |

**Readiness signal:** 12+ points with no criterion at 1 → ready for the
unit assessment. A 1 in any row → re-teach that row's lesson content in
Session 15 before assessing.
