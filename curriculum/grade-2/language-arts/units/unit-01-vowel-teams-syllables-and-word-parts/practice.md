# Practice Sessions — Unit 01: Vowel Teams, Syllables, and Word Parts

Named sessions referenced by the [16-session pacing](README.md#16-session-pacing).
Each runs 25–30 minutes. Adult observes and notes which objective each
session served.

## Session A — Long/short vowel sort game

*Serves Objective 1. Pairs with Lesson 01.*

Word cards (adult-written, large print): *cap, cape, pin, pine, hop, hope,
tub, tube, kit, kite, cub, cube*.

1. Sort into LONG and SHORT trays, saying the vowel sound as each card
   lands. 12 cards.
2. Read the SHORT tray aloud, tapping once per word. Then the LONG tray,
   tapping twice.
3. **Speed round:** the adult holds up cards in random order; the learner
   reads and taps. 12 cards, aim for ≤1 error.
4. **Switch seats:** the learner holds the cards; the adult sorts and
   reads — the learner judges ("Check my work, teacher!"). The adult
   makes exactly 2 planted errors (*pine* in SHORT, *cub* in LONG) for the
   learner to catch.

## Session B — Team word building + decodable sentences

*Serves Objective 2. Pairs with Lesson 02.*

1. With tiles, build and read: *rain, train, day, play, bee, tree, leaf,
   sea*. Name each team aloud before blending. 8 words.
2. **Team swap:** change one letter to make a new team word — *rain →
   train* (add t), *day → say* (change d), *bee → see*, *leaf → leap*?
   ("Leap" is real — check the team still says /ē/! Yes.)
3. Read these original decodable sentences, pointing under each word:
   "The rain came down on the green leaf." / "The bee sat by the sea to
   read." / "We play all day in the clay."
4. **Dictation:** the adult dictates *paint, away, dream, each* — the
   learner writes or tiles them, then reads back and names the team.

## Session C — Vowel-team sort race

*Serves Objective 2. Pairs with Lesson 03.*

Six trays: ai/ay, ee/ea, igh, oa/ow, oo, ou/ow. Word cards: *snail, clay,
clean, each, bright, high, road, yellow, food, look, house, brown, wait,
say, dream, sea* (16 cards).

1. Sort all 16 cards into the six trays, reading each word aloud as it
   lands. Time it — record the time on the tray.
2. Re-sort, trying to beat the time with zero errors. Two rounds max;
   accuracy beats speed.
3. **Two-sound check:** pull every oo and ow card; the learner says both
   sounds for each team's two personalities (*moon/book, snow/cow*) and
   re-sorts those 6 cards into four sub-piles.
4. The learner picks the 4 trickiest cards; those become tomorrow's warm-up.

## Session D — Clap-and-break races + decodable passage

*Serves Objective 3. Pairs with Lesson 04.*

1. **Beat race:** 10 word cards — *sunset, picnic, basket, rabbit, lemon,
   paper, robot, tiger, music, pencil*. Clap the beats, break, read each
   beat, blend. Count how many needed zero help.
2. **Cover-and-reveal pairs:** the adult covers the second beat of *open,
   even, baby, happy, window, pocket*; the learner reads beat 1, then the
   reveal, then blends.
3. Read this original passage (clap-and-break any stuck word):
   > "Lemon is a small robot. The robot can run and hop. At sunset, Lemon
   > sits by the window and hums a happy song. A rabbit hops by the paper
   > basket. 'Come and play!' says the rabbit. Lemon opens the basket. It
   > is full of music!"
4. **Retell check:** "Who is Lemon? What does Lemon do at sunset?" (A
   small robot; sits by the window and hums.) Reread once for smoothness.

## Session E — Build-a-word prefix/suffix workshop

*Serves Objective 4. Pairs with Lesson 05.*

Root cards: *tie, tell, help, hope, play, care, lock, kind*. Part cards:
*un-, re-, pre-, -ful, -less, -ed, -ing*.

1. Build 8 real words by joining one part card to one root card; read
   each and state its meaning ("*un* + *tie* = *untie* — do the opposite
   of tie"). Reject non-words at the real-word check.
2. **Meaning match:** the adult reads 4 definitions ("not kind," "tell
   again," "full of care," "without hope"); the learner builds the word.
   (*unkind, retell, careful, hopeless*.)
3. **Time travel:** build *play → played → playing*; *hop → hopped →
   hopping*. Say what time each word tells.
4. **Sentence fit:** "The (fearless / fearful) kitten hid under the bed."
   (Neither fits perfectly — *fearless* = without fear, so it would NOT
   hide. Discuss: which word fits the hiding kitten? *fearful* is not in
   our set — good noticing. Rewrite: "The fearless kitten explored the
   dark hall.")
