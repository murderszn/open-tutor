# Unit 01 — Phonics: Vowel Teams, Syllables, and Word Parts

**Grade:** 2 · **Subject:** Language Arts · **Track weeks:** 3–6
**Backlog:** [issue #16](https://github.com/murderszn/open-tutor/issues/16), section U01
**Status:** validated draft (this unit); not educator-reviewed, not merged

Long vowels often travel in pairs. In this unit the learner re-secures the
long/short vowel distinction, learns the vowel teams that spell long-vowel
sounds (ai/ay, ee/ea, igh, oa/ow, oo, ou/ow), breaks two-syllable words into
readable parts, and uses prefixes, suffixes, and compound words to read new
words and figure out what they mean.

## Essential question

How can knowing the "teams" inside a word help me read it the first time I see it?

## Prerequisites

From the diagnostic weeks (track weeks 1–2) and the grade-1 track:

- Blend and segment sounds in spoken one-syllable words; tell long from short vowel sounds by ear
- Common consonant digraphs (sh, ch, th, wh); final-e long-vowel spellings
- Grade-appropriate high-frequency words (grade-1 set)

The diagnostic re-teaches nothing here; Lesson 01 re-teaches long/short vowel
reading rather than assuming it is secure.

## Measurable objectives

By the end of the unit the learner will be able to (adult-observed):

1. Distinguish long and short vowels when reading regularly spelled
   one-syllable words (e.g., *cap/cape*, *pin/pine*).
2. Read additional common vowel teams in one-syllable words: ai/ay, ee/ea,
   igh, oa/ow, oo, ou/ow — and name which team spells the vowel sound.
3. Decode regularly spelled two-syllable words with long vowels by breaking
   them into syllables (clap-and-break), reading each part, and blending.
4. Decode words with common prefixes (un-, re-, pre-) and suffixes (-ful,
   -less, -ed, -ing); use the "prefix + known word" strategy to predict a
   word's meaning.
5. Read grade-appropriate irregularly spelled words (*said, have, of, was,
   were, some, come, what*) and name words with inconsistent but common
   spelling-sound correspondences (oo as in *moon* vs. *book*; ow as in
   *snow* vs. *cow*; ea as in *leaf* vs. *head*).
6. Use compound-word parts to predict meaning (*birdhouse* = a house for
   birds); spell new words by generalizing learned patterns (*cage* →
   *badge*; *boy* → *boil*).

## Vocabulary (taught with sounds and words first, definitions second)

decodable · anchor word

## Unit word sets (authored for this unit; reused across lessons and practice)

**Long/short review:** cap/cape, pin/pine, hop/hope, tub/tube, kit/kite

**Vowel teams:**
- ai/ay: rain, train, paint, wait, snail, day, play, say, away, clay
- ee/ea: bee, see, tree, green, leaf, read, sea, each, dream, clean
- igh: light, night, bright, high, sigh
- oa/ow: boat, road, coat, snow, show, grow, yellow
- oo: moon, soon, room, food, book, look, foot
- ou/ow: cloud, house, out, cow, down, brown

**Two-syllable:** sunset, picnic, basket, rabbit, lemon, paper, robot, tiger,
music, pencil, open, even, baby, happy, rainbow, baseball, cowboy, window,
pocket, napkin, follow, silent

**Prefixes:** unhappy, unkind, untie, unlock · retell, redo, replay, rewrite ·
preview, preschool, preheat

**Suffixes:** helpful, careful, thankful · hopeless, fearless, careless ·
played, wanted, hopped, jumped · playing, running, singing, reading

**Irregular/tricky:** said, have, of, was, were, some, come, what, where,
again, laugh, would, could, should, friend, people, water, only, because

**Compounds:** birdhouse, lighthouse, sunshine, rainbow, baseball, cowboy,
backpack, popcorn, football, mailbox, toothbrush, bedroom

All word sets are original curriculum-authored lists; no worksheet or
published list is reproduced.

## 16-session pacing

Four sessions per week, 25–30 minutes each. K–2 tasks stay oral, pointing,
drawing, manipulative, or adult-scribed as needed; grade 2 begins short
independent written practice (6–8 tasks) that the adult reviews the same day.

| Session | Week | Activity | Success criterion |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | 3 | Lesson 01: Long or short? | Learner sorts 10 cap/cape-type words into long/short with ≤1 error |
| 2 | 3 | Practice A: long/short vowel sort game | Reads all 10 words aloud correctly after sorting |
| 3 | 3 | Lesson 02: ai/ay and ee/ea teams | Names the team in 6 of 8 new ai/ay/ee/ea words |
| 4 | 3 | Practice B: team word building + decodable sentences | Builds and reads 8 team words; reads 3 sentences with ≤2 errors |
| 5 | 4 | Lesson 03: igh, oa/ow, oo, ou/ow teams | Names the team in 6 of 8 new words; explains the oo/ow "two-sound" cases |
| 6 | 4 | Practice C: vowel-team sort race | Sorts 16 mixed team words correctly in under 5 minutes |
| 7 | 4 | Lesson 04: clap-and-break two-syllable words | Decodes 6 of 8 two-syllable words by breaking first |
| 8 | 4 | Practice D: clap-and-break races + decodable passage | Reads the "Lemon the Robot" passage with ≤4 errors |
| 9 | 5 | Lesson 05: prefixes and suffixes | Strips prefix/suffix and reads the root in 6 of 8 words |
| 10 | 5 | Practice E: build-a-word prefix/suffix workshop | Builds 8 correct new words; predicts meaning of 4 |
| 11 | 5 | Lesson 06: compound words and tricky spellings | Reads 5 of 6 compounds; reads 6 of 8 tricky words |
| 12 | 5 | Project Part 1: plan the Casebook | 6 team pages drafted with real words checked against the sets |
| 13 | 6 | Review: sort, race, and rebuild games | Learner leads one full vowel-team sort from memory |
| 14 | 6 | Project Part 2: assemble, rehearse, teach-back | Casebook complete; learner teaches one team page to the adult |
| 15 | 6 | Formative quiz (week-04 quiz) + reteach | Missed items re-taught the same session; learner can name the fix |
| 16 | 6 | Unit assessment | Adult scores against the answer key; reteach list recorded |

If a lesson runs long, move its application to the next practice session
rather than rushing the exit check — the exit check is your data.
