# Practice — Unit 05

**Adult note:** read items aloud as needed; the learner may answer
orally, by pointing, by drawing, or in writing. Review the same day.
Answers are in [answer-key.md](answer-key.md) only — never on this
page. Each set has 6 tasks.

## Set A — Group words (after Lesson 01)

1. Match each group to its group word: birds, cows, players, bees →
   flock, herd, team, swarm.
2. Fill in the group word: a ___ of bees flew over the flowers.
3. Fill in the group word: a ___ of cows stood in the field.
4. Write or say a sentence using the group word *class*.
5. **Error-analysis:** "A pack of birds flew by." Is that right? Fix
   it if it is wrong.
6. Draw or point to a picture of fish swimming together, and say the
   group word. (If no picture is handy, the adult says "fish swimming
   together" and the learner answers.)

## Set B — Plural and self words (after Lesson 02)

1. One foot → two ___. One tooth → two ___.
2. One woman → two ___. One person → two ___.
3. One deer → three ___. One sheep → three ___.
4. "I brushed my teeth ___." (Fill in the *-self* word.)
5. "The cat cleaned ___ after dinner." (Fill in the *-self* word.)
6. **Error-analysis:** "Two childs played on the slide." Is that
   right? Fix it if it is wrong.

## Set C — Describing words (after Lesson 03)

Use the generated picture ([Lesson 03](lessons/lesson-03-adjectives-and-adverbs.md))
or its text-only list.

1. "The (quick / quickly) dog runs across the grass." Which word fits?
2. "Five (small / smallly) birds fly in the sky." Which word fits?
3. "The turtle crawls (slow / slowly) on the stone path." Which
   word fits?
4. "The girl jumps (happy / happily)." Which word fits?
5. "The boy throws the ball (gentle / gently)." Which word fits?
6. Write or say two sentences about the picture: one with an adjective
   and one with an adverb. Point to or underline the describing word
   in each.

## Set D — Yesterday words and bigger sentences (after Lesson 04)

Use "What Sam Did Yesterday" ([Lesson 04](lessons/lesson-04-irregular-past-tense-and-expanding-sentences.md)).

1. Yesterday, he (sit) on the grass. Write the sentence with the right
   verb.
2. She (catch) the ball. Write the sentence with the right verb.
3. They (swim) in the pool. Write the sentence with the right verb.
4. Expand "Ben read." by adding where and when.
5. Join with *but*: "It was cold. We played outside." Remember the
   comma.
6. Rearrange: "Ana drew a bird in art class." Move the where part to
   the front.

## Set E — Spelling patterns, dictionary, formal and informal (Week 22 review)

1. **Spelling pattern — cage → badge:** the words *cage* and *badge*
   share the /j/ sound spelled two ways. Which of these fits the
   *badge* pattern (the /j/ sound after a short vowel, spelled -dge)?
   bridge, boy, cage. Say why.
2. **Spelling pattern — boy → boil:** the words *boy* and *boil*
   share the "oy/oi" sound spelled two ways. Which of these fits the
   *boil* pattern (the sound spelled -oi- inside the word)? coin,
   cage, boy. Say why.
3. **Dictionary:** with the adult, look up *badge* in a beginning
   dictionary (see [resource-pack.md](resource-pack.md), reference 6).
   What does the dictionary say *badge* means? Write the meaning in
   your own words. If no dictionary is at hand, the adult reads the
   entry aloud and the learner repeats the meaning.
4. Formal or informal? "Good morning, Mrs. Lee." Say why.
5. Formal or informal? "Hey, gimme that!" Say why.
6. Rewrite this the formal way: "Gimme some juice!" (Use full words
   and a polite ask.)
