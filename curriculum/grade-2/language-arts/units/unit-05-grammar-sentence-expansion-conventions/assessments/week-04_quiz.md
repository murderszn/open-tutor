# Formative Quiz — Unit 05 (Week 22, Session 15)

**Adult note:** read questions aloud if needed; learner may answer
orally, by pointing, by drawing, or in writing. 8 of 10 correct is the
ready mark; re-teach any miss the same day. Answers are in
[answer-key.md](../answer-key.md) only — never on this page.

Use the lessons, the generated picture ([Lesson 03](../lessons/lesson-03-adjectives-and-adverbs.md)),
"What Sam Did Yesterday" ([Lesson 04](../lessons/lesson-04-irregular-past-tense-and-expanding-sentences.md)),
and "A Letter from Grandma Rosa" ([Lesson 05](../lessons/lesson-05-capitals-and-commas-in-letters.md)).

1. What is the group word for a group of birds?
2. One child → two ___. (Write the plural.)
3. Finish the sentence: "We made the fort ___." (Use the word that
   points back to *we*.)
4. Yesterday, I (go) to the park. Write the sentence with the right
   verb.
5. In "The dog runs quickly," is *quickly* an adjective or an adverb?
   Say what it tells about.
6. Add a where to this sentence: "Ana read."
7. Fix the capitals: "We will visit chicago at thanksgiving."
8. Add the missing comma: "Dear Mr. Chen" (a letter greeting).
9. Make a contraction from *will* + *not*.
10. "The ___ (Mia) bike is red." Fill in the possessive.
