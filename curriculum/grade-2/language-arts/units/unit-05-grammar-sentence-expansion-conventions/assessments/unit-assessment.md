## Task A — Group words and special plurals (pass: 5 of 6)

1. What is the group word for a group of cows?
2. What is the group word for a group of players?
3. One foot → two ___.
4. One tooth → two ___.
5. One mouse → two ___.
6. One sheep → three ___.

## Task B — Self words and yesterday words (pass: 5 of 6)

1. "I tied my shoes ___." (Fill in the *-self* word.)
2. "They built the fort ___." (Fill in the *-self* word.)
3. Yesterday, Sam (sit) on the grass.
4. Last night, Ana (tell) a story.
5. Yesterday, we (swim) in the pool.
6. She (write) a note. (Use the yesterday word.)

## Task C — Describing words from the picture (pass: 4 of 6)

Use the generated picture or its text-only list in Lesson 03.

1. "The (quick / quickly) dog runs." Which word fits?
2. "The dog runs (quick / quickly)." Which word fits?
3. "The (slow / slowly) turtle crawls." Which word fits?
4. "Five (small / smallly) birds fly." Which word fits?
5. Write or say one sentence about the picture with an adjective.
6. Write or say one sentence about the picture with an adverb.

## Task D — Bigger sentences (pass: 3 of 4)

1. Expand "The cat slept." by adding where and when.
2. Join with *and*: "Mia read a book. Ben drew a picture." Remember
   the comma.
3. Join with *so*: "It rained. We stayed inside." Remember the comma.
4. Rearrange: "We ate lunch at noon in the kitchen." Move the when
   part to the front.

## Task E — Capitals, commas, apostrophes (pass: 5 of 6)

1. Fix the capitals: "My birthday is on halloween."
2. Fix the capitals: "We drove to texas for the summer."
3. Write the greeting for a letter to Ms. Patel, with the comma.
4. Write the closing for a letter to a friend, with the comma.
5. Make a contraction from *can* + *not*.
6. "The ___ (children) coats are on the hooks." Fill in the possessive.

## Task F — Spelling patterns, dictionary, formal/informal, and fluency reread (pass: 3 of 4, plus the reread)

1. **Spelling pattern — cage → badge:** which of these fits the
   *badge* pattern? bridge, boy, cage. Say why.
2. **Spelling pattern — boy → boil:** which of these fits the *boil*
   pattern? coin, cage, boy. Say why.
3. With the adult, look up *boil* in a beginning dictionary. What
   does the dictionary say *boil* means? Say it in your own words.
4. Formal or informal? "May I please borrow a pencil?" Say when to
   use it.
5. **Fluency reread (Unit 02 skill revisit):** read "The Park on
   Saturday" aloud with accuracy and expression. (Pass: reads it
   accurately on a second reading, self-correcting with context if
   needed. This item is observed, not scored out of 4 — record it
   separately in the teacher guide.)

**"The Park on Saturday" (original; for the fluency reread):** On
Saturday, Sam and Ana went to the park. A quick brown dog ran fast
across the grass. A slow green turtle crawled along the stone path.
Five small birds flew high in the blue sky. Ana jumped high, and Sam
threw a yellow ball. The flock of birds sang, and the children laughed.
It was a happy day at the park.
