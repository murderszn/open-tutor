# Resource Pack — Unit 05: Grammar, Sentence Expansion, and Conventions

**Goal in one sentence:** help a grade-2 learner use collective nouns,
irregular plurals and past-tense verbs, reflexive pronouns, adjectives
and adverbs, expanded and compound sentences, and the capitals, commas,
and apostrophes of a friendly letter — with free, no-account routes for
every core objective; videos supplement, never replace, the lessons.

**Internal references (repository, adult-side unless noted):**

- [Unit README](README.md) — objectives, texts, pacing.
- [Track scope-and-sequence](../../scope-and-sequence.md) — Unit 05
  weekly goals (Weeks 19–22) and the L.2 standards crosswalk.
- `resources/language_arts_parts_of_speech.md` (teacher-side only) —
  adult background on collective nouns, reflexive pronouns, adjectives
  and adverbs, and possessives; never assigned to the learner.
- `resources/language_arts_sentence_structure.md` (teacher-side only)
  — adult background on subjects, predicates, and complete vs.
  compound sentences; never assigned to the learner.

texts, picture, and text alternative; no account, purchase, or video is
required. Outside links are adult-previewed before any learner use, and
no child account is ever required. Videos, where used, supplement the
explicit teaching in the lessons; they do not replace it. The dictionary
check uses the free Wordsmyth children's dictionary; any print
