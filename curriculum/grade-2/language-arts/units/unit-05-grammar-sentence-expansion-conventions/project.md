# Project — A Letter to Someone Special

**Deliverable:** a finished friendly letter to a real person the
learner knows (a grandparent, a teacher, a friend, or a family member —
the adult helps choose), written on paper, with a drawing. The letter
stays in private storage; no address, name, or letter is ever put in
this public repository.

## Materials

Paper, a pencil, crayons or colored pencils. A beginning dictionary
for the spelling check in Step 3. Nothing to buy. The model letter in
[Lesson 05](lessons/lesson-05-capitals-and-commas-in-letters.md) and
the word cards from Lessons 01–04 may be kept on the table.

## Steps

1. **Plan (Project work 1).** Pick the person and the news: what
   happened, and why write about it? Write or dictate the plan:
   - Who the letter is for, and why.
   - The greeting ("Dear ___ ,") and the closing ("Love," or "Your
     friend,").
   - Six sentence jobs to include: (a) one collective noun, (b) one
     irregular plural, (c) one reflexive pronoun or irregular past
     verb, (d) one adjective and one adverb, (e) one contraction,
     (f) one possessive — plus a capital for a holiday, a product, or
     a place.
2. **Draft (Project work 2).** Write or dictate the letter: a greeting
   with a comma, at least four expanded sentences (each with a where,
   when, or how detail), and a closing with a comma. Add a drawing
   that shows one thing the letter talks about.
3. **Revise and share (Project work 3).** Check the letter against
   the checklist below. Fix capitals, commas, and apostrophes. Look
   up one spelling in the beginning dictionary and fix it if needed.
   Read the letter aloud to the adult (or record it, if the adult
   prefers). The adult asks one who/what/where question and one
   why/how question about the letter, and the learner answers in
   complete sentences.

## Checklist (learner checks each box with the adult)

- [ ] Greeting has a comma: "Dear ___ ,"
- [ ] Closing has a comma: "Love," or "Your friend,"
- [ ] At least four expanded sentences (where, when, or how added)
- [ ] One collective noun used correctly
- [ ] One irregular plural used correctly
- [ ] One reflexive pronoun or irregular past verb used correctly
- [ ] One adjective and one adverb used correctly
- [ ] One contraction used correctly
- [ ] One possessive used correctly
- [ ] Capitals for a holiday, a product, or a place (at least one)
- [ ] One spelling checked in a beginning dictionary
- [ ] Drawing matches something the letter talks about

## Rubric

| Criterion | 3 — Strong | 2 — Growing | 1 — Starting |
|---|---|---|---|
| Letter shape | Greeting and closing both present, both with commas; reads like a real letter | Greeting and closing present; one comma missing | Greeting or closing missing |
| Expanded sentences | 4+ sentences with where/when/how details; at least one compound sentence joined with and/but/so and a comma | 2–3 expanded sentences; joining attempted | Sentences are all short and unexpanded, or fragments |
| Grammar words | Collective noun, irregular plural, and reflexive pronoun or irregular past verb all used correctly | Two of the three used correctly | One or none used correctly |
| Describing words | Adjective and adverb both used correctly, each telling about the right word | One used correctly; the other mixed up | Adjective/adverb confused throughout |
| Conventions | Contraction and possessive both correct; holiday/product/place capitalized; its/it's right if used | One convention error | Two or more convention errors |
| Sharing | Reads the letter aloud clearly; answers both questions in complete sentences | Reads with support; answers one question in a complete sentence | Cannot read the letter or answer with a complete sentence |

**Ready:** mostly 3s, no 1s. **Reteach:** any 1 — revisit the matching
lesson (letter shape: Lesson 05; sentences: Lesson 04; grammar words:
Lessons 01–02; describing words: Lesson 03; conventions: Lessons
05–06) and revise that part before the unit is marked complete.
