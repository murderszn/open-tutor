# Resource Pack — Unit 02: Fluency, Vocabulary, and Comprehension Monitoring

## Summary

**Goal:** give the guiding adult free, no-account routes to support
grade-2 oral reading fluency (accuracy, rate, expression on successive
readings), self-monitoring/self-correction, sentence-level context clues,
adjectives/adverbs and shades of meaning, and retelling key ideas from
read-alouds — plus songs and games a 7-year-old will enjoy.
**Age fit:** grade 2 (about 7–8); all items are visual, musical, or
hands-on, with minimal reading required of the learner. Adult planning
judgment: videos supplement the lessons; they never replace explicit
teaching or the adult check.
