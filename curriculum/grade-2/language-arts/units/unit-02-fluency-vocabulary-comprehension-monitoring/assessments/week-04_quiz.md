# Week-10 Formative Quiz — Unit 02

**Session:** 15 · **Time:** 15–20 minutes · **Format:** adult-led, mostly
oral; the learner may point, draw, or dictate. This is a check, not a
test — missed items are re-taught the same session.
**Answers:** [answer-key](../answer-key.md#week-10-formative-quiz).

## Part 1 — Fluency observation (adult scores while the learner reads)

The learner reads the first paragraph of Passage A aloud ("It was a windy
day… tall grass.").

1. **Accuracy:** count the errors. (0–1 = strong; 2–3 = growing; 4+ = reteach.)
2. **Rate:** was the speed conversational — not racing, not crawling?
3. **Expression:** did the voice lift on "up, up, up" and pause at "Then — snap!"?

## Part 2 — Monitoring and vocabulary

4. The adult reads: "Sam pulled the kite out of the **glass**." What do
   you do? (Stop, check the letters — g-r-a-s-s says *grass* — and the
   sense — kites fall in grass; reread correctly.)
5. "The wind is a strong, **invisible** force." What does *invisible* mean,
   and what are your clue words? (Cannot be seen; clue: you cannot see wind,
   only what it moves — the sentence calls it a force you feel, not see.)
6. Sort into adjectives and adverbs: *red, quickly, tall, gently*.
   (Adjectives: *red, tall*. Adverbs: *quickly, gently*.)
7. Which is the strongest shade: *happy, joyful, glad*? (*joyful*.)
8. **Retell:** the adult reads Passage B aloud once. The learner tells back
   the big idea and two key details in order.
