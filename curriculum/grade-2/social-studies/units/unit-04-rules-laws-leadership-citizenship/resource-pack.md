# Resource Pack — Unit 04: Rules, Laws, Leadership, and Citizenship

**Goal (one sentence):** Give the guiding adult short, grade-2-appropriate
videos, references, and search routes that reinforce why rules exist,
rule versus law, people in authority, government services, fairness,
points of view, and fair voting.
**Age fit:** grade 2 (ages 7–8); the adult previews every video and
co-views with the learner.

**Fact-check record (2026-10-30):** C3 Framework K–2 indicator wordings
used in this unit (D2.Civ.1, 2, 3, 5, 6, 7, 8, 9, 10, 11, 12, 14; D1.2;
D4.1, D4.8) were verified against the framework's K–2 indicator table
opened 2026-10-30 (the same table used for the track audit 2026-10-03).
D2.Civ.4 and D2.Civ.13 begin in grades 3–5 and are not taught here. The
Maple Lane teacher, principal, mayor, council, park, garden, and library
stories are fictional practice material invented for this unit, not
claims about any real town, law, or officeholder. Community-law examples
are stated generally (stop at a red light; do not take what belongs to
someone else); exact laws differ by place. No video below is claimed as
a verified single episode: all video entries are clearly labeled
preferred-channel search links, and the adult picks and previews one
short episode.
