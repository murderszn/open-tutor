# Week-04 Quiz — Unit 04: Rules, Laws, Leadership, and Citizenship

**Session 14 of the unit. The adult reads every item aloud; the learner
answers orally, by pointing, or by drawing. The adult reviews the quiz
with the learner the same day and re-teaches any miss immediately.
Answers are in the [answer key](../answer-key.md) — never beside a
student question.**

1. Name one rule at home and tell what it is for.
2. Rule or law? "Raise your hand in class."
3. Rule or law? "Stop at a red light."
4. Who leads a town or city?
5. Name one service the government provides for everyone.
6. Fair or not fair? "Only children wearing blue get the new books first."
7. Sam wants to play tag. Priya wants to play hide-and-seek. Name one
   fair way to decide.
8. What does "one person, one vote" mean?
9. Four children vote: 3 choose story time, 1 chooses block time.
   Which choice wins?
10. Name one job a child does in a community.

**Bar:** 7 of 10. Below 7, re-teach with the [teacher guide](../teacher-guide.md)
targeted follow-ups before the unit assessment.
