# Unit Assessment — Unit 04: Rules, Laws, Leadership, and Citizenship

**Session 16 of the unit. The adult reads every item aloud; the learner
answers orally, by pointing, or by drawing, unless an item says
otherwise. Answers and scoring guidance are in the
[answer key](../answer-key.md) — never beside a student question.**

## Part 1 — Rules and laws (4 points)

1. Sort these four into RULE or LAW: "raise your hand in class," "use
   quiet voices in the library," "stop at a red light," "do not take
   what belongs to someone else."
2. In your own words, what is a law?
3. Name one rule for a public place and tell what it is for.
4. What is different about what happens when a law is broken, compared
   with when a classroom rule is broken?

## Part 2 — Leaders and services (4 points)

5. Match each person to their job: teacher, principal, mayor,
   firefighter — with: leads the town, teaches the class, puts out
   fires and rescues people, leads the school.
6. In your own words, what is a government?
7. Name two services a government provides for everyone.
8. Name one community helper who is not a leader, and tell why that job
   matters.

## Part 3 — Fairness and decisions (5 points)

9. Use the fairness test on this rule: "Everyone waits in line for the
   slide." Ask the three questions and give a verdict: fair or not fair?
10. Sam wants to play tag. Priya wants to play hide-and-seek. Tell
    Sam's point of view and Priya's point of view.
11. Five children vote: 4 choose block time, 1 chooses story time.
    Which choice wins? Was the vote fair? Why?
12. Your choice loses a vote, 3–2. Was the vote unfair? Explain.
13. Give a one-reason speech for a classroom choice: "We should choose
    ___ because ___." Then name two discussion rules you followed while
    giving it.

**Scoring:** 13 points (1 per item). Sort and match items (1 and 5)
score the point when at least 3 of 4 parts are correct. **Bar:** 10 of
13, with item 2 (what a law is) and item 11 (vote winner and fairness)
correct. Below the bar, re-teach with the
[teacher guide](../teacher-guide.md) targeted follow-ups before Unit 05.
