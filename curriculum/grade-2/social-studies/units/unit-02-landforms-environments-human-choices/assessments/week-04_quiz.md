# Formative Quiz — Unit 02, Week 4 (Track Week 10)

**When:** Session 15. **How:** the adult reads each item aloud; the
learner answers orally, by pointing, or by drawing. The adult reviews
every miss the same day and re-teaches before Session 16. **Passing:**
7 of 10. Scoring guidance is in [answer-key.md](../answer-key.md) —
never show the key to the learner.

**Materials:** the printed [landform diagrams](../assets/landform-diagrams.svg),
the printed [Riverside Valley illustration](../assets/riverside-valley-illustration.png)
(or its text-only description in [Lesson 03](../lessons/lesson-03-people-change-places.md)),
paper and crayons.

1. Point to each panel of the landform diagrams and name all six
   landforms. *(1 point for 5–6 correct; 0 for fewer.)*
2. "Water is flowing past our town, moving over rocks." Is this a river
   or a lake? How do you know?
3. "It is sunny today, so we will eat lunch outside." Weather or
   climate? What is your clue?
4. "It snows every winter here, so our house has a heater." Weather or
   climate? What is your clue?
5. Look at the Riverside Valley picture. Point to **two** things people
   built or made, and name each one.
6. Name one job and the landform where that work fits best. Tell me why
   they fit.
7. "Children play in a small park between tall buildings, and food
   arrives on trucks." Is this the countryside or the city? How do you
   know?
8. Draw a **coast** and label it. Then tell me what a coast is.
9. Name one good made near our community and one good made far away.
   *(The adult accepts true local answers — see the answer key for
   examples.)*
10. Our unit question: "How does where you live change how you live?"
    Give one example from anything we learned.

**After the quiz:** the adult marks the score, celebrates what is solid,
and re-teaches each miss on the spot (one sentence of re-teaching per
item, then the learner answers again). Items 2–4 and 6–7 may be answered
orally; items 1, 5, and 8 use pointing or drawing.
