# Culminating Unit Assessment — Unit 02

**When:** Session 16. **How:** the adult reads each part aloud; the
learner answers orally, by pointing, or by drawing. This is a check of
learning, not a race — allow the full session. **Scoring:** 14 points
total; 11+ shows readiness for Unit 03, 8–10 calls for one targeted
review session, below 8 calls for re-teaching the weakest part. Full
scoring guidance is in [answer-key.md](../answer-key.md).

**Materials:** the printed [landform diagrams](../assets/landform-diagrams.svg),
the printed [Riverside Valley illustration](../assets/riverside-valley-illustration.png)
(or its text-only description in [Lesson 03](../lessons/lesson-03-people-change-places.md)),
paper and crayons.

## Part 1 — Landforms (4 points)

1. Name all six landforms while pointing to each panel of the diagrams.
   *(2 points: 6 correct = 2; 4–5 correct = 1; fewer = 0.)*
2. Draw one landform from memory and label it. Under the drawing,
   dictate one sentence that proves your drawing is correct. *(2 points:
   recognizable shape + correct label = 1; true proving sentence = 1.)*

## Part 2 — Weather and climate (3 points)

3. The adult reads: (a) "The wind is strong this afternoon." (b) "Summers
   here are hot every year." For each, say **weather** or **climate** and
   name the clue word. *(2 points: 1 per item, needing both the label and
   a time clue.)*
4. "How does a rainy climate change the houses people build?" *(1 point:
   any true adaptation — slanted roofs, umbrellas by the door, raised
   floors; accept reasonable answers.)*

## Part 3 — People and places (4 points)

5. Look at the Riverside Valley picture. Name **two** ways people changed
   this place. *(2 points: 1 per change — farm fields, road, houses,
   bridge.)*
6. "A fisher works at the lake." Why does the lake fit this job?
   *(1 point: fish live in water.)*
7. Name one thing people in our community make or grow, and one thing
   that comes from another community. *(1 point: one true local example
   and one true far-away example; the adult judges against local facts.)*

## Part 4 — Two communities (3 points)

8. "Which community grows its food nearby — Riverside Valley or Harbor
   City? How does the other community get food?" *(2 points: valley grows
   nearby = 1; city brings food by truck/boat from far away = 1.)*
9. "Name one way the city and the countryside are different, and one
   daily need they both meet." *(1 point: any true difference + food,
   water, or shelter.)*

## Project score (reported, not re-tested)

The [project](../project.md) rubric score (0–8) is reported alongside this
assessment. A project score of 4–5 with an assessment score of 8–10 still
calls for one review session before Unit 03.

**After the assessment:** the adult scores with the answer key, shares
one strength and one next step with the learner, and records the score
privately (never in this repository). The unit's [teacher guide](../teacher-guide.md)
describes what "ready for Unit 03" looks like.
