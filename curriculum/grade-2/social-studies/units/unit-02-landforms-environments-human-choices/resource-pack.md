# Resource Pack — Unit 02: Landforms, Environments, and Human Choices

**Goal (one sentence):** Give the guiding adult short, grade-2-appropriate
videos, references, and search routes that reinforce the six landforms,
weather versus climate, how people change places, how places shape work
and choices, and the city-versus-countryside comparison.
**Age fit:** grade 2 (ages 7–8); the adult previews every video and
co-views with the learner.

was opened and verified today — title, channel/publisher, and

## Web references

1. **"What's the Difference Between Weather and Climate?" — NOAA
   NESDIS** —
   https://www.nesdis.noaa.gov/about/k-12-education/understanding-our-planet/whats-the-difference-between-weather-and-climate
   *What it covers:* a K-12 explainer with a "Short Answer" box —
   weather as short-term events versus climate as long-term averages;
   meteorologists vs. climatologists. Best as teacher background for
   Lesson 02. *Verified 2026-10-17; free.*
2. **"What is the difference between weather and climate?" — NOAA Ocean
   Service** — https://oceanservice.noaa.gov/facts/weather_climate.html
   *What it covers:* a one-page fact sheet with the classic line
   "Climate is what you expect, weather is what you get," plus
   kid-readable examples (snow in the Northeast in January; a hot July
   day). The adult can read the two examples aloud in Lesson 02.
   *Verified 2026-10-17; free.*
3. **"landform" — Britannica Kids** —
   https://kids.britannica.com/kids/article/landform/603632
   *What it covers:* a student-level landform definition with linked
   entries for mountain, valley, and coast. *Caveat:* rendered at the
   "Students" reading level — a step above grade 2 — so use it as
   teacher background or guided reading, not independent reading.
   *Verified 2026-10-17; free.*
4. **"Weather" — National Geographic Education (encyclopedic entry)** —
   https://education.nationalgeographic.org/resource/weather-ee
   *What it covers:* weather basics (temperature, storminess, rainfall),
   seasons, and how weather differs by place (deserts, mountains).
   Educator-facing text — the adult excerpts or reads aloud for Lesson
   02. *Verified 2026-10-17; free.*
5. **NASA Science kids Earth hub** — https://climatekids.nasa.gov/weather-climate/
   *What it covers:* NASA's kids Earth page, which embeds the
   "What's the Difference Between Weather and Climate?" video plus
   kid-friendly reads on the water cycle and related topics. A safe
   co-viewing page for Lesson 02 follow-up. *Verified 2026-10-17; free.*

## Access notes

Every video above is free on YouTube with no account; every web
reference is free with no login. Videos supplement explicit teaching —
they never replace the lesson, the diagrams, the read-aloud, or the
adult check. The adult previews each video for ads, comments, and age
fit before co-viewing, and keeps a printable/text-only path (the
diagrams, the illustration's text description, the read-aloud passage)
for every core objective so no objective depends on a screen.
