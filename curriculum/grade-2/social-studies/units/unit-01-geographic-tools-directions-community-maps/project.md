# Project — The Welcome Visitor Map Packet

**A map-building project.** Sessions 11–12 of the unit pacing (draw the map,
then dictate the directions), showcased in session 15. Adult-supervised
throughout; the adult reviews the packet the same day.

## Driving question

*How can a map help someone who has never been to our classroom (or our
home) find their way?*

## Materials

Large paper, crayons or colored pencils, a ruler, the printed
[Maple Lane map](assets/maple-lane-map.png) as a model (or its text
description in [Lesson 03](lessons/lesson-03-reading-a-community-map.md)),
vocabulary word cards. No real addresses, learner photos, or personal
details go on the map.

## Steps

1. **Choose your place.** With the adult, pick one familiar indoor place to
   map: our classroom *or* one room at home (for example, the bedroom or
   living room). Stay inside one room — small places make good first maps.
2. **Plan the parts.** Before drawing, tell the adult your plan: what will
   your **title** say? Which **3 or more symbols** will you use (door,
   window, desk, bed, table — your choice)? What will your **labels** name?
   The adult approves the plan.
3. **Draw the map.** Draw the room from above (like a bird looking down).
   Include all five map parts:
   - a **title** that tells what the map shows;
   - **symbols** (at least 3) for real things in the room;
   - a **key** box that shows each symbol and what it means;
   - **labels** naming at least 3 places or things;
   - a **compass rose** with N, S, E, W in the right positions.
4. **Dictate the directions.** Pretend a newcomer is standing at the room's
   door. Dictate **3 or more steps** that lead them to something in the
   room (for example, to your seat or your bed), using **cardinal direction
   words** in order: "Start at the door. Walk **south** to the bookshelf.
   Turn **east**…" The adult scribes the dictation word-for-word under the
   map.
5. **Check with the five-part checklist.** Together, check off: title,
   symbols, key, labels, compass rose. Fix anything missing before the
   showcase.

## Deliverables

1. The labeled map with all five parts (title, 3+ symbols, key, 3+ labels,
   compass rose).
2. The dictated newcomer directions (3+ ordered steps, 2+ cardinal
   direction words), scribed by the adult.
3. An oral showcase (session 15): the learner presents the packet, points to
   each map part, and reads or recites the directions.

## Rubric

| Criterion | 2 — shows skill | 1 — developing | 0 — not yet observed |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Map parts** | Title, 3+ symbols, key, 3+ labels, compass rose — all present and correct | Four of the five parts present, or one part incorrect (e.g., key missing a symbol) | Fewer than four parts; adult draws most of the map |
| **Key and symbols** | Every symbol appears in the key with the right meaning; symbols are simple pictures, not words | Key present but one symbol missing or mismatched | No usable key; symbols unlabeled |
| **Compass rose and directions** | Rose has N/S/E/W in correct positions; dictated route uses 2+ cardinal words in order and actually leads to the target | Rose present with one error, or directions use 1 cardinal word or are out of order | No rose, or directions have no direction words |
| **Showcase talk** | Points to each part by name and gives the full route aloud | Names most parts; route incomplete | Adult presents; learner observes |

**Readiness to move on:** 6+ of 8 rubric points with the map drawn by the
learner. Below that, re-teach with the [teacher guide](teacher-guide.md)
before Unit 02 — the map-parts checklist and compass-rose routine return in
later units.
