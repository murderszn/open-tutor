# Formative Quiz — Unit 01 (review week)

**Given in:** session 13 (the unit's review week). **Time:** 15–20 minutes.
The learner may answer orally, by pointing, or by drawing; the adult
scribes dictated words and reviews the same day. Solutions in the
[answer key](../answer-key.md).

1. Point to the **title** on the Maple Lane map. What does a title tell us?
2. What is a **symbol** on a map? Name one pictured place on the Maple Lane
   map (for example, the schoolhouse picture) and tell what real thing it
   stands for.
3. What does the map **key** do? What would happen if a map had no key?
4. Name the four **cardinal directions**.
5. Stand up and face north. Point east. *(Adult checks against the room's
   fixed north.)*
6. Use the Maple Lane map. *(Text-only alternative: the map's top is north.
   Maple Street runs east–west across the map; Oak Avenue runs north–south
   through the middle. School: northwest block (north of Maple Street, west
   of Oak Avenue). Fire Station: northeast block. Library: southwest block.
   Pond: southwest, just south of the library. Park: southeast block. Grocery
   Store: southeast block, east of the park. Every place is labeled
   directly; there is no key box.)*
   a. Which place is **north** of Maple Street and **west** of Oak Avenue?
   b. Is the Grocery Store east or west of Oak Avenue?
7. Give directions from the School to the Library. Use direction words and
   say the steps in order.
8. Three tools: a map, a globe, a photograph. Which one shows **Earth's
   round shape** best? Tell why.
9. Which tool — map, globe, or photograph — would best help a visitor walk
   from the school to the library? Tell why.
10. How many continents does Earth have? Name three. How many oceans does
    Earth have? Name two.
