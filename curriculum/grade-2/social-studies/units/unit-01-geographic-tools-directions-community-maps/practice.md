# Practice Sets — Unit 01

Three sets matched to the pacing in the [unit README](README.md). **Adult
directions for every set:** read each item aloud; the learner may answer
orally, by pointing, by drawing, or with manipulatives — never require long
written answers. The adult scribes dictated words on request. Solutions are
only in the [answer key](answer-key.md) — no answers appear below. Every
image-based item is answerable from the text description in
[Lesson 03](lessons/lesson-03-reading-a-community-map.md).

## Week 3 — Map parts and directions (Practice set A)

**For this set** the adult uses a hand-drawn sample map *with* a key
(title, 3 symbols with a key box, 2 labels, compass rose) — Lesson 01's
teaching map. (The Maple Lane map labels places directly and has no key
box; it is used in set B.)

1. Point to each part on the sample map and name it: title, key, one
   symbol, one label. Say what each part does.
2. Sort: the adult names five things — "title," "a tree symbol," "the word
   *library*," "the key box," "the compass rose." The learner points to each
   on the sample map as it is named.
3. The adult covers the key with a hand. "What does the star symbol mean?"
   The learner guesses; then the adult uncovers the key and they check
   together. Was the guess right? What did the key actually say?
4. Draw your own symbol for "playground" and add it to a small key box with
   its label.
5. Stand up, face north, and play direction Simon-says: "Point east… point
   south… take one step west… point north." The adult calls five directions.

## Week 4 — Reading community maps (Practice set B)

Use the Maple Lane map (picture or the text description in Lesson 03).
Map facts: School = northwest block; Fire Station = northeast block;
Library = southwest block; Pond = southwest, south of the library; Park =
southeast block; Grocery Store = southeast block, east of the park. Maple
Street runs east–west; Oak Avenue runs north–south.

1. Read the labels, then scan: find the Library. Tell which block it is
   in, using north/south of Maple Street and east/west of Oak Avenue.
2. A visitor at the School wants to buy food. Which place should they
   visit? Point to it on the map.
3. Give the visitor directions from the School to that place, using
   direction words and street names, in order.
4. True or false — point and explain: "The Pond is east of the Library."
5. Draw (or dictate) a route with your finger from the Fire Station to the
   School. Name each step with a direction word.

## Week 5 — Continents and oceans (Practice set C)

Use the globe.

1. Chant the seven continents while pointing to each one on the globe.
2. Chant the five oceans while pointing to each one on the globe.
3. Point to the continent we live on. Name it, then name the ocean on its
   west side and the ocean on its east side.
4. Find Australia. Tell which two oceans touch it.
5. Think of two places you know (for example, our town and a relative's
   town). Say one way their *nature* (weather, land, water) is different
   and one way their *people-parts* (languages, foods, holidays) are
   different.
