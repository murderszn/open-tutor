# Practice Sets — Unit 05: Work, Resources, Trade, and Economic Choices

For the guiding adult. Every task can be answered orally, by pointing, by
arranging cards, or by drawing; the adult scribes dictated writing. Work
is reviewed the same day. Solutions are in the [answer key](answer-key.md).
The Maple Lane market, its prices, and its play coins are fictional
practice material. The banana fact is the unit's one checked real-world
claim (USDA, 2026-11-05).

## Week 19 — Wants, needs, and choices

1. **Want or need?** The adult names: "a drink of water," "a toy car,"
   "a warm coat in winter," "a bunch of flowers," "food for dinner."
   The learner sorts each into WANT or NEED and gives one reason.
2. **Scarcity at the stall.** "The market has 2 apples and 3 children
   want one. What is the problem, and what can the children do?" The
   learner names scarcity (not enough for everyone) and one fair fix
   (share, take turns, choose something else).
3. **Benefit and cost.** The learner picks from the price list with 10
   coins: "Buy the toy car (5 coins). What is your benefit? What is
   your cost?" Then: "Buy the book (4) and the apple (2) instead. What
   is the benefit now, and what is the cost?"
4. **Can you buy it?** With 10 coins, can the learner buy the toy car
   (5), the book (4), and the flowers (2)? The learner adds (5 + 4 + 2
   = 11) and explains why not (11 is more than 10).
5. **Spend it all.** The learner finds one way to spend exactly 10
   coins from the price list (apple 2 + bread 3 + flowers 2 + haircut 3
   = 10) and names what was given up (the book and the toy car).

## Week 20 — Work, prices, and money

1. **Good or service?** The adult names: "a loaf of bread," "a haircut,"
   "a picture book," "a bus ride," "a bunch of flowers." The learner
   says "good" or "service" for each.
2. **Producer and consumer.** "The baker makes bread. Who is the
   producer? Who is the consumer when you buy the bread? Who earns
   income?" The learner names the baker as producer, the buyer as
   consumer, and the baker as the one who earns income.
3. **Skill and tool.** The learner picks one worker (farmer, teacher,
   haircutter, bus driver) and names one skill and one tool for that
   job.
4. **Image questions (with text alternative).** Using the
   [market illustration](assets/maple-lane-market.png) or its text
   alternative in Lesson 04: (a) Name two goods for sale at the stall.
   (b) What does the SAVE jar hold? (c) What is the baker holding out to
   the child?
5. **The baker's coins.** "Flour costs 1 coin. Oven and work time cost
   1 coin. The loaf sells for 3 coins. How many coins did it cost to
   make the loaf, and how many coins are left over?" The learner works
   1 + 1 = 2 coins cost, 3 − 2 = 1 coin left over.

## Week 21 — Saving, trade, and government services

1. **Maya's weeks.** "Maya earns 3 coins in week 1 and 3 coins in week
   2. How many coins does she have? The book costs 4 coins. She buys
   it. How many coins does she save?" The learner works 3 + 3 = 6 and
   6 − 4 = 2.
2. **Spend or save?** "You have 6 coins. You want a book (4 coins) and
   flowers (2 coins). If you buy both, how many coins do you save? If
   you buy only the book, how many do you save?" The learner works
   6 − 6 = 0 saved, and 6 − 4 = 2 saved.
3. **From far away or from near here?** The adult names: "banana,"
   "bread baked in Maple Lane," "flowers grown in Maple Lane," "apple
   from a farm near our town." The learner sorts each into FAR AWAY or
   NEAR HERE, and says how the banana travels (truck and ship).
4. **What does a bank do?** The learner explains in their own words:
   a bank keeps money safe and keeps a record of how much each person
   saved. Then: "Why do people save?" (To buy something later, or for
   something important later.)
5. **Government services.** The adult names: "a road," "a school," "a
   library," "a park," "a loaf of bread for sale." The learner says
   which four are government services everyone can use, and which one
   is a good sold at a market (the bread).
