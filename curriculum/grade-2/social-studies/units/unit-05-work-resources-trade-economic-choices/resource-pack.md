# Resource Pack — Unit 05: Work, Resources, Trade, and Economic Choices

**Goal (one sentence):** Give the guiding adult short, grade-2-appropriate
videos, references, and search routes that reinforce wants and needs,
scarcity, benefits and costs, work and income, prices and money, saving,
banks, trade, and government services.
**Age fit:** grade 2 (ages 7–8); the adult previews every video and
co-views with the learner.

**Fact-check record (2026-11-05):** C3 Framework K–2 indicator wordings
used in this unit (D2.Eco.1–7, 9–10, 12–15; D2.Geo.7, 11; D1.3, D1.5;
D4.2) were verified against the framework's K–2 indicator table in the
Los Angeles County Office of Education planning guide (opened and read
2026-11-05). The Maple Lane market, its prices, its play coins, Maya,
and the baker are fictional practice material invented for this unit,
not claims about any real place. The unit's one real-world claim — most
bananas sold in the United States grow in warm countries far away, with
Guatemala, Ecuador, and Costa Rica the largest suppliers — was checked
against USDA Specialty Crops Market News banana shipment data
(2026-11-05; the report series was discontinued in April 2025 and its
data is historical) and corroborated by 2024 U.S. import figures. No
video below is claimed as a verified single episode: all video entries
are clearly labeled preferred-channel search links, and the adult picks
and previews one short episode.

## Web references

1. **Consumer Financial Protection Bureau — "School-age children and
   saving" (Money as You Grow)** —
   https://www.consumerfinance.gov/consumer-tools/money-as-you-grow/school-age-children-preteens/explore-saving/
   *Why good:* free, no-account U.S. government guidance for ages 6–12 on
   saving: save a dime of every dollar, set a savings goal, visit a
   federally insured bank, and the "Learn to Save with Money Monsters"
   activity. Adult background and activity ideas for Lesson 05; the
   unit's Maya story mirrors its goal-setting approach. *Verified live
   2026-11-05; free, no account; adult previews.*
2. **Council for Economic Education (EconEdLink) — K–5 Elementary
   collection** — https://econedlink.org/resources/collection/k-5-elementary/
   *Why good:* the Council's elementary collection, including K–2 lessons
   "Toys for Me: A Lesson on Choice" (scarcity as "this OR that," benefits
   and costs), "The Little Red Hen is a Producer and a Consumer," and "We
   are Consumers and Producers." Adult background and re-teaching ideas
   for Lessons 01–03; the collection is aimed at the right band.
   *Verified via search 2026-11-05; free, no account; adult previews.*
3. **Council for Economic Education (EconEdLink) — "Toys for Me: A Lesson
   on Choice"** — https://econedlink.org/resources/toys-for-me/
   *Why good:* a K–2 lesson in which learners categorize and prioritize
   wants to learn about scarcity and choice, with the "this OR that, not
   this AND that" framing this unit's Lesson 02 uses. Adult re-teaching
   for Lessons 01–02. *Verified via search 2026-11-05; free, no account.*
4. **U.S. Mint — Coin Classroom (kids)** —
   https://www.usmint.gov/learn/kids
   *Why good:* the U.S. Mint's free grade-browsable activities, lesson
   plans, and coin facts for children — the adult picks one short
   coin-counting or money activity to support Lesson 04's coin work.
   *Verified live 2026-11-05; free, no account; adult previews.*
5. **U.S. Department of Agriculture — Fruit and Vegetable Weekly U.S.
   Commodity Shipment Reports: Bananas** —
   https://mymarketnews.ams.usda.gov/viewReport/3249
   *Why good:* the source of the unit's one real-world claim. The linked
   report shows banana shipments by origin ("BANANAS-IMPORT": Guatemala,
   Ecuador, Costa Rica, and others) — the adult's evidence that most
   bananas sold in the United States grow in warm countries far away.
   Note: the report series was discontinued in April 2025; the data is
   historical, and the ranking is corroborated by 2024 U.S. import
   figures. Adult reference only; the learner meets the fact as "checked
   by grown-ups," never the report itself. *Verified live 2026-11-05;
   free, no account.*
6. **C3 Framework — K–2 indicators guide** —
   https://5il.co/p0qk
   *Why good:* the K–2 indicator table whose wordings this unit's
   standards notes follow (economics indicators D2.Eco.1–15, geography
   D2.Geo.7 and D2.Geo.11, D1.3, D1.5, D4.2 in the K–2 band). Adult
   reference only; not assigned to the learner. No state adoption or
