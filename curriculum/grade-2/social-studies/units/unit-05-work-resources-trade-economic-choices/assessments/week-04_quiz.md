# Formative Quiz — Unit 05, Week 22 (Unit Week 04)

**For the guiding adult.** Ten items, answered orally, by pointing, by
arranging cards, or by drawing; the adult scribes dictated writing. Review
the same day; re-teach every miss before the unit assessment. Solutions are
in the [answer key](../answer-key.md). The Maple Lane market, its prices,
and its play coins are fictional practice material. This quiz is a classroom
check, not a standardized or validated assessment.

1. **Want or need?** The adult names: "a drink of water" and "a toy car."
   The learner labels each want or need and gives one reason.
2. **Scarcity.** The adult asks: "What does scarcity mean?" (Accept: there
   is not enough of something for everyone to have all they want, so we
   have to choose.)
3. **Benefit and cost.** "You have 10 coins. You buy the picture book (4
   coins) and the toy car (5 coins). How many coins are left? Name one
   thing you gave up." (4 + 5 = 9 spent; 1 coin left; gave up e.g. the
   apple, bread, flowers, or haircut.)
4. **Good or service?** The adult names: "a loaf of bread" and "a
   haircut." The learner labels each good or service.
5. **Producer and income.** "Who makes the bread, and how do they earn
   income?" (The baker makes it; she earns income because she is paid
   for her work.)
6. **Skill and tool.** "Name one skill the farmer needs and one tool the
   farmer uses." (Skill: knowing when apples are ripe; tool: a ladder or
   baskets — accept any sensible pair.)
7. **Prices.** "An apple costs 2 coins and a loaf of bread costs 3 coins.
   You buy one of each. How many coins do you spend, and how many are
   left from 10?" (2 + 3 = 5 spent; 10 − 5 = 5 left.)
8. **The baker's coins.** "Flour costs 1 coin. Oven and work time cost 1
   coin. The loaf sells for 3 coins. How many coins did it cost to make,
   and how many are left over?" (1 + 1 = 2 coins cost; 3 − 2 = 1 coin
   left over.)
9. **Saving.** "Maya earns 3 coins a week. After two weeks, how many
   coins does she have? She buys a book for 4 coins. How many does she
   save?" (3 + 3 = 6 coins; 6 − 4 = 2 coins saved.)
10. **Image question (with text alternative).** Using the
    [market illustration](../assets/maple-lane-market.png) or the text
    alternative in Lesson 04: "Name two goods for sale at the stall.
    What does the SAVE jar hold?" (Any two of: apples, bread, flowers,
    picture books. The SAVE jar holds gold coins.)
