# Culminating Unit Assessment — Unit 05: Work, Resources, Trade, and Economic Choices

**For the guiding adult.** Thirteen parts, answered orally, by pointing,
by arranging cards, or by drawing; the adult scribes dictated writing.
Score with the [answer key](../answer-key.md); readiness per the
[teacher guide](../teacher-guide.md). The Maple Lane market, its prices,
and its play coins are fictional practice material. This assessment is a
classroom check, not a standardized or validated assessment.

## Part 1 — Want or need? (3 points)

The adult names three items; the learner sorts each into WANT or NEED and
gives one reason (1 point each): (a) a drink of water; (b) a toy car;
(c) a warm coat in winter.

## Part 2 — A 10-coin choice (4 points)

The learner has 10 play coins and the fixed price list (apple 2 · bread
3 · flowers 2 · book 4 · toy car 5 · haircut 3).

- (a) The learner buys the picture book (4) and the toy car (5). How many
  coins are spent, and how many are left? (2 points: 1 for 9 spent,
  1 for 1 left.)
- (b) The learner names one benefit and one cost of that choice.
  (2 points: 1 for a benefit — a book to read and a car to play with;
  1 for a cost — something given up, e.g., the apple, bread, flowers,
  or haircut.)

## Part 3 — Work and prices (3 points)

- (a) Is a haircut a good or a service? (1 point: service.)
- (b) The baker's loaf costs 2 coins to make and sells for 3 coins. How
  many coins are left over? (1 point: 1 coin.)
- (c) Name one skill and one tool the baker needs. (1 point: any sensible
  pair, e.g., measuring flour and the oven.)

## Part 4 — Saving and trade (3 points)

- (a) Maya earns 3 coins a week. After two weeks she has 6 coins. She
  buys a book for 4 coins. How many coins does she save? (1 point: 2
  coins.)
- (b) Name one good that comes from far away and one good Maple Lane
  makes. (1 point: banana from far away; bread, flowers, or apple from
  near here.)
- (c) Name one good or service the town government provides for everyone.
  (1 point: road, school, library, or park — accept fire truck /
  firefighters.)

**Total: 13 points.** Readiness bar: 10+ of 13, with Part 2(a) correct
and Part 4(a) correct. See the [teacher guide](../teacher-guide.md)
for re-teaching moves.
