# Practice Sets — Unit 03: Local History, Timelines, and Evidence

For the guiding adult. Every task can be answered orally, by pointing, by
arranging cards, or by drawing; the adult scribes dictated writing. Work
is reviewed the same day. Solutions are in the [answer key](answer-key.md).
The Maple Lane examples are fictional practice material.

## Week 11 — Ordering and timelines

1. **Past, present, future sort.** The adult names: "yesterday," "right
   now," "tomorrow," "last year," "next week." The learner sorts each
   into past, present, or future (cards or three piles).
2. **Order a day.** The adult names, mixed up: "went home, woke up, ate
   lunch at school." The learner puts them in order with first, next,
   last.
3. **First and last on the strip.** Using the Maple Lane practice strip
   (schoolhouse opens → library opens → swings added → today), the
   learner points to the first event and the last event, then names the
   event between the library and today.
4. **Fix the strip.** The adult lays out the four Maple Lane cards out of
   order (library, today, schoolhouse, swings). The learner reorders them
   and reads the strip start to end.
5. **Before or after?** The adult asks: "Did the swings come before or
   after the library opened? Did the schoolhouse open before or after the
   swings were added?" The learner answers both, pointing to the strip.

## Week 12 — Then and now and sources

1. **Past or today?** The adult names: "horse cart," "school bus," "gas
   lamp," "electric streetlight." The learner says "past" or "today" for
   each (or sorts picture cards into PAST and TODAY piles).
2. **Image questions (with text alternative).** Using the
   [then-and-now illustration](assets/maple-lane-then-and-now.png) or its
   text alternative in Lesson 03: (a) What pulls the cart in the PAST
   panel? (b) What is big in the TODAY panel that is small in the PAST
   panel? (c) Name one thing that is the same in both panels.
3. **Change or sameness?** The adult names pairs: "dirt road / paved
   road," "a school on the corner in both times," "basket / backpack."
   The learner says "change" or "sameness" for each.
4. **Name the kind.** The adult describes: (a) an old photograph of a
   schoolhouse; (b) a metal lunch pail; (c) a person answering questions
   about the past; (d) a letter beginning "From Ana, Maple Lane, June
   12." The learner names the kind of each source.
5. **Maker, date, place.** From Ana's letter heading, the learner names
   the maker, the date, and the place. Then: "Fact or opinion: 'Ana's
   letter is dated June 12'? Fact or opinion: 'Ana's school was
   wonderful'?"

## Week 13 — Questions, reasons, and evidence

1. **Ask two questions.** About Maple Lane's bigger library, the learner
   asks two questions (who, when, why, or what). The adult notes which
   questions the practice set can answer and which need another source.
2. **Two accounts.** The adult re-reads Account 1 (people wanted more
   books and room) and Account 2 (a storm broke the roof). The learner
   retells each account in one sentence.
3. **Match evidence to reason.** The learner sorts three evidence cards —
   crowded-room photograph, neighbors' letter, "no storm record in our
   set" — under "supports reason 1," "supports reason 2," or "supports
   neither."
4. **Pick the likelier reason.** The learner picks the likelier reason for
   the bigger library and completes (orally or dictated): "I think ___ is
   likelier because ___."
5. **Fact or opinion, again.** The adult names: "The reading room was
   crowded" (shown in the photograph); "The new library is the best
   library in the world." The learner labels each fact or opinion and
   says how they can tell.
