# Formative Quiz — Unit 03, Week 14 (Unit Week 04)

**For the guiding adult.** Ten items, answered orally, by pointing, by
arranging cards, or by drawing; the adult scribes dictated writing. Review
the same day; re-teach every miss before the unit assessment. Solutions are
in the [answer key](../answer-key.md). The Maple Lane examples are
fictional practice material. This quiz is a classroom check, not a
standardized or validated assessment.

1. **Order these events.** The adult names, mixed up: "started grade 2,
   was a baby, learned to walk." The learner puts them in order
   (first, next, last).
2. **Past, present, or future?** The adult names: "yesterday," "right
   now," "tomorrow." The learner labels each past, present, or future.
3. **First and last.** Using the Maple Lane strip (schoolhouse opens →
   library opens → swings added → today), the learner names the first
   event and the last event.
4. **Then or now?** The adult names: "horse cart on a dirt road" and
   "yellow school bus on a paved road." The learner says which is past
   and which is today.
5. **Which is a historical source?** The adult offers: "(a) an old
   photograph of a schoolhouse, (b) a brand-new cartoon about a talking
   bus." The learner picks the historical source and says why (it gives
   information about the past).
6. **Maker, date, place.** The adult reads Ana's letter heading again:
   "From Ana, Maple Lane, June 12." The learner names the maker, the
   date, and the place.
7. **Fact or opinion?** The adult names: "The schoolhouse had a bell
   tower" and "The little school was the best school ever." The learner
   labels each fact or opinion.
8. **Why do accounts differ?** The adult asks: "Two people tell different
   stories about the same event. Give one reason their accounts might
   differ." (Accept: they saw different parts, remember differently, or
   care about different things.)
9. **Likelier reason.** The adult asks: "Why was Maple Lane's bigger
   library built — because people wanted more books and room, or because
   a storm broke the roof? Which reason has evidence in our practice
   set?" The learner picks one and names one piece of evidence.
10. **Image question (with text alternative).** Using the
    [then-and-now illustration](../assets/maple-lane-then-and-now.png) or
    the text alternative in Lesson 03: "In the PAST panel, what carries
    boxes and hay along the dirt road? In the TODAY panel, what is big
    that was small in the PAST panel?" (Answerable fully from the text
    alternative: a horse-drawn cart; the oak tree — the school building
    is also acceptable.)
