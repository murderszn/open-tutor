# Culminating Unit Assessment — Unit 03: Local History, Timelines, and Evidence

**For the guiding adult.** Thirteen parts, answered orally, by pointing,
by arranging cards, or by drawing; the adult scribes dictated writing.
Score with the [answer key](../answer-key.md); readiness per the
[teacher guide](../teacher-guide.md). The Maple Lane examples are
fictional practice material. This assessment is a classroom check, not a
standardized or validated assessment.

## Part 1 — Timeline order (4 points)

The adult lays out four Maple Lane cards out of order: library opens,
today (new school building), schoolhouse opens, swings added. The learner
arranges them in order and reads the strip start to end. One point per
card in its correct position (schoolhouse = 1, library = 2, swings = 3,
today = 4).

## Part 2 — Then and now (4 points)

The learner completes a then-and-now table (orally or drawn), one point
per row:

| | Past | Today |
|---|---|---|
| Travel | ? | ? |
| School building | ? | ? |
| Street light | ? | ? |
| Carrying things | ? | ? |

Accept any accurate pair per row (travel: horse cart / school bus;
school: small schoolhouse / bigger modern school; light: gas lamp /
electric light; carrying: basket / backpack).

## Part 3 — Kinds of sources (3 points)

The adult describes three items; the learner names the kind of each
(1 point each): (a) an old photograph of a schoolhouse; (b) a metal lunch
pail; (c) a person answering questions about what they remember.

## Part 4 — The likelier reason (2 points)

The learner answers: "Why was Maple Lane's bigger library built?" One
point for picking the likelier reason (people wanted more books and
room); one point for naming one supporting piece of evidence (the
crowded-room photograph, or the neighbors' letter asking for more room
and books). Naming the storm reason with no evidence scores 0 for this
part; the adult re-teaches Lesson 05 the same day.

**Total: 13 points.** Readiness bar: 10+ of 13, with Part 1 in correct
order and Part 4's reason correct. See the [teacher guide](../teacher-guide.md)
for re-teaching moves.
