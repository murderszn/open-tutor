## Web references (verified 2026-10-21)

1. **National Archives — 19th Amendment milestone documents**
   https://www.archives.gov/milestone-documents/19th-amendment?utm_source=chatgpt.com
   Amendment passed Congress May–June 1919; Tennessee the 36th state
   to ratify, August 18, 1920; certified August 26, 1920. Notes that
   ratification did not ensure full enfranchisement. Supports
   Objective 5; Lesson 5; Investigation B.

2. **National Archives Foundation — "Amending America: 19th Amendment"**
   https://archivesfoundation.org/amendingamerica/19th-amendment/
   Timeline: introduced 1878, approved by Congress 1919, ratified
   August 18, 1920; NWSA 1869; Minor v. Happersett context. Supports
   Objective 5; Lesson 5.

3. **National Archives at Seattle — Chinese Exclusion Act records**
   https://www.archives.gov/seattle/finding-aids/chinese-exclusion-act
   The 1882 Act: barred Chinese laborers for 10 years; extended 1892
   (Geary Act) and 1902; repealed by the Magnuson Act (1943).
   Enforcement history (Customs → Bureau of Immigration → INS).
   Supports Objective 3; Lessons 1 and 3.

4. **U.S. Census Bureau — "March 2016: The Triangle Shirtwaist Fire"**
   https://www.census.gov/about/history/stories/monthly/2016/march-2016.html
   March 25, 1911; 146 garment workers killed; locked exits, no
   sprinklers, ladders reaching only the sixth floor; 600 employees
   working 12-hour days. Supports Objective 4; Lesson 4.

5. **NY Department of Labor — "Triangle Shirtwaist Factory: Born From Fire"**
   https://dol.ny.gov/triangle-shirtwaist-factory-born-fire
   Locked doors, refused sprinklers, ~30-minute disaster; the fire's
   role in forging New York labor law; Frances Perkins's role.
   Supports Objective 4; Lesson 4; Investigation B.

6. **Gale Blog — "The History of Angel Island Immigration Station"**
   https://blog.gale.com/the-history-of-angel-island-immigration-station/
   Ellis Island 12M+ (1892–1924 peak window); Angel Island 300,000+
   (1910–1940), ~175,000 Asian immigrants; detention and
   interrogation under the Exclusion laws. Supports Objectives 1
   and 3; Lessons 1 and 3; Investigation A.

7. **FamilySearch — "Angel Island: The Ellis Island of the West"**
   https://www.familysearch.org/en/blog/angel-island-the-ellis-island-of-the-west
   Station completed January 1910; main Pacific port; 12M+ through
   Ellis Island (1892–1954) for scale contrast. Supports Objective
   1; Lesson 1; Investigation A.

8. **Encyclopedia.com — "Jungle, The"**
   https://www.encyclopedia.com/history/dictionaries-thesauruses-pictures-and-press-releases/jungle
   Sinclair's 1906 novel, Packingtown research, the "heart/stomach"
   aim-result gap; prompted the Meat Inspection Act and Pure Food
   and Drug Act (1906). Supports Objective 5; Lesson 5.

9. **EBSCO Research Starters — "Magnuson Act Repeals the Chinese Exclusion Act"**
   https://www.ebsco.com/research-starters/law/magnuson-act-repeals-chinese-exclusion-act
   Repeal December 17, 1943; 105-person annual quota; Chinese
   population decline 1880–1940 (107,475 → 77,504). Supports
   Objective 3; Lesson 3 (extension).

10. **Fiveable — Labor Movements and Workers' Rights study guide**
    https://library.fiveable.me/united-states-history-since-1865/unit-3/labor-movements-workers-rights/study-guide/upsI9LxJhxWbq2xy
    Great Railroad Strike 1877, Haymarket 1886, Homestead 1892,
    Pullman 1894: triggers, violence, and outcomes (federal troops,
    injunction, Debs jailed). Study-guide level; cross-check dates
    against the lesson. Supports Objective 4; Lesson 4.

11. **Library of Congress — general search portal**
    https://www.loc.gov/ (search: "Chinese Exclusion Act" /
    "Ellis Island" / "tenement")
    Public-domain photographs, maps, cartoons, and documents; use
    the search with the adult for Investigation A case files and
    Lesson 3 cartoon analysis. Supports Objectives 1–3;
    Investigations A/B.

12. **Thinkport / Library of Congress — "Reconstruction and an
    Expanding America" inquiry kit**
    https://thinkport.org/tps/ushist/inquiry-kits.html?unit=06&topic=03
    "Government and Public Responses to Immigration" kit: LOC
    sources on the Chinese Exclusion Act, anti-Chinese cartoons, and
    late-1800s immigration policy. Supports Objective 3; Lesson 3.
