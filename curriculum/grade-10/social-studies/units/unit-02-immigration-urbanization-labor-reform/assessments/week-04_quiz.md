# Unit 02 — Formative Quiz (end of Week 8)

**Purpose:** check Lessons 1–3 before labor and reform begin. The adult
scores with `answer-key.md` and re-teaches misses in Week 9. Not a
grade for the record — a diagnostic.

**Instructions to the learner:** Answer in complete sentences. Use
specific facts (names, dates, laws). 25 minutes.

1. (2 pts) Define *push factor* and *pull factor* in one sentence
   each.

2. (3 pts) Name **two** differences between "old" and "new"
   immigration (origins, timing, or settlement).

3. (3 pts) Compare Ellis Island and Angel Island: state the scale
   (rough numbers), the operating years, and the key legal
   difference behind the inspection process.

4. (4 pts) The urban share of the U.S. population was 25.7% in 1870
   and 51.2% in 1920. (a) Compute the change in percentage points.
   (b) State the Census definition of "urban" used here. (c) In one
   sentence, explain what the 51.2% figure does *not* prove.

5. (4 pts) Name the **three mechanisms** of the Chinese Exclusion
   Act (1882) and, for each, state which population it affected.

6. (4 pts) **Source question.** A reformer's 1895 report describes a
   tenement as "a moral disaster," while an immigrant's 1905 memoir
   recalls the same building as "the first place we could afford."
   For *each* account, name one likely strength and one likely
   blind spot.

**Total: 20 points.** Readiness rule: 14+ and no zeros on Q4/Q5 →
proceed to Week 9. Otherwise, re-teach data reading and the
Exclusion Act's mechanisms before Lesson 4.
