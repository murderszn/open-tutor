# Unit 03 — Formative Quiz (after Lesson 3, end of Week 12)

10 questions. Solutions are in `answer-key.md` (adult only). This quiz
is formative: the adult records misses and re-teaches before Week 13.

1. The transcontinental railroad was completed in 1869. Name two
   mechanisms by which railroads changed the West's land politics
   (not just "they helped settlers").
2. The Dawes Act (1887) divided tribal land into individual
   allotments and sold the "surplus." What happened to total tribal
   landholdings between 1887 and 1934 — about 138 million acres to
   about what?
3. Wounded Knee (December 29, 1890): the Army buried 146 Lakota in a
   mass grave, but historians estimate total Lakota deaths at about
   what range?
4. The USS *Maine* exploded in Havana harbor on February 15, 1898.
   Was the cause proven to be a Spanish attack in 1898? Answer yes or
   no, with one reason.
5. Under the Treaty of Paris (1898), Spain relinquished Cuba and
   ceded three territories to the United States. Name the three, and
   state what the U.S. paid for one of them.
6. One anti-imperialist argument was "consent of the governed." Name
   one expansionist argument from Lesson 2 and one piece of evidence
   that weakens it.
7. Classify each by its form of U.S. control in 1905: (a) the
   Philippines, (b) Cuba under the Platt Amendment, (c) the Panama
   Canal Zone. Choose from: colony, protectorate, canal zone,
   territory.
8. The Roosevelt Corollary (1904) claimed a U.S. right to act as an
   "international police power" in which region?
9. The Open Door Notes (1899, 1900) asked the powers to keep China
   open to all trade and to preserve China's territorial integrity.
   Were the notes a treaty signed by China? Answer yes or no, with
   one reason.
10. Short response (3–4 sentences): Using Lessons 1–3, answer: did
    the United States become an empire between 1865 and 1914? Give
    one piece of evidence for your answer and name one limitation of
    that evidence.
