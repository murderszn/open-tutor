## Web references (verified 2026-10-27)

1. **Library of Congress — "World of 1898: Treaty of Paris of 1898"**
   https://guides.loc.gov/world-of-1898/treaty-of-paris
   Paris conference from October 1, 1898; the Philippines dispute;
   $20 million paid; Puerto Rico and Guam under American control;
   Spain relinquished Cuba; signed December 10, 1898. Supports
   Objectives 2 and 3; Lessons 2 and 3; Investigation A.

2. **Encyclopaedia Britannica — "Wounded Knee Massacre"**
   https://www.britannica.com/event/Wounded-Knee-Massacre
   December 29, 1890; approximately 150–300 Lakota killed; the Great
   Sioux Reservation's shrinkage (60 million acres in 1868, 21.7
   million by 1877, 12.7 million after the Dawes Act). Supports
   Objective 1; Lesson 1; practice Set A.

3. **Encyclopaedia Britannica — "Treaty of Paris (1898)"**
   https://www.britannica.com/event/Treaty-of-Paris-1898
   Signed December 10, 1898; $20 million for the Philippines; Senate
   approval February 6, 1899, by a single vote over the threshold;
   hostilities with Aguinaldo's forces began two days earlier.
   Supports Objective 2; Lesson 2; Investigation A.

4. **Brigham Young University — WWI Document Archive, "Post-1918
   Documents"**
   http://wwi.lib.byu.edu/index.php/Post_-_1918_Documents
   Full texts and chronology: the Treaty of Versailles (June 28,
   1919), the Covenant of the League of Nations, Lodge's August 12,
   1919 speech against the League, the November 1919 reservations,
   and the chronology of the League fight (1918–1921). Supports
   Objective 5; Lesson 5; Investigation B.

5. **U.S. Army University Press (Military Review) — "The Zimmermann
   Telegram"**
   https://www.armyupress.army.mil/Journals/Military-Review/English-Edition-Archives/March-April-2017/ART-017/
   Full decoded text of the telegram (unrestricted submarine warfare
   from February 1; Mexico offered Texas, New Mexico, and Arizona);
   the March 1917 sinkings of U.S. ships; the April 6, 1917
   declaration. Supports Objective 4; Lesson 4; practice Set C.

6. **Bill of Rights Institute — "President Woodrow Wilson and the
   First World War"**
   https://billofrightsinstitute.org/activities/president-woodrow-wilson-and-the-first-world-war/
   Wilson's April 2, 1917 war message in context; the War Industries
   Board, Food and Fuel Administrations, and Committee on Public
   Information as the mobilization state. Supports Objectives 4 and
   5; Lessons 4 and 5.

7. **U.S. Capitol Visitor Center — "Certified copy of the Treaty of
   Versailles (Unperfected Treaty), section on the League of
   Nations, c. 1919"**
   https://www.visitthecapitol.gov/artifact/certified-copy-treaty-versailles-unperfected-treaty-section-league-nations-c-1919
   National Archives artifact record: Wilson's League priority and
   the Senate's rejection of the treaty largely over the League
   provision. Supports Objective 5; Lesson 5; Investigation B.

8. **National Archives (U.S.) — "Studying 4 Major Issues of the
   Post-World War I and 1920s Era with Primary Sources"**
   https://www.archives.gov/files/legislative/resources/education/1920s-issues-activity/primary-source-sheets.pdf
   Center for Legislative Archives source sheets: Lodge's objection
   to Article X (mutual defense) and the League fight's constitutional
   stakes. Supports Objective 5; Lesson 5; Investigation B.

9. **U.S. Department of State, Office of the Historian — Foreign
   Relations of the United States, 1919, Paris Peace Conference,
   Volume XIII**
   https://history.state.gov//historicaldocuments/frus1919Parisv13/toc
   The official documentary record of the Paris negotiations,
   including the Treaty of Versailles text and the Covenant of the
   League of Nations. Advanced reading; the adult selects excerpts.
   Supports Objective 5; Lesson 5 (extension).

10. **Library of Congress — general search portal**
    https://www.loc.gov/ (search: "Wounded Knee" / "Spanish-American
    War" / "Zimmermann telegram" / "League of Nations")
    Public-domain photographs, maps, cartoons, and documents; use
    the search with the adult for Investigation A case files and
    Lesson 1–2 image/cartoon analysis. Supports Objectives 1–5;
    Investigations A/B.
