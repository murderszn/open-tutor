# Unit 01 — Formative Quiz (end of Week 4)

**Purpose:** check Lessons 1–3 before industrialization begins. The adult
scores with `answer-key.md` and re-teaches misses in Week 5. Not a grade
for the record — a diagnostic.

**Instructions to the learner:** Answer in complete sentences. Use
specific facts (names, dates, laws). 25 minutes.

1. (2 pts) State the ratification year of each Reconstruction Amendment
   and, in one phrase each, what it changed.

2. (3 pts) Explain the 13th Amendment's punishment exception in your
   own words, and name one historical practice that exploited it.

3. (3 pts) Name **two** federal policies from 1865–1871 that tried to
   enforce the amendments, and for each state *which* amendment it
   enforced and *how*.

4. (4 pts) A Southern state in 1892 requires voters to pass a literacy
   test administered by white officials. Using the 15th Amendment's
   text, explain why this law was (a) constitutional under the
   amendment's wording and (b) effective at disenfranchising Black
   voters.

5. (4 pts) List **four** causes of Reconstruction's end and, in one
   sentence each, explain how two of them interacted (e.g., how one
   cause made another worse).

6. (4 pts) **Source question.** A historian writes: "Reconstruction
   failed because the federal government tried to do too much too
   fast." Identify one strength and one weakness of this interpretation,
   each tied to a specific fact from Lessons 1–3.

**Total: 20 points.** Readiness rule: 14+ and no zeros on Q4/Q5 → proceed
to Week 5. Below 14, or a zero on Q4 or Q5, → the adult re-teaches
amendment-text analysis (Lesson 2, Set A) before the Week 5 sessions.
