## Web references (verified 2026-10-14)

1. **National Archives — Amendments 11–27**
   https://www.archives.gov/founding-docs/amendments-11-27
   Full public-domain texts of the 13th (ratified Dec 6, 1865), 14th
   (July 9, 1868), and 15th (Feb 3, 1870) Amendments. Supports
   Objectives 1–2; Lessons 1–2 close reads; Investigation A.

2. **U.S. Census Bureau — "May 2023: The Transcontinental Railroad"**
   http://census.gov/about/history/stories/monthly/2023/may-2023.html
   Railroad statistics: track mileage built and completed (20,199 miles
   in 1855; 87,801 at the 1880 census), railroad investment data.
   Supports Objective 3; Lesson 4 data tasks.

3. **Gilder Lehrman Institute — Transcontinental Railroad Fact Sheet**
   https://www.gilderlehrman.org/sites/default/files/inline-pdfs/Transcontinental%20Railroad%20Fact%20Sheet.pdf
   May 10, 1869 completion at Promontory Summit, Utah; 1,776 miles;
   13,000+ Chinese laborers on the Central Pacific; 1870 ticket prices.
   Supports Objective 3; Investigation B.

4. **American Association of Railroads — Freight Railroads Chronology**
   (via proxy PDF)
   https://across.to.bridge-and-swap.app/proxy/https/www.aar.org/wp-content/uploads/2020/07/AAR-Chronology-Americas-Freight-Railroads-Fact-Sheet.pdf
   ~35,000 miles in 1865 → ~254,000 peak in 1916; the "golden age"
   framing. Supports Objective 3; Set C.

5. **LibreTexts — "Steel: Carnegie and Creative Destruction"**
   https://eng.libretexts.org/Under_Construction/Impact_of_Materials_on_Society/07%3A_Steel-_Carnegie_and_Creative_Destruction
   Bessemer process, Carnegie's cost-cutting, open-hearth transition;
   university-hosted open text. Supports Objective 3; Lesson 4 Part 2.

6. **MRS (Materials Research Society) — Module 8: Carnegie**
   https://www.mrs.org/docs/default-source/programs-and-outreach/imos-course/module-8/module-8---carnegie-creative-destruction-and-american-steel-2015-beta-wmrk.pdf?sfvrsn=1ff4dd11_6
   1877 steel-rail vs. iron-rail crossover figures (432,169 vs.
   332,540 tons); tariff protection context. Supports Objective 3;
   Set C3.

7. **Library of Congress — general search portal**
   https://www.loc.gov/ (search: "Reconstruction" / "Freedmen's Bureau")
   Public-domain photographs, maps, and documents; use the search with
   the adult for Investigation A case files. Supports Objectives 1–2,
   5; Investigation A/B.
