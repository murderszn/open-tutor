# Unit 04 — Teacher Guide (adult only)

This guide supports the adult guiding a grade-10 learner through
Weeks 15–18. It does not replace previewing sources: read every
primary-source excerpt and Resource Pack link before assigning it,
and frame period stereotypes as evidence of prejudice.

## Pacing notes

- **Week 15 (Lesson 1):** the installment/margin arithmetic is the
  lesson's engine — do it on paper, slowly. Learners who rush to
  "the 1920s were great" have missed the farm-price mechanism;
  return to the wheat-and-debt example (Lesson 1, Part 3) before
  moving on.
- **Week 16 (Lessons 2–3 + quiz):** two lessons in one week is
  demanding by design (the U03 pattern). If time runs short, cut
  Lesson 3's applied-task draft, not the image-study limitation
  check — the reconstruction-vs.-evidence distinction carries into
  the Week 18 evidence lab and the assessment.
- **Week 17 (Lesson 4):** the Three R's sorting chart is the week's
  anchor. Accept primary + secondary aim answers; reject
  single-box answers that ignore a program's second effect.
- **Week 18 (Lesson 5 + assessment):** the verdict paragraph
  (Lesson 5, Worked example 3) is the assessment's rehearsal. A
  learner who cannot yet state a measure should not attempt Part C
  cold — re-teach with the worked example first.

## Misconceptions and re-teaching

| Misconception | Re-teach with |
|---|---|
| "Everyone prospered in the 1920s" | Lesson 1, Part 3 (farm prices vs. debts); ask "prosperous for whom?" |
| "The crash caused the Depression, full stop" | Lesson 2, Part 2 (five interacting causes) and the 1930–33 banking collapse timing |
| "The Dow fell 10.8%" | Lesson 2, Worked example 1: fall + remainder = 100% |
| "Percentage points = percent" | Practice A6: 10.6 percentage points vs. ~42.6% relative fall |
| "A picture proves it happened" | Lesson 3 image study: reconstruction, wagon caution, FSA photographs' purposes |
| "The Dust Bowl was only weather" | Lesson 3, Part 2: plowing, wheat economics, drought interaction |
| "The Hundred Days ended the Depression" | Unemployment table: 21.7% in 1934, 20.1% in 1935 |
| "The New Deal ended the Depression" | Unemployment table: 14.6% in 1940; wartime mobilization is Unit 05 |
| "Court-packing succeeded" | Lesson 5, Part 3: Congress refused; the Court's shift came separately |
| "Exclusion means programs did nothing" | Lesson 5, Part 2: real relief and real exclusion coexisted |

## What signals readiness

- The learner can compute a percentage fall and a percentage-point
  change without confusing them (quiz Q4, Q7; assessment Part D).
- The learner can sort a program by primary and secondary aim and
  name who it left out with a mechanism (project rubric).
- The learner can write a verdict that states its measure, cites
  two facts, names an exclusion, and states one limitation
  (Lesson 5, Worked example 3; assessment Part C).

## Scoring guidance

- **Quiz (10 questions, formative):** record misses by objective,
  not just by total. Misses on Q3–Q5 mean re-teach Lesson 2's
  margin/causation work before Week 17; misses on Q8/Q10 mean
  re-teach Lesson 3's Dust Bowl/image work.
- **Assessment (100 points):** Part A 20, Part B 20, Part C 30,
  Part D 30 (see `answer-key.md` for point breakdowns). Part C is
  scored on structure (measure, three programs, exclusion, Court
  paragraph, limitation) as much as on conclusion — accept any
  defensible verdict that meets the structure.
- **Project (20 points):** use the rubric in `project.md`. Below
  12, name the one section to revise before the assessment.
- These scores measure this unit's objectives only. They are not
  standardized or validated, and they must not be recorded as
  grades in any public repository.

## Sensitive content and alternatives

- Poverty, hunger, eviction, racial exclusion, and the Bonus Army
  dispersal are handled through documentary text only; no graphic
  imagery is assigned. Offer the Lesson 3 reflection alternative
  (a written reflection instead of the diary entry) for any learner
  who finds the material overwhelming.
- Period photographs (FSA/Lange/Rothstein) are public domain and
  are referenced, not reproduced, in this unit; the adult previews
  any photograph before showing it and frames government
  commissioning as a purpose to discuss, not a neutral fact.
- The generated illustration is labeled an AI reconstruction in
  its caption, its lesson, and `assets/README.md`; never present it
  as a photograph.
