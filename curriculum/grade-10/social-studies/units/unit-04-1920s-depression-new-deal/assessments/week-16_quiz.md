# Unit 04 — Formative Quiz (after Lesson 3, end of Week 16)

10 questions. Solutions are in `answer-key.md` (adult only). This quiz
is formative: the adult records misses and re-teaches before Week 17.

1. A radio costs $120 cash, or $10 down plus $10 a month for 12
   months. What is the installment total, and what is the extra cost
   of buying on time?
2. The 19th Amendment (1920) barred denying the vote on account of
   sex. Name one group of women who still could not vote freely in
   1925, and one barrier they faced.
3. A buyer puts $10 down and borrows $90 for $100 of stock. Prices
   fall 20% and the buyer sells and repays the loan. What is the
   buyer's net result?
4. Compute the Dow's percentage fall from 381.17 (September 3, 1929)
   to 41.22 (July 8, 1932). Show the arithmetic.
5. Classify each as trigger or long-term cause/condition, with one
   reason: (a) Black Tuesday, October 29, 1929; (b) the absence of
   federal deposit insurance before 1933.
6. Name two of the five interacting causes of the Depression from
   Lesson 2, and for each, name one group hurt first.
7. Using Lesson 2's table, state unemployment in 1929, 1933, and
   1938, and explain in one sentence why the 1938 figure matters
   for judging recovery.
8. Name two human causes and one natural cause of the Dust Bowl.
9. What happened to the Bonus Army encampment in July 1932, and why
   did the episode damage Hoover politically? (Two sentences.)
10. From Lesson 3's image study (or its text alternative): name
    three features shown in the illustration, and state one thing
    the illustration cannot prove about the Dust Bowl.
