# Unit 05 — Formative Quiz (Week 22, after Lesson 3)

**20 points · about 25 minutes · closed book except the aircraft
table in Lesson 2.** Covers Lessons 1–3 only. Write in your
notebook; the adult checks against `answer-key.md`.

1. (2 pts) Which of the following was a step toward the Allies
   *before* Pearl Harbor?
   a. The Neutrality Acts
   b. The Lend-Lease Act
   c. The Civil Liberties Act
   d. The GI Bill

2. (2 pts) In one sentence, what did President Roosevelt mean by
   calling the United States the "Arsenal of Democracy"?

3. (2 pts) Place these four events in order: (a) Japan invades
   Manchuria; (b) Lend-Lease Act; (c) attack on Pearl Harbor; (d)
   Executive Order 9066.

4. (2 pts) U.S. aircraft production was 6,086 in 1940 and 96,318 in
   1944. By about what factor did it grow? Show the division.

5. (2 pts) In 1940 about 12 million women worked (27% of the
   workforce); by 1945 over 19 million worked (37%). State the change
   in one sentence, using both a number and a percentage.

6. (2 pts) In one sentence, what did Executive Order 9066 authorize?

7. (2 pts) About how many people of Japanese descent were
   incarcerated, and what fraction were U.S. citizens?
   a. About 12,000; about one-third citizens
   b. About 120,000; about two-thirds citizens
   c. About 120,000; about one-third citizens
   d. About 1,200,000; about two-thirds citizens

8. (2 pts) In one sentence each, state what *Korematsu v. United
   States* (1944) decided and what *Ex parte Endo* (1944) decided.

9. (2 pts) In 2–3 sentences, explain why the 1943 loyalty
   questionnaire put incarcerated Nisei in an impossible position.

10. (2 pts) ★ A classmate says, "The United States was neutral until
    Pearl Harbor." Correct that claim in 2–3 sentences, using two
    facts from Lesson 1.

**Scoring:** 18–20 = ready to move on; 15–17 = review the missed
lessons' worked examples; below 15 = re-teach Lessons 1–3 with the
teacher guide before Week 23.
