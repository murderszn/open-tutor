## Web references (verified 2026-11-13)

1. **National Archives — "Executive Order 8802: Prohibition of
   Discrimination in the Defense Industry (1941)"**
   https://www.archives.gov/milestone-documents/executive-order-8802
   The order's text and context: June 25, 1941; the March on
   Washington Movement; the Fair Employment Practice Committee.
   Supports Objective 4; Lesson 4; Practice Set C. Free, no account.

2. **National Women's History Museum — "Honoring Rosie the Riveter
   and the Women Who Won the War"**
   https://www.womenshistory.org/articles/honoring-rosie-riveter-and-women-who-won-war
   Female labor force grew to 19 million, more than a third of the
   civilian labor force, 1940–1945; industrial production doubled
   1939–1945. Supports Objective 2; Lesson 2; Practice Set B. Free,
   no account.

3. **New York Public Library — "TeachNYPL: World War II and the
   Double V Campaign (Gr. 10-12)"**
   https://www.nypl.org/blog/2013/11/12/classroom-connections-wwii-double-v-campaign-gr-10-12
   Thompson's January 31, 1942 letter; the Courier's February 7,
   1942 launch; Double V clubs and activities. Supports Objective 4;
   Lesson 4; Investigation A. Free, no account.

4. **Go For Broke National Education Center — "442nd Regimental
   Combat Team"**
   https://www.goforbroke.org/learn/units/442nd-regimental-combat-team
   Activated February 1, 1943; most decorated unit for its size and
   length of service; 21 Medals of Honor. Supports Objective 4;
   Lesson 4. Free, no account.

5. **National Archives (Unwritten Record) — "Atomic Bombings of
   Hiroshima and Nagasaki"**
   https://unwritten-record.blogs.archives.gov/2020/08/04/atomic-bombings-of-hiroshima-and-nagasaki/
   Hiroshima August 6, 1945 and Nagasaki August 9, 1945; death-toll
   ranges (Hiroshima 90,000–166,000 in four months; Nagasaki
   40,000–75,000 immediately); the tolls are estimates. Supports
   Objective 5; Lesson 5; assessment Part C. Free, no account.

6. **The Army Air Forces in World War II, Vol. VI, ch. 10 — "The
   Production Record" (ibiblio)**
   http://www.ibiblio.org/hyperwar/AAF/VI/AAF-VI-10.html
   The official production record: almost 300,000 military aircraft,
   July 1940–August 1945; 1944 output nearly sixteen times 1940.
   Supports Objective 2; Lesson 2; Practice Set B; the SVG chart's
   source. Free, no account.

7. **Wikipedia — "Internment of Japanese Americans"**
   https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Internment_of_Japanese_Americans
   Summary with dates and figures: about 120,000 people, about
   two-thirds citizens, ten camps; EO 9066 (February 19, 1942);
   Korematsu 6–3 and Ex parte Endo, both December 18, 1944. Adult
   previews before assigning; cross-check figures against the
   National Archives and Densho. Supports Objective 3; Lesson 3;
   Investigation B. Free, no account.
