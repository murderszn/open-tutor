# Teacher Guide — Unit 04: Language, Syntax, Tone, Vocabulary, Conventions

**Grade 10 Language Arts · Track weeks 15–18 · Issue #48, section U04**
Companion to the [unit README](README.md). Answer key: [answer-key.md](answer-key.md).

## What this unit is for

U01–U03 taught reading and judging texts. U04 teaches the writer's
own sentence tools: clause analysis (L01), phrase variety (L02),
semicolon/colon punctuation (L03), a repeatable vocabulary system
(L04), figures of speech and tone (L05), and three-pass editing with
a style-manual preview (L06). The throughline: **every small choice
is a meaning choice**, and editing is deciding on purpose. These
tools are enforced in every later unit's editing passes; U05's
narratives and U06's arguments will both be edited with them.

## How to use the lessons

Each lesson is built for a teach session followed by an independent
session. The worked examples are *models*, not scripts — read them
with the learner, then cover the reasoning and ask the learner to
reproduce it on the guided items before attempting independent work.
The exit check is a daily pulse: if it fails two days running,
re-teach before advancing. The image in Lesson 05 must be shown
with its text-only alternative available; never test a learner on
image-only information.

## Misconception log (watch for these)

1. **Phrase-as-clause** — participial phrases (*carrying a
   lantern*) labeled as clauses (L01). Fix: the subject-plus-finite-
   verb test.
2. ***That* always relative** — noun clauses after verbs like
   *knew* mislabeled (L01). Fix: does the group describe a noun,
   or fill a noun's job?
3. **Dangling modifiers** — phrases attached to the wrong noun
   (L02). Fix: name the actor; put the phrase beside it.
4. **Semicolon as fancy comma** — semicolons before phrases,
   dependent clauses, and conjunctions (L03). Fix: both sides must
   stand alone.
5. **Colon after a fragment** — *The things are:* (L03). Fix: the
   before-colon test (could it end with a period?).
6. **Guess-and-stop vocabulary** — plausible guesses never verified
   (L04). Fix: the substitution test on every entry.
7. **Thesaurus synonym as exact replacement** — *blunt* for
   *candid* (L04). Fix: connotation check in the sentence.
8. **Single-word tone** — tone attributed to one word (L05). Fix:
   require two or more quoted choices pulling the same way.
9. **Editing backwards** — commas fixed before sentences are
   decided (L06). Fix: hold the pass order (structure, language,
   conventions).
10. **Formal as "better"** — register judged as quality (L06).
    Fix: fit-to-audience language, using Lesson 06's pair.

## Reteach guidance

- If the quiz score is below 9: re-teach L01–L03 with new sentences
  (not the quiz items), then re-take a parallel quiz the adult
  writes from the answer key's item specs.
- If fragments/run-ons persist: back to Lesson 01 guided practice
  plus Practice A items 4–5 as a standalone drill; do not advance
  to the portfolio revision until the learner repairs three
  consecutive non-sentences correctly.
- If semicolons are sprinkled: Lesson 03's both-sides test as a
  chant ("can both sides stand alone?") before every semicolon in
  Practice C and the portfolio.
- If the portfolio's tones blur: re-do Lesson 05's Version A/B
  comparison with the learner's own two-tone draft beside it, and
  require two quoted choices per tone claim.
- If the word bank is guess-only: return every entry without a
  verification line; no entry counts until substitution is shown.

## Differentiation

- **Support:** clause/phrase labels may be dictated or drawn;
  practice items may be halved (odd items only) with the rest as
  adult-modeled examples; the portfolio's revised piece may be
  200–300 words; the two-tone passage may be read aloud.
- **Extension:** Lesson 02/05 extensions; the portfolio may revise
  two pieces (one narrative, one argumentative) and compare which
  tools each genre needed most; the assessment's Part B tone-pair
  may add a third register (a formal notice version).

## Readiness signals (unit complete)

The learner: labels clauses and phrases without help; repairs
fragments, run-ons, and dangling modifiers; justifies every
semicolon and colon by rule; completes word-bank entries with
verification; supports tone claims with quoted word choice and
syntax; and runs the three-pass edit in order. Quiz ≥ 10/12 and
assessment Part A ≥ 16/20 with Part B ≥ 15/20 indicate readiness
for U05 (narrative craft and literary analysis essays).
