# Week-18 Formative Quiz — Unit 04

**Session 18 · Formative: the adult scores it, logs misconceptions,
and re-teaches before the unit assessment. It is not a final grade.**
**Answer-key reference:** [answer-key § Quiz](../answer-key.md#quiz)

Answer each item on paper. For punctuation items, write the full
corrected sentence. For vocabulary items, give the verified meaning
and one clue or pattern that supports it.

1. Label the clauses in: *After the tide turned, the keeper lit the
   lamps that lined the quay.* Name the sentence type.
2. Name the clause kind: (a) *what the letter said* in "She
   repeated what the letter said." (b) *who kept the light* in
   "The woman who kept the light retired in spring." (c) *because
   the road was closed* in "They waited because the road was
   closed."
3. Identify as sentence, fragment, or run-on, and repair every
   non-sentence: (a) *The archive, silent and cold.* (b) *The
   committee voted the motion failed.* (c) *When the votes were
   counted, the chair announced the result.*
4. Name the phrase type of each named group: (a) *carrying a
   lantern* in "The keeper, carrying a lantern, crossed the quay."
   (b) *its sails torn* in "The boat, its sails torn, came in
   slowly." (c) *through the channel* in "The boat came through
   the channel."
5. Fix the dangling modifier: *Waiting for the tide, the quay
   seemed empty.*
6. Punctuate with a semicolon (add a conjunctive adverb if one
   fits): *The translation was accurate it missed the poem's humor.*
7. Punctuate with a colon or write NO COLON and explain why:
   *The drawer contained paper pens and string.*
8. Correct or write OK: (a) *She kept the light; however, no ship
   came.* (b) *She kept the light, however, no ship came.* (c)
   *She kept the light; and no ship came.*
9. Use context and a dictionary to give the verified meaning of
   *reluctant* in: *Reluctant to disturb the archive, she copied
   the letter by hand.* Name the clue type.
10. Give the word-change family for *analyze* (verb): its noun and
    adjective forms, each used in a sentence.
11. Name the figure of speech and its role: *The company called the
    layoffs "a workforce adjustment."* What plain fact does it
    soften?
12. Name the tone of each sentence with one quoted word as
    evidence: (a) *The report, meticulously checked, was published
    on time.* (b) *The report, slapped together, was dumped on the
    desk.*
