## town-hall-speaker.png

- **File:** `assets/town-hall-speaker.png` (PNG, raster, 1920 × 1280)
- **Prompt (verbatim):** "A warm, painterly illustration of a town-hall
  meeting: a speaker stands at a simple wooden podium on a small stage,
  one hand raised mid-sentence, facing rows of seated listeners in a
  plain community hall with tall windows and wooden chairs. Some
  listeners lean forward attentively, one takes notes, one looks
  skeptical with arms crossed. No text, words, signs, or banners
  anywhere in the image. Muted warm colors, calm civic mood, no real or
  recognizable people."
- **Tool/model:** Muse built-in image generation (`media.generate_image`),
  2026-10-27. No model version reported by the tool.
- **Date generated:** 2026-10-27
- **Intended use:** Lesson 06 ("Accounts in Different Mediums, and
  Evaluating a Speaker") — the image activity: which listener behaves
  most like a source evaluator, what the image can show that a
  transcript cannot, and what it cannot show. Used with a specific
  question set and a text-only alternative.
- **Factual review (inspected 2026-10-27):** The image matches the
  prompt: a speaker at a wooden podium with one hand raised, rows of
  seated listeners in a hall with tall windows and wooden chairs, one
  listener in the foreground writing in a notebook, another sitting
  with arms crossed. No text, words, signs, or banners appear. No real
  or recognizable person is depicted. Labeled in the lesson as an
  AI-generated illustration of an invented scene, not a record of a
  real meeting and not evidence about any real speaker.
- **Caption (as used in Lesson 06):** "AI-generated illustration of a
  town-hall meeting — the speaker at the podium, one listener taking
  notes, another listening with arms crossed. This is an invented scene,
  not a record of a real meeting, and not evidence about any real
  speaker."
- **Alt text:** "Painterly illustration of a town-hall meeting: a
  speaker at a wooden podium gestures toward rows of seated listeners
  in a community hall with tall windows; one listener in the foreground
  takes notes while another sits with arms crossed."
- **Known limitations:** A single still image cannot show what the
  speaker said, the tone of voice, the audience's decision, or anything
  about the proposal's merits. The lesson teaches these limits
  explicitly: the image prompts questions about listening, it never
  settles a question about the proposal. The text-only alternative in
  the lesson carries the visual content for screen-reader users.
