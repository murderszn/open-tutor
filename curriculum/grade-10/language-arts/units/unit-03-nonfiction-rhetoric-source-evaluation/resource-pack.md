# Resource Pack — Unit 03: Nonfiction Rhetoric, Reasoning, and Source Evaluation

**Goal:** give a grade-10 learner and guiding adult verified references
for tracing arguments, naming rhetorical strategies, testing reasoning,
and reading seminal documents — with a free, no-account route for every
core objective. **Age fit:** adult planning judgment — grade 10 (ages
about 15–16), adult previews every link before the learner opens it.
**Checked:** 2026-10-27. No video title, runtime, caption claim, or
standard code below is asserted beyond what was checked on that date;
where a video could not be verified directly, a clearly labeled search
link is given instead.

## Web references (verified 2026-10-27)

- **National Archives — "Declaration of Independence: A Transcription"**
  (https://www.archives.gov/founding-docs/declaration-transcript).
  The authoritative transcription of the 1776 document; spelling and
  punctuation reflect the original. Free, no account. Use for Lesson 05
  and the full text behind the unit's excerpts. Public-domain document.
- **FIDH — "The Universal Declaration of Human Rights" (full text)**
  (https://www.fidh.org/en/what-is-fidh/The-Universal-Declaration-of-Human/).
  Full text of the 1948 declaration as adopted by the UN General
  Assembly (resolution 217 (III)), reproduced by the International
  Federation for Human Rights. Free, no account. Use for Lesson 05;
  the issuing body is the UN Office of the High Commissioner for Human
  Rights. Short classroom quotations in the unit's excerpts file are
  attributed to the declaration.
- **Purdue OWL — "Classical Argument"**
  (https://owl.purdue.edu/owl/general_writing/academic_writing/historical_perspectives_on_argumentation/classical_argument.html).
  University writing-lab guide to classical rhetoric: ethos (credibility),
  logos (logic), pathos (emotion), and kairos (timing). Free, no
  account. Use for Lesson 03 (appeals) and Lesson 06 (speaker
  evaluation).
- **Common Core State Standards — ELA-Literacy, Reading: Informational
  Text, grades 9–10** (via https://www.thecorestandards.org/ELA-Literacy/).
  The reference framework for this unit's standards notes; codes and
  descriptions were checked for this track's audit on 2026-10-07.
  Planning reference only — no claim of state adoption, accreditation,
  or certification is made. Free, no account.
- **Unit texts (internal, no account, no link needed):**
  [`texts/the-library-editorial.md`](texts/the-library-editorial.md),
  [`texts/the-library-counter-editorial.md`](texts/the-library-counter-editorial.md),
  [`texts/the-library-speech.md`](texts/the-library-speech.md),
  [`texts/library-use-data.md`](texts/library-use-data.md),
  [`texts/seminal-documents-excerpts.md`](texts/seminal-documents-excerpts.md).
  All original to this unit except the public-domain Declaration
  excerpts and the short attributed UDHR quotations noted in the
  excerpts file.
