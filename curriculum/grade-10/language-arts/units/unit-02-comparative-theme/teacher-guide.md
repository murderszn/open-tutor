# Teacher Guide — Unit 02: Comparative Theme Structure and Perspective

**Grade 10 Language Arts · Track weeks 7–10 · Issue #48, section U02**
Companion to the [unit README](README.md). Answer key: [answer-key.md](answer-key.md).

## What this unit is for

U01 taught reading *within* a text — context, POV, translation, theme.
U02 teaches reading *across* texts: fair comparison (L01), comparative
thesis (L02), dual theme tracking (L03), structural comparison (L04),
source transformation (L05), and cross-medium comparison (L06). The
throughline: **a comparison is an argument**, and arguments need a shared
question, comparable evidence, and a "so what."

## How to use the lessons

Each lesson is built for a teach session followed by an independent
session. The worked examples are *models*, not scripts — read them with
the learner, then cover the reasoning and ask the learner to reproduce
it on the guided items before attempting independent work. The exit
check is a daily pulse: if it fails two days running, re-teach before
advancing.

## Misconception log (watch for these)

1. **Comparison as ranking** ("which is better") — redirect to the
   shared question (L01). Persistent? Re-do Practice A item 3.
2. **Announcement thesis** ("I will compare…") — missing "so what"
   (L02). Fix: the skeptic test.
3. **Topic-as-theme** ("the theme is hospitality") — require the claim
   form "the story claims *that*…" (L03).
4. **Comparison before tracking** — comparative claims written from v1
   guesses (L03). Fix: no comparison until both texts reach v3.
5. **Structure without effects** — naming devices, not analyzing (L04).
   Require the *→ effect* arrow every time.
6. **Layer-2 stopping** — transformation analysis that lists changes
   without reaching the theme shift (L05). Ask: *what does the new work
   claim that the source did not?*
7. **Fidelity judging** — rating the illustration "accurate/inaccurate"
   instead of analyzing emphasis (L06). Redirect to emphasized/absent.

## Reteach guidance

- If the quiz score is below 12: re-teach L01–L02 with new paired
  micro-texts (not the quiz passages), then re-take a parallel quiz the
  adult writes from the answer key's item specs.
- If the essay thesis is a topic: back to L02 guided practice + the
  skeptic test; do not accept the draft until a reader could disagree.
- If quotations are dropped, not handled: Practice F item 1 as a
  standalone drill on three quotations before revision continues.
- If the casebook's second text is too hard: the adult pre-reads and
  selects 3–4 key passages; the learner works from those (note the
  adaptation in the project log).

## Differentiation

- **Support:** outlines and trackers may be dictated; quotations may be
  close paraphrases with paragraph references; the essay may be 700–900
  words with the adult as scribe for the learner's spoken argument.
- **Extension:** L03/L05/L06 extensions; the casebook may use two
  *unfamiliar* texts instead of one unit text; the essay may add a third
  text as a counter-voice.

## Readiness signals (unit complete)

The learner: states the three fairness rules unprompted; revises a theme
statement when details demand it; attaches every structural observation
to an effect; distinguishes kept/changed/transformed; and writes a
thesis a skeptic cannot agree with for free. Quiz ≥ 15 and essay ≥ 9
(assessment) indicate readiness for U03 (nonfiction rhetoric).
