# Resource Pack — Unit 02: Comparative Theme Structure and Perspective

**Summary:** Supports a grade-10 unit on comparative literary analysis:
fair comparison method, arguable comparative thesis, dual theme tracking
(RL.9-10.2), structural comparison of order/pacing/POV (RL.9-10.5),
source transformation (RL.9-10.9), cross-medium scene comparison
(RL.9-10.7), and a comparative essay (W.9-10.2, W.9-10.9) — for the
learner's practice and the adult's background. All items free; no
account required for any core objective. Links checked 2026-10-20.
