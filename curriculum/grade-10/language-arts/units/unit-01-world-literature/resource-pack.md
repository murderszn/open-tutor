# Resource Pack — Unit 01: World Literature Cultural Context and Interpretation

**Summary:** Supports a grade-10 unit on reading world literature with
cultural context: separating textual evidence from reader assumption,
analyzing point of view and cultural experience (RL.9-10.6), reading in
translation, tracking theme across a full selection, and writing an
interpretive essay — for the learner's practice and the adult's
background. All items free; no account required for any core objective.
Links opened and checked 2026-10-14.
