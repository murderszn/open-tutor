# Resource Pack — Unit 05: Narrative Craft and Literary Analysis Essays

**Summary:** Supports a grade-10 unit on writing narratives (story
architecture, point of view, dialogue, pacing, revision) and turning
craft knowledge into literary analysis essays (RL.9-10.3, RL.9-10.5,
W.9-10.3) — for the learner's practice and the adult's background. All
items free; no account required for any core objective. Links opened and
checked 2026-11-12 unless noted otherwise.

## Web references

1. **Common Core State Standards: Writing, grades 9–10** —
   <https://www.thecorestandards.org/ELA-Literacy/W/9-10/> — official
   W.9-10.3.a–e, W.9-10.5, and W.9-10.9 descriptions the unit's
   standards crosswalk paraphrases. *Opened and verified 2026-11-12.*
2. **Common Core State Standards: Reading Literature, grades 9–10** —
   <https://www.thecorestandards.org/ELA-Literacy/RL/9-10/> — official
   RL.9-10.3 and RL.9-10.5 descriptions the unit's standards crosswalk
   paraphrases. *Opened and verified 2026-11-12.*
3. **Purdue OWL — "Writing a Literary Analysis" presentation** —
   <https://owl.purdue.edu/owl/resources/teaching_resources/writing_a_literary_analysis_presentation.html>
   — what a literary analysis is (an argument, not a summary), literary
   concepts, thesis statements, and supporting a thesis. Free, no
   account. *Opened and verified 2026-11-12.*
4. **Purdue OWL — "Writing in Literature: Writing the Prompt Paper"** —
   <https://OWL.Purdue.edu/owl/subject_specific_writing/writing_in_literature/writing_in_literature_detailed_discussion/the_prompt_paper.html>
   — prompt types (compare and contrast, discuss a theme, role of a
   character or group) and strategies for answering them. Free, no
   account. *Opened and verified 2026-11-12.*
5. **ReadWriteThink — "Make a Splash! Using Dramatic Experience to
   'Explode the Moment'"** —
   <https://www.readwritethink.org/classroom-resources/lesson-plans/make-splash-using-dramatic>
   — elaborating a moment with vivid sensory language; graphic organizer
   for what was seen, felt, thought, said, and heard. *Opened and
   verified 2026-11-12. Note: the lesson's model books are middle-grade
   titles — use it as adult background for the sensory-ladder work in
   Lesson 03, not as a grade-10 assignment.*
6. **CommonLit — Text Library** — <https://www.commonlit.org/> — free
   library of literature and informational texts for extension reading
   and model stories; grades 6–12. *Opened and verified 2026-11-12.
   Note: full library access requires a free account; browsing and the
   homepage are free without one. Extension reading only — no core
   objective depends on it.*
7. **Project Gutenberg — short stories search** —
   <https://www.gutenberg.org/ebooks/search/?query=short+stories> —
   public-domain short-story collections for extension reading and model
   analysis (including "The Gift of the Magi," "The Monkey's Paw," "The
   Yellow Wallpaper"). Free, no account. *Opened and verified
   2026-11-12; the adult previews any story before assigning.*
