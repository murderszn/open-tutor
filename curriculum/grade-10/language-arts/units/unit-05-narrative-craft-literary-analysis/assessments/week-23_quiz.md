# Week-23 Formative Quiz — Unit 05: Narrative Craft and Literary Analysis

*Adult-scored with the [answer key](../answer-key.md#week-23-formative-quiz).
12 items, about 25 minutes. Open story (the anchor story may be consulted);
closed notes for items 1–3 and 9–11, which test recall of the unit's tools.*

1. **Want vs. need.** Which of these is Lian's *need* in "The Kite
   Maker's Last Flight"?
   - a. To win the brass compass and the fifty crowns.
   - b. To see the heron kite in the air again, with her grandfather
     standing beside it.
   - c. To build the fastest kite at the festival.
   - d. To impress the judges at the judges' table.

2. **Event chain.** Here are four events from the story in order. One is
   a decoration — it can be cut without changing anything. Which one, and
   why (one sentence)?
   - (a) Lian rushes the glue on her kite.
   - (b) Sef corrects her: "You don't hurry a promise."
   - (c) A gull steals a fisherman's lunch on the hill.
   - (d) Lian slows down and learns the knots.

3. **Point of view.** The anchor story's narrator is:
   - a. First person, Lian telling her own story.
   - b. Third-person limited, staying close to Lian.
   - c. Third-person omniscient, entering every character's mind.
   - d. Second person, addressing the reader as "you."

4. **What POV hides.** In one sentence, name one thing the story's point
   of view hides from the reader, and quote the line that shows the
   hiding.

5. **Dialogue.** Which line best reveals character *through the spoken
   words alone* — and what does it reveal (one sentence)?
   - a. "The wind was strong today."
   - b. "Efficient is a word people use when they're rushing and want it
     to sound clever."

6. **Sensory ladder.** Which of these sentences is at rung 3 (embodied,
   sensory, with consequence) — and what sense beyond sight does it use?
   - a. "The kite flew high."
   - b. "The red kite flew high over the harbor."
   - c. "The string burned a thin red line across Lian's palm as the red
     diamond climbed."

7. **Scene vs. summary.** Label each passage *scene* or *summary* and
   give a one-sentence reason:
   - (a) "Over the next three days she built the kite the slow way."
   - (b) "'You're rushing the glue,' he said without looking up."

8. **Flashback.** In two sentences, explain what the workshop flashback
   ("Three days earlier…") changes about the hilltop opening scene.

9. **Revision order.** Which revision pass comes first, and why (one
   sentence)?
   - a. Conventions pass — clean the surface before anything else.
   - b. Language pass — fix the sentences, then the structure.
   - c. Structure pass — fix the architecture before polishing sentences.

10. **Thesis.** Which thesis is arguable — a claim a skeptic could
    disagree with for a reason?
    - a. "The story is about a girl, her grandfather, and kites."
    - b. "The story uses imagery and symbolism to create a vivid picture."
    - c. "The story withholds Sef's grief until the hilltop climax, so
      the heron kite's flight lands as a decision rather than a sentiment."

11. **Semicolon (U04 tool).** Which sentence uses the semicolon
    correctly?
    - a. "The kite climbed; the string hummed."
    - b. "The kite climbed; because the wind was strong."
    - c. "The kite climbed; and the crowd cheered."

12. **Reflective conclusion vs. moral.** Which ending is a reflective
    conclusion rather than a moral — and why (one sentence)?
    - a. "The lesson is that you should never give up on your dreams."
    - b. "She had come to the hill wanting a prize, and she was going
      home with something else: the weight of a string in her hand, her
      grandfather beside her, and a kite in the sky that was not hers
      and was hers now, both at once."

**Scoring:** 12 items; items 1, 3, 6, 9, 10, 11 are 1 point each (correct
choice); items 2, 4, 5, 7, 8, 12 are 2 points each (correct answer with
reasoning). 18–15 = secure; 14–11 = review the missed lesson before the
unit assessment; below 11 = re-teach the missed lessons with the adult
before attempting Part B.
