# Formative Quiz — End of Week 4 (Lessons 01–04)

**25 points · ~30 minutes · Calculator allowed · Adult supervises**

This quiz checks the first four lessons before the unit moves to uncertainty and the density lab.
It is formative: gaps found here drive the Session-10 reteach, not a grade in a book.

1. (2) A procedure says "gently heat the water and record its temperature every minute." Name two
   lab-safety contract rules this step triggers and what the adult must do first.
2. (2) You need the mass of 30 mL of water. Name the instrument, the unit, and one thing you must
   do with the balance before adding the water.
3. (2) Circle the SI base units: meter · liter · gram · second · mole · milliliter.
4. (3) Convert 0.680 L to milliliters. Show the factor-label setup with the units canceled.
5. (3) Convert 18.0 °C to kelvin, rounded correctly. Then answer: why can't you convert °C→K with
   a conversion factor?
6. (3) Using `../assets/meniscus-reading-scale.svg`, state the volume reading with units and
   explain in one sentence why the answer has a decimal digit.
7. (3) Count the significant figures: (a) 0.00520 kg; (b) 40.0 mL; (c) 3 trials (state measured or
   exact).
8. (3) Add 22.5 cm + 3.75 cm. Show the calculator result, then the correctly rounded answer with
   the rule you used.
9. (2) Divide 25.00 g by 5.0 mL. Give the unrounded value and the correctly rounded answer with
   units.
10. (2) A learner writes "24 mL" for a cylinder with 1 mL marks where the meniscus sits halfway
    between 24 and 25. What did they do wrong, and what is the correct reading?

## Reteach guide (adult)

- **Misses on 1–2:** re-run Lesson 01's guided scenarios aloud; the learner must cite rule numbers.
- **Misses on 3:** rebuild the base-vs-derived table from memory; test with "g/mL" and "cm³."
- **Misses on 4:** back to Lesson 02 §2 — the learner narrates each cancellation aloud for three
  fresh conversions before moving on.
- **Misses on 5:** re-teach the shift-vs-scale distinction with a number line showing 0 °C = 273.15 K.
- **Misses on 6 or 10:** physical re-demo with any clear container and water; eye-level practice
  until the learner traces the meniscus bottom unprompted.
- **Misses on 7–9:** drill the two rules separately (five +/− items, then five ×/÷ items) before
  mixing them; use the reference card from Lesson 04.

**Readiness signal:** ≥ 20/25 with no more than one miss in each of the pairs (4,5), (6,10), (7–9).
Below that, reteach the flagged lesson in Session 10 before starting Lesson 05.
