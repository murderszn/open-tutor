# Resource Pack — Unit 01

**Goal:** support a grade-10 learner through safe lab measurement, SI conversion, instrument
reading, significant figures, uncertainty, and the density investigation — with a free, no-account
route for every core objective. All external links were opened and checked on **2026-10-13**; the
check notes below record exactly what was verified. Videos supplement the written lessons; they do
not replace them.

## Web references (4 verified + 1 labeled search link)

1. **PhET "Density" simulation** — https://phet.colorado.edu/en/simulations/density (checked
   2026-10-13; page loads; title "Density | Volume • Mass • Density | PhET Simulations").
   HTML5, runs in browser on PC/Mac/Chromebook; free nonprofit, no account (donation banner only).
   Direct-launch link: https://phet.colorado.edu/sims/html/density/latest/density_en.html.
   *Supports: density investigation simulation alternative (project.md), Session 20 extension.*
   The adult previews the sim for advertising/age suitability before the learner opens it.
2. **NIST "SI Units"** — https://www.nist.gov/pml/owm/metric-si/si-units (checked 2026-10-13;
   lists all seven SI base units, a 24-prefix table, and student/teacher resources; page updated
   July 22, 2025). Authoritative, no account. *Supports: Lessons 01–02, Practice Sets A–B.*
3. **LibreTexts "1.6: Measurement Uncertainty, Accuracy, and Precision"** —
   https://chem.libretexts.org/Courses/Los_Angeles_Southwest_College/LASC__Chemistry_60_-_Introduction_to_General_Chemistry/01%3A_Essential_Ideas/1.06%3A_Measurement_Uncertainty_Accuracy_and_Precision
   (checked 2026-10-13; teaches reading "the bottom of the meniscus," the one-tenth-of-division
   rule, significant figures, and accuracy vs. precision with worked examples; OpenStax-based).
   No account. *Supports: Lessons 03–05, Practice Sets C–E.*
4. **UT Dallas "Safety Equipment — Chemistry and Biochemistry"** —
   https://chemistry.utdallas.edu/resources/safety-equipment/ (checked 2026-10-13; goggles/ANSI
   Z87, no working alone, no eating/drinking, attire, spill reporting, SDS reference; points to
   ACS "Safety in Academic Laboratories"). Undergraduate-level but plainly written; no account.
   *Supports: Lesson 01, project hazard review.* (The University of Toronto lab-safety page was
   substituted out — it served a Cloudflare bot wall to automated fetch.)
5. **Labeled search link (unverified):** Royal Society of Chemistry Learn Chemistry measurement
   resources — https://www.google.com/search?q=site:edu.rsc.org+measurement+uncertainty+chemistry+classroom
   (not opened; adult previews before assigning). *Candidate support: Lesson 05–06 enrichment.*
