# Resource Pack — Unit 02

**Goal:** support a grade-10 learner through atomic models, isotope notation, electron
configurations, average atomic mass, periodic trends, and qualitative nuclear change — with a
free, no-account route for every core objective. All external links were checked on
**2026-10-21**; the check notes below record exactly what was verified. Videos and simulations
supplement the written lessons; they do not replace them. The adult previews every video and
simulation before assigning it.

## Free, no-account coverage

Every core objective has a free, no-account route: notation and configurations via the lessons
plus PhET; averages via the lessons plus Khan Academy's free videos; trends via the lessons
plus the RSC table; the project via the internal CSV. No objective requires a paid account, a
sign-in, or a video alone — videos supplement, and every video-backed lesson also has a
complete written explanation and a text-only alternative where an image is used.
