# Formative Quiz — Unit 02 (Week 08)

**25 points · ~30 minutes · calculator allowed · periodic table allowed**

This quiz checks Lessons 01–04 only (particles, models, configurations, isotopes). It does not
assess Lessons 05–06 trends. The adult marks it with `answer-key.md` and sets a reteach plan
from the teacher guide before Session 11. Show all working for calculations.

1. **(2 pts)** An atom has 16 protons, 17 neutrons, and 18 electrons. Give its element, mass
   number, charge, and full isotope notation.
2. **(2 pts)** Define "isotope" in one sentence, then state whether ¹²₆C and ¹⁴₇N are isotopes
   of each other, with a reason.
3. **(3 pts)** Boron: boron-10, 10 u, 19.9%; boron-11, 11 u, 80.1%. Calculate boron's average
   atomic mass, showing the fraction conversion and both sanity checks.
4. **(3 pts)** Write the electron configuration of magnesium (Z = 12) and state its valence
   electrons.
5. **(2 pts)** Put these models in historical order: Bohr's model, Dalton's model, the
   quantum-mechanical model, Rutherford's model, Thomson's model.
6. **(3 pts)** In the gold-foil experiment, most alpha particles passed straight through the
   foil, but a very few bounced almost straight back. State the conclusion Rutherford drew
   from *each* part of that pattern (two conclusions).
7. **(2 pts)** Bromine sits in group 17, period 4. State its valence-electron count and the ion
   it most likely forms, each with a one-phrase reason.
8. **(3 pts)** Rank Na, Mg, Al by atomic radius (largest to smallest) and give the reason for
   the ranking.
9. **(3 pts)** Calcium forms Ca²⁺ and chlorine forms Cl⁻. Predict the formula of calcium
   chloride, showing the charge balance.
10. **(2 pts)** The periodic table lists chlorine's atomic mass as 35.45 u. Explain in one or
    two sentences why it is not a whole number.
