# Resource Pack — Unit 04

**Goal:** support a grade-10 learner through reaction types, balancing, the mole, stoichiometry,
limiting reactants, and percent yield — with a free, no-account route for every core objective.
All external links were checked on **2026-11-03**; the check notes below record exactly what
was verified. Videos and simulations supplement the written lessons; they do not replace them.
The adult previews every video and simulation before assigning it.

## Videos and simulations

1. **PhET "Balancing Chemical Equations" (University of Colorado Boulder)** —
   https://phet.colorado.edu/en/simulation/balancing-chemical-equations (page opened and
   checked 2026-11-03; title "Balancing Chemical Equations | Chemical Equations •
   Conservation of Mass | PhET Simulations"; free, no account needed). Balance equations with
   visual atom counts and a bar-graph check. *Supports: Lesson 02; Practice Set B; Session 4.*
2. **PhET "Reactants, Products and Leftovers" (University of Colorado Boulder)** —
   https://phet.colorado.edu/en/simulation/reactants-products-and-leftovers (page opened and
   checked 2026-11-03; title "Reactants, Products and Leftovers | Chemical Reactions •
   Limiting Reactants | PhET Simulations"; free, no account needed). Build sandwiches and
   molecules to see limiting reactants and leftovers directly. *Supports: Lesson 05; project
   planning; Session 9.*
3. **Khan Academy — preferred-channel search link, title unverified:** search Khan Academy for
   "stoichiometry" and "limiting reactant" rather than following a guessed video URL. Khan
   Academy pages returned an anti-bot challenge screen to automated fetches in earlier unit
   runs, so no video title or runtime is claimed here; the adult opens the search result and
   checks fit before assigning. *Supports: Lessons 03–05; Sessions 7, 12, 14.*
4. **OpenStax Chemistry 2e, Chapter 4 summary (Rice University, free, no account)** —
   https://openstax.org/books/chemistry-2e/pages/4-summary (page opened and checked
   2026-11-03; title "Ch. 4 Summary - Chemistry 2e | OpenStax"; sections 4.1 balancing,
   4.3 reaction stoichiometry, 4.4 reaction yields confirmed in the page text). Use as reading
   and reference, not as a replacement for the lessons. *Supports: Lessons 02, 04, 05;
   Session 5.*
5. **Chemistry LibreTexts, "Stoichiometry of Chemical Reactions" chapter (free, no account)** —
   https://chem.libretexts.org/Courses/City_College_of_San_Francisco/Foundations_-_Review_Source_for_Chem_101A/04:_Stoichiometry_of_Chemical_Reactions
   (chapter title and section list verified from a search result 2026-11-03; the adult opens
   the chapter and checks the section before assigning). Sections cover writing and balancing
   equations, reaction stoichiometry, and reaction yields. *Supports: Lessons 02–05;
   Session 10 flex reading.*
