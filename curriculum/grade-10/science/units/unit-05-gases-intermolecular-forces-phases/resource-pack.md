# Resource Pack — Unit 05

**Goal:** support a grade-10 learner through kinetic molecular theory, gas laws, intermolecular
forces, phases, and the HS-PS1-3 bulk-properties investigation — with a free, no-account route
for every core objective. All external links were checked on **2026-11-12**; the check notes
below record exactly what was verified. Videos and simulations supplement the written lessons;
they do not replace them. The adult previews every video and simulation before assigning it.

## Videos and simulations

1. **PhET "Gas Properties" (University of Colorado Boulder)** —
   https://phet.colorado.edu/en/simulation/gas-properties (page opened and checked 2026-11-12;
   title "Gas Properties | PV Work • Ideal Gas Law • Diffusion | PhET Simulations"; free, no
   account needed for the simulation itself). Change pressure, volume, temperature, and particle
   count and watch the gas respond; includes diffusion. *Supports: Lessons 02, 03; Session 5
   and Session 8 investigations.*
2. **PhET "States of Matter" (University of Colorado Boulder)** —
   https://phet.colorado.edu/en/simulation/states-of-matter (confirmed via PhET search results

   Boyle's, Gay-Lussac's, Charles's, and Avogadro's laws; free to watch, no account needed).

## Free, no-account coverage

Every core objective has a free, no-account route: KMT and gas laws via the lessons plus PhET
and Khan Academy's free videos; IMF rankings via the lessons plus the verified NIST-sourced
data table; phases via the lessons plus the heating-curve diagram and PhET; the investigation
via the bench procedure or the PhET simulation version. No objective requires a paid account,
a sign-in, or a video alone — videos supplement, and every video-backed lesson also has a
complete written explanation and a text-only alternative where an image is used.
