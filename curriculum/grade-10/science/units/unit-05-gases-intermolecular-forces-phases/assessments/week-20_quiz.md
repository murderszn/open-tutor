# Formative Quiz — Unit 05, Week 20

**Lessons 01–03 · 25 points · ~25 minutes.** Show the set-up for every calculation: the law,
the substitution with units, and a one-line check. Solutions are in `answer-key.md` (adult
only). No new resource is needed; use your lesson notes.

1. (2 pts) Convert 37 °C to kelvin. Show the conversion.
2. (2 pts) State any one postulate of kinetic molecular theory in your own words, and name one
   observation it explains.
3. (3 pts) A gas at 1.00 atm occupies 5.00 L. At constant temperature it is compressed to
   2.50 L. Find the new pressure (Boyle's law).
4. (3 pts) A gas occupies 2.50 L at 300 K and constant pressure. It is cooled to 150 K. Find
   the new volume (Charles's law).
5. (3 pts) A rigid tank holds gas at 200 kPa and 300 K. It is heated to 450 K. Find the new
   pressure in kPa (Gay-Lussac's law).
6. (3 pts) A gas occupies 3.00 L at 2.00 atm and 300 K. It expands to 6.00 L and the pressure
   falls to 1.00 atm. Find the new temperature (combined gas law).
7. (3 pts) How many moles of gas occupy 6.72 L at STP? (Molar volume at STP: 22.4 L/mol.)
8. (3 pts) What pressure does 1.50 mol of gas exert in a 5.00 L tank at 273 K? Use
   PV = nRT with R = 0.08206 L·atm/(mol·K).
9. (3 pts) At the same temperature, how much faster does methane (CH₄, 16.04 g/mol) diffuse
   than oxygen (O₂, 32.00 g/mol)? Show the Graham's-law set-up.
