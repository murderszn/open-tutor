# Formative Quiz — Unit 03, Week 12 (25 points)

Adult-supervised. No notes for questions 1–6; the electronegativity table from Lesson 01 is
provided for questions 1 and 7. Solutions are in `answer-key.md` (adult only).

1. **(3 pts)** Classify the bond in sodium chloride. EN(Na) = 0.93, EN(Cl) = 3.16. Show the
   ΔEN calculation, name the bond type, and state in one sentence what the electrons do.
2. **(4 pts)** Draw the Lewis structure for water, H₂O, showing all lone pairs. State the
   total valence-electron count and show that your drawing matches it.
3. **(4 pts)** For ammonia, NH₃: count the electron domains on nitrogen, name the shape,
   give the approximate bond angle, and state whether the molecule is polar or nonpolar,
   with the symmetry reason.
4. **(4 pts)** Explain why solid sodium chloride does not conduct electricity but salt water
   does. Your answer must mention what is locked and what is free in each case.
5. **(3 pts)** Rank these from lowest to highest melting point, with one reason for the
   order: sugar (sucrose), sodium chloride, copper. Use Lesson 04's reference data.
6. **(3 pts)** The unit's generated image has three panels: a repeating lattice of alternating
   spheres, separate small clusters of spheres, and a grid of identical spheres with tiny
   dots scattered between them. Match each panel to a bond type (ionic, covalent molecular,
   metallic) and give one structural reason per panel. (Text alternative: see Lesson 01,
   Activity A.)
7. **(2 pts)** A metal wire conducts electricity and bends without breaking. Explain both
   properties with one structural idea.
8. **(2 pts)** Hydrogen fluoride has ΔEN = 1.78, above the 1.7 ionic guide, yet it is
   covalent. Give one reason why the guide fails here.
