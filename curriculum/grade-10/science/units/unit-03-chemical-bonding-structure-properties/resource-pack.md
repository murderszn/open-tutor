# Resource Pack — Unit 03

**Goal:** support a grade-10 learner through bond classification, Lewis structures, VSEPR
shape and polarity, bulk properties, designed materials, and unknown identification — with a
free, no-account route for every core objective. All external links were checked on
**2026-10-27**; the check notes below record exactly what was verified. Videos and
simulations supplement the written lessons; they do not replace them. The adult previews
every video and simulation before assigning it.
