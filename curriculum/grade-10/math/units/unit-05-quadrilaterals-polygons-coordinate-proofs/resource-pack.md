# Resource Pack — Unit 05: Quadrilaterals, Polygons, and Coordinate Proofs

**Goal:** Give a grade-10 learner (and guiding adult) free, no-account
routes to every core objective: polygon angle sums, parallelogram
theorems, trapezoid and kite properties, the quadrilateral hierarchy
and symmetry, coordinate proofs by slope/distance/midpoint, the
section formula, and coordinate perimeter and area. **Age fit:**
high-school geometry (grade 10); the adult previews every link for
advertising, comments, and suitability before the learner opens it.

## Focused queries

1. `polygon interior angle sum (n-2) 180 proof triangulation regular polygon exterior 360`
2. `parallelogram theorems proof opposite sides angles diagonals bisect rectangle rhombus`
3. `trapezoid isosceles trapezoid kite properties midsegment diagonals area`
4. `classify quadrilateral coordinate plane slope distance midpoint diagonal bisect`
5. `section formula point dividing segment ratio internal division coordinates`
6. `shoelace formula area polygon coordinates proof`

## Session 13 reading guidance

The learner reads (adult-selected, previewed) the LibreTexts 5.19
vocabulary table and the Math is Fun quadrilaterals page, then writes
a short response answering: (a) how does each source define
"trapezoid," and do they agree? (b) under the inclusive definition,
is a parallelogram a trapezoid — and does that change any theorem in
this unit? (c) why is it useful for a course to state its definition
up front, as this unit does in its README? No copyrighted text is
reproduced in this unit; the adult locates and previews the reading.
