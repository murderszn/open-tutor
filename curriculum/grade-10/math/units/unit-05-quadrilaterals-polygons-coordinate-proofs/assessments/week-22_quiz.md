# Week-22 Formative Quiz — Unit 05

**Grade 10 · Math · Unit 05 · Session 18 · 30 points · ~30 minutes**

Show your work. Exact values unless a decimal is asked for. All
solutions are in `answer-key.md` (adults only).

1. (3 points) Find the interior-angle sum of a regular octagon.
2. (3 points) A regular polygon has each exterior angle 40°. How
   many sides does it have, and what is each interior angle?
3. (3 points) In parallelogram ABCD, ∠A = 68°. Find ∠B, ∠C, and
   ∠D.
4. (3 points) The diagonals of parallelogram ABCD meet at E.
   AE = 8 and DE = 5. Find AC and BD.
5. (3 points) An isosceles trapezoid has bases 9 and 15 and legs
   of length 5. Find its height and its midsegment.
6. (3 points) A kite has diagonals 12 and 16. Find its area.
7. (3 points) Classify the quadrilateral A(0, 0), B(5, 0), C(6, 3),
   D(1, 3). Give the slope evidence.
8. (3 points) Find the point dividing the segment from (4, 2) to
   (10, 8) in the ratio 1:2.
9. (3 points) Find the area of the triangle (0, 0), (5, 0), (0, 2)
   by the shoelace formula.
10. (3 points) True or false, with a one-sentence reason: "A square
    is a rectangle."
