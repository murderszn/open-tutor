# Formative Quiz — Unit 01 (given course Week 6, Session 18)

**Time:** ~25 minutes · **Total:** 26 points · Covers Lessons 01–03
(definitions, constructions, precision). This is a *formative* check: the
adult scores it the same day and records a reteach plan for any item
below proficiency before the unit assessment (Session 19). Solutions are
in `answer-key.md` — adults only.

**Instructions to the learner:** Classify before you compute. For
performance items (4–6), label every point and keep construction arcs
visible — the process is part of the answer.

1. (2 pts) Define *line segment* using only the undefined notions
   (point, line, distance). One or two sentences.
2. (2 pts) Points *R*, *S*, *T* are collinear with *S* between *R* and
   *T*. Name the ray with endpoint *S* that passes through *T*. Then
   explain why "ray *TS*" names a different ray.
3. (3 pts) A diagram shows two lines with arrowhead markings (parallel),
   cut by a transversal. Angle 2 (upper-right at the top intersection)
   and angle 6 (upper-right at the bottom intersection) each carry one
   arc mark. (a) What two facts are you entitled to use in a proof?
   (b) Name one thing the diagram *suggests* but you may not use.
4. (3 pts) **Performance.** Copy the segment the adult draws (~5 cm)
   onto your working line. Measure the original and the copy to the
   nearest millimeter; report both numbers and the difference.
5. (3 pts) **Performance.** Copy the angle the adult draws (~60°).
   Label every arc-crossing point with a letter. Measure the copy with a
   protractor to the nearest degree and report both measures.
6. (4 pts) **Performance.** Construct the perpendicular bisector of the
   segment the adult draws (7 cm). (a) Label the two arc-crossing points
   *P* and *Q*. (b) Verify by measuring both halves to the nearest
   millimeter and checking the crossing angle with a protractor; report
   all three numbers.
7. (3 pts) In two or three sentences, explain *why* the perpendicular
   bisector construction works, citing the equidistance theorem and the
   "two points determine a line" postulate.
8. (2 pts) Your millimeter ruler measures a constructed segment as
   63 mm. Which of these reports is honest — 6 cm, 6.3 cm, or 6.30 cm?
   Explain what the dishonest one(s) falsely claim.
9. (2 pts) **Error analysis.** A learner's copied angle came out too
   large. Name the single most likely step where the error entered and
   describe the check that would catch it.
10. (2 pts) In your own words: what is the difference between a
    *postulate* and a *theorem*? Give one example of each from this unit.

**Reteach guide (adult):** Items 1–3 weak → revisit Lesson 01 notation
and diagram-reading (Practice Set A). Items 4–5 weak → rebuild the
transfer habit: "set — carry — mark — check" (Lesson 02, Practice Set
B). Items 6–7 weak → re-teach the equidistance engine with the
fold-check (Lesson 03). Item 8 weak → N-Q.3 mini-lesson: tool precision
vs. reported digits. Items 9–10 weak → Lesson 04's allowed-reasons list
as preview. Record the plan in the learner's notebook before Session 19.
