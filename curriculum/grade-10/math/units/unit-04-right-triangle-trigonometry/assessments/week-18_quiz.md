# Week-18 Formative Quiz — Unit 04

**Unit 04 · Week 18, Session 18 · ~50 minutes · 30 points (3 points each)**

**Instructions:** Show a labeled diagram and the sentence "I know
___, so I use ___" for every problem. Calculator in degree mode.
Round only at the end. Solutions are in `../answer-key.md` (adults
only). This quiz is formative: scoring below 20 postpones the unit
assessment until the reteach in the teacher guide is done.

1. A right triangle has legs 6 and 8 and hypotenuse 10. For the
   acute angle opposite the leg of length 8, give sin, cos, and tan.
2. Give sin 60°, cos 30°, and tan 45° exactly, with no calculator.
3. An observer 15 m from a monument measures an angle of elevation
   of 63° to its top from eye level. How far above eye level is the
   top?
4. From a point 70 m above the ground, the angle of depression to an
   object is 40°. How far is the object, horizontally, from the
   point directly below the observer?
5. A right triangle has hypotenuse 20 and one acute angle 35°. Find
   both legs.
6. Triangle *ABC*: *A* = 38°, *B* = 72°, *c* = 18. Find *C* and
   sides *a* and *b*.
7. Triangle *ABC*: sides *b* = 8, *c* = 12, included angle
   *A* = 70°. Find side *a*.
8. Find the area of a triangle with sides 10 and 14 and included
   angle 50°.
9. SSA: *a* = 9, *b* = 15, *A* = 30°. How many triangles are there?
   Justify with the supplement test.
10. Error analysis: a learner writes, "Right triangle, adjacent side
    12, angle 25°. Opposite side = 12 × sin 25° ≈ 5.07." Identify
    the error, explain it, and give the correct computation and
    answer.
