## Lesson 01

**Guided practice.**
1. θ = ∠*B*: opposite side *AC* = 5, adjacent side *BC* = 12,
   hypotenuse *AB* = 13. sin θ = 5/13, cos θ = 12/13,
   tan θ = 5/12.
2. The hypotenuse is the longest side, so opposite ÷ hypotenuse < 1
   for any acute angle. 65° = 90° − 25°, so sin 65° = cos 25° by the
   cofunction relationship (the side opposite 65° is adjacent to
   25°).
3. Each leg = sin 45° × 8√2 = (√2/2) × 8√2 = 8. Both legs are 8.

**Independent practice.**
4. sin = 9/15 = 3/5, cos = 12/15 = 4/5, tan = 9/12 = 3/4.
5. Hypotenuse = 6 ÷ cos 60° = 6 ÷ (1/2) = 12. Opposite side =
   6 × tan 60° = 6√3 ≈ 10.39. (Check: 6² + (6√3)² = 36 + 108 =
   144 = 12². ✓)
6. In a 45–45–90 triangle the legs are equal, so
   tan 45° = opposite ÷ adjacent = 1 ÷ 1 = 1. Accept any answer
   that cites leg equality, not a calculator display.

**Applied task.**
7. Rise = 6 × sin 30° = 6 × (1/2) = 3 m exactly (3.0 m).

**Exit check.**
8. sin 30° = 1/2, cos 60° = 1/2, tan 45° = 1. sin 30° = cos 60°
   because 30° and 60° are complementary (the side opposite 30°
   is adjacent to 60°).

## Lesson 03

**Independent practice.**

## Lesson 05

**Guided practice.**
1. 0.6² + 0.9² = 0.36 + 0.81 = 1.17 ≠ 1, violating
   sin²θ + cos²θ = 1. Most likely cause: a side misnamed
   (sin/cos swapped with another angle) or an arithmetic error.
2. tan θ = (opposite ÷ hypotenuse) ÷ (adjacent ÷ hypotenuse) =
   opposite ÷ adjacent. With sin θ = 3/5, cos θ = 4/5:
   tan θ = (3/5) ÷ (4/5) = 3/4.
3. sin 38° must exceed sin 30° = 0.5 (sine increases across acute
   angles), and 0.296 does not; the radian-mode value is the sine
   of 38 radians, not 38°. Correct: sin 38° ≈ 0.616 (check:
   cos 52° ≈ 0.616 ✓).

**Independent practice.**
4. The values are right but the labels are swapped: adjacent =
   10 × cos 60° = 5, opposite = 10 × sin 60° ≈ 8.66. The learner
   wrote sin where cos belongs and vice versa.
5. Missing: the supplement test. Second candidate *B* ≈ 180° −
   53.1° = 126.9°. It survives only if *A* + 126.9° < 180° — the
   learner stopped before checking, so the solution is incomplete
   as written (with the lesson's data, *A* is needed to decide).
6. Accept any four lines matching the chain: U01 definitions and
   proof; U02 rigid-motion congruence; U03 similarity, AA, and
   Pythagoras; U04 ratios, laws, and area from similarity.

**Applied task.**
7. tan 30° = *h*/(*d* + 80), tan 45° = *h*/*d*. Since tan 45° = 1,
   *h* = *d*; then *h* = (*h* + 80) × tan 30°, so
   *h* ≈ 109.3 m above eye level. One observation gives one
   equation in the two unknowns *h* and *d* and cannot fix both.

**Exit check.**
8. (1) Wrong side named → label H/O/A relative to θ first.
   (2) Radian mode → check DEG; acute sines lie in (0, 1).
   (3) Missing eye height → end with "plus eye height."
   (4) Untested supplement → supplement test as a written step.
   (5) Middle rounding → carry full precision, round once at the
   end.
