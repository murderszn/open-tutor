# Resource Pack — Unit 04: Right-Triangle Trigonometry and Applications

**Goal:** Give a grade-10 learner (and guiding adult) free, no-account
routes to every core objective: trig ratios defined from similarity,
special-triangle exact values, solving right triangles, angles of
elevation and depression, the laws of sines and cosines, the area
formula, and the ambiguous case. **Age fit:** high-school geometry
(grade 10); the adult previews every link for advertising, comments,
and suitability before the learner opens it.

## Session 13 reading guidance

The learner reads (adult-selected, previewed) one account of how
surveyors measure what they cannot reach — from the CK-12 or Khan
Academy material above, or a library text on surveying — and writes
a short response answering: (a) what triangle did the surveyor
actually construct? (b) which parts were measured and which were
computed? (c) why would a second observation make the result more
trustworthy? No copyrighted text is reproduced in this unit; the
adult locates and previews the reading.
