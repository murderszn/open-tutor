# Practice — Unit 04: Right-Triangle Trigonometry and Applications

**Instructions:** Work with a labeled diagram for every problem:
name the angle θ, mark hypotenuse (H), opposite (O), and adjacent
(A) relative to θ, and write the sentence "I know ___, so I use ___"
before computing. Check degree mode before the first calculator
problem. Round only at the end, to 2–3 significant figures unless a
problem asks for an exact value. Solutions are in `answer-key.md`
(adults only).

## Set A — Ratios, cofunctions, special triangles (Lesson 01; S2)

1. A right triangle has legs 5 and 12 and hypotenuse 13. For the
   acute angle opposite the leg of length 5, give sin, cos, and tan
   as fractions.
2. A similar right triangle has hypotenuse 26 and the same acute
   angles as problem 1. Find its legs and show its ratios match
   problem 1's.
3. A right triangle has legs 7 and 24 and hypotenuse 25. For the
   angle opposite the leg of length 7, give sin and cos, and verify
   sin² + cos² = 1 by arithmetic.
4. A right isosceles triangle has hypotenuse 9. Find both legs
   exactly, then as decimals.
5. A right triangle has a 60° angle and hypotenuse 12. Find the
   other two sides exactly.
6. If sin θ = 8/17 and cos θ = 15/17, find tan θ without a
   calculator, and explain the step.
7. If sin 20° ≈ 0.342, what is cos 70°? Explain in one sentence.
8. Error analysis: a learner writes "sin θ = adjacent ÷ hypotenuse"
   for a right triangle. Correct the statement and explain why the
   learner's version would give cos θ's value instead.

## Set B — Solving right triangles (Lesson 02; S4)

1. An observer 12 m from a flagpole measures an angle of elevation
   of 52° to its top from eye level 1.5 m above the ground. How
   tall is the flagpole?
2. From a point 60 m above the ground, the angle of depression to
   an object is 32°. How far is the object from the point directly
   below the observer, horizontally?
3. A ladder 7 m long leans against a vertical wall at 72° to the
   ground. How high up the wall does it reach, and how far is its
   foot from the wall?
4. A right triangle has opposite side 7 and adjacent side 24 for
   one acute angle. Find that angle to the nearest tenth of a
   degree, then find the hypotenuse two ways.
5. A right triangle has the side adjacent to a 30° angle equal to
   9. Find the hypotenuse exactly and as a decimal.
6. A kite string is 60 m long, held 1.5 m above the ground, making
   a 50° angle with the horizontal. How high is the kite?
7. A right triangle has hypotenuse 13 and one leg 5. Find the other
   leg and the acute angle opposite the leg of length 5.
8. A ramp's sloped surface is 6 m long and it rises 0.9 m. Find the
   ramp's angle to the horizontal to the nearest tenth of a degree.

## Set C — Laws of sines and cosines (Lesson 03; S7)

1. Triangle *ABC*: *A* = 42°, *B* = 71°, *c* = 15. Find *C* and
   sides *a* and *b*.
2. Triangle *ABC*: *b* = 6, *c* = 8, included angle *A* = 60°.
   Find side *a* and the triangle's area.
3. SSA: *a* = 15, *b* = 9, *A* = 40°. How many triangles are there?
   Give the angles and the third side of each.
4. A triangle has sides 7, 8, 9. Find its largest angle to the
   nearest tenth of a degree.
5. Find the area of a triangle with sides 12 and 16 and included
   angle 40°.
6. SSA: *a* = 6, *b* = 14, *A* = 50°. Show that no such triangle
   exists, and explain what the computation is telling you.
7. Triangle *ABC*: *A* = 30°, *B* = 45°, *a* = 10. Find *b* and *c*.
8. Explain in two or three sentences why the law of sines cannot be
   the first tool for an SAS triangle, and which law you would use
   instead.

## Set D — Choosing the tool, surveying, forces (Lesson 04; S9)

1. Two survey stations are 90 m apart. The angles to a distant point
   from the stations are 38° and 55°. Find the point's distance from
   the second station.
2. Two forces of 20 N and 30 N act at right angles to each other.
   Find the resultant's magnitude.
3. A triangular plot has sides 14 m and 18 m with included angle
   62°. Find its area and its third side.
4. From a lighthouse 45 m above sea level, the angle of depression
   to a boat is 28°. How far is the boat from the base of the
   lighthouse, horizontally?
5. Explain why sides 3, 7, and 10 cannot form a genuine (non-degenerate)
   triangle — check the triangle inequality — and compute the
   cosine of the angle opposite the side of length 10 to show what
   happens in the degenerate limit.
6. A triangular field has sides 70 m and 90 m with included angle
   48°. Find its area.
7. A triangle has sides 5, 12, 13. Find the angle opposite the side
   of length 5 and the triangle's area — and say which theorem let
   you skip the laws entirely.
8. A learner measures a baseline to 2 significant figures and
   reports a tree height of 23.397 m. Explain what is wrong with
   the report and give the correctly rounded form (23 m).

## Set E — Synthesis and error analysis (Lesson 05; S12)

1. A learner computes sin θ = 0.5 and cos θ = 0.8 for the same
   angle. Use the Pythagorean identity to show a mistake was made.
2. Find and repair the error: "Right triangle, hypotenuse 14,
   angle 30°. Opposite side = 14 × cos 30° ≈ 12.12."
3. A calculator in radian mode reports sin 50 ≈ −0.262. Explain why
   that cannot be sin 50° for an acute angle, and give the correct
   value to 3 decimal places.
4. SSA with *A* = 20° and sin *B* = 0.5: list both candidate values
   of *B*, test each against the 180° sum, and give the full angle
   set of every surviving triangle.
5. An observer 28 m from a tree measures an angle of elevation of
   39° from eye level 1.7 m. How tall is the tree?
6. If sin 72° ≈ 0.951, what is cos 18°? Name the relationship used.
7. Two-observation problem: a treetop is sighted at 35° from point
   *P* and at 50° from point *Q*, where *Q* is 60 m closer to the
   tree than *P* (same eye height, flat ground). Set up the two
   tangent equations and solve for the height above eye level.
8. In two or three sentences, explain which Unit 03 result justifies
   defining sine, cosine, and tangent at all — and what would fail
   if that result were false.
