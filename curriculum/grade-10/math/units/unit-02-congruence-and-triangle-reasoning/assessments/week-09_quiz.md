# Week-09 Formative Quiz — Unit 02

**Unit 02 · Week 10, Session 18 · ~30 minutes · 30 points**

Full worked solutions are in `../answer-key.md` (adults only). This quiz
is formative: it locates gaps before the unit assessment. The adult
scores it the same day and reteaches missed objectives in the remainder
of S18.

**Readiness bands:** 25–30 = on track for the assessment; 20–24 =
review the missed lessons' practice sets tonight; 15–19 = reteach the
missed objectives before S19; below 15 = postpone the assessment and
re-teach the unit's core (Lessons 01–04) first.

---

1. (3 pts) Define *rigid motion* in your own words. Is a dilation with
   scale factor 1.5 a rigid motion? Why or why not?

2. (3 pts) Translate point *P*(2, 5) by the vector "4 left, 3 up",
   then reflect the result across the *x*-axis. Give the final
   coordinates, showing each step.

3. (3 pts) A figure is reflected across each of two parallel lines
   5 cm apart. Name the resulting single motion, how far it moves
   every point, and in what direction relative to the mirror lines.

4. (4 pts) △*ABC* has *AB* = 4, *BC* = 5, *CA* = 6. △*XYZ* has
   *XY* = 6, *YZ* = 4, *ZX* = 5. (a) Are the triangles congruent?
   By which criterion? (b) Write the congruence statement with
   correct correspondence order.

5. (3 pts) ∠*M* = ∠*R*, *MN* = *RS*, ∠*N* = ∠*S*. Which triangle
   congruence criterion applies? Is the known side included?

6. (3 pts) State the perpendicular-bisector theorem — *both*
   directions — in your own words.

7. (3 pts) A triangle's angles measure 45°, 2*x*°, (*x* + 15)°.
   Find *x* and each angle measure. Check your sum.

8. (3 pts) △*ABC* is isosceles with legs *AB* = *AC* and vertex angle
   ∠*A* = 30°. Find the base angles. Name the theorem you used.

9. (3 pts) Surveying data: ∠*A* = 25°, *AB* = 10 m, *BC* = 7 m, where
   ∠*A* is not between the known sides. The perpendicular distance
   from *B* to the 25° ray is 10·sin 25° ≈ 4.23 m. Does this data
   determine △*ABC*? Explain with the arc-and-ray picture.

10. (2 pts) True or false, with a one-sentence reason: "A reflection
    preserves orientation."
