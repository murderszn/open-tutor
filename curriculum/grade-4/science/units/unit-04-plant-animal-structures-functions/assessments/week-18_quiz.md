# Week-18 Formative Quiz

**Adult administers in Session 14.** 10 items covering all five unit
objectives. Oral, drawn, or manipulative answers are acceptable; the
adult records them. Keys in [answer-key.md](../answer-key.md) (adult
only).
**This is a practice check, not a standardized or validated test.**

1. Name the four plant parts from Lesson 01 and give one job for each.
   *(Objective 1)*
2. Look at the sunflower picture in
   [assets/sunflower-structures.png](../assets/sunflower-structures.png).
   Which part is below the soil, and what are its two jobs?
   *(Objective 1)*
3. A duck has webbed feet and a flat beak; a turtle has a shell and
   legs. For each animal, name one structure and its job.
   *(Objective 2)*
4. Feathers cover a bird. Name two jobs feathers do.
   *(Objective 2)*
5. Name the five internal structures from Lesson 03 (heart, stomach,
   lungs, brain, skin) and give one job for each. *(Objective 3)*
6. A dog runs, then rests. What happens to its heart rate and breathing
   in each stage, and why do they change together? *(Objective 3)*
7. Sort into Claim or Evidence: (a) "Thorns protect plants." (b) "A
   goat avoided thorny stems twice." *(Objective 4)*
8. A learner argues: "Feathers help birds survive because feathers are
   pretty." What is wrong with this argument? Give one observation
   that would fix it. *(Objective 4)*
9. Compare a sunflower and a rabbit: name one structure from each, its
   job, and whether it supports survival, growth, behavior, or
   reproduction. *(Objective 4)*
10. Your journal claims: "Roots make food for the plant." Is this
    correct? If not, correct it and name the structure that actually
    helps make food. *(Objective 5)*

**Scoring guide:** 9–10 = strong, ready for the unit assessment; 7–8 =
review the missed objectives with the matching practice set, then
proceed; ≤6 = reteach the missed lessons before Session 16 (see
teacher-guide).
