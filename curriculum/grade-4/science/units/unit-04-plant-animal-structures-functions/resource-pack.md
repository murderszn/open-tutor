# Resource Pack — Unit 04: Plant and Animal Structures and Functions

**Goal (one sentence):** Give the guiding adult short, grade-4-appropriate
videos, references, and search routes that reinforce plant and animal
structures, their functions, systems working together, and arguments
with evidence.
**Age fit:** grade 4 (ages 9–10); the adult previews every video and
co-views with the learner.

## Focused queries

1. `plant parts functions roots stem leaves flower grade 4 kids`
2. `animal structures functions skin fur feathers scales beak claws elementary`
3. `heart stomach lungs brain body systems kids how body works`
4. `NGSS 4-LS1-1 structures functions survival growth behavior reproduction`
5. `claim evidence reasoning elementary science argument kids`

## Videos (labeled search links — adult previews and picks)

1. **SciShow Kids plant parts — preferred-channel search** (search link):
   https://www.youtube.com/results?search_query=SciShow+Kids+plant+parts+roots+stem+leaves+flower
   *Why good:* the channel structures episodes around NGSS for
   elementary grades; the adult picks one short episode on plant parts
   and their jobs. *Labeled search — adult previews and picks; free, no
   account.*
2. **Crash Course Kids animal structures — preferred-channel search**
   (search link):
   https://www.youtube.com/results?search_query=Crash+Course+Kids+animal+structures+adaptations+elementary
   *Why good:* the series' upper-elementary episodes match this unit's
   structure-function language; the adult picks one episode on animal
   structures and skips any content beyond the macroscopic boundary.
   *Labeled search — adult previews; free, no account.*
3. **Khan Academy human body systems — preferred-channel search**
   (search link):
   https://www.youtube.com/results?search_query=Khan+Academy+human+body+systems+heart+lungs+stomach+kids
   *Why good:* Khan Academy's body-systems material states the same
   five jobs this unit teaches (heart pumps, stomach breaks down food,
   lungs breathe, brain controls, skin protects); the adult uses only
   the qualitative, macroscopic segments and skips all cellular or
   chemical detail. *Labeled search — adult previews and selects only
   grade-4-appropriate segments; free, no account.*
4. **Parts of a plant song — preferred-channel search** (search link):
   https://www.youtube.com/results?search_query=parts+of+a+plant+song+kids+roots+stem+leaves+flower
   *Why good:* a short song is a memory aid for Lesson 01's four parts
   and jobs; the adult checks that the song's jobs match the unit
   (roots take in water, stem carries, leaves catch sunlight, flowers
   make seeds) before playing it. *Labeled search — adult previews;
   free, no account.*

## Web references

1. **NGSS 4-LS1-1 text (reproduction of the standard)** —
   https://thenaturecollective.org/wp-content/uploads/2021/12/4thDiscoveringAdaptationsNGSS.pdf
   *Why good:* reproduces the authoritative performance-expectation
   wording for 4-LS1-1, its clarification examples (thorns, stems,
   roots, colored petals, heart, stomach, lung, brain, skin), and its
   assessment boundary (macroscopic structures only) — the wording,
   examples, and boundary used in this unit's standards notes. Adult
   background and verification source. *Wording verified 2026-10-30
   via the document text; free, no account.*
2. **SDSU Extension — "Plant Part Powers!"** —
   https://extension.sdstate.edu/plant-part-powers
   *Why good:* a university extension lesson giving the same
   plant-part vocabulary and jobs this unit teaches (roots take in
   water and hold steady; stem holds up and carries; leaves collect
   sunlight and make food; flowers attract pollinators and make
   seeds), plus a matching worksheet and a plant-parts craft activity
   the adult can adapt. *Opened and read 2026-10-30; free, no account.*
3. **PNC Grow Up Great — "Parts of a Plant" lesson** —
   https://www.pnc.com/en/about-pnc/corporate-responsibility/grow-up-great/lesson-center/spring/parts-of-a-plant.html?lnksrc=relres
   *Why good:* an early-elementary lesson stating each part's job in
   plain language (roots absorb water and nutrients and hold the
   plant; stem gives strength and delivers water and nutrients;
   leaves collect sunlight to make food; flowers make seeds), matching
   Lesson 01 exactly. *Checked 2026-10-30 via search-result
   description; adult previews before use; free, no account.*
4. **NASA Space Place** — https://spaceplace.nasa.gov/
   *Why good:* NASA's kids' site from the curriculum resource map;
   free, no account, elementary reading level. The adult previews and
   selects one plant- or animal-related activity as enrichment.
   *Starting point from the resource map; the specific activity is
   chosen and checked by the adult — not pre-verified here.*
