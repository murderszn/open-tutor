# Week-22 Formative Quiz

**Adult administers in Session 14.** 10 items covering all five unit
objectives. Oral, drawn, or manipulative answers are acceptable; the
adult records them. Keys in [answer-key.md](../answer-key.md) (adult only).
**This is a practice check, not a standardized or validated test.**

1. Name the five senses and give one kind of information for each.
   *(Objective 1)*
2. Look at the deer picture in
   [assets/deer-freezes-at-twig-snap.png](../assets/deer-freezes-at-twig-snap.png).
   Which sense picked up the twig snapping, and what response is the
   deer making? *(Objectives 1, 4)*
3. Your eyes see a red bird, but they cannot tell you its song. Your
   ears hear the song, but they cannot tell you the bird is red.
   What does this show about the senses? Answer in one sentence.
   *(Objective 2)*
4. A learner tests smell with eyes open and gets all six scents right.
   Is that a fair test of smell? Explain why or why not.
   *(Objective 2)*
5. Put these steps in the right order: "the body responds," "a sense
   picks up information," "the brain works out what it means."
   *(Objective 3)*
6. You smell dinner cooking and walk to the kitchen. Name the sense,
   the kind of information, what the brain works out, and the
   response. *(Objective 3)*
7. A deer and a rabbit both hear a loud sound. The deer freezes; the
   rabbit flees. Name each animal's response, and say in one sentence
   why both can be good answers. *(Objective 4)*
8. Read the fictional response tally (made-up practice data from a
   5-minute meadow video): deer freeze 3, rabbit flee 2, squirrel
   investigate 2, bird call out 1. (a) How many responses were
   counted in all? (b) Which response happened most often?
   *(Objective 4)*
9. A model says: "Rabbit sees a fox → rabbit runs." What step is
   missing? Add it in one sentence. *(Objective 5)*
10. Name the four things a complete sense-to-response model must show.
    *(Objective 5)*

**Scoring guide:** 9–10 = strong, ready for the unit assessment; 7–8 =
review the missed objectives with the matching practice set, then
proceed; ≤6 = reteach the missed lessons before Session 16 (see
teacher-guide).
