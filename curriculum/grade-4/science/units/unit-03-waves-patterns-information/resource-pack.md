# Resource Pack — Unit 03: Waves, Patterns, and Information

**Goal (one sentence):** Give the guiding adult short, grade-4-appropriate
videos, references, and search routes that reinforce wave models
(amplitude, wavelength), sound vibrations, light reflection and seeing,
and pattern codes for sending information.
**Age fit:** grade 4 (ages 9–10); the adult previews every video and
co-views with the learner.

## Videos (verified or labeled search links)

1. **"What Is Sound? for Kids"** — Learn Bright —
   https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=D5ejEJNFjo4
   *Why good:* its own description explains sound as vibrations —
   small, fast back-and-forth movements — and covers pitch (faster
   vibrations = higher) and volume (intensity = loudness), the exact
   Lesson 02 language. Made for kids with a downloadable lesson plan
   on the publisher's site. *Checked 2026-10-24 via search-result
   description; adult previews before showing; free, no account.*
2. **SciShow Kids sound waves — preferred-channel search** (search link):
   https://www.youtube.com/results?search_query=SciShow+Kids+sound+waves+vibrations
   *Why good:* the channel structures episodes around NGSS for
   elementary grades; the adult picks one short episode on sound or
   vibrations. *Labeled search — adult previews and picks; free, no
   account.*
3. **Crash Course Kids waves and light — preferred-channel search**
   (search link):
   https://www.youtube.com/results?search_query=Crash+Course+Kids+waves+light+sound
   *Why good:* the series' upper-elementary episodes match this
   unit's fair-test and model language (the same series Unit 01 uses
   for fair tests); the adult picks one episode on waves or light.
   *Labeled search — adult previews; free, no account.*
4. **Khan Academy sound and light — preferred-channel search** (search
   link):
   https://www.youtube.com/results?search_query=Khan+Academy+sound+waves+light+reflection+kids
   *Why good:* Khan Academy's wave material distinguishes transverse
   and compression waves for older learners — the adult uses only
   the qualitative parts (waves carry energy; sound is a compression
   pattern) and skips all quantitative treatment, which is outside
   the grade-4 boundary. *Labeled search — adult previews and selects
   only grade-4-appropriate segments; free, no account.*
5. **Morse code for kids — preferred-channel search** (search link):
   https://www.youtube.com/results?search_query=Morse+code+for+kids+dots+dashes+messages
   *Why good:* matches Lesson 04's tap code directly; the adult picks
   a short, slow demonstration and pairs it with the unit's own code
   card rather than asking learners to memorize an alphabet.
   *Labeled search — adult previews; free, no account.*

## Web references

1. **NSTA — NGSS topic page for 4-PS4 (Waves and Their Applications
   in Technologies for Information Transfer)** —
   https://my.nsta.org/ngss/DisplayStandard.aspx?view=topic&id=16
   *Why good:* the authoritative performance-expectation wording for
   4-PS4-1, 4-PS4-2, and 4-PS4-3, including the clarification examples
   (drums, 1/0 picture grids, Morse code) and the 4-PS4-1 assessment
   boundary used in this unit's standards notes. Adult background
   and verification source. *Checked 2026-10-24; free, no account.*
2. **Utah SEEd — Grade 4 lesson plans, Strand 4.3: Wave Patterns** —
   https://www.uen.org/core/displayLessonPlans.do?courseNumber=3041&standardId=98649&objectiveId=98661
   *Why good:* a state education partnership's grade-4 wave-pattern
   strand page stating the unit's core ideas in plain language
   (amplitude as wave height, wavelength as peak spacing; light
   reflected into the eye; patterns transferring information) and
   giving the design-a-solution standard this unit's project mirrors.
   *Checked 2026-10-24; free, no account.*
3. **NASA Spotlite — Interactions of Light, teacher packet (PDF)** —
   https://assets.science.nasa.gov/content/dam/science/cds/eclips/assets/documents/nasa-spotlite-interactive-lesson-interactions-of-light-teacher.pdf
   *Why good:* NASA's teacher-facing light-interactions activities
   model light as arrows traveling in straight lines and show
   reflection/refraction investigations the adult can adapt — the
   same arrow-model language as Lesson 03. Adult background and
   activity source, not assigned to the learner. *Checked 2026-10-24;
   free, no account.*
4. **Kesler Science — 4-PS4-2 lesson page: Reflecting Light and Vision** —
   https://keslerscience.com/ngss-4-ps4-2-reflecting-light-vision
   *Why good:* teacher-facing page restating the 4-PS4-2 chain
   (source → object → eye; remove any link and seeing stops) and its
   assessment boundary (no specific colors reflected, no eye-cell or
   retina mechanisms) — the boundary this unit keeps. Adult
   background only. *Checked 2026-10-24; free, no account.*
5. **NASA Space Place** — https://spaceplace.nasa.gov/
   *Why good:* NASA's kids' site from the curriculum resource map;
   free, no account, elementary reading level. The adult previews and
   selects one light- or waves-related activity as enrichment.
   *Starting point from the resource map; the specific activity is
   chosen and checked by the adult — not pre-verified here.*
