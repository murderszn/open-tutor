# Week-06 Formative Quiz

**Adult administers in Session 15.** 10 items covering all five unit
objectives. Oral, drawn, or manipulative answers are acceptable; the adult
records them. Keys in [answer-key.md](../answer-key.md) (adult only).
**This is a practice check, not a standardized or validated test.**

1. Is this testable? "Which lunchbox keeps food coldest?" Name the
   measurement. *(Objective 1)*
2. A book measures 22 cm. How many millimeters is that? *(Objective 2)*
3. Convert: 5 L = ___ mL. *(Objective 2)*
4. "Does the spoon size change how much water it holds? I test a big spoon
   and a small spoon, pouring the same water, 3 tries each." Fair or
   unfair? *(Objective 3)*
5. Look at the [measurement picture](../lessons/lesson-02-measuring-and-converting.md):
   what is the one rule the wrong station breaks first? *(Objective 2)*
   **Text-only alternative:** The left station shows a ruler laid flat
   alongside a yellow pencil with the ruler's zero end exactly at the
   pencil's tip. The right station shows a ruler tilted at an angle next to
   an identical yellow pencil, with its zero end lifted away from the
   pencil's tip instead of aligned with it.
6. A test changed the ramp height AND pushed the car. Can it answer "does
   ramp height matter?" Why or why not? *(Objective 3)*
7. Practice data (fictional): two plants measured 3 times — Plant X: 12,
   12, 13 cm; Plant Y: 18, 17, 18 cm. Which plant is taller, and by about
   how much? *(Objective 4)*
8. On a line plot, the X's for one brand's trials sit far apart (20, 35,
   50 mL). What does that tell you? *(Objective 4)*
9. Name the six parts of the investigation report. *(Objective 5)*
10. Write one criterion and one constraint for this design problem: "a bag
    that carries library books without tearing." *(Objective 5)*

**Scoring guide:** 9–10 = strong, ready for the unit assessment; 7–8 =
review the missed objectives with the matching practice set, then proceed;
≤6 = reteach the missed lessons before Session 16 (see teacher-guide).
