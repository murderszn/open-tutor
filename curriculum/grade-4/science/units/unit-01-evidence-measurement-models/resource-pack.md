# Resource Pack — Unit 01: Evidence, Measurement, and Scientific Models

**Goal (one sentence):** Give the guiding adult short, grade-4-appropriate
videos, references, and search routes that reinforce testable questions,
standard measurement and metric conversion, fair-test structure, data
displays, and the criteria-and-constraints design language.
**Age fit:** grade 4 (ages 9–10); the adult previews every video and
co-views with the learner.

## Focused queries

1. `"fair test" kids experiment change one thing keep the same trials`

## Web references

1. **Science Learning Hub — "Fair testing"** —
   https://www.sciencelearn.org.nz/videos/2066-fair-testing
   *Why good:* short explainer defining fair testing (one variable
   changed, rest kept the same), with linked classroom activities — and it
   uses the paper-towel absorbency question as its example, the same
   investigation as this unit's project. From New Zealand's national
   science-education hub. *Checked 2026-10-11; free, no account.*
2. **Khan Academy — "Metric units of length review (mm, cm, m, & km)"
   (article)** —
   https://www.khanacademy.org/math/grade-7-math-cambridge/xffb7c9f797a6b534:area-perimeter/xffb7c9f797a6b534:unit-conversion/a/metric-units-of-length-review
   *Why good:* a clean reference table of the conversion facts
   (10 mm = 1 cm, 100 cm = 1 m, 1,000 m = 1 km) with worked larger→smaller
   and smaller→larger examples — the adult's answer-check for Lesson 02
   conversions. *Checked 2026-10-11; free, no account.*
3. **NASA Space Place** — https://spaceplace.nasa.gov/
   *Why good:* NASA's kids' site from the curriculum resource map; free,
   no account, elementary reading level. The adult previews and selects
   one measurement or data activity as enrichment. *Starting point from the
   resource map; the specific activity is chosen and checked by the adult
   — not pre-verified here.*
4. **Fizzics Education — "Variables & fair testing"** —
   https://www.fizzicseducation.com.au/articles/variables-teaching-the-heart-of-science-experiments/
   *Why good:* teacher-facing walkthrough of variable testing and controls
   with classroom questioning prompts. Adult background only — not
   assigned to the learner. *Title verified 2026-10-10; free, no account.*
