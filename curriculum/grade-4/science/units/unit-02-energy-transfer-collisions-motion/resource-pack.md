# Resource Pack — Unit 02: Energy Transfer, Collisions, and Motion

**Goal:** give a guiding adult vetted, free starting points for the unit's
three questions (faster = more energy? how does energy travel? where does it
go in collisions?), pitched at ages 9–10, with no account required for the
learner-facing picks.
**Age fit:** grade 4 (about 9–10 years old); short, visual, qualitative —
nothing in this unit uses speed numbers or energy calculations.
