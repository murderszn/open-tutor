# Week-09 Quiz — Energy Transfer, Collisions, and Motion (formative)

**Total: 12 points.** Taken at the end of Week 9 (Session 10), after Lessons
01–04 and Practice Sets A–C. The adult scores against the
[answer key](../answer-key.md) the same day; items missed become Week 10
warm-ups. No speed numbers or energy calculations anywhere.

1. **(1 pt)** A ball rolls fast into clay and makes a deep dent. The *same*
   ball rolls slowly into fresh clay and makes a shallow dent. Which ball
   had more energy?
2. **(1 pt)** In question 1, what is the *evidence* for your answer?
3. **(2 pts)** A learner tests "faster = more energy" by rolling a heavy
   ball fast and a light ball slowly, one trial each. Name **two** things
   wrong with this test.
4. **(2 pts)** Complete the frame: "Energy moved from the drum to the rice
   grains by ___. I know because ___."
5. **(1 pt)** A lamp shines on white paper for two minutes. The paper feels
   warmer afterward, and the lamp never touched the paper. What traveled
   from the lamp to the paper?
6. **(2 pts)** A battery, wires, and a bulb are connected in a loop and the
   bulb glows. The adult removes one wire and the bulb goes dark. What does
   this tell you about how energy was reaching the bulb? (Two sentences.)
7. **(2 pts)** A fast marble hits a still marble. The still marble rolls
   away and you hear a *clack*. Name **two** places the fast marble's energy
   went, with the evidence for each.
8. **(1 pt)** Look at [`collision-energy-ramp.webp`](../assets/collision-energy-ramp.webp)
   (left: deep dent, red marble inside; right: shallow dent, blue marble
   beside it). Which marble was moving faster — red or blue — and what is
   the one observation that proves it?

**Scoring guide:** 11–12 exceeding · 9–10 meeting · 7–8 approaching (revisit
the missed lesson's worked example before Week 10) · below 7: re-teach the
missed objectives in Week 10 warm-ups before the unit assessment.
