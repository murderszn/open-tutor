# Learner — Ecosystem Habitat Model (Grade 4 STEM)

## Goal
Model how plants and animals interact in a habitat.

## Tasks
1. Pick one habitat (forest, ocean, desert, wetland, etc.).
2. Add at least 5 living things to your model.
3. Draw 5 arrows to show who depends on whom.
4. Write what could happen if one species disappears.
