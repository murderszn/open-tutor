# Grade 4 — Explaining a Volcano

**Blank reusable assignment.**

## Goal and Instructions

Draw a labeled cross-section of a volcano using a trusted science source. Distinguish magma and lava, identify a vent, and explain how gas and pressure can contribute to eruptions. Evaluate one analogy and describe where it breaks down; do not shake or pressurize containers.
