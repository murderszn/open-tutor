## Menu for This Exercise
Use a fictional one-day menu of plain white toast and juice for breakfast, plain pasta for lunch, crackers for a snack, and plain rice for dinner. Compare it with a varied menu using educator-provided nutrient information. A single day cannot establish a deficiency. This is nutrition literacy, not diagnosis; do not use real medical histories or recommend supplements without a clinician.
