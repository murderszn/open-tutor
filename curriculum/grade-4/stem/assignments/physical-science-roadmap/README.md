# 🔬 Physical Science Roadmap (extension track)

## 📗 Learner — Grade 6 Track (Catch‑Up)

### Module List (mirrors Learner at lighter depth)
1) Measurement & Units
- Metric system basics (meter, liter, gram) and common prefixes.
- Simple US↔metric conversions using an educator-provided equivalent reference

2) Matter & Changes
- Solids/liquids/gases; melting/freezing/evaporation; mixtures vs pure substances.

3) Energy & Heat
- Potential vs kinetic; heat transfer idea: conduction vs convection vs radiation (examples).

4) Motion & Forces
- Speed vs time; friction; gravity as a pull; simple machines (inclined plane, lever).

5) Waves & Light (Everyday)
- Sound (vibrations), light (straight lines, reflections), color basics.

6) Biology & Anatomy (Basics)
- Cells (as tiny factories); simple organ system overview (bones/muscles, heart/lungs).

### Deliverables per Module
- Short notes (half page to one page) + labeled sketch if helpful.
- 1 lab from the Learner Lab Menu with a simplified lab sheet.

## ✅ Parent Evaluation Checklist (per module)
- Notes capture key ideas and vocabulary (clear, in student’s own words).
- Lab chosen is appropriate and safely executed; data recorded.
- Calculations (if any) are shown and units labeled.
- Conclusion references evidence/data and explains sources of error.
