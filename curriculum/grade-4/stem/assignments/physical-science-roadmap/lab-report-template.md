# Grade 4 — Observation Lab Note

- **Question:** What do I want to find out?
- **Prediction:** What do I think will happen?
- **Materials:** What did I use?
- **Safety:** Which adult-approved safety steps did I follow?
- **Steps:** Draw or number the procedure.
- **Observations:** Sketch and label what I noticed; record simple measurements with units.
- **Conclusion:** What happened? Was my prediction supported?
- **Next question:** What would I investigate next?

Perform hands-on investigations with appropriate adult supervision.
