# Resource Pack — Unit 03: Informational Main Ideas, Structures, and Source Comparison

## Summary

**Goal:** give the guiding adult free, no-account routes to support
grade-4 informational reading: main idea and key details (RI.4.2),
events/procedures and domain vocabulary (RI.4.3/RI.4.4), text
structures (RI.4.5), firsthand/secondhand accounts and visual
information (RI.4.6/RI.4.7), reasons and evidence (RI.4.8), and
integrating two texts with notes and source lists (RI.4.9/W.4.8).
**Age fit:** grade 4 (about 9–10); all items are visual, musical, or
hands-on, with minimal reading required of the learner. Adult
planning judgment: videos supplement the lessons; they never replace
explicit teaching or the adult check.
