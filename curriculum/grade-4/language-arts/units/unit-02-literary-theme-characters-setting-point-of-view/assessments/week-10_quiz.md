# Formative Quiz — Unit 02 (Week 10)

**12 points.** Closed-note except the anchor story, which the learner may
re-read. The adult scores with the [answer key](../answer-key.md) the
same day; results guide reteaching, not grades.

**1.** (1 pt) Is "a girl who lights a lantern" a topic or a theme?
Explain the difference in one sentence.

**2.** (1 pt) Write a full-sentence theme for "The Lantern on Gray
Hill" and quote one detail that supports it.

**3.** (1 pt) Name one **thought**, one **word**, and one **action** of
Nora's from the story (three short answers).

**4.** (1 pt) Describe the setting of Gray Hill in two sentences using
at least three story details.

**5.** (1 pt) Label each as **poem**, **drama**, or **prose**:
(a) "October Wind" (b) "The Lost Compass" (c) "The Lantern on Gray Hill"

**6.** (1 pt) In "The Lost Compass," what is the purpose of the
bracketed notes like *(grinning)*? What are they called?

**7.** (1 pt) "Repairing the stone wall was a **Herculean** task." What
does *Herculean* mean, and which myth does it point to?

**8.** (1 pt) "I struck the match with shaking hands." First or third
person? Quote the word that proves it.

**9.** (1 pt) Rewrite "She wiped the glass with her sleeve" in first
person as Nora.

**10.** (1 pt) Name the shared theme of "The Tortoise and the Hare" and
"Momotaro" in one full sentence.

**11.** (1 pt) Give one way the Greek and Japanese tales tell their
stories differently.

**12.** (1 pt) The story says "the tower door groaned on its hinges";
the illustration cannot show a groan. What does this show about comparing
a text with a picture? (One sentence.)
