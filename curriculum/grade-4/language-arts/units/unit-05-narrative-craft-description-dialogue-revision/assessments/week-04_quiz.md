# Week-22 Quiz — Narrative Craft (Formative)

**Unit 05, Grade 4 Language Arts · 10 items, 1 point each.**
The adult reviews the quiz the same day. 9–10 = ready for the unit
assessment; 7–8 = brief reteach of the missed skill; below 7 =
reteach the lesson for that skill before the assessment. Full
solutions: [answer key](../answer-key.md) § Week-22 quiz.

## Questions

1. Put these events from "The Day the Kite Got Stuck" in order
   (number them 1–4): (a) The kite drops into the maple tree.
   (b) Theo and Mara pull together and free the kite. (c) Theo
   holds the kite while Mara runs with the string. (d) Biscuit
   grabs the loose end of the string.
2. Choose the best transition: "The sun was shining. ___ dark
   clouds covered the sky." (a) First (b) Suddenly (c) Finally.
3. Which dialogue line is punctuated correctly? (a) "Let's go."
   said Mara (b) "Let's go," said Mara. (c) Lets go, said Mara.
4. Punctuate this line correctly: *theo said look the kite is
   free*
5. Which word is more concrete and precise? (a) "nice" (b) "bright
   yellow" — and what does it describe better than the other word?
6. Which sentence uses a sensory detail? (a) "The park was nice."
   (b) "Orange leaves rattled across the grass." (c) "We went to
   the park." Explain your choice in one sentence.
7. A story ends like this: "We went home." What is missing? (a)
   dialogue (b) a sense of closure (c) a transition. Explain in
   one sentence.
8. A draft repeats the word "nice" in every sentence. Which
   revision move fixes it? (a) Add (b) Remove (c) Move
   (d) Swap. Explain in one sentence.
9. Who is the narrator of "The Day the Kite Got Stuck"? How do you
   know? Answer in one sentence.
10. Write a two-line dialogue exchange between two characters
    about a lost mitten. Start a new paragraph each time the
    speaker changes and punctuate both lines correctly.
