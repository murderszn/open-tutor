# Resource Pack — Unit 04: Grammar, Sentence Combining, Punctuation, and Spelling

## Summary

**Goal:** give the guiding adult free, no-account routes to support
grade-4 grammar and conventions: relative pronouns and adverbs
(L.4.1.a), progressive tenses and modal auxiliaries (L.4.1.b–c),
prepositional phrases and adjective order (L.4.1.d–e), fragments,
run-ons, sentence combining, and confused words (L.4.1.f–g, L.4.2.c),
capitalization and direct-speech punctuation (L.4.2.a–b), spelling
with references, precise words, punctuation for effect, and formal
vs. informal English (L.4.2.d, L.4.3.a–c, SL.4.6, W.4.5).
**Age fit:** grade 4 (about 9–10); all items are short, captioned or
text-based where possible, with minimal reading required of the
learner. Adult planning judgment: videos supplement the lessons; they
never replace explicit teaching or the adult check.
