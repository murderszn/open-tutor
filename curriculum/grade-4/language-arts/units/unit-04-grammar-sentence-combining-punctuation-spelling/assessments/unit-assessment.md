## Part A — Choose the right word or form (1 point each, 4 points)

1. "This is the park ___ we play after school." (a) *who* (b) *where*
   (c) *when*
2. "Right now, Jonah ___ flying his kite." (a) *is* (b) *was*
   (c) *will be*
3. "You ___ return the book by Friday." (The library rule requires
   it.) (a) *may* (b) *can* (c) *must*
4. Which group of words is a prepositional phrase? (a) "the brown
   dog" (b) "under the old oak tree" (c) "ran quickly"

## Part B — Fix and build sentences (2 points each, 8 points)

5. Fix this fragment: "After school in the park."
6. Fix this run-on: "The sun was warm the children played outside."
7. Combine these sentences with *but*: "Lena wanted to read outside.
   The rain started."
8. Reorder the adjectives: "She carried a red small bag to school."

## Part C — Dialogue and editing (4 points each, 8 points)

9. Punctuate this two-line conversation correctly (one line
   tag-first, one line speech-first):
   - jonah said the wind is strong today
   - i can see the kite maya said
10. Edit this sentence. It has 4 errors (capitals, spelling, and a
    missing comma in a compound sentence):
    "last tuesday, maya and jonah went to the libary and they read
    books"

## Part D — Formal English (4 points)

11. Rewrite this informal request in formal English, as if speaking
    to a teacher:
    "Hey! Can I grab that book? It's awesome!"

## Part E — Park paragraph (6 points)

12. Look at [park-sentence-scene.png](../assets/park-sentence-scene.png)
    (or read the text-only alternative in Lesson 04). Write a
    4-sentence paragraph about the picture. Your paragraph must
    include: (a) a relative clause (2 points), (b) a compound
    sentence with a comma before a conjunction (2 points),
    (c) a progressive-tense verb or a modal auxiliary (1 point), and
    (d) a prepositional phrase (1 point). Label each required part in
    the margin after you write it, or underline it and name it below
    your paragraph.
