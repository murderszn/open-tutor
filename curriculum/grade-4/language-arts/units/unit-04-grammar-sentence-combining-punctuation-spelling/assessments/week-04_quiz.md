# Formative Quiz — Unit 04 (Week 18, Session W18 S15)

**Name (private — do not publish):** __________ **Date:** __________
**Score:** ______ / 10 · **Purpose:** formative only. This quiz checks
what to reteach before the unit assessment; it is not a standardized
test. Solutions are in [../answer-key.md](../answer-key.md) — the adult
holds the key.

Answer each item. Read every sentence aloud before answering.

1. Choose the right word: "The girl ___ rides a blue bicycle is
   Maya." (a) *who* (b) *where* (c) *when*
2. Choose the right verb form: "Tomorrow at noon, we ___ planting
   seeds." (a) *are* (b) *was* (c) *will be*
3. Choose the right modal: "You ___ wash your hands before lunch."
   (The school rule requires it.) (a) *may* (b) *must* (c) *can*
4. Underline the prepositional phrase(s) in this sentence: "The dog
   ran across the grass after lunch."
5. Fix this fragment by adding what is missing: "Because it rained."
6. Fix this run-on with a comma and a conjunction: "Maya rode her
   bike Jonah flew his kite."
7. Picture item: look at
   [park-sentence-scene.png](../assets/park-sentence-scene.png) (or
   read the text-only alternative in Lesson 04). Write one compound
   sentence about two things happening in the picture, with a comma
   before *and, but, or,* or *so*.
8. Punctuate this dialogue line (tag first): maya said the kite is
   high
9. Fix the capitals in this sentence: "on friday, sam went to maple
   park."
10. Choose the right words, in order: "___ dog is over ___."
    (a) *Their / there* (b) *There / their* (c) *Their / their*

**Scoring (teacher guide):** 9–10 = ready for the unit assessment;
7–8 = brief reteach of the missed skill (see the teacher guide);
below 7 = reteach the lesson for each missed skill before the
assessment.
