# Unit 04 — Grammar, Sentence Combining, Punctuation, and Spelling

**Grade:** 4 · **Subject:** Language Arts · **Track weeks:** 15–18
**Backlog:** [issue #24](https://github.com/murderszn/open-tutor/issues/24), section U04
**Status:** validated draft (this unit); not educator-reviewed, not merged

Good sentences do more than follow rules — they say exactly what the
writer means, in an order a reader can follow. In this unit the learner
builds sentences with **relative pronouns and adverbs**, uses
**progressive verb tenses** and **modal auxiliaries** to show time and
possibility, adds **prepositional phrases** and puts **adjectives in
conventional order**, fixes **fragments and run-ons** by combining
sentences, sorts out **frequently confused words**, capitalizes and
punctuates **direct speech** correctly, spells grade-appropriate words
by **consulting references**, chooses words **precisely**, punctuates
**for effect**, and switches between **formal and informal English**
for the situation. Every passage in this unit is original, written for
this unit; no published text is reproduced.

## Essential question

How do writers build sentences that are complete, clear, and exactly
right for the reader and the moment?

## Prerequisites

From Units 01–03 and the track's diagnostic weeks:

- Parts of speech: nouns, pronouns, verbs, adjectives, adverbs,
  prepositions, conjunctions (see the track's legacy
  `assignments/parts-of-speech-practice.md` and
  `assignments/sentence-parts-practice.md`, reviewed in the audit)
- Subject and predicate; simple and compound subjects and predicates
- Quoting exact text evidence; main idea, key details, and summary
- Reading grade-level prose orally with accuracy, rate, and expression;
  using context clues and reference materials for unknown words
- Writing a paragraph in order, with guidance, and revising it

New in this unit: relative pronouns (*who, whose, whom, which, that*)
and relative adverbs (*where, when, why*); progressive verb tenses;
modal auxiliaries (*can, may, must*); prepositional phrases;
conventional adjective order; fragments and run-ons; sentence
combining with coordinating conjunctions; frequently confused words
(*to, too, two; there, their*); capitalization; commas and quotation
marks for direct speech; comma before a coordinating conjunction in a
compound sentence; spelling with references; precise word choice;
punctuation for effect; formal vs. informal English; editing and
revising with guidance.

## Measurable objectives

By the end of the unit the learner will be able to (adult-observed):

1. Use **relative pronouns** (*who, whose, whom, which, that*) and
   **relative adverbs** (*where, when, why*) to connect a clause that
   describes a person, thing, place, time, or reason (L.4.1.a).
2. Form and use **progressive verb tenses** (past, present, and
   future) and **modal auxiliaries** (*can, may, must*) to show time
   and conditions such as ability, permission, and necessity
   (L.4.1.b–c).
3. Form and use **prepositional phrases** and order **adjectives**
   according to conventional patterns — opinion, size, age, color,
   material — before a noun (L.4.1.e, L.4.1.d).
4. Produce **complete sentences**, recognizing and correcting
   inappropriate **fragments and run-ons**; combine short sentences
   into compound sentences; and correctly use frequently confused
   words (*to, too, two; there, their*) (L.4.1.f–g; L.4.2.c).
5. Use correct **capitalization** and use **commas and quotation
   marks** to mark direct speech and quotations from a text
   (L.4.2.a–b).
6. Spell grade-appropriate words correctly, **consulting references**
   as needed; **choose words and phrases precisely**, choose
   **punctuation for effect**, and **differentiate formal from
   informal English**, revising and editing writing with guidance
   (L.4.2.d; L.4.3.a–c; SL.4.6; W.4.5).

## 16-session pacing (4 sessions per week, about 30–35 minutes each)

| Session | Focus | Success criterion |
|---|---|---|
| W15 S1 | Lesson 01 — relative pronouns and adverbs | Learner joins two sentences with the correct relative word and explains what the clause describes |
| W15 S2 | Practice A + reread of Lesson 01 examples | Learner completes 8 of 10 relative-word items and writes 2 correct combined sentences |
| W15 S3 | Lesson 02 — progressive tenses and modal auxiliaries | Learner rewrites a sentence in all three progressive tenses and chooses *can, may,* or *must* with a reason |
| W15 S4 | Practice B + sentence-sorting game (past / present / future progressive; ability / permission / necessity) | Learner sorts 8 of 10 sentences correctly by tense or modal meaning |
| W16 S5 | Lesson 03 — prepositional phrases and adjective order | Learner adds a prepositional phrase that answers *where, when,* or *how,* and reorders adjectives conventionally |
| W16 S6 | Practice C + phrase hunt in a short paragraph | Learner finds 4 prepositional phrases and fixes 3 adjective-order errors |
| W16 S7 | Lesson 04 — fragments, run-ons, sentence combining; confused words; picture study | Learner fixes 2 fragments and 2 run-ons and combines 2 picture-sentence pairs |
| W16 S8 | Practice D + picture-sentence writing | Learner writes 4 picture sentences, then combines 2 pairs with a comma and a conjunction |
| W17 S9 | Lesson 05 — capitalization and direct-speech punctuation | Learner punctuates 3 dialogue lines correctly and fixes 3 capitalization errors |
| W17 S10 | Practice E + dialogue rewrite (a short conversation, punctuated) | Learner rewrites a conversation with quotation marks, commas, and capitals in place |
| W17 S11 | Lesson 06 — spelling with references, precise words, punctuation for effect, formal vs. informal, revision | Learner edits a draft paragraph: 3 spelling fixes, 2 precise-word swaps, 1 formal rewrite |
| W17 S12 | Practice F + project launch (choose a park-visit topic; draft 4 sentences) | Learner starts a project draft with at least 1 relative clause and 1 compound sentence |
| W18 S13 | Project work session — draft and revise the park paragraph | Draft has 8–10 sentences, a dialogue line, and a checked spelling list |
| W18 S14 | Project presentations (formal English) + peer editing | Learner presents in formal English and names one revision made from feedback |
| W18 S15 | Formative quiz (Week 18) + reteach of the weakest skill | Learner scores 9–10, or reteaches the missed skill before the assessment |
| W18 S16 | Unit assessment + midyear review of Units 01–04 conventions | Learner completes the assessment; adult records one strength and one next step per objective |

## How to use this unit

- Teach the lessons in order: each lesson's applied task feeds the
  project. Lessons 01–03 build sentence parts; Lesson 04 puts them
  together; Lessons 05–06 polish them.
- Student pages never contain answers. All solutions, with reasoning
  and acceptable variation, are in [answer-key.md](answer-key.md);
  teaching notes are in [teacher-guide.md](teacher-guide.md). Do not
  hand the answer key to the learner.
- Every check has an oral, pointing, or dictation mode: the learner
  may say answers aloud while the adult scribes, then read them back
  for checking.
- The park illustration's text-only alternative (Lesson 04) carries
  every fact the picture activities need; no activity requires
  seeing the picture.
