# Project — My Park Paragraph: Write, Revise, and Present

**Deliverable:** a revised 8–10 sentence paragraph about a park visit
(real or imagined), plus a dialogue line, a checked spelling list,
and a short formal presentation (2–3 sentences spoken aloud).
**Time:** Sessions W17 S12 (launch), W18 S13 (draft and revise),
W18 S14 (present and edit) · **Standards:** L.4.1.a–g, L.4.2.a–d,
L.4.3.a–c, SL.4.6, W.4.5

## Materials

- Notebook or writing paper, pencil
- The park illustration
  [park-sentence-scene.png](assets/park-sentence-scene.png) (or its
  text-only alternative in Lesson 04) as a model of a busy scene
- A dictionary or glossary (print or digital, adult-selected)
- The Lesson 06 spelling list

## Steps

1. **Choose your visit (W17 S12).** Pick a park visit — one you
   remember, or one you imagine. Name the park, who went with you,
   and one thing that happened. Draft 4 sentences to start, including
   at least 1 relative clause and 1 compound sentence.
2. **Draft the paragraph (W18 S13, first half).** Expand to 8–10
   sentences. Your paragraph must include, somewhere in it:
   - at least 1 relative clause (*who, which, that, where, when,* or
     *why*)
   - at least 1 progressive-tense verb (*was / is / will be* +
     verb-*ing*)
   - at least 1 modal auxiliary (*can, may,* or *must*) used
     correctly
   - at least 1 prepositional phrase
   - at least 1 set of adjectives in conventional order
   - at least 1 compound sentence with a comma before *and, but, or,*
     or *so*
   - at least 1 dialogue line, punctuated with quotation marks and a
     comma
   - at least 5 words from the Lesson 06 spelling list, spelled
     correctly (check each one in the dictionary or glossary)
   - no fragments and no run-ons
3. **Revise, then edit (W18 S13, second half).** First revise: swap
   at least 2 vague words for precise ones, and add at least 1 detail
   a reader could picture. Then edit: check every capital, every
   comma and quotation mark, and every spelling-list word. Have the
   adult read the draft aloud while you follow along and mark fixes.
4. **Present formally (W18 S14).** Present your paragraph (or its
   opening 2–3 sentences) to the class or the adult in **formal
   English**: greet the listeners, use *may* for polite requests, use
   full sentences, and thank them at the end. Listeners name one
   strength and one revision you made from feedback.
5. **Hand in.** The revised paragraph, the dialogue line marked
   inside it, the checked spelling list (each word ticked after
   checking), and one sentence naming a revision you made and why.

## Model paragraph (original; written for this unit)

> "Last Saturday, Maya and I visited Maple Park, where we fly kites
> in the spring. While we were walking along the path, we saw a brown
> dog chasing a yellow ball across the grass. Maya said, "The dog is
> faster than my bicycle!" We must stay on the path when other people
> are walking, so we rode slowly past the pond. A boy in a yellow
> shirt flew a red kite, and two ducks swam in the small pond. It was
> a beautiful, sunny afternoon, and we went home together, tired and
> happy."

Notice: a relative clause (*where we fly kites*), a progressive verb
(*were walking*), a modal (*must*), prepositional phrases (*along
the path, across the grass*), adjectives in order (*small pond* is
size only; *beautiful, sunny* are both opinion — either order of two
opinions is acceptable, and the key notes this), a compound sentence
with a comma before *and*, a dialogue line, and spelling-list words
(*beautiful, together*). The model is 6 sentences; your paragraph is
8–10 — longer, not copied.

## Rubric (40 points)

| Criterion | 10 points | 7–9 points | 4–6 points | 0–3 points |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Ideas and scene (10) | A clear park visit in order, with details a reader can picture; dialogue fits the scene | Mostly in order, some pictured details; dialogue present | Hard to follow, or few details; dialogue missing or off-topic | No clear visit, or only a list of sentences |
| Grammar: clauses, tenses, phrases (10) | Relative clause, progressive verb, modal, and prepositional phrase all present and correct | 3 of the 4 present and correct | 2 present, or several errors | 1 or none, or errors throughout |
| Sentences: combining, order, confused words (10) | No fragments or run-ons; at least 1 correct compound sentence with a comma; adjectives in order; confused words correct | 1 fragment or run-on, or 1 combining/comma error; adjectives mostly in order | 2–3 sentence errors, or adjectives out of order | Fragments or run-ons throughout |
| Punctuation, capitals, spelling, revision (10) | Dialogue punctuated correctly; capitals correct; 5+ spelling-list words correct and checked; at least 2 precise-word revisions named | 1–2 punctuation, capital, or spelling errors; 1 revision named | 3–4 errors; revisions vague or unnamed | Errors throughout; no revision shown |

**Scoring:** 36–40 = exceeds; 28–35 = meets; 20–27 = meets with
reteaching notes (name the skill and the lesson to revisit); below
20 = revise with the adult and re-present one paragraph section.
A first draft is never graded for perfection; the grade follows the
revised paragraph.
