# Practice — Unit 04: Grammar, Sentence Combining, Punctuation, and Spelling

Work one set per session, in order. Say each answer aloud before
writing it, and check it against the lesson rule — not against a
guess. All solutions are in [answer-key.md](answer-key.md); do not
read the key until you have tried the set.

## Practice A — Relative pronouns and adverbs (Lesson 01)

1. Fill in: "The boy ___ won the race is my brother." (*who / where*)
2. Fill in: "This is the classroom ___ we read every morning."
   (*when / where*)
3. Fill in: "Do you know the reason ___ she left early?"
   (*why / whose*)
4. Fill in: "The woman ___ we thanked is our librarian." (*who / whom*)
5. Combine with *that*: "I read a book. The book was about space."
6. Combine with *whose*: "That is the girl. Her bicycle is red."
7. Combine with *when*: "It was a morning. We planted seeds that
   morning."
8. Fix the error: "The park who we visit on Saturdays is near our
   house."
9. Write a sentence about a person using *who*.
10. Write a sentence about a place using *where*.

## Practice B — Progressive tenses and modal auxiliaries (Lesson 02)

1. Present progressive: "Maya reads a book" → right now.
2. Past progressive: "Maya reads a book" → yesterday evening.
3. Future progressive: "Maya reads a book" → tomorrow evening.
4. Fill in with *can, may,* or *must*: "You ___ wear a seat belt in
   the car." (Rule.)
5. Fill in with *can, may,* or *must*: "___ I borrow your eraser,
   please?" (Polite permission.)
6. Fill in with *can, may,* or *must*: "Jonah ___ ride a bicycle
   without training wheels now." (Ability.)
7. Fill in with *can, may,* or *must*: "It ___ snow tonight."
   (Possibility.)
8. Fix the error: "She can swimming across the pool."
9. Fix the error: "Tomorrow, they was playing soccer."
10. Write two sentences: one with *must* (a rule) and one with *may*
    (permission).

## Practice C — Prepositional phrases and adjective order (Lesson 03)

1. Add a *where* phrase to: "The children played."
2. Add a *when* phrase to: "We ate lunch."
3. Underline the prepositional phrase(s) in: "The book on the wooden
   shelf belongs to Maya."
4. Underline the prepositional phrase(s) in: "After the rain, the
   ducks swam in the pond."
5. Reorder: "She wore a red small coat."
6. Reorder: "He carried two blue big bags."
7. Reorder: "They sat on an old wooden beautiful bench."
8. Write a sentence with a prepositional phrase telling *how* (use
   *with*).
9. Write a sentence with three adjectives in conventional order
   before a noun.
10. Fix the fragment: "In the morning, before school." (Add a subject
    and a verb.)

## Practice D — Fragments, run-ons, combining, confused words; picture sentences (Lesson 04)

Use [park-sentence-scene.png](assets/park-sentence-scene.png), or the
text-only alternative in Lesson 04, for items 3 and 6.

1. Fragment or complete? "Because the kite got stuck." Fix it.
2. Fragment or complete? "The boy flew his kite." Explain.
3. Write two short sentences about two different things happening in
   the park picture, then combine them with a comma and *and*.
4. Fix this run-on by splitting it: "The ducks swam in the pond the
   dog ran across the grass."
5. Fix this run-on by combining it: "Maya rode her bike Jonah read
   his book." (Use *and*.)
6. Combine with *but*: "The boy wanted to fly his kite. The wind
   was weak."
7. Choose: "We walked ___ school, and we were ___ excited to go."
   (*to / too / two* — in order)
8. Choose: "___ house is over ___." (*There / Their* — in order)
9. Choose: "I have ___ brothers, and they want to come, ___."
   (*to / too / two* — in order)
10. Write a compound sentence of your own about the park, with a comma
    before *or* or *so*.

## Practice E — Capitalization and direct speech (Lesson 05)

1. Fix the capitals: "on monday, maya visited her aunt in austin."
2. Fix the capitals: "dr. patel said, wash your hands before lunch."
3. Punctuate (tag first): lena said the pond is full of ducks
4. Punctuate (speech first): watch out jonah shouted
5. Punctuate (speech first, question): is that your ball maya asked
6. Punctuate (tag first, question): sam asked are we staying long
7. Find the error: '"I found a shell". Lena said.' Rewrite it.
8. Write a dialogue line in the tag-first pattern about a kite.
9. Write a dialogue line in the speech-first pattern about a kite.
10. Quote from a text (tag first): the author wrote ducks swim in
    ponds in spring

## Practice F — Spelling, precise words, punctuation for effect, formal vs. informal (Lesson 06)

Use the spelling list in Lesson 06 and a dictionary or glossary.

1. Correct the misspelling in each pair: (a) *beautiful / beautifull*
   (b) *libary / library* (c) *tomorow / tomorrow*.
2. Write *together* and *carefully* correctly from memory, then check
   them letter by letter.
3. Replace the vague verb: "He went to the store." (Use a verb that
   shows how: *walked, pedaled, hurried* — pick one and keep the
   sentence sensible.)
4. Replace the vague adjective: "It was a nice cake." (Use *delicious*
   if you mean taste, or *beautiful* if you mean looks — name your
   meaning.)
5. Punctuate for effect: write "The fireworks lit up the sky" as an
   excited sentence, then as a calm sentence.
6. Label formal or informal: (a) "Good morning, class. I will present
   my park paragraph." (b) "Hey, wanna hear about the park?"
7. Rewrite informally, then formally: "I need that pencil." (Informal:
   talking to a friend. Formal: asking a teacher politely.)
8. Edit this sentence (4 errors: capital, spelling, capital, comma
   in a compound sentence): "last week, we visited the libary and
   maya read a book"
9. Revise this sentence (vague words): "We did stuff and it was fun."
   (Add a precise verb and a precise detail.)
10. Edit and revise: "yesterday, me and lena went to the park and it
    was nice." (Fix the capital, the pronoun order, the vague word,
    and add the compound-sentence comma.)
