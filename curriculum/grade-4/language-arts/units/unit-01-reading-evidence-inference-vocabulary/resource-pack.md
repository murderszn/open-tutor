# Resource Pack — Unit 01: Reading Evidence, Inference, and Vocabulary

## Summary

**Goal:** give the guiding adult free, no-account routes to support
grade-4 text evidence and inference (RL.4.1/RI.4.1), multisyllable
decoding with Greek/Latin morphology (RF.4.3.a), context clues and
reference skills (L.4.4.a–c), academic vocabulary (L.4.6), and oral
reading fluency (RF.4.4.a–c).
**Age fit:** grade 4 (about 9–10); all items are visual, musical, or
hands-on, with minimal reading required of the learner. Adult planning
judgment: videos supplement the lessons; they never replace explicit
teaching or the adult check.

themselves teach each skill with the unit's original passages; FCRR
routines, Merriam-Webster lookups, and Khan Academy practice are all
free without accounts. Videos are supplements — preview for ads,

account. Print alternative: every video-linked activity also works
with the lesson text alone.
