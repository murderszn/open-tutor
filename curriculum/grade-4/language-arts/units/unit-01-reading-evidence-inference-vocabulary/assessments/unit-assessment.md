# Culminating Unit Assessment — Unit 01

**Total: 40 points.** Administer after session 15. The learner has
already read both passages during the unit; this is a supported
assessment, not a cold read. Every item is solved in the [answer
key](../answer-key.md#unit-assessment). Ready for Unit 02: ≥ 32/40
with Part D attempted in full.

## Part A — Explicit evidence (10 points)

Re-read **"Beaver Builders"** ([Lesson
01](../lessons/lesson-01-evidence-quote-the-text.md)). Answer with exact
quotes. 2 points each (1 answer + 1 word-for-word quote).

1. What do beavers chew with, and what kinds of trees are named?
2. What three things go into a dam, and what does the dam form?
3. What is a lodge, and how do beavers get inside it?
4. What protects the lodge, and from what?
5. Why must beavers keep chewing? Quote the reason.

## Part B — Inference (10 points)

Re-read **"The Cracked Sidewalk"** ([Lesson
02](../lessons/lesson-02-inference-detective.md)) and study the
[window-scene illustration](../assets/inference-window-scene.png).
Each answer needs the inference, the detail, and the background
knowledge. 2 points each (deduct 1 if the knowledge or check is
missing).

6. How did Mr. Abernathy feel about the missing tomatoes? Prove it.
7. Who most likely took the tomatoes? Prove it.
8. In the illustration: what season is it most likely to be? Give two
   details and the knowledge behind them.
9. In the illustration: will the girl need the umbrella in an hour?
   Infer and defend with detail + knowledge.
10. **Busted theory:** "The dog caused the muddy footprints." Check
    this theory against the illustration's details. Keep, repair, or
    drop it — and say why.

## Part C — Vocabulary (12 points)

11. Break *microscope* into syllables; name the root parts and their
    meanings; assemble the word's meaning. (3 points)
12. Break *autograph* into syllables; name the parts (*auto-* +
    *graph*); assemble the meaning. (3 points)
13. "The *summit*, or top of the mountain, gleamed white." Name the
    clue type, point to the signal, and give the meaning of *summit*.
    (2 points)
14. "Nocturnal hunters, for example owls and bats, sleep all day."
    Name the clue type, point to the signal, and give the meaning of
    *nocturnal*. (2 points)
15. A dictionary lists for *light*: **1.** brightness that lets us see;
    **2.** not heavy; **3.** pale in color. Which fits "a light
    jacket for spring," and what ruled the others out? (2 points)

## Part D — Written response with evidence (8 points)

16. **Short paragraph (5–7 sentences):** In "The Empty Bowl," what kind
    of person is Maya? Answer with a claim about her character, support
    it with **two quoted details** from the story, and use at least
    two unit academic words (*evidence, inference, describe,
    conclude*) precisely. Scored with the rubric below.

| Points | What earns it |
|---|---|
| 2 | Clear claim about Maya's character (e.g., responsible, caring, thoughtful) |
| 2 | Two exact quotes from the story, each connected to the claim |
| 2 | Two academic words used precisely and grammatically |
| 1 | 5–7 sentences, organized (claim → evidence → closing) |
| 1 | Inferences labeled as inferences, not stated as text facts |

17. **Fluency check (separate, ungraded-for-score but required):** read
    "The Empty Bowl" aloud a final time. Record errors and expression
    (1–3). File in the Evidence Case File. Reteach trigger: more than
    4 uncorrected errors → revisit Lesson 06 before Unit 02.
