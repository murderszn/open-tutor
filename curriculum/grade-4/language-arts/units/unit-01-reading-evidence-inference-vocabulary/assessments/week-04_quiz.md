# Formative Quiz — Unit 01, Week 4 (Session 16)

**Total: 30 points.** Adult administers; learner may answer orally with
the adult scribing. Every item is solved in the [answer
key](../answer-key.md#week-04-quiz). Passing: ≥ 24/30.

## Part A — Quote the evidence (8 points)

Read "The Empty Bowl" ([Lesson
01](../lessons/lesson-01-evidence-quote-the-text.md)). Answer each with
an exact quote. 2 points each: 1 for the correct answer, 1 for the
word-for-word quote.

1. What did Maya do right after reading the note?
2. What did the clock say, and what did that tell Maya about when Leo
   would return?
3. What two things did Maya put in the bowls?
4. What did Maya say about Leo and the water?

## Part B — Inference (6 points)

Use the [window-scene illustration](../assets/inference-window-scene.png)
(items 5–6) and "The Cracked Sidewalk" ([Lesson
02](../lessons/lesson-02-inference-detective.md)) (item 7). Each answer
needs the inference AND the detail behind it. 2 points each.

5. What had the weather been like earlier? Name two visible details.
6. Is the girl upset about the rain? What do you see that tells you?
7. Why did Mr. Abernathy put the basket by the front gate? Give the
   detail and the background knowledge.

## Part C — Word parts (8 points)

8. Break *telescope* into syllables, name its parts (*tele-* + *scope*),
   and tell what the word means. (2 points)
9. Break *photograph* into syllables, name its parts, and tell what the
   word means. (2 points)
10. What does the root *port-* mean? Use it to explain *transport*.
    (2 points)
11. The word *preview* has the prefix *pre-*. What does *pre-* mean,
    and what does *preview* mean? (2 points)

## Part D — Context clues and dictionary (8 points)

12. "The *weary* travelers, exhausted from the climb, rested." Name
    the clue type and the meaning of *weary*. (2 points)
13. "Bring *sturdy* shoes, such as boots or sneakers." Name the clue
    type and the meaning of *sturdy*. (2 points)
14. A dictionary entry for *bank* lists: **1.** the land beside a
    river; **2.** a place that keeps money safe. Which meaning fits
    "They picnicked on the river bank," and what ruled the other out?
    (2 points)
15. Use the academic word *evidence* in a sentence about "The Empty
    Bowl." (2 points: word used precisely in a grammatical sentence)
