# Answer Key — Unit 01: Reading Evidence, Inference, and Vocabulary

For the guiding adult only. Every item below was solved independently
against the lesson texts; where an answer is legitimately open (a
defensible inference, a sentence upgrade), the key gives the acceptable
range and the reasoning that earns credit.

**Independent practice**

   sunlight. Knowledge: rainbows require sun + rain.
2. Calm/pleased, not upset. Detail: slight smile, raincoat + boots +
   umbrella (prepared). Knowledge: prepared people who smile are not
   upset.
3. Generous / has plenty to share. Detail: whole basket, "TAKE ONE."
   Knowledge: giving away homegrown food is generous.
4. So passersby can reach it. Detail: "by his front gate." Knowledge:

**Exit check:** any genuine inference + 1 detail + 1 knowledge (e.g.,

## Lesson 03 {#lesson-03}

**Guided practice**
1. *microphone*: mi-cro-phone; *micro-* "small" + *phon* "sound" — a
   device that makes small sounds big.
2. *telegraph*: *tele-* "far" + *graph* "write" — writing sent far
   away.
3. *geography*: ge-og-ra-phy; *geo-* "earth" + *graph* "write" —
   writing about the earth.
4. *transport* (*trans-* "across" + *port* "carry"); *symphony*
   (*sym-* "together" + *phon* "sound"); *export* (*ex-* "out" + *port*
   "carry").
5. The adult's "PHO-to-graf" is wrong: *ph* says /f/ and the stress is
   PHO-tuh-graf. Re-blend and re-read.
6. *autograph* (self-writing = signature); *automobile* (self-moving
   vehicle); *automatic* (working by itself).

**Independent practice**
1. *periscope*: per-i-scope — *peri-* "around" + *scope* "see": see
   around (corners). *symphony*: sym-pho-ny — sounds together.
2. *import*: im-port — carry in. *export*: ex-port — carry out.
3. telephone, television, telescope, telegraph — all involve distance
   ("far").
4. *autobiography*: *auto-* self + *bio-* life + *graph* write —
   writing about one's own life.
5. Knowing *port-* = "carry" lets you expect the chunk and its sound;
   the word breaks itself at the root boundary.
6. Count correct of 6 (adult's cards vary).

**Exit check:** *microscope*: mi-cro-scope — *micro-* small + *scope*
see: see small things. *television*: tel-e-vi-sion — *tele-* far +
*vis* see: see far (pictures from far away).

## Lesson 05 {#lesson-05}

**Guided practice**
1. *cautious*: adjective; the "careful, watchful" numbered meaning.
2. *triumph*: noun/verb; "a great victory."
3. Correction: "not thinking" is the wrong numbered sense for "a
   vacant lot" — the sentence needs "empty, not occupied."
4. Accept meaning-checked pairs, e.g., "The *lovely* garden grew
   *enormous* tomatoes."
5. *predict* — a forecast made from evidence (dark clouds).
6. Accept: "The *evidence* shows she is sad." / "The *details*
   *describe* her sadness." (Two academic words, precise.)

**Independent practice**
1. *sturdy*: adjective; the "strong, durably built" sense for "a
   sturdy bridge."
2. *shimmer*: the "sparkling light" sense for "the shimmer of the
   lake."
3. *cite*: to quote as evidence. *explicit*: stated directly, no
   guessing needed. (Found in the mini-glossary.)
4. Accept defensible pairs, e.g., "The *delicious* soup was
   *steaming*." — each swap must survive a meaning check.
5. Accept, e.g., "I *observe* the beavers piling mud." / "I
   *conclude* the dam protects the lodge."
6. Accept: clue → parts → verify — clues get you close; the
   dictionary confirms before you report.

**Exit check:** *pitcher* (baseball player) vs. *poured* — different
words; for a true multiple-meaning demo use e.g. *bat* (animal vs.
sports stick): name part of speech, the sentence-correct numbered
meaning, and what ruled the other out.

**Independent practice**

signal behind ≥1 fix.
