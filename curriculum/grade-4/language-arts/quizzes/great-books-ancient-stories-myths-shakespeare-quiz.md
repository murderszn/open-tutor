# Grade 4 Language Arts — Great Books — Ancient Stories, Myths & Shakespeare Quiz

**Topic:** Great Books — Ancient Stories, Myths & Shakespeare

**Assignment:** Read **Parts 1 and 2** (Ancient Stories & Mythology and Shakespeare) in the [Great Books & Classic Stories](../../../../resources/language_arts_great_books_and_stories.md) guide.

Please answer the following 10 questions based on your reading.

---

**1.** What is The Odyssey? Who wrote it, approximately when, and what is it about? Why is the word "odyssey" still used in everyday English today?

**2.** Choose three Greek myths from the guide (Prometheus, Icarus, King Midas, Pandora, Medusa, Hercules, or Orpheus). For each one: summarize the myth in 2–3 sentences and explain what lesson or symbol it represents.

**3.** From the table of Greek gods, name and give the domain of at least six gods. For each, also give their Roman name.

**4.** Choose three of Aesop's Fables from the guide. State the title, give a one-sentence summary, and explain the moral in your own words.

**5.** Who was William Shakespeare? When did he live, where did he work, and why is he considered important to the English language?

**6.** Shakespeare is said to have invented over 1,700 English words. Name at least five words from the guide that he invented or popularized.

**7.** Summarize Romeo and Juliet in 3–4 sentences. What type of play is it (tragedy, comedy, or history)? What is one famous quote from it?

**8.** Summarize Hamlet in 3–4 sentences. What central question does Hamlet struggle with? What is the most famous quote from this play?

**9.** Summarize Macbeth in 3–4 sentences. What causes Macbeth's downfall? What does this story teach about ambition and power?

**10.** What is a Shakespearean sonnet? How many lines does it have, and what is its rhyme scheme? Write out the rhyme scheme using letters (A, B, C…) and give the opening line of either Sonnet 18 or Sonnet 116.
