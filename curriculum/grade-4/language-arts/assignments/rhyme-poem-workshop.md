# Grade 4 — Rhyme Workshop

**Blank reusable assignment.**

## Goal and Instructions

Choose a topic unrelated to private personal details. Build two sets of rhyming words, draft a four-line poem, and revise for meaning and rhythm. Read it aloud and explain one revision.
