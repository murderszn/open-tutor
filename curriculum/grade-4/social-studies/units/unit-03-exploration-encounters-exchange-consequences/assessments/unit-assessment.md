# Unit Assessment — Unit 03: Exploration, Encounters, Exchange, and Consequences

**Name:** ______________ **Date:** ______________ **Points:** 12
tasks, scored by the teacher guide's criteria (see
[teacher-guide.md](../teacher-guide.md)). The adult may read items
aloud; spoken and drawn answers count. Answers are in
[answer-key.md](../answer-key.md) — this page contains no answers.

**Task 1 — Timeline.** Place these five voyages in order with years:
Hudson, Columbus, Cartier, Cabot, de Soto's Mississippi crossing.

**Task 2 — Gaps.** How many years passed between 1492 and 1609? Between
1497 and 1534? Show both subtractions.

**Task 3 — Sought and found.** For Cartier and for Hudson, write one
"sought" sentence and one "found" sentence each (four sentences total).

**Task 4 — Tools.** A captain on a cloudy night still knows which way
is north. Which tool helps, and why does the astrolabe not help that
night?

**Task 5 — Exchange sort.** Sort these eight items by direction
(four each way): maize, sheep, peanut, coffee, pig, squash, banana,
turkey.

**Task 6 — Trace.** Choose one crop from each direction of the
exchange. For each, write its direction and one probable effect.

**Task 7 — Consequence chain.** Build a three-link chain for
sugarcane: crop → forced labor → profit. Each link is one sentence,
and the labor link must use the word "forced."

**Task 8 — Two accounts.** Using the two Lesson 04 passages, name one
fact both passages agree on, one thing only Passage B mentions, and
one thing neither passage proves.

**Task 9 — Audience and purpose.** Passage A says, "I wrote down the
shape of the bay so our captain could find it again." State its
audience and purpose in two sentences, quoting the clue.

**Task 10 — Fact and opinion.** Sort these four statements — Fact /
Opinion / Unknown — using only the two Lesson 04 passages: (a) The
ship anchored in the morning. (b) The bay will make a good harbor.
(c) The strangers came to take things. (d) Food was offered from canoes.

**Task 11 — Claim.** Build a claim about what changed in the
St. Lawrence region after contact, using the frame: claim + two
different evidences + "So" sentence.

**Task 12 — Continuation and uncertainty.** Write two sentences:
one thing that continued in the St. Lawrence region after contact,
and one thing about this unit's history that you are still unsure
about (a question, not a guess).
