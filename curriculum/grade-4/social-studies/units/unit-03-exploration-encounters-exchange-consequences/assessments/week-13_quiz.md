**Q1.** Which voyage came first?
A. Hudson (1609) B. Cartier (1534) C. Columbus (1492) D. Cabot (1497)

**Q2.** What did Cabot seek on his 1497 voyage?
A. Gold in the Southeast B. A westward route to Asia
C. The Mississippi River D. A fur-trading post

**Q3.** Which item moved from the Americas to the Old World?
A. Wheat B. Horses C. Potatoes D. Smallpox

**Q4.** Which item moved from the Old World to the Americas?
A. Maize B. Tomatoes C. Cattle D. Turkeys

**Q5.** Passage A (sailor's log, Lesson 04) says, "I wrote down the
shape of the bay so our captain could find it again." What does this
clue tell you about the passage's purpose?
A. To entertain children B. To make a usable record for returning
sailors C. To prove the strangers were friendly D. To count the fish

**Q6.** Which sentence is an opinion?
A. "Twelve canoes came out." B. "The ship anchored in the morning."
C. "This bay will make a good harbor." D. "Food was offered."

**Q7.** How many years passed between Columbus (1492) and Hudson
(1609)? Show the subtraction.

**Q8.** Name one motive for sailing besides trade and wealth, and give
one clue phrase a historian could look for.

**Q9.** In one sentence, explain why the word "discovery" is misleading
for 1492.

**Q10.** A claim says: "Contact brought new foods to both sides, but it
also brought disease and forced labor, so its effects were mixed and
unequal." Name one piece of evidence from the unit that supports the
"disease" part of this claim, and one that supports the "forced labor"
part. (Two evidences; 1 point total only if both are given.)
