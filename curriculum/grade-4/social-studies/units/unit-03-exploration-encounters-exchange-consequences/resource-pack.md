# Resource Pack — Unit 03: Exploration, Encounters, Exchange, and Consequences

**Goal (one sentence):** Give the guiding adult short, grade-4-appropriate
videos, references, and search routes that reinforce voyage timelines,
route maps, the Columbian Exchange, two-perspective source work, and
evidence-based claims.
**Age fit:** grade 4 (ages 9–10); the adult previews every video and
co-views with the learner. Encounter, disease, and forced-labor content
is adult-led at the unit's plain, non-graphic level.

**Fact-check record (2026-10-24):** voyage years verified against
standard voyage tables (Columbus 1492, Cabot 1497, Cartier 1534–35,
de Soto's Mississippi crossing 1541 within an expedition of about
1539–1542, Hudson 1609); exchange items verified against Britannica
Kids, "Columbian Exchange" (maize, potatoes, tomatoes to the Old
World; wheat, sugarcane, horses, cattle, pigs, smallpox, measles to the
Americas). **Caveats for every video below:** no video in this pack was
found as a single verified episode link on 2026-10-24, so all video
entries are clearly labeled preferred-channel search links — the adult
picks one episode, previews it, and checks it against the unit's
"encounter, not discovery" framing before showing it. Any video that
calls the Americas "discovered" or "empty" must be paused and corrected
out loud, or skipped. **Free, no-account routes support every core
objective:** the Britannica Kids article, the Library of Congress item
page, and the internal repository guides below are all free to read
without an account; videos supplement teaching, never replace it.
