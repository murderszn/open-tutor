# Formative Quiz — Unit 02 (Week 9, Session 4)

**Purpose:** check Lessons 01–04 before the final stretch. The adult
scores the same day and re-teaches any objective below 3/4 items
correct. Answers in `answer-key.md` — never beside these questions.

**Name:** ____________________ **Date:** __________

**1.** Write four of the eight culture areas and one environment word
for each. (Objective 1)

**2.** A nation builds homes from sun-dried earth bricks because the
desert is hot and dry. Is this **adapt** or **modify**? What is your
evidence? (Objective 2)

**3.** A nation digs channels to bring river water to dry cornfields. Is
this **adapt** or **modify**? What changed — the people or the land?
(Objective 2)

**4.** Look at the unit illustration
([`culture-areas-environments.png`](../assets/culture-areas-environments.png),
an AI-generated teaching picture). The top-right panel shows tall
evergreens, a carved pole, and fish drying on a rack.
a. Which culture area is it?
b. Name two visible clues that prove it.
(Objective 1)

**Text-only alternative for item 4:** "A panel shows tall evergreen
trees, a tall carved wooden pole with animal faces, a wooden house, and
fish hanging on a wooden rack." Answer a–b from the description.

**5.** Complete this chart row for the **Tlingit** (Northwest Coast):
Food = ? Shelter = ? Give one evidence note for each. (Objective 3)

**6.** Two accounts describe the same 1621 gathering. Account A
(colonist) stresses the harvest and the invitation. Account B
(Wampanoag) stresses the homeland, the sickness, and diplomacy. Give
**one reason** from each side's situation that explains what it
stresses. (Objective 4)

**7.** What are the **five questions** historians ask about an account?
(Objective 4)

**8.** "Different perspectives mean nobody can know what really
happened." Agree or disagree? Explain in one sentence. (Objective 4)

---

**Scoring guide (adult):** 8 items, 1 point each (items 4 and 6 earn
half points per part). 7–8: on track. 5–6: review the missed
objectives' lessons before Week 10. Below 5: re-teach Lessons 01–04
with the teacher guide and re-quiz with new items.
