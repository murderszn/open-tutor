# Resource Pack — Unit 04: Colonial Regions, Communities, and Economic Life

**Goal (one sentence):** Give the guiding adult short, grade-4-appropriate
videos, references, and search routes that reinforce the three colonial
regions, production chains and specialization, trade webs and ports,
colonial money, and beginnings of self-rule.
**Age fit:** grade 4 (ages 9–10); the adult previews every video and
co-views with the learner. Enslavement and land-taking content is
adult-led at the unit's plain, non-graphic level.

**Fact-check record (2026-10-31):** region groupings and products
verified against standard colonial-region summaries (New England:
NH, MA, RI, CT — rocky soil, fishing, shipbuilding; Middle: NY, NJ,
PA, DE — wheat, "breadbasket"; Southern: MD, VA, NC, SC, GA —
tobacco, rice, indigo, plantation labor); money facts verified
against the National Park Service "Spanish Coins" page and the Federal
Reserve Bank of Philadelphia "Money in Colonial Times" page (piece of
eight, eight bits, two bits = a quarter, Pine Tree Shilling 1652,
bills of credit); self-rule facts verified against standard summaries
(House of Burgesses 1619, Mayflower Compact 1620, New England town
meetings). **Caveats for every video below:** no video in this pack was
found as a single verified episode link on 2026-10-31, so all video
entries are clearly labeled preferred-channel search links — the adult
picks one episode, previews it, and checks it against the unit's
framing before showing it. Any video that presents enslavement as
voluntary, calls Indigenous land "empty," or treats plantation profit
without forced labor must be paused and corrected out loud, or
skipped. **Free, no-account routes support every core objective:**
the NPS and Philadelphia Fed pages, the internal repository guides,
and the unit's own tables are all free to read without an account;
videos supplement teaching, never replace it.

## Web references

1. **U.S. National Park Service — "Spanish Coins"** —
   https://www.nps.gov/articles/000/spanish-coins.htm
   *Why good:* explains the piece of eight, its eight bits, and why
   Spanish coin circulated in the colonies, matching Lesson 04.
   *Checked 2026-10-31 via search result; free, no account.*
2. **Federal Reserve Bank of Philadelphia — "Money in Colonial Times"** —
   https://www.philadelphiafed.org/education/money-in-colonial-times
   *Why good:* colonial coins, the Pine Tree Shilling (1652), and
   paper bills of credit for Lesson 04's four payment kinds.
   *Checked 2026-10-31 via search result; free, no account.*
3. **Britannica Kids — thirteen colonies articles (adult search)** —
   search `site:kids.britannica.com thirteen colonies regions` and pick
   the student article matching the region being taught.
   *Why good:* reading-level-checked background for Lesson 01.
   *Labeled search route — adult previews; free, no account.*
4. **Library of Congress — colonial America resources (adult search)** —
   search `site:loc.gov colonial America thirteen colonies classroom`
   and pick an educator page.
   *Why good:* primary-source holdings and classroom materials for
   adult background to Lessons 03–05; the adult selects any excerpt
   and reads it aloud. *Labeled search route — adult previews; free,
   no account. Learners never cite the unit's invented passages as if
   they came from here.*
5. **National Museum of the American Indian (Smithsonian) — NK360°
   educator resources (adult search)** — search
   `site:americanindian.si.edu NK360 colonial Native perspectives` and
   pick an educator page.
   *Why good:* Native knowledge and perspectives for colonial
   teaching, per the track audit's guidance to prefer Indigenous
   authorship or museum/tribal sources where available.
   *Labeled search route — adult previews; free, no account.*
6. **U.S. Mint — coin education (adult search)** — search
   `site:usmint.gov colonial coins history` and pick the Mint page
   used to verify the Lesson 04 coin designs.
   *Why good:* official-mint verification for the kept

   *Labeled search route — adult previews; free, no account.*
