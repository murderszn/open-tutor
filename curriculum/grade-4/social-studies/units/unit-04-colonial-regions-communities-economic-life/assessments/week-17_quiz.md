# Formative Quiz — Week 17 (Unit 04)

**Name:** ______________ **Date:** ______________ **Points:** 10
(1 point each unless noted). The adult may read items aloud; spoken
and drawn answers count. Answers are in
[answer-key.md](../answer-key.md) — this page contains no answers.

**Q1.** Which region fits? "Rocky soil, short growing season, fishing
villages, and shipbuilding."
A. Southern Colonies B. Middle Colonies C. New England D. West Indies

**Q2.** Which colony belongs to the Middle Colonies?
A. Georgia B. Pennsylvania C. Massachusetts D. South Carolina

**Q3.** What is specialization?
A. Every region making everything itself B. A region concentrating on
what it can make best and trading for the rest C. A tax on imported
goods D. A kind of colonial coin

**Q4.** In the tobacco chain, which step comes right after "leaves cut
and dried"?
A. Loaded on a ship B. Packed in barrels C. Grown in a field
D. Sold as cloth in Britain

**Q5.** A barrel of flour travels from Philadelphia to the West Indies.
Which trade scale is this?
A. Between regions only B. Across the Atlantic to Britain only
C. Between a colony and the West Indies (Atlantic trade)
D. Trade within one town only

**Q6.** Why did ports like Boston, New York, and Charleston grow large?
A. They were far from water B. Ships could load and unload there, so
trade, workshops, and workers gathered there C. They banned all trade
D. They had no rivers

**Q7.** A basket costs 3 bits. You pay with one piece of eight. How many
bits of change do you get? Show the subtraction.

**Q8.** Name one way colonists paid besides coins, and give one problem
with that way of paying.

**Q9.** In one sentence, explain why "plantation profits" is a misleading
phrase if it does not mention enslaved labor.

**Q10.** A claim says: "Trade connected the colonies, but it did not
benefit everyone equally." Name one group that benefited from colonial
trade and one group that was harmed by it, with a reason for each.
(Two parts; 1 point total only if both are given.)
