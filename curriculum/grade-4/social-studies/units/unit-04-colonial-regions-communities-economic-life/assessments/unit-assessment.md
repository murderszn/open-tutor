# Unit Assessment — Unit 04: Colonial Regions, Communities, and Economic Life

**Name:** ______________ **Date:** ______________ **Points:** 12
tasks, scored by the teacher guide's criteria (see
[teacher-guide.md](../teacher-guide.md)). The adult may read items
aloud; spoken and drawn answers count. Answers are in
[answer-key.md](../answer-key.md) — this page contains no answers.

**Task 1 — Sort the colonies.** Sort these six colonies into regions:
Connecticut, Delaware, North Carolina, Rhode Island, New Jersey,
Maryland. (Two per region.)

**Task 2 — Environment explains work.** A colony has fertile soil, a
mild climate, and wide rivers. Which region is it most likely in, what
grain did it famously grow, and why did that grain grow well there?
(Three parts.)

**Task 3 — Order a chain.** Put these fish-chain steps in order:
"packed in barrels," "caught at sea," "shipped to the West Indies,"
"salted or dried." Number them 1–4.

**Task 4 — Resources.** For the wheat chain, name one natural, one
human, and one physical-capital resource, labeled N, H, and P.

**Task 5 — Specialization.** A New England shipbuilder and a Southern
tobacco grower trade with each other. Explain in 2–3 sentences why
trading beats each doing both jobs, using the word "specialization."

**Task 6 — Trace a good.** Trace a barrel of Middle Colony flour to a
distant buyer in four steps, naming a worker at each step.

**Task 7 — Port and settlement.** In 2–3 sentences, explain why a town
with a good harbor grew faster than an inland town with no port or
river landing. Use the words "port" and "trade."

**Task 8 — Money math.** Solve with subtractions shown: (a) A tool
costs 6 bits; change from one piece of eight? (b) Two items cost 3
bits and 2 bits; total, and change from one piece?

**Task 9 — Coin as evidence.** A cut Spanish coin is found in a colonial
port town. Write one evidence sentence (what it suggests) and one
limit sentence (what it does not prove).

**Task 10 — Self-rule timeline.** Order Jamestown (1607), the House of
Burgesses (1619), and the Mayflower Compact (1620), and give the gap
in years between each neighboring pair. Show both subtractions.

**Task 11 — Match self-rule.** Match: (a) Mayflower Compact,
(b) town meeting, (c) House of Burgesses — to: (i) elected
representatives make colony laws, (ii) settlers sign a written
self-government agreement, (iii) townspeople gather and vote directly.

**Task 12 — Perspectives and fairness.** Using Lesson 05's Passages A
and B (labeled invented teaching passages), explain the road decision
from each perspective in one sentence each, then answer "fair for
whom?" in one sentence. (Three sentences total.)
