# Formative Quiz — Week 22 (Unit 05)

**Name:** ______________ **Date:** ______________ **Points:** 10

**Q1.** Which level of government is it? "A governor leads it, and
it writes laws for one state."
A. National B. State C. Local D. Federal court

**Q2.** Which branch carries out laws?
A. Legislative B. Executive C. Judicial D. The Senate alone

**Q3.** What is a bill?
A. A law that is already in force B. A proposed law, not yet voted
into a law C. A tax bill sent to a family D. A court decision

**Q4.** In the bill-to-law path, which step comes right after
"committee work"?
A. The president signs B. Floor debate and vote C. The idea
D. The conference committee

**Q5.** The president vetoes a bill. How many votes does the Senate
need to override the veto?
A. 51 B. 60 C. 67 D. 100

**Q6.** A town collects 100 practice cents in tax. It spends 40¢ on
schools, 25¢ on roads and water, 20¢ on firefighters and police, and
15¢ on parks and libraries. How much is left unspent? Show the
addition.

**Q7.** Which agency helps families after a hurricane, and which
department is it part of?

**Q8.** Name one tax and one public service that tax money pays for.

**Q9.** In one sentence, explain why the 19th Amendment mattered for
who could vote.

**Q10.** A claim says: "The government is just the president in
Washington." Name one level of government and one branch the claim
leaves out, with a job each does. (Two parts; 1 point total only if
both are given.)
