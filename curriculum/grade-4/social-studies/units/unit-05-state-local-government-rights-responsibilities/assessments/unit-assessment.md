# Unit Assessment — Unit 05: State and Local Government: Rights and Responsibilities

**Name:** ______________ **Date:** ______________ **Points:** 12
tasks, scored by the teacher guide's criteria (see
[teacher-guide.md](../teacher-guide.md)). The adult may read items
aloud; spoken and drawn answers count. Answers are in
[answer-key.md](../answer-key.md) — this page contains no answers.

**Task 1 — Sort the officials.** Sort these six officials into
levels — national, state, or local: president, governor, mayor,
senator, city council member, school board member. (Two per level.)

**Task 2 — Branches and jobs.** A new law says every town must keep
its park open on Saturdays. Which branch wrote it (if it is a
national law), which branch carries it out, and which branch would
explain what it means if people disagreed? (Three parts.)

**Task 3 — Rights timeline.** Order these events and give the gap in
years between each neighboring pair: Bill of Rights (1791), 13th
Amendment (1865), 19th Amendment (1920), 26th Amendment (1971).
Show all three subtractions.

**Task 4 — Rights and responsibilities.** Pick one right from the
Bill of Rights. Write the right in your own words, name its
amendment, and name one responsibility that pairs with it. (Three
parts.)

**Task 5 — Federalism and places.** Explain federalism in 2–3
sentences, and name one way a U.S. territory differs from a U.S.
state.

**Task 6 — Bill to law.** Order these steps: "president signs,"
"committee studies the bill," "idea," "House votes," "bill
introduced," "Senate votes." Number them 1–6.

**Task 7 — Veto override.** The president vetoes a bill. The House
votes 300 to 135 to override, and the Senate votes 70 to 30. Does
the bill become a law? Show the numbers it needed and the numbers
it got.

**Task 8 — Tax math.** Solve with work shown: (a) A family buys a
$10 book. The store charges 10% sales tax. How much tax, and what is
the total? (b) A town collects 100 practice cents and spends 40¢ on
schools, 25¢ on roads and water, 20¢ on firefighters and police, and
15¢ on parks and libraries. How much is left?

**Task 9 — Agencies.** Match each job to its department or agency:
(a) exploring space, (b) emergency help after a hurricane, (c)
checking that food in stores is safe — to: (i) FEMA, (ii) FDA,
(iii) NASA.

**Task 10 — Decision procedures.** A class must choose a name for
its reading corner, and a fire alarm rings during the discussion.
Which procedure fits each moment, and why? (Two procedures, two
reasons.)

**Task 11 — Changing laws.** A town law says "No dogs in the park."
Many families want dogs allowed on leashes. Name two lawful ways to
change the law.

**Task 12 — Deliberation and fairness.** Using Lesson 05's Passages A
and B (labeled invented teaching passages), explain what Mara values
most and what Mr. Ortiz values most, in one sentence each, then
write one sentence saying how a fair rule could honor both. (Three
sentences total.)
