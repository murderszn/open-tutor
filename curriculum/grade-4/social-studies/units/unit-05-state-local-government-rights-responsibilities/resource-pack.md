# Resource Pack — Unit 05: State and Local Government: Rights and Responsibilities

**Goal (one sentence):** Give the guiding adult short, grade-4-appropriate
videos, references, and search routes that reinforce the three levels
and three branches of government, the Bill of Rights and the amendments
that expanded voting, how a bill becomes a law, how taxes pay for
services, and how people participate and change laws.
**Age fit:** grade 4 (ages 9–10); the adult previews every video and
co-views with the learner. Rights-history content (slavery, exclusion
from voting) is adult-led at the unit's plain, non-graphic level.

**Fact-check record (2026-11-06):** government structure verified
against Ben's Guide to the U.S. Government (U.S. Government
Publishing Office) and Congress.gov's legislative-process overview
(House 435 members by population; Senate 2 per state, 100 total;
both chambers must agree on the same bill; presidential veto and
override); Bill of Rights verified against Ben's Guide ("Bill of
Rights: 1789-91," full amendment texts, ratified December 15, 1791)
and the National Constitution Center; amendment years verified
against standard sources (13th, 1865; 15th, 1870; 19th, 1920; 26th,
1971); state populations verified against the 2020 U.S. Census
(California 39,538,223, largest; Wyoming 576,851, smallest).
**Caveats for every video below:** no video in this pack was found
as a single verified episode link on 2026-11-06, so all video
entries are clearly labeled preferred-channel search links — the
adult picks one episode, previews it, and checks it against the
unit's framing before showing it. Any video that names current
parties or candidates, or presents one side of a live political
debate as fact, must be paused and corrected out loud, or skipped.
**Free, no-account routes support every core objective:** Ben's
Guide, Congress.gov, the National Constitution Center, USA.gov, and
the internal repository guides are all free to read without an
account; videos supplement teaching, never replace it.
