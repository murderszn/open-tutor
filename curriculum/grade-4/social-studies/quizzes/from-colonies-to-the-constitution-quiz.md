# Grade 4 Social Studies — From Colonies to the Constitution Quiz

**Topic:** From Colonies to the Constitution

**Assignment:** Read **Sections 1, 2, and 4** in the [United States — Understanding & Principles](../../../../resources/united_states_understanding_and_principles.md) guide.

Please answer the following 10 questions based on your reading.

---

**1.** Why did many settlers leave European nations to come to the American colonies in the 1600s and 1700s?

**2.** What does "taxation without representation" mean, and why did it anger the colonists?

**3.** In what year was the Declaration of Independence written, and what did it say about where a government gets its power?

**4.** What was the Revolutionary War fought over, and when did it end?

**5.** What were the Articles of Confederation, and why did they fail?

**6.** What happened at the Constitutional Convention of 1787?

**7.** Name at least four principles built into the U.S. Constitution.

**8.** How is the Constitution amended (changed)? Describe both steps of the process.

**9.** What is a monarchy, and why did the Founders NOT want one for the United States?

**10.** What is a constitutional republic, and how is it different from a monarchy?
