# Grade 4 Social Studies — Government Agencies & Departments Quiz

**Topic:** Government Agencies & Departments

**Assignment:** Read the **Executive Branch** section (Executive Departments and Independent Agencies) in the [Government Basics](../../../../resources/government_basics.md) guide.

Please answer the following 10 questions based on your reading.

---

**1.** Which executive department handles foreign policy and represents the United States to other countries?

**2.** Which department is responsible for managing the U.S. military?

**3.** The Department of Justice (DOJ) enforces federal law. Name one well-known agency that falls under it.

**4.** Which department is responsible for farming, food safety, and rural programs?

**5.** Which department oversees health-related agencies like the CDC and FDA? What do the initials CDC and FDA stand for?

**6.** What does DHS stand for, and name two agencies that operate under it?

**7.** NASA is listed as an independent agency. What is NASA responsible for?

**8.** What does the EPA do, and why is it kept independent from political departments?

**9.** What does the SEC regulate, and why does that matter for everyday Americans?

**10.** Name two executive departments we didn't discuss in the questions above and briefly describe what each one does.
