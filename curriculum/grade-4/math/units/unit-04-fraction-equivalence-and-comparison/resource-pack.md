# Resource Pack — Unit 04: Fraction Equivalence and Comparison

**Goal and age fit (one sentence):** Grade-4 learners explain and
generate equivalent fractions and compare fractions with visual models,
benchmarks, and common denominators — these resources supplement (never
replace) the unit's own lessons, which teach every objective fully
without any account or external site.

**Checked:** 2026-10-30. Video entries below are **clearly labeled
preferred-channel search links**, not verified direct videos — the adult
should preview any video before assigning it, and no title, runtime, or
caption claim is made for them. Web references were opened and verified
on the check date.

## Web references (verified 2026-10-30)

1. **Common Core State Standards, Grade 4 Number & Operations —
   Fractions** — thecorestandards.org:
   <https://www.thecorestandards.org/Math/Content/4/NF/> — Official
   wording of 4.NF.A.1 and 4.NF.A.2, opened and read 2026-10-30, plus
   the footnote limiting grade-4 denominators to 2, 3, 4, 5, 6, 8, 10,
   12, and 100. Teacher reference; the unit paraphrases, never claims
   adoption.
2. **The Math Learning Center, Fractions (free web app)**:
   <https://www.mathlearningcenter.org/apps/fractions> — Bar and circle
   fraction models with denominators 1–100; superimpose fractions to
   compare them or see equal parts. Page opened and verified
   2026-10-30; free, no account required for the web preview. Use for:
   Lessons 01–02 (equivalence models) and Lesson 04 (comparison
   overlays). Note: the app allows denominators beyond the grade-4
   list — the adult selects in-list denominators for this unit.
3. **The Math Learning Center, free manipulatives index**:
   <https://www.mathlearningcenter.org/free-manipulatives> — Paper
   fraction strips and tiles for learners without a device. Listed in
   Unit 03's pack (verified 2026-10-23); not re-opened for this unit,
   so the adult should confirm the page before assigning. Use for:
   all strip-model sessions.

## Free, no-account routes for every core objective

Every core objective is taught in full inside this unit's lessons, with
paper strips, pencil, and household items — no account, video, or website
is required to learn it. The MLC Fractions web preview (ref 2) and the
paper manipulatives index (ref 3) are the free, no-account supplements
for the model work; the CCSS page (ref 1) is a free public teacher
reference. No core objective depends on a video or an account.
