- **Prompt (verbatim):** "Flat, cheerful children's-book illustration of

  daylight, simple shapes, clean background."

- **Date generated:** 2026-10-30.
- **Learning purpose:** gives Lesson 02 a story-prompt input — the
  learner reads the two loaves, names 1/5 and 2/5 for the cut loaf, and
  generates 2/5 = 4/10 by imagining each fifth cut in half. The image
  is an illustration, not a photograph and not evidence of a real bakery.
- **Factual review (inspected before linking, 2026-10-30):** the left
  tray holds one whole loaf; the right tray holds an identical loaf cut
  into 5 strips (counted off the rendered image). No text, letters, or
  numbers appear. The lesson's activity uses exactly this count (5
  strips → fifths), so the activity matches the image.
- **Caption:** "Two identical loaves at a bakery counter. The left loaf
  is whole; the right loaf is cut into 5 equal strips."

  rectangular flatbreads in woven trays: the left loaf whole, the right
  loaf cut into 5 equal strips."

  holds two identical rectangular loaves in woven trays. The left loaf
  is whole. The right loaf is cut into 5 equal strips. Every question
  can be answered from this description alone.
- **Known limitations:** the drawn strips are only approximately equal
  (perspective makes the middle strips look narrower), so the picture
  is a story prompt, not a measuring tool — Lesson 02 says so explicitly
  and directs exact work to the learner's own equal strips or folded
  paper. This is an AI illustration, not a primary source or a record
  of a real bakery.
