# Formative Quiz — Week 16 (Unit 04)

**Session:** 7 · **Time:** 35 minutes · **How to use:** closed-book for
the benchmark card (fraction strips allowed). Show a model or a rewrite
for every item — a bare symbol scores half credit. Score against
[../answer-key.md](../answer-key.md). Reteach any item type below 80%.

1. Fill in the blank: 1/2 = ___/8.
2. Simplify 6/8. Show the division of both numbers.
3. Compare 3/5 and 1/2. Record with `>`, `=`, or `<`, and justify with
   the benchmark.
4. Compare 2/3 and 3/4. Record the symbol and show the rewrites.
5. Compare 5/6 and 5/8. Record the symbol and name your strategy.
6. Compare 7/12 and 5/12. Record the symbol and name your strategy.
7. **Same-whole check:** "Tari ate 2/3 of her cake. Dev ate 2/3 of his
   cake. So they ate the same amount of cake." Is this conclusion valid
   from the fractions alone? Explain.
8. Write two fractions equivalent to 2/5, and name the multiplier for
   each.
9. Order 1/4, 3/8, and 1/2 from least to greatest. Show the rewrites.
10. True or false, with a reason: 3/6 = 1/2.
11. Word problem: "Two identical pizzas are shared. Ann eats 5/8 of one
    pizza. Bo eats 3/4 of the other pizza. Who eats more pizza? Justify
    with a rewrite."
12. **Reasoning check:** "3/4 < 5/6 because 3 < 5 and 4 < 6." The final
    symbol happens to be correct. Is the reasoning valid? Explain, and
    show the correct comparison.
