# Resource Pack — Unit 03: Multi-Digit Multiplication and Division

**Goal and age fit (one sentence):** Grade-4 learners multiply and divide
multi-digit whole numbers with area models, partial products, and partial
quotients, and interpret remainders — these resources supplement (never
replace) the unit's own lessons, which teach every objective fully
without any account or external site.

**Checked:** 2026-10-23. Video entries below are **clearly labeled
preferred-channel search links**, not verified direct videos — the adult
should preview any video before assigning it, and no title, runtime, or
caption claim is made for them. Web references were opened and verified
on the check date, except where noted as a search result listing.

## Free, no-account routes for every core objective

Every core objective is taught in full inside this unit's lessons, with
paper, pencil, and household items — no account, video, or website is
required to learn it. The MLC Number Pieces web preview (ref 5) and the
paper manipulatives index (ref 6) are the free, no-account supplements
for the manipulative work; the CCSS pages (refs 1–2) are free public
teacher references. No core objective depends on a video or an account.
