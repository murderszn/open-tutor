- **Used in:** [Lesson 02](../lessons/lesson-02-area-models-partial-products.md)
  (market-stall image activity: 36 × 14 via the area model) and referenced
  in the unit [README](../README.md) pacing (Session 2).
- **Prompt (verbatim):** "Flat, cheerful children's-book illustration of a
  school market stall with a wooden table stacked with crates of red
  apples. Two large, plain, highly legible signs hang on the stall. The
  first sign reads exactly: 14 CRATES. The second sign reads exactly: 36
  APPLES IN EACH CRATE. No other text, no people, no price tags, bright
  daylight, simple shapes, clean background."
- **Generation tool:** the assistant's built-in image-generation pipeline
  (`media.generate_image`), 2026-10-23. No model name was reported by the
  tool, so none is claimed here.
- **Date generated:** 2026-10-23.
- **Learning purpose:** gives Lesson 02 a real activity input — the
  learner reads the two signs, sets up 36 × 14, and builds the area model
  (300 + 120 + 60 + 24 = 504). The image is an illustration, not a
  photograph and not evidence of a real stall.
- **Factual review (inspected before linking, 2026-10-23):** both signs
  were read off the rendered image and match the prompt exactly —
  "14 CRATES" and "36 APPLES IN EACH CRATE". No other text appears. The
  picture shows only a few crates on the table; it does **not** show 14
  crates, so the lesson never asks learners to count crates in the
  picture — the signs are the data, and the text-only alternative states
  them.
- **Caption:** "A school market stall. Its signs read: 14 crates, 36
  apples in each crate. How many apples are there in all?"
- **Alt text:** "Illustration of a market stall under a yellow-and-blue
  striped awning, with wooden crates of red apples on a table and two
  signs reading '14 CRATES' and '36 APPLES IN EACH CRATE'."
- **Text-only alternative (also printed in Lesson 02):** the market stall
  shows two large signs — 14 CRATES and 36 APPLES IN EACH CRATE. The
  picture shows only a few crates as an illustration; it is not a
  counting picture. Every question can be answered from the two signs
  alone.
- **Known limitations:** the number of crates drawn does not equal 14;
  the apple count per crate is not countable from the drawing. Both
  limitations are handled by the sign-based activity design and the text
  alternative above. This is an AI illustration, not a primary source or
  a record of a real market.
