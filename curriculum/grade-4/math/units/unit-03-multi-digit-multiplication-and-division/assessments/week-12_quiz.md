# Week 12 Formative Quiz — Division and Remainders

**Session:** 7 · **Time:** 20 minutes · **Instructions:** Show partial
quotients on every division item. A final answer without work earns
partial credit only. Items 1–8 are revised from the legacy
`quizzes/division-concepts-quiz.md` (deduplicated — the legacy list asked
130 ÷ 5 twice — and re-sequenced); items 9–10 are new remainder
interpretation items. Scored against
[../answer-key.md](../answer-key.md).

1. Divide: 68 ÷ 4. Show partial quotients and the check.
2. Divide: 285 ÷ 3. Show partial quotients and the check.
3. Divide: 130 ÷ 5. Show partial quotients and the check.
4. Divide: 480 ÷ 6. Show partial quotients and the check.
5. Divide: 492 ÷ 6. Show partial quotients and the check.
6. Divide: 567 ÷ 7. Show partial quotients and the check.
7. Divide: 747 ÷ 9. Show partial quotients and the check.
8. Divide: 1,365 ÷ 5. Show partial quotients and the check.
9. A club has 200 snacks to pack into bags of 7. (a) How many *full*
   bags? (b) How many snacks are left over? (c) If every snack must be
   packed, how many bags are needed?
10. A teacher shares 847 pencils equally among 6 classes, as far as
    possible. How many pencils does each class get, and how many are left
    over? Show partial quotients and the check, and write one sentence
    saying what the remainder means here.

**Scoring:** 1 point per division item (items 1–8, including the check),
3 points for item 9 (one per part), 3 points for item 10 (quotient,
remainder with check, interpretation sentence). Total: 14 points. Below
11 points: reteach partial quotients and remainder interpretation before
Week 13.
