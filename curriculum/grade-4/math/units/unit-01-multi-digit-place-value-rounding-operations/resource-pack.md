# Resource Pack — Unit 01: Multi-Digit Place Value, Rounding, and Operations

**Goal:** support an adult teaching a grade-4 learner to generalize place value
to 1,000,000, round to any place, add and subtract multi-digit numbers with the
standard algorithm, and estimate to check reasonableness. **Age fit:** grade 4
(ages 9–10); adult-led, 35-minute sessions.

All external links were checked on **2026-10-10**. Direct pages were opened
and read; video links are clearly labeled preferred-channel **search links**
(the adult previews before showing). Every core objective has a free,
no-account route.

## Web references (verified 2026-10-10)

1. **Common Core State Standards — Grade 4, Number & Operations in Base Ten**
   `https://www.thecorestandards.org/Math/Content/4/NBT/`
   Official source for the 4.NBT codes used in this unit. Opened 2026-10-10:
   the page renders 4.NBT.A.1 (with the 700 ÷ 70 = 10 example), 4.NBT.A.2,
   4.NBT.B.5, and 4.NBT.B.6, plus the footnote limiting grade-4 expectations to
   whole numbers ≤ 1,000,000; the 4.NBT.A.3 and 4.NBT.B.4 wordings were
   corroborated during the track audit (2026-10-03). Adult reference; no
   account needed.
2. **The Math Learning Center — free math apps**
   `https://www.mathlearningcenter.org/apps`
   Free web apps with no account required for basic use: **Number Line**
   (rounding benchmarks — "visualize and work with numbers in sequence on a
   virtual number line") and **Number Pieces** (virtual base-ten pieces for
   multi-digit place value). Opened 2026-10-10; confirmed free and
   browser-based.
3. **Wikipedia — Rounding**
   `https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Rounding`
   Background on rounding conventions, including the round-half-up rule this
   unit uses. Opened and read 2026-10-10 (recorded in the sibling grade-3
   unit's resource pack, delivered the same day). Adult reference — the
   article's reading level is above grade 4.
4. **Repository: `resources/math_fundamentals.md`** — adult/teacher-side
   reference only (its band is grades 4–7, in-band for this unit per the track
   audit): consult its Place Value section (to millions) when writing reteach
   plans. Never assigned to the learner whole.
5. **Repository: unit SVG `assets/rounding-number-line-hundreds.svg`** — the
   reproducible 4,700–4,900 number-line zoom used in Lesson 03; print it for
   the learner's desk.
6. **Repository: generated illustration
   `assets/estimation-market-scene.png`** — the Session 7 / Lesson 04 market
   estimation activity image (AI illustration; amounts are fictional practice
   data).
