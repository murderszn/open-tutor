# Formative Quiz — Unit 01 (Week 4 check)

**Session:** 15 · **Time:** 20 minutes · **Coverage:** objectives 1–5
**Scoring:** see [answer-key.md](../answer-key.md#week-04-quiz). 8/10 meets;
below 8, reteach the missed objectives before the unit assessment.

*Show your work for every computation: estimate first, compute exactly, then a
one-word verdict.*

1. In 73,508, what does the 7 represent? In 7,358, what does the 7 represent?
   How many times greater is the first value than the second?
2. Write 408,615 in expanded form.
3. Compare with `>`, `=`, or `<`: 621,440 ○ 612,440. Explain with places.
4. Round 56,789 to the nearest thousand. Show the digit that decides.
5. Round 123,456 to the nearest ten thousand. Show the benchmarks.
6. 45,678 + 27,345 = ? Estimate first, then compute.
7. 80,000 − 36,254 = ? Estimate first, compute, and check with addition.
8. A classmate says 62,410 + 38,295 = 100,705. Estimate to the nearest
   thousand, give a verdict, then confirm by computing exactly.
9. **Error analysis:** "Sam says 9,965 rounded to the nearest hundred is
   9,900." Explain Sam's mistake and give the correct rounding.
10. A stadium held 75,209 fans on Saturday and 68,847 on Sunday. About how many
    more fans came Saturday? Estimate first, then compute exactly.
