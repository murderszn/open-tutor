# Practice — Unit 02 Mixed Practice Set

**Use:** Session 14 mixed review (and any reteach session). All solutions are
in [answer-key.md](answer-key.md#practice); do not copy them here.

## A. Factor pairs (objective 1)

1. List **all** factor pairs of **42**, in order, using the hunt routine.
2. List **all** factor pairs of **56**, in order.
3. Which number has more factor pairs — **72** or **90**? Show both full lists
   and give the verdict.
4. List **all** factor pairs of **100**. What ends the hunt here?
5. True or false: "The factor pairs of 16 are (1, 16), (2, 8), (4, 4) — and
   that is all." Justify.

## B. Multiples (objective 2)

6. List the first six multiples of **8**.
7. Is **63** a multiple of 7? Is it a multiple of 8? Show the partner hunt
   for each.
8. "45 is a multiple of each of its factors." List the factor pairs of 45,
   then write every one-digit number that 45 is a multiple of.
9. Find the smallest number that is a multiple of **6** and also a multiple
   of **8**. Show your lists.

## C. Prime and composite (objective 3)

10. Classify each as prime, composite, or neither, with a one-sentence
    factor-list justification: **29, 33, 51, 2**.
11. True or false: "91 is prime because it ends in 1." Explain with factors.
12. List **all** the prime numbers between 50 and 70. Show how you know none
    are missing.

## D. Patterns (objective 4)

13. Rule "Add 4", start 3: generate 8 terms, name a hidden feature, and
    explain informally why it continues.
14. Rule "multiply by 2", start 5: generate 7 terms. Name a hidden feature
    about the ones digits and explain it.
15. **Predict:** rule "Add 7", start 4. Without writing all the terms, what
    is the parity (odd/even) of the 30th term? Defend your answer.
16. Square-dot pattern (1, 4, 9, 16, …): how many dots are in figure 8?
    Show the gap reasoning from Lesson 05.

## E. Multiplication strategies (objective 5)

17. Compute **9 × 12** by breaking 12 into known facts. Show the array cut.
18. Compute **16 × 5** by doubling/halving. Explain the restack.
19. Compute **6 × 18** two ways: breaking into known facts AND
    doubling/halving. Both must agree — show they do.
20. **Error analysis:** "Mia listed the factors of 48 as 1, 2, 3, 4, 6, 8,
    12, 16, 24. She says 48 has 9 factors, and since 9 is odd, 48 must be a
    square number." Find every mistake in her work and reasoning, and give the
    correct factor list.
