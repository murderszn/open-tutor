# Formative Quiz — Unit 02 (Week 10 check)

**Session:** 14 · **Time:** 20 minutes · **Coverage:** objectives 1–5
**Scoring:** see [answer-key.md](../answer-key.md#week-10-quiz). 8/10 meets;
below 8, reteach the missed objectives before the unit assessment.

*Show the hunt routine, the partner check, or the array reasoning for every
answer — a bare number earns no credit.*

1. List **all** factor pairs of **28**, in order. What ends your hunt?
2. List **all** factor pairs of **49**. Is 49 prime or composite? Justify
   with the definition.
3. Is **81** a multiple of 9? Is it a multiple of 7? Show each check.
4. "60 is a multiple of each of its factors." Write the factor pairs of 60,
   then circle every one-digit factor of 60 in your list.
5. Classify each with a one-sentence justification: **1, 17, 39, 97**.
6. Rule "Add 6", start 1: generate 6 terms. Name a hidden feature and explain
   informally why it continues.
7. Rule "multiply by 2", start 7: generate 5 terms. What hidden feature do you
   see after the first term? Explain it.
8. Compute **14 × 6** by doubling/halving. Then compute **9 × 15** by
   breaking into known facts.
9. Sketch a rectangle for **5 × 12**. Cut it into two smaller rectangles that
   show 5 × 12 = (5 × 10) + (5 × 2), and label each piece.
10. **Retrieval:** round 5,682 to the nearest hundred, then estimate 31 × 3.
    (Unit 01 skills still count.)
