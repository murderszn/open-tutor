# Answer Key — Unit 05: Fraction Addition, Subtraction, and Whole-Number Products

**For the guiding adult only.** Every question in the lessons, practice
set, quiz, assessment, and project is solved here, independently of the
student pages. Do not let the learner read this file while working.
Accept equivalent forms (for example, 1 2/6 for 1 1/3) when the reasoning
is shown; accept any valid decomposition whose parts add back to the
original numerator.

## Lesson 01 — Joining parts: adding fractions

- **Image activity:** 8 strips in the tray, 2 on the plate, 10 equal
  strips in all. Plate: 2/10. Tray: 8/10. Together:
  2/10 + 8/10 = 10/10 = 1 whole flatbread.
- **Worked example 1:** 3/8 + 2/8 = 5/8 of the ribbon colored; 3/8
  remains uncolored.
- **Worked example 2:** 5/6 + 4/6 = 9/6 = 1 3/6 = **1 1/2 sandwiches**.
- **Guided 1:** 1/4 + 2/4 = **3/4**.
- **Guided 2:** 2/5 + 2/5 = **4/5**.
- **Guided 3:** 7/10 + 5/10 = 12/10 = **1 2/10 = 1 1/5**. Yes, greater
  than 1.
- **Guided 4 (Lee):** wrong — the pieces stay thirds. 1/3 + 1/3 = **2/3**.
- **Independent 5:** 4/12 + 5/12 = 9/12 = **3/4**.
- **Independent 6:** 3/8 + 5/8 = 8/8 = **1**.
- **Independent 7:** 2/3 + 2/3 = 4/3 = **1 1/3**.
- **Independent 8 (Ana):** wrong — denominators do not add.
  3/10 + 4/10 = **7/10**.
- **Applied:** 2/5 + 1/5 = **3/5**.
- **Exit:** 4/6 + 1/6 = **5/6**.

## Lesson 02 — Separating parts: subtracting fractions

- **Worked example 1:** 7/8 − 3/8 = 4/8 = **1/2 of the ribbon remains**.
  Check: 4/8 + 3/8 = 7/8.
- **Worked example 2:** 1 = 8/8; 8/8 − 5/8 = **3/8 of the sandwich left**.
  Check: 3/8 + 5/8 = 1.
- **Guided 1:** 5/6 − 1/6 = 4/6 = **2/3**.
- **Guided 2:** 4/5 − 2/5 = **2/5**. Check: 2/5 + 2/5 = 4/5.
- **Guided 3:** 2 = 12/6; 12/6 − 5/6 = 7/6 = **1 1/6**.
- **Guided 4 (Jay):** wrong — subtracting does not change the piece
  size, and a denominator of 0 is impossible. 7/10 − 3/10 = 4/10 = **2/5**.
- **Independent 5:** 9/12 − 5/12 = 4/12 = **1/3**.
- **Independent 6:** 1 = 4/4; 4/4 − 3/4 = **1/4**.
- **Independent 7:** 11/10 − 6/10 = 5/10 = **1/2**, which is less than 1
  (no mixed number).
- **Independent 8 (Priya):** wrong — denominators do not subtract.
  5/8 − 2/8 = **3/8**.
- **Applied:** 6/8 − 2/8 = 4/8 = **1/2**. Check: 4/8 + 2/8 = 6/8.
- **Exit:** 5/6 − 2/6 = 3/6 = **1/2**. Check: 1/2 + 2/6 = 3/6 + 2/6 = 5/6.

## Lesson 03 — Decomposing a fraction

- **Worked example 1 (5/6):** 1/6 + 1/6 + 1/6 + 1/6 + 1/6; 2/6 + 3/6;
  also 1/6 + 4/6. Checks: 1+1+1+1+1 = 5; 2+3 = 5; 1+4 = 5.
- **Worked example 2 (2 1/8):** 1 + 1 + 1/8; 8/8 + 8/8 + 1/8 (= 17/8);
  also 1 1/8 + 1 = 9/8 + 8/8.
- **Guided 1:** 3/8 = 1/8 + 1/8 + 1/8 and 3/8 = 1/8 + 2/8.
- **Guided 2:** 7/10 = 3/10 + 4/10 and 7/10 = 1/10 + 6/10.
- **Guided 3:** 4/5 = 2/5 + 2/5 and 4/5 = 1/5 + 1/5 + 1/5 + 1/5.
- **Guided 4 (Noah):** wrong — 2 + 2 = 4, not 5. Correct: 5/6 = 2/6 + 3/6.
- **Independent 5:** 6/8 = 3/8 + 3/8 and 6/8 = 1/8 + 5/8.
- **Independent 6:** 1 2/3 = 1 + 2/3 and 1 2/3 = 3/3 + 2/3 (= 5/3).
- **Independent 7:** 9/12 = 4/12 + 5/12 and 9/12 = 3/12 + 3/12 + 3/12.
- **Independent 8 (Maya):** wrong — 5/10 + 5/10 = 10/10 = 1, not 7/10.
  Correct: 7/10 = 5/10 + 2/10.
- **Applied:** 4/6 = 1/6 + 1/6 + 1/6 + 1/6; 4/6 = 2/6 + 2/6;
  4/6 = 3/6 + 1/6. Checks: 1+1+1+1 = 4; 2+2 = 4; 3+1 = 4.
- **Exit:** 3/4 = 1/4 + 1/4 + 1/4 and 3/4 = 1/4 + 2/4.

## Lesson 04 — Adding and subtracting mixed numbers

- **Worked example 1:** 2 1/4 + 1 2/4 = **3 3/4 cups**. Check:
  9/4 + 6/4 = 15/4 = 3 3/4.
- **Worked example 2:** 4 1/8 = 3 9/8; 3 9/8 − 2 5/8 = 1 4/8 =
  **1 1/2 meters**. Check: 1 1/2 + 2 5/8 = 4 1/8.
- **Guided 1:** 1 3/5 + 2 1/5 = **3 4/5**.
- **Guided 2:** 3 2/3 − 1 1/3 = **2 1/3** (no regrouping: 2/3 > 1/3).
- **Guided 3:** 2 7/8 + 1 5/8 = 3 12/8 = **4 1/2**.
- **Guided 4 (Kim):** wrong — the fractions were subtracted backwards.
  Regroup: 3 1/4 = 2 5/4; 2 5/4 − 1 3/4 = 1 2/4 = **1 1/2**.
- **Independent 5:** 2 3/10 + 1 6/10 = **3 9/10**.
- **Independent 6:** 4 5/6 − 3 1/6 = 1 4/6 = **1 2/3**.
- **Independent 7:** 1 5/12 + 2 7/12 = 3 12/12 = **4** (the fraction part
  makes a whole).
- **Independent 8 (Alex):** 3 9/8 is unfinished: 9/8 = 1 1/8, so the
  answer is **4 1/8**.
- **Applied:** 3 2/5 = 2 7/5; 2 7/5 − 1 4/5 = **1 3/5**.
- **Exit:** 2 5/6 − 1 1/6 = 1 4/6 = **1 2/3**. Check:
  1 2/3 + 1 1/6 = 1 4/6 + 1 1/6 = 2 5/6.

## Lesson 05 — Multiplying a fraction by a whole number

- **Worked example 1:** 5/4 = 5 × (1/4) = **1 1/4**.
- **Worked example 2:** 3 × (2/5) = 6/5 = **1 1/5**. Check:
  2/5 + 2/5 + 2/5 = 6/5.
- **Worked example 3:** 5 × (3/8) = 15/8 = **1 7/8 pounds**, between
  **1 and 2**.
- **Guided 1:** 4/3 = 4 × (1/3) = **1 1/3**.
- **Guided 2:** 2 × (3/4) = 6/4 = **1 1/2**.
- **Guided 3:** 6 × (2/8) = 12/8 = **1 1/2**.
- **Guided 4 (Sam):** wrong — multiplying by a whole number makes more
  pieces, not smaller ones. 3 × (2/5) = 6/5 = **1 1/5**.
- **Independent 5:** 5 × (2/3) = 10/3 = **3 1/3**.
- **Independent 6:** 4 × (3/10) = 12/10 = **1 1/5**.
- **Independent 7:** 7 × (5/6) = 35/6 = **5 5/6 meters**, between
  **5 and 6**.
- **Independent 8 (Zoe):** wrong — the denominator stays 4.
  2 × (3/4) = 6/4 = **1 1/2**.
- **Applied:** 4 × (2/3) = 8/3 = **2 2/3 sandwiches** (2 whole
  sandwiches and 2/3 of another).
- **Exit:** 3 × (1/6) = 3/6 = **1/2**.
