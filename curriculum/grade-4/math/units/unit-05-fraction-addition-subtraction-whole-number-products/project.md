# Project — Fraction Picnic Plan

**Sessions:** 10, 12, 13 · **Time:** three 35-minute sessions (draft,
finish, present) · **Goal:** plan a picnic for a group using fraction
addition, subtraction, decomposition, and fraction-by-whole-number
multiplication — and prove every amount with a model.

## The job

You are planning a picnic. Every flatbread is baked in the **same-size
pan**, every sandwich loaf is the same size, and every fruit bar is the
same size — so every fraction in your plan refers to a stated whole.
Your plan must let an adult see, at a glance, how much food each part
of the picnic needs, how much is left over, and that your arithmetic is
right.

## Deliverables

1. **Three food models.** Draw three identical-length bars for three
   foods, each cut into equal parts from the grade-4 denominator list
   (2, 3, 4, 5, 6, 8, 10, 12): a flatbread cut into 8, a sandwich loaf
   cut into 10, and a fruit bar cut into 12. Shade one portion on each
   bar and label it (for example, 3/8 of the flatbread, 7/10 of the
   loaf, 5/12 of the fruit bar). At least one portion must be greater
   than 1/2 (so the plan is not all small pieces).
2. **Serving table.** Three rows, each with a model, an equation, and
   an answer (as a mixed number where needed):
   - Row A — fraction times a whole number: 6 people each eat 1/4 of
     a pie. How many pies is that?
   - Row B — adding mixed numbers: the fruit table needs 1 3/10 liters
     of juice in the morning and 2 6/10 liters in the afternoon. How
     many liters in all?
   - Row C — subtracting mixed numbers: the sandwich table starts with
     3 1/6 meters of ribbon for decorations and uses 1 5/6 meters. How
     much ribbon is left?
3. **Leftovers.** Start with 2 whole flatbreads. In the morning the
   group serves 1 3/8 of a flatbread; at lunch it serves 4/8 more. How
   much flatbread is left? Show the two servings added together, then
   subtracted from 2 wholes, with a strip model for each step.
4. **Decomposition poster.** Show 7/10 in three ways — all unit
   fractions, two chunks, and one chunk plus a remainder — with a strip
   model for each way and a numerator-add-back check under each equation.

## Materials

Paper strips, grid paper, colored pencils, ruler, the unit's
[`assets/sandwich-tray-strips.png`](assets/sandwich-tray-strips.png)
as a story prompt (the picture's strips are a picnic scene, not your
measuring tool — draw your own equal strips for exact work).

## Steps

1. **Session 10 — Draft.** Choose your three cuttings and shade your
   three portions. Build the serving table (deliverable 2). Check: are
   all three bars in deliverable 1 exactly the same length? If not,
   redraw — different-length bars break every comparison.
2. **Session 12 — Finish.** Do the leftovers computation (deliverable 3)
   and the decomposition poster (deliverable 4). Self-check every
   equation against a strip model and every decomposition with a
   numerator-add-back check.
3. **Session 13 — Present.** Present the serving table and the leftovers
   computation to the adult (or a partner). Then check a peer's plan for
   an added-denominators error or a different-whole error: are all their
   bars the same length? Did they add any denominators?

## Rubric (adult scores; 3 = strong, 2 = developing, 1 = needs reteach)

| Criterion | 3 — Strong | 2 — Developing | 1 — Needs reteach |
|---|---|---|---|
| Food models (deliverable 1) | Three same-length bars, correctly cut and labeled; at least one portion greater than 1/2 | Bars mostly same length or one label wrong | Bars differ in length, or cuttings/labels wrong |
| Serving table (deliverable 2) | All three rows correct with model, equation, and mixed-number answer where needed | Two rows correct, or one row missing its model/equation | One or fewer rows correct, or denominators added anywhere |
| Leftovers (deliverable 3) | Servings added correctly (1 7/8), subtracted from 2 wholes correctly (1/8 left), models for each step | One step correct, or the final answer right without models | Servings not combined, or subtraction from 2 wholes wrong |
| Decomposition poster (deliverable 4) | 7/10 in three valid ways, each with a model and a correct add-back check | Two valid ways, or one check missing/wrong | One or fewer valid ways, or parts that do not add back to 7/10 |

## Sample solutions (for the adult — learner work must show its own models)

- Row A: 6 × (1/4) = 6/4 = 1 2/4 = **1 1/2 pies**.
- Row B: 1 3/10 + 2 6/10 = 3 9/10 liters.
- Row C: 3 1/6 − 1 5/6 = 2 7/6 − 1 5/6 = **1 2/6 = 1 1/3 meters**.
- Leftovers: 1 3/8 + 4/8 = 1 7/8 served; 2 − 1 7/8 = 16/8 − 15/8 =
  **1/8 of a flatbread left**.
- Poster: 7/10 = 1/10 + 1/10 + 1/10 + 1/10 + 1/10 + 1/10 + 1/10;
  7/10 = 3/10 + 4/10; 7/10 = 2/10 + 5/10. Checks: 1+1+1+1+1+1+1 = 7;
  3 + 4 = 7; 2 + 5 = 7.
