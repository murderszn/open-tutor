# Formative Quiz — Week 20

**Session:** 7 · **Time:** 20 minutes · **How to use:** closed-book.
Draw a strip model or show equation work for every item. Score against
[../answer-key.md](../answer-key.md) — the key is a separate file; do not
read it while working. Reteach any item type below 80%.

This quiz replaces the legacy duplicate quizzes
(`quizzes/adding-subtracting-fractions-quiz.md` and
`quizzes/adding-subtracting-fractions-quiz-set-02.md`): those two files
are exact duplicates (they differ only in the title line), their 20
items use denominators 7 and 9 (outside the grade-4 denominator list),
repeat items, contain no subtraction, and have no key. Every item below
uses an in-band denominator (2, 3, 4, 5, 6, 8, 10, 12, 100).

1. Compute 3/8 + 4/8.
2. Compute 7/10 − 2/10. Simplify the answer.
3. Compute 5/6 + 3/6. Write the answer as a mixed number.
4. Compute 1 − 3/4. Show the rewrite of 1 first.
5. Write 7/8 as a sum in two different ways. Check each by adding the
   numerators.
6. Compute 2 1/5 + 1 3/5.
7. Compute 4 5/6 − 2 1/6. Simplify the fraction part.
8. Compute 3 × (2/5). Write the answer as a mixed number.
9. Word problem: "Mia ran 3/10 of a mile in the morning and 4/10 of a
   mile in the afternoon. How far did she run in all?"
10. **Error analysis:** "Sam says 2/5 + 3/5 = 5/10." Explain why Sam is
    wrong, and give the correct answer.
