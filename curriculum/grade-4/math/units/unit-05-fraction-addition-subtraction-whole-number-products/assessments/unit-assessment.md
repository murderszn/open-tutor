# Culminating Assessment — Unit 05

**Session:** 16 · **Time:** 35 minutes · **How to use:** closed-book and
independent. Draw a strip model or show equation work for every item.
Total: **18 marks**. Score against [../answer-key.md](../answer-key.md)
— the key is a separate file; do not read it while working. Readiness
for Unit 06: 14 marks or more, with no objective scoring below half
marks (see the teacher guide).

1. (1 mark) Compute 5/8 + 2/8.
2. (1 mark) Compute 9/10 − 4/10. Simplify the answer.
3. (1 mark) Compute 3/4 + 3/4. Write the answer as a mixed number.
4. (2 marks) Write 5/6 as a sum in two different ways, and check each
   by adding the numerators.
5. (1 mark) Compute 1 − 2/3. Show the rewrite of 1 first.
6. (1 mark) Compute 3 2/5 + 1 1/5.
7. (1 mark) Compute 5 3/8 − 2 7/8. Show the regrouping step.
8. (1 mark) Compute 4 × (3/8). Write the answer as a mixed number.
9. (1 mark) Word problem: "A ribbon is 4/5 of a meter long. 1/5 of a
   meter is cut off. How much ribbon is left?"
10. (2 marks) Word problem: "If each person at a party will eat 3/8 of
    a pound of roast beef, and there will be 5 people at the party, how
    many pounds of roast beef will be needed? Between what two whole
    numbers does your answer lie?"
11. (1 mark) Compute 2 5/12 + 1 7/12.
12. (2 marks) **Error analysis:** "Lee says 1/2 + 1/2 = 2/4." Explain
    why Lee is wrong, and give the correct answer.
13. (2 marks) Write 2 3/10 as a sum in two different ways.
14. (1 mark) Word problem: "A recipe needs 2 3/4 cups of flour. You have
    already added 1 1/4 cups. How much more flour is needed?"
