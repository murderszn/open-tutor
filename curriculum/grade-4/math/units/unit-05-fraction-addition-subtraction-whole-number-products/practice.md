# Practice — Unit 05: Fraction Addition, Subtraction, and Whole-Number Products

**Session:** 11 · **Time:** 35 minutes · **How to use:** complete in
order. Draw a strip model for every add/subtract item until the adult
says models are optional. Show every numerator-count step. Simplify
fraction parts of mixed-number answers. Score against
[answer-key.md](answer-key.md) — the key is a separate file; do not read
it while working.

## A. Adding and subtracting (Lessons 01–02)

1. Draw a bar split into eighths. Shade 2/8, then shade 3 more eighths.
   Write the addition equation for the total shaded amount.
2. Compute 7/12 − 5/12. Simplify the answer.
3. Compute 4/5 + 3/5. Write the answer as a mixed number.
4. Compute 1 − 7/10. Show the rewrite of 1 first.
5. Compute 9/10 − 3/10. Simplify the answer.
6. **Error analysis:** "6/8 + 1/8 = 7/16, because I added the tops and
   the bottoms." Explain the error and give the correct answer.

## B. Decomposing (Lesson 03)

7. Write 4/5 as a sum in two different ways. Check each by adding the
   numerators.
8. Write 1 3/4 as a sum in two ways: one keeping the whole as 1, one
   writing the whole as fourths.
9. Write 8/10 as a sum of two fractions, then as a sum of four fractions.
10. **Error analysis:** "6/8 = 3/8 + 4/8." Explain the error and give a
    correct decomposition.

## C. Mixed numbers (Lesson 04)

11. Compute 1 2/3 + 2 1/3. What happens to the fraction part?
12. Compute 5 3/8 − 3 6/8. Show the regrouping step.
13. Compute 2 1/6 + 3 5/6. Write the answer as a whole number.
14. Compute 4 − 2 3/5. Show the rewrite of 4 first.

## D. Fraction times a whole number (Lesson 05)

15. Compute 4 × (2/3). Write the answer as a mixed number.
16. Word problem: "Three bottles each hold 5/8 of a liter of juice. How
    many liters of juice is that in all? Between what two whole numbers
    does your answer lie?"

## E. Legacy-frame set (adapted from `assignments/fraction-word-problem-workshop.md`)

The legacy workshop asked for 8 story problems, a model for at least 4
of them, an inverse-or-estimate check, and 2 original problems — but
gave no teaching and no key. Now that Lessons 01–05 have taught the
skills, complete its frame properly:

17. Solve legacy problems 1–4 and 8 (all like-denominator
    addition/subtraction). Draw a visual model for at least 4 of the 5,
    and check each answer with an inverse operation or an estimate.
    - Add 2/8 hour and 3/8 hour of practice.
    - Find the distance remaining in a 7/10-mile route after 4/10 mile.
    - Add 1/6 and 2/6 of a tray.
    - Subtract 2/5 of a ribbon from 4/5.
    - A 3/4-hour session is shortened by 1/4 hour. How long remains?
18. Solve legacy problems 5–7 (Unit 04 retrieval — these are not
    addition/subtraction, so use what Unit 04 taught):
    - How many 1/4 portions make a whole?
    - Compare 3/8 and 1/2 of the same-size pizza. Which is greater?
      Justify.
    - Find an amount equivalent to 2/3 using sixths.
19. Write 2 original fraction word problems of your own — one addition
    or subtraction problem and one fraction-by-whole-number problem —
    and solve each with a model and an equation. Write the answer key
    for your own problems on a separate sheet.
20. **Unit 03 bridge (multistep):** "A baker makes 4 trays of 28 rolls.
    Then 96 rolls are sold. How many rolls remain?" Represent the problem
    with an equation using a letter for the unknown, and assess the
    reasonableness of your answer with an estimate.
