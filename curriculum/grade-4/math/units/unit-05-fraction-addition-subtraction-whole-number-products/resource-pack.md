# Resource Pack — Unit 05: Fraction Addition, Subtraction, and Whole-Number Products

**Goal and age fit (one sentence):** Grade-4 learners add and subtract
fractions with like denominators, decompose fractions, work with mixed
numbers, and multiply a fraction by a whole number — these resources
supplement (never replace) the unit's own lessons, which teach every
objective fully without any account or external site.

**Checked:** 2026-11-06. Video entries below are **clearly labeled

## Web references (verified 2026-11-06)

1. **Common Core State Standards, Grade 4 Number & Operations —
   Fractions** — thecorestandards.org:
   <https://www.thecorestandards.org/Math/Content/4/NF/> — Official
   wording of 4.NF.B.3.a–d and 4.NF.B.4.a–c, opened and read 2026-11-06,
   including the standard's own examples (3/8 decompositions;
   2 1/8 = 1 + 1 + 1/8; 5/4 = 5 × (1/4); 3 × (2/5) = 6/5; the
   roast-beef problem) and the footnote limiting grade-4 denominators
   to 2, 3, 4, 5, 6, 8, 10, 12, and 100. Teacher reference; the unit
   paraphrases, never claims adoption.
2. **The Math Learning Center, Fractions (free web app)**:
   <https://www.mathlearningcenter.org/apps/fractions> — Bar and circle
   fraction models with denominators 1–100; superimpose fractions to
   compare them or see equal parts. Page opened and verified
   2026-11-06; free, no account required for the web preview. Use for:
   Lessons 01–02 (joining/separating with bars) and Lesson 05 (unit
   fractions as repeated parts). Note: the app allows denominators
   beyond the grade-4 list — the adult selects in-list denominators
   for this unit.
3. **Math is Fun — Fractions**:
   <https://www.mathsisfun.com/fractions.html> — Same-denominator
   addition worked with pizza models (1/4 + 1/4 = 2/4 = 1/2;
   5/8 + 1/8 = 6/8 = 3/4), with the explicit warning "Don't add the
   denominators together." Page opened and verified 2026-11-06; free,
   no account. Use for: Lessons 01–02. Note: the page also covers
   unlike-denominator addition further down — the adult should keep the
   learner on the same-denominator section, since unlike-denominator
   work is not a grade-4 requirement.
4. **The Math Learning Center, free manipulatives index**:
   <https://www.mathlearningcenter.org/free-manipulatives> — Free paper
   fraction strips and tiles for learners without a device. Page opened
   and verified 2026-11-06; the paper pieces are free, while some linked
   lesson suggestions ask for an educator-site account. Use for: all
   strip-model sessions.

## Free, no-account routes for every core objective

Every core objective is taught in full inside this unit's lessons, with
paper strips, pencil, and household items — no account, video, or website
is required to learn it. The MLC Fractions web preview (ref 2) and Math
is Fun (ref 3) are the free, no-account supplements for the model work;
the paper manipulatives index (ref 4) covers learners without a device;
the CCSS page (ref 1) is a free public teacher reference. No core
objective depends on a video or an account.
