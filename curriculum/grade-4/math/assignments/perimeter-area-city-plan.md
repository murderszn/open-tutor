# Perimeter and Area City Plan (Grade 4 Math)

## Objective
Design a mini city map while applying perimeter and area.

## Tasks
1. Draw at least 6 city blocks on grid paper.
2. Calculate perimeter and area for each block.
3. Label one park, one school, and one library with dimensions.
4. Answer reflection questions about total park space.

## Deliverable
- Labeled city map.
- Perimeter/area table.
- Reflection answers.

## Reflection Questions
Which block has the largest area? Which has the longest perimeter? How much area is park space? How could you increase park space without changing the outside boundary?
