# Probability Experiment Lab - Learner

Shared spec: Shared reference(use the selected grade’s private workspace)

## Learner Checklist

- [ ] Choose a coin, number cube, spinner, or bag draw experiment.
- [ ] List the possible outcomes.
- [ ] Make a prediction before starting.
- [ ] Run at least 30 trials and tally every result.
- [ ] Make a table and bar graph.
- [ ] Write 4-6 sentences explaining what happened most, least, and whether the results matched your prediction.
