# Decimal Money Math Market (Grade 4 Math)

## Objective
Practice adding and subtracting decimals in money contexts.

## Tasks
1. Build a pretend market list with 12 items and prices.
2. Solve 10 purchase scenarios with a budget.
3. Compute change due for each scenario.
4. Identify three ways to stay under budget.

## Deliverable
- Price list.
- Solved scenarios.
- Budget strategy notes.

## Ten Scenario Prompts
Use your fictional price list to calculate: two different items paid from $20; three items paid from $30; two copies of one item; three copies of another; four items paid from $50; the change from $40 after two purchases; the difference between two basket totals; a basket totaling less than $15; replacing an item with a cheaper one; and the greatest number of one item affordable under $25. State your chosen prices and quantities.
