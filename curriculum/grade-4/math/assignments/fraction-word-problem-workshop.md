# Fraction Word Problem Workshop (Grade 4 Math)

## Objective
Solve real-life fraction word problems using drawings and equations.

## Tasks
1. Solve 8 fraction story problems (pizza, time, distance, and money).
2. Draw a visual model for at least 4 problems.
3. Check each answer with an inverse or estimate.
4. Write 2 original fraction word problems.

## Deliverable
- Completed problem set.
- Visual models.
- Two original problems + answer key.

## Eight Blank Story Problems
1. Add 2/8 hour and 3/8 hour of practice.
2. Find the distance remaining in a 7/10-mile route after 4/10 mile.
3. Add 1/6 and 2/6 of a tray.
4. Subtract 2/5 of a ribbon from 4/5.
5. How many 1/4 portions make a whole?
6. Compare 3/8 and 1/2 of the same-size pizza.
7. Find an amount equivalent to 2/3 using sixths.
8. A 3/4-hour session is shortened by 1/4 hour. How long remains?
Show a model for four problems and check the units.
