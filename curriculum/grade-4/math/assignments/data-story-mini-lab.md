# Data Story Mini Lab - Learner

Shared spec: Shared reference(use the selected grade’s private workspace)

## Learner Checklist

- [ ] Choose one data question.
- [ ] Collect at least 8 data points.
- [ ] Create a table and bar graph or line plot.
- [ ] Identify the largest value, smallest value, and range.
- [ ] Write 4-6 sentences explaining what the numbers show.

Submit the table, graph, and data story.
