# Data Graphs and Comparisons (Grade 4 Math)

## Objective
Collect, graph, and interpret data using line plots and bar graphs.

## Tasks
1. Collect one week of data (weather, reading minutes, steps, etc.).
2. Make one bar graph and one line plot.
3. Write 5 comparison statements using more than/less than language.
4. Answer 6 interpretation questions.

## Deliverable
- Raw data table.
- Two graphs.
- Comparison and analysis responses.

## Six Interpretation Questions
What is the greatest value? What is the least? What is the range? What value occurs most often? Which two values differ most? What limitation could affect your conclusion?
