# Learner — Fractions & Equivalent Models (Grade 4)

## Goal
Show and compare equivalent fractions.

## Tasks
1. Draw at least 6 fraction models (circles, bars, or grids).
2. Show 3 equivalent fraction pairs (example: 1/2 = 2/4).
3. Put 5 fractions in order from least to greatest.
4. Write one real-life fraction story problem and solve it.
