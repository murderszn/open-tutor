# Learner — Multi-Digit Math Workshop (Grade 4)

## Goal
Practice place value and multi-digit operations.

## Tasks
1. Solve 6 multi-digit addition/subtraction problems.
2. Solve 4 multiplication problems (2-digit by 1-digit or 2-digit).
3. Explain one strategy you used (place value, partial products, or regrouping).
4. Check 2 answers using a different method.
