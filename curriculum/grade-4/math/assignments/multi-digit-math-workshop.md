# Learner — Multi-Digit Math Workshop (Grade 4)

## Goal
Practice place value and multi-digit operations.

## Prerequisites
Read and write multi-digit numbers by place value; know basic multiplication facts and be ready to regroup in addition and subtraction. Use place-value blocks or a written place-value chart if helpful.

## Tasks
1. Solve 6 multi-digit addition/subtraction problems.
2. Solve 4 multiplication problems (2-digit by 1-digit or 2-digit).
3. Explain one strategy you used (place value, partial products, or regrouping).
4. Check 2 answers using a different method.

## Submit
Submit all 10 answers, show one place-value or partial-products strategy, and check two answers with a different method. Include enough written work for an adult to follow your reasoning.

## Practice Set
Addition/subtraction: 2,486+3,759; 5,204−1,876; 6,318+2,047; 8,005−3,468; 4,972+1,685; 9,320−5,147.
Multiplication: 36×7; 58×6; 24×13; 42×21.

2. 5,204 − 1,876 = **3,328**
3. 6,318 + 2,047 = **8,365**
4. 8,005 − 3,468 = **4,537**
5. 4,972 + 1,685 = **6,657**
6. 9,320 − 5,147 = **4,173**

## Quick Review

- **Accuracy:** Are all 10 results correct?
- **Strategy:** Is one place-value, regrouping, or partial-products method shown clearly?
- **Checking:** Do two answers include a reasonable second check?
