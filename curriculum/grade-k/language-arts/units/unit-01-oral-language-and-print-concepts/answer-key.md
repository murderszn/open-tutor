# Answer Key — Unit 01: Oral Language Listening and Print Concepts

Adult-facing. Keep separate from all learner materials. Every item below
was solved independently against its lesson/practice/quiz/assessment;
where a response is open-ended, the key gives the acceptable range and
sample reasoning.

## Lesson 01

**Guided practice:** 1. Points to/holds the cover with the title and main
picture. 2. Turns to the cover without title/blurb. 3. Picture upright
("the dog stands on the ground"). 4. One observation about the cover while
holding the stick ("I see a dog / red letters / a big sun").
**Independent practice:** 1. Book ends front-facing and right-side up.
2. Opens to the first page after the front cover (any of the first 2–3
pages acceptable). 3. Three single-page turns, front to back. 4. One more
cover observation with a clean turn exchange.
**Application:** states one talk rule in own words — accept "listen", "wait
your turn", "one person talks", "talk about the book".
**Exit check:** 1. Front shown. 2. Front-to-back gesture or words. 3. Any
one of listen / take turns / stay on the book.

## Lesson 02

**Illustration questions (Worked example 1):** the pointing hand touches
"The" (first word, top line, leftmost). The word after "The" on the road
is "sun".
**Guided practice:** 1. Pointer lands under each of the four words in
order. 2. Points to the top-left of the image. 3. Finger traces left to
right. 4. Sweep cue executed — pointer returns to the left edge and drops
to the next line.
**Independent practice:** 1. Four words pointed in order (top line, then
bottom line); word naming not required. 2. "hot." 3. "is." 4. Three page
turns, pointer placed top-left each time.
**Exit check:** 1. Top left. 2. Left to right. 3. Sweep back to the left
and down to the next line. 4. Turn the page; start again at the top left.

## Lesson 03

**Space counts:** "The sun is hot." laid out as cards has 4 words and 3
spaces. On the illustration: line 1 ("The sun") has 1 space; line 2 ("is
hot.") has 1 space.
**Guided practice:** 1. Four touches, one per card. 2. Names the covered
word ("sun"/"is"/etc. — whichever was covered). 3. Spaces pointed and
counted: 3. 4. Tracks at normal reading speed without rushing.
**Independent practice:** 1. Four touches. 2. "Four." 3. Identifies that
spaces are gone; re-spaces. 4. "I see a cat." — four touches, count of
four.
**Exit check:** 1. Four touches. 2. Finger between two words. 3. Two
words — "there is a space between them."
**Extension (if used):** on one line, words = spaces + 1.

## Lesson 04

Acceptable **questions** (any genuine question about the book): who/what/
where/when/why/how forms, or "I wonder..." phrasings. Acceptable
**answers**: a key detail from the pages read (character name, action,
place). Sample, for a generic picture book about a girl finding a key:
"Who is carrying the basket?" → "The girl." "What is in the basket?" →
"Apples." "Where did they go?" → "Into the garden."
**Guided practice:** 1–2. Key-detail answers. 3. Any learner question
(coach statements into questions once). 4. A "what does ___ mean?"
request about a real unknown word.
**Exit check:** 1. "Asking something you want to know" (any phrasing).
2. A real question. 3. A key-detail answer (who the story was about).

## Lesson 05

**A–M names:** A "ay", B "bee", C "cee", D "dee", E "ee", F "eff", G "gee",
H "aych", I "eye", J "jay", K "kay", L "ell", M "em". (Adult: use the
standard letter names; do not teach sounds here.)
**Confusion pairs to watch:** B/D (two bellies vs. one), E/F (extra bar),
I/J (dot and hook), M/N (N not yet taught — expect "which one is M?").
**Guided practice:** 1. Echo names A–M. 2. Finds E and 3–4 target
letters. 3. Mouth-awareness play (no wrong answers). 4. Straight-line
family: A, E, F, H, I, K, L, M; curvy family: B, C, D, G, J.
**Independent practice:** 1. Five drawn cards named (log hits/misses).
2. Name-letters within A–M named. 3. A–M placed in order on the arc.
4. Any pair with a visual reason.
**Exit check:** 3 of 5 shuffled cards named = secure for this lesson;
pointing to M and B; A–M in order (song allowed).

## Practice sessions

- **A:** pointer rides with the reading; start spot found on new pages by
  session end.
- **B:** names each "sickness" (upside down / opened at back / too many
  pages / reading pictures only) and demonstrates the fix.
- **C:** one step per word, left to right; sweep-back at line ends; works
  with the reordered cards.
- **D:** takes turns without reminders; states one rule; asks or answers
  one question; (Session 8) names/points 5 letters.
- **E:** one touch per word × 3 rounds; 3 spaces found and counted;
  missing word named; "I see a cat." done independently.

## Formative quiz (week 4)

1. Front found, right-side up, opened near the front. 2. Top-left start;
   left-to-right gesture. 3. Four touches in order. 4. Finger between two
   words. 5. Four (by clap, touch, or speech). 6. Note per-letter
   hits/misses; "point to K" correct. 7. At least one key detail answered.
   8. A genuine question (not a statement). Scoring: 7–8 secure; 5–6
   re-teach missed items in Session 16; below 5 use a flexible week.

## Unit assessment

- **Task 1:** front found ("where it begins"), right-side up, opened near
  front, single-page turns; narrates ≥2 steps.
- **Task 2:** "The"; four touches in order; traces the road; "top left"
  for a new page.
- **Task 3:** "Spaces" (missing); cards re-spaced; four touches; "four
  words, three spaces."
- **Task 4:** 10 of 13 named = secure; name-letters within A–M named;
  any defensible tricky pair with a reason.
- **Task 5:** states one talk rule; asks a genuine question; answers the
  adult's who/what/where with a key detail; turn-taking observed.
- **Totals:** 13–15 secure → Unit 02; 10–12 developing → re-teach lowest
  task(s); below 10 → flexible-week re-teach before Unit 02.
