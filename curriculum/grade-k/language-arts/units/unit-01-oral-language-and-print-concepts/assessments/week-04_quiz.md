# Formative Quiz — Unit 01 (Week 4)

**Format:** oral and observed; the adult reads each prompt aloud, watches
the learner respond, and scribes answers. No writing required of the
learner. **Time:** about 10 minutes, inside Session 15.
**Coverage:** objectives 1–5. Answer key:
[answer-key.md](../answer-key.md#formative-quiz-week-4).

**How to score:** each item is secure / developing / not yet. 7–8 secure =
ready for the unit assessment; 5–6 = re-teach the missed items in Session
16 before assessing; fewer than 5 = use a flexible week to re-teach the
unit's lessons before assessing.

1. **Book handling.** The adult hands the learner a closed book upside
   down and backwards. "Get this book ready to read."
   *(Observe: front found, right-side up, opened near the front.)*

2. **Direction.** Open to any page of large print. "Point to where we
   start reading. Now show me which way we go."
   *(Observe: top-left start; left-to-right gesture.)*

3. **Tracking.** The adult reads the line "The sun is hot." slowly from a
   large-print card. "Touch each word as I say it."
   *(Observe: one touch per spoken word, left to right.)*

4. **Spaces.** "Point to a space — not a word, a space."
   *(Observe: finger lands between two words.)*

5. **Word count.** "I will say a sentence: 'I see a cat.' How many words
   did you hear? Show me on the cards."
   *(Observe: four — by clapping, touching cards, or saying "four".)*

6. **Letters.** The adult holds up 5 shuffled uppercase cards from A–M.
   "Name each letter." Then: "Point to K."
   *(Observe: naming and recognition; note which letters are missed.)*

7. **Answering.** After a 2-page read-aloud: "Who was this story about?
   What happened?"
   *(Observe: at least one key detail answered.)*

8. **Asking.** "What is one thing you wonder about this story? Ask me."
   *(Observe: a genuine question — who/what/where or other — not a
   statement.)*
