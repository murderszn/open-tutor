# Unit Assessment — Unit 01: Oral Language Listening and Print Concepts

**Format:** five performance tasks, adult-observed and scribed, about 15
minutes total, inside Session 16. The learner may respond by speaking,
pointing, gesturing, or demonstrating — no writing required.
**Answer key and scoring guidance:**
[answer-key.md](../answer-key.md#unit-assessment).

## Task 1 — Book expert (Objective 1; RF.K.1)

The adult hands the learner an unfamiliar picture book, closed, upside
down, and backwards.

"Show me you are a book expert: get the book ready, and tell me what you
are doing as you do it."

**Look for:** finds the front ("this is where it begins"), holds it
right-side up (picture cues), opens near the front, turns pages one at a
time front to back. The learner narrates at least two steps in their own
words.

## Task 2 — Reading road (Objective 2; RF.K.1.a)

Using the generated illustration
[`assets/big-book-tracking.png`](../assets/big-book-tracking.png) (or its
text alternative):

1. "Which word is the pointing hand touching?" *(The)*
2. "Point to each word while I read: 'The sun is hot.'" *(Four touches,
   left to right, top line then bottom line.)*
3. "The green arrow shows the reading road. Show me the road with your
   finger, then tell me: where do we start on a brand-new page?"
   *(Traces left to right; "top left" / points to top left.)*

## Task 3 — Words and spaces (Objective 3; RF.K.1.b, RF.K.1.c)

Word cards "The | sun | is | hot." laid out **touching, with no spaces**.

1. "Something is wrong with these words. What is missing?" *(Spaces.)*
2. "Fix it." *(Re-spaces the cards.)*
3. The adult says the sentence slowly: "Touch one word for each word you
   hear." *(Four touches.)*
4. "How many words? How many spaces?" *(Four words; three spaces.)*

## Task 4 — Letter round-up (Objective 4; RF.K.1.d)

All 13 uppercase cards A–M, shuffled, face up.

1. "Name as many as you can." *(Adult notes each; 10 of 13 = secure.)*
2. "Find the letters in your first name that are on this table. Name
   them." *(Only letters A–M count; others are skipped without penalty.)*
3. "Which two letters could trick someone? Why?" *(Any defensible pair
   with a reason — e.g., "B and D, they both have bellies.")*

## Task 5 — Talk circle (Objective 5; SL.K.1, SL.K.2, SL.K.3)

The adult reads two pages of a picture book aloud, then leads a
mini talk circle with the talking stick:

1. "What is one rule for our talking?" *(Listen / take turns / stay on the
   book.)*
2. "Ask me a question about what we just read." *(A genuine question.)*
3. The adult asks one **who/what/where** question; the learner answers
   with a key detail. *(Observed turn-taking throughout.)*

## Scoring

Each task: **Secure (3)** — independent and accurate; **Developing (2)**
— accurate with a reminder or cue; **Getting started (1)** — needs
hand-over-hand help or cannot do it yet. Total 5–15.

- **13–15:** secure — begin Unit 02.
- **10–12:** developing — re-teach the lowest task(s) using the
  [teacher guide](../teacher-guide.md#re-teach-triggers), then begin
  Unit 02.
- **Below 10:** re-teach the matching lessons during a flexible week
  before Unit 02; record which objectives were not yet secure.

This is a curriculum-embedded performance check, not a standardized or
validated test.
