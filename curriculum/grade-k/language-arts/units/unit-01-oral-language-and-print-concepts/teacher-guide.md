# Teacher Guide — Unit 01: Oral Language Listening and Print Concepts

Adult-facing. Learner materials never link here with answers visible; keep
this file and [answer-key.md](answer-key.md) out of the learner's reach.

## How this unit runs

Four 15–20-minute sessions per week. Every session opens with the same
30-second ritual — "reading hands ready?" (book or pointer in hands, eyes
on the page) — and closes with one sentence of specific praise tied to the
objective ("You swept back to the left all by yourself"). Short, warm, and
repetitive beats long and novel at this age.

**Pacing judgment:** the [16-session pacing](README.md#16-session-pacing-4-weeks--4-sessions-15-20-minutes-each)
puts two lessons in Session 13. That works only if Lessons 04 and 05 are
each trimmed to their core: L4 = one read-aloud with two questions; L5 =
echo round plus the 5-card exit check. If the learner is tired, split them
across Session 13 and the Session 12 re-teach slot instead — the unit's
design survives the swap.

## Session-by-session guidance

- **Sessions 1–4 (book basics):** model more than you explain. Children
  learn handling by watching your hands, not by hearing rules. Narrate
  your own hands for the first week ("I am turning one page... now the
  next").
- **Sessions 5–8 (tracking):** shadow the learner's pointer hand with yours
  until the left-to-right motion is smooth, then fade. Expect the motion to
  fall apart the first time a page has three lines — that is normal; cue
  the sweep.
- **Sessions 9–10 (words/spaces):** keep sentences at 3–5 words. Longer
  sentences overload the one-touch-per-word skill.
- **Session 11/14 (project):** scribe the learner's dictation verbatim.
  Large print, generous spaces — your handwriting is the lesson.
- **Session 13 (questions + letters):** keep the wonder wall visible all
  unit; questions asked in Session 4 can be re-asked here.
- **Sessions 15–16 (check + assess):** do the quiz in the morning slot and
  the project share after a break. Never quiz a tired kindergartner and
  trust the result.

## Misconceptions — quick reference

| You see | It usually means | Do this |
|---|---|---|
| Opens the book from the back | Nearest-side habit, not defiance | "Books begin at the front — help me find it" |
| Pointer parks on one word | Doesn't yet link pointer to voice | Shadow their hand; "ride along with the words" |
| Starts tracking at the right | Home-language direction knowledge | Honor it, then practice the English road explicitly |
| Calls a letter a word | Hasn't got the space rule yet | Trace the whole clump: "the word is everything between the spaces" |
| Chants a memorized line without touching | Memory outrunning matching | Slow down: "one touch, one word — wait for each word" |
| Sings A–M but can't name a shuffled K | Song ≠ knowledge | Always test out of order; the song is a finding tool |
| Answers a question with unrelated talk | Lost the question thread | Re-anchor once: "my question was *who*..." |
| Offers statements instead of questions | Doesn't have the question shape yet | Accept, then model: "'The dog ran' → '**Where** did the dog run?'" |

## Re-teach triggers

Use these after Session 12 observations and the Session 15 quiz:

- **Tracking not smooth by Session 8:** repeat
  [Session A](practice.md#session-a-read-aloud-pointing) with two-line
  pages only; add the floor-road variant from Lesson 02.
- **Word/space matching below 3 of 4 touches:** repeat
  [Session E](practice.md#session-e-word-touch-practice) with 3-word
  sentences ("I see Sam."); keep cards large.
- **Fewer than 8 of 13 letters named:** pull the missed letters into a
  daily 3-minute card game (find-it, name-it) for a week; do not drill all
  13 at once.
- **Won't ask questions:** switch to choice prompts — "Do you wonder *who*
  she is or *where* she is going?" — then fade the choices.
- **Talk rules forgotten:** re-run the Session 1 stick ritual at the start
  of every session for a week; praise each clean turn exchange.

## Readiness criteria — moving to Unit 02

The learner is ready when the unit assessment totals 13+, **or** totals
10–12 with the re-teach above completed. Below 10: use a flexible week
(the track provides diagnostic/catch-up weeks) to re-teach the matching
lessons — Unit 02's rhyme and syllable play does not require secure
tracking, but a child who cannot yet find the front of a book will
struggle with shared reading in Unit 05, so do not skip the re-teach.

## Accessibility notes for the adult

- Face the learner when modeling speech; mouth shapes in the mirror
  support sound awareness that Unit 02 builds on.
- Large-print, high-contrast cards; textured letters for low-vision
  learners (sandpaper or puffy-paint letters work).
- Every check accepts pointing/gesture/drawing; scribe everything.
- Movement breaks between sessions, not within — a 15-minute session
  should not need one, but watch the learner, not the clock.
- Home-language labels for vocabulary (front/back, question words) are
  welcome alongside English; note them in your records for consistency.
