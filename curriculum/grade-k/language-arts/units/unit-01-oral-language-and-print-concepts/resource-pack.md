# Resource Pack — Unit 01: Oral Language Listening and Print Concepts

## Summary

**Goal:** give the guiding adult free, no-account routes to support
kindergarten print concepts (book handling, left-to-right tracking,
word/space matching), talk-circle discussion rules, asking and answering
questions during read-alouds, and uppercase letter names A–M — plus songs
and read-aloud models a 5-year-old will enjoy.
**Age fit:** kindergarten (about 5–6); all items are visual, musical, or
hands-on, with no reading required of the learner.
