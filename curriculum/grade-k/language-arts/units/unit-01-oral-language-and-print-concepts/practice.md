# Practice, Game, and Review Sessions — Unit 01

Named sessions referenced by the [16-session pacing](README.md#16-session-pacing-4-weeks--4-sessions-15-20-minutes-each).
Each session is 15–20 minutes. All responses may be oral, pointing, or
drawing; the adult scribes.

## Session A — Read-aloud pointing practice

*Used in: Session 2 (pictures + words), Session 6 (pointer reading).*

1. Adult reads a picture book aloud at a natural pace. The learner rests a
   finger or pointer under the words being read — riding along, not parked.
2. At each page turn, the learner places the pointer at the new page's top
   left before the adult starts reading. ("Where do we start?")
3. Twice during the reading, the adult pauses mid-line: the learner points
   to the word the adult just said. (One touch per word.)
4. Closing: "Show me the last word on the last page we read."

**Success:** pointer moves left to right with the reading; start spot
found on new pages without help by the end of the session.

## Session B — Book doctor game

*Used in: Session 3.*

The adult is the "patient" and the book is "sick." The learner is the
doctor.

1. Round 1: adult holds the book upside down. "Doctor, what is wrong with
   this book?" (Upside down — fix it.)
2. Round 2: adult opens the book from the back. "Doctor, what is wrong?"
   (Opened at the back — find the front.)
3. Round 3: adult turns three pages at once. "Doctor, what is wrong?"
   (Too many pages — one at a time.)
4. Round 4 (challenge): adult holds the book correctly but reads the
   picture only, ignoring the words. "Doctor, am I doing it right?" (The
   words carry the story too — point to the words as you read.)

**Success:** learner names each problem in their own words and demonstrates
the fix.

## Session C — Reading train game

*Used in: Session 7.*

1. Lay word cards for "The sun is hot." in a long line on the floor, left
   to right, with big spaces between them.
2. The learner is the train; the adult is the conductor calling "All
   aboard at 'The'!" The learner steps to the first word.
3. Conductor reads the sentence slowly; the train moves one step per word,
   left to right. At the line end: "Sweep back!" — the train loops around
   to the next line's start.
4. Second trip: cards for "I see a cat." Third trip (challenge): the adult
   mixes the order — the train must still travel left to right from the
   new start.

**Success:** one step per word, no skipped words, sweep-back at line ends.

## Session D — Talk-circle review

*Used in: Session 4 (L1 review), Session 8 (L1–L2 review).*

1. Talking stick circle (even with two people): each person says one thing
   they remember about how books work (Session 4) or about the reading
   road (Session 8). Turns are taken; listening is visible.
2. "Teach-back": the learner teaches the book (or the pointer) one rule:
   "Books start at the front." / "We read left to right."
3. Wonder revisit: pick one question from the Lesson 04 wonder wall (if
   started) or ask one new question about the week's book.
4. Letter flash (Session 8 only): 5 shuffled A–M cards — name or point.

**Success:** takes turns without reminders; states one rule accurately;
asks or answers one question.

## Session E — Word-touch practice

*Used in: Session 10.*

1. Word cards "The | sun | is | hot." spaced on the table. Adult says the
   sentence; learner touches one card per word. Three rounds, faster each
   time — accuracy over speed.
2. Space detectives: learner puts a finger in every space between the
   cards and counts the spaces. ("Three cards apart means...?" — three
   spaces for four words.)
3. Cover-up: adult covers one card; learner says the sentence and names
   the missing word.
4. New sentence "I see a cat." — learner lays out, spaces, touches, and
   counts independently.

**Success:** one touch per spoken word; spaces found and counted; missing
word named from memory of the sentence pattern.

## Re-teach session (Session 12)

Not a fixed activity — the adult picks the highest-need objective using
the [re-teach triggers](teacher-guide.md#re-teach-triggers) and repeats the
matching session above (A for tracking, E for word/space matching, D for
talk rules/questions, or Lesson 05's card games for letters), with easier
material (shorter lines, fewer cards) and more modeling.
