# Practice Sessions — Unit 05

All sessions are 15–20 minutes. The adult reads any story text
aloud; the learner answers orally, by pointing, by drawing, or by
acting, and the adult scribes dictated words. Do not show the
[answer key](answer-key.md) to the learner.

## Session A — Question game (Session 2, after Lesson 01)

*Goal: ask and answer who/what/where questions about Milo's story.*

1. The adult reads *Milo and the Lost Mitten* (Lesson 01 text) once.
   The learner answers: "Who is in the story?" (Milo and Biscuit.)
2. "What happened to Milo's mitten?" (It fell off on the snowy path.)
3. "Where were Milo and Biscuit walking?" (To Grandma's house.)
4. The learner asks the adult two questions about the story; the
   adult answers from the story and names each question word.
5. The adult asks a question the story does *not* answer ("What
   did Grandma cook for lunch?"). The learner's job is to say:
   "The story does not tell us." (This is a correct answer.)
6. Quick round: the adult points to a question word card and the
   learner makes up a question about Milo with that word.

## Session B — Character and setting sort (Session 4, after Lesson 02)

*Goal: sort story elements into characters and settings/things.*

Cards (adult-made, pictures with spoken labels): Ana, Ben, Milo,
Biscuit, garden, snowy path, seed packet, watering can, blue
mitten, sunflower.

1. Sort into two piles: *who* (Ana, Ben, Milo, Biscuit) and
   *where/things* (garden, snowy path, seed packet, watering can,
   blue mitten, sunflower).
2. "Which *who* cards belong to Ana's story?" (Ana, Ben.) "Which
   belong to Milo's?" (Milo, Biscuit.)
3. "Which setting belongs to Ana's story?" (The garden.) "Which to
   Milo's?" (The snowy path.)
4. Tricky card: "Is the sunflower a character?" (No — it grows but
   does not talk or choose.) "Is Biscuit a character?" (Yes — she
   chooses to pick up the mitten and bring it back.)
5. The learner picks one *who* card and one *where* card and says a
   sentence: "___ is in ___." (For example, "Ben is in the garden.")
6. Extension: add a new card the learner draws (a neighbor, a cat)
   and decide together which pile it belongs in and why.

## Session C — Sequence card race (Session 6, after Lesson 03)

*Goal: order 3 events for each of two stories, fast and correctly.*

Milo cards: walking in snow; mitten on the ground; Biscuit
bringing the mitten back. Ana cards: planting the seed; sprout
coming up; sunflower standing tall.

1. Milo round: the adult mixes Milo's cards; the learner orders
   them and says *first*, *next*, *last*.
2. Ana round: the same with Ana's cards.
3. Mixed round: the adult lays one card from each story side by side
   and asks, "Which story is this from, and is it first, next, or
   last in its story?" (Three pairs, learner answers each.)
4. Wrong-order detective: the adult orders Ana's cards as sprout,
   sunflower, planting, and asks, "What is wrong?" (Planting must
   come first; a sprout cannot come before the seed is planted.)
5. Speed round: the learner orders Milo's cards again, timed
   playfully by the adult counting slowly to ten — accuracy beats
   speed, and a correct slow order beats a fast wrong one.
6. The learner teaches the order to a stuffed animal, one sentence
   per card, for each story.

## Session D — Prop retell (Session 8, after Lesson 04)

*Goal: retell a familiar story with props, in order, with key details.*

Props: blue mitten (or paper mitten), toy dog, seed packet or
picture of a sunflower.

1. The learner retells *Ana Plants a Seed* using the props: plant
   (mime digging), water (mime pouring), grow (arms up tall like
   the sunflower).
2. The learner retells *Milo and the Lost Mitten* using the mitten
   and the toy dog: walk, drop, find, bring back, thank you.
3. The adult asks after each retelling: "What did you leave out
   that you did not need?" (Any non-key detail, e.g., hat color.)
   "What did you keep that the story needs?" (Any key detail.)
4. The learner retells Milo's story with the sequence cards face
   down — props only.
5. Partner switch: the adult retells Ana's story *leaving out the
   watering*, and the learner catches the missing key event.
   ("You forgot Ben pouring the water!")
6. The learner picks the story they retell best and tells it once
   more, start to finish, for the adult to scribe as a "star
   retelling" to keep.

## Session E — Draw-and-tell (Session 10, after Lesson 05)

*Goal: draw a story moment and tell what it shows, including what
it does not show.*

1. The adult reads *Sam's Rainy-Day Picnic* (Lesson 05 text) once.
2. The learner draws one moment: packing the basket, rain on the
   grass, or the indoor blanket picnic.
3. The learner tells the adult: "My picture shows..." (the moment),
   "This is the beginning / middle / end," and one detail visible
   in the drawing.
4. The adult asks: "What does your picture NOT show?" (The other
   two moments.) "Does that mean they are not important?" (No.)
5. The learner draws a second moment from Sam's story, and puts the
   two drawings in order, saying *first* and *next* (or *next* and
   *last*, depending on the moments chosen).
6. Gallery walk: the drawings go on the wall or table; the learner
   walks past them like an illustrator showing their work and tells
   each picture's moment to the adult or a toy listener.

## Session F — Review games (Session 13, review week)

*Goal: revisit the highest-need objective through play, not re-testing.*

Pick the games that match what the learner most needs (see the
[teacher guide](teacher-guide.md#re-teach-triggers)):

1. **Question ball:** the adult tosses a soft ball; whoever catches
   it asks or answers a Milo/Ana/Sam question.
2. **Sequence relay:** cards face down in a pile; the learner flips
   and places them in order, one story at a time.
3. **Picture detective:** the adult shows the generated
   illustration and asks three questions — what do you see, what
   moment is it, what do you guess?
4. **Same/different clap:** the adult says a pair ("Both have a
   helper" — clap for same; "One is snow, one is garden" — hands
   apart for different).
5. **Author/illustrator show:** with any picture book from home, the
   learner points to words (author's job) and pictures
   (illustrator's job) and says each job aloud.
6. **Star retelling:** the learner retells their best story once
   more, start to finish, and the adult notes which objectives are
   now secure for Session 16 planning.
