# Answer Key — Unit 05: Shared Reading, Retelling, and Sequence

**For the guiding adult only. Do not show to the learner.**
Acceptable variations are noted; a response that is true to the
story and in order counts even if the wording differs from what is
written here. Every item was solved independently from the story
texts in the lessons and teacher guide.

## Lesson 01

- **Guided 1:** Who walks to Grandma's house — Milo and Biscuit.
  What Milo wears on his hands — two blue mittens.
- **Guided 2:** What fell off — one blue mitten. Who found it —
  Biscuit.
- **Guided 3:** How it ended — Biscuit brought the mitten back,
  Milo said thank you, put it on, and they walked on.
- **Guided 4:** Any *who/what/where/when/why/how* question about
  Milo's story; the adult answers from the story and names the
  question word.
- **Guided 5:** Author writes the words; illustrator draws the
  pictures. (If the home book's cover names only one maker, that
  job is named and the other is noted as not named on this cover.)
- **Independent 1:** Beginning key detail — Milo and Biscuit
  walking to Grandma's house in the snow.
- **Independent 2:** Middle key detail — the mitten fell off and
  Biscuit picked it up.
- **Independent 3:** End key detail — Biscuit brought it back;
  Milo said thank you.
- **Independent 4:** Any story question using a question word.
- **Independent 5:** Words = author's job (point to text); picture
  = illustrator's job (point to picture).
- **Exit 1:** Biscuit. **Exit 2:** On the snowy path. **Exit 3:**
  Author; illustrator. **Exit 4:** No — the story still works
  without the hat color; the mitten is the key detail.

## Lesson 02

- **Guided 1:** Ana and Ben. **Guided 2:** Ana's garden.
- **Guided 3:** *Who* pile — Ana, Ben. *Where/things* pile —
  garden, seed packet, watering can (and, in the full set, Milo,
  Biscuit, snowy path, blue mitten, sunflower sorted by story in
  Practice Session B).
- **Guided 4:** Ana digs, plants, looks every day. Ben pours the
  water and smiles at the sunflower.
- **Guided 5:** Milo's animal character — Biscuit. Ana's story has
  no animal character.
- **Independent 1:** Ana, Ben. **Independent 2:** Ana's garden.
- **Independent 3:** Ben is the character; the garden is the setting.
- **Independent 4:** Any true action each (see Guided 4).
- **Independent 5:** No — the sunflower does not talk, choose, or
  do things on purpose; it grows.
- **Exit 1:** Ana and Ben. **Exit 2:** Ana's garden. **Exit 3:**
  No — it is a thing. **Exit 4:** Biscuit.

## Lesson 03

- **Milo order:** First — Milo and Biscuit walk through the snow
  to Grandma's house. Next — the blue mitten slips off and Biscuit
  picks it up. Last — Biscuit brings the mitten back and Milo says
  thank you.
- **Ana order:** First — Ana plants the seed. Next — a sprout
  comes up. Last — a big sunflower stands tall.
- **Guided 3 (wrong order):** The order is wrong because Biscuit
  cannot return a mitten that has not fallen yet.
- **Guided 4:** First plant, next sprout, last sunflower.
- **Independent 5:** The middle card is *next* — after the walk
  starts, before the return.
- **Exit 1:** Walking; mitten falls / Biscuit finds it; Biscuit
  brings it back. **Exit 2:** *First*; *last*. **Exit 3:** "The
  mitten falls off" is the event.

## Lesson 04

- **Retell (secure):** who (Milo, Biscuit) and where (snowy path to
  Grandma's house) named; all three major events in order; key
  details present (mitten falls, Biscuit brings it back, thank you).
  Nearly there: 2 of 3 events in order. Paraphrase counts fully;
  changed non-key details (hat color) are not errors.
- **Guided 3 (picture):** The last event — Biscuit returning the
  mitten. Evidence: the dog holds the mitten; Milo reaches for it
  and wears only one mitten.
- **Guided 4:** Must not leave out — any of Milo, Biscuit, the
  mitten falling, Biscuit bringing it back. May leave out — hat
  color, coat color, snowflakes.
- **Independent 4 (picture):** Biscuit is holding/bringing the
  mitten; Milo will take it and say thank you.
- **Exit 1:** As retell-secure above (2 of 3 events minimum for
  "nearly there"). **Exit 2:** Biscuit bringing the lost mitten
  back to Milo. **Exit 3:** Key — mitten falling / Biscuit bringing
  it back. Leave out — hat or coat color.

## Lesson 05

- **Guided 1:** Child in red coat and red hat, small brown dog,
  blue mitten in the dog's mouth, one blue mitten on the child's
  hand, pine tree, small house with a warm window, snowflakes.
- **Guided 2:** The end of Milo's story — the dog is returning the
  mitten Milo lost.
- **Guided 3:** Not shown — Grandma, the mitten falling, Milo
  walking at the start. Not shown does not mean unimportant.
- **Guided 4:** Happy / pleased — Milo is smiling and reaching for
  the mitten. ("Looks happy" is the precise phrasing; a feeling
  read from a face is a guess, not a shown fact.)
- **Guided 5 (dog covered):** Without the dog, the moment is
  unclear — the story's key event disappears. Pictures and words
  need each other.
- **Independent 2:** The dog holding the mitten out to Milo, or
  Milo's single worn mitten, proves the end.
- **Independent 3:** Just before — the mitten fell off and Biscuit
  found it.
- **Independent 4:** Just after — Milo takes the mitten, says
  thank you, and they walk on to Grandma's house.
- **Independent 5:** "I think" the house is Grandma's house (warm
  window, path) — a guess, not a pictured fact; guesses phrased as
  guesses count as correct.
- **Draw-and-tell (Sam):** beginning — packing the basket /
  planning the picnic under the big tree; middle — rain falling on
  the grass; end — blanket picnic indoors. Any one moment, correctly
  labeled, counts.
- **Exit 1:** The end — Biscuit bringing the mitten back.
  **Exit 2:** Any two visible things (see Guided 1). **Exit 3:**
  Guess — Milo is happy / the mitten fell earlier. Not shown —
  Grandma / the fall itself.

## Lesson 06

- **Comparison table:** Milo — characters Milo + Biscuit (dog),
  setting snowy path, job lost mitten, helper Biscuit, ending thank
  you and walk on. Ana — characters Ana + Ben (people), setting
  garden, job plant and grow a seed, helper Ben (water), ending
  sunflower and smiles.
- **Same (any):** both have a helper; both end happily; both happen
  outside; both have two characters.
- **Different (any):** snow vs. garden; dog helper vs. person
  helper; lost thing found vs. seed grown; one day's walk vs. a
  summer of growing.
- **Guided 4 / Exit 3:** A seed needs soil, water, and sun.
- **Guided 5:** The root grows first, under the soil; then the stem
  and leaves above the soil.
- **Independent 5:** Ana's seed grows into a big yellow sunflower
  — answered from Ana's story, not from *How Seeds Grow*.
- **Application:** Sam is defensibly like Milo (same-day problem
  fixed with help) or like Ana (friends doing something together
  outside); the defense is the scored part, not the pick.

## Practice sessions

- **Session A:** 1 — Milo and Biscuit. 2 — It fell off on the snowy
  path. 3 — To Grandma's house. 4 — Any two story questions,
  question words named. 5 — "The story does not tell us" (correct).
  6 — Any question built from the pointed-to question word.
- **Session B:** *Who* — Ana, Ben, Milo, Biscuit. *Where/things* —
  garden, snowy path, seed packet, watering can, blue mitten,
  sunflower. Ana's *who* — Ana, Ben. Milo's *who* — Milo, Biscuit.
  Ana's setting — garden. Milo's setting — snowy path. Sunflower —
  not a character. Biscuit — a character. Example sentence — "Ben
  is in the garden."
- **Session C:** Milo order — walking; mitten on ground; Biscuit
  bringing it back. Ana order — planting; sprout; sunflower. Wrong
  order (sprout, sunflower, planting) — planting must come first.
- **Session D:** Ana retell with props — dig/plant, pour water,
  grow tall. Milo retell — walk, drop, find, bring back, thank you.
  Adult's Ana retell missing the watering — learner catches it.
- **Session E:** Sam moments — beginning: packing the basket /
  planning under the big tree. Middle: rain on the grass. End:
  blanket picnic indoors. Two drawings in order with *first/next*
  or *next/last* as appropriate.
- **Session F:** Games scored by the same keys as the lessons they
  revisit; the star retelling is judged by the Lesson 04 retell
  standard (2 of 3 events in order = nearly there; 3 = secure).

## Project

- **Milo book:** Page 1 — Milo and Biscuit walking in snow (to
  Grandma's house). Page 2 — mitten on the ground, Biscuit finding
  it. Page 3 — Biscuit bringing it back, Milo saying thank you.
- **Ana book:** Page 1 — Ana and Ben in the garden with the seed
  packet. Page 2 — planting and watering, sprout coming up.
  Page 3 — tall sunflower, Ana and Ben smiling.
- **Sentences:** one dictated sentence per page, in order, naming
  a key detail for that page. Exact wording varies; order and key
  details are scored, not wording. See the project rubric for the
  2/1/0 scoring per row.

## Unit assessment (Session 16)

- **Task 1 (Sam retell):** secure — Sam and June named; outside
  then inside named; all three events in order (plan/pack picnic;
  rain comes; indoor blanket picnic, still fun). Nearly — two
  events in order, or events in order with characters/setting
  missing. Not yet — one event, wrong order, or adult-led.
- **Task 2 (Sam sequence):** (a) packing the basket — *first*;
  (b) rain on the grass — *next*; (c) indoor blanket picnic —
  *last*. Secure — correct order with order words. Nearly — correct
  order without order words, or one swap self-corrected.
- **Task 3:** author writes the words; illustrator draws the
  pictures (if the home book names only one maker, that role named
  and the other noted as unnamed). Picture — the end (Biscuit
  returning the mitten); two visible details (see Lesson 05
  Guided 1); one not-shown element (Grandma; the fall; the start
  of the walk).
- **Task 4 (Milo vs. Sam):** same — same-day problem fixed with
  help / happy ending / friend or helper present. Different — lost
  mitten vs. changed picnic plan; snow vs. rain; dog helper vs.
  sister helper. Two unrelated facts are not a comparison.
- **Task 5 (information):** soil, water, sun; root first. Nearly —
  two of three needs, or needs complete with root/stem swapped.
- **Task 6 (questions):** Sam and June; rain; inside, on a blanket
  on the living-room floor. Secure — 3 of 3. Nearly — 2 of 3.
