# Formative Quiz — Unit 05 (Week 22, Session 15)

**Format:** oral, pointing, and drawing; adult-scribed where noted.
15–20 minutes. The adult reads any story text aloud. Do not show the
[answer key](../answer-key.md) to the learner.

**How to score (adult only):** 9–10 = secure; 7–8 = nearly there,
re-teach the missed items in Session 16's planning time; 6 or fewer
= use a flexible week to re-teach before Unit 06. The quiz is
formative: it guides teaching, it does not grade the child.
Acceptable responses and scoring notes are in the
[answer key](../answer-key.md#formative-quiz-session-15).

## Items

1. The adult reads *Milo and the Lost Mitten* (Lesson 01 text) once.
   "Who are the characters?"
2. "Where does Milo's story happen?"
3. "What happened first in Milo's story?"
4. "What happened last?"
5. The adult shows the generated illustration
   [`../assets/milo-lost-mitten.png`](../assets/milo-lost-mitten.png).
   "What moment does this picture show?"
6. "Who writes the words in a book?" "Who draws the pictures?"
7. The adult reads *Ana Plants a Seed* (Lesson 02 text) once. "Who
   helped Ana?"
8. "Name one way Milo's story and Ana's story are the same, and one
   way they are different."
9. The adult reads *How Seeds Grow* (Lesson 06 text) once. "What
   three things does a seed need?"
10. Draw-and-tell: the learner draws one moment from *Sam's
    Rainy-Day Picnic* (Lesson 05 text, read once before drawing)
    and tells the adult which moment it is (beginning, middle, or
    end) and one detail in the drawing.
