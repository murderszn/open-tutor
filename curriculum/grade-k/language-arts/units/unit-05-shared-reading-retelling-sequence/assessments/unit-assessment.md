# Unit Assessment — Unit 05 (Week 22, Session 16)

**Format:** performance tasks; oral, pointing, drawing, and acting;
adult-scribed where noted. 15–20 minutes, plus re-teach planning.
The adult reads any story text aloud. Do not show the
[answer key](../answer-key.md) to the learner.

**How to score (adult only):** each task is scored 2 (secure), 1
(nearly there), or 0 (not yet), using the criteria under each task;
the specific expected responses are in the
[answer key](../answer-key.md#unit-assessment-session-16). Total
10–12 = secure; 7–9 = nearly there (targeted re-teach in a flexible
week); 6 or fewer = re-teach the unit's weakest objective before
Unit 06. The assessment is formative: it guides teaching, it does
not grade the child.

## Task 1 — Retell a new story (Objectives 1, 2)

The adult reads *Sam's Rainy-Day Picnic* (Lesson 05 text) twice.
Then, without the adult telling it first, the learner retells it
beginning, middle, and end, with key details. Props welcome: a
basket or bag, a blanket, paper food (sandwich, apple, juice
pictures).

- Secure (2): characters and both settings named; all three major
  events in order.
- Nearly there (1): two of the three events in order, or events in
  order but characters or setting missing.
- Not yet (0): one event only, events out of order, or the learner
  cannot retell without the adult telling it first.

## Task 2 — Sequence three events (Objective 3)

The adult prepares three Sam picture cards before the assessment
(simple drawings or cut-out pictures): (a) Sam packing the picnic
basket, (b) rain falling on the grass, (c) Sam and June eating on a
blanket indoors. The adult mixes them. The learner puts them in
order and says *first*, *next*, *last* for each.

- Secure (2): correct order, order words used.
- Nearly there (1): correct order without order words, or one card
  swapped and self-corrected.
- Not yet (0): order wrong and not corrected.

## Task 3 — Author and illustrator, picture talk (Objective 4)

(a) With any picture book from home, the learner names the author
and the illustrator (or, if the cover names only one, says which
job that person does and that the other job is not named on this
cover) and defines each role in their own words. (b) With the
generated illustration
[`../assets/milo-lost-mitten.png`](../assets/milo-lost-mitten.png),
the learner says what moment it shows, names two things visible in
it, and names one thing it does not show.

- Secure (2): both roles defined correctly; picture moment correct;
  two visible details named; one not-shown element named.
- Nearly there (1): one role confused, or picture moment correct
  but only one detail named.
- Not yet (0): roles swapped or unknown, or picture moment wrong.

## Task 4 — Compare two stories (Objective 5, compare)

"Tell me one way Milo's story and Sam's story are the same, and one
way they are different." The learner may use the Milo sequence
cards and the Sam cards as prompts.

- Secure (2): one true same and one true different, with the same
  story element compared on both sides (helper, setting, problem,
  or ending).
- Nearly there (1): one of the two correct.
- Not yet (0): neither correct, or two unrelated facts instead of a
  comparison.

## Task 5 — Answer from information (Objective 5, recall)

The adult reads *How Seeds Grow* (Lesson 06 text) once. "What does
a seed need? What grows first — the root or the stem?"

- Secure (2): all three needs named; growth order correct.
- Nearly there (1): two of the three needs, or needs complete but
  growth order wrong.
- Not yet (0): one or no needs named, even after one re-reading.

## Task 6 — Key-detail question round (Objective 1)

The adult asks three questions about *Sam's Rainy-Day Picnic*,
without re-reading: "Who went on the picnic?" "What ruined the
outside picnic?" "Where did they eat in the end?"

- Secure (2): all three correct.
- Nearly there (1): two correct.
- Not yet (0): one or none correct.

## Re-teach planning (adult, after scoring)

Write down the weakest objective and the next teaching step from
the [teacher guide](../teacher-guide.md#re-teach-triggers) before
starting Unit 06. If Tasks 1–2 are weak, repeat sequence-card work
with Milo and Ana before introducing decodable texts. If Task 3 is
weak, repeat picture-talk with the generated illustration and any
home picture book. If Tasks 4–5 are weak, repeat the Lesson 06
comparison table aloud and re-read *How Seeds Grow* across two
short sessions rather than one.
