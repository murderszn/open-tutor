# Resource Pack — Unit 02: Rhyme, Syllables, and Phonemic Awareness

## Summary

**Goal:** give the guiding adult free, no-account routes to support
kindergarten phonological awareness — recognizing and producing rhymes,
clapping and blending syllables, and blending/segmenting onsets and
rimes — plus songs and modeled activities a 5-year-old will enjoy.
**Age fit:** kindergarten (about 5–6); all items are musical, visual, or
hands-on, with no reading required of the learner.
