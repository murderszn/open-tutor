# Formative Quiz — Unit 02 (Week 4)

**Format:** oral and observed; the adult reads each prompt aloud, watches
the learner respond, and scribes answers. No writing required of the
learner. **Time:** about 10 minutes, inside Session 15.
**Coverage:** objectives 1–6. Answer key:
[answer-key.md](../answer-key.md#formative-quiz-week-4).

**How to score:** each item is secure / developing / not yet. 7–8 secure =
ready for the unit assessment; 5–6 = re-teach the missed items in Session
16 before assessing; fewer than 5 = use a flexible week to re-teach the
unit's lessons before assessing.

1. **Rhyme recognition.** "Do *cat* and *hat* rhyme?"
   *(Observe: yes — clap or words.)* Then: "Do *cat* and *dog* rhyme?"
   *(Observe: no.)*

2. **Rhyme production.** "Tell me a word that rhymes with *bee*."
   *(Observe: tree, see, me, knee, or a silly rhyme — any end-match.)*

3. **Syllable counting.** "Clap the parts in *pencil*." *(Observe: two
   claps — pen-cil.)* Then: "Clap the parts in *banana*." *(Observe:
   three claps.)*

4. **Syllable blending.** In robot voice: "ta ... ble." "What word did
   the robot say?" *(Observe: table.)*

5. **Onset-rime blending.** "Beginning /d/, rest *-og*. Put them
   together." *(Observe: dog.)*

6. **Onset-rime segmenting.** "Say *sun* in two parts — the beginning
   and the rest." *(Observe: /s/ ... -un, with or without the hand
   gestures.)*

7. **Two-syllable take-apart.** "Take apart *window*." *(Observe: win
   ... dow.)*

8. **Talk.** "What is one rule for our talk circle?" *(Observe: listen /
   take turns / stay on the topic.)* "Share one rhyme pair you learned."
   *(Observe: any correct pair, e.g., cat/hat.)*
