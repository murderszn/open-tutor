# Unit Assessment — Unit 02: Rhyme, Syllables, and Phonemic Awareness

**Format:** five performance tasks, adult-observed and scribed, about 15
minutes total, inside Session 16. The learner may respond by speaking,
pointing, gesturing, clapping, or demonstrating — no writing required.
**Answer key and scoring guidance:**
[answer-key.md](../answer-key.md#unit-assessment).

## Task 1 — Rhyme judge (Objectives 1, 2; RF.K.2.a)

The adult reads four word pairs slowly, stretching the endings. After
each pair the learner gives the rhyme-catch clap (rhymes) or a head shake
(does not rhyme).

1. *bed / red*
2. *cake / moon*
3. *mop / top*
4. *fish / dish*

Then: "Make up a rhyme for *ball*." *(Any end-match: tall, fall, call,
wall, or a silly rhyme.)*

**Look for:** 3 of 4 judgments correct; at least one produced rhyme with
a matching ending.

## Task 2 — Family sort (Objective 2; RF.K.2.a)

Using the generated illustration
[`assets/rhyme-family-cards.png`](../assets/rhyme-family-cards.png) (or its
text alternative):

1. "These three cards are a family. What makes them a family?"
   *(They rhyme — same at the end, -at.)*
2. The adult shows three new picture cards — *dog, sun, bee* — and a
   *frog* card. "Which family does *frog* join?"
   *(The dog family — frog/dog rhyme.)*
3. "Make one more rhyme for the -at family — a new word."
   *(sat, mat, rat, pat, or a silly rhyme.)*

**Look for:** names the rhyme reason; sorts frog with dog; produces one
new -at rhyme.

## Task 3 — Beat counter (Objectives 3, 4; RF.K.2.b)

1. "Clap the parts in *turtle*." *(Two claps: tur-tle.)*
2. "Clap the parts in *elephant*." *(Three claps: el-e-phant.)*
3. Robot voice: "mon ... key." "What word?" *(Monkey.)*
4. "Take apart *basket*." *(Bas ... ket.)*

**Look for:** claps match syllables (2, then 3); blends *monkey*; takes
apart *basket* into two parts.

## Task 4 — Beginning and rest (Objective 5; RF.K.2.c)

1. "Blend: beginning /f/, rest *-ish*." *(Fish.)*
2. "Blend: beginning /p/, rest *-ig*." *(Pig.)*
3. "Take apart *mop* — beginning and rest." *(/m/ ... -op.)*
4. "Which part makes the rhyme in *sun, fun, run*?" *(The rest — -un;
   "the ending" accepted.)*

**Look for:** 3 of 4 correct; the rime identified as the rhyming part.

## Task 5 — Sound-play talk circle (Objective 6; SL.K.1)

The adult starts a mini talk circle with the talking stick:

1. "What is one rule for our talking?" *(Listen / take turns / stay on
   the topic.)*
2. "Teach me one thing you learned about word sounds." *(Any accurate
   item: a rhyme pair, a clapped word, a take-apart, the rhyme recipe.)*
3. The learner asks the adult one question about sounds or rhymes.
   *(Any genuine question — "Can you make a rhyme for *zebra*?" counts.)*

**Look for:** follows the turn-taking; shares one accurate sound-play
item; asks a genuine question.

## Scoring guidance

Each task is secure (all "look for" items) / developing (half or more) /
not yet (fewer than half). 4–5 tasks secure = ready for Unit 03. 3 =
re-teach the missed tasks' lessons in the flexible week before Unit 03.
Fewer than 3 = re-teach the full unit's highest-need lessons (see
[teacher guide](../teacher-guide.md#re-teach-triggers)) before moving on.
