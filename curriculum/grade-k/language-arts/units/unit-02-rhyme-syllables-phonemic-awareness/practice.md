# Practice, Game, and Review Sessions — Unit 02

Named sessions referenced by the [16-session pacing](README.md#16-session-pacing-4-weeks--4-sessions-15-20-minutes-each).
Each session is 15–20 minutes. All responses may be oral, pointing,
gesturing, clapping, or drawing; the adult scribes.

## Session A — Rhyme song basket

*Used in: Session 2.*

1. The adult sings or chants two short original rhyming verses slowly,
   with a steady beat (drum optional). Example verse (original):
   > "The bee in the tree / is buzzing with glee, / she sips and she
   > sups / on honey in cups!"
2. The learner's job: make the rhyme-catch clap every time two words
   rhyme (*tree/glee*, *sups/cups*). The adult pauses after each pair at
   first, then sings through.
3. Second verse — a traditional public-domain rhyme the adult knows
   (e.g., "Hickory Dickory Dock"): the learner claps the rhymes
   (*dock/clock*, *mouse/house*).
4. Closing: "Which rhymes did you catch today?" The learner names or
   re-says at least one pair.

**Success:** claps land on actual rhyme pairs (not on every beat) by the
end of the session.

## Session B — Rhyme detective

*Used in: Session 3.*

The learner is a detective; the adult reads three words, and the
detective finds the one that does **not** rhyme.

1. Round 1: *cat, hat, dog.* ("Dog is the odd one out — it doesn't
   rhyme!")
2. Round 2: *sun, fun, pig.*
3. Round 3: *bee, tree, ball.*
4. Round 4 (challenge): *mop, top, cup.* Then: *box, fox, fish.*
5. Detective's report: the learner explains one catch in their own words
   ("*Cup* doesn't rhyme — the end is different").

**Success:** 4 of 5 odd-ones-out found; the explanation names the ending
(or demonstrates it) at least once.

## Session C — Picture sort relay

*Used in: Session 6.*

1. The generated illustration
   [`assets/rhyme-family-cards.png`](assets/rhyme-family-cards.png) is
   displayed as the model: "The -at family: cat, hat, bat. Keep the
   ending, change the beginning!"
2. Three 'house' cards on the mat: DOG, SUN, BEE. A pile of picture
   cards: frog, log, fun, run, tree, pig, moon, cow.
3. The learner carries one card at a time across the room (or table) and
   places it in its family's house — or on the "waiting bench" if it
   belongs nowhere today. Correct placements: frog→DOG, log→DOG,
   fun→SUN, run→SUN, tree→BEE; pig, moon, cow wait on the bench.
4. After sorting, the learner reads each family aloud: "The -og family:
   dog, frog, log!"

**Success:** at least 4 of 5 family cards placed correctly; bench cards
not forced into families.

## Session D — Robot says

*Used in: Session 10.*

1. The adult is the robot: "pen ... cil." The learner blends and points
   to the matching object or picture (*pencil!*).
2. Six rounds: *ta-ble, ap-ple, win-dow, bas-ket, tur-tle, ba-na-na*
   (three parts on the last one).
3. Swap: the learner is the robot and the adult blends — slowly at
   first, then faster. The learner judges: "Right!" or "Try again!"
4. Closing challenge: the robot says a **one**-part word ("dog") — the
   learner notices: "That robot is broken — only one part!"

**Success:** 5 of 6 blends correct; the learner can spot the one-part
"broken robot."

## Session F — Rhyme round-up review

*Used in: Session 4.*

1. Quick re-sing of the Session A verse; rhyme-catch claps.
2. Five rapid rhyme-detector pairs (yes/no): *pig/wig, bed/sun, cake/
   snake, mop/moon, ball/tall.*
3. Each learner-made rhyme from the week is celebrated: "Who made a
   rhyme for *fun*? Say it loud!"
4. Re-check of Lesson 01 exit items for anyone who missed them.

**Success:** 4 of 5 detector pairs correct; every learner has produced
at least one rhyme this week.

## Session G — Beat review

*Used in: Session 8.*

1. Name-clap warm-up: clap everyone's name in the room.
2. Syllable sort: picture cards sorted into 1-clap, 2-clap, 3-clap piles
   (*cat, sun* → 1; *pencil, window* → 2; *banana, elephant* → 3).
3. Robot round: three blends (*mon-key, rab-bit, sock-et*).
4. Rhyme lightning: adult says a word, learner fires back a rhyme
   (*bed → red!*, *cake → snake!*).
5. Re-check of Lesson 02–03 exit items for anyone who missed them.

**Success:** syllable piles 5 of 6 correct; blends 2 of 3 correct;
at least one lightning rhyme produced.
