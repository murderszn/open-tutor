# Unit Assessment — Unit 04 (Session 16)

**Format:** performance tasks, oral/pointing/writing; adult-scribed
where noted. 15–20 minutes, split across two sittings if the learner
tires. Do not show the [answer key](../answer-key.md) to the learner.

**How to score (adult only):** each task is rated secure, nearly
there, or re-teach using the bands below. Tasks 1–3 at "secure" and
Tasks 4–5 at "secure" or "nearly there" = ready for Unit 05. Any task
at "re-teach" = a flexible week on that objective first (see the
[teacher guide](../teacher-guide.md#re-teach-triggers)). The
assessment is formative: it guides teaching, it does not grade the
child.

## Task 1 — Blend eight words (objective 1)

The adult sounds out each word, one sound at a time; the learner says
the word.

1. /h/ /o/ /t/
2. /f/ /i/ /n/
3. /b/ /e/ /d/
4. /t/ /u/ /b/
5. /l/ /o/ /g/
6. /s/ /a/ /d/
7. /w/ /i/ /g/
8. /r/ /u/ /n/

**Bands:** 7–8 correct = secure; 5–6 = nearly there; 4 or fewer =
re-teach.

## Task 2 — Isolate six sounds (objective 2)

The adult says each word; the learner says the asked-for sound.

1. *Dog* — middle sound.
2. *Mop* — first sound.
3. *Hen* — last sound.
4. *Cup* — middle sound.
5. *Sit* — first sound.
6. *Red* — last sound.

**Bands:** 6 correct = secure; 4–5 = nearly there; 3 or fewer =
re-teach.

## Task 3 — Segment six words (objective 3)

The adult says each word; the learner pushes one chip per sound, in
order, and says the sounds back.

1. *Hat*
2. *Pin*
3. *Rug*
4. *Jet*
5. *Fog*
6. *Cub*

**Bands:** 6 correct = secure; 4–5 = nearly there; 3 or fewer =
re-teach.

## Task 4 — Build two word chains (objective 4)

The learner builds each chain with letter tiles, changing exactly one
sound per step, real words only.

- Chain (a): start at *cat*; change the first sound to /h/; then
  change the last sound to /m/; then change the first sound to /j/.
  (Three new words.)
- Chain (b): start at *sun*; change the first sound to /b/; then
  change the last sound to /g/. (Two new words.)

**Bands:** both chains correct = secure; one chain correct = nearly
there; neither chain correct = re-teach.

## Task 5 — Sight words, spelling, look-alikes (objectives 5–6)

- **Sight words:** the adult shows the, of, to, you mixed up; the
  learner reads each by sight.
- **Spelling:** the adult dictates *dog*, *pen*, *cup*, *sit*; the
  learner writes each word, then reads it back by blending.
- **Look-alikes:** the adult shows MOP and MAP; the learner says
  which letter differs and what sounds the two letters say.

**Bands:** secure = 4/4 sight words + 3–4/4 spellings in sound order
+ look-alike right; nearly there = 3/4 sight words + 2/4 spellings,
or the look-alike missed; re-teach = fewer than 3/4 sight words or
fewer than 2/4 spellings in sound order.

## After the assessment

Record the task ratings and the re-teach plan in private storage
(never in this public repository). If every task is secure or nearly
there, Unit 05 may begin; otherwise use a flexible week first.
