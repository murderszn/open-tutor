# Formative Quiz — Unit 04 (Week 18, Session 15)

**Format:** oral, pointing, and short writing; adult-scribed where
noted. 15–20 minutes. The learner may use the letter tiles as a
reference for item 10 only if the adult notes that help; items 1–9
are without reference. Do not show the
[answer key](../answer-key.md) to the learner.

**How to score (adult only):** 9–10 = secure; 7–8 = nearly there,
re-teach the missed items in Session 16's planning time; 6 or fewer =
use a flexible week to re-teach before Unit 05. The quiz is
formative: it guides teaching, it does not grade the child.

## Items

1. Blend: the adult says the sounds /m/ /a/ /t/; the learner says
   the word.
2. Blend: the adult says the sounds /p/ /i/ /g/; the learner says
   the word.
3. Blend: the adult says the sounds /s/ /u/ /n/; the learner says
   the word.
4. Isolate: "What is the first sound in *dog*?"
5. Isolate (two parts, both needed for the point): "What is the
   middle sound in *bed*?" and "What is the last sound in *cup*?"
6. Segment: the adult says *tap*; the learner pushes one chip per
   sound, in order, and says the sounds back.
7. Change one sound: "The word is *cat*. Change the last sound to
   /p/. What is the new word?"
8. Sight words: the adult shows the four sight-word cards mixed up
   (the, of, to, you); the learner reads each by sight.
9. Look-alikes: the adult shows PIN and PAN; the learner says which
   letter differs and what sounds the two letters say.
10. Spelling: the adult dictates *mop*; the learner writes the word
    on paper, then reads it back by blending.

## After the quiz

Share the flip-book project (see [project.md](../project.md#part-3--share))
after a break. Record which items were missed; they set Session 16's
re-teach plan.
