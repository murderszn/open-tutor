# Resource Pack — Unit 04: Blending, Segmenting, and Regular CVC Words

## Summary

**Goal:** give the guiding adult free, no-account routes to support
kindergarten blending, segmenting, sound substitution, sight-word
reading, and phonetic spelling of regular CVC words — the first real
reading and spelling moves.
**Age fit:** kindergarten (about 5–6); all items are oral, musical,
or hands-on, with no sentence reading required of the learner.
