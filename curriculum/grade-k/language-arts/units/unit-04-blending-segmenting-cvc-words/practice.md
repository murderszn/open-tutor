# Practice, Game, and Review Sessions — Unit 04

All sessions are 15–20 minutes, adult-led, oral/pointing/drawing
responses unless a session says "write." The adult scribes dictated
words. Answers for every item are in the
[answer key](answer-key.md#practice-sessions).

## Session A — Blending train practice (Session 2)

Three toy cars, boxes, or floor spots in a row: car 1, car 2, car 3.
The adult loads one sound per car, then the train "arrives" as one
word.

1. Cars: /c/ /a/ /t/ → *cat*.
2. Cars: /d/ /o/ /g/ → *dog*.
3. Cars: /h/ /a/ /t/ → *hat*.
4. Cars: /s/ /i/ /t/ → *sit*.
5. Cars: /m/ /u/ /g/ → *mug*.
6. The learner loads the cars for *pen* (/p/ /e/ /n/) and drives
   the train: *pen*.

## Session B — Picture blend game (Session 3)

Show the generated illustration
[`assets/cvc-blending-pictures.png`](assets/cvc-blending-pictures.png)
on a screen or printed large. It shows three pictures in a row: a
gray cat sitting on a blue mat, a pink pig, and a smiling yellow sun.

1. The adult blends slowly: /c/ /a/ /t/ — "Point to the picture!"
   (Cat.)
2. The adult blends: /p/ /i/ /g/ — "Point to the picture!" (Pig.)
3. The adult blends: /s/ /u/ /n/ — "Point to the picture!" (Sun.)
4. The learner blends for the adult: /c/ /a/ /t/, and the adult
   points (cat) — the learner checks the adult.
5. Speed round: the adult blends all three words, faster each time;
   the learner points to each picture in order: cat, pig, sun.
6. Bonus: name one more word from the word bank that rhymes with
   each picture word — *cat* → *hat*; *pig* → *big*; *sun* → *fun*.
   (Any real word-bank rhyme counts.)

**Text-only alternative:** the picture shows three pictures in a
row, left to right: a gray cat sitting on a blue mat, a pink pig,
and a smiling yellow sun. Every question in this session can be
answered from this sentence alone, without seeing the picture.

## Session C — Blending review (Session 4)

1. Blend: /f/ /a/ /n/ → *fan*.
2. Blend: /b/ /e/ /d/ → *bed*.
3. Blend: /t/ /o/ /p/ → *top*.
4. Blend: /r/ /u/ /n/ → *run*.
5. Blend: /w/ /i/ /g/ → *wig*.
6. Re-check of Lesson 01 exit items: /c/ /a/ /p/ → *cap*;
   /s/ /i/ /t/ → *sit*; "How many sounds are in *cat*?" (Three.)

## Session D — Sound detective practice (Session 6)

The adult says a word; the learner names the asked-for sound.

1. *Dog* — first sound? (/d/.)
2. *Dog* — middle sound? (/o/.)
3. *Dog* — last sound? (/g/.)
4. *Mop* — last sound? (/p/.)
5. *Hen* — first sound? (/h/.)
6. *Cup* — middle sound? (/u/.)
7. *Pin* — last sound? (/n/.)
8. *Red* — middle sound? (/e/.)

## Session E — Sound bingo game (Session 7)

Draw a 3×3 bingo grid of pictures (or words the adult reads):
cat, dog, pig, sun, mop, bed, hen, cup, hat. The adult calls a sound
and a position ("Find a word whose **last** sound is /t/"); the
learner marks every grid word that fits.

1. Last sound /t/: cat, hat. (Both end in /t/.)
2. First sound /m/: mop. (Only mop starts with /m/.)
3. Middle sound /i/: pig. (Only pig has /i/ in the middle.)
4. First sound /h/: hen, hat. (Both start with /h/.)
5. Middle sound /u/: sun, cup. (Both have /u/ in the middle.)
6. Last sound /g/: dog, pig. (Both end in /g/.)

## Session F — Isolation review (Session 8)

1. *Fan* — middle sound? (/a/.)
2. *Jet* — last sound? (/t/.)
3. *Kit* — first sound? (/k/.)
4. *Bug* — first, middle, last? (/b/, /u/, /g/.)
5. *Red* — where does /e/ live? (Middle.)
6. Re-check of Lesson 01–02 exit items: /c/ /a/ /p/ → *cap*;
   *hat* first sound /h/; *pig* middle sound /i/; *ten* last
   sound /n/.

## Session G — Sound-box practice (Session 10)

Three boxes on paper, three chips. The learner segments each word,
one chip per sound, left to right, then says the sounds back.

1. *Sun* → /s/ /u/ /n/, three chips.
2. *Mop* → /m/ /o/ /p/, three chips.
3. *Bed* → /b/ /e/ /d/, three chips.
4. *Fan* → /f/ /a/ /n/, three chips.
5. *Pig* → /p/ /i/ /g/, three chips.
6. *Tub* → /t/ /u/ /b/, three chips; then swap chips for tiles:
   T U B, and read it back: *tub*.

## Session H — Mixed review (used in Session 12 re-teach planning)

The adult picks the learner's weakest skill from Sessions 1–11 and
re-runs its session with new words from the word bank. Suggested
word sets if no weakness stands out:

- Blending: /l/ /e/ /g/ → *leg*; /f/ /i/ /n/ → *fin*;
  /c/ /o/ /b/ → *cob*.
- Isolation: *leg* middle /e/; *fin* last /n/; *cob* first /k/.
- Segmenting: *leg* → /l/ /e/ /g/; *fin* → /f/ /i/ /n/;
  *cob* → /c/ /o/ /b/.
- Change-one-sound: *leg* → *peg* (first); *fin* → *pin* (first);
  *cob* → *mop* is two changes — do not use; use *cob* → *mob*
  (first).
