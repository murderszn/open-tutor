# Answer Key — Unit 04: Blending, Segmenting, and Regular CVC Words

**Adult only. Do not show this file to the learner.** Every answer
below was solved independently of the student materials and reconciled
against them. Accept any equivalent correct response (for example, a
pointed picture instead of a spoken word) where the lesson allows it.

## Lesson 01 — Blend sounds to read CVC words

- **Guided practice:** 1. *pig* (/p/ /i/ /g/). 2. *sun* (/s/ /u/ /n/).
  3. *mop* (/m/ /o/ /p/). 4. *hen* (/h/ /e/ /n/); *bed* (/b/ /e/ /d/).
  5. Any real word-bank word built from three tiles, e.g., H A T →
  *hat*, C A P → *cap*, S U N → *sun*.
- **Independent practice:** 1. *hat*. 2. *fan*. 3. *ten*. 4. *log*.
  5. *bug*.
- **Application ("Word squisher"):** /r/ /u/ /n/ → *run*; the acted
  word and the learner's comment vary — the blended word must be
  *run*.
- **Exit check:** 1. *cap*. 2. *sit*. 3. Three sounds: /c/ /a/ /t/.

## Lesson 02 — First, middle, and last sounds

- **Guided practice:** 1. *Dog*: first /d/, middle /o/, last /g/.
  2. *Hen* middle: /e/. 3. *Mop*: first /m/, last /p/. 4. In *pin*:
  /p/ is first, /i/ is middle, /n/ is last. 5. *Mop* is the odd one
  out (starts with /m/; *sun* and *sit* start with /s/).
- **Independent practice:** 1. *Hat* first: /h/. 2. *Pig* middle:
  /i/. 3. *Ten* last: /n/. 4. *Bug*: /b/, /u/, /g/, in order.
  5. /e/ in *red* is the middle sound.
- **Application ("Sound detective badge"):** qualifying cards are
  *dog* (middle /o/) and *mop* (middle /o/). *Cat* (/a/), *pig*
  (/i/), and *sun* (/u/) do not contain /o/ and do not qualify.
- **Exit check:** 1. *Fan* middle: /a/. 2. *Jet* last: /t/.
  3. *Kit* first: /k/.

## Lesson 03 — Pulling words apart

- **Guided practice:** 1. *Sun*: /s/ /u/ /n/, three chips.
  2. *Bed*: /b/ /e/ /d/, three chips. 3. *Fan*: /f/ /a/ /n/, three
  chips. 4. *Cup*: /c/ /u/ /p/, three chips, said back touching each
  chip. 5. No — /d/ /og/ is two parts (onset-and-rime); the
  three-sound segmentation is /d/ /o/ /g/, three chips.
- **Independent practice:** 1. *Hat*: /h/ /a/ /t/. 2. *Pin*:
  /p/ /i/ /n/. 3. *Rug*: /r/ /u/ /g/. 4. *Jet*: /j/ /e/ /t/.
  5. *Tub*: /t/ /u/ /b/; tiles in order T U B.
- **Application ("Chip counter"):** *hen* → 3 sounds; *mop* → 3;
  *sit* → 3; *dog* → 3; *bun* → 3. All five land in the "3 sounds"
  pile.
- **Exit check:** 1. *Log*: /l/ /o/ /g/, three chips. 2. *Wig*:
  /w/ /i/ /g/, three chips. 3. Three sounds: /s/ /u/ /n/.

## Lesson 04 — Change one sound, make a new word

- **Guided practice:** 1. *Cat* → *hat* (first /c/ → /h/).
  2. *Hat* → *ham* (last /t/ → /m/). 3. *Pin* → *pan* (middle
  /i/ → /a/). 4. *Mop* → *top* (first /m/ → /t/). 5. *Sun* →
  *bun* (first /s/ → /b/); the middle sound of *bun* is already
  /u/, so "change /u/ to /u/" changes nothing — the learner must
  say so, then change the last sound /n/ → /g/ to make *bug*.
- **Independent practice:** 1. *Dog* → *log* (first /d/ → /l/).
  2. *Log* → *leg* (middle /o/ → /e/). 3. *Sit* → *sip* (last
  /t/ → /p/). 4. *Red* → *bed* (first /r/ → /b/). 5. Example chain:
  *fan* → *pan* (first /f/ → /p/) → *pin* (middle /a/ → /i/) →
  *pig* (last /n/ → /g/). Any chain with one sound changed per
  step and all real words counts.
- **Application ("Word-chain race"):** one correct chain from *cat*
  to *hop* is *cat* → *cap* → *map* → *mop* → *top* → *hop*
  (five steps). Any correct chain counts, however long.
- **Exit check:** 1. *Hen* → *pen* (first /h/ → /p/). 2. *Pen* →
  *pin* (middle /e/ → /i/). 3. One sound only — the last sound,
  /t/ → /p/.

## Lesson 05 — Sight words and look-alike words

- **Guided practice:** 1. Cards mixed up: you, of, the, to — the
  learner reads each by sight. 2. The learner's sight words in the
  phrases: "the pig" (*the*); "a cup of water" (*of*); "to the sun"
  (*to*, *the*); "you and the dog" (*you*, *the*). 3. *Mop* vs.
  *mob*: last letter differs — P /p/ vs. B /b/. 4. *Sit* vs. *sat*:
  middle letter differs — I /i/ vs. A /a/. 5. *Sun* vs. *bun*:
  first letter differs — S /s/ vs. B /b/.
- **Independent practice:** 1. Sight words in order: to, you, the,
  of. 2. Point to *you*, then to *of*, from mixed-up cards.
  3. *Hen* vs. *pen*: first letter — H /h/ vs. P /p/. 4. *Dog* vs.
  *log*: first letter — D /d/ vs. L /l/. 5. *Bug* vs. *bag*:
  middle letter — U /u/ vs. A /a/.
- **Application ("Sight-word hunt"):** sight word *The* appears in
  all three sentences ("The cat sat." "The pig ran." "The dog
  sat."); CVC words read by blending: cat, sat, pig, ran, dog.
- **Exit check:** 1. Sight words mixed up: of, you, to, the.
  2. *Cap* vs. *map*: first letter — C /k/ vs. M /m/. 3. *Of* is
  pronounced /uv/, not the way its letters sound, so it is
  memorized whole instead of sounded out.

## Lesson 06 — Spelling CVC words the way they sound

- **Guided practice:** 1. *Cat*: C A T. 2. *Sun*: S U N. 3. *Pig*:
  P I G. 4. *Ht* is missing the middle vowel A; the fix is H A T.
  5. *Mpo* has O and P swapped; the fix is M O P.
- **Independent practice:** 1. *Dog*: D O G. 2. *Pen*: P E N.
  3. *Cup*: C U P. 4. *Sit*: S I T. 5. *Bug*: B U G, read back by
  blending as /b/ /u/ /g/ → *bug*.
- **Application ("Word shop"):** labels — *hat* (H A T), *mop*
  (M O P), *sun* (S U N). A label counts when its letters are in
  sound order, even if a shape is reversed.
- **Exit check:** 1. *Cap*: C A P. 2. *Red*: R E D. 3. Read the word
  back by blending to check it matches the dictated word.

## Practice sessions

- **Session A:** 1. *cat*. 2. *dog*. 3. *hat*. 4. *sit*. 5. *mug*.
  6. *pen*.
- **Session B:** 1. Cat (left picture). 2. Pig (middle picture).
  3. Sun (right picture). 4. Cat — the adult points, the learner
  checks. 5. In order: cat, pig, sun. 6. Rhymes: *cat* → *hat*
  (also bat, mat, sat, rat); *pig* → *big* (also fig, dig, wig);
  *sun* → *fun* (also bun, run).
- **Session C:** 1. *fan*. 2. *bed*. 3. *top*. 4. *run*. 5. *wig*.
  6. *cap*; *sit*; three sounds in *cat*.
- **Session D:** 1. /d/. 2. /o/. 3. /g/. 4. /p/. 5. /h/. 6. /u/.
  7. /n/. 8. /e/.
- **Session E:** 1. Cat, hat. 2. Mop. 3. Pig. 4. Hen, hat. 5. Sun,
  cup. 6. Dog, pig.
- **Session F:** 1. /a/. 2. /t/. 3. /k/. 4. /b/, /u/, /g/.
  5. Middle. 6. *cap*; *hat* first /h/; *pig* middle /i/; *ten*
  last /n/.
- **Session G:** 1. *Sun*: /s/ /u/ /n/. 2. *Mop*: /m/ /o/ /p/.
  3. *Bed*: /b/ /e/ /d/. 4. *Fan*: /f/ /a/ /n/. 5. *Pig*:
  /p/ /i/ /g/. 6. *Tub*: /t/ /u/ /b/; tiles T U B; read back *tub*.
- **Session H:** blending — *leg*, *fin*, *cob*; isolation — *leg*
  middle /e/, *fin* last /n/, *cob* first /k/; segmenting — *leg*
  /l/ /e/ /g/, *fin* /f/ /i/ /n/, *cob* /c/ /o/ /b/;
  change-one-sound — *leg* → *peg*, *fin* → *pin*, *cob* → *mob*
  (first sound each time). *Cob* → *mop* is NOT a one-sound change
  (first and middle sounds both change) and must not be accepted.

## Project — My CVC Flip Book

- **Part 1 families:** -at: cat, hat, mat, bat, sat, rat; -an: fan,
  pan, can, van, man; -ig: pig, big, fig, dig, wig. At least three
  scribed words per page; learner-written words suggested: *cat*,
  *fan*, *pig*.
- **Part 2 families:** -op: mop, top, hop, pop; -un: sun, bun, run,
  fun. Learner-written words suggested: *mop*, *sun*.
- **Sight-word page sentences:** "The cat sat." (sight: The; CVC:
  cat, sat.) "The pig ran." (sight: The; CVC: pig, ran.) "The dog
  sat." (sight: The; CVC: dog, sat.)
- **Rubric scoring:** see [project.md](project.md#rubric) — 13–15
  ready for Unit 05; 9–12 ready with re-teach; 5–8 repeat weakest
  pages in a flexible week.

## Formative quiz (assessments/week-04_quiz.md)

1. *mat*. 2. *pig*. 3. *sun*. 4. First sound of *dog*: /d/.
5. Middle sound of *bed*: /e/; last sound of *cup*: /p/ (both parts
   needed for the point). 6. *Tap*: /t/ /a/ /p/, three chips.
7. *Cat* with last sound changed to /p/: *cap*. 8. Sight words,
   mixed up: the, of, to, you — all four read by sight. 9. *Pin*
   vs. *pan*: the middle letter differs — I /i/ vs. A /a/.
10. *Mop* spelled: M O P (letters in sound order; a reversed shape
    still counts if the order is right).

**Scoring:** 9–10 = secure; 7–8 = nearly there, re-teach the missed
items in Session 16's planning time; 6 or fewer = use a flexible
week to re-teach before Unit 05. The quiz is formative: it guides
teaching, it does not grade the child.

## Unit assessment (assessments/unit-assessment.md)

- **Task 1 (blend 8):** hot, fin, bed, tub, log, sad, wig, run.
  Secure 7–8; nearly there 5–6; re-teach 4 or fewer.
- **Task 2 (isolate 6):** *dog* middle /o/; *mop* first /m/; *hen*
  last /n/; *cup* middle /u/; *sit* first /s/; *red* last /d/.
  Secure 6; nearly there 4–5; re-teach 3 or fewer.
- **Task 3 (segment 6):** *hat* /h/ /a/ /t/; *pin* /p/ /i/ /n/;
  *rug* /r/ /u/ /g/; *jet* /j/ /e/ /t/; *fog* /f/ /o/ /g/;
  *cub* /c/ /u/ /b/. Secure 6; nearly there 4–5; re-teach 3 or fewer.
- **Task 4 (chains):** (a) *cat* → *hat* (first /c/ → /h/) →
  *ham* (last /t/ → /m/) → *jam* (first /h/ → /j/); (b) *sun* →
  *bun* (first /s/ → /b/) → *bug* (last /n/ → /g/). Secure = both
  chains correct; nearly there = one chain correct; re-teach =
  neither chain correct.
- **Task 5 (sight, spelling, look-alikes):** sight words the, of,
  to, you (4/4); spelling dog D O G, pen P E N, cup C U P, sit
  S I T; look-alike *mop* vs. *map*: middle letter differs —
  O /o/ vs. A /a/. Secure = 4/4 sight + 3–4/4 spelling + look-alike
  right; nearly there = 3/4 sight + 2/4 spelling, or look-alike
  missed; re-teach = fewer than 3/4 sight or fewer than 2/4 spelling.

**Overall:** Tasks 1–3 at "secure" and Tasks 4–5 at "secure" or
"nearly there" = ready for Unit 05. Any task at "re-teach" = a
flexible week on that objective first (see the
[teacher guide](teacher-guide.md#re-teach-triggers)).
