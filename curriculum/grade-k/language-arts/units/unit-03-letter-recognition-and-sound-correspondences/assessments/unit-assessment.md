# Unit Assessment — Unit 03 (Session 16)

**Format:** five performance tasks, one per unit objective, observed
by the adult. 20–30 minutes across two sittings if the learner tires;
never assess a tired kindergartner and trust the result. Do not show
the [answer key](../answer-key.md) to the learner.

**Readiness rule:** the learner is ready for Unit 04 when Tasks 1–2
are at "secure" and Tasks 3–5 are at "secure" or "nearly there" (see
scoring under each task). A task below "nearly there" gets a
flexible-week re-teach of that objective before Unit 04 begins.

## Task 1 — Name all uppercase letters (objective 1)

The adult shows all 26 uppercase cards, first in order, then shuffled.
The learner names each.

- **Secure:** 23–26 correct in order and 23–26 correct shuffled.
- **Nearly there:** 18–22 correct in either round.
- **Re-teach:** fewer than 18 correct in either round.

## Task 2 — Name all lowercase letters (objective 2)

The adult shows all 26 lowercase cards, first in order, then shuffled.
The learner names each. The adult also shows b, d, p, q twice each in
random order as a tricky-pair check inside this task.

- **Secure:** 23–26 correct in order and 23–26 correct shuffled,
  with at least 6 of 8 tricky-pair items correct.
- **Nearly there:** 18–22 correct in either round, or 4–5 of 8
  tricky-pair items correct.
- **Re-teach:** fewer than 18 correct in either round, or fewer
  than 4 of 8 tricky-pair items correct.

## Task 3 — Say consonant sounds (objective 3)

The adult shows the 21 consonant cards (B, C, D, F, G, H, J, K, L, M,
N, P, Q, R, S, T, V, W, X, Y, Z) shuffled. The learner says the
primary sound for each (Q with U as /kw/; X as /ks/).

- **Secure:** 19–21 correct.
- **Nearly there:** 15–18 correct.
- **Re-teach:** fewer than 15 correct.

## Task 4 — Say vowel sounds, short and long (objective 4)

The adult shows A, E, I, O, U twice: once asking for the short sound,
once for the long sound.

- **Secure:** 9–10 correct across both rounds.
- **Nearly there:** 7–8 correct.
- **Re-teach:** fewer than 7 correct.

## Task 5 — Match letters to sounds, both directions, and write (objective 5)

Part A — sound → letter: the adult says ten sounds — /b/, /f/,
/l/, /n/, /s/, /w/, /z/, /a/ (short), /o/ (short), /ā/ (long A) —
and the learner points to the right letter each time.

Part B — letter → sound: the adult shows ten letters — D, H, J, K,
P, R, T, V, Y, E (long) — and the learner says the sound for each.

Part C — writing: the adult dictates eight sounds — /b/, /d/, /g/,
/m/, /p/, /r/, /e/ (short), /u/ (short) — and the learner writes
the letter for each.

- **Secure:** Part A 9–10, Part B 9–10, Part C 7–8.
- **Nearly there:** Part A 7–8, Part B 7–8, Part C 5–6.
- **Re-teach:** below "nearly there" on any part.

## Scoring summary (adult records privately)

| Task | Secure | Nearly there | Re-teach |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 — Uppercase names | 23–26 / 23–26 | 18–22 | < 18 |
| 2 — Lowercase names | 23–26 / 23–26, 6–8 tricky | 18–22, or 4–5 tricky | < 18, or < 4 tricky |
| 3 — Consonant sounds | 19–21 | 15–18 | < 15 |
| 4 — Vowel sounds | 9–10 | 7–8 | < 7 |
| 5 — Match + write | A 9–10, B 9–10, C 7–8 | A 7–8, B 7–8, C 5–6 | below nearly there |

**Next teaching steps:** re-teach any "re-teach" objective with the
[teacher guide](../teacher-guide.md#re-teach-triggers) routine for that
objective in a flexible week; "nearly there" objectives get one
Session 12-style review before Unit 04. Record the outcome privately;
never publish learner results.
