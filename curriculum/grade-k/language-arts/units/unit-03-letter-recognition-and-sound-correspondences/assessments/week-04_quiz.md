# Formative Quiz — Unit 03 (Week 14, Session 15)

**Format:** oral, pointing, and short writing; adult-scribed where
noted. 15–20 minutes. The learner may use the letter cards as a
reference for the writing items only if the adult notes that help;
items 1–8 are without reference. Do not show the
[answer key](../answer-key.md) to the learner.

**How to score (adult only):** 9–10 = secure; 7–8 = nearly there,
re-teach the missed items in Session 16's planning time; 6 or fewer =
use a flexible week to re-teach before Unit 04. The quiz is
formative: it guides teaching, it does not grade the child.

## Items

1. Name these uppercase letters mixed up: T, A, Z, M, Q.
2. Name these lowercase letters mixed up: g, s, q, a, w.
3. Tell b from d: the adult shows d, b, b, d in that order; the
   learner names each.
4. Say the sound for each letter: B, S, G, Z.
5. Say the sound for each letter: M, Q, X, V.
6. Say the short sound for each vowel: A, E, I, O, U.
7. Say the long sound for each vowel: A, E, I, O, U.
8. Sound → letter: the adult says /r/, /k/ (as in *cat*), /n/,
   /y/; the learner points to the right letter each time.
9. The adult says a word; the learner says its first sound and the
   letter that says it: *sun*, *pig*, *web*, *fox* (for *fox*, the
   letter is X and the sound /ks/ is at the **end** of the word).
10. Writing: the adult dictates five sounds — /m/, /a/ (short A),
    /t/, /s/, /o/ (short O) — and the learner writes the letter for
    each on paper.
