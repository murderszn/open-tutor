# Resource Pack — Unit 03: Letter Recognition and Sound Correspondences

## Summary

**Goal:** give the guiding adult free, no-account routes to support
kindergarten letter recognition and letter–sound knowledge — naming
all uppercase and lowercase letters, saying primary consonant sounds
and long/short vowel sounds, matching letters to sounds in both
directions, and writing letters for sounds.
**Age fit:** kindergarten (about 5–6); all items are visual, musical,
or hands-on, with no reading required of the learner beyond letter
naming.
