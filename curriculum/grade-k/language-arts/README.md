## What this track covers

A full-year (about 36 weeks) kindergarten language arts course organized around
oral language and listening, print concepts, phonological awareness, letter–sound
knowledge, emergent reading, shared reading with retelling, early informational
reading, and drawing-to-writing in opinion, informative, and narrative forms.
Sessions are short (15–20 minutes, about four per week) and built on read-alouds,
sound play, pointing, drawing, and adult-guided conversation — the way
kindergarteners actually learn to read and write.

## Track objectives (measurable, adult-assessed)

By the end of the year the learner will be able to:

1. Handle print with purpose: track words left-to-right, top-to-bottom,
   page-by-page; point to words separated by spaces; name all upper- and
   lowercase letters.
2. Play with spoken language: recognize and produce rhymes; count, blend, and
   segment syllables; blend and segment onsets and rimes; isolate initial,
   medial, and final sounds in simple CVC words; change one sound to make a new
   word.
3. Know letter–sound correspondences for consonants and the long/short sounds of
   the five major vowels; read common high-frequency words by sight.
4. Read simple emergent-reader and decodable texts with purpose and understanding.
5. Retell familiar stories with key details; identify characters, settings, and
   major events; describe what an illustration shows about the story.
6. Ask and answer questions about key details in informational texts; name the
   main topic; use illustrations to describe people, places, things, or ideas.
7. Compose opinion, informative, and narrative pieces by drawing, dictating, and
   writing; add details with adult guidance.
8. Join conversations about topics and texts: follow discussion rules, ask and
   answer questions, describe familiar people, places, things, and events, and
   add drawings for detail.
9. Use grade-level language conventions: question words and common prepositions;
   regular plural nouns in speech; write letters for most consonant and
   short-vowel sounds; spell simple words phonetically; sort objects into
   categories; connect words to opposites and real-life uses.

kindergarten strands (verified against the framework; not a claim of state

- Reading: Informational Text: RI.K.1–4, RI.K.6–9
- Writing: W.K.1–3, W.K.5–8
- Speaking and Listening: SL.K.1–5
- Language: L.K.1 (c–e), L.K.2 (c–d), L.K.4–6

## For the guiding adult

Kindergarten language arts is oral first: every skill starts in the ear and the
mouth — listening, talking, rhyming, clapping syllables — before it reaches the
eye and the pencil. Read the scope-and-sequence before starting; it lists safe
household materials, accessibility supports, and the diagnostic, catch-up, and
review weeks that keep pacing humane. All reading passages in future units will
be original or lawful public-domain texts, and every unit will include its own
teacher guide and answer key.
