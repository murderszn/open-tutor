# Unit Assessment — Unit 04: Maps, Symbols, and Nearby Places

**Format:** performance tasks, adult-observed and scribed (Session
16). No reading or writing is required. The learner may respond in
words, by pointing, by placing toys, by drawing, or by gesture.
Allow a hint on any task and record whether it was needed; a task
done with a hint is Developing, not Secure.

**Materials:** the generated illustration
[`assets/classroom-birdseye-map.png`](../assets/classroom-birdseye-map.png),
the learner's project map(s), the globe or land-and-water ball, two
toys, large paper and crayons.

## Tasks (one per objective)

### Task 1 — Say what a map is (Objective 1)

"Tell me what a map is. What does a map show?"

- **Secure:** a child-words definition covering both parts: a
  picture of a place, seen from above, that shows where things are
  (for example: "A map is a picture from above that shows where
  stuff is").
- **Developing:** one part only ("a picture of a room") or correct
  after a hint ("seen from where? …from above").
- **Not yet:** cannot say, or describes a non-map picture ("a
  picture of a face").

### Task 2 — Read the picture map with the legend (Objective 2)

"Using the legend, point to four places on this map: the rug, the
reading corner, a table, and the door."

- **Secure:** all four pointed to correctly, with at least one
  legend check the learner does unprompted or on request.
- **Developing:** three of four, or all four only after hints.
- **Not yet:** two or fewer, even with hints.

### Task 3 — Describe places with position words (Objective 3)

"Tell me where three things are on this map, using our position
words." (Prompt once per thing if needed: "Is the reading corner
above or below the rug?" counts as a prompt, not a free answer.)

- **Secure:** at least four correct position-word uses across the
  three descriptions (for example: "The reading corner is above the
  rug. The toy shelf is below the rug. The windows are on the left
  side. The plant is next to the table.").
- **Developing:** two or three correct uses, or correct only with
  prompts.
- **Not yet:** fewer than two correct uses, even with prompts.

### Task 4 — Follow and give a route (Objective 4)

**Part A (follow):** "Start at the door. Go to the rug. Then go to
the reading corner." The learner moves a toy along the route.
**Part B (give):** the learner hides a toy on the map and gives the
adult a two-step route to it.

- **Secure:** Part A lands correctly and Part B's route lands on
  the toy on the first or second try.
- **Developing:** one part correct, or both correct only with
  adult help.
- **Not yet:** neither part works, even with help.

### Task 5 — Build a map and find land and water (Objective 5)

**Part A (build):** the learner draws a new small map of a familiar
place (classroom, or the picture-map classroom) with at least three
symbols and dictates a legend for them.
**Part B (globe):** the adult points to a blue patch and a green
patch: "Water or land?" Then: "Is our town on land or in water?"

- **Secure:** Part A has 3+ symbols with a matching legend, and
  Part B answers all three questions correctly.
- **Developing:** Part A has 3 symbols but a mismatched legend, or
  Part B misses one question.
- **Not yet:** Part A has fewer than 3 symbols or no legend, or
  Part B misses two or more questions.

## Overall result

- **Ready for Unit 05:** Secure or Developing on all five tasks,
  with at most one Developing that the teacher guide's re-teach
  path covers in a single session.
- **Re-teach, then Unit 05:** any task at Not yet — use the
  [teacher guide](../teacher-guide.md#re-teaching-paths) for that
  objective, then re-check just that task.
- **Use a flexible week:** two or more tasks at Not yet — repeat
  the matching practice sessions across a flexible week before
  starting Unit 05. This is a normal use of the track's flexible
  weeks, not a failure.

Worked answers and acceptable variations are in the
[answer key](../answer-key.md#unit-assessment).
