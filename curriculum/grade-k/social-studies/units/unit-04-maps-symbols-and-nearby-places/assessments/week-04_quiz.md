# Formative Quiz — Unit 04, Week 4 (Session 15)

**Format:** oral/observed, adult-scribed. The adult reads each item
aloud. The learner may answer in words, by pointing, by placing a
toy, or by gesture. There is no writing requirement and no time
limit; allow a second try after a hint on any item, and record
whether the hint was needed.

**Materials:** the generated illustration
[`assets/classroom-birdseye-map.png`](../assets/classroom-birdseye-map.png),
the globe or land-and-water ball, two toys.

## Items

1. **What is a map?** "Tell me what a map is, in your own words."
2. **Read the picture map.** "Point to the rug on this map. Now
   point to the reading corner. Now point to the door." (Three
   points; each counts separately.)
3. **Use the legend.** The adult covers the legend list and points
   to the brown rectangle in the top-right. "What is this? How do
   you know?" (Then uncover the legend and let the learner check.)
4. **Position words.** "Where is the reading corner — above or
   below the rug? Which side of the map are the windows on — left
   or right? Is the teacher's desk near the toy shelf or far from
   it?" (Three answers.)
5. **Follow a route.** "Start at the door. Go to the rug. Then go
   next to the plant." The learner moves a toy along the route.
   (Counts as correct if the toy ends next to the plant, even if
   the path wobbles.)
6. **Land and water.** The adult points to a blue patch and then a
   green patch on the globe. "Is this water or land? And this? Is
   our town on land or in water?"
7. **Cumulative — rules (Unit 02).** "Name one rule for walking
   around our school."
8. **Cumulative — helpers (Unit 03).** "Name one helper and the
   place where that helper works."

## Scoring

Record each item as **Secure / Developing / Not yet**:

- **Secure:** correct without a hint (item 2 needs all three points;
  item 4 needs all three answers; item 6 needs both patches plus the
  town question).
- **Developing:** correct after one hint, or partially correct
  (for example, two of three points on item 2).
- **Not yet:** incorrect or no answer even after a hint.

**Readiness rule:** a learner who is Secure or Developing on items
1–6 is ready for Unit 05, with re-teaching on any single Not-yet
item during the flexible weeks. A learner with two or more Not-yet
items on items 1–6 should repeat the matching practice sessions
(see the [teacher guide](../teacher-guide.md#re-teaching-paths))
before Unit 05. Items 7–8 are midyear-review checks: a Not-yet there
means revisit the Unit 02/03 game in Practice G, not a reason to
hold back Unit 05 on its own.

Worked answers are in the [answer key](../answer-key.md#formative-quiz).
