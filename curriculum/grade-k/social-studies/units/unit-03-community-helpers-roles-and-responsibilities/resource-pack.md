# Resource Pack — Unit 03: Community Helpers: Roles and Responsibilities

## Goal and age fit

Give the guiding adult background reading, printable activities, and short
videos that reinforce this unit's goals — school helpers, neighborhood
helpers, tools matched to jobs, helpers working together in chains, city
helpers, and respectful thank-yous — for a kindergarten learner (ages 4–6).
The adult previews everything; videos supplement the lessons, never replace
them. All core learning is achievable with the free, no-account resources
below.

## Videos (labeled search links — adult previews required)

Direct video verification was not available in this run, so these are
**clearly labeled preferred-channel search links**, not verified individual
videos. Open each, watch fully, and confirm age fit, captions, and ad
behavior before showing the learner. Prefer official PBS KIDS / Sesame
Workshop uploads.

1. **YouTube search — Sesame Street "People in Your Neighborhood" clips**
   <https://www.youtube.com/results?search_query=Sesame+Street+people+in+your+neighborhood+community+helpers>
   *Why:* The classic segment introduces one helper at a time — job, tools,
   workplace — exactly this unit's three-question routine. Relevance:
   Lessons 01, 02. Account: none needed; ads vary — preview first.
2. **YouTube search — Daniel Tiger neighborhood jobs (PBS KIDS)**
   <https://www.youtube.com/results?search_query=Daniel+Tiger+neighborhood+jobs+PBS+KIDS>
   *Why:* Daniel Tiger's "Neighborhood Jobs" episode and clips model that
   every job matters and helpers work together. Relevance: Lessons 02, 03.
3. **YouTube search — community helper songs for kindergarten**
   <https://www.youtube.com/results?search_query=community+helpers+song+kindergarten>
   *Why:* Call-and-response songs that name helpers and tools — good
   warm-ups before Lessons 01, 02, and the Session 12 song. Relevance:
   whole unit (supplement). Preview lyrics: skip any song that names a
   helper's job incorrectly.
4. **YouTube search — how mail travels for kids**
   <https://www.youtube.com/results?search_query=how+mail+travels+mail+carrier+sorting+for+kids>
   *Why:* Short explainers that show collecting, sorting, and delivering —
   the Lesson 03 chain in video form. Relevance: Lesson 03, Practice E.
5. **PBS LearningMedia search — community helpers (PreK–K)**
   <https://www.pbslearningmedia.org/search/?q=community+helpers>
   *Why:* Standards-tagged clips and lesson plans from member stations;
   search "community helpers," "fire safety," or "mail" for PreK–K items.
   Relevance: whole unit (supplement). Access: free; the search page loads
   as an interactive app — the adult browses and previews items. A free
   educator account unlocks saving/favoriting.

## Web references (checked 2026-10-22 unless noted)

1. **Sesame Workshop — "How Helpers Keep Us Safe"** (activity page)
   <https://sesameworkshop.org/activities/how-helpers-help/>
   Verified live 2026-10-22 (page loads; related resources listed). The
   activity asks children which helpers they can find in each place and how
   they might get help — for example, a firefighter at the fire station,
   reached by calling 9-1-1 — and notes other ways to get help: talking to
   an adult (such as a teacher) or asking an adult to call for help. This
   unit follows that framing: children tell a trusted adult; adults make
   emergency calls. Relevance: teacher background for Lessons 02, 05 and
   the unit's safety rules.
2. **C3 Framework for Social Studies State Standards**
   <https://www.socialstudies.org/standards/c3>
   Landing page and K–2 indicator codes/descriptions verified 2026-10-02
   during the track audit (see the
   [scope-and-sequence](../../scope-and-sequence.md)). No state adoption or
   accreditation claimed. Relevance: standards notes in every lesson.
3. **Sesame Workshop — "Little Neighbors: Building a Better World Together"**
   Labeled search link (individual resource pages not verified this run):
   <https://www.sesameworkshop.org/> — search the site for "Little
   Neighbors." Bilingual resources on community values (safety, compassion,
   being a good neighbor) for young children and providers. Relevance:
   Lessons 01, 05, project background.
4. **NAEYC — "Instead of Discipline, Use Guidance"** (Teaching Young
   Children, Summer 2021)
   <https://www.naeyc.org/resources/pubs/tyc/summer2021/discipline-guidance>
   Verified live 2026-10-16 during Unit 02 (full article loads). Guidance
   as coaching: children learn from mistakes through firm, friendly
   mediation rather than punishment. Carried forward here for the
   Helper Fair's visitor role: visitors ask questions and thank, never
   scold a learner in role. Relevance: teacher background for Lesson 05
   and the project.
5. **Vanderbilt CSEFEL — Module 1 training deck: "Promoting Children's
   Success: Building Relationships and Creating Supportive Environments"**
   <http://csefel.vanderbilt.edu/modules-archive/module1/presenter.html>
   Verified live 2026-10-16 during Unit 02 (slide deck loads). General
   guidelines about rules and directions, and involving children in
   developing classroom routines — carried forward here as "helpers follow
   rules too" (Session 4 retrieval). Relevance: teacher background for
   Lesson 01, Session 4.

## Safety and access notes (2026-10-22)

- All listed resources are free. PBS LearningMedia may invite a free
  educator account for saving items; nothing in this unit requires an
  account.
- YouTube links are **search links, not verified videos**: the adult must
  open and preview each candidate for age fit, captions, comments, and
  advertising before the learner watches.
- No resource above asks the learner to enter a name, photo, or personal
  information. Keep it that way: use printables offline where possible.
- Fire-safety content stays at the kindergarten level used in Lesson 02:
  get out, stay out, tell a grown-up. Never stage a fire, use matches, or
  show frightening footage.
- External pages change: if a link breaks, re-run the focused queries above
  rather than substituting an unverified look-alike.
