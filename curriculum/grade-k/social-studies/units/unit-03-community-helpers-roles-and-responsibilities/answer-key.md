# Answer Key — Unit 03: Community Helpers

**Audience:** the guiding adult only. Keep this file separate from student
materials. Every practice item, quiz question, assessment task, and project
criterion below has a worked answer or an acceptable-response range. Open
responses accept any answer that matches the reasoning shown, not only the
exact wording.

## Lesson 01

- **Guided practice 1 (nurse):** school nurse; helps when someone is hurt
  or feels sick at school; tool: bandage or thermometer.
- **Guided practice 2 (bus driver):** the stop sign on the bus tells cars
  to stop; the bus driver takes children to school and home safely.
- **Guided practice 3 (error analysis):** mixed up — the custodian cleans
  (sweeps, mops) and the teacher teaches (reading, stories). Jobs belong
  to the helper trained for them.
- **Guided practice 4 (tool sort):** book → teacher; broom → custodian;
  bandage → nurse; toy bus → bus driver.
- **Guided practice 5 (scraped knee):** the school nurse — and the nurse
  calls a grown-up if it is serious.
- **Guided practice 6 (sentence):** "the bus driver helps us by driving us
  to school and home safely."
- **Independent practice:** any school helper drawn with a tool that
  belongs to that job counts; the "how does this helper help us?" answer
  must match the job (for example, custodian → "keeps the school clean and
  safe").
- **Exit check:** for each of two cards, at least two of three questions
  answered (who, what they do, what tool). Second probe: any one of the
  four school helpers counts.

## Lesson 02

- **Guided practice 1 (firefighter):** coiled hose; it sprays water on a
  fire.
- **Guided practice 2 (doctor):** a stethoscope around her neck; she
  listens to a heartbeat and breathing.
- **Guided practice 3 (error analysis):** no — the firefighter puts out
  fires, with a hose. The mail carrier delivers mail, with a mailbag.
- **Guided practice 4 (grocer):** the grocer (green apron); apples in her
  basket.
- **Guided practice 5 (tool match):** hose → firefighter; envelope →
  mail carrier; play apples → grocer; listening tube → doctor.
- **Guided practice 6 (smoke):** get out, stay out, tell a grown-up. Never
  hide, never go back inside, never touch matches.
- **Independent practice:** any neighborhood helper drawn with a tool
  that belongs to that job counts; the "how does this helper help us?"
  answer must match the job (for example, mail carrier → "our letters
  reach the right people").
- **Exit check:** for each of two figures, at least two of three questions
  answered. Second probe: the firefighter.

## Lesson 03

- **Guided practice 1 (first step):** the mail carrier collects first;
  the truck drives later.
- **Guided practice 2 (order):** collect → sort → drive → deliver.
- **Guided practice 3 (error analysis):** no — letters do not fly by
  themselves. Helpers moved it, step by step: carrier collects, sorter
  sorts, driver drives, carrier delivers.
- **Guided practice 4 (sick chain):** the school nurse helps first; then a
  grown-up takes the child to the doctor.
- **Guided practice 5 (one helper, one step):** any correct pair counts —
  carrier/collects, sorter/sorts, driver/drives, carrier/delivers.
- **Guided practice 6 (sentence):** any version of "one helper cannot do
  the whole job alone" / "the job is too big for one person."
- **Independent practice:** four boxes in order (mailbox, sorting piles,
  truck, house) with the star letter in every box; helper named at each
  step counts as secure, two steps as developing.
- **Exit check:** at least three of four steps in order and at least two
  helpers named. Second probe: without sorting, the letter does not get
  to the right town — the chain breaks.

## Lesson 04

- **Guided practice 1 (stop sign):** the crossing guard's tool; it tells
  cars to stop so children can cross safely.
- **Guided practice 2 (shovel):** the road worker's tool; the job needs
  digging and smoothing to fix roads.
- **Guided practice 3 (error analysis):** no — apples cannot spray water.
  The firefighter needs a hose.
- **Guided practice 4 (shared book):** teacher and librarian; no, their
  jobs differ — teaching vs. helping find and borrow books.
- **Guided practice 5 (toolbox scramble):** book → teacher or librarian;
  broom → custodian; bandage → nurse; hose → firefighter; mailbag → mail
  carrier; apples → grocer.
- **Guided practice 6 (skill):** the bus driver needs to know how to drive
  safely, when to stop, and how to keep children safe on the bus. Any one
  of these counts; all three is secure.
- **Independent practice:** three helper → tool lines matched by purpose;
  the "what would go wrong" answer must name the tool's real purpose
  (for example, "a hose sprays water, so it cannot sell apples").
- **Exit check:** at least three of four matches correct (hose →
  firefighter, apples → grocer, mailbag → mail carrier, listening tube →
  doctor) and at least two purposes stated. Second probe: no — the grocer
  cannot use the hose to sell apples; wrong tool for the job.

## Lesson 05

- **Guided practice 1 (puppet thank-you):** all three parts count —
  wait for a pause, name the work ("thank you for selling food"), name
  the help ("you help us by giving us food to eat"). Two parts is
  developing.
- **Guided practice 2 (error analysis):** no — never interrupt someone
  driving, using a machine, or doing dangerous work. Wait for a safe
  pause, or thank them later with a card.
- **Guided practice 3 (city helper):** any correct pair counts — road
  worker fixes roads; trash collector keeps streets clean; librarian
  keeps the library open.
- **Guided practice 4 (role-play):** tool, uniform clue, and job words
  must match the chosen helper (for example, firefighter + hose +
  "I put out fires!").
- **Guided practice 5 (child helper):** any small, safe, permitted action
  counts — hold a door, help pick up crayons, put toys away, use kind
  words. Never machines, roads, or fires.
- **Guided practice 6 (sentence):** any matched pair counts — "thank you
  for driving the bus; you help us by getting us to school safely."
- **Independent practice:** the card drawing must show the helper's
  actual job and tool; the puppet delivery must include waiting for a
  pause and saying the sentence.
- **Exit check:** at least two of the three thank-you parts, and tool
  matched to job in the role-play swap. Second probe: do not interrupt
  when a helper is driving, using a machine, or doing dangerous work —
  wait for a safe pause.

## Practice sessions

- **Session A:** book → teacher; broom → custodian; bandage → nurse;
  bus → bus driver. Mix-up fix: the bandage belongs to the nurse, not
  the bus driver.
- **Session B:** picture finds — letter: mail carrier; hose: firefighter;
  stethoscope: doctor; apples: grocer. Picture-walk helpers — library:
  librarian; road being fixed: road worker; trash truck: trash collector;
  crossing: crossing guard. Odd-one-out: the teacher (works at school,
  not in the Lesson 02 neighborhood set).
- **Session C (Ms. Rosa):** retell order — sweep hallway, wipe tables, mop
  the lunchtime spill, empty trash bins. (Spill placement at lunchtime;
  "sweep first, empty trash last" is the required skeleton.) Tools —
  broom and mop for floors, cloth for tables. Ms. Rosa is a school helper.
- **Session D:** school mat — teacher, custodian, school nurse, bus
  driver; street mat — firefighter, mail carrier, grocer, doctor. Bus
  driver on either mat with the explanation "the bus goes to school"
  counts. Rule retrieval — bus driver: drive safely, stop at the stop
  sign, wait for children to sit; firefighter: wear the helmet, stay safe
  near fire. Shared tool: books (teacher's and librarian's).
- **Session E:** relay order — collect, sort, drive, deliver. Without the
  sorting station the letter cannot reach the right town; the chain
  needs every link. Second chain — nurse first ("I help and call a
  grown-up"), doctor next ("I check and help you feel better").
- **Session F:** wrong pairs — firefighter + apples (apples cannot spray
  water); grocer + hose (a hose cannot sell food); mail carrier +
  listening tube (a tube cannot carry letters). Shared tools — book:
  teacher and librarian; thermometer / listening tube: nurse and doctor.
- **Session G:** five or more helpers named without help is secure;
  three to four is developing. Chain retell and city-helper answers as
  in Lesson 03 and Lesson 05. Picture round answers as in Lesson 02.

## Formative quiz

1. Any three unit helpers count.
2. All four matches count as one question; 3–4 correct = correct, 2 or
   fewer = re-teach with Lesson 04 / Practice F.
3. Grocer: "sells food we eat" (any version counts).
4. Collect → sort → drive → deliver.
5. Any city helper with a correct city job counts (road worker / trash
   collector / librarian).
6. At least two of the three thank-you parts (wait for a pause, name the
   work, name the help) counts as correct.

## Unit assessment

Scoring is in [unit-assessment.md](assessments/unit-assessment.md): each
task 2 / 1 / 0, total 7/10 or more signals readiness for Unit 04.

- **Task 1:** 2 = five helpers, each with a correct job and "how it
  helps"; 1 = three to four helpers with jobs, or five helpers with jobs
  but no "how it helps"; 0 = fewer than three helpers with correct jobs.
- **Task 2:** 2 = all four picture pairs (mail carrier / letter + mailbag;
  firefighter / coiled hose; doctor / stethoscope; grocer / basket of
  apples); 1 = two to three pairs; 0 = fewer than two.
- **Task 3:** 2 = all four steps in order with helpers at three or more
  steps; 1 = three steps in order, or all four steps with helpers at one
  to two steps; 0 = fewer than three steps in order.
- **Task 4:** 2 = both prompts correct (city helper + city job; a
  two-link chain in order); 1 = one prompt correct; 0 = neither.
- **Task 5:** 2 = tool matches role, job sentence correct, thank-you has
  at least two of three parts; 1 = tool matches but job sentence or
  thank-you incomplete; 0 = tool mismatch, or no attempt.

## Project (Helper Fair)

Scoring is in [project.md](project.md): six criteria, each 2 / 1 / 0,
total 8/12 or more signals readiness on the project's objectives.

- **Helper and tool match:** 2 = all stations match; 1 = most match, one
  mismatch fixed with a hint; 0 = mismatches persist with hints.
- **Job explanation:** 2 = what each helper does AND how it helps, all
  stations; 1 = one side only; 0 = cannot say what a helper does.
- **Helping chain:** 2 = letter journey in order, helpers at three or more
  steps; 1 = most steps ordered, or helpers at one to two steps; 0 =
  cannot order the steps.
- **Thank-you delivery:** 2 = safe pause + names the work + names the
  help; 1 = one to two parts; 0 = will not deliver, or interrupts
  busy/dangerous work.
- **City helper awareness:** 2 = names a city helper and what they do for
  the whole city; 1 = names the helper only; 0 = cannot name one.
- **Reflection:** 2 = one thing learned about a helper's job AND one thing
  noticed about thanking; 1 = one of the two; 0 = neither.
