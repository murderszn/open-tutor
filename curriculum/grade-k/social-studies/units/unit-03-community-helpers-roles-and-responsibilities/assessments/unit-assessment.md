# Unit Assessment — Unit 03: Community Helpers (Week 14)

**Format:** five performance tasks, adult-observed. The adult reads every
prompt aloud and scribes the learner's answers for private storage.
**Time:** about 20 minutes, split across two sittings if needed ·
**Session 16 of 16**
**Scoring:** each task is scored 2 (secure), 1 (developing), or 0 (not yet)
— see [answer-key.md](../answer-key.md#unit-assessment) for the full
scoring guidance. A total of 7 or more out of 10 signals readiness to move
on to Unit 04; below 7, re-teach the lowest tasks (see the
[teacher guide](../teacher-guide.md)) using the track's flexible weeks.

## Task 1 — Name five helpers and their jobs (Objectives 1, 3)

"Name five helpers, and for each one, tell me what they do."
The learner names five helpers from the unit. For each, the adult asks,
"And how does that help us?" if the learner does not say it unprompted.
- **2:** five helpers, each with a correct job and a correct "how it
  helps."
- **1:** three to four helpers with jobs, or five helpers with jobs but
  no "how it helps."
- **0:** fewer than three helpers with correct jobs.

## Task 2 — Match tools to helpers in the picture (Objectives 1, 2)

Show the generated illustration
[`../assets/community-helpers-street.png`](../assets/community-helpers-street.png).
"Point to each helper and tell me their tool." The four figures are: mail
carrier (letter and mailbag), firefighter (coiled hose), doctor
(stethoscope), grocer (basket of apples).
- **2:** all four helpers named with the correct tool.
- **1:** two to three correct helper–tool pairs.
- **0:** fewer than two correct pairs.

*Text-only alternative:* "Four helpers stand on a street. The mail carrier,
in blue, holds a letter and a mailbag. The firefighter, in yellow with a
yellow helmet, holds a coiled hose. The doctor, in a white coat, wears a
stethoscope around her neck. The grocer, in a green apron, holds a basket
of apples." The learner names each helper and tool from the description.

## Task 3 — Walk the letter's journey (Objective 4)

Set out the four stations out of order: mailbox, sorting table, truck,
house, with the star envelope. "Show me the letter's journey, in order,
and name the helper at each step."
- **2:** all four steps in order (collect → sort → drive → deliver) with
  helpers named at three or more steps.
- **1:** three steps in order, or all four steps with helpers named at
  one to two steps.
- **0:** fewer than three steps in order.

## Task 4 — Name a city helper and a helping chain (Objectives 4, 5)

Two prompts, scored together:
(a) "Name one helper who takes care of the whole city. What do they do?"
(b) "Name one chain of helpers: who helps first, and who helps next?"
(Accept the letter chain, or nurse → grown-up → doctor.)
- **2:** both prompts answered correctly.
- **1:** one prompt answered correctly.
- **0:** neither prompt answered correctly.

## Task 5 — Play a helper and say thank you (Objectives 2, 6)

The learner picks a helper station, puts on the dress-up piece, picks the
helper's tool, says one job sentence, and then gives the adult (playing a
school helper) a three-part thank-you: wait for a pause, name the work,
name the help.
- **2:** tool matches the role, job sentence is correct, and the thank-you
  has at least two of the three parts.
- **1:** tool matches but the job sentence or thank-you is incomplete.
- **0:** tool does not match the role, or the learner will not attempt
  the role-play or thank-you.

## After the assessment

Record the total and the per-task scores in private storage (never in the
repository). If the total is 7 or more, the learner is ready for Unit 04
(Maps, symbols, and nearby places) — with a note of any task scored 1, to
revisit in Unit 04's retrieval warm-ups. If the total is below 7, use the
[teacher guide](../teacher-guide.md) re-teaching paths for the lowest
tasks before starting Unit 04.
