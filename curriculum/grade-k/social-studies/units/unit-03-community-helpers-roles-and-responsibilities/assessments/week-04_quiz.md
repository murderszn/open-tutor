# Formative Quiz — Unit 03: Community Helpers (Week 14)

**Format:** oral / observed / pointing / drawing. The adult reads every
question aloud and scribes the learner's answers for private storage.
**Time:** about 10 minutes · **Session 15 of 16**
**Scoring:** see [answer-key.md](../answer-key.md#formative-quiz). Four or
more correct out of six signals readiness for the unit assessment; fewer
than four means re-teach the missed objectives (see the
[teacher guide](../teacher-guide.md)) before Session 16.

1. **Name three helpers.** "Name three helpers you met in this unit."
   (Any three from the unit count: teacher, custodian, school nurse, bus
   driver, firefighter, mail carrier, grocer, doctor, librarian, crossing
   guard, trash collector, road worker.)
2. **Match the tool.** The adult lays out four tool cards — hose, mailbag,
   basket of apples, listening tube — and four helper cards — firefighter,
   mail carrier, grocer, doctor — mixed up. "Match each tool to its
   helper." (All four matches are scored as one question: 3–4 correct
   counts as correct.)
3. **How does this helper help?** Pointing to the grocer card: "How does
   the grocer help us?" (Any version of "sells food we eat" counts.)
4. **Order the letter's journey.** The adult lays out four step cards out
   of order: truck, deliver, collect, sort. "Put them in order, left to
   right." (Collect → sort → drive → deliver.)
5. **Name a city helper.** "Name one helper who takes care of the whole
   city, and tell me one thing they do." (Road worker fixes roads; trash
   collector keeps streets clean; librarian keeps the library open.)
6. **Thank a helper.** The adult plays the custodian with a broom. "Say
   thank you the strong way: name what they did, and how it helps."
   (Any thank-you with at least two of the three parts — wait for a pause,
   name the work, name the help — counts.)

**Response modes:** spoken words, pointing, mime, or drawing all count.
A learner who answers by pointing to the right card and miming the job has
answered correctly.
