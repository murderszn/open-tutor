# Unit 01 — Identity, Belonging, and Classroom Community

**Grade:** Kindergarten · **Subject:** Social Studies · **Track weeks:** 3–6
**Backlog:** [issue #9](https://github.com/murderszn/open-tutor/issues/9), section U01
**Status:** validated draft (this unit); not educator-reviewed, not merged

A classroom community is a group of people who belong together, take care of
each other, and get things done together. In this unit the learner discovers
what makes them unique (a favorite, a talent, an interest), practices belonging
routines — greeting, including others, doing a helper job — and learns kindness
in action: listening, gentle hands, kind words, and repairing hurt feelings with
words.

**Privacy by design:** identity work at this age must not collect personal
information. No lesson asks the learner to say or write their name, address,
school, or family details. Favorites and talents are shared through drawings,
gestures, and anonymous picture cards; the class banner carries drawings and
role pictures, never names. The adult keeps all personal "all about me" work in
private storage.

## Essential question

What makes our classroom community special — and how do we help everyone
belong in it?

## Prerequisites

- Can sit for a 5–10 minute circle time with movement breaks (from diagnostic
  weeks 1–2)
- Follows one-step directions; two-step directions emerging
- Can name a favorite food, color, or activity (emerging is fine — Unit 01
  teaches this explicitly)
- No reading or writing required. The adult reads everything aloud and scribes.

## Measurable objectives

By the end of the unit the learner will be able to (adult-observed):

1. Name or show one thing that makes them unique — a favorite, a talent, or an
   interest — in words, a drawing, or a gesture.
2. Take part in at least two classroom belonging routines: a greeting, an
   including-others action, and/or a classroom helper role.
3. Show kindness in action: listen when others speak, use gentle hands, and use
   kind words.
4. When feelings are hurt, use words to repair them (say what happened, say
   sorry, name what to do differently) with adult support.
5. Explain that everyone in the class community matters and belongs, and that
   class products stay anonymous (no names or photos) to keep everyone safe.

## Vocabulary (taught with pictures and actions first, word second)

community · belong · unique · favorite · talent · greeting · include / invite ·
helper role · listen · gentle hands · kind words · sorry / repair · feelings ·
safe · anonymous (the adult's word: "a picture with no name on it")

## Materials

Household or dollar-store items (see the track
[scope-and-sequence](../../scope-and-sequence.md#8-safe-materials) for the full
safe-materials list): large paper, crayons or markers, picture cards (cut from
magazines or drawn — favorite foods, animals, toys, activities), a soft ball or
stuffed animal for greeting circle, puppets or stuffed animals for scenarios,
toy blocks, the generated illustration
[`assets/belonging-circle.png`](assets/belonging-circle.png). No sharp tools;
props checked for choking hazards; no food allergens used as counters or props
without checking.

Session model follows the track plan: core lesson → practice → story/game →

## Accessibility

Every check accepts oral, pointing, drawing, or gesture responses; the adult
scribes. Each lesson includes a seated-table and a floor-play variant, a
language-access support, an alternate response mode, and an extension.
Favorites can be expressed with a favorite object brought from home instead of
words. The generated illustration ships with a text-only alternative carrying
every action the activity asks about.
