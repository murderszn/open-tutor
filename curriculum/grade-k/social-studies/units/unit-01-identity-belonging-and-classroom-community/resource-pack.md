# Resource Pack — Unit 01: Identity, Belonging, and Classroom Community

## Goal and age fit

Give the guiding adult background reading, printable activities, and short
videos that reinforce this unit's goals — uniqueness without personal
disclosure, belonging routines, helper roles, kindness tools, and repair —
for a kindergarten learner (ages 4–6). The adult previews everything; videos
supplement the lessons, never replace them. All core learning is achievable
with the free, no-account resources below.

## Videos (labeled search links — adult previews required)

Direct video verification was not available in this run, so these are
**clearly labeled preferred-channel search links**, not verified individual
videos. Open each, watch fully, and confirm age fit, captions, and ad
behavior before showing the learner. Prefer official PBS KIDS / Sesame
Workshop uploads.

1. **YouTube search — Daniel Tiger kindness songs (PBS KIDS)**
   <https://www.youtube.com/results?search_query=Daniel+Tiger+kindness+song+PBS+KIDS>
   *Why:* Daniel Tiger's Neighborhood models exactly this unit's tools
   (greeting songs, "saying sorry" episodes) in 2–3 minute songs. Relevance:
   Lessons 02, 04, 05. Account: none needed; ads vary — preview first.
2. **YouTube search — Sesame Street "The Power of We" special**
   <https://www.youtube.com/results?search_query=Sesame+Street+The+Power+of+We+special>
   *Why:* Sesame Workshop special about standing up for others and belonging;
   pairs with Lesson 02's picture reading. Relevance: Lessons 01–02.
3. **YouTube search — Sesame Street kindness**
   <https://www.youtube.com/results?search_query=Sesame+Street+kindness+preschool>
   *Why:* Short clips (Elmo, Abby) naming kind actions — good primers before
   Lesson 04's three tools. Relevance: Lessons 04–05.
4. **PBS LearningMedia search — classroom community (PreK–K)**
   <https://www.pbslearningmedia.org/search/?q=classroom+community>
   *Why:* Standards-tagged clips and interactives from member stations;
   several PreK–K community collections exist. Relevance: whole unit. Access:
   free; a free educator account unlocks saving/favoriting.

## Safety and access notes (2026-10-07)

- All listed resources are free. PBS LearningMedia may invite a free educator
  account for saving items; nothing in this unit requires an account.
- YouTube links are **search links, not verified videos**: the adult must open
  and preview each candidate for age fit, captions, comments, and advertising
  before the learner watches.
- No resource above asks the learner to enter a name, photo, or personal
  information. Keep it that way: use printables offline where possible.
- External pages change: if a link breaks, re-run the focused queries above
  rather than substituting an unverified look-alike.
