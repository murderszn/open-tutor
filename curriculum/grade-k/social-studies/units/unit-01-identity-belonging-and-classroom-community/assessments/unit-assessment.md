# Unit Assessment — Unit 01: Identity, Belonging, and Classroom Community

**Session 16 of 16** · **Culminating performance assessment.**
Five tasks, each matched to unit objectives. The adult administers orally and
observes; the learner may answer by speaking, pointing, drawing, gesture, or
puppet play. Split across two days if the learner tires. Full scoring
guidance and acceptable responses are in the
[answer key](../answer-key.md#unit-assessment).

**Privacy:** all products are anonymous — drawings carry no names.

## Task 1 — My unique contribution (Objectives 1, 5)

"Make something that shows one thing that makes you unique AND one way you
help our class community." The learner draws (or builds with blocks, or acts
with a puppet) a two-part picture: "me" (a favorite/talent) and "my help" (a
kind action or helper job). The adult asks: "Tell me about your picture. What
stays private?" (Checks the safety rule in context.)

*Looks for:* one identifiable unique thing; one identifiable helping action;
names the safety rule (names/addresses/photos stay private).

## Task 2 — Welcome the newcomer (Objectives 2, 3)

The adult introduces a puppet: "This is Pip. It is Pip's first day. Pip feels
shy." The learner must: (a) greet Pip with all three parts, (b) include Pip
(make room, invite to play), and (c) use one kindness tool with Pip. The adult
narrates Pip's responses but does not coach the learner.

*Looks for:* three-part greeting; an including action; listening ears, gentle
hands, or kind words used spontaneously.

## Task 3 — Read the community picture (Objectives 2, 3, 5)

Show [`belonging-circle.png`](../assets/belonging-circle.png) (or read the
[text alternative](../assets/README.md#text-only-alternative) aloud).

1. "Point to two different ways these children help everyone belong."
2. "Which child is being welcomed? How can you tell?"
3. "Is everyone in this picture important to the circle? Why?"

*Looks for:* two distinct belonging actions identified; the newcomer with the
backpack identified with evidence (waving child, open space); "yes, everyone
matters" reasoning (everyone has a place / every job and kindness helps).

## Task 4 — Fix it with words (Objective 4)

Puppet scenario: "Pip knocked over your block tower. You feel upset. Now YOU
are Pip — show me the four repair steps." The learner performs the repair as
Pip (slow, honest, with a plan). Then reverse: "Now you are you again. Pip
did the repair. What do you say?" (Accepts the repair graciously: "Okay" /
"Thank you" / "I need a minute.")

*Looks for:* all four steps (stop, name what happened, sorry-and-mean-it,
new plan); gracious receiving (no forced instant forgiveness, no retaliation).

## Task 5 — Our class community (Objectives 2, 5)

"Tell me three things that make our classroom community special." (Accept any
three true things from the unit: greetings, helper jobs, kindness tools,
including others, repairs, the banner, the song, "everyone belongs.") Then:
"Why does our banner have no names on it?" (The safety rule, explained in the
learner's own words.)

*Looks for:* three distinct community elements; safety-rule explanation
(keeping everyone safe / private things stay private).

## Scoring

Each task: **Secure (3)** — complete, correct, unprompted · **Developing
(2)** — complete with one hint/prompt · **Emerging (1)** — partial or
incorrect after prompting · **Not yet (0)** — no response.

| Total | Meaning | Next step |
|---|---|---|
| 13–15 | Secure | Ready for Unit 02; celebrate with the belonging song |
| 9–12 | Developing | Re-teach the lowest tasks using the [teacher guide](../teacher-guide.md) paths, then move on |
| 5–8 | Emerging | Take a flexible catch-up week on the two weakest objectives before Unit 02 |
| 0–4 | Not yet | Re-teach the unit's core (L2–L4) across two catch-up weeks; check for unmet needs (language, sensory, emotional) with the adult's judgment |

This assessment is **not standardized or validated** — it is a teacher-made
check of this unit's objectives, scored by the guiding adult against the
rubric above.
