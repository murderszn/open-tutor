# Formative Quiz — Unit 01 (Week 4)

**Session 15 of 16** · **For the guiding adult to administer orally.**
The learner responds by speaking, pointing, drawing, or gesture; the adult
scribes. Call it "show-me time," not a test. Stop if the learner is upset —
an unfinished quiz is data, not failure. Scoring guidance is in the
[answer key](../answer-key.md#formative-quiz).

**Safety rule reminder:** no names, addresses, or photos in any response.

## Q1 — Unique me (Objective 1)

"Show me one thing that makes you unique — a favorite, a talent, or an
interest." (Words, drawing, gesture, or pointing to a picture card all count.)

## Q2 — Safety rule (Objective 5)

The adult holds up five cards: "favorite food," "talent," "my name," "my
address," "my photo." "Sort these: safe to share on our class banner, or
stays private?" (Two piles.)

## Q3 — Greeting parts (Objective 2)

"Show me a greeting with all three parts." (Eyes, kind face, hello
words/wave.)

## Q4 — Picture reading (Objectives 2, 3)

Show [`belonging-circle.png`](../assets/belonging-circle.png). "Point to one
way these children help everyone belong." Then: "Point to someone using
gentle hands."

## Q5 — Helper job (Objective 2)

Lay out the five job cards. "Point to a helper job. What does this helper do?
How does it help our class?"

## Q6 — Kindness tools (Objective 3)

"Show me the three kindness tools." (Listening ears, gentle hands, kind
words — gestures.) "Your friend drops their crayons. Which tool do you use,
and what do you do?"

## Q7 — Repair steps (Objective 4)

"You took a toy and your friend is sad. What are the four repair steps? What
do you say?" (Prompt with the icon card if needed: stop, say what happened,
say sorry, make a plan.)

## Q8 — Everyone matters (Objective 5)

"Is the clean-up captain's job as important as the line leader's? Why?"
(Accept: yes — every job helps the class; the class runs on everyone.)

## Scoring (adult records)

For each question: **Secure** (correct/complete without help), **Developing**
(correct with a hint or partial), **Not yet** (incorrect or no response after
two tries). 6+ Secure = on track; 3–5 Secure = re-teach the "Developing"
objectives in the Session 16 review window; fewer than 3 = use a flexible
catch-up week before Unit 02 (see the [teacher guide](../teacher-guide.md)).
