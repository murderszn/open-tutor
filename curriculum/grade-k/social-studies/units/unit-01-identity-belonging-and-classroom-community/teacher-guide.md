# Teacher Guide — Unit 01: Identity, Belonging, and Classroom Community

**For the guiding adult.** Student materials are in [lessons/](lessons/),
[practice.md](practice.md), [project.md](project.md), and
[assessments/](assessments/). **Keep this guide and the
[answer key](answer-key.md) separate from the learner** — never place a key
beside a student question.

## How to run the unit

- **Session rhythm:** 4 sessions a week, 15–20 minutes each. Short and daily
  beats long and occasional. End while it's still fun; a tired kindergartner
  learns nothing from a 25th minute.
- **Your role:** model first, then fade. In Week 1 you do the greeting while
  the learner watches; by Week 3 the learner leads and you observe.
- **Scribe everything** the learner can't write yet. Their job is thinking;
  writing is yours. Date the back of every drawing for private storage.
- **The safety rule is sacred:** "names, addresses, and photos stay private."
  Say it at the start of every session. Redirect warmly and instantly, every
  time — never shame a child for offering their name.
- **Every session** ends with one belonging moment: a greeting, a kindness
  noticed, or the song. Belonging is the unit's spine the way "how many?" was
  math's.

## Misconception table

| What you see | What it means | Response |
|---|---|---|
| "Unique means I'm the best" | Unique = different, not better | "Different is what makes our community interesting." |
| Offers name/address as "about me" | Safety rule not yet internalized | Warm redirect every time: "That stays private. Give me a favorite instead." |
| Greeting = saying "hi" | Three parts not yet learned | "Eyes, kind face, hello — all three, every time." |
| Including = giving up your toy | Sharing confused with belonging | "Including means making room. Sharing is one way, not the only way." |
| Only leaders matter | Big idea not yet formed | "Who watered the plant? What if nobody did? The class runs on everyone." |
| Kindness = sitting still and quiet | Compliance confused with kindness | "Kindness is DOING: helping, asking, including." |
| Fast "sorry!" to escape | Repair = evasion | Model the four slow steps; never accept a rushed sorry as complete. |
| "Sorry erases it" | Steps 1, 2, 4 skipped | "Sorry is step 3 of 4. The plan is what stops it happening again." |
| Won't forgive instantly | Thinks forgiveness is required | "You can say 'I need a minute.' Forgiving is your choice." |
| Points at wheelchair child as "needs help" | Pity instead of belonging | "She is part of the circle, just like everyone. She belongs." |

## Re-teaching paths

- **Unique me / safety rule (Obj. 1, 5):** replay Practice A with 3 cards for
  a week. Make the safety sort a daily 2-minute game. The rule must be
  automatic before the banner goes up.
- **Greeting/including (Obj. 2):** greet every real person you meet together
  for a week — the mail carrier, a neighbor. Real reps beat drills. Re-read
  the generated picture and find one new belonging action each time.
- **Helper roles (Obj. 2):** shrink to one job done excellently for three
  days rather than rotating. "Director mode" (learner directs your hands)
  works when the learner can't do the physical job.
- **Kindness tools (Obj. 3):** pick ONE tool for a whole week (e.g., kind
  words only). Narrate your own tool use aloud all day: "I'm using listening
  ears right now."
- **Repair steps (Obj. 4):** never demand a repair as punishment — model it
  yourself on small real moments ("I interrupted you — stop, name it, sorry,
  plan"). Let the learner watch ten repairs before doing one.

Use the track's flexible diagnostic/catch-up weeks for re-teaching — do not
compress Unit 02 to "catch up." A shaky Unit 01 makes Unit 02 (rules and
fairness) twice as hard, because fairness assumes belonging.

## Readiness for Unit 02

The learner is ready when the [unit assessment](assessments/unit-assessment.md)
shows Secure on 4 of 5 tasks with no "Not yet." Specifically: the safety rule
is automatic, greetings have all three parts, and the learner can name the
four repair steps. Unit 02 builds fairness and rule-making on top of this
belonging foundation — a child who doesn't feel they belong can't reason
about what's fair.

## Differentiation

- **Need more time:** repeat any week as a "week 3.5" using the practice
  sessions; the 36-week track has slack built in. Belonging cannot be rushed.
- **Need more challenge:** extensions are in every lesson (invent-a-job,
  teach-the-tools, two-panel repair stories, "hardest step" metacognition).
  Do not introduce Unit 02's voting/decision content early as a reward.
- **Language:** actions first, words second; home-language greetings, kind
  words, and repairs count fully. Visual word wall: community, belong,
  unique, include, listen, gentle, kind, sorry, safe.
- **Mobility/vision:** pointing, eye-gaze + adult action, large high-contrast
  cards, textured materials; every check has an alternate response mode. Eye
  contact is modeled, never forced.
- **Big feelings days:** the lesson IS the practice — name the feeling, model
  the tools on the learner, count the session.

## Safety

Household/dollar-store materials only; props checked for choking hazards;
check food-allergen safety before any food-based favorite cards; outdoor
tasks are adult-supervised with indoor alternatives. Dramatic-play repair
scenarios stay pretend — never reenact a real hurt the learner is still upset
about; wait for calm, or use puppets only. See the track
[scope-and-sequence](../../scope-and-sequence.md#8-safe-materials).
