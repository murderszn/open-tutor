# Resource Pack — Unit 05: Needs, Wants, and Simple Exchanges

## Goal and age fit

Give the guiding adult background reading, printable activities, and short
videos that reinforce this unit's goals — needs vs. wants, goods vs.
services, work and earning, prices and play-money exchange, choosing
under scarcity, saving, and near/far trade — for a kindergarten learner
(ages 4–6). The adult previews everything; videos supplement the lessons,
never replace them. All core learning is achievable with the free,
no-account resources below.

## Videos (labeled search links — adult previews required)

Direct video verification was not available in this run, so these are
**clearly labeled preferred-channel search links**, not verified individual
videos. Open each, watch fully, and confirm age fit, captions, and ad
behavior before showing the learner. Prefer official PBS KIDS / Sesame
Workshop uploads.

1. **YouTube search — needs and wants songs for kindergarten**
   <https://www.youtube.com/results?search_query=needs+and+wants+song+kindergarten>
   *Why:* Call-and-response songs that sort needs from wants — good
   warm-ups before Lesson 01 and Practice A. Relevance: Lesson 01,
   Practice A. Account: none needed; ads vary — preview first.
2. **YouTube search — goods and services for kids**
   <https://www.youtube.com/results?search_query=goods+and+services+for+kids+kindergarten>
   *Why:* Short explainers that contrast things you can hold with help
   someone does for you — the Lesson 02 question in video form.
   Relevance: Lesson 02, Practice B.
3. **YouTube search — classroom store play money for kindergarten**
   <https://www.youtube.com/results?search_query=classroom+store+play+money+kindergarten>
   *Why:* Videos of pretend shops with price tags and coin counting —
   the Lesson 03 exchange in video form. Relevance: Lesson 03,
   Practice E, project.
4. **YouTube search — saving money piggy bank for kids**
   <https://www.youtube.com/results?search_query=saving+money+piggy+bank+for+kids>
   *Why:* Short clips about putting coins aside for later — the Lesson
   04 save jar in video form. Relevance: Lesson 04, Practice F.
   Preview: skip any clip that names real family finances or asks
   children about money at home.
5. **PBS LearningMedia search — needs and wants (PreK–K)**
   <https://www.pbslearningmedia.org/search/?q=needs+and+wants>
   *Why:* Standards-tagged clips and lesson plans from member stations;
   search "needs and wants," "goods and services," or "money" for
   PreK–K items. Relevance: whole unit (supplement). Access: free; the
   search page loads as an interactive app — the adult browses and
   previews items. A free educator account unlocks saving/favoriting.

## Web references (checked 2026-11-04 unless noted)

1. **C3 Framework for Social Studies State Standards**
   <https://www.socialstudies.org/standards/c3>
   Landing page re-checked live 2026-11-04 (framework organization and
   inquiry arc load); K–2 indicator codes/descriptions verified
   2026-10-02 during the track audit (see the
   [scope-and-sequence](../../scope-and-sequence.md)). No state adoption or
   accreditation claimed. Relevance: standards notes in every lesson.
2. **Kids' Money — "Kindergarten Money Lesson Plans & Worksheets"**
   <https://www.kidsmoney.org/teachers/kindergarten/>
   Verified live 2026-11-04 (page loads; updated 2025-10-08). Names the
   kindergarten set of ideas this unit teaches — wants, needs, scarcity,
   choice, goods, services, money denominations — and models a classroom
   store and a save-for-a-bigger-reward choice. Teacher background only;
   the page's downloadable lesson plans are not assigned. Relevance:
   teacher background for Lessons 01–04 and Practice F.
3. **U.S. Mint Coin Classroom — coin activities (kindergarten–grade 4)**
   <https://kids.usmint.gov/resources/coin-activities/coin-count-n-roll>
   Found via search 2026-11-04 (Coin Classroom activities for
   kindergarten and up, including coin counting and rolling). This unit
   uses play coins only and never requires real coins; the Mint pages
   are teacher background for coin handling and counting, not assigned
   activities. Relevance: teacher background for Lesson 03.
4. **Sesame Workshop — "How Helpers Keep Us Safe"** (activity page)
   <https://sesameworkshop.org/activities/how-helpers-help/>
   Verified live 2026-10-22 during Unit 03 (page loads; related resources
   listed). Helpers are introduced as people to look for and ask a
   trusted adult about; getting help means telling an adult or asking an
   adult to call for help. Carried forward here for the grocer/baker
   helpers and the trusted-adult rule in trading. Relevance: teacher
   background for Lessons 02, 05 and the unit's safety rules.
5. **NAEYC — "Instead of Discipline, Use Guidance"** (Teaching Young
   Children, Summer 2021)
   <https://www.naeyc.org/resources/pubs/tyc/summer2021/discipline-guidance>
   Verified live 2026-10-16 during Unit 02 (full article loads). Guidance
   as coaching: children learn from mistakes through firm, friendly
   mediation rather than punishment. Carried forward here for the market:
   a wrong price or a broken turn is coached, never scolded. Relevance:
   teacher background for Lesson 03 and the project.
