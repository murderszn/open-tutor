# Formative Quiz — Unit 05: Needs, Wants, and Simple Exchanges (Week 22)

**Format:** oral / observed / pointing / drawing. The adult reads every
question aloud and scribes the learner's answers for private storage.
**Time:** about 10 minutes · **Session 15 of 16**
**Scoring:** see [answer-key.md](../answer-key.md#formative-quiz). Four or
more correct out of six signals readiness for the unit assessment; fewer
than four means re-teach the missed objectives (see the
[teacher guide](../teacher-guide.md)) before Session 16.

1. **Need or want?** The adult lays out four cards — apple, ball, warm
   coat, teddy bear — mixed up. "Sort them: needs on this mat, wants on
   that mat." (Needs: apple, warm coat. Wants: ball, teddy bear. All
   four correct counts as correct; 3 correct counts as correct only if
   the learner self-corrects with the Lesson 01 question.)
2. **Good or service?** The adult holds up two cards — crayons and a
   song sung. "Which one is a good you can hold? Which one is a service
   someone does for you?" (Crayons: good. Song: service. Both correct
   counts as correct.)
3. **Buy and count.** The learner gets 5 play coins. "Buy bread. It
   costs 2 coins. How many coins are left?" (Give 2; 5 − 2 = 3 left.
   Both the payment and the count must be right.)
4. **Choose and give up.** The learner gets 5 play coins and two cards
   — ball (3) and teddy bear (3). "Can you buy both? Choose one, and
   tell me what you get and what you give up." (No — 3 + 3 = 6, more
   than 5. Either choice counts, as long as both parts of the sentence
   are said. Coins left after either choice: 2.)
5. **Save or spend?** The adult holds up the SAVE jar and the SPEND
   mat. "You have 2 coins left. You want a ball tomorrow. Do you save
   or spend? Tell me why." (Either jar counts with a reason tied to
   later or now — for example, "I save, for the ball tomorrow" or "I
   spend, because I want an apple now." A choice without a reason does
   not count.)
6. **Near or far?** The adult holds up two cards — banana and bread.
   "Which one came from far away? Which one was made nearby? What is
   trading?" (Banana: far away. Bread: nearby. Trading: giving
   something and getting something back — any version counts.)

**Response modes:** spoken words, pointing, mime, or drawing all count.
A learner who answers by pointing to the right card and counting coins
onto the counter has answered correctly.
