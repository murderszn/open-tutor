# Unit Assessment — Unit 05: Needs, Wants, and Simple Exchanges

**Format:** performance tasks, oral / observed / pointing / drawing.
The adult reads every task aloud and scribes the learner's answers for
private storage. **Time:** about 15 minutes, splittable after Task 3 if
attention fades · **Session 16 of 16**
**Scoring:** see [answer-key.md](../answer-key.md#unit-assessment). Each
task is worth 2 points (see the scoring notes per task); 7/10 or more
signals readiness for Unit 06, with the lowest task re-taught from its
lesson. This assessment is a teaching check, not a standardized or
validated test.

## Task 1 — Sort needs and wants (Objective 1; 2 points)

The adult lays out 6 cards mixed up: apple, water bottle, warm coat,
ball, teddy bear, sticker. "Sort them: needs on this mat, wants on that
mat. Then tell me why one need is a need and why one want is a want."

- 2 points: 5–6 cards correct, with a correct reason for at least one
  card on each mat.
- 1 point: 3–4 cards correct, or correct sort with no reason.
- 0 points: fewer than 3 correct.

## Task 2 — Sort goods and services (Objective 2; 2 points)

The adult lays out 6 cards mixed up: apple, bread, crayons, haircut,
story read aloud, bus ride. "Sort them: goods on this mat, services on
that mat. Then tell me one good and one service in our community."

- 2 points: 5–6 cards correct, plus one community good and one
  community service named (for example, "the grocer sells apples" and
  "the bus driver drives us").
- 1 point: 3–4 cards correct, or correct sort with no community example.
- 0 points: fewer than 3 correct.

## Task 3 — Shop exchange (Objective 3; 2 points)

The learner gets 5 play coins. "Buy bread for 2 coins. How many coins
are left? Now buy an apple for 1 coin. How many coins are left now?
How do people earn money?"

- 2 points: both purchases paid exactly (2, then 1), both counts right
  (3 left, then 2 left: 5 − 2 = 3, 3 − 1 = 2), and "by working" given in
  some form.
- 1 point: one purchase or count wrong, or the earning question missed.
- 0 points: no purchase completed correctly.

## Task 4 — Choice and saving (Objectives 4 and 5; 2 points)

The learner gets 5 play coins and two cards — ball (3) and teddy bear
(3). "Can you buy both? Choose one and tell me what you get and what
you give up. You have 2 coins left. Show me saving, and tell me what
saving means."

- 2 points: says both cannot be bought (6 is more than 5), chooses one
  with both parts of the sentence, puts a coin in the SAVE jar, and
  gives a version of "keeping money for later."
- 1 point: one of the four parts missing (both-check, choice sentence,
  save action, saving meaning).
- 0 points: two or more parts missing.

## Task 5 — Near, far, and trading (Objective 6; 2 points)

The adult holds up the banana card, the bread card, and the generated
illustration (or its text alternative). "Which one came from far away?
Which one was made nearby? Point to one need and one want in the market
picture. What is trading?"

- 2 points: banana far away, bread nearby, one need and one want
  pointed to correctly (needs: apples, bread, water bottle, coat;
  wants: ball, teddy bear), and a version of "giving something and
  getting something back."
- 1 point: one of the four parts missing or wrong.
- 0 points: two or more parts missing or wrong.

**Readiness:** 7/10 or more, with no task at 0, signals readiness for
Unit 06. A task at 0 means re-teach that task's lesson (see the
[teacher guide](../teacher-guide.md)) and re-check with the matching
quiz question before moving on.
