# Answer Key — Unit 05: Needs, Wants, and Simple Exchanges

**Audience:** the guiding adult only. Keep this file separate from student
materials. Every practice item, quiz question, assessment task, and project
criterion below has a worked answer or an acceptable-response range. Open
responses accept any answer that matches the reasoning shown, not only the
exact wording. Prices throughout: apple 1, bread 2, water bottle 2, ball 3,
teddy bear 3, warm coat 3; every buyer starts with 5 play coins.

## Lesson 01

- **Guided practice 1 (bread):** need — food keeps our bodies strong.
- **Guided practice 2 (ball):** want — fun to play with, but our body
  stays healthy and safe without it.
- **Guided practice 3 (water bottle):** need — water to drink keeps our
  bodies working.
- **Guided practice 4 (error analysis):** no — wanting a sticker a lot
  does not make it a need. Without a sticker our body is still healthy
  and safe, so a sticker is a want.
- **Guided practice 5 (bed):** need — shelter, a safe place to sleep.
- **Guided practice 6 (sentence):** "a need is something we must have to
  stay healthy and safe."
- **Independent practice:** all 8 cards sorted: needs — apple, bread,
  water bottle, warm coat, bed; wants — ball, teddy bear, sticker. One
  reason per mat must match the Lesson 01 question.
- **Exit check:** coat → need; sticker → want. At least one reason
  given. Second probe: any correct need/want pair counts.

## Lesson 02

- **Guided practice 1 (apple):** good — you can hold it and take it home.
- **Guided practice 2 (story read aloud):** service — someone reads to
  you; you cannot hold the reading.
- **Guided practice 3 (teddy bear):** good — you can hold it and take it
  home.
- **Guided practice 4 (error analysis):** no — the ticket is a good you
  can hold, but the ride itself is a service: the driver driving you
  somewhere. The ticket is not the ride.
- **Guided practice 5 (helper service):** the bus driver — the ride is a
  service. The grocer sells goods.
- **Guided practice 6 (sentence):** "a service is help someone does for
  you."
- **Independent practice:** goods — apple, bread, teddy bear, crayons;
  services — haircut, story read aloud, song sung, bus ride. One reason
  per mat must match the Lesson 02 question.
- **Exit check:** crayons → good; song sung → service. At least one
  reason given. Second probe: any correct good/service pair counts.

## Lesson 03

- **Guided practice 1 (ball):** 3 coin dots; give 3; 5 − 3 = 2 left.
- **Guided practice 2 (water bottle):** price 2; 5 − 2 = 3 left.
- **Guided practice 3 (earn):** by working in the shop — selling goods
  to buyers.
- **Guided practice 4 (error analysis):** no — the teddy bear costs 3
  coins. A favorite number does not change the price.
- **Guided practice 5 (picture):** one need from the illustration or its
  text alternative (apples, bread, water bottle, coat) and one want
  (ball, teddy bear). Any one of each counts.
- **Guided practice 6 (sentence):** "people earn money by working."
- **Independent practice:** bread (2 → 3 left), then apple (1 → 2 left).
  Both exchanges scribed: "Bought bread for 2, 3 left. Bought apple for
  1, 2 left."
- **Exit check:** give 3 coins for the teddy bear; 2 left. Second probe:
  people earn money by working — any version counts.

## Lesson 04

- **Guided practice 1 (both?):** no — 3 + 3 = 6, and 6 is more than 5.
- **Guided practice 2 (choose):** any choice counts with both parts:
  "I get the ___, and I give up the ___." Coins left after either
  choice: 5 − 3 = 2.
- **Guided practice 3 (bread, then choice):** 3 left after bread
  (5 − 2 = 3). Either choice counts with a reason: spend 1 on an apple
  ("I am hungry") or save 3 ("I want something bigger tomorrow").
- **Guided practice 4 (error analysis):** no — ball + teddy bear cost
  3 + 3 = 6 coins, more than 5, and paying 3 leaves 2, not 5. You cannot
  get both and keep everything.
- **Guided practice 5 (show saving):** a coin in the SAVE jar, "for
  later" in words, gesture, or by pointing to the "later" picture.
- **Guided practice 6 (sentence):** "saving means keeping money for
  later."
- **Independent practice:** any two items fitting in 5 coins, for
  example ball (3) + apple (1) = 4, leaving 1; or bread (2) + apple (1)
  = 3, leaving 2. Leftover coin placed in the SAVE jar or spent, with
  the give-up sentence for the item not bought.
- **Exit check:** no to both; one chosen with both parts of the
  sentence. Second probe: saving means keeping money for later — any
  version counts.
- **Extension:** apple (1) + bread (2) = 3 spent; 5 − 3 = 2 left. Any
  sensible "later" idea counts.

## Lesson 05

- **Guided practice 1 (banana):** far away — grown in a warm, faraway
  place; traveled by truck and ship to our shop.
- **Guided practice 2 (bread):** nearby — baked by a baker nearby;
  short journey.
- **Guided practice 3 (trade game):** we traded — we gave something and
  got something back. Both sides agreed.
- **Guided practice 4 (error analysis):** no — bananas grow in warm
  places far away and travel a long way to our shop. A banana in our
  shop still came from far away.
- **Guided practice 5 (helper):** the truck driver, the ship's crew, or
  the grocer who sells them — any one counts.
- **Guided practice 6 (sentence):** "trading means giving something and
  getting something back."
- **Independent practice:** NEAR — bread, apple from a nearby tree,
  crayons. FAR AWAY — banana, toy ball (accept the ball as nearby with
  a journey reason, since balls are made in many places; the reasoning
  is the learning). One kind trade completed with adult agreement.
- **Exit check:** banana → far away; bread → nearby. Trading: any
  version of "giving something and getting something back." Second
  probe: any sensible faraway item with a journey reason counts (for
  example, oranges from a warm, faraway place).

## Practice sessions

- **Session A:** needs — apple, bread, water bottle, warm coat, bed;
  wants — ball, teddy bear, sticker. 7–8 correct is secure.
- **Session B:** goods — apple, book, crayons; services — haircut,
  song, bus ride. Retrieval: grocer sells goods; bus driver does a
  service; baker makes a good (bread). 5–6 correct is secure.
- **Session C (story):** retell order — (1) Sam buys bread for 2 coins;
  (2) 3 coins left (5 − 2 = 3); (3) Sam saves instead of buying the
  ball; (4) Sam says what Sam got and gave up. Drawing: any of the
  three scenes, with one dictated sentence scribed.
- **Session D:** Round 1 — either choice, coins left 2. Round 2 — pair
  (2 + 1 = 3, leaving 2) or ball (3, leaving 2); either way 2 left.
  Round 3 — coat + apple = 4 fits in 5 (1 left); coat + ball = 6 does
  not fit in 5.
- **Session E:** apple (1) → 4 left; bread (2) → 3 left; ball (3) → 2
  left; water bottle (2) → 3 left; teddy bear (3) → 2 left. Each round
  starts fresh with 5 coins. Speed round: 4–5 correct is secure.
- **Session F:** Scenario 1 — spend 2 on bread, 3 left. Scenario 2 —
  either jar counts with a reason tied to later or now. Scenario 3 —
  any split counts if the total is shown: coins in jars + coins in hand
  = 5. Scenario 4 — savings spent: jar empty, 0 coins, learner has the
  teddy bear.
- **Session G:** lightning sorts — needs: apple, coat, bread, water
  bottle; wants: ball, teddy bear. Goods: crayons, bread; services:
  song, bus ride. Shop: bread (2) from 5 → 3 left, then apple (1) from
  3 → 2 left. Choice: either item with both parts of the sentence.
  Save: a coin in the SAVE jar, "for later." Near/far: banana far away,
  bread nearby; trading = giving something and getting something back.

## Project

Rubric (12 points): see [project.md](project.md#rubric-12-points-adult-observed).
Worked checks: unit items keep unit prices (apple 1, bread 2, water
bottle 2, ball 3, teddy bear 3, coat 3); project-only items are banana
picture 2 and service 1. Purchase record must show at least two
exchanges per buyer with prices and coins left; each buyer must have
one choice sentence and one save-or-spend decision with a reason.
9–12 is strong; 6–8 is developing; below 6 means re-run Part 2 with 3
items.

## Formative quiz

1. Needs: apple, warm coat. Wants: ball, teddy bear. (3 correct with a
   self-correction via the Lesson 01 question also counts.)
2. Crayons: good. Song: service. Both required.
3. Give 2; 5 − 2 = 3 left. Both payment and count required.
4. No — 3 + 3 = 6, more than 5. Either choice with both parts of the
   sentence; 2 coins left.
5. Either jar counts with a reason tied to later or now. A choice
   without a reason does not count.
6. Banana: far away. Bread: nearby. Trading: any version of "giving
   something and getting something back."

## Unit assessment

- **Task 1 (2 points):** needs — apple, water bottle, warm coat;
  wants — ball, teddy bear, sticker. 5–6 correct + a reason for at
  least one card per mat = 2; 3–4 correct or no reason = 1; fewer than
  3 = 0.
- **Task 2 (2 points):** goods — apple, bread, crayons; services —
  haircut, story read aloud, bus ride. 5–6 correct + one community good
  and one community service = 2; 3–4 correct or no community example
  = 1; fewer than 3 = 0.
- **Task 3 (2 points):** bread 2 → 3 left (5 − 2 = 3); apple 1 → 2 left
  (3 − 1 = 2); people earn money by working. All parts = 2; one part
  wrong or missed = 1; no purchase correct = 0.
- **Task 4 (2 points):** both cannot be bought (6 > 5); one chosen with
  both parts of the sentence; a coin in the SAVE jar; saving = keeping
  money for later. All four parts = 2; one part missing = 1; two or
  more missing = 0.
- **Task 5 (2 points):** banana far away; bread nearby; one need and
  one want from the illustration (needs: apples, bread, water bottle,
  coat; wants: ball, teddy bear); trading = giving something and
  getting something back. All four parts = 2; one missing or wrong =
  1; two or more = 0.
- **Readiness:** 7/10 or more, with no task at 0, signals readiness for
  Unit 06.
