# Resource Pack — Unit 02: Rules, Fairness, and Cooperative Choices

## Goal and age fit

Give the guiding adult background reading, printable activities, and short
videos that reinforce this unit's goals — why rules exist (safe/fair),
co-creating rules with children, fair turns with a timer, a first class
vote, rules beyond the classroom, and repairing broken rules — for a
kindergarten learner (ages 4–6). The adult previews everything; videos
supplement the lessons, never replace them. All core learning is achievable
with the free, no-account resources below.

## Web references (checked 2026-10-16 unless noted)

1. **NAEYC — "Instead of Discipline, Use Guidance"** (Teaching Young
   Children, Summer 2021)
   <https://www.naeyc.org/resources/pubs/tyc/summer2021/discipline-guidance>
   Verified live 2026-10-16 (full article loads). Guidance as coaching:
   children learn from mistakes through firm, friendly mediation rather than
   punishment; includes a worked playdough-sharing mediation in five steps
   and the argument that solving problems together builds democratic life
   skills. Directly supports this unit's repair design (Lesson 05) and its
   no-punishment stance. Relevance: teacher background for Lessons 01, 05.
2. **Vanderbilt CSEFEL — Module 1 training deck: "Promoting Children's
   Success: Building Relationships and Creating Supportive Environments"**
   <http://csefel.vanderbilt.edu/modules-archive/module1/presenter.html>
   Verified live 2026-10-16 (slide deck loads). Includes "General Guidelines
   about Rules and Directions" (keep a few simple rules, involve children in
   developing them, post them visually, teach them systematically, reinforce
   them) and "Involving Children in Developing the Rules" (children generate
   rules, demonstrate them, help decorate the poster). This is the
   evidence-based backbone of the unit's rule co-creation (project Part 1).
   Relevance: teacher background for Lessons 01–02, project.
3. **C3 Framework for Social Studies State Standards**
   <https://www.socialstudies.org/standards/c3>
   Landing page verified live 2026-10-16; K–2 indicator codes/descriptions
   verified 2026-10-02 during the track audit (see the
   [scope-and-sequence](../../scope-and-sequence.md)). No state adoption or
   accreditation claimed. Relevance: standards notes in every lesson.
4. **Sesame Workshop — "Little Neighbors: Building a Better World Together"**
   Labeled search link (individual resource pages not verified this run):
   <https://www.sesameworkshop.org/> — search the site for "Little
   Neighbors." Bilingual resources on community values (safety, compassion,
   being a good neighbor) for young children and providers. Relevance:
   Lessons 02, 04, project background.
5. **Committee for Children (Second Step program)**
   Labeled search link: search "Committee for Children Second Step early
   learning." Educator background on the social-emotional skills this unit
   teaches (emotion management, problem-solving, decision-making). Relevance:
   teacher background for Lessons 03, 05.

## Safety and access notes (2026-10-16)

- All listed resources are free. PBS LearningMedia may invite a free educator
  account for saving items; nothing in this unit requires an account.
- YouTube links are **search links, not verified videos**: the adult must open
  and preview each candidate for age fit, captions, comments, and advertising
  before the learner watches.
- No resource above asks the learner to enter a name, photo, or personal
  information. Keep it that way: use printables offline where possible.
- External pages change: if a link breaks, re-run the focused queries above
  rather than substituting an unverified look-alike.
