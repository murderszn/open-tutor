# Formative Quiz — Unit 02 (Week 4)

**Session 15 of 16** · **For the guiding adult to administer orally.**
The learner responds by speaking, pointing, drawing, or gesture; the adult
scribes. Call it "show-me time," not a test. Stop if the learner is upset —
an unfinished quiz is data, not failure. Scoring guidance is in the
[answer key](../answer-key.md#formative-quiz).

**Safety rule reminder:** no names, addresses, or photos in any response.

## Q1 — Why rules exist (Objective 1)

"Why do we need rules?" (Words, the shield/scale gestures, or pointing to
the two sides of a drawing all count.)

## Q2 — Safe or fair (Objective 1)

The adult names three rules: "Walking feet in the hallway." "One person
talks at a time." "Everyone must wear purple socks." For each: "Real rule or
a wish? Safe or fair?"

## Q3 — Our class rules (Objective 2)

Point to the class rule poster. "Tell me our rules." (The learner names the
3–4 co-created rules in their own words, with prompting allowed.)

## Q4 — Fair turn (Objective 3)

Hand the learner the sand timer and the coveted toy. "Show me a fair turn."
(Learner plays for one sand run, passes when it runs out, shows a calm
waiting pose.) Then: "What does 'not yet' mean?"

## Q5 — Picture reading (Objectives 1, 3)

Show [`fair-turns.png`](../assets/fair-turns.png). "Point to the child who
is passing the turn. What are they passing?" Then: "Point to the child who
is waiting fairly. How can you tell?"

## Q6 — Vote steps (Objective 4)

"Show me the four vote steps." (Hear the choices, choose, count, follow the
decision — words or gestures.) "What do we do if our choice loses?"

## Q7 — Rules beyond the classroom (Objective 5)

"Tell me one school rule and one neighborhood rule." For each: "What does it
keep safe or fair?"

## Q8 — Repair steps (Objective 6)

A puppet knocks over the learner's block tower. "The rule got broken. Show
me the four repair steps." (Prompt with the gesture chain if needed: stop,
fix what you can, say the rule / say sorry, try again.)

## Scoring (adult records)

For each question: **Secure** (correct/complete without help), **Developing**
(correct with a hint or partial), **Not yet** (incorrect or no response after
two tries). 6+ Secure = on track; 3–5 Secure = re-teach the "Developing"
objectives in the Session 16 review window; fewer than 3 = use a flexible
catch-up week before Unit 03 (see the [teacher guide](../teacher-guide.md)).
