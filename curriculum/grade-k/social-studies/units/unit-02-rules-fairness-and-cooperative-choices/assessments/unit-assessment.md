# Unit Assessment — Unit 02: Rules, Fairness, and Cooperative Choices

**Session 16 of 16** · **Culminating performance assessment.**
Five tasks, each matched to unit objectives. The adult administers orally and
observes; the learner may answer by speaking, pointing, drawing, gesture, or
puppet play. Split across two days if the learner tires. Full scoring
guidance and acceptable responses are in the
[answer key](../answer-key.md#unit-assessment).

**Privacy:** all products are anonymous — drawings carry no names; the
fair-turn log records actions, never who misbehaved.

## Task 1 — Safe or fair, and why (Objectives 1, 2)

The adult presents four rule cards (two from the class poster, two new):
"For each rule: is it about safe or fair — and WHY?" Then the wish test:
"Everyone must eat pickles for lunch — real rule or wish? Why?"
*Looks for:* safe/fair sorting with a reason for each; the wish rejected
because it is neither safe nor fair.

## Task 2 — The fair-turn challenge (Objective 3)

One coveted toy, the sand timer, the learner, and a puppet. Three full
rotations, learner starts. Midway through rotation 2, the puppet "forgets"
and keeps the toy when the sand runs out. The learner must hold the rule:
say "not yet" kindly, restate the timer rule, and get the turn passed.
*Looks for:* passing without grabbing across all rotations; calm "not yet";
kind, firm rule-holding when the puppet tests it.

## Task 3 — Read the fair-turn picture (Objectives 1, 3)

Show [`fair-turns.png`](../assets/fair-turns.png) (or read the
[text alternative](../assets/README.md#text-only-alternative) aloud).

1. "Point to who is passing the turn. What are they passing, and what does
   it mean?"
2. "Point to the child who is waiting fairly. How can you tell they are
   waiting fairly?"
3. "Is this picture fair? Why — what in the picture shows fairness?"

*Looks for:* the timer identified as the turn-passer with its meaning; the
waiting child identified with body evidence (folded hands, calm smile); a
fairness explanation grounded in the image (everyone gets a turn, everyone
has blocks, waiting looks calm).

## Task 4 — Run a real vote (Objective 4)

The learner runs a genuine class vote with a real consequence (e.g., which of
two activities closes the session): presents the choices, collects one vote
per voter (adult + puppets), counts aloud, announces the winner — and then
the adult engineers the learner's LOSS (the puppets outvote the learner's
pick). The learner must follow the decision.
*Looks for:* all four steps run honestly (one person, one vote; visible
count); graceful acceptance of losing — follows the winning choice without
refusal or re-vote demands. (If the learner happens to win, re-run with
swapped puppet votes so the loss is practiced.)

## Task 5 — Repair the broken rule (Objectives 2, 6)

Puppet scenario: during cleanup, a puppet throws blocks instead of putting
them away (breaking "we clean up our toys"). The learner must: name the
broken rule, walk the puppet through all four repair steps, and then state
one school rule and one neighborhood rule with their safe/fair reasons
(Objective 5 folded in).
*Looks for:* rule named in child words; all four steps sequenced (stop, fix,
say the rule / sorry, try again); one school + one neighborhood rule each
with a correct safe/fair reason.

## Scoring

Each task: **Secure (3)** — complete, correct, unprompted · **Developing
(2)** — complete with one hint/prompt · **Emerging (1)** — partial or
incorrect after prompting · **Not yet (0)** — no response.

| Total | Meaning | Next step |
|---|---|---|
| 13–15 | Secure | Ready for Unit 03; celebrate with the Fair Rules Song |
| 9–12 | Developing | Re-teach the lowest tasks using the [teacher guide](../teacher-guide.md) paths, then move on |
| 5–8 | Emerging | Take a flexible catch-up week on the two weakest objectives before Unit 03 |
| 0–4 | Not yet | Re-teach the unit's core (L2–L3) across two catch-up weeks; check for unmet needs (language, sensory, emotional) with the adult's judgment |

This assessment is **not standardized or validated** — it is a teacher-made
check of this unit's objectives, scored by the guiding adult against the
rubric above.
