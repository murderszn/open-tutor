# Unit Assessment — Unit 01: Counting and Classifying Objects

**When:** Session 16 of 16 · **Format:** performance tasks, oral/pointing/
drawing; adult scribes · **Time:** about 20 minutes
**Unit:** [U01](../README.md) · Full solutions and scoring:
[answer-key.md](../answer-key.md#unit-assessment)

> This assessment is educator-made for this unit. It is **not** a standardized
> or validated test; use it to decide readiness for Unit 02, not to label the
> learner.

## Task 1 — Count a mixed line (Objectives 1, 2)

Place 8 counters in a loose line (not evenly spaced). "Count them. How many?"
Then: "Close your eyes." The adult removes 2. "Count again. How many now?
How do you know it changed?"

*Looks for:* one-to-one to 8; last-word answer; noticing the total changed
after recounting (6).

## Task 2 — Sort, count, compare (Objectives 4, 5)

Mix 5 yellow and 3 green buttons. "Sort these. Tell me your rule. Count each
group. Which group has more? Which has fewer?"

*Looks for:* rule named ("color"); accurate counts (5 and 3); "yellow has
more, green has fewer" (any clear wording accepted).

## Task 3 — Count out (Objective 3)

Pile of 14 counters, empty cup. "Put 9 counters in the cup." After: "Count
your cup to check. Did you get 9?"

*Looks for:* stopping at 9; checking by recounting; fixing an overshoot if
one happens (self-correction counts as success).

## Task 4 — Draw and count (Objectives 1, 2, 4)

"Draw 4 big circles and 6 little circles, in two groups. Count each group.
Which group did you draw more of?"

*Looks for:* two separated groups; one mark per count; counts 4 and 6;
"little circles — more."

## Task 5 — The picture round (Objectives 4, 5)

Show [`assets/counting-sorting-tray.png`](../assets/counting-sorting-tray.png)
(or read its text alternative: "A tray has 4 boxes — 5 red apples, 3 yellow
lemons, 4 blue balls, 2 green pears.").

a. "Point to the box with the most things. What is in it? How many?"
b. "Which two boxes have different counts but the same kind of thing?"
   *(Trick question — none do; every box holds a different kind. Accept "none"
   or "they are all different kinds.")*
c. "If we put the pears and the lemons together in one big box, how many
   would be in it?" *(2 + 3 = 5. The learner may count on fingers or recount
   the two boxes: acceptable at this stage.)*

## Scoring

Each task: **Secure** (correct, at most one self-corrected slip) ·
**Developing** (correct after a hint or second try) · **Not yet**
(incorrect or breakdown despite support).

- **Ready for Unit 02:** Secure on at least 4 of 5 tasks, no "Not yet."
- **Ready with support:** 2+ Developing, no "Not yet" → start Unit 02 and
  re-run the corresponding [practice sessions](../practice.md) once a week.
- **Re-teach first:** any "Not yet" → see
  [teacher guide](../teacher-guide.md#re-teaching-paths); use the track's
  flexible weeks before Unit 02.

Keep the scored notes private — never in this repository.
