# Formative Quiz — Unit 01 (Week 4)

**When:** Session 15 of 16 · **Format:** oral / pointing / drawing; the adult
scribes and observes · **Time:** about 15 minutes
**Unit:** [U01](../README.md) · Scoring guidance: [answer-key.md](../answer-key.md#formative-quiz)

This is a low-stakes check, not a test. Sit beside the learner, keep it
playful ("show me what you know"), and stop if frustration rises — note where
you stopped and re-teach there.

## Tasks

**Q1 — Touch and count.** Place 6 counters in a line. "Touch each one and
count. How many?"

**Q2 — Count in a circle.** Arrange 7 buttons in a ring. "Count them. Show me
your start first. How many?"

**Q3 — Sort.** Mix 4 red and 3 blue crayons. "Sort these by color. What is
your rule?"

**Q4 — Count each group.** (Same crayons, now sorted.) "Count the reds. Count
the blues. Which group has more?"

**Q5 — Count out.** Set out a pile of 12 counters and an empty bowl. "Give me
5 counters, please."

**Q6 — Rote sequence.** "Let's count together as high as we can!" Count
aloud together; note how far the sequence stays stable.

## What counts as ready

- **Secure:** Q1–Q5 correct (one-to-one holds, last word = total, sort rule
  kept, count-out stops at 5); Q6 stable to at least 15.
- **Developing:** 1–2 errors with self-correction on a recount or a hint
  ("what was your last word?"). Note which objective wobbled and re-teach it
  in Session 16's flex time.
- **Not yet:** 3+ errors or a breakdown (e.g., cannot keep one-to-one past 4).
  Do not proceed to Unit 02's Week-1 plan unchanged — use the
  [teacher guide](../teacher-guide.md#re-teaching-paths): repeat the
  corresponding practice sessions across the track's flexible weeks before
  moving on.

Record the result (secure / developing / not yet, plus which items) in private
notes — never in this repository.
