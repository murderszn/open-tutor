# Practice, Game, and Review Sessions — Unit 01

These are the named non-lesson sessions in the
[16-session pacing](README.md#16-session-pacing-4-weeks--4-sessions-15-20-minutes-each).
Each runs 15–20 minutes. The adult observes and notes; nothing here is graded.

## Session A: sorting tray by color

**Goal:** sort mixed objects by color quickly and accurately; count each
group. (Supports Lessons 01–02; used in pacing Session 2.)

**Materials:** 12–16 mixed counters in 2–3 colors, 3 trays.

1. Warm-up: "Touch and count these 5" (one-to-one re-check).
2. Dump the mixed counters. "Sort by color. Say your rule first."
3. The learner sorts. The adult watches: does each object get exactly one
   tray? Is the rule kept the whole time?
4. "Count each tray. How many in each?"
5. Round 2 with a new mix (change the counts — e.g., 7 red, 5 blue, 4 yellow).
6. Challenge: "Sort by color, then point to the tray with the most."

**Observe and note:** rule consistency, one-to-one while counting groups,
whether the learner recounts a group instead of remembering (fine at this
stage — note it).

## Session B: counting tray to 10

**Goal:** count sets to 10 in lines and circles; answer "how many?" with the
last word. (Supports Lesson 03; used in pacing Session 6.)

**Materials:** 10 counters, tray.

1. Warm-up: rote count to 10 together, clapping.
2. Line round: the adult lays out 4, then 7, then 10 counters in a line. The
   learner counts each and answers "how many?"
3. Circle round: arrange 6, then 8, then 9 in a ring. "Show me your start.
   Stop when you get back." Count and answer "how many?"
4. Switch round: the learner arranges 10 counters however they like (line or
   circle); the adult counts and the learner checks the adult's count.
   (Checking someone else's count builds the habit.)
5. Cool-down: "Close your eyes. I am taking some away." Remove 3 from 10.
   "Count now — how many?" (8; recount to check.)

**Observe and note:** start-marking in circles, the "last word is the answer"
habit, stability of the 6–10 sequence.

## Session C: counting songs and games

**Goal:** stabilize the rote count sequence through music and movement.
(Supports K.CC.A.1; used in pacing Sessions 3 and 7.)

**Materials:** none required; optional: the
[Jack Hartmann counting songs](resource-pack.md#youtube) from the Resource Pack.

1. **Echo count:** the adult counts "1, 2, 3" and the learner echoes; build to
   1–10, then try 1–20.
2. **Movement count:** march in place, one step per number, to 10; then to 20.
   "Freeze at 10! Now keep going — 11, 12..."
3. **Count and freeze:** the adult counts aloud and stops at a random number
   ("...7 — FREEZE!"); the learner says the number the adult stopped on.
4. **Backward challenge** (extension): count backward from 5, then from 10.
   (Not required — play only.)
5. **Number hunt:** the adult hides 5 number cards (1–5, then later 1–10)
   around the room; the learner finds them and puts them in order, counting
   aloud.

**Observe and note:** which numbers wobble (often 7, 9, 11–15); whether the
learner can start from 1 reliably every time.

## Session D: draw, count, and circle review

**Goal:** connect counting to drawing; review the week's objectives on paper.
(Used in pacing Sessions 4, 8, and 12.)

**Materials:** paper, crayons.

Give these as oral directions, one at a time; the adult scribes any words.

1. "Draw 4 apples. Count them. Circle your drawing and tell me how many."
2. "Draw 3 stars and 2 moons, sorted into two groups" (draw a line between the
   groups). "Count each group. Which group has more?"
3. "Draw 6 dots in a circle. Count them — remember your start."
4. "Draw the sorting-tray picture from Lesson 02 from memory: 4 boxes. Put 5
   red apples in one box and 3 yellow lemons in another. Count each box."
   (Approximation is fine; the counting is the point.)
5. "Draw 7 balls. Now draw 2 more. Count all of them — how many?" (9; "one
   more" preview through drawing.)

**Observe and note:** one mark per count while drawing, group separation on
paper, whether "how many?" gets the last-word answer without recounting.

## Session E: count-out practice

**Goal:** count out requested numbers 1–10 from a larger pile, stopping
exactly. (Supports Lesson 04; used in pacing Session 10.)

**Materials:** pile of 15 counters, a bowl ("the order bowl").

1. Warm-up: "Count these 9. How many?"
2. Play **store**: the learner is the shopkeeper. The adult orders: "I would
   like 3, please." The learner counts 3 into the bowl. "Check your bowl."
3. Orders, in this order: 2, 5, 4, 7, 6, 8, 10, 1, 9.
   (Mix small and larger; end with 1 and 9 for confidence and stretch.)
4. **Wrong-order round:** the adult (as a silly customer) counts out 6 when
   asked for 4. "Check my order — did I get it right? Fix it for me."
5. The learner becomes the customer and orders from the adult; the learner
   checks the adult's bowl.

**Observe and note:** the stop-at-the-number moment (the core skill),
overshooting, whether checking the bowl is becoming a habit.
