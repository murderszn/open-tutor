# Teacher Guide — Unit 02: Numbers to 10 and One-to-One Correspondence

**For the guiding adult.** This guide holds session guidance, common
misconceptions, re-teaching moves, and readiness criteria. Student-facing
materials never contain answers — keep this guide and the
[answer key](answer-key.md) separate from the learner's pages.

## Unit at a glance

| Week (track) | Focus | New content |
|---|---|---|
| 7 | Numerals 0–5 | Read, match, write 0–5; zero as "none" |
| 8 | Numerals 6–10 | Read, order, match 6–10; the 10 basket picture |
| 9 | Tricky counting | Count on from a number; scattered keep-track strategies |
| 10 | Review + assess | Number-line walk, missing-numeral games, quiz, assessment |

## Session-by-session guidance

- **Sessions 1, 5, 9 (core lessons 1–3):** introduce only what the lesson
  says. Resist teaching 6–10 during week 7 — the 0–5 set needs to be solid
  first. Shuffle cards constantly; a memorized row is not reading.
- **Sessions 2, 6, 10 (practice):** these are fluency builders, not tests.
  Keep them playful and stop while the learner is succeeding.
- **Sessions 3, 7 (games):** movement days. The numeral-hop and count-on
  march build the sequence into muscle memory.
- **Sessions 4, 8, 12 (review):** run the circuit; the station with the
  most errors becomes the next session's warm-up.
- **Session 11 (project part 1):** pages 0–5 only. Do not rush ahead to 6–10
  — anticipation is motivation.
- **Session 13 (lessons 4+5 combined):** teach count-on first (15 min), take
  a movement break, then scattered counting (15 min). If the learner fades,
  split across two days and shift the project part 2 one session later —
  the pacing survives it.
- **Session 14 (project part 2):** pages 6–10 are labor-intensive; offer
  stickers/stamps without insisting. Bind the book in order at the end.
- **Session 15 (quiz + book reading):** quiz first (fresh mind), then the
  celebratory book reading. Repairs are part of the lesson.
- **Session 16 (assessment):** may split into two sittings (tasks 1–3, then
  4–6). Score after, not during — stay warm and neutral while assessing.

## Misconceptions and re-teaching moves

| What you see | What it means | Re-teach move |
|---|---|---|
| Names numerals only in 0–10 order; lost when shuffled | Memorized the row, not the shapes | Shuffle every round; play "find 7" from a face-down spread |
| Says "nothing" but cannot pick 0 | Zero not yet a numeral | Empty-bowl routine daily: "how many? zero — show me 0" |
| Reads 6 as 9 (or reverse) | 6/9 visual confusion (very common) | "6 sits its belly down; 9 holds its balloon up" + trace both large |
| Reads 10 as "one" | Two-mark numeral not yet parsed | Always count ten things to 10; cover the 1, "what is left? 0 — ten is 1 and 0 together" |
| Writes 3 or 5 backwards | Normal mirror-writing at 5 | Reteach the path ("top curve first"); never erase-punish; accept-then-repractice |
| Restarts at 1 when asked to count on | "Start at 1" is a strong habit | The cloth: hide the already-counted part so restarting is impossible; announce the covered number every time |
| Skips the starting number ("start at 4" → "5, 6…") | Starting number dropped from total | "Say the number you are standing on first — 4 — then step: 5, 6…" |
| Double-counts scattered objects | No keep-track strategy yet | Insist on the done bowl for every scattered count until the habit is automatic |
| Counts the word faster than the hand | Unit 01 issue resurfacing | "Freeze your finger after each touch" — slow the hand, not the mouth |

## Catch-up guidance

- **Numeral naming (Obj. 1):** replay Practice Session A with 3 cards at a
  time, adding one card per successful round. Pair each card with its set
  every time — never drill shapes alone.
- **Writing (Obj. 2):** drop back to sand-tray tracing and sky-writing for
  two sessions before paper. One numeral per session is fine.
- **Count-on (Obj. 3):** the floor number line is the cure. Walk it daily:
  "stand on 4, take 3 steps." Do not remove the line until the learner
  counts on without it.
- **Scattered counting (Obj. 4):** shrink the pile (5 objects) and require
  the done bowl. Grow by one object per successful session.
- **If 3+ objectives are "not yet" after Session 16:** repeat weeks 7–8
  core content (Lessons 01–03, Practice A/B) in a flexible track week
  before starting Unit 03. Unit 03 (comparing) assumes numeral reading.

## Readiness criteria for Unit 03

The learner is ready for Unit 03 (Comparing numbers and quantities) when:
at least 4 of 5 unit objectives are secure, including Objective 1 (naming
0–10) — comparison work is meaningless if the numerals are not yet symbols.
Objective 2 (writing) may still be developing; Unit 03 needs reading more
than writing.

## Notes on the generated illustration

The basket picture
([assets/numeral-baskets-6-8-10.png](assets/numeral-baskets-6-8-10.png)) is an
AI illustration, not a photograph. Its counts were verified before use (6
apples, 8 bananas, 10 blue balls). It is used for count-and-match activities
only — never as evidence of a real observation. A text-only alternative with
