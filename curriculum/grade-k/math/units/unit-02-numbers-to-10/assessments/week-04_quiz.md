# Formative Quiz — Unit 02, Week 4 (Track Week 10)

**Format:** oral and observed; the adult asks, watches, and scribes.
**Time:** 10–15 minutes · **Pacing:** Session 15 of 16
**Unit:** [U02](../README.md)

Give every item. Mark each **secure / developing / not yet** and note the
exact response. There are no trick questions — this quiz tells the adult
what to re-teach, not the learner what they failed.

## Items

**Q1 — Name the numerals (Objective 1).** Shuffle cards 0–10. Hold each up:
"What numeral is this?"
*Secure: 10–11 correct. Developing: 8–9. Not yet: fewer than 8.*

**Q2 — Zero (Objective 1, 5).** Show the empty bowl. "How many beans are in
the bowl? Show me the numeral that means that."
*Secure: says "zero/none" and picks 0. Developing: says "nothing" but needs
a prompt to name it zero. Not yet: cannot connect empty to 0.*

**Q3 — Match numeral to set (Objective 1, 5).** Hand the learner the **7**
card. "Build this many." Then show 9 counters: "How many? Find the numeral."
*Secure: builds 7 (one-to-one) and matches 9→9. Developing: one of the two
correct. Not yet: neither.*

**Q4 — Write (Objective 2).** "Write 2, 0, and 5 for me."
*Secure: all three readable. Developing: two readable. Not yet: fewer than
two.*

**Q5 — Count on (Objective 3).** "Start at 5. Count to 9."
*Secure: "5, 6, 7, 8, 9" with no restart. Developing: restarts at 1 once,
then self-corrects. Not yet: restarts at 1 or cannot continue past 5.*

**Q6 — Scattered count (Objective 4).** Scatter 7 counters. "Count these —
use the done bowl."
*Secure: 7, one move per word. Developing: correct total with one
self-corrected slip. Not yet: misses or double-counts without noticing.*

## Scoring and next steps

- **All secure:** proceed to the [unit assessment](unit-assessment.md).
- **1–2 developing:** re-teach those objectives with the matching practice
  session ([A](../practice.md#session-a-numeral-set-memory-match),
  [B](../practice.md#session-b-trace-and-write-practice),
  [E](../practice.md#session-e-basket-counting-practice), or the Lesson 04/05
  activities), then take the unit assessment.
- **Any not yet, or 3+ developing:** use a catch-up session before the unit
  assessment — see the [teacher guide](../teacher-guide.md#catch-up-guidance).
  The track's flexible weeks exist for exactly this.
