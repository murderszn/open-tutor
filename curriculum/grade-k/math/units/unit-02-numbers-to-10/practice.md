# Practice, Game, and Review Sessions — Unit 02

Named sessions referenced by the [16-session pacing](README.md#16-session-pacing-4-weeks--4-sessions-15-20-minutes-each).
Each runs 15–20 minutes. The adult leads, watches, and scribes; the learner
responds orally, by pointing, drawing, or moving objects.

---

## Session A — Numeral–set memory match

*(Pacing: Session 2 — after Lesson 01.)*

**Goal:** match numeral cards 0–5 to sets of 0–5 counters, from memory.

**Setup:** six numeral cards (0–5) face-up in a row; a pile of counters.

**Play:**
1. The adult points to a numeral. The learner names it and builds the
   matching set beside it (0 = empty space — "leave it empty on purpose").
2. Next round: the adult builds a set; the learner finds the numeral card.
3. Memory round: turn all cards face-down. The adult names a numeral
   ("find 4"); the learner flips cards until 4 appears, then builds the set.
4. Finish with the zero round: "Build me zero." The learner shows empty
   hands. "What numeral means zero?" (0)

**Watch for:** naming by position instead of shape — shuffle often. Celebrate
every correct zero: it is the trickiest card.

**Answer key:** [answer-key.md](answer-key.md#practice-session-a).

## Session B — Trace-and-write practice

*(Pacing: Session 6 — after Lesson 02.)*

**Goal:** write 0–5 readably, then match each written numeral to a set.

**Setup:** paper strips with "parking spots" (boxes), thick crayons, numeral
cards 0–5 as models, counters.

**Practice:**
1. Sky-write 0–5 with a whole arm, saying each formation path aloud.
2. Trace each model card with a finger, saying the path.
3. Write 0–5 in the parking spots (models visible — this is practice, not a
   test).
4. Cover the models. The adult dictates: "Write 3. Write 0. Write 5." The
   learner writes from memory.
5. Match: count out counters under each written numeral.

**Watch for:** reversals of 3 and 5 — reteach the path ("top curve first"),
never erase-punish. A readable-but-wobbly numeral is a success.

**Answer key:** [answer-key.md](answer-key.md#practice-session-b).

## Session C — Numeral songs and movement

*(Pacing: Sessions 3 and 7 — game days.)*

**Goal:** make the 0–10 sequence and numeral shapes joyful and automatic.

**Activities (pick 2–3 per session):**

1. **Count-and-freeze:** music or clapping while the learner dances; when it
   stops, the adult holds up a numeral card — the learner shouts its name
   and freezes in its shape (1 = tall and straight, 0 = arms in a circle,
   8 = two stacked circles with arms... silly is good).
2. **Numeral hop:** floor cards 0–5 (Session 3) or 0–10 (Session 7) in a
   winding path. The adult calls "hop to 4!" — the learner hops there and
   says "four!"
3. **Zero statue:** when the adult calls "zero!", the learner curls into a
   tiny ball — "zero means none showing!"
4. **Count-on march:** Session 7 — march in place while the adult calls
   "start at 3!" and the learner chants "3, 4, 5, 6, 7" marching.

**Watch for:** this is fluency play, not assessment — keep it fast, silly,
and short. End while it is still fun.

## Session D — Draw, trace, and match review

*(Pacing: Sessions 4, 8, and 12 — review days.)*

**Goal:** reconnect numerals, sets, and writing in one calm circuit.

**Circuit (about 4 minutes per station):**

1. **Draw:** the adult names a numeral (0–5 in Sessions 4/8; 0–10 in
   Session 12). The learner draws that many simple marks (dots, stars,
   apples) and counts them back.
2. **Trace:** the learner traces the matching numeral card with a finger,
   then writes it on paper.
3. **Match:** three sets on the table (e.g., 2, 5, 3 buttons); three numeral
   cards. The learner matches all three and reads the matches back:
   "2 — two. 5 — five. 3 — three."

**Re-teach rule:** the station with the most errors becomes the next
session's warm-up. In Session 12, add the missing-numeral game: lay 0–10 in
order, remove one card while eyes are closed — "which is missing?"

**Answer key:** [answer-key.md](answer-key.md#practice-session-d).

## Session E — Basket counting practice

*(Pacing: Session 10 — after Lesson 03.)*

**Goal:** count the 6-, 8-, and 10-object groups in the generated
illustration and match numeral cards.

**Setup:** the illustration
[`assets/numeral-baskets-6-8-10.png`](assets/numeral-baskets-6-8-10.png)
(or its text alternative), numeral cards 6, 8, 10, plus 10 real counters.

**Practice:**
1. Count the apples in the picture together: point to each, "1, 2, 3, 4, 5,
   6 — six." Place the **6** card on the basket.
2. The learner counts the bananas alone (8), then the blue balls (10),
   placing cards.
3. **Real-object check:** build 8 counters on the table. "Is this the same
   number as the bananas? How do you know?" (Count both — eight and eight.)
4. **Tricky round:** the adult counts the blue balls wrong — "1, 2, 3, 4,
   5, 6, 7, 8, 9 — nine!" The learner recounts and corrects: "ten — you
   stopped too early."
5. Missing-numeral: lay 6, 8, 10 in order; the adult removes one — "which
   basket's numeral is missing?"

**Text-only alternative** (if the image is unavailable): "Three baskets — 6
red apples, 8 yellow bananas, 10 blue balls." Run the same rounds from the
description; the real-counter check in step 3 becomes the main activity.

**Answer key:** [answer-key.md](answer-key.md#practice-session-e).
