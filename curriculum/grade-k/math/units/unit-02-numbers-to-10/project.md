# Project — My Number Book 0–10

**Type:** multi-session investigation and creation project · **Sessions 11, 14, 15**
**Unit:** [U02](README.md) · **Objectives addressed:** 1, 2, 5 (numerals,
writing, set↔numeral both directions)

The learner authors an 11-page counting book — one page per numeral 0–10.
Each page carries the written numeral and exactly that many marks (dots,
stickers, stamps, or drawings). The finished book is read aloud to the adult
("read" = name each numeral and count its marks), proving that every numeral
0–10 is known, writable, and matched to its quantity.

## Adult directions (read before starting)

- This is a **three-part** project across two weeks. Part 1 (Session 11):
  pages 0–5. Part 2 (Session 14): pages 6–10 plus a cover. Part 3
  (Session 15): the learner "reads" the book to the adult and fixes errors.
- The adult prepares: 11 half-sheets of paper (or a folded booklet),
  crayons/markers, dot stickers or a stamp pad (optional), numeral cards
  0–10 as models.
- **Zero page rule:** the 0 page shows the numeral 0 and an EMPTY space —
  "zero means none, so this page stays empty." Do not let the learner draw
  "zero things" as a picture of nothing; the emptiness IS the content.
- The adult may scribe nothing on the pages — the numerals and marks are the
  learner's work. Wobbly numerals are fine if readable.
- Keep the finished book in private storage (never in this public repo).

## Part 1 — Pages 0–5 (Session 11, ~15 minutes)

1. The adult folds/staples the booklet and writes nothing on the pages yet.
2. For each numeral 0–5 in order:
   - The learner **writes the numeral** large at the top (model card
     visible).
   - The learner **makes exactly that many marks** below it (dots,
     stickers, or simple drawings — e.g., 3 = three apple dots).
   - The learner **counts the marks back** and the adult asks "does it
     match?" If not, the learner adds or removes marks until it matches.
3. Special attention to the **0 page**: "Write 0. How many marks go below?
   None — the page stays empty. That is the whole point."

## Part 2 — Pages 6–10 and cover (Session 14, ~15–20 minutes)

1. Same routine for 6–10. These pages take longer (more marks!) — stickers
   or stamps help; drawing 10 items is allowed but tiring, so offer the
   faster tools without requiring them.
2. **Cover:** the learner writes/draws a title ("My Number Book") and
   decorates it. The adult writes the learner's *first name only* on the
   cover if the learner asks — or the learner writes it.
3. The learner numbers... no — the learner puts the pages **in order 0–10**
   and the adult staples/binds them.

## Part 3 — Reading and repair (Session 15, ~10 minutes + quiz time)

1. The learner "reads" the book page by page: names the numeral, counts the
   marks, confirms the match. The adult listens and notes errors.
2. **Repair round:** any mismatched page is fixed on the spot (add/remove
   marks or rewrite the numeral). Repairs are normal — mathematicians revise.
3. The learner presents the finished book (to the adult, a stuffed animal
   audience, or on a video call with family — learner's choice).

## Deliverables

1. An 11-page book: pages 0–10 in order, each with a readable numeral and
   exactly that many marks (0 page empty).
2. A decorated cover.
3. A completed "reading" of the book (adult-observed, Session 15).

## Rubric

| Criterion | Secure (2) | Developing (1) | Not yet (0) |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Numerals** (Obj. 1, 2) | All 11 numerals readable and in order | 8–10 readable; order has 1–2 slips | Fewer than 8 readable |
| **Quantity match** (Obj. 5) | Every page's marks equal its numeral (0 empty) | 1–3 pages off by one mark | 4+ pages mismatched |
| **Counting check** (Obj. 4) | Learner count-backs are one-to-one, correct totals | Count-backs mostly correct, 1–2 slips | Cannot count-back reliably |
| **Revision** | Finds and fixes own mismatches in Part 3 | Fixes with adult pointing to the page | Does not attempt fixes |

**Scoring:** 7–8 = secure; 4–6 = developing (re-teach the weakest pages
before Unit 03); 0–3 = not yet (repeat Parts 1–2 with fewer pages per
session before the unit assessment).

## Safety and access notes

- Dot stickers and stamp pads: check for skin allergies; washable ink only.
- Scissors (if the learner cuts the booklet): blunt-tip, adult-supervised.
- Alternate response modes: the learner dictates marks while the adult
  places stickers; or builds each page's quantity with counters photographed
  by the adult (photos stay in private storage).
- For learners who fatigue: split Part 2 across two sittings (6–8, then
  9–10) — the pacing allows it.
