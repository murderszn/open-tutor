# Resource Pack — Unit 02: Numbers to 10 and One-to-One Correspondence

## Summary

**Goal:** give the guiding adult free, no-account routes to support
kindergarten numeral recognition and writing (0–10), numeral↔set matching,
counting on from a given number, and scattered-set keep-track strategies —
plus music and interactive practice a 5-year-old will actually enjoy.
**Age fit:** kindergarten (about 5–6); all items are visual, musical, or
hands-on, with no reading required of the learner.

## Web references

Checked 2026-10-15 unless noted.

1. **The Math Learning Center — free math apps**
   `https://www.mathlearningcenter.org/apps`
   *Why:* free web apps, no account needed. **Number Frames** (place
   counters in five/ten frames — numeral↔set matching, Lessons 01–03),
   **Number Line** (virtual number line — count-on practice, Lesson 04),
   and **Number Rack** (slide beads — counting out and count-on, Lessons
   04–05). Verified live 2026-10-15: apps page states the apps are free
   with web versions for modern browsers. Adult-guided use.
2. **PBS LearningMedia — Counting and Cardinality collection**
   `https://www.pbslearningmedia.org/subjects/mathematics/k-8-mathematics/counting-and-cardinality/`
   *Why:* curated K–8 counting resources including one-to-one correspondence
   and numeral activities. Found via web search and verified reachable
   2026-10-01; collection description matches K.CC.B.4/B.5. Free; core
   videos viewable without an account.
3. **NCTM Illuminations**
   `https://illuminations.nctm.org`
   *Why:* standards-based math lessons and interactive tools for grades
   PK–12 from the National Council of Teachers of Mathematics. Verified

   recognition, ordering). Free, no account.
4. **Common Core State Standards for Mathematics — kindergarten**

   *Why:* **adult reference only** — the authoritative text of K.CC.A.2,

   verified against the official framework on 2026-10-01 during the track

5. **K–5 Math Teaching Resources (k-5mathteachingresources.com) — free

   frames, and counting mats from a teacher resource site; adult previews
