# Project — My Addition Story Book

**Deliverable:** a stapled book of 3 story pages (plus a cover) that
the learner illustrates, dictates, and can re-read and re-solve.
**Time:** Sessions 11–12 (about 40 minutes total, split across two
days) · **Materials:** paper, crayons, stapler, counters

## What to make

A book with a cover ("My Addition Story Book" — adult writes the
title; the learner decorates it) and exactly 3 story pages:

- **Page 1 — a putting-together story.** Two groups join into one.
  (Lesson 01 kind.)
- **Page 2 — an adding-to story.** A group starts, then more join it.
  (Lesson 02 kind.)
- **Page 3 — a parts story.** One whole (5 or 6) split into two
  parts. (Lesson 05 kind.)

Each page needs all three things:

1. A drawing of the story, with the two parts kept visibly separate
   (two areas, two colors, or an arrow showing newcomers arriving).
2. The story in the learner's own words, dictated to the adult, who
   writes it under the drawing.
3. The number sentence, written by the adult from the learner's
   dictation (e.g. `4 + 3 = 7`, or `6 = 3 + 3` for the parts page).

Totals must stay within 10; parts-page wholes must be 5 or 6.

## Steps

1. **Plan (Session 11, first 10 minutes).** The learner picks the
   three stories aloud and acts each one out with counters before
   drawing anything. The adult checks each total by counting with
   the learner.
2. **Draw and dictate (Session 11, rest).** One page at a time:
   draw, dictate the story, dictate the sentence, adult writes.
   Pages 1–2 are finished in Session 11.
3. **Finish and share (Session 12).** Page 3 is finished, the book
   is stapled, and the learner "reads" the book to the adult:
   for each page, retell the story, point at the parts, and say the
   sentence. The adult re-solves one page with counters as a check.
4. **Keep the book.** It stays with the learner's other unit work
   and is re-read in Week 22's review game.

## Rubric

Score each row as Getting started, On the way, or Strong. "Strong"
on all four rows is a complete project; "On the way" on any row is
still a finished book — note the row for Week 22 review.

| Row | Getting started | On the way | Strong |
|---|---|---|---|
| Story kinds | Only one kind is told, or pages repeat the same kind | Two of the three kinds are told correctly | All three kinds (putting-together, adding-to, parts) are told correctly |
| Parts and total | Parts are missing or the total is guessed | Parts are shown but the total is found by recounting with help | Both parts are shown separately and the total is found by counting all or counting on, unaided |
| Sentence match | Sentence does not match the drawing | Sentence matches with one adult prompt | Each sentence matches its drawing and the learner reads it back, pointing at each part |
| Drawing clarity | Drawing does not show two countable parts | Drawing shows parts but they are hard to count (overlapping, jumbled) | Drawing shows two clearly countable parts |

## Adult notes

- Never "fix" a drawing by redrawing it — if a total is wrong, act
  the story out again with counters and let the learner correct
  their own page.
- If a learner wants a total above 10, celebrate the ambition and
  park it: "That story is bigger than 10, so it goes in your next
  book. Pick a story to 10 for this page."
- The book is the learner's own work: no sample book is provided, so
  no page can be copied from one.
