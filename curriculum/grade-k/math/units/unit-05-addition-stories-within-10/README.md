# Unit 05 — Addition Stories Within 10

**Grade:** Kindergarten · **Subject:** Mathematics · **Track weeks:** 19–22
**Backlog:** [issue #6](https://github.com/murderszn/open-tutor/issues/6), section U05
**Status:** validated draft (this unit); not educator-reviewed, not merged

Units 01–04 taught the learner to count sets to 10, read and write the
numerals 0–10, compare groups and numerals, and work with teen numbers
on ten frames. This unit answers the next question: **when two groups
come together, or when more joins a group, how many are there now?**
The learner acts out, draws, and tells addition stories within 10 —
first "putting together," then "adding to" — says each story as a
number sentence, solves mixed stories to 10, and breaks 5 and 6 into
parts in more than one way. By the end, the learner can solve an
addition story within 10 with objects or a drawing, say and record
(through the adult) its number sentence, and show two ways to make 5
and two ways to make 6.

## Essential question

How do we find how many there are altogether when groups join together?

## Prerequisites

- Units 01–02: touch-count sets to 10 with one-to-one correspondence;
  state the total when asked "how many?"; count out a requested number
  1–10; name, write, and match numerals 0–10
- Unit 03: compare two groups and say which has more, fewer, or the
  same; make two groups equal
- Unit 04: teen numbers as "ten and some more" on a ten frame
  (ten-frame fluency helps, but every story here is solved with
  objects or drawings first)
- Can point, move counters, and draw simple marks (adult scribes words
  and number sentences)
- Rote count sequence to at least 10, in order

No addition vocabulary or symbols are assumed — this unit teaches
*altogether, plus, equals,* and the written sentence `3 + 2 = 5`
from acted-out stories first, symbols second.

## Measurable objectives

By the end of the unit the learner will be able to (adult-observed):

1. Act out and draw a **"putting together"** story within 10 (two
   groups join into one) and say the total, e.g. "4 red apples and
   3 green apples — 7 apples altogether."
2. Act out and draw an **"adding to"** story within 10 (a group starts,
   then more join it) and say the total, e.g. "5 ducks, then 2 more
   ducks join — 7 ducks altogether."
3. Say a story as a **number sentence** and match it to objects or a
   drawing, e.g. "4 plus 3 equals 7," and recognize the written
   sentence `4 + 3 = 7` (adult writes; the learner reads the numerals
   and says the sentence).
4. **Solve addition story problems within 10** — mixed putting-together
   and adding-to stories, totals to 10 — using objects or drawings,
   and check the answer by counting all.
5. **Decompose 5 and 6 into pairs in more than one way** with objects
   or drawings, e.g. 5 as 2 + 3 and as 4 + 1; 6 as 3 + 3 and as 5 + 1
   (and a third way for 6), and tell a short story for at least one
   pair.

## Vocabulary (taught with objects and stories first, words second)

altogether / in all · join · plus (+) · equals (=) · number sentence ·
part · whole · putting together · adding to · story problem ·
count on · check by counting

Note for the adult: *altogether* and *in all* both ask for the total
of a join; accept either. At this grade the learner is never required
to write a number sentence alone — the adult writes what the learner
says, and the learner points to each part as they read it back.

- 20 small counters (buttons, dried beans, or blocks) in two colors

- Ten frame (from Unit 04) and number line 0–10, kept visible
- Paper, crayons, and a stapler for the story book project
- Story cards: the adult writes or draws one short story per card

## Session plan (16 sessions × 15–20 minutes, adult-led)

Lessons are oral, manipulative, and drawing-based; the adult reads
every story aloud, scribes words, and writes number sentences the
learner dictates. A session is one row below.

| Session | Week | Activity | Success criterion |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | 19 | Lesson 01 — Putting together | Learner acts out 2 join stories and says both totals |
| 2 | 19 | Practice A — Put-together stories with counters | 4 of 5 stories correct by counting all |
| 3 | 19 | Lesson 02 — Adding to | Learner acts out 2 adding-to stories and says both totals |
| 4 | 19 | Practice B — Adding-to stories; retell check | 4 of 5 stories correct; learner retells one story in own words |
| 5 | 20 | Lesson 03 — Number sentences | Learner says 2 number sentences and points to each part |
| 6 | 20 | Practice C — Sentence match game | Matches 4 of 5 sentences to drawings |
| 7 | 20 | Lesson 04 — Solving stories to 10 | Learner solves 3 mixed stories with objects or drawings |
| 8 | 20 | Practice D — Mixed stories; error-analysis story | 4 of 5 stories correct; learner spots the counting error in the acted story |
| 9 | 21 | Lesson 05 — Parts of 5 and 6 | Learner shows 2 ways to make 5 and 2 ways to make 6 |
| 10 | 21 | Practice E — Part-part stories | Tells a story for 3 of 4 part pairs |
| 11 | 21 | Project — My Addition Story Book (draft) | 2 story pages drafted with drawings and dictated sentences |
| 12 | 21 | Project — Finish, share, retrieval game | Book complete (3 stories); learner re-solves a Unit 03 comparison as warm-up |
| 13 | 22 | Review — Story-problem card game | Wins/plays 2 full rounds; totals to 10 correct by counting |
| 14 | 22 | Practice F — Mixed review, fluency within 5 | 4 of 5 within-5 stories answered without recounting from 1 (counting on allowed) |
| 15 | 22 | Formative quiz (assessments/week-04_quiz.md) | See quiz scoring note |
| 16 | 22 | Unit assessment (assessments/unit-assessment.md) + celebration | See assessment scoring note |

Retrieval built in: Sessions 4, 8, 12, and 14 revisit counting,
comparison (Unit 03), and ten-frame totals (Unit 04) as warm-ups, so
older skills stay warm while addition is learned.

## Differentiation and supports

- **If counting to 10 is still shaky:** keep every total at 5 or below
  until Session 7; use only one color of counter so color never
  distracts from count.
- **If the learner rushes to a guessed total:** cover the joined group
  and ask "how many in this part? And in this part? Now count them
  all together, touching each one." Guessing is treated as a signal to
  slow down, never as a wrong answer to be ashamed of.
- **Alternate response modes:** point, move counters, draw, clap the
  count, or use the ten frame — any mode that shows the parts and the
  total counts as a correct response.
- **Language access:** every story is acted out before it is drawn;
  the adult repeats the story while pointing at each group.
- **Extension:** totals of 9 and 10 from Session 7 on; "what if one
  more joined?" follow-ups; the learner invents a story for a given
  sentence (e.g. make up a story for `2 + 6 = 8`); a third way to make
  6, and a first way to make 7 (not assessed).

## What success looks like

The learner hears a short story, shows it with objects or a drawing,
counts all, and says a sentence like "3 birds and 2 birds — 5 birds
altogether." Written equations are read, not yet independently
written, and every answer is checked by counting — the habit this
unit is really teaching.
