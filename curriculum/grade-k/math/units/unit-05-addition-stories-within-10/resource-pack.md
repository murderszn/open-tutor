# Resource Pack — Unit 05: Addition Stories Within 10

## Summary

**Goal:** give the guiding adult free, no-account routes to support
kindergarten addition stories — putting-together and adding-to
stories within 10, number sentences, solving to 10, and parts of 5
and 6 — plus songs and hands-on practice a 5-year-old will actually
enjoy.
**Age fit:** kindergarten (about 5–6); all items are visual, musical,
or hands-on, with no reading required of the learner.

## Web references

Checked 2026-11-04. All are free; none requires an account for the
uses described here. "Direct" means the page itself is the resource;
"search link" means it is a starting point to search from.

1. **Common Core State Standards, Operations and Algebraic
   Thinking (kindergarten)** — the official K.OA.A.1–A.5 texts quoted
   in this unit's standards table.
   `http://www.thecorestandards.org/Math/Content/OA/`
   *Why (direct):* verifies every standard code and description used
   in Lessons 01–05. Adult reference, not a learner page.
2. **The Math Learning Center, Free Math Apps** — Number Frames and
   Number Rack apps (free) for showing addition stories and ten
   frames on screen.
   `https://www.mathlearningcenter.org/topics/free-math-apps`
   *Why (direct):* the Number Frames app shows the same part-part
   pictures this unit builds with counters; supports Lessons 04–05
   and the project. Preview the app before handing it to a child.
3. **Khan Academy Kids, math** — free early-learning app page
   describing addition and subtraction practice with visuals and
   stories for preschool–2nd grade.
   `https://www.khanacademy.org/kids/math`
   *Why (direct):* a free, no-account-browser alternative route to
   guided addition stories; supports Lessons 01–04 as supplement,
   not replacement, for adult-led acting out.
4. **KCM Family Math, "Race to Write" (ten-frame activity)** — a
   family activity using ten-frame cards to see numbers 1–10 in
   groups and find ways to make 10.
   `http://kcm.nku.edu/knp/family.php?family_activity=2207.2&prefix=S`
   *Why (direct):* ten-frame fluency that backs Lesson 04's full-frame
   check for 10; the "different ways to put numbers together"
   framing matches Lesson 05. Use the 0–6 cards for this unit.
5. **Khan Academy Blog, Kindergarten Math Games** — a teacher-written
   overview of kindergarten math skills, including basic addition
   within 5 by the end of kindergarten, with free game pointers.
   `https://blog.khanacademy.org/khan-kids-kindergarten-math-games/?_thumbnail_id=12964`
   *Why (direct):* adult background on pacing and expectations;
   confirms this unit's within-5 fluency goal sits inside the
   end-of-kindergarten expectation.
