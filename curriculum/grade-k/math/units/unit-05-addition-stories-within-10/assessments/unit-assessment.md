# Unit Assessment — Unit 05: Addition Stories Within 10 (Session 16)

**Format:** adult-led, oral/manipulative/drawing responses, about 20
minutes. The adult reads every task aloud. The learner may use
counters, a ten frame, or drawings for any task. Solutions and
scoring guidance are in the [answer key](../answer-key.md), never on
this page.

**Readiness rule (adult only):** a task passes when the learner
shows both parts and states the correct total (or, for Tasks 5–6,
two distinct splits with correct sentences). 5 of 6 tasks passed
signals readiness for Unit 06. If Task 1 or 2 fails, reteach Lessons
01–02; if Task 3 fails, reteach Lesson 03; if Task 5 or 6 fails,
reteach Lesson 05 — before starting Unit 06, whatever the total.

## Task 1 — Putting together (Objective 1)

"3 yellow ducks and 5 white ducks swim together. How many ducks are
swimming altogether?" Show the parts, count all, say the sentence.

## Task 2 — Adding to (Objective 2)

"7 beads are on a string. 2 more beads are added. How many beads are
on the string now?" Show the start group and the newcomers, count,
say the sentence.

## Task 3 — Number sentence (Objective 3)

"4 bees buzz. 4 more bees join them." Act it out, say the sentence,
and read it back as the adult writes it, pointing at each part.

## Task 4 — Solving to 10 (Objective 4)

"2 red cars and 8 blue cars park together. How many cars are there
in all?" Solve it with counters, ten frame, or a drawing — your
choice — and check by counting all.

## Task 5 — Parts of 5 (Objective 5)

"Show me two different ways to split 5 into parts. Say each split as
a sentence." Then: "Tell me a short story for one of your splits."

## Task 6 — Parts of 6 (Objective 5)

"Show me two different ways to split 6 into parts. Say each split as
a sentence." Then: "Tell me a short story for one of your splits."

## Celebration

After the assessment, the learner re-reads their Addition Story Book
([project](../project.md)) to the adult, one page of their choice,
as the unit's closing. The book, not the score, is what goes on the
shelf.
