# Unit 03 — Comparing Numbers and Quantities

**Grade:** Kindergarten · **Subject:** Mathematics · **Track weeks:** 11–14
**Backlog:** [issue #6](https://github.com/murderszn/open-tutor/issues/6), section U03
**Status:** validated draft (this unit); not educator-reviewed, not merged

Units 01–02 taught the learner to count sets to 10, say "how many?", and
read and write the numerals 0–10. This unit answers the next question:
**which group has more, which has fewer, and when are two groups the
same?** The learner compares first by matching objects one-to-one, then
by counting, then by reading written numerals — and learns the words
*more, fewer, same, greater than, less than,* and *equal to*. By the end,
the learner can compare any two groups or numerals from 1 to 10, find
one more and one fewer, and make two groups equal.

## Essential question

How do we know which group has more — and how can we prove it, not just
guess it?

## Prerequisites

- Units 01–02: touch-count sets to 10 with one-to-one correspondence;
  state the total when asked "how many?"; count out a requested number
  1–10; name, write, and match numerals 0–10
- Rote count sequence to at least 10, in order
- Can point, move counters, and draw simple marks (adult scribes words)

No comparison vocabulary is assumed — this unit teaches *more, fewer,
same, greater, less,* and *equal* from objects first, words second.

## Measurable objectives

By the end of the unit the learner will be able to (adult-observed):

1. Compare two groups of up to 10 objects **by matching** (pairing one
   object from each group) and say which group has more, which has
   fewer, or whether the groups are the same/equal.
2. Compare two groups of up to 10 objects **by counting**, state both
   counts, and say which count is greater, which is less, or whether
   the counts are equal.
3. Compare two written numerals from 1 to 10 and say which numeral is
   greater, which is less, or whether they are equal — without needing
   objects.
4. Find **one more** and **one fewer** than any number from 1 to 10
   (one more than 10 and one fewer than 1 are extension only), and show
   the step on a number line or with counters.
5. **Make two groups equal** by adding to or taking away from one group,
   and explain the change ("I added 2, now both have 6 — they are
   equal").

## Vocabulary (taught with objects first, word second)

more · fewer · same · equal / equal to · greater than · less than ·
match / pair · leftover / extra · one more · one fewer · compare ·
number line

Note for the adult: *more* and *greater than* mean the same comparison
here; *fewer* and *less than* likewise. This unit uses *more/fewer* for
groups of things and *greater/less* for numbers and numerals, and
accepts either word when the comparison is right.

## Materials (whole unit)

- 20 identical counters (buttons, beans, or blocks) plus a second set of
  20 in another color for matching work
- Numeral cards 1–10 (adult-made, large, dark marker) and a number line
  1–10 on the floor or a strip (tape + paper squares work)
- Two trays, bowls, or placemats ("Group A" and "Group B" zones)
- Paper, crayons/markers, dot stickers (optional)
- The unit's generated picture:
  [two rows of apples](assets/two-rows-apples-5-and-8.png) — see
  [assets/README.md](assets/README.md) for its caption, alt text,
  and text-only alternative
- A stuffed-animal or doll "judge" for the project fair (optional, fun)

All tasks are oral, pointing, drawing, or manipulative; the adult
scribes any words. Sessions are 15–20 minutes with a movement break
built in.

## For the guiding adult

Comparison is a reasoning skill, not a guessing game. Always ask "how
do you know?" and accept matching, counting, or number-line evidence —
any proof beats a lucky guess. Never let size fool the lesson: a group
of 4 big blocks does **not** have more than a group of 7 small buttons
unless it counts more. Spread objects out and scrunch them together on
purpose (Lesson 02) so the learner trusts counting over looking. Keep
completed learner work, names, photos, schedules, and grades in private
storage — never in this public repository.
