# Formative Quiz — Unit 03, Week 4 (Track Week 14)

**Format:** oral/observed, adult-scribed · **Time:** 15 minutes ·
**Session 15 of 16** · **Unit:** [U03](../README.md)

This is a low-stakes check, not a test: the adult watches, listens, and
notes what to re-teach before the unit assessment. The learner may use
counters, the number line, and matching for every item. Praise proof
("how do you know?") more than speed. Mark each item
**secure / developing / not yet** and note the exact response.

## Items

**Item 1 — Matching (Objective 1).** Groups of 3 and 6 counters.
"Match them. Which group has more? Which has fewer?"
*Secure: pairs one-to-one, names the 6-group more and the 3-group
fewer, points to leftovers. Developing: correct call without pointing
to proof. Not yet: wrong side named, or no matching attempted.*

**Item 2 — Counting (Objective 2).** Groups of 8 and 5 counters,
scattered. "Count both groups. Tell me both numbers. Which is greater?
Which is less?"
*Secure: both counts accurate, both numbers stated, greater/less
correct. Developing: one count slip, self-corrected, comparison
correct. Not yet: comparison from wrong or missing numbers.*

**Item 3 — Numerals (Objective 3).** Cards 4 and 9, number line
available, counters put away. "Which numeral is greater? Which is
less?"
*Secure: greater and less both correct, line position cited or pointed
to. Developing: one of the two correct. Not yet: neither, or the pair
read backwards.*

**Item 4 — Equal numerals (Objective 3).** Cards 7 and 7. "Greater,
less, or equal?"
*Secure: "equal" with no hesitation about sameness. Developing: says
"the same" but needs a prompt to say "equal." Not yet: names one card
greater.*

**Item 5 — One more / one fewer (Objective 4).** "Start at 6. What is
one more? What is one fewer?" (Number line allowed.)
*Secure: both answers correct. Developing: one of the two correct.
Not yet: neither, or direction reversed.*

**Item 6 — Make equal (Objective 5).** Groups of 2 and 5. "Make these
groups equal by adding to the smaller group. How many did you add?"
*Secure: adds to the smaller group until equal and states how many
were added. Developing: makes equal with help counting the added
counters. Not yet: adds to the wrong group, or cannot reach equal.*

**Item 7 — Picture (Objectives 2, 3).** Show the unit picture
([two rows of apples](../assets/two-rows-apples-5-and-8.png)). "Count
each row. Which row is greater? How many apples would you add to the
top row to make the rows equal?"
*Secure: both rows counted accurately, greater row correct, added
amount correct. Developing: counts right but the added amount is off
by one. Not yet: comparison from looks, not counts.*

**Item 8 — Explain your reasoning (Objectives 1–2).** Groups of 10 and
4, spread-out 4 vs. bunched 10. "Which group has more? How do you
know? Don't guess from how they look."
*Secure: correct group named with a counting or matching proof.
Developing: correct group named, proof vague or prompted. Not yet:
answer from looks alone, even if it happens to be right.*

## Scoring and next steps

- **7–8 secure:** proceed to the unit assessment (Session 16) with
  confidence.
- **5–6 secure:** re-teach the missed items only (use their lessons'
  guided practice), then proceed.
- **0–4 secure:** do not rush to the assessment. Re-run
  [comparison stations](../practice.md#session-e-comparison-stations),
  re-teach the missed objectives at numbers 5 and under, and use a
  flexible catch-up session before Session 16.

Record which items were missed: the pattern (matching vs. counting vs.
numerals vs. one-more) tells the adult exactly what to re-teach. A
counting slip that the learner self-corrects counts as secure.
