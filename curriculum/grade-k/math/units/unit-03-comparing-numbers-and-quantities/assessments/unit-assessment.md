# Unit Assessment — Unit 03: Comparing Numbers and Quantities

**Format:** performance tasks, oral/observed, adult-scribed ·
**Time:** 15–20 minutes · **Session 16 of 16** · **Unit:** [U03](../README.md)

The learner may use counters, the number line, and matching throughout
— this assesses comparison reasoning, not memory. Each task names its
objective(s). The adult records the learner's words verbatim where
possible ("how do you know?" answers matter as much as right answers).

## Task 1 — Match and compare (Objective 1)

Groups of 4 and 9 counters. "Match them one-to-one. Which group has
more? Which has fewer? Show me the leftovers."
*Record: pairs correctly? More/fewer named correctly? Leftovers
pointed to?*

## Task 2 — Count and compare (Objective 2)

Groups of 6 and 10 counters, one scattered. "Count both groups. Tell me
both numbers. Which number is greater? Which is less?"
*Record: both counts accurate (keep-track used)? Both numbers stated?
Greater/less correct?*

## Task 3 — Numerals alone (Objective 3)

Cards, in this order: (8, 3), (5, 5), (2, 7). For each pair: "Which
numeral is greater? Which is less? Or are they equal?" Number line
allowed; counters put away.
*Record: how many of the 3 pairs fully correct?*

## Task 4 — One more, one fewer (Objective 4)

"Start at 8. One more? One fewer? Start at 1. One fewer? Start at 10.
One fewer?"
*Record: each answer. ("One more than 10" is not asked — it belongs to
Unit 04.)*

## Task 5 — Apple picture (Objectives 2, 3)

Show the unit picture
([two rows of apples](../assets/two-rows-apples-5-and-8.png)).
"Count each row. Which row is greater? Which row is less? How many
apples would you add to the top row to make the rows equal?"
*Record: both row counts, greater/less calls, added amount.*

## Task 6 — Make equal two ways (Objective 5)

Groups of 3 and 8. "First, make them equal by adding to the smaller
group. How many did you add? Now rebuild them as 3 and 8 and make them
equal by taking away from the bigger group. How many did you take
away?"
*Record: both final equal counts and both amounts moved.*

## Task 7 — The trick question (Objectives 1, 2)

4 big blocks spread far apart vs. 7 small beans bunched together.
"Which group has more? How do you know? Do not guess from how they
look."
*Record: group named, and whether the proof was counting, matching, or
looks alone. An answer from looks alone is not secure, even if it
happens to be right — re-ask "how do you know?" once.*

## Scoring guidance

| Task | Secure (2) | Developing (1) | Not yet (0) |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 — match and compare | Pairs correctly, more/fewer correct, leftovers shown | Correct call, proof vague or prompted | Wrong side, or no matching |
| 2 — count and compare | Both counts accurate, both stated, greater/less correct | One self-corrected slip, comparison correct | Wrong numbers, or comparison from one number only |
| 3 — numerals alone | 3 of 3 pairs fully correct | 2 of 3 pairs fully correct | 1 or 0 pairs correct |
| 4 — one more, one fewer | All four answers correct | 3 of 4 correct | 2 or fewer correct |
| 5 — apple picture | Both rows counted accurately, greater/less correct, added amount correct | Counts right, added amount off by one | Comparison from looks, not counts |
| 6 — make equal two ways | Both ways reach equal, both amounts stated correctly | One way correct, or amounts need prompting | Cannot reach equal either way |
| 7 — trick question | Correct group with counting or matching proof | Correct group, proof prompted | Looks-only answer, or wrong group |

**Totals:** 12–14 = secure — begin Unit 04. 8–11 = developing — re-teach
the lowest tasks (see
[teacher guide](../teacher-guide.md#catch-up-guidance)), then re-check
those tasks only. 0–7 = not yet — repeat the unit's core sessions
(Lessons 01–03 + Practice A/B/C) before Unit 04; use a flexible track
week.

Do not report this assessment as standardized or validated — it is a
teacher-made, adult-observed performance check for this unit's
objectives.
