# Practice, Game, and Review Sessions — Unit 03

Named sessions referenced by the [16-session pacing](README.md#16-session-pacing-4-weeks--4-sessions-15-20-minutes-each).
Each runs 15–20 minutes. The adult leads, watches, and scribes; the learner
responds orally, by pointing, drawing, or moving objects.

---

## Session A — Matching partners

*(Pacing: Session 2 — after Lesson 01; repeated Session 4.)*

**Goal:** compare groups to 10 by matching and name more/fewer/same.

**Setup:** two trays, 20 counters in two colors.

**Play:**
1. The adult builds two groups (e.g., 4 red, 7 blue). The learner pairs
   them one-to-one, pushing partners together.
2. The learner announces: "Blue has more. Red has fewer." (or "the
   same!") and points to the leftovers — or their absence.
3. Repeat with 5 new pairs: (2, 2), (9, 5), (1, 10), (6, 6), (8, 3).
4. Swap roles: the learner builds the groups, the adult "guesses" wrong
   on purpose once ("the spread-out group must have more!"), and the
   learner proves the adult wrong by matching.

**Watch for:** deciding from looks instead of matching; forgetting to
check the second group. Re-match slowly whenever it happens.

**Answer key:** [answer-key.md](answer-key.md#practice-session-a).

## Session B — Count-and-compare trays

*(Pacing: Session 6 — after Lesson 02; repeated Session 8.)*

**Goal:** count two groups, state both numbers, and compare.

**Setup:** two trays, counters, numeral cards 1–10.

**Practice:**
1. The adult fills two trays (start: (5, 8), (3, 3), (10, 6), (2, 9)).
2. The learner counts each tray aloud, places the numeral card for
   each count beside its tray, and says: "___ is greater than ___" or
   "They are equal."
3. Scattered round: one tray scattered, one lined up — the learner uses
   the move-aside keep-track strategy, then compares.
4. Mystery round: the adult counts one tray behind a screen and
   announces its number; the learner counts the visible tray and
   decides without seeing the hidden one.

**Watch for:** stating only one number before deciding; counting the
same counter twice in scattered groups. Insist on both numbers.

**Answer key:** [answer-key.md](answer-key.md#practice-session-b).

## Session C — Numeral compare card game

*(Pacing: Session 10 — after Lesson 03.)*

**Goal:** compare written numerals 1–10 quickly and accurately.

**Setup:** numeral cards 1–10, shuffled, split into two face-down piles
(adult's and learner's). A number line 1–10 stays visible.

**Play ("Compare War"):**
1. Both flip a top card at the same time.
2. The learner names both numerals, points to them on the line, and
   says which is greater, which is less, or "equal!" (equal = both keep
   their cards; otherwise the greater card wins both).
3. Play 10 rounds. The player with more cards at the end wins — and
   both players count their cards to prove it (a final comparison!).
4. Close-pair bonus: if the cards differ by exactly 1 (e.g., 6 and 7),
   the round is worth double — close comparisons deserve celebrating.

**Watch for:** naming the first-flipped card as greater regardless of
value; guessing on close pairs instead of checking the line.

**Answer key:** [answer-key.md](answer-key.md#practice-session-c).

## Session D — Number-line walk: one more, one fewer

*(Pacing: folded into Lessons 04–05 review and Session 8/15 re-teaching
as needed; also used as a warm-up any session.)*

**Goal:** find one more and one fewer fluently, with direction sense.

**Setup:** floor number line 1–10 (tape + paper squares), a token.

**Practice:**
1. The adult calls a start number; the learner stands on it, hops right
   ("one more!"), then back, then hops left ("one fewer!"), naming each
   landing number.
2. Round of 8 starts: 4, 9, 2, 7, 1, 10, 5, 3 — one more and one fewer
   for each (for 10: one more is "past our line"; for 1: one fewer is
   0).
3. Reverse hops: "I landed on 8 by hopping right. Where did I start?"
   (7.) Three reverse riddles.
4. Hop-and-compare: "Stand on 6. Hop right — is your new number
   greater or less than 6?" (Greater — one more is always greater.)

**Watch for:** hopping the wrong direction; treating 10's "one more"
as 0 or 1. Correct direction errors with the chant "right is more,
left is fewer."

**Answer key:** [answer-key.md](answer-key.md#practice-session-d).

## Session E — Comparison stations

*(Pacing: Session 15 — review week, before the formative quiz.)*

**Goal:** revisit every comparison method in one circuit; surface the
highest-need idea for re-teaching.

**Setup:** four stations (2–3 minutes each, adult moves with the
learner).

**Stations:**
1. **Matching station:** 6 vs. 9 counters — match, name more/fewer.
2. **Counting station:** scattered 7 vs. lined-up 10 — count both,
   state both numbers, compare.
3. **Numeral station:** cards 4 and 8 — greater/less/equal, line
   position as proof.
4. **Make-equal station:** groups of 3 and 7 — make them equal two ways
   (add 4 to the 3-group; take 4 from the 7-group) and say "now they
   are equal."

**Watch for:** whichever station stalls is the re-teach target for the
rest of Session 15 and for Session 16 planning — note it in the
[teacher guide](teacher-guide.md).

**Answer key:** [answer-key.md](answer-key.md#practice-session-e).

## Session F — More-or-fewer movement game

*(Pacing: Sessions 3 and 7 — game days.)*

**Goal:** make comparison vocabulary automatic through the body.

**Setup:** open floor space; two signs (adult-scribed): "MORE" and
"FEWER" on opposite walls. Counters for the count version.

**Play (pick 2–3 rounds per session):**
1. **Matching version (Session 3):** the adult shows two groups; the
   learner runs to the MORE wall, points at the group with more, and
   shouts "that one has more!" Then runs to FEWER and points at the
   other group. Equal groups = stand in the middle, arms out: "the
   same!"
2. **Count version (Session 7):** same game, but the learner counts
   both groups aloud first, then runs to the wall matching the bigger
   number.
3. **Numeral version (bonus, after Lesson 03):** the adult holds up two
   numeral cards; the learner runs to MORE if the first card is
   greater, FEWER if it is less, middle if equal.
4. **Song round:** sing to any familiar tune: "More means bigger,
   fewer means small, same means equal — we compared them all!" while
   marching to the correct wall.

**Watch for:** running before counting (Session 7 version) — the rule
is "count first, run second," every time.

**Answer key:** [answer-key.md](answer-key.md#practice-session-f).
