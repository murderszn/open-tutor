# Practice Sessions — Unit 04: Teen Numbers and Ten Frames

**Unit:** [U04](README.md) · **Sessions:** 15–20 minutes each, adult-led,
oral/manipulative/drawing responses. The adult scribes any words.

Each session below names its activity and its success criterion, as
the pacing table requires. Repeat a session with fresh numbers whenever
the learner needs more practice — never repeat it as a punishment.

## Session A — Ten-frame fill (Session 2; repeat in Session 4)

**Activity:** the adult says a number from 0 to 10; the learner fills
a ten frame to match it, top row first, then says "full" or "not
full," and, if not full, counts the empty boxes.

**Rounds (adult's choice of order):** 5, 10, 3, 8, 0, 7, 10, 2.

**Success criterion:** the learner fills top row first in at least 6
of 8 rounds and states the empty-box count correctly in at least 6 of
8 rounds. A full frame is named "ten" without one-by-one counting.

**Re-teach move:** if the learner fills bottom row first, re-model
Lesson 01, Worked example 1, with the learner's hand guided for the
first two counters of the top row.

## Session B — Build 11–15 (Session 6)

**Activity:** the adult says a number from 11 to 15; the learner builds
it with a full frame + extras (or a bundle + loose sticks) and says
the sentence "Ten and ___ more make ___."

**Rounds:** 12, 15, 11, 14, 13, 12, 15.

**Success criterion:** at least 5 of 7 builds correct, each with the
sentence said aloud. Accept either the frame or the bundle picture.

**Re-teach move:** if the learner builds 11–15 by counting one by one
from 1 and loses track, cover the extras, point at the full frame,
and say "this part is already ten — now count only the extras."

## Session C — Build 16–19 and order (Session 10)

**Activity (two parts):**

1. The adult says a number from 16 to 19; the learner builds it and
   says the sentence (rounds: 18, 16, 19, 17, 18).
2. The learner lays numeral cards 11–19 in order on the number line,
   then the adult removes one card (for example, 16) and the learner
   names the missing teen and rebuilds it.

**Success criterion:** at least 4 of 5 builds correct; the ordered
strip is correct; the missing teen is named correctly on the first or
second try.

**Re-teach move:** if 16 and 19 swap, re-say the names slowly —
"six-teen, seven-teen, eight-teen, nine-teen" — while pointing at each
build's extras.

## Session D — Writing teens (extra practice; use in Sessions 4 or 8 if needed)

**Activity:** the adult builds a teen number; the learner writes the
numeral, saying "ten first" before writing the 1. Then the reverse:
the adult writes a numeral; the learner builds it.

**Rounds (build → write):** 14, 17, 12, 19, 11. **Rounds (write →
build):** 15, 13, 18, 16.

**Success criterion:** at least 7 of 9 numerals correct with the ten
first. Reversals (41 for 14) are corrected on the spot with the build
as referee, not counted as failures on the first occurrence.

## Session E — Midyear stations (Session 15, with the formative quiz)

**Activity:** four stations, about 4 minutes each. The learner rotates
with the adult.

1. **Count-and-build station:** count out 17 counters into frames
   (full frame + 7 extras) and say the sentence.
2. **Compare station (Unit 03 retrieval):** two groups, 7 buttons and
   9 buttons — "Which has more? Which has fewer? How do you know?"
   (Matching or counting both accepted.)
3. **Write-it station:** dictation — write 13, 18, 11, 16.
4. **Partner station:** ten frames with 2, 8, 5, and 6 counters — name
   each partner that makes 10 (8, 2, 5, 4).

**Success criterion:** at least 3 of 4 stations completed with the
key answer correct; any missed station becomes a Session 16 re-teach
item (see the [teacher guide](teacher-guide.md)).

## Session F — Fill-the-frame race (Session 3, game)

**Activity:** two ten-frame mats (one for the learner, one for the
adult, who plays slowly and sometimes "loses"). The adult calls a
number 0–10; both race to fill their frame correctly, top row first.
First correct frame wins the round. Play 6 rounds. The learner may
call the number for the last two rounds.

**Success criterion:** the learner fills correctly, top row first, in
at least 4 of 6 rounds. Speed is never the criterion — a slow correct
frame beats a fast wrong one, and the adult says so.

## Session G — Ten-and-some-more sort (Session 7, game)

**Activity:** the adult prepares 8 small bags (or trays) with 11–19
counters each, labeled only with a letter (A–H). The learner opens a
bag, builds its number with frames, says the sentence, and sorts the
bag into "teens with few extras" (11–14) or "teens with many extras"
(15–19). Use at least one bag for each of 11, 13, 15, 17, and 19.

**Success criterion:** at least 6 of 8 bags built and sorted
correctly. Sorting errors are fixed by recounting the extras, not by
appealing to the label.

## Review sessions (Sessions 4 and 8)

Session 4 repeats Session A with fresh numbers (10, 6, 9, 4, 10, 1)
plus a count-to-20 warm-up: count aloud together to 20, then count
from 10 to 20 ("start at ten — ten, eleven, twelve…").

Session 8 re-teaches the highest-need idea from Lessons 01–02: if
"full = ten" is shaky, replay Session A; if 11–15 sentences are shaky,
replay Session B with 11, 13, 15 only. End with both exit checks from
Lessons 01–02, re-administered once.
