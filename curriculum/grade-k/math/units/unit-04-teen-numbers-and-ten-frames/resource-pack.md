# Resource Pack — Unit 04: Teen Numbers and Ten Frames

## Summary

**Goal:** give the guiding adult free, no-account routes to support
kindergarten teen numbers — full ten frames, composing and
decomposing 11–19 as ten ones and some more ones, writing teen
numerals ten-first, and partners that make 10 — plus songs and
interactive practice a 5-year-old will actually enjoy.
**Age fit:** kindergarten (about 5–6); all items are visual, musical,
or hands-on, with no reading required of the learner.

1. **"Teen Numbers: Ten Ones and Some More Ones" — IQ Learning USA**
   — a kindergarten lesson teaching that every number from 11 to 19
   is made of ten ones and some more ones (14 is ten ones and four
   more ones), with ten-frame builds and equations like 14 = 10 + 4;
   aligned to K.NBT.A.1.
   `https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=84G4aaUFccM`
   *Why:* directly teaches Lessons 01–03 content — full frames,
   ten-and-some-more sentences, and equations; supports Practice
   Sessions A, B, and C.
2. **"Teen Numbers for Kids: Never Mix Up 14 and 41!" — Mimi the
   Fairy** — a story lesson for ages 4–6 about why fourteen tricks
   the ears (we say the four first but write the ten first), using a
   "full jar is one ten" image and a "tens room first" rule.
   `https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=V62MZA5Ylzw`
   *Why:* directly teaches Lesson 04's reversal trap — ten first when
   writing teen numerals; supports Practice Session D.
3. **Ten-frame and teen-number songs (search link).**
   Search: `https://www.youtube.com/results?search_query=kindergarten+ten+frame+song+teen+numbers`
   *Why:* find a short captioned song for Lessons 01–02's ten-frame
   and ten-and-some-more vocabulary; preview for accuracy (words
   only — no symbols needed at this grade) before assigning.

## Access notes

Every core objective is teachable offline with ten-frame mats and
counters — no video or app is required. All web items above are free;
items 1, 3, and 4 need no account for their core content. Videos
supplement teaching; they never replace the adult-led frame work.
Preview every video for ads, comments, and age fit before showing it,
and keep the learner's use adult-guided throughout.
