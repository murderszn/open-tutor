# Formative Quiz — Unit 04: Teen Numbers and Ten Frames

**Unit:** [U04](../README.md) · **Session 15 of 16** · **Format:**
oral/observed, adult-administered, 15–20 minutes. The learner answers
by pointing, moving counters, drawing, or speaking; the adult scribes
any words. **This page contains student questions only — answers are
in the separate [answer key](../answer-key.md).**

**How to score (adult only):** 8–10 correct = secure, move on after
Session 16 planning; 5–7 = re-teach the missed pattern in Session 16
(see the [teacher guide](../teacher-guide.md)); 0–4 = replay the
missed lessons before the unit assessment. Items 9–10 are midyear
retrieval from Units 01–03, not new Unit 04 content.

1. **Full frame.** The adult shows a full ten frame. "How many — and
   did you count one by one?"
2. **Build 13.** "Build thirteen with a frame and extras. Say the
   sentence."
3. **Name the build.** The adult builds a full frame + 8 extras.
   "What number is this? How do you know?"
4. **Write it.** The adult says "seventeen." The learner writes the
   numeral (no build shown first).
5. **Partner.** The adult shows a ten frame with 7 counters. "How many
   empty boxes? What number goes with 7 to make 10?"
6. **Order.** The adult gives numeral cards 12, 19, 15, 11, 17 shuffled.
   "Put these teen numbers in order, smallest to largest."
7. **Picture.** Show
   [ten and four apples](../assets/ten-and-four-apples.png).
   "Count the rows the smart way. How many apples in all? How do you
   know?"
8. **Missing teen.** The adult lays out 14, 15, 16, 18, 19 in order.
   "Which teen number is hiding?"
9. **Retrieval — compare (Unit 03).** Two groups: 7 buttons and 9
   buttons. "Which group has more? Which has fewer? Prove it."
10. **Retrieval — count out (Units 01–02).** "Count out 8 counters for
    me. Now count out 6 more. How many do you have in all?"
