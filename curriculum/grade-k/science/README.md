# Kindergarten Science

Independent, grade-specific materials in the public OpenTutor curriculum library.
This track is a **draft in progress**: the audit and scope-and-sequence are delivered;
the eight units are planned, not yet written. See [scope-and-sequence.md](scope-and-sequence.md).

**Do not refer a learner to another grade track.** This folder is self-contained for
one kindergarten learner at a time; a guiding adult adapts pacing, materials, and
supports. Keep completed learner work, names, photos, schedules, and grades in
private storage — never in this public repository.

See also: [Grade K track](../README.md), [curriculum index](../../README.md).

## What this track covers

A full-year (about 36 weeks) kindergarten science course organized around
observation, pushes and pulls, materials and simple design, plant and animal
needs, weather patterns, sunlight and shade, and caring for the local
environment. Sessions are short (15–20 minutes, about three to four per week)
and built on hands-on investigation, drawing and dictating observations,
and adult-guided questioning — the way kindergarteners actually do science.

Every investigation is adult-supervised and age-appropriate, with
observation-only or simulation alternatives where a task would otherwise
involve a hazard (severe weather, heat, living creatures).

## Track objectives (measurable, adult-assessed)

By the end of the year the learner will be able to:

1. Observe carefully with the senses (and simple tools like a hand lens),
   describe what is noticed using words or drawings, and ask testable questions.
2. Plan and carry out simple push-and-pull investigations, and tell whether a
   design solution changes how an object moves as intended.
3. Compare everyday materials by observable properties and design a simple
   object or structure that solves a stated problem.
4. Use observations to describe patterns in what plants need to live and grow,
   and care for a growing plant over several weeks.
5. Use observations to describe patterns in what animals need to survive, and
   model how an animal's needs relate to where it lives.
6. Observe, record, and share local weather conditions over time, and describe
   daily and seasonal patterns.
7. Make observations to determine how sunlight warms Earth's surface, and
   design and build a simple structure that reduces the warming effect of
   sunlight on an area.
8. Communicate a solution that reduces the impact of people on the land, water,
   air, or living things in the local environment.

## Standards reference

Objectives reference the Next Generation Science Standards (NGSS) kindergarten
performance expectations (verified against the official framework; not a claim
of state adoption, accreditation, or alignment certification). Full crosswalk in
[scope-and-sequence.md](scope-and-sequence.md#standards-crosswalk):

- Motion and Stability: K-PS2-1, K-PS2-2
- Energy: K-PS3-1, K-PS3-2
- From Molecules to Organisms: K-LS1-1
- Earth's Systems: K-ESS2-1, K-ESS2-2
- Earth and Human Activity: K-ESS3-1, K-ESS3-2, K-ESS3-3
- Engineering Design (K–2 band): K-2-ETS1-1, K-2-ETS1-2, K-2-ETS1-3

## Structure

- [Scope and sequence](scope-and-sequence.md) — audit, standards crosswalk,
  36-week pacing with weekly goals, session model, materials, safety,
  accessibility.
- `units/` — eight units, planned (not yet written):
  1. Observing, describing, and asking questions
  2. Pushes, pulls, and moving objects
  3. Comparing materials and designing simple solutions
  4. Plant needs and growth observations
  5. Animal needs, habitats, and patterns
  6. Weather observations and daily patterns
  7. Sunlight, shade, and warming surfaces
  8. Caring for local environments and engineering solutions

## For the guiding adult

Kindergarten science is concrete: every concept first lives in things the child
can touch, move, plant, pour, and watch over time. Read the scope-and-sequence
before starting; it lists safe household materials, safety boundaries, and the
diagnostic, catch-up, and review weeks that keep pacing humane. Each future
unit will include its own teacher guide and answer key.
