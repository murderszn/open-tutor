# Formative Quiz — Unit 05 (Week 22, Session 15)

**Format:** oral and observed; the adult reads each item, the learner
answers by speaking, pointing, or drawing; the adult scribes. 15–20
minutes. This quiz checks progress — it is not a grade. Any item the
learner misses becomes a re-teach prompt in Session 16, not a failure.

**Materials:** the pond illustration
[`../assets/pond-habitat-scene.png`](../assets/pond-habitat-scene.png),
animal cards, habitat mats (pond, forest, burrow), the four need cards.

1. **Name the needs.** "Name the four things every animal needs."
2. **Pond picture.** Show the pond illustration. "Point to the frog.
   Where does the frog live? What does the pond give the frog?"
3. **Food pattern.** "What does a deer eat? Where does a deer live?
   Why does it live there?"
4. **Pond needs.** "Name one need the pond gives the duck, and one
   need the pond gives the fish."
5. **Change and evidence.** "A squirrel buries nuts in the ground.
   What changed? What need does it meet? How do you know?"
6. **Missing need.** "A turtle's log is gone, and there is nowhere to
   rest out of the water. Which need is missing? What can a person do
   to help safely?"

**Scoring guidance (adult only; see the
[answer key](../answer-key.md#formative-quiz) for full solutions):**
5–6 items correct or with one prompt = on track; 3–4 = re-teach the
missed objective with the matching practice session before the unit
assessment; 0–2 = re-teach Lessons 01–02 and Practice Session A, then
re-check with items 1, 2, and 4 before Session 16.
