# Unit Assessment — Unit 05 (Week 22, Session 16)

**Format:** five performance tasks, oral and observed; the adult reads
each task, the learner responds by speaking, pointing, drawing, or
building; the adult scribes and scores. 20–30 minutes across two short
sittings if needed. This assessment is a check of the unit's objectives
— it is not a grade and is not standardized or validated.

**Materials:** the pond illustration
[`../assets/pond-habitat-scene.png`](../assets/pond-habitat-scene.png),
need cards, animal cards, habitat mats, the learner's habitat model
from the project (or a fresh tray and materials if the model was not
kept).

## Task 1 — Needs sort (Objective 1)

Sort eight cards into four groups: food, water, shelter, air. Cards:
apple, leaves, cup of water, pond, nest, burrow, wavy air lines, a ball
(distractor — "not a need").

**Ready when:** at least six of the eight cards are placed correctly
and the learner can name all four needs. (The ball must land in "not a
need" or be named as not a need.)

## Task 2 — Pond picture talk (Objectives 1, 2)

Look at the pond illustration. Name two animals in the picture and, for
each, name one need the pond meets for it.

**Ready when:** two animals are named and each is matched to a correct
need (duck–water/food/shelter; fish–water/food; frog–shelter/food;
turtle–shelter/water).

## Task 3 — Habitat model show-and-tell (Objective 3)

Show the habitat model (or build a quick tabletop model for a duck).
Explain, part by part, how the model gives the animal food, water,
shelter, and air or space.

**Ready when:** the learner points to a food part, a water part, and a
shelter part, and says the top is open for air (or the animal has room
to move), with at most one prompt.

## Task 4 — Change and evidence (Objective 4)

The adult shows the squirrel card and the bird-nest card, one at a
time. For each: "What changed? What need does it meet? How do you
know?"

**Ready when:** for at least one card, the learner gives all three
parts (change, need, evidence: "I saw…"). For the second card, two of
three parts is enough.

## Task 5 — Missing need (Objective 5)

"A pet goldfish has a bowl with no water in it. What is missing? What
should a person do?" Then: "A wild duck lives in a pond. Should you
take the duck home? Why not?"

**Ready when:** the learner names water as the missing need and a safe
helping action for the pet (an adult puts clean water — and food — in
the bowl), and says wild animals are left alone (watch from far away,
tell an adult).

## Overall readiness

- **Ready for Unit 06:** Tasks 1–3 ready, and at least one of Tasks 4–5
  ready. Note any "with help" task in the journal and re-visit it in
  the flexible review weeks.
- **Re-teach first:** If Task 1 or Task 2 is not ready, re-teach
  Lessons 01–02 with Practice Session A before Unit 06. If Task 3 is
  not ready, re-run Project Part 2 with Practice Session D. If Task 4
  is not ready, re-teach Lesson 05 with Practice Session E.
