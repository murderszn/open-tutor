# Resource Pack — Unit 05: Animal Needs, Habitats, and Patterns

**Goal and age fit:** Support a kindergarten learner (age ~5) in
naming the four things animals need, finding patterns in what different
animals eat, matching animals to their habitats, building a habitat
model, and arguing with evidence that animals change their surroundings
to meet a need — all through short, play-based, adult-guided sessions.
Every core objective below has at least one free, no-account route.

## Focused queries

1. `kindergarten animals need food water shelter air lesson`
2. `kindergarten animal habitats pond forest burrow sorting activity`
3. `K-LS1-1 K-ESS3-1 K-ESS2-2 kindergarten animals needs habitats patterns`
4. `kindergarten squirrel buries food bird builds nest change environment evidence`
