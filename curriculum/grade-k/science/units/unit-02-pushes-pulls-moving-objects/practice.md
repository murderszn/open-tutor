# Practice, game, and review sessions — Unit 02

Named sessions referenced by the [unit pacing](README.md#16-session-pacing-4-weeks--4-sessions-15-20-minutes-each).
Each runs 15–20 minutes, adult-supervised; the adult scribes all words the
learner dictates.

## Session A — Push/pull scavenger hunt (Session 2)

**Success criterion:** the learner finds and correctly names one push and
one pull in the room.

1. Walk the room together. The adult models first: pushes the door —
   "Push! It moved *away* from me." Pulls a drawer open — "Pull! It came
   *closer*."
2. The learner hunts: "Find something you can **push**." (Chair, toy, door,
   ball.) Act it out, say "push."
3. "Find something you can **pull**." (Drawer, wagon, sleeve, string toy.)
   Act it out, say "pull."
4. Round 2 (faster): the adult points at five things in quick succession;
   the learner calls "push" or "pull" for each. Aim for 4 of 5.
5. Journal: the learner draws one push and one pull found today; the adult
   labels them with the dictated words.

**Re-teach pointer:** if pushes and pulls mix up, return to
[Lesson 01](lessons/lesson-01-push-or-pull.md)'s body motions (push air
away, pull sleeve close) before hunting again.

## Session B — Start-and-stop game (Session 3)

**Success criterion:** the learner starts a ball rolling with a push and
stops it with a push, naming both.

1. On the floor: "Push the ball to me." (A push **starts** it.)
2. The adult rolls it back: "Now **stop** it. What will you use?" (A hand —
   a push **stops** it.) Let the learner discover the stopping push.
3. Trade roles three times: starter and stopper.
4. Challenge: "Start the ball with a **gentle** push so I can stop it
   easily." Then: "Start it with a **strong** push — harder to stop!"
   (Previews Lesson 02's strength idea; the stopper may need two hands.)
5. Exit: "Name the two jobs a push did today." (Started the ball; stopped
   the ball.)

**Safety:** stopper keeps hands low and fingers together; no catching
fast-rolling balls against a wall.

## Session C — Test-and-measure (Session 7)

**Success criterion:** the learner marks gentle-push and strong-push stops
and says which set went farther.

1. Set the ramp exactly as in
   [Lesson 02](lessons/lesson-02-gentle-strong-pushes.md). "Same ramp, same
   ball — check!" (The learner points to the ramp and the ball: "same,
   same.")
2. Three **gentle** pushes; the adult marks each stop with tape. "Look at
   your three marks — close together or far apart?" (Close.)
3. Three **strong** pushes; mark each. "Which set is farther from the
   start?" (The strong set.)
4. The learner draws the two sets of marks in the journal (two short lines,
   two long lines); the adult scribes: "gentle = short way, strong = long
   way."
5. Error check (oral): "What if I used a different ball for the strong
   pushes — would that be fair?" (No — the ball changed. Previews
   [Lesson 04](lessons/lesson-04-same-test-three-times.md).)

## Session D — Share-and-review circle (Sessions 4, 8)

**Success criterion:** the learner shows one thing learned this week and
re-passes the week's exit checks.

1. The adult shares first, imperfectly ("I pushed the car and it went…
   somewhere. I forgot to mark it!").
2. The learner demonstrates: Session 4 — one push and one pull (Lesson 01
   exit); Session 8 — a gentle and a strong push plus which went farther
   (Lesson 02 exit).
3. "I notice / I wonder" from Unit 01: one notice about this week's
   testing, one wonder ("I wonder what happens with a steeper ramp" —
   saved for later units, not tested now).
4. Any missed exit check is re-taught right here with the lesson's guided
   practice, not carried as a failure.

## Session E — Course free-play and design (spare / catch-up)

Use in any review session that finishes early, or as a catch-up slot.

1. Free play on the Lesson 06 obstacle course with one rule: "Name every
   move before you do it."
2. "Invent a station": the learner adds one new station (a new push, pull,
   turn, or stop) and teaches it to the adult.
3. Journal: draw the invented station; the adult scribes the move name.
