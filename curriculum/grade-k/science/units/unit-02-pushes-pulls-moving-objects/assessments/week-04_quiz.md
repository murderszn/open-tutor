# Formative quiz — Unit 02 (week 4)

**Format:** oral and observed; the adult asks, the learner answers by
speaking, pointing, acting out, or drawing; the adult scribes. **Time:**
10–15 minutes. **Not graded** — this check tells the adult what to re-teach
before the unit assessment (Session 16). Use the ramp, ball, string toy,
and two targets.

**Scoring guide (for the adult):** 2 = shows the skill independently; 1 =
shows it with a prompt or choices; 0 = not yet — re-teach the named lesson.
The [answer key](../answer-key.md#week-04-quiz) gives acceptable responses.

## Questions

1. **Push or pull?** The adult pushes a toy car toward the learner, then
   pulls it back with the string. "Which move was the **push**? Which was
   the **pull**?" *(Objective 1; re-teach: Lesson 01.)*
2. **Start and stop.** "Push this ball so it rolls to me. Now — **stop**
   it before it reaches me. How did you stop it?" (Accept: "I pushed it" /
   "my hand pushed it.") *(Objective 1; re-teach: Lesson 01.)*
3. **Gentle or strong?** "Watch: which push will send the car **farther**
   — a gentle one or a strong one? Tell me first, then test it." *(Objective
   2; re-teach: Lesson 02.)*
4. **Pull direction.** "Pull the truck to the **red box**." Then: "Now
   pull it to the **blue box**. What changed?" (Accept: "the direction" /
   pointing to the new target.) *(Objective 3; re-teach: Lesson 03.)*
5. **Fair test.** "I want to test gentle vs. strong pushes. What two things
   must stay the **same**?" (Accept: the ball/car and the ramp/floor;
   "same toy, same hill.") *(Objective 4; re-teach: Lesson 04.)*
6. **Build a turn.** "Build something that makes this rolling ball
   **turn**. Roll it. Did it work the way you wanted?" *(Objective 5;
   re-teach: Lesson 05.)*
7. **Home moves.** "Name one **push** and one **pull** you can do at home."
   *(Objective 1; re-teach: Lesson 01.)*

## What the results mean

- **12–14:** secure — proceed to the unit assessment.
- **8–11:** mostly secure — re-teach the missed lessons' guided practice
  (Session 15 has time for this), then assess.
- **0–7:** re-teach whole lessons for missed objectives using the review
  sessions, and consider the track's flexible catch-up time before Unit 03.
  See the [teacher guide](../teacher-guide.md#readiness-and-reteaching).
