# Resource Pack — Unit 02: Pushes, Pulls, and Moving Objects

**Goal and age fit:** Support a kindergarten learner (age ~5) in naming
pushes and pulls, comparing gentle vs. strong pushes, pulling in different
directions, repeating a simple test three times, and building and improving
a bumper that turns a rolling ball — all through short, play-based,
adult-guided sessions. Every core objective below has at least one free,
no-account route.
