# Formative quiz — Unit 01 (week 4)

**Format:** oral and observed; the adult asks, the learner answers by
speaking, pointing, or drawing; the adult scribes. **Time:** 10–15 minutes.
**Not graded** — this check tells the adult what to re-teach before the unit
assessment (Session 16). Use a familiar object the learner has handled before
(a shell, leaf, or block) plus the unit illustration.

**Scoring guide (for the adult):** 2 = shows the skill independently; 1 =
shows it with a prompt or choices; 0 = not yet — re-teach the named lesson.
The [answer key](../answer-key.md#week-04-quiz) gives acceptable responses.

## Questions

1. **Three notices.** "Look at this object. Tell me three things you notice."
   *(Objective 1; re-teach: Lesson 01.)*
2. **Senses.** "Which senses did you use just now?" Accept any correct
   pairing (eyes/see, hands/feel, nose/smell, ears/hear). *(Objective 1;
   re-teach: Lesson 01.)*
3. **Closer look.** The adult hands the learner the hand lens (or invites
   nose-close looking). "Look closer. Tell me one new thing you could not see
   before." *(Objective 2; re-teach: Lesson 02.)*
4. **Describe.** "Describe this object so I could find it with my eyes
   closed — give me two details." *(Objective 4; re-teach: Lesson 03.)*
5. **Sort and name the rule.** The adult offers 6 mixed objects. "Sort these
   the way you want, then tell me your rule." *(Objective 3; re-teach:
   Lesson 04.)*
6. **Notice or wonder.** The adult says: "I notice this object is smooth. I
   wonder if it is heavy." Then: "Which of my sentences was something I saw,
   and which was a question?" *(Objective 5; re-teach: Lesson 05.)*
7. **Make your own.** "Now you try: give me one 'I notice…' and one 'I
   wonder…' about this object." *(Objective 5; re-teach: Lesson 05.)*

## What the results mean

- **12–14:** secure — proceed to the unit assessment.
- **8–11:** mostly secure — re-teach the missed lessons' guided practice
  (Session 15 has time for this), then assess.
- **0–7:** re-teach whole lessons for missed objectives using the review
  sessions, and consider the track's flexible catch-up time before Unit 02.
  See the [teacher guide](../teacher-guide.md#readiness-and-reteaching).
