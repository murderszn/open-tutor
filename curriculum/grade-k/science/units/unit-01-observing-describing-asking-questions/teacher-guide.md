# Teacher guide — Unit 01: Observing, Describing, and Asking Questions

For the guiding adult. Learner-facing pages are the lessons, practice,
project, and assessments; this guide and the
[answer key](answer-key.md) stay with the adult.

## How this unit works

This unit teaches the *practices* of science before any content unit does:
observe carefully, describe checkably, sort by what is observed, and separate
seeing from guessing. Every later unit (pushes and pulls, plants, weather)
assumes these moves, so the time spent here pays off all year. Sessions are
15–20 minutes; stop while the learner is still engaged rather than finishing
a page.

## Session-by-session guidance

| Session | Watch for | If it goes sideways |
|---|---|---|
| 1 (L1) | The learner names story details instead of seen details | Use the Lesson 01 redirect: "What do your eyes see?" Point together. |
| 2 (Practice A) | Drawings from imagination, not the object | Put the object next to the paper; draw one detail at a time together. |
| 3 (Practice B) | Wanting to peek in the bag | Let them peek *after* two touch details — the check is the reward. |
| 4 (Review) | Shyness about sharing | The adult shares first, imperfectly ("I only noticed two things"). |
| 5 (L2) | Lens treated as a toy | Slow ritual: "Eyes first, then lens, then tell me." Adult holds the lens at first. |
| 6 (Project 1) | Choosing an object that will not last (ice, a banana) | Steer to durable choices; explain scientists pick things they can revisit. |
| 7 (Play) | "The lens made lines!" | The Lesson 02 correction: the lines were already there. |
| 8 (Review) | Forgetting Lesson 01 language | Re-run one L1 guided task as the warm-up. |
| 9 (L3) | "It is nice/pretty" descriptions | The Lesson 03 redirect: "Can my eyes check 'nice'?" |
| 10 (Project 2) | Wonder is a statement ("I wonder it is old") | Recast together: "I wonder IF it is old — now it is a question." |
| 11 (L4) | Sorting by story ("my favorites") | "Can my eyes check 'favorite'? What can they check?" |
| 12 (L5) | Filing every sentence under "I notice" | Re-model with two exaggerated examples (see Lesson 05). |
| 13 (L6) | Rushing past stops | The stop sequence card (eye → lens → mouth → ?) enforces the pause. |
| 14 (Project 3) | Reluctance to share | Audience of one (a stuffed animal) counts. |
| 15 (Quiz) | Quiz anxiety | Call it "show me what you can do"; allow a second sitting. |
| 16 (Assessment) | New objects feel unfair | Remind the learner: "Scientists observe NEW things — that is the job." |

## Misconceptions to expect (and the fix that works)

1. **"I see it wants water / it is lonely."** (Lessons 01, 05) — The core
   confusion of the unit: feelings and stories filed as observations. Fix:
   "What did your senses tell you? The drooping leaf is the observation;
   'thirsty' is the guess. Both are allowed — in different columns."
2. **"The lens makes things."** (Lesson 02) — Tool/object confusion. Fix:
   "The lens helped your eyes find what was already there."
3. **"Describe means say the name."** (Lesson 03) — "It is an apple" is
   identification, not description. Fix: "Now tell me about the apple so I
   could find it."
4. **"I wonder it is…"** (Lesson 05) — Wonder followed by a statement. Fix:
   recast with "if/whether/why/how" together until the pattern sticks.

## Readiness and reteaching

**Ready to move on** looks like: three checkable notices unprompted (quiz Q1
or assessment Task 1a at score 2), one lens-found detail, two named sorting
rules, and a correctly filed notice/wonder pair. The unit assessment's
9–12 band is the formal bar.

**Reteach moves (use before repeating a whole lesson):**
- Objective 1 weak → one more Mystery-bag session (Practice B) + Lesson 01
  guided practice with the illustration.
- Objective 2 weak → Practice C with three different objects in one session;
  let the learner be the "lens teacher" for a stuffed animal.
- Objective 3 weak → Practice E with the adult sorting first (learner names
  the rule) before the learner sorts.
- Objective 4 weak → Lesson 03's description game with the adult deliberately
  giving weak descriptions for the learner to fix (error analysis is
  motivating at this age).
- Objective 5 weak → Practice F card sort daily for a week, 5 minutes;
  exaggerate the first examples ("I notice this table is made of cheese").

**When to use flexible time:** a 0–5 on the unit assessment, or two
objectives still at 0 after Session 16, means using the track's flexible
catch-up time before Unit 02. One shaky objective (score 1) can travel into
Unit 02 with a weekly 5-minute warm-up — Unit 02's investigations will
exercise it naturally.

## Safety and accessibility reminders

- All investigations are adult-supervised; the full boundaries are in the
  track [scope-and-sequence](../../scope-and-sequence.md) §8.
- Never taste unknown or found objects; taste only known safe foods with
  explicit adult permission.
- Sun rule: nobody looks at the sun, with or without the lens.
- Every check accepts oral, pointing, or drawing responses; the adult
  scribes. Non-visual observation (touch, hearing, smell) is first-class
  science here, not a consolation.
