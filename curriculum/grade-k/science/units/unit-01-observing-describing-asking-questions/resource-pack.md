# Resource Pack — Unit 01: Observing, Describing, and Asking Questions

**Goal and age fit:** Support a kindergarten learner (age ~5) in observing
with the senses, looking closer with simple tools, describing details, and
asking explorable questions — all through short, play-based, adult-guided
sessions. Every core objective below has at least one free, no-account route.

## Focused queries

1. `kindergarten science observation activities five senses`
2. `teaching kindergarteners "I notice I wonder" science`
3. `hand lens magnifier activities kindergarten close observation`
4. `observation vs inference early childhood science lesson`
