# Project — My Observation Journal

**Type:** multi-session investigation (runs across Sessions 6, 10, and 14 of
the unit pacing). **Time:** three 15–20-minute sessions plus sharing.
**Product:** a 3–4-page science journal — drawn and adult-scribed — that
records careful observations and genuine questions about one chosen object or
place.

## Driving question

"What can I discover about one thing by observing it carefully three times?"

## Materials

Science journal (stapled paper or notebook), crayons or markers, hand lens,
the chosen object (a leaf, shell, stone, toy, or houseplant — safe, familiar,
and available for all three sessions). The adult scribes all dictated words.

## Steps

### Part 1 — Choose and observe (Session 6)

1. The learner chooses one object to study for the whole project. ("Scientists
   often watch one thing for a long time.")
2. **Observe with the senses:** name or point to three things noticed. The
   adult scribes them on journal page 1 as "I notice…" statements.
3. **Draw** the object with at least two visible details.
4. Adult checks: are the notices checkable with the senses? If a guess snuck
   in, file it on the wonder side together (Lesson 05 repair in action).

### Part 2 — Look closer and wonder (Session 10)

1. **Re-observe** the same object. "What do you notice today that is the same?
   Anything different?"
2. **Use the hand lens** (or careful close looking): find and draw **one new
   detail** not recorded in Part 1. Dictate the "Without the lens… / With the
   lens…" pair; the adult scribes it on page 2.
3. **Ask:** dictate one genuine "I wonder…" question about the object. The
   adult writes it on page 3 and helps the learner mark whether it is a
   question we *could* explore by looking/testing or one we *cannot*
   (Lesson 05, both are allowed).

### Part 3 — Finish and share (Session 14)

1. The learner reviews all three pages: "Which observation are you most sure
   about? Which wonder is your favorite?"
2. **Draw a final portrait** of the object (page 4) that includes the new
   detail from Part 2 — the "scientist's best drawing."
3. **Share:** the learner presents one page to the adult (or family): reads
   or points through one "I notice", the lens detail, and the wonder
   question. The adult asks one follow-up: "How did you find that out?"

## Deliverables

- Journal pages 1–4: three "I notice" statements (Part 1), a lens-detail
  drawing with the with/without pair (Part 2), one "I wonder" question with
  its explorable/not-explorable mark (Part 2), and a final portrait (Part 3).
- A 1–2-minute share: one notice, the lens detail, one wonder.

## Rubric

| Criterion | 2 — shows skill | 1 — developing | 0 — not yet observed |
|---|---|---|---|
| Careful observing | Three checkable notices across Parts 1–2 | Two notices, or one is a guess | Notices are guesses or copied |
| Tool use | Names/draws one detail found only with closer looking | Closer look attempted; detail unclear | No closer look attempted |
| Describing | Final portrait shows the new detail; drawing matches object | Drawing shows effort; detail missing | Drawing unrelated to object |
| Noticing vs. wondering | Notice and wonder are correctly separated and the wonder is a real question | Correct with adult help | Mixed up; wonder is a statement |
| Sharing | Tells one notice, the lens detail, and the wonder; answers "how did you find out?" | Shares with heavy prompting | Does not share |

**Scoring:** 8–10 = ready to move on; 5–7 = secure with the week's review
sessions, then move on; 0–4 = use the track's flexible catch-up time to
re-teach Lessons 02 and 05 before Unit 02 (see the
[teacher guide](teacher-guide.md)).

## Accessibility and safety

- All journal text may be adult-scribed; drawing or pointing may replace any
  spoken part. A learner may "draw" by arranging stickers or textured shapes.
- If no hand lens is available, "careful close looking" (nose-close
  observation with good light) is the documented alternative — the objective
  is a new detail, not the tool.
- Object must be safe to handle for three sessions; wash hands after soil or
  outdoor objects. Never use unknown plants, mushrooms, or found food.
