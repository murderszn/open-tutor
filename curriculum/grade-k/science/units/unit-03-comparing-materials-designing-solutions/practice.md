# Practice sessions — Unit 03

Short, play-based sessions referenced in the unit pacing. All responses
may be spoken, pointed to, acted out, drawn, or adult-scribed. The adult
pours all water. Answer-key references are in
[`answer-key.md`](answer-key.md#practice).

## Session A: Materials sort game

**When:** Session 2 (after Lesson 01). **Time:** 15 minutes.
**Materials:** the real materials kit, two trays.

1. Mix all kit items on the table. "Sort them by **material** — all the
   wood together, all the cloth together, and so on." (Wood: block.
   Cloth: red cloth. Sponge: sponge. Foil: foil sheet. Plastic: bag.
   Paper: white paper, paper towel. Cardboard: cardboard piece.)
2. "Now re-sort the same things by **one property**: bendy things here,
   stiff things there." (Bendy: cloth, paper, paper towel, bag, foil.
   Stiff: block, cardboard. Sponge: either pile with a reason.)
3. "Which sort was harder? Why?" (Accept any reason tied to looking vs.
   touching — the property sort needs testing, not just looking.)
4. Speed round: the adult holds up one item; the learner names its
   material in one word. Six items, adult-scribed score optional — the
   game, not the score, is the point.

## Session B: Property detective

**When:** Session 3 (after Lesson 01, before Lesson 02). **Time:**
15 minutes. **Materials:** the kit, the cloth mystery bag.

1. Mystery bag round: the learner feels one hidden item and names two
   properties (rough/smooth, bendy/stiff) before peeking. Three rounds.
2. Room hunt: "Find something **rough**, something **smooth**, something
   **bendy**, and something **stiff** in this room." (Rough: a brick,
   tree bark, a rough sponge. Smooth: a tabletop, a window. Bendy: a
   ribbon, a cloth. Stiff: a book, a board.) The learner points or
   brings each one.
3. "Which property would matter most for a spoon? For a blanket?"
   (Spoon: stiff — it must not fold while stirring. Blanket: bendy and
   soft — it must fold around you.)
4. Error hunt: the adult says, "This cotton ball is stiff!" The learner
   tests it and corrects: "No — it squashes. It is soft, not stiff."

## Session C: Water tub tests

**When:** Session 7 (after Lesson 02, before Lesson 03's fair test).
**Time:** 15–20 minutes. **Materials:** the shallow tub of water (adult
fills and holds it), a towel, the kit items, a dry cloth for checking.
**Safety:** the adult holds the tub; the learner dips only large items;
no splashing; dry hands and floor at once.

1. **Soak or keep out (free exploration):** The learner dips one item at
   a time, lifts it, and says "soaked up" or "kept out," judged by the
   item and the dry cloth underneath. (Soaks: sponge, cloth, paper towel,
   paper, cotton ball. Keeps out: plastic bag, foil.)
2. **Sink or float:** "Drop each item gently in the tub. Does it sink or
   float?" (Floats: plastic bag, foil sheet on the surface, paper
   briefly, sponge, cotton ball, wooden block — wood floats. Sinks:
   nothing in the kit sinks quickly except a waterlogged paper towel or
   a soaked sponge pressed under — the adult notes that soaking changes
   floating, and that is a discovery, not a mistake.)
3. **Warm or cool:** "Hold the metal spoon (if handy) and the cloth.
   Which feels cooler to touch?" (Metal feels cool; cloth feels warmer —
   a first warm/cool property, from the track's Week 11 goals. If no metal
   item is handy, skip this step; it is not assessed.)
4. "Which of these would you use to mop up a spill? Which to cover food
   in the rain?" (Mop: sponge or paper towel — they soak up. Cover:
   plastic bag or foil — they keep water out.)

## Session D: Materials market

**When:** Session 4 and Session 8 review circles, and again in Week 14.
**Time:** 15 minutes. **Materials:** the kit, the Lesson 01 picture,
the journal.

1. The adult "sells" one material at a time ("I have foil! Who wants
   it?"). The learner "buys" it only for a job it fits, naming the
   property: "I buy foil to cover food, because it keeps water out."
2. "Sell" a bad fit: "I have a paper towel to cover Teddy in the rain!"
   The learner refuses with a reason: "No — it soaks up water. Teddy
   would get wet."
3. Re-checks: the adult re-runs one exit check from each lesson so far
   (Lesson 01: name two materials; Lesson 02: two properties of one
   item; Lesson 03, in Session 8: predict soak/keep-out for one item).
4. The learner picks their favorite material so far and tells why, in one
   sentence, for the journal.
