# Resource Pack — Unit 03: Comparing Materials and Designing Simple Solutions

**Goal and age fit:** Support a kindergarten learner (age ~5) in
describing everyday materials by observable properties, defining a
simple problem a cover must solve, sketching how a cover's shape helps,
and testing, comparing, and improving two covers — all through short,
play-based, adult-guided sessions. Every core objective below has at
least one free, no-account route.

## Focused queries

1. `kindergarten materials properties rough smooth bendy stiff activities`
2. `kindergarten waterproof soak up water test materials science`
3. `K-2-ETS1-1 K-2-ETS1-2 K-2-ETS1-3 kindergarten engineering design cover`
4. `kindergarten design test improve two solutions compare lesson`
