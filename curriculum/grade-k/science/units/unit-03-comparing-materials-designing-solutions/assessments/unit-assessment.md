# Unit assessment — Unit 03

**Format:** five performance tasks, adult-observed and adult-scribed.
Each task is scored 0–2 (see the
[answer key](../answer-key.md#unit-assessment) for descriptors). Total:
10 points. **Bands:** 9–10 = ready to move on; 6–8 = secure with the
week's review sessions, then move on; 0–5 = use the track's flexible
catch-up time before Unit 04 (see the
[teacher guide](../teacher-guide.md)). These are teacher-made checks,
not standardized or validated tests. The adult pours all water.

## Task 1 — Describe materials (Objective 1)

The adult lays out four kit items the learner has handled (block, cloth,
foil, sponge). For each, the learner says what it is made of and one
property (rough/smooth, bendy/stiff, or what water does on it).
**Score 2:** all four materials named, each with a correct property.
**Score 1:** two or three correct. **Score 0:** fewer than two, or
materials named by color only.

## Task 2 — Define the problem (Objective 2)

"Teddy is going out in the rain. What is the problem? What must a good
cover do?" **Score 2:** states the problem (Teddy gets wet) and at
least two "must do" rules (cover all, keep water out, stay up).
**Score 1:** states the problem and one rule, or the problem only with
prompting. **Score 0:** cannot state the problem.

## Task 3 — Sketch the shape (Objective 3)

"Draw a cover for Teddy. Show the shape, and show where the water
goes." **Score 2:** the sketch shows a cover shape and a water path
that helps (for example, a slope shedding water, or a wrap keeping water
out). **Score 1:** a cover is drawn but the water's path is missing or
does not help. **Score 0:** no cover drawn, or a picture unrelated to
the cover.

## Task 4 — Compare two covers (Objective 4)

The adult tests two covers in the same three-spoonful rain (a flat
cardboard cover and a sloped plastic cover, or the learner's own two
project covers). After each test the learner checks Teddy. "Which cover
was better? How do you know?" **Score 2:** picks the drier cover and
gives the evidence (Teddy dry vs. a little wet) plus a reason tied to
shape or material. **Score 1:** picks the drier cover but gives no
evidence, or gives evidence for the wrong pick. **Score 0:** cannot pick
or gives no comparison.

## Task 5 — Improve and judge (Objective 5)

"Change one thing about the winning cover to make it better, then we
will test it again." The learner makes one change; the adult re-tests
with the same rain. "Is Teddy drier, the same, or wetter? Did your
change help?" **Score 2:** changes one thing, and judges correctly
from the re-test result. **Score 1:** changes something but cannot
judge, or changes several things at once. **Score 0:** no change
attempted.
