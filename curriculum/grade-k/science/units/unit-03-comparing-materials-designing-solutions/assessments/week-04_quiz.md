# Formative quiz — Unit 03 (Week 14)

**Format:** oral/observed, adult-scribed. The adult reads each item, the
learner answers by speaking, pointing, acting out, or drawing. There are
no written questions for the learner. Keep it to 10 minutes; allow a
second sitting. Scoring guidance is in the
[answer key](../answer-key.md#formative-quiz).

1. **Name the material.** The adult holds up three kit items, one at a
   time (wooden block, red cloth, plastic bag). "What is this made of?"
2. **Two properties.** The adult hands the learner the sponge. "Tell me
   two things about this material — rough or smooth? Bendy or stiff?
   What does it do with water?"
3. **Predict the water test.** The adult holds up a dry paper towel.
   "If I pour water on this, will it soak it up or keep water out?
   Tell me before I pour." The adult pours one spoonful and the learner
   checks underneath.
4. **State the problem.** "Teddy is going out in the rain. What is the
   problem we are trying to solve?"
5. **Judge a cover.** The adult shows a cover that leaves Teddy's feet
   sticking out. "Would this cover work? Why or why not?"
6. **Pick the better cover.** The adult shows the two tested covers'
   records (or re-tests quickly): cover A left Teddy a little wet, cover
   B left Teddy dry. "Which cover was better? How do you know?"
