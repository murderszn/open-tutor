# Resource Pack — Unit 04: Plant Needs and Growth Observations

**Goal and age fit:** Support a kindergarten learner (age ~5) in naming
what plants need, planting and tending a bean seed, recording growth
over weeks, running one fair light test, and modeling plant parts and
care — all through short, play-based, adult-guided sessions. Every core
objective below has at least one free, no-account route.

## Focused queries

1. `kindergarten plant needs water light soil activities`
2. `kindergarten bean seed growing observation journal`
3. `K-LS1-1 kindergarten plants need water light pattern`
4. `kindergarten fair test light plant dark plant lesson`
5. `kindergarten plant parts roots stem leaves model`
