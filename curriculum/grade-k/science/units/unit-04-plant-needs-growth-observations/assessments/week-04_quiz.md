# Formative quiz — Unit 04 (Week 18)

**Format:** oral/observed, adult-scribed. The adult reads each item, the
learner answers by speaking, pointing, acting out, or drawing. There are
no written questions for the learner. Keep it to 10 minutes; allow a
second sitting. Scoring guidance is in the
[answer key](../answer-key.md#formative-quiz).

1. **Name the needs.** "Tell me the four things a plant needs."
2. **Sort a need.** The adult shows picture cards: a cup of water, a sun
   picture, a pot of soil, and a toy car. "Which ones does a plant
   need? Which one does it not need?"
3. **Order the planting steps.** The adult shows three picture cards
   out of order — water, seed, soil. "Put them in the order we plant
   a seed."
4. **Order the growth stages.** Using the generated illustration, the
   adult points to the four pots in mixed order. "Which pot comes
   first? Which comes last? What comes after the sprout?"
5. **Judge the fair test.** The adult shows the two test plants (or
   their journal drawings). "Which plant grew greener and stronger?
   How do you know — what did you see?"
6. **Care decision.** The adult shows two cups of soil: one dry, one
   damp. The learner touches both. "This plant's soil feels like this
   one" (adult points to the dry cup). "What do you do? And what do
   you do if the soil feels like this one?" (adult points to the damp
   cup).
