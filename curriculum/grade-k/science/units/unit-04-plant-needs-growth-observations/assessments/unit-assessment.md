# Unit assessment — Unit 04 (Week 18)

**Format:** performance tasks, oral/observed, adult-scribed. The adult
reads each task, the learner answers by speaking, pointing, acting out,
drawing, or doing. Keep it to 20 minutes across two sittings if needed.
Scoring guidance is in the
[answer key](../answer-key.md#unit-assessment). This assessment is not
standardized or validated; it checks this unit's objectives only.

## Task 1 — Plant needs from observations (Objective 1)

The adult shows a healthy classroom plant and a plant with dry soil and
drooping leaves (or describes the second plant if only one plant is
available). "Look at both plants. Tell me four things plants need, and
for one need, tell me what you see that shows it." (One observation
tied to one need is required; naming all four needs is the full mark.)

## Task 2 — Plant a seed (Objective 2)

The learner plants a new bean seed with the materials on the table
(soil, seed, small watering cup, tray), while the adult watches without
ordering the steps. Then the learner tells the steps back in order, or
orders the three picture cards (soil, seed, water).

## Task 3 — Growth pattern and measuring (Objective 3)

Using the generated illustration and the learner's journal, the learner
(a) puts the four growth stages in order, (b) measures the learner's
plant in cubes from the soil to the tallest leaf, and (c) tells the
growth pattern in their own words (seed, sprout, stem and leaves,
taller plant).

## Task 4 — Fair-test conclusion (Objective 4)

The adult shows the light and dark test plants (or their journal
drawings with cube counts). "What was the same about these two plants?
What was different? Which grew stronger and greener, and how do you
know?" The learner must name light as the only difference and support
the conclusion with at least one observation (color, strength, or
cube count).

## Task 5 — Model and care (Objective 5)

The learner draws or points to a plant model (their Lesson 06 model may
be used as the starting point, but the learner must name the parts
without the adult naming them first): roots, stem, leaves, and what
each part does. Then: "Your friend just got a plant. Tell them where
to put it and how to care for it." (Bright place for light; water only
when the soil feels dry; gentle hands.)

## Scoring

- **Ready to move on:** Tasks 1–5 each show the core evidence (four
  needs named with one observation; planting order correct; stages in
  order with a cube measure; light as the only difference with an
  observed reason; three parts with jobs plus a care routine with
  water-when-dry and light).
- **Re-teach and re-check:** any task missing its core evidence gets a
  targeted re-teach from the teacher guide (needs: Lesson 01; planting
  order: Lesson 02 picture cards; stages/measuring: Lesson 03;
  fair test: Lesson 04–05 comparison; model/care: Lesson 06) and one
  re-check sitting before Unit 05.
- **Notes:** a plant that grew slowly or not at all is not a failed
  task — score the learner's observing, ordering, measuring, and
  reasoning. Predictions that turned out wrong are not penalized;
  changing a mind with evidence is the expected outcome in Task 4.
