# Project — Our Bean Garden

**Type:** multi-session investigation (runs across Sessions 5, 10, and
14 of the unit pacing, with tending on every tending session between).
**Time:** three 15–20-minute project sessions plus daily tending and
the final sharing.
**Product:** a tended bean plant (or plants), a science journal with at
least three dated growth drawings and cube counts, a fair-test record
(light plant vs. dark plant), and a garden story told from the journal
at the showcase.

## Driving question

"Can you grow a bean plant, prove what it needs, and tell its growth
story from your journal?"

## Materials

Clear plastic cups, potting soil, dried bean seeds, a small watering
cup, a tray, counting cubes, paper and crayons, the science journal, a
hand lens, the generated illustration
[`assets/bean-growth-stages.png`](assets/bean-growth-stages.png). The
adult handles the soil bag and scissors and pours water from the jug;
the learner waters only with the small cup handed over. Wash hands
after soil work.

## Steps

### Part 1 — Plant and start the journal (Session 5, with Lesson 02)

1. The learner plants bean seeds following Lesson 02's order: soil,
   seed, water. (One seed per cup; a second cup is planted the same
   way for the fair test in Lesson 04, or the adult plants two matched
   cups in advance if the learner's cups differ.)
2. **First journal entry:** the learner draws the planted cup and the
   adult scribes: "My bean seed is planted. I gave it soil, a seed,
   and water." The date is added.
3. **Care plan:** the learner dictates where the plant will live
   (bright window) and what the daily check is (look, touch soil,
   water only when dry). The adult scribes it on the journal's first
   page.

### Part 2 — Mid-unit garden check (Session 10, after Lesson 04)

1. **Tending round:** the learner does the care check for every plant
   (look, touch soil, water only if dry, check light) and measures
   each plant in cubes.
2. **Fair-test journal:** the learner draws both test plants side by
   side, and the adult scribes the prediction from Lesson 04 beside
   the new drawings.
3. **Garden talk:** "What has changed since planting? What is still
   the same?" The learner names one change (sprout appeared, leaves
   opened, taller) and one sameness (same cup, same window, same
   watering rule).
4. Adult checks: are journal entries dated and in order? Is there a
   drawing from planting day and a drawing from today? If a drawing
   is missing, it is added now from the plant in front of the learner
   — never backdated as if drawn earlier.

### Part 3 — Showcase and garden story (Session 14)

1. **Final measure and drawing:** the learner measures the plant(s)
   in cubes one last time and draws the final stage.
2. **Garden story:** using only the journal (no new facts invented),
   the learner tells the plant's story in order: planted, sprouted,
   grew leaves, light test result, how it was cared for. The adult
   scribes any words the learner wants kept.
3. **Show and tell:** the learner shows the plant, the journal, and
   the model drawing from Lesson 06, and answers one question from
   the adult ("What does your plant need?" or "What did the light
   test show?").
4. **What next:** the learner says what happens to the plant next
   (kept by the window and watered when dry, or planted outside when
   the adult says the weather is right). No plant is left unwatered
   as a "test" after the unit ends.

## Deliverables

- At least three dated journal drawings of the learner's plant across
  the unit, each with a cube count (or "not sprouted yet" honestly
  recorded for early entries).
- A fair-test record: Lesson 04 prediction, side-by-side drawings of
  the light and dark plants, and the Lesson 05 pattern statement.
- A labeled plant model (Lesson 06) showing roots, stem, and leaves.
- A garden story told in order at the showcase.

## Rubric

| Criterion | Growing (1) | Steady (2) | Strong (3) |
|---|---|---|---|
| Planting in order | Steps out of order even with picture cards | Steps in order with picture-card help | Steps in order (soil, seed, water) without help |
| Observing and recording | One drawing, no count | Two drawings with counts, or three drawings with one count missing | Three or more dated drawings, each with a cube count (or honest "not sprouted yet") |
| Fair-test reasoning | Cannot say what was the same or different | Names light as the difference with adult prompts | Names what was the same (seed, soil, cup, water) and light as the only difference, and states the pattern from observations |
| Model and care | Parts unnamed, or care is "water every day lots" | Two parts named with jobs, or care routine missing one step | Roots, stem, and leaves named with jobs; care routine includes look, touch soil, water only when dry, light, gentle hands |
| Garden story | Story out of order or invented (facts not in the journal) | Story mostly in order, from the journal, with one gap | Story in order, told from the journal, including the light-test result |

Scoring note: the plant's actual growth is never scored — a slow seed
is not a low score. Score the observing, recording, reasoning, and
telling. A learner who honestly records "not sprouted yet" and keeps
tending earns full recording credit for that entry.
