# Practice sessions — Unit 04

Short, play-based sessions referenced in the unit pacing. All responses
may be spoken, pointed to, acted out, drawn, or adult-scribed. The adult
pours all water from the jug; the learner waters only with the small
cup handed over. Answer-key references are in
[`answer-key.md`](answer-key.md#practice).

## Session A: Needs sort game

**When:** Session 2 (after Lesson 01). **Time:** 15 minutes.
**Materials:** picture cards or objects: cup of water, sun picture, pot
of soil, toy car, shoe, sandwich, book.

1. Mix the cards. "Sort them: things a plant needs here, things a plant
   does NOT need there." (Needs: water, sun/light, soil. Not plant
   needs: toy car, shoe, book. Sandwich: a person need, not a plant
   need — accept "people food" as the reason it goes in the not-need
   pile, with praise for the distinction.)
2. "Which need is hardest to see?" (Air — it is all around but
   invisible. The learner waves a hand to feel it.)
3. Speed round: the adult names a need; the learner points to its card
   or object. Four rounds, one per need.
4. Error hunt: the adult puts the shoe in the needs pile and says,
   "Plants need shoes!" The learner moves it out and corrects: "No —
   plants need water, light, air, and soil."

## Session B: Plant observation walk

**When:** Session 3 (after Lesson 01). **Time:** 15–20 minutes.
**Materials:** the science journal, crayons, a hand lens. Stay indoors
with houseplants or step just outside with the adult; never pick plants
or leaves, and never handle wild plants.

1. "I notice / I wonder" walk: at each plant, the learner says one
   "I notice…" (green leaves, tall stem, soil in a pot) and the adult
   scribes it. Two plants minimum.
2. "Where does this plant get its light? Its water? Its soil?" The
   learner points to the window or sun, the pot, and the soil for each
   plant. (Outdoor plants: sun, rain and soil in the ground.)
3. Hand-lens close look: one leaf and one patch of soil, close up.
   "What do you see now that you could not see before?" (Leaf lines,
   tiny soil crumbs, a small root at the soil edge. Any new detail
   counts.)
4. "Which plant looks like it has everything it needs? How can you
   tell?" (Green, standing up, damp soil. Any one piece of evidence
   tied to a need counts.)

## Session C: Soak and look

**When:** Session 6 (after Lesson 02). **Time:** 15 minutes.
**Materials:** one dry bean seed, one bean seed soaked overnight in
water (adult-prepared), a hand lens, the journal. No tasting.

1. "Look at the dry bean. Feel it." (Hard, smooth, small.) "Now look
   at the soaked bean. Feel it." (Bigger, softer, wrinkly skin, a tiny
   split where the sprout will come out.)
2. "What changed when the bean sat in water?" (It got bigger and
   softer. Water went in.) The adult scribes the learner's words.
3. Hand-lens look at the split in the soaked bean: "That is where the
   sprout will push out." (If no split is visible, "The skin is looser
   and wrinkly" counts.)
4. "Why do we plant the seed in damp soil, not dry soil?" (So the seed
   can take in water and start growing — the soaked bean shows what
   water does first.)
5. Journal drawing: dry bean beside soaked bean, with the adult
   scribing "Water made the bean bigger and softer."

## Session D: Tending and measuring

**When:** Session 8 (after Lesson 03). **Time:** 15 minutes.
**Materials:** the learner's bean plant, counting cubes, journal.

1. Care check, in order: look at the plant; touch the soil (dry or
   damp?); water a little only if dry; check it is in the light.
2. Measure: stack cubes from the soil to the tallest leaf, count
   aloud, and compare with the last recorded count. "Did it grow? How
   many cubes taller?" (Subtract with cubes: new count minus old
   count, counted out loud with the adult.)
3. Journal drawing: today's plant with the cube count scribed beside
   it, plus one "I notice…" sentence.
4. "What do you think it will look like next time? Draw your guess
   small in the corner." (Revisited next tending session; right or
   wrong, it is a prediction.)

## Session E: Water detective

**When:** Session 12 (after Lesson 05). **Time:** 15 minutes.
**Materials:** two small cups of soil — one dry, one damp (adult-
prepared) — a small watering cup, a towel.

1. Touch test, eyes open: "Which soil is dry? Which is damp?" The
   learner touches both and names them. (Dry: dusty, light, falls
   apart. Damp: darker, cool, holds together.)
2. Touch test, eyes closed: the adult swaps the cups; the learner
   feels and names dry vs. damp again. (Touch alone is enough evidence.)
3. "Which soil would a plant like better? Why?" (Damp — plants need
   water, and damp soil has water in it.)
4. Watering demo: the learner pours a little water on the dry soil
   with the adult's help. "What changed?" (Darker, damp, holds
   together.) "What if we poured and poured?" (Flooded — too much
   water is not better. The adult stops the pour before flooding.)
5. Rule in the learner's words, scribed: "Water when the soil feels
   dry. Wait when it feels damp."

## Session F: Garden story retell (review game)

**When:** Session 4 or 14 review circles, and as a warm-up before the
quiz. **Time:** 15 minutes. **Materials:** the journal, the generated
illustration, the bean plant.

1. Journal walk: the learner flips the journal from the first drawing
   to the last and tells the garden story in order (planted, sprouted,
   grew leaves, light test, caring).
2. Picture match: "Which pot in the picture matches our plant today?"
   (Same matching task as Lesson 03, with the current plant.)
3. Needs chant (call and response): adult: "Water?" learner: "Plants
   need water!" — light, air, soil and room in turn.
4. "One thing I would tell a friend about plants:" the learner says
   one pattern from the unit (needs, growth order, light pattern, or
   care rule). Any one correct pattern counts.
