# Lesson 06 — How a plant grows, and how we care for it

**Goal (read to the learner):** "Today you build a model of a plant —
a drawing that shows its parts and what each part does. Then you tell
me how you will care for a plant every day. A **model** is a drawing
or a thing we make to show how something works."

**Time:** 15–20 minutes. **Materials:** the science journal with all
the unit's drawings, paper and crayons, the learner's bean plant, a
small cup of water, counting cubes.

## Readiness check (2 minutes)

"Tell me the growth pattern." (Seed, sprout, stem and leaves, taller
plant.) "Tell me the light pattern from our test." (Plants with light
grow greener and stronger.) The adult names the job today: "Now we put
it all together in one model."

## Teach and model

Explain in your own words: *A plant has three parts we can see. The
**roots** grow down into the soil — they hold the plant in place and
take in water. The **stem** grows up — it holds the plant up and lifts
the leaves toward the light. The **leaves** spread out in the light —
they are the part that catches the light the plant needs. The parts
match the needs, and they match the plant's place: roots belong in
soil, leaves belong in the light, and the whole plant needs air all
around it and room to grow. That is what a model shows: parts, needs,
and place, all together.*

**Modeled example 1 — the adult's model.** The adult draws a simple
plant: roots in brown soil, a green stem, three leaves. Pointing to
each part: "Roots — in the soil, for water. Stem — holding up. Leaves
— in the light. Soil, light, air all around, room to grow. This drawing
is a model: it shows how the parts and the needs go together."
(Modeled reasoning: draw, label by pointing, tie each part to a need.)

**Modeled example 2 — caring, step by step.** "Every day I look at my
plant. I touch the soil. Dry? I give a little water. Damp? I wait. I
check it is in the light. I am gentle — I do not pull leaves. That is
caring: look, touch, water only when dry, light, gentle hands."

## Guided practice (adult-guided)

1. **Guided:** The learner draws (or the adult draws as the learner
   directs) a plant with roots, stem, and leaves. The adult scribes
   one label per part as the learner points: "roots — water," "stem —
   holds up," "leaves — light."
2. **Guided:** "Where does your model plant live?" The learner adds
   the place: soil at the bottom, a sun or window at the top, air all
   around (the adult scribes "air is all around" if the learner says
   it, or draws wavy air lines on request).

## Independent practice

3. **Independent part naming:** The adult points to each part of the
   learner's model in mixed order; the learner names the part and what
   it does. (Roots: in soil, take in water. Stem: holds plant up.
   Leaves: catch light.)
4. **Independent care routine:** "Tell me your care routine, step by
   step." (Look; touch the soil; water a little only if dry; keep in
   light; gentle hands. Four of five steps counts; the adult scribes.)
5. **Independent journal walk:** The learner flips through the unit's
   journal drawings, first to last, and tells the growth story:
   "First my seed… then a sprout… then leaves… now it is tall."
   (Order correct counts; exact words do not matter.)
6. **Error-analysis (guided):** The adult points to the roots in the
   model and says, "These leaves are in the soil!" The learner
   corrects: "No — those are roots. Roots go in the soil. Leaves go
   up in the light."

## Application

7. **A plant for a friend:** "Draw a little care card for a friend's
   new plant: one picture of where it should live, and one care step."
   (Bright window; water when the soil feels dry. Any one correct
   place and one correct step counts.)

## Exit check

The learner's model shows roots in soil, a stem, and leaves, with the
learner naming what each part does, and the learner states a care
routine with at least the water-when-dry and light steps. The adult
records the parts named correctly and the care steps stated.

## Supports and extension

- **Reading/language access:** Part words are taught by pointing at
  the real plant first, the word second. The labeled model stays in
  the journal as the reference for Unit 05.
- **Alternate response mode:** Pointing to parts, acting out the plant
  (feet as roots, body as stem, arms as leaves), or building the model
  from blocks and paper counts fully; the adult scribes.
- **Extension:** "How is a plant's home like an animal's home? What
  does each living thing need from where it lives?" (Both need water,
  air, and a place with what they need; plants need light and soil,
  animals need food and shelter — a bridge to Unit 05, kept to one
  sentence.)
- **Table and floor variants:** Seated at a table drawing the model,
  or on the floor building a big model with the learner's body and
  paper leaves; both work.

## Teacher note

**Common misconception:** "Leaves drink water." Redirect: "Leaves
catch light. Roots take in water from the soil. Each part has its own
job — that is why the model labels matter."

**Common misconception:** "Caring means watering every day, lots."
Redirect: "Caring means checking first. Damp soil means wait. Too much
water floods the roots."

**Answer-key reference:** [`../answer-key.md`](../answer-key.md#lesson-06) —
roots: in soil, water; stem: holds up; leaves: light; care routine:
look, touch soil, water only when dry, light, gentle hands.
