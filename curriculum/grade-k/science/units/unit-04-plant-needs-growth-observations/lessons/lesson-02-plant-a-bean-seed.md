# Lesson 02 — Plant a bean seed

**Goal (read to the learner):** "Today you plant a real bean seed.
You will put soil in a cup, put the seed in the soil, and give it a
little water — in that order. Then you will draw your seed in your
journal, so we can watch it grow."

**Time:** 15–20 minutes. **Materials:** clear plastic cups (one per
plant), potting soil, dried bean seeds, a small watering cup, a tray,
the science journal, crayons. The adult checks for seed and soil
allergies before starting and holds the soil bag.

## Readiness check (2 minutes)

"Tell me the four things a plant needs." (Water, light, air, soil with
room to grow.) "Today we give a seed all four. Where will its soil be?
Where will its light come from?" Accept "in the cup" and "by the
window."

## Teach and model

Explain in your own words: *A **seed** is a tiny package with a baby
plant inside, waiting for water, light, air, and soil. We plant it in
steps, in order, so the seed gets what it needs. First, soil in the
cup. Second, the seed in the soil — not on top where it dries out, and
not buried so deep it cannot reach up. A bean seed goes about as deep
as your fingertip's first joint, and we cover it gently. Third, a
little water — just enough to make the soil damp, not a flood. Then
the cup goes in a bright place, where air can reach it, and we wait
and watch.*

**Modeled example 1 — the adult plants first.** The adult plants one
seed slowly, saying each step aloud: "Soil in the cup. Seed in the
soil, covered gently. A little water — see, the soil turns darker and
damp. Now it goes by the window." (Modeled reasoning: say the step,
do the step, check the result — damp soil, seed covered.)

**Modeled example 2 — the wrong order.** The adult mimes pouring water
into an empty cup first, then adding soil on top: "If I water first
and then pile dry soil on top, the seed's soil is dry underneath and
wet on top. That is not the order. Soil, then seed, then water — that
is the order that works." The learner fixes the order with the adult.

## Guided practice (adult-guided)

1. **Guided:** "What goes in the cup first?" (Soil.) The learner fills
   the cup most of the way with soil while the adult holds the bag.
   Prompt with "More soil — up to here" and a finger mark if needed.
2. **Guided:** "Now the seed. Push it in gently and cover it." The
   adult checks depth (about a fingertip joint) and that the seed is
   covered.

## Independent practice

3. **Independent watering:** The learner pours a small cup of water
   the adult hands over, slowly, over the soil. (Damp, not flooded:
   soil darker, no puddle standing on top.)
4. **Independent ordering:** The adult shows three picture cards out of
   order — water, seed, soil — and the learner puts them in planting
   order: soil, seed, water.
5. **Independent journal drawing:** The learner draws the planted cup
   in the journal: cup, soil, and a note the adult scribes — "My bean
   seed is planted. I gave it soil, a seed, and water." The learner
   adds the date (adult writes the numbers if needed).
6. **Error-analysis (guided):** The adult holds up an empty cup and
   says, "I will plant my seed in this empty cup — no soil!" The
   learner corrects: "No — a seed needs soil. Soil first, then seed,
   then water."

## Application

7. **Care plan:** "Where will your plant live? What will you do for it
   every day?" (By the window for light; check the soil and water only
   when it feels dry; look and draw what changes.) The adult scribes
   the learner's care plan in the journal.

## Exit check

The learner plants (or re-tells with the picture cards) the three steps
in order — soil, seed, water — without the adult ordering them first,
and the planted cup is left in a bright place with damp, not flooded,
soil. The adult records the steps the learner named in order.

## Supports and extension

- **Reading/language access:** Steps are taught by doing first, words
  second. The three picture cards stay in the journal as the
  step-reminder all unit.
- **Alternate response mode:** Ordering the picture cards, acting out
  the steps, or drawing them counts fully; the adult scribes.
- **Extension:** "What would happen if we planted two seeds in one
  cup? Would they have room to grow?" (They would crowd each other;
  one cup, one seed gives room — or, if two are planted, the adult
  thins to one later. Do not actually crowd the class plants.)
- **Table and floor variants:** Seated at a table with the tray under
  the cups, or on the floor on a towel; both work. Hands are washed
  after soil work either way.

## Teacher note

**Common misconception:** "More water is better." Redirect: "Too much
water floods the seed. Damp soil is just right — like a wrung-out
sponge. We check with a finger: dry on top means water; damp means
wait."

**Common misconception:** "The seed must be planted deep so it is
safe." Redirect: "Too deep and the sprout cannot reach the light. A
fingertip deep is just right."

**Safety note:** Bean seeds are small. The adult supervises every seed;
no seed goes near a mouth, and seeds stay out of reach of children
under 3 in shared settings. No tasting of seeds or soil.
