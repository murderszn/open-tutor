# Learner — Earth Systems Data Story (Grade 7 STEM)

## Goal
Use real-world weather or climate data to make a scientific claim.

## Tasks
1. Choose one location and gather 7 days of temperature or precipitation data.
2. Create a line graph or bar chart.
3. Describe one pattern and one anomaly.
4. Connect findings to water cycle, energy transfer, or climate systems.
