# Grade 7 — Experimental Lab Report

1. **Question and variables:** Identify the independent, dependent, and controlled variables.
2. **Hypothesis:** State a testable prediction and a reason.
3. **Materials and safety:** List equipment and adult-approved precautions.
4. **Method:** Number reproducible steps; identify how many trials to run.
5. **Data:** Record measurements with units in a table.
6. **Analysis:** Calculate a mean where appropriate and describe patterns or anomalies.
7. **Conclusion:** Use evidence to evaluate the hypothesis.
8. **Limitations:** Identify likely sources of error and one improvement.
9. **Sources:** Cite references used.

Keep this as the independent report for this grade track.
