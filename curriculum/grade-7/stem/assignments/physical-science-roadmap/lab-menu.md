# 🧪 Learner Lab Menu (Grade 9 Track)

Pick one per module. Use the Lab Report Template.

1) Measurement & Units
- Dimensional analysis challenge: Convert 65 miles/hour to meters/second; validate by timing a rolling object over a measured distance.

2) Matter & Atomic Structure
- Density column: Layer water, oil, and syrup; measure mass/volume to compute densities; predict floating order and verify.

3) Bonding & Reactions (Intro)
- Safe reaction rate: Observe a small educator-controlled vinegar-and-baking-soda reaction in an open container. Never seal a reacting container; compare bubbling qualitatively.

4) Energy & Heat
- Specific heat demo (comparative): Compare educator-provided temperature data for equal masses of different materials; do not heat metal at home.

5) Motion & Forces
- Pendulum period vs length; graph T vs √L; discuss linearity. Or cart on ramp: measure distance vs time to estimate acceleration.

6) Waves & Light
- Flashlight and slit/edge: observe diffraction/spread (qualitative). Or sound speed estimate via echo (distance/time).

7) Biology & Anatomy
- Heart rate vs activity: record resting, after light exercise, and recovery; plot over time and discuss homeostasis.

8) Lab Skills & Communication
- Graphing and error analysis exercise: re‑graph prior lab data with best‑fit line; compute percent error if a reference is available.

## Safety
An educator must approve the setup. Use small, light objects on a stable low surface, keep people clear of moving objects, and stop if anything is unsafe. Use warm—not hot—water; no flames, lasers, pressurized containers, sharp tools, or mains electricity. Substitute provided data or diagrams when equipment or supervision is unavailable. Keep observations and identifying images private.
