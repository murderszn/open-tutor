# Learner — Ecosystems & Energy Flow (Grade 7 STEM)

## Goal
Model how energy moves through a food web.

## Tasks
1. Build a food chain with at least 5 organisms.
2. Expand it into a food web (minimum 8 arrows).
3. Explain what happens if one key species is removed.
4. Add one human-impact example and one possible solution.
