# Grade 7 Social Studies — The Three Branches of Government Quiz

**Topic:** The Three Branches of Government

**Assignment:** Read the **Three Branches Overview**, **Legislative Branch**, **Executive Branch**, **Judicial Branch**, and **How a Bill Becomes a Law** sections in the [Government Basics](../../../../resources/government_basics.md) guide.

Please answer the following 10 questions based on your reading.

---

**1.** What are the names of the three branches of the U.S. government?

**2.** Which branch is responsible for writing and voting on laws?

**3.** What are the two parts that make up Congress, and how is representation in each determined?

**4.** What are three things the Legislative Branch has the power to do?

**5.** What does the Executive Branch do, and who leads it?

**6.** What does the Judicial Branch do, and what is the name of the highest court in the United States?

**7.** In your own words, explain what "checks and balances" means and why it matters.

**8.** What does the GAO do, and which branch does it serve?

**9.** What does the CBO do?

**10.** Walk through the steps a bill goes through to become a law, from start to finish.
