# Grade 7 Social Studies — Rights, Responsibilities & U.S. States Quiz

**Topic:** Rights, Responsibilities & U.S. States

**Assignment:** Read **Sections 3 and 5** in the [United States — Understanding & Principles](../../../../resources/united_states_understanding_and_principles.md) guide.

Please answer the following 10 questions based on your reading.

---

**1.** What is the Bill of Rights, and what kinds of freedoms does it protect?

**2.** Name at least three rights protected by the Bill of Rights.

**3.** What did the 13th Amendment accomplish?

**4.** What did the 19th Amendment accomplish?

**5.** What are civic responsibilities, and why do they matter for keeping a free society strong?

**6.** What is federalism, and how does it describe the relationship between state governments and the federal government?

**7.** How many states make up the United States? What is the difference between a U.S. state and a U.S. territory?

**8.** What is Washington, D.C., and why is it different from both a state and a territory?

**9.** Name two U.S. territories and share one fact about each.

**10.** Which U.S. state has the largest population, and which state has the smallest population?
