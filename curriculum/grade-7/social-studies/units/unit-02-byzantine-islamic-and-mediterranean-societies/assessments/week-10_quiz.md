# U02 Week-10 Quiz (formative)

**Unit:** [U02 — Byzantine, Islamic, and Mediterranean societies](../README.md)
**Session:** 14 of 16 (Week 10, review week) · **Time:** ~25 minutes
**Points:** 20 · **Adult:** score the same day; re-teach every missed item
before the unit assessment (Session 15).

Answer in the inquiry notebook. One to three sentences each unless noted.

1. (3 pts) Name the three worlds studied this unit. For each, name **one**
   institution, practice, or achievement studied in its lesson.
2. (2 pts) A gold solidus stamped with Justinian's name is found in a
   market town far from Constantinople. State **one** thing the coin proves
   and **one** thing it cannot prove.
3. (3 pts) Match each description to its Pillar: **shahada, salat, zakat,
   sawm, hajj**.
   a. Fasting during the month of Ramadan
   b. The pilgrimage to Mecca
   c. Almsgiving to those in need
4. (2 pts) Pepper travels from Alexandria to Venice. Name **two** different
   people who handle it along the way and what each one does with it.
5. (2 pts) "Greek learning was preserved in Baghdad and later translated
   into Latin in Toledo." Classify this development as **change**,
   **continuity**, or **both**, and cite **one** piece of evidence for your
   verdict.
6. (2 pts) A Byzantine chronicler and a Venetian merchant describe the 1204
   sack of Constantinople very differently. Name **one** perspective factor
   (role, faith, interests, audience) that helps explain the difference,
   and say how.
7. (2 pts) Using the unit's Mediterranean schematic: which strait does
   Constantinople guard, and in one sentence why does guarding a strait
   matter for trade?
8. (2 pts) What kind of source is the AI harbor-market illustration —
   primary, secondary, or modern reconstruction? Name **one** question
   about the medieval world it can never answer.
9. (2 pts) **Error repair.** A classmate writes: "The schematic proves
   Venice was the richest Mediterranean city." In two sentences, explain
   what is wrong with this use of the schematic and what evidence you
   would need instead.

**Scoring guide for the adult:** 17–20 = ready for the unit assessment;
14–16 = re-teach missed objectives in Session 13/15 warm-up, then assess;
below 14 = re-teach Lessons 03–05 core ideas before assessing. Record only
the band.
