# U02 Unit Assessment (culminating)

**Unit:** [U02 — Byzantine, Islamic, and Mediterranean societies](../README.md)
**Session:** 15 of 16 (Week 10) · **Time:** ~45 minutes · **Points:** 30
**Adult:** supervise; the learner may use the unit schematic and the
vocabulary list, but not notes or the answer key. Score within one day.

This assessment is **not standardized or validated** — it measures this
unit's objectives as taught here.

---

## Part A — Institutions and societies (6 pts)

1. (2 pts) Explain **two** of the following in one sentence each, in
   context: *basileus*, *Corpus Juris Civilis*, *theme system*,
   *sharia*, *House of Wisdom*.
2. (2 pts) A learner says, "Islam spread only by conquest." Using the
   unit's material, correct this claim in two sentences: name **two**
   non-military ways Islam spread and **one** region where each mattered.
3. (2 pts) Why do historians call the empire "Byzantine" while its people
   called themselves Romans? What does that naming gap teach us about
   historical labels? (Two sentences.)

## Part B — Exchange and geography (6 pts)

Use [assets/mediterranean-exchange-schematic.svg](../assets/mediterranean-exchange-schematic.svg).

4. (2 pts) Trace the journey of **paper** from Baghdad toward Italy:
   list three stops in order and, for one stop, name who carries the
   knowledge onward.
5. (2 pts) Choose **Venice** or **Genoa**. In two sentences, explain how
   its physical position shaped its identity as a maritime republic.
6. (2 pts) The schematic shows routes, not wealth. Name **two** things
   about Mediterranean trade the schematic **cannot** show.

## Part C — Change and continuity (5 pts)

7. (3 pts) Classify **each** of the following as change, continuity, or
   both, with **one** piece of evidence for each verdict:
   a. The use of Roman law from Justinian's code to later legal systems.
   b. Constantinople's continuous habitation from 330 to 1453.
   c. The carriers of Silk Road goods across the centuries.
8. (2 pts) For **one** of your answers in Q7, name a **second source
   kind** (different from the one your evidence came from) that could
   support the same verdict, and say what it would show.

## Part D — Perspectives (7 pts)

Read the two model accounts. **(Original model texts written for this
unit — not historical quotations.)**

> **Account X** — *in the voice of a Byzantine chronicler-kind writer:*
> "In the year of our Lord 1204, the Latins who had taken the cross for
> Jerusalem turned their swords upon the Queen of Cities. They broke the
> holy icons, stripped the Great Church, and divided among themselves what
> Christian emperors had gathered over eight centuries. The city that had
> sheltered Christendom's eastern wall now lay open, and we who remained
> counted our dead by parishes."
>
> **Account Y** — *in the voice of a Venetian merchant-kind writer:*
> "When the fleet reached Constantinople in 1204, our captains collected
> the debts the Greeks owed and more besides. Three shiploads of silk and
> plate came to my warehouse that autumn — goods I could never have bought
> at any fair. The young clerks called it a tragedy; I call it the year
> the sea finally paid what it was owed."

9. (2 pts) For **each** account, infer the maker's **audience** and
   **purpose** (one phrase each, with one clue from the text supporting
   each inference).
10. (2 pts) Name **one** thing the two accounts **agree** on and **one**
    thing they **disagree** on. For the disagreement, explain how each
    maker's perspective accounts for it.
11. (3 pts) **Claim and counterclaim.** *"Account Y is more useful than
    Account X for understanding the economic effects of the 1204 sack."*
    Defend the claim with two reasons. Then state the counterclaim with
    one reason. End with one sentence: which account would you trust
    **less** on the question of what the sack meant to the city's people,
    and why?

## Part E — Essential question (6 pts)

12. (6 pts) ***How did living on the same sea produce such different
    societies — and what did they make of each other?*** Write 150–250
    words (or dictate to the adult) answering this question with:
    - **one** similarity and **one** difference between two of the
      unit's societies, each supported by **one** piece of evidence;
    - **one** sentence explaining how perspective shaped what one
      society noticed about another;
    - a concluding sentence stating what the comparison reveals about
      the medieval Mediterranean.
