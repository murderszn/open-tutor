# U02 Project — Two Societies, One Sea

**Unit:** [U02 — Byzantine, Islamic, and Mediterranean societies](README.md)
**Spans:** Sessions 12, 14, 16 (Weeks 9–10) · **Presented:** Session 16

## The task

Choose **two** of the three worlds studied this unit — the Byzantine
state, an Islamic society (Baghdad *or* Córdoba), or a Mediterranean
trading power (Venice *or* Genoa). Write an **evidence-based comparison**
(400–600 words, or an adult-scribed equivalent) answering this compelling
question:

***In what one way were your two societies most alike, and in what one
way were they most different — and what does the difference reveal about
how each society saw its place in the world?***

You are arguing from evidence, not summarizing everything. Pick **one**
similarity and **one** difference, and defend both.

## Deliverables

1. **Comparison essay** (400–600 words) with:
   - a **thesis** stating your one similarity and one difference;
   - **at least four evidence rows** — each naming the claim, the evidence,
     and the **source kind** (map, traveler's account, artifact, coin,
     chronicle, or secondary interpretation). At least **two different
     source kinds** across the four rows;
   - a **perspective paragraph**: for your strongest piece of evidence,
     name whose perspective it carries (creator, audience, purpose) and
     one limitation that perspective creates;
   - a **counterclaim paragraph**: the best argument *against* your
     thesis, in two to three sentences, with one reason it is weaker than
     your thesis.
2. **Sketch map** (on paper, using the unit schematic as a model): both
   societies' key cities, one exchange route connecting them, a key, and a
   one-sentence caption stating what the map shows — and one sentence
   stating what it cannot show.
3. **Source log** (4+ rows): for each source kind used — what it is, what
   you used it for, and one limitation.

## Working plan

- **Session 12:** choose societies; draft thesis; fill 4 evidence rows in
  the notebook; sketch the map. The adult checks that every evidence row
  names a source kind before you leave the table.
- **Session 14:** finish the essay draft and source log; self-check
  against the rubric below (circle the level you think you earned per
  row, and write one sentence saying what you would improve).
- **Session 16:** present in 3–4 minutes (read the thesis, walk through
  the map, state your counterclaim). Audience: the adult, plus anyone the
  adult invites.

## Rubric

| Criterion | Strong (3) | Developing (2) | Starting (1) |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Thesis** | One clear similarity and one clear difference, both arguable | Similarity and difference named but vague or unarguable | Missing one side, or restates facts without a claim |
| **Evidence** | 4+ rows; each ties a claim to evidence and names a source kind; 2+ kinds used | 3 rows, or kinds named but not tied to claims | Fewer than 3 rows, or no source kinds named |
| **Perspective** | Names creator, audience, purpose for the key evidence and states one real limitation | Names perspective but the limitation is generic ("it might be biased") | No perspective analysis |
| **Counterclaim** | States the best opposing argument fairly and explains why the thesis still wins | Counterclaim present but straw-manned or unexplained | No counterclaim |
| **Map** | Both societies' cities, one route, key, caption; states what the map cannot show | Cities and route present; key or caption missing | Map missing cities, route, or key |
| **Source log** | 4+ rows with use and limitation each | 3 rows, or limitations missing | Fewer than 3 rows |

**Scoring:** 15–18 = ready for U03; 11–14 = revise one weakest row with the
adult, then move on; below 11 = re-teach evidence rows (Practice Set D)
before U03. The adult records only the readiness band, never in this
repository.

## Misconceptions to watch (adult)

- *"Different means worse."* The rubric rewards the difference that
  reveals the most about each society's self-understanding, not the
  biggest value judgment. Redirect any "which was better" framing to
  "what did each one value, and why."
- *Citing the AI illustration as evidence.* The harbor-market picture is
  labeled a reconstruction in every lesson — it cannot appear in an
  evidence row. If it appears, the row scores 0 for evidence until
  replaced.
- *One society as background.* Both societies need evidence rows; an essay
  that is really about one society with the other as decoration cannot
  score above Developing on thesis or evidence.
