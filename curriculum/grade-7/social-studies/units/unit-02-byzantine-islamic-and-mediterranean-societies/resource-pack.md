# U02 Resource Pack

**Unit:** [U02 — Byzantine, Islamic, and Mediterranean societies](README.md)

**Goal:** Give the guiding adult verified, age-appropriate starting points
for every core objective of this unit — the Byzantine state, Islamic
societies in their own context, Mediterranean exchange, change and
continuity, and perspective comparison. **Age fit:** 12–13 (grade 7),
adult-guided; the adult previews every video for ads, age suitability,
and accuracy, and pre-selects all real-world examples. Religious content
is presented descriptively (beliefs and practices in their own context);
the adult previews all material for tone before assigning.

Web references below were checked 2026-10-19 unless noted. Videos are
**clearly labeled search links, not verified videos** — no title, runtime,
or channel is claimed. The adult opens the search, previews the top
results, and picks one that matches the lesson. Every core objective has a
free, no-account route.

## Focused queries

Use these when you need more than the curated links. Prefer museum, archive,
university, and government (.gov) results.

1. `Hagia Sophia Justinian 537 museum` — Lesson 01 background: the Great
   Church as law-and-faith statement (adult background; reconcile any
   tourist-site claims with a museum source).
2. `Corpus Juris Civilis Tribonian summary` — Lesson 01: what Justinian's
   code organized and why it mattered (adult background).
3. `House of Wisdom Baghdad translation movement` — Lesson 02: the
   translation movement and the scholars named in the unit.
4. `Cairo Geniza merchant letters` — Lesson 02, Practice Set B4: the real
   document kind the model letter imitates (adult background).
5. `Toledo translators 12th century Gerard of Cremona` — Lesson 03: the
   Latin translation movement (adult background; verify any biographical
   claims before assigning).
6. `al-Idrisi world map 1154 Roger II Sicily` — Lesson 03 extension: a real
   medieval map made at a cross-cultural court.
