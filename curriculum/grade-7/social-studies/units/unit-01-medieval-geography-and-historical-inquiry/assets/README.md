## silk-road-oasis-market.png

- **File:** `silk-road-oasis-market.png` (PNG, 1600×1066, generated
  2026-10-13, downscaled from the 1920×1280 original for
  repository size; no content altered)
- **Prompt (verbatim):** "Educational illustration for a middle-school
  history lesson: a medieval Silk Road caravan arriving at a desert oasis
  market town around the year 1200. Camels with loaded packs, merchants in
  varied robes and turbans trading goods, fabric tents and awnings,
  clay-brick flat-roofed buildings, palm trees, a well, distant hazy
  mountains, warm late-afternoon daylight. Painterly but clear and
  readable for 12-year-olds. No text, no captions, no labels, no modern
  objects, no people who look like photographs of real individuals."
- **Tool:** built-in image generation (media pipeline), 2026-10-13.
- **Learning purpose:** used in
  [Lesson 02](../lessons/lesson-02-source-kinds-of-the-medieval-world.md)
  as the object of the **reconstruction test** — learners observe the
  image, then learn it was generated in 2026, and practice classifying it
  as a *modern reconstruction*, not a historical source. The lesson's
  thesis ("pretty is not the same as true") depends on this image being
  AI-generated.
- **Factual review (2026-10-13):** inspected at full size. No text, no
  modern objects, no real individuals depicted. Scene is a plausible
  *imagined* oasis market (camels, robes, well, palm trees, clay-brick
  buildings) — but it depicts **no real place or event** and must never
  be presented as evidence of one. Labeled "AI illustration generated
  2026" in the lesson, the caption, and the alt text.
- **Caption:** *AI illustration generated 2026 for this lesson — a modern
  reconstruction, not historical evidence.*
- **Alt text:** "Painterly scene of a desert oasis market; three loaded
  camels in the foreground; merchants in long robes trading cloth,
  pottery, and sacks of goods at stalls; a stone well with a wooden
  frame; palm trees; flat-roofed clay-brick buildings; hazy mountains
  behind."
- **Text-only alternative:** provided in Lesson 02 (a written description
  carrying everything needed to answer the lesson's questions).
- **Known limitations:** stylized painterly rendering; architectural and
  clothing details are generic "medieval" signifiers, not documented
  reconstructions of any specific culture; must not be used as evidence
  for any historical claim.
