## Part 1 — Questions (3 points)

1. Write one **supporting** question about medieval trade, and name the
   source kind that could answer it. (2 pts)
2. Explain in one sentence why *"Was the medieval world fair?"* is not a
   supporting question. (1 pt)

## Part 2 — Maps (4 points)

*Use the corridor schematic
([trade-corridors-schematic.svg](../assets/trade-corridors-schematic.svg))

3. Name the four trade corridors and one city on each. (2 pts)

## Part 3 — Sources (4 points)

6. Classify each by kind: (a) a coin stamped with a ruler's name; (b) the
   AI-generated market illustration in Lesson 02; (c) a 2026 textbook
   chapter on the Silk Road. (1.5 pts)
7. A traveler's account says a desert well was dry. Name **two**
   evaluation tests from Lesson 04 and apply each to this claim in one
   sentence. (2 pts)
8. A classmate cites the Lesson 02 market illustration as proof that
   medieval markets were colorful. What is wrong with this, in one
   sentence? (0.5 pt)

## Part 4 — Review seeds (4 points)

*Use [`resources/world_facts.md`](../../../../../../resources/world_facts.md)
with the corrected headings below.*
