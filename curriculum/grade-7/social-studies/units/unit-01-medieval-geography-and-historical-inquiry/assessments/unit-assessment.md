# Unit Assessment — U01 culminating assessment

**Unit:** [U01 — Medieval Geography: Sources and Historical Inquiry](../README.md) · **Session 15 (Week 6)**
**Time:** ~45 minutes · **Adult:** supervises; scores with [answer-key.md](../answer-key.md) §UA the same day.
Covers Objectives 1–5. The learner may use the corridor schematic, the
practice map, and the vocabulary list — but not the answer key or the
teacher guide.

## Task 1 — Inquiry (Objectives 1, 3) — 6 points

Read this compelling question: *"Why did Timbuktu become a center of
learning in the medieval world?"*

a. Write **three supporting questions** that help answer it. (3 pts)
b. For each supporting question, name the source kind you would check
   FIRST and one limitation you would work around. (3 pts)

## Task 2 — Map evidence (Objective 2) — 6 points

*Use the practice map
([oasis-town-practice-map.svg](../assets/oasis-town-practice-map.svg)).*

a. A messenger must run from the east gate to the well. What is the
   straight-line distance, and how do you know? (2 pts)
b. Write **two evidence claims** the map proves (cite the map tool for
   each: grid, scale bar, key, or compass rose). (2 pts)
c. Write **two things** the map cannot show, and for each name what source
   kind COULD show it. (2 pts)

## Task 3 — Source evaluation (Objectives 3, 4) — 8 points

Read the two model texts from Lesson 04 again (merchant's note; Chronicle
of Qasr Oasis, 1187).

a. Classify each by kind. (1 pt)
b. Run **three** of the five evaluation tests on the chronicle entry,
   one sentence each. (3 pts)
c. The merchant's note says water at the third station costs double.
   Name **two independent source kinds** that could corroborate this, and
   say exactly what you would look for in each. (2 pts)
d. Infer the chronicle's most likely **audience** and cite the clue. (1 pt)
e. In one sentence, explain why the Lesson 02 market illustration cannot
   corroborate either text. (1 pt)

## Task 4 — Change and continuity (Objective 5) — 5 points

*"By 1400, paper was made from Samarkand to Spain, and books cost far less
to produce than in 800 — yet people still wrote chronicles, contracts, and
holy books, just as before."*

a. Classify this development as change, continuity, or both, with
   reasoning. (2 pts)
b. Name **two source kinds** that could evidence the "books cost less"
   part of the claim, and one limitation of each. (2 pts)
c. In one sentence: why is "both" often the most honest verdict? (1 pt)

**Total: 25 points.** Score guide: 21–25 = secure, proceed to U02;
15–20 = targeted reteach on the missed objective(s) before U02; below 15 =
reteach the strand(s) with the adult using the Session 13 review
materials, then re-attempt the missed tasks only.
