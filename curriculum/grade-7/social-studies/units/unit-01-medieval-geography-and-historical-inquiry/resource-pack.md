# U01 Resource Pack

**Unit:** [U01 — Medieval Geography: Sources and Historical Inquiry](README.md)

**Goal:** Give the guiding adult verified, age-appropriate starting points
for every core objective of this unit — framing historical questions,
classifying medieval source kinds, reading and building maps of the trade
corridors, evaluating sources, and weighing change against continuity.
**Age fit:** 12–13 (grade 7), adult-guided; the adult previews every video
for ads, age suitability, and accuracy, and pre-selects all real-world
examples.

All links below were checked 2026-10-13 unless noted. Videos are
**clearly labeled search links, not verified videos** — no title, runtime,
or channel is claimed. The adult opens the search, previews the top
results, and picks one that matches the lesson. Every core objective has a
free, no-account route.

## Focused queries

Use these when you need more than the curated links. Prefer museum, archive,
university, and government (.gov) results.

1. `site:loc.gov classroom materials maps primary source analysis` —
   Library of Congress map sets and analysis routines.
2. `site:archives.gov docsteach document analysis worksheet` — National
   Archives media-type analysis worksheets.
3. `UNESCO Silk Roads trade routes map cities` — corridor background for
   the adult (Lessons 03–05, project).
4. `Catalan Atlas 1375 medieval map museum` — a real medieval map to
   contrast with the unit's schematic (Lesson 03 extension).
5. `map projection distortion explained middle school` — orange-peel-style
   projection explainers to supplement Lesson 03, Part C (verify any
   classroom demo before assigning).
