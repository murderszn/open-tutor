# U03 Resource Pack

**Unit:** [U03 — African Kingdoms: Trade, Knowledge, and Cultural Exchange](README.md)

**Goal:** Give the guiding adult verified, age-appropriate starting points
for every core objective of this unit — the West African gold–salt
trade, Timbuktu's learning culture, Swahili monsoon trade, Great
Zimbabwe's stone city, and multiple-cause historical explanation.
**Age fit:** 12–13 (grade 7), adult-guided; the adult previews every
video for ads, age suitability, and accuracy, and pre-selects all
real-world examples.

All links below were checked 2026-10-26 unless noted. Videos are
**clearly labeled search links, not verified videos** — no title,
runtime, or channel is claimed. The adult opens the search, previews
the top results, and picks one that matches the lesson. Every core
objective has a free, no-account route.

## Focused queries

Use these when you need more than the curated links. Prefer museum,
archive, university, and government (.gov) results.

1. `UNESCO Timbuktu Sankore manuscripts Ahmed Baba Institute` —
   manuscript preservation background for the adult (Lesson 02).
2. `Mansa Musa hajj 1324 Cairo gold chronicles` — source-kind
   background: what chroniclers reported vs. what is counted (Lesson 01).
3. `Kilwa Kisiwani Great Mosque coral stone UNESCO` — coastal
   architecture and trade background (Lesson 03).
4. `Great Zimbabwe dry stone Great Enclosure Conical Tower debate` —
   what is settled vs. debated about the site (Lesson 04).
5. `trans-Saharan trade gold salt Taghaza caravan` — route background
   for the adult (Lesson 01, project).
6. `Swahili coast monsoon dhow trade winds explained students` —
   classroom explainers for the monsoon round trip (Lesson 03).
