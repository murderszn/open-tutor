# Week-04 Quiz — Unit 03 (formative, review week)

**Unit:** [U03 — African Kingdoms: Trade, Knowledge, and Cultural Exchange](../README.md) · **Session:** 13 of 16
**Adult:** this quiz is formative. Score it with the learner, correct
every miss on the spot, and re-teach any objective below 70% before the
unit assessment. Answers are in [answer-key.md](../answer-key.md) only.

1. Salt traveled in which direction across the Sahara, and gold in
   which direction? Name one city where they met.
2. Which kingdom came first — Ghana, Mali, or Songhai — and what was
   its kings' main source of wealth from trade?
3. What did scholars at Sankoré in Timbuktu study? Name two subjects.
4. What is a monsoon, and why did it set the calendar for Swahili
   coast trade?
5. Name two goods exported from the Swahili coast and two goods
   imported to it.
6. What building technique was used at Great Zimbabwe, and what does
   the Great Enclosure's wall prove about its builders?
7. What is one thing Great Zimbabwe's walls cannot prove?
8. Classify as change, continuity, or both, with one reason: "Timbuktu
   remained a market city under both Mali and Songhai."
9. Name two causes of Songhai's fall, and one source kind behind each.
10. A classmate says, "The Conical Tower at Great Zimbabwe was the
    king's grain store." Why is that statement unsafe as written?
