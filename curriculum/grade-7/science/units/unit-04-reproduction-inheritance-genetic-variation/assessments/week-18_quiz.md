# Formative Quiz — Week 18 (Unit 04)

**Adult-scored; 10 items, 20 minutes.** Score with the learner,
then reteach any objective below 8/10 using the teacher-guide
prompts. Keys in [answer-key.md](../answer-key.md#week-18-quiz).
This quiz is a practice check, not a standardized or validated
assessment.

**Q1.** A strawberry plantlet grows from a runner of its parent
plant. Is this asexual or sexual reproduction? State how many
parents are involved and whether the plantlet's genetic information
is identical to or varied from the parent's.

**Q2.** A pea body cell has 14 chromosomes. After mitosis, how many
cells are there and how many chromosomes does each have? After
meiosis, how many cells are there and how many chromosomes does
each have?

**Q3.** Put these in order and state what each step does: trait,
protein, gene.

**Q4.** A mutation changes a gene but the protein works exactly as
before. Is the mutation harmful, beneficial, or neutral? Give one
reason.

**Q5.** Cross Rr × Rr in pea seed shape (R = round, dominant;
r = wrinkled, recessive). Give the genotype ratio and the phenotype
ratio.

**Q6.** True or false, with one reason: "A Punnett square that
predicts 3 round : 1 wrinkled guarantees that any four seeds will
be three round and one wrinkled."

**Q7.** A plant is crossed with rr and half its offspring are
wrinkled. The plant itself is round-seeded. What is its genotype?
Explain in one sentence.

**Q8.** Sort each trait as inherited or acquired: (a) a pea plant's
seed shape; (b) a scar from a fall. Give the test you used, in one
sentence.

**Q9.** Use these day-14 means: Variety A full light 19.0 cm, A low
light 25.0 cm, Variety B full light 14.4 cm, B low light 19.4 cm.
State one sentence for the variety effect and one sentence for the
light effect, each with numbers.

**Q10.** A bird builds a nest in a thorny bush before laying eggs.
Explain how this behavior affects the probability of successful
reproduction, naming the mechanism in one sentence.
