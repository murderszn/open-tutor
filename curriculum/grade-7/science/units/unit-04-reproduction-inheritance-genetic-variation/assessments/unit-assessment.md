# Unit Assessment — Unit 04: Reproduction, Inheritance, and Genetic Variation

**Adult-scored; about 45 minutes.** Keys and scoring guidance in
[answer-key.md](../answer-key.md#unit-assessment). This is a
classroom-style unit check, not a standardized or validated
assessment. Learners may use their chromosome strips and allele
cards but not the answer key.

## Part A — Short answers (8 items, 2 points each)

**A1.** Compare asexual and sexual reproduction in four sentences:
number of parents, source of genetic information, identical vs.
varied offspring, and one example of each.

**A2.** A pea body cell (14 chromosomes) divides once by mitosis
and once by meiosis (two separate cells). For each division, state
the number of resulting cells, the chromosomes per cell, and
whether the cells are identical or varied.

**A3.** A gene's instructions change, the protein it controls stops
working, and a seedling grows pale and small. Trace the chain
(gene → protein → trait) in three sentences, then judge the
mutation harmful, beneficial, or neutral in a sunlit garden, with
one reason.

**A4.** A mutation changes a flower's color. In one meadow
pollinators strongly prefer the new color; in another they visit
both colors equally. Judge the mutation in each meadow, with one
reason each. What does this show about judging mutations?

**A5.** Cross RR × Rr (pea seed shape, R round dominant). Show the
four boxes and give the genotype and phenotype ratios.

**A6.** Cross Rr × rr. Show the four boxes and give the genotype
and phenotype ratios. In a group of 60 offspring from this cross,
how many wrinkled offspring are expected? Show the multiplication.

**A7.** Sort each trait as inherited or acquired, with one reason
each: (a) a dog's coat color; (b) a muscle built by exercise;
(c) a plant's flower color; (d) a stem bent toward a window.

**A8.** A plant species has hard-shelled nuts that squirrels bury.
Explain how this structure affects the probability of successful
reproduction, naming the mechanism, in two sentences.

## Part B — Data and explanation (14 points)

**B1. Growth data (8 points).** A second class ran the same
investigation with two new varieties, C and D. Day-14 heights (cm),
four plants per group:

- Variety C, sunny: 12, 13, 11, 12
- Variety C, shade: 16, 17, 15, 16
- Variety D, sunny: 9, 10, 8, 9
- Variety D, shade: 13, 12, 14, 13

(a) Find each group's mean height. Show each addition and division.
(b) State the variety effect in one sentence with numbers.
(c) State the light/shade effect in one sentence with numbers.
(d) The shade plants are recorded as paler and thinner. A learner
says "shade is better because the plants are taller." Correct this
in two sentences.

**B2. CER paragraph (6 points).** Answer: "Do genetic factors,
environmental factors, or both influence the growth of organisms?"
Write a labeled CER paragraph (claim, evidence, reasoning) using
the B1 means. A complete paragraph claims both factors, cites at
least two means as evidence, and reasons that the same variety
pattern repeats across conditions and the same light pattern
repeats across varieties.

## Scoring

- Part A: 16 points (2 per item; 1 point for a partly correct
  answer that names the right process but misses a number or a
  reason).
- Part B: 14 points (B1: means 4 points — 1 per group; effects
  2 points; correction 2 points. B2: 6 points — claim 1, evidence
  2, reasoning 2, labels 1).
- Total: 30 points. 24/30 or above with no Part A item at zero
  indicates readiness for Unit 05; otherwise reteach the weakest
  objective with the teacher-guide prompts.
