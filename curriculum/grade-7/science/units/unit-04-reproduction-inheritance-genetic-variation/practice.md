# Practice Bank — Unit 04: Reproduction, Inheritance, and Genetic Variation

24 items across the unit's practice sessions. Keys in
[answer-key.md](answer-key.md#practice-bank). Items marked ★ are
revised from the legacy `grade-7/stem/assignments/reproduction.md`
tasks, rewritten with new contexts and full keys.

## A. Asexual and sexual reproduction (Sessions 2, 12, 14)

**P1. ★** A strawberry plant sends out a runner that roots and
grows into a new plant. A pea plant produces seeds in a pod. For
each case state: number of parents, whether the offspring's genetic
information is identical or varied, and the reproduction type.

**P2. ★** Give one real example of asexual reproduction and one of
sexual reproduction (neither may be the strawberry or the pea), and
explain a benefit of genetic variation in a changing environment.

**P3.** A bacterium divides into two bacteria. A coral releases
eggs and sperm into the water, and the fertilized eggs grow into
new corals. Which case is asexual, which is sexual, and what is the
genetic consequence of each?

**P4.** A learner says: "Asexual reproduction has no parent, so the
offspring are weaker." Correct both errors in two sentences.

**P5.** Two rose bushes are crossed and their seeds planted. The
seedlings differ from both parents and from each other. Explain why,
using the words "combination" and "variation."

## B. Mitosis and meiosis (Sessions 6, 12, 14)

**P6. ★** In 6–8 sentences, compare mitosis and meiosis covering
purpose, number of divisions, chromosome number, and whether the
cells are identical or varied. Use pea numbers (body cells 14,
gametes 7).

**P7.** A pea body cell (14 chromosomes) divides by meiosis. How
many cells result and how many chromosomes does each have? Show
the division count and the halving.

**P8.** A learner's diagram shows mitosis producing four cells with
7 chromosomes each. Name the two errors and give the correct
numbers for mitosis.

**P9.** Two pea gametes (7 chromosomes each) combine at
fertilization. What chromosome number does the offspring's body
cell have? Explain why the number does not double every generation.

**P10.** A gardener takes a cutting from a rose bush and roots it.
Does the new plant need meiosis? Explain in two sentences, naming
the division process that actually makes the new plant's cells.

## C. Genes, proteins, and mutations (Sessions 6, 12, 14)

**P11.** Put these in order and explain each arrow in one sentence:
protein, gene, trait.

**P12.** A mutation changes a gene, but the protein it controls
works exactly as before and no trait changes. Is this mutation
harmful, beneficial, or neutral? Explain in two sentences.

**P13.** A modeled mutation makes a plant's flowers white instead of
purple. In a meadow where pollinators visit purple flowers far more
often, judge the effect. In a meadow where pollinators visit both
colors equally, judge it again. Give one reason for each judgment.

**P14.** Correct this claim in two sentences: "A plant mutated
because it needed white flowers, and all mutations damage the
plant."

## D. Punnett squares and probability (Sessions 9, 12, 14)

**P15.** Cross RR × Rr in pea seed shape (R = round, dominant;
r = wrinkled, recessive). Show the four boxes and give the genotype
ratio and the phenotype ratio.

**P16.** Cross Rr × Rr. Give the genotype ratio (as a ratio and as
fractions) and the phenotype ratio (as a ratio and as percentages).

**P17.** In a group of 200 offspring from Rr × Rr parents, how many
wrinkled offspring are expected? Show the multiplication and explain
in one sentence why the real count could differ.

**P18.** A round-seeded plant of unknown genotype is crossed with a
wrinkled plant (rr). Half the offspring are wrinkled. What is the
round parent's genotype? Explain in two sentences.

**P19.** A learner says: "My Punnett square says 3 round : 1
wrinkled, so my four seeds will be exactly three round and one
wrinkled." Explain the error in two sentences.

## E. Growth, traits, and reproductive success (Sessions 12, 14)

**P20.** Sort each trait as inherited or acquired, with the test
for each: a pea plant's seed shape; a scar from a fall; a dog's
coat color; a person's ability to play a song learned by practice.

**P21.** Use the project's practice dataset means — Variety A full
light 19.0 cm, A low light 25.0 cm, Variety B full light 14.4 cm,
B low light 19.4 cm — to write two sentences: one stating the
variety effect with numbers, one stating the light effect with
numbers.

**P22.** A learner concludes: "Low light is better for plants
because they grow taller in low light." Correct this conclusion in
two sentences, using color and sturdiness as well as height.

**P23.** A bird species builds nests in thorny bushes. Explain how
this behavior affects the probability of successful reproduction,
naming the mechanism (protection from what, for whom).

**P24.** A plant species has bright, nectar-rich flowers. Explain
how this structure affects the probability of successful
reproduction, naming the mechanism (who is attracted, what they
carry, what results).
