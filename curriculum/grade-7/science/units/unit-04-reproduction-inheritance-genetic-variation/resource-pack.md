# Resource Pack — Unit 04: Reproduction, Inheritance, and Genetic Variation

**Summary (adult planning judgment):** Grade 7 learners meeting
reproduction, inheritance, and mutation do best when short videos
*supplement* — never replace — the illustration comparison, the
chromosome-strip models, the Punnett-square and coin-toss work, and
the growth investigation. The resources below support the unit's six
objectives: asexual vs. sexual reproduction; mitosis vs. meiosis;
genes, proteins, and mutations; Punnett prediction; growth factors;
and behaviors/structures that affect reproductive success. The NSTA
listing, the Britannica page, and the Wikipedia Mitosis page were
verified 2026-11-01; the remaining web references are carried from
the legacy reproduction assignment and the adult checks each link
before assigning it. Video entries are labeled search links. At
least one free, no-account route supports every core objective.
