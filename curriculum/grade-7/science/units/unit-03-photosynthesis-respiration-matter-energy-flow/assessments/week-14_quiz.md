# Formative Quiz — Week 14 (Unit 03)

**Adult-scored; 10 items, 20 minutes.** Score with the learner,
then reteach any objective below 8/10 using the teacher-guide
prompts. Keys in [answer-key.md](../answer-key.md#week-14-quiz).
This quiz is a practice check, not a standardized or validated
assessment.

**Q1.** Write the photosynthesis equation in symbols. Label the
reactants and the products.

**Q2.** In 6CO₂, how many carbon atoms and oxygen atoms are there
in total? Show the counting.

**Q3.** A tree gains 100 kilograms of dry mass in a year while its
soil loses almost nothing. Where did most of that mass come from?
Name the substance and where the plant got it.

**Q4.** Name the three fates of the sugar a plant makes, with one
example of each.

**Q5.** Write the cellular respiration equation in words. Name
the organelle where most of it happens.

**Q6.** True or false, with one reason: "Plants photosynthesize
during the day and respire only at night."

**Q7.** A rabbit eats grass; a fox eats the rabbit. Draw the food
chain with arrows pointing from food toward eater, then state
whether matter or energy is the one that cycles — and why the
other one does not.

**Q8.** A food web has grass, rabbits, mice, foxes, and hawks.
The foxes disappear. Predict one short-term consequence for the
rabbits and one longer-term consequence for the grass. Justify
each in one sentence.

**Q9.** A learner's model shows energy arrows in a circle:
grass → rabbit → fox → grass. Name the error and correct it in
one sentence.

**Q10.** Write a three-sentence CER paragraph answering: "Why is
photosynthesis the entry point for energy into a meadow
ecosystem?" Label your claim, evidence, and reasoning.
