# Resource Pack — Unit 01: Scientific Evidence, Microscopy, and Cell Models

**Summary (adult planning judgment):** Grade 7 learners building
evidence habits (observation vs. inference, SI measurement) and meeting
cell theory for the first time do best when videos *supplement* — never
replace — hands-on sorting, measuring, microscope (or micrograph) work,
and model building. The resources below support the unit's five
objectives: scientific evidence, measurement, microscopy and scale,
the "living things are made of cells" investigation, and explanatory
cell models. All external links were checked 2026-10-12 unless marked
as open search links or carried over from the track's 2026-10-05 audit.

## Web references (verified 2026-10-12 unless noted)

   <https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Cell_theory> — opened and read
   2026-10-12. The three tenets, Hooke (1665), Leeuwenhoek (1670s,
   ~270×), Schleiden/Schwann (1839), Virchow (1855, *Omnis cellula e
   cellula*). Reputable tertiary overview; supports Lesson 03's
   history. Free, no account.

   <https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Plant_cell> — opened 2026-10-12.
   Cell wall, chloroplasts, central vacuole, and tissue types.

   deeper than the unit needs — adult should steer to the Structure
   section.)
