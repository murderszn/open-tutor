# Formative Quiz — Unit 01 (Week 4 / Session 15)

**Instructions:** Answer in your notebook. Show units on every number.
For explanations, 1–2 sentences. This quiz checks the measurement and
lab-skills banks (Sessions 3–4 and 7); it does not cover microscopy or
cell models — those are on the unit assessment. Self-score with the
[answer key](../answer-key.md#formative-quiz-week-04) afterward, then
reteach any missed objective before Session 16.

**Objectives checked:** 1 (evidence), 2 (measurement, data tables,
accuracy/precision, variables).

1. Sort each statement: observation (O) or inference (I).
   (a) "The liquid is blue." (b) "The liquid is blue because someone
   added food coloring." (c) "The scale reads 45 g."
2. Name the SI unit for (a) length, (b) mass, (c) temperature.
3. Convert 2.4 km to meters. Show the multiplication.
4. A cyclist rides 300 m in 20 s. What is the average speed in m/s?
5. A learner measures a pencil three times: 15.1 cm, 15.0 cm,
   15.2 cm. The true length is 15.1 cm. Are the measurements accurate?
   Are they precise? Explain each in one sentence.
6. A balance reads 102 g, 98 g, 105 g for a 100 g standard mass. Is
   the problem accuracy or precision? What should the learner do next?
7. Fix two defects in this data table:

   | | temp |
   |---|---|
   | one | 21 |
   | two | 23 |

8. In an experiment testing whether the amount of water affects how
   tall a bean plant grows in two weeks: name the independent
   variable, the dependent variable, and one variable to control.
9. Give two sources of error when timing a pendulum with a stopwatch,
   and one way to reduce each.
10. What three things must the conclusion of a lab report do?

**Scoring:** 10 items, 2 points each (1 for the answer, 1 for the
explanation/unit where asked) = 20 points. 16+ = ready for the unit
assessment; 12–15 = review the missed items with the answer key, then
re-attempt similar items from [practice.md](../practice.md); below 12
= reteach Lessons 01–02 before Session 16.
