# Formative Quiz — Week 23 (Unit 05)

**Adult-scored; 10 items, 20 minutes.** Score with the learner,
then reteach any objective below 8/10 using the teacher-guide
prompts. Keys in [answer-key.md](../answer-key.md#week-23-quiz).
This quiz is a practice check, not a standardized or validated
assessment.

**Q1.** A cliff shows three layers: top — bird bones; middle —
dinosaur bone; bottom — trilobite. List the fossils from oldest
to youngest and state the rule you used.

**Q2.** On the paper timeline (2 m = 4.6 billion years), the first
life appears about 3.8 billion years ago. How far from the left
end (Earth's formation) does it sit? Show the calculation.

**Q3.** A human arm and a bat wing share the same bones in the
same order but do different jobs. Are they homologous or
analogous structures? Give one reason.

**Q4.** Whales have small hip bones buried inside their bodies.
What kind of structure is this, and what is it a leftover of?

**Q5.** In Kettlewell's experiments, 40% of dark moths and 19% of
light moths were recaptured in polluted woods. Which form
survived better there, and what does that show about camouflage?

**Q6.** A moth population has 1,000 moths. Before
industrialization about 2% are dark; later about 85% are dark.
How many dark moths is that at each time?

**Q7.** True or false, with one reason: "Individual moths turned
dark because they needed to hide from birds."

**Q8.** Name two features that fish, chicken, and human embryos
all share early in development. What do those shared features
show about their relationship?

**Q9.** Broccoli was bred from wild mustard by selecting for big
flower buds. What part of the plant was selected, and who did
the selecting — the environment or humans?

**Q10.** A learner says: "Evolution means species get better and
better until they are perfect." Correct the error in two
sentences, using the peppered moth as evidence.
