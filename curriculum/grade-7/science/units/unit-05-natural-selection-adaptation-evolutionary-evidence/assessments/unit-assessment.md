# Unit Assessment — Unit 05: Natural Selection, Adaptation, and Evolutionary Evidence

**Adult-scored; about 45 minutes.** Keys and scoring guidance in
[answer-key.md](../answer-key.md#unit-assessment). This is a
classroom-style unit check, not a standardized or validated
assessment. Learners may use their timeline, diagrams, and the
Kettlewell chart, but not the answer key. Species names and dates
are given in the questions; recalling them from memory is not
assessed.

## Part A — Short answers (8 items, 2 points each)

**A1.** A cliff shows four layers. Order the fossils from oldest
to youngest and name one group that disappears from the record.

| Layer | Fossils found |
|---|---|
| Top (youngest) | Horse teeth, grass seeds |
| Second | Dinosaur bone, ammonite shell |
| Third | Fish fossil, fern fronds |
| Bottom (oldest) | Trilobite, brachiopod shells |

**A2.** Sort each pair as homologous or analogous, with one reason
each: (a) a whale flipper and a human arm; (b) a bird wing and an
insect wing.

**A3.** Tiktaalik is a fossil animal about 375 million years old.
Name two fish features and two four-legged-animal features it
has, and state what "transitional form" means in one sentence.

**A4.** Fish, chicken, and human embryos all share the same early
pattern. Name the shared early features, name one way their later
development differs, and state in one sentence what the pattern
shows about their relationship.

**A5.** A beach of light sand is covered by dark volcanic ash. A
mouse population there has light and dark mice. Predict which
form will become more common over the next ten generations, and
explain the prediction with the four steps of natural selection
in four sentences.

**A6.** A beetle population has 800 beetles. At first 30% are
spotted; after birds eat many plain beetles, 55% are spotted.
How many spotted beetles is that at each time? Show both
multiplications, and state the change in the number of spotted
beetles.

**A7.** Modern corn descends from teosinte, a Mexican grass with
tiny hard kernels. Name the wild ancestor, the trait humans
selected, who did the selecting, and the technology used
(selective breeding).

**A8.** Sort each as an adaptation or an acquired trait, with one
reason each: (a) a dark moth's camouflage on a sooty trunk;
(b) skin tanned by the sun; (c) a finch's deep beak in a drought;
(d) a scar from a fall.

## Part B — Data and explanation (14 points)

**B1. Trait-frequency data (8 points).** A class ran the paper-
moth simulation on a dark background and recorded these counts.
(The dataset is labeled practice data from the project.)

| Generation | Light moths | Dark moths | Total |
|---|---|---|---|
| 0 (start) | 20 | 20 | 40 |
| 1 | 24 | 36 | 60 |
| 2 | 34 | 66 | 100 |
| 3 | 54 | 126 | 180 |

(a) Find the dark percentage for Generation 1 and for
Generation 3. Show each division.
(b) State the trend across the four generations in one sentence
with numbers.
(c) If the background were cleaned back to light bark, predict
the direction of the dark percentage over the next generations
and give one reason.
(d) A learner says "the dark moths turned dark to match the
soot." Correct this in two sentences.

**B2. CER paragraph (6 points).** Answer: "How do fossils,
anatomy, embryos, and DNA together support the idea that living
things share common ancestors?" Write a labeled CER paragraph
(claim, evidence, reasoning) using at least two different lines
of evidence from this unit. A complete paragraph claims common
ancestry, cites at least two lines of evidence with a concrete
detail each, and reasons that independent lines of evidence
agreeing makes the conclusion stronger than any one line alone.

## Scoring

- Part A: 16 points (2 per item; 1 point for a partly correct
  answer that names the right process but misses a number or a
  reason).
- Part B: 14 points (B1: percentages 2 points — 1 per
  generation; trend 2 points; prediction 2 points; correction
  2 points. B2: 6 points — claim 1, evidence 2, reasoning 2,
  labels 1).
- Total: 30 points. 24/30 or above with no Part A item at zero
  indicates readiness for Unit 06; otherwise reteach the weakest
  objective with the teacher-guide prompts.
