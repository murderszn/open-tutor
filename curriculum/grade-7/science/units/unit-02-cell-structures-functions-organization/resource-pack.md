# Resource Pack — Unit 02: Cell Structures, Functions, and Organization

**Summary (adult planning judgment):** Grade 7 learners meeting
organelle function, membrane transport, and organization levels do
best when videos *supplement* — never replace — the kitchen-safe
diffusion/osmosis demonstrations, the specialized-cells matching
activity, and the five-level tracing work. The resources below support
the unit's five objectives: organelle whole-cell function, membrane
selectivity with diffusion/osmosis evidence, specialized cells and
tissues, levels of organization, and the cell-as-system argument. All
external links were checked 2026-10-19 unless marked as open search
links or carried over from the track's 2026-10-12 verification (noted).

   [Diffusion](https://phet.colorado.edu/sims/html/diffusion/latest/diffusion_en.html)
