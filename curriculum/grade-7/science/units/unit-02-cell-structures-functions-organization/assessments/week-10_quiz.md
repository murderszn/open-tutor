# Formative Quiz — Unit 02, Week 10

**Administer at Session 15.** 10 items, ~25 minutes, closed notes.
The adult scores with the [answer key](../answer-key.md#formative-quiz-week-10)
immediately after; items missed drive Session 16's reteach before the
unit assessment. This is a low-stakes check — it diagnoses, it does
not punish.

**Q1.** Name the organelle described in each case, and state its job
for the whole cell: (a) holds the DNA instructions; (b) releases
energy from food; (c) captures light energy in plant cells;
(d) controls what enters and leaves the cell.

**Q2.** A plant cell is placed in a dark closet for a week (with
water). Which organelle can no longer do its job? What must the
cell's mitochondria do instead?

**Q3.** Define *diffusion*. Then define *osmosis* in one sentence
that shows its relationship to diffusion.

**Q4.** A red blood cell is placed in pure water. Using the word
*osmosis*, predict what happens to the cell and explain why. (Track
the water, not the cell's contents.)

**Q5.** Look at the four specialized cells in
[Lesson 03's illustration](../assets/specialized-cells-illustration.png)
(or its text alternative in Lesson 03). Which cell is specialized for
absorbing water from soil? Cite one shape feature as evidence.

**Q6.** Put these in order from smallest to largest: *organism,
tissue, organ system, cell, organ*. Then write the plant-column
example for each level from Lesson 04.

**Q7.** A learner says, "The nucleus is the most important part of
the cell because it controls everything." Write a two-sentence
evaluation of this claim using systems reasoning.

**Q8.** Data task (fictional practice dataset): two identical potato
pieces, each 40 mm long. After 30 minutes, the piece in plain water
measures 42 mm and the piece in salt water measures 36 mm. (a) What
is the length change for each piece? (b) Which piece lost water?
(c) In which direction did water move by osmosis for the salt-water
piece?

**Q9.** A muscle cell and a leaf mesophyll cell carry different
structures (e.g., only one has chloroplasts). Explain in two
sentences why their structures differ, using the idea of
specialization.

**Q10.** Name one strength and one limit of your labeled cell diagram
as a *model* of a real cell.
