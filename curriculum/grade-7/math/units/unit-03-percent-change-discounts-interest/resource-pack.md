# Resource Pack — Unit 03: Percent Change, Discounts, and Simple Interest

**Summary (adult planning judgment):** Grade 7 percent instruction
works best when every video or article supplements, never replaces,
the unit's one discipline: name the base before computing. The
resources below support the unit's five objectives: percent increase
and decrease (Lesson 01), discounts and markups (Lesson 02), tax, tips,
commissions, and fees (Lesson 03), simple interest (Lesson 04), and
percent error (Lesson 05), with multi-step synthesis in Lesson 06. All
external links were checked 2026-10-25; unverified items are labeled
as open search links. All prices, rates, and bills in this unit are
fictional practice data.
