# Unit Assessment — Unit 03: Percent Change, Discounts, and Simple Interest

**Unit:** [Unit 03](../README.md) · **Objectives 1–5** · **Standard 7.RP.A.3**
**Session:** 16 · **Time:** 45 minutes · **Calculator:** checking only
**Submit with this assessment:** the [Back-to-School Bundle Comparison](../project.md)

Show your work. For every percent, name the base it acts on. Answers
are in the separate [answer key](../answer-key.md).

1. A price falls from $140 to $105. What is the percent decrease?
2. A $96 bag is 25% off. What is the sale price?
3. A watch costs a shop $80. The shop marks it up 40%. What is the
   selling price?
4. A $90 dinner has 8% tax and a 15% tip on the pre-tax amount. What
   is the total?
5. Deposit $1,500 at 6% simple interest for 4 years. Find the interest
   and the total amount.
6. Estimate 250, actual 200. What is the percent error?
7. A $500 bike is 20% off, then 5% sales tax applies. What is the
   final price?
8. A salesperson earns 3% commission on a $2,500 sale. What is the
   commission?
9. A $75 item rises 20%, then falls 20%. What is the final price?
10. Deposit $640 at 2.5% simple interest for 2 years. How much
    interest is earned?
11. A $180 ticket carries a 15% fee. What is the final price?
12. **Error analysis.** Jordan says: "20% off, then 20% on, brings any
    price back to itself, because −20% + 20% = 0." Use a $100 example
    to show whether Jordan is right or wrong, and explain the mistake
    in one or two sentences.
