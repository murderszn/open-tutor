# Week-14 Formative Quiz — Unit 03

**Unit:** [Unit 03](../README.md) · **Objectives 1–5** · **Standard 7.RP.A.3**
**Session:** 15 · **Time:** 45 minutes · **Calculator:** checking only

Show your work. For every percent, name the base it acts on. Answers
are in the separate [answer key](../answer-key.md).

1. A price rises from $80 to $100. What is the percent increase?
2. A $60 jacket is 20% off. What is the sale price?
3. A $50 meal has 10% tax and a 20% tip on the pre-tax amount. What is
   the total?
4. Deposit $800 at 5% simple interest for 3 years. How much interest
   is earned?
5. Estimate 60, actual 50. What is the percent error?
6. A $200 item increases by 15%. What is the new price?
7. A salesperson earns 7% commission on a $1,400 sale. What is the
   commission?
8. A $120 item falls 10%, then rises 10%. What is the final price?
9. Deposit $300 at 4% simple interest for 6 months. How much interest
   is earned?
10. A measured length is 96 cm; the actual length is 120 cm. What is
    the percent error?
