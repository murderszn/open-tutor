# Practice — Unit 03: Percent Change, Discounts, and Simple Interest

**Unit:** [Unit 03](README.md) · **Objectives 1–5** · **Standard 7.RP.A.3**

Work by hand; use a calculator only to check. For every item, write
the base each percent acts on. Answers are in the separate
[answer key](answer-key.md) — never beside the questions.

## Session 2 — Percent change (Lesson 01)

**P1.** A price rises from $60 to $75. Find the percent increase.
**P2.** A price falls from $150 to $120. Find the percent decrease.
**P3.** An $80 jacket increases in price by 30%. What is the new price?
**P4.** A population falls from 500 to 425. Find the percent decrease.

## Session 4 — Discounts and markups (Lesson 02)

**P5.** A $120 coat is 40% off. What is the sale price?
**P6.** A lamp costs a shop $60. The shop marks it up 30%. What is the
selling price?
**P7.** A $240 bike is 25% off. What is the sale price?
**P8.** A $45 game is 20% off. What is the sale price?

## Session 6 — Tax, tips, commissions, fees (Lesson 03)

**P9.** An $80 meal has 5% tax and a 15% tip on the pre-tax amount.
Find the total.
**P10.** A salesperson earns 5% commission on a $900 sale. Find the
commission.
**P11.** A $250 ticket carries a 10% fee. What is the final price?
**P12.** A $64 shirt is 50% off, then 5% sales tax applies. Find the
final price.

## Session 8 — Simple interest (Lesson 04)

**P13.** Deposit $700 at 3% simple interest for 2 years. Find the
interest and the total amount.
**P14.** Deposit $1,600 at 4.5% simple interest for 2 years. Find the
interest.
**P15.** Deposit $350 at 6% simple interest for 1 year. Find the
interest.
**P16.** Deposit $2,400 at 5% simple interest for 6 months. Find the
interest.

## Session 10 — Percent error (Lesson 05)

**P17.** Estimate 90, actual 100. Find the percent error.
**P18.** A measured length is 199 cm; the actual length is 200 cm.
Find the percent error.
**P19.** Estimate 140, actual 112. Find the percent error.
**P20.** Estimate 55, actual 44. Find the percent error.
