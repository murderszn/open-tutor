# Resource Pack — Unit 01: Rational Number Operations

**Summary (adult planning judgment):** Grade 7 rational-number instruction
works best when every video or article supplements, never replaces,
number-line reasoning with opposite-quantities stories. The resources
below support the unit's three objectives: adding/subtracting rationals
with number-line diagrams and distance-as-absolute-value, multiplying and
dividing with property-justified sign rules and long-division decimal
forms, and multi-step real-world problems with estimation checks. All
external links were checked 2026-10-12; unverified items are labeled as
open search links.
