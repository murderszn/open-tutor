# Unit Assessment — Unit 01: Rational Number Operations (Session 16)

**Time:** 45 minutes · **Calculator:** checking only, after hand work is
shown · **Total:** 40 points.
Show your work on every task. A correct answer without work earns half
credit.

## Task 1 — Number-line reasoning (6 points)

Plot −6, −2.5, 0, 3/4, and 5 on a number line.
(a) Order them from least to greatest (2 pts).
(b) Name the opposite of −2.5 and find |−2.5| (2 pts).
(c) In one sentence, explain why −6 < −2.5 even though 6 > 2.5 (2 pts).

## Task 2 — Add and subtract (8 points)

Compute, showing the rewrite step for each subtraction:
(a) −17 + 12 (2 pts) · (b) −4 − (−9) (2 pts) · (c) 5/6 − 3/4 (2 pts) ·
(d) −2.4 + (−3.6) (2 pts).

## Task 3 — Multiply, divide, and justify (8 points)

(a) Compute (−8)(−6) and 54 ÷ (−9) (4 pts).
(b) In two to three sentences, explain why (−1)(−1) = 1 using the
distributive property, following the argument in Lesson 04 (4 pts).
(A correct rule statement without the property argument earns 1 pt.)

## Task 4 — Decimal forms (6 points)

(a) Convert −9/20 to a decimal and state whether it terminates or repeats
(3 pts).
(b) Convert 8/15 to a decimal with bar notation; show the remainder that
repeats (3 pts).

## Task 5 — Error analysis (6 points)

Maya's work: "Problem: (−3/4) ÷ (1/2). Solution: flip both fractions to
get (−4/3) × (2/1) = −8/3."
(a) What did Maya do wrong? (2 pts)
(b) Show the correct steps and answer. (2 pts)
(c) Give a one-sentence estimation check that would have caught her error.
(2 pts)

## Task 6 — Multi-step real-world problem (6 points)

A research submarine starts at −180 m. It rises 75 m, then descends at a
rate of 25 m per minute for 4 minutes.
(a) Write one expression for its final depth (2 pts).
(b) Compute the final depth with a reason check (2 pts).
(c) On the way back up it must stop every 50 m for safety checks, starting
from its final depth. The stops are at −150 m, −100 m, −50 m. Did it pass
a stop depth during the descent in part (a)? Explain using the numbers
(2 pts).
