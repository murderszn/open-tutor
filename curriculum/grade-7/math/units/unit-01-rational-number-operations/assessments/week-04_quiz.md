# Formative Quiz — Unit 01 (Session 15)

**Unit:** Rational Number Operations · **Time:** 30 minutes · **Calculator:**
checking only, after hand work is shown.
**Scoring:** 24 points (2 per item). 20+ = ready for the unit assessment;
16–19 = review the missed objective's lesson with the teacher guide;
below 16 = reteach before the assessment.

Write a reason check (estimate, number-line sketch, or one-sentence story
check) beside at least four items.

**Objective 1 — Add and subtract**

1. Compute: −14 + 9.
2. Rewrite 6 − (−11) as an addition and compute it.
3. Compute: −7/8 + 3/4. Show the common-denominator step.
4. What is the distance between −9 and 2? Write the absolute-value
   expression and evaluate it.

**Objective 2 — Multiply, divide, decimal forms**

5. Compute: (−12) × (−4) and (−12) ÷ (−4).
6. Simplify the complex fraction (−3/5)/(9/10). Show the
   division-as-multiplication step.
7. Convert 7/12 to a decimal. Is it terminating or repeating? Show the
   repeating remainder.
8. Which is greater: −0.8̄ or −0.81? Explain with a number-line reason.

**Objective 3 — Multi-step problems**

9. A hiker starts at elevation −25 m, climbs 60 m, then descends 90 m.
   Write one expression and find the final elevation.
10. The daily temperature changes (°C) for four days are −3, +5, −4, +2.
    (a) What is the total change? (b) What is the mean daily change?
11. **Error analysis.** "−5 − (−8) = −13 because subtracting makes it more
    negative." Identify the error and give the correct answer with the
    rewrite step.
12. A tank loses 1.5 liters per minute for 8 minutes, then 5 liters are
    added. Write one expression for the net change in liters and evaluate
    it.
