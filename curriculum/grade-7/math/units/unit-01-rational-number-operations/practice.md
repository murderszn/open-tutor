# Practice Bank — Unit 01: Rational Number Operations

Mixed practice for Sessions 2, 4, 6, 9, and 13. Items are grouped by
objective; work them in any order. Show a reason check (estimate,
number-line sketch, or story check) on every item. Full solutions are in
[answer-key.md](answer-key.md#practice-bank).

This bank consolidates and replaces the legacy drill sets
(`rational-numbers-negatives-quiz.md`,
`rational-numbers-negatives-quiz-set-02.md`,
`operations-with-fractions-quiz.md`, and the fraction items of
`ratios-rates-quiz.md`) with context variety and keys; see the unit README
for the audit trail.

## Objective 1 — Add and subtract (P1–P10)

**P1.** Compute: (a) −18 + 7; (b) −18 − 7; (c) 18 − (−7); (d) −18 − (−7).

**P2.** A penguin dives from the surface to −24 m, swims up 10 m, then
dives 15 m. Write the expression and find its final depth.

**P3.** Compute: (a) −5/6 + 1/2; (b) 7/8 − (−3/8); (c) −2.6 + 1.9;
(d) 4.05 − (−2.3).

**P4.** Find the distance between −13 and 5, and between −13 and −5. Show
the absolute-value expression for each.

**P5.** Fill the missing number: (a) −9 + __ = 4; (b) __ − (−6) = −10;
(c) 3/4 − __ = −1/4.

**P6.** Order from least to greatest: −2.5, −2.05, −3, 0, −1/2.

**P7.** A bank account starts at $85. A $120 automatic payment posts, then
a $45 refund arrives. Write one expression and find the ending balance.
Is the account overdrawn?

**P8.** Compute: −(4) − |−7| + (−2) − (−9). Show each step.

**P9.** The overnight low was −11°F and the afternoon high was 23°F.
What was the temperature swing? Write it as a distance expression.

**P10.** **Error analysis.** "−6 − 4 = −2 because 6 − 4 = 2 and the answer
is negative." Correct the reasoning, give the right answer, and show the
rewrite step.

## Objective 2 — Multiply, divide, decimal forms (P11–P18)

**P11.** Compute: (a) (−6)(−9); (b) 7(−8); (c) (−48) ÷ (−6); (d) 55 ÷ (−11).

**P12.** Compute: (a) (−4/5)(15/8); (b) (−7/9) ÷ (14/3); (c) simplify
(−5/6)/(−10/9).

**P13.** Compute: (a) (−0.4)(−2.5); (b) 3.6 ÷ (−0.9); (c) (−1.1)².

**P14.** Convert to decimals: (a) 7/8; (b) −5/6; (c) 11/20; (d) −4/11.
Mark each terminating or repeating.

**P15.** A submarine descends 2.5 m per second. Write the signed rate, then
find its depth change after 40 seconds. After how many seconds is it at
−175 m?

**P16.** Simplify: (a) (2/3)/(−8/15); (b) (−0.75)/(−2.5). Then write each
as a division and verify by multiplying back.

**P17.** Which is greater: −7/9 or −0.7̄7̄? Write both as decimals to
decide, and explain the number-line reason.

**P18.** **Error analysis.** "(−2)³ = −8, and −2³ = −8, so parentheses never
matter with exponents." Give a counterexample with a square, and state
the rule for when parentheses change the result.

## Objective 3 — Multi-step problems (P19–P24)

**P19.** Start at −20. Add 35, subtract 48, add the opposite of −17, then
subtract half of 26. Final value? Show estimate + exact.

**P20.** A store's weekly profit/loss ($) for four weeks: −320, 450,
−180, 210. (a) Total? (b) Mean weekly result? (c) If week 5 must bring
the 5-week mean to +$100, what must week 5's result be?

**P21.** A recipe uses 3/4 cup of sugar per 2/3 cup of cocoa. (a) Write the
complex fraction for cups of sugar per cup of cocoa and simplify it.
(b) How much sugar for 2 cups of cocoa?

**P22.** Elevation check: a trailhead is at 1,240 m. A hiker descends 350 m,
climbs 180 m, descends 620 m to a lake, then climbs 3/4 of the way back
up to the trailhead's elevation gain from the lake. Wait — compute
carefully: what is the lake's elevation, and how far does the final climb
go? Final elevation?

**P23.** Compute: (−2.5 + 1.5) × (−4) − (−18) ÷ (−3). Show every step and
a reason check on the sign of each term.

**P24.** **Design your own.** Write a three-step real-world problem using
all four operations on rational numbers (at least one fraction or
decimal, at least one negative). Solve it, and write the estimation check
you would use to catch a sign error.
