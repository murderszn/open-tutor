# Resource Pack — Unit 05: Scale Drawings, Angles, and Geometric Constructions

**Summary (adult planning judgment):** Grade 7 geometry works best
when the learner's hands are on the tools — ruler, protractor,
compass — and the video or article only supplements the construction.
The resources below support the unit's three objectives: scale
drawings and areas (Lessons 01–02), triangle constructions and
uniqueness (Lesson 03), and angle pairs and angle equations (Lessons
04–06). All external links were checked 2026-11-07; unverified items
are labeled as open search links. All lengths, areas, and measurements
in this unit are fictional practice data.
