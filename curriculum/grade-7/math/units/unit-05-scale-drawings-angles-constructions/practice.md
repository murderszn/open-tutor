# Practice Bank — Unit 05

Worked solutions for every item are in the [answer key](answer-key.md).
Sessions 2, 4, 6, 8, and 10 use these items (see the unit README
pacing table). Show the scale, the equation, or the angle fact you
used — not just the answer.

## Scale lengths (P1–P4; Lesson 01)

**P1.** A floor plan uses 1 cm = 3 m. A hallway is drawn 7 cm long.
How long is the real hallway?

**P2.** A model uses 1 in = 2 ft. A shelf is drawn 6.5 in long. How
long is the real shelf?

**P3.** A garden bed is really 30 m long. The plan scale is
1 cm = 5 m. How long is the bed drawn?

**P4.** A map uses 1 cm = 25 km. Two towns are drawn 8.4 cm apart.
How far apart are they really?

## Scale areas and a different scale (P5–P8; Lesson 02)

**P5.** A patio is drawn 4 cm by 6 cm. The scale is 1 cm = 3 m. What
is its actual area?

**P6.** A square is drawn with side 5 cm. The scale is 1 cm = 2 m.
What is its actual area? Check your answer two ways (scale the side;
scale the drawn area).

**P7.** A poster is drawn 9 cm by 6 cm at 1 cm = 2 m. Reproduce it at
1 cm = 3 m. What are the new drawn dimensions?

**P8 (error analysis).** A drawn shape has area 40 cm². The scale is
1 cm = 5 m. A learner computes the actual area as 40 × 5 = 200 m².
What is the error, and what is the correct actual area?

## Triangle conditions (P9–P12; Lesson 03)

**P9.** Sides 6 cm, 8 cm, 10 cm. Does a triangle exist? Is it unique?
(Extra: is it right-angled? Check 6² + 8² against 10².)

**P10.** Sides 2 cm, 3 cm, 6 cm. Does a triangle exist? Show the
failing check.

**P11.** Two angles are 40° and 80°, and the side between them is
7 cm. Is the triangle unique? What is the third angle?

**P12.** Three angles are given — 30°, 60°, 90° — and no side lengths.
How many triangles fit these measures? Explain in one sentence.

## Angle pairs (P13–P16; Lesson 04)

**P13.** Find the complement and the supplement of 64°.

**P14.** Two lines cross and one angle is 112°. What is the vertical
angle? What is each adjacent angle?

**P15.** Two adjacent angles sit on a straight line. One is 47°. What
is the other?

**P16.** Two complementary angles: one is twice the other. Find both
angles.

## Angle equations (P17–P20; Lesson 05)

**P17.** Two complementary angles are x and x + 30. Find both angles.

**P18.** Two supplementary angles measure 4x − 10 and 2x + 40 (in
degrees). Find x and both angle measures.

**P19.** Two vertical angles measure 2x + 15 and 4x − 25. Find x and
the angle measure.

**P20.** Two adjacent angles on a straight line measure x + 15 and
3x − 45. Find both angles.

## Synthesis (P21–P24; Lesson 06)

**P21.** A triangle is drawn with sides 5 cm, 12 cm, 13 cm. The scale
is 1 cm = 4 m. What are the real side lengths and the real perimeter?

**P22.** A square is drawn with side 3 cm. The scale is 1 cm = 6 m.
What is its actual area? Check with the drawn area and the squared
scale factor.

**P23.** A triangle has two angles of 52° and 68°. What is the third
angle? Classify the triangle by its angles.

**P24 (error analysis).** Jae says a triangle with sides 4 cm, 9 cm,
and 14 cm exists "because 4 + 9 + 14 = 27." Is Jae right? Use the
triangle inequality to explain.
