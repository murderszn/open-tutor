# Unit Assessment — Unit 05

**Grade 7 Math · Unit 05 · Session 16 · 12 items · Objectives 1–3**
Show your work. The [answer key](../answer-key.md#unit-assessment)
gives every solution; never place the key beside a student question.

**1.** A map uses the scale 1 cm = 12 km. Two landmarks are drawn
7.5 cm apart. How far apart are they really?

**2.** A blueprint uses 1 in = 4 ft. A room is drawn 3.25 in by
2.5 in. What are the room's actual dimensions, and what is its
actual area?

**3.** A drawing is 10 cm by 4 cm at a scale of 1 cm = 3 m.
Reproduce it at a scale of 1 cm = 6 m. What are the new drawn
dimensions?

**4.** Sides 7 cm, 10 cm, 15 cm. Does a triangle exist? Is it unique?
Show the checks.

**5.** Sides 5 cm, 11 cm, 17 cm. Does a triangle exist? Show the
failing check.

**6.** A triangle has angles 55° and 65°, and the side between them
is 6 cm. Is the triangle unique? What is the third angle?

**7.** Two angles are complementary. One is 41°. What is the other?

**8.** Two lines cross and one angle is 131°. What is the opposite
(vertical) angle? What is each adjacent angle?

**9.** Two supplementary angles measure 2x + 10 and x + 20 (in
degrees). Find x and both angle measures.

**10.** Two vertical angles measure 5x − 20 and 3x + 30. Find x and
the angle measure.

**11.** A triangle has two angles of 48° and 72°. What is the third
angle? Classify the triangle by its angles.

**12.** Use the garden plan from Lesson 01 (scale 1 cm = 2 m; each
bed drawn 9 cm by 2 cm). (a) What is one bed's actual area? (b) What
is the total actual area of all four beds?
