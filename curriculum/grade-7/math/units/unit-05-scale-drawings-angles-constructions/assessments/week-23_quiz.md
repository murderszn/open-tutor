# Week-23 Formative Quiz — Unit 05

**Grade 7 Math · Unit 05 · Session 15 · 10 items · Objectives 1–3**
Show your work. The [answer key](../answer-key.md#week-23-formative-quiz)
gives every solution; never place the key beside a student question.

**1.** A map uses the scale 1 cm = 6 m. A path is drawn 4.5 cm long.
How long is the real path?

**2.** A warehouse wall is really 45 m long. The plan
scale is 1 cm = 5 m. How long is the wall drawn?

**3.** A rectangle is drawn 3 cm by 5 cm. The scale is 1 cm = 2 m.
What is its actual area?

**4.** Can sides 4 cm, 5 cm, and 9 cm form a triangle? Show the
deciding check.

**5.** A triangle has two angles of 70° and 30°, with the side between
them given. Is the triangle unique? What is the third angle?

**6.** What is the complement of 25°?

**7.** What is the supplement of 123°?

**8.** Two lines cross and one angle is 84°. What is the vertical
angle? What is each adjacent angle?

**9.** Two complementary angles are x and 2x + 15 (in degrees). Find x
and both angle measures.

**10.** A square is drawn with side 3 cm. The scale is 1 cm = 8 m.
What is its actual area?
