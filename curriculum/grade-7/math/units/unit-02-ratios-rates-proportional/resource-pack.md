# Resource Pack — Unit 02: Ratios, Rates, and Proportional Relationships

**Summary (adult planning judgment):** Grade 7 proportional-reasoning
instruction works best when every video or article supplements, never
replaces, the two hands-on tests: equivalent ratios in a table and the
straight-line-through-the-origin graph. The resources below support the
unit's three objectives: unit rates from fractional ratios (Lessons
01–02), testing proportionality in tables and graphs (Lessons 03–04),
and the constant of proportionality with y = kx (Lessons 05–06). All
external links were checked 2026-10-18; unverified items are labeled as
open search links.
