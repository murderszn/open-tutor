# Practice Bank — Unit 02: Ratios, Rates, and Proportional Relationships

24 items across Sessions 2, 4, 6, and 10. Every item is solved in
[answer-key.md](answer-key.md#practice-bank). Show the "per" sentence and
a reason check on every item; the adult reviews the same day.

## Session 2 — Unit rates (P1–P6)

Context-varied rebuild of the legacy `ratios-rates-quiz.md` car-speed
items (the original five were near-identical "car travels X miles in Y
hours" drills).

**P1.** A car travels 180 miles in 4 hours. Speed in miles per hour?
**P2.** A 3/4-pound block of cheese costs $6. Unit price in dollars per pound?
**P3.** A cyclist rides 33 kilometers in 1.5 hours. Speed in km/h?
**P4.** A conveyor moves 5/8 ton of gravel in 1/4 day. Rate in tons per day?
**P5.** Seven concert tickets cost $52.50. Price per ticket?
**P6.** A snail crawls 9/10 meter in 3/5 second. Speed in meters per second?

## Session 4 — Unlike-unit rates and conversions (P7–P12)

**P7.** Convert 70 miles per hour to feet per second (nearest tenth).
**P8.** Convert 12 meters per second to kilometers per hour.
**P9.** A runner covers 5 kilometers in 20 minutes. Speed in km/h?
**P10.** A 1.7-liter bottle of oil costs $4.25. Price per liter?
**P11.** A typist writes 200 words in 2.5 minutes. (a) Words per minute?
(b) How many minutes for 1000 words at that rate?
**P12.** A metal sample has mass 96 grams and volume 12 cubic centimeters.
Density in grams per cubic centimeter? (Density is a rate: mass per volume.)

## Session 6 — Proportionality testing (P13–P18)

Genuine rewrite of the mislabeled legacy `proportional-relationships-quiz.md`
(its 20 items were fraction drills, not proportionality items). For each
table: give the verdict, show the y ÷ x test on every row, and name k when
it exists.

**P13.** (6, 18), (9, 27), (12, 36)
**P14.** (5, 12), (10, 22), (15, 32)
**P15.** (1, 2.5), (2, 5), (4, 10)
**P16.** (0, 0), (3, 12), (5, 20)
**P17.** (2, 3), (4, 8), (6, 13)
**P18.** A museum charges $8 per adult. (a) Table for 2, 5, 8 adults:
proportional? k? (b) The museum adds a $2 per-visit booking fee (so 2
adults pay $18). New table: proportional? Explain what changed.

## Session 10 — Constant of proportionality and y = kx (P19–P24)

**P19.** Table (3, 21), (7, 49), (10, 70). Find k with units, write
y = kx, and say what (1, k) means if x is hours and y is miles.
**P20.** A proportional graph passes through (0, 0) and (5, 2). Find k
and write the equation. (Fractions are fine.)
**P21.** y = 1.5x. What is k? If x is hours worked and y is dollars
earned, what does k mean? What does (0, 0) mean?
**P22.** "A bakery makes 24 cookies per batch." Identify k with units,
write the equation (c = cookies, b = batches), and interpret (0, 0) and
(1, k).
**P23.** Table (4, 10), (8, 20), (12, 30). Proportional? k? Equation?
**P24.** Three graphs: (a) a straight line through the origin; (b) a
straight line that crosses the y-axis at (0, 4); (c) a curve through the
origin that gets steeper. Which could show a proportional relationship?
For each rejected graph, state exactly which part of the test it fails.
