# Week-10 Formative Quiz — Unit 02

**Unit 02 · Session 15** · 25 minutes · Objectives 1–3
Calculator for checking only. Show the "per" sentence and a reason check
where marked. All items are solved in [answer-key.md](../answer-key.md#week-10-quiz).

**Q1.** A recipe uses 2/3 cup of flour for every 1/4 batch of muffins.
What is the unit rate in cups of flour per batch? (Objective 1)

**Q2.** A runner covers 13.5 miles in 1.5 hours. Speed in miles per hour?
Include units. (Objective 1)

**Q3.** Convert 50 miles per hour to feet per second (nearest whole
number). (Objective 1)

**Q4.** Table: (4, 14), (6, 21), (10, 35). Proportional? Show the test.
(Objective 2)

**Q5.** Table: (3, 10), (6, 19), (9, 28). Proportional? Explain with the
test and in words. (Objective 2)

**Q6.** Four points are plotted: (0, 0), (2, 9), (4, 18), (6, 27). Could
they show a proportional relationship? Give the exact two-part graph
test in your answer. (Objective 2)

**Q7.** A proportional relationship has the table (5, 30), (8, 48),
(12, 72). Find the constant of proportionality with units, and write
the equation y = kx. (Objective 3)

**Q8.** A graph of a proportional relationship passes through (1, 4.5).
(a) What is the constant of proportionality? (b) What does the point
(0, 0) mean if x is pounds of coffee and y is dollars? (Objective 3)

**Q9.** A store sells 3-lb bags of oranges for $4.50 and 8-lb bags for
$11.20. (a) Find both unit prices. (b) Is price proportional to weight?
(c) Which bag is the better value? (Objectives 1–2)

**Q10.** **Explain your reasoning.** A learner says: "The table
(2, 5), (4, 10), (6, 15) is proportional with k = 2.5, so the equation
is y = 2.5x + 0." Is the "+ 0" needed? Is it wrong? What does this tell
you about where proportional lines cross the y-axis? (Objective 3)
