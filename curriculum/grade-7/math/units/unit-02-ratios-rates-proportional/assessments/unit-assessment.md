# Unit Assessment — Unit 02: Ratios, Rates, and Proportional Relationships

**Unit 02 · Session 16** · 45 minutes · Objectives 1–3
Calculator for checking only. Show all work; a bare answer earns partial
credit at most. All items are solved in
[answer-key.md](../answer-key.md#unit-assessment).

## Part A — Unit rates (Objective 1)

**A1.** A faucet fills 5/6 of a bucket in 1/3 hour. What is the rate in
buckets per hour?

**A2.** A 2.5-kg bag of dog food costs $11.25. What is the unit price in
dollars per kilogram?

**A3.** A high-speed train travels 330 kilometers in 1.5 hours. (a) Speed
in km/h? (b) Convert to meters per second (nearest whole number).

## Part B — Testing proportionality (Objective 2)

**B1.** A paint store mixes 4 liters of tint with 10 liters of base for a
custom color. The table shows three batches: (4, 10), (6, 15), (10, 25)
(tint, base). Is the mixture proportional across batches? Show the test.

**B2.** A parking garage charges $4 for the first hour and $3 for each
additional hour. Table for 1, 2, 3, 4 hours: (1, 4), (2, 7), (3, 10),
(4, 13). Proportional? Prove it with the table test and explain the
real-world reason in one sentence.

**B3.** Points (0, 0), (3, 8), (6, 16), (9, 24) are plotted. (a) Do they
lie on a straight line through the origin? (b) What does your answer
tell you about the relationship?

## Part C — Constant of proportionality (Objective 3)

**C1.** The table (2, 7), (5, 17.5), (8, 28) shows hours (x) and dollars
earned (y). (a) Find k with units. (b) Write y = kx. (c) What do (0, 0)
and (1, k) mean here?

**C2.** A proportional graph passes through (0, 0) and (8, 5). (a) Find
k. (b) Write the equation. (c) If x is cups of flour and y is cups of
sugar, what does k tell the baker?

## Part D — Multi-step (Objectives 1–3)

**D1.** A school orders pencils: 12-pack $2.40; 36-pack $6.48; 100-pack
$16.00. (a) Compute all three unit prices. (b) Is price proportional to
count? (c) Which pack is the best value, and by how much per pencil
versus the worst value?

**D2.** A recipe calls for 2.5 cups of stock for 4 servings. (a) Write
the equation s = kx (s = stock, x = servings) and identify k. (b) How
much stock for 18 servings? (c) Show a reason check using scaling.

**Scoring guide (adult):** A1–A3, B1–B3, C1–C2: 4 points each
(correct method 2, correct value with units 1, reason check or
explanation 1). D1–D2: 6 points each (each sub-part 2). Total 60.
Proficient ≥ 45; exemplary ≥ 54. Below 40: revisit the lessons flagged
in [teacher-guide.md](../teacher-guide.md#reteach-triggers) before
Unit 03 (percent builds directly on this unit).
