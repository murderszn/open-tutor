# Resource Pack — Unit 04: Expressions, Equations, and Inequalities

**Summary (adult planning judgment):** Grade 7 algebra works
best when every video or article supplements, never replaces,
the unit's one discipline: keep the balance, check by
substitution, and flip the sign when you multiply or divide by
a negative. The resources below support the unit's four
objectives: expanding, factoring, and rewriting expressions
(Lessons 01–02), multi-step rational-number problems (Lesson
03), equations px + q = r and p(x + q) = r (Lessons 04–05),
and inequalities with graphs (Lesson 06). All external links
were checked 2026-11-01; unverified items are labeled as open
search links. All prices and budgets in this unit are fictional
practice data.
