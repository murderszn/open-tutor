# Week-18 Formative Quiz — Unit 04

**Unit:** [Unit 04](../README.md) · **Objectives 1–4** · **Standards 7.EE.A.1–2, 7.EE.B.3–4**
**Session:** 15 · **Time:** 45 minutes · **Calculator:** checking only

Show your work. Check equations by substitution. For
inequalities, describe the graph (circle type and arrow
direction). Answers are in the separate
[answer key](../answer-key.md).

1. Expand 3(4x − 2).
2. Factor 12x − 9.
3. Rewrite m − 0.10m in one-term form, and say what percent
   change it shows.
4. Compute −5 + 3.75 × 2, and give a one-line estimate check.
5. Solve 4x + 7 = 31, and check by substitution.
6. Solve 3(x − 4) = 24, and check by substitution.
7. Solve 2x + 9 > 25, and describe its graph.
8. Solve −3x + 4 < 22, and describe its graph.
9. Five bags each hold x stickers plus 2 bonus stickers, for
   45 stickers in total. Write the equation, solve it, and
   check it.
10. You have $80. You buy $20 shoes, then as many $9 shirts as
    you can afford without going over $80. Write the
    inequality, solve it, and answer in a sentence: how many
    shirts at most? (Note: shirts are whole items.)
