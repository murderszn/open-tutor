# Unit Assessment — Unit 04: Expressions, Equations, and Inequalities

**Unit:** [Unit 04](../README.md) · **Objectives 1–4** · **Standards 7.EE.A.1–2, 7.EE.B.3–4**
**Session:** 16 · **Time:** 45 minutes · **Calculator:** checking only
**Submitted with:** the [Budget Challenge](../project.md)

Show your work. Check equations by substitution and
inequalities with a boundary value and a test value. Answers
are in the separate [answer key](../answer-key.md). This is a
classroom assessment, not a standardized or validated test.

1. Simplify 2(3x + 5) − 4x.
2. Factor 14x + 21, and check by expanding.
3. Rewrite 2a + 0.5a in one-term form, and explain in one
   sentence what the rewritten form shows.
4. A temperature starts at −6.5°F, rises 4.25°F, then falls
   2°F. What is the final temperature? Estimate first.
5. Compute (3/4) × 8 − 2.5.
6. Solve (5/6)x − 2 = 8, and check by substitution.
7. Solve −2(x − 3) = 14, and check by substitution.
8. Solve 6 − 2x ≥ 18, and describe its graph.
9. Solve 3x + 11 < 29, and describe its graph.
10. Four crates each hold x bottles plus 3 bonus bottles, for
    52 bottles in total. Write the equation, solve it both
    ways, and check it.
11. A gym charges a $15 membership fee plus $12.50 per class.
    You will spend less than $100. Write the inequality,
    solve it, graph it (described in words), and answer: how
    many classes at most? (Classes are whole items.)
12. **Error analysis.** Sam solved −2x > 10 and wrote x > −5.
    Is Sam right? Identify the error, give the correct
    solution and graph description, and test one value that
    proves your answer.
