# Practice — Unit 04: Expressions, Equations, and Inequalities

**Unit:** [Unit 04](README.md) · **Objectives 1–4** · **Standards 7.EE.A.1–2, 7.EE.B.3–4**
Work by hand; use a calculator only to check. Check every
equation by substitution and every inequality with a boundary
value and a test value. Answers are in the separate
[answer key](answer-key.md) — never beside the questions.

## Session 2 — Expand and combine (Lesson 01)

**P1.** Expand 2(3x − 5).
**P2.** Simplify 4x + 9 − 2x + 3.
**P3.** Expand −3(2x + 1).
**P4.** Simplify (1/2)(6x − 8) + x.

## Session 4 — Factor and rewrite (Lesson 02)

**P5.** Factor 10x + 15.
**P6.** Rewrite b + 0.20b in one-term form, and say what percent
change it shows.
**P7.** Factor 6x − 18.
**P8.** Simplify 4x + 8 − 2x, then factor the result.

## Session 6 — Multi-step rational problems (Lesson 03)

**P9.** An account holds $72.50. Three withdrawals of $14.25
each are made. Find the balance, with an estimate first.
**P10.** A temperature starts at −2.75°F, rises 5.5°F, then
falls 1.25°F. What is the final temperature? Estimate first.
**P11.** A recipe needs 2/3 cup of flour per batch. How many
cups are needed for 3 batches? Give a fraction and a decimal.
**P12.** A $48.90 bill is split equally among 3 people. How
much does each pay? Estimate first, then compute exactly.

## Session 8 — Equations px + q = r (Lesson 04)

**P13.** Solve 3x − 8 = 19, and check by substitution.
**P14.** Solve (2/3)x + 5 = 11, and check by substitution.
**P15.** Solve −4x + 9 = 25, and check by substitution.
**P16.** A club charges a $12 fee plus $8 per month. A member's
total is $100. How many months is that? Write the equation
first, then solve and check.

## Session 10 — Equations p(x + q) = r (Lesson 05)

**P17.** Solve 4(x + 3) = 32 both ways, and check.
**P18.** Solve 2(x − 9) = 14 both ways, and check.
**P19.** Solve −5(x + 2) = −35, and check by substitution.
**P20.** Three boxes each hold x books plus 5 bonus books, for
51 books in total. Write the equation, solve it, and check.

## Session 12 — Inequalities (Lesson 06)

**P21.** Solve 5x − 3 < 22 and describe its graph.
**P22.** Solve −2x + 7 ≥ 19 and describe its graph.
**P23.** Solve 4(x + 1) > 28 and describe its graph.
**P24.** You have $50. You pay an $8 entry fee, then ride rides
costing $6 each, without going over $50. Write the inequality,
solve it, graph it, and answer: how many rides at most?
