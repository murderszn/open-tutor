# Probability Game Lab (Grade 7 Math)

## Objective
Compare theoretical and experimental probability by designing and testing a simple game.

## Tasks
1. Design a dice/spinner/card game with clear win conditions.
2. Predict probability of winning in fraction, decimal, and percent form.
3. Run 30 trials and record results.
4. Compare predicted vs observed outcomes and explain differences.

## Deliverable
- Trial data table.
- Probability calculations.
- Final recommendation: fair, unfair, or needs revision.
