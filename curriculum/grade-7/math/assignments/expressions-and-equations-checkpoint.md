# Learner — Expressions & Equations Checkpoint (Grade 7)

## Goal
Practice translating word phrases into algebra and solving equations.

## Tasks
1. Write algebraic expressions for 8 word phrases.
2. Solve 8 one-step and two-step equations.
3. Create 2 real-life equation problems and solve them.
4. Check all answers by substitution.

## Practice Set
Translate: a number plus 7; four times a number; a number minus 9; half a number; 3 more than twice a number; 8 less than a number; five equal groups of a number; a number divided by 6.
Solve: x+8=19; y−4=11; 5a=35; b/3=9; 2c+5=23; 3d−7=14; 4e+2=30; 6f−8=28. Verify by substitution.
