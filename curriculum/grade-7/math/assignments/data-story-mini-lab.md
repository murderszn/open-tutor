# Data Story Mini Lab - Learner

Shared spec: Shared reference(use the selected grade’s private workspace)

## Learner Checklist

- [ ] Choose one data question.
- [ ] Collect at least 12 data points.
- [ ] Create a labeled table and graph.
- [ ] Calculate mean, median, range, and one ratio or percent comparison.
- [ ] Write a 7-9 sentence data story with one limitation.

Submit the table, graph, calculations, and data story.
