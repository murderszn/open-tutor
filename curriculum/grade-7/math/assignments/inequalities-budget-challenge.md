# Inequalities Budget Challenge (Grade 7 Math)

## Objective
Model real-world spending limits with one-step and two-step inequalities.

## Tasks
1. Pick a budget target ($120, $200, or $300).
2. Build 6 inequality equations for purchases and fees.
3. Solve each inequality and interpret what the solution means.
4. Create one scenario where two constraints must both be true.

## Deliverable
- Solved inequalities with check steps.
- Short reflection on which budget was hardest and why.
