# Week-04 Formative Quiz — Unit 03

**Time:** ~30 minutes · **Use:** Week 14, Session 15, with review the
same day. The adult scores with [answer-key.md](../answer-key.md) and
reteaches any missed skill before the unit assessment.

Answer in your own words unless a question asks you to quote. Use
Articles A and B and the Lesson 06 illustration.

1. **(RI.7.2)** State one central idea of Article A in a full sentence.
   Then name one section that develops it and what that section adds.
2. **(RI.7.2)** Read this summary sentence: "Article A proves that
   trees are the best thing a town can do." Name two faults in it
   (think: objective? supported?).
3. **(RI.7.3)** Draw a three-link chain from Article A: start with
   "leaves release water vapor (transpiration)" and end with "people
   linger on the street." Label each arrow.
4. **(RI.7.5)** Name the structure of Article A, section 2 ("How Shade
   Cools a Street") and the job that section does for the whole article
   (introduce / explain / prove / widen / decide).
5. **(RI.7.4)** Article A says the air on Maple Street "felt soft."
   What does "soft" connote here that "cool" would not?
6. **(RI.7.6)** State Article B's position in one sentence. Then quote
   one sentence from Article B that distinguishes its position from
   Article A's.
7. **(RI.7.8)** Judge this claim from the Lesson 05 argument passage:
   "Most people agree trees are wonderful, which proves this plan is
   right." Give two verdicts: is the reasoning sound, and is the
   evidence relevant? One reason each.
8. **(RI.7.8)** A claim's evidence is relevant but insufficient. In one
   sentence, explain what fix that claim needs — a different argument,
   or more evidence?
9. **(RI.7.9)** Both articles use the same temperature study. Name the
   number Article A emphasizes, the number Article B emphasizes, and
   one sentence each for what each author concludes from it.
10. **(RI.7.7)** Look at the Lesson 06 illustration (or read its text
    alternative). Name one thing the image portrays well and one thing
    it cannot portray that Article B raises.
