# Assets — Unit 05: Narrative Craft

## rainy-bus-stop-scene.png

- **Filename:** `rainy-bus-stop-scene.png` (1920 × 1280, PNG)
- **Prompt:** "Warm, gentle storybook-style illustration for a
  middle-school narrative writing lesson: a 13-year-old girl with a
  dark braid stands alone at a bus stop on a rainy evening, holding a
  slightly crumpled paper grocery bag with a small birthday-cake box
  peeking out of the top, looking hopefully down an empty wet street.
  A glowing streetlight, puddles reflecting amber light, a bus-stop
  sign, soft rain. Clear setting, visible emotion, and story clues a
  student could write about. No text or words in the image."
- **Generation tool:** Muse built-in image generation
  (`media.generate_image`), 2026-11-07.
- **Learning purpose:** Lesson 02 activity — the learner uses the
  image as a story prompt, turning visible details into *showing*
  sentences (sensory description, precise words), and may use it as
  the project's story prompt or setting. The image shows the exact
  moment in the anchor story "The Cake" when June waits at the bus
  stop.
- **Factual review (2026-11-07):** inspected before linking. The
  image matches the prompt and the story: a girl of about thirteen
  with a long dark braid, green raincoat over a yellow sweater, paper
  bag with a pale-blue cake box (small cake with pink frosting and one
  candle visible through the box window), rainy evening bus stop,
  empty wet street, glowing streetlights reflected in puddles, blue
  bus-stop sign with a bus pictogram. No words or text appear in the
  image. It is a fictional illustration, not a photograph, and is
  labeled as an AI illustration wherever it is used.
- **Caption:** *Figure 1 — AI illustration: June waits at the bus stop
  with the cake. The image is a fictional illustration for this
  writing activity, not a photograph.*
- **Alt text:** "A watercolor-style illustration of a thirteen-year-old
  girl with a long dark braid standing alone at a rainy bus-stop
  shelter at dusk. She wears a green raincoat over a yellow sweater
  and holds a crumpled paper bag with a pale-blue cake box peeking
  out, a small cake with pink frosting visible through the box window.
  She looks hopefully down an empty wet street lined with houses,
  where streetlights glow amber and reflect in puddles. Rain falls; a
  blue bus-stop sign stands beside the shelter."
- **Text alternative:** the full text alternative is printed in
  [Lesson 02](../lessons/lesson-02-sensory-description-precise-words.md)
  with the image, so the activity works without seeing the image.
- **Known limitations:** the illustration shows one invented moment;
  it cannot show what happens before or after (the bakery, the bus
  arriving, the ride home), and it cannot show June's thoughts — only
  what she does and where she stands. The project asks learners who
  use it as a prompt to add one sentence saying what the image cannot
  show about their story.
