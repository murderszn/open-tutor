# Week-04 Formative Quiz — Unit 05

**Time:** ~30 minutes · **Use:** Week 22, Session 15, with review the
same day. The adult scores with [answer-key.md](../answer-key.md) and
reteaches any missed skill before the unit assessment.

Answer in your own words unless a question asks you to choose. Use
"The Cake" (Lesson 01) and the Lesson 02 illustration.

1. **(W.7.3.a)** Which opening best orients the reader? Choose one.
   a. "One day, a boy did something."
   b. "The rain started the minute I left the bakery, which felt personal."
   c. "He was a boy who liked things."
   d. "It was a day like any other day, and nothing happened."
2. **(W.7.3.d)** Which sentence *shows* rather than tells? Choose one.
   a. "June was worried about the cake."
   b. "June held the box tighter and stepped forward before the doors even opened."
   c. "June was very, very nervous."
   d. "The cake was important to June."
3. **(W.7.3.b)** Which line of dialogue is punctuated correctly?
   Choose one.
   a. `"I'm ready," Dev said.`
   b. `"I'm ready." Dev said.`
   c. `Dev said, "I'm ready".`
   d. `"I'm ready" Dev said.`
4. **(W.7.3.d)** Rewrite this telling sentence as showing, in one or
   two sentences. Do not use the word "terrified": "Mara was terrified
   before her solo."
5. **(W.7.3.b)** What does this line reveal about Mrs. Alvarez — and
   how is it different from the narrator simply saying she is wise?
   "Because I carried it," Mrs. Alvarez said.
6. **(W.7.3.c)** Which of these signals a *flashback* (a move backward
   in time)? Choose one.
   a. "then"
   b. "later"
   c. "I had missed the earlier bus because"
   d. "next"
7. **(W.7.3.b, W.7.3.c)** Which version slows the pace at the important
   moment — the bus arriving? Choose one.
   a. "The bus came and June got on."
   b. "The bus turned the corner then, slow through the puddles, its headlights doubling in the wet street."
   c. "June waited twenty minutes."
   d. "Later, June was home."
8. **(W.7.3.e)** Which ending *reflects* on the events, rather than
   just stopping or moralizing? Choose one.
   a. "Then June went home. The end."
   b. "And that is why you should always be on time."
   c. "Sitting there with the box safe on her knees, June understood that the cake mattered because she had carried it."
9. **(W.7.5)** Which of these is a *revision* change, not an editing
   fix? Choose one.
   a. Fixing a misspelled word.
   b. Adding a comma after a dialogue tag.
   c. Cutting a scene that does not belong.
   d. Capitalizing the first word of a sentence.
10. **(W.7.3.d)** Look at the Lesson 02 illustration (or read its text
    alternative). Name one visible detail and write one showing
    sentence from it — no feeling words allowed.
