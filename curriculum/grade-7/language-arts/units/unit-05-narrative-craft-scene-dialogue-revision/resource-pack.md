# Resource Pack — Unit 05: Narrative Craft, Scene, Dialogue, and Revision

**Goal in one sentence:** give a grade-7 learner and guiding adult
reliable background and practice routes for narrative orientation,
sensory description, dialogue, pacing, transitions, reflective endings,
and revision — at grade level, free, and without an account for core
learning.

**Age fit:** grade 7 (ages ~12–13). The anchor story and all practice
and assessment passages in this unit are original and rights-clean;
external items below are adult-previewed background, not assigned
reading. Videos supplement the lessons; they never replace them.

## Internal references and datasets

- No external dataset is required for this unit. The anchor story
  "The Cake," the assessment passage "The Tryout," and every practice
  passage are **original fictional texts** written for this unit. Any
  numbers inside them (June is thirteen, her grandmother is turning
  seventy, the bus is twenty minutes late, Dev is twelve) are story
  details, not real measurements, and learners must never cite them
  as real-world facts.
- The generated illustration is an **AI illustration of a fictional
  scene**. It is a story prompt, not a photograph and not evidence
  about any real person or place.
