# Formative Quiz — Unit 01 (Week 4 of unit / track Week 6)

**Standards:** RL.7.1, RL.7.2, RL.7.3 · **Time:** 30–40 minutes
**Text:** "The Lantern on Greyrock Point" ([Lesson
01](../lessons/lesson-01-citing-textual-evidence.md)) — the learner may
re-read the story during the quiz.

*Revised from the legacy track quiz
`quizzes/literary-terms-story-elements-narrative-devices-quiz.md` (audit
decision: re-anchor to a read passage, add an answer key). The answer key is
in [answer-key.md](../answer-key.md) § Quiz — never beside these questions.*

Answer in complete sentences. Cite paragraph numbers for every answer that
asks "how do you know" or "support."

1. **Plot stages.** Name the five stages of plot structure. Then identify
   which paragraph of the story contains the **climax**, and explain in one
   sentence why that paragraph — not an earlier or later one — is the
   turning point.
2. **Conflict.** Name two different types of conflict present in the story
   (person vs. person, vs. self, vs. nature, vs. society). For each, cite
   one detail that shows it.
3. **Setting.** Describe the story's setting using all four parts (place,
   time, weather/physical conditions, social conditions). Then explain one
   way the setting shapes a character's choice, with a citation.
4. **Character change.** Is Mara a static or a dynamic character? Support
   your answer with one detail from the beginning and one from the end of
   the story.
5. **Characterization.** Give one example of **direct** characterization and
   one of **indirect** characterization from the story, and label which is
   which.
6. **Theme vs. topic.** "Courage" is a topic. Write a complete **theme
   statement** about courage for this story, then check it against the three
   tests (complete sentence / no character names / story-provable) in
   writing.
7. **Theme development.** Trace how the theme "responsibility to others can
   outweigh obedience" develops across the story: give one cited detail from
   the **beginning** (¶¶1–5), one from the **middle** (¶¶6–8), and one from
   the **end** (¶¶9–12).
8. **Inference.** What can you infer about *why* Hale gives Mara his brass
   keeper's badge? Cite two pieces of evidence and name one alternative
   reading the evidence also allows.
9. **Objective summary.** Write a 4–5 sentence objective summary of the
   story. (No opinion words — they cost points.)
10. **Elements interact.** Complete this chain with citations:
    setting detail → character choice → plot event → theme it helps
    produce. Then, in one sentence, explain what your chain proves about
    how story elements interact.

**Scoring guide (adult):** 3 points per question (30 total): 1 for accuracy,
1 for the required citation(s), 1 for explanation quality. 24+ = secure;
18–23 = re-teach the missed element with the [teacher
guide](../teacher-guide.md) re-teaching map; below 18 = repeat Practice
sessions for the missed objectives before the unit assessment.
