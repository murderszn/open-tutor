# Formative Quiz — Unit 04 (Week 18)

**10 points** · Answer in writing. The teacher guide holds the key;
do not look at it before attempting the quiz. This quiz also serves
as the midyear cumulative check's grammar half: it covers only Unit 04
skills, and the adult should note any U01–U03 gaps separately during
review.

1. Is *after the game ended* a phrase or a clause? If it is a clause,
   is it independent or dependent, and what job does it do in "After
   the game ended, we walked home"?
2. In "The river rose because the rain fell all night," underline the
   independent clause.
3. Label this sentence simple, compound, complex, or
   compound-complex: "Although it rained, we played, and the crowd
   stayed."
4. Combine as one complex sentence showing cause: "The bridge
   closed. Traffic stopped."
5. Repair this misplaced modifier: "She gave cookies to the children
   on a blue plate." (Name the fault first.)
6. Repair this dangling modifier: "Running to school, my backpack
   broke." (Name the fault first.)
7. Punctuate correctly: "It was a cold rainy day." Explain your
   punctuation with one test.
8. Punctuate correctly: "He wore a new leather jacket." Explain your
   punctuation with one test.
9. Revise concisely with no meaning lost: "Due to the fact that he
   was sick, he stayed home."
10. (a) Complete the analogy, naming the relationship first: "begin :
    end :: arrive : ___" (options: *depart, stay, travel*).
    (b) Choose the praising word for a careful spender: *thrifty* or
    *stingy*? Explain what the other word connotes.
