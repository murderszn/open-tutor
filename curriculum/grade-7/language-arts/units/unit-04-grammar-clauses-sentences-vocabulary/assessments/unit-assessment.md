# Unit Assessment — Unit 04

**40 points** · Answer in writing. Do not use the teacher guide or
answer key. Spelling and punctuation count only where the question
says so; elsewhere, a correct idea in imperfect spelling still earns
credit, and the adult notes the spelling separately.

## Part A — Edit the passage (12 points)

The passage below contains 6 faults: one dangling modifier, one
misplaced modifier, two missing coordinate-adjective commas, one
spelling/confusion error, and one wordy phrase. For each numbered
sentence, name the fault type (1 point) and write the repaired
sentence (1 point). A sentence may contain more than one fault; the
fault list above tells you how many of each type to find in total.

> (1) Walking to the library, the rain started again.
> (2) Maya carried a heavy wet backpack and found a seat near the
> window.
> (3) Due to the fact that the library was quiet, she finished her
> work early.
> (4) Their were three books on the table, but she only needed two.
> (5) She read a long interesting article about oceans.
> (6) She borrowed a pencil from a student with a broken eraser.

## Part B — Sentence types (12 points)

1. (4 points) Label each sentence simple, compound, complex, or
   compound-complex:
   (a) "The orchestra played."
   (b) "The orchestra played, and the audience cheered."
   (c) "Because the hall was full, the orchestra played louder."
   (d) "Although the hall was full, the orchestra played louder, and
   the audience cheered."
2. (2 points) Rewrite "The gate was shut" as a complex sentence
   showing cause. (You may add a clause; do not change the fact that
   the gate was shut.)
3. (2 points) Combine into one compound-complex sentence: "The wind
   was light. The sailors raised the sails. The ship moved forward."
4. (4 points) Write four sentences about a rainy morning — one
   simple, one compound, one complex, one compound-complex. Label
   each type.

## Part C — Vocabulary (12 points)

1. (4 points) Guess each word's meaning from context, and name the
   clue kind (definition, example, contrast, or cause):
   (a) "The hiker was wary of the narrow bridge, so she tested each
   board before stepping on it."
   (b) "Unlike his gregarious sister, who talked to everyone, Tomas
   ate lunch alone."
2. (4 points) Break each word into parts and guess its meaning, then
   state the verified dictionary meaning (use a dictionary for the
   verification step):
   (a) *import* (b) *telephone*
3. (2 points) Complete each analogy, naming the relationship first:
   (a) "up : down :: arrive : ___" (options: *depart, stay, travel*)
   (b) "chef : kitchen :: teacher : ___" (options: *classroom,
   student, lesson*)
4. (2 points) Choose the word that fits the stated feeling, and name
   what the other word connotes:
   (a) Compliment a careful speaker: *diplomatic* or *condescending*?
   (b) Describe a careful spender neutrally: *economical* or *stingy*?

## Part D — Concise revision (4 points)

Revise this 55-word paragraph to 40 words or fewer with no meaning
lost (2 points for keeping the meaning, 2 points for meeting the word
limit with clean, grammatical sentences):

> "Due to the fact that the train was late, a large number of
> passengers waited at the station in order to catch the next train.
> In the event that the next train was also late, they planned to
> return back home. The unexpected surprise was that the train
> arrived soon, so they were very happy."
