# Resource Pack — Unit 04: Grammar, Clauses, Sentence Structure, and Vocabulary

**Goal in one sentence:** give a grade-7 learner and guiding adult
reliable background and practice routes for phrases and clauses,
sentence types, modifier repair, coordinate-adjective commas,
spelling proofreading, precise and concise language, and vocabulary
strategies — at grade level, free, and without an account for core
learning.

**Age fit:** grade 7 (ages ~12–13). External items below are
adult-previewed background, not assigned reading. Videos supplement
the lessons; they never replace them. At least one free, no-account
route supports each core objective (the lessons themselves, the
internal guides, and the dictionary/thesaurus references below).
