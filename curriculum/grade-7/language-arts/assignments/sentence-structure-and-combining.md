# Learner — Sentence Structure & Combining (Grade 7)

## Goal
Strengthen sentence clarity using subjects, predicates, and sentence combining.

## Tasks
1. Write 5 simple sentences (clear subject + predicate).
2. Expand each sentence with one detail phrase.
3. Combine pairs of simple sentences into 3 compound or complex sentences.
4. Check punctuation and capitalization.

## Submit
- Highlight one sentence you improved the most.
