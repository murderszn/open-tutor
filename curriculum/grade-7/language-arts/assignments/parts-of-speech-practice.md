# Learner — Parts of Speech Practice (Grade 7)

## Goal
Identify and use the 8 parts of speech in context.

## Tasks
1. Read one short article/chapter section (10–15 minutes).
2. Copy 12 sentences.
3. Label at least one example of each part of speech across the set:
   - noun, pronoun, verb, adjective, adverb, preposition, conjunction, interjection.
4. Write 6 original sentences using at least 3 different parts of speech each.

## Submit
- Include the source title you read.
