# Learner — Reading/Podcast Response Log (Grade 7)

## Goal
Practice comprehension and communication after reading or listening.

## Tasks
1. Complete 20–30 minutes of assigned reading, podcast, or audiobook.
2. Record:
   - Title + author/host
   - 5 key takeaways
   - 2 vocabulary words
   - 1 real-world connection
3. Write a 6–8 sentence summary paragraph.
