# Learner — Argument Paragraph Builder (Grade 7 LA)

## Goal
Write a clear claim with evidence and reasoning.

## Tasks
1. Pick one topic you care about (school, tech, sports, books, etc.).
2. Write one claim sentence.
3. Add at least 2 pieces of evidence (quote, fact, or example).
4. Explain how each evidence point supports your claim.
5. End with a strong closing sentence.
