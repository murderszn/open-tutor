# Grade 7 Language Arts — Parts of a Sentence — Phrases, Clauses & Sentence Types Quiz

**Topic:** Parts of a Sentence — Phrases, Clauses & Sentence Types

**Assignment:** Read **Sections 6 through 10** in the [Parts of a Sentence](../../../../resources/language_arts_sentence_structure.md) guide.

Please answer the following 10 questions based on your reading.

---

**1.** What is a phrase? How is it different from a clause? Give one example of each.

**2.** Describe a prepositional phrase. Write three sentences, each using a different prepositional phrase — one showing location, one showing time, and one showing direction.

**3.** What is a participial phrase? What job does it do in a sentence (what part of speech does it act as)? Give one example sentence.

**4.** What is a gerund phrase? What job does it do in a sentence? Write an example sentence using a gerund phrase as the subject.

**5.** What is an appositive phrase? Write a sentence using one and explain what noun it renames.

**6.** What is an independent clause? What is a dependent clause? How are they different? Give one example of each.

**7.** Name and describe the three types of dependent clauses (adjective, adverb, and noun clauses). Give a signal word and an example sentence for each.

**8.** Define and give an example sentence for each of the four sentence types by structure: simple, compound, complex, and compound-complex.

**9.** Define and give an example for each of the four sentence types by purpose: declarative, interrogative, imperative, and exclamatory.

**10.** Identify the error in each of the following and explain how to fix it:
   - *Running to catch the bus he dropped his phone.*
   - *I was late I missed breakfast.*
   - *She studied hard, she still failed the test.*
