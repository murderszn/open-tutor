# Grade 7 Language Arts — Parts of Speech — Nouns, Pronouns & Verbs Quiz

**Topic:** Parts of Speech — Nouns, Pronouns & Verbs

**Assignment:** Read the **Noun**, **Pronoun**, and **Verb** sections in the [Parts of Speech](../../../../resources/language_arts_parts_of_speech.md) guide.

Please answer the following 10 questions based on your reading.

---

**1.** What is a noun? Name and give one example of each of these noun types: common, proper, concrete, and abstract.

**2.** What is the difference between a singular noun and a plural noun? Give two examples of irregular plurals (nouns that don't just add -s).

**3.** What is a collective noun? Give three examples.

**4.** What is a pronoun, and why do we use them in writing?

**5.** List all of the personal subject pronouns and all of the personal object pronouns.

**6.** What is the difference between a possessive pronoun and a reflexive pronoun? Give one example of each.

**7.** What is a verb? Explain the difference between an action verb and a linking verb, and give two examples of each.

**8.** What is a helping (auxiliary) verb? List at least six helping verbs and show one in a sentence.

**9.** Explain the difference between a transitive verb and an intransitive verb. Give one example sentence of each.

**10.** Write a sentence in each of the following tenses using the verb *to run*: simple present, simple past, simple future, and present perfect.
