# Grade 7 Language Arts — Literary Terms — Figurative Language Quiz

**Topic:** Literary Terms — Figurative Language

**Assignment:** Read **Part 1: Figurative Language** in the [Literary Terms & Devices](../../../../resources/language_arts_literary_terms.md) guide (Simile through Symbolism).

Please answer the following 10 questions based on your reading.

---

**1.** What is a simile? Write two original simile sentences of your own (not from the guide).

**2.** What is a metaphor? How is it different from a simile? Write two original metaphors of your own.

**3.** What is an extended metaphor? Give an example of how a writer might extend a single metaphor across several sentences.

**4.** What is personification? Write two original sentences that use personification, each giving a different non-human thing a human quality.

**5.** What is alliteration? Write one sentence of your own that uses alliteration with the letter "B" and one with the letter "S."

**6.** What is onomatopoeia? Give five examples of onomatopoeia words and use two of them in original sentences.

**7.** What is hyperbole? Write two original hyperbole sentences — one about school and one about food.

**8.** What is an idiom? Choose three idioms from the guide and explain in your own words what each one really means.

**9.** What is imagery? Write a 3–4 sentence paragraph describing a rainstorm that uses at least three different senses (sight, sound, smell, touch, taste).

**10.** What is symbolism? Choose two symbols from the guide's table and explain: (a) what the symbol is, (b) what it often represents, and (c) think of one story, song, or movie where you have seen that symbol used.
