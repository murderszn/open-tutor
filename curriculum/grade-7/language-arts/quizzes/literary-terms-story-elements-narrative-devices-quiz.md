# Grade 7 Language Arts — Literary Terms — Story Elements & Narrative Devices Quiz

**Topic:** Literary Terms — Story Elements & Narrative Devices

**Assignment:** Read **Part 2: Story Elements & Narrative Devices** in the [Literary Terms & Devices](../../../../resources/language_arts_literary_terms.md) guide (Plot through the Other Important Devices table).

Please answer the following 10 questions based on your reading.

---

**1.** Name and describe each of the five stages of plot structure. Draw or describe how they connect (think of the "story mountain" shape).

**2.** Name and describe all six types of conflict. For each one, give a real example from a book, movie, or TV show you know.

**3.** What is setting? What four elements make up a story's setting? Explain how a story's setting can affect the mood and the characters' behavior.

**4.** Explain the difference between a protagonist and an antagonist, and the difference between a static and a dynamic character. Give a real example of each from a story you know.

**5.** What is the difference between direct characterization and indirect characterization? Write one example of each about the same fictional character.

**6.** What is a theme? How is it different from a topic? Take the topic "loyalty" and write a full theme statement (a complete sentence expressing a lesson or message).

**7.** Describe the four points of view (first person, second person, third person limited, third person omniscient). Which pronouns does each use? Write 2 sentences describing the same event from first person and third person omniscient.

**8.** What is foreshadowing? What is a flashback? How do writers use each device, and what effect does each create for the reader?

**9.** What is irony? Describe all three types (verbal, situational, dramatic) and give an original example of each.

**10.** What is the difference between tone and mood? Give five examples of tone words and five examples of mood words. Then read this sentence and identify its tone AND its mood: *"The general sat alone in the empty throne room, staring at the crown he had sacrificed everything to win."*
