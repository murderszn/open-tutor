# Grade 7 Language Arts — Great Books — Classic Novels, Poems & Short Stories Quiz

**Topic:** Great Books — Classic Novels, Poems & Short Stories

**Assignment:** Read **Parts 3 through 7** (Classic American Literature through Essential Literary Movements) in the [Great Books & Classic Stories](../../../../resources/language_arts_great_books_and_stories.md) guide.

Please answer the following 10 questions based on your reading.

---

**1.** Choose three classic American novels from the guide. For each one: state the title, author, and year; give a 2-sentence summary; and name one major theme.

**2.** What is *To Kill a Mockingbird* about? When is it set, and what major theme does it explore? Why do you think it is still read and taught today?

**3.** What is *Animal Farm* about? Who wrote it? On the surface it's a story about farm animals — but what is the deeper message the author is really making?

**4.** Choose two classic British or world literature titles from the guide. For each: summarize the story in 2–3 sentences and name one theme.

**5.** Choose three poems from Part 5 of the guide. For each: name the poet, write what the poem is about in 1–2 sentences, and explain what you think the poem's message is.

**6.** What is *"The Gift of the Magi"* about? What type of irony appears at the end of the story? Why does it make the ending both sad and touching at the same time?

**7.** What is *"The Tell-Tale Heart"* about? What literary device does Poe use heavily — and what does the beating heart represent in the story?

**8.** What is the Harlem Renaissance? When did it take place? Name two authors or poets from that movement and explain why it was significant in American literary history.

**9.** Match each literary movement to its key idea and era. Explain in your own words the difference between Realism and Romanticism. Give one author associated with each.

**10.** Project question: Of all the books, poems, myths, or stories in this guide, which one interests you most and which one surprises you most — and why? Write at least one full paragraph for each.
