# Grade 7 Language Arts — Parts of a Sentence — Subject, Predicate & Objects Quiz

**Topic:** Parts of a Sentence — Subject, Predicate & Objects

**Assignment:** Read **Sections 1 through 5** in the [Parts of a Sentence](../../../../resources/language_arts_sentence_structure.md) guide.

Please answer the following 10 questions based on your reading.

---

**1.** What are the two core parts that every complete sentence must have? What happens if one is missing?

**2.** What is the difference between a simple subject and a complete subject? Write one example sentence and identify both.

**3.** What is a compound subject? Write a sentence that contains one.

**4.** What is the difference between a simple predicate and a complete predicate? Write one example sentence and identify both.

**5.** What is a compound predicate? Write a sentence that uses one.

**6.** What is a direct object? What question does it answer? Write a sentence that includes a direct object and underline it.

**7.** What is an indirect object? What question does it answer? How do you tell the difference between a direct and indirect object in the same sentence?

**8.** What is a predicate nominative? Give an example sentence and explain why the underlined word is a predicate nominative.

**9.** What is a predicate adjective? Give an example sentence and explain why the underlined word is a predicate adjective. List at least four linking verbs that can introduce a predicate adjective.

**10.** Identify the subject (S), verb (V), direct object (DO), and indirect object (IO) in the following sentence: *"The coach threw Marcus the winning ball after the game."*
