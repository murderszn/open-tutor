# Week 10 Quiz — Supply, Demand, and Elasticity (formative)

**Objectives 1–4 · 30 points · Complete before the Week 10 evidence
lab.** All data below are fictional practice data. Show your work
for every computation: a bare elasticity or gap number earns no
credit. Answers are in [../answer-key.md](../answer-key.md) — try
the quiz before looking.

## Question 1 — Read the Riverside market (Objectives 1–2; 6 pts)

The Riverside apple market, per week (fictional data):

| Price per pound | Quantity demanded (lb/week) | Quantity supplied (lb/week) |
|---:|---:|---:|
| $1 | 100 | 20 |
| $2 | 80 | 40 |
| $3 | 60 | 60 |
| $4 | 40 | 80 |
| $5 | 20 | 100 |

(a) What are the equilibrium price and quantity? (2 pts)
(b) At $4, is there a surplus or a shortage, and how large?
(2 pts)
(c) At $2, is there a surplus or a shortage, and how large?
(2 pts)

## Question 2 — Shifts (Objective 3; 6 pts)

For each event, name the curve that shifts (demand or supply) and
its direction (increase/right or decrease/left). For events (a)
and (c) only, also predict the new equilibrium price and quantity
directions.

(a) Buyers' incomes rise, and the good is a normal good.
(b) A key input becomes more expensive.
(c) The price of a complement falls.
(d) The good's own price rises.

## Question 3 — Elasticity (Objective 4; 6 pts)

A good's price rises from $5 to $7, and quantity demanded falls
from 90 to 70 units per week (fictional data).

(a) Compute the price elasticity of demand with the midpoint
formula, showing both percentage changes. (3 pts)
(b) Classify demand as elastic, inelastic, or unit elastic.
(1 pt)
(c) Did the seller's total revenue rise or fall? Show the two
revenue figures. (2 pts)

## Question 4 — Two shifts at once (Objective 3; 6 pts)

In a market, demand increases and supply decreases at the same
time.

(a) What happens to equilibrium price — for certain? Explain in
one or two sentences. (3 pts)
(b) What happens to equilibrium quantity? Explain why the answer
is different from (a). (3 pts)

## Question 5 — Error analysis (Objectives 1, 3; 6 pts)

A student writes: "The price of bus rides rose from $2 to $3, so
demand for bus rides fell from 800 to 600 rides a day."

(a) Identify the wording error and rewrite the sentence correctly.
(3 pts)
(b) Explain what *would* count as demand for bus rides falling,
giving one concrete example. (3 pts)
