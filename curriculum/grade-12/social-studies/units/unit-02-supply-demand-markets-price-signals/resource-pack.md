# Resource Pack — Unit 02: Supply, Demand, Markets, and Price Signals

**Summary (adult planning judgment):** Supports a grade-12 learner's
four weeks on supply, demand, equilibrium, shifts, elasticity, and
price controls — with free, no-account readings and videos that
supplement (never replace) the unit's explicit lessons. Checked
2026-10-22 unless noted.

## Internal references

- [Supply & demand economics guide](../../../../../resources/supply_and_demand_economics.md)
  — adult-side reference for market vocabulary and shift logic; its

  every explanation for grade 12 and never assigns it as lesson
  text. It presents no elasticity formula; the unit's Lesson 4
  treatment governs.
- [Government basics guide](../../../../../resources/government_basics.md)

  — adult-side reference for revenue and price arithmetic behind

  — drove this pack's format.
