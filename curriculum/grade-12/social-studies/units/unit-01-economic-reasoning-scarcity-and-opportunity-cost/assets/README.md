  frontier. The same image returns in the
  [unit assessment](../assessments/unit-assessment.md) (Task 2) so the
  learner re-analyzes a familiar visual under assessment conditions.

  the left, tall green corn fields on the right, a farmer at the visible
  boundary, sun and clouds above. No text, letters, or numbers appear
  anywhere. The wheat/corn split is a stylized symbolic illustration of a
  two-good tradeoff, not a depiction of any real farm; it is labeled an AI
  illustration in the caption and lesson. Nothing in it asserts a historical
  or statistical fact; it is a reasoning prompt, not evidence.
