# Week 6 Quiz — Economic Reasoning (formative)

Take in W6S4 before the project defense. ~20 minutes. Closed-note except
your vocabulary list. Every item is solved in [answer-key.md](../answer-key.md).
All figures are fictional practice data.

**1.** Define *scarcity* in one sentence. (2 pts)

**2.** You choose a weekend job paying $120 over tutoring younger students
(which you value at $90 of satisfaction) and over rest (valued at $50).
What is the opportunity cost of the job? Why is it not $140? (3 pts)

**3.** The Greenfield Farm schedule (Lesson 02): A(100 wheat, 0 corn),
B(80, 30), C(50, 50), D(20, 65), E(0, 75). Moving from B to C, what is the
per-unit opportunity cost of corn in bushels of wheat? Show the division.
(3 pts)

**4.** On the same PPF, is the point (30 wheat, 40 corn) efficient,
inefficient, or unattainable? Explain in one sentence. (2 pts)

**5.** A city raises parking-meter rates downtown. Name the incentive type,
the target group, and the predicted behavior change. (3 pts)

**6.** A school pays students $10 for each A on a report card, and some
students start pressuring teachers for grade changes instead of studying
more. In incentive terms, what happened? (2 pts)

**7.** Marginal benefit per unit: 18, 14, 10, 6. Marginal cost per unit:
4, 8, 12, 16. How many units are optimal? Show the step-by-step comparison.
(3 pts)

**8.** In your own words: why do economists compare the *next* unit's
benefit and cost rather than the totals? (2 pts)

**9.** A country has private businesses and farms, but the government owns
the airlines and sets bread prices. Which system type best describes it,
and what is one market feature and one command feature? (3 pts)

**10.** Explain the difference between a *shift* of the PPF and a movement
*along* the PPF, with one example of each. (3 pts)

**Total: 28 points.** Scoring: 25–28 = ready for the assessment; 20–24 =
review Lessons 2 and 4 before W6S5; below 20 = re-teach with the adult
using the practice set.
