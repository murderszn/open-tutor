# Formative Quiz — Week 17 (Unit 04)

**Points: 20.** Closed book except your own Lesson 01–04 notes and one
blank graph. All numbers are fictional practice data. Show setups for
computations. Solutions are in the [answer key](../answer-key.md) —
this page contains no answers.

**Q1 (2 points).** A factory's smoke harms neighbors who buy nothing
from the factory. (a) Is this a positive or negative externality, and
does it arise in production or consumption? (b) Does the market
over- or under-produce the factory's good?

**Q2 (4 points).** Demand is P = 80 − Q and marginal private cost is
P = 20 + Q. Each unit imposes a $10 external cost. Find (a) the
market quantity, (b) the efficient quantity, (c) deadweight loss.
Show MSC in your setup.

**Q3 (2 points).** Classify each good with both tests and name its
box: (a) light from a streetlamp; (b) fish in an open-access lake.
Give one reason per classification.

**Q4 (2 points).** Used cars: good cars are worth $6,000 to buyers
and $5,500 to sellers; bad cars are worth $2,000 to buyers and $1,500
to sellers. Half the cars are good, and buyers cannot tell. Compute
the expected value, and state whether good-car sellers stay in the
market.

**Q5 (2 points).** Two firms must cut 80 tons of emissions. Firm A's
abatement cost is $10/ton; Firm B's is $40/ton. Compute the cost of
equal cuts (40 tons each) and of the cheapest allocation, and state
the saving.

**Q6 (2 points).** Option P costs $80 and benefits $150; Option Q
costs $180 and benefits $240 (fictional annual data). Compute both
net benefits and the marginal net benefit of moving P → Q. Which
option is efficient?

**Q7 (2 points).** Before a tax, a good sells at $30. A tax of $8 on
sellers leaves buyers paying $34 and sellers keeping $26. Split the
$8 between buyers and sellers in dollars and percentages.

**Q8 (2 points).** A tax takes $600 from a $30,000 income and $1,200
from a $120,000 income. Compute each share of income and classify the
tax.

**Q9 (1 point).** State the two conditions under which Coase
bargaining can reach the efficient outcome without a tax.

**Q10 (1 point).** Define "free rider" in one sentence, using the word
*nonexcludable* correctly.
