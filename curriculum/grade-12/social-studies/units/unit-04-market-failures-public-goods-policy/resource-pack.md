# Resource Pack — Unit 04: Market Failures, Public Goods, and Policy Tradeoffs

**Summary (adult planning judgment):** Supports a grade-12 learner's
four weeks on externalities, public goods and common resources,
information failures and market power, policy evaluation, and
distributional tradeoffs — with free, no-account readings and videos
that supplement (never replace) the unit's explicit lessons. Checked
2026-11-04 unless noted.

## Internal references

- [Supply & demand economics guide](../../../../../resources/supply_and_demand_economics.md)
  — adult-side reference for market vocabulary and shift logic; its
  kid-friendly framing targets grades 5–8, so the adult re-levels
  every explanation for grade 12 and never assigns it as lesson
  text. It presents no externality schedules; the unit's Lesson 01
  treatments govern.
- [Government basics guide](../../../../../resources/government_basics.md)
  — adult-side reference for rule-of-law, federalism, and tax/spend
  vocabulary behind Lessons 04–05.
- [U.S. principles guide](../../../../../resources/united_states_understanding_and_principles.md)
  — adult-side reference for rule-of-law and constitutional-economy
  framing behind Lesson 05's institutions discussion.
- [Resource Finder prompt](../../../../../teachers/ai-assistants/resource_finder.md)
  — drove this pack's format.

## Web references (checked 2026-11-04 unless noted)

1. **EconLib — "Roles of Government" (high-school topics page)** —
   the unit's definitions anchor: most arguments for intervention
   rest on public goods and externalities; includes the *Concise
   Encyclopedia of Economics* entries on public goods (nonexcludability
   and nonrivalrous consumption, fireworks example) and externalities,
   plus government-failure and income-distribution entries.
   <https://www.econlib.org/library/Topics/HighSchool/RolesofGovernment.html?to_print=true>
   (page opened 2026-11-04; free, no account needed).
2. **Social Sci LibreTexts — "31.20: Externalities and Public Goods"**
   — a concise open-textbook treatment matching Lessons 01–02:
   externality definition, the inefficiency result, subsidy/tax and
   market-creation remedies, excludability/rivalry definitions, and
   the public-good conclusion. Published 2020-03-31, updated
   2024-06-10.
   <https://socialsci.libretexts.org/Bookshelves/Economics/Introductory_Comprehensive_Economics/Economics_-_Theory_Through_Applications/31%3A_Toolkit/31.20%3A_Externalities_and_Public_Goods>
   (page opened 2026-11-04; free, no account needed).
3. **Marginal Revolution University (homepage)** — free video
   economics courses; see the Videos section above for the
   confirmation note. <https://www.mruniversity.com/>
4. **National Council for the Social Studies — C3 Framework landing
   page** — the framework behind the unit's standards references:
   the four-dimension inquiry arc and its guiding principles.
   <https://www.socialstudies.org/standards/c3>
   (page opened 2026-11-04; the indicator descriptions in the unit
   follow the track audit's source-backed crosswalk, not this
   landing page alone).
5. **Federal Reserve Education** — free K-12 economics and
   personal-finance lessons and activities from the Federal Reserve
   System. <https://www.federalreserveeducation.org/>
   (homepage confirmed in Unit 03's pack, checked 2026-10-28; free,
   educator-oriented, no account needed for resources; the adult
   re-confirms before assigning).
