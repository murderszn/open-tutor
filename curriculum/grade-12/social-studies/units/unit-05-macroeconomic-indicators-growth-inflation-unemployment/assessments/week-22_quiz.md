# Formative Quiz — Unit 05 (Week 22)

**20 points · about 30 minutes · closed book except the formula sheet
below**

Formula sheet (supplied with the quiz): GDP = C + I + G + NX ·
Real GDP = (Nominal GDP ÷ GDP deflator) × 100 · CPI = (current basket
cost ÷ base basket cost) × 100 · Inflation rate = (CPI now − CPI
before) ÷ CPI before × 100 · Unemployment rate = (Unemployed ÷ Labor
force) × 100 · Participation rate = (Labor force ÷ Working-age
population) × 100.

All numbers are **fictional practice data** unless a source is named.
Show your work; a bare answer earns half credit. Solutions are in the
[answer key](../answer-key.md).

**1. (2 points)** Fictional practice data ($ billions): consumption
6,000; investment 1,500; government purchases 1,800; exports 900;
imports 1,200. Compute net exports and GDP.

**2. (2 points)** Nominal GDP is $9,000 billion and the GDP deflator
is 112.5. What is real GDP? Show the division.

**3. (2 points)** Classify each as counted in this year's GDP or not,
with one reason each: (a) a used car sold by its owner; (b) a new road
built by the government.

**4. (2 points)** A basket costs $250.00 in the base year and $262.50
this year. Compute the CPI (base year = 100) and the inflation rate.

**5. (2 points)** A worker's wage rises from $18.00 to $18.90 an hour
while the CPI rises 6 percent. Compute the real wage in last year's
dollars and say whether the worker is better or worse off.

**6. (2 points)** A labor force of 40,000 has 2,000 unemployed people.
What is the unemployment rate? Show the arithmetic in one line.

**7. (2 points)** Match each case to frictional, structural, or
cyclical unemployment: (a) a recent graduate searching for a first
job; (b) a hotel worker laid off when tourism collapses in a
recession; (c) a switchboard operator whose job no longer exists.

**8. (2 points)** Name two categories of valuable activity that GDP
leaves out, with one example of each.

**9. (2 points)** In Q3 2026, U.S. real GDP grew 0.8 percent in the
quarter and 3.4 percent at an annual rate. Explain in two sentences
what the two numbers mean and why they differ.

**10. (2 points)** The BLS did not publish a 12-month inflation rate
for October 2026. Why not, in two sentences?
