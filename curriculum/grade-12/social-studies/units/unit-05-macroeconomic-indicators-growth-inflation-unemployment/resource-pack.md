# Resource Pack — Unit 05: Macroeconomic Indicators

**Goal:** support a grade-12 learner in computing GDP, inflation, and
unemployment from data, reading three real U.S. statistical releases,
and judging the state of the economy. Every external recommendation
below was checked on **2026-11-13**; the adult re-verifies live figures
at teaching time. All videos are free; no account is required to watch
the Khan Academy videos or to use FRED or the BLS/BEA releases.

Direct links verified 2026-11-13 (free, no account required):

1. **Khan Academy — "Real GDP and nominal GDP"** (video). The
   one-good (apples) demonstration of why nominal GDP can rise while

   <https://www.khanacademy.org/economics-finance-domain/ap-macroeconomics/economic-iondicators-and-the-business-cycle/real-vs-nominal-gdp/v/real-gdp-and-nominal-gdp>

   matches Lesson 01's "what GDP misses."

   (video). A worked deflator computation using the real 2011 U.S.

   <https://www.khanacademy.org/economics-finance-domain/old-macroeconomics/gdp-topic-old/real-nominal-gdp-tutorial/v/example-calculating-real-gdp-with-a-deflator>

## Web references

Verified 2026-11-13; the adult re-verifies at teaching time:

1. **Bureau of Economic Analysis — "GDP (Advance Estimate), 3rd
   Quarter 2026"** (news release, October 29, 2026, BEA 26-45). Real
   GDP +3.4 percent annual rate (0.8 percent quarterly); Q2 +2.2
   percent; current-dollar GDP +5.6 percent; PCE price index +2.1
   percent. The release the learner reads in Week 20 S3.
   <https://bea.gov/news/2026/gdp-advance-estimate-3rd-quarter-2026>
2. **Bureau of Labor Statistics — "Consumer Price Index — October
   2026"** (news release, November 10, 2026, USDL-26-1685). CPI-U
   +0.4 percent seasonally adjusted; shelter +0.3 percent; food +0.5
   percent; energy +0.7 percent; index level 337.511 (1982–84 = 100);
   no 12-month changes published. The release the learner reads in
   Week 21 S3.
   <https://www.bls.gov/news.release/cpi.nr0.htm>
3. **Bureau of Labor Statistics — "The Employment Situation —
   October 2026"** (news release, November 6, 2026, USDL-26-1632).
   Payrolls +165,000; unemployment rate 4.1 percent; 7.1 million
   unemployed; group rates (teenagers 13.9 percent; Black 6.6 percent;
   Asian 2.8 percent; Hispanic 4.7 percent). The release the learner
   reads in Week 22 S3.
   <https://www.bls.gov/news.release/archives/empsit_11062026.htm>
4. **FRED (Federal Reserve Bank of St. Louis)** — free economic data
   portal; graph and download series such as real GDP, real GDP per
   capita, the CPI, and the unemployment rate. No account required to
   view. The tool for the Lesson 04 extension and the project.
   <https://fred.stlouisfed.org/>
5. **National Council for the Social Studies — C3 Framework**
   (landing page). The reference framework for this unit's indicator
   codes (D2.Eco.10.9-12, D2.Eco.11.9-12, D2.Eco.13.9-12); codes exact,
   descriptions paraphrased; no state adoption claimed.
   <https://www.socialstudies.org/standards/c3>
6. **Marginal Revolution University — OpenStax Macroeconomics
   video-mapped syllabus** (see Videos, item 4). The course map behind
   the video recommendations above.
   <https://mru.org/teacher-resources/university-video-mappings/openstax-macroeconomics-textbook-video-mapped-syllabus>
