# Resource Pack — Unit 03: Firms, Labor, Competition, and Market Structures

**Summary (adult planning judgment):** Supports a grade-12 learner's
four weeks on business forms, firm costs and profit, labor markets
and human capital, market structures, and entrepreneurship and
institutions — with free, no-account readings and videos that
supplement (never replace) the unit's explicit lessons. Checked
2026-10-28 unless noted.

## Web references (checked 2026-10-28 unless noted)

1. **U.S. Bureau of Labor Statistics — median weekly earnings by
   educational attainment, table 37b** — the unit's real dataset:
   2024 annual averages for full-time workers age 25 and over
   (less than high school $738; high school $930; some college or
   associate $1,056; bachelor's only $1,543; advanced $1,901).
   <https://www.bls.gov/cps/cpsaat37b.htm> (page opened 2026-10-28;
   the table's own note warns that its 2025 estimates are 11-month
   averages excluding October 2025 and are not strictly comparable,
   so this unit uses 2024 only).
2. **U.S. Bureau of Labor Statistics — "Education pays, 2024"**
   (Career Outlook, May 2025) — the same earnings pattern with
   unemployment rates by education, useful for the Lesson 3
   discussion of what the table shows and does not show.
   <https://www.bls.gov:443/careeroutlook/2025/data-on-display/print/education-pays.htm>
   (linked from BLS search results 2026-10-28; the adult previews it
   before assigning).
3. **Federal Reserve Education** — free classroom economics and
   personal-finance resources from the Federal Reserve System.
   <https://www.federalreserveeducation.org/> (homepage confirmed
   in Unit 02's pack, checked 2026-10-22; the adult re-confirms
   before assigning).
4. **Marginal Revolution University** — free economics video
   courses and practice tools; founded by Tyler Cowen and Alex
   Tabarrok, housed at the Mercatus Center at George Mason
   University. <https://www.mruniversity.com/> (homepage confirmed
   in Unit 02's pack, checked 2026-10-22; the adult re-confirms

5. **C3 Framework standards page, National Council for the Social

   the repository's internal supply/demand guide; the adult
