# Week 13 Quiz — Firms, Labor, and Market Structures (formative, 30 points)

Covers objectives 1–4 (business forms, costs, labor, structures).
Fictional numbers are practice data unless a question names the BLS
table. Answer in the space provided; show arithmetic where asked.
Solutions are in [answer-key.md](../answer-key.md) — do not open it
until the adult collects the quiz.

1. (2 points) A founder owns a shop alone and is personally liable
   for its debts. What is this form called?
2. (2 points) A corporation fails owing more than it owns. A
   shareholder invested $3,000. What is the most she can lose as a
   shareholder, and what is the principle called?
3. (4 points) Use the Harbor Bakery schedule (unit README, price $9).
   (a) What is profit at 50 loaves? (b) What is MC per loaf for the
   step from 50 to 60 loaves? (c) Should the bakery take that step?
   Why, in one sentence?
4. (3 points) The market price for a firm's product is $4 and its AVC
   at its best operating output is $6. Its fixed cost is $200 a week.
   Should it operate or shut down in the short run? What loss does
   each choice produce at that output, in words (you need not compute
   an exact operating loss — state which loss is larger and why)?
5. (3 points) One more worker adds 50 units a day, each selling for
   $3. The daily wage is $160. Compute VMP and state the hiring
   decision with a reason.
6. (3 points) Demand for an occupation's product falls. Predict the
   direction of the wage and of employment, and name the curve that
   shifted.
7. (4 points) From the BLS 2024 table: (a) the weekly difference
   between the bachelor's-only median ($1,543) and the high school
   median ($930); (b) one careful sentence about what that difference
   does *not* prove.
8. (3 points) A market has many sellers, differentiated products, and
   easy entry. Classify it, and name one feature that distinguishes
   it from perfect competition.
9. (3 points) A market has one seller and a patent barrier. Classify
   it, and state the expected price and output effects compared with
   a competitive market (higher/lower for each).
10. (3 points) A very large firm sells a product with many close
    substitutes and faces low barriers to entry. A learner calls it a
    monopoly because it is large. Is the learner right? Explain with
    the three tests in two to three sentences.
