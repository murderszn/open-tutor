# Resource Pack — Unit 05: Biodiversity, Ecosystem Services, and Conservation

**Goal:** Give a grade-12 learner (and guiding adult) free, no-account
routes to every core objective: measuring biodiversity, how it
changes, ecosystem services and valuation, threats and their
evidence, and conservation design. **Age fit:** high-school Earth
and Space Science (grade 12); the adult previews every link for
advertising, comments, reading level, and suitability before the
learner opens it. This pack is an adult planning judgment, not a
prescription. Every core objective also has a free, no-account,
text-based route inside the unit itself — no video or simulation is
required to learn the material.

## Focused queries

1. `Shannon diversity index explained worked example richness evenness`
2. `species area relationship S=cA^z island biogeography explained`
3. `Millennium Ecosystem Assessment four categories provisioning regulating cultural supporting`
4. `IPBES 2019 one million species threatened five drivers biodiversity loss`
5. `Living Planet Index 73% what it measures average population decline`
6. `Kunming-Montreal Global Biodiversity Framework Target 3 30 by 30 explained`

## Web references

Checked 2026-11-13 unless noted.

1. **IPBES — Global Assessment Report on Biodiversity and Ecosystem
   Services** — free, no account; the 2019 assessment behind the
   unit's headline figures (about 1 million of an estimated 8
   million animal and plant species threatened; five drivers of
   loss). Page opened and scope confirmed ("Summary for
   Policymakers, approved … May 2019 in Paris"; full report 1,148
   pages; suggested citation with DOI 10.5281/zenodo.3553579).
   Use for: Lessons 02 and 04 background; the adult reads the
   Summary for Policymakers, not the full report, with the
   learner. Real reference data — record the access date with
   every reading.
   <https://www.ipbes.net/global-assessment>
2. **IUCN Red List of Threatened Species** — free, no account; the
   authoritative source for species' extinction risk, with a
   species search the learner can use directly. Page opened and
   contents confirmed 2026-11-13: more than 49,500 species
   threatened (28% of assessed species), more than 175,900
   species assessed; nine categories (Not Evaluated, Data
   Deficient, Least Concern, Near Threatened, Vulnerable,
   Endangered, Critically Endangered, Extinct in the Wild,
   Extinct) with definitions; group figures including amphibians
   41%, mammals 26%, birds 11%, reef corals 44%. Use for:
   Lesson 04 categories and the extension task; project species
   checks. Real reference data — record the access date with
   every reading.
   <https://www.iucnredlist.org/>
3. **Convention on Biological Diversity — Kunming-Montreal Global
   Biodiversity Framework** — free, no account; the official
   framework page. Page opened and contents confirmed 2026-11-13:
   adopted at COP 15 after a four-year negotiation process; 4
   goals for 2050 and 23 targets for 2030; official text in
   decision 15/4. Use for: Lesson 05 (Targets 2 and 3); adult
   background on 30 by 30.
   <https://www.cbd.int/gbf>
4. **WWF — Living Planet Report 2024: key findings** — free, no
   account; the report's key-messages page. Contents confirmed
   2026-11-13 via the WWF Living Planet site: a 73% decline in
   the average size of monitored wildlife populations over 50
   years (1970–2020), steepest in Latin America and the Caribbean
   (−95%), Africa (−76%), Asia–Pacific (−60%), and freshwater
   ecosystems (−85%). Use for: Lesson 04, with the lesson's
   reading of what the figure is and is not. Real reference data
   — record the access date with every reading.
   <https://livingplanet.panda.org/en-GB/living-planet-report-2024-key-messages>
5. **HHMI BioInteractive — "Some Animals Are More Equal than
   Others: Keystone Species and Trophic Cascades"** — free, no
   account; short film (under 30 minutes) on Paine's starfish
   experiment and Estes's sea-otter/kelp work, with an audio
   descriptive version and a film guide. Page opened and contents
   confirmed 2026-11-13 (description, learning targets, NGSS
   HS-LS2.A/B/C and HS-LS4.C, AP Biology and AP Environmental
   Science topics). Use for: Lesson 02 / Session S7 film session;
   the worked-data alternative in the lesson stands in if the
   film is unavailable.
   <https://www.biointeractive.org/classroom-resources/some-animals-are-more-equal-others-keystone-species-and-trophic-cascades>
6. **iNaturalist** — free, nonprofit, community science platform;
   browsing observations needs no account (an account is needed
   only to contribute). Page opened and contents confirmed
   2026-11-13: 300 million+ observations, 550,000+ species
   documented, 7,000+ papers citing iNaturalist data; "research
   grade" observations enter the global scientific record.
   **Privacy note:** the learner browses public observations only
   and does not create an account, post locations, or share
   personal information; the adult checks the area searched and
   the access date. Use for: project public-data alternative and
   identification help.
   <https://www.inaturalist.org/>
7. **GBIF — Global Biodiversity Information Facility** — free, no
   account; open access to biodiversity occurrence data worldwide.
   Page opened and contents confirmed 2026-11-13: "Free and open
   access to biodiversity data"; GBIF-mediated data used for IUCN
   Red List range and Area of Occupancy calculations. Use for:
   project public-data alternative; adult background on where
   biodiversity data comes from. Real reference data — record the
   access date and search terms with every reading.
   <https://www.gbif.org/>
