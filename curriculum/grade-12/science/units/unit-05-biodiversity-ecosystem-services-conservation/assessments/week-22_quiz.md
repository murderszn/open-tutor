# Week-22 Formative Quiz — Unit 05

**40 points · ~50 minutes · Calculator allowed · All datasets are
fictional practice data unless stated otherwise**

Work alone. Show every calculation step. The adult scores same-day;
worked solutions are in the adult-only `answer-key.md`.

## Part A — Measuring biodiversity (Objectives 1; 12 pts)

1. (4 pts) A fictional plot has richness 6 and a Shannon index
   H′ = 1.79. Given ln 6 = 1.79, what is its evenness J? What
   does your answer mean in plain words?
2. (4 pts) Compute H′ for a fictional sample with counts 80, 10,
   10 (total N = 100, richness S = 3). Then compute evenness J
   (ln 3 = 1.10). Show each p·ln p term. (Use ln 0.80 = −0.22,
   ln 0.10 = −2.30.)
3. (4 pts) Two fictional stream surveys: Team 1 waded for 30
   minutes and found 5 fish species; Team 2 waded for 3 hours
   and found 9 fish species. A student concludes the second
   stream is more diverse. Explain in two sentences why that
   conclusion is not supported.

## Part B — How biodiversity changes (Objective 2; 10 pts)

4. (5 pts) A fictional island group follows S = 4 · A^0.25.
   Compute S for A = 16 km² and for A = 256 km². Show the fourth
   roots you use.
5. (5 pts) Give one piece of evidence that life changed Earth's
   systems and one piece that Earth's systems changed life, and
   write one sentence saying why the pair together makes a
   coevolution argument.

## Part C — Services and threats (Objectives 3, 4; 12 pts)

6. (4 pts) Classify each service into one MEA category
   (provisioning, regulating, cultural, supporting): (a) timber
   harvested from a forest; (b) a wetland slowing a flood;
   (c) the spiritual value of a sacred grove; (d) nutrient
   cycling in soil.
7. (4 pts) The Living Planet Index fell 73% on average between
   1970 and 2020. In two sentences, say what that figure
   measures, and name one thing it does not mean.
8. (4 pts) (a) A species assessed as "facing a very high risk of
   extinction in the wild" belongs in which IUCN Red List
   category? (b) Which category means there is too little data
   to judge a species' risk?

## Part D — Conservation design (Objective 5; 6 pts)

9. (6 pts) A conservation design scores biodiversity gain 2
   (weighted ×2), cost 2, feasibility 3, and community support 2
   on the Lesson 05 matrix. What is its total out of 15? Then
   name one refinement you would ask for before approving the
   design, and the evaluation finding it would answer.
