# Week-06 Formative Quiz — Unit 01

**Session S19 · ~40 minutes · 40 points · closed notebook except the isotope table below**

t½ values (real reference data): U-238 → Pb-206: 4.468 Ga; K-40 →
Ar-40: 1.248 Ga; Rb-87 → Sr-87: 48.8 Ga; C-14: 5,730 y.
Equations: N = N₀·(1/2)^(t/t½); t = t½·ln(N₀/N)/ln 2;
N/N₀ = parent/(parent + daughter).

**1. (Objective 1 · 8 pts)** A cliff shows, bottom to top: shale,
sandstone, limestone. A basalt dike cuts all three. An eroded surface
caps the limestone, and conglomerate sits on top. List every event
from oldest to youngest, citing one stratigraphic principle for each
ordering step.

**2. (Objective 1 · 6 pts)** On the one-meter Earth-history strip
(1 m = 4.54 Ga), the Phanerozoic eon began 541 Ma ago. How many
centimeters from the "today" end does it begin? Show the conversion.

**3. (Objective 1 · 6 pts)** A volcanic rock has K-40 and Ar-40 in a
1:3 ratio (parent:daughter = 1:3). Assuming a closed system and no
initial Ar-40, compute its age in Ga. Show the parent-fraction
conversion and every step.

**4. (Objective 1 · 4 pts)** A rock dated by K-Ar was later heated, and
much of its Ar-40 escaped before analysis. Is the computed age too
young, too old, or unaffected? Name the failed assumption in one
sentence.

**5. (Objective 2 · 6 pts)** A mid-ocean ridge spreads at a full rate
of 4 cm/yr. Seafloor sampled 600 km from the ridge axis: estimate its
age in Ma. Show the half-rate conversion and the division.

**6. (Objective 2 · 4 pts)** Magnetic stripes on the two flanks of a
ridge are mirror images of each other. In two to three sentences,
explain why this symmetry supports seafloor spreading rather than
fixed continents.

**7. (Objective 3 · 4 pts)** Describe the mantle convection loop in
three to four sentences: where rock rises, where it sinks, and what
"cycles" means for the matter involved.

**8. (Objective 4 · 2 pts — error analysis)** A learner's model report
reads: "d = 4 cm/yr × 50,000,000 yr = 200,000,000." Name two things
missing from this report that a scientific model report must include.

*Scoring: 36–40 exceeds · 30–35 meets · 24–29 approaching (reteach with
the adult) · below 24 redo after reteaching. This quiz is formative:
it diagnoses, it does not close the unit.*
