# Resource Pack — Unit 01: Earth-System Evidence, Geologic Time, and Quantitative Models

**Goal:** Give a grade-12 learner (and guiding adult) free, no-account
routes to every core objective: deep time and relative dating,
radiometric-dating calculations, plate-tectonics evidence from crustal
ages, mantle convection, and a first quantitative model with
uncertainty. **Age fit:** high-school Earth and Space Science
(grade 12); the adult previews every link for advertising, comments,
reading level, and suitability before the learner opens it. This pack
is an adult planning judgment, not a prescription.

## Access notes

- Every core objective has a free, no-account route: Lessons 01–03
  via the in-unit teaching and PhET "Radioactive Dating Game";
  Lesson 04 via the in-unit transect/SVG work plus the two public
  data sites; Lesson 05 via the in-unit model build; the project
  via the PhET alternative if the data sites fail.
- No video is required viewing — all four are labeled search links
  the adult previews and selects from. Never assign a video
  unwatched: check for ads, comments, reading level, and the
  specific content notes above.
- Captions: confirm captions are available on any assigned video
  before assigning (accessibility rule, scope-and-sequence §7).
- The USGS and Smithsonian sites are data sources, not lessons:
  the adult stays nearby during all browsing and logs the preview.

## Review notes

- Relevance: references 1–4 map directly onto unit objectives 1–4;
  reference 5 is agency background. All are primary or
  authoritative (federal science agency, Smithsonian research
  program, University of Colorado PhET).
- Credibility: USGS and the Smithsonian GVP are the authoritative
  public sources for earthquake and volcano data; PhET sims are
  built and tested by the University of Colorado for classroom use.
- Factual caveats: the PhET "Radioactive Dating Game" simplifies
  (e.g., it does not teach the closed-system or initial-daughter
  assumptions) — pair it with Lesson 03, never as a standalone.
  The "Plate Tectonics" sim illustrates boundary processes; it is
  not a data source for real quake/volcano locations — the project
  requires learners to label it as substitute data when used.
- Checked 2026-10-14. Re-check any URL the learner will open if more
  than a few months pass; agency sites reorganize.
