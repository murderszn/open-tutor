# Resource Pack — Unit 04: Climate Evidence, Feedbacks, and Uncertainty

**Goal:** Give a grade-12 learner (and guiding adult) free, no-account
routes to every core objective: the energy budget and greenhouse
effect, feedbacks, the quantified carbon cycle, independent
evidence lines, and model/scenario interpretation. **Age fit:**
high-school Earth and Space Science (grade 12); the adult previews
every link for advertising, comments, reading level, and suitability
before the learner opens it. This pack is an adult planning judgment,
not a prescription. Every core objective also has a free, no-account,
text-based route inside the unit itself — no video or simulation is
required to learn the material.

## Simulations and data tools

- **PhET Greenhouse Effect simulation** (University of Colorado,
  free, no account; teacher guide found and scope confirmed
  2026-11-03): explore how greenhouse-gas concentration changes
  infrared escape and surface temperature. The simulation models
  the drivers of the greenhouse effect, not the effects of
  climate change, and shows only two radiation types (visible
  and infrared) — the adult states both limits before assigning.
  Search PhET for "Greenhouse Effect" and preview the Waves and
  Layer Model screens before assigning. Teacher guide:
  <https://phet.colorado.edu/files/teachers-guide/greenhouse-effect-html-guide_en.pdf>
  (Use for: Lesson 01, Session S3; the lesson's text alternative
  stands in if the simulation is inaccessible.)

## Web references

Checked 2026-11-03 unless noted.

1. **NASA Science — "Carbon Dioxide" Earth indicator** — free, no
   account; the Mauna Loa record since 1958, the September 2026
   value of 426 ppm, and the more-than-50% rise since the 18th
   century. Page found and scope confirmed 2026-11-03. Use for:
   Lessons 01, 03–04; project public-data alternative.
   <https://science.nasa.gov/earth/explore/earth-indicators/carbon-dioxide/>
2. **NOAA Global Monitoring Laboratory — Trends in CO₂** — free,
   no account; weekly, monthly, and annual Mauna Loa means with
   dates (weekly average for the week beginning October 25, 2026:
   427.28 ppm; September 2026 monthly mean: 426.07 ppm; values
   preliminary pending recalibration). Page found and scope
   confirmed 2026-11-03. Use for: Lesson 03 conversions; project
   public-data alternative. Learners record the date accessed.
   <https://gml.noaa.gov/ccgg/trends/>
3. **IPCC AR6 Synthesis Report — Summary for Policymakers headline
   statements** — free, no account; the headline finding that
   human activities have unequivocally caused warming, with
   global surface temperature reaching 1.1 °C above 1850–1900 in
   2011–2020, and the scenario discussion. Page found and scope
   confirmed 2026-11-03. Use for: Lessons 04–05; assessment
   question 12's observed-rate source.
   <https://www.ipcc.ch/report/ar6/syr/resources/spm-headline-statements/>
4. **California Department of Education — NGSS content standards
   (HS-ESS3-5 and HS-ESS2-4)** — free, no account; the performance
   expectations restated with clarification statements and
   assessment boundaries used for this unit's crosswalk. Page
   found and scope confirmed 2026-11-03. Use for: standards
   notes; adult verification only.
   <https://www2.cde.ca.gov/cacs/id/web/29180>
5. **NSTA — HS Weather and Climate topic page** — free, no
   account; HS-ESS2-4, HS-ESS2-2, and HS-ESS3-5 restated with
   clarification statements (including the ice–albedo feedback
   example for HS-ESS2-2). Page found and scope confirmed
   2026-11-03. Use for: standards notes; adult verification only.
   <https://static.nsta.org/ngss/20130509/topic-individual/HS-WeatherAndClimate.pdf>
6. **NOAA Global Monitoring Laboratory — Weekly average CO₂ at
   Mauna Loa** — free, no account; the most recent weekly values
   and the one-year and ten-year comparisons. Page found and
   scope confirmed 2026-11-03. Use for: Lesson 03; project
   public-data alternative when a weekly value is needed.
   <https://gml.noaa.gov/ccgg/trends/weekly.html>
