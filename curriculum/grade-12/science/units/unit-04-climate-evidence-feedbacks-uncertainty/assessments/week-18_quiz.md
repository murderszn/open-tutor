# Week-18 Formative Quiz — Unit 04: Climate Evidence, Feedbacks, and Uncertainty

**Name:** ______________ **Date:** __________ **Time:** ~25 minutes.
No notes for questions 1–8; questions 9–10 may use your project data
table. The adult scores this the same day and logs gaps for review.

1. Incoming solar radiation averages 340 W/m² and planetary albedo
   is 0.30. What are the reflected and absorbed fluxes?
2. A planet with no greenhouse effect absorbs 238 W/m². What is
   its effective radiating temperature in K and °C? What is
   Earth's observed surface temperature, and what explains the
   gap?
3. Trace the ice–albedo feedback loop in four arrows and label it
   reinforcing or balancing.
4. A forcing alone gives ΔT₀ = 1.0 °C and f = 0.5. What is the
   total warming in the gain model? Name one thing the model
   leaves out.
5. Convert 400 ppm of CO₂ to GtC. If human emissions are
   11 GtC/yr and 5.1 GtC/yr stay airborne, what is the airborne
   fraction?
6. Name two independent evidence lines for recent warming and,
   for each, one thing it measures.
7. The stratosphere cools while the troposphere warms. Which
   proposed cause does this fingerprint argue against?
8. Under SSP2-4.5, warming by 2081–2100 is 2.1–3.5 °C relative to
   1850–1900. Is that a prediction or a scenario range? Explain
   in one sentence.
9. Using your project data (or the public-data alternative):
   state your measured temperature difference (or ice-extent
   trend) and whether your prediction held.
10. **Explain your reasoning:** A single cold winter is reported
    in your town. Does it weaken the claim that the planet is
    warming? Defend your answer using weather vs. climate and
    local vs. global.

**Scoring (adult):** 1 point per question, 10 points total.
8–10: proceed to the unit assessment. 6–7: correct errors with the
answer key, then re-take the missed items orally. Below 6: re-read
the flagged lessons with the adult before the unit assessment.
