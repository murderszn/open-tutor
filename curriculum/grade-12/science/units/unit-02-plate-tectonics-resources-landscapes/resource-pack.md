# Resource Pack — Unit 02: Plate Tectonics, Resources, and Landscape Processes

**Goal:** Give a grade-12 learner (and guiding adult) free, no-account
routes to every core objective: boundary→landform reasoning,
weathering/erosion/deposition, topographic-map reading, resource
distribution, and hazard settlement decisions. **Age fit:**
high-school Earth and Space Science (grade 12); the adult previews
every link for advertising, comments, reading level, and suitability
before the learner opens it. This pack is an adult planning judgment,
not a prescription. Every core objective also has a free, no-account,
text-based route inside the unit itself — no video or simulation is
required to learn the material.

## Web references

Checked 2026-10-21 unless noted.

1. **PhET — "Plate Tectonics"** — free, no account; browser-based
   simulation of plate-boundary processes (buoyancy, density,
   boundary types). Page opened and title confirmed ("Plate
   Tectonics | Buoyancy • Density • Plate Tectonics | PhET
   Simulations"). The adult verifies keyboard operability before
   assigning; the worked-data alternative is Lesson 01's practice
   sets. Use for: Lesson 01 / Session S3 simulation session;
   project simulation alternative.
   <https://phet.colorado.edu/en/simulation/plate-tectonics>
2. **USGS (U.S. Geological Survey)** — free, no account; the federal
   science agency behind reference data used across the unit
   (earthquakes, geology, mapping, mineral resources). Homepage
   opened and agency scope confirmed 2026-10-21 ("Science for a
   changing world"; topography, natural hazards, water, and mineral
   mapping named on the page). Use for: adult background checks on
   any geologic claim; starting point for USGS topographic maps
   (search the site for "topographic maps") and mineral-resource
   pages.
   <https://www.usgs.gov/>
3. **USGS Earthquake Hazards Program** — free, no account; the
   authoritative U.S. source for real earthquake locations,
   magnitudes, and depths (Latest Earthquakes map & list; ComCat
   archive search). Homepage opened and program scope confirmed
   2026-10-21. Use for: Lesson 05 hazard background; project
   hazard-connection checks. Real reference data — record the
   access date with every reading.
   <https://earthquake.usgs.gov/>
4. **Smithsonian Institution — Global Volcanism Program** — free, no
   account; the authoritative catalog of Holocene/Pleistocene
   volcanoes and eruptions (Volcanoes of the World database).
   Homepage opened 2026-10-21; program scope (documents,
   disseminates, and archives eruption information; database of
   volcanoes and eruptions from the past 12,000 years) confirmed.
   Use for: Lesson 05 volcano background. Real reference data —
   record the access date with every reading.
   <https://volcano.si.edu/>
5. **USGS — "This Dynamic Earth: The Story of Plate Tectonics"**
   (Kious and Tilling) — **NOT verified 2026-10-21:** the USGS
   publication page at
   <https://pubs.usgs.gov/publications/text/dynamic.html> returned
   only "Your request was blocked" in both a text fetch and a live
   browser check, so its title and contents could not be confirmed
   this run. Do not assign it until the adult opens it successfully
   and confirms it is the Kious–Tilling publication. The adult may
   instead search the USGS publications site for the title. Use
   for: Lesson 01 adult background only, after verification.
