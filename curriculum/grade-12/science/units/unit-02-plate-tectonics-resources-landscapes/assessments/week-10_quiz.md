# Week-10 Formative Quiz — Unit 02

**Session S18 · ~40 minutes · 40 points · closed notebook**
**Adult scores same-day; solutions are in `../answer-key.md` (adult only).**

**1. (Objective 1 · 8 pts)** For each scenario, name the boundary
type and predict one landform it builds, with the mechanism in one
sentence:

(a) Two oceanic plates move apart at 2.5 cm/yr (full rate).
(b) An oceanic plate sinks beneath a continental plate.
(c) Two continental plates slide sideways past each other along a
fault.

**2. (Objective 1 · 6 pts)** At the full spreading rate in 1(a),
how far apart do two points on opposite sides of the ridge move in
4 million years? Show the multiplication and every unit conversion,
and give the answer in kilometers.

**3. (Objective 2 · 6 pts)** A river bends. On the outside of the
bend the bank is steep and undercut; on the inside of the bend
there is a gentle sandy beach. Which side is eroding and which side
is depositing? Explain, using water speed, in two or three
sentences.

**4. (Objective 3 · 8 pts)** A trail drops 200 ft in elevation over
a horizontal distance of 0.5 miles. Compute the gradient as a
decimal and as a percent. Show the miles→feet conversion and every
step. (1 mile = 5,280 ft.)

**5. (Objective 3 · 4 pts)** A topographic map has a contour
interval of 40 ft. Two points are separated by 5 contour intervals
on a steady slope, with no hilltop or depression contour between
them. What is their elevation difference? Show the multiplication.

**6. (Objectives 4–5 · 8 pts)** A fictional town, Riverbend, sits on
a wide, flat floodplain beside a river. Its soil grows excellent
crops, and the town's records (fictional practice data) show floods
reaching the lowest streets about once every 20 years. The town
council is choosing between building a levee along the river and
relocating the lowest homes to higher ground.

(a) In two or three sentences, explain why people settled in
Riverbend in the first place, citing two pieces of evidence from
the description.
(b) Name one advantage and one limit of EACH option (levee;
relocation). Do not claim one option is best for every town.
