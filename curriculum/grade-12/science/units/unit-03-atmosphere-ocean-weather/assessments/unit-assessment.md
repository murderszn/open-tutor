## Part 1 — Short answers (2 points each, 12 points)

1. Explain why deserts cluster near 30° latitude, naming the cell
   and the vertical air motion involved.
2. A ship at 50° N sails eastward across the Atlantic. Which wind
   belt helps it, and why does that belt blow toward the east?
3. Water has a specific heat of 4.18 J/g·°C; dry soil about 0.84
   J/g·°C. A 1 kg sample of each absorbs 4,180 J. Compute both
   temperature changes and explain what the difference means for a
   coastal vs. an inland city.
4. State the two density factors that drive thermohaline
   circulation, and explain why fresher North Atlantic surface water
   would slow deep-water sinking.
5. Decode "cP" and predict the weather it brings in winter.
6. On a surface chart, a blue line with triangles points east across
   your town. What is it, which way is it moving, and what weather
   change should you expect as it passes?

## Part 2 — Data task (8 points)

*Fictional practice data — not observations.* A station records at
the same time each day:

| 1 | 1024 | steady | W | 17 | clear |

| 5 | 1017 | rising | NW | 14 | clear |

7. On which day did the cold front pass? Cite three pieces of
   evidence from the table. (3 pts)
8. Which air mass was in place on Day 3, and which on Day 5?
   Justify each with two observations. (3 pts)
9. A classmate claims "the temperature fell on Day 4 because it
   rained." Explain why that mechanism is wrong and give the
   correct one. (2 pts)

## Part 3 — Explanation (10 points)

10. **Circulation model (5 pts).** Draw and label the Northern
    Hemisphere three-cell model (cells, surface wind belts, and the
    rising/sinking zones). In three sentences, explain how unequal
    heating plus rotation produces the pattern. (Your drawing is
    scored on correct cells, belts, and directions, not artistry.)
11. **Forecast judgment (5 pts).** A forecaster issues "30% chance
    of rain" on 60 days; rain falls on 17 of them. A second
    forecaster always says 0% or 100% and is right on 45 of 60 days.
    Which forecaster is better calibrated, and why? What is the
    flaw in judging either forecaster by a single day's outcome?
