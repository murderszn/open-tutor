# Week-14 Formative Quiz — Unit 03: Atmosphere, Ocean Circulation, and Weather

**Name:** ______________ **Date:** __________ **Time:** ~25 minutes.
No notes for questions 1–8; question 9–10 may use your project data
table. The adult scores this the same day and logs gaps for review.

1. At solar noon the zenith angle is 60°. What fraction of the
   overhead energy per square metre reaches the surface? Show the
   cosine step.
2. A surface with albedo 0.1 receives 900 W/m². How much energy is
   absorbed? How much is reflected?
3. Name the surface wind belt at 20° N and state which way Coriolis
   deflects its air (right or left).
4. A current flows south along the west coast of North America.
   Warm or cold? Name the current and justify with the gyre rule.
5. North Atlantic surface water becomes cold and salty as it moves
   north. What happens to it next, and what drives that motion —
   wind or density?
6. Decode "mT" and state the weather it typically brings.
7. A station reports: pressure falling, wind SE, temperature rising,
   clouds thickening. Is a warm front or a cold front approaching?
   Give one reason.
8. On a weather map, isobars are packed tightly together along a
   front. What does that say about the wind there?
9. An ensemble of 25 members shows rain in 10 of them. What is the
   probability of precipitation? In one sentence, what does that
   number mean?
10. **Explain your reasoning:** A forecast says "20% chance of rain"
    and it rains. Was the forecast wrong? Defend your answer using
    calibration over many days.

**Scoring (adult):** 1 point per question, 10 points total.
8–10: proceed to the unit assessment. 6–7: correct errors with the
answer key, then re-take the missed items orally. Below 6: re-read
the flagged lessons with the adult before the unit assessment.
