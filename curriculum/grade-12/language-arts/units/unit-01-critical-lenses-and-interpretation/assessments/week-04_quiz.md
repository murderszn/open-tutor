## Part 3 — Application (15 points)

Read this passage — the story's opening line, which you have studied —
and answer with the lens named:

> IT was said that a new person had appeared on the sea-front: a lady
> with a little dog.

11. **(Feminist lens, 5 points)** In 3–4 sentences, what does this
    sentence reveal about how the story positions its central woman?
    Quote at least one phrase from the sentence itself.
12. **(Marxist lens, 5 points)** In 3–4 sentences, what economic world
    does this sentence assume (leisure, resort, spectatorship)? Quote
    at least one phrase.
13. **(Lens-fit, 5 points)** Which of the two readings above is
    stronger *on this single sentence*, and what is your criterion
    for "stronger"? (3–4 sentences.)

## Scoring

- 26–30: solid; review any missed item by reapplying its lens
  question to the passage.
- 20–25: review the missed lens's question bank (Practice Set A),
  then redo the missed part.
- Below 20: reteach with the adult using the teacher guide's
  reteaching moves before the unit assessment.

This quiz is formative: it diagnoses, it does not brand. Errors are
corrected by reapplying the lens questions, not by memorizing
definitions.
