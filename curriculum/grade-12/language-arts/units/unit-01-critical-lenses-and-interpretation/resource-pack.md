# Resource Pack — Unit 01: Critical Lenses and Interpretation

**Goal:** Give a grade-12 learner (and guiding adult) free, no-account
routes to every core objective: the close-reading routine, the five
critical lenses, the Chekhov anchor text, the Kipling poem, the
*Gatsby* reread passages, and the multi-lens essay. **Age fit:**
high-school grade 12; the adult previews every link for advertising,
comments, and suitability before the learner opens it. Checked
2026-10-15 unless noted. "Verified" = the page was opened and the
content confirmed on that date; "search link" = a clearly labeled
preferred-channel search the adult runs and previews (no invented
titles, runtimes, or captions).
