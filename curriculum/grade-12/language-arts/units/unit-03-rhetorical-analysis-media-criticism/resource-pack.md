# Resource Pack — Unit 03: Rhetorical Analysis and Media Criticism

**Goal:** Give a grade-12 learner (and guiding adult) free,
no-account routes to every core objective: rhetorical situation and
appeals, point of view and style, argument structure, fallacies,
media criticism and SIFT, and speaker evaluation. **Age fit:**
high-school grade 12; the adult previews every link for advertising,
comments, and suitability before the learner opens it. Checked
2026-10-28 unless noted. "Verified" = the page was opened and the
content confirmed on that date; "search link" = a clearly labelled
preferred-channel search the adult runs and previews (no invented
titles, runtimes, or captions).
