# Resource Pack — Unit 04: Precision Style: Grammar and Vocabulary Refinement

**Goal:** Give a grade-12 learner (and guiding adult) free,
no-account routes to every core objective: contested usage
resolved against references, syntax varied for effect,
sentence-level diagnosis, and academic vocabulary inferred and
verified. **Age fit:** high-school grade 12; the adult previews
every link for advertising, comments, and suitability before
the learner opens it. Checked 2026-11-03 unless noted.
"Verified" = the page was opened and the content confirmed on
that date; "search link" = a clearly labeled preferred-channel
search the adult runs and previews (no invented titles,
runtimes, or captions).

## Web references

1. **Purdue OWL — Punctuation in Sentences.** Verified
   2026-11-03 (search result opened; page confirms sentence
   types and punctuation rules for independent and dependent
   clauses). Free, no account:
   <https://owl.purdue.edu/owl/general_writing/punctuation/punctuation/punctuation_in_sentences.html>.
   (Use for: Lessons 02–03 punctuation decisions inside syntax
   revision; the adult previews for the relevant section.)
2. **Merriam-Webster — Fewer vs. Less: Correct Usage Guide.**
   Verified 2026-11-03 via search result: the guide reports the
   1770 origin of the received rule, the measurement
   exceptions (distance, money, time, weight, statistical
   counts), and that "less" before ordinary plural count nouns
   is rare in writing and usually better avoided. Free, no
   account; the adult opens the current page and previews it
   before assigning (search: "Merriam-Webster fewer vs less").
   (Use for: Lesson 01, Worked example 1, and the case bank.)
