# Formative Quiz — Unit 04: Precision Style (Week 19, Session 19)

**For the learner.** This quiz checks the unit's method, not
your memory of the answer key. Show your reasoning where a
question asks for it: a choice without a reason scores as
incomplete, even when the choice is right. The answer key is a
separate file (`../answer-key.md`) — do not open it before
completing the quiz. Time: about 30 minutes, adult-supervised.

## Part A — Usage (4 points each; 16 points)

For each sentence, (a) mark it **contested**, **settled
error**, or **settled correct**, and (b) give the choice for a
formal essay, with the reference or rule behind it in one
sentence.

1. "There are less chairs in the seminar room than last term."
2. "Each reader should note their first impression in the
   margin."
3. "The report was written by Maya and I."
4. "The study comprises four experiments."

## Part B — Syntax and sentence repair (6 points each; 18 points)

5. Rewrite this sentence as a periodic sentence, and state in
   one sentence what the periodic shape emphasizes: "The
   author reveals the narrator's motive in the final scene,
   after withholding it for the whole novel."
6. Diagnose and repair this sentence, naming every fault
   (Lesson 03 numbers 1–4): "The utilization of evidence by
   the author makes possible the achievement of trust, due to
   the fact that the reader is involved in a process of
   verification."
7. Rewrite this sentence so its parallel structure is correct,
   and name what was unequal: "The revision requires cutting
   redundancy, to strengthen verbs, and clarity."

## Part C — Vocabulary (4 points each; 16 points)

For each word in its sentence, (a) name the clue type or word
parts you used, (b) give the meaning in your own words, and
(c) write an original sentence using the word accurately.
(Verification against a dictionary is allowed and expected —
note the dictionary you checked.)

8. "The *criterion* for acceptance was stated before any
   paper was read."
9. "Her *reluctance* to publish, despite three invitations,
   puzzled her colleagues."
10. "The two accounts are *irreconcilable*: each denies the
    central fact the other asserts."

**Total: 50 points.** Scoring guidance is in the teacher
guide; worked solutions are in `../answer-key.md`. Errors are
corrected by reapplying the method (reference test, four
questions, four-step vocabulary check), not by memorizing the
key's sentence.
