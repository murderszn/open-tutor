# Resource Pack — Unit 02: Comparative World Literature and Historical Context

**Goal:** Give a grade-12 learner (and guiding adult) free, no-account
routes to every core objective: translation comparison, the
historical-context method, the five anchor texts, satire, and the
comparative essay. **Age fit:** high-school grade 12; the adult
previews every link for advertising, comments, and suitability before
the learner opens it. Checked 2026-10-21 unless noted. "Verified" =
the page was opened and the content confirmed on that date; "search
link" = a clearly labeled preferred-channel search the adult runs and
previews (no invented titles, runtimes, or captions).
