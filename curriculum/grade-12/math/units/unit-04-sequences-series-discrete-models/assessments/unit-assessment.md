# Unit Assessment — Unit 04: Sequences, Series, and Discrete Models

**Session 18.** Show a justification at every step. Money contexts
are educational models, not financial advice. This assessment is a
classroom instrument, not a standardized or validated test. Answers
and scoring guidance are in `answer-key.md` and `teacher-guide.md`.

1. **Sequence analysis (Objectives 1).** The sequence begins
   `5, 9, 13, 17, …`. (a) Identify its family and justify with
   differences or ratios. (b) Give recursive and explicit forms.
   (c) Find `a_20`.
2. **Sigma and arithmetic series (Objective 2).** Evaluate
   `Σ_{k=1}^{20} (5k + 2)` two ways: by expanding the structure
   (sum of `5k` plus sum of `2`) and by the arithmetic series
   formula. Confirm the two answers agree.
3. **Finite geometric series (Objective 3).** Find the sum of the
   first 8 terms of `81 + 27 + 9 + …`. Give the exact fraction and
   a decimal to two places.
4. **Infinite series and total distance (Objective 4).** A ball is
   dropped from 12 meters and each rebound rises to 3/4 of the
   previous height. (a) Show the rebound-height series converges
   and find its sum. (b) Find the ball's total travel in the
   idealized model, and state one assumption the model makes.
5. **Binomial Theorem (Objective 5).** Expand `(3x − 2)^4` and
   check the expansion by setting `x = 1`.
6. **Induction (Objective 6).** Prove by induction that
   `2 + 4 + 6 + … + 2n = n(n + 1)` for all `n ≥ 1`. Write the base
   step and inductive step as labeled lines.
7. **Model choice (Objectives 1–3).** Two savings plans each run
   12 months. Plan A deposits $40 in month 1 and adds $15 each
   month. Plan B deposits $30 in month 1 and grows each deposit by
   10% per month. (a) Compute the total deposited under each plan
   (Plan B to the nearest cent). (b) Recommend a plan for a saver
   who values the larger 12-month total, and state one reason the
   recommendation could change (for example, risk, effort, or
   changing circumstances).
8. **Midyear retrieval (Objective 7).** Two short items from
   Units 01–02. (a) Find the domain of
   `f(x) = √(x − 3) + 1/(x − 5)`. (b) Simplify
   `(sin²θ)/(1 − cosθ)` for `sin θ ≠ 0`, justifying each step,
   and state the resulting expression.
