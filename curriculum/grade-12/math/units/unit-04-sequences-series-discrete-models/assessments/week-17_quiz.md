# Formative Quiz — Unit 04 (Week 17, Session 15)

Answer each question with working shown. This quiz is formative:
it names gaps for reteaching before the unit assessment. It is not
a standardized or validated test. Answers are in `answer-key.md`.

1. Write an explicit formula for the arithmetic sequence
   `11, 8, 5, 2, …`.
2. A geometric sequence has `a_1 = 6` and `r = −2`. Find `a_6`.
3. Evaluate `Σ_{k=2}^{5} (4k − 3)`.
4. Find the sum of the first 6 terms of `2 + 10 + 50 + …`.
5. Find the sum of `30 + 12 + 4.8 + …` (continuing forever), or
   explain why it has none.
6. Expand `(x − 2)^4`.
7. In `(a + b)^7`, what is the coefficient of the term containing
   `a³`?
8. True or false, with a reason: "Induction proves `P(n)` for all
   `n ≥ 1` if `P(1)` holds and `P(k)` implies `P(k + 1)` for every
   `k ≥ 1`."
9. Write `0.636363…` as a fraction using the series method.
10. A savings plan makes 10 deposits: the first is $100 and each
    later deposit is 5% larger than the previous one. Set up
    `a_1`, `r`, and `n`, and find the total deposited, in dollars
    and cents.
