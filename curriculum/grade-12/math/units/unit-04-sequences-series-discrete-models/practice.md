# Practice — Unit 04: Mixed Retrieval and Application

Work each problem with a justification at every step. Show the
setup (`a_1`, `r` or `d`, `n`) before computing. Answers are in
`answer-key.md`; do not open it until you have attempted every item.

1. A sequence is defined by `a_n = 7 − 3n`. Write its first four
   terms and find `a_25`.
2. A geometric sequence has `a_3 = 12` and `a_6 = 96`. Find `a_1`
   and `a_10`.
3. Evaluate `Σ_{k=1}^{5} (k² − k)` by expanding.
4. Find the sum of the first 30 terms of `9, 14, 19, …`.
5. Find the sum of the first 9 terms of `6 + 3 + 3/2 + …`. Give
   the exact fraction and a decimal to three places.
6. Find the sum of `18 − 6 + 2 − …` (continuing forever), or
   explain why it has none.
7. Write `0.181818…` as a fraction using the series method.
8. Find the coefficient of `x²` in `(x + 3)^5`.
9. Expand `(2x − y)³`.
10. Prove by induction that
    `1 + 4 + 7 + … + (3n − 2) = n(3n − 1)/2` for all `n ≥ 1`.
11. A savings plan deposits $50 in month 1 and increases each
    monthly deposit by $10. What is the total deposited over 24
    months? Name the sequence family and the values used.
12. **Error analysis.** A learner evaluates `Σ_{k=1}^{5} 3^k` as
    `(3^5 − 1)/(3 − 1) = 121`. The correct sum is 363. Explain the
    learner's error in one paragraph, and show the correct setup.
