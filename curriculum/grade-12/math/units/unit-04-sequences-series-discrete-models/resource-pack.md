grade-12 precalculus learner working with sequences, series, the
Binomial Theorem, and induction; every core objective has a free,
no-account route. The adult previews every video and page before

## Web references (checked 2026-11-03 unless noted)

1. **OpenStax *Precalculus 2e*, Chapter 11 key concepts** —
   <https://openstax.org/books/precalculus-2e/pages/11-key-concepts>
   — free textbook (CC BY). Sections 11.1 (sequences and their
   notations), 11.2 (arithmetic sequences), 11.3 (geometric
   sequences), 11.4 (series and their notations), and 11.6
   (Binomial Theorem) map to Lessons 01–05. Section list
   confirmed by search 2026-11-03.
2. **OpenStax *Precalculus 2e*, Chapter 11 review exercises** —
   <https://openstax.org/books/precalculus-2e/pages/11-review-exercises>
   — worked review items including recursive and explicit
   sequences, arithmetic and geometric series, infinite geometric
   sums, and the bouncing-ball total-distance problem. Confirmed
   by search 2026-11-03.
3. **Mathematics LibreTexts, *Precalculus 2e* (OpenStax),
   Chapter 11 key concepts** —
   <https://math.libretexts.org/Bookshelves/Precalculus/Precalculus_2e_(OpenStax)/11%3A_Sequences_Probability_and_Counting_Theory/11.09%3A_Chapter_Review/11.9.03%3A_Key_Concepts>
   — a second route to the same Chapter 11 material, including
   the note that an infinite geometric sum exists when
   `−1 < r < 1`. Confirmed by search 2026-11-03.
4. **Paul's Online Math Notes (Lamar University)** —
   <https://tutorial.math.lamar.edu/> — free notes with worked
   solutions. The Calculus II "Series & Sequences" notes model
   partial-sum and convergence reasoning used in Lesson 04; the
   Algebra/Trig Review covers sequence notation. Home page opened
   and read by an earlier unit run; the adult navigates to the
   series sections from there. No account required.
5. **Desmos graphing calculator** — <https://www.desmos.com/calculator>
   — free in the browser; no account needed to graph (an account
   only saves work). The unit's verify step for sequence tables
   and partial-sum trends. Checked by an earlier unit run.
6. **CCSS-M High School Mathematics pages** —
   <https://www.thecorestandards.org/Math/> — the standards
   framework the unit's codes are quoted from; F-IF.3, F-BF.2,
   and A-SSE.4 re-verified against official-text reproductions
   2026-11-03, and A-APR.5 (+) verified by the track audit runs
   on 2026-10-07 and 2026-10-08 (paraphrases in the unit README,
   not reproductions).
7. **Internal references** — `resources/math_fundamentals.md`
   (arithmetic fluency warm-ups only; it is aimed at grades 4–7
   and is never assigned as grade-12 instruction) and
   `resources/financial_tools_and_principles.md` (grade 8–9 band;
   verify any rates, prices, or dates before reuse; the unit's
   money contexts are educational examples, not advice).
