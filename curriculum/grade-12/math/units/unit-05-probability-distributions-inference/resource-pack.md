# Resource Pack — Unit 05: Probability Distributions and Statistical Inference

**Summary (adult planning judgment):** a one-unit shelf for a grade-12
precalculus learner building probability distributions, computing
expected value, recognizing binomial settings, weighing decisions, and
using the normal model for inference; every core objective has a free,
no-account route. The adult previews every video and page before
assigning; videos supplement teaching, they do not replace it.

## Web references (checked 2026-11-12 unless noted)

1. **OpenStax *Introductory Statistics 2e*, §4.2: Mean or Expected
   Value and Standard Deviation** —
   <https://openstax.org/books/introductory-statistics-2e/pages/4-2-mean-or-expected-value-and-standard-deviation>
   — free textbook (CC BY 4.0). Expected value as the long-term
   average, the `μ = Σ(x ∙ P(x))` formula, and worked examples
   (including the soccer-team example, `E = 1.1` days per week). Maps
   to Lessons 01 and 03. Confirmed by search 2026-11-12.
2. **OpenStax *Introductory Statistics 2e*, Chapter 4: Discrete Random
   Variables** — the chapter containing §4.1 (probability distribution
   functions), §4.2 (expected value), and §4.3 (binomial distribution).
   Reached from the §4.2 page above; the adult navigates to §4.1 and
   §4.3 from the chapter menu. Maps to Lessons 01–03. Confirmed by
   search 2026-11-12.
3. **OpenStax *Introductory Statistics 2e*, Appendix F: Mathematical
   Phrases, Symbols, and Formulas** —
   <https://openstax.org/books/introductory-statistics-2e/pages/f-mathematical-phrases-symbols-and-formulas>
   — the binomial formula `P(X = x) = C(n, x) p^x q^(n−x)`, binomial
   mean and variance, and the normal density, in one reference page.
   Maps to Lessons 02 and 05. Confirmed by search 2026-11-12.
4. **OpenStax *Introductory Business Statistics 2e*, §6.1: The Standard
   Normal Distribution** —
   <https://openstax.org/books/introductory-business-statistics-2e/pages/6-1-the-standard-normal-distribution>
   — z-scores, the standard normal distribution, and the empirical rule
   (68% / 95% / 99.7%) with worked examples. Maps to Lesson 05.
   Confirmed by search 2026-11-12.
5. **Khan Academy — Probability** —
   <https://www.khanacademy.org/math/probability> — free course hub:
   random variables, expected value, binomial probability, and normal
   distributions, with practice. No account needed to watch and
   practice (an account only saves progress). Maps to Lessons 01–05.
   Confirmed by search 2026-11-12.
6. **Desmos graphing calculator** — <https://www.desmos.com/calculator>
   — free in the browser; no account needed to graph (an account only
   saves work). Used to graph distribution tables and to check binomial
   and normal curves. Checked by the Unit 01 run on 2026-10-14.
7. **Rice Virtual Lab in Statistics — Normal Approximation to the
   Binomial** —
   <http://www.ruf.rice.edu/~lane/stat_sim/binom_demo.html> — an older
   (Java-era) interactive demonstration of the normal approximation
   with a continuity correction; the adult previews it before assigning
   and uses the project's spreadsheet simulation if the applet does not
   run in the learner's browser. Maps to Lesson 05. Confirmed by search
   2026-11-12.
8. **CCSS-M High School Statistics & Probability pages** —
   <https://www.thecorestandards.org/Math/> — the standards framework
   the unit's codes are quoted from; verified against the official text
   by the track audit runs on 2026-10-07 and 2026-10-08 (paraphrases in
   the unit README, not reproductions).

  normal model is not automatic.

  generator used for this shelf.
