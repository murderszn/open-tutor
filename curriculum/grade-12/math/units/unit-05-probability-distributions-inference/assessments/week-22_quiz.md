# Formative Quiz — Unit 05 (Week 22)

**20 points · ~30 minutes · calculator for approximations only.**
Exact answers are required where the problem says "exactly." Show a
reason for each step. Solutions are in `answer-key.md`
(adults only).

1. (2 pts) Two fair dice are rolled. Find `P(the sum is 9)` exactly.
2. (2 pts) Which of these tables is a valid probability distribution?
   Explain with the rule it passes or fails.
   - (a) `P(0) = 0.2, P(1) = 0.5, P(2) = 0.3`
   - (b) `P(0) = 0.4, P(1) = 0.4, P(2) = 0.4`
3. (2 pts) For `X ~ B(8, 0.25)`, find `P(X = 2)` to three decimal
   places.
4. (2 pts) A random variable takes values 0, 1, 2, 3 with
   probabilities 0.1, 0.3, 0.4, 0.2. Find `E(X)`.
5. (2 pts) The prize spinner in `assets/prize-spinner-game.png` pays
   $2 for red (probability 1/2), $1 for blue (1/4), $0 for green
   (1/8), and $5 for yellow (1/8). What is the fair ticket price?
6. (2 pts) `X ~ N(100, 15)`. Use the empirical rule to estimate
   `P(X > 115)`.
7. (2 pts) A value `x = 84` comes from a distribution with mean 70
   and standard deviation 8. Find its z-score.
8. (2 pts) For `X ~ B(50, 0.1)`, can the normal approximation be used?
   Show the large-counts check.
9. (2 pts) A poll of 100 voters finds 52% support. Using a
   conservative margin of error (`p = 0.5`), give the plausible range
   for true support.
10. (2 pts) A game has expected net gain +$1.30 per play. Explain in
    two sentences why that does **not** mean you will win $1.30 on
    your next play.

**Scoring (adult):** 2 points per item: full credit requires the exact
answer (or correct interval reasoning) with the key step shown; 1 point
for a correct answer with missing or wrong reasoning; 0 for a wrong
answer. Items 2, 8, and 10 test rules and interpretation, not just
answers.
