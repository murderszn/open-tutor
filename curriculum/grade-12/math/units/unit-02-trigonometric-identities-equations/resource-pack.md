# Resource Pack — Unit 02: Trigonometric Identities, Equations, and Applications

**Summary (adult planning judgment):** a one-unit shelf for a grade-12
precalculus learner proving identities, solving trigonometric equations,
and modeling periodic situations; every core objective has a free,
no-account route. The adult previews every video and page before
assigning; videos supplement teaching, they do not replace it.

## Web references (checked 2026-10-21 unless noted)

1. **OpenStax *Precalculus 2e*, Chapter 7: Trigonometric Identities
   and Equations** —
   <https://openstax.org/books/precalculus-2e/pages/7-introduction-to-trigonometric-identities-and-equations>
   — free textbook (CC BY-NC-SA 4.0). Sections 7.1 (simplifying and
   verifying identities), 7.2 (sum and difference), 7.3 (double-angle,
   half-angle, reduction), 7.5 (solving equations), and 7.6 (modeling)
   map to Lessons 01–06. Chapter outline and section list confirmed by
   search 2026-10-21.
2. **OpenStax *Precalculus 2e*, §7.2** —
   <https://openstax.org/books/precalculus-2e/pages/7-2-sum-and-difference-identities>
   — sum/difference formulas with exact-value examples (including
   `cos(5π/4 − π/6)` and `sin 75°` patterns). Confirmed by search
   2026-10-21.
3. **OpenStax *Precalculus 2e*, §7.5** —
   <https://openstax.org/books/precalculus-2e/pages/7-5-solving-trigonometric-equations>
   — solving with identities, double-angle equations, and multiple-angle
   equations. Confirmed by search 2026-10-21.
4. **Paul's Online Math Notes (Lamar University)** —
   <https://tutorial.math.lamar.edu/> — free notes with worked solutions.
   The Calculus I "Solving Trig Equations" sections model the
   factor-and-check method of Lesson 05; the Algebra/Trig Review covers
   unit-circle values used throughout. Home page opened and read by the
   Unit 01 run on 2026-10-14; the adult navigates to the trig-equation
   sections from there. No account required.
5. **Desmos graphing calculator** — <https://www.desmos.com/calculator>
   — free in the browser; no account needed to graph (an account only
   saves work). The unit's verify step for every model graph and every
   equation's solution set. Checked by the Unit 01 run on 2026-10-14.
6. **NOAA Tide Predictions (Tides & Currents)** —
   <https://tidesandcurrents.noaa.gov/tide_predictions.html> — official
   U.S. tide predictions for 3,000+ stations, free, no account needed
   to view predictions. The project's optional extension uses one
   station's predictions; the adult verifies the station and the date
   range before assigning. Confirmed by search 2026-10-21.
7. **CCSS-M High School Functions pages** —
   <https://www.thecorestandards.org/Math/> — the standards framework
   the unit's codes are quoted from; verified against the official text
   by the track audit runs on 2026-10-07 and 2026-10-08 (paraphrases in
   the unit README, not reproductions).
