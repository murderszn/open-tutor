# Culminating Assessment — Unit 02

**50 points · ~50 minutes · calculator for approximations only.**
Exact answers are required where the problem says "exactly." Justify
every identity step and check every equation solution in the original
equation. Solutions and scoring notes are in `answer-key.md`
(adults only).

## Task 1 — Proof (10 points)

Prove the identity `sin θ/(1 − cos θ) + sin θ/(1 + cos θ) = 2 csc θ`
for all `θ` where both sides are defined. (Work on one side only; name
the identity used at each step; state the values of `θ` excluded by
the denominators and by the final cosecant.)

## Task 2 — Exact values (10 points)

(a) (4 pts) Find `tan 15°` exactly, showing the split and the
rationalization.
(b) (3 pts) Find `sin 22.5°` exactly, with the quadrant sign reasoning.
(c) (3 pts) Given `sin θ = 5/13` with `θ` in Quadrant II, find
`sin 2θ` and `cos 2θ` exactly.

## Task 3 — Inverse functions (8 points)

(a) (4 pts) Evaluate `cos(arctan(−8/15))` exactly, with a triangle and
a quadrant argument.
(b) (4 pts) Evaluate `arcsin(cos(5π/6))`, explaining why the answer is
not `5π/6`.

## Task 4 — Equations (10 points)

Solve each equation on `[0, 2π)`, showing the method step and checking
every candidate in the original equation.

(a) (4 pts) `2sin²x − sin x − 1 = 0`.
(b) (3 pts) `cos 2x = −√3/2`.
(c) (3 pts) `sin x = cos x − 1` — solve by squaring, and identify any
extraneous candidate by checking.

## Task 5 — Model interpretation (12 points)

A Ferris wheel has center 18 m above the ground and radius 15 m. A
capsule starts at the top at time `t = 0`, and the wheel turns once
every 12 minutes.

(a) (4 pts) Write the height model `h(t)`, and verify it at `t = 0`,
`t = 3`, and `t = 6` minutes.
(b) (4 pts) Find all times in the first 12 minutes when the capsule is
at 25.5 m, and state whether it is rising or falling at each.
(c) (4 pts) In a paragraph: a second wheel has the same center and
radius but turns once every 24 minutes. Explain, without recomputing
every value, how the two models differ and whether the times in part
(b) would be earlier, later, or unchanged — and why.

**Scoring (adult):** Task 1: 4 pts for a correct one-sided proof, 3 pts
for naming identities per step, 3 pts for the excluded values stated.
Task 2: as marked; exact form required. Task 3: triangle/quadrant
reasoning is scored, not just the value. Task 4: an unchecked solution
set loses 1 point per task; an extraneous solution kept loses 2 points
in (c). Task 5: (a) 2 pts model, 2 pts verifications; (b) 2 pts times,
2 pts rising/falling; (c) reasoning scored, not length.
