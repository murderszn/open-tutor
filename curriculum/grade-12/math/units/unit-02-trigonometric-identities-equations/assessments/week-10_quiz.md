# Formative Quiz — Unit 02 (Week 10)

**20 points · ~30 minutes · calculator for approximations only.**
Exact answers are required where the problem says "exactly." Show a
reason for each identity step. Solutions are in `answer-key.md`
(adults only).

1. (2 pts) Given `sin θ = −7/25` with `θ` in Quadrant III, find
   `cos θ` exactly.
2. (2 pts) Simplify `(tan θ)/(sec θ)` to a single trigonometric
   function, stating any restriction.
3. (2 pts) Find `sin 105°` exactly, showing the angle split you used.
4. (2 pts) Find `cos 2θ` given `cos θ = 3/5` and `θ` in Quadrant IV,
   using the form of the double-angle identity that fits the data.
5. (2 pts) Evaluate `arctan(−1)`. State the principal range you used.
6. (2 pts) Evaluate `sin(arccos(−12/13))` exactly.
7. (2 pts) Solve `2cos x − 1 = 0` on `[0, 2π)`.
8. (2 pts) Solve `sin 2x = 0` on `[0, 2π)` completely. (A two-answer
   response is incomplete — say why.)
9. (2 pts) A model is `h(t) = 6 − 4 cos(πt/3)`, `t` in hours. State the
   midline, amplitude, and period, and find `h(0)`.
10. (2 pts) Prove that `(1 − sin θ)(1 + sin θ) = cos²θ` by expanding
    the left side — or explain why no proof is needed beyond one
    identity name.

**Scoring (adult):** 2 points per item: full credit requires the exact
answer (or correct interval reasoning) with the key step shown; 1 point
for a correct answer with missing or wrong reasoning; 0 for a wrong
answer. Items 8 and 10 test completeness and justification, not just
answers.
