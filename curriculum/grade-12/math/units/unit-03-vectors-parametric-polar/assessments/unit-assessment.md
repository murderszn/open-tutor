# Culminating Assessment — Unit 03

**6 tasks, 50 points.** All five unit objectives are sampled. State
every direction with its reference and every unit. Exact answers where
exact values exist; otherwise round to two decimal places.

## Task 1 — Vectors (10 points; Objective 1)

A search team walks in two legs: first `<8, 6>` km, then `<−6, 8>` km.

(a) Find each leg's magnitude and direction angle. (4)
(b) Find the total displacement vector, its magnitude, and its
direction. (4)
(c) Explain why the total distance walked is not the displacement
magnitude, using the numbers from (a) and (b). (2)

## Task 2 — Forces and the dot product (8 points; Objective 2)

Forces `F₁ = <9, 12>` N and `F₂ = <−12, 5>` N act on one object.

(a) Find the resultant force (vector, magnitude, direction). (4)
(b) Compute `F₁ · F₂` and the angle between the forces. Interpret the
angle in one sentence. (4)

## Task 3 — Parametric motion (9 points; Objective 3)

A projectile follows `x(t) = 20t`, `y(t) = −5t² + 40t` (meters, seconds).

(a) Find its landing time and distance, and its maximum height and
when it occurs. (4)
(b) Eliminate the parameter, giving the rectangular equation with its
domain. (3)
(c) A second projectile follows `x(t) = 40t`, `y(t) = −20t² + 80t`.
Does it trace the same path? Does it land at the same time? Justify
both answers with computation. (2)

## Task 4 — Polar conversions and graphs (10 points; Objective 4)

(a) Convert `(−3, 3)` to polar form with `r > 0`, and convert
`(4, 7π/6)` to rectangular form exactly. (4)
(b) Convert `r = 2 cos θ` to a rectangular equation and name the
curve with center and radius. (3)
(c) Predict the family, petal count or loop structure, symmetry, and
maxima of `r = 5 cos(3θ)`, then state how you would verify the
prediction with technology. (3)

## Task 5 — Complex numbers in polar form (8 points; Objective 5)

(a) Write `3 + 3i` in polar form exactly. (2)
(b) Find all cube roots of `27 cis 180°`, in polar form, and describe
their positions geometrically. (4)
(c) Find the distance between `5 + 2i` and `1 − 2i` on the complex
plane. (2)

## Task 6 — Modeling judgment (5 points; Objectives 2, 3, 6)

A ferry crosses a river steering directly at its destination on the
far bank. The river current pushes it sideways, so it lands downstream
of the destination. Its captain says: "Next time I will steer directly
at the destination again, but go faster — speed will fix the drift."

(a) Explain, with a vector argument, why going faster in the same
direction reduces but does not eliminate the downstream landing error.
(3)
(b) State what the captain must change instead, and what the ferry's
ground track will then be. (2)
