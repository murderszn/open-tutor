# Formative Quiz — Unit 03 (Week 14)

**10 items, 20 points.** Formative: the adult scores it the same day,
classifies every error (concept / algebra / reading), and re-works one
error of each type with the learner before the culminating assessment.
State every direction with its reference. Exact answers where exact
values exist.

1. (2) For `v = <6, −8>`, find its magnitude and direction angle.
2. (2) A displacement runs from `(2, 5)` to `(−4, 13)`. Give its
   components and its unit vector.
3. (2) For `u = <3, 1>` and `v = <−2, 4>`, compute `u + v` and `u · v`.
4. (2) A boat steams 12 km/h due east; a current runs 5 km/h due north.
   Find the boat's speed over ground and its direction (with reference).
5. (2) Eliminate the parameter for `x(t) = 4t`, `y(t) = 3t + 2`, and
   state the direction of travel as `t` increases.
6. (2) Convert the polar point `(6, 5π/6)` to rectangular coordinates
   exactly.
7. (2) Convert the rectangular point `(−4, −4)` to polar form with
   `r > 0` and `0 ≤ θ < 2π`.
8. (2) Classify `r = 4 + 4 sin θ`: family, symmetry, zero, maximum.
9. (2) Write `−2 + 2√3 i` in polar form exactly.
10. (2) Compute `(3 cis 40°)(2 cis 20°)` geometrically, and give the
    product in rectangular form approximately (calculator allowed for
    the trigonometric values).
