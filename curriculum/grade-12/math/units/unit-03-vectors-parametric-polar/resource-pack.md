# Resource Pack — Unit 03: Vectors, Parametric Equations, and Polar Representations

**Summary (adult planning judgment):** a one-unit shelf for a grade-12
precalculus learner working with vectors, parametric motion, polar
coordinates and graphs, and complex numbers in polar form; every core
objective has a free, no-account route. The adult previews every video
and page before assigning; videos supplement teaching, they do not
replace it.

## Web references (checked 2026-10-28 unless noted)

1. **OpenStax *Precalculus 2e*, Chapter 8: Further Applications of
   Trigonometry** —
   <http://openstax.org/books/precalculus-2e/pages/chapter-8>
   — free textbook (CC BY-NC-SA 4.0). Section list confirmed from
   this chapter's answer key on 2026-10-28: §8.3 Polar Coordinates,
   §8.4 Polar Coordinates: Graphs, §8.5 Polar Form of Complex Numbers,
   §8.6 Parametric Equations, §8.7 Parametric Equations: Graphs,
   §8.8 Vectors — mapping to Lessons 01–06. Chapter page opened and
   read 2026-10-28.
2. **Desmos graphing calculator** — <https://www.desmos.com/calculator>
   — free in the browser; no account needed to graph (an account only
   saves work). Polar and parametric modes are the unit's verify step
   for every graph and every path. Checked by the Unit 01 run on
   2026-10-14 and re-used by Unit 02.
3. **Paul's Online Math Notes (Lamar University)** —
   <https://tutorial.math.lamar.edu/> — free notes with worked
   solutions. The Precalculus chapters on parametric equations and
   polar coordinates model the elimination and conversion methods of
   Lessons 03–04. Home page opened and read by the Unit 01 run on
   2026-10-14; the adult navigates to the parametric/polar sections
   from there. No account required.
4. **CCSS-M High School Number & Quantity pages** —
   <https://www.thecorestandards.org/Math/> — the standards framework
   the unit's N-VM and N-CN codes are quoted from; verified against
   the official text by the track audit runs on 2026-10-07 and
   2026-10-08 (paraphrases in the unit README, not reproductions).
5. **NOAA Tide Predictions (Tides & Currents)** —
   <https://tidesandcurrents.noaa.gov/tide_predictions.html> — official
   U.S. tide predictions, free, no account needed to view predictions.
   Not used for a numbered task in this unit; listed as the adult's
   optional extension route if a learner wants a real current/tide
   context for the harbor model, with the station and date range
   verified by the adult before assigning. Confirmed by search
   2026-10-21 (Unit 02 run).
