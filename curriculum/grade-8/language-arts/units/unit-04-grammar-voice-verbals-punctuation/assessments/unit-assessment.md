# Culminating Assessment — Unit 04

**Time:** ~45 minutes. **Note to the learner:** do not consult
the answer key. Open responses are scored on reasoning, not on
matching one exact wording; the teacher guide gives the scoring
rules.

## Part A — Identify (8 items, 1 point each)

Label each item as asked:

1. In "To finish early is his goal," what job does *to finish
   early* do?
2. In "Finishing early, Jon left the hall," what does
   "finishing early" describe?
3. "The award was presented by the mayor." Active or passive?
4. "The mayor presented the award." Active or passive?
5. "I wish I were on the team." What mood is *were*, and is it
   correct?
6. "If it snows, we will cancel the trip." What pattern is this
   — a real possibility or a contrary-to-fact condition?
7. In "The rain stopped; however, the field stayed wet," what
   punctuation pattern joins the two clauses?
8. In "She brought one thing: her notebook," why is the colon
   correct?

## Part B — Revise (6 items, 2 points each: 1 for the repair, 1 for the reason)

Repair each sentence and write the rule or reason in one phrase:

1. "If Jon was older, he would drive."
2. "The coach insists that Mara runs every day."
3. "Mara painted the fence, she was proud."
4. "He bought: paint, brushes, and tape."
5. "Their leaving early, its a long drive."
6. "The letter was written by Mara." (Rewrite in active voice
   and state what the active version emphasizes.)

## Part C — Paragraph revision (8 points: 1 per fault repaired)

Revise this paragraph. There are 8 faults to repair; do not
change the facts.

> "the club met on Tuesday. Mara loves to garden, she waters
> daily. Jon weeded the beds, the soil was rich. If Mara
> was free, she would help. The leader requires that every member
> brings gloves. Their meeting on Friday. Its at noon. The rain
> fell, however the garden thrived. Mara smiled because she had
> finished."

## Part D — Editor's note (8 points)

Write a paragraph of 4–5 sentences explaining **four** choices
you made in Part C: one verbal or voice choice, one mood choice,
one punctuation choice, and one capitalization or confused-word
choice. For each choice, name the tool, quote the before and
after versions, and state the effect on the reader. (Scoring:
2 points per choice — 1 for correctly naming tool and change,
1 for stating an effect.)

## Scoring

- Part A: 8 points. Part B: 12 points. Part C: 8 points.
  Part D: 8 points. **Total: 36 points.**
- 32–36 = exceeds; 26–31 = meets; 19–25 = approaching (reteach
  the missed tool, then revise); below 19 = reteach with
  Lessons 01–06 and retake a parallel form from the teacher
  guide.
