# Formative Quiz — Unit 04 (Week 18, with midyear cumulative items)

**Time:** ~30 minutes. **Note to the learner:** this quiz checks
Unit 04 and reviews Units 01–03. It is formative: errors are
reviewed the same day and retaught in Session 19. Do not consult
the answer key.

## Part 1 — Unit 04 (items 1–10)

1. In "Swimming laps builds endurance," what is *swimming laps*,
   and what job does it do?
2. In "Swimming laps, Mara crossed the pool," what does
   "swimming laps" describe, and what kind of phrase is it?
3. Rewrite "The report was finished by Jon" in active voice.
   What does the active version emphasize that the passive
   version does not?
4. A sentence reads, "Mistakes were made during the project."
   Name the voice, and explain in one sentence why a reader
   might object to it.
5. Label the mood of the verb *were* in "If I were in charge, I
   would change the schedule." Is *were* correct? Why?
6. Repair this sentence and name the rule: "The principal
   requires that every student brings a notebook."
7. Repair this sentence and name the rule: "The garden was wet,
   however the path was dry."
8. Repair this sentence and name the rule: "She packed: a tent, a
   lantern, and food."
9. Repair this sentence and name the rule: "Their going to the
   garden, its open today." (Hint: there are three faults.)
10. Choose the better sentence for a report whose point is that
    the *results* matter, not who produced them, and explain your
    choice in one sentence:
    a. "The team measured the rainfall every morning."
    b. "The rainfall was measured every morning."

## Part 2 — Midyear cumulative review (items 11–14)

Read this original passage before answering items 11–12:

> "Two writers describe the same new library. Writer A writes
> that it opened in March and holds 20,000 books. Writer B writes
> that it is the town's proudest achievement and will transform
> reading forever."

11. Which detail from the passage is a verifiable fact, and which
    is an interpretation? Quote one of each.
12. A classmate says the theme of the passage is "libraries."
    Is that a topic or a theme? Explain the difference in one
    sentence, and give a theme statement the passage could
    support.
13. A writer argues, "School should start later because teens
    need more sleep." Which of these pieces of evidence is
    relevant, and which is irrelevant? Explain in one sentence
    each: (a) "A study found teens who sleep longer score higher
    on morning tests." (b) "The school colors are blue and gold."
14. Do Writers A and B disagree about the library on a matter
    of fact anywhere in this passage? Explain, using only
    details from the passage, and name one way their accounts
    differ that is a matter of interpretation rather than fact.
