# Resource Pack — Unit 04: Grammar, Voice, Verbals, Punctuation, and Style

**Goal in one sentence:** give a grade-8 learner and guiding
adult reliable background and practice routes for verbals,
active and passive voice, verb moods, semicolons, colons, and
conjunctive adverbs — at grade level, free, and without an
account for core learning.

**Age fit:** grade 8 (ages ~13–14). External items below are
adult-previewed background, not assigned reading. Videos
supplement the lessons; they never replace them. At least one
free, no-account route supports each core objective (the lessons
themselves, the internal guides, and the dictionary reference
below).
