# Resource Pack — Unit 05: Narrative Perspective, Pacing, and Crafted Scenes

**Goal in one sentence:** give a grade-8 learner and guiding
adult reliable background and free reading routes for point of
view, narrator reliability, dramatic irony, pacing, scene craft,
and structure — at grade level, free, and without an account
for core learning.

**Age fit:** grade 8 (ages ~13–14). External items below are
adult-previewed background and reading, not assigned reading.
Videos supplement the lessons; they never replace them. At
least one free, no-account route supports each core objective
(the lessons themselves, the internal guides, and the public-
domain stories below).

## Web references (checked 2026-11-08 unless noted)

1. **Common Core State Standards, Writing, Grade 8** — official
   standard texts for W.8.3 and W.8.3.a–e, W.8.4, W.8.5,
   W.8.10; wording verified verbatim 2026-11-08 against the
   published standards. Free, no account.
   <https://www.thecorestandards.org/ELA-Literacy/W/8/>
2. **Common Core State Standards, Reading: Literature, Grade 8**
   — official standard texts for RL.8.1, RL.8.3, RL.8.5,
   RL.8.6; wording verified verbatim 2026-11-08. Free, no
   account.
   <https://www.thecorestandards.org/ELA-Literacy/RL/8/>
3. **"The Gift of the Magi" by O. Henry (1905)** — full text,
   Project Gutenberg eBook #7256; public domain. The mentor
   story for Practice R1–R2 (third-person omniscient narration,
   dramatic irony, scene/summary pacing). Free, no account.
   <https://www.gutenberg.org/cache/epub/7256/pg7256-images.html>
4. **"An Occurrence at Owl Creek Bridge" by Ambrose Bierce
   (1890)** — full text, Project Gutenberg eBook #375; public
   domain. The mentor story for Practice R3 (time expansion,
   flashback, withheld fact). Adult preview first — the story
   centers on a hanging and an imagined escape. Free, no
   account.
   <https://www.gutenberg.org/ebooks/375.txt.utf-8>
5. **"The Story of an Hour" by Kate Chopin (1894)** — full text
   in *The Awakening and Selected Short Stories*, Project
   Gutenberg eBook #160; public domain. The mentor story for
   Practice R4 (third-person limited narration, one-hour
   pacing, dramatic irony). Adult preview first — the story
   centers on a reported death, a marriage, and a sudden death
   at the end. Free, no account.
   <https://www.gutenberg.org/files/160/160-h/160-h.htm>
6. **Merriam-Webster Dictionary and Thesaurus** — free,
   no-account dictionary (meanings, pronunciations, parts of
   speech) and thesaurus for precise-word choices in Lessons
   04–06 and the project. A print dictionary works equally
   well; the online version is the no-cost route.
   <https://www.merriam-webster.com/> and
   <https://www.merriam-webster.com/thesaurus>
7. **LibriVox (search route, not a direct link)** — free
   public-domain audiobook recordings; the adult searches the
   story title on the LibriVox site and previews the recording
   before assigning it as a listening alternative for the three
   mentor stories. Free, no account; recordings vary in reader
   and quality, so preview first.
8. **Track scope-and-sequence, Unit 05 section (internal)** —
   the track's own weekly goals and standards map for this unit.
   [../../scope-and-sequence.md](../../scope-and-sequence.md)
9. **Internal literary-terms guide (internal)** — point of view,
   foreshadowing, and flashback sections reused in Lessons 01,
   02, and 05; excerpted by the adult, never assigned whole.
   [../../../../../resources/language_arts_literary_terms.md](../../../../../resources/language_arts_literary_terms.md)
