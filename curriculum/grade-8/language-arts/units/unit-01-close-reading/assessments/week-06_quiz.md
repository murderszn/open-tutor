# Week-6 Formative Quiz — Unit 01

**Session:** 18 · **Time:** ~30 minutes · **Scoring:**
[answer key](../answer-key.md#week-6-formative-quiz) · 12 items, 24 points

Read the passage, then answer. This quiz checks the unit's five
objectives; it is formative — mistakes here set the review agenda for
Session 19, not a final grade.

## The Replacement (original passage for this quiz)

When Coach Rivera announced that eighth-grader Dana Okafor would start
at point guard on Friday, the gym went quiet. Dana had been on the team
for three weeks. Maya, who had run the offense all season, stared at
the floor and tightened the laces on shoes that were already tied.
After practice, Rivera found Maya alone in the hallway, turning her gym
bag's strap over and over in her hands. "You earned that spot in
September," Rivera said quietly. "Dana earned it in October. Friday, you
run the second unit like it's the first, and you show her how we do
things here." Maya nodded without looking up. On Friday, when Dana's
first pass sailed out of bounds, it was Maya who was first off the
bench, clapping.

## Questions

**Objective 1 — Evidence and inference (8 pts)**

1. (1 pt) Who was named starting point guard for Friday's game? Quote
   the exact words.
2. (2 pts) Write one inference about how Maya feels about the decision.
   Support it with **two** details from the passage.
3. (2 pts) Which single detail is the *strongest* evidence for your
   inference in #2? Explain in one sentence why it beats the other
   detail.
4. (2 pts) Mark each **E** (explicit) or **I** (inference):
   - (a) Dana had been on the team for three weeks.
   - (b) Maya respects Coach Rivera.
5. (1 pt) Paraphrase this line in your own words: "You earned that spot
   in September. Dana earned it in October."

**Objective 2 — Theme and summary (7 pts)**

6. (2 pts) State a theme of the passage as a **full sentence**. (Any
   defensible, whole-text-supported statement earns credit.)
7. (3 pts) Which is the best **objective** summary? Explain what's wrong
   with each of the other two.
   - (a) Coach Rivera unfairly benched Maya, who was clearly the better
     player, in favor of a newcomer nobody wanted.
   - (b) After losing her starting spot to Dana Okafor, Maya is upset
     but handles it maturely, supporting Dana from the bench on Friday.
   - (c) Maya, the team's point guard all season, loses her starting
     position to Dana; Rivera tells Maya to lead the second unit, and
     on Friday Maya is first off the bench clapping after Dana's bad
     pass.
8. (2 pts) Is the passage's main claim better called a *theme* or a
   *central idea*? Why?

**Objective 4 — Word choice (6 pts)**

9. (2 pts) "Maya… tightened the laces on shoes that were already tied."
   What does this detail suggest about Maya's state of mind? Name the
   **connotation** at work (not just the dictionary meaning).
10. (2 pts) Describe Coach Rivera's **tone** in the hallway conversation
    in one word, and quote the word choice that most creates it.
11. (2 pts) Fix the punctuation: *Rivera said quietly "You earned that
    spot in September."*

**Objective 5 — Vocabulary (3 pts)**

12. (3 pts) In "the gym went quiet," what does *quiet* suggest beyond
    "no noise"? Use **context** (the surrounding sentences) to explain,
    then confirm with a dictionary-style definition in your own words.
