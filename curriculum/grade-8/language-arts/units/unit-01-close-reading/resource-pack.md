# Resource Pack — Unit 01: Close Reading, Inference, Theme, Evidence Synthesis

**Summary (adult planning judgment):** Grade-8 close reading works best
when every video or article supplements — never replaces — the learner's
own evidence work on short, complete texts. The resources below support
the unit's five objectives: distinguishing explicit meaning from
inference, tracking theme, writing objective summaries, analyzing word
choice and tone, and attacking unknown vocabulary. All external links
were checked **2026-10-13**; unverified items are labeled as open search
links for the adult to preview.
