# Resource Pack — Unit 03: Informational Rhetoric and Conflicting Accounts

**Goal:** give a grade-8 learner free, no-account routes into reading
Douglass and Lincoln, naming rhetorical appeals, delineating arguments,
and comparing conflicting accounts. **Age fit:** 13–14; the Douglass
excerpts carry the adult preview note from the unit README.

All links checked **2026-10-26**. Videos supplement the lessons; they
do not replace them. Items marked *(search link)* were not
individually previewed — the adult should skim before assigning.
