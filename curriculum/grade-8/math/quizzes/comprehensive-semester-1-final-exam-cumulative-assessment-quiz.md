## 🔑 Parent Answer Key (Educator)

> [!NOTE]
> *This section is for Educator (educator) to verify answers, assess computational precision, and provide targeted feedback.*

**Question 1:**  
- **Target Answer & Key Steps:** Numerator: $8x^9y^{-6}$. Divide by $4x^5y^4$: $\frac{8}{4} \cdot x^{9-5} \cdot y^{-6-4} = 2x^4y^{-10} = \frac{2x^4}{y^{10}}$.

**Question 2:**  
- **Target Answer & Key Steps:** $(7.2 \div 1.8) \times 10^{8 - (-4)} = 4.0 \times 10^{12}$.

**Question 3:**  
- **Target Answer & Key Steps:** $8x - 20 - 3x - 3 = 2x + 13 \implies 5x - 23 = 2x + 13 \implies 3x = 36 \implies x = 12$.

**Question 4:**  
- **Target Answer & Key Steps:** $6k + 12 = 6k + 12$. **Infinitely many solutions** (all real numbers).

**Question 5:**  
- **Target Answer & Key Steps:** Slope $m = \frac{-5 - 11}{5 - (-3)} = \frac{-16}{8} = -2$. Intercept: $11 = -2(-3) + b \implies 11 = 6 + b \implies b = 5$. Equation: $y = -2x + 5$.

**Question 6:**  
- **Target Answer & Key Steps:** Add equations: $6x = 24 \implies x = 4$. Substitute into Eq 2: $2(4) - 3y = 2 \implies 8 - 3y = 2 \implies -3y = -6 \implies y = 2$. Solution: $(4, 2)$.

**Question 7:**  
- **Target Answer & Key Steps:** Differences in $y$: $7 - 3 = 4$; $13 - 7 = 6$; $21 - 13 = 8$. Since rate of change is not constant, the function is **non-linear** (quadratic).

**Question 8:**  
- **Target Answer & Key Steps:** $a^2 + 15^2 = 17^2 \implies a^2 + 225 = 289 \implies a^2 = 64 \implies a = 8\text{ cm}$.

**Question 9:**  
- **Target Answer & Key Steps:** $d = \sqrt{(7 - (-5))^2 + (3 - (-2))^2} = \sqrt{12^2 + 5^2} = \sqrt{144 + 25} = \sqrt{169} = 13\text{ units}$.

**Question 10:**  
- **Target Answer & Key Steps:** $V = \frac{1}{3}\pi (9^2)(12) = \frac{1}{3}\pi (81)(12) = 324\pi\text{ cm}^3$.

**Question 11:**  
- **Target Answer & Key Steps:** $V = \frac{4}{3}\pi (3^3) = \frac{4}{3}\pi (27) = 36\pi\text{ in}^3$.

**Question 12:**  
- **Target Answer & Key Steps:** $y = 12(6) + 50 = 72 + 50 = \$122/\text{hr}$.
