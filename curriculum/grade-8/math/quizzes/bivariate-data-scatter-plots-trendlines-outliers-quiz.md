## 🔑 Parent Answer Key (Educator)

> [!NOTE]
> *This section is for Educator (educator) to verify answers, assess computational precision, and provide targeted feedback.*

**Question 1:**  
- **Target Answer & Key Steps:** Bivariate data involves pairs of numerical values for two variables on the same subject. Example: Hours spent studying ($x$) and score on an exam ($y$).

**Question 2:**  
- **Target Answer & Key Steps:** a) Negative association (faster speed = less time)<br>b) Negative association (warmer weather = fewer hot drinks)<br>c) No association<br>d) Positive association (taller players tend to have longer wingspans)

**Question 3:**  
- **Target Answer & Key Steps:** In a linear association, data points cluster along a straight path. In a non-linear association, points cluster along a curve (e.g., U-shape, exponential rise) rather than a straight line.

**Question 4:**  
- **Target Answer & Key Steps:** An outlier is a data point that falls substantially outside the general pattern or cluster of the data. An outlier can skew or tilt the slope and y-intercept of a line of best fit.

**Question 5:**  
- **Target Answer & Key Steps:** The slope means that for each additional hour spent studying, a student's predicted exam score increases by 6.5 points.

**Question 6:**  
- **Target Answer & Key Steps:** The y-intercept means that a student who studies 0 hours is predicted to score 45 points on the exam.

**Question 7:**  
- **Target Answer & Key Steps:** $\hat{y} = 6.5(7) + 45 = 45.5 + 45 = 90.5$ points.

**Question 8:**  
- **Target Answer & Key Steps:** Interpolation predicts values within the range of collected data points (reliable). Extrapolation predicts values beyond the range of collected data (risky and less reliable).

**Question 9:**  
- **Target Answer & Key Steps:** Relative frequency $= \frac{15}{100} = 0.15 = 15\%$.

**Question 10:**  
- **Target Answer & Key Steps:** Slope $m = \frac{50 - 20}{10 - 4} = \frac{30}{6} = 5$. Use $(4, 20)$: $20 = 5(4) + b \implies 20 = 20 + b \implies b = 0$. Equation: $y = 5x$.

**Question 11:**  
- **Target Answer & Key Steps:** No. Correlation does not imply causation. A strong association can be caused by a lurking third variable or coincidental correlation (e.g., ice cream sales and swimming accidents both correlate with hot summer days).

**Question 12:**  
- **Target Answer & Key Steps:** $y = -2.4(5) + 180 = -12 + 180 = 168^{\circ}\text{F}$.
