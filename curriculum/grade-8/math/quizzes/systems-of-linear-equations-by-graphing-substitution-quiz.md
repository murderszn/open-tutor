## 🔑 Parent Answer Key (Educator)

> [!NOTE]
> *This section is for Educator (educator) to verify answers, assess computational precision, and provide targeted feedback.*

**Question 1:**  
- **Target Answer & Key Steps:** The solution represents the point of intersection $(x, y)$ where both lines cross and simultaneously satisfy both equations.

**Question 2:**  
- **Target Answer & Key Steps:** Set equal: $2x - 1 = -x + 5 \implies 3x = 6 \implies x = 2$. Then $y = 2(2) - 1 = 3$. Solution: $(2, 3)$.

**Question 3:**  
- **Target Answer & Key Steps:** Substitute $y = 3x$: $2x + 3x = 15 \implies 5x = 15 \implies x = 3$. Then $y = 3(3) = 9$. Solution: $(3, 9)$.

**Question 4:**  
- **Target Answer & Key Steps:** Substitute $x$: $3(y + 4) - 2y = 18 \implies 3y + 12 - 2y = 18 \implies y = 6$. Then $x = 6 + 4 = 10$. Solution: $(10, 6)$.

**Question 5:**  
- **Target Answer & Key Steps:** Substitute: $4x - 7 = 4x + 2 \implies -7 = 2$. False. **No solution** (the lines are parallel).

**Question 6:**  
- **Target Answer & Key Steps:** Substitute: $2x + (-2x + 8) = 8 \implies 8 = 8$. Identity. **Infinitely many solutions** (coincident lines).

**Question 7:**  
- **Target Answer & Key Steps:** Check equation 1: $4 + 2(-1) = 4 - 2 = 2$ (True). Check equation 2: $3(4) - (-1) = 12 + 1 = 13$ (True). Yes, $(4, -1)$ is a solution.

**Question 8:**  
- **Target Answer & Key Steps:** Substitute $y$: $5x - 2(2x + 3) = -4 \implies 5x - 4x - 6 = -4 \implies x = 2$. Then $y = 2(2) + 3 = 7$. Solution: $(2, 7)$.

**Question 9:**  
- **Target Answer & Key Steps:** Equation 1: $2t + 3v = 21$<br>Equation 2: $2t + 4v = 24$

**Question 10:**  
- **Target Answer & Key Steps:** Subtract Eq 1 from Eq 2: $v = 3$. Substitute into Eq 1: $2t + 3(3) = 21 \implies 2t + 9 = 21 \implies 2t = 12 \implies t = 6$. Laser tag is $6, VR is $3.

**Question 11:**  
- **Target Answer & Key Steps:** It has **zero solutions (no solution)** because the lines are parallel and never intersect.

**Question 12:**  
- **Target Answer & Key Steps:** From Eq 1: $x = 3y + 9$. Substitute into Eq 2: $2(3y + 9) + y = 4 \implies 6y + 18 + y = 4 \implies 7y = -14 \implies y = -2$. Then $x = 3(-2) + 9 = 3$. Solution: $(3, -2)$.
