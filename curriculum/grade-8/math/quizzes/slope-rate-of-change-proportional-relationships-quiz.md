## 🔑 Parent Answer Key (Educator)

> [!NOTE]
> *This section is for Educator (educator) to verify answers, assess computational precision, and provide targeted feedback.*

**Question 1:**  
- **Target Answer & Key Steps:** $m = \frac{17 - 5}{6 - 2} = \frac{12}{4} = 3$.

**Question 2:**  
- **Target Answer & Key Steps:** $m = \frac{-4 - 8}{1 - (-3)} = \frac{-12}{4} = -3$.

**Question 3:**  
- **Target Answer & Key Steps:** $m = \frac{7 - 7}{-2 - 4} = \frac{0}{-6} = 0$.

**Question 4:**  
- **Target Answer & Key Steps:** $m = \frac{9 - (-1)}{5 - 5} = \frac{10}{0}$ which is **undefined**.

**Question 5:**  
- **Target Answer & Key Steps:** Unit rate $= \frac{45}{3} = 15\text{ mph}$. Equation: $d = 15t$.

**Question 6:**  
- **Target Answer & Key Steps:** $\Delta y = 16 - 7 = 9$; $\Delta x = 5 - 2 = 3$. Slope $m = \frac{9}{3} = 3$.

**Question 7:**  
- **Target Answer & Key Steps:** A positive slope rises from left to right, indicating that as $x$ increases, $y$ increases. A negative slope falls from left to right, indicating that as $x$ increases, $y$ decreases.

**Question 8:**  
- **Target Answer & Key Steps:** $k$ represents the constant of proportionality (the slope). The graph always passes through the origin $(0, 0)$.

**Question 9:**  
- **Target Answer & Key Steps:** Car A speed $= 65\text{ mph}$. Car B speed $= \frac{280 - 140}{4 - 2} = \frac{140}{2} = 70\text{ mph}$. Car B is traveling faster ($70 > 65$).

**Question 10:**  
- **Target Answer & Key Steps:** $m = \frac{-15 - (-6)}{-1 - (-4)} = \frac{-9}{3} = -3$.

**Question 11:**  
- **Target Answer & Key Steps:** Since the water is draining, the rate is decreasing: slope $m = -25\text{ gallons/min}$.

**Question 12:**  
- **Target Answer & Key Steps:** Yes. The ratio $\frac{y}{x}$ is constant: $\frac{12}{3} = \frac{24}{6} = \frac{36}{9} = 4$. It passes through $(0, 0)$ with equation $y = 4x$.
