## 🔑 Parent Answer Key (Educator)

> [!NOTE]
> *This section is for Educator (educator) to verify answers, assess computational precision, and provide targeted feedback.*

**Question 1:**  
- **Target Answer & Key Steps:** $\sqrt{169} = 13$ because $13^2 = 169$.

**Question 2:**  
- **Target Answer & Key Steps:** $\sqrt[3]{216} = 6$ because $6^3 = 216$.

**Question 3:**  
- **Target Answer & Key Steps:** $x = \pm 9$ (both $+9$ and $-9$).

**Question 4:**  
- **Target Answer & Key Steps:** $y = -4$ because $(-4)^3 = -64$.

**Question 5:**  
- **Target Answer & Key Steps:** a) Rational (fraction of integers)<br>b) Irrational (non-repeating, non-terminating decimal)<br>c) Rational (repeating decimal)<br>d) Irrational (transcendental constant)

**Question 6:**  
- **Target Answer & Key Steps:** Let $x = 0.777\ldots$. Then $10x = 7.777\ldots$. Subtract: $9x = 7 \implies x = \frac{7}{9}$.

**Question 7:**  
- **Target Answer & Key Steps:** Let $x = 0.4545\ldots$. Then $100x = 45.4545\ldots$. Subtract: $99x = 45 \implies x = \frac{45}{99} = \frac{5}{11}$.

**Question 8:**  
- **Target Answer & Key Steps:** Since $6^2 = 36$ and $7^2 = 49$, $\sqrt{38}$ is between 6 and 7. $38$ is very close to $36$, so $\sqrt{38} \approx 6.1$ or $6.2$ (actual $\approx 6.16$).

**Question 9:**  
- **Target Answer & Key Steps:** Since $8^2 = 64$ and $9^2 = 81$, $\sqrt{75}$ is between 8 and 9. Therefore, $-\sqrt{75}$ lies between $-9$ and $-8$.

**Question 10:**  
- **Target Answer & Key Steps:** Side length $s = \sqrt{144} = 12\text{ ft}$. Perimeter $= 4s = 4(12) = 48\text{ ft}$.

**Question 11:**  
- **Target Answer & Key Steps:** Edge length $e = \sqrt[3]{343} = 7\text{ meters}$.

**Question 12:**  
- **Target Answer & Key Steps:** True. A rational number can be written as $\frac{a}{b}$ and another as $\frac{c}{d}$. Their sum is $\frac{ad+bc}{bd}$, where both numerator and denominator are integers ($bd \ne 0$), which fits the definition of a rational number.
