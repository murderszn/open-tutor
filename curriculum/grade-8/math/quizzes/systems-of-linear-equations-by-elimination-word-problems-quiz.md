## 🔑 Parent Answer Key (Educator)

> [!NOTE]
> *This section is for Educator (educator) to verify answers, assess computational precision, and provide targeted feedback.*

**Question 1:**  
- **Target Answer & Key Steps:** Add equations: $8x = 32 \implies x = 4$. Substitute: $3(4) + 2y = 19 \implies 12 + 2y = 19 \implies 2y = 7 \implies y = 3.5$. Solution: $(4, 3.5)$.

**Question 2:**  
- **Target Answer & Key Steps:** Subtract equations: $3y - (-y) = 25 - 13 \implies 4y = 12 \implies y = 3$. Substitute: $4x - 3 = 13 \implies 4x = 16 \implies x = 4$. Solution: $(4, 3)$.

**Question 3:**  
- **Target Answer & Key Steps:** Multiply Eq 1 by $-2$: $-4x - 10y = -40$. Add to Eq 2: $-x = -5 \implies x = 5$. Substitute: $2(5) + 5y = 20 \implies 10 + 5y = 20 \implies 5y = 10 \implies y = 2$. Solution: $(5, 2)$.

**Question 4:**  
- **Target Answer & Key Steps:** Multiply Eq 1 by 3 and Eq 2 by 4: $9x + 12y = 54$ and $8x - 12y = -20$. Add: $17x = 34 \implies x = 2$. Substitute: $3(2) + 4y = 18 \implies 4y = 12 \implies y = 3$. Solution: $(2, 3)$.

**Question 5:**  
- **Target Answer & Key Steps:** Let numbers be $x$ and $y$. System: $x + y = 48$ and $x - y = 14$. Add: $2x = 62 \implies x = 31$. Substitute: $31 + y = 48 \implies y = 17$. The numbers are 31 and 17.

**Question 6:**  
- **Target Answer & Key Steps:** Let $s$ = student tickets, $a$ = adult tickets. $s + a = 200$ and $5s + 10a = 1450$. Multiply Eq 1 by $-5$: $-5s - 5a = -1000$. Add: $5a = 450 \implies a = 90$ adult tickets. $s = 200 - 90 = 110$ student tickets.

**Question 7:**  
- **Target Answer & Key Steps:** Multiply Eq 2 by 2: $-6x + 2y = -10$. Add to Eq 1: $0 = 0$. **Infinitely many solutions**.

**Question 8:**  
- **Target Answer & Key Steps:** Multiply Eq 1 by $-2$: $-4x - 6y = -24$. Add to Eq 2: $0 = -6$. **No solution**.

**Question 9:**  
- **Target Answer & Key Steps:** Let $x$ = lbs of $8 bean, $y$ = lbs of $14 bean. $x + y = 30$ and $8x + 14y = 10(30) = 300$. Multiply Eq 1 by $-8$: $-8x - 8y = -240$. Add: $6y = 60 \implies y = 10\text{ lbs}$. Then $x = 20\text{ lbs}$.

**Question 10:**  
- **Target Answer & Key Steps:** Substitution is most efficient because the first equation is already solved for $y$ in terms of $x$, allowing immediate replacement into the second equation.

**Question 11:**  
- **Target Answer & Key Steps:** Multiply Eq 2 by 3: $6x + 3y = 21$. Add to Eq 1: $11x = 44 \implies x = 4$. Substitute: $2(4) + y = 7 \implies 8 + y = 7 \implies y = -1$. Solution: $(4, -1)$.

**Question 12:**  
- **Target Answer & Key Steps:** Downstream speed: $c + w = 24 / 2 = 12\text{ mph}$. Upstream speed: $c - w = 24 / 4 = 6\text{ mph}$. Add: $2c = 18 \implies c = 9\text{ mph}$. Current: $9 + w = 12 \implies w = 3\text{ mph}$.
