## 🔑 Parent Answer Key (Educator)

> [!NOTE]
> *This section is for Educator (educator) to verify answers, assess computational precision, and provide targeted feedback.*

**Question 1:**  
- **Target Answer & Key Steps:** Apply product of powers rule: $4^{3+6} = 4^9$.

**Question 2:**  
- **Target Answer & Key Steps:** Apply quotient of powers rule: $7^{9-4} = 7^5$.

**Question 3:**  
- **Target Answer & Key Steps:** Apply power of a power rule: $3^{4 \times 5} = 3^{20}$.

**Question 4:**  
- **Target Answer & Key Steps:** $5^{-3} = \frac{1}{5^3} = \frac{1}{125}$.

**Question 5:**  
- **Target Answer & Key Steps:** $(-8)^0 = 1$ and $12^1 = 12$, so $1 + 12 = 13$.

**Question 6:**  
- **Target Answer & Key Steps:** Numerator: $x^{5 + (-2)} = x^3$. Quotient: $\frac{x^3}{x^7} = x^{3-7} = x^{-4} = \frac{1}{x^4}$.

**Question 7:**  
- **Target Answer & Key Steps:** Distribute power: $2^3 \cdot (a^3)^3 \cdot (b^4)^3 = 8a^9b^{12}$.

**Question 8:**  
- **Target Answer & Key Steps:** Invert and apply exponent: $\left(\frac{3}{2}\right)^3 = \frac{27}{8}$ or $3\frac{3}{8}$.

**Question 9:**  
- **Target Answer & Key Steps:** By quotient rule: $n - 3 = 8 \implies n = 11$.

**Question 10:**  
- **Target Answer & Key Steps:** Inside: $5^{2-6} = 5^{-4}$. Power of power: $(5^{-4})^2 = 5^{-8}$. Choice A.

**Question 11:**  
- **Target Answer & Key Steps:** Numerator: $10^5$. Denominator: $10^2 \cdot 1 = 10^2$. Result: $10^{5-2} = 10^3 = 1{,}000$.

**Question 12:**  
- **Target Answer & Key Steps:** Multiply operations by time: $2^5 \cdot 2^{10} = 2^{15}$ operations (which equals 32,768).
