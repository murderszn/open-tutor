## 🔑 Parent Answer Key (Educator)

> [!NOTE]
> *This section is for Educator (educator) to verify answers, assess computational precision, and provide targeted feedback.*

**Question 1:**  
- **Target Answer & Key Steps:** $7x - 3x = 15 + 5 \implies 4x = 20 \implies x = 5$.

**Question 2:**  
- **Target Answer & Key Steps:** $8y - 6 = 5y + 12 \implies 3y = 18 \implies y = 6$.

**Question 3:**  
- **Target Answer & Key Steps:** $6w + 12 = 6w + 12 \implies 0 = 0$ (identity). **Infinitely many solutions** (all real numbers).

**Question 4:**  
- **Target Answer & Key Steps:** $4k - 8 = 4k + 11 \implies -8 = 11$ (false statement). **No solution**.

**Question 5:**  
- **Target Answer & Key Steps:** $-3p - 12 = 2p - 12 - 5p \implies -3p - 12 = -3p - 12$. **Infinitely many solutions**.

**Question 6:**  
- **Target Answer & Key Steps:** $2m - 4 = 3m - 7 \implies -m = -3 \implies m = 3$. **One solution** ($m = 3$).

**Question 7:**  
- **Target Answer & Key Steps:** $0.4b = 10 \implies b = 25$.

**Question 8:**  
- **Target Answer & Key Steps:** Sample answer: $5x + 3 = 5x - 7$ (same variable coefficient on both sides with different constants).

**Question 9:**  
- **Target Answer & Key Steps:** Sample answer: $2(3x + 4) = 6x + 8$ (expressions on both sides simplify to the exact same terms).

**Question 10:**  
- **Target Answer & Key Steps:** Equation: $15m + 30 = 20m \implies 5m = 30 \implies m = 6\text{ months}$.

**Question 11:**  
- **Target Answer & Key Steps:** $3x - 5 = 3x - 5$. True for all $x$. **Infinitely many solutions**.

**Question 12:**  
- **Target Answer & Key Steps:** Multiply all terms by common denominator 10: $4c + 30 = 5c - 10 \implies -c = -40 \implies c = 40$.
