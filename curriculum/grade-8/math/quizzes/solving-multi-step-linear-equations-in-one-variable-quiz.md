## 🔑 Parent Answer Key (Educator)

> [!NOTE]
> *This section is for Educator (educator) to verify answers, assess computational precision, and provide targeted feedback.*

**Question 1:**  
- **Target Answer & Key Steps:** $3x = 35 - 14 = 21 \implies x = 7$.

**Question 2:**  
- **Target Answer & Key Steps:** $-5y = 27 + 8 = 35 \implies y = -7$.

**Question 3:**  
- **Target Answer & Key Steps:** $8p - 12 = 36 \implies 8p = 48 \implies p = 6$.

**Question 4:**  
- **Target Answer & Key Steps:** $7m - 3m - 12 = 16 \implies 4m - 12 = 16 \implies 4m = 28 \implies m = 7$.

**Question 5:**  
- **Target Answer & Key Steps:** $\frac{2}{3}w = 12 \implies w = 12 \cdot \frac{3}{2} = 18$.

**Question 6:**  
- **Target Answer & Key Steps:** $k - 7 = 24 \implies k = 31$.

**Question 7:**  
- **Target Answer & Key Steps:** $0.4x = 4.8 - 1.6 = 3.2 \implies x = \frac{3.2}{0.4} = 8$.

**Question 8:**  
- **Target Answer & Key Steps:** $5z + 10 - 3z + 3 = 23 \implies 2z + 13 = 23 \implies 2z = 10 \implies z = 5$.

**Question 9:**  
- **Target Answer & Key Steps:** $-6a + 10 + 4a = 18 \implies -2a + 10 = 18 \implies -2a = 8 \implies a = -4$.

**Question 10:**  
- **Target Answer & Key Steps:** $P = 2l + 2w = 2(2w + 5) + 2w = 64 \implies 4w + 10 + 2w = 64 \implies 6w = 54 \implies w = 9\text{ cm}$. Length $l = 2(9) + 5 = 23\text{ cm}$.

**Question 11:**  
- **Target Answer & Key Steps:** $6n - 9 = 27 \implies 6n = 36 \implies n = 6$.

**Question 12:**  
- **Target Answer & Key Steps:** Equation: $14m + 25 = 179$. Subtract 25: $14m = 154$. Divide by 14: $m = 11\text{ months}$.
