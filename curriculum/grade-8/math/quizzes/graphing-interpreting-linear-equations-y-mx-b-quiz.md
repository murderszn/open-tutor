## 🔑 Parent Answer Key (Educator)

> [!NOTE]
> *This section is for Educator (educator) to verify answers, assess computational precision, and provide targeted feedback.*

**Question 1:**  
- **Target Answer & Key Steps:** Slope $m = -4$; y-intercept $b = 9$ (or coordinate $(0, 9)$).

**Question 2:**  
- **Target Answer & Key Steps:** $2y = -3x + 12 \implies y = -\frac{3}{2}x + 6$.

**Question 3:**  
- **Target Answer & Key Steps:** $y = \frac{2}{5}x - 3$

**Question 4:**  
- **Target Answer & Key Steps:** Crosses y-axis at $(0, -4)$ (when $x = 0$). Crosses x-axis at $(2, 0)$ (set $y = 0 \implies 2x = 4 \implies x = 2$).

**Question 5:**  
- **Target Answer & Key Steps:** Slope $m = 0$; y-intercept is $(0, 7)$.

**Question 6:**  
- **Target Answer & Key Steps:** $-y = -4x + 8 \implies y = 4x - 8$.

**Question 7:**  
- **Target Answer & Key Steps:** $y = 0.05x + 35$

**Question 8:**  
- **Target Answer & Key Steps:** Substitute $x = 3$: $y = 3(3) + 2 = 9 + 2 = 11$. Yes, $(3, 11)$ satisfies the equation and lies on the line.

**Question 9:**  
- **Target Answer & Key Steps:** $y - 4 = -2x \implies y = -2x + 4$. Slope $m = -2$; y-intercept $(0, 4)$.

**Question 10:**  
- **Target Answer & Key Steps:** Line 2 is steeper because steepness is measured by the absolute value of the slope: $|-7| = 7 > |5| = 5$.

**Question 11:**  
- **Target Answer & Key Steps:** $y = 5$

**Question 12:**  
- **Target Answer & Key Steps:** The y-intercept $3000$ means the hiker started at an elevation of 3,000 feet. The slope $-150$ means the hiker is descending at a rate of 150 feet per hour.
