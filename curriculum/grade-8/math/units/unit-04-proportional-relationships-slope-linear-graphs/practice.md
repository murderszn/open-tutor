# Practice — Unit 04: Proportional Relationships, Slope, and Linear Graphs

Student-facing practice sets. Solutions are in the separate
[answer key](answer-key.md) — do not work from the key while
practising.

## Practice A — Same ratio every time? (Session 2)

1. A shop sells 5 pounds of grapes for $15. Find the unit rate,
   write the equation, and find the cost of 9 pounds.
2. A cyclist rides 200 miles in 8 hours at a steady pace. Find the
   unit rate, write the equation, and find the distance in 5 hours.
3. Table: `x`: 2, 3, 6; `y`: 12, 18, 36. Proportional? Find `k`,
   write the equation, and predict `y` when `x = 10`.
4. Table: `x`: 0, 1, 2, 3; `y`: 2, 6, 10, 14. Proportional? Say
   which test fails, and describe the pattern that actually holds.
5. A machine fills 90 bottles in 3 minutes. Write the equation
   (bottles in terms of minutes) and find how many bottles it
   fills in 11 minutes. Is the relationship proportional? Why?
6. **Explain your reasoning.** Two tables both "go up steadily":
   Table 1: `x`: 1, 2, 3; `y`: 4, 8, 12. Table 2: `x`: 1, 2, 3;
   `y`: 5, 9, 13. Which is proportional, and what is the exact
   difference between them? Use the zero test in your answer.

## Practice B — Read the graph, name the rate (Session 4)

1. A proportional graph passes through the origin and (3, 18).
   What is its unit rate? Write its equation and predict `y`
   when `x = 7`.
2. Compare: a table shows 4 hours → 28 miles; an equation reads
   `d = 8t`. Which is faster, and by how much per hour? How far
   does each go in 9 hours?
3. Two proportional graphs both pass through the origin. One
   passes through (5, 15), the other through (5, 40). Which is
   steeper? What are the two unit rates, and what does each mean
   if the graphs are distance-time graphs?
4. Graph `y = 5x` for `x` from 0 to 4 on graph paper. Mark the
   point (1, 5) and explain why it must lie on the line.
5. A learner says the graph through (0, 0) and (4, 16) has unit
   rate 16 "because the point's `y` is 16." Correct the learner
   and give the right unit rate with its working.
6. **Application.** Pump A fills proportionally and its graph
   passes through (6, 72) gallons in minutes. Pump B's equation is
   `G = 10m`. Which pump is faster, and how many gallons does
   each deliver in 15 minutes?

## Practice C — Rise over run, checked twice (Session 6)

1. Slope of the line through (0, 0) and (5, 20).
2. Slope of the line through (2, 3) and (5, 15). Check it with the
   point (4, 11): is that point on the same line?
3. Slope of the line through (1, 10) and (4, 4). Rising or falling?
   How does the sign tell you?
4. Slope of the line through (3, 8) and (3, 14). What happens in
   the formula, and what kind of line is this?
5. Triangles under a line have legs (run 4, rise 12) and (run 7,
   rise 21). Are the triangles similar? What is the slope? Show
   the ratio for both.
6. **Error analysis.** A learner computes the slope from (3, 7)
   to (7, 19) as (7 − 3)/(19 − 7) = 4/12 = 1/3. What went wrong?
   Give the correct slope and the corrected working.

## Practice D — Write the equation (Session 8)

1. Line through (0, 2) and (4, 14). Find `m`, `b`, and the
   equation. Proportional or not? Why?
2. Table: `x`: 0, 1, 3; `y`: 1, 4, 10. Find `m` and `b`, write the
   equation, and predict `y` when `x = 8`.
3. Table: `x`: 2, 4, 6; `y`: 11, 19, 27. No `x = 0` row is given.
   Find `m`, then use (2, 11) to find `b`, and write the equation.
4. A rental charges $40 per day plus a $25 one-time fee. Write the
   equation (total cost in terms of days) and find the cost of a
   4-day rental.
5. A line has slope −3 and intercept 11. Write its equation and
   find `y` when `x = 5`. Does it rise or fall?
6. **Explain your reasoning.** Why does every line through the
   origin have an equation of the form `y = mx`, and why does
   that make every origin line a proportional relationship?

## Practice E — Tanks, plans, and predictions (Session 10)

1. A candle burns by `H = −2t + 24` (centimetres, hours). State
   slope and intercept with meanings. Find the height after 7
   hours and when the candle burns out.
2. A pool drains by `V = −5t + 100` (gallons, minutes). When is it
   empty? How much is left after 8 minutes? What does the model
   say after 20 minutes, and why should you not believe it?
3. Two gym plans: G charges `G = 20m`, H charges `H = 12m + 40`
   (dollars, months). Build a table for 2, 5, and 10 months.
   Where do they tie? Who wins on each side?
4. A cyclist rides `d = 14t` while a second cyclist, starting 9
   miles ahead, rides `d = 11t + 9`. Build a table for 0, 1, 2,
   and 3 hours. When are they at the same place? Who is ahead
   after that?
5. A plant grows by `H = 2w + 9` (centimetres, weeks). Find the
   height after 6 weeks. If the plant were proportional instead
   (same slope, intercept 0), what would its height be after 6
   weeks, and what real difference does the 9 centimetres make?
6. **Error analysis.** A learner reads `C = 3m + 2` and says "the
   taxi costs $3 to start and $2 per mile." Correct the reading,
   and find the cost of a 15-mile ride.

## Practice F — Who wins, and where do they cross? (Session 11 review of Lesson 06)

1. Walkers: W: `W = 4t`, V: `V = 3t + 5` (miles, hours). Table for
   0, 2, and 5 hours. When does W catch V? Who is ahead at 2
   hours?
2. Phone plans: A: `A = 9g`, B: `B = 5g + 24` (dollars, gigabytes).
   Find the tie from a table (try 0, 3, 6). Which plan is cheaper
   for 2 gigabytes? For 10 gigabytes?
3. Two lines have slope 10 and intercepts 0 and 15. Do they ever
   cross? Which is always ahead, and by how much?
4. A graph shows two lines crossing at (4, 28). One line passes
   through the origin. What is its equation? If the other line has
   intercept 12, what is its slope?
5. **Error analysis.** A learner compares `y = 6x` and `y = 4x + 5`
   and says "the first is steeper, so it is always greater." Test
   `x = 1` and `x = 10`, then correct the claim.
6. **Application.** A fair stall offers pay-per-ride `P = 3r` or a
   wristband `W = 2r + 12` (dollars, rides). A learner plans 5
   rides; a friend plans 15 rides. What should each choose, and
   where do the plans tie? Show the table.

## Review — Midyear review clinic (Session 13)

Mixed problems across Units 01–04 (track objectives 1–7). Work
each one, then say which unit's skill it uses.

1. **Unit 01.** Between which two whole numbers does √50 lie?
   Justify with squares, and give a one-decimal estimate.
2. **Unit 02.** Evaluate `3² × 3⁻⁵` as a fraction, showing the
   exponent rule you used. Then write 420,000 in scientific
   notation.
3. **Unit 03.** Solve `3(x − 2) = 15`, showing every move, and
   check by substitution. Then classify `2x + 3 = 2x + 5` (one,
   none, or infinitely many solutions) and explain the final form.
4. **Unit 04.** A line passes through (3, 12) and (6, 24). Find its
   slope, write its equation, and say whether the relationship is
   proportional, with the reason.
5. **Unit 04.** A tank drains by `V = −6t + 72`. State the slope
   and intercept with meanings, and find when the tank is empty.
6. **Unit 04, error analysis.** A learner computes the slope from
   (2, 5) to (5, 11) as (5 − 2)/(11 − 5) = 1/2. Correct the working,
   give the right slope, and name the mistake.
7. **Mixed.** Two plans: A: `A = 13h`, B: `B = 9h + 16` (dollars,
   hours). Build a table for 0, 4, and 8 hours. Where do they tie?
   Which is cheaper for a 2-hour job? For a 10-hour job?
8. **Mixed, explain your reasoning.** A learner says "a line with
   slope 0 and a line with undefined slope are the same thing,
   because both have a zero somewhere." Correct the learner, with
   one example of each.
