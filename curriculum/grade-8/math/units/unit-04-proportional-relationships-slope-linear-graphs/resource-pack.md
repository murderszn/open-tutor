# Resource Pack — Unit 04: Proportional Relationships, Slope, and Linear Graphs

**Goal:** a grade-8 learner graphs proportional relationships,
interprets unit rate as slope, derives `y = mx` and `y = mx + b`,
and interprets slope and intercept in context.
**Age fit:** ~13–14; adult previews all video ads and comments
sections before assigning.

All external items below were checked on **2026-11-01** unless noted.
"Search link" items are clearly labeled; nothing here claims captions
or runtimes that were not checked. Direct pages listed below were
confirmed via search-result text on 2026-11-01 (the text browser
could not render the full pages); preview in a real browser before
assigning. Free, no-account routes support every core objective:
the standard text, the Achieve the Core coherence entries, and the
Desmos/GeoGebra tools are all openly readable, and the Khan Academy
eighth-grade pages are free to read.
