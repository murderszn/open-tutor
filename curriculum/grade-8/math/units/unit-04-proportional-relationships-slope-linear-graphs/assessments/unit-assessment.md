# Unit Assessment — Unit 04: Proportional Relationships, Slope, and Linear Graphs

Student-facing. 11 tasks, 30 points total. Show all working;
context answers need units and a meaning sentence where asked.
Solutions are in the separate [answer key](../answer-key.md).

## Part A — Proportional relationships (6 points)

1. (3 points) A table shows `x`: 2, 3, 5 and `y`: 14, 21, 35. Is
   the relationship proportional? Give the unit rate, write the
   equation, and predict `y` when `x = 8`.
2. (3 points) A table shows `x`: 0, 2, 4 and `y`: 4, 10, 16. Is
   the relationship proportional? Explain with the ratio test and
   the zero test, and write the equation that does describe the
   table.

## Part B — Slope (6 points)

3. (2 points) Find the slope of the line through (2, 7) and
   (5, 19).
4. (2 points) Two triangles under one line have legs (run 3, rise
   12) and (run 5, rise 20). Are the triangles similar? What is
   the slope? Explain with ratios.
5. (2 points) Find the slope of the line through (0, 8) and
   (6, 2). Is the line rising or falling? How does the sign tell
   you?

## Part C — Writing equations (6 points)

6. (3 points) A line passes through (0, 5) and (4, 17). Write its
   equation, showing how you found the slope and the intercept,
   and check it with a third point on the line.
7. (3 points) A cleaning service charges a $30 booking fee plus
   $12 per hour. Write the equation (total cost in terms of
   hours) and find the cost of a 7-hour job. Is the relationship
   proportional? Why or why not?

## Part D — Slope and intercept in context (6 points)

8. (3 points) A tank drains by `H = −3t + 60`, where `H` is gallons
   and `t` is minutes. State what the slope and the intercept mean
   in the story (with units), and find when the tank is empty.
9. (3 points) Two runners: A runs by `A = 8t` (miles, hours);
   B's table shows 2 hours → 20 miles at a steady pace. Who is
   faster, and by how much per hour? How far does each run in 4
   hours?

## Part E — Compare and analyze (6 points)

10. (3 points) Two plans charge `P = 11x` and `Q = 7x + 24`
    (dollars). Build a table for 3, 6, and 10 units. Where do they
    tie? Which plan is cheaper for a small user (3 units) and for
    a large user (10 units)? Explain with slopes and intercepts.
11. (3 points) **Error analysis.** A learner compares `y = 6x` and
    `y = 4x + 5` and writes: "The first line is steeper, so it is
    always greater." Test both equations at `x = 1` and at
    `x = 10`, state whether the learner is right, and correct the
    claim in one sentence that uses both a slope and an intercept.
