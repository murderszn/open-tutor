# Formative Quiz — Unit 04, Week 18

Student-facing. 10 items, 1 point each, 10 points total. Show
working for every item. Solutions are in the separate
[answer key](../answer-key.md).

1. A table shows `x`: 2, 4, 6 and `y`: 10, 20, 30. Is the
   relationship proportional? If so, give the unit rate and the
   equation.
2. A shop charges $24 for 8 pounds of apples. What is the unit
   rate (price per pound)?
3. A proportional graph passes through the origin and (4, 20).
   What is its unit rate (slope)?
4. Compare: a graph passes through (3, 27) (distance in miles,
   time in hours) and an equation reads `d = 7t`. Which moving
   object is faster, and by how much per hour?
5. Find the slope of the line through (1, 4) and (5, 16).
6. A line passes through (0, 3) and (2, 11). Write its equation.
7. A table shows `x`: 0, 1, 2 and `y`: 6, 9, 12. Write the equation
   of the line.
8. A tank drains by `W = −6t + 72`, where `W` is gallons and `t`
   is minutes. What does the intercept 72 mean in the story?
9. Two plans charge `A = 9x` and `B = 6x + 15` (dollars). What does
   each plan cost at `x = 5`? What does that tell you about the
   two plans?
10. Which of these is NOT a proportional relationship, and why:
    `y = 2x`, `y = 2x + 1`, or `y = 5x`?
