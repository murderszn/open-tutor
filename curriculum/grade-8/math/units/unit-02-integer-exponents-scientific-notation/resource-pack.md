# Resource Pack — Unit 02: Integer Exponents and Scientific Notation

**Goal:** a grade-8 learner builds the integer-exponent rules and learns
to write, estimate, and compute with scientific notation.
**Age fit:** ~13–14; adult preview all video ads and comments sections.

All external items below were checked on **2026-10-19** unless noted.
"Search link" items are clearly labeled; nothing here claims captions or
runtimes that were not checked. Khan Academy lesson pages required a
client-side challenge in the text browser, so the three Khan items are
listed with URLs recovered verbatim from search results and topic fit
confirmed against their published transcripts — treat them as
curated-but-not-click-verified.

## Videos (curated, not fully click-verified)

1. **"Negative exponents" — Khan Academy.**
   <https://www.khanacademy.org/kmap/equations-inequalities-c/x098678cf39d169eb:exponent-properties-roots/x098678cf39d169eb:negative-exponents/v/negative-exponents>
   Topic fit confirmed via published transcript (2⁻⁴ as dividing by 2
   four times). Free; requires a Khan Academy account for full
   exercises, videos play without one. Relevance: Lessons 01–02.
   Note: pages showed a client challenge in text browsing on 2026-10-19;
   preview in a real browser before assigning.
2. **"Multiplying & dividing powers (integer exponents)" — Khan Academy.**
   <https://en.khanacademy.org/math/ncert-class-8/x4eb5a6db275bcce1:exponents-and-powers/x4eb5a6db275bcce1:exponents-and-powers-10-1/v/multiplying-and-dividing-powers-with-integer-exponents>
   Transcript confirms product/quotient rule treatment at class-8 level.
   Same account note as above. Relevance: Lesson 03.
3. **"Exponent properties with quotients" — Khan Academy (8th-grade
   Illustrative Math track).**
   <https://www.khanacademy.org/math/8th-grade-illustrative-math/unit-7-exponents-and-scientific-notation/lesson-6-what-about-other-bases/v/exponent-properties-involving-quotients>
   Unit 7 is literally "Exponents and Scientific Notation" at grade 8.
   Same account note. Relevance: Lessons 03–04.
4. **"Powers of Ten" (1977) — Charles and Ray Eames, via the Eames Office

   Runtime 9 min; existence and official availability confirmed via the

   ideal launch for Lesson 05. Preview for ads/comments; adult watches

5. **Search link (labeled):** YouTube search for "scientific notation
   operations khan academy" —
   <https://www.youtube.com/results?search_query=scientific+notation+operations+khan+academy>

## Web references

1. **Common Core State Standards, Grade 8 » Expressions & Equations.**
   <https://www.thecorestandards.org/Math/Content/8/EE/> — official text
   of 8.EE.A.1, 8.EE.A.3, 8.EE.A.4 read in full on 2026-10-19. No account.
2. **NASA — Solar System Temperatures / Planetary Fact Sheet.**
   <https://science.nasa.gov/resource/solar-system-temperatures/> —
   authoritative planet data reference (opened 2026-10-19; page updated
   2024-11-01). No account.
3. **Eames "Powers of Ten" — film facts.**
   <https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Powers_of_Ten_(film_series)> — release
   year, runtime, narrator, and basis (Boeke's *Cosmic View*) confirmed
   2026-10-19. Background reading for the adult, not assigned to the
   learner as math instruction.
4. **U.S. Census Bureau — Population Clock.**
   <https://www.census.gov/popclock/> — current U.S./world population
   figures for refreshing the 8.EE.A.3 magnitude example (page opens but
   the live clock needs JavaScript; treat shown figures as estimates).
   No account. Checked 2026-10-19.
5. **Desmos scientific calculator.**
   <https://www.desmos.com/scientific> — free, no-account calculator for
   deliberately producing E notation during Lesson 06. Checked
   2026-10-19 (landing page loads).
6. **University of Maryland lecture demo — Orders of Magnitude
   Simulations.** <https://lecdem.physics.umd.edu/tools-and-resources/directory-of-simulations/38-mathematics-simulations/453-orders-magnitude-sims.html>
   — .edu page listing the official Eames film link and related scale
   simulations; background for the adult. Opened 2026-10-19.
