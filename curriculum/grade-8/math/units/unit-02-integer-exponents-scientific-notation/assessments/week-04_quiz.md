# Unit 02 — Formative Quiz (Week 4 check)

**Unit:** [U02 — Integer Exponents and Scientific Notation](../README.md)
· **Session 15 of 16** · ~25 minutes · **Standards:** 8.EE.A.1, 8.EE.A.3, 8.EE.A.4

Answer in the notebook. Show every step — a bare answer earns half credit.
No calculator for questions 1–6; calculator allowed for checking on 7–10.

## Questions

1. Write 10⁵ as a decimal. Then use the dividing-down pattern to predict
   the value of 10⁻¹ as a decimal.
2. Evaluate: (a) 7⁰ (b) 4⁻². Write each answer as a fraction and as a
   decimal.
3. Simplify, leaving no negative exponents: 5³ × 5⁻⁶.
4. Simplify: (2⁴)² ÷ 2⁵. Show each rule you use.
5. Write 0.00036 in scientific notation.
6. Write 9.1 × 10⁵ as a decimal number.
7. Earth's diameter is about 1.3 × 10⁷ m; the Sun's is about
   1.4 × 10⁹ m. How many times wider is the Sun? Show the exponent work.
8. Compute (3 × 10⁴) × (2 × 10⁻⁶). Normalize your answer.
9. Compute 5 × 10⁷ + 8 × 10⁶. Show the exponent-matching step.
10. A calculator displays `7.2E-5` for your answer. Write the number in
    scientific notation and as a decimal. What does the E mean?

**Scoring guide (adult-held):** 8+ correct with shown work = ready for
the unit assessment; 6–7 = review the missed objective with the lesson
it came from; below 6 = re-teach before Session 16.
