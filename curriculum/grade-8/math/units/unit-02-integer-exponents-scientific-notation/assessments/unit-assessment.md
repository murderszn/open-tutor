# Unit 02 — Culminating Assessment

**Unit:** [U02 — Integer Exponents and Scientific Notation](../README.md)
· **Session 16 of 16** · ~40 minutes · **Standards:** 8.EE.A.1, 8.EE.A.3, 8.EE.A.4

Show all work. No calculator for Part A; calculator allowed for checking
in Part B. Partial credit rewards correct rule use even when arithmetic
slips.

## Part A — Integer exponents (8.EE.A.1)

A1. Evaluate each: 9⁰, 10⁻³, (−2)⁻². Give fraction and decimal forms.
A2. Simplify to one power: 6² × 6⁻⁷. Then write it with a positive
    exponent and evaluate the fraction.
A3. Simplify: (10⁻²)³ ÷ 10⁻⁴. Name each rule you use.
A4. Error analysis: "Casey simplifies 4³ × 2³ as 8⁶." Explain the
    error with an expansion argument, then give the correct
    simplification of 4³ × 4³.
A5. Find two different pairs of negative integers (m, n) such that
    (3ᵐ × 3⁵) ÷ 3ⁿ = 3⁻². Show both solutions work.

## Part B — Scientific notation (8.EE.A.3, 8.EE.A.4)

B1. Write in scientific notation: (a) 52,700,000 (b) 0.00000084.
B2. Write as decimals: (a) 2.06 × 10⁶ (b) 7 × 10⁻⁴.
B3. The U.S. population is about 3.4 × 10⁸; the world population is about
    8 × 10⁹. Show two ways that the world population is more than 20
    times the U.S. population.
B4. A red blood cell is 7 × 10⁻⁶ m wide. A pinhead is 2 × 10⁻³ m wide.
    How many red blood cells fit across the pinhead? Show the work.
B5. Compute, showing every step: (4.5 × 10⁶) − (8 × 10⁵).
B6. A phone battery holds 15 Wh; a sensor draws 4 × 10⁻⁷ W. How many
    hours can the sensor run? Write the answer in scientific notation,
    then choose a better unit than "hours" and explain your choice.

## Part C — Explain your reasoning

C1. Your friend missed this unit. In three to four sentences, explain
    why 5⁰ = 1 and why 5⁻² = 1/25, using the dividing-down pattern.

accuracy/reasoning). C1: 3 points (pattern cited, both rules explained,
clear language). Total 25. 20+ = secure; 15–19 = targeted re-teach of
missed objectives; below 15 = revisit Lessons 02–06 before Unit 03.
