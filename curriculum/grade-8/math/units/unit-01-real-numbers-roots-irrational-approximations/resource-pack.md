# Resource Pack — Unit 01: Real Numbers, Roots, and Irrational Approximations

**Summary (adult planning judgment):** Eighth-grade real-number instruction
works best when every video or article supplements, never replaces, hand
division, root bracketing, and measuring. The resources below support the
unit's five objectives: rational/irrational classification, repeating-decimal
conversion, square and cube roots with root equations, the √2 irrationality
argument, and rational approximation with number-line placement. All external
links were checked 2026-10-12; unverified items are labeled as open search
links and the adult previews them before assigning.
