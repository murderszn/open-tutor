# Formative Quiz — Unit 01 (Week 4 check)

**Unit:** Real Numbers, Roots, and Irrational Approximations ·
**Session 15 of 16** · Show your reasoning; exact answers preferred unless
rounding is specified. Calculator allowed only for checking.

## Questions

### 1
Evaluate √144.

### 2
Evaluate ∛125.

### 3
Solve for x: x² = 64. List **all** solutions.

### 4
Solve for y: y³ = −8.

### 5
Classify each number as **rational** or **irrational**, with one reason:

a) 5/9
b) √50
c) 0.3̄6̄
d) π

### 6
Convert the repeating decimal 0.8̄ into a fraction in simplest form.

### 7
Between which two consecutive **tenths** does √20 lie? Show the squaring
you used.

### 8
Order from least to greatest: 1.6, √3, π − 1.5. Show the approximations
you used.

### 9
A square garden has an area of 75 m². Between which two consecutive
integers is its side length? Explain without using a calculator's root key.

### 10 — Error analysis
Maya writes: "√9 + √16 = √25, so √(a + b) = √a + √b for all a, b." Use a
*different* pair of numbers to show Maya's rule is false, and explain what
she did wrong.
