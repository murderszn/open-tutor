# Practice and Review Sessions — Unit 01

Named sessions referenced by the [unit pacing](README.md#16-session-pacing).
All items have worked solutions in [answer-key.md](answer-key.md#practice-sessions).

## Practice A — Decimal detectives (Session 2)

*Supports Lessons 01. Predict → divide by hand → classify.*

For each fraction: (a) predict whether the decimal terminates or repeats,
(b) divide to 6 decimal places, (c) write the result with bar notation and
name the repetend (or "terminating").

1. 5/8
2. 7/9
3. 11/6
4. 3/11
5. 17/20
6. −5/12
7. Challenge: 4/7 — find the full 6-digit repetend.
8. Which of the fractions above are *equal* as decimals? (None pair up —
   but 7/9 = 0.7̄ foreshadows Lesson 03.)

## Review — Expansion card sort (Session 4)

*Re-checks L1–L2 exit items, then a sorting game.*

**Re-check (oral, 10 min):** the adult re-asks the L1 and L2 exit checks.
Anything not secure goes on the Session 13 re-teach list.

**Card sort (25 min):** the adult prepares 12 cards in advance:

> 0.25 · 0.3̄ · √16 · π · 0.1010010001… · 5/9 · √7 · 0.125 · 2.7182818284…
> (digits of e) · −√81 · 0.4̄5̄ · ∛(−27)

The learner sorts into RATIONAL / IRRATIONAL / NEEDS-MORE-INFO, then
defends three placements aloud. (Answers: rational — 0.25, 0.3̄, √16 = 4,
5/9, 0.125, −√81 = −9, 0.4̄5̄, ∛(−27) = −3; irrational — π, 0.1010010001…,
√7, e-digits. None need more info — that's the point.)

## Practice B — Conversion lab (Session 6)

*Supports Lesson 03.*

**Part 1 — convert (20 min):** 0.4̄, 0.1̄8̄, 1.5̄, 0.23̄ (delayed: 0.2333…),
2.0̄6̄. Simplify every answer; verify by re-dividing.

**Part 2 — error analysis (15 min):** two flawed conversions to diagnose:

> (a) "0.5̄ = 5/10 = 1/2." (b) "0.13̄ = 13/99."

(Answers: (a) treats repeating as terminating — correct is 5/9; 1/2 = 0.5
terminates, but 0.5̄ = 0.555… > 0.5. (b) shifts by the repetend length but
ignores the delay — 0.1333… needs 10x/100x: 90x = 12, x = 12/90 = 2/15.)

**Part 3 — prove it (10 min):** use the method to show 0.8̄ = 8/9, then
explain why this proves *every* repeating decimal is rational.

## Practice C — Roots and squares (Session 8)

*Supports Lesson 04.*

1. Evaluate: √144, −√81, √(49/64), √200 (bracket between integers — no
   calculator root key).
2. Solve, all real solutions: x² = 121, x² = 45, x² = −36, x² = 2.
3. Geometry: a square rug has area 2.25 m². Side length? (1.5 m — a
   non-integer perfect square; good discussion.)
4. A square's side is √18 cm. Is the area a whole number? (Yes: 18 cm² —
   nice "root times root" moment foreshadowing exponent rules in U02.)

## Reference — Real-number system map (Session 10)

*Reading/reference session (adult-led, 45 min).*

1. **Draw the map (15 min):** the learner draws the nested diagram from a
   verbal description (no copying a picture): the biggest circle is REAL
   NUMBERS; inside it two non-overlapping circles, RATIONAL and IRRATIONAL;
   inside RATIONAL, nested: INTEGERS, inside that WHOLE NUMBERS, inside
   that NATURAL (counting) NUMBERS. Place 10 numbers from the unit (−3,
   0.75, √2, π, 5, −√16, 0.3̄, ∛8, −1/2, √10) in the correct innermost
   region.
2. **Read (15 min):** the adult reads aloud the [resource
   pack](resource-pack.md)'s "for the adult" note on why the real numbers
   complete the number line (every point has a decimal expansion), while
   the learner follows the nested diagram.
3. **Connect (15 min):** for each unit objective 1–5, the learner points to
   where it lives on the map and states one thing it lets them do.

## Review — Re-teach menu (Session 13)

The adult picks from the Session 4 re-check list and the week's exit
checks. Suggested stations (15 min each, pick two):

- **Station 1 — Expansion patterns:** re-divide 5/6, 4/11, 2/7 by hand;
  name each repetend.
- **Station 2 — Conversion clinic:** 0.6̄, 0.2̄7̄, 0.83̄3̄ with the
  multiply-and-subtract steps written in full.
- **Station 3 — Root equations:** x² = 169 vs. x³ = −27 — solve side by
  side; count solutions; explain the difference.
- **Station 4 — Bracketing sprint:** √11, √30, √110 between consecutive
  tenths (squaring only).
