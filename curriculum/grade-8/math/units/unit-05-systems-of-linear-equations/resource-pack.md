# Resource Pack — Unit 05: Systems of Linear Equations

**Goal:** a grade-8 learner tests ordered pairs, solves systems by
graphing, substitution, and elimination, classifies solution cases,
and models real situations with two linear equations.
**Age fit:** ~13–14; adult previews all video ads and comments
sections before assigning.

All external items below were checked on **2026-11-08** unless noted.
"Search link" items are clearly labeled; nothing here claims captions
or runtimes that were not checked. Direct pages listed below were
confirmed via search-result text on 2026-11-08; preview in a real
browser before assigning. Free, no-account routes support every core
objective: the standard text, the Open Up Resources family materials,
the LibreTexts unit, and the Desmos calculator are all openly
readable, and the Khan Academy eighth-grade pages are free to read.
