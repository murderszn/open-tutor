## Questions

### Question 1 (1 point)

Explain what the solution to a system of two linear equations
represents on a coordinate graph.

### Question 2 (2 points)

Solve the system by graphing (find the intersection point):

`y = x + 2`

`y = −2x + 5`

### Question 3 (1 point)

Determine whether the ordered pair `(2, −1)` is a solution to the
system:

`x + 2y = 0`

`3x − y = 7`

Show both checks.

### Question 4 (1 point)

Solve the system using substitution:

`y = x + 4`

`2x + y = 13`

### Question 5 (1 point)

Solve the system using elimination:

`x + 3y = 15`

`x − y = 3`

### Question 6 (1 point)

Classify the system, without solving it:

`2x − y = 4`

`2x − y = 9`

How many solutions does it have? Explain in one sentence.

### Question 7 (2 points)

A school store sells notebooks and pens. Three notebooks and two
pens cost $11. Two notebooks and two pens cost $8. Let `n` be the
price of one notebook and `p` the price of one pen. Write the
system of equations, then find the price of one notebook and the
price of one pen.

### Question 8 (1 point)

Which method (graphing, substitution, or elimination) is most
efficient for solving the following system? Explain why.

`y = 2x − 1`

`3x + y = 14`
