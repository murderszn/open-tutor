# Resource Pack — Unit 03: Linear Equations and Solution Types

**Goal:** a grade-8 learner solves multi-step linear equations in
one variable, classifies solution types, and models real situations
with one-variable equations.
**Age fit:** ~13–14; adult previews all video ads and comments
sections before assigning.

All external items below were checked on **2026-10-26** unless noted.
"Search link" items are clearly labeled; nothing here claims captions
or runtimes that were not checked. Direct pages listed below were
confirmed via search-result text on 2026-10-26 (the text browser
could not render the full pages); preview in a real browser before
assigning. Free, no-account routes support every core objective:
the standard text, the Open Up Resources lesson, and the Connecticut
lesson materials are all openly readable, and the Khan Academy
eighth-grade pages are free to read.
