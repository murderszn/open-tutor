## Part A — Short answers (8 items × 3 points = 24 points)

1. (3 points) A wave diagram is described in words: the distance
   from one crest to the next crest is 5 cm, and the height from
   the rest line to a crest is 3 cm. (a) State the wavelength.
   (b) State the amplitude. (c) A second wave on an identical rope
   has amplitude 1.5 cm. Which wave carries more energy, and why?
2. (3 points) A wave has frequency 8 Hz and wavelength 0.75 m.
   (a) Find its speed. (b) Find its period.
3. (3 points) (a) Why can astronauts on the Moon not hear each
   other shout, even face to face? (b) Rank air, water, and steel
   from slowest to fastest sound travel. (c) Why do you hear a
   distant train through the rails before you hear it through
   the air?
4. (3 points) You clap once facing a wall and hear the echo 1.0 s
   later. How far away is the wall? Show the divide-by-2 step and
   explain why it is needed.
5. (3 points) For each material, state the dominant thing that
   happens to a flashlight beam — mostly reflected, mostly
   absorbed, or mostly transmitted: (a) clear plastic, (b) wax
   paper (note: it scatters the light), (c) black cardboard.
6. (3 points) A blue ball sits under white light. (a) Why does it
   look blue? (b) The white light is replaced by red-only light.
   What does the ball look like now, and why?
7. (3 points) (a) Put these in order from lowest to highest
   frequency: gamma, radio, infrared, visible. (b) Which of the
   four carries the most energy? (c) Which has the longest
   wavelength?
8. (3 points) State the claim of Lesson 06 about digitized vs.
   analog signals, and give two pieces of evidence for it: one
   about what happens to an analog copy, and one about what a
   pulse receiver has to decide.

## Part B — Extended tasks (4 tasks × 4 points = 16 points)

9. (4 points) **Design a wave.** Design a wave that travels at
   4 m/s. State a frequency and wavelength pair that works, show
   the calculation, and describe how you would make it on a rope
   (how fast to shake, and whether to shake big or small to
   carry a lot of energy).
10. (4 points) **Sound in two media.** A sound travels 1,480 m.
    (a) How long does it take through water? (b) How long does it
    take through air? (Give the air time to two decimal places.)
    (c) In one sentence, explain why the two times differ.
11. (4 points) **Light lab predictions.** A beam of light is
    aimed, in turn, at three things: (a) a mirror, arriving at
    25° from the normal; (b) a black cloth; (c) a clear glass
    pane. For each, predict the dominant outcome (reflect, absorb,
    or transmit) and, for the mirror, the outgoing angle. Then
    state, for each object, whether the light continues straight
    past it, and why.
12. (4 points) **Argue from evidence.** Write a claim–evidence–
    reasoning paragraph supporting the claim that digitized
    signals are a more reliable way to encode and transmit
    information than analog signals. Your evidence must come from
    the noisy-channel comparison (the shape-copy chain vs. the
    pulse chain), and your reasoning must explain what the pulse
    receiver decides. Do not use binary counting in your answer.
