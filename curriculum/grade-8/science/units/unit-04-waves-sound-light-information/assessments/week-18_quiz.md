# Formative Quiz — Unit 04, Week 18

**Unit 04** · 20 minutes · closed notes except the formula card
($v = f\lambda$, echo distance $= v \times t / 2$) · 10 points,
1 point each · **Answer key:**
[answer key](../answer-key.md#week-18-quiz)

*The adult scores the same day. 8/10 is on track; below 8, use
the re-teach plan in the [teacher guide](../teacher-guide.md)
before the unit assessment.*

1. Wavelength is best defined as:
   A) the height from the rest line to a crest
   B) the length of one full repeat, crest to crest
   C) the number of waves passing per second
   D) the time for one wave to pass
2. A wave has frequency 4 Hz and wavelength 1.5 m. What is its
   speed?
3. Two waves travel on identical ropes. Wave A has amplitude
   2 cm; wave B has amplitude 8 cm. Which carries more energy?
4. Sound CANNOT travel through which of these?
   A) air B) water C) steel D) empty space
5. An echo returns 0.6 s after a clap. How far away is the
   reflecting wall? (Use 343 m/s.)
6. Wax paper lets light through but scatters it, so you cannot
   read through it. Is wax paper transparent, translucent, or
   opaque?
7. A green object under white light reflects green light. Under
   blue-only light, it most likely looks:
   A) bright green B) dark C) bright blue D) white
8. Which has the higher frequency: infrared or ultraviolet?
9. A digital message sent as pulses survives a noisy trip better
   than a smooth analog message mainly because:
   A) pulses travel faster than smooth waves
   B) the receiver only has to decide "pulse or not" (short or
   long), so small noise does not change the decision, and
   copies can be cleaned and re-sent exactly
   C) pulses do not need a medium
   D) pulses are louder
10. (Midyear retrieval) A 4 kg object moves at 2 m/s. What is its
    kinetic energy? ($KE = \tfrac12 mv^2$)
