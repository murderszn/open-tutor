# Resource Pack — Unit 05: Electric and Magnetic Interactions and Circuits

**Summary (adult planning judgment):** Weeks 19–20 need a
static-electricity explainer at grade-8 level and a live
simulation for the balloon and magnet observations; Week 20
needs an electromagnet explainer that stays at proportional
reasoning; Week 21 needs a series/parallel circuit reference
and a troubleshooting routine the adult can run without
equipment. Videos supplement the lessons; they never replace
the learner's own observations, builds, and counts. All external
links were checked 2026-11-08; unverified items are labeled as
open search links, and the adult previews every video before
showing it.

   — balloon near a water stream, electric force, more charge

   Lesson 01 (charge model, proportional rules). Free,

2. **Verified direct video (checked 2026-11-08):** Khan Academy,
   "Electric field | Electric charge, electric force, and
   voltage | Physics" —
   [https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=0YOGrTNgGhE](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=0YOGrTNgGhE)
   — the field idea: every charge produces a field around it,
   and the field puts a force on another charge. Matches
   Lesson 03 (fields exist in space). Free, no account; adult
   previews — the video is high-school level in places, so use
   the field idea only and skip the formal treatment.
3. **Verified direct video (checked 2026-11-08):** Crash Course
   Physics #25, "Electric Charge" —
