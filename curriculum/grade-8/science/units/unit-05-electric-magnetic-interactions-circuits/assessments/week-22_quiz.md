# Formative Quiz — Unit 05, Week 22

**Unit 05** · 20 minutes · closed notes · 10 points, 1 point
each · **Answer key:**
[answer key](../answer-key.md#week-22-quiz)

*The adult scores the same day. 8/10 is on track; below 8, use
the re-teach plan in the [teacher guide](../teacher-guide.md)
before the unit assessment.*

1. Two balloons are both rubbed on wool and hung from strings.
   They swing away from each other. Why?
   A) They have opposite charges
   B) They have the same kind of charge, and like charges repel
   C) They are both neutral
   D) The wool gave them magnetic poles
2. A charged balloon sticks to a neutral wall. The best
   explanation is:
   A) The balloon created new charge on the wall
   B) The balloon's charge pushed the wall's negative charges
   away and pulled its positive charges closer, so the wall's
   surface near the balloon is slightly positive
   C) The wall is a conductor, so the charge flowed into it
   D) The balloon's charge disappeared into the wall
3. Which of these is a conductor?
   A) A rubber band
   B) A plastic spoon
   C) A steel nail
   D) A wooden ruler
4. A compass needle placed near a bar magnet lines up with the
   magnetic field. The needle's north-seeking end points:
   A) Toward the magnet's north pole
   B) Away from the magnet, in a random direction
   C) Along the field lines, toward the magnet's south pole
   D) Straight up, away from the table
5. An electromagnet with 10 turns lifts 4 paper clips. With the
   same battery and core, about how many clips should 20 turns
   lift?
   A) 2
   B) 4
   C) 8
   D) 16
6. Two electromagnets are identical except for the core: one has
   an iron nail core and the other has no core (air). Which
   lifts more paper clips?
   A) The air-core coil, because air is lighter
   B) The iron-core coil, because iron concentrates the field
   C) They lift the same number
   D) Neither lifts any clips
7. Two identical bulbs are wired in series on one battery. One
   bulb burns out. What happens to the other bulb?
   A) It stays lit, because each bulb has its own path
   B) It goes out, because the single loop is broken
   C) It gets brighter, because it gets all the push
   D) It gets dimmer, because it shares the push with the dead bulb
8. Two identical bulbs are wired in parallel on one battery. One
   bulb burns out. What happens to the other bulb?
   A) It goes out, because the loop is broken
   B) It stays lit, because it has its own path to the battery
   C) It gets brighter, because it gets the dead bulb's push
   D) It gets dimmer, because the current is split
9. In a parallel circuit, bulb A is dark and bulb B is lit. Where
   must the fault be?
   A) The battery is dead
   B) The main switch is open
   C) Bulb A's branch — bulb A or its branch wire
   D) The wire after bulb B
10. (Retrieval, Unit 03) A battery lights a bulb. The energy
    changes form in this order:
    A) Light → electrical → chemical
    B) Chemical → electrical → light and thermal
    C) Thermal → chemical → light
    D) Electrical → chemical → thermal
