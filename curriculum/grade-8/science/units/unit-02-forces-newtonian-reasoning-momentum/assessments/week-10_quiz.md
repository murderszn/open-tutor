# Formative Quiz — Week 10 (Session 16)

**Unit 02: Forces, Newtonian Reasoning, and Momentum** · 30 minutes ·
closed notes · **Answer key:**
[answer key](../answer-key.md#formative-quiz)

*Purpose: check readiness for the design presentations and the unit
assessment. The adult scores the same day and re-teaches any objective
where the learner scores below 70%.*

1. A book on a table: gravity pulls down 8 N, the table pushes up 8 N.
   (a) Net force? (b) Balanced or unbalanced? (c) What does the book
   do?
2. Two forces on a sled: 70 N forward, 45 N backward (friction).
   Net force and direction?
3. Draw a free-body diagram for a picture frame hanging motionless on a
   nail. Label both arrows with name and source.
4. A bus moving at steady speed brakes suddenly. Using Newton's first
   law, predict what an untied-down box on the floor does — one
   sentence.
5. A 4 kg cart feels a net force of 20 N. What is its acceleration?
6. The net force on a 2 kg object doubles from 6 N to 12 N. What
   happens to its acceleration? State the proportional rule.
7. Name the action–reaction pair when a rocket's engine pushes exhaust
   gases downward.
8. A 3 kg cart moves at 2 m/s. What is its momentum (with units)?
9. A red car and a blue car, same mass and speed, hit identical walls.
   The red car's bumper is rigid; the blue car's bumper crumples.
   Which passenger experiences the smaller peak force, and why — in
   one sentence using stopping time?
10. **Explain your reasoning (3–4 sentences):** On the Moon, a hammer
    and a feather dropped together hit the ground at the same time.
    What does this show about gravity — and what does it tell us about
    why a flat sheet of paper falls slowly on Earth?

**Scoring:** 2 points per item (1 for a correct setup/partial), 20
points total. 14+ = on track; below 14 triggers the Session 17
re-teach plan in the [teacher guide](../teacher-guide.md).
