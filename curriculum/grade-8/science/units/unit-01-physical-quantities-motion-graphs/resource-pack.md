## Web references (4–7, checked 2026-10-13)

1. **NIST — SI Units** —
   <https://www.nist.gov/pml/weights-and-measures/si-units> —
   **opened and read directly 2026-10-13**: the seven SI base units
   (length — meter (m); time — second (s); mass — kilogram (kg)), the
   prefix system, and velocity (m/s) as a derived unit. Page updated
   July 22, 2025. Free, no account. The unit's authoritative reference
   for Lessons 1–2.
2. **PhET — "The Moving Man" simulation** —
   <https://phet.colorado.edu/en/simulations/moving-man> — **verified
   to exist 2026-10-13** (page title: "The Moving Man | Velocity •
   Acceleration • Position | PhET Simulations"); the adult opens and
   previews before assigning. Interactive position/velocity/acceleration
   graphs that draw as the figure moves. Free, no account; runs in the
   browser. Observation alternative for the motion lab and Lesson 6
   extension.
3. **Khan Academy — "Position-time graphs (video)"** — see Videos #1
   above; listed here as the unit's core external explainer for d–t
   graphs (Lessons 4–5).
4. **Khan Academy — "Velocity vs. time graphs"** — see Videos #2 above;
   the unit's core external reference for Lesson 6's v–t sketches and
   the area idea.
