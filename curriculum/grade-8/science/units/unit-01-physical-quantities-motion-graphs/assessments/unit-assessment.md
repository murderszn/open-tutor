# Unit Assessment — Unit 01: Physical Quantities, Motion and Graphs

**Session 20** · ~45 minutes · calculator allowed · **Answer key:**
[answer key](../answer-key.md#unit-assessment)

Write units on every numerical answer. Show the setup (formula with
numbers) for calculation items.

## Part 1 — Measurement (Objectives 1–2)

1. Convert, showing each step: (a) 2.5 m → cm; (b) 1750 g → kg;
   (c) 0.8 km → m. (3 pts)
2. Name the best instrument **and** the SI unit you would record in for:
   (a) the mass of a textbook; (b) the time for 10 jumping jacks;
   (c) the length of a hallway. (3 pts)
3. A learner times a rolling ball three times: 8.4 s, 8.6 s, 8.5 s.
   (a) Are these trials repeatable? (b) What single value would you
   report? (c) Name one variable that must have been controlled for
   these trials to be comparable. (3 pts)

## Part 2 — Speed (Objective 3)

4. A skater covers 240 m in 60 s. What is the average speed? (2 pts)
5. A train travels 180 km in 3 h. Give its speed in km/h **and** in m/s.
   (3 pts)
6. How far does a jogger moving at 2.5 m/s travel in 2 minutes? (2 pts)
7. A runner's 10-m split times: 2.5 s, 2.5 s, 2.6 s, 2.5 s. Was the
   speed closer to constant or changing? Justify with the numbers.
   (2 pts)

## Part 3 — Graphs (Objectives 4–5)

Use this **fictional practice dataset** (a remote-control car; not your
own measurements):

| Time (s) | 0 | 4 | 8 | 12 | 16 | 20 |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| Distance (m) | 0 | 12 | 24 | 24 | 36 | 48 |

8. Plot the distance–time graph on graph paper: labeled axes with units,
   sensible scale, all six points, straight segments, title. (4 pts)
9. Compute the speed on the segment 0–8 s and on the segment 12–20 s.
   Show rise, run, and slope for each. (4 pts)
10. What happened between 8 s and 12 s? Point to the exact feature of
    the graph that shows it. (2 pts)
11. What is the overall average speed for the 20 s? It is lower than the
    fastest segment's speed — explain why in one sentence. (3 pts)
12. Sketch the velocity–time graph for this motion (three segments:
    0–8 s, 8–12 s, 12–20 s). Label both axes and each segment's height.
    (4 pts)
13. Compute the total distance from the areas under your v–t graph and
    confirm it matches the d–t graph's final distance. (3 pts)

## Part 4 — Reasoning

14. **Claim–evidence–reasoning.** A classmate claims: "A horizontal
    segment on a distance–time graph proves the mover teleported back
    to the start." Do you agree or disagree? State your claim, cite one
    piece of evidence from the dataset above, and give the reasoning
    that connects them. (4 pts)

(re-teach per the [teacher guide](../teacher-guide.md#re-teach-plan-session-17)) ·
