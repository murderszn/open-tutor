# Formative Quiz — Unit 01: Physical Quantities, Motion and Graphs

**Session 16** · ~30 minutes · calculator allowed · **Answer key:**
[answer key](../answer-key.md#formative-quiz)

Write units on every numerical answer. A missing unit loses the point
for that answer.

1. Name the SI base units **and symbols** for length, mass, and time.
   (3 pts)
2. Convert 3.2 km to meters. Show the step. (2 pts)
3. A stopwatch reads to 0.01 s; a wall clock reads to the nearest
   minute. Which would you use to time a 100-meter dash, and why?
   (2 pts)
4. Three trials of a desk's width: 120.5 cm, 120.7 cm, 120.6 cm. What
   value do you report, and why is it trustworthy? (2 pts)
5. A cyclist covers 500 m in 100 s. What is the average speed? (2 pts)
6. How long does it take to travel 900 m at a steady 3 m/s? Give the
   answer in seconds and in minutes. (3 pts)
7. On a distance–time graph, segment A is steeper than segment B. Which
   segment shows faster motion? (1 pt)
8. A distance–time graph is flat (horizontal) from 10 s to 18 s. What
   was the mover doing during those 8 seconds? (2 pts)
9. Sketch and label the axes of a velocity–time graph (quantity + unit
   on each axis). At what height would you draw the line for a steady
   5 m/s walk? (3 pts)
10. A velocity–time line sits at 4 m/s for 6 s. What distance does the
    area represent? Show the area calculation with units. (3 pts)

**Total: 23 points.** 20+ = ready for the project presentations; 16–19 =
review Lessons 3–5 before Session 18; below 16 = targeted re-teach in
Session 17 (see [teacher guide](../teacher-guide.md#re-teach-plan-session-17)).
