# Formative Quiz — Week 14 (Session 16)

**Unit 03: Energy, Work, Simple Machines, and Conservation** ·
30 minutes · closed notes · **Answer key:**
[answer key](../answer-key.md#formative-quiz)

*Purpose: check readiness for the design presentations and the unit
assessment. The adult scores the same day and re-teaches any objective
where the learner scores below 70% (below 14 of 20).*

1. A learner pushes a box with 40 N and it slides 6 m in the push
   direction. How much work is done?
2. A learner pushes a wall with 250 N. The wall does not move. How
   much physics work is done? Explain in one sentence.
3. A 3 kg cart moves at 4 m/s. What is its kinetic energy?
4. The cart from question 3 doubles its speed to 8 m/s. What is its
   new kinetic energy? By what factor did it change?
5. A 4 kg object is held 2.5 m above the floor. What is its
   gravitational potential energy (floor as reference,
   $g = 9.8$ m/s²)?
6. A rubber band is stretched farther than before. Does it store
   more or less elastic potential energy? What happens to that
   energy when it is released?
7. A ball falls from rest: at the top it has PE 60 J and KE 0 J.
   Halfway down (ignoring air), what are its PE and KE? What is
   the total?
8. A lever lifts a 240 N load with an effort of 80 N. What is its
   mechanical advantage?
9. A ramp job has work in 400 J and useful work out 320 J. What is
   its efficiency? How many joules went to friction?
10. **Explain your reasoning (3–4 sentences):** A rolling toy car
    slows down and stops on a flat floor. A learner says its energy
    disappeared. Answer them: where did the energy go, and what
    does that show about conservation?

**Scoring:** 2 points per item (1 for a correct setup/partial),
20 points total. 14+ = on track; below 14 triggers the Session 17
re-teach plan in the [teacher guide](../teacher-guide.md).
