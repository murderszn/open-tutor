# Resource Pack — Unit 03: Energy, Work, Simple Machines, and Conservation

**Summary (adult planning judgment):** Weeks 11–12 need a work and
kinetic-energy explainer at the right level and a live energy-bar
simulation for the ramp investigation; Week 13 needs a simple
machine reference with worked force–distance tradeoffs; Week 14
needs a conservation reference for the argument lesson. Videos
supplement the lessons; they never replace the learner's own
calculations, graphs, and builds. All external links were checked
2026-10-26; unverified items are labeled as open search links,
and the adult previews every video before showing it.

## Web references (4–7, checked 2026-10-26)

1. **PhET — "Energy Skate Park: Basics" simulation** —
   <https://phet.colorado.edu/en/simulation/energy-skate-park-basics>
   — **opened and title-verified 2026-10-26** ("Energy Skate Park:
   Basics | Thermal Energy • Energy • Conservation of Energy |
   PhET Simulations"). Bar graphs and pie charts show kinetic,
   potential, and thermal energy as a skater moves; a friction
   control adds the thermal column. Free, no account; runs in the
   browser. The unit's core digital tool: observation alternative
   for the Sessions 8–9 lab and the Lesson 04/06 energy-account
   demonstrations.
2. **NSTA — MS-Energy topic document (NGSS official text)** —
   <http://static.nsta.org/ngss/20130509/topic-individual/MS-Energy.pdf>
   — the MS-PS3 performance expectations (MS-PS3-1, MS-PS3-2,
   MS-PS3-5) with assessment boundaries, used to re-check this
   unit's standards notes 2026-10-26. Adult background only; not
   assigned to the learner.
3. **Virtual Science Teachers — NGSS MS-PS3 page** —
   <https://virtualscienceteachers.org/ngss-ms-ps3/>
   — plain-language MS-PS3 summaries and simulation links, used to
   cross-check the MS-PS3-1/MS-PS3-2/MS-PS3-5 paraphrases in the
   unit README. Adult background; free, no account.
4. **LibreTexts — "Simple Machines" (Madera Community College,
   Concepts of Physical Science)** —
   <https://chem.libretexts.org/Courses/Madera_Community_College/Concepts_of_Physical_Science/05%3A_Work_and_Energy/5.07%3A_Simple_Machines>
   — the six simple machines, mechanical advantage formulas
   (lever $d_i/d_o$, pulley $n$, inclined plane $l/h$), and the
   conservation-of-energy basis for simple machines, cross-checked
   against Lesson 05. Level note: written above grade 8 in places,
   so the adult selects sections rather than assigning the page
   whole. Free, no account.
5. **Khan Academy — work and energy (via search link, Video #1
   above)** — the unit's candidate external explainer for Lessons
   01–02; unverified as a direct video, so it is listed only as a
   labeled search link and the adult previews before showing.
   Free, no account.
