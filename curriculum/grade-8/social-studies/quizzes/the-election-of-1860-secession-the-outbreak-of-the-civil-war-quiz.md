## 📜 Historical Context & Primary Source
> *"In your hands, my dissatisfied fellow-countrymen, and not in mine, is the momentous issue of civil war. The Government will not assail you. You can have no conflict without being yourselves the aggressors. You have no oath registered in heaven to destroy the Government, while I shall have the most solemn one to 'preserve, protect, and defend it.'"*  
> — **Abraham Lincoln, First Inaugural Address (March 4, 1861)**

### Questions

**1.** Name the four major candidates who ran for president in the pivotal **Election of 1860**, along with their respective political parties.

**2.** How was Abraham Lincoln able to win the presidency in the Electoral College despite winning only approximately 39.8% of the popular vote and not appearing on the ballot in ten Southern states?

**3.** What was the first Southern state to pass an ordinance of secession from the United States on December 20, 1860, and what reason did its declaration cite as the primary justification?

**4.** What was the official name of the new government established by the seceding Southern states in February 1861, and who was chosen as its provisional president?

**5.** In his famous "Cornerstone Speech" in March 1861, what did Confederate Vice President Alexander H. Stephens proclaim to be the "cornerstone" upon which the Confederacy rested?

**6.** Where and on what date were the first shots of the American Civil War fired when Confederate batteries opened fire on a federal garrison in Charleston Harbor?

**7.** What was President Lincoln’s immediate response following the surrender of Fort Sumter, and how did this action prompt four additional upper Southern states (Virginia, North Carolina, Tennessee, Arkansas) to secede?

**8.** Compare the strategic advantages of the **Union** (North) and the **Confederacy** (South) at the beginning of the war regarding population, manufacturing, and military leadership.

**9.** What was the **Anaconda Plan**, developed by Union General Winfield Scott, and what were its three main strategic components?

**10.** What were the four critical slave-holding **"Border States"** that chose to remain in the Union, and why was keeping them essential to Lincoln's war strategy?

<summary><strong>Parent Answer Key (Click to Expand)</strong></summary>

1. **Abraham Lincoln** (Republican), **Stephen A. Douglas** (Northern Democrat), **John C. Breckinridge** (Southern Democrat), and **John Bell** (Constitutional Union).
2. **The Democratic Party split geographically into Northern and Southern wings.** Lincoln swept the populous, high-electoral-vote Northern and Western free states, securing an outright majority in the Electoral College (180 out of 303 votes).
3. **South Carolina.** Its secession declaration explicitly cited Northern hostility to slavery, the non-enforcement of the Fugitive Slave Act, and the election of a president whose opinions were hostile to slavery.
4. **The Confederate States of America (CSA); Jefferson Davis** (of Mississippi).
5. **Chattel slavery and racial inequality.** Stephens stated: *"Our new government is founded upon exactly the opposite idea; its foundations are laid, its cornerstone rests, upon the great truth that the negro is not equal to the white man; that slavery subordination to the superior race is his natural and normal condition."*
6. **Fort Sumter, Charleston, South Carolina; April 12, 1861.**
7. **Lincoln issued a proclamation calling for 75,000 state militia volunteers to suppress the rebellion.** The upper South viewed this as federal coercion and invading sovereign states, prompting Virginia, NC, TN, and AR to secede.
8. **Union advantages:** Far larger population (22 million vs. 9 million, of whom 3.5 million were enslaved), 90% of U.S. manufacturing/factories, extensive railroad networks, established navy, and liquid capital. **Confederate advantages:** Fighting a defensive war on familiar home terrain, passionate defense of homeland, and superior early senior military commanders (e.g., Robert E. Lee, Stonewall Jackson).
9. **A long-term naval blockade and river squeeze strategy:** 1) Blockade Southern Atlantic and Gulf ports; 2) Seize control of the Mississippi River to split the Confederacy in half; 3) Squeeze and capture Richmond, Virginia.
10. **Maryland, Delaware, Kentucky, and Missouri.** If Maryland seceded, Washington, D.C., would be completely surrounded by Confederate territory; Kentucky and Missouri controlled vital Ohio and Mississippi river arteries and held significant manufacturing and white manpower.
