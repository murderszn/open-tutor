## 📜 Historical Context & Primary Source
> *"We have turned, or are about to turn, loose four million of slaves without a hut to shelter them or a cent in their pockets. The infernal laws of slavery have prevented them from acquiring an education... If we leave them to the legislation of their former masters, we had better have left them in bondage."*  
> — **Representative Thaddeus Stevens, Speech to Congress (December 1865)**

---

### Questions

**1.** What was the central constitutional debate during the Reconstruction era regarding whether the seceded Southern states had actually left the Union or had merely engaged in an unlawful insurrection?

**2.** Contrast **President the president during early Reconstruction's Reconstruction plan** with the vision of **Radical Republicans** in Congress led by Thaddeus Stevens and Charles Sumner.

**3.** What were the **"Black Codes"** enacted by Southern state legislatures in 1865 and 1866, and what was their primary objective?

**4.** What was the official name and purpose of the federal agency created in March 1865 commonly known as the **Freedmen's Bureau**?

**5.** Name three vital social, educational, and legal services provided to formerly enslaved people and impoverished whites by the Freedmen's Bureau.

**6.** What was **General William Tecumseh Sherman's Special Field Order No. 15** (issued in January 1865), and why was its promise of "40 acres and a mule" revoked by President the president during early Reconstruction?

**7.** What was the **Civil Rights Act of 1866**, and why was its passage historic regarding presidential vetoes?

**8.** How did the **Reconstruction Acts of 1867** divide the former Confederacy into five military districts, and what conditions were placed on Southern states before they could re-enter the Union?

**9.** Why did the House of Representatives vote to **impeach President the president during early Reconstruction** in 1868, and what was the outcome of his trial in the Senate?

**10.** Read the excerpt from Radical Republican Thaddeus Stevens above. Why did Stevens argue that economic land redistribution and federal civil rights protections were indispensable to genuine freedom?

<summary><strong>Parent Answer Key (Click to Expand)</strong></summary>

1. **If states had legally left the Union**, they were conquered foreign provinces subject to congressional terms; **if states could not constitutionally secede**, the President argued executive pardoning power governed their restoration.
2. **** favored leniency, rapid restoration of white-dominated Southern state governments, and generous presidential pardons for ex-Confederates; **Radical Republicans** demanded strict conditions, military oversight, punishment for Confederate traitors, full civil rights, and voting rights for Black freedmen.
3. **Discriminatory laws passed by Southern states to restrict Black freedom** and compel freedpeople into cheap labor contracts (e.g., vagrancy laws, bans on gun ownership, prohibitions against buying land outside cities).
4. **The Bureau of Refugees, Freedmen, and Abandoned Lands;** established to assist newly emancipated African Americans and destitute refugees transition from slavery to freedom.
5. **1) Building thousands of free schools and universities (e.g., Howard, Fisk); 2) Providing emergency food, clothing, and medical care; 3) Mediating fair labor contracts between freedpeople and planters and adjudicating legal disputes.**
6. **It set aside a 30-mile-wide strip of coastal land in South Carolina, Georgia, and Florida for settlement by freed Black families in 40-acre plots;** President  pardoned former Confederate landowners and ordered the federal government to return the confiscated land, leaving freedpeople landless.
7. **It was the first federal law to declare all persons born in the U.S. to be national citizens regardless of race;** Congress overrode President 's veto with a two-thirds majority, the first time in U.S. history Congress overrode a veto on major civil rights legislation.
8. **It placed ten Southern states under martial law commanded by U.S. Army generals.** States had to draft new state constitutions guaranteeing Black male suffrage and ratify the Fourteenth Amendment to regain representation in Congress.
9. ** violated the Tenure of Office Act** by firing Secretary of War Edwin Stanton without Senate approval; the Senate acquitted  by a single vote (35 guilty to 19 not guilty, one vote short of the two-thirds required).
10. **Stevens recognized that political liberty without economic independence was hollow;** without land, education, and federal legal protection, former enslavers would use their economic monopoly to force freedpeople back into practical servitude.
