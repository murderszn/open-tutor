## branches-checks-diagram.svg

- **File:** `branches-checks-diagram.svg` (SVG, deterministic — drawn from the constitutional text, not generated).
- **Learning purpose:** Lesson 4 reference diagram — the three branches, their core powers, and the main checks each holds over the others. Learners use it to trace a veto-override chain and an appointment chain in practice Task E.
- **Factual review (2026-10-27):** Powers and checks match Articles I–III of the Constitution: Congress makes laws and controls money; the president executes laws, commands the armed forces, and can veto; courts interpret laws. Checks shown: presidential veto and two-thirds override; Senate confirmation of appointments; House impeachment and Senate trial; congressional control of appropriations. Judicial review is deliberately **not** shown as a 1787 check — it was established later, in *Marbury v. Madison* (1803), and Lesson 4 says so explicitly.
