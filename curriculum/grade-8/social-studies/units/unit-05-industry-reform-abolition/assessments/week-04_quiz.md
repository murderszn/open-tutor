# Formative Quiz — Unit 05: Industry, Reform, Abolition, and Social Change

Given in three parts (Weeks 20, 21, 22, session S5). The adult scores with
[answer-key.md](../answer-key.md). This quiz is formative: it finds gaps, it
does not grade the learner.

## Part A — The market revolution and Lowell (Week 20 S5)

1. State the Erie Canal's length, completion date, and cost.
2. By about how much did canal freight from Buffalo to New York fall, in
   dollars per ton and as a percentage?
3. What was the first public telegraph message, when was it sent, and
   between which two cities?
4. Define supply and demand in your own words (one sentence each), then
   name one supply change and one demand change from Lesson 1.
5. Who worked in the Lowell mills, how many hours a week, and what were
   the wage and board ranges?
6. A mill girl earns $2.50 a week and pays $1.25 for board. How much is
   left after board? Show the subtraction.
7. What was a "turnout," and in which years did Lowell women walk out?
8. What did *Commonwealth v. Hunt* (1842) decide? (Two sentences.)

## Part B — Reform and Seneca Falls (Week 21 S5)

1. Name the four reform movements in Lesson 3 and give each one's main
   method in one phrase.
2. What did the Maine Law of 1851 ban that earlier laws had not?
3. What did Horace Mann build, and in what year did he become secretary
   of the Massachusetts Board of Education?
4. What is a memorial in Dorothea Dix's sense, and what did her 1843
   memorial ask the Massachusetts legislature to do? (Two sentences.)
5. State the opening of the Declaration of Sentiments as Stanton wrote it.
6. Why was the suffrage resolution the most contested at Seneca Falls?
   (Two sentences.)
7. How many people signed the Declaration of Sentiments, and how many of
   them were women?
8. True or false: the Seneca Falls Convention gave American women the
   right to vote. Explain in one sentence.

## Part C — Abolition and the Underground Railroad (Week 22 S5)

1. Define gradualism and immediatism in your own words (one sentence each).
2. Name the editor of *The Liberator* and the year of its first issue.
3. What was the House gag rule, and in which years was it in force?
4. Name two Black leaders in this unit and one thing each did.
5. What did Douglass's *North Star* argue about who should lead the fight
   against slavery? (Two sentences.)
6. How many trips did Harriet Tubman make, about how many people did she
   lead to freedom, and what did she say about losing passengers?
7. How did the Fugitive Slave Act of 1850 change Tubman's work?
   (Two sentences.)
8. Why does this unit refuse to quote Sojourner Truth's "Ain't I a Woman?"
   as her exact words? (Two sentences.)
