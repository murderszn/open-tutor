## Task A — The bank debate, sorted (Lesson 1; Week 15 S2)

Sort each argument to Hamilton (loose construction) or Jefferson (strict
construction) and give a one-line reason:

1. "The Constitution does not list a bank, so Congress may not charter one."
2. "A bank is a proper means of carrying out the powers to borrow, coin, and
   tax."
3. "'Necessary' means indispensable, not merely convenient."
4. "Every power includes the right to use the means fairly suited to it."
5. "If Congress may charter a bank, it may charter anything, and listed
   powers mean nothing."
6. "The Constitution forbids no bank; the means are not precluded, so they
   are permitted."

Then answer: Washington sided with which side, and what happened to the bank
bill? (One sentence.)
