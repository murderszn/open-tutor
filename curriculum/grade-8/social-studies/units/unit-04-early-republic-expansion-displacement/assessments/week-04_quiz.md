# Formative Quiz — Unit 04: Early Republic, Expansion, and Displacement

Given in three parts (Weeks 15, 16, 17, session S5). The adult scores with
[answer-key.md](../answer-key.md). This quiz is formative: it finds gaps, it
does not grade the learner.

## Part A — Two visions and first parties (Week 15 S5)

1. State Hamilton's position on the national bank in one sentence, and
   Jefferson's in one sentence.
2. Define strict construction and loose construction in your own words (one
   sentence each).
3. Name two Washington precedents and explain why each mattered.
4. How did the Federalists and Democratic-Republicans differ on the strength
   of the national government? (Two sentences.)
5. Why is the election of 1800 called a peaceful transfer of power, even
   though the Electoral College tied? (Two sentences.)
6. True or false: the Democratic-Republicans of 1800 are the same party as
   today's Democratic Party. Explain in one sentence.

## Part B — The Louisiana Purchase (Week 16 S5)

1. State the Louisiana Purchase's price, size in square miles, signing date,
   and Senate ratification vote.
2. Show the per-acre arithmetic: $15,000,000 ÷ (828,000 × 640). Give the
   answer in cents per acre and explain the National Archives' "roughly 4
   cents an acre" in one sentence.
3. What was Jefferson's constitutional dilemma about the purchase? (Two
   sentences.)
4. Sequence these admissions from `us_states.csv`: Ohio, Louisiana,
   Missouri, Kentucky, Maine. Mark the two from the purchase itself.
5. What did the Missouri Compromise (1820) decide, and what did it postpone?
   (Two sentences.)
6. In one sentence, why was New Orleans worth more than its acreage?

## Part C — War and doctrine (Week 17 S5)

1. Name three causes of the War of 1812.
2. What did the Treaty of Ghent settle, and what did it leave unsettled?
   (Two sentences.)
3. Why is the Battle of New Orleans (January 8, 1815) a strange "victory"?
   (Two sentences.)
4. State the Monroe Doctrine's two main claims about Europe and the Americas
   in your own words.
5. In 1823, who actually enforced the Monroe Doctrine's claims in practice,
   and why? (Two sentences.)
6. Name one Native interest in the War of 1812 that was distinct from British
   and American interests. (One sentence.)
