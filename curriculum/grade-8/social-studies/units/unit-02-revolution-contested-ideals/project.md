# Project — Revolution Evidence Portfolio

**Unit 02 · Weeks 7–10 · presented Week 10 S4–S5**

Build a four-panel portfolio that answers the compelling question: **Whose liberty did the American Revolution secure — and how do we know?** Each panel is built in its week and revised in Week 10.

## Materials

- Large paper or a digital slide deck (4 panels), timeline strip, outline map, note cards
- Unit excerpts (Declaration, Wheatley, Dunmore), engraving notes, reconstruction image
- Notebook for drafts

## Steps

### §1 — Timeline panel: change and continuity, 1763–1783 (Week 7)

1. Place at least **10 events** from 1763–1783 on a dated timeline (use Lessons 1–3; include at least one event per year-cluster: 1763–66, 1767–70, 1773–74, 1775–76, 1777–78, 1781–83).
2. Code each event **change**, **continuity**, or **both**, with a one-sentence reason (Lesson 1's method).
3. Write a 2–3 sentence caption: what does the timeline show about *when* independence became likely?

### §2 — Map panel: turning points and alliance (Week 8)

1. On an outline map, mark **Lexington/Concord, Saratoga, Yorktown**, and the Atlantic route of French aid.
2. Annotate each mark with one cause and one effect (Lesson 3's chain).
3. Write a 2–3 sentence caption: why did geography and the French alliance matter more than any single battle?

### §3 — Perspectives panel: whose liberty? (Week 9)

1. Create five columns: **Patriot, Loyalist, Enslaved person, Woman, Native nation**.
2. Place at least **8 evidence slips** across the columns (a quotation or fact per slip, each with a cited source). Every column needs at least one slip.
3. For two slips, add a "maker's purpose" note: how did that person's position shape what their source shows? (D2.His.6)
4. Write a 2–3 sentence caption: which group's liberty changed most, and which changed least?

### §4 — Argument panel: the turning-point verdict (Week 10)

1. Draft the Practice Task H essay (4–6 paragraphs) on the panel or as an attached page.
2. Attach a **limit note** listing two things your evidence cannot establish (e.g., "my sources show leaders' arguments, not what ordinary farmers believed"; "the engraving proves persuasion, not the event").
3. Present the portfolio in 3–4 minutes: one finding per panel, one limit acknowledged, one question you would still investigate.

## Deliverables

1. Four completed panels (timeline, map, perspectives, argument) with captions.
2. At least 8 cited evidence slips on the perspectives panel.
3. The Task H essay with an explicit limit note.
4. A 3–4 minute presentation covering all panels.

## Rubric

| Criterion | 4 — Strong | 3 — Meets | 2 — Developing | 1 — Beginning |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Timeline: accuracy & coding | 10+ events dated correctly; every code defended with a reason; caption answers *when* | 10 events dated; codes present with mostly sound reasons | 7–9 events or several misdated; codes without reasons | Fewer than 7 events; no coding |
| Map: causal annotation | 3 turning points + French route, each with accurate cause *and* effect; caption explains geography/alliance | All marks present; most annotations accurate | Marks present; annotations vague or one-sided | Missing marks or no annotations |
| Perspectives: evidence & purpose | 8+ slips, all five columns, every slip cited; 2+ purpose notes show how position shaped the source | 8 slips across five columns, cited; 1–2 purpose notes | Slips missing columns or citations; purpose notes absent | Few slips; no citations |
| Argument: claim, counterclaim, limits | Qualified thesis; genuine counterclaim with evidence; 3+ sources (1 primary); explicit limit note with two specific limits | Thesis + counterclaim + 3 sources; limit note present | Thesis without qualifier or strawman counterclaim; sources thin | No argument structure |
| Presentation | Covers all four panels, states one limit aloud, answers one evidence question from the portfolio | Covers all panels; names a limit | Skips a panel or reads without explanation | No presentation |

**Scoring:** 16–20 = strong portfolio; 12–15 = meets; 8–11 = revise one panel with the adult; below 8 = rebuild with guidance. See [answer-key.md](answer-key.md) for sample "meets" responses and common deductions.
