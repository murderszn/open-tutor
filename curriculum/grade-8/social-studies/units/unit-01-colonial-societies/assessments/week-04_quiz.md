# Formative Quiz — Unit 01 (given in three parts)

**Grade 8 Social Studies · Unit 01: Colonial Societies**
Give Part A at the end of Week 3 (S5), Part B at the end of Week 4 (S5), Part C at the end of Week 5 (S5). Learners may use their maps and charts. The adult scores with [answer-key.md](../answer-key.md) and records gaps for re-teaching.

## Part A — The Atlantic world and the exchange (Week 3)

1. Label on the provided Atlantic outline: Europe, Africa, North America, South America, the Atlantic Ocean. (5 points — 1 per label)
2. Draw and label the trade winds and the westerlies with their directions. (2 points)
3. Explain in 2–3 sentences why ships sailing from Europe to the Americas first sailed south along Africa instead of heading straight west. (3 points)
4. Complete the table with **two items per direction** and **one effect per direction**:

   | Direction | Two items | One effect |
   |---|---|---|
   | Americas → Europe/Africa | | |
   | Europe/Africa → Americas | | |

   (6 points — 1 per item, 1 per effect)
5. **Reasoning:** "The Columbian exchange gave everyone new foods, so it was mostly good." In 2–3 sentences, explain what this claim leaves out. (3 points)

**Part A total: 19 points**

## Part B — Colonial regions and labor (Week 4)

1. Match each description to its region (New England / Middle / Southern). (6 points — 1 each)
   a. "Breadbasket" colonies; wheat and grain exports; mixed labor including indentured servants.
   b. Rocky soil and short growing season; fishing, shipbuilding, timber; mostly family and wage labor.
   c. Long growing season; tobacco, rice, and indigo plantations; enslaved labor dominant.
   d. A German immigrant works off his passage over several years.
   e. A planter's ledger lists dozens of people as property.
   f. A coastal town builds ships and salts cod for trade.
2. Explain the environment → crop → labor chain for **one** region in 3–4 sentences. (4 points)
3. **Error analysis:** A classmate says, "The Southern colonies used enslaved labor because the hot climate made it impossible for Europeans to work the fields." Identify the factual error and the reasoning error. (4 points)

**Part B total: 14 points**

## Part C — Enslavement as a system; resistance (Week 5)

1. Name the **four requirements** of the plantation system and give **one example** of each. (8 points — 1 per requirement, 1 per example)
2. The Voyages database reports about 12.5 million Africans embarked and about 10.7 million landed between 1501 and 1866. What does the difference (~1.8 million) represent? Show the subtraction. (3 points)
3. Place each on the resistance spectrum (everyday / escape / revolt / protest): the Stono Rebellion; a work slowdown; the 1688 Germantown petition; a runaway advertisement. (4 points)
4. **Source reasoning:** Why do historians treat the 1740 South Carolina laws banning gatherings and literacy as *evidence of resistance*? Answer in 2–3 sentences. (3 points)

**Part C total: 18 points**

## Whole-quiz total: 51 points

**Readiness guide:** 45+ = secure; 36–44 = minor gaps (re-teach flagged items in S5 reviews); below 36 = re-teach the weakest part before the unit assessment. See [teacher-guide.md](../teacher-guide.md) for reteaching moves.
