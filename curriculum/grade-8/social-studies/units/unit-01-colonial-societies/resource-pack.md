# Resource Pack — Unit 01: Colonial Societies, Atlantic Exchange, Labor, Resistance

**Goal (one sentence):** Give grade-8 learners reputable, free, no-account sources that support mapping the Atlantic world, comparing colonial labor systems, analyzing enslavement and resistance, and arguing from primary sources — all checked 2026-10-13.

**Age fit:** Grade 8 (ages 13–14). Videos are supplements to the lessons, not replacements; primary-source excerpts in the lessons are adult-selected and brief.

## Web references (checked 2026-10-13)

1. **Voyages: The Trans-Atlantic Slave Trade Database** (slavevoyages.org) — the scholarly database behind the 12.5-million-embarked figure; searchable voyage data, maps, and teaching resources. Free, no account. Relevance: Lessons 4 and 6; the project Map panel. (Figure verified via Emory University and Smithsonian Magazine reporting, 2026-10-13.)
2. **National Museum of African American History and Culture — "The Journey to Emancipation: the Germantown Protest, 1688"** (nmaahc.si.edu) — Smithsonian article on the 1688 petition with the Golden-Rule quotation verified for this unit. Free, no account. Relevance: Lesson 5; Task I. https://nmaahc.si.edu/explore/stories/journey-emancipation-germantown-protest-1688
3. **Library of Congress — Classroom Materials / Colonial America** (loc.gov) — primary-source sets and lesson resources on colonization. Free, no account. Relevance: Lessons 1–3 map and region work; general primary-source browsing. (Part of the track's verified starting shelf.)
4. **Bill of Rights Institute — Germantown Friends Protest Against Slavery, 1688** (PDF, source: loc.gov) — classroom-ready primary source with comprehension questions. Free, no account. Relevance: Lesson 5 close read; Task I source comparison. https://bri-docs.s3.amazonaws.com/Germantown+Friends+Protest+Against+Slavery,+1688.pdf
5. **Brycchan Carey / "The Middle Passage — Olaudah Equiano"** (brycchancarey.com) — public-domain excerpt of Equiano's Chapter II with context notes. Free, no account. Relevance: Lesson 4 close read. (Excerpt wording verified against this and other public-domain editions, 2026-10-13.) https://brycchancarey.com/equiano/extract3.htm
6. **National Archives — Founding-era documents** (archives.gov) — public-domain founding documents; useful for seeing where this unit's period leads (Unit 02+). Free, no account. Relevance: forward link to the Revolution unit.
7. **Historical Society of Montgomery County — 1688 Petition transcription** (historicasmh.org, PDF) — full transcription of the Germantown petition used to verify this unit's quotation. Free, no account. Relevance: Lesson 5; teacher verification. https://www.historicasmh.org/s/ASMH-1688-Petition-Transcription.pdf
