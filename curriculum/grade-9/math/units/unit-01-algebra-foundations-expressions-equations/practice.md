# Practice Sets A–E

Strategy-based practice for Unit 01. Each set targets one strategy from the
lessons. Solve every numbered item; the answer key shows full solutions in
[answer-key.md](answer-key.md#practice).

---

## Practice A — Expression structure and equivalence (Session 3)

Strategy: *name the parts before you touch the symbols; verify rewrites by
substitution.*

1. In `R = 60 − 1.5t` (a candle's remaining height in cm after `t` hours),
   name the terms, the coefficient of `t`, and the constant term. What does
   each mean for the candle?
2. Evaluate `R` for `t = 8`. Write one sentence interpreting the result.
3. In `V = 3(2x + 7) − x`, identify the terms and the chunk you would view as
   one entity.
4. Simplify: (a) `4(3m − 2) + 5m`, (b) `−3(2 − y) + 7y`, (c) `2a + 5(a + 1) − 3a`.
5. Verify (4a) with `m = 2` by substituting into both the original and your
   simplified form.
6. Factor completely: (a) `15x + 10`, (b) `8t − 24`.
7. **Error analysis:** "Dez wrote `2(x − 3) = 2x − 3`." Explain the error with
   a tile-style counting argument and write the correct equivalence.
8. A rectangle's length is `x + 5` and width is `x`. Write two equivalent
   expressions for its perimeter and explain which form shows the structure
   better.
9. **Explain your reasoning:** Is `6n + 4` equivalent to `2(3n + 4)`?
   Justify with algebra and one substitution check.
10. In `(x − 2)(x − 2)`, name the repeated single entity and describe in words
    what the expression represents without expanding.

## Practice B — Solving with justifications (Session 5)

Strategy: *write the reason with the step; check by substitution.*

1. Solve `9x + 4 = 5x + 20` with one justification per step. Check.
2. Solve `4(t − 3) + 2 = 3t + 1` with justifications. Check.
3. Solve `(3/4)x + 5 = 17 − (1/4)x`. Show the denominator-clearing step and
   justify it. Check.
4. Solve `−6(2 − n) = 4n − 8` with justifications. Check.
5. **Error analysis:** "Priya solved `5x + 10 = 30` as `5x = 30 + 10`,
   `5x = 40`, `x = 8`." Name the illegal move, explain the correct inverse
   operation, and solve correctly.
6. Create a three-step equation whose solution is `x = 4`. Trade with a
   partner (or solve your own backwards) with full justifications.
7. A bike shop charges $30 plus $15 per hour of labor: `C = 30 + 15h`. A bill
   is $120. Write and solve for `h` with justifications; state the answer in
   a sentence.
8. **Explain your reasoning:** Why is it legal to divide both sides of
   `3x = 12` by 3, but not legal to divide only the left side? Use the word
   *equality* in your answer.

## Practice C — Solution types and letter coefficients (Session 7)

Strategy: *simplify first, then read the ending; letters obey the same rules
as numbers.*

Classify each as one solution, infinitely many, or no solution; solve when
there is a solution, and check it.

1. `5(x + 2) = 5x + 10`
2. `7n − 4 = 7n + 3`
3. `3(2x − 1) = 6x − 3`
4. `−2(y + 4) = −2y − 7`
5. `10 − 3x = 4x + 24`
6. Solve for the indicated letter: (a) `d = rt` for `t` (`r ≠ 0`);
   (b) `I = Prt` for `P` (assume `r, t ≠ 0`); (c) `px + 5 = 17` for `x`.
7. **Explain your reasoning:** Give one equation whose solution is exactly
   `x = 0` and one equation with no solution. Explain how you can tell them
   apart without solving.
8. For what value of `c` is `4(x + c) = 4x + 20` an identity? Show your
   reasoning.

## Practice D — Inequalities in context (Session 9)

Strategy: *mirror, don't memorize — flip only when multiplying/dividing by a
negative; always test boundary, inside, outside.*

1. Solve and graph: `2x + 5 < 3x − 4`.
2. Solve and graph: `−5x + 2 ≥ 27`.
3. Solve and graph: `−4 < 5 − 2x ≤ 9`. (Hint: subtract 5 everywhere first.)
4. For #2, show the full test trio with substitutions.
5. A summer job pays $12/hour. You need at least $800 for a laptop and have
   already saved $200. Write and solve an inequality for the hours needed;
   interpret the answer as a whole number of hours.
6. **Error analysis:** "Sam solved `−3x > 12` as `x > −4`." Explain the
   mistake using the number-line mirror idea and give the correct solution
   set with a graph description.
7. Write a compound inequality for "all numbers between −3 and 5, including
   −3 but not 5," and describe its graph.
8. **Explain your reasoning:** Is `x = −4` a solution to `−3x > 12`? Is
   `x = −5`? What does this tell you about which direction the solution set
   goes?

## Practice E — Creating equations and rearranging formulas (Session 12)

Strategy: *four-step modeling habit; undo operations in reverse when
rearranging.*

1. A streaming bundle costs $15/month plus $6 per premium channel. The bill
   is $45. How many premium channels? (Four steps; check.)
2. A recipe's cost is `C = 2.5n + 8` dollars for `n` servings. With $58, how
   many servings? Interpret with sensible accuracy.
3. A charity needs to raise at least $2,000. Tickets are $25 each and $400 is
   already donated. What is the smallest whole number of tickets to sell?
   Explain the rounding.
4. Rearrange for the indicated quantity: (a) `A = πr²` — solve for nothing
   new; instead explain why this formula *cannot* be rearranged with
   Unit-01 tools alone (what operation blocks you?); (b) `F = (9/5)C + 32`
   for `C`; (c) `ax − b = c` for `x` (`a ≠ 0`).
5. **Error analysis:** "Jo rewrote `S = vt + d` as `t = S − v − d`." Explain
   the error with the "undo" idea and give the correct rearrangement.
6. The triangle area formula `A = (1/2)bh` was solved for `b` in Lesson 6.
   Now solve it for `h`, and use your result to find `h` when `A = 60` in²
   and `b = 15` in. Check with units.
7. **Explain your reasoning:** When you rearranged `P = 2l + 2w` for `w` in
   Lesson 4, you got `w = (P − 2l)/2`. Show two different legal first steps
   that both lead to equivalent answers, and verify the answers are
   equivalent by substituting `P = 30`, `l = 9`.
