# Formative Quiz — Unit 01 (Session 15)

**Grade 9 Math · Unit 01: Algebra Foundations** · ~25 minutes · Calculator
allowed for checking only. This quiz diagnoses gaps before the review session;
it is not the unit grade.

**Objective key:** O1 = expression structure · O2 = equivalent expressions ·
O3 = solving & solution types · O4 = modeling · O5 = formulas & accuracy

---

1. (O1) A parking garage charges $6 plus $2.50 per hour: `C = 6 + 2.5h`.
   Name the terms, the coefficient of `h`, and the constant term, and explain
   what the constant term means for a driver. (4 pts)

2. (O2) Rewrite `8(x − 3) + 2x` in simplest form. Verify your answer by
   substituting `x = 5` into both forms. (4 pts)

3. (O3) Solve `5(x + 2) − 3 = 2x + 14`. Write a justification for each step,
   then check your solution. (5 pts)

4. (O3) Classify each equation — one solution, infinitely many, or no
   solution. Solve when a solution exists.
   (a) `4x − 7 = 4x + 2` (b) `3(2x + 1) = 6x + 3` (4 pts)

5. (O3) Solve `−3x + 8 > 20`. Describe the graph of the solution set
   (boundary point, open or filled circle, direction). (4 pts)

6. (O3) Solve `−1 ≤ 2x + 5 < 13`. Write the solution set and describe its
   graph. (4 pts)

7. (O4) A tutor charges $20 plus $35 per session: `C = 20 + 35s`. A family
   pays $195. (a) Write the equation, (b) solve it showing steps, (c) answer
   in a sentence. (5 pts)

8. (O5) The perimeter formula is `P = 2l + 2w`. Solve for `l`. Then find `l`
   when `P = 44` cm and `w = 7` cm, and check your answer in the original
   formula. (5 pts)

9. (O2/O3) **Error analysis.** "Kai solved `−2(x + 4) = 10` as
   `−2x + 4 = 10`, then `−2x = 6`, then `x = −3`." There are two errors.
   Find both, explain each, and solve correctly. (5 pts)

10. (O3) **Explain your reasoning.** When solving `−4x < 16`, why must the
    inequality sign change? Use the number-line mirror idea in your answer,
    and give the correct solution set. (4 pts)

**Total: 44 pts.** Scoring guide: 38+ = on track; 30–37 = review flagged
objectives in Session 17; below 30 = targeted reteach before the unit
assessment. Solutions: [answer-key.md](../answer-key.md#formative-quiz).
