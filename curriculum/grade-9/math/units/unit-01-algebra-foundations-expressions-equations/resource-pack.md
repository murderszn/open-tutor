# Resource Pack — Unit 01: Algebra Foundations

**Goal (one sentence):** Give a grade-9 learner free, no-account routes to
see expression structure, equation solving, inequalities, and formula
rearrangement explained a second way, with practice beyond this unit's pages.
**Age fit:** 14–15; assumes the grade-8 prerequisite fluency listed in the
unit README.

## Web references (verified 2026-10-12 via web search; free, no account)

1. **OpenStax — Elementary Algebra 2e, §2.4 "Use a General Strategy to Solve
   Linear Equations."** Step-by-step strategy (simplify, collect variable
   terms, collect constants, isolate, check) with worked examples and "Try
   It" practice. Directly supports Lessons 3–4.
   <https://openstax.org/books/elementary-algebra-2e/pages/2-4-use-a-general-strategy-to-solve-linear-equations>
2. **OpenStax — Elementary Algebra 2e, §2.5 "Solve Equations with Fractions
   or Decimals."** LCD method for clearing fractions — the strategy taught in
   Lesson 3, Worked example 2.
   <https://openstax.org/books/elementary-algebra-2e/pages/2-5-solve-equations-with-fractions-or-decimals>
3. **OpenStax — Elementary Algebra 2e, Chapter 2 Key Concepts.** One-page
   summary of equation strategies, formula-solving, and inequality
   properties — good retrieval-review sheet for Session 14.
   <https://openstax.org/books/elementary-algebra-2e/pages/2-key-concepts>
4. **OpenStax — Elementary Algebra 2e (full free textbook).** Chapters 1–3
   cover all five unit objectives; free web view and PDF, no account.
   <https://openstax.org/books/elementary-algebra-2e/pages/preface>
