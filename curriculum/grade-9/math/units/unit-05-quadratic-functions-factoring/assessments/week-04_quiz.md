# Week 04 Quiz — Quadratic Functions and Factoring

**Session 16 · Formative · 20 points (2 points each) · About 25 minutes**

Show your work. A factorization without a check earns 1 point at most.
Do not use the answer key — the adult scores this the same day and
writes the reteach list from it.

1. Factor `5x² − 20x` completely.
2. Factor `x² + 9x + 14`.
3. Factor `4x² − 9`.
4. Factor `x² − 12x + 36`.
5. Factor `2x² + 5x − 12` by the ac method, showing the
   split-middle-term step.
6. Write `f(x) = x² + 10x + 24` in vertex form and name the vertex.
7. For `f(x) = x² − 6x + 5`, state the zeros and the y-intercept.
8. Describe the graph of `g(x) = (x − 3)² + 2` as a transformation of
   `f(x) = x²`, and name its vertex.
9. A ball's height is `h(t) = −16t² + 32t + 6` feet after `t`
   seconds. What is its maximum height, and when does it happen?
10. Explain in two or three sentences why `x² + 7x + 12` and
    `(x + 3)(x + 4)` are the same function written in two forms, and
    say which form shows the zeros directly.

## Scoring guide (adult)

- 18–20: ready for the unit assessment; use Session 17 for the
  extension task.
- 14–17: reteach the lowest-scoring objective in Session 17
  (factoring items 1–5 map to Objective 1; item 6 to Objective 3;
  items 7–8 to Objectives 2 and 4; item 9 to Objective 5; item 10 to
  Objectives 1–2).
- Below 14: reteach factoring (Lessons 01–02) and vertex form
  (Lesson 04) in Session 17 before the assessment; allow the learner
  to redo two missed items for partial credit.
