# Resource Pack — Unit 05: Quadratic Functions and Factoring

**Goal (one sentence):** Give a grade-9 learner free, no-account routes
to see quadratic factoring, completing the square, parabola
transformations, and key-feature analysis explained a second way, with
practice beyond this unit's pages.
**Age fit:** 14–15; assumes the grade-8 prerequisite fluency and the
Unit 01–02 solving and graphing skills and Unit 04 polynomial
multiplication listed in the unit README.

## Focused queries

1. `factor quadratic trinomial x squared plus bx plus c explained`
2. `difference of squares perfect square trinomial factoring pattern`
3. `completing the square vertex form quadratic function`
4. `graphing quadratic functions transformations shifts stretches`
5. `quadratic function vertex axis symmetry maximum minimum word problems`
6. `compare quadratic functions different representations table graph equation`

## Web references (verified 2026-11-08 by direct page check; free, no account)

1. **OpenStax — Intermediate Algebra 2e, §6.2 "Factor Trinomials."**
   Factoring `x² + bx + c` and `ax² + bx + c` by trial and error and
   the ac method, with worked examples and a prime-trinomial
   discussion; directly supports Lessons 1–2 and Practices A–B.
   <https://openstax.org/books/intermediate-algebra-2e/pages/6-2-factor-trinomials>
2. **OpenStax — Intermediate Algebra 2e, §6.3 "Factor Special
   Products."** Perfect-square trinomials, differences of squares,
   and sums/differences of cubes, with the three-part pattern checks;
   directly supports Lesson 3 and Practice C.
   <https://openstax.org/books/intermediate-algebra-2e/pages/6-3-factor-special-products>
3. **OpenStax — Intermediate Algebra 2e, §9.2 "Solve Quadratic
   Equations by Completing the Square."** Completing the square of a
   binomial expression step by step, including the `a ≠ 1` case;
   directly supports Lesson 4 and Practice D (the unit uses the
   method for vertex form, not for solving equations — that is
   Unit 06).
   <https://openstax.org/books/intermediate-algebra-2e/pages/9-2-solve-quadratic-equations-by-completing-the-square>
4. **OpenStax — College Algebra 2e, §5.1 "Quadratic Functions."**
   Parabola characteristics, vertex form, domain and range, and
   maximum/minimum applications including the garden-fencing problem;
   directly supports Lessons 4–6, the project, and Practice F.
   <https://openstax.org/books/college-algebra-2e/pages/5-1-quadratic-functions>
5. **OpenStax — College Algebra 2e, §3.5 "Transformation of
   Functions."** Vertical and horizontal shifts, reflections,
   stretches and compressions, including the ball-thrown-from-a-
   building example; directly supports Lesson 5 and Practice E.
   <https://openstax.org/books/college-algebra-2e/pages/3-5-transformation-of-functions>
