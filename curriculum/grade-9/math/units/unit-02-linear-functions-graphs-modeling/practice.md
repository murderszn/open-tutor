# Practice Sets A–E

Strategy-based practice for Unit 02. Each set targets one strategy from the
lessons. Solve every numbered item; the answer key shows full solutions in

## Practice A — Function concept, notation, domain/range (Session 3)

Strategy: *one output per input; read `f(x)` as "f of x"; the story sets the
domain.*

1. Is `R = {(2, 5), (3, 7), (4, 7), (5, 2)}` a function? Give its domain and
   range.
2. Is `S = {(−1, 0), (0, 1), (−1, 4)}` a function? Explain in one sentence.
3. A table gives `x`: 1, 2, 3, 4 and `y`: 3, 3, 5, 7. Does it define `y` as a
   function of `x`? Find `f(2)` and `f(4)`.
4. For `f(x) = 4x − 7`, find `f(3)`, `f(−2)`, and `f(0)`.
5. A hiking club trip costs `H(a) = 48 + 12a` dollars for `a` adults ($48 bus
   rental + $12 per adult). Interpret `H(10) = 168` in a sentence. For what
   `a` is `H(a) = 252`?
6. A pump fills `B(t) = 12t` liters, `t` minutes after it starts, for
   `0 ≤ t ≤ 6`. Give the domain and range with units.
7. **Error analysis:** "Kai says `f(x)` means f times x, so `f(3)` is f
   times 3." Explain the mistake and state the correct reading.
8. Is "zip code → city" a function? Is "city → zip code" a function?
   Explain each.
9. A line segment runs from `(0, 3)` to `(4, 11)`. Write the domain and range
   as inequalities and find `f(2)`.
10. **Explain your reasoning:** Can a function have domain "all real numbers"
    but a range that is not all real numbers? Give an example or explain why
    it is impossible.

## Practice B — Graphing and representations (Session 5)

Strategy: *table first, then plot; label axes and scales; verify with
intercepts.*

1. Graph `y = −x + 4` by hand: make a three-row table, plot the points,
   label the axes. State the intercepts.
2. From the Lesson 03 SVG: verify by substitution that `(−0.5, 0)` is the
   x-intercept of `y = 2x + 1`.
3. From the SVG: the red line passes through `(0, 4)` and `(4, 0)`. Compute
   its slope from these two points and confirm it matches `y = −x + 4`.
4. **Error analysis:** "Rin graphed `y = 2x + 1` using the points (0, 0),
   (1, 2), (2, 4) and drew the line." What did Rin forget, and how would an
   intercept check have caught it?
5. Graph `y = (1/2)x − 3` by hand with labeled axes. Give the x- and
   y-intercepts and verify each by substitution.
6. A kayak rental costs `C(h) = 8h + 5` dollars for `h` hours, `0 ≤ h ≤ 6`.
   Make a table for `h = 0, 2, 4, 6`, sketch the graph (axes labeled "hours"
   and "dollars"), and read both intercepts in context. Should the line have
   arrows past `h = 6`? Why not?
7. Table P: `x = −1, 0, 1, 2` → `y = 5, 8, 11, 14`. Table Q:
   `x = 0, 1, 2, 3` → `y = 14, 17, 20, 23`. Write each function's equation
   and explain whether they are the same function.
8. **Explain your reasoning:** The graphs of `y = 3x − 2` and `y = −x + 6`
   intersect at one point. Find it algebraically, then explain what the
   intersection tells you about the equation `3x − 2 = −x + 6`.

## Practice C — Average rate of change (Session 7)

Strategy: *later minus earlier, on top and bottom; keep the units; a constant
rate is the linear signature.*

1. A sensor records height `h` meters at time `t` seconds: `t`: 0, 2, 4, 6 →
   `h`: 3, 11, 19, 27. Find the average rate of change over `[0, 6]` and over
   `[2, 4]`, with units. What does the comparison tell you?
2. For `f(x) = −3x + 10`, find the average rate of change over `[1, 5]` and
   over `[0, 2]`. What do you notice?
3. A pond drains as `V(t) = 800 − 40t` liters, `t` in minutes. Find the
   average rate of change over `[0, 20]` and interpret the sign and units in
   a sentence.
4. A line passes through `(1, 2)` and `(5, 14)`. Find the average rate of
   change between these points. If the points are `(hours worked, dollars
   earned)`, what are the units and meaning?
5. **Explain your reasoning:** Why is the average rate of change of a linear
   function the same on every interval? (Use the word *slope*.)
6. **Error analysis:** "Priya computed the average rate of `f` over [2, 6]
   as `f(6)/6 − f(2)/2`." Explain what the formula actually requires, then
   compute the correct rate for `f(x) = 5x + 1` over `[2, 6]`.
7. A van's odometer reads `M(t) = 55t + 12000` miles, `t` hours into a trip.
   Find the average rate of change over `[0, 3]`. What do the units of your
   answer tell you?
8. Data: `x`: 0, 1, 2, 3 → `y`: 10, 16, 20, 22. Compute the rate on each
   unit interval. On which interval was the rate fastest? Is the function
   linear? Justify.

## Practice D — Building linear models (Session 10)

Strategy: *quantities and units first; slope from the data; intercept by
substitution; verify against both pairs.*

1. A copy shop charges $6 for 20 copies and $22 for 100 copies. Define the
   quantities with units, build `C(n)`, constrain the domain, and predict
   the cost of 60 copies. Verify your model against both given pairs.
2. A line passes through `(0, 25)` and `(5, 65)`. Write its equation in
   `y = mx + b` form and give a one-sentence story it could model.
3. From a description: "A gym charges a $40 signup fee plus $15 per month."
   Define the quantities, write `G(m)`, constrain the domain, and find the
   cost of 12 months.
4. Table: `x`: 0, 2, 4, 6 → `y`: 11, 17, 23, 29. Show the rate is constant,
   then write the model.
5. A dog-walking business charges $12 per walk plus a $5 weekly travel fee.
   Write `W(n)` with quantities, units, and domain. What does `W(4)` mean,
   and what is its value?
6. **Error analysis:** "Jo built a model from (2, 10) and (5, 25) and got
   slope `(5 − 2)/(25 − 10) = 1/5`." Name the mistake and give the correct
   slope and model.
7. A line passes through `(2, 9)` and `(6, 21)`. Write its equation.
8. **Explain your reasoning:** After building a model from two pairs, why
   must you check that it reproduces *both* pairs — not just one?

## Practice E — Interpreting models and limits (Session 12)

Strategy: *slope = "each additional…"; intercept = "when the input is
zero…"; name what the model leaves out.*

1. A caterer charges `C(g) = 45g + 300` dollars for `g` guests. Interpret
   the slope and intercept in sentences with units, and compute `C(50)`.
2. A walking model gives a rate of 1.2 meters per second. Convert to meters
   per minute and say which unit fits a "30-minute walk" story better, and
   why.
3. Table P: `x`: 0, 1, 2, 3, 4 → `y`: 100, 130, 160, 190, 220. Table Q:
   `x`: 0, 1, 2, 3 → `y`: 100, 120, 145, 175. Which table could show a
   linear function? Justify with differences.
4. For the van model `C(m) = 0.62m + 85` (Lesson 06): name two real factors
   the model ignores, and explain why using it for a 2000-mile week would be
   extrapolation.
5. **Error analysis:** "Sam says the intercept of `C(t) = 200 − 15t` is
   (0, 200) and it means the cost grows by $200 per day." Identify both
   mistakes and give the correct interpretation.
6. A newspaper reports quarterly sales of 2000, 2100, 2250, 2450 copies and
   calls the growth "linear." Check the differences and write a two-sentence
   correction or confirmation with evidence.
7. Project prep: for the Fare Audit's ZipGo plan `Z(m) = 1.8m + 4.5`, write
   one sentence interpreting the slope and one interpreting the intercept.
