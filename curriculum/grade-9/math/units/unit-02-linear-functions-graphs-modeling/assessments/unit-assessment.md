# Unit Assessment — Unit 02: Linear Functions (Session 18)

**Grade 9 Math** · ~50 minutes · Calculator allowed for checking only.
Show all work. Graphs need labeled axes.

**Objective key:** O1 = function concept & notation (F-IF.1, F-IF.2) ·
O2 = domain & range (F-IF.5) · O3 = graphs & representations (F-IF.4, F-IF.7,
A-CED.2, A-REI.10–11) · O4 = average rate of change (F-IF.6, N-Q.1) ·
O5 = building models (F-BF.1.a, F-LE.2, N-Q.2) ·
O6 = interpreting models (F-LE.5, F-LE.1.a/b, N-Q.2)

---

## Part A — Function concept and notation (O1)

1. Decide whether each relation is a function, and explain each in one
   sentence:
   (a) `{(−2, 3), (0, 1), (−2, 5)}`
   (b) `x`: 1, 2, 3, 4 → `y`: 9, 9, 4, 4
   (c) the rule "input: a person; output: that person's shoe size." (2+2+2 pts)
2. For `f(x) = −4x + 11`, find `f(3)`, `f(0)`, and the input `x` with
   `f(x) = 27`. (3 pts)
3. A print shop charges `M(n) = 2.50n + 60` dollars for `n` posters ($2.50
   per poster plus a $60 design fee). What does `M(20) = 110` mean in words?
   (3 pts)

## Part B — Domain and range (O2)

4. A baker makes `P(h) = 18h` loaves, `h` hours into the shift, for
   `0 ≤ h ≤ 10`. Give the domain and range with units, and explain in a
   sentence why the domain stops at 10. (4 pts)
5. A line segment runs from `(−1, 2)` to `(3, 10)`. Write the domain and
   range as inequalities, and find `f(1)` and the input with output 9.
   (4 pts)

## Part C — Graphs and representations (O3)

6. Graph `y = −2x + 5` by hand with labeled axes. State the x- and
   y-intercepts and verify each by substitution. (5 pts)
7. A table gives `x`: −2, 0, 2, 4 → `y`: 7, 4, 1, −2. Write the function's
   equation and show how you found the slope. (4 pts)
8. The graphs of `y = 2x + 1` and `y = −x + 4` intersect at `(1, 3)`.
   Explain in words what "the graphs intersect at (1, 3)" tells you about
   the equation `2x + 1 = −x + 4`. (3 pts)

## Part D — Average rate of change (O4)

9. A tank's volume is recorded: `t` (hours): 0, 1, 3, 6 → `V` (gallons):
   240, 210, 150, 60. (a) Find the average rate of change over `[0, 6]`
   with units. (b) Find it over `[1, 3]`. (c) Interpret the sign, and say
   whether the drain looks linear — justify with the rates. (2+2+3 pts)
10. For `g(x) = 7 − 0.5x`, find the average rate of change over `[2, 10]`.
    If `x` is hours and `g(x)` is liters, state the units and meaning. (3 pts)

## Part E — Building models (O5)

11. A taxi charges $8.50 for a 2-mile ride and $21.00 for a 7-mile ride.
    (a) Define the quantities with units. (b) Build the fare model `T(m)`.
    (c) State the domain with a reason. (d) Predict the fare for 10 miles.
    (2+3+2+2 pts)
12. From a description: "A community garden charges a $20 annual fee plus
    $3.50 per square meter of plot." Define the quantities and write `G(s)`
    with its domain. (3 pts)

## Part F — Interpreting models (O6)

13. A field-trip bus company charges `R(d) = 12d + 150` dollars, where `d`
    is the number of students and the $150 covers the bus rental. Interpret
    the slope and intercept in context with units, and name one thing the
    model leaves out. (4 pts)
14. **Explain your reasoning.** Which table could show a linear function?
    Table A: `x`: 0, 2, 4, 6 → `y`: 5, 9, 13, 17. Table B: `x`: 0, 2, 4, 6
    → `y`: 5, 9, 15, 23. Justify with differences. (3 pts)
