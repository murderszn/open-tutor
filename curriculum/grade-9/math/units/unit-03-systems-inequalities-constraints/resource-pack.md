# Resource Pack — Unit 03: Systems, Inequalities, and Constraints

**Goal (one sentence):** Give a grade-9 learner free, no-account routes
to see systems of equations, elimination's justification, constraint
modeling, and systems of inequalities explained a second way, with
practice beyond this unit's pages.
**Age fit:** 14–15; assumes the grade-8 prerequisite fluency and the
Unit 01–02 solving and graphing skills listed in the unit README.

## Web references (verified 2026-10-26 via direct search-result page check; free, no account)

1. **OpenStax — Intermediate Algebra 2e, §4.1 "Solve Systems of Linear
   Equations with Two Variables."** Graphing, substitution, and
   elimination methods with worked examples, plus applications;
   directly supports Lessons 1–3 and the method-choice work in
   Practice C.
   <https://openstax.org/books/intermediate-algebra-2e/pages/4-1-solve-systems-of-linear-equations-with-two-variables>
2. **OpenStax — Intermediate Algebra 2e, Chapter 4 Key Concepts.**
   One-page summary: solving by graphing (intersecting / parallel /
   same line), by substitution, and by elimination, with the check-in-
   both-equations step — a good retrieval-review sheet for Session 17.
   <https://openstax.org/books/intermediate-algebra-2e/pages/4-key-concepts>
3. **OpenStax — College Algebra, Chapter 7 Key Concepts.** System
   classification in one place: independent (one solution), dependent
   (infinitely many), inconsistent (no solution); graphing,
   substitution, and addition methods; revenue/cost/profit
   applications — supports Lessons 3–4.
   <https://openstax.org/books/college-algebra/pages/7-key-concepts>
4. **Mathematics LibreTexts — §7.8 "Graphing Linear Inequalities in Two
   Variables" (OpenStax-derived, Ellis & Burzynski).** Half-planes,
   boundary lines, solid vs. dashed, and the test-point method with
   worked checks — supports Lessons 5–6.
   <https://math.libretexts.org/Bookshelves/Algebra/Elementary_Algebra_(Ellis_and_Burzynski)/07:_Graphing_Linear_Equations_and_Inequalities_in_One_and_Two_Variables/7.08:_Graphing_Linear_Inequalities_in_Two_Variables>
