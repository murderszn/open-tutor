# Resource Pack — Unit 04: Exponents, Polynomials, and Operations

**Goal (one sentence):** Give a grade-9 learner free, no-account
routes to see rational exponents, radical simplification, polynomial
operations, area models, and special products explained a second
way, with practice beyond this unit's pages.
**Age fit:** 14–15; assumes the grade-8 prerequisite fluency and the
Unit 01–03 solving, graphing, and checking skills listed in the unit
README.

## Web references (verified 2026-11-01 via direct search-result page check; free, no account)

1. **OpenStax — College Algebra, §1.3 "Radicals and Rational
   Exponents."** Evaluating roots, product/quotient rules for
   radicals, rationalizing denominators, and rational exponents;
   directly supports Lessons 1–2.
   <https://openstax.org/books/college-algebra/pages/1-3-radicals-and-rational-exponents>
2. **OpenStax — Intermediate Algebra 2e, Chapter 8 Key Concepts.**
   One-page summary: simplifying roots, rational exponents
   `a^(1/n)` and `a^(m/n)`, exponent properties, and special
   products — a good retrieval-review sheet for Session 17.
   <https://openstax.org/books/intermediate-algebra-2e/pages/8-key-concepts>
3. **OpenStax — Intermediate Algebra 2e, §5.1 "Add and Subtract
   Polynomials."** Degree, like terms, addition and subtraction
   with worked examples; directly supports Lesson 03 and
   Practice C.
   <https://openstax.org/books/intermediate-algebra-2e/pages/5-1-add-and-subtract-polynomials>
4. **OpenStax — Algebra and Trigonometry 2e, §1.4 "Polynomials."**
   Degree and leading coefficient, adding/subtracting, multiplying
   with the distributive property and FOIL, and special products;
   directly supports Lessons 03–05.
   <https://openstax.org/books/algebra-and-trigonometry-2e/pages/1-4-polynomials>
5. **OpenStax — Intermediate Algebra 2e, §6.3 "Factor Special
   Products."** The reverse direction of Lesson 05 (perfect-square
   trinomials and differences of squares as patterns), useful for
   previewing Unit 05 and for checking Lesson 05 answers by
   recognizing the patterns.
   <https://openstax.org/books/intermediate-algebra-2e/pages/6-3-factor-special-products>
6. **OpenStax — College Algebra 2e, Chapter 1 Key Concepts.**
   One-page summary including polynomial degree, leading
   coefficient, multiplication by the distributive property, and
   special products; a second retrieval sheet for Session 17.
   <https://openstax.org/books/college-algebra-2e/pages/1-key-concepts>
7. **Desmos graphing calculator (free, no account for basic use).**
   Not a reading reference: use it to check a polynomial product
   by graphing `y = (x + 3)(x + 2)` and `y = x² + 5x + 6` and
   confirming the graphs coincide. The adult previews for
   advertising and age suitability before assigning.
   <https://www.desmos.com/calculator>
