# Formative Quiz — Unit 04 (Session 16)

10 items, 1 point each. This quiz is formative: use it to choose
the Session 17 reteach focus, not as a final grade. Show the
root-first step for rational exponents and the partial products or
pattern step for polynomial products. Do not round unless asked.

1. Evaluate `64^(1/3)` and `100^(1/2)`.
2. Evaluate `27^(2/3)` and `16^(-1/4)`.
3. Simplify `√72` and `∛40`.
4. Add `(3x² − x + 5) + (x² + 4x − 2)`. Give the result in
   standard form and name its degree.
5. Subtract `(5x² + 2x − 3) − (2x² − x + 4)`.
6. Multiply `(x + 4)(x − 3)` and `(3x + 2)(x − 5)`.
7. Expand `(x − 7)²` and multiply `(2x + 3)(2x − 3)`.
8. Classify `√3 · √12` and `√3 · √7` as rational or irrational,
   showing the multiplication under one radical.
9. Give `√48` exactly and approximately to two decimal places
   (use `≈` for the approximation).
10. A rectangle has length `(x + 6)` feet and width `(x + 1)` feet.
    Write its area as a polynomial, and find the area when `x = 2`.
