# Culminating Assessment — Unit 04 (Session 18)

Part A: 2 points each (1 for the correct value, 1 for the reasoning
or check shown). Part B: 6 points each (2 for setup, 2 for correct
computation, 2 for interpretation, justification, or a correct
conclusion). Total: 48 points. This is a draft classroom
assessment, not a standardized or validated test.

## Part A — Short items

1. Evaluate `81^(1/4)` and `125^(2/3)`.
2. Evaluate `49^(-1/2)` and `32^(3/5)`.
3. Simplify `√108` and `∛16`. (For `∛16`, factor `16 = 8 · 2`.)
4. Rewrite `√(x⁵)` with a rational exponent (for `x ≥ 0`), and
   rewrite `y^(3/4)` with a radical sign.
5. Add `(2x³ + 5x − 1) + (−x³ + 2x + 6)` and subtract
   `(2x³ + 5x − 1) − (−x³ + 2x + 6)`. Give both results in
   standard form.
6. Multiply `(x − 8)(x + 5)` and `(2x + 3)(3x − 4)`.
7. Expand `(4x − 1)²` and multiply `(x² − 3)(x² + 3)`.
8. Classify each as rational or irrational, with a rule or a
   computation: `4 + √6`; `√5 · √45`; `√5 · √11`.
9. Give `√125` exactly and approximately to the nearest tenth
   (use `≈` for the approximation).
10. State the degree and leading coefficient of the product
    `(2x² − x + 1)(x³ + 4)` *without* fully multiplying it, and
    explain how you know.

## Part B — Extended tasks

11. **Area model.** A rectangle has length `(2x + 3)` feet and
    width `(x − 2)` feet. (a) Draw an area model (or list the four
    partial products) and expand the area as a polynomial in
    standard form. (b) Find the area when `x = 7` two ways —
    substitute-then-multiply and expand-then-substitute — and
    confirm they agree. (c) For what value of `x` does the width
    become zero, and why is that value excluded from a real
    rectangle's dimensions?
12. **Structure, not brute force.** Compute `1002 · 998` and
    `75²` using special-product patterns, showing the pattern
    step for each. Then explain, in two or three sentences, why
    `(x + 9)²` cannot equal `x² + 81`, using the middle term.
13. **Exact vs. approximate in context.** A square's side is
    `√60` feet. (a) Give the side exactly (simplified) and to the
    nearest hundredth of a foot. (b) A second square has side
    `√15` feet. Simplify `√60 + √15` exactly. (c) A builder adds
    the two rounded side lengths (to the nearest hundredth) and
    gets a total. Compare that total with the exact sum rounded
    to the nearest hundredth: are they the same? Explain what
    this shows about rounding before adding.
14. **Cumulative (Units 01–03).** (a) Solve `4x − 9 = 2x + 5`,
    justifying each step. (b) Solve the system `x + 2y = 11` and
    `x − y = 2` by a method of your choice, checking in both
    equations. (c) A line has slope `3` and `y`-intercept `−4`.
    Write its equation and find `y` when `x = 5`.
