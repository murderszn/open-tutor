## Reference session guide: OpenStax Biology 2e section 11.1

*Session 6.* The learner reads §11.1 ("The Process of Meiosis") with the
adult, working top to bottom: (1) the opening paragraphs — why must the
chromosome number be halved before fertilization? Write the
doubling-generation argument in one sentence; (2) the Meiosis I
subsections — find crossing over (prophase I) and independent
assortment (metaphase I) and match each to the Lesson 3 pipe-cleaner
model; (3) the "2^n" passage — check the human number (2^23, over eight
million) against Lesson 3's worked example; (4) the Meiosis II
subsection — confirm that no S phase occurs between the divisions. The
adult confirms the CC BY license notice on the page and that no account
is required. Anything in §11.1 that contradicts the lesson is brought
to the adult — the textbook wins ties, and the lesson gets a correction
note.
