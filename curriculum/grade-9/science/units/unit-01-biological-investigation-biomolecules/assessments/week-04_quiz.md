# Formative Quiz — Unit 01 (Session 16)

**Purpose:** check the four unit objectives before the review week ends.
Short written or oral responses; the adult scores the same day. Every
answer is worked in the [answer key](../answer-key.md#formative-quiz).

## Objective 1 — fair investigations (5 points)

1. (2 pts) A learner tests whether light affects seed sprouting: one tray
   in a sunny window, one tray in a dark closet. Name the independent
   variable and the dependent variable.
2. (2 pts) Name one controlled variable for the trays above, and explain
   why it must be controlled.
3. (1 pt) The learner uses 12 seeds per tray instead of 1. In one
   sentence, explain why.

## Objective 2 — observations, inferences, CER (5 points)

4. (2 pts) "The iodine drop on the potato turned blue-black within 30
   seconds." Is this an observation or an inference? Explain your
   classification in one sentence.
5. (3 pts) Write a complete claim–evidence–reasoning explanation for:
   "Does the potato contain starch?" Use the observation in question 4 as
   your evidence.

## Objective 3 — biomolecule classes (5 points)

6. (2 pts) Name the four biomolecule classes and the atoms each is built
   from.
7. (2 pts) A label reads: carbohydrate 2g, fat 11g, protein 1g per
   serving. Which biomolecule class dominates? Which atoms dominate with
   it?
8. (1 pt) Every living cell contains nucleic acids. In one sentence,
   explain what that implies about a fresh strawberry.

## Objective 4 — building large molecules (5 points)

9. (2 pts) Two glucose molecules (C₆H₁₂O₆ each) join by dehydration
   synthesis. Show the atom inventory: starting total, water released,
   final C/H/O counts and total.
10. (2 pts) An amino acid contains nitrogen, but glucose does not. In 2–3
    sentences, explain where the nitrogen comes from and what that means
    for the claim "sugars alone become amino acids."
11. (1 pt) A token model shows a sugar's atoms recombining with nitrogen
    into a larger molecule. Name one thing the model shows accurately and
    one thing it leaves out.

**Scoring:** 16–20 = on track; 12–15 = review the missed objective's
lesson before Session 17's re-teach; below 12 = re-teach that objective in
Session 17 and re-check with two new items from the
[teacher guide](../teacher-guide.md#re-check-bank).
