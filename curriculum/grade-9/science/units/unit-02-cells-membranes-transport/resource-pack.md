## Reference session guide: OpenStax Biology 2e section 5.1

*Session 6.* The learner reads §5.1 ("Components and Structure") with the
adult, working top to bottom: (1) the fluid mosaic model paragraph —
underline the sentence defining the model; (2) the phospholipid subsection
— check it against your Lesson 3 bilayer model (heads out? tails in?);
(3) the protein subsection — find integral vs. peripheral and match each to
your diagram's channel and pump; (4) "Membrane Fluidity" — write one
sentence connecting cholesterol to the "frozen vs. falling apart" range
from Lesson 3's independent practice. The adult confirms the CC BY license
notice on the page and that no account is required. Anything in §5.1 that
contradicts the lesson is brought to the adult — the textbook wins ties,
and the lesson gets a correction note.
