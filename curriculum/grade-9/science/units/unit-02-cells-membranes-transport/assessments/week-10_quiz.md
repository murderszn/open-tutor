# Formative Quiz — Unit 02: Cells, Membranes, and Transport

**Session 16 of 20** · About 25 minutes · Matched to the five unit
objectives · The adult reviews it the same day and re-teaches the
lowest-scoring objective in Session 17. Any response mode is accepted
(written, oral, typed, drawn, modeled); the adult scribes when writing
stamina lags. Worked solutions are in the [answer
key](../answer-key.md) — kept separate from this page.

**1.** Name the three structures found in plant cells but not in animal
cells. For *one* of them, explain what it makes possible for the plant.
(Objective 1)

**2.** A cell's ribosomes stop working but everything else is intact.
Which cell job fails first, and why? (Objective 1)

**3.** The membrane's bilayer has hydrophilic heads facing outward and
hydrophobic tails tucked inside. In one sentence, explain *why* the heads
face the water. (Objective 2)

**4.** For each substance, say whether it crosses the bilayer freely or
needs a protein — and give the reason (size/charge): (a) carbon dioxide,
(b) a sodium ion, (c) glucose. (Objective 2)

**5.** A substance is highly concentrated outside a cell and scarce
inside. The membrane is permeable to it. Which way does it move, net?
What is this process called, and does it cost the cell energy?
(Objective 3)

**6.** An egg (shell removed, 60.0 g) sits 24 hours in distilled water and
masses 66.0 g. (a) Compute the percent change. (b) Was the distilled
water hypertonic, hypotonic, or isotonic *to the egg*? (c) Explain the
mass change with the gradient model in one sentence. (Objectives 3–4)

**7.** A cell pumps a mineral ion inward even though the ion is already
more concentrated inside than outside. (a) With or against the gradient?
(b) Does this cost ATP? Explain. (c) Name the transport type.
(Objective 5)

**8.** Error analysis: "Osmosis is the movement of salt across a membrane
to even things out." Identify the two errors and correct them.
(Objectives 3, 5)

**9.** Mini-CER: A learner claims, "Facilitated diffusion needs energy
because it uses a protein." Write a 3-sentence response: your claim
(agree/disagree), your evidence (from the gradient model), and your
reasoning. (Objectives 3, 5)

**10.** A white blood cell engulfs a bacterium. (a) Name the transport
process. (b) Is it passive or active? Justify with the three-question
machine. (Objective 5)
