# Formative Quiz — Unit 03 (Track Week 14)

**Time:** about 25 minutes · **Closed book for questions 1–8; the yeast
table in question 7 is provided.** Write or speak your answers; the adult
scores from the [answer key](../answer-key.md). Each question is worth
1 point unless stated otherwise.

1. Balance the photosynthesis equation by filling in the coefficients:
   __CO₂ + __H₂O → __C₆H₁₂O₆ + __O₂.
2. Which of these is an OUTPUT of photosynthesis, not an input?
   (a) carbon dioxide (b) water (c) light energy (d) oxygen
3. In which stage and structure is water split during photosynthesis?
   (a) Calvin cycle, stroma (b) light-dependent reactions, thylakoid
   membrane (c) Calvin cycle, thylakoid membrane (d) light-dependent
   reactions, stroma
4. The oxygen released by photosynthesis comes from:
   (a) carbon dioxide (b) water (c) glucose (d) the air
5. Is this respiration equation balanced? C₆H₁₂O₆ + 6O₂ → 6CO₂ + 6H₂O.
   Answer yes or no, and give the carbon count on each side.
6. In cellular respiration, the energy released by forming new bonds is
   greater than the energy spent breaking old bonds. What does this
   mean? (a) The cell must pay energy to respire. (b) There is a net
   release of energy the cell can use. (c) No energy changes form.
   (d) The reaction destroys matter.
7. Use the yeast table (fictional practice data): A (warm + sugar) rose
   to 38 cm in 30 minutes; B (warm, no sugar) stayed at 2 cm; C (cold +
   sugar) reached 9 cm. Which comparison proves sugar is needed, and
   what variable does it isolate?
8. Yeast fermentation of glucose produces:
   (a) carbon dioxide and water only (b) carbon dioxide and ethanol
   (c) lactate only (d) oxygen and glucose
9. Coal belongs to which carbon reservoir?
   (a) atmosphere (b) biosphere (c) hydrosphere (d) geosphere
10. Why are food chains usually short? Answer in one sentence using the
    idea of usable energy and heat.

**Scoring:** 9–10 secure; 7–8 developing (re-teach the missed objective
before the assessment); below 7 re-teach and re-quiz with new numbers
before Session 20.
