## Reference session guide — OpenStax Biology 2e, Section 8.1

**Session 6 (50 minutes).** The learner reads §8.1 (or listens to the
adult read it aloud) with three questions in hand, then answers them in
writing or speech:

1. Why does the section call photosynthesis "the only biological process
   that can capture energy that originates from sunlight"? (Because
   only photoautotrophs convert light into chemical compounds other
   organisms can use for metabolism.)
2. Where in the leaf and chloroplast does each stage happen, and what
   does each stage make? (Light-dependent: thylakoid membrane — ATP,
   NADPH, oxygen; Calvin cycle: stroma — G3P/sugar from CO₂.)
3. A plant closes its stomata on a hot, dry day. Using the section's
   account of stomata and gas exchange, explain what happens to
   photosynthesis and why.

The adult checks answers against the section text, not against memory,
and re-teaches any stage/location confusion before Lesson 4. If the
learner cannot access the page, use Lesson 02's teaching section as the
reference text instead — the session's goal is the three answers, not
the website.
