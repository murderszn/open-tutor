# Formative Quiz — Unit 05, Week 22 (Track Week 22)

**Session 16 · 50 minutes · adult scores same-day.** Ten short items,
matched to the five unit objectives. Written, oral, typed, drawn, or
modeled answers are all accepted; the adult scribes when needed. Solutions
are in the [answer key](../answer-key.md#week-22-quiz).

1. **(Objective 1)** Write the complementary DNA strand (with direction)
   for 5'-A C G T T A-3'.
2. **(Objective 1)** A DNA sample is 26% guanine. What percentage is
   cytosine? What percentage is adenine? Show the reasoning.
3. **(Objective 2)** A DNA template strand reads 3'-TAC GTT AAT-5'.
   Transcribe it to mRNA and translate it using the unit's codon chart.
4. **(Objective 2)** An mRNA codon changes from UUU to UUC. Does the
   protein change? Name the kind of change.
5. **(Objective 3)** Two pea plants, both Bb for flower color, are
   crossed. Draw the Punnett square and give the phenotype ratio.
6. **(Objective 3)** A purple-flowered plant of unknown genotype is
   crossed with a white-flowered plant, and 1 of their 12 offspring has
   white flowers. What is the purple parent's genotype, and how do you
   know?
7. **(Objective 4)** In a Bb × Bb cross, what is the probability that two
   offspring in a row are both white? Show the rule you used.
8. **(Objective 4)** A class counts 152 purple and 48 white flowers in
   200 F2 plants from a Bb × Bb cross. What counts does the 3:1 model
   predict? How far off is each class, in plants and in percentage
   points?
9. **(Objective 5)** Explain in 2–3 sentences why a skin cell and a
   muscle cell, which share a genome, make different proteins.
10. **(Objective 5)** A pedigree shows two parents with free earlobes
    having a child with attached earlobes. Is attached earlobe dominant
    or recessive? What are the parents' genotypes? Explain in one
    sentence each.

**Scoring:** 1 point per item (items 2, 3, 5, 8, 10 carry a half point per
part; 10 points total). 9–10 = secure; 7–8 = review the missed objective
in Session 17; below 7 = re-teach the lowest objective before the unit
assessment. This quiz is a formative check, not a standardized or
validated assessment.
