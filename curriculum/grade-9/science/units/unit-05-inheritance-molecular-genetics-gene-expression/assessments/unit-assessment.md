# Unit Assessment — Unit 05: Inheritance, Molecular Genetics, and Gene Expression

**Session 20 · 50 minutes · adult scores same-day.** Twelve tasks matched
to the five unit objectives. Written, oral, typed, drawn, or modeled
answers are all accepted; the adult scribes when needed. Solutions and
scoring guidance are in the [answer key](../answer-key.md#unit-assessment).
This assessment is a unit check written for this course, not a standardized
or validated assessment.

## Part A — DNA and proteins (Objectives 1–2)

1. A DNA strand reads 5'-G C A T A G C C-3'.
   a. Write the complementary strand with direction.
   b. How many hydrogen bonds hold the two strands together across these
      8 base pairs? Show the count.
2. A DNA template strand reads 3'-TAC CCG TTT TGC-5'. Transcribe it to
   mRNA and translate it with the unit's codon chart. (Your protein
   should start with Met and end with Thr.)
3. A mutation changes the DNA so that an mRNA codon changes from CAA to
   UAA. What kind of change is this, and what happens to the protein?
   Contrast it with a change from CAA to CAG.
4. Explain the central dogma in three sentences: what DNA does, what
   mRNA does, and what the ribosome does.

## Part B — Inheritance predictions (Objectives 3–4)

5. In pea plants, round seeds (R) are dominant to wrinkled (r). Two
   heterozygous plants (Rr × Rr) are crossed.
   a. Draw the Punnett square.
   b. Give the genotype ratio and the phenotype ratio.
   c. What is the probability that a given offspring is wrinkled?
6. A gardener has a round-seeded plant of unknown genotype and crosses it
   with a wrinkled-seeded plant (rr). All 15 offspring are round-seeded.
   What is the gardener's plant's genotype? Explain, including why 15
   offspring are enough to be confident.
7. Mendel's F2 flower-color counts were 705 purple and 224 white out of
   929 plants.
   a. What counts does an exact 3:1 ratio predict?
   b. How far off was Mendel, in plants and in percentage points?
   c. In one sentence, why was a sample of 929 plants a better test than
      a sample of 9 plants?
8. Two heterozygous pea plants (Yy × Yy, yellow dominant) have three
   offspring. What is the probability that all three have yellow seeds?
   Show the rule you used.

## Part C — Expression, patterns, pedigrees (Objective 5)

9. Siamese cats have dark fur on their cooler ears and paws and pale fur
   on their warm body. Explain this pattern in 3–4 sentences using gene
   expression, and state what would happen to a shaved patch kept warm
   while it regrows.
10. A pedigree (fictional) shows a family where a grandmother has
    attached earlobes (ee), her son has free earlobes, and her grandson
    (the son's child with a free-earlobed partner) has attached earlobes.
    a. What is the son's genotype? Explain the deduction.
    b. What must be true about the son's partner's genotype? Explain.
    c. What is the probability the son and his partner's next child has
       attached earlobes?
11. In snapdragons, red (RR) × white (rr) gives pink (Rr) flowers.
    a. What pattern of inheritance is this?
    b. Cross two pink plants. Give the genotype and phenotype ratios.
12. A color-blind man (X^b Y) and a woman who is not color-blind and not a
    carrier (X^B X^B) have a daughter.
    a. What is the daughter's genotype? Will she be color-blind?
    b. Could the daughter's future sons be color-blind? Explain in two
       sentences.
