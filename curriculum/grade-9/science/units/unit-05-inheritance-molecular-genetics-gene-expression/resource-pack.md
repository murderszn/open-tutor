## Reference session guide — OpenStax Biology 2e §12.1

*Session 6 · 50 minutes · adult reads aloud; the learner tracks ideas.*

1. **Before reading (5 min):** recall Lesson 3's Punnett square. Ask:
   "Mendel never saw DNA. How could he be sure traits were passed as
   separate units?" Keep the question open.
2. **Read (25 min):** the adult reads §12.1 aloud in three chunks —
   Mendel's model system (why peas), the crosses (P0 → F1 → F2), and the
   probability basics (product/sum rules). After each chunk, the learner
   restates one idea in their own words.
3. **Check the numbers (10 min):** find the 705:224 count in the text.
   Compute the expected counts at 3:1 for 929 plants (696.75 and 232.25)
   and the deviations (+8.25, −8.25). Discuss: why did Mendel need
   hundreds of plants to see the pattern?
4. **Close (10 min):** the learner writes two sentences: one thing Mendel
   proved without knowing about DNA, and one thing DNA explains that
   Mendel could not. If the session stalls, use the accessibility
   fallback: the ideas are the requirement, not the source — the learner
   may answer from the Lesson 3 notes instead.
