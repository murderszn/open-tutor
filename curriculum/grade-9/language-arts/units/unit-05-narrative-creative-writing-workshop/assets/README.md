## Generated illustration

**File:** `long-way-home-illustration.png`
**Prompt:** "Warm, painterly storybook illustration for a high-school
creative-writing lesson: a 16-year-old girl with a dark braid stands at
dusk at the rusted gate of an old rail yard, holding a small glowing
lantern; a scruffy brown dog sits beside her looking up at her; behind
the gate, overgrown railway tracks disappear into blue evening mist; at
the far end of a long road a single house window glows warm yellow; the
girl pauses, deciding whether to take the shortcut through the yard or
the long way around. Quiet, thoughtful, hopeful mood. No text or words
in the image."
**Generation tool:** Muse built-in image generation
(`media.generate_image`); model not reported by the tool.
**Date generated:** 2026-11-11.
**Learning purpose:** used in Lesson 1, before the learner reads the
mentor story. The learner answers three questions from the image alone
(what is the girl deciding? what shows the shortcut is not the safe
choice? what does the dog's posture tell you?) and then checks those
inferences against the story — teaching the difference between what an
illustration can *show* about a decision and what words can *tell*
about it.
**Factual review (inspected 2026-11-11):** the image matches the
story's decision moment — the girl with a dark braid, the glowing
lantern, the scruffy brown dog, the rusted rail-yard gate, overgrown
tracks in blue evening mist, and one lit house window down the road.
No text appears in the image. The girl reads slightly younger than the
story's sixteen; the caption describes her as "a teenage girl" rather
than giving an age. The dog's collar tag is not legible, which is
correct — the tag's text ("BARNABY — 14 ALDER LANE") is a story fact
the reader learns by reading, not by looking.
**Caption:** AI-generated illustration of the decision moment in "The
Long Way Home" — Samira at the rail-yard gate with Barnaby. (Labeled AI
illustration; not a primary source.)
**Alt text:** "A teenage girl with a dark braid stands at dusk at the
rusted gate of an old rail yard, holding a small glowing lantern; a
scruffy brown dog sits beside her looking up; behind the gate,
overgrown railway tracks disappear into blue evening mist; at the far
end of a long road a single house window glows warm yellow."
**Text-only alternative:** provided in Lesson 1, directly below the
image, so the illustration question can be answered without seeing the
picture.
**Known limitations:** AI illustration, not a photograph and not a
depiction of a real person; the character's apparent age is
approximate; the image is a reconstruction of a fictional scene and
must never be presented as evidence about a real place, person, or
event.

## Deterministic diagram (supplement, not a generated image)

**File:** `story-arc-diagram.svg`
**Purpose:** a precise, reproducible diagram of the mentor story's
narrative arc, with paragraph references, for Lesson 1's event-chain
work. SVG is used because the arc's labeled stages and their order are
exact information — a generated image must not introduce false labels
or measurements.
**Contents:** the six plot stages (exposition, inciting incident,
rising action, climax, falling action, resolution) plotted on an arc,
each labeled with the mentor story's corresponding paragraphs and the
event that happens there.
