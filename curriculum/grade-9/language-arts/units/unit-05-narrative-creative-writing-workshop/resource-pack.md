# Resource Pack — Unit 05 (Grade 9 Language Arts)

**Summary:** supports a grade-9 (ages 14–15) four-week narrative and
creative writing workshop — story architecture, point of view,
dialogue, pacing, sensory language, drafting, and revision — adult
planning judgment; preview everything before assigning. All links below
were checked on **2026-11-11** unless labeled otherwise.

## Internal resources

- [The Long Way Home](texts/the-long-way-home.md) — original mentor
  story (problem, want vs. need, event chain, dialogue, scene vs.
  summary, reflective conclusion) with a generated illustration of its
  decision moment.
- [Story seeds](texts/story-seeds.md) — three original seeds ("The
  Unsent Letter," "The Last Game," "The Recipe Box"), each with a
  character, a want, a need, a complication, and an opening line.
- [Craft vignettes](texts/craft-vignettes.md) — four original paired
  passages (scene vs. summary; telling vs. revealing dialogue; flat vs.
  sensory description; two points of view, one moment).
- [Story-arc diagram](assets/story-arc-diagram.svg) — deterministic
  diagram of the mentor story's arc, with paragraph references.
- [`resources/language_arts_literary_terms.md`](../../../../../resources/language_arts_literary_terms.md)
  — teacher-side vocabulary bank (plot, POV, irony, tone); accurate at
  its band; grade-9 lessons build past it toward RL.9-10.4/5.
- [`resources/language_arts_sentence_structure.md`](../../../../../resources/language_arts_sentence_structure.md)
  — sentence-structure guide (subject/predicate, phrases, clauses,
  types); adult-side reference for the conventions revision pass in
  U04 terms.
- [`resources/language_arts_great_books_and_stories.md`](../../../../../resources/language_arts_great_books_and_stories.md)
  — background on classic stories and public-domain discovery; adult
  background only, never learner text.

## Web references

1. **Purdue OWL — "Fiction Writing Basics"**
   ([owl.purdue.edu/owl/subject_specific_writing/creative_writing/writers/fiction_writing_basics/](https://owl.purdue.edu/owl/subject_specific_writing/creative_writing/writers/fiction_writing_basics/))
   — verified 2026-11-11; opened and read. "Plot is created by the
   manner in which the writer arranges and organizes particular actions
   in a meaningful way" and "a sequence of events causes other events
   to happen" — the adult-facing articulation of W.9-10.3.a/c used in
   Lesson 1; also covers character ("present her knowledge of the
   characters indirectly, through dialogue and action") and conflict.
   Free, no account. (Note: OWL restructured its URLs in past years;
   this address was live on the check date.)
2. **Project Gutenberg — "The Gift of the Magi" by O. Henry, ebook
   #7256** ([gutenberg.org/ebooks/7256](https://www.gutenberg.org/ebooks/7256))
   — verified 2026-11-11; opened and read. First published 1905;
   public domain in the USA; free, no account, readable online. The
   recommended strong-reader extension (twist structure, withheld
   information, want vs. need) for Lesson 5 and the teacher guide's
   extension tasks. The story is linked, not reproduced.
3. **ReadWriteThink — "Make a Splash! Using Dramatic Experience to
   'Explode the Moment'"**
   ([readwritethink.org/classroom-resources/lesson-plans/make-splash-using-dramatic](https://www.readwritethink.org/classroom-resources/lesson-plans/make-splash-using-dramatic))
   — verified 2026-11-11 (page text read via search result). Teaches
   elaborating a single moment with descriptive, sensory language
   ("explode the moment") — the technique behind Lesson 4's sensory
   work and the extension task. Note: the lesson as written uses
   chapter books and a staged classroom surprise; the *technique*
   adapts to grade 9, but the adult previews and adapts the materials
   rather than assigning the lesson as written. Free to read, no
   account.
4. **The Writing Center, UNC-Chapel Hill — "Writing Concisely"**
   ([writingcenter.unc.edu/tips-and-tools/conciseness-handout](https://writingcenter.unc.edu/tips-and-tools/conciseness-handout))
   — verified 2026-11-11 (page text read via search result). Strategies
   for cutting wordiness: redundant pairs, unnecessary qualifiers,
   prepositional phrases, phrase → word. Used in Lesson 6 and Practice
   H (revision pass 2, language). Free, no account. Written for college
   writers; the adult selects the strategies that fit grade 9.
