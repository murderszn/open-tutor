# Resource Pack — Unit 03 (Grade 9 Language Arts)

**Summary:** supports a grade-9 (ages 14–15) four-week unit on
informational rhetoric, argument evaluation, seminal documents,
and media literacy — adult planning judgment; preview
everything before assigning. All links below were checked on
**2026-10-27** unless labeled otherwise.

## Internal resources

- [Library hours editorial](texts/library-hours-editorial.md)
  — original anchor text (central idea, rhetoric).
- [Two editorials, one empty lot](texts/two-editorials.md)
  — original paired texts (rhetoric inventory, comparison).
- [The Fallacy Set](texts/fallacy-set.md) — original
  argument snippets (sound vs. fallacious reasoning).

  original editorial for the unit assessment.

## Web references

1. **Common Core State Standards — Speaking & Listening,
   grades 9–10**
   ([thecorestandards.org/ELA-Literacy/SL/9-10/](https://www.thecorestandards.org/ELA-Literacy/SL/9-10/))
   — verified 2026-10-27. Official text of SL.9-10.2
   (integrate multiple sources, evaluate credibility and
   accuracy) and SL.9-10.3 (evaluate a speaker's point of
   view, reasoning, evidence, and rhetoric). Free, no account.
2. **Common Core RI.9-10 standards text (mirror)**
   ([coursemason.com/Standards/English-Language-Arts/Grade-9-10/Reading-Information-Text](https://www.coursemason.com/Standards/English-Language-Arts/Grade-9-10/Reading-Information-Text))
   — checked 2026-10-27; text matches the official standard
   wording for RI.9-10.1–9 as used in this unit. A mirror, not
   the official site; the unit paraphrases descriptions and
   claims no state adoption or alignment certification.
3. **National Park Service — Gettysburg Address**
   ([nps.gov/linc/learn/historyculture/gettysburgaddress.htm](https://www.nps.gov/linc/learn/historyculture/gettysburgaddress.htm))
   — verified 2026-10-27. Notes the Bliss copy as the standard
   signed version and reproduces its text; the unit's quotation
   follows this copy. Free, no account.
4. **Austin Community College research guide — Lateral
   Reading**
   ([researchguides.austincc.edu/c.php?g=612891&p=10459000](https://researchguides.austincc.edu/c.php?g=612891&p=10459000))
   — verified 2026-10-27. Explains lateral reading via the News
   Literacy Project's "20 seconds" guidance; supports Lesson 5.
   Free, no account.
5. **Monmouth University LibGuide — SIFT method**
   ([guides.monmouth.edu/media_literacy/tips_tools](http://guides.monmouth.edu/media_literacy/tips_tools))
   — verified 2026-10-27. Summarizes Caulfield's SIFT method
   (Stop, Investigate the source, Find better coverage, Trace
   the claim); supports Lesson 5 and Practice I. Free, no
   account.
6. **Oregon State University — News Literacy Guide**
   ([guides.library.oregonstate.edu/c.php?g=1036841&p=7517073](https://guides.library.oregonstate.edu/c.php?g=1036841&p=7517073))
   — verified 2026-10-27. Checklist for evaluating news
   articles (consider the source, read beyond the headline,
   check the author, determine whether sources support the
   story); supports Lesson 5. Free, no account.
7. **Edutopia — "What Fact-Checkers Know About Media
   Literacy"**
   ([edutopia.org/article/what-fact-checkers-know-about-media-literacy-and-students-should-too](https://www.edutopia.org/article/what-fact-checkers-know-about-media-literacy-and-students-should-too))
   — verified 2026-10-27. Adult background on lateral vs.
   vertical reading from Stanford's Wineburg research; frames
   Lesson 5's teaching. Free, no account.

## Access and review notes

- Every core objective is reachable with **free, no-account**
  materials: the unit's own texts, the three internal guides,
  and the references above.
- No runtimes or caption claims are made for the search-link
  videos because the specific items were not verified — the
  adult selects and previews.
- Sensitive content: the Gettysburg Address concerns the Civil
  War and its dead; the unit contains no graphic content (see
  the content note in the texts file and teacher guide).
- The modern response in the Gettysburg file is labeled
  fictional wherever it appears; it is never presented as a
  historical document.
- Review date for all "verified" items above: **2026-10-27**.
