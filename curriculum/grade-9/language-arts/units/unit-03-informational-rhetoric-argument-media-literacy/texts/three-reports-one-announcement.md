# Three Reports, One Announcement

**Original practice materials written for this unit. All people,
places, outlets, and figures are fictional. Nothing here describes
a real town, outlet, or event.**

The question: **is the new Willow Creek farmers' market pavilion
open yet?** Four accounts appeared on the same Saturday. Read
them in time order and track where each claim comes from.

---

## A — Willow Creek Town Council notice, 8:00 a.m.

> "The new farmers' market pavilion on Mill Street is complete and
> will open to vendors this Saturday. Our inspection is finished
> and the pavilion is ready for public use."

## B — Willow Creek Gazette (online article), 9:30 a.m.

> "The town council says the new Mill Street pavilion has passed
> inspection and will welcome vendors this Saturday, according to
> the council's morning notice. The Gazette has not independently
> inspected the pavilion."

## C — The Mill Street Blog (post), 11:00 a.m.

> "Great news for Saturday shoppers! The Gazette reports that the
> pavilion is inspected and ready. Vendors, bring your tents — the
> market is on!"

## D — Vendor observation, 12:00 noon (reported by a student journalist)

> "I walked to Mill Street at noon to set up my stall. A fence
> still blocked the pavilion entrance, and a sign on the gate read
> NOT IN SERVICE. I did not go inside, did not test anything, and
> do not know whether an inspection happened. I only know what the
> sign said."

## E — Television evening report, 6:00 p.m. (transcript excerpt)

> Anchor: "The pavilion story continues. The council says
> inspected and ready; one vendor says the gate was fenced at
> noon. We called the council office for the dated inspection
> record and have not yet received it. We will update this story
> when the record arrives or when a reporter can verify the gate
> in person."

## F — The numbers both sides cite

The council notice links to a market-attendance table from last
year's open-air market (same location, no pavilion):

| Month | Saturdays held | Average vendors | Average shoppers |
|---|---|---|---|
| May | 4 | 18 | 310 |
| June | 5 | 22 | 420 |
| July | 4 | 24 | 460 |
| August | 5 | 26 | 480 |

The Gazette's article emphasizes the August row ("480 shoppers").
The blog emphasizes "vendors up nearly 45 percent since May"
(18 → 26). The TV report notes that none of these figures
describe the new pavilion, which has not yet held a market day.

---

**How to use this set:** Lesson 5 (media literacy) models the
claim-tracing for A→B→C, the independent observation in D, and
the speaker evaluation in E. Practice I asks learners to draw the
source map, rewrite the blog's claim honestly, and decide what
each medium emphasizes in F. A text-only alternative to the
Lesson 5 image is included in the lesson itself.
