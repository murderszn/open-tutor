# Unit Assessment — Unit 03 (Session 19)

**50 points · ~60 minutes · the assessment passage only
([texts/assessment-passage.md](../texts/assessment-passage.md))
plus your notes from Lessons 1–6.** Do not use the answer key.
The adult scores with the rubric in the answer key.

## Part A — Delineate and evaluate (26 pts)

Read "A Case for Later Start Times" twice before writing.

1. **(8 pts)** Draw a claim map: the passage's central idea,
   its three main reasons, the evidence offered for each, and
   the counterclaim (objection) it admits. One row per reason.
2. **(10 pts)** Identify the passage's two planted
   weaknesses: (a) the Brookfield comparison and (b) the
   closing popularity move. For each: name what kind of
   weakness it is (insufficient evidence, fallacy, or
   unanswered objection), quote the relevant words, and
   explain the flaw in two sentences.
3. **(8 pts)** Evaluate the author's treatment of the bus/job
   objection: is it fair? Quote the sentence that most shows
   fairness or unfairness, and explain in three sentences what
   the author still owes the reader before a vote (for
   example, a specific missing figure or answer).

## Part B — Evaluation essay (24 pts)

4. **(24 pts)** Write an evaluation essay (400–500 words) on
   the passage: a precise overall claim about its argument's
   strength; one strength developed with a quotation; one
   weakness developed with a quotation; a fair counterclaim
   (the strongest point on the other side, stated well);
   formal tone; and a conclusion naming the single change
   that would most strengthen the argument.

**Scoring guide (for the adult):** Part A, 26 pts as marked
above (map: 2 pts per complete row + 2 for the central idea;
weaknesses: 5 pts each; objection evaluation: 3 quotation +
5 reasoning). Part B: claim 4, strength 5, weakness 5,
counterclaim 4, tone 2, conclusion with named fix 4.
Below 38 total: reteach Lessons 3 and 6, then reassess with a
new passage. Model responses are in
[answer-key.md](../answer-key.md#unit-assessment).
