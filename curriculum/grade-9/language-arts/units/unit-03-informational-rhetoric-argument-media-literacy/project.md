# Project — The Source Audit Dossier

**Due:** end of week 14 (session 20 gallery). **Weight:** the
dossier is the unit's project grade; the unit assessment
(session 19) is separate.

## Task

Audit one argument the way this unit taught you to. Choose one
of these options (adult approves the choice):

- **Option 1:** one editorial from this unit (library hours,
  skate park, or garden).
- **Option 2:** an adult-selected published opinion piece or
  speech (public, age-appropriate; the adult previews it and
  confirms it is lawful to quote briefly for school analysis).

## Deliverables

1. **Claim map (1 page):** the argument's central idea, its
   reasons, its evidence, and any counterclaim it admits —
   each with a short quotation or location (paragraph number).
2. **Evidence table (1 page):** for each major piece of
   evidence: what it is, whether it is relevant, whether it is
   sufficient for the claim it supports, and what would
   strengthen it.
3. **Source audit (1 page, only for Option 2; for Option 1,
   audit the unit's media set instead):** trace one claim in
   the piece to its root. Is the claim independently supported,
   repeated from one root, or unverified? Show the map.
4. **Evaluation essay (500–700 words):** a precise claim
   about the argument's overall strength, fair counterclaim,
   quoted evidence, formal tone, and a named fix — the single
   change that would most strengthen the argument.
5. **Source list:** every text used, with author, title, date
   (or "undated"), and where it was found. For unit texts,
   cite the unit file name.

## Rubric

| Criterion | Exceeds (4) | Meets (3) | Approaches (2) | Below (1) |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Claim map | Central idea is a precise, arguable claim; every reason and counterclaim is mapped with quotations | Central idea is a claim; reasons mapped; minor gaps in counterclaims | Central idea is a topic, or reasons are listed without quotations | Map missing, or claims invented rather than traced |
| Evidence table | Every major piece judged on relevance *and* sufficiency, with a specific strengthening step | Major pieces judged on both tests; strengthening steps general | Judgments given but only one test applied, or evidence summarized without judgment | No judgments, or judgments without reference to the text |
| Source audit | Root traced exactly (repeat vs. independent vs. unverified), with the consequence stated | Root traced correctly; consequence vague | Map drawn but root misidentified, or repeats counted as confirmations | No audit, or audit invents a source |
| Evaluation essay | Precise claim, steelmanned counterclaim, quoted evidence in every body paragraph, formal tone, specific named fix | All elements present; one element thin (e.g., fix is vague) | Missing one element, or counterclaim weakened on purpose | No claim, no evidence, or informal/attacking tone |
| Source list | Complete and accurate; unit texts cited by file | Complete with minor errors | Missing one source, or dates guessed | Missing or fabricated |

**Scoring:** 18–20 = exceeds; 14–17 = meets; 10–13 =
approaches; below 10 = revise and resubmit one deliverable.
The adult scores with the learner present and names one
revision step for any criterion below 3.
