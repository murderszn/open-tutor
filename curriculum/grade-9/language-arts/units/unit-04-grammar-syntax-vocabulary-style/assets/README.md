## Generated illustration

- **File:** `lighthouse-logbook.png` (1920 × 1280 PNG)
- **Prompt (verbatim, as submitted):** "A warm, clear
  educational illustration, no text or words anywhere in the
  image: a lighthouse keeper sits at a wooden desk inside a
  stone lighthouse at dusk, writing in an open logbook with a
  pencil. A brass lantern glows beside the logbook. Through a
  round window behind the keeper, waves break against rocks
  and a few gulls fly in a dusky sky. On the desk are a coiled
  rope, a pair of spectacles, and a small stack of paper. The
  keeper looks thoughtful, pausing mid-sentence. Calm,
  detailed, realistic storybook style."
- **Tool/model:** Muse built-in image generation, 2026-11-02.
- **Learning purpose:** Lesson 1's sentence-combining
  activity — the learner observes the scene and writes it in
  four sentence types (simple, compound, complex,
  compound-complex), labeling each clause.
- **Factual review (inspected 2026-11-02 before linking):**
  the image matches the prompt on every checkable element —
  keeper at a desk writing in a logbook with a pencil, lit
  brass lantern, coiled rope, spectacles, stack of papers,
  large round window, waves breaking on rocks, gulls in a
  dusky sky. No text or words appear in the image, so no
  garbled text is present. The keeper is a fictional
  illustration, not a real person, and the lesson caption says
  so.
- **Alt text:** "An older lighthouse keeper in a blue knit
  cap and green coat sits at a wooden desk in a stone
  lighthouse, writing in an open logbook with a pencil; a lit
  brass lantern, a coiled rope, spectacles, and a stack of
  papers are on the desk, and through a large round window
  behind him waves break against rocks under a dusky sky with
  gulls flying."
- **Caption:** "AI-generated illustration for 'The Lighthouse
  Log' — the keeper pauses mid-sentence. (Labeled AI
  illustration; not a primary source or a real person.)"
- **Text-only alternative:** given in full in Lesson 1; it
  lists every scene element the activity needs (keeper,
  logbook, lantern, rope, spectacles, papers, window, waves,
  rocks, dusk sky, gulls), so the activity can be completed
  without the image.
- **Known limitations:** the illustration is an imagined
  scene, not evidence about real lighthouse keeping; its
  logbook pages are blank, so no log text can be read from
  it — the unit's log texts are the written passages in
  `texts/sentence-bank.md`, not the image.
