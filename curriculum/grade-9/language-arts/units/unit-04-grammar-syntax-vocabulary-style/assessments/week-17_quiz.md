# Formative Quiz — Week 17 (Unit 04)

**Time:** ~30 minutes · **Total:** 20 points ·
**Reteach if below 16.** Answers are in the separate
[`answer-key.md`](../answer-key.md) — do not work from the key.

## Part A — Identification (8 points, 1 each)

For each sentence, name its type (simple, compound, complex,
compound-complex) and label each clause as independent or
dependent (naming the dependent kind).

1. The keeper trimmed the wick.
2. Mara knew that the bell had rung.
3. Although the fog was thick, the boats kept their course.
4. The lamp burned all night, and the boats found the harbor.
5. The chart that Mara trusted was out of date.
6. When the tide rose, the crew secured the boat, and the keeper logged the hour.
7. Tired, the keeper climbed the tower.
8. The sea lay flat because the wind had gone.

## Part B — Correction (8 points, 2 each)

Repair each item. Name the error and give one correct version.

1. The work is steady, the pay is fair.
2. Because the storm passed.
3. The lamp was lit, however, the boats could not see it.
4. The keeper wrote the log the crew checked the lamps.

## Part C — Vocabulary application (4 points)

Write a paragraph of four to five sentences about a keeper
preparing for a storm. Use **two** of these words accurately:
*meticulous, anticipate, consequence, evaluate*. Underline the
two words you use. Then, in one sentence each, state the
context clue that would let a reader guess each word's meaning
from your paragraph.
