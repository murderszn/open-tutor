# Resource Pack — Unit 02 (Grade 9 Language Arts)

**Summary:** supports a grade-9 (ages 14–15) four-week unit on
narrative structure, characterization, point of view, pacing, and
word choice — adult planning judgment; preview everything before
assigning. All links below were checked on **2026-10-20** unless
labeled otherwise.

  (in medias res, flashback, complex protagonist, foil).

## Web references

1. **Project Gutenberg — "The Monkey's Paw," ebook #12122**
   ([gutenberg.org/ebooks/12122](https://www.gutenberg.org/ebooks/12122))
   — verified 2026-10-20; the complete public-domain story (~3,800
   words), free, no account. The unit's three excerpts were
   spot-checked against this text's plain-text edition. Also the
   source for strong readers assigned the full story.
2. **Wikipedia — "The Monkey's Paw"**
   ([en.wikipedia.org/wiki/The_Monkey's_Paw](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/The_Monkey's_Paw))
   — verified 2026-10-20. Publication history (Harper's Monthly,
   September 1902; *The Lady of the Barge*), adaptations, and links
   to the LibriVox public-domain audiobook (free listening
   alternative for Lesson 2/4 rereads). Background for the adult;
   learners may use the publication-history section only.
3. **ReadWriteThink — "Plot Structure: A Literary Elements
   Mini-Lesson"**
   ([readwritethink.org/classroom-resources/lesson-plans/plot-structure-literary-elements](https://www.readwritethink.org/classroom-resources/lesson-plans/plot-structure-literary-elements?tab=4))
   — verified 2026-10-20. Plot-diagram interactive and lesson
   sequence; supports Practice A. Free; some interactives may ask
   for (free) registration — the unit's paper plot maps are the
   no-account alternative.
4. **Purdue OWL — "Fiction Writing Basics"**
   ([owl.purdue.edu/owl/subject_specific_writing/creative_writing/writers/fiction_writing_basics/](https://OWL.Purdue.edu/owl/subject_specific_writing/creative_writing/writers/fiction_writing_basics/index.html))
   — verified 2026-10-20. "Plot is created by the manner in which
   the writer arranges and organizes particular actions" — the
   adult-facing articulation of RL.9-10.5; also covers character and
   POV. Free, no account. (Note: OWL restructured its URLs in past
   years; this address was live on the check date.)
5. **Internal — literary terms guide** (see Internal resources
   above): the adult's vocabulary reference for plot, POV, irony,
   and tone terms used across Lessons 1–5.

## Access and review notes

- Every core objective is reachable with **free, no-account**
  materials: the unit's own texts, the two internal guides, and the
  Gutenberg/Wikipedia/OWL pages above.
- No runtimes or caption claims are made for the search-link videos
  because the specific items were not verified — the adult selects
  and previews.
- The LibriVox audiobook is linked from the Wikipedia article's
  external links (checked 2026-10-20 that the article lists it);
  the recording itself was not auditioned — adult previews before
  assigning as a listening alternative.
- Sensitive content: "The Monkey's Paw" is a horror story; the
  horror works by suggestion (see the content note in the excerpts
  file). The adult previews Excerpt C before assigning.
- Review date for all "verified" items above: **2026-10-20**.
