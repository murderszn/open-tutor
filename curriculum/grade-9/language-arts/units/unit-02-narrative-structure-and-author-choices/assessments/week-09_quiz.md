# Formative Quiz — Unit 02 (Session 15)

**30 points · ~30 minutes · closed-text** (no unit texts open; quotes
below are provided). Covers Lessons 1–4: structure, character, point
of view, pacing. **Reteach threshold:** below 24 — revisit the missed
skill in session 16 before Lesson 5. Solutions in
[answer-key.md](../answer-key.md#quiz).

**Directions:** answer in complete sentences where the points say so.
Partial credit is given for partially correct reasoning — show the
steps, not just the label.

## Part 1 — Structure (10 pts)

1. (2 pts) List the six plot stages in order.
2. (3 pts) A story opens with this line: "The handcuffs were already
   cold on June's wrists before she understood that the field trip was
   over." Name the structural choice and explain in one sentence what
   the reader gains *and* one sentence what the reader loses versus a
   chronological opening.
3. (2 pts) In "Night Train," the theft is the inciting incident but is
   told in a flashback *after* the train scene. Which plot stage does
   the train scene itself belong to?
4. (3 pts) **Error analysis:** "A flashback is just the author
   explaining the backstory." Agree or disagree in 2–3 sentences,
   using the Night Train flashback as your evidence.

## Part 2 — Character (8 pts)

5. (3 pts) Define **foil** in your own words. Then name the foil
   relationship in "Night Train" and state in one sentence what the
   foil reveals about Maya.
6. (2 pts) Sergeant-Major Morris says "I don't know" twice when asked
   whether he would take three more wishes, yet he also throws the paw
   on the fire and warns White "solemnly." Name **two** conflicting
   motivations shown in this exchange.
7. (3 pts) Is Mrs. White in Excerpts A–C a static or dynamic
   character? Take a position and defend it with **two** quoted
   details from different excerpts.

## Part 3 — Point of view and pacing (12 pts)

8. (2 pts) Name the three points of view studied and, for each, one
   thing that POV *cannot* show the reader.
9. (4 pts) Read the unreliable-narrator passage (Vignette A, Version
   3). List **two** details that contradict the narrator's claim that
   the plan is excellent and they are not scared. For each, state what
   the detail suggests.
10. (3 pts) In Vignette B ("The last hundred meters"), the first six
    sentences are very short and the last sentence is very long.
    Explain the effect of that pattern in 2–3 sentences.
11. (3 pts) In "Monkey's Paw" Excerpt C, the climax happens
    **offstage** — the reader hears knocks, bolts, and a chair
    scraping, but never sees what is at the door. Explain in 2–3
    sentences why Jacobs's choice to *withhold* the sight is more
    frightening than showing it would be.

**Scoring note:** items 2, 4, 7, 9, 10, 11 require reasoning, not just
labels — a correct label with no explanation earns half credit. See
the answer key for acceptable variation.
