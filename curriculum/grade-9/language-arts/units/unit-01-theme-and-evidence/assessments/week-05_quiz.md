# Formative Quiz — Unit 01 (Session 15)

*Administered in week 5 after Lessons 1–5. 30 points. The adult scores with
[answer-key.md](../answer-key.md#week-05-quiz) the same day; anything below
24 triggers targeted reteaching before the essay sessions (16–18). This quiz
is formative: revise wrong answers with the adult rather than recording a
grade.*

**Instructions to the learner:** Answer in complete sentences. For every
question that asks for evidence, give the quotation *and* its location.

---

## Part 1 — Evidence: observation, inference, strength (10 pts)

1. (2 pts) State one observation and one inference about the generated
   illustration in Lesson 1 (the girl with the salt box). Label each.
2. (3 pts) **Claim:** *Maya's brother avoids the kitchen work at the start
   of "The Salt Box."* Quote the single strongest line supporting this
   claim, give its paragraph number, and write one sentence explaining why
   it is stronger than the line "Her brother had taken the garage."
3. (2 pts) A classmate cites this line for the claim in question 2: "and
   her brother had thirds" (par. 20). Explain in two sentences why this
   evidence is weak *for this claim* — and name the different claim it
   actually supports well.
4. (3 pts) What is the difference between *strong* evidence and *thorough*
   evidence? Answer in two sentences, then name which one is missing when a
   claim has a single perfect quotation.

## Part 2 — Theme and summary (12 pts)

5. (2 pts) Which of these is a theme and which is a topic? *"Memory" /
   "Memory only survives when someone chooses to re-enact it."* Explain the
   difference in one sentence.
6. (4 pts) Draft a theme sentence for "The Audition" (one sentence). Then
   name one detail from the story that *complicates* your theme — a detail
   your first draft didn't account for.
7. (3 pts) Write an objective summary of "The Audition" in 50–70 words.
8. (3 pts) A classmate's summary says: *"Marcus is irresponsible about his
   job but his lazy brother surprisingly saves the day, which is sweet."*
   Identify three specific problems with this summary (opinions smuggled in,
   missing beats, or inaccuracies).

## Part 3 — Character (8 pts)

9. (4 pts) Name two conflicting motivations Marcus has in "The Audition."
   For each, quote one line (with paragraph number) that shows it.
10. (4 pts) Darnell says little but changes the story. In 3–4 sentences,
    explain what Darnell's *actions* reveal that his *words* hide, quoting
    one line of dialogue and one described action (with paragraph numbers).

---

*Total: /30. Scoring guide and sample answers are in the answer key — the
learner never sees them.*
