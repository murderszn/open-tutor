# Week 22 Formative Quiz — World Wars, Ideologies, Genocide, and Evidence

**Unit 05 · Week 22, Session 5 · ~20 minutes · Formative (not graded as a final mark)**

Answer in your notebook. The adult scores with the
[answer key](../answer-key.md) (Quiz section) and records a reteach
plan in the [teacher guide](../teacher-guide.md) §6. One point per
question unless noted.

1. **Causes.** Name the four MAIN causes of WWI and give one fact for
   each. (4 points)
2. **Trigger vs. cause.** In one sentence, explain the difference
   between the assassination of Franz Ferdinand and the alliance
   system as explanations for WWI.
3. **Versailles.** Name two terms of the Treaty of Versailles and, for
   each, name one group that resented it and why. (2 points)
4. **Ideologies.** Match each description to liberalism, fascism, or
   communism: (a) one-party rule in the name of the workers, state
   ownership; (b) individual rights, elections, free markets;
   (c) ultranationalism, a glorified leader, suppression of dissent.
5. **WWII scale.** Total WWII deaths are estimated at 70–85 million.
   About how many were civilians, and why is the figure a range?
6. **Evidence kinds.** A historian has three items about a genocide: a
   railway timetable, a survivor's account, and a population census.
   Name the evidence kind for each, and state one limit of the
   survivor's account.
7. **Genocide definition.** What two words do the work in the UN
   Genocide Convention's definition, and what does each one mean?
   (2 points)
8. **Institutions.** Name one postwar institution, state its purpose
   in one sentence, and name one of its limits.

**Total: 14 points.** 12–14 = ready to move on; 9–11 = review the
flagged lessons with the teacher guide's reteach steps; below 9 =
re-teach Lessons 01–04 before Week 23.
