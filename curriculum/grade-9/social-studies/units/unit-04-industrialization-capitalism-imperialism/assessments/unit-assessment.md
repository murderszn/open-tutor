# Unit Assessment — Unit 04: Industrialization, Capitalism, Imperialism, and Social Change

**Grade 9 · Social Studies · Week 18, Session 5 · ~50 minutes**

Covers all five unit objectives. The adult scores with `answer-key.md`
§Unit assessment. The dossier (project.md) is submitted with this
assessment; the two together close the unit.

## Part A — Data task (Objectives 1–2)

Use the pig-iron table from Practice Set A (1740: 17,000 tons; 1788:
70,000; 1805: 250,000; 1830: 650,000; 1852: 2,700,000) and the coal
figures (1700: just under 3 million tons; 1815: ~16 million; 1830:
over 30 million).

1. Compute the growth factor for pig iron from 1788 to 1830. Show your
   division.
2. In absolute tons, which grew more between its first and last
   figures: pig iron (1740–1852) or coal (1700–1830)? Show both
   subtractions.
3. In two sentences, explain what the two data series together show
   about the scale of industrial change — and one thing they do not
   show (for example, wages, working conditions, or who profited).

## Part B — Source comparison (Objectives 2, 4)

Re-read model passages C (administrator) and D (colonized
intellectual) in Lesson 04. Both are original modeled writing for this
unit, not real documents.

4. State two facts on which C and D agree.
5. State the judgment on which they most differ, and quote or
   paraphrase one line from each that shows it.
6. Which passage's claims are easier to verify, and how would you
   verify one of them? Name a concrete check (a record, a visit, a
   count).

## Part C — Map and explanation (Objective 3)

7. On a blank Africa map, mark: (a) the Congo Free State's region,
   (b) Egypt and the Suez route, (c) Ethiopia, (d) Liberia. Label each
   with its status by 1914 (European-controlled or independent, and by
   whom where applicable).
8. In three sentences, explain the "effective occupation" rule and the
   unintended consequence it produced.

## Part D — Argument essay (Objective 5)

9. Write a 3–4 paragraph essay answering: *Was the new imperialism
   primarily economic or primarily political?* Requirements: a thesis
   in the first paragraph; at least three pieces of evidence from
   different lessons or source kinds; one counterclaim, answered; and
   a final sentence stating what your sources cannot show.

**Scoring:** Part A: 6 points (2 per item). Part B: 6 points (2 per
item). Part C: 6 points (map 3, explanation 3). Part D: 12 points
(thesis 2, evidence 6, counterclaim 2, source-limit 2). Total 30.
24+ = unit standard met; 19–23 = revise the weakest part with the
teacher guide's reteach notes; below 19 = reteach Weeks 17–18 before
U05.
