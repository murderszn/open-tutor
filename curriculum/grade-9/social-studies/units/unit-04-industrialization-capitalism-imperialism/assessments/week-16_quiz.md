# Week-16 Formative Quiz — Unit 04

**Grade 9 · Social Studies · Week 16, Session 5 · ~25 minutes · 10 items**

Covers Lessons 01–02 (Weeks 15–16). The adult scores with
`answer-key.md` §Quiz and records a reteach plan in the teacher guide.

1. Name two of Britain's four advantages for industrialization from
   Lesson 01, and explain briefly why each one mattered.
2. What did Watt improve about the steam engine, and why did that
   improvement matter for factories? (Two-part answer.)
3. British pig-iron output was about 17,000 tons in 1740 and about
   2,700,000 tons in 1852. By about what factor did it grow? Show your
   division.
4. Explain the difference between cottage industry and the factory
   system in two sentences: who owned the tools, and who set the hours?
5. The mill-town image in Lesson 02 shows chimneys, a canal with a
   barge, rows of identical cottages, and a church spire. Name one
   inference the image supports and one claim it does *not* prove.
6. State two provisions of the 1833 Factory Act and the abuse each
   one targeted.
7. Why did the 1833 Act still leave most child labor in place? Give
   two reasons.
8. A classmate says, "Luddites hated machines." In one or two
   sentences, correct the claim using the croppers' wage motive.
9. In the inspector/owner model passages (Lesson 02), which writer
   gives more checkable details, and why does that matter?
10. Short argument (3–5 sentences): *Did industrialization improve
    ordinary lives by 1850?* State a claim, give one piece of
    evidence, and name one limit of your claim.

**Scoring:** Items 1–9 are worth 2 points each (1 for a partial
answer); item 10 is worth 4 points (claim 1, evidence 1, limit 1,
clarity 1). Total 22. 18+ = on track; 14–17 = review Lesson 01 or 02
concepts and retest the missed items; below 14 = reteach the week
before continuing (teacher guide §5).
