# Resource Pack — Unit 04: Industrialization, Capitalism, Imperialism, and Social Change

**Goal:** support a grade-9 learner (with a guiding adult) in explaining
why industrialization began in Britain, analyzing its effects on labor
and cities, connecting industrial demand to the new imperialism, and
comparing resistance, reform, and competing economic visions.
**Age fit:** grade 9; the adult previews all Week 17 sources and all
video choices before use.

**Checked 2026-11-02.** Every external item below states whether it is
a direct resource or a labeled search link. No runtime, caption, or age
claim is made that was not checked. Videos supplement teaching; they do
not replace it. At least one free, no-account route supports every core
objective: the UK Parliament Factory Act page, the General Act full
text, the internal repository guides, and this unit's lessons
themselves.

## Web references (direct, checked 2026-11-02)

1. **World History Encyclopedia — British Industrial Revolution.**
   <https://www.worldhistory.org/British_Industrial_Revolution/> —
   overview of causes (coal, coke from 1709, high wages, capital),
   inventions, and urbanization, dated c. 1760–1840. Free, no account.
   Use for Lesson 01 background. Confirmed live in search results,
   2026-11-02.
2. **UK Parliament — The 1833 Factory Act.**
   <https://www.parliament.uk/about/living-heritage/transformingsociety/livinglearning/19thcentury/overview/factoryact/> —
   official summary: under-9 ban, 48-hour week and 2 hours' schooling
   for ages 9–13, 12-hour day for 13–18, and the four-man inspectorate
   for ~4,000 mills. Free, no account. Use for Lesson 02.
3. **General Act of the Conference at Berlin, 1884–1885 (full text).**
   <https://african-volunteer.net/general_act_of_the_berlin_conference_full_text.html> —
   full-text transcription of the February 26, 1885 General Act
   (free trade, Congo and Niger navigation, rules for future
   occupation). Free, no account. Treat as a transcription for
   reference; this unit teaches the Act in paraphrase. Use for
   Lesson 03.
4. **C3 Framework, Dimension 2 (all social studies).**
   <https://history.appstate.edu/sites/history.appstate.edu/files/c3_framework_-_dimension_2_-_all_social_studies.pdf> —
   teacher-side reference confirming the D2.Eco.1, D2.Eco.2, D2.Eco.3,
   D2.Eco.7, and D2.Eco.8 indicator wordings used in this unit. Free,
   no account. Teacher use only.
5. **Scramble for Africa background (labeled search).** Search the
   query `site:britannica.com scramble for Africa Berlin Conference`
   and choose only britannica.com results; preview with the adult
   before assigning. (Labeled search; no article title is claimed as
   verified.)
