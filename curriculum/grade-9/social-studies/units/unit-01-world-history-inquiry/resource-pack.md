# Unit 01 Resource Pack

**Summary (adult planning judgment):** A source shelf for a grade-9
learner's first world-history inquiry unit — Afro-Eurasian trade systems,
traveler accounts, American and Pacific societies circa 1450, and the
source-analysis toolkit. All core objectives have a free, no-account path.

4. `Mexica Triple Alliance Tenochtitlan tribute chinampa`

## Access notes

- Every web reference above is free with no account. Khan Academy and
  OER Project work without login; Met, Google Arts & Culture, and UCLA
  pages are open.
- Core learning never depends on video: each video search link has a
  text alternative in the lessons.
- The adult previews all external pages for ads, comments, and age fit
  before assigning; YouTube searches must be previewed, never assigned
  blind.

## Review notes

- URLs verified 2026-10-13 via search-result resolution (titles and
  publishers matched); pages were not all opened end-to-end — the adult
  opens each before assigning.
- Population and size figures in lessons are labeled estimates with
  ranges; the unit never presents a single disputed number as fact.
- The two modeled traveler passages are original retellings written for
