# Unit 01 — Formative Quiz (end of Week 4)

**Grade 9 Social Studies · 25 minutes · Closed-note except your Lesson 01 map**
*Covers Lessons 01–02. Solutions in `answer-key.md`.*

**1.** Name the three Afro-Eurasian connection systems. (3 pts)

**2.** For the Indian Ocean network, name: (a) the wind system that
scheduled voyages, (b) two port cities, (c) two goods traded. (5 pts)

**3.** The core trans-Saharan exchange was gold for _______. Name one
West African source of each. (3 pts)

**4.** In 2–3 sentences, explain why the Silk Roads are better described
as a *web* than a *road*. (3 pts)

**5.** A historian reads this line from a traveler's account: *"The
markets of Calicut overflowed with pepper, and ships from Arabia crowded
the harbor."* State: (a) whether it is a primary or secondary source,
(b) one thing it can show, (c) one thing it cannot show. (3 pts)

**6.** Name two *non-goods* that traveled Afro-Eurasian networks and give
the route each used. (4 pts)

**7.** Short effect chain: *"The winter monsoon blows from Asia toward
East Africa."* Give two linked effects on trade. (4 pts)

**8.** Which connection model — Ibn Battuta's merchant network or Zheng
He's state fleets — was still operating in 1450, and why did the other
stop? (3 pts)

**9.** Look at the port illustration (`assets/`): name two exchange items
visible and one sentence the image cannot prove about 1450. (3 pts)

**10.** **Exit-style:** In one sentence, explain what "circa" signals
when a historian writes "circa 1450." (2 pts)

**Total: 33 pts.** 28+ = on track · 22–27 = review Lesson 02 with the
adult · below 22 = reteach week 4 before continuing (see teacher guide).
