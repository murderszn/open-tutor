# Unit 01 — Culminating Assessment (end of Week 6)

**Grade 9 Social Studies · 60 minutes · Open lessons and map; closed
practice sets.** *Solutions and scoring guidance in `answer-key.md`.
This is a draft assessment, not a standardized or validated instrument.*

---

## Part A — Source analysis (30 pts)

Read the two modeled sources, then answer.

**Source 1** (modeled retelling of a Rihla episode, written for this
unit): *"In the Maldive islands the people dry fish for trade, weave
rope from coconut fiber, and sell cowrie shells that serve as money as
far away as Mali. I stayed eighteen months and found their trade reached
every shore of the ocean."*

**Source 2** (modeled Venetian merchant ledger entry, written for this
unit): *"12 March 1451, Alexandria — bought 40 sacks Malabar pepper at
22 ducats the sack; freight to Venice 6 ducats. Sold 3 April at 41
ducats the sack."*

**A1.** Classify each source: kind (primary/secondary), maker, and one
limit as evidence about circa-1450 trade. (12 pts — 6 per source)

**A2.** What can the two sources show *together* that neither shows
alone? Answer in 3–4 sentences. (8 pts)

**A3.** A historian wants to know whether ordinary porters in Calicut
prospered from this trade. Explain in 2–3 sentences why *neither* source
answers that well, and name the source type that could. (10 pts)

## Part B — Map and data (20 pts)

**B1.** On the outline map provided by the adult: shade the three
Afro-Eurasian connection systems, label six nodes, and annotate two
goods per system with their origin regions. (12 pts)

**B2.** Using `resources/un_countries.csv` (region, subregion, area_km2
columns only): list three countries whose `subregion` is "South-Eastern
Asia," give their `area_km2` values, and write two sentences explaining
what this modern data can and cannot tell us about Malacca's importance
circa 1450. (8 pts)

## Part C — Argument essay (30 pts)

**C1.** *Which connection mattered most to the world circa 1450?*
Write 300–400 words: an arguable claim with an explicit yardstick, at
least two evidence pieces from different source kinds, a fairly stated
counterclaim, and a limits sentence. (30 pts — see project rubric rows
*Evidence use*, *Argument structure*, *Historical thinking*.)

---

**Total: 80 pts.** 68+ = exemplary · 54–67 = proficient · 40–53 =
developing · below 40 = reteach and reassess Part(s) below proficient
(see teacher guide). Part C below proficient requires revision before
U02 regardless of total.
