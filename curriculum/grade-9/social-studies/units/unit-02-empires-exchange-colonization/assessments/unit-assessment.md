# Unit 02 Assessment — Empires, Exchange, Colonization, and Resistance

**Week 10, S5 · ~50 minutes · Part A closed book; Part B open dossier**

## Part A — Knowledge and reasoning (closed book, 25 min)

**A1. Maritime turn (4 pts).** Explain in 4–5 sentences *why* Portuguese and
Spanish ships crossed oceans after 1450 (at least two motives) and *how*
they could do it (at least two means).

**A2. Columbian exchange (5 pts).** Draw and label a two-arrow diagram:
Americas → Afro-Eurasia and Afro-Eurasia → Americas. Place at least three
correct items on each arrow (crops, animals, or disease). Below the diagram,
write two sentences explaining one demographic effect and one economic
effect of the exchange, using hedged language where scholars debate the
numbers.

**A3. Conquest causes (5 pts).** "The Inca fell because Pizarro had guns."
Write a paragraph (5–6 sentences) replacing this single-cause claim with a
multi-cause explanation. Name at least three interacting causes and explain
how they connected.

**A4. Slave trade as a system (6 pts).** Using the Slave Voyages figures,
answer: (a) About how many people were embarked, disembarked, and died in
the Middle Passage between 1501 and 1866? (b) What do these figures
*exclude*? (c) Name two business institutions that made the trade routine,
and explain in one sentence why calling it a "capitalized business" matters
for understanding its 365-year persistence.

## Part B — Source analysis and argument (open dossier, 25 min)

**B1. Source limits (6 pts).** You are given two sources (adult-selected
from the unit): one European merchant's record and one account by or about
an enslaved or Indigenous person. For *each* source, state: maker, likely
purpose, one thing it shows, one thing it hides, and one inquiry it is
useful for. (D2.His.8.9-12; D3.1–D3.4.9-12)

**B2. Empire comparison (6 pts).** Choose two of the five empires from
Lesson 04. Compare them on *two* of the four frame questions (legitimacy,
governance of diversity, wealth source, trade connection). State one
similarity and one difference, each supported by a fact.

**B3. Unit argument (8 pts).** Answer in 150–250 words: *Between 1450 and
1750, did exchange or empire change the world more?* Defend your claim with
at least two pieces of evidence from the unit, acknowledge the strongest
counterargument in one sentence, and name one limit of the sources your
argument relies on.

**Total: 40 pts.** Bands: 34–40 exemplary; 27–33 proficient; 20–26
developing (targeted reteach per [teacher-guide](../teacher-guide.md) §7);
below 20: revisit the unit's weakest week with the adult before U03.
This assessment is a course draft instrument, **not** a standardized or
validated test.
