# Week 08 Formative Quiz — Unit 02

**Weeks 7–8 coverage · ~25 minutes · closed book, then open-note correction**

*Administer at the Week 8 S5 review. Score with the
[answer key](../answer-key.md). After scoring, the learner corrects missed
items with notes open — the correction, not the first score, is the learning
record (see [teacher-guide](../teacher-guide.md) §7).*

**1. Vocabulary (4 pts).** Match each term to its definition:

| Term | Definition |
|---|---|
| a. caravel | i. triangular sail letting ships sail closer to the wind |
| b. lateen sail | ii. small, fast ship combining square and triangular sails |
| c. encomienda | iii. rotational Indigenous labor draft used at Potosí |
| d. mita | iv. crown grant of Indigenous labor/tribute to a colonist |

**2. Motives and means (3 pts).** Name the three motives historians
summarize as "God, gold, and glory," and name two *means* (technologies or
knowledge) that made ocean crossings possible.

**3. Sequencing (2 pts).** Order these events earliest → latest:
(a) da Gama reaches Calicut, (b) Dias rounds the Cape of Good Hope,
(c) Treaty of Tordesillas, (d) Columbus reaches the Caribbean.

**4. Multiple causes (3 pts).** The fall of Tenochtitlan (1521) is best
explained by:
(a) Spanish guns alone · (b) disease, Indigenous alliances, political
capture/siege, and military technology acting together · (c) Moctezuma's
personal cowardice · (d) a single decisive battle.
Choose and justify in two sentences.

**5. Two-direction exchange (4 pts).** Name two things that moved from the
Americas to Afro-Eurasia and two that moved the other way after 1492.
For *one* of your four, state one effect on the receiving society.

**6. Labor systems (4 pts).** In two sentences each, explain how labor was
recruited under (a) the encomienda and (b) the Potosí mita. Then name one
similarity between them.

**Total: 20 pts.** Scoring bands: 17–20 secure (proceed); 13–16 partial
(reteach missed concepts in Week 9 S5); below 13 (revisit Lessons 01–02
with the teacher guide's reteach prompts before the Week 9 sessions).
