# 🌌 Astronomy Fundamentals

This guide covers the basics of our solar system, stars, and galaxies.

## 1. The Solar System
Our solar system consists of a central star (the Sun), eight planets, dwarf planets, moons, asteroids, and comets.

### The Inner Planets (Terrestrial)
- **Mercury**: Closest to the sun, extremely hot, no atmosphere.
- **Venus**: Thick, toxic atmosphere, hottest planet due to greenhouse effect.
- **Earth**: The only planet known to support life, has liquid water.
- **Mars**: The "Red Planet", cold desert world, has the largest volcano (Olympus Mons).

### The Outer Planets (Gas Giants)
- **Jupiter**: Largest planet, massive storms like the Great Red Spot.
- **Saturn**: Known for its spectacular ring system made of ice and rock.
- **Uranus**: Ice giant, rotates on its side.
- **Neptune**: Furthest planet, incredibly strong winds, deep blue color.

## 2. Stars and The Sun
A **star** is a massive ball of glowing gas (mostly hydrogen and helium) that produces energy through nuclear fusion. 
- **The Sun** is a medium-sized yellow dwarf star. It provides the energy necessary for life on Earth.
- **Life Cycle of a Star**:
  1. **Nebula**: A cloud of gas and dust.
  2. **Protostar**: Gravity pulls the dust together.
  3. **Main Sequence**: The star burns hydrogen (where our Sun is now).
  4. **Red Giant / Supergiant**: The star expands as it runs out of fuel.
  5. **White Dwarf / Neutron Star / Black Hole**: The final stage depending on the star's original mass.

## 3. Galaxies and The Universe
A **galaxy** is a huge collection of gas, dust, and billions of stars and their solar systems, held together by gravity.
- **The Milky Way**: Our home galaxy, a spiral galaxy.
- **The Universe**: Contains all of space and time, including all galaxies, stars, and planets.
