# 🖥️ Computer Parts & Custom PC Basics

Learn the core parts of a modern desktop computer, what each part does, and how they work together in a complete system.

## Core Parts of a Functional PC

1. **CPU (Central Processing Unit)**
   - The “brain” of the computer.
   - Runs instructions and calculations.
   - Key terms: cores, threads, clock speed.

2. **Motherboard**
   - The main circuit board connecting all components.
   - Determines CPU compatibility, RAM type, and expansion options.
   - Key terms: socket, chipset, form factor (ATX/mATX/ITX).

3. **RAM (Memory)**
   - Short-term working memory for active programs.
   - More RAM helps with multitasking and bigger projects.
   - Key terms: DDR4/DDR5, capacity (GB), speed (MT/s).

4. **Storage (SSD/HDD)**
   - Long-term file storage (operating system, games, projects).
   - SSDs are much faster than HDDs.
   - Key terms: NVMe, SATA, capacity (GB/TB).

5. **Graphics Card (GPU)**
   - Renders images, video, and 3D graphics.
   - Important for gaming, 3D work, and video editing.
   - Key terms: VRAM, ray tracing, display outputs.

6. **Power Supply (PSU)**
   - Converts wall power into safe power for components.
   - Must provide enough wattage and quality protection.
   - Key terms: wattage, 80 PLUS rating, modular cables.

7. **Case**
   - Physical enclosure that holds and protects parts.
   - Affects airflow, cooling, and build size.
   - Key terms: airflow, fan mounts, cable management.

8. **Cooling**
   - Keeps components at safe temperatures.
   - Can include air coolers, case fans, or liquid cooling.
   - Key terms: intake/exhaust, thermal paste.

## How the Parts Work Together

- The **motherboard** is the foundation.
- The **CPU** computes instructions.
- The **RAM** holds active data for quick access.
- The **storage** keeps files permanently.
- The **GPU** draws visuals.
- The **PSU** powers everything.
- The **case + cooling** keep the build protected and stable.

## Example Modern Midrange Parts List (Reference)

- CPU: AMD Ryzen 5 7600 or Intel Core i5-14400F
- Motherboard: B650 (AM5) or B760 (LGA1700)
- RAM: 32GB (2x16GB) DDR5-6000
- Storage: 1TB NVMe Gen4 SSD
- GPU: NVIDIA GeForce RTX 4060 / AMD Radeon RX 7600
- PSU: 650W 80+ Gold
- Case: Airflow-focused ATX mid tower
- Cooling: 1 tower CPU cooler + 2–4 case fans

> Note: Exact best-value parts change over time. Always re-check pricing and compatibility before buying.

## Build-a-Computer Exercise (PCPartPicker)

1. Open the **System Builder**.
2. Create one build for your goal:
   - Budget school/work PC
   - 1080p gaming PC
   - Creator/editing PC
3. Add all required parts (CPU, motherboard, RAM, storage, GPU if needed, PSU, case, cooling).
4. Check for compatibility notes/warnings.
5. Export or screenshot your parts list.
6. Write a short reflection:
   - Why you chose each part.
   - Total cost and one way to lower it.
   - One future upgrade path.
