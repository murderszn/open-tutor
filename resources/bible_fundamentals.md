# Bible Fundamentals — Overview, Study Habits, and Core Themes

## Summary
A family-friendly Bible study guide with age-appropriate structure for upper elementary and middle school learners.

## Big Picture Structure
- **Old Testament:** Creation, covenant, kings, prophets, wisdom writings.
- **New Testament:** Jesus’ life and teachings, early church, letters, Revelation.

## Core Themes
1. God’s character (holy, loving, just, merciful)
2. Wisdom and daily choices
3. Faith, obedience, and courage
4. Forgiveness, grace, and reconciliation
5. Service, generosity, and community

## Study Method (SOAP)
- **S — Scripture:** Write the key verse/passage.
- **O — Observation:** What is happening? Who is speaking?
- **A — Application:** What does this mean for my life today?
- **P — Prayer:** Short response prayer.

## Advanced learner (Grade 8) Prompt Set
- Explain the context of this passage in 3–5 sentences.
- Identify one repeated word or idea and why it matters.
- Compare this passage with another related text.
- Write one concrete action you can take this week.

## Core learner (Grade 5) Prompt Set
- Who are the main people in this story?
- What happened first, next, and last?
- What is one lesson from this story?
- Draw a scene and label 2 details.

## Suggested Summer Sequence (8 Weeks)
1. Creation and identity (Genesis 1–2)
2. Wisdom for choices (selected Proverbs)
3. Courage under pressure (David and Goliath)
4. Trust and prayer (selected Psalms)
5. Jesus’ parables (Luke 10, Luke 15)
6. Serving others (Mark 10:45; James 2)
7. Forgiveness and restoration (Luke 15; Philemon overview)
8. Review and testimony week (favorite passage + reflection)
