# K–8 Planning Hub

This hub is a parent/teacher planning and placement reference for K–8. The canonical instructional material lives under the matching independent [grade track](../../curriculum/README.md); choose that one grade track for one learner at a time. These planning notes support adults as they sequence and review instruction. They are not a parallel curriculum, standards crosswalk, accreditation claim, or substitute for local requirements.

## Start here

1. Read [coverage and limits](./coverage.md) to understand what is present and what is not.
2. Use [assessment and placement](./assessment-and-placement.md) to choose a starting point from current work, not age alone.
3. Open the matching grade profile below, then continue only within that grade's folder in `curriculum/`.
4. Review the grade's current coverage status and select/supply full-year instructional materials where the track remains incomplete.

## Design notes

- The profiles are examples for adult planning. They do not overrule the matching canonical grade track or establish mastery.
- The four quarters are planning blocks, not fixed dates or daily pacing. Revisit prerequisites and extend a block when evidence shows it is needed.
- Read-alouds, oral responses, manipulatives, dictation, drawing, and adult scribing are valid ways for a young learner to show thinking. Young children need not use a screen, type, or maintain an account.
- In K–2, protect daily adult-led oral language, phonemic awareness, explicit systematic phonics, cumulative decodable reading, handwriting, and composition. Read-aloud comprehension and knowledge-building continue alongside decoding.
- A parent/teacher chooses or supplements the year-long core materials, provides needed consumables and texts, reviews work privately, and checks local requirements. This hub does not provide a complete phonics scope and sequence, literature set, math practice program, lab safety plan, or state compliance map.
