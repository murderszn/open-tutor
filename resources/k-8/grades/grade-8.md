# Grade 8 Planning Profile

## Example measurable outcomes

This is a supplemental planning example. Use only the matching [canonical Grade 8 track](../../../curriculum/grade-8/README.md); its audit/scope and sequence takes precedence if this outline differs.

- **Math:** Solve linear equations with rational coefficients and systems of two linear equations; describe slope and intercept in context; apply integer exponents and scientific notation; use the Pythagorean theorem and compare bivariate patterns.
- **Language arts:** Analyze how evidence, structure, and word choice develop an argument or theme; integrate and cite multiple sources; write a sustained argument or explanatory essay with counterclaim, logical organization, and revision; deliver an evidence-based presentation.
- **Science:** Explain matter/energy or force relationships with a model and quantitative evidence; connect natural selection to population-level change using evidence; evaluate a designed solution and explain trade-offs.
- **Social studies:** Develop a focused inquiry question; corroborate 3 or more sources while noting perspective/context; make a defensible historical, geographic, economic, or civic claim; propose an informed action and evaluate a likely consequence.

## Four-quarter sequence

| Quarter | Sequence, prerequisite, and evidence check |
|---|---|
| 1 — Linear relationships | **Prerequisite:** rational operations, expressions and two-step equations. **Math:** graph proportional and linear relationships, interpret slope/intercept. **ELA:** analyze argument structure and evaluate supporting evidence. **Science:** forces/matter models and quantitative relationships. **Social studies:** inquiry question and source context. **Check:** represent one relationship in table, graph and equation; annotate a claim and test its support; explain source creator/context. |
| 2 — Equations and systems | **Prerequisite:** one-variable equations and coordinate plane. **Math:** solve multi-step linear equations and systems by graphing or substitution. **ELA:** develop a defensible thesis, counterclaim and evidence chain. **Science:** natural selection or energy transformation with a model and data. **Social studies:** corroborate primary/secondary accounts. **Check:** solve and verify a system in context; produce an outline with citations; support a science explanation with two data points. |
| 3 — Geometry and research | **Prerequisite:** square roots/squares, linear equations and source notes. **Math:** Pythagorean theorem, volume, transformations and bivariate association. **ELA:** draft, cite, revise and present a research-based argument. **Science:** design/evaluate a solution with criteria and constraints. **Social studies:** analyze institutional or economic choices and consequences. **Check:** solve a practical geometry task; submit cited draft; compare a design against criteria and trade-offs. |
| 4 — Transition and synthesis | **Prerequisite:** revisit algebra fluency and evidence attribution. **Math:** connect linear models, systems, exponents and statistics; preview high-school readiness gaps. **ELA:** independently synthesize multiple texts and present a polished claim. **Science:** defend a model and name limits. **Social studies:** complete an inquiry with evidence and evaluate an action. **Check:** portfolio conference and fresh transfer tasks; record what remains to teach before high-school course placement. |

## Beyond the core

Encourage arts electives or portfolio work, a regular movement/fitness plan, meal preparation, budgeting and comparison shopping, digital privacy habits, basic repair, and planning a community contribution.
