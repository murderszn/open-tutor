# Grade 4 Planning Profile

## Example measurable outcomes

This is a supplemental planning example. Use only the matching [canonical Grade 4 track](../../../curriculum/grade-4/README.md); its audit/scope and sequence takes precedence if this outline differs.

- **Math:** Multiply a 4-digit number by a 1-digit number and divide up to 4-digit dividends by 1-digit divisors with a strategy; compare fractions with related denominators; convert within one measurement system; find area/perimeter and interpret data. First-quadrant coordinate plotting can be an optional preview.
- **Language arts:** Summarize literary and informational texts; refer to evidence and explain an inference; write a multi-paragraph informative piece with grouped facts, transitions, and a source list; edit sentence boundaries and common spelling patterns.
- **Science:** Use evidence to explain how external structures support survival; model energy transfer in a simple system; plan an investigation with a measurable variable and repeated data.
- **Social studies:** Locate regions and physical features; compare primary/secondary sources for perspective; explain how local/state institutions address a public need; use examples of scarcity and trade-offs.

## Four-quarter sequence

| Quarter | Sequence, prerequisite, and evidence check |
|---|---|
| 1 — Multiplicative reasoning | **Prerequisite:** multiplication/division facts and place value. **Math:** multi-digit multiplication and division models. **ELA:** close reading, summary, paragraph evidence. **Science:** structures and function. **Social studies:** maps, regions and local/state government. **Check:** solve and estimate a multiplication/division pair; quote/paraphrase a detail to support an inference; label 5 map features. |
| 2 — Fractions and research | **Prerequisite:** equivalent groups and fraction unit meanings. **Math:** equivalent fractions, comparison, fraction operations with like denominators. **ELA:** research question, notes from 2 sources, multi-paragraph draft. **Science:** energy transfer and observable systems. **Social studies:** source types and state/regional history. **Check:** model equivalent fractions; produce notes with source titles; explain a science model with a labeled diagram. |
| 3 — Measurement and data | **Prerequisite:** fraction comparison and unit conversions in familiar cases. **Math:** measurement conversion, area/perimeter and data (optional first-quadrant coordinate preview). **ELA:** revise for organization, transitions, evidence and conventions. **Science:** design controlled test, graph data. **Social studies:** scarcity, production, exchange and civic problem-solving. **Check:** solve a multistep measurement problem; revise a paragraph; graph 5+ measurements and explain pattern. |
| 4 — Integrate | **Prerequisite:** check division strategies and research note quality. **Math:** mixed operation and fraction applications. **ELA:** present researched explanation with evidence. **Science:** connect data to a claim and limitation. **Social studies:** compare two sources and present a regional inquiry. **Check:** portfolio includes one original solution, revised writing, investigation and evidence-based civic/history explanation. |

## Beyond the core

Include visual art and design, music practice, regular aerobic and strength play, cooking with fractions, simple mending/building, map-based outings, and shared household responsibilities.
