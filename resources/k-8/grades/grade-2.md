# Grade 2 Planning Profile

An adult should continue to teach foundational reading directly where needed; grade placement alone does not mean decoding is automatic. Learners can show knowledge orally or with paper materials.

This is a supplemental planning example. Use only the matching [canonical Grade 2 track](../../../curriculum/grade-2/README.md); its audit/scope and sequence takes precedence if this outline differs.

## Example measurable outcomes

- **Math:** Add/subtract within 100 using place-value strategies; explain hundreds/tens/ones in a 3-digit number; use equal groups/arrays to model early multiplication; measure in standard units and answer questions from a bar graph.
- **Language arts:** Build oral vocabulary and comprehension through discussion and read-alouds; assess and practice phonemic awareness as needed; continue explicit systematic phonics with cumulative decodable text matched to taught patterns; decode unfamiliar words with taught vowel teams/syllable patterns, spell taught patterns, form letters legibly, and read connected text accurately enough to retell; summarize an informational text with 3 key facts and write a paragraph with a topic, details, and ending.
- **Science:** Describe how a plant/animal depends on its environment; use a simple model of how water changes state or moves; plan a fair test by changing one factor and recording repeated observations.
- **Social studies:** Compare 2 community sources/maps for what each shows; describe how rules and decision-making serve a group; distinguish a need from a want and give an example of exchange; explain one past/present change using a source detail.

## Four-quarter sequence

| Quarter | Sequence, prerequisite, and evidence check |
|---|---|
| 1 — Place value and connected reading | **Prerequisite:** assess within-20 facts, two-digit understanding, decoding and oral retell. **Math:** extend place value to hundreds. **ELA:** cumulative phonics, spelling, decodable/connected text, vocabulary and read-aloud comprehension. **Science:** compare habitats. **Social studies:** maps and community roles. **Check:** explain 326 with base-ten pieces; decode a new taught-pattern list and retell a paragraph; identify a map key. |
| 2 — Add/subtract and describe | **Prerequisite:** compose tens and read taught patterns. **Math:** add/subtract within 100 with models, then written strategies. **ELA:** paragraph planning, sentence expansion, text evidence orally/in writing. **Science:** observe a life cycle or local habitat. **Social studies:** needs, wants, goods/services. **Check:** solve a two-step within-100 context problem and write a 4–5 sentence fact paragraph citing a source. |
| 3 — Equal groups and measurement | **Prerequisite:** secure addition/subtraction strategies. **Math:** arrays/equal groups as a bridge to multiplication; standard measurement and graphs. **ELA:** word parts and longer taught patterns; informational text features. **Science:** design one-variable comparison and record results. **Social studies:** compare past/present objects or accounts. **Check:** draw two arrays for the same total, measure/graph a small data set, and label observation vs conclusion. |
| 4 — Explain and synthesize | **Prerequisite:** revisit decoding gaps and place-value regrouping. **Math:** mixed add/subtract, groups, time/money contexts and data. **ELA:** independent reading at instructional level, summarize and revise paragraph. **Science:** explain a system/change with a model. **Social studies:** make a claim from two sources. **Check:** new problem, unfamiliar short text and investigation explanation; use results to plan grade 3. |

## Beyond the core

Add visual art, singing/instrument patterns, dance or sport, gardening, meal preparation, simple budgeting with play money, and neighborhood observation. Adult manages safety and any online work.
