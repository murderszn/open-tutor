# Grade 7 Planning Profile

## Example measurable outcomes

This is a supplemental planning example. Use only the matching [canonical Grade 7 track](../../../curriculum/grade-7/README.md); its audit/scope and sequence takes precedence if this outline differs.

- **Math:** Add, subtract, multiply, and divide rational numbers; solve proportional and percent problems; simplify linear expressions and solve two-step equations; compare populations with random samples and summarize variability.
- **Language arts:** Analyze how an author develops a claim or theme; cite multiple relevant details; write a coherent argument with reasons, evidence, and a response to an opposing view; present a short research synthesis with attributed sources.
- **Science:** Explain how matter and energy move through an ecosystem; interpret motion/force or chemical-change data; evaluate a design by testing and revising against explicit criteria.
- **Social studies:** Evaluate sourcing, perspective, and corroboration across 3 sources; explain a historical/geographic pattern with a map or timeline; analyze how a rule, policy, or market decision affects groups differently.

## Four-quarter sequence

| Quarter | Sequence, prerequisite, and evidence check |
|---|---|
| 1 — Rational number fluency | **Prerequisite:** fraction/decimal computation and ratio reasoning. **Math:** all rational operations; proportional relationships. **ELA:** claims, themes, inference and evidence. **Science:** ecosystems and matter/energy models. **Social studies:** maps, chronology and source evaluation. **Check:** solve a multistep rational context; annotate 3 relevant text details; model a matter/energy pathway. |
| 2 — Proportions and argument | **Prerequisite:** unit rate and integer operations. **Math:** percent, scale, proportions and sampling. **ELA:** evidence-based argument and counterargument. **Science:** motion/force or chemical change with data tables. **Social studies:** compare historical accounts and policy impacts. **Check:** solve a percent/scale problem and justify; write a claim with two sourced reasons; identify one data trend and one caveat. |
| 3 — Equations and investigation | **Prerequisite:** equivalent expressions and inverse operations. **Math:** two-step equations and angle/area contexts. **ELA:** research question, source synthesis and citation. **Science:** plan controlled investigation or iterative design. **Social studies:** economics, institutions and civic decision-making. **Check:** solve and verify equation; create source matrix from 3 sources; document one design revision linked to test evidence. |
| 4 — Analyze and communicate | **Prerequisite:** revisit operation errors and evidence integration. **Math:** statistics, sampling and mixed applications. **ELA:** revise argument for logic and counterpoint; oral presentation. **Science:** explanatory model from evidence. **Social studies:** communicate a historical or civic inquiry conclusion. **Check:** interpret an unseen sample/data set and defend conclusions orally or in writing. |

## Beyond the core

Provide arts electives, team/individual sport or movement goals, cooking and food safety, personal organization, household maintenance, and practice comparing costs for a planned purchase.
