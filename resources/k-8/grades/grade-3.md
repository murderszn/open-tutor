# Grade 3 Planning Profile

## Example measurable outcomes

This is a supplemental planning example. Use only the matching [canonical Grade 3 track](../../../curriculum/grade-3/README.md); its audit/scope and sequence takes precedence if this outline differs.

- **Math:** Recall multiplication/division facts within 100 with strategies; solve one-step equal-group and additive-comparison problems; add/subtract within 1,000; identify unit fractions and interpret area/perimeter.
- **Language arts:** Read grade-appropriate text with accuracy and phrasing; summarize a story and an informational passage; cite a specific detail when answering; write an organized paragraph with evidence and conventions reviewed.
- **Science:** Build a model explaining how organisms obtain what they need; describe how forces affect motion; use a table or picture graph to compare repeated observations.
- **Social studies:** Use a map scale/key and cardinal directions; compare two accounts of a local or regional event; describe how a community makes a shared decision and how goods move from producer to consumer.

## Four-quarter sequence

| Quarter | Sequence, prerequisite, and evidence check |
|---|---|
| 1 — Multiplication foundations | **Prerequisite:** check place value, addition/subtraction and fluent decoding. **Math:** equal groups, arrays, repeated addition and division as sharing. **ELA:** fluency, vocabulary, story structure and evidence-based answers. **Science:** organism needs/habitats. **Social studies:** map skills and local community. **Check:** represent 4×6 with two models, retell a text with a cited detail, label a local map. |
| 2 — Operations and systems | **Prerequisite:** understand equal groups and arrays. **Math:** multiplication/division facts and within-100 problems; add/subtract within 1,000. **ELA:** informational text features, paragraph structure, spelling and revision. **Science:** forces/motion or life-cycle investigation. **Social studies:** local history from artifacts and accounts. **Check:** solve a two-step equal-group problem; write a paragraph using two text facts; record observations over 3 trials. |
| 3 — Fractions and measurement | **Prerequisite:** multiplication models and equal partitioning. **Math:** unit fractions, area/perimeter, measurement and data. **ELA:** compare texts on a topic; write an explanatory response with evidence. **Science:** compare materials/forces using controlled observations. **Social studies:** needs, production, exchange and community roles. **Check:** partition a shape and explain unit fraction; interpret graph; compare two sources' details. |
| 4 — Transfer | **Prerequisite:** revisit fact strategies and paragraph organization. **Math:** mix operations, fractions, area and data contexts. **ELA:** plan, draft, revise and present a short report. **Science:** explain a model or investigation using evidence. **Social studies:** present a local inquiry conclusion. **Check:** independent unfamiliar task in each subject; note support and plan next steps. |

## Beyond the core

Offer drawing/model-making, music and rhythm, dance/team games, gardening, measuring during cooking, library visits, and age-appropriate household planning such as sorting a shopping list.
