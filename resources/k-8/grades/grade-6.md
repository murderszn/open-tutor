# Grade 6 Planning Profile

## Example measurable outcomes

This is a supplemental planning example. Use only the matching [canonical Grade 6 track](../../../curriculum/grade-6/README.md); its audit/scope and sequence takes precedence if this outline differs.

- **Math:** Compute with positive rational numbers; write and evaluate expressions with whole-number exponents; solve ratio/rate and percent problems; find area, surface area, and volume in contexts; summarize a data set with center and spread.
- **Language arts:** Cite relevant evidence and explain how it supports an inference; determine a central idea; write a structured explanatory or argumentative response with a counterpoint or qualification; revise for sentence clarity and source attribution.
- **Science:** Model how cells and body systems support life; explain patterns in weather/climate or forces using data; design a solution against criteria and constraints.
- **Social studies:** Use maps/data to explain a regional pattern; distinguish primary from secondary sources and corroborate a claim; compare civic institutions and economic decisions using evidence.

## Four-quarter sequence

| Quarter | Sequence, prerequisite, and evidence check |
|---|---|
| 1 — Ratios and reading evidence | **Prerequisite:** fraction/decimal operations and paragraph response. **Math:** ratios, rates and unit rate. **ELA:** central idea, inference, evidence, vocabulary. **Science:** cells and systems model. **Social studies:** maps and source reliability. **Check:** solve a ratio situation with table/diagram; cite and explain two text details; label a cell model and explain one function. |
| 2 — Rational numbers and argument | **Prerequisite:** multiplicative comparisons and integer number line. **Math:** fraction/decimal division, negative numbers and coordinate plane. **ELA:** claim/reasons/evidence, counterclaim introduction. **Science:** weather or force patterns from data. **Social studies:** compare civic systems and perspectives. **Check:** place/compare rational numbers and solve a context; write a sourced claim; interpret a graph and state evidence. |
| 3 — Expressions and systems | **Prerequisite:** understand ratio equivalence and operation properties. **Math:** expressions/equations, area/surface area/volume. **ELA:** research across 2–3 sources, paraphrase and cite. **Science:** design test/solution with criteria, controls and repeated measurements. **Social studies:** economic choices, scarcity, trade and regional systems. **Check:** evaluate an expression and solve one-step equation; maintain source notes; describe a variable and limitation in a test. |
| 4 — Data and synthesis | **Prerequisite:** revisit arithmetic gaps and source use. **Math:** statistical distributions and measures; mixed applications. **ELA:** revise and present an evidence-based explanation. **Science:** connect model/data to a claim. **Social studies:** write an inquiry conclusion using 2 sources. **Check:** new data interpretation, short oral/written defense, and portfolio review. |

## Beyond the core

Offer studio art/design, instrument or music production, regular strength/cardio/mobility, meal planning, laundry/care routines, basic budgeting, and age-appropriate community participation.
