# Grade 1 Planning Profile

Use an adult-led, responsive pace. Oral explanation, counters, drawing, dictation, and paper work are all appropriate evidence modes.

This is a supplemental planning example. Use only the matching [canonical Grade 1 track](../../../curriculum/grade-1/README.md); its audit/scope and sequence takes precedence if this outline differs.

## Example measurable outcomes

- **Math:** Solve addition/subtraction story problems within 20 with an equation/model; show that two-digit numbers contain tens and ones; measure/compare lengths with same-size units; read a simple picture graph.
- **Language arts:** Read taught phonics patterns in unfamiliar words and cumulative decodable text; spell words with taught patterns; read connected text with improving accuracy; retell a text with 3 details; write 3 related sentences with capitals/end punctuation after planning.
- **Science:** Compare materials by observable properties, record a question/prediction/result from a fair comparison, and use a drawing plus 2 observations to explain a pattern in light/sound or plant growth.
- **Social studies:** Sequence 4 events with before/after language; identify a map key and compass direction; describe one role of a local public helper and one way people exchange goods/services.

## Four-quarter sequence

| Quarter | Sequence, prerequisite, and evidence check |
|---|---|
| 1 — Number and words | **Prerequisite:** Check counting, letter-sound knowledge, and oral comprehension. **Math:** count/read/write to 120; add/subtract within 10. **ELA:** oral vocabulary, phonemic awareness as needed, systematic cumulative phonics, handwriting, decodable text; read-aloud discussion. **Science:** observe daily sky/weather. **Social studies:** routines, roles, past/present. **Check:** solve a within-10 story with a drawing; decode 8 unfamiliar taught-pattern words; retell a read-aloud. |
| 2 — Tens, sentences, materials | **Prerequisite:** one-to-one counting and taught correspondences. **Math:** compose tens/ones; add/subtract within 20 using strategies. **ELA:** continue phonics, spelling/encoding, fluent rereading of matched text, 3-sentence composition. **Science:** classify materials by 2 properties and compare. **Social studies:** neighborhood map/key and needs/services. **Check:** represent 46 as tens/ones; read new decodable sentences; compare two objects using evidence. |
| 3 — Measure and investigate | **Prerequisite:** understand tens/ones and sentence sequence. **Math:** measure with repeated equal units and organize data. **ELA:** decode taught digraphs/longer patterns from selected sequence; ask/answer questions using text details. **Science:** plan and record a simple comparison. **Social studies:** timeline and community helper inquiry. **Check:** measure an object with units without gaps; write a question/claim with a text detail; explain what changed in test. |
| 4 — Apply and review | **Prerequisite:** check fluency, number combinations, spelling patterns. **Math:** solve mixed within-20 problems, compare two-digit numbers, interpret graph. **ELA:** read a new cumulative passage, retell, compose/revise 3–5 sentences. **Science:** communicate evidence with labeled drawing. **Social studies:** explain one community need and map location. **Check:** fresh task in each domain; record prompts and set grade 2 entry priorities. |

## Beyond the core

Use singing, drawing, collage, hand-clapping rhythms, playground movement, simple cooking, sorting/recycling, and a small plant-care routine. Provide adult supervision for tools, food, and outdoor work.
