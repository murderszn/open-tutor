# Grade 5 Planning Profile

## Example measurable outcomes

This is a supplemental planning example. Use only the matching [canonical Grade 5 track](../../../curriculum/grade-5/README.md); its audit/scope and sequence takes precedence if this outline differs.

- **Math:** Add/subtract fractions with unlike denominators; multiply fractions and divide unit fractions by whole numbers and whole numbers by unit fractions using models; perform decimal operations in contexts; explain volume as unit cubes; graph ordered pairs in the first quadrant.
- **Language arts:** Compare themes or ideas across texts; support an opinion or explanation with organized reasons and source evidence; write multi-paragraph work with a clear structure, transitions, and a checked bibliography; summarize without copying.
- **Science:** Explain matter conservation in a simple model; describe interactions among organisms and environment; use data to compare Earth surface processes or a designed solution.
- **Social studies:** Analyze how geography, migration, and resources shape a U.S. region; sequence historical events using evidence from sources; explain a civic institution and compare economic choices with trade-offs.

## Four-quarter sequence

| Quarter | Sequence, prerequisite, and evidence check |
|---|---|
| 1 — Fractions and evidence | **Prerequisite:** fraction equivalence, multi-digit operations and paragraph structure. **Math:** unlike-denominator fraction addition/subtraction. **ELA:** source notes, summary and evidence. **Science:** ecosystem interactions. **Social studies:** geography and regional comparison. **Check:** solve a fraction context with a visual; summarize a source in own words; diagram one food-web relationship. |
| 2 — Decimals and matter | **Prerequisite:** place-value models and fraction meaning. **Math:** decimals to thousandths, operations and volume. **ELA:** compare two accounts; develop claim/reasons. **Science:** particle/material models and observable changes. **Social studies:** chronology, migration and regional history. **Check:** estimate and solve a decimal problem; cite one difference between accounts; explain a model with data or observation. |
| 3 — Measurement and design | **Prerequisite:** decimal place value, fraction operations and measurement units. **Math:** volume, coordinate grid, fraction division/modeling and multi-step contexts; for example, model 3 ÷ 1/4 as “how many fourths are in 3?” and verify 12. **ELA:** research, outline, draft and revise a multi-paragraph report. **Science:** investigate Earth process or design a solution with criteria. **Social studies:** civics/economics, institutions and trade-offs. **Check:** calculate volume; model 2/3 × 3/4 with a diagram and explain 6/12 = 1/2; keep source notes; test a design against 2 criteria and record results. |
| 4 — Synthesis | **Prerequisite:** review fraction/decimal errors and source attribution. **Math:** mixed fraction/decimal/volume applications. **ELA:** present a researched claim with evidence and bibliography. **Science:** revise explanation based on evidence. **Social studies:** present an evidence-based historical/geographic/civic conclusion. **Check:** use an unfamiliar task and portfolio review to identify middle-grade priorities. |

## Beyond the core

Try music ensemble, illustration/photography, team or individual sports, cooking and meal planning, budgeting for a small project, gardening, and a community or nature observation walk.
