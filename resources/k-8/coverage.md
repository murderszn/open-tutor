# Coverage and Limits

## What this hub provides

- One supplemental planning profile for each grade, K–8, with example outcomes in math, language arts, science, and social studies.
- A four-quarter order with a stated prerequisite or readiness check and concrete evidence to review.
- Arts, movement, practical-life and offline/adult-led suggestions.
- Links to the matching canonical grade track and the repository's existing reference guides.
- Assessment and placement guidance to help an adult choose, adapt, or revisit material.

## What it does not provide

This is not a complete nine-year curriculum, a validated scope and sequence, daily lesson plans, a full set of books or worksheets, intervention services, a state standards crosswalk, or an accredited program. The outcome bullets are adult planning examples, not lesson coverage claims; they do not cover every expectation in any framework. The quarters give a possible sequence, not a guarantee that a learner will master each skill on schedule. Use one matching grade folder only for a learner.

Families should review each track, select or supply full-year instructional materials where needed, and verify local homeschool notice, subject, evaluation, recordkeeping, and attendance rules. Early reading in particular requires a planned, cumulative sequence and regular practice; one starter lesson cannot replace it. Consider qualified evaluation or specialist support if persistent reading, language, math, sensory, or motor difficulties interfere with learning.

| Area | Present now | Important gap |
|---|---|---|
| Grades 4, 5, 7, and 8 | Expanded draft assignment and quiz libraries, including some short project prompts | Not complete teachable lesson sequences. Some briefs lack worked examples, answer keys, explicit prerequisites, or materials/data needed to complete them; see the [eight-item spot review](../../docs/curriculum-spot-review.md). | Grades K, 1, 2, 3, and 6 | One draft starter lesson per subject (math, science, language arts, social studies) in each track. Full scope, pacing, materials, assessment keys, and review remain |

| Math | Existing draft assignment/quiz libraries in the four expanded grades; one starter lesson in each developing grade | No validated, complete K–8 course or comprehensive cumulative practice sequence |
| Science | Existing biology/physics/chemistry topic guides, legacy STEM assignments, and one starter lesson in each developing grade | Coverage is selective; no complete K–8 science course, safety manual, or equipment plan |
| Social studies | Existing government, U.S., wars, and world-facts guides, legacy assignments, and one starter lesson in each developing grade | Coverage is selective; no complete K–8 geography/history/economics/civics sequence or primary-source collection |
| Arts, health, movement, life skills | Suggestions within adult planning profiles | No complete instruction or assessment sequence |

## How to describe progress honestly

Record the date, task/material, amount of adult help, and actual evidence in private storage. Distinguish “introduced,” “practiced,” and “independently demonstrated.” Do not infer a whole subject's mastery from a single project or worksheet, and do not put learner work in this public repository.
